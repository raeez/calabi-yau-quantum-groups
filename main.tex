{\scshape Abstract}

\medskip

\noindent
A Calabi--Yau category $\cA$ of dimension $d$ produces first a native
holomorphic $E_d$-factorisation algebra $\PhiFA_d(\cA)$ on the
Calabi--Yau target $X$. A closed $(d{-}1)$-cycle
$\Sigma_{d-1}\subset X$ and a reference curve $C\subset X$ then produce
the chiral shadow
\[
 \Phi_d^{(\Sigma_{d-1},C)}(\cA)
 =
 \SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\bigl(\PhiFA_d(\cA)\bigr),
\]
with native curve level $E_\infty,E_2,E_1$ at $d=1,2,d\geq3$.
The positive effective geometry inside $\PhiFA_d(\cA)$ gives a
cohomological-Hall positive half $Y^+(X)$; the quantum group is the
Drinfeld double once the Hall pairing, completion, and descent data are
installed. On $\mathbb{C}^3$ and standard toric CY$_3$ charts this is
a constructed hCS/Hall positive-half object, and the local chart
transition maps glue the positive halves. On compact non-toric CY$_3$
targets the critical CoHA, Serre-compatible quasi-NCCR charts, and Hall
double remain conditional on named data: compact critical atlas,
PBW/Hall pairing, integral form, stable-envelope transport, and
Drinfeld-double descent. The seed calculation is $X=K3\times E$:
\[
 \kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}=0,\qquad
 \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Hodge}}=0,\qquad
 \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}=3,\qquad
 \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Delta_5)=5,\qquad
 \kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}=24.
\]
These five measurements cannot be collapsed to one invariant; the false
additive formula
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}=\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}+\chi(\cO_{\mathrm{fiber}})$
already fails at $N=1$ in the total-space convention. Their separation
forces the three-tier architecture of the volume: CY-intrinsic data,
Stage--1 native factorisation data, and Stage--2 chiral specialisations.
At $d=3$ six-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons supplies the physical
realisation of $\PhiFA_3$ on the constructed formal and object-level loci;
the one-loop obstruction is the quartic
$\int_X\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{ad}}(A(F_A)^3)$, not a cubic Casimir, and
ordinary three-dimensional Chern--Simons knot theory is not the source
of this six-dimensional avatar. The $\mathbb{C}^3$ specialisation recovers
$\CoHA(\mathbb{C}^3)=Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$, the positive
half and not $\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$; $\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$
appears only after Drinfeld double/centre and vacuum evaluation. The
$(K3,E)$ specialisation is the Hall--Drinfeld/Borcherds construction
problem whose Borcherds-product value is the Gritsenko--Nikulin
denominator $\Delta_5$. The reduced-DT scalar uses
\(\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}=D_5^2\) with \(D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5\),
whereas the unnormalised Igusa square is
\(\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_5^2\). Across the
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery atlas the
universal Borcherds weight identity
\[
 \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2
\]
holds with the cover group selected by weight integrality. The
manuscript constructs the functorial input, the operadic level, the
K3 / K3 x E refinement, and the landscape of examples; the unconstructed
compact extensions are stated as exact comparison maps, not as
ambiguities in the architecture.

The compact CY$_3$ lane has the same separation of proved object and
comparison map. Generic smooth projective CY$_3$ targets carry Joyce's
vertex algebra $V_X$ and KPS orientation data; MNOP becomes the unique
$E_2$-centre automorphism of the $E_3$ factorisation algebra on the
constructed module surfaces. The $S^3$-framing on generic compact
CY$_3$ Lagrangians is chain-level on the Costello--Paquette
defect-chiral model, while the comparison to a global
Hall--Borcherds/Drinfeld object is an additional bridge. The K3
Yangian refinement is therefore not a single Yangian: the proved abelian
Mukai branch, the $\mathfrak{so}(4,20)$ representation-target
trichotomy, and the completed Hall--Drinfeld/Borcherds envelope occupy
different strata.

\begin{center}
{\scshape Structural Synopsis}
\end{center}

\medskip

\noindent\itshape
The $d$-indexed correspondence $\{\Phi_d\}_{d \geq 1}$ is a
per-$d$ family of symmetric-monoidal assignments rather than
a single category-theoretic functor
(Remark~\ref{rem:phi-not-unified-functor}),
\[
 \Phi_d \colon \CY_d\text{-}\Cat \longrightarrow
 \En\text{-}\mathrm{ChirAlg}(\cM_d),
 \qquad
 n = \begin{cases}\infty, & d = 1, \\ 2, & d = 2, \\
 1, & d \ge 3,\end{cases}
\]
characterised by four universal properties:
(U1)~\emph{Hochschild pullback},
$B^{\mathrm{ord}}(\Phi_d(\cC)) \simeq \mathrm{CC}_\bullet(\cC)$
as factorisation coalgebras;
(U2)~\emph{CY-morphism functoriality}, wall-crossing in
$\cC$ pulls to $R$-matrix gauge transformations on $\Phi_d(\cC)$;
(U3)~\emph{Drinfeld-centre compatibility},
$\cZ(\Rep^{\Eone}(\Phi_d(\cC))) \simeq
\Rep^{\Etwo}(\Zder(\Phi_d(\cC)))$ at $d \ge 3$;
(U4)~\emph{standard-input recovery},
$\Phi_1(\Coh(E)) = $ lattice VOA at $d = 1$,
$\Phi_2(D^b(K3)) = $ Mukai Heisenberg at $d = 2$,
	$\CoHA(\C^3)=Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$ at the framed toric
	CY$_3$ positive-half comparison.
The four-step recipe (cyclic $\Ainf \to$ Lie conformal
$\to$ factorisation envelope $\to$ $\mathbb{S}^d$-enhanced
quantisation) proves existence and uniqueness.
\textbf{At $d \ge 3$, $\Phi_d(\cC)$ is $\Eone$-chiral; the
$\Etwo$-braiding lives on $\cZ(\Rep^{\Eone}(\Phi_d(\cC)))$
only}, never on the chiral algebra itself
($\pi_1(\Conf_2(\R^d)) = 0$ for $d \ge 3$).

\textbf{CY-A} ($d \in \{1,2\}$ unconditional; $d = 3$
unconditional on toric/formal CY$_3$; $d \ge 4$ via
stabilisation). $\Phi_d$ exists at every $d$. At $d = 2$ via
Kontsevich--Vlassopoulos $\mathbb{S}^2$-framing (CY-A$_2$,
unconditional). At $d = 3$ via the $(\infty,1)$-categorical
vanishing $\HH^{-2}_{\Eone}(\cC) = 0$ and Goodwillie
contractibility of the $E_3$-lifting space (CY-A$_3$,
Theorem~\ref{thm:derived-framing-obstruction}); unconditional
for toric and formal CY$_3$ ($\C^3$, conifold, local $\bP^2$,
local $\bP^1 \times \bP^1$) and realized on the compact
non-formal instances (quintic, $K3 \times E$, generic
complete-intersection) through the three named inputs:
Tradler-style $A_\infty$-strictification of $\HH_\bullet$,
chain-level $B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{TCFT}} \simeq
B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{naive}}$, and Yukawa-curvature connectivity.
At $d \ge 4$
via $\Eone$-stabilisation
(Theorem~\ref{thm:e1-stabilization-cy}); the effective framing
obstruction $\Obs_{\mathrm{eff}}(d)$ is the colimit of the complex
tower $\pi_d(BU) = \mathrm{KU}^{-d}$ (2-periodic: $\Z$ at even $d$,
$0$ at odd $d$) and the symplectic refinement
$\pi_d(B\Sp) \subset \pi_d(BO) = \mathrm{KO}^{-d}$ (8-periodic, real
Bott). $\Obs_{\mathrm{eff}}(d) = 0$ at $d \bmod 8 \in \{1, 3, 7\}$,
is $\Z$-valued at every even $d$ (from $\pi_d(BU)$), and is
$\Z/2$-valued at $d \equiv 5 \pmod 8$ (from the Sp-refinement, since
$\pi_d(BU) = 0$ at odd $d$).
The restricted lift $\Psi|_{d \in \{2,3\}}$ surjects onto
CY-$d$-derivable Gritsenko--Nikulin reflective
Borcherds--Kac--Moody algebras with input lattice of
signature $(n,2)$ for $n \in \{1, 10, 18, 19\}$ (paramodular
Siegel branch); the Fake-Monster row on $\mathrm{II}_{25,1}$
(signature $(25, 1)$) sits in a separate $\Psi$-sibling branch
with doubled-Leech-rank convention, and the Monster row on
$\mathrm{II}_{1,1}$ Cartan (signature $(1, 1)$) enters through
the $j$-invariant input of Borcherds $1992$.
The $23$ non-Leech Niemeier Borcherds--Kac--Moody algebras
(including $\mathfrak g^{(24A_1)}$ with defining lattice
$\mathrm{II}_{25,1}$ of rank $26$) refute unrestricted
$\Psi$-surjectivity onto Gritsenko--Nikulin
Siegel-automorphic-product Borcherds--Kac--Moody algebras;
unrestricted surjectivity onto the full Gritsenko--Nikulin list
fails already at the $24A_1$ Niemeier row.

\textbf{CY-B} ($d = 3$, toric/formal unconditional; compact
conditional on CY-A$_3$ hypotheses).
$\Eone$-Koszul duality on $\cA = \Phi_3(\cC)$ induces an

Verdier spectral functor, identified page-by-page on the bar
spectral sequence.

\textbf{CY-D} (proved, $d = 2$; programme at $d \ge 3$).
At $d = 2$,
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(\Phi_2(\cC)) = \chi^{\CY}(\cC)$ via
Serre duality $\mathbb{S}_\cC = [2]$, which kills the

$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}} = \chi(\cO_X)$ fails: Serre's pairing
$h^{0,q} = h^{0,d-q}$ forces $\chi(\cO_X) = 0$ at every odd

($\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}(E) = 1$,
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3 \times E) = 3$).
Theorem~\ref{thm:kappa-stratification-by-d} records a
Beauville--Bogomolov tri-stratum at $d \ge 3$ (odd-$d$ Serre
stratum, strict-CY even-$d$, holomorphic-symplectic) with
distinct mechanisms on each branch.

\[
 \ell_X/\ell_Y \;=\; c_+(L_X)/c_+(L_Y)
\]
holds universally across the four Fricke rows Monster / Enriques /

universal identity (Chapter~\ref{ch:k3e-bkm},
Corollary~\ref{cor:bkm-conway-not-monster-not-fakemonster}).

\textbf{CY-C} (six-route convergence). The quantum vertex chiral group
$G(X)$ is assembled from the positive half, Drinfeld double, and

intertwiners
$\beta_{13}, \beta_{34}, \beta_{45}, \beta_{56}, \beta_{61}$
(Proposition~\ref{prop:cy-c-pentagon-colimit}), with $R_2$
(Borcherds branch) as source. The Conway module
$V^{s\natural} = A(\Lambda_{24})^+ \oplus A(\Lambda_{24})^{\mathrm{tw},+}$
(Duncan $2007$ \emph{Duke Math.~J.}~$139$, \S3--4) at central charge

\emph{Invent.~Math.}~$172$ Thm~$3.2$), equivalently the

\part{\texorpdfstring{The Categorical Input: Calabi--Yau Categories and Cyclic $\Ainf$}{The Categorical Input: Calabi-Yau Categories and Cyclic A-inf}}
\label{part:cy-categories}
\label{part:I}

\noindent
A Calabi--Yau category of complex dimension $d$ is a smooth proper
$\Ainf$-category $\cC$ over a field $k$ of characteristic zero,
equipped with a non-degenerate cyclic class
$[\sigma] \in \HC^-_d(\cC)$ whose image
$\Tr_\cC \colon \HH_d(\cC) \to k$ is a non-degenerate trace and whose
data encode the $\bS^d$-framing of the Hochschild complex. Write
$\CY_d\text{-}\Cat$ for the category of CY-$d$ categories.

\begin{theorem}[PTVV {\rm \cite{PTVV2013}}]
\label{thm:part1-ptvv}
The moduli stack $\cM(\cC)$ of objects in a CY$_d$-category $\cC$
carries a canonical $(2-d)$-shifted symplectic structure.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Gerstenhaber bracket of degree $1-d$ {\rm
(Deligne; Kontsevich--Tamarkin)}]
\label{thm:part1-gerstenhaber}
The Hochschild cohomology $\HH^\bullet(\cC)$ of a CY$_d$-category $\cC$
carries a canonical $E_2$-algebra structure refined by the cyclic
class $[\sigma]$ to a Gerstenhaber bracket of degree $1 - d$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Tier-(i) intrinsics]
\label{thm:part1-tier-i}
The following are intrinsic to $\cC$, before any Stage-$1$
construction is invoked:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item the Mukai pairing on $K_0(\cC)$ \cite{Mukai1987};
\item the Hodge supertrace
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Hodge}}(\cC) = \sum_q (-1)^q h^{0, q}(X)$
when $\cC = D^b\Coh(X)$ for a compact CY$_d$ variety $X$;
\item the categorical Euler characteristic
$\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}(\cC) = \chi(\cO_X)$;
\item the K-theory class $[\cC] \in K_0$.
\end{enumerate}
At $d = 2$ on $X = K3$, the Mukai lattice
$\widetilde{H}^*(K3, \Z) = \mathrm{II}_{4, 20}$ has rank $24$ and
signature $(4, 20)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{definition}[Equivariance strata of $\CY_d\text{-}\Cat$]
\label{def:part1-equivariance-strata}
The four equivariance strata of $\CY_d\text{-}\Cat$ are
\begin{description}
\item[Toric $T^d$.] $\C^d$, conifold, local $\bP^2$, local
$\bP^1 \!\!\times\!\! \bP^1$.
\item[Reduced $\C^\times \!\!+\! \mathrm{Aut}$.] $K3$, $K3 \!\times\! E$,
abelian threefolds, K3 fibrations.
\item[Orbifold inertia $I(X/G)$.] $[\C^d / \Gamma]$ for
$\Gamma \subset \mathrm{SU}(d)$ finite; Mathieu $M_{24}$ family.
\item[Lattice-polarised period domain.] CY$_d$ moduli polarised by a
signature-$(2, n)$ lattice $L$; Borcherds-lifted $K3$;
Gritsenko--Nikulin and Igusa families.
\end{description}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Shift law]
\label{thm:part1-shift-law}
Theorem~\ref{thm:part1-ptvv} and Dunn--Lurie additivity
\cite{LurieHA} fix the dimensional dispatch
\[
 (d, \mathrm{shift}, E_n^{\mathrm{cl}})
 \;\in\;
 \{(2, -2, \Etwo),\; (3, -1, \Eone),\; (4, 0, E_0),\; (5, +1, E_5\text{-Poisson})\}
\]
of the operadic level forced by the CY pairing at dimension $d$. This
fixes the Stage-$1$ output level on the target $X$ and, after
Proposition~\ref{prop:holomorphic-dunn-lurie}, the Stage-$2$ shadow
level $n(d) = \infty, 2, 1$ at $d = 1, 2, d \geq 3$ on the curve $C$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Hochschild as receptacle]
\label{thm:part1-hochschild-receptacle}
The triple
$(\HH_\bullet(\cC),\, \HH^\bullet(\cC),\, \mathfrak{L}_\cC)$ with
$\mathfrak{L}_\cC = (\HH_{\bullet+1}(\cC),\, \{\cdot_\lambda \cdot\},\, \partial)$
the Lie conformal algebra carrying the Gerstenhaber bracket and the
$S^1$-action carries simultaneously the $\bS^d$-framing (from
$[\sigma]$), the $(2-d)$-shifted symplectic structure
(Theorem~\ref{thm:part1-ptvv}), and the Gerstenhaber bracket of degree
$1-d$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:part1-gerstenhaber}). This triple is consumed
by the Stage-$1$ functor $\PhiFA_d$ of Part~\ref{part:bridge}
(Definition~\ref{def:stage1-functor}) via Kontsevich--Tamarkin
$E_d$-formality and Costello--Gwilliam--Li holomorphic locality on
the target $X$.
\end{theorem}

$E_d$-holomorphic factorisation algebras on the target $X$, and the
Stage-$2$ specialisation $\mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Sigma_{d-1}, C}$
along a complex $(d-1)$-dimensional submanifold $\Sigma_{d-1} \subset X$
restricted to a reference curve $C \subset X$. The level $n(d)$ on $C$
takes the values $\infty, 2, 1$ at $d = 1, 2, d \geq 3$ respectively.

\begin{definition}[Stage-$1$ holomorphic factorisation algebra]
\label{def:stage1-functor}
For each $d \geq 1$, the Stage-$1$ assignment
\[
 \PhiFA_d \colon \CY_d\text{-}\Cat \longrightarrow E_d\text{-}\mathrm{HolFA}(X)
\]
sends a smooth proper Calabi--Yau $A_\infty$-category $\cC$ of complex
dimension $d$, equipped with cyclic class
$[\sigma] \in \HC^-_d(\cC)$, to the canonical $E_d$-holomorphic
factorisation algebra on the target $X$ assembled from the
Gerstenhaber bracket of degree $1 - d$ on $\HH^\bullet(\cC)$ via
Kontsevich--Tamarkin $E_d$-formality
\cite{Kontsevich1999, Tamarkin2007} and the Costello--Gwilliam--Li
holomorphic-locality descent of the BV quantisation
\cite{CostelloGwilliam2017, CostelloLi2016, CostelloLi2020}. The
assignment is functorial at fixed $d$ and canonical up to the
$\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsor of formality trivialisations
\cite{Willwacher2015}; on the formal locus the construction is
unconditional, and on a compact non-formal target it is the chain-level
framing, anomaly-completion, and Costello correction recorded at
Theorem~\ref{thm:cya3-existence-rigidity}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Stage-$2$ chart specialisation]
\label{def:stage2-functor}
For chart datum $(\Sigma_{d-1}, C)$ with $\Sigma_{d-1} \subset X$ a
complex $(d-1)$-dimensional submanifold and $C \subset X$ a reference
curve, the Stage-$2$ specialisation is the holomorphic factorisation
homology
\[
 \mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Sigma_{d-1}, C}(\cF)
 \;=\;
 \bigl(\!\textstyle\int^{\mathrm{hol}}_{\Sigma_{d-1}} \cF\bigr)\bigr|_C
 \quad \in \quad
 E_{n(d)}\text{-}\mathrm{ChirAlg}(C)
\]
of $\cF \in E_d\text{-}\mathrm{HolFA}(X)$ over $\Sigma_{d-1}$ restricted
to $C$, in the sense of Francis--Gaitsgory and Ayala--Francis
\cite{FrancisGaitsgory2012, AyalaFrancis2015}.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Holomorphic Dunn--Lurie additivity]
\label{prop:holomorphic-dunn-lurie}
Restriction along the cycle $\Sigma_{d-1}$ drops the holomorphic
operadic level by $\dim_{\C} \Sigma_{d-1} = d - 1$:
\[
 \!\textstyle\int^{\mathrm{hol}}_{\Sigma_{d-1}} E_d
 \;\simeq\;
 E_{d - (d-1)}
 \;=\;
 E_1.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Native operadic level on the curve]
\label{thm:native-en-level}
$\Phi^{(\Sigma_{d-1}, C)}_d(\cC)$ is an $E_{n(d)}$-chiral algebra on $C$
with
\[
 n(d)
 \;=\;
 \begin{cases}
 \infty, & d = 1, \\
 2, & d = 2, \\
 1, & d \geq 3.
 \end{cases}
\]
At $d \geq 3$ the $\Etwo$-braided refinement lives on the derived chiral
centre,
\[
 \cZ\bigl(\Rep^{\Eone}(\Phi^{(\Sigma_{d-1}, C)}_d(\cC))\bigr)
 \;\simeq\;
 \Rep^{\Etwo}\bigl(\Zder(\Phi^{(\Sigma_{d-1}, C)}_d(\cC))\bigr),
\]
the level-$3$ bulk of Part~\ref{part:bulk}; the level-$2$ chiral
algebra on $C$ itself is $\Eone$ (Ben-Zvi--Francis--Nadler;
Corollary~\ref{cor:zder-drinfeld}).
\end{theorem}

Two distinct specialisations $(\Sigma_{d-1}, C) \neq (\Sigma'_{d-1}, C')$
on the same Stage-$1$ output produce two distinct $E_{n(d)}$-chiral
shadows of one $\PhiFA_d(\cC)$. The six routes to $G(K3 \times E)$ are
six $(\Sigma_2, C)$-specialisations of the single
$\PhiFA_3(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E))$, not six $\Phi_3^{(\Sigma_2, C)}$-applications
(Remark~\ref{rem:multiple-e1-specialisations}). The collection
$\{\Phi^{(\Sigma_{d-1}, C)}_d\}_{d, (\Sigma, C)}$ across $(d, \Sigma, C)$
is a two-parameter correspondence programme; the collection
$\{\PhiFA_d\}_{d \geq 1}$ at fixed $d$ is a functor on $\CY_d$-categories
(Remark~\ref{rem:phi-d-chain-vs-inf1-functor}).

\begin{theorem}[PTVV shift law]
\label{thm:ptvv-shift-law-platonic}
The native operadic level $n(d)$ is forced by the PTVV $(2-d)$-shifted
symplectic structure on $\cM(\cC)$ \cite{PTVV2013} together with
Proposition~\ref{prop:holomorphic-dunn-lurie}:
\[
 (d, \mathrm{shift}, E_n^{\mathrm{cl}})
 \;\in\;
 \{(2, -2, \Etwo),\; (3, -1, \Eone),\; (4, 0, E_0),\; (5, +1, E_5\text{-Poisson})\}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Two periodicities, distinct]
\label{rem:bott-periodicities}
The complex K-theory periodicity $\pi_d(BU) \cong \pi_{d+2}(BU)$ has
period $2$. The real K-theory periodicity $\pi_d(BO) \cong \pi_{d+8}(BO)$
has period $8$ and contributes a $\Z/2$ at $d \equiv 5 \pmod 8$,
locating the Fake Monster at the $d = 5$ entry of
Theorem~\ref{thm:ptvv-shift-law-platonic} on $K3 \times K3 \times E$ via
the Dunn--Lurie decomposition $E_5 \simeq E_2 \otimes E_2 \otimes E_1$.
\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[Chart datum]

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Four equivalent realisations of $\PhiFA_d$]
\label{thm:four-lanes}
On the verified locus, $\PhiFA_d(\cC)$ admits four equivalent
realisations as an $E_d$-holomorphic factorisation algebra:
\begin{description}
\item[\rm L1, mathematical perturbative]
The Costello--Gwilliam--Li factorisation-algebra quantisation
\cite{CostelloGwilliam2017, CostelloLi2016}, with the

\item[\rm L2, $5$d holomorphic Chern--Simons]

BV action on $\R \times \C^2$ to the Yangian VOA

\item[\rm L3, $6$d holomorphic Chern--Simons]

\cite{CostelloLi2020}, with one-loop obstruction

\item[\rm L4, mixed holomorphic-topological strings]

\end{description}
The four realisations agree on the locus where two or more apply.
\end{theorem}

 \cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A_X)

 Y^{+}(X)

\begin{theorem}[Centre identification]
\label{thm:part3-centre-identification}
For $A_X$ an $\Eone$-chiral algebra on $C$,
\[
 \cZ\!\bigl(\Rep^{\Eone}(A_X)\bigr)
 \;\simeq\;
 \Rep^{\Etwo}\!\bigl(\cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A_X)\bigr)
 \;\simeq\;
 \Rep^{\Etwo}\!\bigl(G(X)\bigr).
\]
The first equivalence is Ben-Zvi--Francis--Nadler 2010
\cite{BenZviFrancisNadler2010}; the second is the Yang--Zhao
identification of the Drinfeld double with the centre.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Chain fusion {\rm

of an open factorisation dg-category on the tangential log curve

$\bS^d$-framing. The conjecture is verified at $\C^3$, local $\bP^2$,
the resolved conifold, and $K3 \times E$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:bulk-chain-fusion-verified}).
\end{theorem}

\part{\texorpdfstring{The Seven Faces of $r_{\mathrm{CY}}(z)$}{The Seven Faces of r-CY(z)}}
\label{part:connections}
\label{part:V}

The seven faces of $r_{\mathrm{CY}}(z)$ are the
level-$2$ cross-axis structure of the two-stage factorisation
$\Phi_d = \SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1}, C}\circ\PhiFA_d$.
A single chosen Stage-$1$ datum
$\cF_X = \PhiFA_d(\cC) \in \EdHolFA(X)$
admits two orthogonal seven-fold decompositions: the
\emph{three-tier vertical} stratification along the universal-arrow
levels~$0$, $1$, $2$, and the \emph{seven-presentation horizontal}
slicing by algebraic language at level~$2$. The R-matrix
$r_{\mathrm{CY}}(z)\in\cA_\cC\otimes\cA_\cC\,(\!(z)\!)$
is the binary collision residue of the universal Maurer--Cartan
element of the Stage-$2$ chiral algebra; it organises the entire
output side.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Three tiers along the universal-arrow vertical.}
Each face occupies one tier of the two-stage factorisation:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \emph{Tier~\textup{(i)}, CY-datum intrinsic} (level~$0$, chart-free,
ambient ordinary): invariants of $(X, \cC, \Omega_\cC)$ alone. The Mukai
pairing on $\widetilde H^*(K3) = \mathrm{II}_{4, 20}$, the Hodge supertrace
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Hodge}}(X) = \sum_q (-1)^q h^{0,q}(X)$,
the holomorphic volume class $[\Omega_\cC]$, the
$\Ainf$-coassociator $\delta^{(3)}$ at degree~$3$ of the shadow tower.
Read directly off $\cC_X$.
\item \emph{Tier~\textup{(ii)}, Stage-$1$ invariant of $\cF_X$}
(level~$1$, chart upstream of $(\Sigma_{d-1}, C)$, ambient
$\EdHolFA(X)$): properties of the canonical
$\PhiFA_d(\cC)\in\EdHolFA(X)$ before any specialisation. The K3-fibre
rank $\kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}(K3\times E) = 24$, the Atiyah class
$\At(T_X)$, the Yukawa $Y_3 \in H^{0,3}(X)$, the
Costello--Li BCOV curving
$\alpha_{\mathrm{BCOV}} = (\chi(X)/24)\cdot[\Omega_X]^{0,1}$, the
Maulik--Okounkov stable envelope on the derived moduli of the CY$_d$
target, the Costello $5d$ hCS tree amplitude on the $4d$ twistor
background of the CY$_3$ target.
\item \emph{Tier~\textup{(iii)}, $(\Sigma_{d-1}, C)$-specialisation}
(level~$2$, chart-dependent, ambient $\EnHolFA(C)$ at the recalled
depth $n(d)$): the genuine Stage-$2$ siblings, all distinct
$\SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1}, C}$-evaluations of the same $\cF_X$. The BKM face
$(K3, E)\mapsto\fg_{\Delta_5}$ with $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}} = 5$; the
Niemeier $23$-twist family indexed by Conway--Norton root data; the

\begin{theorem}[Universal Borcherds-weight identity {\rm
(Borcherds 1995 \emph{Invent.\ Math.}~$120$;
Gritsenko 1999 Theorem~$1.2$)}]
\label{thm:part6-universal-borcherds-weight}
For every even Lorentzian lattice $L$ of signature $(b^+, b^-)$ with
$b^+ \geq 2$ and every Borcherds singular theta lift $\Phi_N$ on $L$
attached to a weak Jacobi form of principal-part datum $c_N(0)$, the
Siegel weight is
\[
 \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N) \;=\; \frac{c_N(0)}{2}.
\]
The identity is universal in the input.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Two scopes of the universal identity]
\label{thm:part6-two-scopes}
The CHL ladder $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ on $K3 \times E$ at $d = 3$
produces the monotone tuple
$(\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N))_N = (5, 4, 3, 2, 1)$ on its
paramodular Borcherds witnesses. The Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 8-form
catalogue produces the non-monotone tuple
$(5, 2, 3, 1, 2, \tfrac12, \tfrac32, 1)$ on its triple indexing
$(t, N; k)$, with multiplier-system half-integer weights of
$v_\eta^3 \times v_H$-tensor type. Both scopes satisfy
Theorem~\ref{thm:part6-universal-borcherds-weight} on their own
indexing sets.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Dimension-stratified BKM siblings]
\label{thm:part6-bkm-siblings}
The K3 BKM $\fg_{\Delta_5}$ at $d = 3$ on the rank-$3$ hyperbolic
sublattice $\Lambda^{2, 1}_{\mathrm{II}} \subset \Lambda^{3, 2}$ gives
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Delta_5) = 5$. The Borcherds Monster Lie
algebra $\mathfrak{m}$ at $d = 3$ on $\mathrm{II}_{1, 1}$ sits at the
scope boundary $b^+ = 1$, with $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\mathfrak{m}) = 0$
read from the genus-$0$ property of the Hauptmodul $J(\tau)$. The Fake
Monster $\fg_{\mathrm{FM}}$ at $d = 5$ on $\mathrm{II}_{25, 1}$ via
the Dunn--Lurie additivity $E_5 \simeq E_2 \otimes E_2 \otimes E_1$ on
$K3 \times K3 \times E$ gives $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_{12}) = 12$.
The Conway and Leech moonshine modules bridge $d = 4$, with no
constructed CY$_4$ host on its own.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Saito--Kurokawa central-value residue]
\label{thm:part6-saito-kurokawa-residue}
The spinor $L$-function of the Saito--Kurokawa lift attached to
$\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}} = \Delta_5^2$ has a simple pole at the
Andrianov central value $s_{\mathrm{Andr}} = 10$, related to the
programme central value $s_{\mathrm{prog}} = 5/2$ on $\Delta_5$ by the
rescale factor~$4$; the residue is
\[
 \mathrm{Res}_{s = s_{\mathrm{Andr}}}
 L_{\mathrm{spin}}(s, \Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}, \mathrm{spin})
 \;=\;
 -15120 \cdot a_{10}(g) \cdot \Omega^-(g),
\]
with $g = \Delta \cdot E_6 \in S_{18}(\mathrm{SL}_2(\Z))$ and
$\Omega^-(g)$ the Manin minus-period of the rank-two motive $M(g)$.
\end{theorem}

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Front~1: nonabelian $Y(\mathfrak{g}_{K3})$.}
Abelian: $24$ Heisenberg generators, Mukai-signature Serre constructed.
Nonabelian extension: matrix Miura transform on
$V_k(\widehat{\fsl}_2)$; $\fsl_2$ Serre constraint
\[
 P_2(D)
 \;=\;
 \sum_\alpha \alpha^2 \bigl(k + \tfrac{c_\alpha}{D}\bigr)
 \;=\;
 0,
 \qquad
 D = 3,
\]
($P_2 = 0$ at leading order proved; exact-vanishing conjectural).
The Yangian candidate sitting over the Mukai lattice
$H^{\mathrm{ev}}(K3, \Z)$ of signature $(4,20)$ is
$Y_\hbar(\mathfrak{so}(4, 20))$, not $Y(\mathfrak{gl}(4|20))$ and not
Kac's $\osp(4|20)$: the Mukai form is symmetric indefinite
(orthogonal), symmetric on both even and odd parts, hence the
structure-preserving Lie algebra is $\mathfrak{so}(4, 20)$. A
Hodge-parity $\Z/2$-super-extension $Y_\hbar(\mathfrak{so}(4|20))$
(symmetric on both parts, non-Kac) is available when the
Hodge even/odd split on $H^{\mathrm{ev}}(K3, \Z)$ is imposed;
Kac's $\osp(4|20)$ is not the envelope because $\osp$ requires a
symplectic form on the odd part. The rank-$(4,20)$ reflection
equation (Arnaudon--Cramp\'e--Doikou--Frappat--Ragoucy) and the
Borcherds / BKM denominator identity with the Molev--Ragoucy
reflection Berezinian are the open directions.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Front~2: geometric Langlands.}
At critical level $k = -h^\vee$:
\[
 \mathrm{Fun}\bigl(\mathrm{Op}_{G^\vee}(D)\bigr)
 \;\simeq\;
 \mathfrak{z}\bigl(\widehat{\mathfrak{g}}_{-h^\vee}\bigr)
 \qquad
 \text{(Feigin--Frenkel centre)}.
\]
CY Langlands conjectural reformulation: $\Phi$ is a correspondence
programme with distinct bulk and boundary legs, not a single
functor on the nose; the boundary leg attaches a chiral algebra
$\cA$ to $\cC$, the bulk leg attaches the derived centre
$Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA)$, and the Langlands kernel is
the Drinfeld centre $\cZ(\Rep^{\Eone}(\cA))$ on the boundary side.
The correspondence carries a universal trace identity linking the
Vol~I Koszul conductor $K = c + c^! = -c_{\mathrm{ghost}}(\mathrm{BRST})$
on the chiral side to the Borcherds-weight invariant
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ on the automorphic side;
this is a reflection identity across the two conductor families,
not a scalar equality. ``Langlands $=$ Koszul duality'' is the
conjectural identification of this correspondence with the
Koszul-duality $\cA \leftrightarrow \cA^!$ of Part~\ref{part:connections}.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Front~3: Zamolodchikov tetrahedron.}
Factorisation at charge~$2$ gives $S^{\mathrm{fact}}$ satisfying the
quantum Yang--Baxter equation pairwise but failing tetrahedron at
$O(\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^2)$. Closure:
\[
 S^{\mathrm{corr}} \;=\; S^{\mathrm{fact}} + \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^2 T,
 \qquad
 T \in \ker\bigl(\text{extended ZTE deformation complex}\bigr),
\]
with explicit $T$ (rank~$35/36$, $1$-dimensional kernel computed
entry-by-entry). Full operadic closure outstanding.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Front~4: $\Sp_4(\Z)$ modularity of the $E_3$
$S$-matrix.}
Igusa cusp form $\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}} \in M_{10}(\Sp_4(\Z))$ conjecturally
controls the $E_3$ $S$-matrix via Fourier--Jacobi expansion:
\[
 \Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}(\tau_1, z, \tau_2)^{-1}
 \;=\;
 \sum_{m \ge -1} \psi_m(\tau_1, z) \, p^m,
 \qquad
 p = e^{2\pi i \tau_2},
\]
each Fourier--Jacobi coefficient $\psi_m$ a Jacobi form of weight
$10$ and index~$m$; $m = 1$ gives the shadow obstruction at
$\Theta^{E_2}_{\cA, 1}$.

Upward: abelian $\to$ nonabelian (Front~1).
Outward-d3: K3 family $\to$ general compact CY$_3$ (chart-gluing

Outward-d4: CY$_3$ $\to$ $p_1$-twisted CY$_4$; on $K3 \times K3$

twisted-current double algebra $Y_{p_1}(X)$ is the natural target.
Downward: chiral algebra $\to$ representation theory
(CY-C: CY categories produce braided monoidal categories
equivalent to those of classical quantum groups through the centre
comparison).

``Langlands $=$ Koszul duality''.
