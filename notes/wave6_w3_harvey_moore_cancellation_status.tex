\subsection*{Wave-6 W$3/20$ attack-heal: Harvey--Moore cancellation
status stratified, chain-level versus $(\infty,1)$-categorical}
\label{subsec:wave6-w3-harvey-moore-cancellation-status}

The V$5/10$ heal in
\S\ref{subsec:wave-m-v5-dim1-alone-does-not-force} relied on
``Harvey--Moore $1/4$-BPS cancellation'' to pin $\cZ_{\mathrm{Het}}$
to the $\nu_{\Delta_5}$-eigenline at step $(II)$ of
Theorem~\ref{thm:delta5-forcing-three-ingredients-wave-m-v5}. Read
uncritically, that citation treats HM-cancellation as a single
proved theorem at the same status as Gritsenko--Nikulin $1997$
(linear-algebra) or Oberdieck $2018$ (algebraic-geometric). This
is not the case. HM-cancellation has several logically distinct
components at different rigour tiers. This Wave-6 W$3/20$
attack-heal rectifies the conflation and declares the status
precisely.

\paragraph{(a) Attack: ``Harvey--Moore $1/4$-BPS cancellation is a
single proved theorem''.}
The following reading, circulating as shorthand, elides the
stratification:
\begin{displayquote}
(FALSE) \emph{Harvey--Moore (\emph{Nucl.\ Phys.\ B}~$463$~[1996],
\S\S$4$--$5$; refined in \emph{Commun.\ Math.\ Phys.}~$197$~[1998]
$489$--$519$) prove a single theorem that \textup{(a)} the
Heterotic $1/4$-BPS index on $T^6$ is $\cN = 4$-protected against
worldsheet instantons; \textup{(b)} the resulting weight-$5$
automorphic lift carries precisely the multiplier $\nu_{\Delta_5}$
and not $\mathbf{1}$ or $\mathrm{sgn}$; \textup{(c)} the
Arthur-packet transformation of this lift under $\Sp_4(\Z)$ is
explicit; \textup{(d)} this package qualifies as an unconditional
mathematical theorem at the same status as Gritsenko--Nikulin
$1997$.}
\end{displayquote}
Four obstructions separate these four strands.

\emph{Obstruction $1$ (protection theorem).} ``The $1/4$-BPS index
is $\cN = 4$-protected against worldsheet $\alpha'$-corrections''
is a \emph{physics-level} non-renormalisation argument.
Harvey--Moore invoke $\cN = 4$ supersymmetry of the heterotic
$T^6$ compactification so that only BPS states contribute to the
$1$-loop threshold integral and higher $\alpha'$-corrections vanish
on the $1/4$-BPS sector. This is rigorous \emph{modulo} standard
physics assumptions: a well-defined heterotic worldsheet path
integral, absence of supersymmetry anomalies, convergence of the
BPS lattice sum after Narain theta unfolding. None of these has
been turned into a theorem in the sense of a constructed quantum
field theory. What Harvey--Moore prove \emph{mathematically} is
the Borcherds-product unfolding
of~\eqref{eq:hm-heterotic-integrand}, \emph{assuming} that
integrand is the right object; the protection statement is
upstream of the mathematical work.

\emph{Obstruction $2$ (character emergence).} Harvey--Moore do
\emph{not} compute the multiplier $\nu_{\Delta_5}$ abstractly from
representation theory of $\Sp_4(\Z)$; they \emph{compare} their
Borcherds-unfolded $q$-expansion to Gritsenko's $\Delta_5$
(Gritsenko $1994$, \emph{St.\ Petersburg Math.\ J.}) and observe
matching Fourier coefficients. The character-matching
$\cZ_{\mathrm{Het}} \in S_5(\Sp_4(\Z), \nu_{\Delta_5})$ is
established \emph{via} $\cZ_{\mathrm{Het}} = C \cdot \Delta_5$
itself --- the conclusion of the V$5/10$ forcing
Theorem~\ref{thm:delta5-forcing-three-ingredients-wave-m-v5}. A
first-principles computation of
$\nu_{\Delta_5}(\cZ_{\mathrm{Het}})$ from the Heterotic
$\Gamma^{2,2}$ lattice quadratic refinement --- without comparing
with $\Delta_5$ --- is not in Harvey--Moore $1996$ or $1998$. The
character-matching is \emph{consistent with}, but not
\emph{derived from}, HM-cancellation alone.

\emph{Obstruction $3$ (Arthur-packet transformation).} The
Langlands--Arthur parameter of $\Delta_5$ is modern input
(Pitale--Schmidt classification of $\Sp_4(\Z)$-invariant cuspidal
automorphic representations;
cf.~Theorem~\ref{thm:delta10-saito-kurokawa-arthur-parameter} for
$\Delta_{10}$) not known in $1996/1998$. Harvey--Moore did not
compute the Arthur packet of $\Delta_5$; they established the
Borcherds-product form and left the Langlands-parameter content
implicit. Their $\Sp_4(\Z)$-transformation is classical
(weight-$5$ automorphy with a specific $\Z/2$ multiplier), not
Arthur-theoretic.

\emph{Obstruction $4$ (theorem status).} Harvey--Moore $1996$ is a
physics paper; Harvey--Moore $1998$ refines the Borcherds-product
identity and is mathematically rigorous at the level of
$q$-expansion comparison. The ``cancellation theorem'' as
advertised --- no contributions outside the
$\nu_{\Delta_5}$-eigenline survive --- depends on the physics
assumptions of Obstruction $1$. As pure mathematics, what is
proved is~\eqref{eq:hm-humbert-delta5} at the Humbert locus
$\cH_{N=1}$, treated as a definition of $I_{\mathrm{HM}}$ rather
than an assertion about an independently defined path integral.

\paragraph{(b) Heal: stratified status across the chain-level /
$(\infty,1)$-categorical divide.}

\begin{proposition}[HM-cancellation status, stratified]
\label{prop:hm-cancellation-status-stratified-wave6-w3}
\ClaimStatusProvedElsewhere\
Harvey--Moore $1/4$-BPS cancellation on Heterotic/$T^6$ at the
Humbert locus $\cH_{N=1}$ decomposes into four statements at
distinct status tiers:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(HM.1)}] \emph{Borcherds-product unfolding of
$I_{\mathrm{HM}}(U)$ at Humbert locus:}
\ClaimStatusProvedElsewhere\
as~\eqref{eq:hm-humbert-delta5} on $q$-expansions at $\cH_{N=1}$.
Harvey--Moore $1998$~\S\S$4$--$6$; Borcherds \emph{Invent.\
Math.}~$132$ ($1998$) $491$--$562$. Mathematical theorem, no
physics input beyond the definition
of~\eqref{eq:hm-heterotic-integrand}.
\item[\textup{(HM.2)}] \emph{$\cN = 4$ protection of
$I_{\mathrm{HM}}$ against $\alpha'$-corrections:}
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ at rigorous-QFT level;
\emph{physics-rigorous} in the standard string-theory sense
(Harvey--Moore~$1996$~\S$4$). No fully constructive mathematical
proof: the heterotic worldsheet QFT has not been constructed to
the Glimm--Jaffe or Kontsevich--Soibelman standard. Modulo the
physics assumption that the heterotic $1$-loop integrand is
exactly $\Theta_{\Gamma^{22,6}}/\eta^{24}$ with no higher-order
corrections, the BPS lattice-sum evaluation is exact. Treated as
theorem in physics, conjectural from mathematics.
\item[\textup{(HM.3)}] \emph{Character emergence
$\cZ_{\mathrm{Het}} \in S_5(\Sp_4(\Z), \nu_{\Delta_5})$
independently of comparison with $\Delta_5$:} Open. The
$\Sp_4(\Z)$-covariance of $I_{\mathrm{HM}}|_{\cH_{N=1}}$ is
visible from (HM.1), but the specific Maa{\ss} multiplier
$\nu_{\Delta_5}$ is read off by \emph{identifying} the
$q$-expansion with $\Delta_5$. A direct computation of the
multiplier from the $\Gamma^{2,2}$-quadratic-refinement on
$H^1(T^4;\Z/2)$, without using $\Delta_5$ as an oracle, would
make $(II)$ of
Theorem~\ref{thm:delta5-forcing-three-ingredients-wave-m-v5}
independent of circular-reasoning concerns. Not in HM $1996/1998$;
\ClaimStatusConjectured.
\item[\textup{(HM.4)}] \emph{Arthur-packet compatibility at
$\infty$:} Out of scope for HM $1996/1998$. The Saito--Kurokawa
non-tempered-CAP identification of $\Delta_{10}$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:delta10-saito-kurokawa-arthur-parameter}) is
$21$st-century input (Pitale--Schmidt; Arthur $2013$); HM did
not access it. Orthogonal layer.
\end{enumerate}
At \emph{chain-level} (explicit Borcherds $q$-expansion), HM.1
suffices for $(II)$ given that $\Delta_5$ is fixed as comparison
target; the forcing
Theorem~\ref{thm:delta5-forcing-three-ingredients-wave-m-v5} is
rigorous modulo HM.2 accepted as physics-rigorous. At
$(\infty,1)$-\emph{categorical} level --- interpreting
$\cZ_{\mathrm{Het}}$ as an automorphic section of a dualisable
object in the HM $(\infty,1)$-category of threshold integrands,
per the scope remark in \S\ref{sec:hm-heterotic-1-loop-t6} --- the
cancellation becomes a functor
$(\mathrm{CY}_3\text{-frames}) \to (\mathrm{ChirAlg})$ preserving
Arthur-packet data; that enhancement is \ClaimStatusConjectured.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{(c) Hidden: a mathematical proof of character-matching
requires CAP-block resolution at $\ell$.}

The circular-reasoning risk in (HM.3) is this. The V$5/10$
argument uses $(II)$ (character-matching) together with $(I)$
(one-dimensionality) to conclude $\cZ_{\mathrm{Het}} = C_\star
\Delta_5$. If $(II)$ is established only by \emph{recognising}
the HM $q$-expansion as matching $\Delta_5$, the argument
collapses to $\cZ_{\mathrm{Het}} = C_\star \Delta_5 \Rightarrow
\cZ_{\mathrm{Het}} \in S_5(\Sp_4(\Z), \nu_{\Delta_5}) \Rightarrow
\cZ_{\mathrm{Het}} = C_\star \Delta_5$, a tautology. A rigorous
proof of $(II)$ without this circularity requires the following
independent input.

\begin{conjecture}[CAP-block resolution at $\ell = 2$ for the
Heterotic $\Gamma^{2,2}$-multiplier]
\label{conj:cap-block-resolution-heterotic-gamma22-multiplier}
\ClaimStatusConjectured\
Let $\Gamma^{2,2}$ denote the even unimodular lattice of signature
$(2,2)$ (two copies of the hyperbolic plane $U$), viewed as a
sublattice of the Heterotic Narain $\Gamma^{22,6}$ at the Humbert
locus $\cH_{N=1}$. Let $\ell = 2$ be the torsion prime. There
exists an Arthur-packet stratification of $S_5(\Sp_4(\Z))$ by
$\ell$-adic CAP-blocks,
\[
S_5(\Sp_4(\Z)) = S_5^{\mathrm{CAP}(2)}(\Sp_4(\Z)) \oplus
S_5^{\mathrm{CAP}(\neq 2)}(\Sp_4(\Z))
\oplus S_5^{\mathrm{non\text{-}CAP}}(\Sp_4(\Z)),
\]
such that the image of the Heterotic $\Gamma^{2,2}$-lattice-sum
map into $S_5(\Sp_4(\Z))$ lies in the $2$-adic CAP-block
$S_5^{\mathrm{CAP}(2)}(\Sp_4(\Z))$, and this block is precisely
$\C \cdot \Delta_5 = S_5(\Sp_4(\Z), \nu_{\Delta_5})$. The Maa{\ss}
multiplier $\nu_{\Delta_5}$ is identified with the
$\Sp_4(\Z)$-action on the mod-$2$ discriminant form
$A_{\Gamma^{2,2}} = \Gamma^{2,2}/2\Gamma^{2,2}$ equipped with its
$\Z/2$-valued quadratic refinement.
\end{conjecture}

This is the mathematical substance of Obstruction $3$. Were
Conjecture~\ref{conj:cap-block-resolution-heterotic-gamma22-multiplier}
proved, it would:
\begin{itemize}
\item Give $(II)$ in
Theorem~\ref{thm:delta5-forcing-three-ingredients-wave-m-v5}
\emph{without} comparing $q$-expansions to $\Delta_5$, breaking
the circularity and making $(II)$ a first-principles computation
on the discriminant form of $\Gamma^{2,2}$.
\item Provide an intrinsic definition of $\nu_{\Delta_5}$ as the
CAP-block label at $\ell = 2$ in the Pitale--Schmidt--Arthur
classification of $\Sp_4(\Z)$-invariant cuspidal automorphic
representations of weight $5$.
\item Upgrade HM-cancellation from physics-rigorous (accepting
HM.2) to mathematics-rigorous: CAP-block resolution replaces the
$\cN = 4$ protection argument with a finite-group cohomology
statement on $A_{\Gamma^{2,2}}$.
\end{itemize}
Techniques required: $\ell$-adic Galois representations attached
to Siegel cusp forms (Weissauer $2005$, compatible systems for
$\Delta_5$ via the Saito--Kurokawa lift); discriminant forms of
lattices (Nikulin $1979$); the Arthur trace formula for $\Sp_4$
restricted to $\Sp_4(\Z)$-invariant vectors (Pitale--Schmidt
classification).
