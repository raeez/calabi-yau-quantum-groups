           A BSTRACT. Motivated by considerations in arithmetic algebraic geometry, we investigate a relationship between the siegel modular form
           known as the Igusa cusp form ∆5 of weight 5 and a certain weak Jacobi form φ0,1 of weight 0 and index 1. Following Kac-Moody, Borcherds,
           Gritsenko and Nikulin et. al., we work out in elementary detail the derivation of an infinite product formula for ∆5 as an expansion at the
           cusp. In doing so, we give an elementary construction of a pair of an infinite dimensional generalized kac-moody superalgebra g along with
           its automorphic correction g ⊂ g∆5 , both constructed from an underlying real (in the case of g) (respectively real and imaginary in the case of
           g∆5 ) root datum. Both of these sets of root datum are realized in the geometry of the lattice Λ3,2 of signature (3, 2), its hyperbolic sublattices
           Λ2,1 , Λ2,1
                   I I ,the associated Weyl group(s) and Weyl vector. The macdonald identity for the automorphic correction algebra witnesses the
           infinite product formula for the cusp form ∆5 via the Weyl-Kac character formula applied to the trivial one dimensional representation
           C. The weight 0 index 1 weak Jacobi form φ0,1 is related to ∆5 as a Jacobi form counting the super dimensions of weight spaces in the
           automorphic correction g∆5 .
