\section*{The lattice-polarised family
  $\gL/\cM_{L}$: positive effective geometry (Wave-17 D4/20)}
\label{sec:wave17-d4-gL-positive-geom-family}

\paragraph{Thesis.}
The $L$-polarised K3\,$\times\,E$ fibration over
$\cM_{L} = \Gamma_{L}\backslash\Om_{L}$ admits a reduced
Donaldson--Thomas moduli stack
$\cM^{\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{DT}}(\cX_{L}/\cM_{L},\gamma,n)$
whose $\Cstar_{E}$-equivariant fibrewise integration produces
the Borcherds lift $\Phi_{L}$ of $\phi_{0,L}$; the universal
identity $\kBKM(\gL) = c_{L}(0)/2$ holds fibre-wise and is
base-change compatible along every Nikulin-admissible sublattice
tower $L \hookrightarrow L'$.

\subsection*{Attack--heal 1: Nikulin admissibility
  of $L$}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} An even non-degenerate lattice $L$ of
signature $(1,t)$, $t = \rk(L)-1$, is \emph{Nikulin-admissible} when
(i) $L$ is primitively embedded in $\LamK = 2 E_{8}(-1) \oplus 3 U$,
and (ii) $L^{\perp} \subset \LamK$ contains a hyperbolic plane
$U$. Condition (i) is controlled by the Nikulin embedding criterion
(Nikulin 1980, Thm.~1.12.2): the discriminant form $A_{L} = L^{\vee}/L$
must satisfy $\ell(A_{L}) \leq 22 - \rk(L)$ and embed as a
direct-summand genus of $A_{\LamK^{\perp}} = -A_{L}$. Condition (ii)
is Dolgachev's mirror criterion (Dolgachev 1996, \S6): the splitting
$L^{\perp} = U \oplus \check L$ defines the mirror polarisation
$\check L$ of rank $20 - t$, opening the Arnol'd--Dolgachev
strange-duality pairing $(L,\check L)$.
\textbf{Flaw.} A lattice may be primitively embeddable in $\LamK$
without admitting the mirror split: at $\rk(L) = 10$ with
$L = E_{8}(-1)\oplus U$, primitive embedding holds but $U \oplus
\check L$ factorisation requires $\check L$ rank $10$, which for
certain overlattices fails indecomposability.
\textbf{Heal.} Restrict to the Nikulin admissibility cone
$\mathsf{Nik} \subset \mathsf{Lat}$: lattices satisfying both
criteria. Gritsenko--Hulek--Sankaran 2007 Thm.~1.5 prove $\mathsf{Nik}$
is closed under primitive sublattices $L \hookrightarrow L'$ of
the same signature type, and the Dolgachev mirror
$L \mapsto \check L$ preserves $\mathsf{Nik}$ on the hyperbolic
locus. The positive effective geometry is built over $\mathsf{Nik}$
only; outside, the reduced virtual class need not exist.

\subsection*{Attack--heal 2: the period
  domain $\Om_{L}$ and its arithmetic quotient}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} For $L \in \mathsf{Nik}$ with $T = L^{\perp}$
of signature $(2, 19-t)$, the period domain
\[
  \Om_{L} = \bigl\{[\omega] \in \mathbb{P}(T\otimes\C) :
    \langle\omega,\omega\rangle = 0,\ \langle\omega,\bar\omega\rangle
    > 0\bigr\}^{+}
\]
is a Type IV Hermitian symmetric domain of complex dimension
$19 - t$ (Dolgachev 1996, Thm.~3.1). The arithmetic group
$\Gamma_{L} = \Oneg(T)$ stabilising a component acts
properly discontinuously, and
$\cM_{L} = \Gamma_{L}\backslash\Om_{L}$
is the coarse moduli of $L$-polarised K3s. Baily--Borel
compactification $\overline{\cM_{L}}^{\mathrm{BB}}$ is a
normal projective variety whose boundary is stratified by
cusps indexed by $\Gamma_{L}$-classes of isotropic sublattices
$I \subset T$ (Baily--Borel 1966; Gritsenko--Hulek--Sankaran
2007 \S2.4). The Hodge bundle $\cE \to \cM_{L}$ of relative
holomorphic two-forms carries a natural metric of Petersson type,
with $\Phi_{L}$ a section of $\cE^{\otimes k_{L}}$ vanishing along
the Humbert divisor $H_{L} \subset \cM_{L}$ of discriminant-form
specialisations.

\subsection*{Attack--heal 3: the $L$-polarised
  K3\,$\times\,E$ universal family}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} (Oberdieck--Shen 2020 \texttt{arXiv:1612.00292}
Thm.~1; Oberdieck 2018 \texttt{arXiv:1605.05238} \S2.) Let
$\pi_{L} : \cX_{L} \to \cM_{L}$ denote the universal family of
$L$-polarised K3 surfaces; its fibre over $[(X,\iota_{L})] \in \cM_{L}$
is the K3 $X$ with chosen $L$-polarisation $\iota_{L} : L \hookrightarrow
\Pic(X)$. Twisting by an elliptic curve $E$ (fixed, smooth, arbitrary
modulus $\tau_{E} \in \HH$) produces the family
$\cX_{L} \times E \to \cM_{L}$ whose fibre is the CY$_{3}$
threefold $K3\times E$ at the corresponding K3 modulus. The
transcendental lattice $T(X,\iota_{L}) = L^{\perp}$ is locally
constant on $\cM_{L}$; Hodge-theoretic variations are confined to
the $T\otimes\C$ direction.
\textbf{Flaw.} At non-primitive specialisations
$\iota_{L}$ may drop rank, and the polarisation datum degenerates.
\textbf{Heal.} Restrict to the open substack
$\cM_{L}^{\circ} \subset \cM_{L}$ of strictly $L$-polarised surfaces
--- Humbert divisor complement --- on which $\iota_{L}$ is an
isomorphism onto a primitive sublattice of $\Pic$.

\subsection*{Attack--heal 4: positive
  effective geometry via reduced DT}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} Fix a primitive K3-class $\gamma \in L$ with
$\gamma^{2} = 2h-2$. The Donaldson--Thomas moduli stack
$\cM_{\mathrm{DT}}(\cX_{L}/\cM_{L},\gamma,n)$ carries a relative
obstruction theory whose Serre-dual-induced cosection
$\sigma_{L}^{*} : \mathrm{Ob} \to \cO$ is the symplectic anomaly
on K3-primitive classes (Maulik--Pandharipande--Thomas 2010; Kiem--Li
2013). Reduction yields
\[
  \Mred_{\mathrm{DT}}(\cX_{L}/\cM_{L},\gamma,n)
  \;=\;\bigl\{I^{\bullet} \in D^{b}(\cX_{L}/\cM_{L}) :
    \mathrm{ch}(I^{\bullet}) = (1,0,-\gamma,-n)\bigr\}^{\mathrm{red}}
\]
of relative virtual dimension $\mathrm{vd}^{\mathrm{red}} = 1$
over $\cM_{L}$. The fibrewise virtual class
$\virred_{L} \in A_{1}(\Mred_{\mathrm{DT}}(\cX_{L,[X]},\gamma,n))$
is nonzero precisely when $\gamma \in \Pic(X)$ is primitive
(Oberdieck 2018 Thm.~3.2). The $\Cstar_{E}$-equivariant reduced
invariant
\[
  N^{\mathrm{red}}_{L}(\gamma,n) \;:=\; \int_{\virred_{L}} 1
\]
is locally constant in $[X] \in \cM_{L}^{\circ}$ on each
primitive-class stratum.
\textbf{Positive effective geometry.} The stack
$\Mred_{\mathrm{DT}}(\cX_{L}/\cM_{L})$ carries a positive
effective divisor $D_{\Phi_{L}}$ --- the vanishing locus of the
Borcherds section of $\cE^{\otimes k_{L}}$ pulled back via the
period map --- and its cohomology class represents the
Pandharipande--Thomas partition function
$Z^{\mathrm{PT}}_{L}(q,q') = \sum N^{\mathrm{red}}_{L}(\gamma,n) q^{\gamma} q'^{n}$
(Oberdieck 2018 Thm.~1; Oberdieck--Pixton 2017 Conj.~A).

\subsection*{Attack--heal 5: fibrewise
  universal identity and base change}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} On every fibre $[X,\iota_{L}] \in \cM_{L}^{\circ}$,
the Borcherds denominator $\Phi_{L}$ realises the
lattice-polarised Gritsenko--Nikulin algebra $\gL$ with
singular weight $\kBKM(\gL) = c_{L}(0)/2$
(Borcherds 1998 Thm.~13.3; Gritsenko 1999 Thm.~2.1;
Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 Thm.~4.1). The identity holds uniformly
over $\cM_{L}^{\circ}$: the coefficient $c_{L}(0)$ depends only
on the lattice datum, not the point $[X]$, and the Borcherds
product converges on every Weyl chamber in $\Om_{L}$.
\textbf{Flaw.} A primitive sublattice embedding $L \hookrightarrow L'$
induces a pullback map $\cM_{L'} \to \cM_{L}$ of moduli, but
the Borcherds lifts $\Phi_{L}, \Phi_{L'}$ live on different
type IV domains of different dimensions; naive restriction fails.
\textbf{Heal.} Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008 Prop.~2.3 constructs the
Hecke--Maass descent
\[
  \Phi_{L'}\big|_{\Om_{L}} = \Phi_{L} \cdot
    \prod_{\lambda \in L'\setminus L} (1 - e^{2\pi i\langle Z,\lambda\rangle})^{c_{L'}(Q(\lambda))}
\]
expressing the $L'$-lift as the $L$-lift times a factor concentrated
on the orthogonal-complement directions. On the GBKM side, this
is the Cartan-subalgebra extension
$\gL \hookrightarrow \gfrak_{L'}$ of Wave-15 M1 / Wave-16 U2
with the complement vectors providing additional real simple roots.
\textbf{Resolution.} Universal identity is base-change compatible
in the functorial sense:
\[
  \kBKM(\gfrak_{L'}) - \kBKM(\gL) \;=\;
    \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(c_{L'}(0) - c_{L}(0)\bigr)
\]
matches the singular-weight shift of the descent factor. The
numerical values at rank 1--2 from Wave-15 M1 / Wave-16 U2 ---
$\kBKM = 5, 2, 1$ for $L = \langle 2d\rangle$, $d \in \{1,2,3\}$;
$\kBKM = 12, 10, 8, 6$ for $L \in \{U, \langle 2\rangle^{\oplus 2},
U(2), U(3)\}$; $\kBKM = 4$ for both $\langle 4\rangle^{\oplus 2}$
and $U(4)$ --- extend to the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery eight-throat
sequence $(10, 6, 4, 3, 5/2, 2, 12/7, 5/3)$ of Wave-16 V5.

\paragraph{Synthesis.}
The sixth holographic layer of Wave-15 R5 acquires rigorous
content: it is the fibred stack
$(\cX_{L}\!\times\!E,\gL,\Mred_{\mathrm{DT}})$ over
$\cM_{L}^{\circ}$, with positive effective divisor
$D_{\Phi_{L}}$ providing the Borcherds denominator section.
The lattice-polarised Nikulin stratification $\mathsf{Nik}$
organises the family; fibrewise $\kBKM(\gL) = c_{L}(0)/2$ is
invariant under $[X,\iota_{L}]$-variation, and base-change
compatibility under $L \hookrightarrow L'$ reads the
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery Hecke descent as a Cartan extension of GBKMs.
The Dolgachev mirror $(L,\check L) \leftrightarrow (\check L, L)$
exchanges complex- and K\"ahler-moduli on the $L$-polarised CY$_{3}$
$K3\times E$; the four invariants $\kch(X_{L}\!\times\!E) = 0$,
$\kcat(X_{L}\!\times\!E) = 0$, $\kBKM(\gL) = c_{L}(0)/2$,
$\kfib(X_{L}\!\times\!E) = 24$ of the four distinct constructions
are locally constant on $\cM_{L}^{\circ}$ and obstruct non-trivially
only at the Humbert boundary.
