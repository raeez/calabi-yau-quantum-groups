\section*{The Order-7 Nikulin Boundary}
\label{wn:sec:N7-nikulin-boundary}

The integer \(7\) belongs to Nikulin's list of symplectic K3
automorphism orders and lies outside the primitive CHL
\(K3\times E\) ladder.  The primitive CHL levels are exactly
\[
  N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\},
\]
the orders for which the elliptic factor admits an origin-fixing
cyclotomic automorphism.  Equivalently
\(\varphi(N)\mid2\).  Since \(\varphi(7)=6\), the cyclotomic order
exceeds the endomorphism rank of an elliptic curve:
\[
  \Z[\zeta_7]\ \hbox{has rank }6,\qquad \End(E)\otimes\Q
  \hbox{ has rank }\leq 2 .
\]
The order-7 row is therefore a non-CHL Nikulin automorphic boundary
row, outside the primitive \(K3\times E\) quotient theorem.
Its automorphic weight is an automorphic datum alone.  A CY-to-chiral
realisation requires a separately constructed Calabi--Yau source,
orientation data, and a Hall or DT comparison.

\subsection*{Automorphic Constant}

Let \(S_7\) be a projective K3 surface with a symplectic automorphism
\(g_7\in\Auts(S_7)\) of order \(7\).  Nikulin's classification gives
\[
  \#\Fix(g_7)=3,
\]
and Mukai's Mathieu description gives frame shape \(1^3 7^3\).  The
paramodular lift attached to this symplectic order is the Gritsenko
order-\(7\) product of weight \(1\).  Equivalently, in the theta-split
normalisation of the twined K3 elliptic genus, the order-\(7\) Jacobi
trace parameter and singular-theta correction are
\[
  T^{\mathrm{Jac}}_7=6,\qquad
  A_7=2.
\]
Thus the Jacobi input has constant term
\[
  c_7(0)=T^{\mathrm{Jac}}_7-2A_7=6-4=2.
\]
Borcherds' singular-theta weight formula gives
\[
  \wt(\PhiNik)
  =
  \kBKMaut(\PhiNik)
  =
  \frac{c_7(0)}{2}
  =
  1.
\]
The superscript in \(\kBKMaut\) records that this is an automorphic
Borcherds weight.  It is promoted to a BKM-denominator invariant when a
denominator algebra and a Calabi--Yau Hall or DT comparison have been
constructed.
The symbol \(N=7\) here denotes the order of the Nikulin symplectic
automorphism.  The Cl\'ery--Gritsenko diagonal-divisor catalogue is
indexed by triples \((t,N;k)\).  Its order-four multiplier row is
\[
  F_{1,4}=\nabla_{3/2}\in
  M_{3/2}(\Gamma_0^{(2)}(4),\chi_4),
\]
a separate row with a separate product theorem, cover character, and
scalar-square convention.

\subsection*{The Stack, Coarse Quotient, and Crepant Resolution}

Let
\[
  G_7=\langle g_7\times\mathrm{id}_E\rangle\cong \Z/7,
  \qquad
  \mathcal Y_7=[S_7\times E/G_7],
  \qquad
  Y_7=(S_7\times E)/G_7 .
\]
The stack \(\mathcal Y_7\) is a compact stacky Calabi--Yau threefold:
the action preserves the K3 holomorphic two-form and the elliptic
one-form.  Its stabiliser is \(G_7\) exactly along the three elliptic
curves below and is trivial elsewhere.  The coarse space \(Y_7\) is a
compact singular Gorenstein Calabi--Yau threefold.

The product action has fixed locus
\[
  \Fix(g_7\times\mathrm{id}_E)
  =
  \bigsqcup_{i=1}^{3} \{p_i\}\times E .
\]
At \(p_i\), the symplectic condition gives local K3 eigenvalues
\((\zeta_7,\zeta_7^{-1})\).  Since the elliptic factor is fixed
pointwise, the local analytic quotient is
\[
  \bigl(\C^2/\langle\mathrm{diag}(\zeta_7,\zeta_7^{-1})\rangle\bigr)
  \times E,
\]
an \(A_6\) surface singularity along an elliptic curve.  The singular
locus consists of three elliptic curves.  The isolated terminal
threefold model with cyclic weights \((1,2,4)\) is excluded by the
fixed elliptic tangent direction.

The minimal resolution of the \(A_6\) surface factor produces six
exceptional curves over each fixed K3 point, fibrewise over \(E\).
It gives the crepant resolution
\[
  \pi\colon
  X_7^{\mathrm{res}}
  :=
  \widetilde{S_7/\langle g_7\rangle}\times E
  \longrightarrow
  Y_7 ,
\]
where \(\widetilde{S_7/\langle g_7\rangle}\) is again a K3 surface.
Thus \(X_7^{\mathrm{res}}\) is a compact smooth Calabi--Yau threefold.
This resolution lies outside the primitive CHL denominator construction;
the CHL cyclotomic linearisation, reduced \(K3\times E\)
Donaldson--Thomas comparison, Hall root datum, and BKM recognition
theorem remain separate data.

With a \(7\)-torsion translation \(t\in E[7]\), the action
\(g_7\times t\) is free.  The quotient
\[
  X_7^{\mathrm{fr}}=(S_7\times E)/\langle g_7\times t\rangle
\]
is a compact smooth Calabi--Yau threefold with trivial stabilisers.
It is a different geometric problem: the elliptic one-form is
translation-invariant, while the primitive compact CHL denominator
construction uses the origin-fixing cyclotomic action, reduced
obstruction theory, and automorphic comparison.

\subsection*{Cheeger--Simons Characters and \texorpdfstring{\(\mu_4\)}{mu4} Gerbes}

Cheeger--Simons differential characters of degree \(3\) fit into
\[
0\to H^{2}(X;\R/\Z)\to \cH^{3}(X;\Z)
\xrightarrow{(\delta_1,\delta_2)}
\Omega^{3}_{\Z}(X)\times_{H^{3}(X;\R)}H^{3}(X;\Z)\to 0.
\]
Brylinski identifies this group with smooth Deligne cohomology, and
degree \(3\) classifies abelian gerbes with connection and curving.
A banded \(\muFour\)-gerbe with flat connection requires a torsion
class
\[
  \alpha\in H^{3}(X;\Z)_{\mathrm{tors}},
  \qquad 4\alpha=0,
\]
together with a differential lift
\(\widehat\alpha\in\cH^{3}(X;\Z)\) satisfying
\(\delta_1(\widehat\alpha)=0\) and
\(\delta_2(\widehat\alpha)=\alpha\).

For \(X_7^{\mathrm{res}}\), K\"unneth gives
\[
  H^3(X_7^{\mathrm{res}};\Z)
  \cong
  H^2(\widetilde{S_7/\langle g_7\rangle};\Z)
  \otimes H^1(E;\Z),
\]
which is torsion-free.  Hence
\[
  H^3(X_7^{\mathrm{res}};\Z)[4]=0.
\]
The crepant compact resolution has trivial degree-\(3\), order-\(4\)
Cheeger--Simons torsion.

The order-7 stabilisers in the Nikulin quotient produce \(7\)-primary
orbifold data.  For the stabiliser group \(G=\Z/7\),
\[
  H^{i}(G;\mu_4)=0\quad(i>0),
\]
because multiplication by \(7\) is invertible on \(\mu_4\).  With
integral coefficients,
\[
  H^\bullet(B\Z/7;\Z)=\Z[u]/(7u),\qquad \deg u=2,
\]
so the cohomological torsion contributed by the stabiliser is
\(7\)-primary and \(H^3(B\Z/7;\Z)=0\).  A \(\muFour\)-gerbe is
therefore extra torsion data on the chosen global space, beyond the
\(\Z/7\) McKay stabiliser and the exceptional \(A_6\)-surface chain.

Spin and Pfaffian orientation data are \(2\)-primary determinant-line
questions.  They affect signs or square roots only after a determinant
line and a quadratic refinement have been constructed.  They leave the
Borcherds constant \(c_7(0)=2\) unchanged, and
\(H^3(X_7^{\mathrm{res}};\Z)[4]\) remains zero.  A
degree-\(3\), order-\(4\) Cheeger--Simons class is an additional global
datum on a chosen space with actual \(4\)-torsion in \(H^3\).

\subsection*{Twisted \texorpdfstring{\(K\)}{K}-Theory}

For a torsion class \(\alpha\in H^3(X;\Z)\), twisted topological
\(K\)-theory \(K^\bullet(X,\alpha)\) is defined through an Azumaya
representative or, equivalently, through the associated bundle of
compact operators.  A \(\Z/4\)-graded decomposition of a BPS
coefficient theory requires a constructed \(\muFour\)-banded gerbe,
its Azumaya representative, and an orientation character for the
moduli problem.

The order-7 Nikulin datum determines the Borcherds product \(\PhiNik\),
its Lorentzian root lattice, Weyl vector, positive chamber, reflection
character, and signed root multiplicities.  A \(\Z/4\)-graded
\(K\)-theory coefficient system is separate data.  A gerbe-twisted
\(K\)-theoretic Hall construction becomes meaningful after a class
\(\alpha_7\) and its compatibility with the Hall product have been
specified.

\subsection*{Donaldson--Thomas and BPS Recognition}

The \(K3\times E\) reduced Donaldson--Thomas comparison is governed by
the \(N=1\) normalisation
\[
  D_5=64^{-1}\Dfive,\qquad
  \PhiTenUn=\Dfive^2,\qquad
  \PhiTenOP=D_5^2=4096^{-1}\Dfive^2,
\]
and
\[
  Z^{\red}_{\DT}(K3\times E)=
  -(\PhiTenOP)^{-1}
  =
  -D_5^{-2}
  =
  -4096\,\Dfive^{-2}.
\]
This scalar-square comparison belongs to the primitive \(K3\times E\)
row.  It gives \(\kBKM(\Dfive)=5\).  The order-7 Nikulin value is
\(\kBKMaut(\PhiNik)=1\).

For an order-7 compact comparison theorem one must first choose the
geometric input:
\[
  \mathcal Y_7=[S_7\times E/G_7],\qquad
  X_7^{\mathrm{res}}=
  \widetilde{S_7/\langle g_7\rangle}\times E,\qquad
  X_7^{\mathrm{fr}}=(S_7\times E)/\langle g_7\times t\rangle .
\]
The stacky quotient carries \(\Z/7\)-stabilisers along three elliptic
curves; the crepant resolution is compact smooth with
\(H^3(X_7^{\mathrm{res}};\Z)[4]=0\); the free quotient has trivial
stabilisers.
After this choice, the comparison requires:
\[
\begin{gathered}
  \text{a reduced obstruction theory},\\
  \text{orientation data, possibly gerbe-valued},\qquad
  \text{a Jacobi input with }c_7(0)=2,\\
  \text{the root lattice, Weyl vector, chamber, and reflection
  character of }\PhiNik,\\
  \text{Hall--Borcherds bracket compatibility, PBW comparison, and
  relation preservation}.
\end{gathered}
\]
With these data in place, the target identity would have the form
\[
  Z^{\red}_{7,L}(p,q,y)=C_7\,\PhiNik(p,q,y)^{-1},
  \qquad C_7\in\Q^\times,
\]
in a normalisation where \(\PhiNik\) is the weight-\(1\) denominator.
The displayed identity is a theorem once those data are constructed and
the Hall--Borcherds comparison is proved.

\subsection*{Chiral Scope and Branch Separation}

A Calabi--Yau threefold source enters the CY-to-chiral functor at
\(d=3\).  The output algebra \(A=\Phi_3(\mathcal C)\) is \(E_1\)-chiral.
Any \(E_2\)-structure belongs to a derived centre or a representation
category.

The Borcherds branch and the Mukai Yangian branch have different
sources.  The order-7 automorphic object above is a Borcherds/BKM
boundary datum.  A Hall-side compact object, if constructed, must be
compared with a Hall--Drinfeld or Borcherds denominator algebra.  The
Mukai self-mirror Yangian branch is built from K3 stable envelopes and
the Mukai current algebra.

\subsection*{Invariant Normalisations}

The canonical \(K3\times E\) spectrum is
\[
 \{\kcat,\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}},
 \kBKM,\kfib\}(K3\times E)
 =
 \{0,3,5,24\}.
\]
Here
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)=
  \chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot0=0,
\]
the compact Hodge-supertrace value is
\[
  \kch(K3\times E)=
  \sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(K3\times E)=0,
\]
the Heisenberg--Mukai specialisation gives
\(\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}=3\), the BKM denominator
value is \(\kBKM(\Dfive)=5\), and the K3 fibre invariant is the Mukai
rank \(\kfib=24\).

For the order-7 Nikulin automorphic row,
\[
  \kBKMaut(\PhiNik)=1.
\]
For the crepant resolution \(X_7^{\mathrm{res}}=K3'\times E\),
\[
  \kcat(X_7^{\mathrm{res}})=
  \chi(\cO_{K3'})\chi(\cO_E)=0,\qquad
  \kch(X_7^{\mathrm{res}})=
  \sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(X_7^{\mathrm{res}})=0 .
\]
These compact threefold values are independent of the order-7
automorphic weight.  Any specialised branch invariant on a chosen
\(X_7\) requires its own construction.  McKay fractions, exceptional
curve counts, and gerbe phases are topological or twisting data.  A fibre invariant
\(\kfib\) requires its own construction.  The order-7 automorphic
invariant is the weight \(\kBKMaut(\PhiNik)=c_7(0)/2\).

\subsection*{Order-7 Theorem Shape}

The proved order-7 datum is the automorphic constant
\[
  c_7(0)=2,\qquad \kBKMaut(\PhiNik)=1.
\]
The geometric theorem still to be supplied has the following exact
content.  Choose \(\mathcal Y_7\), \(X_7^{\mathrm{res}}\), or
\(X_7^{\mathrm{fr}}\); construct a reduced and oriented DT theory on
that object; construct, if the chosen object permits it, a gerbe class
\[
  \alpha_7\in H^3(X_7;\Z)_{\mathrm{tors}},\qquad 4\alpha_7=0,
\]
and a Hall algebra whose denominator is the Borcherds product
\(\PhiNik\).  The class \(\alpha_7\), if present, must be constructed
as a Cheeger--Simons differential character and checked against the
orientation and Hall product.  For \(X_7=X_7^{\mathrm{res}}\), the
group \(H^3(X_7;\Z)[4]\) is zero.  The \(\Z/7\)-stabiliser supplies
only \(7\)-primary data.
