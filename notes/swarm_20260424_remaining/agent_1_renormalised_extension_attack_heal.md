## Verdict

The fixed abelian chart is a proved finite-mode algebra theorem.  It
kills `o_theta^{fp,+}` and nothing stronger.  It does not construct a
map
`Obs_hCS^q(C3; ghat) -> CoHA_crit^or(C3)`, because the source of the
fixed theorem is a shuffle-normal-form quotient with zero differential,
while the full source is the Costello-Gwilliam/Costello-Li
renormalised local/multilocal BV complex with differential
`Q_hCS + {I[L], -}_BV + hbar Delta_L`.

The honest package for integration is:

1. proved finite-mode shuffle algebra;
2. conditional anomaly-free all-scale hCS package;
3. conditional nuclear LF/DFS and charge/HN continuity estimates;
4. conditional renormalised BV-to-Hall transfer extending the fixed
   chart;
5. conditional Maurer-Cartan, differential, product, and BV-bracket
   compatibility on the full chart;
6. separate DWR/Ran descent nullhomotopies.

No theorem should promote the fixed chart to a full hCS-to-Hall
comparison without these gates.

## RED attack

1. The source objects are not the same.

   `Obs_hCS^{q,fp,+}(C3; ghat)` is the positive torus-fixed abelian
   finite-mode shuffle-normal-form quotient generated by `J_r` with
   zero differential.  `Obs_hCS^q(C3; ghat)` is the completed
   renormalised BV complex of local and multilocal functionals on a
   nuclear compact-support field space.  A map on the former is neither
   an inclusion nor an extension on the latter until the embedding,
   projection, or density statement is named and proved.

2. The finite proof does not touch the renormalised differential.

   On the fixed sector, `d_fp J_r = 0`.  On the full hCS factorisation
   algebra the differential contains Dolbeault, interaction, and
   quantum BV-Laplacian terms.  Extending the chart requires a transfer
   `Theta_L^ren` satisfying
   `d_Hall Theta_L^ren = Theta_L^ren (Q_hCS + {I[L], -}_BV + hbar Delta_L)`
   and RG homotopy independence in `L`.  The shuffle kernel alone proves
   none of this.

3. Continuity is a theorem-sized analytic hypothesis.

   The manuscript's quantum BV/bar comparison already lists the missing
   functional-analytic datum: strict nuclear LF/DFS completions, exact
   strong duality, and continuity of every renormalised graph operation
   `W_{Gamma,L} C_Gamma`.  The Hall target has charge/HN-adic,
   equivariant-localised, hbar-adic completions.  A finite algebra map
   supplies no seminorm estimate from the hCS source topology to those
   target filtrations.

4. Anomaly cancellation is necessary but not sufficient.

   Vanishing of the Costello-Li quartic class or a chosen counterterm
   gives a square-zero renormalised BV differential.  It does not
   produce the Hall-valued transfer, does not identify Feynman graph
   weights with critical CoHA chains, and does not prove continuity.

5. The target is a chain-level oriented critical CoHA, not only the SV
   cohomology algebra.

   The fixed theorem lands in the Schiffmann-Vasserot positive shuffle
   model `CoHA(C3)=Y^+`.  A full chart map must land in
   `CoHA_crit^or(C3)` with Joyce/Kontsevich-Soibelman orientation,
   shifts, Tate twists, and Thom-Sebastiani data.  `W_{1+infty}` remains
   typed only after the Drinfeld double and a Fock/evaluation
   representation.

6. Descent is independent of local analytic extension.

   Even a completed chart map on every `C3` polydisc does not give a
   global morphism.  The DWR/Ran nerve still requires Maurer-Cartan,
   orientation, grading/Tate, Thom-Sebastiani, and factorisation
   nullhomotopies on overlaps.

## GREEN healed package

### Proposition A: finite fixed chart

Status: proved finite-mode algebra.

The map
`theta_C3^fp : Obs_hCS^{q,fp,+}(C3; ghat) -> Y_T^+(ghat)`
is the continuous filtered shuffle-normal-form algebra morphism
`J_r -> z_1^r`.  It is a chain map because both differentials vanish on
the selected fixed abelian sector.  Therefore
`pr_{fp,+}(o_theta)=0`, i.e. `o_theta^{fp,+}=0`.

This is exactly the theorem already inserted in the chapter.

### Definition B: renormalised extension datum

A renormalised C3 extension datum should consist of:

1. an anomaly-free all-scale hCS package
   `(I[L], Delta_L, W_{Gamma,L})`;
2. a strict completed source/target category: nuclear LF/DFS and strong
   dual topology on hCS, hbar-adic plus charge/HN-adic plus
   equivariant-localised topology on Hall;
3. a typed relation between the finite fixed shuffle chart and the full
   source, either by continuous projection, dense subalgebra with
   extension uniqueness, or explicit finite-mode boundary condition;
4. a continuous renormalised transfer
   `Theta_L^ren : Obs_hCS^q(C3; ghat) -> CoHA_crit^or(C3)`;
5. differential and RG compatibility:
   `d_Hall Theta_L^ren = Theta_L^ren D_BV[L]` and scale changes are
   homotopic;
6. Maurer-Cartan, BV bracket, Hall product, factorisation product,
   orientation, shift, Tate, and Thom-Sebastiani compatibility on the
   local chart.

### Theorem C: conditional local extension

Status: conditional.

Assume Definition B.  Then the fixed finite-mode chart extends to a
chartwise hCS-to-Hall comparison map on `C3`; equivalently
`o_theta^{ren}=0`.  The proof is formal from the datum: anomaly
cancellation gives the source differential, continuity makes the map a
morphism in the completed category, differential compatibility gives a
chain map, and the Maurer-Cartan/product clauses kill the mapping dg
Lie obstruction.

The finite theorem supplies the boundary condition on the
`fp,+` projection; it does not construct Definition B.

### Theorem D: DWR descent criterion

Status: proved conditional on local maps and descent data.

Given chartwise renormalised extension data on a DWR-good cover and
nullhomotopies for MC, orientation, grading/Tate, Thom-Sebastiani, and
factorisation defects, the local maps descend to
`Theta_{hCS->Hall}^{or}` by the existing descent criterion.  This kills
`o_theta^{des}`.  Without those nullhomotopies, local extension leaves
`o_theta^{des}` open.

## Remaining open obligations

- Construct or specify the continuous finite-mode projection/inclusion
  between the fixed shuffle chart and the Costello-renormalised
  local/multilocal hCS observable space.
- Prove nuclear seminorm estimates for the renormalised graph
  operations and the Hall localization functional.
- Produce the chain-level Hall target lift from the SV positive-half
  cohomology algebra to oriented critical CoHA with shifts and Tate
  twists.
- Prove graph-by-graph differential compatibility with
  `Q_hCS + {I[L], -}_BV + hbar Delta_L`.
- Prove multiplicativity and BV-bracket compatibility beyond the fixed
  shuffle quotient.
- Supply DWR/Ran overlap nullhomotopies.

## Integration recommendation

Integrate a short definition/proposition package immediately after
`cor:c3-residual-theta-after-fixed-chart`:

1. define a "renormalised C3 extension datum" with the six clauses in
   Definition B;
2. state a conditional proposition that this datum kills
   `o_theta^{ren}`;
3. state a second conditional proposition that DWR/Ran nullhomotopies
   kill `o_theta^{des}`;
4. keep the fixed chart theorem `ProvedHere`, but keep the full
   hCS-Hall comparison `Conditional`.

Do not edit the C3 theorem to say the finite shuffle chart constructs
`Theta_{hCS->Hall}^{or}`.  The truthful upgrade is conditional
renormalised transfer plus descent, with `CoHA(C3)=Y^+` only at the
positive-half Hall stage and `W_{1+infty}` only after Drinfeld doubling.
