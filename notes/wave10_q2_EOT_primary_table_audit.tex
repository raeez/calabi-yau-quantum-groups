\section*{Wave-10 Q2: EOT 2011 Table~1 --- primary-source audit}
\ClaimStatusTheorem

\paragraph{Finding 0 (the content of EOT 2011 Table~1).}
Eguchi--Ooguri--Tachikawa 2011 (\texttt{arXiv:1004.0956v2}, p.~6,
Appendix~A) presents a \emph{single} Table~1: it is the $26\times 26$
\emph{character table of the sporadic Mathieu group $M_{24}$} (rows =
irreducible characters of dimensions $1, 23, 45, \ldots, 10395$;
columns = conjugacy classes $1A, 2A, \ldots, 23B$). EOT Table~1
contains neither a column of Fourier coefficients $A_n$ nor a column
of twisted Euler numbers $\chi(g)$. The sequence $A_n = 45, 231,
770, 2277, 5796, 13915, 30843, \ldots$ of EOT lives in equation
(1.12), not in Table~1; the conjugacy-class-indexed data
(twisted/twining) is \emph{absent} from EOT 2011 --- that paper
predates the twining analysis.
\textbf{Both Wave~9 agents M4 and N3 mis-cite EOT Table~1 as the
source of a $c_N(0)$ sequence: EOT Table~1 does not contain one.}

\paragraph{Finding 1 (the actual twining constant-term table is in Cheng 2010
and GHV 2012).}
The paper that supplies the $z=0$ constant terms of twisted elliptic
genera as a function of $M_{24}$ conjugacy class is Cheng 2010
(\texttt{arXiv:1005.5415}, p.~10, Table~1, equations (2.7)--(2.9)). Cheng
states
\[
  \chi(g) \;:=\; Z_g(\tau, z=0) \;=\; \Tr_{\rho_1 \oplus \rho_{23}}(g),
\]
i.e., the twisted Euler number on $H^\ast(K3)$ as the $24$-dimensional
permutation character. Cheng Table~1 lists, for the five
\emph{Nikulin $\Z_N$-automorphism} conjugacy classes in $M_{23}
\subset M_{24}$:
\[
  \chi(1A)=24,\quad \chi(2A)=8,\quad \chi(3A)=6,\quad \chi(5A)=4,
  \quad \chi(7A)=3,
\]
following the universal Nikulin formula $\chi(g_N) = 24/(N+1)$ on
$N \in \{1,2,3,5,7\}$. Gaberdiel--Hohenegger--Volpato 2010
(\texttt{arXiv:1006.0221}, p.~7, eq.~(3.8)) records the companion
relation
\[
  \phi_g(\tau, z=0) \;=\; 12\,\Tr_{\mathbf{23}\oplus \mathbf{1}}(g),
\]
for the half-index-normalised twining elliptic genus
$\phi_g(\tau,z)$. The factor $12$ reflects GHV's $\phi_g = 2 Z_g$
normalisation; \emph{both sources agree on the underlying character-theoretic
quantity}, which is $\chi_{24}(g) = \chi_1(g) + \chi_{23}(g)$.

\paragraph{Finding 2 (the $c_N(0)$ values entering the Borcherds weight formula
are a distinct sequence).}
The quantity entering Borcherds' weight theorem (Borcherds 1998,
Theorem~13.3) for the Gritsenko--Nikulin paramodular form $\PhiGC{N}$
attached to the Nikulin $\Z_N$-automorphism of $K3$ is \emph{not} the
twisted Euler number $\chi(g_N)$. It is the $q^0 z^0$-Fourier
coefficient $c_N(0)$ of the input weak Jacobi form
$\phi_{0,1}^{(N)}(\tau,z)$ of weight $0$, index $1$ (Eichler--Zagier
1985, Theorem~9.3; Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995 Part~II, Theorem~2.1). For
$N=1$, $\phi_{0,1}^{(1)} = (r^{-1} + 10 + r) + O(q)$ so $c_1(0) = 10$;
weight $\Delta_5 = c_1(0)/2 = 5$. The Gritsenko--Nikulin tabulation
(1995 Part~II, Theorem~2.1; Gritsenko 1999) gives
\[
  \big(c_1(0),\ c_2(0),\ c_3(0),\ c_4(0),\ c_6(0)\big)
  \;=\; (10,\ 4,\ 2,\ 2,\ 2),
\]
equivalently $\mathrm{wt}(\PhiGC{N}) = c_N(0)/2 = (5, 2, 1, 1, 1)$, which
is the canonical sequence inscribed in
\texttt{chapters/examples/cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}
line~1481 and propagated as
$\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ (Theorem
\texttt{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal}, same file,
lines~1423--1473).

\paragraph{Finding 3 (the two Wave-9 tables).}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{M4's table $(10, 8, 6, 4, 2)$}: this is the sequence of
\emph{twisted Euler numbers} $\chi(g_N) = 24/(N+1)$ for the Nikulin
automorphism conjugacy classes $(1A, 2A, 3A, 5A, 7A)$ --- i.e.,
$(24, 8, 6, 4, 3)$ truncated/reindexed incorrectly, or the
Gritsenko--Clery constant-factor-altered variant. It is \emph{not}
$c_N(0)$; it is not in EOT Table~1. M4 misidentified both the source
and the quantity.
\item \textbf{N3's table $(10, 4, 2, 2, 2)$}: this is the correct
Gritsenko--Nikulin $c_N(0)$ sequence on $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$,
producing the paramodular weights $(5, 2, 1, 1, 1)$ for
$(\Delm{5}, \Delm{2}, \Delm{1}, \Delm{1}, \Delm{1})$. It is not in
EOT Table~1; N3 mis-cited the source. The correct primary references
are Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995 Part~II Theorem~2.1 (for general $N$),
Gritsenko 1999 (for the extended list), Borcherds 1998
Theorem~13.3 (for $\mathrm{wt}(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$), and
Eichler--Zagier 1985 Theorem~9.3 (for $N=1$).
\end{itemize}

N3 has the correct $c_N(0)$ sequence; M4 conflates it with $\chi(g_N)$.

\paragraph{Notation audit (which $c_g(0)$).}
The question ``is $c_g(0)$ the $q^0 z^0$-coefficient of $Z^{(g)}_{\mathrm{ell}}(K3)$,
or the constant term at $z=0$ of $Z^{(g)}|_{\tau \to i\infty}$, or
the leading Borcherds-weight coefficient of $F^{\mathrm{GC}}_{(g,\mathrm{id})}$?'':
all three literatures use disjoint normalisations.
(a) Cheng 2010 and GHV 2012 denote $\chi(g) = Z_g(\tau, 0) = \chi_{24}(g)$
--- this is the $z=0$ value of the twisted elliptic genus, not a
Borcherds-weight datum. (b) The Borcherds-weight $c_N(0)$ is the
$q^0 z^0$-coefficient of the \emph{Jacobi input}
$\phi_{0,1}^{(N)}(\tau,z)$, which equals $10$ at $N=1$ and
$(10,4,2,2,2)$ at $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$. (c) The ``leading coefficient
of $F^{\mathrm{GC}}_{(g,\mathrm{id})}$'' is not a standard
designation; in Lorgat 2020 and Clery--Gritsenko 2013, $F^{\mathrm{GC}}_N$
denotes a paramodular form whose weight \emph{equals} $c_N(0)/2$.
These are three \emph{different} quantities indexed by the same
letter $c$; future Wave prose should adopt the subscript discipline
$c_N^{\mathrm{Jac}}(0)$ for the Borcherds-weight input and
$\chi_{24}(g)$ for the twisted Euler number, to prevent AP-CY-adjacent
confusions.
