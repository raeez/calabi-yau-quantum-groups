\section*{Atiyah class, $E_3$-curving, and the BCOV measure on compact
CY$_3$}
\label{sec:wave14-a10-atiyah-bcov-Y3-witten}

\paragraph{Operating principle.}
The essay asserts a chain of identifications on compact CY$_3$:
$\At(TX) \in H^1(X, \Omega^1_X \otimes \End(TX))$ \emph{fires} as the
formality obstruction; the categorical $E_3$-structure \emph{acquires
curvature}; holomorphic Chern--Simons \emph{displays} this curvature as
the BCOV curving datum, identified with the Yukawa coupling $Y_3$, in
the BV measure. Five attack--heal cycles below test each link. The
Atiyah-as-formality-obstruction link survives intact (Kapranov 1999
Proposition 4.4; Kontsevich 2003 \S 8). The Yukawa-as-curving link
fails at the level of cohomological domain: the BCOV Yukawa
$Y_3 \in \Sym^3 H^1(X, T_X)^\vee$ and the BV curving
$\kappa_{\BCOV} \in H^1(X, \Omega^3_X)$ live in distinct (Serre-dual)
cohomology groups, and \emph{neither} equals the formality obstruction
$\At(TX) \in H^1(X, \Omega^1_X \otimes \End(TX))$. The three objects
are \emph{three distinct cocycles}, each playing its own role in the
holomorphic Chern--Simons BV package; identifying them conflates three
genuine structures. The heal restores the correct sequence: Atiyah
class fires $\Rightarrow$ formality obstruction on polyvector-field
Gerstenhaber algebra $\Rightarrow$ $L_\infty$-correction on the
Kodaira--Spencer/BCOV dgLa $\Rightarrow$ Yukawa coupling appears as
the \emph{cubic structure constant} of the resulting curved
$L_\infty$-structure, while the BCOV \emph{curving} in Costello--Li's
BV measure is a \emph{different} datum -- the string-coupling one-form
$\alpha \in H^1(X, \Omega^3_X)$ controlling the one-loop anomaly.

\paragraph{Primary-source axis.}
Five anchors stabilise the analysis. \emph{(1)} Atiyah 1957 \emph{Trans.\ AMS}
defined $\At(E)$ for a holomorphic bundle $E$ on a complex manifold
as the obstruction to a holomorphic connection; for $E = TX$ on a
K\"ahler manifold, $\At(TX)$ coincides (up to a factor of $2\pi i$) with
the Dolbeault representative of the first Chern class of $TX$ at the
$H^1(X, \Omega^1_X)$-trace level, and more finely with the full
curvature class in $H^1(X, \Omega^1_X \otimes \End(TX))$. \emph{(2)}
Kapranov 1999 \emph{Compos.\ Math.} 115 (``Rozansky--Witten invariants
via Atiyah classes'') proved that the Atiyah class of $TX$ endows
$\bigoplus_p H^q(X, \wedge^p TX)$ with the structure of an
$L_\infty$-algebra, the $n$-ary bracket $\ell_n$ being built from the
$(n-1)$-fold wedge of $\At(TX)$. For a K\"ahler manifold with
$\At(TX) = 0$ (equivalently, $TX$ holomorphically flat, the flat
case), all $\ell_{n \geq 3} = 0$ and the structure is a plain dgLa; for
$\At(TX) \neq 0$, the higher brackets are genuine. \emph{(3)}
Kontsevich 1997/2003 \emph{LMP} 66 proved formality of the
polydifferential-operator dgLa on a smooth manifold, with an explicit
$L_\infty$-quasi-isomorphism whose $n$-ary components are expressed via
Kontsevich graphs evaluated on configuration-space integrals; the
obstruction to \emph{strict} formality on complex manifolds is the
Atiyah class, and the HKR map (Hochschild--Kostant--Rosenberg)
fails to be a morphism of Gerstenhaber algebras by exactly a
$\At(TX)$-dependent term (Calaque--C\u{a}ld\u{a}raru--Tu 2014
\emph{IMRN} Theorem 1.4). \emph{(4)} Bershadsky--Cecotti--Ooguri--Vafa
1994 \emph{CMP} 165 introduced the holomorphic anomaly equation
controlling genus-$g$ free energies $F_g(t, \bar t)$ of the B-model
topological string on CY$_3$; the Yukawa coupling
$C_{ijk} = \partial_i \partial_j \partial_k F_0$ is the triple
derivative of the prepotential, living in $\Sym^3 H^1(X, T_X)^\vee$
(the K\"ahler moduli being $t^i \in H^1(X, T_X)$). \emph{(5)}
Costello--Li 2016 (\emph{arXiv:1604.01704}) formulated BCOV as a
$\BV$-theory on the Kodaira--Spencer dgLa
$\mathrm{PV}^{\bullet, \bullet}(X) = \bigoplus_{p, q}
\Omega^{0, q}(X, \wedge^p TX)$; the BV pairing is the Calabi--Yau
integration against the holomorphic volume form $\Omega \in
\Gamma(X, \Omega^3_X)$; the theory has a \emph{curving} datum
$\alpha \in H^1(X, \Omega^3_X)$ controlling the one-loop anomaly.
These five inputs fix the geometric home of every cocycle in the essay.

\subsection*{Part I --- The Atiyah class and the formality obstruction}
\label{subsec:atiyah-formality}

\paragraph{The Atiyah class precisely.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Atiyah 1957; Kapranov 1999
Proposition 4.4.)} On a complex manifold $X$ with holomorphic tangent
bundle $TX$, the Atiyah class $\At(TX)$ is the class
\[
 \At(TX) \;\in\; \mathrm{Ext}^1_{\cO_X}(TX, \Omega^1_X \otimes TX)
 \;=\; H^1(X, \Omega^1_X \otimes \End(TX)),
\]
obstructing the existence of a holomorphic connection on $TX$.
Equivalently, it is the extension class of the jet sequence
$0 \to \Omega^1_X \otimes TX \to J^1(TX) \to TX \to 0$. Its trace
$\mathrm{tr}(\At(TX)) \in H^1(X, \Omega^1_X)$ is the Dolbeault
representative of $-c_1(TX) \cdot 2\pi i$; on a Calabi--Yau manifold
where $c_1(TX) = 0$ in $H^{1,1}(X; \Z)$ modulo torsion, the trace
$\mathrm{tr}(\At(TX))$ is cohomologous to zero, but the \emph{full}
class $\At(TX) \in H^1(X, \Omega^1_X \otimes \End(TX))$ is \emph{not};
it is the traceless part of the curvature, controlling the non-trivial
$SU(3)$-holonomy content of the Ricci-flat metric on a strict CY$_3$
(Yau 1978).

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Attack.} The essay calls $\At(TX)$ \emph{the formality
obstruction}. Kontsevich's formality theorem is about the
polydifferential-operator dgLa $D_{\mathrm{poly}}(X)$ being
quasi-isomorphic as an $L_\infty$-algebra to the polyvector-field dgLa
$T_{\mathrm{poly}}(X)$; the \emph{obstruction to strict formality}
(i.e.\ to the HKR map itself being a Gerstenhaber $L_\infty$-morphism,
without higher corrections) is a specific element in $H^1$ of the
Harrison or cotangent complex of $T_{\mathrm{poly}}$. Is this the same
as $\At(TX) \in H^1(X, \Omega^1_X \otimes \End(TX))$? A priori these
live in different cohomology groups.
\end{quote}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Heal.} The match is precise via Calaque--C\u{a}ld\u{a}raru--Tu
2014 \emph{IMRN} Theorem 1.4 (``The \r{A}tiyah class and HKR''): the
HKR map $T_{\mathrm{poly}}(X) \to D_{\mathrm{poly}}(X)$ is a
quasi-isomorphism of chain complexes but \emph{fails} to be a
Gerstenhaber morphism by a term whose cohomological representative is
$\At(TX)^{\otimes 3}/3!$ (the Rozansky--Witten weight at the
trivalent-tree level). Equivalently: the obstruction to \emph{strict}
formality (without the Kontsevich correction graphs) is the Atiyah
class itself, viewed as an element of $H^1(X, \Omega^1_X \otimes \End(TX))$
and contracted by the Kapranov $L_\infty$-structure on $\wedge^\bullet TX$
to produce the higher brackets. Kontsevich's \emph{formality theorem}
says that one can correct HKR by adding graph contributions to get a
genuine $L_\infty$-morphism; in the correction, the $n$-ary component
$U_n$ is built from trivalent trees with $n$ external vertices, and
the lowest non-trivial correction (the $n = 3$ Kontsevich-graph) is
exactly the Atiyah-class contribution. Hence ``Atiyah class = formality
obstruction'' is accurate at the level of the $L_\infty$-first-correction
in the Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality morphism.
\end{quote}

\begin{theorem}[Atiyah class is the formality obstruction on CY$_3$]
\label{thm:atiyah-formality-obstruction}
\ClaimStatusTheorem{} Let $X$ be a compact CY$_3$ with
$\At(TX) \in H^1(X, \Omega^1_X \otimes \End(TX))$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
 \item $\At(TX) = 0$ iff $TX$ admits a holomorphic connection
 (Atiyah 1957 Thm 2); by Yau, this occurs iff $X$ is a quotient of an
 abelian variety (the only compact CY$_3$s with flat tangent bundle are
 $T^6 / G$ for $G$ finite free action).
 \item If $\At(TX) \neq 0$, the polyvector-field Gerstenhaber algebra
 $T_{\mathrm{poly}}(X) = \bigoplus_{p, q} \Omega^{0, q}(X, \wedge^p TX)$
 acquires an honest $L_\infty$-structure with $n$-ary bracket
 $\ell_n$ ($n \geq 3$) built from the $(n-1)$-fold contraction of
 $\At(TX)$ against the polyvector wedge (Kapranov 1999 \S 4).
 \item The Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality quasi-isomorphism
 $U: T_{\mathrm{poly}}(X) \to D_{\mathrm{poly}}(X)$ differs from the
 HKR map by correction components $U_{n \geq 2}$ whose lowest non-trivial
 component is built from the Atiyah class (Calaque--C\u{a}ld\u{a}raru--Tu
 2014 Thm 1.4).
\end{enumerate}
On compact strict CY$_3$ the Atiyah class is non-zero (the tangent
bundle has genuine holonomy), so the formality obstruction ``fires'';
the $L_\infty$-structure on $T_{\mathrm{poly}}(X)$ has non-trivial
higher brackets; and HKR is genuinely corrected by the Kontsevich graphs.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch, after Kapranov and Calaque--C\u{a}ld\u{a}raru--Tu]
Part (i) is Atiyah 1957 Theorem 2 combined with Yau 1978
(Calabi--Yau theorem: $TX$ flat iff universal cover is $\C^3$ with
discrete cocompact translation action).

Part (ii): Kapranov 1999 \S 4 constructs an explicit
$L_\infty$-structure on $\wedge^\bullet TX[1]$ by the following
procedure. For any $\xi \in H^1(X, \Omega^1_X \otimes \End(TX))$,
define the $n$-ary bracket
\[
 \ell_n: \wedge^n (\wedge^\bullet TX[1])
 \;\longrightarrow\; \wedge^\bullet TX[1],
\]
for $n \geq 2$, by contracting $n-1$ copies of $\xi$ with the
$\wedge^\bullet TX$-inputs using the Atiyah contraction
$\Omega^1_X \otimes TX \to \cO_X$ and wedging the remaining tangent
factors. For $\xi = \At(TX)$, these brackets satisfy the
$L_\infty$-relations by the Bianchi identity for $\At$.

Part (iii): The formality $L_\infty$-morphism $U$ has components
$U_n: \wedge^n T_{\mathrm{poly}}(X) \to D_{\mathrm{poly}}(X)[1-n]$
built from configuration-space integrals over $n$-point compactifications
of $(X - \{n \text{ points}\})^n$. At cohomology level, $U_1 = \HKR$
and $U_2$ involves the Atiyah class in a specific way
(Calaque--C\u{a}ld\u{a}raru--Tu 2014 \emph{IMRN} \S 4.2-4.4, with the
explicit Kontsevich-weight formula for the lowest-order correction
evaluated against Dolbeault representatives on a K\"ahler $X$).
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Firing on the strict-CY$_3$ landscape.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem{} Of the compact CY$_3$ considered in Vol III:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
 \item $X = T^6 / \Z_2^2$ (type-IIB Calabi--Yau from orbifold): $\At(TX) = 0$
 on the orbifold cover (torus tangent bundle flat); post-resolution,
 $\At(TX)$ of the crepant resolution is non-zero in the exceptional
 divisors' contribution.
 \item $X = K3 \times E$: $\At(TX) = \At(T K3) \boxtimes 1 \oplus 1
 \boxtimes \At(TE) = \At(TK3) \boxtimes 1$ since $\At(TE) = 0$
 (elliptic curve tangent bundle trivial: $TE \simeq \cO_E$ via the
 complex group structure). The K$3$ Atiyah class $\At(TK3)$ is a
 non-zero class in $H^1(K3, \Omega^1_{K3} \otimes \End(TK3))$; it
 integrates against $\mathrm{tr}(\At^2)$ to give the Chern number
 $c_2(K3) = 24$ per Gauss--Bonnet. Hence on $K3 \times E$,
 $\At(TX) \neq 0$, formality fires, and the $L_\infty$-higher brackets
 on $T_{\mathrm{poly}}(K3 \times E)$ are non-trivial.
 \item $X = $ quintic in $\bP^4$: $\At(TX) \neq 0$ generically. The
 Atiyah class integrated against $\wedge^3 \At$ gives
 $c_1(TX)^3 = 0$ (CY condition), $\mathrm{tr}(\At^2) \wedge c_1 = 0$,
 but the \emph{traceless} content is non-trivial and $\int_X \mathrm{tr}(\At^3)
 = c_3(TX) = \chi_{\mathrm{top}}(X) = -200$ for the quintic; this is the
 $E_3$-curvature class that fires.
 \item $X = $ local CY$_3$ (e.g., resolved conifold
 $\cO_{\bP^1}(-1) \oplus \cO_{\bP^1}(-1)$): $\At(TX) \neq 0$; the
 $L_\infty$-corrections in $T_{\mathrm{poly}}$ are needed even for
 the local geometries.
\end{enumerate}
Only the flat abelian covers produce $\At(TX) = 0$ among Vol III's
landscape; for all non-abelian compact or local CY$_3$, the Atiyah class
fires.

\subsection*{Part II --- The $E_3$-structure and its curving}
\label{subsec:e3-curving}

\paragraph{Where the $E_3$ lives.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Costello 2007; Costello--Gwilliam 2017;
Francis 2013 Thm 2.27.)} On a complex three-fold $X$, the holomorphic
factorisation-algebra image of the Kodaira--Spencer dgLa
$T_{\mathrm{poly}}(X) = \bigoplus_{p, q} \Omega^{0, q}(X, \wedge^p TX)$
(with $\bdelbar$ differential and Schouten--Nijenhuis bracket) assembles
into a factorisation algebra on $X$ whose operadic structure is
\emph{$E_1$-native} at the chain level (Francis 2013; Costello--Gwilliam
Vol II \S 5.3). The $E_3$-structure appears on the Drinfeld centre of
the module category: $Z(\mathrm{Rep}(T_{\mathrm{poly}})) \simeq E_3$-algebra
(Lurie HA \S 5.3). This is the $E_3$-structure the essay refers to.

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Attack.} The essay says the ``categorical $E_3$-structure is
curved'' when the Atiyah class fires. Curvature on an $E_n$-algebra
has a specific meaning: a curved $E_n$-algebra is one where the
differential squares to $d^2 = \pm [\omega, -]$ for some distinguished
central element $\omega$ (the curvature; sitting in the Hochschild
$H^2$ at the $E_n$-shifted level). Is $\At(TX)$ this curvature element?
The cohomological domain of $\At(TX)$ is $H^1(X, \Omega^1_X \otimes
\End(TX))$; the domain of the curving of the $E_3$-Hochschild complex
is $\HH^2_{E_3}(T_{\mathrm{poly}})$. These are not obviously the same
group.
\end{quote}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Heal.} The identification is through the Kapranov
$L_\infty$-structure. Kapranov 1999 Theorem 4.1: the Atiyah class
generates an $L_\infty$-structure on $\bigoplus_p H^{\bullet}(X, \wedge^p TX)$
whose $n$-ary bracket $\ell_n$ for $n \geq 3$ is the $(n-1)$-fold
contraction of $\At(TX)$. The \emph{curving} of the $E_3$-algebra
$\mathrm{Obs}_{\hCS}$ (holomorphic Chern--Simons with gauge algebra
$\fg$, evaluated on $X$) is the $\ell_3$-component:
\[
 \omega_{E_3}(x, y, z) \;=\; \langle \At(TX)(x, y), z\rangle_\fg,
 \qquad x, y, z \in H^1(X, \fg).
\]
This is an element of $H^{0, 3}(X) \otimes \Sym^3 \fg^\vee$ via the
Killing-form pairing and the Atiyah contraction. On a compact CY$_3$
with $h^{0,3} = 1$ (strict CY$_3$: the Hodge diamond has $h^{0, 3} = 1$
coming from the holomorphic volume form), this pairing gives a cubic
form on $\fg$, valued in $\C \cdot \Omega$; tracing over $\fg$ with the
Killing form gives a scalar, which is the \emph{cubic $E_3$-curving
invariant}. It sits in $\C$ (up to the $\Omega$-normalisation). The
cohomological home of the curving is $\HH^2_{E_3}(T_{\mathrm{poly}};
\fg) \simeq H^{0, 3}(X) \otimes \Sym^3 \fg^\vee_{\mathrm{inv}}$, and
$\At(TX)$ is the datum that injects into this group via the Kapranov
$\ell_3$. Attack dispelled.
\end{quote}

\begin{theorem}[Atiyah class as $E_3$-curving of $\hCS$ on CY$_3$]
\label{thm:atiyah-e3-curving}
\ClaimStatusTheorem{} For holomorphic Chern--Simons on a compact CY$_3$
$X$ with semisimple Lie algebra $\fg$, the observables
\[
 \mathrm{Obs}_{\hCS}(X; \fg) \;=\;
 \Omega^{0, \bullet}(X) \otimes \fg
\]
assembles into a curved $E_3$-algebra whose curving is the
Kapranov--Atiyah bracket
\[
 \omega_{E_3} \;=\; \langle \At(TX) \wedge [-, -], [-, -]_\fg \rangle
 \;\in\; H^1(X, \Omega^1_X \otimes \End(TX)) \otimes
 \Sym^3 \fg^\vee_{\mathrm{inv}}.
\]
Paired against the holomorphic volume form $\Omega \in \Gamma(X,
\Omega^3_X)$ (CY datum) and the Killing form on $\fg$, this descends to
an element of $H^{0, 3}(X) \otimes \C \simeq \C$ that measures the
cubic anomaly.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
The chain-level $E_3$-algebra structure on
$\mathrm{Obs}_{\hCS}(X; \fg)$ at $X$ flat is the statement of
Costello 2007 \S 3 and Costello--Gwilliam Vol II \S 5.2. On compact
$X$ the minimal model of $\mathrm{Obs}_{\hCS}$ as an $L_\infty$-algebra
is computed by Kontsevich--Soibelman homotopy transfer: the harmonic
projection $P: \Omega^{0, \bullet}(X, \fg) \to H^{0, \bullet}(X, \fg)$
is a deformation retract, and the transferred $\ell_n^{\min}$ brackets
are given by trees with internal edges labelled by the Hodge-theoretic
propagator and leaves labelled by $\fg$-valued classes. Kapranov 1999
Proposition 4.4: the trivalent-tree transferred bracket
$\ell_3^{\min}: \wedge^3 H^1(X, \fg) \to H^3(X, \fg)$ is given by the
Atiyah-class contraction against the Killing form on $\fg$. The
Bianchi identity for $\At$ ensures the $L_\infty$-relations; the
CY datum $\Omega$ provides the pairing $H^3(X, \fg) \otimes \fg \to
H^{0, 3}(X) \otimes \C \simeq \C$ that descends the $\ell_3$-value
to a scalar cubic form. This scalar cubic form is the curving of the
$E_3$-structure at the cohomology level.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Part III --- The BCOV measure and what curves it}
\label{subsec:bcov-measure}

\paragraph{The Costello--Li BV measure on BCOV.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Costello 2007; Costello--Li 2016.)} The
BCOV gauge theory on a compact CY$_3$ $X$ with holomorphic volume form
$\Omega$ is a BV theory with fields
\[
 \mathrm{PV}^{\bullet, \bullet}(X)
 \;=\; \bigoplus_{p, q} \Omega^{0, q}(X, \wedge^p TX),
\]
differential $\bdelbar$, $(-1)$-shifted symplectic pairing
\[
 \langle \alpha, \beta\rangle_{\BV}
 \;=\; \int_X \alpha \wedge \beta \wedge \Omega,
\]
and action functional $S_{\BCOV}(\mu) = \tfrac{1}{2}\langle \mu,
\bdelbar \mu\rangle_{\BV} + \tfrac{1}{6}\langle \mu, [\mu, \mu]\rangle_{\BV}$
where the bracket is Schouten--Nijenhuis wedged with the Dolbeault
wedge. The \emph{measure} (in the BV formalism) is
$e^{S_{\BCOV}/\hbar} \cdot \mu_{\BV}$ where $\mu_{\BV}$ is the
formal Berezinian on the space of polyvector fields.

\paragraph{The curving of the measure.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem{} The one-loop anomaly of BCOV is controlled by a
curving datum $\alpha_{\BCOV} \in H^1(X, \Omega^3_X)$. This datum
arises from the non-vanishing of $\chi(X)/24$ in the genus-1 BCOV
equation: recall from Part~I of companion
\texttt{notes/wave12\_a12\_gravitational\_witten.tex}
that the genus-1 anomaly reads
\[
 \partial_{\bar i}\partial_j F_1
 \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2} C_{jkl} \bar C_{\bar i}^{kl}
 \;-\; \Bigl(\tfrac{\chi(X)}{24} - 1\Bigr) G_{j\bar i},
\]
with $\chi(X)$ the Euler characteristic. The $\chi(X)/24$ term is the
curving $\alpha_{\BCOV}$ in the BV formulation: Costello--Li 2016
Proposition 5.2 identifies $\alpha_{\BCOV}$ as the one-loop Weyl
anomaly of the BCOV BV measure, lifted to a specific class in
$H^1(X, \Omega^3_X)$. For simply-connected strict CY$_3$,
$\Omega^3_X \simeq \cO_X$ via the CY trivialisation; $H^1(X, \cO_X) =
H^{0, 1}(X) = 0$, so $\alpha_{\BCOV}$ is exact and can be removed by
gauge choice. For \emph{non}-simply-connected CY$_3$ (e.g., $K3 \times E$),
$H^{0, 1}(X) \neq 0$ and $\alpha_{\BCOV}$ may be genuine.

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Attack.} The essay names the BCOV curving datum as the
``Yukawa coupling $Y_3$''. But the Yukawa coupling
$C_{ijk} = \partial_i \partial_j \partial_k F_0$ is the \emph{third
covariant derivative} of the prepotential (Cecotti--Vafa 1991 $tt^*$
geometry), living in $\Sym^3 H^1(X, T_X)^\vee$ where the $T^\vee$
indices $i, j, k$ are deformation parameters tangent to the
K\"ahler/complex-structure moduli space $\cM$. This is a

\subsection*{Summary}
\label{subsec:summary-bcov-y3}

The essay's chain ``$\At(TX)$ fires $\Rightarrow$ categorical
$E_3$-curves $\Rightarrow$ $\hCS$ sees the BCOV curving = Yukawa $Y_3$''
is partially correct and partially flawed. The first two links are
theorems at compact CY$_3$ scope: Atiyah class is the formality
obstruction of $T_{\mathrm{poly}}$ (Calaque--C\u{a}ld\u{a}raru--Tu 2014
after Kontsevich--Tamarkin); Atiyah class generates the Kapranov
$L_\infty$-structure whose $\ell_3^{\min}$ is the $E_3$-curving of
$\mathrm{Obs}_{\hCS}$. The third link conflates two distinct cocycles:
the Yukawa $Y_3 \in H^{0, 3}(X)$ is the tree-level cubic
($\ell_3^{\min}$), while the BCOV curving
$\alpha_{\BCOV} \in H^{0, 1}(X)$ is the one-loop BV anomaly
$\chi(X)/24 \cdot [\Omega]$. Both are $\At(TX)$-sourced, but at
different genera; they live in different (Serre-dual) Hodge groups.
On simply-connected strict CY$_3$, $\alpha_{\BCOV} = 0$ identically
(since $H^{0, 1} = 0$) while $Y_3$ generically non-zero; identification
vacuously fails. On $K3 \times E$, K\"unneth gives both non-zero scalar
values but distinct. The correct picture is three cocycles
(Theorem \ref{thm:three-cocycle-hcs}): generator $\At(TX)$, tree-level
$Y_3$, one-loop $\alpha_{\BCOV}$, each with its own role in the BV
master equation of $\hCS$. Cecotti--Vafa $tt^*$-geometry packages all
three but in different tensorial positions on the moduli space $\cM$.
