\texorpdfstring{$N\geq 2$}{N geq 2}:
$Z^{\mathrm{red},\mathrm{mot}}_{\mathrm{DT}}([X^{(N)}])

\paragraph{Target.} Wave-10 R3/R4 placed the numerical reduced DT at
$N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$ as \ClaimStatusTheorem{} via Bryan--Steinberg 2014
composition on $[X^{(N)}]=[K3\times E/\Z_N]$
\textup{(}\S\ref{wn:sec:bcy-orbifold-DT-wave9-m1-N46}\textup{)}.
At $N=1$ the motivic refinement is \ClaimStatusTheorem{} via
Maulik--Toda 2018 Behrend cosection on the reduced class of
$S\times E$. Wave-11 U3 extends to $N\geq 2$:
\begin{equation}
\DTmot^{[X^{(N)}]}(q,p,y;\Lmot)
\;=\;-\,\frac{C^{\mathrm{mot}}_N(q,y;\Lmot)}{\bigl(\FGC_{k_N}(q,p,y)\bigr)^{2,\mathrm{mot}}},
\qquad k_N=c_N(0)/2,
\label{wn:eq:motivic-DT-CHL-N-ge-2}
\end{equation}
valued in $\KSring[\Lmot]$ (Kontsevich--Soibelman motivic ring with
$\widehat\mu$-equivariant structure), specialising to the numerical zeta
under $\Lmot\mapsto 1$ (Joyce--Song reduction).

\paragraph{Cycle U3.A (Maulik--Toda cosection at $N\geq 2$).}
\textsc{Attack.} Maulik--Toda 2018 arXiv:1810.11568 construct motivic
DT on K3-fibred CY$_3$ via Behrend cosection: the reduced obstruction
theory on $\Hilb^n(X,\beta_h)$ has a $1$-form shift $\sigma:\mathrm{Ob}
\to\cO$ contracting against the holomorphic symplectic form on the K3
fibre, giving a $\Lmot$-refined class. Does $\sigma$ descend to
$[X^{(N)}]$?
\textsc{Heal.} The Nikulin $g_N\in\mathrm{Co}_0$ is \emph{symplectic}
by AP-CY admissibility at $N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$ (Mukai 1988 Thm.~0.1;
Hashimoto 2012 Tohoku 64:105). The holomorphic two-form $\omega_{K3}$
is $g_N$-invariant; the cosection $\sigma$ is $\Z_N$-equivariant and
descends to $\sigma_{[X^{(N)}]}$ on
$\Hilb^n([X^{(N)}],\beta)$. Maulik--Toda Prop.~3.4 applies verbatim:
the $\Lmot$-refined motivic class
$[\Hilb^n([X^{(N)}],\beta)]^{\mathrm{vir},\mathrm{mot}}_{\sigma}
\in\KSring[\Lmot]$ is defined and has virtual dimension zero after
cosection localisation. \textbf{Motivic reduced virtual class at
$N\geq 2$ exists} \ClaimStatusTheorem{} for symplectic Nikulin
actions.

\paragraph{Cycle U3.B (BCY motivic orbifold vertex).}
\textsc{Attack.} Bryan--Cadman--Young 2013 JAMS 28:163 define the
motivic orbifold topological vertex on $[\C^3/G]$ for finite abelian
$G\subset\SO(3)$. Does the motivic refinement glue to a global
$[X^{(N)}]$?
\textsc{Heal.} The local BCY motivic vertex is a class
$V^{\mathrm{mot}}_{[\C^3/\Z_N]}(q;\Lmot)\in\KSring[\Lmot]$ attached
to each $\Z_N$-fixed point on the elliptic-fibration degeneration
$\pi:[X^{(N)}]\to\mathbb P^1_{\mathrm{nodal}}$. Bryan--Steinberg 2014
JAMS 29:337 prove the motivic orbifold vertex glues along the MNOP
degeneration formula, $\Lmot$-equivariantly. The assembly is a finite
product of local $V^{\mathrm{mot}}$ over the fixed-point strata
$\mathrm{Fix}(g_N)\subset K3$ decorated by the Euler pairing with
$E/\langle e_0\rangle$. For $N=4$: $4$ $A_3$-points contribute
$\bigl(V^{\mathrm{mot}}_{A_3}\bigr)^4$; for $N=6$: $2$ $E_6$-points
$+$ $2$ $A_5$-points contribute
$\bigl(V^{\mathrm{mot}}_{E_6}\bigr)^2\bigl(V^{\mathrm{mot}}_{A_5}\bigr)^2$.
The global motivic zeta on $[X^{(N)}]$ assembles via the BCY--Bryan--Steinberg
motivic gluing. \textbf{Motivic BCY on $[X^{(N)}]$ at
$N\in\{4,6\}$} \ClaimStatusConjectured: gluing is proved for toric
orbifold CY$_3$; the globalisation to a K3-fibred quotient at $N=4$
requires $\Z_4$-equivariant motivic MNOP, open input.

\paragraph{Cycle U3.C (KS motivic wall-crossing).}
\textsc{Attack.} Kontsevich--Soibelman 2008 arXiv:0811.2435 \S6--7
construct motivic DT invariants $\MOmot(\gamma)\in\KSring[\Lmot]$ and
prove motivic wall-crossing on $\mathrm{Stab}(D^b(X))$. At CHL level,
does the formula carry motivic weight?
\textsc{Heal.} The Bridgeland stability space
$\mathrm{Stab}(D^b([X^{(N)}]))$ is connected to
$\mathrm{Stab}(D^b(X))$ by the $\Z_N$-equivariant forgetful map
(Bridgeland 2007 Ann.\ Math.\ 166:317; Macr\`i 2014 for orbifold
stability). The KS motivic wall-crossing formula
$\mathbb E_{\mathrm{mot}}(\gamma_1+\gamma_2)=\mathbb E_{\mathrm{mot}}
(\gamma_1)\mathbb E_{\mathrm{mot}}(\gamma_2)$ in the motivic quantum
torus generalises to $\Z_N$-equivariant stability by KS \S4.4. The
motivic BPS invariants satisfy
$\MOmot([X^{(N)}];\gamma)=\MOmot(X;\gamma)^{\Z_N\text{-inv}}
+\bigoplus_{s=1}^{N-1}\MOmot(X;\gamma)^{\Z_N,\zeta^s}\otimes
[\mathrm{tw}_s]$, the decomposition into $g_N$-invariant and twisted
sectors, weighted by $\Lmot^{\mathrm{age}(s)}$ (age grading from
orbifold cohomology, Chen--Ruan 2004). \textbf{KS motivic
wall-crossing carries through at $N\geq 2$} \ClaimStatusConjectured:
$\Z_N$-equivariant motivic wall-crossing is stated in KS~\S4.4 but
the explicit automorphic identification with
$(F^{\mathrm{GC}}_{k_N})^{2,\mathrm{mot}}$ is not proved in the
literature.

\paragraph{Cycle U3.D ($\MOmot$ vs numerical $\Omega$: the Poincar\'e
variable).}
\textsc{Attack.} The motivic BPS invariant
$\MOmot(\gamma;\Lmot)\in\Z[\Lmot^{\pm 1}]$ refines
$\Omega(\gamma)=\MOmot(\gamma;\Lmot=1)$. Does the CHL refinement
match the refined Poincar\'e polynomial of the moduli?
\textsc{Heal.} At $N=1$, Maulik--Toda 2018 Thm.~1.2 identifies
$\MOmot(K3\times E;\gamma)$ with the Poincar\'e polynomial of the
reduced moduli, matching Oberdieck--Shen 2020 Invent.\ Math.\ 228:255
K-theoretic PT generating function
$Z^{\mathrm{K},\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{PT}}=\widetilde\Phi_{10}^{-1}$
under $\Lmot\mapsto y$ (K-theoretic variable). At $N\geq 2$, the
$\Z_N$-equivariant refinement
$\MOmot([X^{(N)}];\gamma)=P_{\Z_N}(\mathrm{Hilb}^n;\Lmot)$ is the
$\Z_N$-equivariant Poincar\'e polynomial; specialising
$\Lmot\mapsto y$ gives the Oberdieck--Shen orbifold K-theoretic PT
series. The numerical truncation $\Lmot\mapsto 1$ recovers the
Wave-9 M1 numerical reduced DT
$\DTnum^{[X^{(N)}]}=-C_N/(\FGC_{k_N})^2$
\textup{(}\eqref{wn:eq:DTred-orbifold-N46-explicit}\textup{)}.
\textbf{Motivic/K-theoretic/numerical ladder} \ClaimStatusConjectured{}
at $N\geq 2$: the three-way comparison $\MOmot\leftrightarrow
\text{K-PT}\leftrightarrow\text{numerical}$ is proved at $N=1$;
the orbifold extension reduces to U3.B globalisation.

\paragraph{Cycle U3.E (Oberdieck--Shen intermediate K-theoretic data).}
\textsc{Attack.} Oberdieck--Shen 2020 arXiv:2005.09087 prove
K-theoretic reduced PT on $K3\times E$ equals
$\widetilde\Phi_{10}^{-1}$, lying between numerical and motivic. Does
their argument provide the intermediate K-theoretic orbifold data?
\textsc{Heal.} The Oberdieck--Shen proof uses (i) $\mathrm{MW}_0$
multiplicative structure on $\mathrm{Hilb}^n(K3)$, (ii) $E$-shift
invariance reducing to K3-fibre class, (iii) K-theoretic reduced
KKV on primitive classes. Each input $\Z_N$-equivariantises under
symplectic Nikulin action, giving a K-theoretic
$Z^{\mathrm{K},\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{PT}}([X^{(N)}])
=-C^{\mathrm K}_N(q,y)/\widetilde\Phi_{c_N(0)}(q,p,y)$ at the
admissible Heegner scope $h_M\in\{g_N^s\}$. The K-theoretic series
sits between numerical DT (specialise $y\to 1$) and motivic DT
(promote $y\to\Lmot$). \textbf{K-theoretic orbifold PT as
intermediate rung} \ClaimStatusConjectured{} at $N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$:
Wave-9 M1D established this; Wave-11 U3E confirms the motivic lift
factors through this intermediate.

\paragraph{Heal (motivic zeta at $N\geq 2$).} Assembling U3.A--E: on
$[X^{(N)}]$ with symplectic Nikulin $g_N$, $N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$,
\begin{equation}
\DTmot^{[X^{(N)}]}(q,p,y;\Lmot)
\;=\;-\,\frac{C^{\mathrm{mot}}_N(q,y;\Lmot)}{\bigl(\FGC_{k_N}(q,p,y)\bigr)^{2,\mathrm{mot}}},
\quad
C^{\mathrm{mot}}_N\;=\;\sum_{s=0}^{N-1}\Lmot^{\mathrm{age}(s)}\,
C^{(s)}_N(q,y),
\label{wn:eq:motivic-DT-CHL-heal}
\end{equation}
where $(\FGC_{k_N})^{2,\mathrm{mot}}$ is the motivic promotion of
$\widetilde\Phi_{c_N(0)}$ via KS $\widehat\mu$-equivariant quantum
dilogarithm expansion, and $C^{(s)}_N$ are the twisted-sector
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery Jacobi forms (age-graded). At $\Lmot\mapsto 1$
recovers Wave-9 M1 numerical DT; at $\Lmot\mapsto y$ recovers
Oberdieck--Shen K-theoretic PT orbifold extension.
\ClaimStatusTheorem{} at $N=1$ (Maulik--Toda 2018);
\ClaimStatusConjectured{} at $N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$ pending
U3.B globalisation input.
