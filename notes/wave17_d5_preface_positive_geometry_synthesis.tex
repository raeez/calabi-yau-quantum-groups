\section*{Wave-17 D5: the universal positive-geometry grammar for $G(X)$}
\label{wn:wave17-D5-positive-geometry}

\subsection*{Three paragraphs for the preface (draft for user review)}
\label{wn:wave17-D5-three-paragraphs}

\ClaimStatusSynthesis\;\;\emph{Paragraph~1 (universal grammar).}
Every concrete realisation of the CY quantum group $G(X)$ factors
through a single universal equation. Let $\Meff(X)$ denote the moduli
stack of effective objects of the CY category $D^b\Coh(X)$, graded by
the positive cone of the numerical $K$-theory lattice. The positive
half is
\[
 Y^{+}(X) \;=\; \Heq\!\bigl(\Meff(X)\bigr),
\]
with Hall convolution along the stack of short exact sequences, and
Drinfeld double
$
 G(X) \;=\; D\!\bigl(Y^{+}(X)\bigr).
$
This is the Kontsevich--Soibelman
\cite{KontsevichSoibelman2008} cohomological Hall algebra in its
geometric-rather-than-quiver phrasing: $\Meff(X)$ is the universal
positive geometry, and the toric/quiver presentation arises by
\emph{localisation} when a quiver-with-potential $(Q_X, W_X)$ exists.
The Schiffmann--Vasserot identification
\cite{SchiffmannVasserot2013}
$\CoHA(Q_{\C^3}) \cong Y^{+}(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$ is the $\C^3$
instance; Van den Bergh's noncommutative crepant resolution
\cite{VandenBergh2004} is the algebraic backbone that makes the
localisation canonical; the noncommutative coordinate ring
$\mathrm{End}(T_X)^{\mathrm{op}}$ is the Hall site's Koszul dual.

\ClaimStatusSynthesis\;\;\emph{Paragraph~2 (equivariance stratification
by geometric class).}
The equivariance ring carried by $\Heq$ is geometric-class-dependent,
not universal:
\[
 \Heq = \begin{cases}
  H^{*}_{T}\;\;\text{(toric torus $T = (\C^{*})^{d}$)}, & X \text{ toric CY}_d, \\[0.2em]
  H^{*}_{T \times \C^{*}_{\mathrm{red}}}\;\;\text{(reduced direction)}, & X \text{ compact with reduced class}, \\[0.2em]
  H^{*}_{G}\;\;\text{(orbifold inertia stack)}, & X \text{ an orbifold}, \\[0.2em]
  H^{*}_{\mathrm{Hdg}}\;\;\text{(Hodge/mixed structure)}, & X \text{ polarised irrational}.
 \end{cases}
\]
The toric case recovers Kontsevich--Soibelman directly: the Cartan
torus $T$ acts on $\Meff(\C^3)$ and $\Heq = H^{*}_{T}$ is what the
quiver grading measures. The reduced-compact case is where the
language of toric localisation fails and the universal statement
matters: Pandharipande--Oberdieck
\cite{OberdieckPixton2017}
reduced Donaldson--Thomas is the correct equivariant theory on
compact CY$_3$ whose canonical class vanishes, because the absolute
DT partition function on a compact CY$_3$ is trivially zero
(vanishing virtual fundamental class), and the \emph{reduced} class
is the first nontrivial cohomological witness of $\Meff$. The
orbifold case imports the inertia stack as equivariance site (Adem--
Ruan--Zhang), producing CHL quotients $X_N = (K3 \times E)/(\ZZ/N\ZZ)$
whose Hall algebra is $Y^{+}$ of the orbifold inertia geometry,
and whose Borcherds weight $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ is the
corresponding automorphic shadow. The polarised-irrational case
(general smooth projective CY$_d$ without torus action) requires
Hodge-theoretic equivariance: the mixed Hodge structure on
$\Meff$ plays the role the torus plays in the toric case, and
the Hall product is graded by Hodge type rather than torus weight.

\ClaimStatusSynthesis\;\;\emph{Paragraph~3 (canonical non-toric
witness: $K3 \times E$).}
The threefold $K3 \times E$ is the canonical witness that the
universal grammar exceeds the toric grammar: $K3 \times E$ carries no
three-dimensional torus action, no toric fan, no quiver-with-potential
extracted from a dimer; yet $\Meff(K3 \times E)$ exists as a universal
positive geometry, and its equivariant cohomology with respect to
$T_E = \C^{*}$ (the elliptic translation) plus the reduced class
$\C^{*}_{\mathrm{red}}$ (the anti-canonical direction, trivial but
residually measured) produces the K3 abelian Yangian
$Y^{+}(\frakg_{K3})$ of
Theorem~\ref{thm:k3-abelian-yangian-presentation}.
The reduced DT partition function
$\mathrm{DT}^{\mathrm{red}}(K3 \times E)$ of
Oberdieck--Pixton~2017 is exactly the generating series of
BPS invariants that $\Heq(\Meff(K3 \times E))$ is supposed to compute
on its Cartan, and the Borcherds lift of the reduced DT anchor
--- the weight--$5$ Gritsenko form $\Delta_5 = \mathrm{Borch}(\phi_{0,1})$
of Lorgat~2020 \cite{Lorgat2020} --- is the automorphic shadow
$\kBKM = c_1(0)/2 = 5$. The six routes to
$G(K3 \times E)$ enumerated in the pentagon are six presentations
of $D(Y^{+}(\frakg_{K3 \times E}))$ under the universal grammar:
$R_1$ is $\SpCh_{K3, E}(\PhiFA_3(D^b(\Coh(K3 \times E))))$, the
stage-2 specialisation; $R_2$ is the Borcherds branch, the
automorphic shadow; $R_3, R_4, R_5, R_6$ are different presentations
of $\Meff$ under different equivariance and stability choices.
The $\rho^{R_i} \in \{3, 12, 24\}$ stratification reflects which
equivariance ring each route engages, not a defect: $\rho = 24$
records Frenkel--Kac lattice equivariance on the $\Lambda^{3,2}$-VOA
route; $\rho = 12$ records the $\ZZ/2$-orbifold inertia on the
Kummer route; $\rho = 3$ records the minimal Hodge-graded positive
cone on the stage-2 and BLLPR routes.

\subsection*{Weave into the two-stage factorisation}
\label{wn:wave17-D5-two-stage-weave}

The two-stage factorisation
$\Phi_3 = \SpCh_{\Sigma_2, C} \circ \PhiFA_3$
(\ref{wn:sec:two-stage-Phi-factorisation}) is the CY-to-chiral
functor side; the positive-geometry grammar is the CY-to-quantum-group
side. They compose into one picture. Stage~1 produces the $E_3$-hFA
$\cF_X = \PhiFA_3(D^b\Coh(X))$ on $X$. Stage~2 selects a curve $C$
and a transverse positive cycle $\Sigma_2 \subset X$: the data
$(\Sigma_2, C)$ is exactly the data required to present $\Meff(X)$
as a local Hall site over $C$. Concretely, for $X = K3 \times E$
and $(\Sigma_2, C) = (K3, E)$, $\Meff(X)|_{K3} \to \Meff(X)|_{C}$
is the restriction to the K3 fibre, and
$\Heq(\Meff(X)|_{K3})$ is the fibrewise positive half whose
integral along $C$ is $Y^{+}(\frakg_{K3 \times E})$. Stage~2
\emph{selects the positive effective geometry}: the choice of
$\Sigma_2$ is the choice of stable cone in the $\Heq$-equivariance,
and the choice of $C$ is the choice of spectral parameter curve
for the chiral-algebra output. The shadow
$\SpCh_{K3, E}(\cF_{K3 \times E})$ is not just an $E_1$-chiral
algebra on $E$; it is the universal enveloping chiral algebra of
$Y^{+}(\frakg_{K3 \times E})$ in the sense of Beilinson--Drinfeld
($\sec$ \cite{BeilinsonDrinfeld2004}), and the Drinfeld double
$G(K3 \times E) = D(Y^{+})$ is its chiral monodromy.

\subsection*{Primary sources}
\label{wn:wave17-D5-primary}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Kontsevich--Soibelman 2008, arXiv:0811.2435: cohomological
  Hall algebra of $\Meff$ with stability data; the positive-half
  equation $Y^{+} = \Heq(\Meff)$ in its original phrasing.
 \item Oberdieck--Pixton 2017, arXiv:1706.10100: reduced Donaldson--Thomas
  on $K3 \times E$, reduced class as the cohomological witness for
  compact CY$_3$ with vanishing absolute DT.
 \item Van den Bergh 2004 (\emph{Duke Math.\ J.}): noncommutative
  crepant resolutions; the algebraic backbone that makes the
  Hall-site localisation canonical when $\mathrm{End}(T_X)^{\mathrm{op}}$
  exists as tilting object.
 \item Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013, arXiv:1202.2756: the $\C^3$
  instance $\CoHA(\text{Jordan}) \cong Y^{+}(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$,
  as the cleanest localisation to toric torus.
 \item Lorgat 2020, arXiv:2007.14218: explicit Borcherds lift of
  $\phi_{0,1}$ to $\Delta_5$; $N=1$ anchor $\kBKM = c_1(0)/2 = 5$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{What is open}
\label{wn:wave17-D5-open}

\begin{itemize}
 \item Polarised-irrational CY$_3$ (no torus, no orbifold, no
  reduced-class shortcut): does Hodge-graded $\Heq$ give the
  correct Hall algebra, or is a deeper motivic-Hall refinement
  (Joyce--Song; Davison--Meinhardt) required?
 \item Existence of $\Meff(X)$ as a stack-theoretic object at
  $d \geq 4$: Calabi--Yau fourfold BPS invariants are virtual, and
  whether $\Heq(\Meff)$ lands in a chiral bialgebra (CY-A$_4$) is
  presently open.
 \item $K3 \times E$ six-routes-to-one: the pentagon colimit
  (Proposition~\ref{prop:cy-c-pentagon-colimit}) is the conjectural
  universal presentation of $G(K3 \times E) = D(Y^{+}(\frakg_{K3 \times E}))$;
  proving pentagon colimit $=$ Drinfeld double remains a CY-C frontier.
\end{itemize}
