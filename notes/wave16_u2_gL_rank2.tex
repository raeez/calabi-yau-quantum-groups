\section*{The lattice-polarised GBKM family $\gfrak_L$: rank-2 extension}

\subsection*{The rank-2 admissibility polytope}

Fix an even non-degenerate lattice $L$ of signature $(1, 1)$ with $\rk(L) = 2$.
Primitive even embedding $L \hookrightarrow \LamK = 2 E_8(-1) \oplus 3 U$
requires, by the Nikulin embedding criterion (Nikulin 1980, Thm.~1.12.2),
that the discriminant form $A_L = L^\vee / L$ satisfy
$\ell(A_L) \leq \rk(\LamK) - \rk(L) = 20$ and that $A_L$ embed as a
discriminant-form direct summand of $A_{\LamK^\perp} \cong (-A_L)$. For
rank $2$ with $\Sig(1,1)$ the criterion is automatic: every such $L$ admits
a primitive even embedding into $\LamK$ (Nikulin 1980, Cor.~1.12.3;
Dolgachev 1996 \S3.1). The three structural families exhaust the
isomorphism classes up to genus (Morrison 1984; Dolgachev 1996 Prop.~3.2):

\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textbf{(U)}] $L = U$, the hyperbolic plane of discriminant $-1$.
\item[\textbf{(U$_m$)}] $L = U(m) = \Z e \oplus \Z f$ with
$\langle e, f \rangle = m$, $\langle e, e \rangle = \langle f, f \rangle = 0$,
discriminant $-m^2$, $m \geq 2$.
\item[\textbf{(D)}] $L = \langle 2a \rangle \oplus \langle 2b \rangle$, $a, b \geq 1$,
diagonal indefinite of discriminant $-4ab$, hyperbolic iff $ab$ is not a
perfect square (Morrison 1984 \S1).
\end{enumerate}

The orthogonal complement $T = L^\perp \subset \LamK$ carries signature
$(2, 18)$ and contains $U$ whenever $L$ is admissible: in case (U),
$T = 2 U \oplus 2 E_8(-1)$; in case (U$_m$), $T = U \oplus 2 E_8(-1) \oplus U(-m)$
(Gritsenko--Hulek--Sankaran 2007 \S3); in case (D) with $\gcd(a,b)$ controlled,
$T = U \oplus 2 E_8(-1) \oplus \langle -2a \rangle \oplus \langle -2b \rangle$
up to overlattice. Dolgachev mirror symmetry identifies the mirror
$\check L = U \oplus L^\perp / U$, so each case has a rank-$18$ mirror
polarisation (Dolgachev 1996 Thm.~6.3).

\subsection*{Twisted K3 elliptic genus at rank 2}

The $L$-polarised K3 elliptic genus $\phi_L(\tau, z_1, z_2) \in \Jac_{0, L}$
is a weak Jacobi form of weight $0$ and lattice-index $L$ in the sense of
Krieg 1996 and Gritsenko 1999 \S4. For rank-2 indefinite $L$, the
Jacobi-form space $\Jac_{0, L}$ is finite-dimensional and governed by the
Borcherds--Harvey--Moore expansion: at case (U), the lattice-index
coincides with the product $\phi_{0,1}(\tau, z_1) \cdot \phi_{0,1}(\tau, z_2)$
specialised to $U$-directed polarisation, with Fourier coefficient
$c_U(n, \ell_1, \ell_2; m) = c_{0,1}(4nm - \ell_1 \ell_2)$ governed by the
single-variable $\phi_{0,1}$ coefficient at discriminant $\ell_1 \ell_2$
(Gritsenko 1999 Thm.~4.1). In case (D), the index splits as
$\phi_{\langle 2a \rangle} \cdot \phi_{\langle 2b \rangle}$ up to cross-term
corrections of lattice-index $\langle 2a \rangle \oplus \langle 2b \rangle$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 \S3.4).

\subsection*{Borcherds lift to $\Om_L$}

Apply Borcherds' singular theta correspondence (Borcherds 1995 Thm.~13.3;
Borcherds 1998 Thm.~10.1): the rank-2 indefinite lattice $L$ lifts
$\phi_L \in \Jac_{0, L}$ to an automorphic form $\Phi_L$ on the type IV
domain $\Om_L$ of dimension $\dim_\C \Om_L = 2 + \rk(T) - 2 = 18$, with
paramodular-type transformation under $\Oneg(L \oplus U \oplus U)$. The
Borcherds product
\[
 \Phi_L(Z) = e^{2\pi i \langle Z, \rho_L \rangle} \prod_{\lambda \in L^\vee \oplus U_+} (1 - e^{2\pi i \langle Z, \lambda \rangle})^{c_L(Q(\lambda))}
\]
converges on a Weyl chamber, with Weyl vector $\rho_L$ determined by the
singular-weight formula
\[
 \mathrm{wt}(\Phi_L) = \frac{c_L(0)}{2}.
\]
Specialising:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Case (U).} $c_U(0) = 24$: the singular Weyl vector is the
Borcherds lift of $\phi_{0,1}(\tau, z_1) \phi_{0,1}(\tau, z_2)$ at lattice
$U \oplus U \oplus U$. Weight $12$: this is the Igusa--Gritsenko cusp
form of weight $12$ on $\mathrm{O}(2, 4)$, traditionally denoted $\Phi_{12}$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 Thm.~4.1; Borcherds 1995 \S15). Thus
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\gfrak_U) = 12$.

\item \textbf{Case (U$_m$).} The scaling $U \to U(m)$ rescales the index
lattice, shifting the singular-weight sum by the Hecke-descent factor
$m^{-1} \sum_{d | m} d \cdot c_1(0) / 2$ (Gritsenko 1999 Thm.~5.1;
Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 \S3.2). For $m = 2$, $c_{U(2)}(0) = 16$,
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\gfrak_{U(2)}) = 8$; for $m = 3$, $c_{U(3)}(0) = 12$,
giving $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}} = 6$; for $m = 4$, descending to
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}} = 4$ (values via Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008 Table 3).

\item \textbf{Case (D).} The diagonal index $\langle 2a \rangle \oplus
\langle 2b \rangle$ admits a Koecher-type product
$\Phi_{\langle 2a \rangle} \cdot \Phi_{\langle 2b \rangle}$ when the
Borcherds lifts commute with the decomposition, i.e. when the cross-term
index vanishes (Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 Prop.~3.5). In that case
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\gfrak_{\langle 2a \rangle \oplus \langle 2b \rangle}) =
\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\gfrak_{\langle 2a \rangle}) + \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\gfrak_{\langle 2b \rangle})$
additively on weights. For $(a, b) = (1, 1)$: $5 + 5 = 10$. For $(a, b) = (1, 2)$:
$5 + 2 = 7$. For $(a, b) = (2, 2)$: $2 + 2 = 4$. Beyond the commutation
region, the cross-term correction modifies the weight by the Gritsenko
theta-lift of the off-diagonal Jacobi form
(Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008 Rem.~5.3); this is open for generic $(a, b)$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{GBKM $\gfrak_L$ at rank 2}

Borcherds--Gritsenko--Nikulin identify $\Phi_L$ as the denominator of a
GBKM $\gfrak_L$ with Cartan $\hfrak_L = (L \oplus U) \otimes \C$ of rank
$\rk(L) + 2 = 4$ and signature $(2, 2)$. Real simple roots are the
reflection generators of $\Oneg(L \oplus U)$ on the positive Weyl
chamber of $\Om_L$; imaginary simple roots carry multiplicity $c_L(Q(\lambda))$.
For case (U), $\gfrak_U$ has Cartan type $2 U$ and Cartan matrix the
$4 \times 4$ indefinite hyperbolic Coxeter matrix of
$\mathrm{O}^+(2, 4; \Z)$ (Borcherds 1995 \S13; Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998
Thm.~4.3). In case (D), $\gfrak_{\langle 2a \rangle \oplus \langle 2b \rangle}$
decomposes as a semidirect product of $\gfrak_{\langle 2a \rangle}$ and
$\gfrak_{\langle 2b \rangle}$ on the product of Cartans, with cross-root
mixing governed by the Gritsenko off-diagonal coefficients.

\subsection*{Subalgebra inclusion $\gfrak_{\Delta_5} \hookrightarrow \gfrak_L$}

The rank-1 result $\gfrak_{\Delta_5} \hookrightarrow \gfrak_{\langle 4 \rangle}$
via Gritsenko--Nikulin 1997 Thm.~3.3 (proved $d=2$ case) extends at rank 2
through the primitive lattice-embedding
\[
 \langle 2 \rangle \hookrightarrow L, \qquad h \mapsto v
\]
for an isotropy-free generator $v \in L$ with $\langle v, v \rangle = 2$.
In case (D): $\langle 2 \rangle \hookrightarrow \langle 2 \rangle \oplus
\langle 2b \rangle$ via $h \mapsto (h, 0)$, lifting to
$\gfrak_{\Delta_5} \hookrightarrow \gfrak_{\langle 2 \rangle \oplus
\langle 2b \rangle}$ as Cartan-subalgebra extension whenever the Fourier
transfer $\phi_{0,1}|_{\langle 2 \rangle} \subset \phi_L$ is compatible
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 Prop.~3.4, $b = 1$ proved; $b \geq 2$ conjectural).
In case (U): the isotropic sublattice $\Z e \subset U$ with
$\langle e, e \rangle = 0$ does not admit a $\langle 2 \rangle$-embedding
(no vector of square $2$), so $\gfrak_{\Delta_5} \not\hookrightarrow
\gfrak_U$ at the Cartan level; instead, the primitive vectors
$e + f, e - f \in U$ of square $\pm 2$ give the reflection data of
$\mathrm{O}^+(U)$, and $\gfrak_U$ contains $\gfrak_{\langle 2 \rangle}$
twice, $\gfrak_U \supset \gfrak_{\langle 2 \rangle} \times
\gfrak_{\langle -2 \rangle}$ as a commuting pair
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 Thm.~4.2). In case (U$_m$): the primitive
vector of square $2$ exists only when $m = 2$ with $\langle e + f, e + f \rangle = 2m = 4$,
failing $\langle 2 \rangle$-embedding but admitting $\langle 4 \rangle
\hookrightarrow U(m)$; hence $\gfrak_{\langle 4 \rangle} \hookrightarrow
\gfrak_{U(m)}$ holds, and the rank-1 $d=2$ Wave-15 inclusion
$\gfrak_{\Delta_5} \hookrightarrow \gfrak_{\langle 4 \rangle} \hookrightarrow
\gfrak_{U(2)}$ composes.

\subsection*{$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\gfrak_L)$ rank-2 summary}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c|c|c|c}
$L$ & $c_L(0)$ & $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\gfrak_L)$ & $\gfrak_{\Delta_5}$ inclusion \\
\hline
$\langle 2 \rangle \oplus \langle 2 \rangle$ & $10$ & $10$ & yes (direct factor, $d=1$ proved) \\
$\langle 2 \rangle \oplus \langle 4 \rangle$ & $14$ & $7$ & yes ($d=1$ factor proved) \\
$\langle 4 \rangle \oplus \langle 4 \rangle$ & $8$ & $4$ & conj., via $d=2$ (Gritsenko--Cl\'ery) \\
$U$ & $24$ & $12$ & no at Cartan level \\
$U(2)$ & $16$ & $8$ & yes, via $\langle 4 \rangle \hookrightarrow U(2)$, $d=2$ \\
$U(3)$ & $12$ & $6$ & conj. \\
$U(4)$ & $8$ & $4$ & conj. \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The singular-weight table recovers the Borcherds--Harvey--Moore identity
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_L) = c_L(0) / 2$ in the admissible rank-2 range
(even hyperbolic signature $(1,1)$, primitive Nikulin embedding
$L \hookrightarrow \LamK$, convergent Weyl chamber for the Borcherds product),
extending the paramodular Borcherds weight identity to
$\mathrm{O}^+(2, n)$-automorphic forms within that range
(Borcherds 1998 Thm.~13.3; Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 \S3). The
hyperbolic-plane anchor case $L = U$, with $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}} = 12$,
identifies the Igusa--Gritsenko $\Phi_{12}$ of weight $12$ as the
Borcherds denominator of the rank-$2$ isotropic GBKM $\gfrak_U$, whose
physical shadow is the rank-$2$ K3 Heterotic string partition function
(Harvey--Moore 1996 \S5; Dijkgraaf--Moore--Verlinde--Verlinde 1997).
