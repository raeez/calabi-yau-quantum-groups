\section*{Hashimoto trace and chiral transfer at the
\texorpdfstring{$\Delta_5$}{Delta5} multiplier}
\label{note:hashimoto-trace-delta5}

\paragraph{Finite trace object.}
Let \(\nu_{\Delta_5}\) be the order-two multiplier character of the
weight-\(5\) Gritsenko--Nikulin form \(\Delta_5\) on \(\Sp_4(\Z)\).
For a prime \(p\), the trace to compute is
\[
  \Tr\!\left(T_p\mid S_5(\Sp_4(\Z),\nu_{\Delta_5})\right).
\]
A trace formula has three structural inputs: a finite-dimensional,
proper, or completed trace-class object; an orientation or cyclic
pairing making the trace invariant under cyclic rotation; and a
convergence or completion convention for every infinite sum.  Here the
ordinary finite trace exists once the dimension formula for
\(S_5(\Sp_4(\Z),\nu_{\Delta_5})\) is invoked.  If this space is
one-dimensional and generated by the normalized \(\Delta_5\), the
displayed trace equals the \(T_p\)-eigenvalue of \(\Delta_5\) in the
same Hecke normalization.  The class-sum calculation remains the
independent verification of that scalar.

\paragraph{Cyclic orientation.}
For a Calabi--Yau category \(\mathcal C\), the cyclic trace is supplied
by a negative cyclic Calabi--Yau class
\[
  [\sigma]\in HC^-_d(\mathcal C)
\]
and the associated nondegenerate pairing
\[
  \langle-,-\rangle_{\mathcal C}\colon
  \operatorname{Hom}(x,y)\otimes\operatorname{Hom}(y,x)\longrightarrow
  \C[-d].
\]
This pairing gives \(\str_{\mathcal C}(f)\) for perfect endomorphisms
and is the source of the invariant form on the chiral output.  For
local \(\hCS\), noncompact Hall stacks, or infinite charge sectors, the
replacement is compactly supported or equivariant Borel--Moore
homology, followed by a graded completion in which each coefficient has
finite trace.  A chiral trace statement therefore carries its
properness and completion data as part of the theorem.

\paragraph{Hashimoto paramodular class sum.}
Hashimoto's paramodular formula gives the character-twisted finite
trace in the form
\[
\Tr\!\left(T_m\mid S_k(\Gamma_t(N),\nu)\right)
 =
 I_\infty(k)\delta_{m,1}
 +\sum_{\{\gamma\}}
   \nu(\gamma)\,\mathcal O_\gamma(m)\,
   \mathcal H_\infty(k;\gamma),
\]
where \(\gamma\) ranges over the relevant elliptic, hyperbolic, and
parabolic conjugacy classes, \(\mathcal O_\gamma(m)\) is the finite
orbital factor, and \(\mathcal H_\infty(k;\gamma)\) is the holomorphic
discrete-series character.  At
\[
  (k,t,N,\nu)=(5,1,1,\nu_{\Delta_5}),
\]
the computation requires the class representatives, the theta-multiplier
values \(\nu_{\Delta_5}(\gamma)\), the local orbital factors at \(p\),
and the regularized parabolic contribution.  The multiplier signs come
from the theta character on the representatives, together with the same
Hecke normalization used for the Fourier--Jacobi eigenvalue.

\paragraph{Hashimoto--Bass graph zeta.}
Let \(G\) be a finite graph, \(E^\pm(G)\) its oriented edges, and
\(B_G\) the nonbacktracking edge operator
\[
  (B_G)_{e,f}=
  \begin{cases}
  1, & t(e)=o(f)\ \text{and}\ f\ne \bar e,\\
  0, & \text{otherwise.}
  \end{cases}
\]
The graph-zeta trace is the cyclic-word trace
\[
  Z_G(u)=
  \exp\!\left(\sum_{n\geq 1}\frac{\Tr(B_G^n)}{n}u^n\right),
  \qquad |u|\ll 1,
\]
and finite dimensionality gives the determinant identity
\[
  Z_G(u)^{-1}
  =\det(1-uB_G)
  =(1-u^2)^{|E(G)|-|V(G)|}
    \det\!\left(1-uA_G+u^2(D_G-1)\right).
\]
For a quiver, crystal graph, or Hall Fock space with infinitely many
charge sectors, the same formula needs a graded completion for which
each \(\Tr(B_G^n)\) is finite and \(1-uB_G\) has a Fredholm determinant.
This graph trace counts closed nonbacktracking cycles.  Hashimoto's
paramodular trace counts conjugacy classes and orbital integrals.  The
two traces share cyclicity, finite/properness requirements, and
completion requirements; their operators and summation spaces are
distinct.

\paragraph{The square identity.}
The product identity
\[
  \Delta_5^2=\chi_{10}
\]
fixes the modular form, divisor, and multiplier normalization.  Since
\(T_p\) is a linear Hecke operator and lacks product multiplicativity
on Siegel modular forms, the equation
\[
  \lambda_p(\Delta_5)^2=\lambda_p(\chi_{10})
\]
demands a separate Hecke-algebra proof in the chosen normalization.
The square identity supplies the divisor datum; the trace formula or
Fourier--Jacobi extraction supplies the eigenvalue.

\paragraph{Borcherds constants.}
The Borcherds input at level \(1\) is the Eichler--Zagier form
\[
  \phi_{0,1}(\tau,z)=r^{-1}+10+r+O(q).
\]
Therefore
\[
  c_1(0)=10,\qquad
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=c_1(0)/2=5.
\]
These constants are Fourier coefficients of the Borcherds input and are
independent of the Hecke trace.
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)=0,\qquad
  \kch(K3\times E)=0,\qquad
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)=3,\qquad
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=5,\qquad
  \kfib(K3)=24.
\]
These are five named invariants, attached to compact Hodge theory,
the Heisenberg specialization, the Borcherds denominator, and the
Mukai lattice.

\paragraph{Hall and CoHA transfer.}
On the toric chart,
\[
  \CoHA(\C^3)\simeq
  Y^+(\widehat{\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}}_1)
\]
as an \(\Eone\) Hall/shuffle algebra in the Schiffmann--Vasserot
normalization.  The full higher-spin vertex algebra is reached through
the center, double, completion, and evaluation chain
\[
  Y^+(\widehat{\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}}_1)
  \hookrightarrow D(Y^+(\widehat{\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}}_1))
  \xrightarrow{\mathrm{ev}_\lambda}
  \operatorname{End}(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}[\lambda]\text{-vacuum}).
\]
For \(K3\times E\), the Hall--Drinfeld double is built from the
positive Hall object, a Hopf or stable-envelope pairing, and a
completion compatible with the Borcherds denominator.  The K3
self-mirror Yangian branch is a separate representation-theoretic
branch.  The Borcherds denominator algebra \(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5}\)
belongs to the automorphic denominator construction attached to
\(\Delta_5\).

\paragraph{Chiral transfer and \(\hCS\) data.}
The \(d=3\) CY-to-chiral construction is two-stage:
\[
  \Phi_3^{(\Sigma_2,C)}
  =
  \SpCh_{\Sigma_2,C}\circ\PhiFA_3.
\]
Its specialization on \(C\) lands in \(\Eone\)-chiral algebras.  The
braided \(\Etwo\) structure lives on
\[
  \cZ\!\left(\Rep^{\Eone}(A_{\mathcal C})\right),
  \qquad A_{\mathcal C}=\Phi_3^{(\Sigma_2,C)}(\mathcal C),
\]
through half-braidings and \(R\)-matrices.

A compact or global \(6d\) \(\hCS\) invocation must specify the BV field
complex, orientation or framing, a solution of the quantum master
equation, anomaly cancellation, counterterms, descent and sewing
compatibility, and the completion used for infinite state spaces.  Local
\(\C^3\) calculations supply chart data for \(Y^+\).  Gluing those chart
data over \(K3\times E\) demands the same cyclic pairing, properness,
and completion hypotheses used in the trace construction.

\paragraph{Computations demanded by the statement.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Compute \(\dim S_5(\Sp_4(\Z),\nu_{\Delta_5})\) in the same
  multiplier convention used for \(\Delta_5\), citing a primary
  dimension formula with its character normalization.
\item Implement Hashimoto's \(T_p\)-trace at \(k=5,t=1,N=1\) with the
  character \(\nu_{\Delta_5}\): class representatives, archimedean
  characters, finite orbital factors, and the regularized parabolic
  contribution.
\item Evaluate \(\nu_{\Delta_5}\) on Hashimoto's elliptic
  representatives from the theta multiplier, with weight and divisor
  data recorded as separate normalizations.
\item Extract the \(T_p\)-eigenvalue of \(\Delta_5\) from its
  Fourier--Jacobi expansion for at least \(p=2,3\), in the same
  normalization as the trace formula.
\item Compare the class-sum trace with the direct eigenvalue extraction.
  Agreement at \(p=2,3\) gives the first local numerical check; a
  formula for all \(p\) requires the full local-factor calculation.
\item For any graph-zeta comparison, exhibit the finite graph or the
  completed nonbacktracking operator, the determinant convention, and
  the coefficientwise finiteness proof.
\item For any Hall-to-chiral comparison, exhibit the Hall pairing,
  Drinfeld double or center passage, evaluation module, and completion.
\item For any global \(\hCS\) comparison, exhibit the QME solution,
  anomaly cancellation, counterterms, descent/sewing datum, and cyclic
  orientation.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Separation of invariants.}
The trace scalar and the Borcherds weight are different invariants.
The trace scalar depends on the Hecke action on
\(S_5(\Sp_4(\Z),\nu_{\Delta_5})\).  The Borcherds weight is
\[
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=5,
\]
read from the coefficient \(c_1(0)=10\) of the Borcherds input.
