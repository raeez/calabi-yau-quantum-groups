\section*{Slide scan: gluing, covers, effective geometry}

The $953$-line transcript of the automorphic-corrections slides
(\texttt{/tmp/automorphic-slides.txt}) admits no geometric gluing,
no \v{C}ech datum, no toric patch, no NCCR, no quiver cover, and
no explicit Pandharipande--Oberdieck citation. The words
\emph{cover}, \emph{glue}, \emph{chart}, \emph{patch}, \emph{toric},
\emph{NCCR}, \emph{quiver}, \emph{descent}, \emph{sheaf},
\emph{Oberdieck}, \emph{Pandharipande}, \emph{affine} do not
appear. The positive-geometry grammar of Vol III --- effective
divisors assembled from open toric charts, NCCR patches glued by
birational equivalence, Pandharipande--Oberdieck reduced DT
reduction on $K3 \times E$ --- is not anticipated by the slides.

\paragraph{What the slides do contain.}
Three themes carry the effective content.

\emph{(i) Lattice-level fundamental polyhedra.}
Lines $334$--$396$ cut the hyperbolic cone
$\cC(\Lambda^{2,1})_{+} \subset \Lambda^{2,1} \otimes \mathbb{R}$
by the reflection hyperplanes $\mathcal{H}_{\alpha}$ into
fundamental polyhedra $\{\cP, \cP_{I}, \cP_{II}\}$, with
$\cP' = \bigcap_{\delta \in \cP'_{\mathrm{prim}}} \mathcal{H}^{+}_{\delta}$
and automorphism stabilisers
\[
A(\cP) = \langle 1 \rangle, \quad
A(\cP_{I}) = \langle s_{f_{3}} \rangle \cong S_{2}, \quad
A(\cP_{II}) = \langle s_{f_{2}-f_{3}}, s_{f_{-2}-f_{2}} \rangle \cong S_{3}.
\]
The semidirect product
$O(\Lambda^{2,1})_{+} \cong W^{(2)}(\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}) \rtimes S_{3}
\cong \PGL_{2}(\Z)$ (line $396$) gives a tiling, not a cover: the
reflection polyhedra tile hyperbolic space, they do not form a
\v{C}ech cover of a geometric object. The geometry is intrinsic to
the lattice, never transported to $K3 \times E$ as a cover.

\emph{(ii) Primitive-vector discipline.}
The effective-geometry keyword \emph{primitive} occurs five times:
primitive hyperbolic sub-lattice $\Lambda^{2,1} \hookrightarrow
\Lambda^{3,2}$ (line $272$); primitive $\alpha$ with
$(\alpha,\alpha) > 0$ generating reflections (line $285$);
$\Delta^{(k)}(\Lambda^{2,1}) = \{\delta \in \Lambda^{2,1} \mid
(\delta,\delta) = k, \ \delta \ \text{primitive}\}$ (line $358$);
normal subgroups $W^{(2)}(\Lambda^{2,1}_{I}),
W^{(2)}(\Lambda^{2,1}_{II})$ generated by primitive squares
$\delta_{I}, \delta_{II}$ of index $2$ and $6$ respectively (lines
$370$--$372$); square-$2$ primitive orthogonals
$\cP'_{\mathrm{prim}}$ cutting fundamental walls (line $386$). The
discipline is arithmetic: primitivity of a lattice vector, not
primitivity of a homology class. No reduced Gromov--Witten or
reduced DT moduli stack appears.

\emph{(iii) Donaldson--Thomas series, classical form.}
Lines $883$--$936$ present the $K3 \times E$ generating series
\[
Z^{X}(q,t,p)
= \sum_{h=0}^{\infty} \sum_{d=0}^{\infty} \sum_{n \in \Z}
\DT_{n,(\beta_{h},d)} \, q^{n} t^{d-1} (-p)^{\frac{1}{2}\langle
\beta_{h}, \beta_{h}\rangle}
\]
with $\DT_{n,(\beta_{h},d)} = \sum_{k \in \Z} k \cdot
e\!\left(\nu^{-1}(k)\right)$ the Behrend-weighted Euler
characteristic of $\Hilb^{n}(X,\beta_{h})/E$, and $\beta_{h} =
(s_{1} + \cdots + s_{N} + hF)/N$ an $N$-section-plus-fibre class on
an elliptically-fibred K3 $\pi : S \to \Pone$. The closed form at
$N = 1$ is
\[
Z^{S \times E} = -\frac{C}{(\Delta_{5})^{2}}.
\]
The elliptic-genus identity $\chi_{\mathrm{ell}}(K3) = 2\phi_{0,1}$
(line $919$) underwrites the lift $\phi_{0,1} \to \Delta_{5}$. The
slides quotient by the $E$-action but do not reduce in the
Pandharipande--Oberdieck sense (quotienting the virtual class by
the $E$-translation symmetry to kill the trivial deformation).
Reduction is implicit in the $E$-quotient on $\Hilb^{n}(X,
\beta_{h})/E$, never named.

\paragraph{Synthesis.}
The slides live entirely on the automorphic side of the
correspondence: lattice $\Lambda^{3,2}$, reflection polyhedra,
Siegel half-plane $\HH^{IV}$, Jacobi-to-Siegel lift
$\phi_{0,1} \to \Delta_{5}$, GBKM algebra
$\mathfrak{g}_{(g_{N},h_{M})} \subset \mathfrak{g}_{L}$ twisted by
lattice polarisation $L$ and K3 automorphism data. The
CY3 $X = (S \times E)/(\Z/N\Z)$ appears only as the
\emph{target} of a DT partition function; the construction of
$X$ as a covering-theoretic object (open-cover by toric charts,
local NCCR models, Serre-local-duality patches) is absent. The
Conjecture at line $929$ --- all double-divisor Siegel modular
forms arise as $-1/Z^{X}_{L,h_{M}}$ with a GBKM correction
$\mathfrak{g}_{(g_{N},h_{M})}$ given by a twisted elliptic genus
$F^{(g_{N},h_{M})}_{L}$ of weight $0$, index $1$ --- fixes the
$\kBKM$-side data but imposes no gluing datum on the DT side.

\paragraph{Wave-17 D1 finding.}
The positive-geometry grammar --- $X = (S \times E)/(\Z/N\Z)$
assembled from effective open charts, NCCR patch on each toric
stratum, reduced DT on $K3 \times E$ pulled back through the
$\Z/N\Z$-quotient, gluing effective divisors by birational
equivalence across chambers --- is a genuine Vol III contribution,
not a reconstruction. The slides supply the lattice datum
($\Lambda^{3,2}$, reflection polyhedra, $\phi_{0,1} \to \Delta_{5}$
lift) and the closed-form DT series $Z^{S \times E} =
-C/(\Delta_{5})^{2}$ but no cover-theoretic input. The two
effective scaffolds meet only at the equality
$\kBKM(\Delta_{5}) = c_{1}(0)/2 = 5$ (Borcherds weight
$5 = (2 \cdot 5)/2$), and the six distinct constructions of
$G(K3 \times E)$ reach $\kBKM = 5$ through the slide-side
automorphic route; the gluing, cover, and DT-reduction routes are
Vol III original.
