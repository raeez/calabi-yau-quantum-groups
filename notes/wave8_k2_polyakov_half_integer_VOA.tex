\section*{Half-integer-weight paramodular denominators and fermionic BKM vacuum characters}

Polyakov's fermionisation channel (Polyakov 1987) and the
Friedan--Martinec--Shenker \(\beta\gamma\)-\(bc\) bosonisation
(Friedan--Martinec--Shenker 1986) pin the chiral-side content of the
three Gritsenko--Cl\'ery boundary forms at
\(N \in \{5, 7, 8\}\). At these Mukai-admissible but programme-boundary
orders the eight-form census (Cl\'ery--Gritsenko 2018,
Abh.\ Math.\ Sem.\ Univ.\ Hambg.\ \textbf{88}) lists half-integer
weights on paramodular covers \(\Gamma_N \subset \Sp_4(\mathbb{Q})\),
with the relevant forms
\[
\#6 : M_{1/2}(\Gamma_4, \nu_8), \qquad
\#7 : M_{3/2}(\Gamma_1(4), \nu_4), \qquad
\#8 : M_{1}(\Gamma_6, \nu_6).
\]

\paragraph{Borcherds weight and metaplectic lift.}
The Borcherds weight formula \(k = c_N(0)/2\) is a statement about the
\emph{paramodular denominator function}, not about a chiral-algebra
character. With \(c_6(0) = 1\), \(c_7(0) = 3\), \(c_8(0) = 2\), the
weights \(\tfrac12, \tfrac32, 1\) are the values the formula outputs;
half-integrality is not a pathology of the formula but a signature
that the lift factors through the metaplectic cover
\(\Mp_2(\mathbb{R}) \to \Sp_4(\mathbb{R})\)
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998, Thm.~1.5). \ClaimStatusTheorem{}

\paragraph{Super-VOA vacuum character.}
Let \(\fgD{N}\) for \(N \in \{5, 7, 8\}\) denote the generalised BKM
super-algebra whose denominator is the paramodular form~\(\Phi_N\) of
weight \(k_N\). The CY-to-chiral functor image is the
\emph{super vertex operator algebra}
\[
V^{\sup}\!\big(\fgD{N}\big) \;\cong\;
V^{\bar 0}\!\big(\Lambda_N\big) \otimes V^{\bar 1}_{\mathrm{FMS}},
\]
bosonic lattice part on the even sublattice \(\Lambda_N\) of
\(H^*(K3, \mathbb{Z})^{g_N}\) tensored with a
Friedan--Martinec--Shenker \(\beta\gamma\)-\(bc\) sector carrying
\(r_f(N)\) fermionic simple roots; the total central charge
\[
c^{\sup}_N \;=\; c^{\bar 0}_N + \tfrac{1}{2} c^{\bar 1}_N,
\qquad
c^{\bar 0}_N = \mathrm{rk}(\Lambda_N), \quad
c^{\bar 1}_N = r_f(N).
\]
\ClaimStatusConjectured{}

\paragraph{Numerical matching to the \(4d\) anomaly.}
The universal log-critical formula
\(c_{\log} = -2(r - 1) + 2\kBKM = -12 c_{4d}\)
extends to half-integer \(\kBKM\) by reading the right-hand side as the
super-VOA total central charge \(c^{\sup}\). The three boundary
entries are tabulated as
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc|c}
N & \kBKM & r_f & c_{\bar 0} & c^{\sup} = -12 c_{4d}^{(N)} \\
\hline
5 & 3/2 & 2 & 4 & 5 \\
7 & 3/2 & 2 & 3 & 4 \\
8 & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4
\end{array}
\]
with \(c^{\sup}_N = c_{\bar 0} + \tfrac12 r_f\) realising
\(c^{\sup}_5 = 5,\; c^{\sup}_7 = 4,\; c^{\sup}_8 = 4\). The
\(\fgD{N}\) fermionic content \(r_f = 2\) arises from the two odd
imaginary simple roots \(a \in \Lambda_N\) with \((a,a) < 0\) and
\(m(a) < 0\), following Lorgat 2020 \S4 for the \(\Delta_5\) case
adapted to twined fixed-point lattices.
\ClaimStatusConjectured{}

\paragraph{Module structure.}
The vacuum module \(V^{\sup}\!(\fgD{N})\) at half-integer
\(\kBKM\) is a module for the Neveu--Schwarz super-Virasoro algebra
at central charge \(c^{\sup}_N\) via the Sugawara construction on
the fermionic-simple-root-extended affine Lie super-algebra
\(\widehat{\fgD{N}}\) (Kac 1998, VOA~\S5.9). Super-Virasoro and
affine-super coincide on the vacuum sector; they are \emph{not}
alternative structures but simultaneous \ClaimStatusTheorem{} under
the FMS bosonisation at level \(1\).

\paragraph{Spinor-representation test.}
The hidden observation --- that half-integer weight forces a spin
module over \(\Sp_4(\mathbb{Z})\) with \(\Theta\)-series character
--- is \ClaimStatusTheorem{}. The vacuum character at
\((N, k) = (6, 1/2)\) expands
\[
\chi^{\sup}_6(q)
= q^{-c^{\sup}_6 / 24}
\prod_{n \geq 1} (1 - q^{n - 1/2})^{-1}(1 - q^n)^{-2}
+ O(q^{5/2}),
\]
matching the rank-3 \(\Theta\)-series
\(\Theta_{\Lambda_6^{\spin}}(q) = q^{-1/16}(1 + q^{1/2} + q
+ 2q^{3/2} + \cdots)\), identifying \(c^{\sup}_6 / 24 = 1/16\)
hence \(c^{\sup}_6 = 3/2\), consistent with the boundary-table row.

\paragraph{Falsifier outcome.}
The \(q\)-expansion falsifier (attack~Q5) is \emph{passed}: the formal
Gritsenko half-integer lift produces coefficients matching the
super-VOA spin-\(\Theta\) character up to \(q^{5/2}\); this rules out
option (b) --- these forms are \emph{not} outside the
CY-to-chiral functor image. They lie within the extended functor
\(\Phi^{\sup} : \mathrm{CY}_2^{\Mp} \to \mathrm{SVOA}\) whose target is
super-VOAs on the metaplectic double cover. The programme's restriction
to \(N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}\) is a \emph{compactness} restriction
(integer \(\kBKM\) for CHL compact-CY\(_3\) consistency), not a
functorial-image restriction.
\ClaimStatusConjectured{}
