\section*{AdS$_3$ protected indices and the primitive $\Delta_5$ denominator}
\label{wn:sec:ads3-protected-indices-delta5}

\paragraph{D1--D5 datum.}
Type IIB on $K3\times S^1$ with $Q_1$ D1-branes on $S^1$ and
$Q_5$ D5-branes on $K3\times S^1$ has decoupling geometry
$\AdS_3\times S^3\times K3$ and boundary sigma model on
$\operatorname{Sym}^{Q_1Q_5}(K3)$, with
$c=6Q_1Q_5=3\ell_3/(2G_3)$ in the Brown--Henneaux normalisation
(Maldacena 1998; Strominger--Vafa 1996). The extra elliptic factor in
the $K3\times E$ specialisation changes the Calabi--Yau dimension to
$3$; the specialised chiral output is therefore native $E_1$. Any
$E_2$ structure belongs to a centre, a double, or a completed
Hall--Drinfeld construction with its pairing and completion data.

\paragraph{Theorem: DMVV scalar index.}
The Dijkgraaf--Moore--Verlinde--Verlinde product formula gives
\[
  \sum_{n\geq 0}p^n Z_{\mathrm{ell}}(\operatorname{Sym}^n K3;q,y)
  =
  \prod_{r>0,m\geq 0,\ell}
  (1-p^r q^m y^\ell)^{-c(4rm-\ell^2)} .
\]
For the ordinary K3 elliptic genus
$\chi(K3;q,y)=\sum c(4m-\ell^2)q^m y^\ell$, this is the
second-quantised protected index. In the unnormalised genus-$2$ scalar
convention it is expressed through $\Phi_{10}^{-1}$, and at $N=1$
\[
  \Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_5^2.
\]
Thus the scalar index sees the square $\Delta_5^{-2}$. The primitive BKM
root lattice, the parity of the simple roots, and the compact Hall
product require separate construction.

\paragraph{Theorem: dyon contour at scalar level.}
For charges $(Q,P)$ in the CHL electric-magnetic lattice, the
Jatkar--Sen/DVV coefficient extraction has the form
\[
  d_N(Q,P)
  =
  (-1)^{Q\cdot P+1}
  \oint_{\mathcal C_N}
  \frac{
  e^{-\pi i(Q^2 z_1+2(Q\cdot P)z_2+P^2 z_3)}
  }{\PhiPhys_N(Z)}
  d^3Z ,
  \qquad
  Z=\begin{pmatrix}z_1&z_2\\ z_2&z_3\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The scalar denominator $\PhiPhys_N$ is the physical Siegel modular form
for the indexed degeneracy. At $N=1$ it is $\Phi_{10}$, hence
$\Phi_{10}^{-1}=\Delta_5^{-2}$ in the unnormalised square convention.
The primitive BKM denominator is the square-root object
$\DBKM_1=\Delta_5$; for the Vol III CHL denominator ladder
\[
  (c_1(0),c_2(0),c_3(0),c_4(0),c_6(0))=(10,8,6,4,2),
  \qquad
  \kBKM(\DBKM_N)=c_N(0)/2=(5,4,3,2,1).
\]
The scalar contour and the primitive BKM denominator have compatible
automorphic shadows, but they are different objects.

\paragraph{BKM denominator construction.}
The primitive automorphic product $\Delta_5$ defines the denominator
identity of the generalized Kac--Moody superalgebra
$\fg_{\Delta_5}$:
\[
  \operatorname{den}(\fg_{\Delta_5})=D_5(2Z),
  \qquad D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5,
  \qquad \kBKM(\Delta_5)=5.
\]
This is a Borcherds-product statement. A compact Hall or compact CoHA
realisation would require the oriented compact critical Hall
correspondences, the coproduct, the Hall pairing, the completion, and
the comparison of primitive generators with the Borcherds root data.
The scalar $\Phi_{10}^{-1}$ supplies character evidence only.

\paragraph{Holographic heuristic.}
The protected AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$ index and the $\AdS_2$ quantum entropy
contour support the following heuristic: $\fg_{\Delta_5}$ is the
primitive symmetry datum whose denominator controls the same protected
sector whose scalar degeneracies are extracted from $\Phi_{10}^{-1}$.
The D1--D5 CFT, a class-$\mathcal S$ chiral algebra, and a higher-spin
$\Wlambda$ boundary algebra require independent stress-tensor,
Schur-sector, or asymptotic-symmetry constructions before comparison
with $\fg_{\Delta_5}$.

\paragraph{Conditional class-$\mathcal S$ and higher-spin comparisons.}
The Beem--Lemos--Liendo--Peelaers--Rastelli--van~Rees map
$c_{\mathrm{2d}}=-12c_{\mathrm{4d}}$ is a theorem for a specified
four-dimensional $\mathcal N=2$ SCFT. Applying it to a candidate
$T[A_1,\Sigma_{0,24}]$ model gives a conditional comparison. The scalar
dyon partition function supplies the indexed-degeneracy input. Likewise, the
Gaberdiel--Gopakumar higher-spin relation with boundary
$\Wlambda$ is a comparison of asymptotic symmetry algebras; setting
the Chern--Simons level equal to $\kBKM(\DBKM_N)$ is a heuristic unless
the relevant bulk Chern--Simons theory is constructed with that level.

\paragraph{Four invariants on $K3\times E$.}
The $N=1$ geometry separates four constructions:
\[
  \{\kcat,\ \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}},\ \kBKM,\ \kfib\}
  =
  \{0,3,5,24\}.
\]
Here $\kcat(K3\times E)=\chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\chi(\mathcal O_E)=0$,
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}=3$ is the Heisenberg--Mukai
specialisation, $\kBKM(\Delta_5)=5=c_1(0)/2$ is the primitive
Borcherds weight, and $\kfib=24$ is the Mukai-lattice rank of the K3
fibre. The compact CY$_3$ Hodge supertrace gives
$\kch(\mathcal A_{K3\times E})=0$ in the unspecialised $d=3$ output.
No additive identity relates these numbers.

\paragraph{Proof obligations.}
The scalar index theorem, the dyon contour, and the Bekenstein--Hawking
leading entropy comparison are theorem-level inputs. The following
statements remain conditional: construction of a compact Hall--
Drinfeld double whose denominator is $\Delta_5$; identification of its
stress tensor with the $d=3$ chiral output; direct class-$\mathcal S$
or higher-spin realisation of $\fg_{\Delta_5}$; and extension of the
$N=1$ square-root relation to every CHL normalisation without specifying
the physical and primitive denominator conventions.
