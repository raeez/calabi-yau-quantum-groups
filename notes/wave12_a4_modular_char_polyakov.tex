\section*{Modular Characters of \texorpdfstring{$K3\times E$}{K3 x E}}

The product $K3\times E$ has one total-space vanishing shared by the
categorical Euler characteristic and the compact Hodge supertrace, and
three further values produced by separate constructions.  The
total-space categorical Euler characteristic is
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)
  = \chi(\mathcal O_{K3\times E})
  = \chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\chi(\mathcal O_E)
  = 2\cdot 0
  = 0.
\]
On the constructed compact CY-to-chiral locus, the Hodge supertrace gives
the same vanishing:
\[
  \kch(A_{K3\times E})
  = \sum_{q=0}^{3}(-1)^q h^{0,q}(K3\times E)
  = 1-1+1-1
  = 0.
\]
The relative Heisenberg--Mukai specialisation along the elliptic
direction has its own chiral value
\[
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)=3.
\]
The Borcherds denominator attached to the Igusa square-root line has
\[
  \kBKM(\Delfive)=\frac{c_1(0)}{2}=5.
\]
The fibre invariant is the Mukai-lattice rank
\[
  \kfib(K3)=24.
\]
Here the Mukai lattice is
$H^\ast(K3,\mathbb Z)=H^0\oplus H^2\oplus H^4$ with signature $(4,20)$.
Thus the separated values attached to the product are
\[
  \bigl(\kcat,\ \kch,\ \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}},\ \kBKM,\ \kfib\bigr)
  =
  (0,\ 0,\ 3,\ 5,\ 24),
\]
with value set $\{0,3,5,24\}$.  The number $2=\chi(\mathcal O_{K3})$
belongs to the K3 fibre and is absent from the total-space spectrum.

\subsection*{The Jacobi Input}

Let $\phizeroone=\phi_{0,1}^{\mathrm{EZ}}\in J^{\mathrm{wk}}_{0,1}$
be the Eichler--Zagier normalised weak Jacobi form
\[
  \phizeroone(\tau,z)
  = \zeta^{-1}+10+\zeta+O(q),
  \qquad q=e^{2\pi i\tau},\quad \zeta=e^{2\pi iz}.
\]
The K3 elliptic genus in this convention is
\[
  \Zell(K3;\tau,z)=2\phizeroone(\tau,z).
\]
The primitive level-one denominator uses the Eichler--Zagier form
$\phizeroone$ and the constant coefficient
\[
  c_1(0)=10.
\]
Borcherds' weight formula and Gritsenko's additive lift give
\[
  \operatorname{wt}(\Delfive)=\frac{c_1(0)}{2}=5.
\]
The monic denominator section is
\[
  \Dfive=64^{-1}\Delfive,
\]
and the denominator identity for the associated BKM algebra is written
with $\Dfive(2Z)$.  The doubled K3 elliptic genus lands on the squared
Igusa object:
\[
  \PhiTenUn=\Delfive^2,
  \qquad \operatorname{wt}(\PhiTenUn)=10.
\]
In the Oberdieck--Pandharipande reduced-DT scalar convention,
\[
  \PhiTenOP=\Dfive^2=4096^{-1}\Delfive^2.
\]
The primitive denominator has weight $5$; the square has weight $10$.
Substituting the K3 elliptic-genus constant $20$ into the primitive
weight formula computes the square.

\subsection*{Cover Characters}

The level-one denominator is a weight-$5$ form
\[
  \Delfive\in M_5(\Gamma_1,\chi_2),
\]
with order-two character $\chi_2$.  Its square
$\PhiTenUn=\Delfive^2$ is the weight-$10$ scalar Igusa form.  The
primitive CHL ladder uses integral-weight paramodular forms and their
characters.  In the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery catalogue, the two half-integral
entries carry multiplier systems of orders $8$ and $4$; metaplectic
cover data are absent from the primitive CHL ladder.

\subsection*{Primitive CHL Constants}

For the primitive CHL orders
\[
  N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\},
\]
the Borcherds-input constants and BKM weights are
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}

\hline
\text{form}&\Delfive&\Delta_4^{(2)}&\Delta_3^{(3)}&
\Delta_2^{(4)}&\Delta_1^{(6)}\\
c_N(0)&10&8&6&4&2\\
\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2&5&4&3&2&1
\end{array}
\]
in the Vol III primitive CHL normalisation.  These constants are the
constant terms of the Jacobi or vector-valued theta inputs used by the
denominator products.  The physics square convention doubles the
denominator weight because it squares the primitive Borcherds form:
\[
  \operatorname{wt}\bigl((\Phi_N)^{\mathrm{phys}}\bigr)
  =c_N(0)=2\kBKM(\Phi_N).
\]

The Gritsenko--Cl\'ery simplest-divisor catalogue is a separate
eight-entry catalogue indexed by triples $(t,N;k)$:
\[
  (1,1;5),\ (2,1;2),\ (1,2;3),\ (3,1;1),\ (1,3;2),\
  (4,1;\tfrac12),\ (1,4;\tfrac32),\ (2,2;1).
\]
Its weight tuple is
\[
  (5,2,3,1,2,\tfrac12,\tfrac32,1),
\]
with twice-weight tuple
\[
  (10,4,6,2,4,1,3,2).
\]
The common entry $(t,N;k)=(1,1;5)$ is $\Delfive$.  The CHL constants
$(10,8,6,4,2)$ and the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery twice-weight tuple therefore
coincide at the first entry and separate afterwards.

\subsection*{Borcherds Weight Formula}

The Borcherds weight is read from the constant term of the denominator
input.  Level one gives
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_1)=5,
  \qquad
  \kch(A_{K3\times E})+\chi(\mathcal O_E)=0+0=0.
\]
Level two gives
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_2)=4,
  \qquad
  \kch(A_{Z_2})+\chi(\mathcal O_{E_2})=1+0=1.
\]
The universal statement is the Borcherds weight formula
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_N)=\frac{c_N(0)}{2}.
\]
The arithmetic lift supplies the weight directly from the constant term
of its input.  The displayed chiral-plus-fibre sums are separation
tests; they belong to different functorial layers from the Borcherds
weight.

\subsection*{Topological, BCOV, and Worldsheet Numbers}

The quotient $\chitop(X)/24$, a BCOV genus-one anomaly coefficient,
a categorical Euler characteristic, a worldsheet central charge, a
Borcherds weight, and a K3 elliptic-genus constant are distinct data.
They may agree in a stated theorem with a fixed normalisation and a
fixed class of targets.  Absent such a theorem, equality of numbers is
a numerical coincidence.

For $K3\times E$,
\[
  \chitop(K3\times E)=\chitop(K3)\chitop(E)=24\cdot 0=0.
\]
Consequently $\chitop(K3\times E)/24=0$, matching $\kcat$ and the
compact $\kch$ by vanishing.  This vanishing records total-space
topology.  The Borcherds weight $5$, the Heisenberg--Mukai value $3$,
and the Mukai rank $24$ are produced by their named constructions.

For compact smooth CY$_3$ targets, the BCOV genus-one expression
contains a topological term proportional to $\chitop(X)/24$ in the
chosen B-model normalisation.  Local CY$_3$ geometries, lower-dimensional
CY targets, and Borcherds denominator algebras require their own
normalisations.  The relevant invariant is named by the construction
that produces it.

\subsection*{Mathieu Twining}

The Mathieu action enters through twined K3 elliptic genera
\[
  \Zell^{(g)}(K3;\tau,z),
  \qquad g\in \Mtwofour.
\]
These are Jacobi forms with level, multiplier, and constant-term data
depending on the conjugacy class of $g$.  A twined elliptic genus becomes
a BKM denominator input after the corresponding Borcherds product has
an admissible paramodular group, divisor, Weyl vector, and multiplier
character.  The untwined class gives $\Delfive$.  The primitive CHL
orders above give the five-row Borcherds-weight table.  Mathieu classes
outside this automorphic package remain elliptic-genus data.

\subsection*{CY-to-Chiral and Hall Layers}

For $d\geq 3$, the specialised CY-to-chiral output is $E_1$:
\[
  \Phi_d^{(\Sigma_{d-1},C)}
  =
  \SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ \PhiFA_d.
\]
The $E_2$ structure lives on the centre, double, or module category
attached to the $E_1$ algebra.  For the toric model,
\[
  \CoHA(\mathbb C^3)\simeq Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
\]
at the positive-half $E_1$-chiral level.  The full
$\mathcal W_{1+\infty}$ object is recovered after the Drinfeld
double, centre, completion, or evaluation passage appropriate to the
chosen construction.

For $K3\times E$, the same scope discipline separates the compact
Hodge-supertrace value $0$, the relative Heisenberg--Mukai chiral value
$3$, the Borcherds weight $5$, and the fibre rank $24$.  The modular
character of the note is therefore a small diagram of named invariants,
with a separate construction attached to each value.

\subsection*{Source Normalisations}

Borcherds' singular theta weight theorem gives
$\operatorname{wt}\operatorname{Bor}(\varphi)=c_\varphi(0)/2$ for the
denominator input.  Gritsenko's K3-fibred additive lift gives the
level-one form $\Delfive$ and the primitive CHL ladder
$(5,4,3,2,1)$.  Gritsenko and Cl\'ery classify the eight diagonal-divisor
forms by triples $(t,N;k)$, with the half-integral rows governed by
multiplier systems.  Mukai's K3 lattice supplies the signature $(4,20)$
rank-$24$ fibre datum.  These four source normalisations account for
the separated values $\{0,3,5,24\}$.
