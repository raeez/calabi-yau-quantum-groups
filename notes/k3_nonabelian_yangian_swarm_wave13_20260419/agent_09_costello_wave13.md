## Attack–heal cycle 1 — CY-2 $[2]$-shift: re-audit in the Serre-functor lane.

### ATTACK 1 (sharper than Wave 12).

Wave 12 asserted: *the Koszul-dual shift of $\mathbf{H}_{\Delta_5}$ is
$[2]$, via Lurie HA 6.3.1.5 applied with $n = 1$ (chiral $E_1$) and

the "CY-shift audit" table with five interpretations, and picked the
third ("CY-dim of K3 input to $\Phi$"). Good as far as it goes, but
there are **two further Serre-functor conflations** I did not address:

**(α) $D^b\mathrm{Coh}(K3)$ versus $D^b\mathrm{Coh}^{M_{24}}(K3)$.** The
CY-to-chiral correspondence $\Phi_2$ of Chapter~\ref{ch:cy-to-chiral}
(Vol III) is defined on smooth-proper CY categories. $D^b\mathrm{Coh}(K3)$
is indeed CY-2 with Serre functor $[2]$. But the Mathieu-moonshine
sector of $\mathbf{H}_{\Delta_5}$ is produced by
$D^b\mathrm{Coh}^{M_{24}}(K3)$ — the $M_{24}$-equivariant derived
category. *That* category's Serre functor is not just $[2]$: it is
$[2] \otimes \omega_{K3}|_{M_{24}}^{-1}$ where $\omega_{K3}$ is the
canonical, equivariantly trivial on K3 but potentially twisted by
characters of $M_{24}$ on the equivariant part. For $M_{24}$ acting
faithfully on the K3 (a.k.a. a Mukai $M_{24}$-action), the canonical
bundle is equivariantly trivial (Nikulin/Mukai 1988 lattice
classification), so the Serre functor is still $[2]$. But Wave 12 did
not verify this — I just assumed CY-2.

**(β) Twisted K3-twisted-sheaves.** Physical K3 compactifications
(e.g., Type IIB on K3, heterotic on $T^6$ dual) often involve
$\alpha$-twisted sheaves for a Brauer class $\alpha \in H^2(K3,
\mathcal{O}_{K3}^\times)_{\mathrm{tors}} \cong (\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z})^r$.
The derived category $D^b\mathrm{Coh}^{\alpha}(K3)$ is still CY-2 with
Serre $[2]$ iff $\alpha$ is 2-torsion (B-field quantization condition);
otherwise the Serre functor is twisted by $\alpha \otimes \alpha^\vee =
\mathcal{O}_{K3}$ anyway, so shift is still $[2]$. Fine: this one
survives trivially.

**(γ) The Mukai transform on K3.** The Mukai transform
$\Phi_{\mathcal{P}}: D^b\mathrm{Coh}(K3) \to D^b\mathrm{Coh}(K3)$ (where
$\mathcal{P}$ is a Fourier–Mukai kernel) is an auto-equivalence that
exchanges sheaves and their duals. Under $\Phi_{\mathcal{P}}$, the
Serre functor transforms contravariantly; so $\Phi_{\mathcal{P}}(S_{\cC}) =
S_{\cC}^{-1} \circ \Phi_{\mathcal{P}}$. This is fine for CY-2 because
$[2]$ is its own inverse up to a sign (i.e., $S^{-1} = [-2]$ and under
the Mukai transform we get $[2] \to [-2]$ which is a $[4]$-shift in
composition). Wave 12 missed this: a bar–cobar applied through the
Mukai-transform side of the story picks up a **$[4]$-shift, not $[2]$**,
because the Serre functor and its inverse compose to the identity only
up to $[-4]$ on 2-fold iteration.

So: does the Wave 12 HEAL claim
$(\mathbf{H}_{\Delta_5})^! = V(\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5})^{\mathrm{coalg}}[2]$
actually hold after the Mukai transform is applied? If not, it means
the $[2]$-shift is canonical in the *untransformed* picture, but under
Fourier–Mukai duality the claim has to be restated.

equivariance or twisted-sheaf complication (concerns (α) and (β)

**Theorem (Costello, W13-Cos-1, $\ClaimStatusProvedHere$ chain-level +

$$3 = \mathrm{rank}(\mathrm{II}_{2,1}) = \mathrm{rank}(U) + \mathrm{rank}\langle -2\rangle = 2 + 1.$$

$$3 = |\text{real simple roots}| = \mathrm{rank}\, W_{\mathrm{real}}.$$

$$(4, 20) - (1, 19) = (3, 1).$$
So rank 4 after taking signature-sum, but sitting inside the Mukai lattice

complexification. Concretely: $T_S$ has rank 3 for a generic attic K3

All three paths give **3 = rank of the "visible" real BKM Cartan after
