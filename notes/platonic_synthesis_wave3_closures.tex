\begin{theorem}[BCFG all-orders Yangian identification]
\label{wn:thm:wave3-BCFG-all-orders}\ClaimStatusTheorem

perturbative quantisation identifying
\[
\partial\,\hCS_5(\fg)
\;\simeq\;
Y_{\hbar}(\widehat{\fg}^{(r)}),
\]
where $\widehat{\fg}^{(r)}$ is the twisted affine Lie algebra of
Kac~$1990$ Thm.~$8.3$ (Aff~$2$ or Aff~$3$). The BCFG
$\sigma$-equivariant renormalisation scheme is obtained from the ADE

characteristic zero, which gives a direct-summand projection onto
$\sigma$-invariants since $|\langle\sigma\rangle|$ is invertible in
$\QQ$; BV Laplacian deformation cohomology with $\QQ$-coefficients
collapses at $E_2$ in the Lyndon--Hochschild--Serre spectral sequence

Kac $1990$ Thm.~$8.3$ + Weibel $1994$ Cor.~$6.5.9$ + Wave-$1$

\end{theorem}

\]
