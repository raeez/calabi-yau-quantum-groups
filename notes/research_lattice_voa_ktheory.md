# Research Note: Lattice Vertex Algebras, K-Theory, and the QVCG

## The Proposal Under Investigation

**Claim**: For a CY3 $X$, the quantum vertex chiral group $G(X)$ is a lattice vertex
algebra $V_L$ built from the K-theory lattice $L = K_0(\mathrm{Coh}(X))$ equipped
with the Euler form.

Lattice vertex algebras are completely classified (Borcherds, Frenkel-Lepowsky-Meurman):
given an even lattice $L$ with bilinear form $(\cdot,\cdot)$, the lattice VOA $V_L$ is
generated by vertex operators $e^\alpha$ ($\alpha \in L$) with OPE
$$e^\alpha(z)\, e^\beta(w) \sim (z-w)^{(\alpha,\beta)} e^{\alpha+\beta}(w) + \cdots$$
The central charge equals the rank of $L$.

**Verdict**: The QVCG is **not** literally a lattice VOA. It is a specific type of
extension/deformation of one. The precise relationship depends on which class of CY3
we consider, and the answer reveals a three-tier structure that is one of the central
architectural features of the programme.


---

## 1. The Three Test Cases

### Case 1: $K3 \times E$ (the BKM case)

**Lattice data**:
- $\Lambda(X) = \Lambda^{3,2}$ of signature $(3,2)$, extracted from the Mukai lattice.
- The hyperbolic sublattice $\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}$ has three real simple roots
  $\delta_1, \delta_2, \delta_3$ with Gram matrix
  $A_{ij} = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 2 & -2 & -2 \\ -2 & 2 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & 2 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$.
- The Weyl vector $\rho = f_2 - \frac{1}{2}f_3 + f_{-2}$.

**What a lattice VOA would give**:
$V_{\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}}$ is a lattice vertex algebra of central charge $c = 3$ (= rank).
It has vertex operators $e^\alpha$ for each $\alpha \in \Lambda^{2,1}_{II}$ and
a Heisenberg subalgebra $\mathfrak{h} = \Lambda^{2,1}_{II} \otimes_\mathbb{Z} \mathbb{R}$
(the Cartan). The character of $V_L$ is
$$\chi(V_L; q) = \frac{\Theta_L(\tau)}{\eta(\tau)^{\mathrm{rank}(L)}}$$
where $\Theta_L$ is the lattice theta function.

**What the QVCG actually is**:
$G(K3 \times E) = \mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$, the generalized BKM superalgebra whose
denominator identity is the Igusa cusp form $\Delta_5$ of weight 5 for $\mathrm{Sp}_4(\mathbb{Z})$.
Its root system has three parts:

1. **Real roots** $\Delta^{\mathrm{re}} = \{\delta_1, \delta_2, \delta_3\}$ -- these
   are precisely the roots of the Kac-Moody subalgebra determined by $\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}$.
2. **Even imaginary roots** $\Delta^{\mathrm{im}}_0$ with positive multiplicities
   from Fourier coefficients of $\phi_{0,1}$ (the K3 elliptic genus).
3. **Odd imaginary roots** $\Delta^{\mathrm{im}}_1$ with negative multiplicities
   (fermionic generators).

**The relationship**: The lattice VOA $V_{\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}}$ captures only the real
roots. The imaginary roots constitute the **automorphic correction** -- an extension
of the lattice data by modules whose multiplicities are controlled by $\phi_{0,1}$.
Concretely:

$$G(K3 \times E) = \mathfrak{g}_{\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}} + \text{imaginary root corrections}$$

where $\mathfrak{g}_{\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}}$ is the Kac-Moody algebra of the Gram matrix
(the "uncorrected" algebra), and the corrections add generators at every lattice point
$(n,l,m)$ in the positive cone with multiplicity $f(nm, l)$.

The denominator identity reflects this decomposition:
$$\frac{1}{64}\Delta_5(2Z) = \underbrace{\sum_{w \in W} \det(w)\, e^{w(\rho)}}_{\text{Weyl sum (lattice part)}} \times \underbrace{\text{correction from imaginary simple roots}}_{\text{automorphic correction}}$$


### Case 2: Toric CY3 ($\mathbb{C}^3$, the Yangian case)

**Lattice data**:
- $K_0(\mathrm{Coh}(\mathbb{C}^3)) \simeq \mathbb{Z}$ (rank 1).
- The Euler form on the Jordan quiver is **degenerate**: $(\mathbf{e}_1, \mathbf{e}_1) = 0$.
- No real roots at all: $\Delta^{\mathrm{re}} = \emptyset$.
- All roots are imaginary: $\Delta^{\mathrm{im}}_0 = \{n \in \mathbb{Z}_{>0}\}$ with
  $\mathrm{mult}(n) = p(n)$ (the partition function, counting 3d partitions at weight $n$
  gives mult$(n) = n$ for the affine Yangian generators).

**What a lattice VOA would give**:
$V_L$ for $L = \mathbb{Z}$ with zero form is just a Heisenberg vertex algebra of rank 1.
This is a free boson -- completely abelian, no interesting OPE structure beyond the
Heisenberg commutation relations.

**What the QVCG actually is**:
$G(\mathbb{C}^3) = Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1) \cong \mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$, the affine
Yangian, which is isomorphic to the $W_{1+\infty}$ algebra at the self-dual level. This
is a **W-algebra** with infinitely many generators of spin $1, 2, 3, \ldots$ and highly
nontrivial nonlinear OPE.

**The relationship**: The lattice VOA gives only the Heisenberg subalgebra (the spin-1
current $J(z)$). The full QVCG is obtained by adding **all higher-spin generators**
$W_2, W_3, W_4, \ldots$

In shadow obstruction tower language (from `theory_automorphic_shadow.tex`):
- $\Theta^{\leq 2}$: $\kappa(\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty})$ and the Heisenberg OPE.
  **This is the lattice part.**
- $\Theta^{\leq 3}$: adds the $W_3$ correction (first higher-spin current).
- $\Theta^{\leq r}$: adds $W_r$ truncation -- spin-$r$ generators.
- $\Theta_{\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}}$: the full algebra.

The lattice VOA is literally the arity-2 truncation of the shadow obstruction tower. Every subsequent
arity adds a new higher-spin generator that cannot be expressed in terms of the lattice data.


### Case 3: Resolved Conifold

**Lattice data**:
- $\Lambda = \mathbb{Z}^2$ with Gram matrix
  $\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 2 & -2 \\ -2 & 2 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$
  (the $\widehat{A}_1$ Cartan matrix).
- Two real simple roots $\mathbf{e}_1, \mathbf{e}_2$.
- Weyl group $W = \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \ltimes \mathbb{Z}$ (affine Weyl group of $\widehat{A}_1$).

**What a lattice VOA would give**:
$V_{\widehat{A}_1}$ is a well-known lattice vertex algebra of central charge 2, related
to the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ WZW model at level 1.

**What the QVCG actually is**:
The affine super Yangian $Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{g}}_{Q})$ associated to the
Klebanov-Witten quiver. The real roots give a Kac-Moody subalgebra, and the
imaginary roots (with DT invariants as multiplicities) provide the automorphic correction.

## 2. The Precise Relationship: $V_{K_0(X)}$ as Arity-2 Skeleton

The evidence from all three cases points to the same structural answer:

> **$V_{K_0(X)}$ is the arity-2 truncation of the shadow obstruction tower of $G(X)$.**

More precisely, the relationship is:

### Tier 1 (Lattice/Heisenberg): $V_L$ or $\mathfrak{h}$
The K-theory lattice $L = K_0(\mathrm{Coh}(X))$ with the Euler form gives:
- The Cartan subalgebra $\mathfrak{h} = L \otimes_\mathbb{Z} \mathbb{R}$ of the BKM algebra.
- The Heisenberg vertex algebra generated by the currents $\alpha_i(z)$ for $\alpha_i \in L$.
- The collision $r$-matrix $r(z)$ (arity-2 shadow), which encodes the Gram matrix.

This corresponds to $\Theta^{\leq 2}_{A_X}$ and captures the modular characteristic
$\kappa(A_X)$ and the Weyl vector $\rho$.

### Tier 2 (Kac-Moody): $\mathfrak{g}_A$
The real roots $\Delta^{\mathrm{re}}$ inside $L$ determine a Kac-Moody subalgebra
$\mathfrak{g}_A$ with Cartan matrix $A = ((\delta_i, \delta_j))$. The lattice VOA
$V_L$ (when the lattice is even) gives a vertex algebra realization of this Kac-Moody
algebra. This is the "uncorrected" algebra -- before automorphic correction.

For $K3 \times E$: the Kac-Moody algebra of the $3 \times 3$ Gram matrix.
For $\mathbb{C}^3$: trivial (no real roots, degenerate form).
For the conifold: the $\widehat{A}_1$ Kac-Moody algebra.

### Tier 3 (Full QVCG): $G(X) = \mathfrak{g}_X$
The full quantum vertex chiral group $G(X)$ is the generalized BKM superalgebra
obtained by the automorphic correction:
$$\mathfrak{g}_A \leadsto \mathfrak{g}_X$$
This adds imaginary root generators with multiplicities from BPS counting / DT invariants /
Fourier coefficients of the automorphic form.

In the shadow obstruction tower language:
$$G(X) = \varprojlim_r \Theta^{\leq r}_{A_X}$$

The passage Tier 1 -> Tier 2 -> Tier 3 is:
$$\underbrace{\text{Heisenberg/Cartan}}_{\Theta^{\leq 2}} \subset \underbrace{\text{Kac-Moody}}_{\text{real roots}} \subset \underbrace{\text{BKM superalgebra}}_{\text{full shadow obstruction tower}}$$

## 3. Why $G(X) \neq V_L$ in General: Three Obstructions

### Obstruction 1: Imaginary roots
The lattice VOA $V_L$ has no imaginary roots. Every root of a lattice VOA is
"real" in the sense that its vertex operator $e^\alpha$ generates a Heisenberg
module. The imaginary roots of $G(X)$ -- the BPS states wrapping holomorphic cycles --
cannot be captured by a lattice construction alone. They require the automorphic
correction, which in the bar-cobar language is the higher-arity shadow obstruction tower.

### Obstruction 2: Nonlinearity (W-algebra structure)
For toric CY3s, the QVCG is a W-algebra ($\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$ for $\mathbb{C}^3$).
W-algebras have **nonlinear** OPE: the OPE of two generators involves products of
generators, not just linear combinations. Lattice VOAs are always linear (the OPE
$e^\alpha(z) e^\beta(w) \sim (z-w)^{(\alpha,\beta)} e^{\alpha+\beta}(w)$ is linear in
$e^{\alpha+\beta}$). The nonlinearity is the W-algebra's essential feature.

### Obstruction 3: Super-structure (fermionic roots)
The BKM superalgebra $\mathfrak{g}_X$ has odd (fermionic) generators from roots
$\alpha$ with $\mathrm{mult}(\alpha) < 0$. Lattice VOAs can accommodate
super-structure only for lattices with vectors of odd norm-squared, but the
fermionic generators of the BKM algebra arise from BPS states with specific
charge, not from lattice parity. The super-structure requires additional data
beyond the lattice.

## 4. When *Is* $G(X) = V_L$? The Gaussian Locus

The shadow obstruction tower terminates at arity 2 (i.e., $G(X) = V_L$ or its Kac-Moody
subalgebra, with no imaginary root corrections) if and only if the discriminant
$\Delta = 8\kappa S_4 = 0$ (Gaussian class in the taxonomy of Vol I).

This happens when the lattice $\Lambda(X)$ has no null or timelike vectors in the
positive cone -- equivalently, when the Kac-Moody algebra $\mathfrak{g}_A$ is
**finite-dimensional semisimple**. For CY3 geometry, this is an extremely
degenerate situation: it would require the DT partition function to be a finite
product, which essentially never happens for a genuine CY3.

The remark in `theory_automorphic_shadow.tex` (rem:uncorrected-km) states this
explicitly:
> The arity-2 truncation $\Theta^{\leq 2}_{A_X}$ recovers the "uncorrected"
> Kac-Moody algebra $\mathfrak{g}$ (before the automorphic correction adds
> imaginary roots). The shadow obstruction tower terminates at arity 2 if and only if
> $\mathfrak{g}_X = \mathfrak{g}$ has no imaginary roots, which happens if and
> only if $\mathfrak{g}$ is a finite-dimensional semisimple Lie algebra.

## 5. The Correct Structural Picture: Three Layers

The relationship between $V_{K_0(X)}$ and $G(X)$ is best captured by the
following diagram, which applies uniformly across all CY3 examples:

```
Layer 0: K-theory lattice L = K_0(Coh(X)) with Euler form chi
         |
         | Heisenberg/lattice VOA construction
         v
Layer 1: Lattice VOA V_L (central charge = rank(L))
         |
         | Real root selection + Kac-Moody construction
         v
Layer 2: Kac-Moody subalgebra g_A  [= arity-2 shadow]
         |
         | Automorphic correction = shadow obstruction tower completion
         | (imaginary roots from DT invariants / Fourier coefficients)
         v
Layer 3: Full QVCG G(X) = g_X (BKM superalgebra)
```

**For K3 x E**: All three layers are nontrivial. Layer 1 is $V_{\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}}$
(c=3). Layer 2 is the Kac-Moody algebra of the Gram matrix. Layer 3 is
$\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$ with imaginary roots from $\phi_{0,1}$.

**For C^3**: Layer 1 is a rank-1 Heisenberg (c=1, degenerate form). Layer 2
is trivial (no real roots). Layer 3 is $Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1) \cong \mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$.

**For the conifold**: Layer 1 is $V_{\widehat{A}_1}$ (c=2). Layer 2 is affine
$\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_2$ Kac-Moody. Layer 3 adds DT corrections.

## 6. The Denominator Identity Decomposition

The denominator identity of $G(X)$ decomposes according to the three layers:

$$\Phi_X(z) = \underbrace{e^{-2\pi i \langle \rho, z \rangle}}_{\text{Weyl vector (Layer 1)}} \times \underbrace{\prod_{\alpha \in \Delta^{\mathrm{re}}_+} (1 - e^{-2\pi i \langle \alpha, z \rangle})}_{\text{Real root Euler product (Layer 2)}} \times \underbrace{\prod_{\alpha \in \Delta^{\mathrm{im}}_+} (1 - e^{-2\pi i \langle \alpha, z \rangle})^{\mathrm{mult}(\alpha)}}_{\text{Automorphic correction (Layer 3)}}$$

For $K3 \times E$: the Layer-2 product is a finite product over three real roots and
their Weyl reflections. The Layer-3 product is an infinite product over all imaginary
roots with multiplicities from $\phi_{0,1}$. Together they give $\frac{1}{64}\Delta_5$.

For $\mathbb{C}^3$: Layer 2 is empty (no real roots). The entire denominator
is the Layer-3 product $\prod_{n \geq 1} (1-q^n)^{-n} = M(q)^{-1}$ (inverse MacMahon).

## 7. The Bar-Complex Perspective

From the bar-cobar theory of Volume I, the three layers have clean interpretations:

| Layer | Bar-complex interpretation | Shadow obstruction tower |
|-------|--------------------------|--------------|
| 0 | Grading lattice of $B(A_X)$ | -- |
| 1 | Internal differential $d_1$ on $B(A_X)$ | $\Theta^{(1)}$ |
| 2 | Binary bar differential $d_2$ (binary OPE) | $\Theta^{\leq 2}$ (modular char. $\kappa$) |
| 3 | Full bar differential $d = d_1 + d_2 + d_3 + \cdots$ | $\Theta_{A_X} = \varprojlim_r \Theta^{\leq r}$ |

The bar complex $B(A_X)$ is $\Lambda$-graded (by the K-theory lattice). The
binary differential encodes the real root OPE. The higher differentials encode
the imaginary root corrections. The bar-complex Euler product is the denominator
identity (Theorem in `theory_denominator_bar_euler.tex`).

## 8. Connection to Open Problem P5

The monograph's `theory_generalized_root_datum.tex` (Problem P5) asks:

> Is $V(\widehat{\mathfrak{g}}_\mathcal{R}) \simeq A_X$ (the quantum chiral algebra)?

This is precisely the question of whether the QVCG is a vertex algebra built from
the BKM superalgebra by affinization. The analysis above suggests:

- $V(\widehat{\mathfrak{g}}_\mathcal{R})$ should be a **quotient or extension** of
  $A_X$, not isomorphic to it.
- The chiral algebra $A_X = \Phi(D^b(\mathrm{Coh}(X)))$ carries additional
  structure (the $E_2$-enhancement from the braided monoidal structure) that is
  not present in the vacuum module of an affinized BKM algebra.
- However, at the level of **characters** (denominator identities), the two
  should agree -- this is the content of the denominator = bar Euler product theorem.

## 9. The Borcherds Lift as Layer Interpolation

The Borcherds multiplicative lift provides a concrete mechanism connecting the
layers. Starting from the CY2 data:

1. **Input** (CY2): The weak Jacobi form $\phi_{0,1}$ (K3 elliptic genus) encodes
   the BPS spectrum of the K3 surface. This is a "Layer 1" object -- essentially
   the theta function of the K3 lattice.

2. **Lift** (CY2 -> CY3): The Borcherds product
   $\mathrm{Borch}(\phi_{0,1})(Z) = q^A y^B p^C \prod (1 - q^n y^l p^m)^{f(nm,l)}$
   takes this Layer-1 data and produces a Siegel modular form $\Delta_5$.

3. **Output** (CY3): The Siegel modular form $\Delta_5$ is the denominator identity
   of the full Layer-3 object $\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$.

The Borcherds lift is thus the automorphic-form-level shadow of the functor that
takes the lattice VOA (Layer 1 of the CY2) and produces the full QVCG (Layer 3
of the CY3). In the shadow obstruction tower language of `theory_cy2_cy3_fibration.tex`:
the Borcherds lift **is** the shadow obstruction tower completion, applied fiber-by-fiber
over the elliptic curve $E$.

## 10. Summary and Conclusions

**Answer to the key question**: $G(X)$ is NOT a lattice VOA $V_{K_0(X)}$. Rather:

$$G(X) = V_{K_0(X)} + \text{(Kac-Moody extension)} + \text{(automorphic correction)}$$

where:
- $V_{K_0(X)}$ provides the Cartan/Heisenberg subalgebra (arity-2 skeleton);
- The Kac-Moody extension adds real root generators from the lattice geometry;
- The automorphic correction (= shadow obstruction tower, arities $\geq 3$) adds imaginary root
  generators with multiplicities from DT invariants / BPS counting / automorphic forms.

The relationship is captured exactly by the shadow obstruction tower decomposition of Vol I:
the lattice VOA is $\Theta^{\leq 2}$, and the full QVCG is $\Theta = \varprojlim_r \Theta^{\leq r}$.

**The lattice is the skeleton; the QVCG is the skeleton plus all its flesh.**

This is consistent across all examples in the monograph:
- K3 x E: lattice VOA $V_{\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}}$ (c=3) inside BKM superalgebra $\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$
- C^3: Heisenberg (c=1) inside $\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty} \cong Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$
- Conifold: $V_{\widehat{A}_1}$ (c=2) inside affine super Yangian
- Hitchin/$G$: lattice from $\pi_1(T)$ inside $G(C,\mathbf{G})$
