\section*{Drinfeld attack--heal: external $\mathfrak{g}$ in $\hCS$
versus derived $\Aut$ in the CY$_3$ category}
\label{wn:sec:wave14-a4-gauge-external-vs-derived-drinfeld}

\emph{Claim under assault.}
\begin{quote}
A CY$_3$ datum $(\cC,\eta)$ is Morita-invariant: two derived-equivalent
$X$'s give the same $\cC$; $\cC$ extends freely to non-geometric inputs
(Ginzburg $(Q,W)$, $\mathrm{MF}(W)$, $\mathrm{Fuk}(X)$). The categorical
side internalises gauge as derived structure rather than external
parameter: $D^b(\Coh(X))$ already carries $\REnd(\cE)$; gauge group
lives in a $2$-groupoid of autoequivalences. The intrinsic $E_3$
exists before any choice of gauge.
\end{quote}

The quote conflates four notions under one word ``gauge'': (i) the Lie
algebra $\mathfrak{gl}_r$ labelling the rank-$r$ vector bundle in
holomorphic Chern--Simons; (ii) the dg Lie algebra
$\mathfrak{g}\otimes\PV^{\bullet}(X)$ of $\hCS$ fields; (iii) the group
$\Aut(\cC)$ of derived autoequivalences of a CY$_3$ category $\cC$; (iv)
the $\infty$-group / $2$-groupoid $\Autinf(\cC)$ of autoequivalences
together with their natural isomorphisms. Five attack--heal cycles
separate the four and state the honest correspondence.

% =====================================================================
\subsection*{Cycle I. ``$2$-groupoid of autoequivalences'': discrete
group, groupoid, or $\infty$-group?}
% =====================================================================

\ClaimStatusAttack\ (\emph{Type-check the phrase ``$2$-groupoid''.})\
For $\cC = D^b(\Coh(X))$ on a smooth projective CY$_3$, Bondal--Orlov
2001 Theorem~3.1 identifies
\[
\Aut(D^b(\Coh(X)))\;=\;\bigl(\Aut(X)\ltimes\Pic(X)\bigr)\times\Z[1],
\]
once $\omega_X\cong\cO_X$ trivialises the canonical (so the Serre
functor is the shift $[3]$). This is a \emph{discrete group}, not a
groupoid: two Fourier--Mukai kernels $\Ft_{K_1},\Ft_{K_2}$ define the
same autoequivalence iff the kernels $K_1,K_2\in D^b(X\times X)$ are
isomorphic up to a contractible choice of quasi-isomorphism. The
quote calls it a ``$2$-groupoid''; but the set underlying a groupoid
of autoequivalences has no non-identity endomorphisms --- a natural
transformation $\alpha: F\Rightarrow F$ for a Fourier--Mukai kernel
$F=\Ft_K$ is an automorphism of $K\in D^b(X\times X)$, which for
simple kernel is $\C^{\times}$. The $2$-groupoid claim is
dimension-mismatched.

\ClaimStatusHeal\ (\emph{The correct object: the mapping space
$\mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(\cC,\cC)^{\cong}$.})\
Work in $(\infty,1)$-categories. The mapping space
$\Autinf(\cC) := \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{dgCat}_{\C}}(\cC,\cC)^{\cong}$
of $\C$-linear dg autoequivalences is a \emph{Kan complex}; its
homotopy groups are
\begin{align}
\pi_0\,\Autinf(\cC) &= \Aut(\cC)/{\sim} \quad\text{(Bondal--Orlov group)},\\
\pi_1\bigl(\Autinf(\cC), \mathrm{id}\bigr) &= \Aut_{\mathrm{dgNat}}(\mathrm{id}_{\cC})
= Z(\cC)^{\times} = H^0(X,\cO_X)^{\times}\cdot (\text{shifts}),\\
\pi_n\bigl(\Autinf(\cC),\mathrm{id}\bigr) &= Z(\cC)_n^{\times}
\quad(n\geq 2) \;\leftarrow\; \text{Toen chiral centre homotopy groups}.
\end{align}
For $X$ smooth projective CY$_3$ with $H^0(X,\cO_X)=\C$, the
$\pi_1$ component is $\C^{\times}\times\Z$ (scalars and shift).
The ``$2$-groupoid'' picture is the $2$-truncation $\tau_{\leq 2}
\Autinf(\cC)$; the honest object is the full $\infty$-groupoid
$\Autinf(\cC)$, or equivalently the $\infty$-group
$B\Autinf(\cC)$ whose loops are $\Autinf(\cC)$.

\ClaimStatusSharpened\ \emph{Revised claim.}
``Gauge lives in $\Autinf(\cC)$, the $\infty$-group of $\C$-linear
dg autoequivalences'' --- $\infty$-group, not $2$-groupoid.
$\Autinf$ is the $(\infty,1)$-mapping space; its $\pi_0$ is
Bondal--Orlov; its $\pi_1$ is $Z(\cC)^{\times}$; higher $\pi$'s are
$Z_{>0}(\cC)^{\times}$-strata. The quote's ``$2$-groupoid'' is the
honest truncation, shorthand without loss, provided the higher data
is remembered for $E_3$-factorisation purposes.

\subsection*{Cycle II. External $\hCS$ datum: which $\mathfrak{g}$?}
% =====================================================================

\ClaimStatusAttack\ (\emph{The quote says ``external $\mathfrak{g}$''
but $\hCS$ has two Lie algebras.})\
Holomorphic Chern--Simons on $\C^3$ with gauge algebra $\mathfrak{g}$
has \emph{field} $\cA\in\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3)\otimes\mathfrak{g}$
with action $S_{\hCS}(\cA) = \int_{\C^3}\Omega\wedge
\mathrm{tr}(\cA\,\dbar\cA + \tfrac{2}{3}\cA\cA\cA)$. Two algebras
enter: the \emph{external} finite-dimensional $\mathfrak{g}$
(e.g.~$\mathfrak{gl}_r$), and the \emph{internal} dg Lie algebra
$\mathfrak{L}_{\hCS} := \mathfrak{g}\otimes
\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3)$ with differential $\dbar$ and bracket
$[\alpha\otimes\phi,\beta\otimes\psi] = [\alpha,\beta]_{\mathfrak{g}}
\otimes\phi\wedge\psi$. Only the internal $\mathfrak{L}_{\hCS}$ is
the actual Lie algebra of $\hCS$ fields; $\mathfrak{g}$ is datum.
Which one does the quote mean by ``external''?

\ClaimStatusHeal\ (\emph{Both are external to the CY$_3$ manifold $X$,
and both have derived-categorical avatars.})\
Fix a framing $\cE\in\cC = \Perf(X)$. Define the dg algebra of
derived endomorphisms
\[
A_{\cE} := \REnd_{\cC}(\cE),
\quad
H^{\bullet}(A_{\cE}) = \mathrm{Ext}^{\bullet}_{\cC}(\cE,\cE).
\]
Take $\cE = \cO_X^{\oplus r}$, the rank-$r$ trivial framing. By
Koszul duality / Calabi--Yau $\HH$-theorem (Calaque--Van den Bergh,
Kontsevich formality, Schedler; cf.~\texttt{wave14\_i1} for toric
formality):
\[
\REnd_{\cC}(\cO_X^{\oplus r}) \;\simeq\; \mathfrak{gl}_r
\otimes \PV^{\bullet}(X)\bigl[1,2,\dots\bigr]
\quad\text{as $A_{\infty}$-algebra,}
\]
where $\PV^{\bullet}(X) = \bigoplus_{p,q} H^q(X,\wedge^p T_X)$ is
holomorphic polyvector fields, the CY$_3$ volume form $\Omega$ turns
the Schouten--Nijenhuis bracket into a $(-1)$-shifted Poisson
bracket, and the full BV structure is carried by
$\mathfrak{gl}_r\otimes \PV^{\bullet}(X)$ via standard tensor product
of dg Lie and BV structures. Specialising $X = \C^3$:
\[
\PV^{\bullet}(\C^3)\bigr|_{\text{polynomial}}
= \C[X,Y,Z]\otimes \wedge^{\bullet}\langle \partial_X,\partial_Y,\partial_Z\rangle,
\]
the Dolbeault $\Omega^{0,\bullet}$-resolution lifts this to
$\PV^{\bullet}_{\mathrm{Dolb}}(\C^3) = \Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3)
\otimes \wedge^{\bullet} T^{1,0}_{\C^3}$, and
\[
\REnd_{\cC}(\cO_{\C^3}^{\oplus r}) = \mathfrak{gl}_r
\otimes \PV^{\bullet}_{\mathrm{Dolb}}(\C^3).
\]
The external datum ``$\mathfrak{g}$'' of $\hCS$ is precisely the
$\mathfrak{gl}_r$ factor; the internal dg Lie algebra
$\mathfrak{L}_{\hCS}$ is the full tensor product
$\mathfrak{gl}_r\otimes \Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3)$, which is the
$(q=\bullet,p=0)$-component of $\REnd_{\cC}(\cO^{\oplus r})$ under
the shear that identifies $\hCS$ fields with strict zero-polyvector
components. The full $\PV^{\bullet}$-valued object $\REnd_{\cC}$
sees also the $p>0$ polyvector components, which are the
\emph{BV anti-fields} of $\hCS$ (conjugate variables in the
$(-1)$-shifted symplectic pairing).

\ClaimStatusSharpened\ \emph{External $\mathfrak{g}$ of $\hCS$ is
internal framing datum of $\cC$.}
$\mathfrak{gl}_r$ is external to $X$ but \emph{not} external to the
pair $(\cC,\cE)$: it is recovered as $H^0\REnd_{\cC}(\cE)$ for
$\cE = \cO_X^{\oplus r}$. Changing framing $\cE\mapsto\cE'$
replaces $\mathfrak{gl}_r$ by $H^0\REnd(\cE')$, which can be any
finite-dimensional semisimple, reductive, or (for skyscraper
framings) zero-dimensional Lie algebra. The $\hCS$ datum
$\mathfrak{g}$ is the rank-decorated shadow of the framing object
$\cE\in\cC$, full stop.

\subsection*{Cycle III. The asymmetry: what changes between datum and
autoequivalence?}
% =====================================================================

\ClaimStatusAttack\ (\emph{What is the asymmetry the quote claims?})\
$\hCS$ takes $\mathfrak{g}$ as input; the CY$_3$ category ``already
carries'' $\REnd(\cE)$. If framing $\cE$ is also an input (Cycle II),
is the asymmetry vacuous? Or is there a structural difference?

\ClaimStatusHeal\ (\emph{Asymmetry is the direction of functoriality.})\
The framing $\cE\mapsto \REnd_{\cC}(\cE)$ is a \emph{contravariant
pseudo-functor} from the $\infty$-groupoid
$\cC^{\cong}$ of objects of $\cC$ to dg algebras:
\[
\REnd_{\cC}: \cC^{\cong} \longrightarrow
\mathrm{dgAlg}_{\C}^{A_{\infty}},
\qquad
(\cE \xrightarrow{\phi} \cE') \mapsto \bigl(\phi^{*}:
\REnd(\cE') \to \REnd(\cE)\bigr).
\]
External $\hCS$ datum $\mathfrak{g}$ is a \emph{choice of input},
i.e.~a \emph{point} $\mathfrak{g}\in\mathrm{dgAlg}_{\C}^{A_{\infty}}$
without any source. The CY$_3$ category has more structure: a
\emph{continuum family} indexed by $\cE\in\cC^{\cong}$, with
$\Aut_{\cC}(\cE)$-equivariant descent. In the framed-moduli picture:
\begin{align}
\mathrm{ExtGauge}(\hCS) &= \bigl\{\text{points of } \mathrm{dgAlg}_{\C}^{A_{\infty}}\bigr\},\\
\mathrm{IntGauge}(\cC,X) &= \mathrm{Fun}\bigl(\cC^{\cong},
\mathrm{dgAlg}_{\C}^{A_{\infty}}\bigr)\Bigr|_{\REnd} \supsetneq
\mathrm{ExtGauge}(\hCS).
\end{align}
The internal version remembers the \emph{dependence on framing};
the external version is the value at a single chosen framing,
forgetting the moduli. This is the honest asymmetry: not
``external vs internal'', but ``one value vs the full equivariant
family.''

\ClaimStatusSharpened\ \emph{Stratification.}
The asymmetry is a Yoneda--density statement. The external
$\hCS$ datum $\mathfrak{g}$ is the fibre
$\REnd_{\cC}(\cE_0)\cong \mathfrak{g}\otimes\PV^{\bullet}$ at a
\emph{distinguished framing} $\cE_0$; the internal datum
$\REnd_{\cC}(-)$ is the whole sheaf over $\cC^{\cong}$. Different
framings, different external Lie algebras; one internal object. The
mental picture: external $\hCS$ is a sequential vertical slice;
internal CY$_3$ is the whole cylinder. ``The intrinsic $E_3$ exists
before any choice of gauge'' is the statement that the $E_3$
factorisation-algebra structure (Cycle IV) exists on the whole
cylinder, and the external-$\mathfrak{g}$-$\hCS$ is recovered as
factorisation homology of the cylinder over the framing choice.

\subsection*{Cycle IV. $E_3$ before or after framing: what is the
intrinsic factorisation structure?}
% =====================================================================

\ClaimStatusAttack\ (\emph{``Intrinsic $E_3$ exists before any choice
of gauge'' --- evidence?})\
$\hCS$ on $\C^3$ produces, per Costello--Gwilliam, an
$E_3$-factorisation algebra of observables when gauge data
$\mathfrak{g}$ is fixed. For abelian $\hCS$ ($\mathfrak{g} = \C$),
this is the Heisenberg-VOA of Koszul-dual type on a closed curve;
for nonabelian, it is the $\mathfrak{g}$-current-algebra vertex
operator deformation. Both are \emph{after} gauge data is chosen.
The quote claims $E_3$ \emph{before}; where does this live?

\ClaimStatusHeal\ (\emph{The $E_3$ lives on the CY$_3$ category itself,
on $\HH$-cochains, via Kontsevich--Soibelman / Calaque--Van den Bergh.})\
The Hochschild cochain complex
$\HH^{\bullet}(\cC) := \mathrm{Ext}^{\bullet}_{\cC\otimes\cC^{\mathrm{op}}}
(\cC,\cC)$ of a dg category $\cC$ carries:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Gerstenhaber bracket of degree $-1$;
\item[(b)] Kontsevich $E_2$-structure (Deligne conjecture, proved by
Tamarkin);
\item[(c)] For $\cC = \Perf(X)$, an HKR isomorphism
$\HH^{\bullet}(\Perf(X))\simeq\bigoplus_{p,q}H^q(X,\wedge^p T_X)
= \PV^{\bullet}(X)$;
\item[(d)] For CY$_d$ $X$ with trivialised canonical, the Serre-duality
pairing lifts to a \emph{CY structure} on the $(\infty,1)$-category
$\Perf(X)$ in the sense of Kontsevich--Soibelman 2008 §10: a
non-degenerate trace $\HH_{\bullet}(\cC)\to\C[-d]$;
\item[(e)] The $E_2$ structure on $\HH^{\bullet}$ together with the
CY$_d$ trace promotes to an \emph{$E_d$}-algebra structure on the
$\HH$-cochains-with-trace via Kontsevich cyclic formality /
Calaque--Felder--Rossi--Willwacher. For $d=3$, this is $E_3$.
\end{enumerate}
Hence: $\HH^{\bullet}(\Perf(X))$ for CY$_3$ $X$ carries an $E_3$-algebra
structure \emph{with no choice of framing $\cE$}. The $E_3$ is
intrinsic to the dg CY$_3$-categorical datum $(\cC,\eta)$.

The external $\hCS$-output is recovered as follows. A framing $\cE$
determines a \emph{module} $M_{\cE}$ over $\HH^{\bullet}(\cC)$ given
by $\REnd(\cE)$. The $E_3$-action on the $\HH$-cochains induces an
$E_3$-module structure on $M_{\cE}$, and Dunn additivity
$E_3\simeq E_1\otimes E_2$ together with Kontsevich--Tamarkin
formality gives
\[
M_{\cE} \simeq
\bigl(\mathfrak{gl}_r\otimes\PV^{\bullet}(X)\bigr)_{\mathrm{BV}}
\quad\text{as }E_3\text{-module,}
\]
which, restricted to the closed-curve specialisation
$\int_{\Sigma_2}(-)$ with $\Sigma_2\subset X$, produces the
$\hCS_{\mathfrak{gl}_r}$ $E_1$-chiral-algebra shadow on the reference
curve $C$. This is exactly the two-stage factorisation
$\Phi_d = \mathrm{Sp}_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_d$
(\texttt{wave12\_u1\_two\_stage\_functor.tex} Def~\ref{def:two-stage-phi}
companion), specialised at the framing $\cE$.

\ClaimStatusSharpened\ \emph{The intrinsic $E_3$ is the
$\HH^{\bullet}$-Kontsevich-cyclic-$E_3$ on the CY$_3$ category; the
framing turns it into the $E_3$-module of $\hCS$-fields with
external gauge $\mathfrak{gl}_r$.}\
The asymmetry is therefore \emph{unified}, not opposed: the
$\infty$-group $\Autinf(\cC)$ acts on the $E_3$-algebra
$\HH^{\bullet}(\cC)$ by dg autoequivalences; the Bondal--Orlov
$\pi_0$-group acts on $\HH^{\bullet}$ by the Hochschild functoriality
of kernel transforms; and the $\pi_1$-scalars $Z(\cC)^{\times}$ act
as inner automorphisms on $\HH^{\bullet}$.
A framing $\cE$ chooses a \emph{baseline representation}; the
$\Autinf(\cC)$-orbit of $\cE$ produces all framings Morita-equivalent
to $\cE$; the $\hCS$-with-$\mathfrak{gl}_r$ is the baseline
representation of this $\Autinf$-equivariant $E_3$-structure.

\subsection*{Cycle V. Generator--relation dictionary: $\hCS$-fields to
$\REnd$-elements}
% =====================================================================

\ClaimStatusAttack\ (\emph{Explicit dictionary, please.})\
Abstract $E_3$ structure on $\HH^{\bullet}(\Perf(X))$ does not by
itself produce $\hCS$-generators; the quote requires a concrete
identification for $X = \C^3$, $\cE = \cO^{\oplus r}$.

\ClaimStatusHeal\ (\emph{Explicit seven-entry table.})\
The $A_{\infty}$-algebra
$A_{\cE} = \REnd_{\cC}(\cO_{\C^3}^{\oplus r}) =
\mathfrak{gl}_r\otimes\PV^{\bullet}_{\mathrm{Dolb}}(\C^3)$
decomposes additively over bidegree $(p,q)\in\{0,1,2,3\}\times\{0,1,2,3\}$
of $\PV^{p,q} = \Omega^{0,q}\otimes\wedge^p T^{1,0}$. On $\C^3$, only
$p\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ and $q\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ contribute; after
$\dbar$-resolution the cohomology is concentrated at $q=0$ on
$\C^3$ (holomorphic vanishing), so the relevant polyvectors are
$H^0(\C^3, \wedge^p T_{\C^3})$. The dictionary is:
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{tabular}{c|l|l|l|l}
Degree & $\hCS$-field & $\REnd$-component & BV role & Lie/Schouten bracket \\\hline
$(0,0)$ & ghost $c^a T_a$ & $\mathfrak{gl}_r \otimes \cO_{\C^3}$ & ghost & $[T_a,T_b]_{\mathfrak{gl}}\cdot\text{pt-wise}$\\
$(0,1)$ & gauge $\cA^{a}_{\bar j} d\bar z^j T_a$ & $\mathfrak{gl}_r\otimes \Omega^{0,1}$ & field & $[T_a,T_b]\otimes (\alpha\wedge\beta)$\\
$(0,2)$ & anti-$\cA$ via $\star$ & $\mathfrak{gl}_r\otimes \Omega^{0,2}$ & field (BV conjugate) & id.\\
$(0,3)$ & anti-ghost & $\mathfrak{gl}_r\otimes\Omega^{0,3}$ & anti-ghost & id.\\
$(1,0)$ & vector-field ghost $c^a \partial_i T_a$ & $\mathfrak{gl}_r\otimes T_{\C^3}$ & BV conjugate of $\cA$ & $\Schouten$ on $T$-component\\
$(2,0)$ & bivector $c^a \partial_i\wedge\partial_j T_a$ & $\mathfrak{gl}_r\otimes\wedge^2 T_{\C^3}$ & BV conjugate of anti-$\cA$ & id.\\
$(3,0)$ & volume dual $c^a \Omega^{-1}T_a$ & $\mathfrak{gl}_r\otimes\wedge^3 T_{\C^3}$ & coupling (cosmological) & id.\\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The CY$_3$ volume $\Omega_{\C^3} = dX\wedge dY\wedge dZ$ converts
$\wedge^3 T_{\C^3}\cong \cO_{\C^3}$, identifying row-1 and row-7
additively; this is the $(-1)$-shifted-symplectic pairing of the
BV bracket. The Schouten--Nijenhuis bracket on $\PV^{\bullet}$
and the $\mathfrak{gl}_r$-commutator combine to give the BV
bracket
\[
\{\alpha\otimes X,\ \beta\otimes Y\}_{\mathrm{BV}}
= [X,Y]_{\mathfrak{gl}_r}\otimes[\alpha,\beta]_{\mathrm{SN}}
+\text{CY}_3\text{-trace correction on top polyvector},
\]
which is the BV bracket of nonabelian $\hCS_{\mathfrak{gl}_r}$ on
$\C^3$.

\ClaimStatusSharpened\ \emph{The dictionary recovers Costello--Li
$\hCS$.}\
Row $(0,1)$ + row $(1,0)$ form the BV field + BV antifield pair;
their pairing uses rows $(0,3)$ and $(3,0)$ via
$\int_{\C^3}\Omega\wedge(-)\wedge(-)$. The $\dbar$-differential
contributes the kinetic term
$\cA\wedge\dbar\cA$. The Schouten $+$ $\mathfrak{gl}_r$-bracket
contributes the cubic interaction
$\tfrac{1}{3}\mathrm{tr}(\cA\wedge\cA\wedge\cA)$. The seven rows
together form the BV superfield
$\cA^{\mathrm{BV}}\in\mathfrak{gl}_r\otimes\PV^{\bullet}_{\mathrm{Dolb}}(\C^3)$
of ghost number summed over $(p+q)-1\in\{-1,0,1,2,3\}$.
This is the exact content of Costello 2013 \texttt{arXiv:1303.2632}
§2.3 in the nonabelian case, and \texttt{wave12\_a5\_hCS\_BV\_BRST\_explicit}
§2--3 in the abelian $\mathfrak{g}=\C$ case. The external gauge
$\mathfrak{gl}_r$ of $\hCS$ is the $p=q=0$ slice of
$\REnd_{\cC}(\cO^{\oplus r})$.

\subsection*{Synthesis: the reconciled claim}
% =====================================================================

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (\emph{External/internal reconciliation at CY$_3$.})\
\label{wn:thm:external-internal-reconciliation}
Let $X$ be a smooth CY$_3$, $\cC=\Perf(X)$ with its Kontsevich--Soibelman
CY$_3$ structure $\eta$, $\cE\in\cC$ a framing object with
$r=\dim H^0\End(\cE)$, and $\hCS_{\mathfrak{g},X}$ the holomorphic
Chern--Simons theory on $X$ with external Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g} =
H^0\End(\cE)$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\rm (i)] The dg $A_{\infty}$-algebra $\REnd_{\cC}(\cE)$ is
\[
\REnd_{\cC}(\cE) \simeq \mathrm{End}_{\C}(\mathfrak{g})\otimes
\PV^{\bullet}_{\mathrm{Dolb}}(X) \;\supset\;
\mathfrak{g}\otimes\Omega^{0,\bullet}(X),
\]
the inclusion being the BV field-only component at polyvector
degree $p=0$;
\item[\rm (ii)] The Hochschild cochain complex
$\HH^{\bullet}(\cC) \simeq \PV^{\bullet}(X)$ carries the
Kontsevich--Calaque--Van den Bergh $E_3$-algebra structure,
\emph{independent of the choice of framing $\cE$};
\item[\rm (iii)] The framing $\cE$ turns
$\HH^{\bullet}(\cC)$ into the $E_3$-module
$\mathrm{End}_{\C}(\mathfrak{g})\otimes\PV^{\bullet}(X)$, and the
$\hCS_{\mathfrak{g},X}$ $E_3$-factorisation algebra of observables
(Costello--Gwilliam) is exactly the $E_3$-module $\REnd_{\cC}(\cE)$
under this action;
\item[\rm (iv)] The $\infty$-group $\Autinf(\cC)$ acts on both
$\HH^{\bullet}(\cC)$ and on the space of framings
$\cC^{\cong}$; external gauge $\mathfrak{g}$ and internal
$\REnd(-)$ are reconciled via the Morita-invariance of $\cC$ under
the $\pi_0$-autoequivalence group $\Aut(\cC)$ (Bondal--Orlov);
\item[\rm (v)] The two-stage $\Phi_3 = \mathrm{Sp}_{\Sigma_2,C}\circ
\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3$ of
\texttt{wave12\_u1\_two\_stage\_functor.tex} carries: Stage~1 is
framing-independent, outputs the $E_3$-hFA on $X$ from $(\cC,\eta)$
via the Kontsevich cyclic $E_3$-structure on $\HH^{\bullet}(\cC)$;
Stage~2 specialises via the factorisation-homology
$\int_{\Sigma_2}(-)\bigr|_C$ and produces the $\hCS$ $E_1$-chiral
shadow on $C$ coupled to the framing $\cE$.
\end{enumerate}

\ClaimStatusDerivation\ \emph{Cache audit.}\
This reconciliation is CY$_3$-strict:
\begin{itemize}
\item At $d\geq 3$, $\cC$ is an $E_1$-category (associative); the
$E_2$-structure is carried by $\HH^{\bullet}(\cC)$; the $E_3$ is
carried by $\HH^{\bullet}$ with CY$_3$ trace (Kontsevich cyclic
formality). This respects the TOP-15 cache entry ``$d\geq 3$, $A$ is
$E_1$; $E_2$ on $Z(\mathrm{Rep}(A))$''. The $\mathrm{Rep}(A)$
shorthand $= \cC$; its Drinfeld centre $Z(\cC) = \HH^{\bullet}(\cC)$
at the level of endomorphism-centre; the $E_3$-lift at CY$_3$ is via
the trace.
\item The Drinfeld centre is the right adjoint to the forgetful
$Z(\cC)\to\cC$, not an averaging functor (AP176).
\item CoHA on $\C^3$ is $Y^+(\gglone)$ in the current cycle's
dictionary, not $\Wilpha$: the $\hCS$-field theory sector is the
$E_3$-module of $\hCS$-observables (bilateral), while CoHA is the
positive-half shuffle algebra of fixed-point classes. The external
$\mathfrak{g}$ of $\hCS$ is $\mathfrak{gl}_r$; the $Y^+(\gglone)$

\subsection*{Coda. Where ``asymmetry'' mutates into ``specialisation''}
% =====================================================================

The adversarial phrasing opposed two pictures. The rigorous content
unites them. The CY$_3$ category $\cC$ is the ambient object, with
intrinsic $E_3$ on $\HH^{\bullet}(\cC)$ (Stage~1 of $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3$).
The $\hCS$ theory with external gauge $\mathfrak{gl}_r$ is the value of
this intrinsic $E_3$ at a chosen framing $\cE = \cO^{\oplus r}$, under
the identification
$\REnd_{\cC}(\cO^{\oplus r}) = \mathfrak{gl}_r\otimes\PV^{\bullet}(X)$
(Cycle II). The $\infty$-group $\Autinf(\cC)$ reshuffles framings
without changing the intrinsic $E_3$: this is the precise content of
``gauge lives in a $2$-groupoid''. Two derived-equivalent
$X,X'$ giving the same $\cC$ give the same intrinsic $E_3$ but
potentially different Morita-covered external $\hCS$ theories, which
are related by the Fourier--Mukai kernel connecting $X$ and $X'$. The
Morita-invariance of $(\cC,\eta)$ and the framing-variance of the
external $\mathfrak{g}$ are compatible: different framings give
different $\hCS$ theories but the same ambient CY$_3$ datum, and this
is the one-sentence summary of the reconciled picture.

\emph{After Drinfeld:} the external datum is not a prior category;
it is a chosen representation of the intrinsic $E_3$-object, and the
asymmetry the quote names is the Yoneda asymmetry between an abstract
object and its representations. The $E_3$ exists before the framing,
as the $\HH^{\bullet}$-level structure; each framing instantiates it
as an $\hCS$ field theory; the $\infty$-group of autoequivalences acts
on the space of framings while preserving the $E_3$. Nothing is
external that is not also a representation.
