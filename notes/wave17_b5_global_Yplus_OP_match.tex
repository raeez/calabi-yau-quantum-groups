\section{Oriented Critical Positive Half}
\label{sec:global-Yplus-critical-positive-half}

Let $X$ be a smooth projective Calabi-Yau threefold with
$\omega_X\simeq\cO_X$. Fix a numerical charge lattice
$\Gamma=K_0^{\mathrm{num}}(X)$, a positive cone $\Gamma_+\subset\Gamma$,
and a stability condition $\tau$ for coherent sheaves. For
$\gamma\in\Gamma_+$ let $\cM_\gamma(X)$ be the derived moduli stack of
$\tau$-semistable objects of charge $\gamma$. The Calabi-Yau trace gives
$\cM_\gamma(X)$ a $(-1)$-shifted symplectic structure by
Pantev-Toen-Vaquie-Vezzosi 2013, Theorem~2.5. Brav-Bussi-Joyce 2019,
Theorem~5.18 gives local critical charts for the classical truncation.
An orientation datum fixes a compatible square root of the determinant
line and hence a perverse sheaf of vanishing cycles
\[
  \phi_\gamma\in\mathrm{Perv}\bigl(t_0\cM_\gamma(X)\bigr).
\]
The oriented critical positive half is the formal charge-graded complex
\[
  \Yplus_\tau(X)
  :=
  \bigoplus_{\gamma\in\Gamma_+}
  H^*_c\bigl(t_0\cM_\gamma(X),\phi_\gamma\bigr).
\]
Equivalently, the local Darboux critical charts carry the sheaves
\(\phi_\gamma\), and their transition isomorphisms descend to the
global perverse sheaf on \(t_0\cM_\gamma(X)\). The expression above is
the compactly supported global section functor applied after that
descent. The scalar Euler series forgets the descended operations; the
Hall algebra retains them.

The Hall product is defined on finite charge windows. For a finite
\(\Gamma_+\)-window where the stacks are of finite type, the extension
correspondences are proper on the chosen support, and the six-functor
operations are defined, let
$\cE_{\gamma_1,\gamma_2}(X)$ classify short exact sequences
$0\to F_2\to F\to F_1\to0$ with charges $\gamma_i$. The extension
correspondence is
\[
  \cM_{\gamma_1}(X)\times\cM_{\gamma_2}(X)
  \xleftarrow{\;p\;}
  \cE_{\gamma_1,\gamma_2}(X)
  \xrightarrow{\;q\;}
  \cM_{\gamma_1+\gamma_2}(X),
\]
and multiplication is $q_!p^*$ with the Thom shift determined by the
orientation. On an etale critical chart equivalent to a Jacobi algebra
$(Q,W)$ this is the Kontsevich-Soibelman critical CoHA product. In the
quiver-with-potential setting, Davison-Meinhardt 2020, Theorem~A gives
the cohomological Hall algebra.

Thus $\Yplus_\tau(X)$ carries the positive $E_1$ Hall multiplication,
the native output level of \(\Phi_3\). A Hall-Drinfeld double
requires a nondegenerate Hopf pairing, a compatible coproduct, and a
completion. A Borcherds-Kac-Moody recognition further requires a
bracket, root multiplicities, and a denominator identity. These data
are independent of the positive half and of its scalar Euler character.

\begin{lemma}[Hodge trace on compact Calabi-Yau threefolds]
\label{lem:compact-cy3-hodge-trace}
If $X$ is strict, meaning $h^{0,1}(X)=h^{0,2}(X)=0$, then
\[
  R\Gamma(X,\cO_X)\simeq \CC\oplus\CC[-3],
  \qquad
  \sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(X)=0.
\]
For $Y=S\times E$, with $S$ a K3 surface and $E$ an elliptic curve,
\[
  H^\bullet(Y,\cO_Y)
  =
  (H^0(S,\cO_S)\oplus H^2(S,\cO_S)[-2])
  \otimes
  (H^0(E,\cO_E)\oplus H^1(E,\cO_E)[-1]),
\]
so
\[
  \kcat(Y)=\chi(\cO_Y)=1-1+1-1=0.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For strict $X$, connectedness gives $H^0(X,\cO_X)=\CC$, and Serre
duality with $\omega_X\simeq\cO_X$ gives
$H^3(X,\cO_X)\simeq H^0(X,\omega_X)^\vee=\CC$. The middle terms vanish
by hypothesis. The product computation is the Kunneth formula applied
to $H^{0,*}(S)=(1,0,1)$ and $H^{0,*}(E)=(1,1)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Joyce-Song character of the positive half]
\label{prop:Yplus-JS-character}
In any stability chamber where Joyce-Song Hall integration applies, the
formal compactly supported Euler series of the oriented positive half is
\[
  \chi_c^\Gamma\bigl(\Yplus_\tau(X)\bigr)
  :=
  \sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma_+}
  \chi_c\bigl(t_0\cM_\gamma(X),\phi_\gamma\bigr)x^\gamma.
\]
It is the Joyce-Song generalized Donaldson-Thomas character in that
chamber.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The charge decomposition is the definition of $\Yplus_\tau(X)$. On each
oriented critical chart, Behrend 2009, Theorem~4.18 identifies the Euler
characteristic of the vanishing-cycle sheaf with the Behrend weighted
Euler characteristic. Joyce-Song 2012, Theorem~5.8 constructs the
Behrend-weighted Hall integration map to the completed quantum torus,
and Definition~5.13 defines the rational generalized DT invariants. The
displayed series is the additive Euler character of the graded positive
Hall complex under that integration map.
\end{proof}

\section{Primitive Oberdieck-Pixton Scalar}
\label{sec:K3E-OP-primitive-character}

Let $Y=S\times E$, where $S$ is a projective K3 surface and $E$ is an
elliptic curve. Let $\beta=(\beta_S,d[E])$ be a curve class with
$\beta_S$ primitive in $H_2(S,\ZZ)$. Let $\Delta_5$ be the
Gritsenko-Nikulin weight-$5$ denominator. The theta-leading coefficient
of $\Delta_5$ is $64$, hence the monic Borcherds product is
\[
  D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5.
\]
Oberdieck-Pixton use the scalar Igusa normalization
\[
  \PhiOP=D_5^2=4096^{-1}\Delta_5^2.
\]

Oberdieck-Pixton, arXiv:1706.10100, Theorem~1, prove the primitive
reduced theory of $S\times E$ in the Laurent chamber
$|p^kq^h\tilde q^d|<1$ for $4hd-k^2\ge -1$:
\[
  \DTred(Y;p,q,\tilde q)
  =
  \ZOPRed(Y;p,q,\tilde q)
  =
  -\frac{1}{\PhiOP(p,q,\tilde q)}
  =
  -4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}.
\]
The assertion is primitive in the K3 class. Imprimitive classes require
the separate Oberdieck-Pandharipande and Bryan-Oberdieck reduced-pairs
inputs.

\begin{lemma}[OP square and denominator square root]
\label{lem:D5-Phi10-normalization}
The unnormalized Igusa square is
$\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_5^2$, while the OP scalar form is
$\PhiOP=D_5^2$. Consequently
\[
  \PhiOP=4096^{-1}\Delta_5^2,
  \qquad
  \DTred(Y)=-4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The Gritsenko-Nikulin square-root identity gives
$\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_5^2$. The monic denominator section is
$D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5$. Squaring gives
$\PhiOP=D_5^2=4096^{-1}\Delta_5^2$, and the OP sign convention gives
$\DTred(Y)=-(\PhiOP)^{-1}=-4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Positive-half character on the primitive reduced sector]
\label{thm:Yplus-OP-match}
Assume the following data are fixed for \(Y=S\times E\):
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathrm{(i)}&
\text{an oriented critical positive half on primitive finite-type
reduced Hall sectors,}\\
\mathrm{(ii)}&
\text{a Joyce-Song integration map compatible with the OP reduced
obstruction theory,}\\
\mathrm{(iii)}&
\text{the DT/PT chamber and variables in the OP Laurent normalization.}
\end{array}
\]
Then the primitive reduced Euler character of the positive half is the
OP reduced-DT scalar:
\[
  \chi_c^\Gamma\bigl(\Yplus_\tau(Y)\bigr)
  \Big|_{\beta_S\ \mathrm{primitive,red}}
  =
  \DTred(Y)
  =
  -\frac{1}{\PhiOP}
  =
  -4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Proposition~\ref{prop:Yplus-JS-character} identifies the Euler character
of the oriented positive Hall complex with the Joyce-Song DT character
in the chosen stability chamber. Assumption (ii) replaces the ordinary
obstruction theory by the OP reduced obstruction theory, including the
$E$-translation reduction and the K3 holomorphic-symplectic reduction.
The scalar $\chi(\cO_Y)=0$ from
Lemma~\ref{lem:compact-cy3-hodge-trace} is a total-space Hodge
supertrace, separate from \(\kBKM(\Delta_5)\). The reduced invariant
takes its denominator from the OP primitive theorem, which supplies
$\DTred(Y)=-(\PhiOP)^{-1}=-4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}$ in the stated Laurent
chamber.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Recognition data beyond the scalar character]
\label{prop:scalar-character-recognition-boundary}
The equality in Theorem~\ref{thm:Yplus-OP-match} determines the
charge-graded Euler character of the primitive reduced positive half.
An algebra theorem identifying a compact Hall source with a positive
Borcherds half requires the following additional data:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathrm{(a)}&
\text{descent of the Hall product through the critical atlas and reduced
finite windows,}\\
\mathrm{(b)}&
\text{an orientation-compatible coproduct and nondegenerate Hopf
pairing,}\\
\mathrm{(c)}&
\text{a radical quotient, PBW comparison, and kernel equality excluding
extra relations,}\\
\mathrm{(d)}&
\text{a completion supporting the Hall-Drinfeld double,}\\
\mathrm{(e)}&
\text{root multiplicities matching the coefficients of the
Borcherds product \(D_5\),}\\
\mathrm{(f)}&
\text{a centre or vacuum-evaluation passage for any
\(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}\) avatar.}
\end{array}
\]
These data are independent hypotheses. The OP scalar identity is a
necessary character test for them.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The map
\(\Yplus_\tau(Y)\mapsto\chi_c^\Gamma(\Yplus_\tau(Y))\) factors through
the charge-graded Grothendieck group and records alternating dimensions
of compactly supported vanishing-cycle cohomology. Products,
coproducts, Hopf pairings, radicals, PBW filtrations, completions, and
vertex-evaluation maps are structure maps on the complexes themselves.
The denominator identity for \(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5}\) fixes root
spaces and signs whose superdenominator is \(D_5\). Therefore the
scalar OP identity supplies the numerical character constraint;
recognition of a Hall-Drinfeld or BKM object begins with the six data
listed above.
\end{proof}

\section{Borcherds Weight and Separated Invariants}
\label{sec:igusa-borcherds-normalizations}

\begin{lemma}[Borcherds weights]
\label{lem:borcherds-weight-tuples}
For the primitive CHL denominator ladder $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$,
\[
  (c_N(0))=(10,8,6,4,2),
  \qquad
  \bigl(\kBKM(\Phi_N)\bigr)=(5,4,3,2,1).
\]
For the Gritsenko-Clery diagonal-divisor catalogue, a different
eight-row list, the twice-weight tuple is
\[
  (10,4,6,2,4,1,3,2).
\]
At level one,
\[
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)=\kBKM(\Phi_1)=c_1(0)/2=10/2=5.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Borcherds 1998, Theorem~13.3 gives the weight of a Borcherds product as
one half of the constant term of the input vector-valued form. Gritsenko
1999 and the Gritsenko-Nikulin paramodular construction give
$c_N(0)=(10,8,6,4,2)$ on the primitive CHL ladder, hence
$\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2=(5,4,3,2,1)$. The Gritsenko-Clery list is a
separate diagonal-divisor catalogue with its own cover groups and the
twice-weight tuple displayed above.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Separated \(K3\times E\) invariants]
\label{cor:k3e-four-invariants-from-character}
For \(Y=K3\times E\), the four invariants appearing at this interface
are
\[
\begin{array}{ccl}
 \kcat(Y) &=& \chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot0=0,\\[2pt]
 \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(Y) &=& 3,\\[2pt]
 \kBKM(\Delta_5) &=& c_1(0)/2=5,\\[2pt]
 \kfib(Y) &=& \mathrm{rk}\,\widetilde H(K3,\ZZ)=1+22+1=24.
\end{array}
\]
The compact Hodge supertrace
\[
  \sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(Y)=1-1+1-1=0
\]
is the total-space Hodge reading and is separate from the
Heisenberg-Mukai specialization.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The first equality is Kunneth multiplicativity for
$\chi(\cO_Y)$. The fourth is the Mukai-lattice rank of the K3 fibre. The
Heisenberg-Mukai value is the fibre specialization giving the
single-copy chiral invariant. The compact chiral Hodge trace is the
separate value \(\kch(Y)=0\).
The \(\kBKM\) value is Lemma~\ref{lem:borcherds-weight-tuples} at
\(N=1\). The OP scalar
identity records the reduced-DT character
$\DTred(Y)=-4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}$; Hall-Drinfeld doubling and BKM
recognition are further algebraic constructions.
\end{proof}

\section{Boundary of the Character Comparison}
\label{sec:global-Yplus-character-boundary}

Theorem~\ref{thm:Yplus-OP-match} is a scalar character comparison. Its
inputs are the oriented critical positive half, Behrend vanishing
cycles, OP reduced obstruction theory on \(K3\times E\), and the
primitive OP Igusa theorem.
Proposition~\ref{prop:scalar-character-recognition-boundary} records
the extra recognition data needed for the compact Hall-Drinfeld double,
the nondegenerate Hopf pairing, the Borcherds root-space comparison, and
the centre/completion passage.

The local toric anchor is
\[
  \mathrm{CoHA}(\CC^3)=Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1),
\]
the positive half. The full \(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}\) object appears
after Drinfeld double or centre and a vacuum specialization. The BKM
object attached to \(K3\times E\) is the Hall-Drinfeld/Borcherds branch
governed by \(\Delta_5\). The K3 self-mirror Yangian branch is a
different Mukai-lattice construction.
