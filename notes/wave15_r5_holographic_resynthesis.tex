\section*{Holographic re-synthesis of the Lorgat 2020 programme
  (Wave-15 R5/20)}
\label{sec:wave15-r5-holo-resynthesis}

\paragraph{Thesis.}
The Lorgat 2020 Borcherds lift $\phi_{0,1}\mapsto\Deltafive$ and the
superalgebra $\gBKM$ organise not an analogy but a \emph{holographic
tower}: one CY$_3$ datum $(K3\times E,\Omega,\gBKM)$ projects to five
bulk/boundary layers whose consistency pins a single weight-$5$
invariant, $\kBKM(\Phiten)=c_1(0)/2=5$.  Wave~14 sharpened three
layers.  This re-synthesis reads the sharpenings as \emph{holographic
consequences}, and identifies a sixth layer from the lattice-polarised
$\gL$ family.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 1 --- Re-stated five-layer tower}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Layer 1: 4D class-$\cS$.)} The
$(2,0)$ $A_1$ theory on the twenty-four-punctured sphere
$\Sph$ gives $\cT_{24}=\cT[A_1,\Sph]$ with central charge
$c_{4d}=107/6$ (Shapere--Tachikawa; W14-W19 adjudication).

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Layer 2, Wave-14 sharpening.)} The
Beem--Lemos--Liendo--Peelaers--Rastelli chiral algebra
$\cV_{24}=\cV[\cT_{24}]$ is \emph{not} the affine algebra
$L_{-6}(\fe_8)$ but the iterated Drinfeld--Sokolov reduction
\[
  \cV_{24}\;\cong\;
  H^{0}_{\mathrm{DS}}\!\bigl(L_{-2+1/22}(\fsl_2)^{\otimes 22}\bigr),
\]
with twenty-two trinion factors glued along twenty-one tubes by the
pants decomposition of $\Sph$
(Wave-14 K3, \texttt{wave14\_k3\_classS\_Schur\_index.tex};
BLLPR 2015 Thm.~6.1).  Central charge $-214=-12\cdot 107/6$ is the
Kac--Wakimoto match.  The Schur index identifies
$\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{Schur}}(\cT_{24};q)$ with the Miranda--Persson
correction to a twenty-four-punctured $\Sp_4(\Z)$-orbit, and closes
the first reading of the $\gBKM$-twined character.

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Layer 3, Costello--Gwilliam $E_3$.)} The
factorisation algebra $\Obs_{\hCS}$ of six-dimensional $\hCS$ on
$\C^3$ is $E_3$ in $\mathrm{Ch}(\mathrm{Dolb})$; Wave-14 I1 computes
the anomaly density $\omega_{\mathrm{anom}}=\tfrac16\mathrm{Tr}(F^3)\Omega$
and shows $\kanom(K3\times E,\mathfrak{g})=0$.

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Layer 2 $\to$ 4, Miki $\Sthree$-triality.)}
Wave-15 N4 (\texttt{wave15\_n4\_Miki\_triality\_CoHA.tex}):
$\Sthree$ permutes $\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3$ on the
Calabi--Yau torus $T_{\mathrm{CY}}$.  Miki's automorphism of
$\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]$ (Miki 2007; Prochazka--Rap{\v c}{\'a}k
2018) is the image under $\mathrm{ev}_\lambda\colon Y(\gghone)\to
\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]$ of the $\Sthree$-action on
$\CoHA(\C^3)=Y^+(\gghone)$ (Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013; FJMM 2016
\texttt{arXiv:1603.02765}).  Triality is a symmetry of shuffle
structure constants, not a shadow-chiral equivalence:
$\CoHA(\C^3)=Y^+\ne\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$.

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Layer 5, Wave-14 HH rigidity closure.)}
Wave-15 N1 (\texttt{wave15\_n1\_HH2\_E2\_nonab.tex}): the Francis
tangent complex gives
$\HH^2_{E_2}(\partial\hCS_5(\fsl_n),M)=0$ to all orders by
Whitehead's second lemma plus formality of
$V^{\mathrm{vac}}_k(\widehat{\fsl}_n)$.  Hence
$\partial\hCS_5(\fsl_n)\simeq\Yaff(\widehat{\fsl}_n)$ is
unconditional; in particular the abelian shadow
$\partial\hCS_5(\gghone)\simeq\Yaff(\gghone)$ to all orders.

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Layer 5: graviton counting.)} The
Dijkgraaf--Verlinde--Verlinde / Shih--Strominger--Yin formula
\[
  d(Q)\;=\;(-1)^{Q\cdot Q+1}\oint
  \frac{e^{2\pi i\,Q\cdot\Omega}}{\Phiten(\Omega)}\,d\Omega
\]
reads $-\log\Phiten$ as the $\gBKM$-denominator, i.e.\ as a supercharacter
of the chiral-$\AdS_3$ BPS algebra.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 2 --- L/M/N-group questions as holography}

Wave-15 L-, M-, N-triples pose questions whose \emph{holographic
rephrasing} is:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{L (generic CY$_3$):} does the Green--Schwarz
    mechanism close the six-dimensional $\hCS$ anomaly at
    $\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(X)\ne 0$?  This is the bulk-boundary
    question: which CY$_3$ boundaries support a conformal AdS dual.
  \item \textbf{M (lattice-polarised $\gL$):} does every Nikulin
    polarisation $L\subset\Lambda_{K3}$ carry a BKM
    superalgebra with Borcherds weight $\kBKM=c_L(0)/2$?  This is
    the boundary-BPS question: how many inequivalent chiral
    algebras live on the common AdS$_3$ throat of the $L$-polarised
    family.
  \item \textbf{N (non-abelian VOA descent):} does
    $\partial\hCS_5(\fsl_n)\simeq\Yaff(\widehat{\fsl}_n)$ hold beyond
    $n=1$?  Wave-15 N1 answers \emph{yes, to all orders} via HH$^2_{E_2}$
    rigidity --- so the bulk-boundary lift is non-abelian.
\end{itemize}
Under holography, L/M/N are one question read at three radii: bulk
anomaly closure, throat BPS algebra, boundary VOA quantisation.  The
synthesis is that a solution to any one is a solution to the others,
since all three see the same chiral tangent complex
$\mathrm{Tan}_\C\,\cV_X$.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 3 --- $\AdS_3/\mathrm{CFT}_2$: $1/\Deltafive^2$
  sharpened}

The Oberdieck--Pixton identity
$Z^{\mathrm{DT,red}}(K3\times E)=-1/\Phiten$
(\texttt{arXiv:1706.10100}) sits on the untwisted
$N=1$ base of the Gritsenko--Cl{\'e}ry family.  Lorgat 2020
constructs the Borcherds lift
$(1/64)\cdot\Deltafive(2Z)=\Phi(z)$; the squared identity
$\Phiten=\Deltafive^{2}(\cdot/2)/64^{2}$ on the symmetric
branch aligns the dyon partition function with the
$\gBKM$-denominator character (Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998
\emph{Int.\ J.\ Math.} 9; Borcherds 1995 \texttt{alg-geom/9508032}).
Wave-14 $S_3$-triality refines the reading: the
$\Sthree$-orbit of the Igusa root $(n,l,m)$ extends to the Siegel
three-fold via the $\phi_{0,1}$ Fourier expansion, with the
reflection group $W^{(2)}(\Lambda^{2,1}_{II})$ acting on roots so
that the three Yangian parameters
$(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3)$ fix the three $\Deltafive$
real simple roots $(\delta_1,\delta_2,\delta_3)$ (Lorgat 2020
Lemma~1--3).  The holographic dictionary
\[
  d(Q)\;\leftrightarrow\;\chi(\gBKM;Q)\;\leftrightarrow\;
  \mathrm{tr}_{\gBKM^+}\!\bigl(q^{L_0}z^{J_0}\bigr)
\]
now reads through $\Sthree$-equivariance: triality permutes three
equivalent microstate decompositions of the same BTZ throat.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 4 --- Graviton finiteness versus holographic count}

Sen's quantum-entropy function (Sen 2008 \texttt{arXiv:0708.1270})
computes $\log d(Q)=S_{\BH}(Q)+$corrections with $S_{\BH}=
2\pi\sqrt{Q\cdot Q/2}$.  Because $\Deltafive$ is a paramodular
cusp form of weight $5$, $\log d(Q)/\sqrt{Q^2}$ is bounded,
so graviton microstates at fixed charge are \emph{finite}.  The
holographic count is
\[
  d(Q)\;=\;\sum_{\alpha\in\Delta_+}c_1(\alpha)\cdot[\text{root contribution}]
\]
with $c_1(\alpha)=f(nm,l)=O(\exp\sqrt{4nm-l^{2}})$; the
Hardy--Ramanujan bound matches the Bekenstein--Hawking scaling
(Shih--Strominger--Yin 2005 \texttt{hep-th/0506151}).  Wave-14
$\HH^2_{E_2}$ vanishing upgrades this from leading asymptotic to
all-orders: no non-perturbative bulk deformation corrects the
$\gBKM$ denominator expansion, hence no hidden graviton states
appear at finite charge.  Finiteness is the $E_2$-rigidity of the
chiral boundary algebra read on the bulk side.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 5 --- Sixth layer: lattice-polarised $\gL$}

Wave-15 M1--M5 build the rank-$1$ Nikulin family
$L=\langle 2d\rangle\subset\Lambda_{K3}$
(\texttt{wave15\_m1\_gL\_family\_rank1.tex}).  Each admissible $L$
of signature $(1,t)$ determines a period domain
$\Omega_L=\Omega_{T}/\mathrm{O}^{+}(T)$ of type IV, dimension
$19-t$, with $T=L^\perp$.  Dolgachev mirror pairs $(L,\check L)$
give a flow
\[
  L\;\mapsto\;\gL\;\mapsto\;\Phi_L\;\mapsto\;\kBKM(\Phi_L)=c_L(0)/2.
\]
The $L=\langle 2\rangle\oplus U^{\oplus 2}$ case is the Vol~III
$N=1$ instance $\gL=\gBKM$, $\Phi_L=\Deltafive$.  Wave-15 M2--M5
extend to $N\in\{1,\dots,8\}$: the eight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery
paramodular forms are \emph{eight sixth-layer AdS$_3$ throats},
each pinned by a weight identity
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_N)\;=\;c_N(0)/2\qquad(N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}),
\]
with the $N\in\{5,7,8\}$ branches conjectural per Lorgat 2020
Conjecture~1.  The sixth layer is therefore not a single bulk/boundary
pair but a \emph{family over the modular curve} parametrised by
$L$; the holographic tower lifts to a fibration of AdS$_3$ throats
over $\cM_L$, each with its own BPS algebra $\gL$ and cusp form
$\Phi_L$.

\paragraph{Unification.}
$\kch(K3\times E)=0$, $\kcat(K3\times E)=0$,
$\kBKM(\Deltafive)=5$, $\kfib=24$: four invariants of four
constructions of one CY$_3$ (Mukai lattice, Igusa weight, BKM
Borcherds weight, K3 fibre rank).  The six holographic layers
(Class-$\cS$, VOA, $\hCS$ bulk, CoHA/BPS, AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$, $\gL$
family) are six projections, not six $\Phi$-applications.  Wave-14's
three sharpenings (iterated DS for $\cV_{24}$, Miki $\Sthree$,
$\HH^2_{E_2}=0$) are the three rigidity statements that make the
tower consistent at all orders in $\hbar$.  The Lorgat 2020
Conjecture~1 extends the tower from the single $\Deltafive$
throat to the eight-cusp $\gL$ family; this is the Vol III frontier.
