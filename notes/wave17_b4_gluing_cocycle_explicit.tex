\section*{Gluing Cocycle for Positive Hall Charts}

\subsection*{Quiver-Affine Chart Datum}

A positive Hall chart on a CY$_3$ category is a tuple
\[
  \mathfrak U
  =
  (U,Q_U,W_U,\Gamma_U,A_U,\theta_U,o_U,C_U^{+})
\]
with the following data. The set \(U\) is a connected chamber in the
stability manifold of a fixed dg enhancement of \(\Db(\Coh X)\). The
heart \(\sT_U\) is equivalent to finite-dimensional modules over the
Jacobi algebra
\[
  A_U
  =
  \Jac(Q_U,W_U)
  =
  \bC Q_U\Big/
  \big\langle \partial_a W_U : a \in Q_U^1\big\rangle .
\]
The CY$_3$ object is the Ginzburg dg algebra
\[
  \Gamma_U=\Gamma(Q_U,W_U), \qquad H^0(\Gamma_U)=A_U,
\]
whose perfect derived category is \(3\)-Calabi-Yau under the usual
smoothness and finite-potential hypotheses. The Jacobi algebra is the
degree-zero heart algebra; the CY pairing belongs to \(\Gamma_U\).
The symbol \(o_U\) denotes orientation data for the critical
vanishing-cycle sheaf, and \(C_U^{+}\subset \bN^{Q_U^0}\) is the cone of
\(\theta_U\)-semistable dimension vectors used for the positive Hall
half.

The datum is part of the input. For compact CY$_3$ categories, a global
finite Jacobi presentation is additional structure. Local toric charts,
conifold charts, and chosen tilting chambers do supply this form.

\subsection*{Overlap Kernel}

Let \(\mathfrak U\) and \(\mathfrak V\) be two such charts. An overlap
kernel is a perfect \(\Gamma_V\)-\(\Gamma_U\) bimodule \(K_{UV}\) and a
perfect \(\Gamma_U\)-\(\Gamma_V\) bimodule \(K_{VU}\) equipped with
quasi-isomorphisms
\[
  K_{VU}\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\Gamma_V}K_{UV}\simeq \Gamma_U,\qquad
  K_{UV}\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\Gamma_U}K_{VU}\simeq \Gamma_V .
\]
It gives a dg Morita equivalence
\[
  \Psi_{UV}=
  K_{UV}\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\Gamma_U}(-):
  \Perf(\Gamma_U)\xrightarrow{\sim}\Perf(\Gamma_V).
\]
For a Keller-Yang mutation at a vertex \(k\) with the standard
loop-free and two-cycle-free hypotheses, \(K_{UV}\) is the mutation bimodule
constructed from the cone replacing the \(k\)-th projective. On a
source or sink chamber the induced dimension-vector map is
\[
  (\mu_k\mathbf d)_i=d_i \quad (i\ne k),\qquad
  (\mu_k\mathbf d)_k=\sum_{a:i\to k}d_i-d_k ,
\]
restricted to the cone where the right side is non-negative and the
wall-crossing functor carries \(\theta_U\)-semistable objects to
\(\theta_V\)-semistable objects. Outside that cone the mutation is
recorded by a wall-crossing correspondence; a representation-variety
map is absent from the datum.

\subsection*{Positive Hall Algebra}

For a dimension vector \(\mathbf d\), put
\[
  \sH_U(\mathbf d)
  =
  H^{\mathrm{BM},*}_{G_{\mathbf d}}
  \bigl(\Rep(Q_U,\mathbf d),
  \varphi_{\Tr(W_U)}\otimes o_U\bigr),
  \qquad
  G_{\mathbf d}=\prod_{i\in Q_U^0}GL(d_i).
\]
The Hall product is the Kontsevich-Soibelman convolution along the
stack of short exact sequences, with Thom-Sebastiani compatibility for
vanishing cycles. The positive half is
\[
  \sH_U^+
  =
  \bigoplus_{\mathbf d\in C_U^+}\sH_U(\mathbf d).
\]
In toric affine type this algebra is denoted \(\Yp_U\). For the
Jordan quiver of \(\bC^3\),
\[
  \CoHA(\bC^3)
  =
  \Yp(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1),
\]
the positive half of the affine Yangian.

Assume the overlap kernel is realised by a correspondence of critical
stacks
\[
  \sM_U(\mathbf d)
  \xleftarrow{\ p_{UV,\mathbf d}\ }
  Z_{UV,\mathbf d}
  \xrightarrow{\ q_{UV,\mathbf d}\ }
  \sM_V(\mu_{UV}\mathbf d)
\]
compatible with orientation data and with the extension
correspondences. Define
\[
  \phi^+_{UV,\mathbf d}
  =
  (q_{UV,\mathbf d})_!\,p_{UV,\mathbf d}^{!}:
  \sH_U(\mathbf d)\longrightarrow
  \sH_V(\mu_{UV}\mathbf d),
\]
and set
\[
  \phi^+_{UV}
  =
  \bigoplus_{\mathbf d\in C_U^+}\phi^+_{UV,\mathbf d}:
  \sH_U^+\longrightarrow\sH_V^+ .
\]
The map is multiplicative when the diagram obtained by adding one
extension in the source and then transporting equals, as an oriented
derived correspondence, the diagram obtained by transporting both
summands and then forming the extension in the target. This supplies the
finite positive-Hall gluing boundary. The Hall pairing, Drinfeld double,
centre, completion, and vertex evaluation are later layers.

\subsection*{Homotopy-Coherent Cocycle}

Choose dg chain models \(\sH_{U,dg}^+\) and chain maps
\[
  \widetilde\phi^+_{UV}:\sH_{U,dg}^+\longrightarrow\sH_{V,dg}^+
\]
lifting \(\phi^+_{UV}\). On a triple overlap \(U\cap V\cap W\), let
\[
  D_{UVW}
  =
  \widetilde\phi^+_{VW}\widetilde\phi^+_{UV}
  -
  \widetilde\phi^+_{UW}.
\]
This is a closed degree-zero map. A homotopy \(h_{UVW}\) of degree
\(-1\) is a solution of
\[
  d\,h_{UVW}+h_{UVW}\,d=D_{UVW}.
\]
Equivalently, if
\[
  \eta_{UVW}:
  K_{VW}\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\Gamma_V}K_{UV}
  \Longrightarrow K_{UW}
\]
is a degree \(-1\) homotopy between bimodule kernels, the induced Hall
homotopy is
\[
  h_{UVW,\mathbf d}
  =
  (q_{UVW,\mathbf d})_!\,
  \eta_{UVW,\mathbf d}\,
  p_{UVW,\mathbf d}^{!},
\]
where \(Z_{UVW,\mathbf d}\) is the critical correspondence carrying the
universal object through the two kernel compositions. The displayed
identity follows from the differential identity for \(\eta_{UVW}\) and
from functoriality of shriek pullback and proper pushforward on the
oriented correspondence.

On quadruple overlaps the boundary of the \(h_{UVW}\) family is the
degree \(-1\) map
\[
  \widetilde\phi^+_{WX}h_{UVW}
  -h_{UVX}
  +h_{UWX}
  -h_{VWX}\widetilde\phi^+_{UV}.
\]
Vanishing of its cohomology class is the next descent condition. Thus
the gluing object is a homotopy-coherent \v{C}ech cocycle valued in
positive Hall dg algebras. Cyclic \(A_\infty\) formality supplies the
kernel homotopies; the cocycle closes precisely when the displayed
obstruction classes vanish.

\subsection*{Operadic and Representation-Theoretic Boundary}

For \(d=3\), the native CY-to-chiral output
\[
  \Phi_3^{(\Sigma_2,C)}(\mathcal C)
\]
is \(\Eone\)-chiral on the witnessed filtered locus. The positive Hall
algebras above are associative \(\Eone\) objects. Braiding and
\(\Etwo\)-structure live on
\[
  \cZ\bigl(\Rep^{\Eone}(\sH_U^+)\bigr)
  \quad\text{or}\quad
  D(\sH_U^+)
\]
after the negative half, Cartan part, Hopf pairing, radical quotient,
and completion have been supplied.

The allowed chain of structures on a smooth toric \(\bC^3\) chart is
\[
  \CoHA(\bC^3)
  =
  \Yp(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
  \longrightarrow
  D(\Yp)
  =
  Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
  \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{ev}_{\lambda}\ }
  \End\bigl(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}[\lambda]\text{-}\mathrm{vac}\bigr).
\]
The \(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}\) object is reached at the evaluation
layer. The Maulik-Okounkov \(R\)-matrix comparison starts after a common
stable-envelope module, a centre or completed double, and the relevant
Fock or \(K\)-theoretic evaluation representation have been fixed. The
maps \(\phi^+_{UV}\) constructed above are Hall transition maps on the
positive half.

\subsection*{Toric Sanity Check}

For the one-vertex three-loop quiver
\[
  Q_{\mathrm{Jor}}:\quad x,y,z,
  \qquad
  W=x[y,z],
\]
one has
\[
  A=\bC\langle x,y,z\rangle/
  ([x,y],[y,z],[z,x])=\bC[x,y,z],
  \qquad
  \CoHA(Q_{\mathrm{Jor}},W)=\Yp(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1).
\]
The vertex has loops, so the Derksen-Weyman-Zelevinsky vertex-mutation
hypotheses are absent. The correct local check is the \(S_3\)-symmetry
of the equivariant parameters
\((\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3)\) with
\(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+\epsilon_3=0\), acting on the shuffle
presentation of \(\Yp\). Reversing the orientation datum sends the
vanishing-cycle complex to its parity-twisted dual and changes all
equivariant weights by the common sign. It is an orientation change; the
DWZ mutation theorem requires an input vertex, so it stays outside this
check.

The scalar
\[
  \kch(\mathrm{loc}\,\mathbb P^2)=3/2
\]
belongs to the \(dP_0\) McKay quiver for
\(\mathrm{Tot}(\omega_{\mathbb P^2})\). The \(\bC^3\) check uses the
identity \(\CoHA(\bC^3)=\Yp(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)\); the
local-\(\mathbb P^2\) \(\kch\) value is absent from this check.

\subsection*{Result}

The gluing cocycle is the homotopy-coherent system
\[
  \bigl(\sH_U^+,\phi^+_{UV},h_{UVW},h^{(2)}_{UVWX},\ldots\bigr)
\]
on the positive Hall layer. It becomes a global compact CoHA only after
vanishing-cycle realisation, compact-support functoriality, orientation
compatibility, and completion have been supplied. It becomes a Yangian
or \(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}\)-type object only after the completed double
or centre and the representation-theoretic evaluation layer have been
supplied.
