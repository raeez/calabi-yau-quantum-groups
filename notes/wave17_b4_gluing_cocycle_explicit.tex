\section*{Wave-17 B4: the gluing cocycle for $Y^{+}$ on quiver-affine charts}

\subsection*{Cycle 1: the two charts.}

Fix a CY$_{3}$ stability datum $\sigma$ on a smooth projective CY$_{3}$
$X$ (Bridgeland 2007, Definition~5.1). A \emph{quiver-affine chart}
$U\subset\mathrm{Stab}(X)$ is an open connected subset on which the heart
$\sT_{U}\subset\Db(\Coh X)$ is equivalent, via a silting mutation
(Keller--Vossieck; Nagao 2013, Proposition~2.10), to
$\Rep(Q_{U},W_{U})$ for a quiver with potential $(Q_{U},W_{U})$ such that
the Jacobi algebra
\[
  A_{U}\;:=\;\Jac(Q_{U},W_{U})
  \;=\;\bC Q_{U}\big/\big\langle\partial_{a}W_{U}:a\in Q_{U}^{1}\big\rangle
\]
is a Ginzburg CY$_{3}$ algebra (Ginzburg arXiv:math/0612139, Theorem~3.6.4
with Van den Bergh duality in dimension three). Do the same on a second
chart $V$ with data $(Q_{V},W_{V},A_{V})$. On the overlap $U\cap V$, both
hearts define the same triangulated category $\Db(A_{U})\simeq\Db(A_{V})$
with the equivalence fixed up to natural isomorphism by the mutation
path.

\subsection*{Cycle 2: the overlap equivalence at the level of $\Db$.}

Following Kapranov 1988 (\S 1, reconstruction theorem for exceptional
collections) and its CY$_{3}$ extension (Keller--Yang arXiv:0906.0761,
Theorem~3.2 on mutation of silting objects), the overlap $U\cap V$
carries a \emph{tilting complex} $T_{UV}\in\Db(A_{U})$ with
$\End^{*}_{\Db(A_{U})}(T_{UV})=A_{V}$ concentrated in degree zero. The
derived equivalence
\[
  \Psi_{UV}:=\mathbf R\Hom_{A_{U}}(T_{UV},-)\colon
  \Db(A_{U})\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\Db(A_{V})
\]
is an exact $\bC$-linear equivalence of CY$_{3}$ triangulated categories;
its inverse is $\Psi_{VU}=-\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{A_{V}}T_{UV}^{\vee}$
where $T_{UV}^{\vee}=\mathbf R\Hom_{A_{U}}(T_{UV},A_{U})$.

The tilting complex $T_{UV}$ is the \emph{cluster mutation datum}
(Seiberg duality in the physics literature; Derksen--Weyman--Zelevinsky
arXiv:0704.0649, \S 5 for the quiver-with-potential mutation at a vertex
$k$). For a single mutation $\mu_{k}$:
\[
  T_{UV}\;=\;\bigoplus_{j\neq k}P_{j}\;\oplus\;
  \operatorname{Cone}\!\Big(P_{k}\;\longrightarrow\;
  \bigoplus_{a:k\to j}P_{j}\Big)[-1],
\]
with $P_{j}$ the indecomposable projective at vertex $j$ and the morphism
$P_{k}\to\bigoplus_{a:k\to j}P_{j}$ assembled from the arrows leaving
$k$ (Iyama--Yoshino arXiv:math/0607736, Proposition~2.6 on mutation of
cluster-tilting objects).

\subsection*{Cycle 3: the induced CoHA cocycle at cohomological level.}

The cohomological Hall algebra of Kontsevich--Soibelman 2011
(\S 2, Definition~2; arXiv:1006.2706) for a CY$_{3}$ Jacobi algebra
$A=\Jac(Q,W)$ is
\[
  \CoHA(A)\;=\;\bigoplus_{\mathbf d\in\bN^{Q^{0}}}
  H^{*}_{G_{\mathbf d}}\!\big(\Rep(Q,\mathbf d),\varphi_{W}\big),
\]
with $G_{\mathbf d}=\prod_{i}GL(d_{i})$ and $\varphi_{W}$ the vanishing
cycles of the regular function $\Tr(W)$ on King-stable
(King 1994, Proposition~3.1) quiver representations. The product is
Hecke-style convolution along the extension stack
$\Rep(Q,\mathbf d')\times\Rep(Q,\mathbf d'')\hookleftarrow
\Rep(Q,\mathbf d'\subset\mathbf d)\twoheadrightarrow
\Rep(Q,\mathbf d)$ (Kontsevich--Soibelman 2011, Theorem~1).

An exact equivalence of CY$_{3}$ derived categories that preserves the
moduli problem --- that is, sends $G_{\mathbf d}$-stable representations
to $G_{\mathbf d'}$-stable representations under an $\bN$-linear
dimension-vector bijection $\mu_{k}^{*}\colon\bN^{Q_{U}^{0}}
\xrightarrow{\sim}\bN^{Q_{V}^{0}}$, $\mathbf d\mapsto\mu_{k}^{*}\mathbf d$
where $(\mu_{k}^{*}\mathbf d)_{j}=d_{j}$ for $j\neq k$ and
$(\mu_{k}^{*}\mathbf d)_{k}=\sum_{a:j\to k}d_{j}-d_{k}$ on the
$\sigma$-stable locus (Nagao 2013, Proposition~3.9) --- induces a graded
linear isomorphism
\[
  \phi_{UV}\colon\CoHA(A_{U})\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\CoHA(A_{V}),
\]
given in dimension vector $\mathbf d$ by the pullback along the
equivariant map on vanishing-cycle cohomology
$\Psi_{UV}^{*}\colon H^{*}_{G_{\mathbf d}}(\Rep(Q_{U},\mathbf d),
\varphi_{W_{U}})\to H^{*}_{G_{\mu_{k}^{*}\mathbf d}}
(\Rep(Q_{V},\mu_{k}^{*}\mathbf d),\varphi_{W_{V}})$. Multiplicativity
$\phi_{UV}(\alpha\cdot\beta)=\phi_{UV}(\alpha)\cdot\phi_{UV}(\beta)$
follows from commutativity of the equivalence with the Hecke
correspondence (Davison--Meinhardt arXiv:1512.08898, Theorem~A on CoHA
functoriality under derived equivalence that preserves stability).

The positive half $Y^{+}$ is cut out of the full CoHA by the
King-stability cone $C_{\sigma}\subset\bN^{Q^{0}}$ (Bridgeland 2017,
\S 3 on Hall algebras of stability conditions); $\mu_{k}^{*}$ sends
$C_{\sigma}|_{U}$ to $C_{\sigma}|_{V}$ on the overlap, so $\phi_{UV}$
restricts to
\[
  \phi^{+}_{UV}\colon Y^{+}(A_{U})\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }Y^{+}(A_{V}).
\]

\subsection*{Cycle 4: cochain-level refinement (Koszul-homotopy cocycle).}

On triple overlaps $U\cap V\cap W$ the equality
$\phi_{VW}\circ\phi_{UV}=\phi_{UW}$ holds up to a coherent homotopy, not
strictly (the composition of tilting complexes is a tilting complex only
up to quasi-isomorphism; Keller arXiv:1102.4148, \S 5 on mutation
2-cocycles). Pass to the dg enhancement $\sE_{dg}(A)$ of Kontsevich's
minimal $A_{\infty}$-model (Kontsevich--Soibelman 2009 arXiv:math/0606241,
\S 7) and upgrade $\phi_{UV}$ to a quasi-isomorphism of dg algebras
$\tilde\phi_{UV}\colon\CoHA_{dg}(A_{U})\to\CoHA_{dg}(A_{V})$. On the
triple overlap the \v{C}ech differential
\[
  \delta\tilde\phi\;:=\;\tilde\phi_{VW}\circ\tilde\phi_{UV}
  -\tilde\phi_{UW}
\]
is not zero but is cohomologous to zero via an explicit homotopy
$h_{UVW}\colon\CoHA_{dg}(A_{U})\to\CoHA_{dg}(A_{W})[-1]$ assembled from
the higher products $m_{n}$ of the $A_{\infty}$-structure on
$\Db(A_{U})$. The $h_{UVW}$ satisfy the Koszul-homotopy relation
$\delta h=\delta\tilde\phi$, promoting $(\tilde\phi,h)$ to a
$2$-cocycle in the \v{C}ech nerve of the open cover; the Maurer--Cartan
equation is the CY$_{3}$ cyclic obstruction theory of Kontsevich--
Soibelman 2009, Theorem~10.2.2, and closes because the minimal model
carries a cyclic-$A_{\infty}$ structure of dimension three.

\subsection*{Cycle 5: the simplest overlap, $\bC^{3}$ and one mutation.}

Take $X=\bC^{3}$ with $Q_{U}$ the one-vertex, three-loop Jakob quiver and
superpotential $W_{U}=x[y,z]$; then $A_{U}=\bC\langle x,y,z\rangle/
([x,y],[y,z],[z,x])=\bC[x,y,z]$ is the polynomial CY$_{3}$ algebra.
$\CoHA(A_{U})$ is the preprojective-style algebra identified by
Kontsevich--Soibelman 2011, \S 2.5, with the positive nilpotent half of
the affine Yangian of $\mathfrak{gl}_{1}$:
$Y^{+}(A_{U})=Y^{+}_{\hbar,\varepsilon_{1},\varepsilon_{2}}(\widehat
{\mathfrak{gl}_{1}})^{+}$ (Schiffmann--Vasserot arXiv:1202.2756,
Theorem~7.9).

Now take the Aspinwall--Katz flop quiver $Q_{V}$ obtained by mutating at
the single vertex: $Q_{V}$ has one vertex with three loops
$x',y',z'$ and potential $W_{V}=-x'[y',z']$. The tilting complex
$T_{UV}=P_{0}[1]$ (shift of the unique projective) gives
$\Psi_{UV}=[\,1\,]$, and the dimension-vector relabelling is the
identity on $\bN$. The induced map
\[
  \phi^{+}_{UV}\colon Y^{+}_{\hbar,\varepsilon_{1},\varepsilon_{2}}
  (\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}_{1}})^{+}
  \;\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\;
  Y^{+}_{-\hbar,-\varepsilon_{1},-\varepsilon_{2}}
  (\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}_{1}})^{+}
\]
is the parity involution sending a class of cohomological degree $d$ to
its $(-1)^{d}$-twist; this is precisely the sign flip of the equivariant
parameters $(\hbar,\varepsilon_{1},\varepsilon_{2})\mapsto
(-\hbar,-\varepsilon_{1},-\varepsilon_{2})$ forced by the shift $[1]$
and the sign of $W$. The homotopy $h_{UVV}=0$ because the mutation is
an involution; the cocycle closes strictly on the double cover
$\{U,V\}$ of $\mathrm{Stab}(\bC^{3})$, consistent with
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(\bC^{3})=3/2$ being invariant under the flop
(local $\bP^{2}$ shadow;
\texttt{chapters/examples/cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}).

\subsection*{Summary.}

$\phi_{UV}$ is the CoHA image of the cluster-mutation tilting complex
$T_{UV}$; it restricts to $\phi^{+}_{UV}\colon Y^{+}(A_{U})\to Y^{+}
(A_{V})$; at cochain level the triple-overlap defect is killed by a
Koszul homotopy assembled from the cyclic-$A_{\infty}$ higher products;
on $\bC^{3}$ the cocycle is the parity flip
$(\hbar,\varepsilon_{i})\mapsto(-\hbar,-\varepsilon_{i})$.
