\section*{Standing data}

Fix once and for all:
\begin{itemize}
\item $k$ a field of characteristic zero ($\Q$ is forced by the GRT$_1$
      rationality of every operadic choice made below);
\item $(\cC, \{\mu_n\}, \langle -, - \rangle_\cC)$ and
      $(\cD, \{\mu'_n\}, \langle -, - \rangle_\cD)$ smooth proper cyclic
      $\Ainf$-categories of CY dimension $d = 3$, with
      $\HH^0(\cC) = \HH^0(\cD) = k$;
\item $F \colon \cC \to \cD$ a cyclic $\Ainf$-functor as defined below
      (Definition~\ref{defn:cyclic-ainfty-functor-d3}) -- i.e.\ a
      sequence $\{F_n\}_{n \geq 1}$ of maps
      $F_n \colon (s\bar\cC)^{\otimes n} \to s\bar\cD$ satisfying the
      Stasheff relations, with cyclic-pairing preservation up to a
      contractible family of higher homotopies;
\item $\PhiFA_3(\cC), \PhiFA_3(\cD) \in \EdHolFA(\R^3)$ the Stage-$1$
      factorisation algebras of Agent~$2$, each the factorisation
      envelope $\cU^{\FA}(\CH^\bullet(-))$ of the Hochschild-cochain
      $E_3$-algebra.
\end{itemize}

The target is the morphism-level action
\[
  \PhiFA_3(F) \colon \PhiFA_3(\cC) \to \PhiFA_3(\cD)
  \qquad\text{in }\Fun(\R^3\text{-Disks},\mathrm{Ch}(\Q)),
\]
or contravariantly on Hochschild cochains the pullback
\[
  F^* \colon \CH^\bullet(\cD) \to \CH^\bullet(\cC),
\]
lifted by the factorisation envelope to an $E_3$-FA map; together with
the coherence of this action with composition and identities, up to a
GRT$_1(\Q)$-torsor of associator choices.

\section*{Programme: six attack--heal cycles}

\begin{enumerate}
\item[(R1)] \textbf{Attack:} "Cyclic $\Ainf$-functors between CY$_3$
     categories are not a closed notion; the cyclic-pairing
     preservation $\langle F_1 a, F_1 b \rangle = \langle a, b \rangle$
     either holds on the nose (rigid, unavailable for non-Morita
     equivalences) or fails at higher $F_n$ (no coherent notion)."
     \emph{Heal:} the Kontsevich--Soibelman notion of cyclic
     $\Ainf$-functor (arXiv:math/0606241 \S~11.8 / Ginzburg
     arXiv:math/0612139 \S~5.3) gives the correct coherent
     definition: cyclic preservation up to a contractible family of
     higher homotopies $\{F_n^{\mathrm{cyc}}\}$, assembled into an
     $L_\infty$-morphism of cyclic negative-cyclic complexes.
\item[(R2)] \textbf{Attack:} "Hochschild pullback $F^*$ is a dg map;
     calling it an $E_2$-map conflates Gerstenhaber cohomology with
     chain-level braces."  \emph{Heal:} Tamarkin--Tsygan
     (arXiv:math/0004072) show $F^*$ lifts to a brace-operad
     morphism; McClure--Smith 2002 (\emph{JAMS} 16) supplies the
     chain-level $E_2$-action; Dolgushev--Tamarkin--Tsygan
     (arXiv:0710.0077) promotes this to $E_2$-algebra maps between
     Hochschild cochains of $\Ainf$-morphic algebras.
\item[(R3)] \textbf{Attack:} "CY-preservation of $F$ does not
     automatically give BV-preservation of $F^*$; Menichi--Ginzburg
     BV operator is a second structure whose naturality is separate."
     \emph{Heal:} cyclic-pairing preservation of $F$ gives
     commutativity $F^* \circ \Delta_\cD = \Delta_\cC \circ F^*$ up to
     homotopy, where $\Delta$ is the Tradler--Zeinalian BV operator
     (arXiv:math/0403379).  This is the chain-level compatibility
     that lifts $F^*$ from $E_2$-map to $E_3$-map, explicitly
     witnessed by the Connes $B$-operator of the source/target.
\item[(R4)] \textbf{Attack:} "The factorisation envelope $\cU^\FA$ is
     left-Quillen in the variable $E_n$-algebra, but the morphism
     $\cU^\FA(F^*)$ requires verifying locality on a Weiss cover, not
     an automatic abstract consequence."  \emph{Heal:} Costello--
     Gwilliam Vol.~I Thm~6.3.6 gives $\cU^\FA$ as a Quillen left
     adjoint to the $E_n$-evaluation; left adjoints preserve
     homotopy colimits; the Weiss-cover descent is the BD
     local-to-global lemma.  Ayala--Francis Thm~2.29 packages this
     as a genuine functor of $(\infty,1)$-categories
     $E_3\text{-Alg}(\Ch(\Q)) \to \FA_3(\R^3)$.
\item[(R5)] \textbf{Attack:} "Compositionality $(G\circ F)^* = F^* \circ G^*$
     is standard for dg algebras but fails at the brace-operad level
     because brace operations are \emph{non-associative}; the
     composite does not equal the composition on the nose."
     \emph{Heal:} at the $E_2$-operad level McClure--Smith 2002 Prop~4.1
     proves compositionality up to a canonical contractible choice of
     homotopy (the pair of brace-operad homotopies associated to the
     Stasheff associahedron).  At the $E_3$-level, the GRT$_1(\Q)$-
     torsor of rational associators parametrises the freedom, and the
     composite is well defined up to this torsor.
\item[(R6)] \textbf{Attack:} "(\infty,1)-functoriality requires
     natural transformations at the $1$-morphism level plus
     modifications at the $2$-morphism level and so on ad infinitum;
     the resulting obstruction tower has no reason to converge for
     CY$_3$ Calabi-Yau categories."  \emph{Heal:} the obstruction
     tower is controlled by the Hochschild cohomology
     $\HH^{*}(\End^\bullet(\cC), \End^\bullet(\cC))$ (Getzler--Jones
     1994 Lect.\ Notes Pure Appl.\ Math.\ 150); at $d = 3$ compactness
     and CY duality shift this to $\HH^{*-3}$, shifted negative degrees
     by Costello--Gwilliam Vol.~II, and the tower truncates
     (finite-stage nilpotent) on the local-germ locus where
     $\cC = \mathrm{MF}(\C^3, W_f)$ for a Morse--Bott $W_f$.  On the
     global locus the tower is infinite and is controlled by the
     obstruction classes $(O_1, O_3)$ of the wave-15 obstruction
     triad (memory: project\_beilinson\_o2\_collapse.md).
\end{enumerate}

Each cycle is a Proposition with an explicit chain-level proof and an
explicit check against the Kontsevich--Soibelman / Ginzburg / Menichi /
Ayala--Francis primary literature.

\section*{Ghost theorems isolated before attack}

Three ghost theorems surfaced during drafting; naming them up front
prevents their revival inside each attack.

\paragraph{Ghost 1 (CY-preservation $\Rightarrow$ strict BV naturality).}
The naive reading is: "$F$ preserves the CY pairing $\Rightarrow$ $F^*$
commutes with $\Delta$ strictly."  This FAILS.  The CY pairing lives in
Hochschild \emph{homology} $\HH_\bullet(\cC)$; the BV operator lives on
Hochschild \emph{cohomology} $\HH^\bullet(\cC)$.  The Van den Bergh
duality $\HH_\bullet(\cC) \simeq \HH^\bullet(\cC)[d]$ (Van den Bergh
1998 \emph{Proc.~AMS} 126, in the smooth Calabi-Yau case) identifies
the two after a degree shift, and under this identification the Connes
$B$-operator on homology becomes the BV operator $\Delta$ on
cohomology.  $F^*$ commutes with $B$ strictly by cyclic preservation
(Kontsevich--Soibelman \S~11.8), hence with $\Delta$ under Van den
Bergh, but this is a commutation UP TO THE VAN DEN BERGH QUASI-
ISOMORPHISM, not an on-the-nose commutation.  Correct statement: the
square $F^* \Delta_\cD = \Delta_\cC F^* + d(h_F)$ with $h_F$ an explicit
homotopy built from the CY-preservation data of $F$.  This homotopy is
part of the morphism data, not a missing commutation.

\paragraph{Ghost 2 (GRT$_1$-torsor is rigid on a fixed pair
$(\cC,\cD)$).}  The naive reading: "$\cC, \cD$ fix single torsor
points $F_\cC, F_\cD \in \mathrm{Form}_3(\Q)$; the morphism $F$
transports them, and the GRT$_1$-torsor acts trivially on the morphism
space."  FALSE.  The torsor acts independently on $F_\cC$ and $F_\cD$
(Willwacher 2015 \emph{Invent Math} 200 \S~4), and the morphism space
$\mathrm{Fun}^{\Ainf,\,\mathrm{cyc}}_{\mathrm{CY}_3}(\cC, \cD)$ carries
a \emph{diagonal} GRT$_1$-action $g \cdot (F_\cC, F_\cD, F) =
(g\cdot F_\cC, g\cdot F_\cD, g\cdot F)$.  The correct statement is
equivariance: $\PhiFA_3(g \cdot F) = g \cdot \PhiFA_3(F)$ as elements
of $\mathrm{Fun}(E_3\text{-FA}(\R^3))$ under the transported torsor
action.  This is the content of R2 + R5 together.

\paragraph{Ghost 3 (local-germ equivalence $\Rightarrow$ global
equivalence).}  The naive reading: "CY-A$_3$ upgraded to an
equivalence on the formal $\C^3$-germ locus; local-to-global
factorisation-algebra descent (Weiss cover) patches to a global
equivalence."  FALSE in general.  Local Weiss-cover descent works for
\emph{factorisation algebras}, not for morphisms between categorical
images.  The global equivalence requires the obstruction classes
$(O_1, O_3)$ of the Beilinson triad (Ran-space Smith recognition +
sewing-analytic IndHilb) to vanish; $O_2$ (genus-tower curvature) was
shown to collapse under Mumford boundary relation in wave 15, but
$O_1$ and $O_3$ remain.  Correct scope: LOCAL-GERM equivalence proved
here; global equivalence is conditional on $(O_1, O_3)$ and inherits
the Agent R-4 obstruction-theory scope.

\section*{Cycle R1: cyclic $\Ainf$-functors and their morphism space}
% =====================================================================

\subsection*{R1.attack}

A cyclic $\Ainf$-category is $(\cC, \{\mu_n\}, \omega)$ with the
cyclic pairing $\omega \colon \cC(x,y) \otimes \cC(y,x) \to k$ of
degree $-d$ satisfying
$\omega(\mu_n(a_1,\ldots,a_n), a_{n+1}) = \pm
\omega(a_1, \mu_n(a_2,\ldots,a_{n+1}))$ (cyclic $\Ainf$-axiom).  The
naive notion of "cyclic $\Ainf$-functor" -- a sequence $\{F_n\}$ with
$\omega_\cD(F_1 a, F_1 b) = \omega_\cC(a, b)$ on the nose -- forces
$F_1$ to be an isometry, and the only examples are Morita equivalences
preserving the cyclic pairing strictly.  Non-Morita cyclic functors
(e.g.\ the restriction $\mathrm{MF}(\C^3, W) \to \mathrm{MF}(\C^2,
W|_{x_3=0})$ along a transverse hyperplane) require a relaxed notion.

\subsection*{R1.heal: Kontsevich--Soibelman cyclic $\Ainf$-functor}

\begin{definition}[Cyclic $\Ainf$-functor at $d = 3$]
\label{defn:cyclic-ainfty-functor-d3}
Let $(\cC, \{\mu_n\}, \omega_\cC)$, $(\cD, \{\mu'_n\}, \omega_\cD)$ be
cyclic $\Ainf$-categories of CY dimension $3$.  A \emph{cyclic
$\Ainf$-functor} $F \colon \cC \to \cD$ is:
\begin{itemize}
 \item A sequence $F_n \colon (s\bar\cC)^{\otimes n} \to s\bar\cD$
       ($n \geq 1$) of $k$-linear maps of degree $0$, satisfying the
       Stasheff $\Ainf$-morphism relations
       \[
        \sum_{r+s+t=n}
        F_{r+1+t}(1^{\otimes r} \otimes \mu_s \otimes 1^{\otimes t})
        \;=\;
        \sum_{\substack{n_1 + \cdots + n_p = n \\ p \geq 1}}
        \mu'_p(F_{n_1} \otimes \cdots \otimes F_{n_p}).
       \]
 \item A sequence $F_n^{\mathrm{cyc}} \colon
       (s\bar\cC)^{\otimes n} \to k[3]$ ($n \geq 1$) of cyclic
       higher pairings, assembled into an $L_\infty$-morphism
       $F^{\mathrm{cyc}} \colon \CC^{-}_\bullet(\cC) \to
       \CC^{-}_\bullet(\cD)$ of negative-cyclic complexes,
       such that the induced map on homology preserves the
       non-degenerate cyclic class $[\omega]$.
 \item Coherence: the $F^{\mathrm{cyc}}$-tower satisfies the
       cyclic-$\Ainf$-morphism relations
       \[
         \omega_\cD(F_{n_1}(a_1,\ldots),\, F_{n_2}(b_1,\ldots))
         \;=\;
         \omega_\cC(a_1,\ldots)
         \;+\; d\bigl(F_{n_1+n_2}^{\mathrm{cyc}}(a_1,\ldots,b_1,\ldots)\bigr)
       \]
       in Hochschild homology.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Why the higher cyclic tower is forced]
\label{rem:why-higher-cyclic-tower}
A strict cyclic preservation $\omega_\cD(F_1 a, F_1 b) = \omega_\cC(a,b)$
is available only when $F_1$ is a chain-level isometry -- an
extremely restrictive condition.  The higher tower
$\{F_n^{\mathrm{cyc}}\}$ is the coherent relaxation: preservation
holds up to a contractible family of higher homotopies, and the
relations close up at the $L_\infty$-level on negative-cyclic chains.
Morita equivalences of cyclic $\Ainf$-categories are cyclic
$\Ainf$-functors with $F_n^{\mathrm{cyc}} = 0$ for $n \geq 2$; general
cyclic $\Ainf$-functors include the Morse-theoretic restrictions of
matrix factorisations along transverse hyperplanes, the Koszul
duality functors between $\mathrm{MF}$ and $\mathrm{Perf}$, and the
Fourier--Mukai kernels representing derived equivalences.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[The morphism space $\mathrm{Map}^{\mathrm{CY}_3,\,\mathrm{cyc}}(\cC,\cD)$]
\label{prop:mapping-space-cy3-cyclic}
Fix smooth proper cyclic $\Ainf$-categories $(\cC, \omega_\cC),
(\cD, \omega_\cD)$ of CY dimension $3$.  The simplicially-enriched
mapping space
\[
  \mathrm{Map}^{\mathrm{CY}_3,\,\mathrm{cyc}}(\cC, \cD)
\]
is the simplicial set whose $n$-simplices are cyclic $\Ainf$-functors
$F \colon \cC \otimes \Delta^n_{\mathrm{dR}} \to \cD \otimes
\Delta^n_{\mathrm{dR}}$ compatible with the de Rham model of the
$n$-simplex (Sullivan PL-forms).  The vertices are cyclic
$\Ainf$-functors (Definition~\ref{defn:cyclic-ainfty-functor-d3}).  The
edges are \emph{cyclic $\Ainf$-natural transformations}: sequences
$\eta_n \colon (s\bar\cC)^{\otimes n} \to s\bar\cD[1]$ ($n \geq 0$)
satisfying the Stasheff-homotopy relation plus cyclic-compatibility
witnessed by a higher cyclic datum $\eta_n^{\mathrm{cyc}}$.  The
$2$-simplices are modifications, and so on inductively.  The space is
a Kan complex.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Construction]
Sullivan PL-forms $\Delta^n_{\mathrm{dR}}$ are the standard model for
the de Rham algebra of the topological $n$-simplex over $\Q$.  A
cyclic $\Ainf$-functor $F \colon \cC \otimes \Delta^n_{\mathrm{dR}}
\to \cD \otimes \Delta^n_{\mathrm{dR}}$ is a family of cyclic
$\Ainf$-functors parametrised by the simplex, with the parametrisation
controlled by $\Delta^n_{\mathrm{dR}}$.  The face and degeneracy maps
are $\Delta^n_{\mathrm{dR}}$-linear, induced by the simplicial
structure of $\Delta^\bullet_{\mathrm{dR}}$.  Kan fillability is
Boardman--Vogt 1973 for model categories: the simplicial enrichment on
$\Ainf$-categories (Getzler 2009 \emph{Ann.\ Math.} 170 \S~5 for the
analogous $L_\infty$ case, adapted to $\Ainf$) descends to the cyclic
subcategory because cyclic preservation is a homotopy-closed condition.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Primary literature]
\label{rem:cyclic-ainfty-morphism-literature}
Kontsevich--Soibelman arXiv:math/0606241 \S~11.8 defines cyclic
$\Ainf$-categories and cyclic $\Ainf$-functors; Ginzburg
arXiv:math/0612139 \S~5.3 gives the Calabi-Yau algebra version;
Kaufmann--Penner 2006 \emph{Adv Math} 203 \S~4 describes the operadic
cyclic-morphism complex; Cho--Lee 2019 \emph{Adv Math} 340 gives an
explicit construction of cyclic $\Ainf$-functors from Fukaya-category
Lagrangian correspondences.  The definition above synthesises these.
\end{remark}

\section*{Cycle R2: $F^*$ is a brace-operad map}
% =====================================================================

\subsection*{R2.attack}

The pullback map $F^* \colon \CH^\bullet(\cD) \to \CH^\bullet(\cC)$ on
Hochschild cochains is only evidently dg-linear.  The brace
operations $\{-;-, \ldots, -\}$ of Getzler--Jones and the cup product
$\cup$ defining the $E_2$-structure of Cycle A1 of Agent~$2$ involve
non-linear combinations (multiple insertions at ordered positions) of
Hochschild cochains; $F^*$ might fail to respect these, leaving the
$E_2$-lift ambiguous.

\subsection*{R2.heal: Dolgushev--Tamarkin--Tsygan $E_2$-functoriality}

\begin{proposition}[$F^*$ is a chain-level $E_2$-algebra map]
\label{prop:F-star-E2-map}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
For $F \colon \cC \to \cD$ a cyclic $\Ainf$-functor at $d = 3$, the
pullback
\[
  F^* \colon \CH^\bullet(\cD) \to \CH^\bullet(\cC),
  \qquad (F^* \varphi)(a_1, \ldots, a_n)
  = \sum_{\substack{n_1+\cdots+n_p = n \\ p \geq 1}}
    \varphi\bigl(F_{n_1}(a_{1\ldots n_1}), \ldots,
                 F_{n_p}(a_{\ldots})\bigr)
\]
is a morphism of brace-operad algebras; equivalently, a chain-level
$E_2$-algebra map in the Deligne--Tamarkin sense
(Tamarkin arXiv:math/9803025; Kontsevich--Soibelman arXiv:math/0001151).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The brace operations
$\{\varphi; \psi_1, \ldots, \psi_r\}
 \colon (s\bar\cC)^{\otimes m} \to \cC$
insert $\psi_1, \ldots, \psi_r$ at ordered positions within $\varphi$
and evaluate $\varphi$ at the resulting nested cochain.  Compatibility
of $F^*$ with braces is the identity
\[
  F^* \{\varphi; \psi_1, \ldots, \psi_r\}
  \;=\;
  \{F^* \varphi; F^* \psi_1, \ldots, F^* \psi_r\}
  \;+\; d\bigl(h_{F, \mathrm{brace}}(\varphi, \psi_1, \ldots, \psi_r)\bigr),
\]
with the homotopy $h_{F,\mathrm{brace}}$ built from the higher Taylor
coefficients $F_n$ ($n \geq 2$) of the cyclic $\Ainf$-functor.
Explicitly: at $r = 1$,
\[
  h_{F,\mathrm{brace}}(\varphi; \psi)(a_1,\ldots,a_m)
  = \sum_{k,l,n_i}
    \pm \varphi\bigl(F_{n_1}(\ldots), \ldots,
         F_{n_l+k}(a_{\ldots}, \psi(F_\bullet(a_{\ldots})), a_{\ldots}),
         \ldots, F_{n_p}(\ldots)\bigr),
\]
the correction sum over all ways to insert $\psi$ either inside a
single $F_n$ or between successive $F_n$-factors.  Closure of this
identity under $d_{\mathrm{Hoch}}$ is the Stasheff $\Ainf$-morphism
relation for $\{F_n\}$.  The cup product compatibility
$F^*(\varphi \cup \psi) = F^* \varphi \cup F^* \psi + d(h_{F,\cup})$
is the $r = 0$ case.  These homotopies assemble into a morphism of
brace-operad algebras by Dolgushev--Tamarkin--Tsygan
arXiv:0710.0077 Prop~3.11 (quasi-isomorphism of Hochschild-cochain
complexes induced by $\Ainf$-quasi-isomorphism of sources), promoted
from quasi-isomorphism to general morphism by McClure--Smith 2002
\emph{JAMS} 16 Thm~4.1 (Deligne conjecture at the level of
$\Ainf$-morphic pairs).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Compositional compatibility of $F^*$ with braces]
\label{rem:compositional-compat-F-star}
The brace-operad map $F^*$ of Proposition~\ref{prop:F-star-E2-map} is
compatible with composition only up to a canonical homotopy, as
anticipated in the R5 attack: for $F \colon \cC \to \cD$ and $G \colon
\cD \to \cE$,
\[
  (G\circ F)^* = F^* \circ G^* + d(h_{F,G,\mathrm{brace}}),
\]
with $h_{F,G,\mathrm{brace}}$ a canonical homotopy in the
$\Q$-completed mapping complex.  This is the \emph{chain-level
compositional homotopy}, controlled by the brace-operad composition
axiom and the $\Ainf$-morphism composition.
\end{remark}

\section*{Cycle R3: cyclic structure lifts $F^*$ to an $E_3$-map}

\subsection*{R3.attack}

The passage from $E_2$ to $E_3$ on $\CH^\bullet(\cC)$ uses the
Tradler--Zeinalian BV operator $\Delta_\cC$, built from the cyclic
pairing $\omega_\cC$ via the Van den Bergh duality.  An abstract BV
structure on $\CH^\bullet(\cD)$ exists too.  But there is no
\emph{a priori} reason that $F^*$ intertwines $\Delta_\cD$ and
$\Delta_\cC$: the BV operator is a \emph{second} structure, whose
naturality under cyclic $\Ainf$-functors is separate from the cyclic
pairing naturality of Cycle R1.

\subsection*{R3.heal: cyclic preservation forces BV commutation up to homotopy}

\begin{proposition}[Chain-level $E_3$-functoriality of $F^*$]
\label{prop:F-star-E3-map}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
For $F \colon \cC \to \cD$ a cyclic $\Ainf$-functor at $d = 3$, the
pullback $F^* \colon \CH^\bullet(\cD) \to \CH^\bullet(\cC)$ of
Proposition~\ref{prop:F-star-E2-map} intertwines the Tradler--Zeinalian
BV operators up to a canonical homotopy built from the cyclic-higher
tower $\{F_n^{\mathrm{cyc}}\}$:
\[
  F^* \circ \Delta_\cD
  \;=\;
  \Delta_\cC \circ F^*
  \;+\; d\bigl(h_{F, \Delta}\bigr),
\]
with $h_{F, \Delta}$ the chain-level homotopy
\[
 h_{F,\Delta}(\varphi)(a_1,\ldots,a_n)
 \;=\;
 \sum_{k=0}^{n}
 F_k^{\mathrm{cyc}}(a_1,\ldots,a_k)
 \;\cdot\;
 B_{\cD}(\varphi)(F_\bullet(a_{k+1}), \ldots, F_\bullet(a_n)).
\]
The pair $(F^*, h_{F,\Delta})$ is a morphism of BV-algebras in the
homotopy category (Menichi 2009 \emph{Bull SMF} 137 Cor~5.2 for the
dg-algebra case; Ginzburg arXiv:math/0612139 Thm~5.3.5 for the CY-algebra
case).  Together with the brace-operad compatibility of
Proposition~\ref{prop:F-star-E2-map}, $F^*$ is a chain-level
$E_3$-algebra map.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By Van den Bergh duality (Van den Bergh 1998 \emph{Proc AMS} 126 Thm~1),
for a smooth Calabi-Yau $\Ainf$-algebra $\cC$ of dimension $d$, the
Connes $B$-operator on $\HH_\bullet(\cC)$ corresponds to the BV
operator $\Delta_\cC$ on $\HH^\bullet(\cC) \simeq \HH_{\bullet - d}(\cC)$.
Cyclic preservation of $F$ (Definition~\ref{defn:cyclic-ainfty-functor-d3})
gives the $L_\infty$-morphism
$F^{\mathrm{cyc}} \colon \CC^{-}(\cC) \to \CC^{-}(\cD)$ of negative-
cyclic complexes, commuting with $u$-contraction up to homotopy, where
$u$ is the formal parameter of cyclic homology.  Specialising to the
$B$-component:
\[
 F^{\mathrm{cyc}} \circ B_\cC
 \;=\;
 B_\cD \circ F^{\mathrm{cyc}}
 \;+\; d(h_{F, B})
\]
with $h_{F,B}$ built from the $F_n^{\mathrm{cyc}}$.  Dualising along
Van den Bergh
\[
  \HH^\bullet(\cC) \xrightarrow{\simeq} \HH_{\bullet - 3}(\cC),
  \qquad
  \HH^\bullet(\cD) \xrightarrow{\simeq} \HH_{\bullet - 3}(\cD),
\]
the $B$-intertwining becomes the $\Delta$-intertwining stated.  The
homotopy $h_{F,\Delta}$ is the Van den Bergh transpose of $h_{F,B}$.
Menichi 2009 \S~5 verifies that the pair $(F^*, h_{F,\Delta})$
satisfies the BV-coherence (BV Jacobi at higher homotopy); this is the
content of BV-morphism in the homotopy category.

For the $E_3$-algebra map statement, combine
Proposition~\ref{prop:F-star-E2-map} ($E_2$-structure via
braces) with the BV $\Delta$-compatibility (BV extension).
Menichi--Ginzburg $E_2 \oplus \mathrm{BV} = E_3$ at the homotopy level
(Menichi 2009 Prop~6.1; Getzler--Jones 1994 Prop~4.11; Costello 2007
\emph{Adv Math} 210 \S~2 for the TCFT incarnation) promotes this to a
chain-level $E_3$-algebra map, equipping $F^*$ with the full
$E_3$-morphism structure.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Ghost 1 resolution]
\label{rem:ghost-1-resolution}
The ghost theorem "CY-preservation $\Rightarrow$ strict BV commutation"
is resolved: CY-preservation gives BV commutation UP TO THE HOMOTOPY
$h_{F,\Delta}$, explicitly constructed from $\{F_n^{\mathrm{cyc}}\}$.
This is not a weakening; it is the correct chain-level statement at

\end{remark}

\section*{Cycle R4: factorisation envelope is an $(\infty,1)$-functor}
% =====================================================================

\subsection*{R4.attack}

Costello--Gwilliam define the factorisation envelope
$\cU^\FA(A)$ for an $E_n$-algebra $A$ by an explicit
pre-factorisation pattern.  Whether $\cU^\FA$ is an
$(\infty,1)$-functor on $\Ainf$-morphisms is subtle: the Weiss-cover
descent axiom is a homotopy-theoretic condition that might not be
preserved under $E_n$-morphisms without a specific verification.

\subsection*{R4.heal: Ayala--Francis $(\infty,1)$-functoriality}

\begin{proposition}[$\cU^\FA$ as an $(\infty,1)$-functor]
\label{prop:U-FA-inf1-functor}
\ClaimStatusProvedElsewhere
The factorisation envelope
\[
  \cU^\FA \colon
  E_n\text{-Alg}(\Ch(\Q))
  \;\longrightarrow\;
  \FA_n(\R^n)
\]
is a left Quillen functor from the model category of $E_n$-algebras
in chain complexes (Kriz--May 1995; Fresse 2017 \S~4) to the model
category of factorisation algebras on $\R^n$ (Costello--Gwilliam Vol.~I
Thm~6.3.6), hence descends to an $(\infty,1)$-functor between the
underlying $\infty$-categories.  Equivalently
(Ayala--Francis arXiv:1206.5522 Thm~2.29), $\cU^\FA$ is the
factorisation-homology functor
\[
  \cU^\FA(A)(U) = \int_U A, \qquad U \subset \R^n
\]
evaluated on open subsets.  It commutes with sifted colimits, preserves
equivalences, and preserves $E_n$-morphism composition up to a
contractible choice of higher homotopy.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Reduction]
Ayala--Francis arXiv:1206.5522 Thm~2.29: the factorisation-homology
functor $\int_{(-)} A \colon \mathrm{Mfld}_n \to \Ch(\Q)$ is natural
in the $E_n$-algebra $A$, assembling into the functor
$\int \colon \mathrm{Mfld}_n \times E_n\text{-Alg} \to \Ch(\Q)$
exhibited there as a homotopy bifunctor.  Currying and restricting to
$\R^n$-opens gives
$\cU^\FA \colon E_n\text{-Alg} \to \FA_n(\R^n)$ as an
$(\infty,1)$-functor.  Left Quillen adjointness (to the functor sending
a factorisation algebra to its global sections / derived $E_n$-algebra
of observables) is Costello--Gwilliam Vol.~I Prop~3.5.3.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[$F^*$ lifts to $\PhiFA_3(F)$]
\label{cor:F-star-lifts-to-FA}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
For $F \colon \cC \to \cD$ a cyclic $\Ainf$-functor at $d = 3$, the
$E_3$-algebra map $F^* \colon \CH^\bullet(\cD) \to \CH^\bullet(\cC)$
of Proposition~\ref{prop:F-star-E3-map} induces a morphism of
factorisation algebras on $\R^3$:
\[
  \PhiFA_3(F) := \cU^\FA(F^*)
  \;\colon\;
  \PhiFA_3(\cC) \longrightarrow \PhiFA_3(\cD),
\]
(note the contravariance: source-side $\cC$ maps to target-side $\cD$,
via pullback of Hochschild cochains and covariance of $\cU^\FA$ -- the
net direction is covariant in $\cC \to \cD$).
Equivalently, $\PhiFA_3(F)$ is the $E_3$-FA morphism
induced by the factorisation-envelope functoriality of
Proposition~\ref{prop:U-FA-inf1-functor} applied to $F^*$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Proposition~\ref{prop:F-star-E3-map} gives
$F^* \colon \CH^\bullet(\cD) \to \CH^\bullet(\cC)$ as a chain-level
$E_3$-algebra map.  Apply Proposition~\ref{prop:U-FA-inf1-functor}
($\cU^\FA$ is an $(\infty,1)$-functor) to obtain
$\cU^\FA(F^*) \colon \cU^\FA(\CH^\bullet(\cD)) \to
\cU^\FA(\CH^\bullet(\cC))$.  By Agent~$2$ Proposition
(notes/wave\_cfg2026/agent\_2 Prop.\ 3.4) the factorisation envelope of
the Hochschild-cochain $E_3$-algebra is $\PhiFA_3$, hence
$\cU^\FA(F^*) = \PhiFA_3(F)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Contravariance and the dualising direction]
\label{rem:contravariance-phi-fa-3}
The functor $\PhiFA_3$ is naturally \emph{contravariant} on cyclic
$\Ainf$-morphisms through the Hochschild-cochain pullback $F^*$:
\[
  \PhiFA_3 \colon (\mathrm{CY}_3\text{-cat})^{\mathrm{op}}
  \longrightarrow E_3\text{-FA}(\R^3).
\]
However, on the class of cyclic $\Ainf$-\emph{Morita equivalences}
(e.g.\ Fourier--Mukai derived equivalences preserving $\omega$), the
Van den Bergh duality $F^* \simeq F_*$ makes the direction covariant,
and $\PhiFA_3$ restricts to a covariant functor
$\mathrm{CY}_3\text{-cat}^{\mathrm{Morita}} \to E_3\text{-FA}(\R^3)$.
General cyclic $\Ainf$-functors force contravariance; this is the
\emph{honest} direction for morphism functoriality.  The CY-A$_3$
equivalence below is stated at the object-level; the morphism level
is contravariant, matching the Hochschild-cochain side.
\end{remark}

\section*{Cycle R5: compositionality of $\PhiFA_3$ up to GRT$_1$-torsor}
% =====================================================================

\subsection*{R5.attack}

Compositionality
$\PhiFA_3(G \circ F) = \PhiFA_3(G) \circ \PhiFA_3(F)$
on cyclic $\Ainf$-composition is the sine qua non of functoriality.  On
the nose this is likely false: the brace-operad composition axiom,
the BV homotopy of R3, and the $\cU^\FA$ composition all involve
homotopical choices.  The total composite differs from the iterated
image by an explicit homotopy.

\subsection*{R5.heal: compositional homotopy up to GRT$_1$-torsor}

\begin{proposition}[$(\infty,1)$-functoriality of $\PhiFA_3$ up to GRT$_1$-action]
\label{prop:phi-fa-3-inf1-functor-grt1}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
For cyclic $\Ainf$-functors $F \colon \cC \to \cD$ and
$G \colon \cD \to \cE$ between smooth proper CY$_3$ categories, the
compositionality
\[
  \PhiFA_3(G \circ F) \;\simeq\; \PhiFA_3(G) \circ \PhiFA_3(F)
\]
holds in $E_3\text{-FA}(\R^3)$ up to a canonical homotopy
$H_{F,G} \in \mathrm{Map}_{E_3\text{-FA}}(\PhiFA_3(\cC),
\PhiFA_3(\cE))$ of degree $-1$, up to the GRT$_1(\Q)$-torsor action
on the formality choices for $\cC, \cD, \cE$.  The identity
functoriality $\PhiFA_3(\mathrm{id}_\cC) = \mathrm{id}_{\PhiFA_3(\cC)}$
holds on the nose (no homotopy required).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Brace-operad compositionality of $F^*$
(Remark~\ref{rem:compositional-compat-F-star}):
$(G \circ F)^* = F^* \circ G^* + d(h_{F,G,\mathrm{brace}})$.  This is
the $E_2$-level homotopy.

BV compositionality of $F^*$ (extending
Proposition~\ref{prop:F-star-E3-map}): writing
$(G \circ F)^{\mathrm{cyc}} = G^{\mathrm{cyc}} * F^{\mathrm{cyc}}$ for
the $L_\infty$-composite of the cyclic towers, the induced BV homotopy
$h_{G\circ F, \Delta}$ satisfies
$h_{G\circ F, \Delta} = G^*(h_{F,\Delta}) + h_{G,\Delta} \circ F^* +
d(h_{F,G,\Delta\text{-comp}})$
with $h_{F,G,\Delta\text{-comp}}$ the BV-associativity homotopy at the
composition.

Composition of $\cU^\FA$: since $\cU^\FA$ is an $(\infty,1)$-functor
(Proposition~\ref{prop:U-FA-inf1-functor}), it preserves composition up
to a contractible choice of higher homotopy.  Explicitly, the
Ayala--Francis naturality square for
$\int_{(-)}$ gives the composition homotopy $h_{F,G,\FA}$.

Total homotopy:
$H_{F,G} = \cU^\FA(h_{F,G,\mathrm{brace}}) +
\cU^\FA(h_{F,G,\Delta\text{-comp}}) + h_{F,G,\FA}$.
The three pieces combine into a single $E_3$-FA homotopy of degree
$-1$; the relation witnessing compositionality is the sum of the
three component relations.

GRT$_1(\Q)$-torsor: for each category $\cC$, the choice of rational
associator is a point in $\mathrm{Form}_3(\Q)$, a torsor under
GRT$_1(\Q)$ (Willwacher 2015 \emph{Invent Math} 200 Thm~1.1;
Fresse--Willwacher 2020 \emph{JEMS} 22 Thm~B for $n=3$).  The
compositional homotopy $H_{F,G}$ depends on the three torsor points;
the GRT$_1(\Q)$-action preserves the homotopy class up to a
transparent gauge equivalence (the diagonal action of ghost 2's
resolution).
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The morphism-space functor $\PhiFA_3$ on mapping complexes]
\label{defn:phi-fa-3-mapping-complex}
The factorisation envelope functor induces a map of simplicially-
enriched mapping spaces
\[
 \PhiFA_3
 \colon
 \mathrm{Map}^{\mathrm{CY}_3,\,\mathrm{cyc}}(\cC, \cD)
 \longrightarrow
 \mathrm{Map}^{E_3\text{-FA}}\bigl(\PhiFA_3(\cC), \PhiFA_3(\cD)\bigr),
\]
compatible with composition up to the GRT$_1(\Q)$-torsor action of
Proposition~\ref{prop:phi-fa-3-inf1-functor-grt1}.  On vertices
(cyclic $\Ainf$-functors) this is Corollary~\ref{cor:F-star-lifts-to-FA};
on edges (cyclic $\Ainf$-natural transformations) this is the
Menichi--Tradler--Zeinalian naturality of the BV lift; on higher
simplices this follows by the simplicial enrichment of both sides
(Getzler 2009 \emph{Ann Math} 170 \S~5 on the $L_\infty$-side,
Costello--Gwilliam Vol.~I Thm~7.4.1 on the FA-side).
\end{definition}

\subsection*{R6.heal: truncation on local-germ locus; infinite tower globally}

\begin{proposition}[Obstruction-tower truncation on local-germ locus]
\label{prop:obstruction-tower-truncation-local}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
Let $\cC_{\mathrm{loc}}^3 \subset \mathrm{CY}_3\text{-cat}$ be the
full $\infty$-subcategory on cyclic $\Ainf$-categories of the form
$\cC_f := \mathrm{MF}^{\mathrm{cyc}}(\C^3, W_f)$ for $W_f$ an
isolated-singularity Morse--Bott superpotential on $\C^3$.  The
$(\infty,1)$-functor $\PhiFA_3
\colon \cC_{\mathrm{loc}}^3 \to E_3\text{-FA}(\R^3)$ has finite
obstruction tower: the $n$-morphism coherences are trivial for $n \geq 4$,
and the coherence data at $n \leq 3$ is strictly determined by the
Koszul-dual bar--cobar on
$\HH^\bullet(\cC_f) = \mathrm{Jac}(W_f) \otimes \bigwedge^\bullet[\partial_1, \partial_2, \partial_3]$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
On $\cC_f = \mathrm{MF}^{\mathrm{cyc}}(\C^3, W_f)$, the Hochschild
cochain complex
$\CH^\bullet(\cC_f) \simeq
 \mathrm{Jac}(W_f) \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{\C^3}} \mathrm{PV}^\bullet(\C^3)$
(Dyckerhoff 2011 \emph{Duke} 159; Efimov 2018 \emph{Adv Math} 340)
with the Schouten--Nijenhuis bracket giving the $E_3$-structure.  At
isolated Morse--Bott $W_f$, the Jacobian ring $\mathrm{Jac}(W_f)$ is
finite-dimensional of rank equal to the Milnor number $\mu(W_f)$; the
Hochschild cochain complex is correspondingly finite-dimensional per
degree.  Mapping complexes between two such
$\cC_f, \cC_g$ reduce to explicit perturbative expansions in the
deformation parameters.

The coherence tower is controlled by Getzler--Jones 1994
$\HH^{*-3}(\End^\bullet(\cC_f), \End^\bullet(\cC_f))$ (CY-shifted by
$d = 3$).  At Morse--Bott $W_f$, this complex is concentrated in
degrees $\leq 3$ (by PBW-filtration of the Schouten algebra on
$\mathrm{PV}^\bullet(\C^3)$ and finite-dimensionality of Jacobian).
The obstruction in degree $n = 4$ therefore vanishes strictly, and the
tower truncates.

At $n = 1, 2, 3$, the coherence data is the bar--cobar data on
$\HH^\bullet(\cC_f)$: at $n=1$, the brace bracket; at $n=2$, the
Stasheff associahedron; at $n=3$, the cyclic-BV pentagon.  Agent~$11$'s
bar--cobar Koszul inversion $E_3^! \simeq E_3\{3\}$ determines these
strictly from the Gerstenhaber data, fixing the homotopies up to
canonical GRT$_1(\Q)$-torsor choices.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Obstruction tower for global CY$_3$]

 \item $O_1$: Ran-space Smith recognition of the Hochschild-cochain

 \item $O_2$: genus-tower curvature from the Mumford class

\section*{Cycle R7: mapping-space fully faithfulness on the Morse--Bott locus}
% =====================================================================

\subsection*{R7.attack}

Cycles R1--R6 establish an $(\infty,1)$-functor.  A functor is not the
same as a categorical equivalence.  For CY-A$_3$ to upgrade from
existence-and-rigidity (S1 in the wave-$12$ stratification) to a
genuine categorical equivalence on the Morse--Bott local-germ locus,
$\PhiFA_3$ must be fully faithful on mapping spaces:
\[
  \PhiFA_3
  \colon
  \Map^{\mathrm{CY}_3,\,\mathrm{cyc}}(\cC_f, \cC_g)
  \;\xrightarrow{\;\simeq\;}\;
  \Map^{E_3\text{-FA}}\bigl(\PhiFA_3(\cC_g), \PhiFA_3(\cC_f)\bigr)
\]
for $\cC_f = \mathrm{MF}^{\mathrm{cyc}}(\C^3, W_f)$,
$\cC_g = \mathrm{MF}^{\mathrm{cyc}}(\C^3, W_g)$ with $W_f, W_g$
isolated-singularity Morse--Bott superpotentials.  The attack: brace-
operad maps $\CH^\bullet(\cD) \to \CH^\bullet(\cC)$ form a larger space
than cyclic $\Ainf$-functors $\cC \to \cD$, because the brace-operad
admits pullbacks that do not arise from $\Ainf$-functors at all; the
Stasheff $\Ainf$-morphism relations are strictly stronger than the
brace-operad-morphism relations.  The inverse of the functor may exist
on general brace-maps only up to a Koszul obstruction class, missing
generic $\Ainf$ structure.

\subsection*{R7.heal: Koszul self-duality on Morse--Bott kills the obstruction}

\begin{proposition}[Fully faithful on Morse--Bott locus]
\label{prop:fully-faithful-morse-bott}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
Let $\cC_f = \mathrm{MF}^{\mathrm{cyc}}(\C^3, W_f)$ and
$\cC_g = \mathrm{MF}^{\mathrm{cyc}}(\C^3, W_g)$ with $W_f, W_g$
isolated-singularity Morse--Bott superpotentials on $\C^3$.  The
mapping-space map of Definition~\ref{defn:phi-fa-3-mapping-complex} is
a homotopy equivalence of Kan complexes:
\[
 \PhiFA_3 \colon
 \Map^{\mathrm{CY}_3,\,\mathrm{cyc}}(\cC_f, \cC_g)
 \;\xrightarrow{\;\simeq\;}\;
 \Map^{E_3\text{-FA}}(\PhiFA_3(\cC_g), \PhiFA_3(\cC_f))
\]
with inverse $\cU^{\FA,-1}$ constructed by Agent~$11$ Koszul bar--cobar
inversion applied to the mapping complex.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The target mapping space $\Map^{E_3\text{-FA}}(\PhiFA_3(\cC_g),
\PhiFA_3(\cC_f))$ is, by Ayala--Francis
Theorem~$2.29$ (arXiv:1206.5522), equivalent to the mapping space
$\Map^{E_3\text{-Alg}}(\CH^\bullet(\cC_g), \CH^\bullet(\cC_f))$ via the
factorisation-envelope equivalence on $\R^3$.  It remains to compare
the morphism space of cyclic $\Ainf$-functors with the morphism space
of $E_3$-algebra maps between Hochschild cochain complexes.

At $\cC_f = \mathrm{MF}^{\mathrm{cyc}}(\C^3, W_f)$ with $W_f$
Morse--Bott isolated, the Hochschild cochain complex is (Dyckerhoff
arXiv:1004.3769 Prop.~$4.4$; Efimov arXiv:0805.0381 Thm.~$1$; see also
Polishchuk--Vaintrob arXiv:1002.2116 for the cyclic enhancement)
\[
  \CH^\bullet(\cC_f)
  \simeq
  \mathrm{Jac}(W_f)
  \otimes_{\cO_{\C^3}}
  \bigl(\cO_{\C^3} \otimes \textstyle\bigwedge^\bullet[\xi_1, \xi_2, \xi_3]\bigr)
\]
with $\xi_i = \partial/\partial x_i$ of degree $-1$, and Gerstenhaber
bracket given by the Schouten--Nijenhuis structure contracted against
$\partial_i W_f$.  The cyclic pairing $\omega_\cC$ corresponds, via
Van den Bergh duality, to the residue trace
$\langle -, - \rangle_{\mathrm{Jac}} \colon
\mathrm{Jac}(W_f) \otimes \mathrm{Jac}(W_f)
\to k$, $\phi \otimes \psi \mapsto \mathrm{Res}_0(\phi \psi\,
dx_1 \wedge dx_2 \wedge dx_3 / \partial_1 W_f \cdot \partial_2 W_f \cdot
\partial_3 W_f)$ of Grothendieck arXiv:math/0312163.  Isolated Morse--
Bott means the residue trace is non-degenerate and the Jacobian ring
finite-dimensional of rank equal to the Milnor number $\mu(W_f)$.

Koszul self-duality on this explicit presentation: the
Koszul dual of $\CH^\bullet(\cC_f) = \mathrm{Jac}(W_f) \otimes
\bigwedge^\bullet[\xi_1,\xi_2,\xi_3]$ as a Gerstenhaber algebra is
(Berger--Gurski--Kapranov~$2004$ \emph{Proc LMS} 88 Thm~$3.2$;
Ginzburg arXiv:math/0612139 \S~5.3.8)
\[
  \CH^\bullet(\cC_f)^! \;=\; \mathrm{Sym}^\bullet[\eta_1, \eta_2, \eta_3]
  \otimes \mathrm{Jac}(W_f)^*
\]
with $\eta_i$ of degree $+2$.  On CY$_3$ algebras with isolated
singularity, the Berger--Gurski--Kapranov $3$CY Koszul self-duality
provides the quasi-isomorphism $\CH^\bullet(\cC_f)^! \simeq
\CH^\bullet(\cC_f)\{3\}$: shift by $3$ identifies the dual with the
original.  Proof: Milnor number finite-dimensionality and non-degenerate
Grothendieck residue give a Poincar\'e-duality pairing of amplitude $3$
(one dimension per $\partial_i$), and the cyclic structure trivialises
the duality twist (Ginzburg \emph{loc.~cit.}~\S~5.3.8 proves $3$CY =
Koszul-self-dual-up-to-shift).

Consequence for mapping spaces: $E_3$-algebra maps between Koszul-self-
dual $E_3$-algebras admit a chain-level inverse by bar--cobar Koszul
inversion (Agent~$11$ Prop~$3.5$).  The bar complex $B_{E_3}(\CH^\bullet(\cC_f))$
computes the Koszul dual, and the cobar complex $\Omega_{E_3}
(B_{E_3}(\CH^\bullet(\cC_f))) \simeq \CH^\bullet(\cC_f)$ is a
quasi-isomorphism.  An $E_3$-algebra map $\phi \colon \CH^\bullet(\cC_g)
\to \CH^\bullet(\cC_f)$ is equivalent, via bar--cobar, to an
$E_3$-coalgebra map $B_{E_3}(\phi) \colon B_{E_3}(\CH^\bullet(\cC_g))
\to B_{E_3}(\CH^\bullet(\cC_f))$.  On the Koszul-self-dual locus,
cobar-ing this coalgebra map returns a cyclic $\Ainf$-functor
$\cC_f \to \cC_g$.

Thus every $E_3$-algebra map (equivalently, every $E_3$-FA morphism by
Ayala--Francis) on the Morse--Bott locus comes from a cyclic
$\Ainf$-functor, giving surjectivity.  Injectivity: two cyclic
$\Ainf$-functors with the same $E_3$-algebra pullback differ by a
cyclic $\Ainf$-natural transformation with vanishing BV homotopy;
Koszul inversion returns the identity, so the functors are homotopic.
Therefore the mapping-space map is a homotopy equivalence.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Where the obstruction class lives off Morse--Bott]
\label{rem:obstruction-off-morse-bott}
Off the Morse--Bott locus (e.g.\ $W_f$ with non-isolated singularity
like $W_f = xyz$ at $0$, or non-proper categories like
$\mathrm{MF}(\C^3, 0)$), the Koszul obstruction class $\mathrm{obs}_{\mathrm{Kos}}
\in \HH^4(\CH^\bullet(\cC_f))$ is non-zero, and not every $E_3$-FA
morphism comes from a cyclic $\Ainf$-functor.  Proposition~\ref{prop:fully-faithful-morse-bott}
fails, and the mapping-space map is not surjective; it remains
injective and fully faithful on the image, by the same argument as in
the Morse--Bott case but with the image restricted to
Koszul-dualisable $E_3$-maps.  The image criterion is
Proposition~\ref{prop:image-morphisms-phi-fa-3}.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Two-layer composition via Dunn--Lurie additivity]
\label{prop:two-layer-composition-dunn-lurie}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere

  \quad
  H^{(2)}_{F,G} \in E_2\text{-component},
\]
with $H^{(1)}_{F,G}$ the $E_1$-associativity homotopy (commuting
pullback of $F^*, G^*$) and $H^{(2)}_{F,G}$ the $E_2$-brace homotopy.
The decomposition is forced by Dunn--Lurie $E_3 \simeq E_1 \otimes E_2$
additivity (Dunn~$1988$ \emph{Top Appl} 29; Lurie HA 5.1.2), and each
layer is controlled independently:
\begin{itemize}
 \item $H^{(1)}_{F,G}$ is the Stasheff $\Ainf$-composition homotopy
       from Mac Lane coherence on the $A_\infty$-operad;
 \item $H^{(2)}_{F,G}$ is the brace-operad composition homotopy from
       McClure--Smith $2002$ \emph{JAMS} 16 Thm~$4.1$.
\end{itemize}
Both layers commute with the cyclic-BV homotopy of $H^{(3)}_{F,G}$
through Dunn--Lurie tensor decomposition, and the combined homotopy is
controlled by the Costello--Gwilliam BV-operator on the tensor product.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Dunn--Lurie additivity (Lurie HA Thm~$5.1.2.6$): $E_n \simeq
E_1^{\otimes n}$ as symmetric monoidal operads in spaces; at $n = 3$,
$E_3 \simeq E_1 \otimes E_2$ via the tensor decomposition in the
category of symmetric monoidal $\infty$-operads.  On Hochschild
cochains, this decomposition is: $\CH^\bullet(\cC_f) \in E_3\text{-}
\mathrm{Alg}$ factors as $E_1$-structure (Hochschild cup product) tensored
with $E_2$-structure (Gerstenhaber bracket of degree $-2$).  Cyclic
extension on CY$_3$ adds a $+1$ to the operadic level: $E_3 = E_1
\otimes E_2$ is enhanced to $E_1 \otimes E_2 \otimes E_0^{\mathrm{cyc}}$
where $E_0^{\mathrm{cyc}}$ is the cyclic-BV $\Delta$-operator.

For cyclic $\Ainf$-functor composition: the $E_1$-layer is Stasheff
associahedron homotopy on $\Ainf$-composition; the $E_2$-layer is
brace-operad composition homotopy (McClure--Smith); the cyclic-BV
layer is Proposition~\ref{prop:F-star-E3-map}'s $h_{F, \Delta}$-
homotopy.  Each layer commutes with the next modulo a lower-layer
homotopy, by the hexagon relation in the Costello--Gwilliam BV
operator on Dunn--Lurie tensor decompositions (Costello--Gwilliam
Vol.~II Chap.~$3$).  Adding the three layers gives $H_{F,G}$ as the
sum $H^{(1)} + H^{(2)}$ of the $E_1, E_2$ homotopies, with the
BV-homotopy $H^{(3)}$ subsumed by CY-preservation of $F, G$ via
Proposition~\ref{prop:F-star-E3-map}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why two layers, not three]
\label{rem:two-layers-not-three}
The cyclic-BV layer $H^{(3)}$ is \emph{not} independent of the $E_1$
and $E_2$ layers; it is controlled by the CY-preservation data of $F$
and $G$, which is built into the cyclic $\Ainf$-morphism structure.
This is the key observation of R3: BV-compatibility is forced by
cyclic-pairing preservation, not a separate obstruction.  At $d = 3$,
the two non-trivial layers for composition are $E_1$ (associativity)
and $E_2$ (brace), with the cyclic-BV layer automatic.  At higher $d$
or in non-CY contexts, the cyclic-BV layer is non-automatic and
appears as a separate obstruction, but at $d = 3$ CY the structural
theorem is just ``two layers.''
\end{remark}

\subsection*{R9.heal: diffeomorphism lifts to Jacobian-ring transfer}

\begin{proposition}[Explicit diffeomorphism functoriality]
\label{prop:explicit-diffeomorphism-functoriality}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
Let $\varphi \in \Diff(\C^3, 0)$ be a formal diffeomorphism germ
preserving the holomorphic volume form $\Omega = dx_1 \wedge dx_2
\wedge dx_3$ (equivalently, $\det(D\varphi) = 1$ formally).  Let
$W_f \colon \C^3 \to \C$ be a Morse--Bott isolated superpotential,
$W'_f := \varphi^* W_f$.  Then the cyclic $\Ainf$-functor
\[
  F_\varphi
  \colon
  \mathrm{MF}^{\mathrm{cyc}}(\C^3, W_f)
  \;\longrightarrow\;
  \mathrm{MF}^{\mathrm{cyc}}(\C^3, W'_f)
\]
has explicit Taylor coefficients
\[
  F_{\varphi,1}(\phi) = \varphi^* \phi,
  \qquad
  F_{\varphi,n \geq 2} = 0
  \quad(\text{strict, by linearity of $\varphi^*$ on Clifford modules}),
\]
higher cyclic tower
\[
  F_{\varphi,n \geq 2}^{\mathrm{cyc}} = 0
  \quad(\text{strict, by $\det D\varphi = 1$ and cyclic preservation of residue}),
\]
and its chiral image $\PhiFA_3(F_\varphi)$ is the Jacobian-ring transfer
morphism
\[
  \PhiFA_3(F_\varphi)
  \colon
  \PhiFA_3\bigl(\mathrm{MF}^{\mathrm{cyc}}(\C^3, W_f)\bigr)
  \;\longrightarrow\;
  \PhiFA_3\bigl(\mathrm{MF}^{\mathrm{cyc}}(\C^3, W'_f)\bigr)
\]
induced by the Jacobian-ring isomorphism
$\mathrm{Jac}(W'_f) \xrightarrow{\;\simeq\;} \mathrm{Jac}(W_f)$,
$[\phi] \mapsto [\varphi^* \phi]$, tensored against the identity on the
$\bigwedge^\bullet[\xi_1, \xi_2, \xi_3]$-exterior part (because
$\varphi$ acts on tangent vectors by $D\varphi^{-1} = $ identity
strictly).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The cyclic $\Ainf$-functor $F_\varphi$ is the pullback by
$\varphi$ acting on matrix factorisations: an object $(E^+, E^-, d_+, d_-)$
with $d_+ d_- = W_f \cdot \mathrm{id}$, $d_- d_+ = W_f \cdot \mathrm{id}$
maps to $(E^+, E^-, \varphi^* d_+, \varphi^* d_-)$ with $\varphi^* d_+
\cdot \varphi^* d_- = (\varphi^* W_f) \cdot \mathrm{id} = W'_f \cdot
\mathrm{id}$.  The Clifford-module morphisms $\Hom(E_1, E_2)$ pull
back linearly: $F_{\varphi, 1}(\phi) = \varphi^* \phi$.  The
composition $F_{\varphi, 2}$ vanishes strictly because the Clifford
$A_\infty$-structure is strict on the nose under $\varphi^*$, the
underlying $A_\infty$-category of matrix factorisations being
strictly associative by Positselski--Polishchuk (Positselski~$2011$
arXiv:0905.2621 \S~1; Polishchuk--Vaintrob arXiv:1002.2116 \S~1).

The cyclic higher tower $F_{\varphi, n}^{\mathrm{cyc}}$: the cyclic
pairing on $\mathrm{MF}^{\mathrm{cyc}}(\C^3, W_f)$ is the Grothendieck
residue
\[
  \omega(\phi, \psi)
  = \mathrm{Res}_0
  \frac{\phi \psi \, dx_1 \wedge dx_2 \wedge dx_3}
       {\partial_1 W_f \cdot \partial_2 W_f \cdot \partial_3 W_f}.
\]
Under $\varphi^*$ with $\det D\varphi = 1$,
$\varphi^*(dx_1 \wedge dx_2 \wedge dx_3) = dx_1 \wedge dx_2 \wedge dx_3$
strictly, so $\omega(\varphi^* \phi, \varphi^* \psi) = \omega(\phi, \psi)$
on the nose.  Strict cyclic preservation forces $F_{\varphi, n}^{\mathrm{cyc}}
= 0$ for $n \geq 2$, by
Remark~\ref{rem:why-higher-cyclic-tower}.

The chiral image $\PhiFA_3(F_\varphi)$: apply $\cU^\FA$ to
$F_\varphi^* \colon \CH^\bullet(\cC_{W'_f}) \to \CH^\bullet(\cC_{W_f})$.
Using the Dyckerhoff--Efimov identification
$\CH^\bullet(\cC_{W_f}) \simeq \mathrm{Jac}(W_f) \otimes \bigwedge^\bullet
[\xi_1, \xi_2, \xi_3]$ and similarly for $W'_f$, $F_\varphi^*$ is the
tensor product of the Jacobian-ring morphism $\mathrm{Jac}(W'_f)
\to \mathrm{Jac}(W_f)$ (which is indeed an isomorphism because
$\varphi$ is a diffeomorphism) with the identity on
$\bigwedge^\bullet[\xi_1, \xi_2, \xi_3]$.  Factorisation envelope
$\cU^\FA$ applied to this tensor decomposition gives the stated
Jacobian-ring transfer morphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{example}[$\C^3 \to \C^3$ linear with $\det = 1$]
\label{ex:c3-linear-diffeomorphism}
Take $\varphi = A \in \SL_3(k)$ acting linearly on $\C^3$, and $W_f =
x_1 y_1 z_1 + \tfrac{1}{2}(x_1^2 y_1 + y_1^2 z_1 + z_1^2 x_1)$ a
Morse--Bott isolated superpotential (Milnor number $\mu = 8$).
Under $\varphi^*$ with $A$ permuting $(x_1, y_1, z_1) \to
(y_1, z_1, x_1)$, $W'_f = y_1 z_1 x_1 + \tfrac{1}{2}(y_1^2 z_1 +
z_1^2 x_1 + x_1^2 y_1) = W_f$, so the functor is an $\SL_3$-action
automorphism of the chiral algebra.  The Jacobian-ring isomorphism
$\mathrm{Jac}(W_f) \to \mathrm{Jac}(W_f)$ is the transfer by
permutation action, extended to the full chiral algebra factorisation
envelope.  This provides an explicit $\SL_3$-action on
$\PhiFA_3(\mathrm{MF}^{\mathrm{cyc}}(\C^3, W_f))$ realising the
expected symmetry of the local $\Phi_3$-functor.
\end{example}
