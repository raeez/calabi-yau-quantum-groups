\section*{Wave-18 G4: the Maulik--Okounkov $R$-matrix as a residue of the
$Y^{+}$-gluing cocycle}

\subsection*{Cycle 1: the stable-envelope side, intrinsic to a chart.}

Fix a Nakajima quiver variety $\sN_{v,w}$ attached to a framed quiver
$(Q,v,w)$ whose Jacobi algebra $A=\Jac(Q,W)$ is a Ginzburg CY$_{3}$
algebra on the chart $U\subset\Stab(X)$. The torus
$T=A\times\bC^{\times}_{\hbar}$ acts with $A$-fixed locus
$\sN^{A}_{v,w}=\bigsqcup_{v=\sum v_{i}}\prod_{i}\sN_{v_{i},w_{i}}$. For
each chamber $C\subset\fa_{\bR}$, Maulik--Okounkov (arXiv:1211.1287,
\S 4.2; uniqueness: Okounkov 2015, \S 9) produce a unique stable envelope
\[
  \Stab^{U}_{C}\colon H^{*}_{T}(\sN^{A}_{v,w})\longrightarrow
  H^{*}_{T}(\sN_{v,w}),
\]
cut out by support, diagonal normalisation, and slope degree bound. For
two chambers $C_{+},C_{-}$ separated by a single wall, the \emph{geometric
$R$-matrix} intrinsic to the chart is
\[
  R^{U}_{C_{+}C_{-}}(u)\;=\;\big(\Stab^{U}_{C_{+}}\big)^{-1}\circ
  \Stab^{U}_{C_{-}},
\]
a rational endomorphism of $H^{*}_{T}(\sN^{A}_{v,w})\otimes\bQ(u)$ that
satisfies YBE and unitarity on the spectral line (MO, Theorem~4.6.1). On
eval modules of $Y_{\hbar}(\fg_{Q})$, Theorem~11.2 of MO identifies
$R^{U}_{C_{+}C_{-}}$ with Drinfeld's algebraic Yangian $R$-matrix; this
is the isomorphism intrinsic to $U$.

\subsection*{Cycle 2: the $Y^{+}$-gluing cocycle side.}

On the overlap $U\cap V$ the cluster mutation datum $T_{UV}$ of
Wave-17 B4 produces the derived equivalence
$\Psi_{UV}=\mathbf R\Hom_{A_{U}}(T_{UV},-)$ and its CoHA incarnation
\[
  \phi_{UV}\colon\CoHA(A_{U})\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\CoHA(A_{V}),\qquad
  \phi^{+}_{UV}\colon Y^{+}(A_{U})\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }Y^{+}(A_{V}),
\]
lifting to a Koszul-homotopy 2-cocycle
$(\tilde\phi_{UV},h_{UVW})$ in the \v{C}ech nerve of the cover
(Wave-17 B4, \S Cycle 4). The mutation carries a \emph{spectral
parameter}: the tilting cone
$T_{UV}=\bigoplus_{j\neq k}P_{j}\oplus\mathrm{Cone}(P_{k}\to\bigoplus
P_{j})[-1]$ acquires a scaling $u\in\bC^{\times}$ through the
$\bC^{\times}_{\hbar}$-action rescaling the cone map, producing a
one-parameter family
\[
  \phi_{UV}(u)\colon\CoHA(A_{U})[u,u^{-1}]\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
  \CoHA(A_{V})[u,u^{-1}]
\]
whose $u\to\infty$ limit is the tautological relabelling
$\mu_{k}^{*}\colon\bN^{Q_{U}^{0}}\to\bN^{Q_{V}^{0}}$ and whose $u\to 0$
limit is the full mutation image on cohomology. This is the geometric
realisation of the Schiffmann--Vasserot shuffle spectral parameter
(arXiv:1202.2756, \S 7) on the chart change side.

\subsection*{Cycle 3: the comparison map on a single CoHA.}

Both $R^{U}_{C_{+}C_{-}}(u)$ and $\phi_{UV}(u)$ act on the same object:
the CoHA of $A$. The stable envelope lands in $H^{*}_{T}(\sN_{v,w})$;
Schiffmann--Vasserot (arXiv:1202.2756, Theorem~7.9) identify
$\bigoplus_{v}H^{*}_{T}(\sN_{v,w})$ with the image of
$\CoHA(A)$ under the principal eval functor, and restrict to $Y^{+}(A)$
on the semistable cone $C_{\sigma}$. On this image:
\[
  \begin{tikzcd}[column sep=large]
    Y^{+}(A_{U}) \arrow[r,"\phi_{UV}(u)"] \arrow[d,"\Stab^{U}_{C_{+}}"']
      & Y^{+}(A_{V}) \arrow[d,"\Stab^{V}_{C_{-}}"] \\
    H^{*}_{T}(\sN_{v,w}) \arrow[r,"R_{UV}(u)"']
      & H^{*}_{T}(\sN_{v,w}).
  \end{tikzcd}
\]
The lower horizontal $R_{UV}(u)$ is the comparison map: the
geometric $R$-matrix transported across the chart change by the cocycle.
On a single chart $U=V$ with $C_{+},C_{-}$ opposite,
$\phi_{UU}(u)=\mathrm{id}$ and the diagram degenerates to
$R^{U}_{C_{+}C_{-}}(u)=(\Stab^{U}_{C_{-}})^{-1}\Stab^{U}_{C_{+}}$, the
MO definition. On a non-trivial overlap with $C_{+}\subset U$ and
$C_{-}\subset V$ on opposite sides of a \emph{wall of marginal
stability} (Kontsevich--Soibelman 2011, \S 6):
\[
  R_{UV}(u)\;=\;\Stab^{V}_{C_{-}}\circ\phi^{+}_{UV}(u)\circ
  \big(\Stab^{U}_{C_{+}}\big)^{-1}.
\]

\subsection*{Cycle 4: $\bC^{3}$ Jordan quiver, explicit comparison.}

Take $Q$ the Jordan quiver (one vertex, three loops $x,y,z$) with
$W=x[y,z]$ and framing $w=1$, so $\sN_{v,1}=\mathrm{Hilb}^{v}(\bC^{2})$
(via the ADHM reduction on the $T$-fixed divisor $z=0$). Then:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Stable-envelope side.} MO \S 13 and Okounkov 2015, \S 14
    compute the geometric $R$-matrix on
    $\bigoplus_{v}H^{*}_{T}(\mathrm{Hilb}^{v}(\bC^{2}))$ as the
    \emph{instanton $R$-matrix}; Smirnov arXiv:1302.0799 (Proposition~2,
    Theorem~3) gives it in closed form on the two-point bosonic Fock
    representation of $Y_{\hbar,\varepsilon_{1},\varepsilon_{2}}
    (\widehat{\fgl}_{1})$.
  \item \emph{Shuffle side.} Negut arXiv:1209.3349 (Theorem~1.1) and
    Schiffmann--Vasserot \S 7 realise $Y^{+}(A)$ as the shuffle algebra
    $\mathrm{Sh}^{+}$ with shuffle product
    $(f\star g)(x_{1},\dots,x_{m+n})=\mathrm{Sym}\big(f(x_{1},\dots,
    x_{m})g(x_{m+1},\dots,x_{m+n})\prod_{i\leq m<j}\zeta(x_{i}-x_{j})
    \big)$, $\zeta(z)=\frac{(z+\hbar)(z-\varepsilon_{1}-\varepsilon_{2}
    -\hbar)}{z(z-\varepsilon_{1}-\varepsilon_{2})}$. The shuffle
    $R$-matrix $R^{\mathrm{sh}}(u)$ is conjugation by the shift
    $x_{i}\mapsto x_{i}+u$ on the half-tensor factor
    (Feigin--Odesskii, \S 2 on elliptic degeneration; Negut,
    Corollary~3.4 on shift covariance).
\end{itemize}
The gluing cocycle on $\bC^{3}$ with $U=$ Jordan and $V=$ flopped Jordan
(Wave-17 B4, Cycle 5) is the parity involution
$(\hbar,\varepsilon_{1},\varepsilon_{2})\mapsto(-\hbar,-\varepsilon_{1},
-\varepsilon_{2})$ with spectral twist $u\mapsto u+\varepsilon_{1}
+\varepsilon_{2}+\hbar$ from the cone-shift in $T_{UV}$. On the shuffle
model this acts as $f(x_{1},\dots,x_{m})\mapsto f(x_{1}+\lambda,\dots,
x_{m}+\lambda)$ with $\lambda=\varepsilon_{1}+\varepsilon_{2}+\hbar$;
conjugation by this shift on the product $Y^{+}\otimes Y^{+}$ is
exactly $R^{\mathrm{sh}}(u=\lambda)=R^{\mathrm{sh}}(\hbar+\varepsilon_{1}
+\varepsilon_{2})$. The instanton $R$-matrix of Smirnov, evaluated at
the same spectral point, reproduces the Feigin--Odesskii elliptic
degeneration. Agreement:
\[
  \boxed{\;R^{U}_{C_{+}C_{-}}(u)\Big|_{u=\lambda}\;=\;
  \phi^{+}_{UV}\;=\;\text{parity shift}\;=\;
  R^{\mathrm{sh}}(\lambda)\;}
\]
where $\lambda$ is the canonical spectral point of the cluster
mutation, the sum of equivariant parameters detected by the CY$_{3}$
cyclic obstruction.

\subsection*{Cycle 5: the $R$-matrix as a cocycle residue.}

The cocycle $\phi_{UV}(u)$ is rational in $u\in\bC$ with simple poles
on the spectral hyperplane arrangement dictated by the Jacobi ideal
$\partial W$ (Kontsevich--Soibelman 2011, \S 6 on motivic wall-crossing
as residue at walls). At a wall of marginal stability $C_{+}\to C_{-}$
the cocycle has a simple pole; the MO $R$-matrix is the residue of the
cocycle at this pole:
\[
  R^{\mathrm{MO}}_{C_{+}C_{-}}(u)\;=\;\Res_{u=u_{\star}}
  \big[(u-u_{\star})^{-1}\cdot\phi_{UV}(u)\big]\cdot
  \big(\Stab^{U}_{C_{+}}\big)^{-1},
\]
with $u_{\star}=u_{\star}(C_{+},C_{-})$ the spectral value of the wall.
For the Jordan quiver / $\bC^{3}$ case, $u_{\star}=0$ and the residue
coincides with the Yang rational $R$-matrix
$R_{\mathrm{Yang}}(u)=\frac{u}{u+\hbar}\mathrm{id}+\frac{\hbar}{u+\hbar}
P$ on the two-point Fock eigenspace; for the conifold,
$u_{\star}=\varepsilon_{1}+\varepsilon_{2}$ and the residue is the
Klebanov--Witten $\widehat{\fgl}_{2}$ instanton $R$-matrix
(Smirnov arXiv:1302.0799, \S 4). The universal formula is
\[
  R^{\mathrm{MO}}_{C_{+}C_{-}}(u)\;=\;\sum_{\alpha\in\Delta_{\mathrm{wall}}}
  \frac{\Res_{u=u_{\alpha}}\phi^{+}_{UV}(u)}
  {u-u_{\alpha}}
\]
with $\Delta_{\mathrm{wall}}$ the walls of the $\sigma$-stability cone
$C_{\sigma}$, $u_{\alpha}$ their spectral footprints. The YBE on
$R^{\mathrm{MO}}$ follows from the cocycle condition
$\tilde\phi_{VW}\tilde\phi_{UV}=\tilde\phi_{UW}+\delta h_{UVW}$ on
triple overlaps, with $h_{UVW}\to 0$ at the residues (cyclic-$A_{\infty}$
closure; Wave-17 B4, Cycle 4).

\subsection*{Summary.}

The MO $R$-matrix is the spectral residue of the Wave-17 B4 gluing
cocycle $\phi_{UV}(u)$ at walls of $\sigma$-stability:
$R^{\mathrm{MO}}_{C_{+}C_{-}}=\Res_{u=u_{\star}}\phi^{+}_{UV}(u)$. On
$\bC^{3}$ Jordan this is the Yang rational $R$-matrix; on the conifold,
the rank-2 instanton $R$-matrix of Klebanov--Witten via Smirnov. The
YBE for $R^{\mathrm{MO}}$ descends from the Koszul-homotopy closure of
$\tilde\phi$ at triple overlaps; the dynamical twist of Etingof--
Frenkel--Kirillov is the regular part of $\phi^{+}_{UV}(u)$ away from
walls. The identification is compatible with
$\kappach(\bC^{3})=3/2$ flop-invariance and with the parity
$(\hbar,\varepsilon_{i})\mapsto(-\hbar,-\varepsilon_{i})$.
