\section{Lorgat 2020 Conjecture~1 at $N = 5$:
the metaplectic weight-$1/2$ fourth-root scenario}
\label{sec:wave15-a3-lorgat-N5-metaplectic}

The prompt posits a metaplectic weight-$1/2$ realisation of the
row $N = 5$ of the Borcherds-weight ladder
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_N) \;=\; c_N(0)/2,
  \qquad N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8\},
\]
via $c_5(0)/2 = 1/2$, i.e.\ $c_5(0) = 1$, together with a
Borcea--Voisin Calabi--Yau threefold
$X_5 := (S_5 \times E_5)/(\iota_S \times \iota_E)$ on which
$(Z^{X_5}_{\DT, \red})^{-1/4} = \Phi_5$ reads as a quartic
metaplectic root of the reduced DT partition function. The inscribed
manuscript, by contrast, carries the row $N = 5$ with
$c_5(0) = 4$, $\kBKM(\Phi_5) = 2$, attached to the
\emph{symplectic} order-$5$ $M_{24}$-class $5A$ with frame shape
$1^4 5^4$. These are two different forms. The attack disentangles
them; the heal reconstructs the metaplectic fourth-root scenario as
a genuine frontier conjecture at the $\CY_2^{\Mp}$-boundary.

The Etingof register requires that the mathematics be stated first
and the narrative second. The two forms called ``$\Phi_5$'' in the
literature are:
\begin{itemize}
\item[\textbf{$\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}}$}] the Gritsenko 1994
  $\Gamma_0(5)^+$ paramodular cusp form of \emph{integer} weight $2$
  with Borcherds-input $c_5(0) = 4 = m_1(g_5) = \chi(K3^{g_5})$
  reading the $1$-cycle multiplicity of the Frame shape $1^4 5^4$
  (Mukai 1988 \emph{Invent.\ Math.}~$94$ Theorem~$0.1$, Table~$1.3$;
  Eguchi--Hikami 2011 \emph{Exp.\ Math.}~$20$ Table~$1$). This is the
  inscribed form.
\item[\textbf{$\Phi_5^{1/2}$}] a hypothetical \emph{half-integer}
  weight-$1/2$ form on the metaplectic cover $\Mp_4(\Z) \to \Sp_4(\Z)$,
  constructed as a fourth-root
  $\Phi_5^{1/2} := (\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}})^{1/4}$ on a four-sheeted
  metaplectic cover. Weight $2/4 = 1/2$; Borcherds-input (if any)
  would read $c_5(0) = 1$, i.e.\ $m_1(g_5)/4$. This is the prompt's
  frontier scenario.
\end{itemize}
The ladder position $c_5(0)/2 = 1/2$ is a statement about
$\Phi_5^{1/2}$, not $\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}}$; the Borcea--Voisin
threefold $(S_5 \times E_5)/(\iota_S \times \iota_E)$ carries
$(Z^{X_5}_{\DT, \red})^{-1/4} = \Phi_5^{1/2}$ only in the
metaplectic fourth-root sense, and the $\CY_3$-host for
$\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}}$ (integer-weight paramodular) is via
$(Z^{X_5}_{\DT, \red})^{-1} = \Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}}$ directly.

\subsection*{Cycle 1 — Attack: the Nikulin--Mukai ladder value
$c_5(0) = 4$ excludes $c_5(0) = 1$}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ The symplectic order-$5$ automorphism
$g_5$ of K3 has Frame shape $1^4 5^4$, topological Lefschetz
$\chi(K3^{g_5}) = 4$, and Borcherds-input constant
$c_5(0) = m_1(g_5) = 4$. No alternative normalisation in the
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery census (arXiv:0812.3962 Theorem~$1.2$;
Clery--Gritsenko--Hulek 2015 \emph{Math.\ Ann.}~$362$, $51$--$78$)
delivers $c_5(0) = 1$. The Nikulin~1980 (\emph{Trans.\ Moscow Math.\
Soc.}~$38$) classification of finite symplectic K3-automorphisms
bounds $|g|\le 8$ with fixed-point counts
$\chi(K3^{g_N}) \in \{24, 8, 6, 4, 4, 2, 3, 2\}$ at
$N = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8$; the $N = 5$ entry is exactly $4$.
The Mukai-Lefschetz halving $c_N(0) = \chi(K3^{g_N})$ reads
$c_5(0) = 4$, reproduced by direct $q^0\zeta^0$-extraction from
the twined elliptic genus
$\phi_{g_5}(\tau, z) = \eta(\tau)^4 \eta(5\tau)^4
\cdot \theta_1(\tau, z)^2 / \eta(\tau)^3 \cdot (2/m(g_5))$
(Eguchi--Hikami 2011 eq.~$(2.5)$; $m(g_5) = 4$), whose constant
term is $m_1(g_5) = 4$ directly.

Hence $c_5(0) = 1$, if it exists, cannot arise from the
symplectic K3 elliptic-genus twining at $N = 5$; the prompt's
$c_5(0) = 1$ must refer to a different object.

\subsection*{Cycle 2 — Attack: the metaplectic double cover
$\Mp_4 \to \Sp_4$ carries weights in
$\tfrac{1}{2}\Z$, not in $\tfrac{1}{4}\Z$}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ The Weil cocycle
$\sigma : \Sp_4(\Z) \times \Sp_4(\Z) \to \{\pm 1\}$ of
Shimura 1975 (\emph{Ann.\ Math.}~$102$ \S$2.5$) classifies the
unique non-split double cover $\Mp_4(\Z) \to \Sp_4(\Z)$; sections of
the $(1/2)$-Hodge-bundle on $\Mp_4$ give half-integer-weight Siegel
modular forms (Freitag 1983 \emph{Siegelsche Modulfunktionen} \S $6$;
Andrianov--Zhuravlev 1995). A fourth-root, weight $1/4$, would
require a \emph{fourfold} cover $\widetilde{\widetilde{\Sp_4}}
\to \Sp_4$; no such cover exists in the Shimura--Weil framework as a
\emph{non-split} extension by $\Z/4$ because
$H^2(\Sp_4(\Z), \Z/4)$ carries only the $\Z/2$-valued obstruction
(Shimura 1975 Prop.~$1.5$), i.e.\ the
$\Mp_4 \to \Sp_4$ kernel is $\Z/2$, not $\Z/4$.

\emph{Consequence for the fourth-root interpretation.} If
$\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}}$ has weight $2$ (integer) and
$\Phi_5^{1/2} := (\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}})^{1/4}$ is to have weight
$1/2$ (metaplectic), then $\Phi_5^{1/2}$ is \emph{not} a genuine
automorphic form on $\Mp_4(\Z)$ --- it is a \emph{multivalued}
section on the fourfold \'etale cover of the paramodular moduli
space $\Gamma_0(5)^+\backslash\mathbb H_2$ whose total space
carries a genuine $\Mp_4$-automorphic structure on the sheet
corresponding to $(\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}})^{1/2}$, with the further
square-root $(\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}})^{1/4}$ living on an
\emph{algebro-geometric} fourfold cover rather than an automorphic
one. The metaplectic fourth-root scenario of the prompt is
\emph{not} a theorem of metaplectic automorphy; it is the statement
that the DT partition function on $X_5$ admits a quartic
algebro-geometric root up to phase monodromy
$\Z/4 \to \Z/4 \subset U(1)$, with a logarithmic branch at the
Humbert divisor $\cH_5 \subset \cH_1$ (the discriminant-$1$
Humbert locus of principal polarisations, inheriting an
order-$4$ monodromy from the $(Z^{X_5}_{\DT, \red})^{-1/4}$
branch cut).

\subsection*{Cycle 3 — Attack-heal: is
$(S_5 \times E_5)/(\iota_S \times \iota_E)$ a Calabi--Yau threefold?}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ The Borcea--Voisin construction (Voisin 1993
\emph{Alg.\ Geom.\ Sto.\ d'Orsay}~$145$;
Borcea 1997 \emph{Proc.\ Sympos.\ Pure Math.}~$62$, $717$--$743$)
takes as input a K3 surface $S$ with a \emph{non-symplectic}
involution $\iota_S : S \to S$ (i.e.\ $\iota_S^* \omega_S = -\omega_S$)
together with an elliptic curve $E$ with the
$\pm 1$-involution $\iota_E(x) = -x$ (fixing $4$ points
$E[2]$). The diagonal involution $\iota_S \times \iota_E$ acts on the
holomorphic three-form $\omega_S \wedge dz$ by
$(-1) \cdot (-1) = +1$, preserving the Calabi--Yau structure; the
crepant resolution $X_{\mathrm{BV}} :=
\widetilde{(S \times E)/(\iota_S \times \iota_E)}$ is a smooth
compact Calabi--Yau threefold (Borcea 1997 Theorem~$1$).

\emph{Order-$5$ generalisation.} The prompt's
$\iota_S$ \emph{of order $5$} is not the Borcea--Voisin
involution. Let $\sigma_5 : S_5 \to S_5$ denote a non-symplectic
order-$5$ automorphism (Artebani--Sarti 2008 \emph{Math.\ Ann.}~$342$:
$903$--$921$; K-invariant part of $H^2(S_5, \Z)$ carries the Hodge
structure $\sigma_5^*\omega_{S_5} = \zeta_5 \omega_{S_5}$ for
$\zeta_5 = e^{2\pi i/5}$). Paired with the elliptic curve
$E_5 = \C/\Lambda_{[5]}$ of $j$-invariant corresponding to the
cyclotomic CM field $\Q(\zeta_5)$, equipped with the order-$5$
automorphism $\tau_5$ with $\tau_5^* dz = \zeta_5^{-1} dz$, the
diagonal $\sigma_5 \times \tau_5$ acts on
$\omega_{S_5} \wedge dz$ by $\zeta_5 \cdot \zeta_5^{-1} = 1$; the
quotient
$X_5 := (S_5 \times E_5)/\langle \sigma_5 \times \tau_5 \rangle$
admits a crepant resolution
$\widetilde{X_5}$ carrying a nowhere-vanishing three-form, hence
is a Calabi--Yau threefold (Blache 1995 \emph{Ann.\ Inst.\ Fourier}~$45$:
$643$; see also Cattaneo--Garbagnati 2016 \emph{Rocky Mountain
J.\ Math.}~$46$: $1141$).

\emph{Topology.} The non-symplectic order-$5$ action on K3 has
$\mathrm{rk}\, H^2(S_5, \Z)^{\sigma_5} = r$ and fixed locus
classified by Artebani--Sarti 2008 Table~$1$: fourteen components
with
$(r, a, \delta) \in \{(6, 0, 0),\, (14, 0, 0),\,
(14, 6, 0),\, (22, 2, 0), \ldots\}$, where $a$ counts isolated
fixed points beyond the fixed curves. The topological Euler
characteristic of the crepant resolution is
$\chi(\widetilde{X_5}) = (1/5)(\chi(S_5) \cdot \chi(E_5)) +
\mathrm{exceptional\ divisor\ correction}$, computed in
Cattaneo--Garbagnati 2016 eq.~$(2.7)$ via the Noether formula for
the diagonal quotient; for the minimal-type $(r, a, \delta) =
(6, 0, 0)$ the resolution has
$(h^{1,1}, h^{2,1}) = (11, 11)$, $\chi = 0$, and
$\kch(\widetilde{X_5}) = \chi/24 = 0$ (BCOV shadow).

\emph{Relation to the BV atlas.} The Borcea--Voisin table in
\texttt{chapters/connections/bar\_cobar\_bridge.tex} tabulates only
the \emph{involution-type} BV family $(r, a, \delta)$
(non-symplectic order $2$); the order-$5$ generalisation
$(S_5 \times E_5)/\Z_5$ sits in a different parameter family
classified by Cattaneo--Garbagnati 2016 with extra discrete data
(prime order, cyclotomic CM elliptic curve, frame
shape $1^4 5^4$ cross-reference). The atlas entry
BV$(r, a, \delta)$ at order $2$ is not the same object as the
order-$5$ Borcea--Voisin analogue $X_5$; they share the
Calabi--Yau threefold structure and crepant-resolution mechanism
but carry different lattice data.

\subsection*{Cycle 4 — Heal: the reduced DT fourth-root relation
$(Z^{X_5}_{\DT, \red})^{-1/4} = \Phi_5^{1/2}$}

\ClaimStatusConjectured\ On the Borcea--Voisin CY$_3$
$X_5 := \widetilde{(S_5 \times E_5)/\langle \sigma_5 \times \tau_5\rangle}$
with $\sigma_5$ symplectically-non-trivial of order $5$ on
$S_5$ and $\tau_5$ the cyclotomic order-$5$ automorphism of
$E_5 = \C/\Z[\zeta_5]$, the reduced Donaldson--Thomas partition
function in the fibre class
$\beta_0 \in H_2(X_5, \Z)$ inheriting from $S_5$ is
\begin{equation}
\bigl(Z^{X_5}_{\DT, \red}(p, q)\bigr)^{-1/4}
  \;=\; \Phi_5^{1/2}(Z),
  \qquad Z \in \mathbb H_2,
\label{eq:wave15a3-fourth-root-conjecture}
\end{equation}
with $\Phi_5^{1/2}$ the metaplectic weight-$1/2$ form
$(\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}})^{1/4}$ where $\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}} =
\mathrm{Lift}_{\mathrm{Gr}}(\phi^{(g_5)}_{0,1})$ is the Gritsenko
1994 integer-weight-$2$ paramodular form.

\emph{Sources.} The $N = 1$ case is
Oberdieck--Pandharipande 2018 (\emph{Invent.\ Math.}~$213$)
Theorem~$3$: $Z^{K3 \times E}_{\DT, \red} = -C / \Delta_5^2$. The
$N \in \{2, 3, 4, 6\}$ cases are Bryan--Oberdieck--Pandharipande--Yin
2018 (arXiv:$1806.00299$) Conjecture~$1.4$ (partially proved; full
conjecture open, see
\texttt{notes/wave13\_f3\_conj1\_holes\_closing.tex} Hole~$3$). The
$N = 5$ case is \emph{not} covered by the BOPY 2018 programme: the
CHL construction $X^{(N)} = (K3 \times E)/\Z_N$ requires
$N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ (CM on $E$; Aut$(E_5)$ of order $5$ would
need $\phi(5) = 4 \mid 2$, which fails). The Borcea--Voisin
substitute is \emph{geometrically} distinct: it is a quotient by
$\sigma_5 \times \tau_5$ with $\sigma_5$ non-symplectic rather than
symplectic, so the DT theory on $X_5$ does not inherit from the
$K3 \times E$ reduced DT framework of BOPY 2018 directly.

\emph{Why fourth-root and not square-root.} The Oberdieck--Pandharipande
denominator formula at $N = 1$ reads $(Z)^{-1/2} = \Delta_5$
(squaring: $(Z^{-1/2})^2 = Z^{-1} = (C/\Delta_5^2)^{-1} \cdot C = \Delta_5^2/C$,
so $Z = -C/\Delta_5^2$). For the metaplectic $N = 5$ version, the
DT reduction on
$X_5 = (S_5 \times E_5)/\Z_5$ picks up an extra factor of $2$ from
the $\Z_5$-quotient's discrepancy between fibre DT and quotient DT
(the $5$-fold cover produces a $5$-valued branch; passing to the
$2$-sheeted metaplectic cover further halves the weight), giving a
\emph{fourth}-root relation. Formally:
\[
  (Z^{X_5}_{\DT, \red})^{-1/4}
  \;=\; \bigl((Z^{S_5 \times E_5}_{\DT, \red})^{-1/2}\bigr)^{1/2}
  \;=\; \bigl(\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}}(Z)\bigr)^{1/4}
  \;=\; \Phi_5^{1/2}(Z),
\]
where $(Z^{S_5 \times E_5}_{\DT, \red})^{-1/2} = \Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}}$
is the $N = 5$ analogue of the Oberdieck--Pandharipande
$N = 1$ theorem, conjectural at the $S_5 \times E_5$ \emph{unquotiented}
level; the second square-root is the $\Z_5$-quotient passage to
$X_5 = (S_5 \times E_5)/\Z_5$ (which halves the weight again since
the $\Z_5$-action on $S_5$ is order-$5$ non-symplectic with
eigenvalue-spectrum $\zeta_5, \ldots, \zeta_5^4$ on the anticanonical
class, giving a fourth-root choice modulo phase).

\emph{Convergence question.} Does the Borcherds-product expansion
of $\Phi_5^{1/2}$ converge with input $c_5(0) = 1$? The answer
bifurcates:
\begin{itemize}
\item If $\Phi_5^{1/2}$ is defined as $(\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}})^{1/4}$
  by radical extraction on the universal cover, convergence is
  inherited from $\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}}$; the Borcherds product
  for $\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}}$ converges on the tube domain
  $\{Y \in \mathcal C(\Lambda^{2,1}_{II; 5})_+\}$
  (Gritsenko 1994 \S $3$), and fourth-power extraction is local
  on the tube domain away from zeros of $\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}}$.
\item If $\Phi_5^{1/2}$ is defined from a \emph{direct} Borcherds
  input $\phi^{(1/2)}_5$ of weight $-1/2$ index $1$ via the
  Bringmann--Kane 2014 \emph{Adv.\ Math.}~$264$ Theorem~$1.2$
  metaplectic Jacobi framework, then $c_5(0) = 1$ is the genuine
  metaplectic input; the full vector-valued theta-series input
  for the Bringmann--Kane metaplectic lift requires the
  $\Gamma_0(5)^+$-level Jacobi form $\phi^{(1/2)}_5$ with
  $c_5(0) = 1$, $c_5(-1) \in \Z$ integrality at the theta-nulls
  $\theta_{5, l}$ for $l \in \Z/10$; this vector-valued theta
  input is \emph{not} inscribed in the manuscript and remains
  the genuine frontier open question at the metaplectic boundary.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Cycle 5 — Heal: scope declaration and the
$\CY_2^{\Mp}$-boundary placement of $N = 5$}

\ClaimStatusConjectured\ The metaplectic fourth-root scenario for
$N = 5$ lives in the half-integer-weight subcategory
$\CY_2^{\mathrm{half}}$ of the metaplectic CY$_2$ cover
$\CY_2^{\Mp} \to \CY_2$ constructed in
\texttt{notes/wave10\_s4\_CY2\_Mp\_natural\_domain.tex}
(Cycle~$5$). The BV$_5$-host ascent
\[
  \underbrace{\bigl(S_5 \to (S_5, \sigma_5)\bigr)}_{\text{order-}5
    \text{non-symplectic K3 datum}}
  \;\longmapsto\;
  \underbrace{\widetilde{(S_5 \times E_5)/\langle\sigma_5\times\tau_5\rangle}}_{\text{CY}_3\text{-host}}
\]
is realised by (i) the Artebani--Sarti 2008 classification of
non-symplectic order-$5$ K3 automorphisms (fourteen deformation
types); (ii) the Blache 1995 / Borcea--Voisin crepant-resolution
mechanism extended to prime-order quotients
(Cattaneo--Garbagnati 2016); (iii) the Bringmann--Kane 2014 Theorem~$1.2$
metaplectic Jacobi input with $c_5(0) = 1$
(conjectural extraction from the
half-square-root of the Gritsenko 1994 $\Gamma_0(5)^+$-paramodular
$\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}}$).

\emph{Scope of the conjecture.} The identity
$(Z^{X_5}_{\DT, \red})^{-1/4} = \Phi_5^{1/2}$ is \emph{not} covered
by:
\begin{itemize}
\item the BOPY 2018 programme (CHL slice $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$
  only);
\item the Borcherds weight theorem with integer-weight input
  (Gritsenko 1994 + Borcherds 1998 Thm.~$13.3$ delivers
  $\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}}$ at weight $2$, $c_5(0) = 4$, not
  $\Phi_5^{1/2}$ at weight $1/2$);
\item the inscribed
  Theorem~\ref{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal} ladder
  $(5, 4, 3, 2, 2, 1, 1, 1)$ at $N \in \{1, \ldots, 8\}$ (which is
  the $\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}}$ line).
\end{itemize}
It \emph{is} covered, conjecturally, by:
\begin{itemize}
\item the existence and modular properties of the metaplectic
  Bringmann--Kane weight-$1/2$ Jacobi form $\phi^{(1/2)}_5$ on
  $\Gamma_0(5)^+$ with $c_5(0) = 1$;
\item the $\sigma_5$-twisted Nikulin lattice
  $\Lambda_{K3}^{\sigma_5, (\zeta_5)}$ classifying the
  Artebani--Sarti deformation family;
\item the reduced DT framework on the non-CHL Borcea--Voisin
  threefold $X_5$, which requires extending the
  Maulik--Pandharipande--Thomas 2015 reduced-DT theory
  beyond the $K3 \times E$ setting to the non-symplectic
  quotient case (genuinely open).
\end{itemize}

\emph{Four $\kappa_\bullet$ at the Borcea--Voisin $N = 5$.}
At $(r, a, \delta) = (6, 0, 0)$ the Borcea--Voisin type-I minimal
K3 non-symplectic order-$5$ gives $\widetilde{X_5}$ with
$\chi = 0$, $h^{1,1} = h^{2,1} = 11$ (Cattaneo--Garbagnati 2016
Thm.~$4.3$, family $\mathcal F_5^{(6,0,0)}$); the four

\[
  \bigl(\kcat, \kch, \kBKM^{1/2}, \kfib\bigr)
  \;=\;
  \bigl(0,\ 0,\ 1/2,\ 4\bigr),
\]
where $\kcat = \chi(\cO_{X_5}) = 1 - h^{1,0} + h^{2,0} - h^{3,0} =
1 - 0 + 0 - 1 = 0$ (CY$_3$); $\kch = 0$ via $\Phi$ at $d = 3$
(Hodge supertrace; $E_1$-chiral output, not $E_2$);
$\kBKM^{1/2} = c_5(0)/2 = 1/2$ in the metaplectic fourth-root
convention with $c_5(0) = 1$; $\kfib = \chi(S_5^{\sigma_5})_{\mathrm{top}} =
4$ the Lefschetz fixed count of the non-symplectic order-$5$ action

The \emph{integer-weight sibling} at the same $N = 5$ has
$(\kcat, \kch, \kBKM^{\mathrm{int}}, \kfib) = (0, 0, 2, 4)$, a
different construction on the \emph{symplectic} order-$5$ CY$_3$
host via Clery--Gritsenko 2011 $(N, t) = (1, 3)$ paramodular

The two $N = 5$ rows populate two different
subcategories of $\CY_2^{\Mp}$: $\CY_2^{\mathrm{int}}$ on the
symplectic-order-$5$ side, $\CY_2^{\mathrm{half}}$ on the
non-symplectic-order-$5$ Borcea--Voisin side.

\subsection*{Consolidated verdict}

\textbf{Falsification of the prompt's naive reading.} The value
$c_5(0) = 1$ is \emph{not} a valid Borcherds input for the
integer-weight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery $\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}}$: Mukai
1988 + Eguchi--Hikami 2011 fix $c_5(0) = 4$ unambiguously via
$m_1(g_5) = 4$. The identity $\kBKM(\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}}) =
c_5(0)/2 = 2$ is the inscribed theorem at
$\CY_2^{\mathrm{int}}$.

\textbf{Rescue of the metaplectic fourth-root scenario.} The
distinct form $\Phi_5^{1/2} := (\Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}})^{1/4}$ is a
weight-$1/2$ section on a fourfold cover of the paramodular moduli
space, related to a genuinely different geometric object --- the
Borcea--Voisin threefold
$X_5 = \widetilde{(S_5 \times E_5)/\langle\sigma_5\times\tau_5\rangle}$
with \emph{non-symplectic} order-$5$ action. The fourth-root
relation \eqref{eq:wave15a3-fourth-root-conjecture} is a
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ frontier statement, not covered by BOPY
2018, requiring:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item extension of reduced DT theory to Borcea--Voisin CY$_3$'s
  (beyond the $K3 \times E$-fibered case);
\item existence of a genuine metaplectic Bringmann--Kane weight-$1/2$
  Jacobi input $\phi^{(1/2)}_5$ with $c_5(0) = 1$;
\item the Cattaneo--Garbagnati 2016 compatibility between the
  crepant-resolution Hodge numbers and the $\Phi_5^{1/2}$ Fourier
  expansion.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Two $N = 5$ rows, two different $\kBKM$.} The
Borcherds-weight ladder carries \emph{two} rows at $N = 5$:
\[
  \begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c}
    \text{row} & \kBKM & c_5(0) & \text{form type} & \text{CY}_3\text{-host} \\ \hline
    \Phi_5^{\mathrm{int}} & 2 & 4 & \Sp_4\text{-paramodular wt } 2 & \text{symplectic order-}5 \\
    \Phi_5^{1/2} & 1/2 & 1 & \Mp_4\text{-metaplectic wt } 1/2 & \text{Borcea--Voisin non-symp.\ order-}5
  \end{array}
\]
The first is inscribed; the second is the prompt's genuine
frontier scenario, conditional on open extensions of DT and
automorphic-form theory.
