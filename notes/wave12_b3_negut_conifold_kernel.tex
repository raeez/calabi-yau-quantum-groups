\section*{Negu\c{t}'s conifold shuffle kernel}
\label{sec:negut-conifold-kernel}

\paragraph{Kernel.}
For the Klebanov--Witten quiver of the resolved conifold
\[
  X_{\mathrm{con}}=\operatorname{Tot}\bigl(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-1)
  \oplus \mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-1)\bigr),
\]
Negu\c{t}'s shuffle presentation gives the off-diagonal kernel
\[
  \omega_{\mathrm{con}}(z,w)
  =
  \frac{(z-w+\epsilon_1)(z-w+\epsilon_2)}
       {(z-w)(z-w+\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)}.
\]
The parameters $\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2$ are the two fibre weights.  The
compact base direction is recorded by the Serre-dual obstruction weight
$-(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)$; there is no independent third affine weight.

\paragraph{Shuffle construction.}
Negu\c{t}, \emph{Quantum algebras and cyclic quiver varieties}
(\texttt{arXiv:1505.01528}), attaches to a quiver $Q$ with potential
$W$ the shuffle algebra
\[
  \mathcal S_Q
  =
  \bigoplus_{\mathbf d}
  \mathbb F(z_{i,1},\dots,z_{i,d_i})^{\prod_i S_{d_i}},
\]
with product defined by pairwise kernels $\omega_{ij}(z,w)$.  For toric
CY$_3$ quivers the critical positive CoHA embeds in this shuffle algebra
as the wheel-condition subalgebra.  The cyclic $A_0^{(1)}$ case is the
Jordan-quiver chart for $\mathbb C^3$; the cyclic $A_1^{(1)}$ case is
the Klebanov--Witten quiver for the conifold.

\paragraph{Klebanov--Witten input.}
The quiver has vertices $\{\circ,\bullet\}$, arrows
\[
  a_1,a_2:\circ\to\bullet,\qquad
  b_1,b_2:\bullet\to\circ,
\]
and potential
\[
  W=\operatorname{tr}(a_1b_1a_2b_2-a_1b_2a_2b_1).
\]
The two arrows $\circ\to\bullet$ have weights
$\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2$.  They contribute the numerator factors
$(z-w+\epsilon_1)$ and $(z-w+\epsilon_2)$.  The diagonal
$\operatorname{Ext}^1(\mathcal O_\circ,\mathcal O_\bullet)$ contributes
the pole at $z=w$.  Serre duality on the CY$_3$ quiver contributes the
obstruction pole at $z-w=-(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)$.  This gives
\[
  \omega_{\circ\bullet}(z,w)
  =
  \frac{(z-w+\epsilon_1)(z-w+\epsilon_2)}
       {(z-w)(z-w+\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)}.
\]
Changing orientation replaces $+\epsilon_a$ by $-\epsilon_a$ in the
corresponding off-diagonal kernel; the displayed convention is the
$\circ\to\bullet$ convention.

\paragraph{Comparison with the smooth affine chart.}
For the Jordan quiver of $\mathbb C^3$, the Schiffmann--Vasserot kernel
in additive variables is
\[
  k_{\mathbb A^2}(z)
  =
  \frac{(z+t_1)(z+t_2)(z-t_1-t_2)}{z},
\]
with $t_3=-t_1-t_2$.  This is the smooth affine threefold case and
produces
\[
  \CoHA(\mathbb C^3)\cong Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1),
\]
the positive half of the affine Yangian.  The full Yangian and the
$\mathcal W_{1+\infty}$ vacuum action appear after doubling and
evaluation, not at the CoHA output itself.  The conifold kernel is a
different toric CY$_3$ shuffle kernel: one affine numerator direction is
replaced by the Serre-obstruction denominator associated with the compact
curve $\mathbb P^1$.

\paragraph{Conifold invariant.}
The resolved conifold is a non-compact toric CY$_3$ with one compact
curve class.  The local-surface formula for
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$ belongs to geometries of the form
\(\operatorname{Tot}(K_S)\).  The direct McKay calculation
uses the Klebanov--Witten two-vertex quiver and its single compact
generator $\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}$; under the framed $d=3$
CY-to-chiral assignment this generator gives one super-Heisenberg mode:
\[
  \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(A_{X_{\mathrm{con}}})=1.
\]
This is the conifold value in the toric CY$_3$ table and is independent
of the compact Hodge supertrace $\chi(\mathcal O_X)$.

\paragraph{External checks.}
Feigin--Jimbo--Mukhin--Vilkoviskiy 2021
(\texttt{arXiv:2112.14687}) write the quantum toroidal
$\widehat{\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}}_2$ shuffle kernel in multiplicative
variables; its additive limit gives the same two numerator factors and
the same Serre-dual denominator after global sign normalisation.
Awata--Feigin--Shiraishi 2011 supply the parallel smooth
$\mathbb C^3$ structure function
$(1-q_1z)(1-q_2z)(1-q_3z)/(1-z)$ with $q_1q_2q_3=1$; it is a comparison
for the affine chart. The conifold denominator comes from the
Klebanov--Witten two-vertex kernel above.

\paragraph{Proof obligation.}
The remaining local calculation is the line-by-line identification of
Negu\c{t}'s wheel conditions for $A_1^{(1)}$ with the Drinfeld-current
relations of
\[
  U^+_{q_1,q_2}(\widehat{\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}}_2).
\]
FJMV 2021 gives the quantum-toroidal side, and Negu\c{t} 2015 gives the
shuffle side; the explicit wheel-to-current matching for the conifold
case is the missing comparison.
