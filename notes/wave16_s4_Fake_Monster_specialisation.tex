\section{Fake Monster Denominator and the Dimension-Five Boundary}
\label{wn:sec:fake-monster-specialisation}

The Fake Monster Lie algebra $\gFM$ of Borcherds~\cite{Borcherds1990Invent}
lives on the Lorentzian even unimodular lattice
\[
  \IIlor=\Leech\oplus U,
  \qquad \operatorname{rk}\IIlor=26,
  \qquad \operatorname{sign}\IIlor=(25,1),
\]
and has denominator the Borcherds product $\PhiFM$ of weight $12$ on
$\mathrm{O}^{+}(\IIpar)$. This is the Fake-Monster product attached to
$\eta(\tau)^{-24}$; it is not the K3 denominator $\Delta_5$, and it is
not the Monster denominator attached to the Frenkel--Lepowsky--Meurman
module $V^\natural$.

The K3 BKM object $\gDelta$ lives on the rank-$3$ hyperbolic lattice
$U\oplus\langle -2\rangle$ and has denominator $\Delta_5$ of weight $5$.
The Fake-Monster lattice contains the rank-$24$ positive-definite Leech
piece. A single K3 fibre supplies at most a rank-$20$ algebraic
positive-definite surface lattice. Thus the K3-fibre CY$_3$ route
constructs $\gDelta$ rather than $\gFM$.

\subsection{Borcherds Normalisation}
\label{wn:subsec:fake-monster-borcherds-normalisation}

The input at the Fake-Monster cusp is
\[
  \eta(\tau)^{-24}
  =
  q^{-1}\prod_{n\geq 1}(1-q^n)^{-24}
  =
  q^{-1}+24+324q+\cdots .
\]
Write $c_{\eta^{-24}}(0)=24$ for its constant coefficient after the
principal term. Borcherds' weight theorem for the singular theta lift
gives
\[
  \kBKM(\PhiFM)
  =
  \operatorname{wt}(\PhiFM)
  =
  \frac{c_{\eta^{-24}}(0)}{2}
  =
  12.
\]
This formula is a Borcherds/Fake-Monster normalisation statement. It
does not compute $\kch$, $\kcat$, or $\kfib$, and it does not identify
$\PhiFM$ with $\Delta_5$ or with the Monster denominator.

\subsection{K3-Fibre Rank Obstruction}
\label{wn:subsec:k3-fibre-rank-obstruction}

\begin{proposition}[Single-K3-fibre obstruction for $\gFM$]
\label{wn:prop:k3-fibre-fake-monster-obstruction}
\index{Fake-Monster!K3-fibre obstruction}
Let $X\to C$ be a smooth projective K3-fibred CY$_3$ and let
$Y$ be a smooth K3 fibre. Any Stage-$2$ specialisation
$\Sp_{Y,C}(\mathcal F_X)$ whose real-root lattice is supplied by the
algebraic surface part of the Mukai lattice of $Y$ cannot realise
$\gFM$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The algebraic Mukai lattice of a projective K3 surface $Y$ is
\[
  \widetilde H_{\mathrm{alg}}(Y,\mathbb Z)
  =
  H^0(Y,\mathbb Z)\oplus \operatorname{NS}(Y)\oplus H^4(Y,\mathbb Z),
\]
of rank $2+\rho(Y)$ with $\rho(Y)\leq 20$. The two extremal summands
$H^0$ and $H^4$ form the hyperbolic Mukai plane. The positive-definite
algebraic surface part has rank at most $20$.

Borcherds' Fake-Monster construction uses the Leech lattice $\Leech$ as
the positive-definite rank-$24$ summand in
$\IIlor=\Leech\oplus U$. A rank-$24$ positive-definite Leech lattice
cannot be supplied by a rank-$20$ algebraic surface lattice. Hence the
single-K3-fibre CY$_3$ specialisation cannot realise the Fake-Monster
real-root lattice.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Scope of the obstruction]
\label{wn:rmk:fake-monster-cy3-scope}
The proposition excludes the K3-fibre route. It is not a global theorem
excluding every smooth proper CY$_3$ category. Such a theorem would have
to control all possible rank-$24$ positive-definite algebraic root
lattices in the relevant Hochschild or Hall charge lattice and prove that
none can carry the Leech summand of $\IIlor$.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Dimension-Five Candidate}
\label{wn:subsec:fake-monster-d5-candidate}

\begin{conjecture}[Dimension-five Fake-Monster comparison template]
\label{wn:conj:d5-k3-k3-e}
\index{Fake-Monster!$d=5$ candidate}
Let
\[
  X=K3_1\times K3_2\times E,
  \qquad
  \Sigma_4=K3_1\times K3_2,
  \qquad
  C=E .
\]
Then $X$ is a smooth projective CY$_5$ and
\[
  \kcat(X)
  =
  \chi(\mathcal O_{K3_1})\chi(\mathcal O_{K3_2})\chi(\mathcal O_E)
  =
  2\cdot 2\cdot 0
  =
  0 .
\]
The Stage-$2$ specialisation
$\Sp_{\Sigma_4,E}(\mathcal F_X)$ is a comparison template for $\gFM$
after three additional data are supplied:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a $d=5$ Stage-$1$ functor $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_5$ with the required
Hall-comparison theorem;
\item a primitive embedding of a rootless rank-$24$ Niemeier summand
identified with $\Leech$ inside the doubled K3 Mukai charge lattice;
\item an $E_5$ formality and Dunn--Lurie factorisation model whose
curve specialisation has denominator $\PhiFM$.
\end{enumerate}
Without these data the product CY$_5$ is not a constructed compact
host for the Fake-Monster algebra. For $d\geq3$ the specialized chiral
output is $E_1$; an $E_2$ structure can be asserted on a centre or
Drinfeld double only after that centre or double has been constructed.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{proof}[Rank evidence]
For a projective K3 surface $Y$,
$\operatorname{rk}\widetilde H_{\mathrm{alg}}(Y,\mathbb Z)=2+\rho(Y)\leq
22$. At maximal Picard rank the direct sum of the two algebraic Mukai
lattices has rank $44$, enough rank to contain a rank-$24$ positive-definite
lattice once discriminant form, signature, and primitive embedding data
are fixed. The rank inequality is not the construction. Nikulin's
embedding theorems control the missing lattice-theoretic step after those
data are specified.

The elliptic factor supplies the reference curve of the Stage-$2$
specialisation. The conjecture remains conditional until the Leech
embedding, the Hall comparison, and the equality of the resulting
denominator with $\PhiFM$ are proved.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Niemeier selection]
\label{wn:rmk:niemeier-selection}
The Leech lattice is the unique rootless positive-definite even
unimodular lattice of rank $24$. Selecting it from the Niemeier list is
a genuine extra datum. It cannot be replaced by an automatic orthogonal
projection from $\Lambda(K3)^{\oplus 2}$, and it does not follow from
rank accommodation alone. The twenty-three rooted Niemeier lattices give
different Borcherds products and do not construct the Fake-Monster
denominator.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Harvey--Moore Template}
\label{wn:subsec:harvey-moore-template}

Harvey--Moore~\cite{HarveyMoore1996} compute the one-loop threshold
correction for the heterotic string on $K3\times T^2$. The resulting
singular theta lift belongs to the K3/Gritsenko--Nikulin side and
produces the $\Delta_5$ denominator. It does not construct $\gFM$.

A Fake-Monster analogue must replace the K3 elliptic-genus input by the
Leech input $\eta^{-24}$ and must land on
$\mathrm{O}^{+}(\IIpar)$ with product $\PhiFM$. In the CY-to-chiral
programme this is the dimension-five problem attached to
$K3_1\times K3_2\times E$, not a CY$_3$ witness.

\subsection{Sibling Boundary}
\label{wn:subsec:fake-monster-sibling-boundary}

The sibling picture separates three Borcherds objects:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
\text{object} & \text{denominator} & \text{lattice or host} & \text{dimension scope}\\
\hline
\gDelta & \Delta_5 & U\oplus\langle -2\rangle & d=3,\ K3\times E\\
\text{Monster BKM} & J(\sigma)-J(\tau) & V^\natural\text{ / Monster CFT} & d=3\text{ CFT host}\\
\gFM & \PhiFM & \IIlor=\Leech\oplus U & d=5\text{ candidate}
\end{array}
\]
Only the last row carries
$\kBKM(\PhiFM)=c_{\eta^{-24}}(0)/2=12$. The table records dimension
scope, not a construction of a compact CY$_3$ Fake-Monster witness.
