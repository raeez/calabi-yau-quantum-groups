\section*{hCS observables on $\C^3$ and the Hall positive half}

\subsection*{The three objects}

Let $\Obs_{\hCS}(\C^3)$ denote the factorisation algebra of quantum
observables of holomorphic Chern--Simons theory on $\C^3$ with abelian
gauge datum $\mathfrak{gl}_1$, in the BV formalism of
Costello--Gwilliam. Let $\CoHA(\C^3)$ denote the critical CoHA of the
one-vertex three-loop quiver with potential
\[
  W_3=x[y,z],
\]
equivalently the $0$-dimensional sheaf CoHA on $\C^3$. The comparison
uses three distinct structures.

First, $\Obs_{\hCS}^{\mathfrak{gl}_1}$ is a holomorphic factorisation
algebra in $\mathrm{Ch}(\Dolb)$ on the affine CY$_3$ chart. It is acted
on by holomorphic little $3$-disks. A locally constant $E_3$ algebra is
obtained only after a chosen topological transport or Dolbeault
contraction; that choice is extra data.

Second, $\CoHA(\C^3)$ is the associative Hall algebra
\[
  \CoHA(\C^3)
  =
  \bigoplus_{n\geq 0}
  H^{\BM}_{\mathrm{GL}_n\times T}\!\left(
    \Rep_n(\C\langle x,y,z\rangle),
    \varphi_{\mathrm{Tr}(x[y,z])}
  \right),
\]
with convolution along extensions of representations, vanishing-cycle
coefficients, and $T=(\C^\times)^3$ restricted by
$\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+\epsilon_3=0$ on the Calabi--Yau subtorus. After
the standard equivariant localisation and shuffle presentation, its
charge cone is $n\geq0$, hence it is the Hall positive half
\[
  \CoHA(\C^3)=\Ypos(\glhat_1),
\]
the positive half of the affine Yangian of $\glhat_1$ in the
Schiffmann--Vasserot and Tsymbaliuk normalisation.

Third, on the constructed local toric locus, the specialised
CY-to-chiral output $A_{\C^3}$ is an $E_1$-chiral algebra at $d=3$.
Braided $E_2$ data is carried by the Drinfeld centre of the $E_1$
representation category, equivalently by the completed Drinfeld double
when the Hall pairing, Cartan part, negative half, and completion have
been constructed.

\subsection*{Abelian hCS}

Holomorphic Chern--Simons on $\C^3$ with abelian gauge algebra
$\mathfrak{gl}_1$ has BV action
\[
  S(\alpha)=\frac{1}{2}\int_{\C^3}\alpha\,\bar\partial\alpha\wedge\Omega,
  \qquad
  \Omega=dz_1\wedge dz_2\wedge dz_3,
\]
on the shifted field space
$\alpha\in\Omega^{0,\ast}(\C^3)[1]$. The equation of motion is
$\bar\partial\alpha=0$. On affine $\C^3$, without compact-support
conditions, the Dolbeault--Poincare lemma gives the holomorphic
solution ring $\C[z_1,z_2,z_3]$. For a relatively compact Stein polydisc
$U\subset \C^3$ the compactly supported local dg Lie algebra is
\[
  L_{\hCS}(U)=\Omega^{0,\ast}_c(U)[1],
\]
with zero bracket in the abelian theory, and the classical observables
are continuous Chevalley--Eilenberg cochains
\[
  \Obs_{\hCS}^{\mathfrak{gl}_1,\mathrm{cl}}(U)
  \simeq
  C^\bullet_{\CE,\mathrm{cont}}\!\left(L_{\hCS}(U)\right)
  =
  \widehat{\operatorname{Sym}}\!\left(L_{\hCS}(U)^\vee[-1]\right),
\]
with differential induced by $\bar\partial$. Costello--Li construct the
renormalised BV quantisation in this holomorphic local model; for
$\mathfrak{gl}_1$ the cubic vertex vanishes, so the quantum correction is
the free BV correction. The $E_3$ label here belongs to the local
holomorphic factorisation object. The Hall positive half is associative
$E_1$, and the specialised $d=3$ chiral output is $E_1$.

\subsection*{The positive half}

After torus localisation the Hall product is the spherical shuffle
algebra. In the Jordan-quiver normalisation the affine-Yangian structure
polynomial is
\[
  G_{\C^3}(u)=
  \prod_{i=1}^{3}(u-\epsilon_i),
  \qquad
  \epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+\epsilon_3=0.
\]
With generator $e_0(z)=\sum_k e_{0,k}z^{-k-1}$, the positive currents
satisfy
\[
  \prod_{i=1}^{3}(z-w-\epsilon_i)e(z)e(w)
  =
  \prod_{i=1}^{3}(z-w+\epsilon_i)e(w)e(z).
\]
Schiffmann--Vasserot identify the resulting shuffle algebra with the
positive half $Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$ in the cohomological
normalisation. Tsymbaliuk gives the Drinfeld-current presentation of
the corresponding affine Yangian. Rap\v{c}ak--Soibelman--Yang--Zhao give
the toric critical-CoHA statement for the vanishing-cycle model. This is
an associative $E_1$ Hall algebra. Vertex-algebra structure belongs to
the double, centre, completion, and evaluation layer.

\subsection*{Finite hCS--Hall comparison}

\begin{definition}[Finite oriented Rees hCS--Hall datum]
\label{def:c3-finite-oriented-rees-hcs-hall-datum}
A finite oriented Rees datum on $\C^3$ consists of:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a finite disjoint-Weiss/Ran (DWR/Ran) family of relatively
compact holomorphic polydiscs, with bounds $(N,r,L,m)$ and inclusions
closed under disjoint union;
\item the compactly supported hCS dg Lie algebras
$L_{\hCS}(U)=\Omega^{0,\ast}_c(U)[1]$ with the Costello--Gwilliam
factorisation products and the chosen Rees filtration;
\item the finite critical Hall complexes for the tripled Jordan quiver
with orientation line, vanishing-cycle sheaves, and equivariant
parameters satisfying $\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+\epsilon_3=0$;
\item a natural transformation
\[
  \Theta_{\hCS\to\mathrm{Hall}}^{\mathrm{Rees,or};N,r,L,m}
\]
from the finite hCS observable complex to the finite Rees Hall complex
which is compatible with refinements, Thom--Sebastiani for disjoint
unions, the orientation line, and the Hall extension correspondence
$\mathrm{Flag}^{n,m}(\C^3)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

The finite comparison is conditional on this datum.

\begin{theorem}[Finite Rees comparison on \texorpdfstring{$\C^3$}{C3}]
\label{thm:c3-finite-rees-hcs-hall-positive-half}
Assume the finite oriented Rees hCS--Hall datum of
Definition~\ref{def:c3-finite-oriented-rees-hcs-hall-datum}. Then the
finite comparison sends the holomorphic factorisation object attached
to the local toric $\C^3$ category to the Hall-valued assignment
\[
  U \longmapsto H^\ast_T(\Hilb^\bullet(U);\mathbb Q),
  \qquad U\subset \C^3,
\]
with disjoint-support product realised by the Schiffmann--Vasserot
convolution on $\mathrm{Flag}^{n,m}(\C^3)$. After Hall global sections
and the standard equivariant localisation, the target algebra is
\[
  \Gamma_{\mathrm{Hall}}\!\left(
    \Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\mathrm{Perf}(\C^3))
  \right)
  \simeq
  \CoHA(\C^3)
  =
  \Ypos(\glhat_1)
\]
as an associative Hall algebra at finite Rees level.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose the standard cyclic Darboux chart on $\C^3$ with volume form
$dz_1\wedge dz_2\wedge dz_3$ and potential
$W_3=\mathrm{Tr}(XYZ-XZY)$. The affine hCS input is the compact-support
Dolbeault dg Lie algebra with its CY pairing. The single affine chart
\(\C^3\) makes the local statement independent of transition cocycles.
The finite Rees datum supplies the comparison map and its orientation,
refinement, and Thom--Sebastiani compatibilities. These compatibilities
identify disjoint unions of finite Ran polydiscs with the Hall extension
correspondences $\mathrm{Flag}^{n,m}(\C^3)$, so the induced product is
the Schiffmann--Vasserot pull--push product. The last identification is
the critical-CoHA/shuffle theorem for the tripled Jordan quiver, giving
$\CoHA(\C^3)=\Ypos(\glhat_1)$ in the localised associative Hall
normalisation.
\end{proof}

The theorem asserts the finite Rees comparison above. A
quasi-isomorphism
$\Obs_{\hCS}^{\mathfrak{gl}_1}\simeq\CoHA(\C^3)$ of factorisation
algebras requires completion beyond finite Rees level, monoidal
realisation of vanishing cycles, compact-support descent, and
Drinfeld-double formation with their pairing and completion data. The
finite theorem fixes the positive-half Hall target in the local $\C^3$
bridge.

\subsection*{Character and modular characteristic}

The $T$-fixed points of $\Hilb^n(\C^3)$ are monomial ideals,
equivalently plane partitions of size $n$. In the framed cyclic module,
and in the localised PBW normalisation used for the positive half, the
graded character is
\[
  \sum_{n\geq 0}q^n
  \operatorname{rk}_{H^\ast_T(\mathrm{pt})}
  H^\ast_{T,\mathrm{crit}}
  \left(\Hilb^n(\C^3),\varphi_{\mathrm{CS}}\right)
  =
  M(q)
  =
  \prod_{k\geq 1}(1-q^k)^{-k}.
\]
This is MacMahon's plane-partition series. On the hCS side it is the
index reached after the finite Hall projection in
Theorem~\ref{thm:c3-finite-rees-hcs-hall-positive-half}. The character
identity is weaker than a quasi-isomorphism of factorisation algebras:
the map has to preserve the BV bracket, vanishing-cycle differential,
Hall correspondences, and equivariant normalisation.

For the non-compact space $\C^3$, $\kcat$ lies outside the compact
Hodge-supertrace convention. The constructed local toric $d=3$ output has
\[
  \kch(A_{\C^3})=1,
\]
in the regulated Heisenberg/free-boson normalisation. Borcherds
denominator data and $\kBKM$ belong to different constructions.

\subsection*{Non-abelian extension}

For gauge algebra $\mathfrak{gl}_N$, the interaction term in hCS is
\[
  \frac{1}{6}\int_{\C^3}\mathrm{tr}(\alpha[\alpha,\alpha])\wedge\Omega .
\]
Costello--Li and Costello--Gwilliam give the BV quantisation framework
on the holomorphic local model. A rank-$N$ theorem requires a solution
of the quantum master equation, including the vanishing or cancellation
of the local one-loop obstruction in the completed local BV complex. It
also requires a Hall comparison map to the relevant critical Hall
positive half and compatibility with the Hall product, the BV bracket,
the orientation line, and the equivariant normalisation. The
Maulik--Okounkov $R$-matrix and braided representation category arise
after the centre or double construction. The rank-one statement above
supplies the proved abelian local model; rank $N$ is a separate theorem
with the stated hypotheses.

\subsection*{Positive half, double, and vacuum module}

The passage from the Hall algebra to the vacuum-module action has three
typed arrows:
\[
  \CoHA(\C^3)=\Ypos(\glhat_1)
  \hookrightarrow
  D^\wedge\!\bigl(\Ypos(\glhat_1)\bigr)
  =
  Y(\glhat_1)
  \xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{ev}_\lambda\;}
  \End\!\left(\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]\text{-}\mathrm{vac}\right).
\]
The first arrow is the positive Borel inclusion after the negative half,
Cartan part, Hopf pairing, and completion are fixed. The second arrow is
a vacuum evaluation representation of the full affine Yangian. This is
the type-correct form of the $\C^3$ Hall-to-\(\mathcal W\) comparison.
