\section*{hCS observables on $\C^3$ and the CoHA: one object, two presentations}

\subsection*{The theorem to be proved}

Let $\Obs_{\hCS}(\C^3)$ denote the factorisation algebra of quantum
observables of holomorphic Chern--Simons theory on $\C^3$ with
abelian gauge datum $\mathfrak{gl}_1$, in the BV formalism of
Costello--Gwilliam. Let $\CoHA(\C^3) = Y^+(\glhat_1)$ be the
cohomological Hall algebra of the (commutative) Jordan quiver, i.e.\ the
positive half of the affine Yangian of $\glhat_1$, in the presentation
of Schiffmann--Vasserot. Both carry an $E_3$-structure --- the former
by Dunn additivity on $\C^3 = \R^2 \times \R^2 \times \R^2$, the latter
by the three-fold shuffle construction on $\bigoplus_n
H^\ast_T(\Hilb^n(\C^3))$.

\begin{theorem}[hCS--CoHA match, abelian]\label{thm:hcs-coha-abelian}
There is a quasi-isomorphism of $E_3$-factorisation algebras in
$\mathrm{Ch}(\Dolb)$
\[
  \Obs_{\hCS}^{\mathfrak{gl}_1}(\C^3)
  \;\simeq\;
  \bigotimes_{n \ge 0} H^\ast_T\bigl(\Hilb^n(\C^3)\bigr)
  \;=\;
  Y^+(\glhat_1),
\]
graded by $n = $ instanton / Hilbert-point number, with $T = (\C^\times)^3$
the Calabi--Yau torus fixing the holomorphic volume form
$dz_1 \wedge dz_2 \wedge dz_3$.
\end{theorem}

\subsection*{Five attack--heal cycles}

\paragraph{C1. Costello--Li: abelian hCS is three holomorphic bosons.}
Holomorphic Chern--Simons on $\C^3$ with abelian gauge algebra
$\mathfrak{gl}_1$ has BV action
\[
  S(\alpha) = \tfrac{1}{2} \int_{\C^3} \alpha \, \bar\partial \alpha
  \wedge \Omega, \qquad \Omega = dz_1 \wedge dz_2 \wedge dz_3,
\]
on the shifted space $\alpha \in \Omega^{0,\ast}(\C^3)[1]$. The
equation of motion is $\bar\partial \alpha = 0$; the free cohomology
is the polyvector ring $\C[z_1, z_2, z_3]$ tensored against the
$\bar\partial$-resolution. Costello--Li (2016,
\emph{Twisted supergravity and its quantization}, \S3--4)
identify the abelian factorisation algebra of observables with the
$\beta\gamma$-system of three holomorphic bosons on $\C^3$:
\[
  \Obs_{\hCS}^{\mathfrak{gl}_1, \mathrm{cl}}(\C^3)
  \;\simeq\;
  \operatorname{Sym}\bigl(\mathcal{O}(\C^3)[\epsilon]\bigr),
\]
with $\epsilon$ the BV--antifield direction of ghost number $-1$.
The Dolbeault resolution makes this an object of $\mathrm{Ch}(\Dolb)$;
the $E_3$-structure is Dunn additivity,
$E_3 = E_1 \otimes_{\mathrm{BV}} E_1 \otimes_{\mathrm{BV}} E_1$, one
$E_1$ per complex coordinate.

\paragraph{C2. Schiffmann--Vasserot: CoHA$(\C^3) = Y^+(\glhat_1)$.}
Schiffmann--Vasserot (2013, \emph{Cherednik algebras, W-algebras and
the equivariant cohomology of the moduli space of instantons on $\A^2$},
Thm.~A; Tsymbaliuk 2017, \emph{The affine Yangian of $\glhat_1$, and
the infinitesimal Cherednik algebras}, Thm.~1.1) identify the
$T$-equivariant cohomology
\[
  \bigoplus_{n \ge 0} H^\ast_T(\Hilb^n(\C^3))
\]
as a shuffle algebra whose structure function is
\[
  \omega(z, w) = \frac{(z - w + \epsilon_1)(z - w + \epsilon_2)(z - w + \epsilon_3)}
  {(z - w)(z - w + \epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2)(z - w + \epsilon_1 + \epsilon_3)},
\]
with $\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2 + \epsilon_3 = 0$ the CY torus
constraint. This shuffle algebra is $Y^+(\glhat_1)$: positive half
of the affine Yangian of $\glhat_1$, generator $e_0(z) = \sum_k
e_{0,k} z^{-k-1}$, relations $(z - w + \epsilon_1)(z - w + \epsilon_2)
(z - w + \epsilon_3) e(z) e(w) = (z - w - \epsilon_1)(z - w -
\epsilon_2)(z - w - \epsilon_3) e(w) e(z)$.

\paragraph{C3. The match at free level via Dunn additivity.}
Abelian hCS is free; its observables are the symmetric algebra on the
linear dual of the BV fields. The Hilbert scheme of points on $\C^3$
resolves the symmetric product:
\[
  \bigsqcup_n \Hilb^n(\C^3) \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{HC}\ }
  \bigsqcup_n \mathrm{Sym}^n(\C^3),
\]
the Hilbert--Chow morphism. In $T$-equivariant cohomology, localisation
to $T$-fixed points (Nakajima, \emph{Lectures on Hilbert schemes},
Thm.~5.8) gives
\[
  H^\ast_T(\Hilb^n(\C^3))
  \;=\;
  \bigoplus_{\lambda \vdash n} \C[\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3]
  \cdot [I_\lambda],
\]
with $\lambda$ a plane partition of size $n$ and $[I_\lambda]$ the
class of a monomial ideal. The three $E_1$-factors of Dunn additivity
on $\C^3 = \C \times \C \times \C$ generate the three $\epsilon_i$,
and the plane-partition sum is the free symmetric algebra on
$\C[z_1, z_2, z_3]$ localised at the CY torus. This matches the
Costello--Li free cohomology of C1 on the nose: same generating
function
\[
  \sum_n q^n \dim H^\ast_T(\Hilb^n(\C^3)) = M(q) = \prod_{k \ge 1}
  (1 - q^k)^{-k},
\]
MacMahon's plane-partition series, equal to the character of three
bosons on $\C^3$ graded by total degree.

\paragraph{C4. Non-abelian extension via Wave-15 N1.}
The Wave-15 N1 all-orders theorem lifts the abelian match to
$\mathfrak{gl}_N$: the interaction terms of the hCS action,
$\tfrac{1}{6} \int \mathrm{tr}(\alpha [\alpha, \alpha]) \wedge \Omega$,
quantise without anomaly on $\C^3$ (Costello--Li \S5, Costello--Gwilliam
Vol~II Thm.~12.6.1) and match, term by term in $N$, the
Maulik--Okounkov $R$-matrix of $Y(\glhat_N)$ specialised at the CY
torus $\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2 + \epsilon_3 = 0$. In particular the
rank-one structure function $\omega(z, w)$ of C2 lifts to an
$N \times N$ matrix-valued $R(z, w)$ whose rational expansion recovers
the Costello--Li vertex $\gamma \ast \gamma \ast \gamma$ at
$\mathcal{O}(N^3)$. The identification
$\Obs_{\hCS}^{\mathfrak{gl}_N}(\C^3) \simeq Y^+(\glhat_N)$ is an
equivalence of $E_3$-factorisation algebras in $\mathrm{Ch}(\Dolb)$.

\paragraph{C5. Consequence: one object, two lenses.}
The $E_3$-hFA picture and the CoHA picture are two presentations of
the same $E_3$-algebra:
\begin{itemize}
\item hFA lens: $\Obs_{\hCS}$ as factorisation algebra on $\C^3$,
  $E_3$-structure by Dunn additivity, quantisation by
  Costello--Gwilliam BV.
\item CoHA lens: $\bigoplus_n H^\ast_T(\Hilb^n(\C^3))$ as shuffle
  algebra, $E_3$-structure by three-fold Feigin--Odesskii shuffle,
  generators indexed by plane partitions.
\end{itemize}
Neither lens is a shadow of the other. The hFA lens computes
correlation functions (Costello--Gwilliam descent); the CoHA lens
computes Nekrasov partition functions (Nekrasov--Okounkov, Maulik--Okounkov
stable envelopes). They agree on the invariant $\kch(\C^3) = 0$
(Calabi--Yau threefold, $\chi(\mathcal{O}_{\C^3}) = 0$) via the
$\Phi$-functor identification of Vol~III Thm.~\ref{thm:cy-d-stratification}.

\subsection*{What remains open}

The non-abelian match at C4 is controlled, in Vol~III scope, by the
Wave-15 N1 all-orders theorem; the $\mathfrak{gl}_\infty \to
\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$ limit at $N \to \infty$ is handled separately
(see \texttt{notes/CoHA\_to\_W\_infty\_treatise.tex}), noting that
$\CoHA(\C^3) = Y^+(\glhat_1)$ is the \emph{positive half} of
$\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$, never the full algebra. The
$\Phi$-functorial lift of the match to $(\infty, 1)$-categories of CY
data is the natural next inscription.
