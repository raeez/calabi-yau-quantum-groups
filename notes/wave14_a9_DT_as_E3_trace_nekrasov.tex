Donaldson--Thomas theory, Gromov--Witten theory, stable-pair theory,
Nekrasov partition functions, and factorisation-algebra traces carry
different inputs.  The useful statement is a comparison theorem after
the relevant completions and orientations have been supplied.

Let \(X\) be a smooth Calabi--Yau threefold and let
\(\cC=\Perf(X)\).  The two-stage CY-to-chiral construction has a local
middle factorisation-algebra object
\[
  \cF_X := \PhiFA_3(\cC)
  \simeq_{\mathrm{loc}} \Obs^q_{\hCS}(X)
  \in \Ethree\text{-}\mathrm{HolFA}(X),
\]
under a solved quantum master equation, a counterterm scheme, and the
required analytic completion.  A curve specialisation gives
\[
  \Sp_{\Sigma_2,C}(\cF_X)=\Phi_3(\cC)
  \in \Eone\text{-}\mathrm{ChirAlg}(C).
\]
For \(d\geq 3\) this specialised output is \(\Eone\)-chiral.
\(\Etwo\)-structures enter at the derived centre, Drinfeld double, or
module-category layer.

\subsection*{Local Holomorphic \texorpdfstring{$E_3$}{E3} Data and
Compact Trace Data}
\label{subsec:local-e3-compact-trace-data}

The local hCS observable algebra and the compact enumerative trace are
separate objects.  Locally, \(\Obs^q_{\hCS}\) is an
\(\Ethree\)-holomorphic factorisation algebra after the BV
quantisation has solved the \(\QME\).  Globally, a DT trace requires a
trace-class functional on completed observables.  The required input is:
\[
  (\QME,\ \text{quartic anomaly cancellation},\
  \text{counterterms},\ \text{orientation},\
  \text{properness},\ \text{cyclic trace},\
  \text{descent and sewing},\ \text{completion}).
\]
In complex dimension \(3\), the local obstruction slot for the hCS
\(\QME\) has quartic valence.  A quadratic invariant fixes the gauge
pairing data; the trace comparison rests on the quartic local cocycle
and its counterterm trivialisation.

\begin{proposition}[Five inputs for a DT trace]
\label{wn:prop:five-inputs-dt-trace}
A numerical equality between a DT partition function and a trace value
on \(\cF_X\) follows from five pieces of data:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a local \(\Ethree\)-holomorphic hCS observable algebra on \(X\);
\item a cyclic pairing and orientation line on the completed compact
field theory;
\item proper derived moduli carrying the Behrend, virtual, or reduced
virtual integration class;
\item Hall or CoHA integration maps compatible with the chosen
stability chamber;
\item a comparison map from hCS observables to the DT, PT, or GW
moduli insertion being integrated.
\end{enumerate}
The stage-\(2\) chiral object \(\Phi_3(\cC)\) is the
\(\Eone\)-chiral output on the reference curve.  Centre, double, and
module enhancements are the \(\Etwo\)-layers; the scalar DT trace
remains a completed numerical value.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The first item is local Costello--Gwilliam hCS quantisation.  The
second turns local observables into trace-class compact observables.
The third supplies the integration cycle used by enumerative geometry.
The fourth converts Hall multiplication and wall-crossing into a
generating series.  The fifth identifies the field-theoretic insertion
with the sheaf, pair, or map insertion.  Omitting any item leaves an
undefined trace or an unsupported comparison.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{The Four Partition-Function Objects}
\label{subsec:dt-gw-pt-nekrasov-four-objects}

The Donaldson--Thomas series of a projective Calabi--Yau threefold is
the Behrend-weighted ideal-sheaf count
\[
  Z^{\DT}_X(q,Q)
  =
  \sum_{\beta,n}
  \chi\!\left(\Hilb^{n,\beta}(X),\nu_B\right)q^nQ^\beta ,
\]
where \(\nu_B\) is Behrend's constructible function.  In a stability
chamber this series can be reorganised by Kontsevich--Soibelman
\(\BPS\)-invariants
\(\Omega^{\KS}_\sigma(\gamma)\in\QQ\) on
\(K^{\mathrm{num}}_0(\cC)\).  The chamber and heart are part of the
datum.

The Gromov--Witten object is the stable-map free energy
\[
  F^{\GW}_X(u,Q)=
  \sum_{g,\beta}u^{2g-2}N^{\GW}_{g,\beta}Q^\beta ,
  \qquad
  Z^{\GW}_X(u,Q)=\exp F^{\GW}_X(u,Q).
\]
The connected free energy \(F^{\GW}\) and the disconnected partition
function \(Z^{\GW}\) are separate objects.

Pandharipande--Thomas theory counts stable pairs
\[
  \cO_X \longrightarrow F,
  \qquad
  F \text{ pure of dimension }1,
\]
and gives
\[
  Z^{\PT}_X(q,Q)
  =
  \sum_{\beta,n}P_{n,\beta}q^nQ^\beta .
\]
Stable-pair theory supplies the wall-crossing bridge between ideal
sheaf DT theory and the GW expansion after the degree-zero factor has
been removed.

Nekrasov's partition function is an equivariant instanton sum
\[
  Z_{\mathrm{Nek}}(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,a;q)
  =
  \sum_{n\geq0}q^n
  \int_{[\cM_n]^{\mathrm{vir}}}
  \frac{1}{e_T(N^{\mathrm{vir}})} .
\]
It belongs to the \(\Omega\)-deformed gauge theory on equivariant
instantons.  It compares with toric Calabi--Yau threefold curve
counting through geometric engineering and the topological vertex.
It supplies residues on equivariant moduli; compact non-equivariant DT
theory requires the reduced or virtual integration data named in
Proposition~\ref{wn:prop:five-inputs-dt-trace}.

\subsection*{Conditional \texorpdfstring{$E_3$}{E3} Trace Comparison}
\label{subsec:conditional-e3-trace}

\begin{theorem}[Trace comparison under compactness and comparison
hypotheses]
\label{wn:thm:e3-trace-conditional}
Let \(X\) be a smooth compact Calabi--Yau threefold and
\(\cF_X=\PhiFA_3(\Perf(X))\).  Suppose:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the hCS quantum master equation has been solved on the chosen
completion of fields on \(X\), with the quartic local anomaly
cancelled by a chosen counterterm;
\item \(\cF_X\) is dualisable in the completed \(\Ethree\)-linear
category in which the trace is taken, and the trace functional is
continuous for that completion;
\item the cyclic pairing agrees with the orientation data of the
derived moduli of one-dimensional sheaves, stable pairs, and stable
maps;
\item properness or reduced properness supplies the integration class
used by the DT, PT, and GW theories;
\item descent and sewing identify local hCS observables with compact
global insertions;
\item comparison maps identify the hCS trace insertions with the
ideal-sheaf, stable-pair, and stable-map insertions.
\end{enumerate}
Then the categorical trace on \(\cF_X\), the DT series, the PT series,
and the GW series can be compared as partition functions.  Under the
MNOP change of variable
\[
  -q=e^{iu}
\]
the comparison has the form
\[
  Z^{\DT,\prime}_X(q,Q)\big|_{-q=e^{iu}}
  =
  Z^{\PT}_X(q,Q)\big|_{-q=e^{iu}}
  =
  Z^{\GW,\prime}_X(u,Q),
\]
in every chamber where the DT/PT and GW/PT correspondences are
established.  Equality of the numerical partition functions gives
equality of the corresponding completed trace values on the specified
insertions.  Equality of trace maps on all observables requires the
stronger TCFT comparison of the completed theories.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The DT term is the Behrend-weighted integration over Hilbert schemes
or, after wall-crossing, the KS integration over a fixed stability
heart.  The PT term is the Joyce--Song and Toda wall-crossing
replacement by stable pairs.  The GW term is the stable-map integral
with the disconnected exponential.  The trace on \(\cF_X\) becomes an
\(\Ethree\)-linear functional through dualisability, orientation,
properness, descent, sewing, and completion.  The comparison maps in
the hypotheses identify the trace insertions with the
moduli-theoretic insertions.  The displayed equality is the MNOP/PT
correspondence in the chambers where those comparison theorems apply.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{The Affine Toric Test: \texorpdfstring{$\mathbb C^3$}{C3}}
\label{subsec:c3-toric-test}

For \(X=\CC^3\) with the standard
\((\CC^\times)^3\)-action, localisation reduces the DT theory to the
fixed point at the origin.  Plane partitions give
\[
  M(q)=\prod_{m\geq1}(1-q^m)^{-m},
  \qquad
  Z^{\DT}_{\CC^3}(q)=M(-q).
\]
The sign is the Behrend sign in the DT convention.  The corresponding
GW vertex is
\[
  Z^{\GW}_{\CC^3}(u)=M(e^{iu}),
\]
and MNOP identifies the two values through \(-q=e^{iu}\).

\begin{proposition}[The toric trace value]
\label{wn:prop:c3-toric-trace}
Let
\[
  \cF_{\CC^3}=\Obs^q_{\hCS}(\CC^3,\fgl_1).
\]
The equivariant \(\Ethree\)-factorisation-algebra trace on the
vacuum insertion is computed by the DT vertex localisation sum
\[
  \Tr^{\cF_{\CC^3},T}_{\DT}(\mathbf 1;q)
  =
  \sum_{\pi}
  (-q)^{|\pi|}
  \frac{
    e_T\!\left(\operatorname{Ext}^2(I_\pi,I_\pi)_0\right)}
   {e_T\!\left(\operatorname{Ext}^1(I_\pi,I_\pi)_0\right)},
  \qquad t_1t_2t_3=1,
\]
where \(\pi\) ranges over plane partitions and \(I_\pi\) is the
corresponding monomial ideal.  In the unrefined CY specialisation the
vertex weight is the Behrend sign, hence
\[
  \Tr^{\cF_{\CC^3}}_{\DT}(\mathbf 1;q)=M(-q),
  \qquad
  \Tr^{\cF_{\CC^3}}_{\GW}(\mathbf 1;u)=M(e^{iu}),
\]
with equality after \(-q=e^{iu}\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The \(T\)-fixed points of \(\Hilb^n(\CC^3)\) are plane partitions of
size \(n\), whose generating function is \(M(q)\).  The virtual tangent
ratio in the DT vertex gives the unrefined Behrend sign on the
Calabi--Yau slice \(t_1t_2t_3=1\).  The topological vertex gives the
vacuum amplitude \(M(e^{iu})\) on the GW side.  The hCS observable
trace agrees with this localisation trace after the toric comparison
map, and the MNOP variable change identifies the two vacuum values.
\end{proof}

The affine toric Hall algebra input is
\[
  \CoHA(\CC^3)\simeq Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1).
\]
The positive-half CoHA identity is the affine toric input.  Drinfeld
double or centre, completion, and evaluation produce the full
\(\Wilpha\) vertex-algebra layer used for vacuum characters.

\subsection*{\texorpdfstring{$K3\times E$}{K3 x E}: Reduced Modular
Trace Values}
\label{subsec:k3e-reduced-modular-trace}

Let \(Y=S\times E\), with \(S\) a K3 surface and \(E\) an elliptic
curve.  The compact non-toric theory uses reduced obstruction theories
and modular curve-counting formulas.  The \(\Omega\)-background
localisation of \(\CC^3\) has no direct replacement on \(Y\).

The five numerical invariants relevant here are:
\[
\begin{array}{ccl}
\kcat(Y) &=& \chi(\cO_S)\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot0=0,\\[2mm]
\kch^{\mathrm{compact}}(Y) &=&
  \sum_q(-1)^qh^{0,q}(Y)=1-1+1-1=0,\\[2mm]
\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(Y) &=& 3,\\[2mm]
\kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_{5}}) &=& c_1(0)/2=10/2=5,\\[2mm]
\kfiber(Y) &=& \mathrm{rk}\,\widetilde H(S,\ZZ)=24.
\end{array}
\]
They arise from distinct constructions: total-space Hodge theory,
compact chiral Hodge supertrace, Heisenberg--Mukai specialisation,
Borcherds weight, and Mukai-lattice fibre rank.

The normalisations of the Igusa forms must be kept separate.  Let
\[
  D_{5}=64^{-1}\Delta_{5},\qquad
  \Phi^{\mathrm{OP}}_{10}=D_{5}^2=4096^{-1}\Delta_{5}^2,\qquad
  \Phi^{\mathrm{un}}_{10}=\Delta_{5}^2.
\]
In the Oberdieck--Pandharipande DT convention,
\[
  Z^{\DT,\mathrm{red},\prime}_{K3\times E}
  =
  -\bigl(\Phi^{\mathrm{OP}}_{10}\bigr)^{-1}
  =
  -D_{5}^{-2}
  =
  -4096\,\Delta_{5}^{-2}.
\]
The unnormalised reciprocal \(1/\Phi^{\mathrm{un}}_{10}\) is the
corresponding Igusa-square normalisation before the OP scalar is
inserted.  The scalar \(4096\), the sign, the BKM weight, and the
CHL normalisations are separate convention data.

\begin{theorem}[Primitive reduced \(K3\times E\) comparison]
\label{wn:thm:k3e-reduced-comparison}
For primitive curve class on \(K3\times E\), reduced DT theory gives
the OP-normalised scalar
\[
  Z^{\DT,\mathrm{red},\prime}_{K3\times E}
  =
  -\bigl(\Phi^{\mathrm{OP}}_{10}\bigr)^{-1}.
\]
The reduced GW partition function is compared to the same modular
object through the reduced GW/DT correspondence and the change of
variable \(-q=e^{iu}\).  The Borcherds lift of the K3 elliptic genus
supplies
\[
  \mathrm{Bo}(\phi_{0,1})=\Delta_{5},
  \qquad
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_{5}})=5.
\]
Thus the reduced modular partition function, the Borcherds weight, and
the Heisenberg--Mukai specialisation are related by comparison maps
between distinct defining invariants.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Oberdieck--Pandharipande compute the reduced DT series of
\(K3\times E\) in the OP normalisation.  Gritsenko--Nikulin identify
\(\Delta_{5}\) as the Borcherds lift of the K3 Jacobi form
\(\phi_{0,1}\), with constant coefficient \(c_1(0)=10\) and weight
\(5\).  The total-space Euler computation gives
\(\chi(\cO_{K3\times E})=0\), and the compact Hodge supertrace gives
the same zero by \(h^{0,q}=(1,1,1,1)\).  The Heisenberg--Mukai value
\(3\) belongs to the chiral specialisation, and the fibre value \(24\)
is the Mukai rank.  These constructions feed the same modular
curve-counting package through separate maps.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{MNOP as a Comparison, Notation as a Contract}
\label{subsec:mnop-comparison-contract}

For a smooth toric Calabi--Yau threefold \(X\), MNOP gives
\[
  Z^{\DT,\prime}_X(q,Q)\big|_{-q=e^{iu}}
  =
  Z^{\GW,\prime}_X(u,Q).
\]
The prime removes the degree-zero DT factor.  In the PT formulation,
\[
  Z^{\DT,\prime}_X(q,Q)=Z^{\PT}_X(q,Q),
\]
after the wall-crossing comparison.  For compact targets such as
\(K3\times E\), the corresponding statement uses reduced obstruction
theories and the modular formulas above.

The notation
\[
  \Tr^{\cF_X}_{\DT},\qquad
  \Tr^{\cF_X}_{\GW},\qquad
  \Tr^{\cF_X}_{\PT}
\]
denotes trace values on specified completed insertions once the
comparison hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{wn:thm:e3-trace-conditional}
have been supplied.  The symbols do not erase the distinction between
Hilbert schemes, stable pairs, stable maps, instanton moduli, and
hCS observables.
