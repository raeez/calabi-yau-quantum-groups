\section*{Humbert divisors on $\Amoduli$ and the $\Dfive$-order lattice}

\subsection*{Slides audit}
The slides discuss the diagonal divisor $\Hone \subset \Hsieg$ (line~43)
and prove (Thm p.~45--54) that exactly eight Siegel modular forms
vanish to order~$1$ along $\Gamma_t(N)$-translates of $\Hone$, with
$\Dfive = M_5(\Gamma_1,\nu_2)$ the Sp$_4(\mathbb{Z})$-case. The slides
name \emph{no} Humbert divisor $\Hd$ for $d \geq 2$, no Heegner point,
no CM point, no Gritsenko--Hulek 1998 reference, and no explicit
Aut$(\Amoduli)$ action on higher Humbert loci. The $\Dfive$ content
treated is (i) the product expansion via $\phi_{0,1}$, (ii) the
Borcherds--Gritsenko--Nikulin identity $\tfrac{1}{64}\Dfive(2z)=\Phi(z)$
(line~718), and (iii) the GBKM superalgebra $\mathfrak{g}_{\Dfive}$.
Consequently the intersection lattice below is reconstructed
from the primary literature, not the slides.

\subsection*{Humbert divisors: first-principles definition}
For $d \in \mathbb{Z}_{>0}$ with $d \equiv 0,1 \pmod 4$, the
\emph{Humbert surface} $\Hd \subset \Amoduli$ parametrises principally
polarised abelian surfaces $(A,\lambda)$ admitting a symmetric
endomorphism of discriminant $d$ (Humbert 1901; van der Geer 1988,
Ch.~IX). Equivalently, $\Hd = \{\tau \in \Hsieg : \exists (a,b,c,d,e)
\in \mathbb{Z}^5\text{ primitive with } a\tau_1 + b\tau_2 + c\tau_3 +
d(\tau_1\tau_3 - \tau_2^2) + e = 0,\ b^2 - 4ac - 4de = d\}/\Spfour$.
Geometrically:
\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
\item $\Hone$: products $E_1 \times E_2$ of elliptic curves (diagonal
  locus; equation $\tau_2 = 0$).
\item $H_4$: surfaces $(\mathrm{Sym}^2 E, \mathrm{can})$ with
  $\mathrm{End} \supset \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}]$; contains bielliptic
  Jacobians.
\item $H_5$: real multiplication by $\mathbb{Z}[\tfrac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}]$;
  Hilbert modular surface for $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt 5)$.
\item $H_8$: RM by $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt 2]$; Hilbert modular surface for
  $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt 2)$.
\item $H_9$: \emph{reducible}, decomposes as isogeny-$3$ translates of
  $\Hone$ (van der Geer 1988, IX.2.5).
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Order of vanishing of $\Dfive$ along $\Hd$}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Gritsenko 2000, Thm~3.1; Gritsenko--Hulek 1998,
\S 3; Freitag 1983 for $H_1$). The divisor of $\Dfive$ on
$\Amoduli$ is
\[
  (\Dfive) \;=\; \Hone \quad\text{(simple zero)},
\]
with no components along any other Humbert divisor. Thus
\[
  \boxed{\;\mathrm{ord}_{\Hd}\Dfive \;=\; \begin{cases}
    1 & d = 1, \\ 0 & d \in \{4,5,8,9\}.
  \end{cases}\;}
\]
\emph{Proof sketch.} $\Dfive$ is the unique (up to scalar) weight-$5$
cusp form for $\Spfour$ with nontrivial character $\nu_{\Dfive}$
(slides p.~60). Its zero-divisor is Sp$_4(\mathbb{Z})$-invariant. By
the Borcherds product expansion (slides p.~768--773) the only zero
locus comes from roots $(n,\ell,m)$ with $4nm-\ell^2=1$, $(n,\ell,m)$
primitive: these are precisely the $\Gamma_1$-translates of $\tau_2=0$,
i.e.\ $\Hone$. The multiplicity is $f_{0,1}(1,\pm 1) = 1$ from the
Fourier expansion $\phi_{0,1}(z,\tau) = (r^{-1}+10+r) + \cdots$
evaluated at the first Humbert exponent.

\subsection*{Intersection lattice $\Hone \cap \Hd$}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (van der Geer 1988, IX.3; Hermann 1991). Inside
$\Amoduli$, as two-cycles:
\[
  \Hone \cdot \Hd \;=\;
  \sum_{\substack{(Q_1,Q_2)\\ \mathrm{disc}(Q_1\oplus Q_2)=d}}
  [C_{Q_1,Q_2}]
\]
where the sum is over pairs of positive-definite binary quadratic
forms of discriminants $d_1, d_2$ with $d_1 d_2 = d$ (and
$d_i \geq 1$). The components $C_{Q_1,Q_2}$ are modular curves
$X_0(Q_1) \times X_0(Q_2)/\mathrm{Aut}$ parametrising CM-product
abelian surfaces.

$1$ & $1$ & $\Hone$ itself (self-int.) & $\Hone^2 = -\tfrac{1}{2}c_1(\omega)^2$ \\
$4$ & $0$ & $Y_0(2) + Y_0(2)'$ & CM by $\mathbb{Z}[i]$, $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-2}]$ pairs \\

\end{center}

\subsection*{$\mathrm{Aut}(\Amoduli)$ action}
$\mathrm{Aut}(\Amoduli) = \Spfour/\{\pm I\}$ permutes components of
$\Hone \cdot \Hd$ transitively within each isogeny type; the Atkin--
Lehner group $W_d$ acts on $Y_0(d)$-components of $\Hone \cdot \Hd$.

\subsection*{Connection to the derived Humbert germ $\Hdhat$}
Wave-$6$ Z5 identified $\Hdhat$ as the derived completion of
$\mathcal{G}^{\circ}_{\mathrm{mn}}$ at basepoint. Under the
Bruinier--Howard--Yang (2009) arithmetic intersection pairing
on $\widehat{\mathrm{CH}}^2(\Amoduli)$:
\[
  \langle \hat{\Hone}, \hat{\Hd}\rangle
  \;=\; -\tfrac{1}{2}\, c_d(0) \,\log(d) + \cdots,
\]
where $c_d(0)$ is the constant Fourier coefficient of the Borcherds
weight-lift of $\phi_{0,d}$ (Gritsenko series). For $d=1$ this
recovers $\kBKM(\Phi_1) = c_1(0)/2 = 5$, matching
$\mathrm{ord}_{\Hone}\Dfive = 1$.

\subsection*{Scope}
$\kch$ is not affected: $\Hd$-vanishing is an automorphic property of
the $\Amoduli$-side, not of $A_X$ on $K3 \times E$. $\kBKM(\Dfive)=5$
is the $N=1$ Gritsenko specialisation; higher-$N$ analogues
$\Delta_{k(N)}$ on $\Amoduli$ have order-$1$ along paramodular-$\Hone$
translates, not along $\Hd$ for $d \geq 4$.
