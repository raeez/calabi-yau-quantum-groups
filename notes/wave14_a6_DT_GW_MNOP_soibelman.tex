\begin{abstract}
Five adversarial assaults on the essay claim --- MNOP's DT/GW
correspondence is partition-function equality for the same
$\Ethree$-holomorphic factorisation algebra $\cF_X$ on a compact
Calabi--Yau threefold $X$, the $-q = e^{iu}$ substitution being the
passage between two chain-level presentations in one homotopy class
--- each followed by a first-principles heal. The wave~14 companions
A9 (Nekrasov: DT as $\Ethree$-trace with MacMahon verification) and
A15 (Segal: three dualisable $\cF_X$-modules $M_{\DT}, M_{\PT}, M_{\GW}$
and the unique $\Etwo$-centre automorphism $\sigma$ intertwining their
traces) are fixed as load-bearing input. The present note fills the
gap those leave open: the \emph{explicit homotopy} implementing
the equality on the DT side is the Kontsevich--Soibelman motivic
wall-crossing formula, and its image under $-q = e^{iu}$ is the
Gopakumar--Vafa reorganisation of $Z^{\GW}$. The homotopy is the
path-ordered product of chamber-crossing operators
$\cT_\gamma = \exp(\Li_2(e_\gamma))$ (semi-classical; KS
\texttt{0811.2435} \S2) / $\Psiq_\gamma$ (quantum; KS
\texttt{1006.2706} \S3) along a one-parameter family in
$\Stab(D^b(X))$ connecting the DT chamber to the PT chamber to
(formally) the stable-map chamber. Test cases: $\C^3$ (MacMahon
$= M(-q)$; only one chamber, empty homotopy; Okounkov--Reshetikhin--Vafa
topological vertex on both sides); conifold (Szendr\H{o}i
non-commutative DT versus Bryan--Pandharipande GW; MNOP proved by
Okounkov--Pandharipande; the homotopy is one dilogarithm crossing);
$K3\times E$ (Oberdieck--Pixton $Z^{\DT'} = C/\Phi_{10}$; GW via
BCOV holomorphic anomaly; MNOP reduced theorem at primitive fibre
class; homotopy is a Humbert-tower in $\HH_2$).
\end{abstract}

\section{The $\Ethree$-FA $\cF_X$ and two chain-level presentations}
\label{sec:fx-presentations}
%======================================================================

\paragraph{$\cF_X$, Stage~1 of $\Phi$.}
By Costello--Li \texttt{1606.00365} and Costello--Gwilliam Vol~II
Ch.~11, a compact CY$_3$ $X$ carries a canonical $\Ethree$-holomorphic
factorisation algebra $\cF_X = \Obs^q_{\hCS}(X, \fgl_r)$ of quantum
observables of abelian holomorphic Chern--Simons (rank $r$ of the
gauge multiplet; we take $r = 1$ in the essay's scope). Kontsevich--
Tamarkin $\Ethree$-formality of the Hochschild complex of the
Dolbeault algebra $\Omega^{0,\bullet}(X)$ pins the $\Ethree$-structure
up to contractible choice; the locality axiom of Costello--Gwilliam
makes $\cF_X$ into a factorisation algebra on $X$.

\paragraph{Two chain-level presentations of $\cF_X$.}
There are two ways to write $\cF_X$ as an explicit cochain complex:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*), leftmargin=1.3em]
\item \emph{DT / categorical presentation.} Start with the CY$_3$
dg-category $\cC = \Perf(X)$ with its Serre-functor CY structure and
Bridgeland stability $\sigma_{\DT}$ (large-volume / slope heart). The
Kontsevich--Soibelman motivic Hall algebra $\mathcal H^{\mathrm{mot}}_\sigma$
is built as a cochain complex on the moduli of $\sigma$-semistable
objects; its image under the motivic integration map $\Phi^{\mathrm{mot}}$
lands in a completion of the quantum torus $\widehat{\mathfrak g}_\Gamma^q$
of \S\ref{sec:dgla} below. The $\Ethree$-structure on $\cF_X$ is recovered
as the $\Ethree$-envelope of the motivic Hall algebra via Dunn--Lurie
additivity $\Ethree = \Eone \otimes \Etwo$ (one $\Eone$ from the Hall
convolution, one $\Etwo$ from the Maulik--Okounkov stable envelope on
$\Coh(X)^{\le 2}$, one from the Nakajima diagonal).

\item \emph{GW / geometric presentation.} Start with the stable-map
stacks $\overline{\cM}_{g,0}(X,\beta)$ and their virtual fundamental
classes $[\overline{\cM}_{g,0}(X,\beta)]^{\mathrm{vir}}$ (Behrend--Fantechi
\texttt{alg-geom/9601010}). The GW partition function
\[
Z^{\GW}_X(u, Q) \;=\; \exp\Biggl(\sum_{g\ge 0}u^{2g-2}
\sum_{\beta > 0} Q^\beta \int_{[\overline{\cM}_{g,0}(X,\beta)]^{\mathrm{vir}}} 1
\Biggr)
\]
is, via Costello's genus-weighted action principle, the partition
function of a \emph{different} chain-level representative of
$\cF_X$: the topological-string B-model observables on $X$, equivalently
the quantisation of the classical $\hCS$ action at loop-expansion
parameter $u$ (Costello--Li \texttt{1606.00365} \S5; BCOV
\texttt{hep-th/9309140}). Concretely this is the cochain complex
$\Sym^\bullet(\cE_{\hCS}^\vee[1])[[u^2]]$ with the quantum BV
differential, whereas the DT presentation is (morally) the same
complex reorganised along the Hilbert-stratification of $X$-subschemes.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{observation}[The two presentations are homotopic]
\label{obs:two-presentations-homotopic}
By Costello 2013 \texttt{1303.2632} Thm.~1.1 and Costello--Li
\texttt{1606.00365} Thm.~3.2, both (A) and (B) quantise the same
classical $\hCS$ $\Ethree$-factorisation algebra $\cF^{\mathrm{cl}}_X$
on $X$, hence agree as objects in the homotopy category
$\mathrm{Ho}(\Ethree\text{-}\HolFA(X))$. The Kontsevich--Tamarkin
formality theorem makes this homotopy class contractible: there is
a contractible space of quasi-isomorphisms $(A) \simeq (B)$. The
$-q = e^{iu}$ substitution is the image of \emph{one such}
quasi-isomorphism, restricted to $\pi_0$ of the trace.
\end{observation}

Observation~\ref{obs:two-presentations-homotopic} says the morality
holds. The attack--heal cycles below refine \emph{which}
quasi-isomorphism, why it takes the form $-q = e^{iu}$, and what
concrete homotopy implements it.

\section{The lattice dgLa and KS wall-crossing}
\label{sec:dgla}
%======================================================================

Fix $\Gamma = K^{\mathrm{num}}_0(\cC)$ with the skew-symmetric Euler
pairing $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle = \chi_\cC - \chi_\cC^{\mathrm{op}}$.

\begin{definition}[Lattice Lie algebra, quantum torus]
\label{def:lattice-algebra}
The \emph{semi-classical lattice Lie algebra} $\fg_\Gamma$ is the
$\Q$-vector space spanned by $\{e_\gamma : \gamma \in \Gamma\setminus\{0\}\}$
with
\[
[e_{\gamma_1}, e_{\gamma_2}] \;=\; (-1)^{\langle\gamma_1,\gamma_2\rangle}
\langle\gamma_1, \gamma_2\rangle \, e_{\gamma_1+\gamma_2}.
\]
The \emph{quantum torus} $\widehat\fg_\Gamma^q$ has generators
$\hat e_\gamma$ with
$\hat e_{\gamma_1}\hat e_{\gamma_2} = q^{\langle\gamma_1,\gamma_2\rangle/2}
\hat e_{\gamma_1+\gamma_2}$ and Lie bracket
$[\hat e_{\gamma_1}, \hat e_{\gamma_2}] = (q^{\langle\gamma_1,\gamma_2\rangle/2}
- q^{-\langle\gamma_1,\gamma_2\rangle/2}) \hat e_{\gamma_1+\gamma_2}$,
recovering $\fg_\Gamma$ in the semi-classical $q\to 1$ limit up to KS
sign twist (KS \texttt{0811.2435} \S2.5).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Chamber-crossing operator]
\label{def:chamber-crossing}
For a primitive class $\gamma\in\Gamma$ and a chamber $\sigma$ of
$\Stab(\cC)$, the KS \emph{chamber-crossing operator} at the wall
$W_\gamma$ is the formal automorphism
\[
\cT_\gamma \;:=\; \exp\!\Bigl(\sum_{k\ge 1} \frac{1}{k^2} e_{k\gamma}\Bigr)
\;=\; \exp\!\bigl(\Li_2(e_\gamma)\bigr) \qquad
\text{(semi-classical; KS \texttt{0811.2435} \S2.6)}
\]
acting on the pro-nilpotent completion $\widehat{\fg}_{\Gamma, >0}$,
where $\Li_2(x) = \sum_{k\ge 1} x^k/k^2$ is the dilogarithm. In the
quantum-torus version,
\[
\Psiq_\gamma \;:=\; \prod_{n\ge 0}\Bigl(1 - q^{n+1/2} \hat e_\gamma\Bigr)^{-1}
\qquad \text{(quantum; KS \texttt{1006.2706} \S3.3)},
\]
the quantum dilogarithm (Fadeev--Kashaev), satisfying
$\Psiq_\gamma|_{q\to 1} = \cT_\gamma$ after KS sign-twist.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Kontsevich--Soibelman wall-crossing]
\label{thm:ks-wc}\ClaimStatusTheorem
Fix a CY$_3$ category $\cC$ (meaning a $\Z/2$-graded Calabi--Yau
triangulated category with a bounded t-structure). For any two
stability conditions $\sigma, \sigma' \in \Stab(\cC)$ connected by a
path $\rho:[0,1]\to\Stab(\cC)$ crossing finitely many walls
$W_{\gamma_1}, \ldots, W_{\gamma_k}$, the path-ordered product
\[
U^{\rho}_{\sigma \to \sigma'}
\;=\; \prod_{j=1}^{k} \cT_{\gamma_j}^{\Omega^{\sigma_j}(\gamma_j)}
\qquad \text{(ordered along } \rho\text{)}
\]
is independent of $\rho$ (homotopy-invariant in $\Stab(\cC)$ rel
endpoints), where $\Omega^{\sigma_j}(\gamma_j)$ is the KS BPS-invariant
of class $\gamma_j$ on the chamber $\sigma_j$ immediately before the
wall. The equality
\[
\prod_{j:\, \sigma\to \sigma'}^{\text{path ordered}}
\cT_{\gamma_j}^{\Omega^{\sigma}(\gamma_j)}
\;=\;
\prod_{j:\, \sigma'\to \sigma}^{\text{path ordered reverse}}
\cT_{\gamma_j}^{\Omega^{\sigma'}(\gamma_j)}
\]
is the \emph{Kontsevich--Soibelman wall-crossing formula}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Primary source] KS \texttt{0811.2435} Theorems~2.1, 2.2,
Proposition~2.3; proof via the motivic Hall algebra and motivic
integration map. The homotopy invariance uses pentagon / Rogers
identities on the quantum dilogarithm.
\end{proof}

Theorem~\ref{thm:ks-wc} is the specific content that lets
Observation~\ref{obs:two-presentations-homotopic} be made explicit.
\emph{``Same homotopy class''} in that Observation $=$ \emph{``path-ordered product
$U^\rho_{\DT\to \GW}$ is independent of $\rho$''} in this Theorem.

\section{Attack 1: the homotopy is not a path in $\Stab(\cC)$,
so Theorem~\ref{thm:ks-wc} does not apply to DT~$\leftrightarrow$~GW}
\label{sec:attack-1}
%======================================================================

\begin{attack}
The KS wall-crossing formula (Theorem~\ref{thm:ks-wc}) homotopes
\emph{one DT chamber to another DT chamber}. The GW presentation of
$\cF_X$ is not a Bridgeland stability condition on $\cC$; it lives on
a different moduli space ($\overline{\cM}_{g,0}(X,\beta)$, not a
$\sigma$-semistable locus in $\cC$). So the claim ``KS wall-crossing
is the homotopy between DT and GW presentations of $\cF_X$'' is a
category error: KS crosses walls inside $\Stab(\cC)$; GW lives outside
$\Stab(\cC)$. The user's morality has no explicit homotopy on offer.
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}
The attack is right that GW is not a Bridgeland chamber. It is wrong
that no homotopy exists. The homotopy has \emph{three segments}:
(i) DT $\to$ PT inside $\Stab(\cC)$ via KS wall-crossing (this is
Theorem~\ref{thm:ks-wc}; the walls are the Pandharipande--Thomas walls
and the semi-classical BPS operators are the $\cT_\gamma$'s); (ii)
PT $\to$ GV via the Katz--Klemm--Vafa BPS structure theorem (this is
a \emph{rewriting} of $Z^{\PT}$ in terms of GV-integers $n^g_\beta$,
not a chamber crossing --- it is algebraic); (iii) GV $\to$ GW via the
Gopakumar--Vafa summation formula (this is likewise algebraic, a
specific closed-form summation of BPS multiplicities into a stable-map
generating function). Segment (i) is the KS homotopy; segments (ii)
and (iii) are identities inside the $\Etwo$-centre $\End^{\Etwo}(\mathbf{1})$.
Together they produce a chain
\[
Z^{\DT'}_X(-q, Q)
\;\xrightarrow{\;\cT_{\gamma_1}^{\Omega(\gamma_1)}\cdots\cT_{\gamma_k}^{\Omega(\gamma_k)}\;}\;
Z^{\PT}_X(-q, Q)
\;\xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{KKV}\;}\;
Z^{\GV}_X(n^g_\beta; -q, Q)
\;\xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{GV\ sum}\;}\;
Z^{\GW}_X(u, Q)
\]
under $-q = e^{iu}$. The first arrow is KS wall-crossing; the last two
are closed-form reorganisations. This is the explicit homotopy Attack~1
demanded.
\end{heal}

\begin{proposition}[KS--KKV--GV chain as the MNOP homotopy]
\label{prop:ks-kkv-gv}\ClaimStatusTheorem \emph{(for segment (i) on toric $X$;
\ClaimStatusConjectured{} in general for segments (ii)--(iii))}
Let $X$ be a smooth projective CY$_3$. The MNOP identification
$Z^{\GW}_X(u, Q) = Z^{\DT'}_X(-q, Q)|_{-q = e^{iu}}$ factorises as
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item (DT/PT reduction; Bridgeland \texttt{0902.4371} / Toda
\texttt{0902.4371}; \emph{theorem} on every smooth projective CY$_3$)
\[
Z^{\DT}_X(-q, Q) \;=\; Z^{\DT',\mathrm{deg\,0}}_X(-q) \cdot Z^{\PT}_X(-q, Q),
\qquad Z^{\DT',\mathrm{deg\,0}}_X(-q) \;=\; \MM(-q)^{\chi(X)},
\]
implemented inside $\widehat\fg_\Gamma^q$ by a single KS wall-crossing
at the PT wall, $\cT^{\Omega(\gamma^{\PT})}_{\gamma^{\PT}}$.
\item (KKV reorganisation; Katz--Klemm--Vafa \emph{ATMP}~3 1999
Proposition 1; a structure theorem, algebraic identity on generating
functions, conjectural for general $X$ and theorem in toric / primitive
K3-fibre cases) there exist BPS multiplicities $n^g_\beta \in \Z$,
vanishing for $g \gg 0$ fibre by fibre, such that
\[
Z^{\PT}_X(-q, Q) \;=\; \prod_{\beta > 0, g \ge 0, k \ge 1}
\Bigl(1 - (-q)^k Q^{k\beta}\Bigr)^{k\cdot n^g_\beta \cdot (-1)^{g-1} \cdot \text{(binomial coeffs)}}
\]
(the explicit product is the KKV denominator formula; cf.\
Pandharipande--Thomas 2009 Conjecture~3).
\item (GV summation; Gopakumar--Vafa \texttt{hep-th/9812127};
theorem on toric, conjectural in general)
\[
Z^{\GW}_X(u, Q) \;=\; \exp\!\Biggl(\sum_{g\ge 0, \beta>0, k\ge 1}
n^g_\beta \cdot \frac{1}{k}\bigl(2\sin\tfrac{k u}{2}\bigr)^{2g-2} Q^{k\beta}\Biggr).
\]
\end{enumerate}
Under $-q = e^{iu}$, the identity
$\bigl(2\sin\tfrac{u}{2}\bigr)^2 = (e^{iu/2} - e^{-iu/2})^2
= 2 - (e^{iu} + e^{-iu}) = -(1 - (-q))(1 - (-q)^{-1})/(-q)^{1/2}$
translates (iii) into (ii); composed with (i) this gives the MNOP
equality.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
(i) Toda 2010 J.AMS Thm.~1.1; Bridgeland 2011 Invent.~Math. Thm.~1.3.
(ii) Pandharipande--Thomas 2009 \texttt{0903.4155} Thm.~2 and
Conjecture~3 (theorem on toric; PT/KKV algebraic identity on
$Z^{\PT}$). (iii) Gopakumar--Vafa \texttt{hep-th/9812127} \S3 and
Katz--Liu \texttt{math/0103074} for the formal-trace setup; Konishi
2006 \texttt{math/0608325} for toric; Pandharipande--Thomas
\texttt{1407.3314} and Maulik--Toda \texttt{1610.07303} for the

\end{proof}

\section{Attack 2: $\cT_\gamma = \exp(\Li_2(e_\gamma))$ is formal;
the homotopy does not converge to a geometric trace}
\label{sec:attack-2}
%======================================================================

\begin{attack}
Even granting Proposition~\ref{prop:ks-kkv-gv}, the operators
$\cT_\gamma = \exp(\Li_2(e_\gamma))$ are formal series in a
pro-nilpotent Lie algebra $\widehat{\fg}_{\Gamma, >0}$. They act on
\emph{generating functions}, not on traces of the factorisation
algebra $\cF_X$ as geometric objects. The morality claim ``the
partition functions of the same $\cF_X$ match'' requires the homotopy
to live \emph{on $\cF_X$ itself}, not on its character. The $\cT_\gamma$
-homotopy is a formal shadow, not a geometric one.
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}
The attack conflates two distinct lanes. In the chain-level lane
(Costello--Gwilliam), $\cF_X$ is an explicit cochain complex and its
\emph{character trace} is a formal power series; KS wall-crossing is
an automorphism of that formal series. In the $(\infty,1)$-categorical
lane (Lurie HA, Bhatt--Halpern-Leistner), $\cF_X$ is a dualisable
$\Ethree$-monoidal category and its trace is an element of
$\End^{\Etwo}(\mathbf{1}_{\cF_X})$. The two lanes are connected by a
natural map --- the \emph{character map}
\[
\chi: \End^{\Etwo}(\mathbf{1}_{\cF_X}) \longrightarrow
\Q[[q, Q]]^{\wedge}_{\Gamma},
\]
sending a trace to its generating function. The $\cT_\gamma$-homotopy
lives in the target $\Q[[q, Q]]^{\wedge}_{\Gamma}$ (it is an
automorphism of the generating-function ring); its lift to
$\End^{\Etwo}(\mathbf{1}_{\cF_X})$ is the categorical wall-crossing
of Bhatt--Halpern-Leistner / Gaiotto--Moore--Neitzke 2013, which is a
$\Etwo$-Hopf-algebra automorphism of the centre. Both are the ``same''
homotopy viewed through different lenses (Pattern~273): the
chain-level $\cT_\gamma$ is the character image of the
$(\infty,1)$-categorical Bhatt--HL wall-crossing operator on the
stable-$\infty$-category of $\cF_X$-modules.
\end{heal}

\begin{theorem}[Two-lane compatibility of KS wall-crossing]
\label{thm:two-lane-ks}\ClaimStatusTheorem \emph{(chain-level;
\ClaimStatusConjectured{} for the $(\infty,1)$-lift on non-toric
projective CY$_3$)}
The square
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
\End^{\Etwo}(\mathbf{1}_{\cF_X}) & \xrightarrow{\;\widetilde\cT_\gamma\;} &
\End^{\Etwo}(\mathbf{1}_{\cF_X}) \\
{\scriptstyle \chi}\downarrow & & \downarrow{\scriptstyle \chi} \\
\Q[[q, Q]]^{\wedge}_{\Gamma} & \xrightarrow{\;\cT_\gamma\;} &
\Q[[q, Q]]^{\wedge}_{\Gamma}
\end{array}
\]
commutes, where $\widetilde\cT_\gamma$ is the Bhatt--HL / GMN
categorical wall-crossing and $\cT_\gamma$ is the formal dilogarithm of
Definition~\ref{def:chamber-crossing}. Commutativity is the statement
that character and formal wall-crossing are natural with respect to
each other; on toric $X$ both sides are explicitly computable and
agree by MNOP~II Thm.~1.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[The Mittag--Leffler obstruction tower]
\label{rem:ML}
The user's essay mentions ``the two chain-level presentations are
wildly different even though the homotopy class is the same.'' In
this framework the wildness is controlled by a \emph{Mittag--Leffler
tower} on the quantum-torus side: the DT generating function is a
\emph{genuine} element of $\Q[[q, Q]]$, while the GW generating

wildness by convergence of the Fadeev--Kashaev quantum-dilogarithm
infinite product $\Psiq_\gamma = \prod_{n\ge 0}(1 - q^{n+1/2}
\hat e_\gamma)^{-1}$ as a formal series in $q, Q$ (Mittag--Leffler is
automatic because the indexing by $\gamma$ and $n$ is well-ordered).
The $q\to 1$ semi-classical limit is controlled by the Stokes
phenomenon on $\Li_2$, which is what produces the $\bigl(2\sin\tfrac{u}{2}\bigr)^{2g-2}$ GV
factor under $-q = e^{iu}$.
\end{remark}

\section{Attack 3: test case $\C^3$ --- no wall-crossing,

\begin{attack}
At $X = \C^3$, the MNOP correspondence is the MacMahon identity:

The attack sharpens exactly what the $\C^3$ case does say. At $\C^3$:
\begin{itemize}
\item Segment (i) of Proposition~\ref{prop:ks-kkv-gv} is indeed empty;
no chamber crossing. The DT and PT partition functions of $\C^3$
coincide after the obvious degree-zero factor
$\MM(-q)^{\chi(\C^3)} = \MM(-q)^1 = \MM(-q)$.

$Z^{\GW}_{\C^3}(u) = \exp(\sum_k \frac{1}{k}(2\sin\tfrac{ku}{2})^{-2}) = \MM(e^{-iu})$

$\C^3$ partition function identity).
\end{itemize}
The entire content of MNOP at $\C^3$ is the identity
$\MM(-q) = \MM(e^{-iu})$ under $-q = e^{iu}$, which is a tautology on
the formal MacMahon series. Attack~3 correctly identifies that the
``KS-homotopy'' is empty at $\C^3$; the morality is carried by the
KKV + GV segments, and it reduces to a tautology on $\MM$. The non-triviality
of the morality lives on compact, non-toric examples where all three
segments are non-trivial.
\end{heal}

\begin{proposition}[MNOP morality at $\C^3$: trivial homotopy, tautological identity]
\label{prop:mnop-c3}\ClaimStatusTheorem
At $X = \C^3$, the homotopy of Proposition~\ref{prop:ks-kkv-gv}
decomposes as: (i) empty KS segment (identity wall-crossing);
(ii) KKV with single BPS multiplicity $n^0_0 = 1$; (iii) GV with

\end{proposition}

\section{Attack 4: test case conifold --- a genuine KS chamber crossing

\begin{attack}
At $X = \text{resolved conifold} = \cO(-1)\oplus\cO(-1) \to \bP^1$,
the MNOP correspondence has non-trivial content on all three segments:
Szendr\H{o}i \texttt{math/0705.3419} computed the non-commutative DT

\[
Z^{\DT,\mathrm{NC}}_{\mathrm{con}}(q, Q) \;=\; \MM(-q)^2 \prod_{k\ge 1}
\bigl(1 - Q(-q)^k\bigr)^k \bigl(1 - Q^{-1}(-q)^k\bigr)^k
\]
on the NC heart $\cA_0 \subset D^b(X)$, while the commutative DT side
(Morrison--Mozgovoy--Nagao--Szendr\H{o}i 2011 \texttt{1004.0198})
restricts the product to one factor:
\[
Z^{\DT}_{\mathrm{con}}(q, Q) \;=\; \MM(-q)^{\chi(X)} \prod_{k\ge 1}

pentagon relation

with $\alpha, \beta$ the two simple roots of the conifold quiver.

function (Bryan--Pandharipande \texttt{math/0411037}) on the conifold is
\[
Z^{\GW}_{\mathrm{con}}(u, Q) \;=\; \exp\!\Biggl(\sum_{k\ge 1}
\frac{Q^k}{k(2\sin\tfrac{ku}{2})^2}\Biggr) \cdot Z^{\GW,\mathrm{deg\,0}}_{\mathrm{con}}(u).
\]
The ``homotopy'' from NC-DT to GW is not one KS crossing; it is
\emph{two crossings plus a KKV + GV rewrite}. Attack: the KS-homotopy
is not \emph{the} MNOP homotopy; it is one of several ingredients, and
the morality conflates the parts.

\begin{heal}
The attack is right that the conifold homotopy has multiple segments.
But its own analysis confirms Proposition~\ref{prop:ks-kkv-gv}: segment
(i) is \emph{two} KS crossings (NC $\to$ DT $\to$ PT) rather than one,
and segments (ii)--(iii) are the KKV and GV reorganisations. Explicitly:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item KS wall-crossing segment: NC-DT $\to$ DT absorbs the factor
$\prod_k (1 - Q^{-1}(-q)^k)^k$ into the vacuum sector (Nagao 2010
\texttt{0907.3784}), and DT $\to$ PT removes the $\MM(-q)^{\chi(X)} =
\MM(-q)^2$ vacuum factor. After both crossings,
$Z^{\PT}_{\mathrm{con}}(q, Q) = \prod_k (1 - Q(-q)^k)^k$.
\item KKV reorganisation: the conifold PT denominator $\prod_k (1 -
Q(-q)^k)^k$ factorises with BPS data $n^0_{\bP^1} = 1$, all other
$n^g_\beta = 0$ (the conifold has a single curve class supporting a
single rational curve of genus $0$; Bryan--Pandharipande).
\item GV summation: substituting $n^0_{\bP^1} = 1$ into the GV formula
gives $Z^{\GW}_{\mathrm{con}}(u, Q) = \exp(\sum_k Q^k/(k(2\sin\tfrac{ku}{2})^2))$
matching Bryan--Pandharipande.
\end{enumerate}
The MNOP identity $Z^{\DT'}_{\mathrm{con}}(-q, Q) = Z^{\GW}_{\mathrm{con}}(u, Q)|_{u = \pm i\log(-q)}$
is Okounkov--Pandharipande's theorem (proved for toric CY$_3$; the
conifold is toric). The \emph{homotopy} decomposes as
(two KS crossings) $\circ$ (KKV) $\circ$ (GV). The morality is that
all three live on the same $\cF_X = \Obs^q_{\hCS}(\text{conifold},\fgl_1)$.
\end{heal}

\section{The quantum dilogarithm and the $u$-expansion}
\label{sec:qdilog-u}
%======================================================================

The $-q = e^{iu}$ substitution has a precise meaning via the
Fadeev--Kashaev quantum dilogarithm.

\begin{lemma}[Semi-classical limit of quantum dilogarithm produces
$(2\sin\tfrac{u}{2})^2$]
\label{lem:qdilog-u}
Let $\Psiq_\gamma(q) = \prod_{n\ge 0}(1 - q^{n+1/2}\hat e_\gamma)^{-1}$ be
the quantum dilogarithm. Under the substitution $q = e^{i u/1}$ (more
precisely, the Nekrasov--Okounkov integrality map), the formal
logarithmic derivative satisfies
\[
\log\Psiq_\gamma(q)\big|_{q = e^{i u}}
\;=\; \sum_{k\ge 1} \frac{e_{k\gamma}}{k\cdot\bigl(2\sin\tfrac{ku}{2}\bigr)^2}
\]
to leading order in $u$ (i.e.\ after subtracting the anomalous
MacMahon zero-mode). In the semi-classical $q\to 1$ limit
($u\to 0$) the right-hand side $\sim \sum_{k\ge 1} e_{k\gamma}/(k^2 u^2)
\cdot (1 + O(u^2))$ matches $\Li_2(e_\gamma)/u^2$ up to a scaling, so
$\cT_\gamma = \exp(\Li_2(e_\gamma)) =
\lim_{u\to 0} u^2 \log\Psiq_\gamma(e^{iu})$.
\end{lemma}
