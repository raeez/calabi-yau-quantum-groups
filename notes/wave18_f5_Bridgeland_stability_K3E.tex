\section*{Wave-18 F5/20 --- Bridgeland--Toda stability for $\Db(K3\times E)$}

\subsection*{The question the stability manifold answers}

A Calabi--Yau category of dimension $d$ has no slope function intrinsic
to its heart. What plays the role is a Bridgeland datum
$\sigma=(Z,\mathcal{P})$: a central charge $Z\colon K_{0}(\Db)\to\C$ and
a slicing $\mathcal{P}(\phi)\subset\Db$, locked together by the
Harder--Narasimhan axiom and the support property (Bridgeland, Ann.\
Math.\ 166 (2007), 317--345). The manifold
$\Stab(\Db)$ projects locally biholomorphically to
$\Hom_{\Z}(K_{0},\C)$; the heart $\mathcal{A}_{\sigma}=\mathcal{P}((0,1])$
is an abelian subcategory and $Z$ restricts to a stability function
with HN filtrations. The functor
$\Phifun\colon\CY\text{-cat}\to\ChirAlg$ receives its BPS grading from
this datum: a half-plane choice for $Z$ selects which objects survive
into $\Yp$.

\subsection*{Cycle 1. Bridgeland datum and slope ordering}

On $\Db(K3)$ Bridgeland constructed a connected component
$\Stab^{\dagger}(K3)\subset\Stab(\Db(K3))$ covering the period domain
$\mathcal{P}_{0}^{+}(K3)$ of the Mukai lattice
$\widetilde{H}(K3,\Z)\cong U^{\oplus 4}\oplus E_{8}(-1)^{\oplus 2}$
(Bridgeland, Duke 141 (2008), 241--291, Thm.\ 1.1). The central charge
is the Mukai pairing
$Z_{\Omega}(E)=\langle\Omega,v(E)\rangle$, $\Omega\in
\widetilde{H}\otimes\C$ with $\langle\Omega,\Omega\rangle=0$,
$\langle\Omega,\bar\Omega\rangle>0$; the slope $\phi(E)=\arg Z(E)/\pi$
orders HN factors. The Mukai vector
$v(E)=\mathrm{ch}(E)\sqrt{\mathrm{td}(K3)}$ lifts Chern character to the
Mukai lattice and lands in the $(1,1)$-weight sublattice once an
algebraic polarisation is fixed. Stability on the heart of a $(-1)$-class
$[-1]\in\mathrm{Br}(K3)$ is controlled by the Bogomolov--Gieseker
inequality $\Delta(E)\ge 0$, supplying the support property.

\subsection*{Cycle 2. Product CY$_{3}$ and the stability of $\Db(K3\times E)$}

The smooth compact Calabi--Yau threefold $K3\times E$ has
$h^{0,0}=h^{3,0}=1$, $h^{1,0}=h^{2,0}=h^{0,1}=h^{0,2}=1$, so
$\kcat(K3\times E)=\chi(\mathcal{O})=0$ by K\"unneth
multiplicativity against $\chi(\mathcal{O}_{E})=0$, while the K3 fibre
carries $\kcat(K3)=2$. The Mukai/Mukai--N\'eron--Severi lattice
extends to
$\widetilde{H}(K3\times E,\Z)\supset
\widetilde{H}(K3,\Z)\otimes H^{*}(E,\Z)$,
and the product stability datum of Toda (Advances 217 (2008),
2736--2781; Mem.\ AMS 217 (2012), 1078) combines a K3-factor condition
$\sigma_{K3}\in\Stab^{\dagger}(K3)$ with a slope on
$\Db(E)$ governed by the elliptic central charge
$Z_{E}(L)=-\deg(L)+ir$. The external-product heart
$\mathcal{A}_{\sigma_{K3}}\boxtimes\mathcal{A}_{E}$ need not be a heart
on $\Db(K3\times E)$; Toda's induced stability (``polynomial
stability,'' Pub.\ RIMS 45 (2009), 793--829) corrects this through a
one-parameter family of tilted hearts, stable at each slope.

\textsc{Gap / lane discipline.} A full existence proof of
$\Stab(\Db(K3\times E))\neq\emptyset$ with support property on the full
Mukai lattice of $K3\times E$ is not in the literature; what is known
is (i) polynomial stability of DT type (Toda 2009),
(ii) tilt-stability on the BMT inequality for threefolds conditional on
Bayer--Macr\`\i--Toda (Bayer--Macri--Toda, J.\ Alg.\ Geom.\ 23
(2014), 117--163), and (iii) concrete stability in the fibre
direction over $E$. We therefore state the cycle-2 output at the
$(\infty,1)$-categorical lane as a descent claim and at the chain-level
lane as an explicit polynomial-stability construction; the two are
cross-compatible where they overlap.

\subsection*{Cycle 3. Upper half-plane and the BPS selector}

For $\sigma=(Z,\mathcal{P})$ any object $E\in\mathcal{P}(\phi)$ with
$\phi\in(0,1)$ has $Z(E)\in\HH$; slope-semistables with
$Z(E)\in\R_{>0}$ form the extremal rays.
Following Kontsevich--Soibelman (arXiv:0811.2435, Sec.\ 1.4;
arXiv:1006.2706, Sec.\ 2) the motivic DT generating function
assembles over $\gamma\in K_{0}(\Db)$ with $Z(\gamma)\in\HH$:
\[
\mathrm{DT}^{\mathrm{mot}}(\Db(K3\times E),\sigma)
 = \prod_{\substack{\gamma\\ Z(\gamma)\in\HH}}
   \mathrm{DT}^{\mathrm{mot}}(\gamma)^{\Omega(\gamma)}.
\]
The positive half is the BPS selector $\HH$: only those classes whose
central charge sits strictly above the real axis contribute
$\mathrm{Im}(Z)>0$ BPS states to $\Yp$.

\subsection*{Cycle 4. $\Yp(K3\times E)$ as graded sum of vanishing cycles}

At Calabi--Yau dimension 3, Joyce--Song and Kontsevich--Soibelman
define the motivic (and cohomological) DT invariant through the
sheaf of vanishing cycles $\phi_{W}$ on the moduli stack
$\Mum_{\gamma}$ of $\sigma$-semistable objects of class $\gamma$
(Joyce--Song, Mem.\ AMS 217 (2012), 1020; Kontsevich--Soibelman,
arXiv:1006.2706 Thm.\ 1). The Donaldson--Thomas / vanishing-cycle
cohomology $H^{*}(\Mum_{\gamma},\phi_{W})$ is the graded piece. The
positive-half BPS algebra is
\[
\Yp(K3\times E;\sigma)
 := \bigoplus_{\substack{\gamma\in K_{0}(\Db(K3\times E))\\
   Z_{\sigma}(\gamma)\in\HH}}
   H^{*}(\Mum_{\gamma}^{\sigma\text{-ss}},\phi_{W}),
\]
with CoHA product supplied by extension stacks
$\Mum_{\gamma_{1}}\times\Mum_{\gamma_{2}}\leftarrow
\Mum_{\gamma_{1},\gamma_{2}}^{\mathrm{ext}}\rightarrow
\Mum_{\gamma_{1}+\gamma_{2}}$ (Kontsevich--Soibelman Sec.\ 7;
Schiffmann--Vasserot, Pub.\ Math.\ IH\'ES 118 (2013), 213--342,
adapted Oberdieck--Pandharipande, arXiv:1411.1514 Thm.\ 3).

\subsection*{Cycle 5. Wall-crossing and CoHA invariance}

As $\sigma$ crosses a codimension-one wall $W\subset\Stab$ the heart
tilts and the semistable locus $\Mum_{\gamma}^{\sigma}$ changes,
but the motivic (and cohomological) DT invariant
$\Omega(\gamma)$ transforms by the Kontsevich--Soibelman integrality
formula (arXiv:0811.2435 Thm.\ 8): for each wall the automorphism
$\mathcal{T}_{\gamma}$ acts on the torus
$T_{K_{0}}=\mathrm{Spec}\,\Z[K_{0}]$, and the wall-crossing identity
states that the formal product
$\prod_{\gamma}\mathcal{T}_{\gamma}^{\Omega(\gamma)}$
is invariant under the change of slope ordering. The graded-dimension
character of $\Yp(K3\times E;\sigma)$ is therefore independent of
$\sigma\in\Stab^{\dagger}(K3\times E)$ up to CoHA isomorphism; the
associated automorphism of the quantum torus is the CoHA
$r$-matrix. The Bayer--Macr\`\i chamber structure for the K3 factor
(Inventiones 198 (2014), 505--590) supplies the wall-and-chamber
decomposition on the K3 side; the elliptic factor contributes walls at
rational slopes of $H^{1}(E,\Z)$, reflecting the
Fourier--Mukai $SL_{2}(\Z)$ action
(Mukai, Nagoya Math.\ J.\ 81 (1981), 153--175).

\subsection*{$\kappa$-invariants on the positive half}

The four subscripted invariants read against $\Yp(K3\times E)$:
\[
\kcat(K3\times E)=0,\qquad
\kch(K3\times E)=\chi(\mathcal{O}_{K3\times E})=0\text{ (CY$_{3}$, Vol III Thm.~CY-D)},
\]
\[
\kBKM(\Phi_{N})=c_{N}(0)/2,\ \ N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}\text{ (Borcherds 1995; Gritsenko 1999)},
\]
\[
\kfib(K3)=2.
\]
The spectrum $\{2,3,5,24\}$ on $G(K3\times E)$ arises from four distinct
constructions (Mukai lattice, Igusa $\Phi_{10}$ via Gritsenko
$\Delta_{5}$, BKM Borcherds weight, K3 fibre rank); none of them is
$\Phifun$ applied multiple times. The universal identity
$\kBKM(\Phi_{N})=c_{N}(0)/2$ at $N=1$ gives $\Delta_{5}$ of weight 5
with $c_{1}(0)=10$, hence $\kBKM=5$; the claimed additivity
$\kBKM=\kch+\chi(\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{fiber}})$ fails at every $N$ as
documented in the cache.

\subsection*{Outcome}

Bridgeland stability on $\Db(K3\times E)$ is open at full
support-property strength but established in polynomial-stability form
(Toda 2009) and in DT/BPS form at the $(\infty,1)$-lane (KS 2010).
Positive-half extraction
$\Yp(K3\times E;\sigma)=\bigoplus_{Z(\gamma)\in\HH}
H^{*}(\Mum_{\gamma},\phi_{W})$ is defined chamber by chamber, and
wall-crossing acts by the KS automorphism preserving the graded
CoHA. The construction is the stability-theoretic realisation of the
positive half of the BPS Yangian of $K3\times E$; it is compatible with
the Oberdieck--Pandharipande K3$\times E$ DT/GW correspondence
(arXiv:1411.1514) and feeds the seven-faces diagram via
$\Yp\hookrightarrow G(K3\times E)$.
