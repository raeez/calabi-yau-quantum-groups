\section*{Local del Pezzo positive halves}

Let $S_n = \dP_n = \mathrm{Bl}_n(\mathbb{P}^2, p_1, \ldots, p_n)$ for
$n = 0, \ldots, 8$, with $p_i$ in general position. The local surface
$X_n = \mathrm{tot}(K_{S_n})$ is a non-compact Calabi--Yau threefold;
its derived category $D^b\mathrm{Coh}(X_n)$ carries a full strong
exceptional collection away from the discriminant of $S_n$, and its
BPS category is encoded in a quiver with potential $(Q_n, W_n)$
(Cecotti--Vafa 2009, \S 3--5; for $n \le 3$, confirmed by
Bridgeland--King--Reid 2001 via McKay). The positive half is
$\Yp(X_n) := \CoHA(Q_n, W_n)$ in the sense of
Kontsevich--Soibelman and Schiffmann--Vasserot. The chiral-side
output of $\Phi$ at $d = 3$ is $E_1$; $\Yp(X_n)$ is an associative
bialgebra, not a vertex algebra.

\subsection*{Cycle 1 --- $n \le 3$: toric, McKay, Kapranov}

\textbf{Attack.} $S_0 = \mathbb{P}^2$ admits the Beilinson exceptional
collection $\langle \mathcal{O}, \mathcal{O}(1), \mathcal{O}(2)\rangle$;
$K_{\mathbb{P}^2}$ is the crepant resolution of
$\mathbb{C}^3/\mathbb{Z}_3$ with generator $\zeta = \mathrm{diag}
(\omega, \omega, \omega)$, $\omega = e^{2\pi i/3}$
(Bridgeland--King--Reid 2001, Theorem 1.2). The McKay quiver $Q_0$
is the $3$-loop $\widetilde{A}_2$ quiver on three vertices with nine
arrows $(a_i, b_i, c_i)_{i=0,1,2}$ cycled by $\mathbb{Z}_3$, and
$W_0 = \sum_{\mathrm{cyc}} a_i b_{i+1} c_{i+2} - a_i c_{i+1} b_{i+2}$.
For $n = 1, 2, 3$, blowing up toric points corresponds to
\emph{mutation} of $(Q_0, W_0)$ at the vertex of the blown-up fibre
(Derksen--Weyman--Zelevinsky 2008, Theorem 5.7). Concretely:
$Q_1$ is the suspended pinch point; $Q_2$ is the $L^{1,2,1}/\mathbb{Z}_2$
orbifold quiver; $Q_3$ is the $C^3/(\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2)$
quiver whose toric diagram is the hexagon $\mathrm{Hex}$.

\textbf{Heal.} Each $\Yp(X_n)$ for $n \le 3$ inherits from the
Kapranov exceptional collection a $\mathbb{Z}^{n+1}$-grading by
numerical class $[E] \in K_0(S_n) = \mathbb{Z}\langle H, e_1, \ldots,
e_n\rangle$. The generating function of graded dimensions is the
refined topological vertex amplitude $Z_{\mathrm{top}}(X_n; q, t)$ of
Iqbal--Kashani-Poor (2006, \S 4). Cohomological Hall multiplication
on $\Yp(X_n)$ is the Hall--Drinfeld product on quiver representations;
at $n=0$, $\Yp(K_{\mathbb{P}^2}) = Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_3)$
(positive Schiffmann--Vasserot shifted affine Yangian) modulo the
potential $W_0$, and $\kch(X_0) = 3/2$ as recorded in the Vol III
stratification.

\subsection*{Cycle 2 --- $n \ge 4$: non-toric, tilting bundles}

\textbf{Attack.} For $n \ge 4$, $S_n$ is no longer toric: no torus
has a dense orbit. The crepant resolution / orbifold presentation
fails. Replace it by a \emph{tilting bundle} $\TP_n$ on $S_n$
whose direct summands are line bundles arising from the exceptional
basis $\{H, e_1, \ldots, e_n\}$ of $\Pic(S_n)$ together with
$-K_{S_n} = 3H - \sum e_i$ (Cecotti--Vafa 2009, Table 2). Pulling
back to $X_n = \mathrm{tot}(K_{S_n})$ by the projection
$\pi: X_n \to S_n$ yields $\widetilde{\TP}_n = \pi^* \TP_n$, and
$\mathrm{End}_{X_n}(\widetilde{\TP}_n)$ is a $3$-Calabi--Yau algebra
whose Ginzburg DG presentation $(Q_n, W_n)$ is the Donovan quiver.

\textbf{Heal.} For $n = 4, 5$: $(Q_n, W_n)$ contains respectively
$\binom{n+1}{2} + 1$ vertices past $n = 3$, with cubic potential
tracked by the intersection form on $S_n$.  Explicit forms appear
in Cecotti--Vafa 2009, \S 6.2--6.3. The Kontsevich--Soibelman
cohomological Hall algebra of $(Q_n, W_n)$ defines $\Yp(X_n)$, and
DWZ mutation-invariance implies $\Yp(X_n)$ is independent of the
choice of tilting representative.

\subsection*{Cycle 3 --- positive halves as bialgebras}

The general definition is
\[
\Yp(X_n) \;:=\; \CoHA(Q_n, W_n) \;=\; \bigoplus_{\gamma \in
\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}^{Q_n^0}} H^{\mathrm{BM}}_*\!\bigl(
\mathfrak{M}(Q_n, \gamma), \varphi_{W_n}\bigr),
\]
with product the Hall--Drinfeld star and coproduct via Joyce's
cohomological integration map. The BPS Lie algebra of Davison--Meinhardt
identifies the primitive part of $\Yp(X_n)$ and is, for each $n$, a
$\mathbb{Z}^{n+1}$-graded Lie algebra whose Poincar\'e series equals
$\sum_{\gamma} \Omega_\gamma(X_n) q^\gamma$, with $\Omega_\gamma$ the
refined DT invariant.

\subsection*{Cycle 4 --- $n = 6, 7, 8$: $E_n$ structure}

\textbf{Attack.} On $S_n$ for $n \ge 3$, the orthogonal complement
of $K_{S_n}$ inside $\Pic(S_n)$ is a root lattice: $A_2 \oplus A_1$
($n=4$), $D_5$ ($n=5$), $E_6$ ($n=6$), $E_7$ ($n=7$), $E_8$ ($n=8$).
Roots are $(-1)$-curves, and $-K_{S_n}$ pairs with each root trivially
(Manin, \emph{Cubic forms}, Theorem 25.4). The Weyl group of the root
system acts on $D^b\mathrm{Coh}(S_n)$ by spherical twists at the
$(-1)$-classes; this lifts to a $W(E_n)$-action on
$\Yp(X_n)$ by DWZ mutation functors.

\textbf{Heal.} For $n = 6, 7, 8$, conjecturally
$\Yp(X_n) \cong Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{e}}_n)$: the positive half of
the affine Yangian of $E_n$, with Cartan generators for the root
lattice and currents for $-K_{S_n}$. This realises, on the chiral
side, the $E_n$ affinisation predicted by Katz--Morrison--Plesser
and verified at characters by Iqbal--Kashani-Poor 2006, \S 5. The
identification $\kch(X_n) = 9 - n$ matches the rank of $-K_{S_n}^2 =
9-n$ on $S_n$.

\subsection*{Cycle 5 --- Nekrasov matching}

The refined instanton partition function on $X_n$ is
\[
Z_{\mathrm{Nek}}^{X_n}(q, t, Q_1, \ldots, Q_n) = \prod_{\gamma}
\Bigl(1 - q^{\gamma_0} t^{-\gamma_0} \prod_{i} Q_i^{\gamma_i}\Bigr)^{
-\Omega_\gamma(X_n)},
\]
a Gopakumar--Vafa product. Iqbal--Kashani-Poor 2006 compute
$Z_{\mathrm{Nek}}^{X_n}$ for $n \le 3$ by the topological vertex, and
for $n = 6$ match $\prod_\gamma$ against the character of the level-one
$\widehat{\mathfrak{e}}_6$ basic representation. On the CoHA side
(Kontsevich--Soibelman), the left-hand side is the motivic Hilbert
series of $\Yp(X_n)$, so the matching
$\mathrm{Hilb}(\Yp(X_n); q, t, Q) = Z_{\mathrm{Nek}}^{X_n}(q, t, Q)$
is a theorem for $n \le 3$ (toric vertex) and the $E_n$-structure
conjecture (Cycle 4) for $n \ge 6$. Status is
\texttt{\textbackslash ClaimStatusConjectured} for $n \ge 4$.

\subsection*{Summary}

$\Yp(X_n)$ is the cohomological Hall algebra of the Donovan quiver
$(Q_n, W_n)$; at $d = 3$ its image under $\Phi$ is $E_1$, not
$E_2$. The toric regime $n \le 3$ is a theorem via McKay and DWZ;
the non-toric regime $n \ge 4$ is a conjecture stabilised by the
refined topological vertex and the root lattice $E_n$. The identity
\[
\mathrm{Hilb}(\Yp(X_n); q, t, Q) = Z_{\mathrm{Nek}}^{X_n}(q, t, Q),
\qquad n = 0, 1, 2, 3,
\]
is the chain-level witness; the $(\infty,1)$-statement that $\Phi$
carries the derived stack $\mathcal{M}(X_n)$ to $\Yp(X_n)$ is a
parallel statement in derived geometry.
