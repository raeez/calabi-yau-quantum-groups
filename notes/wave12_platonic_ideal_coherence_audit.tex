\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The durable content is the separation of four constructions attached
to $K3 \times E$, the separation of the CHL Borcherds products from the
extended Gritsenko--Clery diagonal-divisor catalogue, and the dimension
scope of the CY-to-chiral specialization.  The note records those
objects directly, with no dependence on process history.
\end{abstract}

\section{Four invariants on $K3 \times E$}

The CY-to-chiral construction has the two-stage form
\[
  \Phi_d =
  \operatorname{Sp}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Sigma_{d-1}, C}
  \circ \Phifa_d .
\]
The first factor produces a holomorphic factorisation algebra from a
CY$_d$ input.  The second factor specializes along
$(\Sigma_{d-1}, C)$ and, for $d \geq 3$, produces an $E_1$-chiral
algebra on the curve.  An $E_2$-structure belongs instead to a centre,
double, or representation category under the corresponding hypotheses.

The compact threefold $K3 \times E$ carries four distinct numerical
readings:
\[
  \kcat(K3 \times E)
    = \chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)
    = 2 \cdot 0
    = 0,
\]
\[
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3 \times E)=3,\qquad
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=5,\qquad
  \kfib(K3)=24 .
\]
Thus the spectrum is
\[
  \{0,3,5,24\}.
\]
The value $\chi(\cO_{K3})=2$ is a fibre computation and does not replace
the Kunneth-multiplicative total-space value.

\section{Borcherds weights}

For every Borcherds product in the CHL family, the BKM weight is the
zero Fourier coefficient divided by two:
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_N)=\frac{c_N(0)}{2}.
\]
For the CHL rows
\[
  N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\},
  \qquad
  (c_N(0))=(10,8,6,4,2),
\]
and hence
\[
  \bigl(\kBKM(\Phi_1),\kBKM(\Phi_2),\kBKM(\Phi_3),
  \kBKM(\Phi_4),\kBKM(\Phi_6)\bigr)
  =(5,4,3,2,1).
\]
This is the CHL-averaged paramodular family.

The extended Gritsenko--Clery diagonal-divisor catalogue is a separate
eight-row object.  Its rows are indexed by their own multiplier and
cover data; they must be cited with that row map. They are not the
CHL sequence.  In the Vol III normalization the catalogue has
weights
\[
  (5,2,3,1,2,1/2,3/2,1)
\]
and Fourier constants
\[
  (10,4,6,2,4,1,3,2),
\]
again satisfying row by row $\kBKM=c(0)/2$.  No weight-zero row and no
quarter-weight row occur in this eight-row diagonal-divisor catalogue.

\section{Positive halves and centres}

On $\C^3$ the cohomological Hall algebra is the positive half:
\[
  \CoHA(\C^3)=\Yplus .
\]
The full $\mathcal W_{1+\infty}$ object is reached through the
Drinfeld double or through an evaluation representation of the
Yangian-type algebra.  It is not the positive half itself.  This
distinction is the same dimension-scope distinction seen in the
CY-to-chiral functor: at $d \geq 3$ the specialized chiral algebra is
$E_1$, while higher commutativity appears after taking centres,
doubles, or representation categories.

\section{The order-six anchor}

At $N=6$, the umbral moonshine Niemeier lattice is $6D_4$.  The cyclic
quotient singularity data are
\[
  2A_5+2A_2+2A_1 .
\]
These are different inputs to the same comparison problem.  The lattice
anchor and the quotient-singularity decomposition should not be
identified.

\section{Proof obligations}

The mathematical obligations left by this synthesis are concrete:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item compute the relevant twisted paramodular dimensions directly,
  for example by a Hashimoto or Ibukiyama--Wakatsuki trace formula;
  \item write the CY$_3$ chain-level construction of $\Phi_3$ beyond
  the toric and $K3 \times E$ cases, including the explicit
  $E_1$-specialization datum;
  \item exhibit the simultaneous-twining group cohomology calculation
  for $H^3(C_{M_{24}}(g_N);U(1))$ at $N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$;
  \item prove the descent from six-dimensional holomorphic
  Chern--Simons observables to the $K3 \times E$ chiral BKM datum.
\end{enumerate}
