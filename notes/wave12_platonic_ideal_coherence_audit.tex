(\texttt{notes/wave12\_master\_session\_synthesis.tex}, fourteen
crystallisations) against the four battle-hardened platonic ideals

$\mathrm{av}\colon\fg^{\Eone}\to\fg^{\mathrm{mod}}$ averaging spine).
Fourteen wave-$12$ crystallisations graded against each: affirmed
with exact-wording alignment, drift requiring text tightening, genuine
disagreement requiring epistemic-hierarchy adjudication, silent-but-%
should-be-added as augmentation. One primary-literature disagreement

platonic ideals against the wave-$12$ synthesis numerics

Gritsenko--Nikulin $1998$ Thm~$2.1$ --- with epistemic adjudication

proposals live in \S\ref{sec:drift}, \S\ref{sec:disagreement},
\S\ref{sec:silent}, \S\ref{sec:disposition} as authorisation-pending
items.
\end{abstract}
