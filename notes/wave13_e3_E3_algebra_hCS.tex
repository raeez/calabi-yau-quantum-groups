\section*{Holomorphic \texorpdfstring{$E_3$}{E3} Algebra of Six-Dimensional hCS Observables}

\paragraph{Local datum.}
Let \(U\subset\C^3\) be a Stein polydisc, or let \(U=\C^3\) with a
fixed polynomial or compact-support boundary condition. Let
\((\fg,\langle-,-\rangle)\) be a finite-dimensional quadratic complex dg
Lie algebra. The six-real-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons field
complex on \(U\) is
\[
  \mathscr L_{\hCS}(V)
  =
  \Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(V,\fg)[1],
  \qquad
  \ell_1=\dbar,\qquad
  \ell_2(\alpha,\beta)=[\alpha\wedge\beta]_{\fg},
\]
for relatively compact opens \(V\subset U\). The shift is the BV shift.
In unshifted Dolbeault degree the \((0,1)\)-part is the physical gauge
field, the \((0,0)\)-part is the ghost, and the \((0,2)\), \((0,3)\)
parts are the BV antifields paired by
\[
  \omega_{\BV}(\alpha,\beta)
  =
  \int_U dz_1\wedge dz_2\wedge dz_3\wedge\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle .
\]
The classical action
\[
  S_{\mathrm{cl}}(a)=
  \int_U dz_1\wedge dz_2\wedge dz_3\wedge
  \left(
    \frac12\,\langle a,\dbar a\rangle
    +\frac16\,\langle a,[a,a]\rangle
  \right)
\]
satisfies the classical master equation because \(\dbar^2=0\), the
wedge-Lie bracket obeys Jacobi, and \(\langle-,-\rangle\) is invariant.

\paragraph{Classical observables.}
The classical hCS factorisation algebra is the Chevalley cochain
factorisation algebra of the local dg Lie algebra:
\[
  \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS}(V)
  =
  C^\bullet_{\CE,\mathrm{cont}}\bigl(\mathscr L_{\hCS}(V)\bigr)
  =
  \widehat{\Sym}\bigl(\mathscr L_{\hCS}(V)^\vee[-1]\bigr),
\]
with differential induced by \(\dbar\) and \(\ell_2\). For pairwise
disjoint opens \(V_1,\ldots,V_k\subset V\), extension by zero gives the
factorisation product
\[
  \mu_{V_1,\ldots,V_k}\colon
  \bigotimes_{i=1}^k\Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS}(V_i)
  \longrightarrow
  \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS}(V).
\]
This construction is Costello--Gwilliam's classical observable
factorisation algebra for a local dg Lie algebra.

\paragraph{Holomorphic \texorpdfstring{$E_3$}{E3} structure.}
The preceding factorisation algebra is holomorphic in three complex
directions. Its operations over \(k\) labelled points are controlled by
the Dolbeault chains of \(\Conf_k(\C^3)\), with composition induced by
collision and insertion of configurations. Hence
\(\Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS}\) is an algebra over the holomorphic
little-\(3\)-disks operad in \(\Ch(\Dolb)\), equivalently an
\(\Ethree^{\mathrm{hol}}\)-factorisation algebra in the sense of
Costello--Gwilliam and Gwilliam--Williams.

The locally constant theorem of Lurie and Francis enters after a
separate reduction requiring coherent topological transport that forgets
the Dolbeault variation along configurations. Under such a
translation-invariant Dolbeault contraction, the reduced stalk is an
ordinary \(\Ethree\)-algebra. Prior to that contraction, the structure is
holomorphic factorisation, with operadic level indexed by the complex
dimension.

\paragraph{Configuration formulas.}
For \(z=(z_1,\ldots,z_k)\in\Conf_k(\C^3)\), the \(k\)-ary operation is
the factorisation product obtained by placing \(k\) compactly supported
observables in disjoint polydisks centred at \(z_i\). Its dependence on
\(z\) is Dolbeault flat up to the standard boundary terms on the
Fulton--MacPherson compactification:
\[
  \dbar_{\Conf} m_k
  +
  \sum_{\substack{S\subset\{1,\ldots,k\}\\2\leq |S|<k}}
  \pm\,
  m_{k-|S|+1}
  \bigl(m_{|S|},\mathrm{id},\ldots,\mathrm{id}\bigr)
  =0 .
\]
This is the operadic Stokes identity. For \(k=2\) it is the binary
factorisation product; higher boundary strata encode the coherent
\(\Ethree^{\mathrm{hol}}\)-associativity data. The underlying
Browder bracket in the topological reduction has degree \(1-3=-2\).

\paragraph{Quantum package.}
A quantum hCS factorisation algebra is an additional datum. It requires
a holomorphic volume form, the BV odd symplectic pairing above, an
elliptic Costello gauge fixing \(Q^{\mathrm{GF}}\), heat kernels
\(K_t\), regularised propagators \(P_{\varepsilon,L}\), completed local
functionals, renormalisation counterterms, orientation and
determinant-line framing, sewing or factorisation descent, and
effective interactions \(I[L]\) satisfying the scale-\(L\) quantum
master equation
\[
  QI[L]+\frac12\{I[L],I[L]\}_L+\hbar\Delta_L I[L]=0 .
\]
When this datum has been fixed and the obstruction classes vanish or
are trivialised by specified counterterms, the quantum observables are
\[
  \Obs^q_{\hCS,L}(V)
  =
  \left(
    \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS}(V)[[\hbar]],
    d_{\Obs}^{\mathrm{cl}}+\{I[L],-\}_L+\hbar\Delta_L
  \right),
\]
and the factorisation maps are the renormalised Costello--Gwilliam
maps. The word \(\Ethree\) for the quantum object refers to this solved
BV package together with the holomorphic or topologically reduced
operadic structure just described.

\paragraph{Quartic anomaly and two-point renormalisation.}
For a representation \(\rho:\fg\to\End(W)\), set
\[
  I_4^\rho(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)
  =
  \frac1{4!}\sum_{\sigma\in S_4}
  \Tr_W\!\left(
    \rho(x_{\sigma(1)})\rho(x_{\sigma(2)})
    \rho(x_{\sigma(3)})\rho(x_{\sigma(4)})
  \right).
\]
In complex dimension three the one-loop gauge obstruction to the QME is
represented by the local functional
\[
  \Theta^\rho_U(\alpha_0,\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3)
  =
  \int_U
  \left[
  I_4^\rho\bigl(
    \alpha_0,\partial\alpha_1,\partial\alpha_2,\partial\alpha_3
  \bigr)
  \right]_{(3,3)},
  \qquad
  \alpha_i\in\Omega_c^{0,\bullet}(U,\fg).
\]
Its class is
\[
  \mathfrak a_{\hCS}(U,\fg,\rho)
  =
  [\,\hbar\,\Theta^\rho_U\,]
  \in
  H^1_{\mathrm{loc}}
  \bigl(\Omega^{0,\bullet}(U,\fg)[1],\cO_{\mathrm{loc}}\bigr).
\]
This is the Costello--Li quartic class; in adjoint ghost-field form it is
the descent representative \(\int_U\Tr_{\mathrm{ad}}(cF_A^3)\).

For dual bases \(\{T_a\}\), \(\{T^a\}\) of \(\fg\), the quadratic
Casimir coefficient
\[
  C_2^\rho=\sum_a\rho(T_a)\rho(T^a)
\]
belongs to the two-point field-strength counterterm, for example
\[
  S_{\mathrm{ct}}^{(2)}
  =
  -\hbar\,\frac{C_2^\rho}{(4\pi)^3}
  \log(L/\varepsilon)
  \int_U dz_1\wedge dz_2\wedge dz_3\wedge\Tr_\rho(a\,\dbar a)
\]
up to the convention for the invariant pairing and heat-kernel
normalisation. The quartic anomaly has arity \(4\) and cohomological
degree \(1\); the Casimir counterterm has arity \(2\) and degree \(0\).

\paragraph{Compact global hCS.}
Let \(Y\) be a compact Calabi--Yau threefold. A global quantum hCS
factorisation algebra on \(Y\) requires a holomorphic volume form,
orientation and determinant-line framing, a quadratic gauge dg Lie
algebra, elliptic gauge fixing, heat-kernel regulator, completed local
functional topology, counterterm scheme, factorisation or TCFT sewing
descent, a vanishing theorem or chosen trivialisation for
\(\mathfrak a_{\hCS}(Y,\fg,\rho)\), and higher-loop obstruction control.
A comparison with Hall or CY-to-chiral structures additionally requires
a completed comparison functor preserving augmentation, coproduct,
pairing, filtration, completion, and specialisation data.

\paragraph{Separation of fields, polyvectors, and CY data.}
The hCS fields are Dolbeault forms with coefficients in a chosen gauge
dg Lie algebra. The BV antifields are complementary Dolbeault
components paired by the holomorphic volume form. Hochschild
polyvectors of a CY category form a different complex; for instance
\[
  \HH^\bullet(\Perf(\C^3))
  \simeq
  \C[x,y,z]\otimes
  \bigwedge\nolimits^\bullet
  \langle\partial_x,\partial_y,\partial_z\rangle .
\]
Categorical CY data consist of a negative-cyclic trace, Serre shift,
and framing. A comparison between these data and hCS requires an
explicit framing object or an hCS-to-Hochschild comparison map.

\paragraph{Chain-level and \texorpdfstring{$(\infty,1)$}{(infinity,1)} lanes.}
The chain-level lane consists of the Dolbeault dg Lie algebra
\(\mathscr L_{\hCS}\), Chevalley cochains, factorisation products, bar
operations, BV propagators, and the local obstruction classes. The
\((\infty,1)\)-categorical lane consists of holomorphic factorisation
\(\infty\)-categories, augmented factorisation-envelope functors,
centres, module categories, and specialisation functors. A comparison
theorem is the data of a functorial map preserving augmentation,
filtrations, completions, and the relevant operadic structures. Both
lanes are load-bearing hypotheses.

\paragraph{Relation to \texorpdfstring{$\Phi_3$}{Phi3}.}
The factorisation \(\Phi_3=\SpCh_{\Sigma_2,C}\circ\PhiFA_3\) separates
the factorisation object from the curve-specialised chiral algebra:
\[
  \PhiFA_3(\mathcal C)\in E_3\text{-}\mathrm{HolFA}(X).
\]
For a witnessed admissible pair \((\Sigma_2,C)\), the specialised
CY-to-chiral output is
\[
  A_C=
  \SpCh_{\Sigma_2,C}\bigl(\PhiFA_3(\mathcal C)\bigr)
  \in \mathrm{Alg}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Eone}(C).
\]
The \(E_3\) structure of \(\PhiFA_3(\mathcal C)\), and of bar objects
such as \(B^{E_3}(\PhiFA_3(\mathcal C))\), is an external bulk or
factorisation-side structure. The \(d = 3\) chiral output \(A_C\) is
native \(\Eone\). The braided \(\Etwo\) layer is recovered from a
centre, double, or module category, for example
\[
  Z\bigl(\Rep^{\Eone}(A_C)\bigr)
  \simeq
  \Rep^{\Etwo}\bigl(\Zchder(A_C)\bigr)
\]
on the constructed Drinfeld-centre locus.

\paragraph{Toric Hall comparison.}
On the toric Hall side the direct critical CoHA output is the positive
half
\[
  \CoHA(\C^3)\simeq Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1),
\]
as in the Kontsevich--Soibelman and Schiffmann--Vasserot normalisation.
The Drinfeld double or centre, completion, and evaluation/Fock-module
operations produce the full \(\cW_{1+\infty}\) vertex algebra. A
comparison between the hCS factorisation algebra and the toric CoHA
requires a separately constructed comparison locus.
