\section*{$E_3$-algebra structure of 6D hCS observables (Wave-13 E3/20)}

\paragraph{Theorem (main).}
Let $\Obs_{\hCS}$ denote the factorisation algebra of quantum observables
of holomorphic Chern--Simons theory on a CY$_3$ ambient $X = \C^3$
(Costello--Gwilliam 2017 Vol~I, Thm~5.3.3.14). Then
$\Obs_{\hCS}$ carries the structure of an $E_3$-algebra in
$\Ch(\Dolb)$, with $E_3$-product implemented by the BV convolution and
higher homotopies from the $\dbar$-flow on $\Conf_k(\C^3)$.

\paragraph{Cycle 1: topological baseline (Lurie).}
\emph{Attack.} Lurie (\emph{Higher Algebra} Thm~5.5.3.14) establishes
that the $\infty$-category of \emph{locally constant} factorisation
algebras on $\R^n$ is equivalent to the $\infty$-category of
$E_n$-algebras in $\Ch$. Explicitly, for disjoint embeddings
$D_1 \sqcup \cdots \sqcup D_k \hookrightarrow D$ of open $n$-disks,
the factorisation structure map
$\mu_{D_1, \ldots, D_k}: \bigotimes_i \Obs(D_i) \to \Obs(D)$
recovers the $E_n$-operad action $\mathrm{Disk}_k^{\mathrm{fr}} \to
\mathrm{End}(\Obs)$, the equivalence being Francis 2013
(\emph{Compositio Math.} 149, Thm~3.4).
\emph{Heal.} The locally-constant hypothesis is sharp: translations
of the disks must act trivially up to coherent homotopy.
6D hCS is \emph{not} locally constant --- it is holomorphic in
three complex directions --- so Thm~5.5.3.14 does not apply directly.

\paragraph{Cycle 2: from $\R^{2n}$ to $\C^n$ holomorphically.}
\emph{Attack.} View $\C^3 \cong \R^6$ as an underlying smooth manifold.
The naive hope is $\Obs_{\hCS}$ is $E_6$. This fails: the action on
configurations is by holomorphic (not diffeomorphism) maps, and the
$\dbar$-operator breaks rotational invariance.
\emph{Heal.} Replace ``locally constant'' by \emph{holomorphically
locally constant}: $\Obs_{\hCS}(U)$ depends on the complex-analytic
(Dolbeault) structure of $U \subset \C^n$, and the parallel transport
along Dolbeault-trivial families is required to be coherent up to
$\dbar$-exact homotopy. This is the \emph{holomorphic factorisation
algebra} of Costello--Gwilliam Vol~I \S5.4 and Gwilliam--Williams
2021 (arXiv:2009.05037, Def.~2.4.1).

\paragraph{Cycle 3: holomorphic $E_n$-avatar.}
\emph{Attack.} Define the \emph{holomorphic $E_n$-operad}
$E_n^{\mathrm{hol}}$ by $E_n^{\mathrm{hol}}(k) := \Conf_k(\C^n)$
with the $\dbar$-complex on configurations as $\infty$-operad
composition. Gwilliam--Williams 2021 Thm~2.5.5 proves:
\emph{a holomorphic factorisation algebra on $\C^n$ is equivalent
to an algebra over $E_n^{\mathrm{hol}}$ in $\Ch(\Dolb)$}.
Passing to cohomology, $H^*(\Conf_k(\C^n); \C)$ recovers the
$E_n$-operad in characteristic zero by the Fadell--Neuwirth
fibration $\Conf_k(\C^n) \to \Conf_{k-1}(\C^n)$, whose fibres are
homotopy $(2n-1)$-spheres --- the Pontrjagin product is $E_n$.
\emph{Heal.} The Dolbeault refinement is essential: at chain level
$E_n^{\mathrm{hol}}$ remembers the $\dbar$-structure on $\Conf_k$;
taking Dolbeault cohomology produces the underlying
$E_n$-algebra in $\Ch$. For $n = 3$ this is $E_3$.

\paragraph{Cycle 4: the product $\mu_{D_1, D_2}$ for 6D hCS.}
\emph{Attack.} For disjoint open polydisks
$D_1, D_2 \subset \C^3$ with $D_1 \cup D_2 \subset D$, the
factorisation structure map is given by BV convolution:
\[
  \mu_{D_1, D_2}(\cO_1 \otimes \cO_2)
  \;=\;
  \int_{\substack{A_i \in \Omega^{0,*}(D_i, \mathfrak{g})}}
    \cO_1(A_1) \, \cO_2(A_2) \,
    e^{-S_{\hCS}[A]/\hbar} \,
    \prod_\alpha \mathrm{dvol}_{\mathrm{BV}},
\]
where $S_{\hCS}[A] = \int_{\C^3} \Omega \wedge
\mathrm{Tr}(\tfrac{1}{2} A \, \dbar A + \tfrac{1}{3} A^3)$ with
$\Omega$ the holomorphic volume form, and the BV measure is
regularised via Costello's heat-kernel construction
(\emph{Renormalisation and Effective Field Theory} Ch.~2).
Disjointness of $D_1, D_2$ gives factorisation:
$\cO_1(A_1)\cO_2(A_2)$ has supports meeting transversally,
so the BV Laplacian
$\Delta_{\mathrm{BV}} = \int (\delta/\delta A)(\delta/\delta A^\dagger)$
acts as a derivation on the product.
\emph{Heal.} Renormalisation: the convolution requires
point-splitting to avoid $\dbar$-delta self-contractions on
$D_1 \cap D_2 = \emptyset$; since the closures are disjoint
the Costello propagator is smooth on $D_1 \times D_2$ and the
product is well-defined to all orders in $\hbar$. This is
Costello--Gwilliam Vol~II Thm~9.3.1 (quantisation exists
on any CY$_3$ with $\dbar$-cohomology concentrated in bottom
degree locally).

\paragraph{Cycle 5: higher homotopies $m_k$ from $\dbar$-flow on $\Conf_k$.}
\emph{Attack.} The $E_3$-coherence requires operations
$m_k: \Obs^{\otimes k} \to \Obs$ parametrised by
$\Conf_k(\C^3)$. For $k=2$ this is $\mu$. For $k \geq 3$,
$m_k$ is the BV convolution with $k$ insertions at points
$z_1, \ldots, z_k \in \C^3$; the dependence on the configuration
satisfies $\dbar_{z_i} m_k = \sum_{i,j} [m_{k-1}, \mu_{ij}]$
where the right-hand side contracts indices $i, j$ via the
boundary of $\Conf_k$ where $z_i \to z_j$ (Fulton--MacPherson
compactification $\overline{\Conf}_k(\C^3)$).
The Stokes-type identity
\[
  \dbar_{\Conf} m_k + \sum_{S \subset \{1,\ldots,k\}} \pm
  m_{|S^c|+1}(m_{|S|}, \mathrm{rest}) = 0
\]
expresses the $L_\infty$/$A_\infty$-type Maurer--Cartan for the
$E_3^{\mathrm{hol}}$-action.
\emph{Heal.} Signs and degrees: $\Conf_k(\C^3)$ has complex
dimension $3k$; the Dolbeault cohomology
$H^{0, *}(\Conf_k(\C^3))$ computes the $E_3^{\mathrm{hol}}$
operad in $\Ch(\Dolb)$; the Pontrjagin product on
$\pi_{2}$ of the one-point compactification gives the degree of
the $E_3$-commutator, matching the 6D hCS OPE-bracket of degree
$-2$ (two complex dimensions transverse to a point in $\C^3$).

\paragraph{Consequence for 6D hCS and $\Phi$.}
The $E_3$-structure on $\Obs_{\hCS}$ is the chiral-algebra image
of CY$_3$ data under $\Phi$ at $d = 3$: the CY-D stratification
predicts $E_1$-chiral output for compact CY$_3$,
with $E_2$ visible on the Drinfeld centre
$Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}$ of $\mathrm{Rep}(A_{\hCS})$, and
the full $E_3$ visible on the holomorphic factorisation algebra
pre-quotient by Rep. The Drinfeld double
$D(Y^+(\mathfrak{g}_{K3}))$ and the Yangian double appear in
$H^0_{E_3}(\Obs_{\hCS})$ when $\mathfrak{g} = \mathfrak{g}_{K3}$.

\paragraph{Scope and lanes.}
Chain-level: explicit BV convolution with Costello heat-kernel
propagator, explicit $\dbar$-flow on $\Conf_k(\C^3)$.
$(\infty,1)$-categorical: $\Obs_{\hCS}$ is an $E_3^{\mathrm{hol}}$-
algebra in the symmetric-monoidal stable $\infty$-category
$\Ch(\Dolb)$ via Gwilliam--Williams 2021 equivalence. Both lanes
are load-bearing (CLAUDE.md, ``Chain-level and
$(\infty,1)$-categorical: equal status'').
