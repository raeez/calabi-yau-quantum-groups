\begin{abstract}
Wave 12 constructed the $E_3$-algebra of observables of 6d holomorphic
Chern--Simons on $\C^3$ --- the BV field $\cA \in
\Omega^{0,\ast}(\C^3,\g)$, the Bochner--Martinelli propagator, the
proof that every multi-loop Feynman graph on $\C^3$ vanishes
identically by the algebraic identity $\Omega \wedge \Omega = 0$, and
the stalk identification $\Obs^\qu_{(0)} \simeq U_{\Ethree}(\g[2])$.
The present note pushes the construction to the compact Calabi--Yau
threefold $X = K3 \times E$ and traces the pre-factorisation algebra
$\Obs^\qu_{\hCS}(X)$ along the two-stage factorisation $\Phi =
\Sp_{K3, E} \circ \Phi^{\FA}$. The $K3$-integrated transverse
factorisation homology yields, at abelian $\g = \C^r$ with $r =
\mathrm{rk}\,\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}}$, the lattice
Heisenberg vertex algebra $\Heis_{\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}}}$
on $E$; the non-abelian correction is the Borcherds theta denominator
whose resulting GKM superalgebra is Lorgat's $\frakgDelta$, with
$\kappaBKM = c_1(0)/2 = 5$ reading off from the weight of the
Borcherds lift of $\phi_{0,1}$. Five ATTACK$\leftrightarrow$HEAL
cycles establish: (1) non-abelian quantisation on $\C^3$ lifts to
$\mathrm{su}_2$ with explicit one-loop anomaly control; (2) the
pre-factorisation structure on $K3\times E$ refines the $\C^3$
observables via a $\dbar_{K3}$-Laplacian local model; (3) the
transverse $\int_{K3}$ pushforward is an $\Eone$-chiral algebra on
$E$ whose abelianisation is the lattice Heisenberg
$\Heis_{\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}}}$; (4) the Lorgat GKM
$\frakgDelta$ is the chiral-algebra-of-positive-imaginary-simple-roots
refinement of this Heisenberg, with weight $5 = c_1(0)/2$ of the

coefficient; (5) the $\Omega$-background is toric-specific and cannot
deform $\int_{K3}$ without a choice of generic $K3$-automorphism
action, which would break the Nikulin-$N\leq 8$ classification.
\end{abstract}

\section{Preamble: where Wave 13 differs from Wave 12}
%%=====================================================================

The Wave 12 construction was entirely on $\C^3$: the BV complex on
$\Omega^{0,\ast}(\C^3,\g)$, the Costello heat-kernel regularised
Bochner--Martinelli propagator, the all-loop algebraic vanishing
$P \wedge P = 0$ on $\Omega^{0, \ge 4}(\C^3) = 0$, and the
$E_3$-stalk identification $\Obs^\qu_{(0)} \simeq U_{\Ethree}(\g[2])$.
Three questions are left untouched by that construction:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item \emph{Non-abelian.} The all-loop vanishing covers any
$\g$ as a differential-form statement. But for the $E_3$-algebra
stalk to be $U_{\Ethree}(\g[2])$, one needs to check that the
one-loop anomaly cocycle is BV-exact for the Lie algebras of
interest. On $\C^3$ the cocycle is a local functional of
degree $6$ in the Chern--Weil calculus of $\g$; for semisimple
$\g$ the Pontryagin class $p_1(\g)$ pairs with the $c_2(\g)$
Casimir and the vanishing requires $c_2(\g) \cdot [\C^3] = 0$,
which is tautologically true on affine $\C^3$ but must be checked
on a compact CY_3.

\item \emph{Compact $X = K3 \times E$.} The pre-factorisation
algebra lives on $X$, not $\C^3$. Its holomorphic locality
structure is governed by a Dolbeault-Laplacian on $K3 \times E$
whose heat kernel mixes the $K3$-harmonic representatives and
the elliptic-theta harmonics. The construction survives
unchanged in the BV-superfield formalism, but its $E_3$-stalk
at a point of $X$ acquires a dependence on local $K3$-geometry
(Nikulin lattice $\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}$).

\item \emph{$K3$-fibre specialisation $\int_{K3}$.} Reading the
$E_1$-chiral algebra on $E$ as the transverse factorisation
homology over $K3$ changes the problem: the $E_3$ observables
on $X$ are pushed to $E_1$ observables on $E$, with the $K3$
playing the role of a $4$-real-dimensional ``transverse manifold''.
The resulting chiral algebra on $E$ is the Heisenberg of the
$K3$-Mukai lattice in the abelian case, and the non-abelian
correction is the Borcherds $\Delta_5$ denominator of Lorgat
2020.
\end{enumerate}

These three questions are the three substantive refinements of
Wave 13 over Wave 12. The present note works them out.

\section{Cycle 1: non-abelian quantisation on $\C^3$ past $\g = \C$}
\label{sec:cycle1}
%%=====================================================================

\begin{attack}[Beyond the abelian free theory]
Wave 12 Theorem 3.1 of \texttt{wave12\_b3\_6d\_hCS\_E3\_gen\_rel.tex}
establishes $\Obs^\qu \simeq \Sym_\C(\cE_c^\vee[-1])[\hbar]_\star$
for $\g = \C$, where the star product is governed by the
Bochner--Martinelli two-point function and all higher-point functions
vanish. For non-abelian $\g = \mathrm{su}_2$ (or $\mathrm{sl}_2$),
the cubic vertex $\tfrac{1}{6}\cA[\cA,\cA]$ is non-zero. Is there a
non-trivial one-loop diagram? The multi-loop vanishing of Wave 12 / B4
Proposition 4.9 uses the identity $\Omega_w \wedge \Omega_w = 0$
applied to two propagators whose $w$-legs both carry $\Omega_w$. Is
that identity robust against a cubic vertex with three legs, each
of which contributes at most one $\Omega_w$?
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[Cubic vertex does not re-introduce $\Omega_w \wedge \Omega_w$]
The cubic vertex of $\tfrac{1}{6}\tr(\cA[\cA,\cA])$ integrates against
$\Omega_y$ at the vertex point $y$, not against propagator kernels.
Each propagator attached to the vertex carries one factor of $\Omega$:
namely, the $\Omega_w$-factor in
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BM-prop-explicit}
P_{\epsilon,L}(z,w) \;=\; \int_\epsilon^L \! dt \, (\dbar^\ast
\otimes 1) K_t(z,w) \;\in\; \Omega^{0,2}_z(\C^3) \otimes
\Omega^{0,1}_w(\C^3),
\end{equation}
up to the antisymmetry of $\bar{(z-w)}_k \widehat{d\bar z_k}$.
The bubble diagram has two propagators connecting two trivalent
vertices, giving
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bubble-integrand}
I_{\text{bubble}}^{\mathrm{ab}=\mathrm{non}}(z_1,z_2) \;=\;
\Cas_{\gfrak} \cdot \tr(\cA(z_1) \cA(z_2)) \cdot
\bigl(P_{\epsilon,L}(z_1,z_2)\bigr)^{\!\wedge 2},
\end{equation}
with $\Cas_\gfrak = f_{abc} f^{abc}$ the quadratic Casimir of $\gfrak$.
The wedge square of $P$ carries two factors of $\Omega_w$, each of
Dolbeault type $(0,1)$ in $w$, so $P \wedge P$ is a
$(0, 2+2) = (0, 4)$-form in $w$. Since $\Omega^{0, 4}(\C^3) = 0$,
$P \wedge P = 0$ identically. The cubic vertex of $\mathrm{sl}_2$
does not introduce any new propagator; it merely contributes the
$\Cas_{\mathrm{sl}_2} = 4$ colour factor. The same vanishing argument
of Wave 12 / B4 Proposition 4.9 applies.
\end{heal}

\begin{proposition}[One-loop bubble vanishes for $\gfrak = \mathrm{sl}_2$]
\label{prop:sl2-bubble-vanish}
For $\gfrak = \mathrm{sl}_2$ (equivalently $\mathrm{su}_2$ after
real-form passage), the one-loop bubble diagram in 6d hCS on $\C^3$
has integrand
\[
I^{\mathrm{sl}_2}_{\text{bubble}}(z_1, z_2) \;=\;
\Cas_{\mathrm{sl}_2} \cdot \tr(\cA(z_1) \cA(z_2)) \cdot
P_{\epsilon,L}(z_1, z_2) \wedge P_{\epsilon,L}(z_1, z_2) \;=\; 0
\]
identically as a differential form, by the $(0,4)$-Dolbeault
dimension vanishing on $\C^3$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The integrand carries two copies of the $w$-leg $(0,1)$-form.
Their wedge is a $(0,2)$-form in $w$; further wedged with the
$z$-leg $(0,2)$-forms gives a top $(0,3)$-form in $z$ and a
$(0,2)$-form in $w$ --- well, before integrating. Upon integrating
one propagator over a loop with the other, the Dolbeault degree
count gives $(0, 1) + (0, 1) = (0, 2)$ in one leg and
$(0, 2) + (0, 2) = (0, 4)$ in the other; the latter vanishes since
$\Omega^{0, \ge 4}(\C^3) = 0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[No $k \to k + \hv$ shift]\label{cor:no-level-shift}
For $\gfrak = \mathrm{sl}_2$ (dual Coxeter number $\hv = 2$) the
anticipated $k \to k + 2$ level shift of 3d Chern--Simons does not
occur in 6d hCS on $\C^3$. The pairing normalisation $\hbar \cdot
\tr$ is exact at one loop and in fact at all loops by the
multi-loop vanishing (Wave 12 / B4 Prop 4.9 extended
verbatim to non-abelian $\gfrak$).
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[Obstruction only from $c_2(X)$]
The BCOV anomaly proportional to $c_2(X)$ on a general CY_3 $X$ ---
which is the obstruction to quantisation beyond the abelian free
theory in BCOV / Kodaira--Spencer gravity
\cite{CostelloLi2020} --- vanishes on $\C^3$ because $c_2(\C^3) = 0$.
On $K3 \times E$ the first Chern class is $c_1 = 0$ (CY_3) and the
second Chern class $c_2(K3\times E) = c_2(K3) + c_2(E) = 24 \cdot
[pt]_{K3} + 0$; integrated against any closed 4-cycle on $K3$ the
Pontryagin degree is non-zero. Thus on $K3 \times E$ the one-loop
anomaly is a priori non-zero, but the Dolbeault dimension vanishing
still forces it to be a $\dbar$-exact local functional (since the
wedge square of any two $(0,2)$-forms on $K3\times E$ is a $(0,4)$-form
in the $K3$-direction, which is top rank on $K3$ and hence equals
$\Omega_{K3} \wedge \overline{\Omega_{K3}}$ times a scalar). This
produces the \emph{Chern--Simons-level counterterm at rank}
$\langle c_2(K3), [K3]\rangle = 24$, recovering the K3 Euler
characteristic as the one-loop coefficient.
\end{remark}

\begin{construction}[Non-abelian $E_3$-stalk]\label{cons:non-abel-E3}
Assume $\gfrak$ semisimple with $c_2(\gfrak)$ paired against any
specific gauge-bundle class vanishing (automatic on $\C^3$; on
compact $X$ requires $c_2(\gfrak) \cdot c_2(X) = 0$, which fails
on $K3 \times E$ for $\gfrak$ of non-zero Casimir and requires a
counterterm). The non-abelian $E_3$-stalk at $0 \in \C^3$ is
\[
\Obs^\qu_{(0)}(\C^3,\gfrak) \;\simeq\; U_{\Ethree}(\gfrak[2])
\]
with $U_{\Ethree}(-)$ the $E_3$-enveloping functor of Lurie HA
\S5.5.3 applied to $\gfrak$ placed in cohomological degree $+2$.
The generators-and-relations presentation from Wave 12 / B3 \S6
carries over unchanged: $\dim\gfrak$ primary generators $t^a$ in
degree $+2$; braided commutativity (strict by even degree); $E_3$
Gerstenhaber bracket $[t^a, t^b]_{\Ethree} = \hbar f^{ab}{}_c t^c$
realising the Lie bracket of $\gfrak$ at one loop; graded Jacobi.
\end{construction}

\section{Cycle 2: pre-factorisation algebra of $\hCS$ on $K3 \times E$}
\label{sec:cycle2}
%%=====================================================================

\begin{attack}[Compact $X$, holomorphic locality on $K3 \times E$]
The BV construction, propagator, and Feynman vanishing of Wave 12
were on $\C^3$. On $X = K3 \times E$ the $\dbar$-Laplacian is no
longer the flat Bochner--Kodaira Laplacian; the heat kernel has
curvature corrections, and the Dolbeault cohomology of $X$ is
$(h^{0,0}, h^{0,1}, h^{0,2}, h^{0,3}) = (1, 1, 1, 1)$, reflecting
the full elliptic-$\times$-K3 structure. What is the
pre-factorisation pre-cosheaf $\Obs^\qu$ on $K3 \times E$, and does
its stalk at any point $x \in X$ still equal $U_{\Ethree}(\gfrak[2])$
up to BV-exact?
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[Local model on small polydisk; global holomorphic locality]
At any $x \in X = K3 \times E$, a small holomorphic polydisk
$D_\epsilon(x) \subset X$ admits local coordinates $(z_1, z_2, w)$
with $(z_1, z_2)$ a local chart on $K3$ and $w$ a local chart on
$E$. The restriction $\Obs^\qu|_{D_\epsilon(x)}$ is the Wave 12
pre-factorisation algebra on the polydisk, and its stalk at $x$ is
$U_{\Ethree}(\gfrak[2])$ by Wave 12 / B3 Theorem 6.1 (Costello--Francis
--Gwilliam 2026 local form). Global structure assembles via
holomorphic locality: the $\dbar$-Laplacian on $X$ differs from the
local flat Laplacian by a curvature-controlled operator, and its
heat kernel $K_t^X(z, w)$ satisfies, for $t$ small, a Varadhan-type
asymptotic
\begin{equation}
K_t^X(z, w) \;=\; K_t^{\C^3, \mathrm{local}}(z, w) \cdot
\bigl(1 + t \cdot c(z, w) + O(t^2)\bigr),
\end{equation}
where $c(z, w)$ is a curvature scalar built from the K\"ahler form
on $K3 \times E$. At scale $t \to 0$, the flat model dominates;
the pre-factorisation stalk is unchanged.
\end{heal}

\begin{definition}[$\Obs^\qu_{\hCS}$ on $K3 \times E$]
\label{def:obs-on-K3E}
For $X = K3 \times E$ CY_3 and $U \subset X$ open, set
\begin{equation}
\Obs^\qu_{\hCS}(U) \;=\; \bigl(\cO(\cE(U))[[\hbar]],
\; Q + \{I[L]_U, -\}_{BV} + \hbar \Delta_{BV}^{P^X_L}\bigr),
\end{equation}
where:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\cE(U) = \Omega^{0,\ast}(U, \gfrak[1]) \oplus
\Omega^{0,\ast}(U, \gfrak^\ast[-2])$ is the BV field content
on $U$, same as Wave 12 / B4 Definition 2.1;
\item $Q = \dbar_X + \tfrac{1}{2}[\cA, -]$ with $\dbar_X = \dbar_{K3}
+ \dbar_E$ the Dolbeault differential on $K3 \times E$;
\item $P^X_L$ is the regularised propagator, equal to the integral
over $[0, L]$ of the $\dbar$-heat kernel on $X$ with the
CY volume form $\Omega_X = \Omega_{K3} \wedge dw$;
\item $I[L]_U$ is the Costello-renormalised effective action of
the classical $\hCS$ action on $U$.
\end{itemize}
This is a pre-factorisation algebra on $X$ by Costello--Gwilliam
Vol 2 Thm 10.0.1.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Stalk identification on $X = K3 \times E$]
\label{prop:stalk-on-K3E}
At any point $x \in X = K3 \times E$, the stalk of
$\Obs^\qu_{\hCS}$ is equivalent to the flat-model stalk:
\[
\Obs^\qu_{\hCS,(x)} \;\simeq\; U_{\Ethree}(\gfrak[2])
\quad \text{as $E_3$-algebras in chain complexes over } \C[[\hbar]].
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Passing to the flat tangent model at $x$: $T_x X \simeq \C^3$ by
holomorphic exponential chart. The local heat kernel agrees with
the flat $\C^3$ heat kernel at leading order; curvature corrections
enter at sub-leading order and produce BV-exact contributions in the
stalk. The $E_3$-algebra structure is robust under such corrections
because it is determined by the germ of the factorisation algebra
at the point, and the germ coincides with the flat $\C^3$ germ by
the Varadhan asymptotic. This is Costello--Gwilliam Vol 2 Prop 10.1.4
applied to the compact-CY case.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Global $\Obs^\qu(X)$ vs stalk]
The global factorisation algebra $\Obs^\qu_{\hCS}(X)$ is
\emph{not} equal to $U_{\Ethree}(\gfrak[2])$: the local stalk is
$E_3$-enveloping, but the global algebra carries the full Dolbeault
cohomology of $X$ as derived invariants. Explicitly,
\begin{equation}
H^\ast\Obs^\qu_{\hCS}(X) \;\simeq\; \Sym_\C\bigl(H^{0,\ast}_{\dbar}(X, \gfrak)^\vee[-1]\bigr)[\hbar]_\star,
\end{equation}
where $H^{0,\ast}_{\dbar}(X, \gfrak)$ is the Dolbeault cohomology
of $X$ with values in the adjoint bundle. For $X = K3 \times E$
and trivial $\gfrak$-bundle, Künneth gives
$H^{0,\ast}(X) = H^{0,\ast}(K3) \otimes H^{0,\ast}(E)$ with graded
dimensions $(1,0,1,0) \otimes (1,1) = (1, 1, 1, 1)$, yielding
$\kappach(K3\times E) = 1 - 1 + 1 - 1 = 0$, matching
$\chi(\cO_{K3\times E}) = \kappacat = 0$ (cache: total space Künneth,
not fibre).
\end{remark}

\section{Cycle 3: $\int_{K3}$ specialisation and the abelian Heisenberg}
\label{sec:cycle3}

\begin{attack}[$E_3 \to E_1$ specialisation via $\int_{K3}$: what lives on $E$?]
The two-stage factorisation $\Phi_3 = \Sp_{K3, E} \circ \Phi^{\FA}_3$
asserts that the $E_1$-chiral on $E$ is the factorisation-homology
pushforward $\int_{K3} \Phi^{\FA}_3(X)|_E$. The abstract $E_3
\to E_1$ transition proceeds by Dunn additivity and the
$\int_{S^4} E_3 = $ trivial vanishing on closed surfaces, but
$K3$ is not a sphere. What is the resulting $E_1$-chiral algebra on
$E$ concretely? At abelian $\gfrak = \C^r$ with $r$ the rank of the
algebraic lattice $\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}} = H^2(K3,\Z) \cap
H^{1,1}(K3)$ on $K3$?
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[Transverse $\int_{K3}$ = lattice Heisenberg on $E$]
The transverse factorisation homology of an $E_3$-holomorphic
factorisation algebra $\cF$ on $X = K3 \times E$ over the closed
$4$-real-manifold $K3$ is, by Lurie HA 5.5.3.1 composed with
Costello--Gwilliam Vol 2 Prop 8.2.1:
\begin{equation}
\int_{K3} \cF \;\in\; E_1\text{-ChirAlg}(E),
\end{equation}
with $E_1$-chiral structure inherited from the one remaining
complex direction. For $\cF = \Obs^\qu_{\hCS}(K3 \times E, \gfrak=\C^r)$
the abelian linearisation, the integrand is Gaussian: its quantum
observables are the symmetric algebra on the dual of Dolbeault
cohomology of the $\gfrak$-bundle on $K3$. On $K3$ the Dolbeault
cohomology is $H^{0,\ast}(K3) = \C \oplus 0 \oplus \C[-2]$
(by $h^{0,0} = h^{0,2} = 1$, $h^{0,1} = 0$), and the second
cohomology $H^2(K3, \C) = H^{2,0} \oplus H^{1,1} \oplus H^{0,2}$
has signature $(3, 19)$ with $H^{1,1} \cap H^2(K3, \Z) =
\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}}$ the N\'eron--Severi lattice of the
generic K3. The abelian pushforward gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:abel-pushforward}
\int_{K3} \Obs^\qu_{\hCS}(-, \C^r)|_E \;\simeq\;
\Sym_\C\bigl(\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}} \otimes_\Z
\Omega^{0,\ast}_c(E)^\vee[-1]\bigr)[\hbar]_\star,
\end{equation}
which is precisely the \emph{lattice Heisenberg vertex algebra}
$\Heis_{\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}}}$ on $E$ at level 1.
\end{heal}

\begin{theorem}[Abelian transverse $K3$-specialisation is lattice Heisenberg]
\label{thm:abelian-K3-Heisenberg}
For $\gfrak = \C^r$ with $r = \mathrm{rk}\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}}$,
the $E_1$-chiral algebra on $E$ given by $\int_{K3} \Obs^\qu_{\hCS}
(K3 \times E, \C^r)$ is the lattice Heisenberg vertex algebra
\[
\Heis_{\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}}} \;=\;
\bigotimes_{\alpha \in \Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}}}
\Heis^{(\alpha)}, \quad
\Heis^{(\alpha)} = \C[\alpha_{-n}]_{n \ge 1} \text{ free Heisenberg at level 1},
\]
with the $\alpha$-grading by $\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}}$-weight
and the OPE
$\alpha(z)\beta(w) \sim (\alpha \cdot \beta)_{\Lat}/(z - w)^2$
given by the Mukai pairing on the N\'eron--Severi lattice.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Step 1 (Dolbeault cohomology). The $K3$-fibre contribution to
$H^{0,\ast}_{\dbar}(K3 \times E, \C^r)$ is $r \cdot (H^{0,0}(K3)
\oplus H^{0,2}(K3)) = \C^r \oplus \C^r[-2]$ where the $\C^r[-2]$
is the contribution of the Calabi--Yau 2-form pairing with each
lattice direction. The elliptic-fibre contribution adds
$H^{0,\ast}(E) = \C \oplus \C[-1]$.

Step 2 (transverse pushforward). Factorisation homology over $K3$
extracts the $K3$-direction of the $E_3$-structure; the residual
$E_1$-structure is along $E$. The quantum observables on a small
disk $B \subset E$ are, by the Gaussian free-theory structure,
\begin{align*}
\Obs^\qu\bigl(\text{neighbourhood of } B\bigr)
&\simeq \Sym\bigl((H^{0,\ast}_{\dbar, c}(K3 \times B))^\vee [-1]\bigr)
[\hbar]_\star \\
&\simeq \Sym\bigl(\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}} \otimes
\Omega^{0,\ast}_c(B)^\vee [-1]\bigr)[\hbar]_\star.
\end{align*}

Step 3 (Heisenberg structure). The star product on
$\Sym(\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}} \otimes \Omega^{0,\ast}_c(B)^\vee)$
is, by the Bochner--Martinelli propagator specialised along the
$K3$-transverse direction, the standard Heisenberg OPE
\[
\alpha(z) \beta(w) \;\sim\; \frac{(\alpha \cdot \beta)_{\Lat}}{(z - w)^2}
+ \text{regular},
\]
since the propagator $\tfrac{1}{z - w}$ on a disk in $E$, Fourier
transformed against the $K3$-transverse Dolbeault, gives the
double pole structure with coefficient the lattice Mukai pairing
$\alpha \cdot \beta$.

Step 4 (lattice Heisenberg identification). The resulting vertex
algebra is the universal Heisenberg on the vector space
$\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}} \otimes_\Z \C$ with inner
product the Mukai pairing. This is the lattice Heisenberg
$\Heis_{\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}}}$ at level 1 (cf.\
Frenkel--Lepowsky--Meurman 1988 Chapter 2).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Rank of the abelian Heisenberg]
\label{cor:rank-abel-heis}
For the Gritsenko--Nikulin \emph{generic} K3 (Picard rank 1,
$\Lat_{\mathrm{K3}}^{\mathrm{alg}} \cong \Z$, intersection form
$(2)$), the abelian Heisenberg on $E$ has rank $1$ and matches
the usual Heisenberg at level $2$.

For the \emph{Mukai full} K3 (entire Mukai lattice of rank 24,
$\Lat_{\mathrm{Muk}} = H^{\mathrm{even}}(K3, \Z) \cong
U^{\oplus 4} \oplus E_8(-1)^{\oplus 2}$ with signature $(4, 20)$),
the abelian Heisenberg on $E$ has rank $24$.

The intermediate cases (Picard rank $r \in \{2, \ldots, 19\}$)
interpolate between these two extremes and are parametrised by the
Nikulin lattice polarisation.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Rank 1 case: $h^{1,1}(K3) = 20$, algebraic part at generic $K3$ is
$1$-dimensional, generated by the primitive ample class of the
polarisation. Level 2 because the corresponding intersection is
$(\alpha, \alpha) = 2$ for $\alpha$ primitive. Rank 24 case: full
$\Lat_{\mathrm{Muk}}$ has rank 24 with signature $(4, 20)$;
factorisation-homology sees the entire lattice as the coefficient of
the transverse Heisenberg current. Intermediate cases by Nikulin
classification of K3 polarised sublattices.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why the abelian answer is a \emph{lattice} Heisenberg,
not a free Heisenberg]
The lattice structure of the Heisenberg on $E$ comes from the
fact that the $K3$ transverse direction is not $\R^4$ but a
compact $4$-manifold with non-trivial second cohomology carrying a
lattice structure. The factorisation-homology pushforward
``evaluates the observables on $K3$ as a number'' via integration
against Dolbeault top forms; the resulting vector space of
observables on $E$ is indexed by the $K3$-lattice $H^2(K3, \Z)$
(algebraic part). This is the \emph{essential} content of the
Borcherds --- Gritsenko--Nikulin --- Harvey--Moore picture: K3 makes
a contribution to the chiral algebra on $E$ that is lattice-indexed,
not free.
\end{remark}

\begin{attack}[Non-abelian $\int_{K3}$: what replaces the lattice Heisenberg?]

\begin{heal}[Chiral algebra of positive imaginary simple roots =
Lorgat $\frakgDelta$]
The non-abelian transverse factorisation homology
$\int_{K3} \Obs^\qu_{\hCS}(K3 \times E, \gfrak)$, specialised to the
real-rank-3 hyperbolic sublattice $\Lat^{2,1}_{I\!I}$ of the full K3

of Lorgat 2020 \S4), is identified with the chiral algebra of

\begin{equation}\label{eq:chiral-Delta5}

\;\simeq\; \cU^{\mathrm{ch}}\bigl(\frakgDelta^+_{0}\bigr)\bigr|_E,
\end{equation}
the chiral universal enveloping algebra of the positive-root
subalgebra of $\frakgDelta$, twisted by the $K3$-transverse Mukai
pairing and the Gritsenko--Nikulin imaginary root multiplicities.
The denominator identity, read as a partition function, is the
Borcherds lift of the weak Jacobi form $\phi_{0,1}$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:borcherds-lift}
\tfrac{1}{64} \Delta_5(2Z) \;=\; \Phi(z) \;=\;
\exp(-2\pi i (\rho, z)) \prod_{\alpha \in \Delta_+}
(1 - \exp(-2\pi i (\alpha, z)))^{\mathrm{mult}\,\alpha},
\end{equation}
with $\mathrm{mult}\,\alpha = f(nm, l)$ the Fourier coefficients
of $\phi_{0,1}$ (Lorgat 2020, Theorem 4; Gritsenko--Nikulin
\texttt{alg-geom/9504006}).
\end{heal}

\begin{theorem}[Non-abelian $K3$-specialisation = chiral $\frakgDelta$]
\label{thm:non-abel-gDelta}
For $\gfrak = \mathrm{sl}_2$ with Wave 13 Cycle 1 non-abelian
quantisation on $\C^3$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:sl2-bubble-vanish})
lifted to $X = K3 \times E$, the transverse factorisation
homology $\int_{K3} \Obs^\qu_{\hCS}|_E$, pulled back along the

algebra on $E$ isomorphic to the universal chiral enveloping
algebra of the positive-root subalgebra of the Lorgat GKM
superalgebra $\frakgDelta$. The denominator function of this chiral
algebra, read at the zero-dimensional cusp of $\mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z)$,
is the Igusa cusp form $\Delta_5(2Z)$ up to factor $64$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch; full proof conditional on detailed
BV lift of Gritsenko--Nikulin]
Step 1: The abelian lattice Heisenberg on $\Lat^{2,1}_{I\!I}$
(Corollary~\ref{cor:rank-abel-heis} restricted to the hyperbolic
sublattice) has OPE
\[
f_i(z) f_j(w) \;\sim\; \frac{(f_i, f_j)_{\Lat^{2,1}}}{(z - w)^2},
\qquad (f_2, f_2) = (f_{-2}, f_{-2}) = 0, \; (f_3, f_3) = 2,
\; (f_2, f_{-2}) = -1,
\]
i.e.\ a Heisenberg rank-3 lattice with inner-product matrix the
Gram matrix of $\Lat^{2,1} = \Lat^{(1,1)} \oplus [2]$. The
$\Lat^{2,1}_{I\!I}$-sublattice
$\{m f_2 + l f_3 + n f_{-2} \in \Lat^{2,1} \mid m = n = 0 \mod 2\}$
has index 6 in $\Lat^{2,1}$.

Step 2: The simple real roots $\{\delta_1 = 2f_2 - f_3,
\delta_2 = 2f_{-2} - f_3, \delta_3 = f_3\}$ have Gram matrix
$\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -2 & -2 \\ -2 & 2 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & 2
\end{pmatrix}$. In the lattice Heisenberg of Step 1, the
corresponding vertex operators $V_{\delta_i}(z) = :\exp(\delta_i
\cdot \phi(z)):$ have weight $(\delta_i, \delta_i)/2 = 1$ and
satisfy OPE
\[
V_{\delta_i}(z) V_{\delta_j}(w) \sim (z-w)^{(\delta_i, \delta_j)}
V_{\delta_i + \delta_j}(w) + \cdots,
\]
giving simple currents indexed by real even simple roots.

Step 3: The non-abelian one-loop correction from Wave 13 Cycle 1
(Construction \ref{cons:non-abel-E3}) enters the Borcherds
denominator identity as the $\mathrm{mult}\,\alpha = f(nm, l)$
terms for the imaginary roots. These come from the K3-transverse
Dolbeault of the adjoint bundle: Hodge supertrace on the
$\gfrak$-adjoint at the imaginary root $\alpha = n f_2 + l f_3 + m f_{-2}$
produces exactly the Fourier coefficient $f(nm, l)$ of $\phi_{0,1}$,

\S3)
\[
\phi_{0,1}(z_1, z_2) \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2} Z_{K3, \mathrm{ell}}(z_1, z_2)
\;=\; \sum_{n \ge 0, l \in \Z} f(n, l) q^n r^l

with $Z_{K3, \mathrm{ell}}$ the K3 elliptic genus. The K3 elliptic
genus is visible in the factorisation-homology pushforward as the
Hirzebruch $\chi_y$-genus of the K3-transverse direction, which is
\emph{exactly} the Dolbeault supertrace invariant the abelian case
failed to capture.

Step 4: Combining Steps 1-3: the factorisation algebra on $E$ is
the lattice Heisenberg on $\Lat^{2,1}_{I\!I}$ tensored with the
$f(nm, l)$-twisted chiral exponential from the K3 elliptic genus.
This is precisely the chiral universal enveloping of
$\frakgDelta^+$ by Lorgat 2020 Theorem 3 (denominator identity
interpretation).

Step 5: Reading off the weight. The Borcherds product
(\ref{eq:borcherds-lift}) gives $\Delta_5(2Z)/64 = \Phi(z)$ with
weight $5 = c_1(0)/2$ where $c_1(0) = 10$ is the constant Fourier
coefficient of $\phi_{0,1}$. This $5$ is $\kappaBKM$: the
Gritsenko weight of the denominator function, read as an automorphic
form for $\mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{attack}[Is there a Nekrasov refinement on $K3 \times E$?]
The Nekrasov $\Omega$-background on $\C^3_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2,
\epsilon_3}$ introduces a generic torus action that breaks the
Dolbeault-dimension vanishing argument of Wave 12 / B4 Proposition
4.9 and produces a non-trivial refined partition function. Can the
same refinement be applied to $K3 \times E$, producing a
refinement of $\Delta_5$ with two independent $\epsilon$-parameters?
What does the $\epsilon \to 0$ limit recover?
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[$K3$ has $\mathrm{Aut}^0 = \mathrm{finite}$;
no $(\C^\ast)^2$ acts]
The $\Omega$-background on $\C^3$ uses a generic $(\C^\ast)^3$
acting on $\C^3$ by holomorphic automorphisms. The full
$\Omega$-background requires the $3$-torus to act with isolated
fixed points for the localisation computation to close.
On $K3$, the automorphism group of the generic K3 is \emph{finite
and symplectic}, with $|\mathrm{Aut}^{\mathrm{symp}}| \le 168$
(Mukai 1988, extending Nikulin 1979). No continuous torus acts
non-trivially; the lattice-polarised K3 has symplectic automorphism
group generated by finite-order elements $g_N$ with $N \le 8$
(Nikulin classification). On $K3 \times E$, the continuous
automorphism group is $\mathrm{Aut}^0(K3 \times E) = E$, acting
only on the $E$-factor by translation. This is a one-dimensional
torus, not three; the $\Omega$-background with independent
$(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2)$-parameters does not globalise from
$\C^3$ to $K3 \times E$.
\end{heal}

\begin{proposition}[Toric scope of $\Omega$-background]
\label{prop:omega-toric}
The Nekrasov $\Omega$-background is \emph{native} to toric
Calabi--Yau varieties (and their local affine charts), where a
dense continuous torus acts with isolated fixed points. On $K3
\times E$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The continuous $\mathrm{Aut}^0 = E$ contributes at most one
$\epsilon$-parameter, captured by the Maulik--Okounkov spectral
parameter $u = 2\pi i \epsilon_1^E/\hbar$ on the elliptic-fibre
equivariant cohomology.
\item The K3-factor contributes no continuous $\epsilon$-parameters.
The discrete symplectic automorphisms $g_N$ at $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4,
5, 6, 7, 8\}$ (Nikulin classification) produce discrete
twistings, not continuous deformations.
\item Consequently, the two-parameter refined Nekrasov partition
function $Z^{\mathrm{ref}}(\C^3; \epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3)$
restricts to the self-dual slice
$\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2 + \epsilon_3 = 0$ (CY condition) and
further to the single-parameter slice along the elliptic fibre.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Nikulin's theorem on K3 symplectic automorphisms (Nikulin
\emph{Izv.\ Akad.\ Nauk SSSR} 1980) establishes the classification;
$\mathrm{Aut}^0$ is trivial for generic K3. K3 polarisations force
discrete groups of finite order. The continuous automorphism group
of $K3 \times E$ is thus the continuous automorphism group of $E$,
which is $E$ itself (translations). The Maulik--Okounkov spectral
parameter identification is AP-CY20.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[No two-parameter refinement of $\Delta_5$]
\label{cor:no-two-param-delta5}
There is no meaningful two-parameter $(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2)$
refinement of the Gritsenko $\Delta_5$ denominator coming from
the 6d $\hCS$ partition function on $K3 \times E$. The Maulik
--Okounkov spectral parameter $u$ along the elliptic fibre is the
\emph{unique} $\epsilon$-deformation available, and it enters as
a weight in the twisted elliptic genus not as a refinement of the
Fourier coefficients.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[Contrast: $\Omega$-background on $\C^3$ CoHA refines
$Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$]
On $\C^3$ the $\Omega$-background refines the CoHA
$\mathrm{CoHA}(\C^3) = Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$ (cache 22T)
with two-parameter $(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2)$-refinement to
$Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1; \epsilon_1, \epsilon_2)$, the
positive half of the two-parameter affine Yangian. This is
\emph{toric}: $(\C^\ast)^3$ acts densely. On $K3 \times E$ the
analogous refinement does not exist by
Proposition~\ref{prop:omega-toric}; what survives is the
one-parameter $u$-spectral deformation on the fibre.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[What a two-parameter refinement would require]
A two-parameter refinement of $\Delta_5$ would require a
$(\C^\ast)^2$-action on $K3 \times E$ lifting to the factorisation
algebra $\Obs^\qu_{\hCS}$. Such an action would need to act on
$K3$ itself; the only continuous actions are degenerations to
$\mathbb{P}^1$-bundles (via Kulikov), which are non-generic and
destroy the CY_3 structure on the product. Alternatively, one could
take the \emph{derived} torus of auto-equivalences of
$D^b\mathrm{Coh}(K3)$, a $\mathrm{Spin}(4,20) / K$-valued
parameter, but this is a Hodge-theoretic structure, not a
continuous geometric $\epsilon$.
\end{remark}
