\title{Route-Coherence Obstruction for the Six Constructions of $G(K3\times E)$}
\author{H06}
\date{2026-04-27}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
For $X=K3\times E$, the six-route problem is not a sixfold Stage-$2$
enumeration of one Stage-$1$ output.  Only route R1 is the framed
CY-to-chiral route
$\Phi_3^{(\Sigma_2,C)}=\SpCh_{\Sigma_2,C}\circ\PhiFA_3$.  Routes
R2--R6 are independent constructions: Borcherds lift, Mukai-lattice
VOA, Kummer/orbifold model, sigma-model half-twist, and
BLLPR/holomorphic-twist construction.  This note records the stable
core and the exact obstruction to upgrading scalar agreements to
completed $\Eone$-chiral route coherence.  The missing data are the
completed bridge maps $\alpha_{ij}$, the cycle homotopy
$H_{\mathrm{cyc}}$, the completed Hall--Borcherds double, and the
oriented hCS-to-Hall primitive witness package on the DWR/Ran nerve.
\end{abstract}

\section{Stable Core}
\label{sec:stable-core}

Let $S$ be a smooth projective K3 surface, let $E$ be an elliptic curve,
and set $X=S\times E$.  The two-stage CY-to-chiral construction, on its
verified $d=3$ locus and after choosing a witnessed admissible
specialisation datum, is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:two-stage}
 \Phi_3^{(\Sigma_2,C)}(\Perf X)
 =
 \SpCh_{\Sigma_2,C}\bigl(\PhiFA_3(\Perf X)\bigr).
\end{equation}
Here $\PhiFA_3(\Perf X)$ is the torsor-pointed $\Ethree$-holomorphic
factorisation algebra and $\SpCh_{\Sigma_2,C}$ is the holomorphic
Stage-$2$ specialisation along a complex surface $\Sigma_2\subset X$ and
a smooth complex curve $C\subset \Sigma_2$.  The datum includes the
tubular normal choice, kernel, Beck--Chevalley cell, Fubini cell, and
completion hypotheses of the two-stage chapter.  A real boundary circle
or CR boundary is not such a datum.

\begin{definition}[The six constructions]
\label{def:six-constructions}
The CY-C route comparison on $X=S\times E$ has six vertices:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{R\arabic*.},leftmargin=2.3em]
\item the framed CY-to-chiral assignment
$A_X^{R_1}:=\Phi_3^{(\Sigma_2,C)}(\Perf X)$ for a fixed witnessed
datum, with the K3-fibre/Elliptic-base datum as the principal worked
locus;
\item the Borcherds multiplicative lift of the K3 elliptic genus
$2\phi_{0,1}$, producing $\Phi_{10}$ and the square-root denominator
$\Delt$ for $\Gdelta$;
\item the Mukai-lattice VOA $V_{\Lambda_{\mathrm{Muk}}(X)}$;
\item the Kummer/orbifold construction with invariant and twisted
sectors;
\item the holomorphic half-twist of the sigma model on $X$;
\item the BLLPR Schur/holomorphic-twist construction from the
class-$\mathcal S$ parent.
\end{enumerate}
Only R1 is an application of \eqref{eq:two-stage}.  R2--R6 are
independent constructions whose comparison with R1 is part of CY-C.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[No sixfold functorial tautology]
\label{rem:no-sixfold-tautology}
The object $\PhiFA_3(\Perf X)$ is single.  The functor
$\Phi_3^{(\Sigma_2,C)}$ becomes an object-level assignment only after the
witnessed specialisation datum is fixed.  Therefore six-route
convergence cannot be derived from functoriality of $\Phi_3$: it is the
separate assertion that five independent constructions admit completed
comparison maps to the R1 output in one route category.
\end{remark}

\section{The Conditional Route Category}
\label{sec:conditional-route-category}

\begin{definition}[Completed route-coherence datum]
\label{def:route-coherence-datum}
A completed route-coherence datum for $X=S\times E$ consists of:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(C\arabic*)},leftmargin=2.5em]
\item a completed presentable monoidal category
$\mathcal C_X^{E_1,\wedge}$ of $\Eone$-chiral algebras, with continuous
relative tensor products and finite colimits;
\item six objects $A_X^{R_i}\in\mathcal C_X^{E_1,\wedge}$ realising the
six constructions of Definition~\ref{def:six-constructions};
\item completed $\Eone$-chiral maps
\[
 \alpha_{12},\alpha_{23},\alpha_{34},\alpha_{45},\alpha_{56},\alpha_{61}
\]
between the route complexes;
\item preservation of OPEs, brackets where present, coproducts where
present, Mukai gradings, route generator ranks $\rhoR^{R_i}$, and the
declared subscripted $\kappa_\bullet$ labels;
\item a cycle homotopy
\[
 H_{\mathrm{cyc}}\colon
 \alpha_{61}\alpha_{56}\alpha_{45}\alpha_{34}\alpha_{23}\alpha_{12}
 \simeq \id_{A_X^{R_1}}
\]
after quotienting only by the declared inner Mukai-grading
automorphisms.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional route colimit]
\label{thm:conditional-route-colimit}
If a completed route-coherence datum in the sense of
Definition~\ref{def:route-coherence-datum} is supplied, then the
positive route diagram has a colimit
\[
 Y^+(X)=\operatorname*{colim}_{R_i} A_X^{R_i}
\]
in $\mathcal C_X^{E_1,\wedge}$.  If, in addition, the completed
Hall--Drinfeld double package of Section~\ref{sec:double-obstruction} is
supplied, the resulting double is the conditional CY-C candidate
$G(K3\times E)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The assumptions give a homotopy-coherent finite diagram in the completed
$\Eone$-chiral category.  Finite colimit closure gives $Y^+(X)$.  The
cycle homotopy is the condition that the spanning-tree presentation of
the diagram is independent of the chosen path around the six-cycle.  The
double is not part of the positive colimit; it is formed only after the
negative half, pairing, completion, radical quotient, and centre
continuity of Section~\ref{sec:double-obstruction} are present.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[What is not supplied here]
\label{rem:not-supplied}
This note supplies no maps $\alpha_{ij}$ and no $H_{\mathrm{cyc}}$.  It
records the typed obstruction.  A scalar character identity, a
denominator identity, a protected index, or a route-rank agreement is
not a completed $\Eone$-chiral map.
\end{remark}

\section{Scalar Witnesses Do Not Build Chiral Maps}
\label{sec:scalar-witnesses}

\begin{proposition}[$\Delt$ is a scalar witness]
\label{prop:delta5-scalar-witness}
The identity
\[
 \kBKM(\Delt)=c_1(0)/2=10/2=5
\]
and the associated Borcherds denominator/character identities are scalar
or graded-character witnesses.  They do not construct any map
$\alpha_{ij}$ preserving OPEs, brackets, coproducts, Hopf pairings, or
the completed topology.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The Borcherds lift identifies a Jacobi-form input and a denominator
character.  A completed route map is a chain map between the route
complexes, compatible with the products and brackets defining the
$\Eone$-chiral structures and, where a double is used, with the Hopf
pairing and coproduct.  Characters forget these operations.  Thus
$\Delt$ pins the weight and root-multiplicity shadow; it does not supply
the chiral-algebra comparison.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[First bridge]
\label{rem:first-bridge}
For $\alpha_{12}$, the Harvey--Moore/Borcherds product and the HKR
shadow give the positive-root and character input.  The missing lift is
the positive-half Hall--Borcherds bracket comparison on route complexes,
with OPE, coproduct, completed Hopf pairing, and framed Mukai grading
preserved.
\end{remark}

\section{The Hall--Borcherds Double Obstruction}
\label{sec:double-obstruction}

\begin{definition}[Completed Hall--Borcherds double package]
\label{def:double-package}
A completed Hall--Borcherds double package consists of:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(D\arabic*)},leftmargin=2.5em]
\item a completed positive half $Y^+(X)$ with coproduct;
\item a completed negative half $Y^-(X)$;
\item a continuous Hopf pairing
$Y^+(X)\widehatotimes Y^-(X)\to\mathbf 1$;
\item a radical quotient making that pairing non-degenerate after
completion;
\item a bracket comparison with $\Gdelta$ preserving the Borcherds--Serre
relations and the Mukai grading;
\item a centre-continuity datum
\[
 Z\bigl(\Rep^{E_1}(Y^+(X))\bigr)
 \simeq
 \Rep^{E_2}\bigl(D_\hbar(Y^+(X))\bigr).
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Positive half is not the double]
\label{rem:positive-half-not-double}
A CoHA positive half or a Borcherds denominator is not a completed
Hall--Drinfeld double.  The double exists only after the negative half,
non-degenerate Hopf pairing, completion/radical quotient, bracket
comparison, and centre-continuity data are supplied.  Until then the
symbol $G(K3\times E)$ denotes a conditional target, not a constructed
object.
\end{remark}

\section{Oriented hCS-to-Hall Primitive Package}
\label{sec:hcs-hall-package}

\begin{definition}[Primitive witness package]
\label{def:primitive-witness-package}
On a DWR-good cover $\mathfrak U$, an oriented hCS-to-Hall comparison is
controlled by a finite typed package
\[
 \mathfrak P_{\Theta}^{\mathrm{or}}
 =
 \bigl(
 \theta^{(0)},\,
 \eta_{\mathrm{MC}},\,
 \lambda_{\mathrm{or}},\,
 \eta_{\mathrm{gr}},\,
 H_{\mathrm{TS}},\,
 H_{\mathrm{fact}},\,
 Q
 \bigr).
\]
The entries have the following meanings:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.2em]
\item $\theta^{(0)}$ is the chartwise family of degree-zero continuous
chain maps
$\Obs^q_{\hCS}(U_i,\mathfrak g)\to
\CoHA_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{or}}(U_i)$;
\item $\eta_{\mathrm{MC}}$ is the positive Cech/Ran filtration correction
making $\theta=\theta^{(0)}+\eta_{\mathrm{MC}}$ solve the
Maurer--Cartan equation;
\item $\lambda_{\mathrm{or}}$ transports Joyce--Kontsevich--Soibelman
orientation square roots on overlaps;
\item $\eta_{\mathrm{gr}}$ transports cohomological shifts and Tate
twists;
\item $H_{\mathrm{TS}}$ trivialises the Thom--Sebastiani associativity
defect for triple extension correspondences;
\item $H_{\mathrm{fact}}$ trivialises the disjoint-Ran factorisation
product defect;
\item $Q$ certifies vertexwise quasi-isomorphism and gives continuous
homotopy inverses compatible with all completions and orientation data.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Subpackages do not globalise]
\label{rem:subpackages}
A one-chart map, a finite torus-fixed $\C^3$ calculation, a
Schiffmann--Vasserot positive-half identification, or an orientation
trivialisation is a proper subpackage.  The compact oriented comparison
exists only when all seven entries of
Definition~\ref{def:primitive-witness-package} are present on the
DWR/Ran nerve and satisfy descent.
\end{remark}

\section{hCS Status Separation}
\label{sec:hcs-status-separation}

The hCS assertions in the route comparison split into distinct status
surfaces:
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{tabular}{p{0.28\linewidth}|p{0.62\linewidth}}
Surface & Required content \\\hline
Formal QME & A renormalised BV differential
$Q_{\hCS}+\{I[L],-\}_{\mathrm{BV}}+\hbar\Delta_L$ with the quantum
master equation solved order by order under the anomaly-cancellation
hypotheses. \\
All-orders formal tower & Compatible higher counterterms and
coderivations in the completed BV/bar category.  This is formal in
$\hbar$ and does not imply analytic convergence. \\
Borel summability & A Gevrey/Borel analytic estimate for the perturbative
series.  This is analytic data and does not imply a Stage-$2$ chiral
identity. \\
Stage-$2$ equality & An $\Eone$-chiral comparison after the K3-fibre
pushforward, preserving OPEs and route gradings.  It requires the
oriented hCS-to-Hall package and the Borcherds/Hall bridge. \\
Nonperturbative completion & The DT/Borcherds product completion,
including the singular-theta lift and automorphic denominator.  It lies
outside the perturbative $\hbar$-series. \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{remark}[No status collapse]
\label{rem:no-status-collapse}
None of these surfaces implies another without the named comparison
data.  In particular, all-orders formal QME, Borel summability,
Stage-$2$ equality with $U_{\mathrm{ch}}(\Gdelta)$, and the
nonperturbative $\Delt^{-2}$ completion are separate obligations.
\end{remark}

\section{The CR-Boundary Exclusion}
\label{sec:cr-boundary-exclusion}

\begin{proposition}[Real boundary data are not Stage-$2$ data]
\label{prop:real-boundary-not-stage2}
Let $D_{\mathrm{hol}}^3$ be a small holomorphic polydisc in $X$ and let
$\partial D_{\mathrm{hol}}^3\cap H$ be a real CR boundary.  The pair
\[
 \bigl(\partial D_{\mathrm{hol}}^3\cap H,\;S^1\bigr)
\]
is not an admissible holomorphic Stage-$2$ specialisation datum for
\eqref{eq:two-stage}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The Stage-$2$ datum used by $\SpCh_{\Sigma_2,C}$ requires a holomorphic
surface $\Sigma_2\subset X$ and a smooth complex curve $C\subset
\Sigma_2$, together with the algebraic/holomorphic kernel and
Beck--Chevalley/Fubini witnesses.  A CR three-manifold and a real
circle do not supply that input.  They may define a boundary-observable
or probe-CFT problem in a different category, but they are not a
holomorphic specialisation of $\PhiFA_3(\Perf X)$ and are not a CY-C
route vertex.
\end{proof}

\section{Subscripted \texorpdfstring{$\kappa_\bullet$}{kappa} Discipline}
\label{sec:kappa-discipline}

\begin{proposition}[The four admissible scalar readouts]
\label{prop:four-kappa-readouts}
For $X=K3\times E$, the scalar readouts used in this note are:
\[
 \kcat(X)=\chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\chi(\mathcal O_E)=2\cdot 0=0,
\]
\[
 \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(X)=3,\qquad
 \kBKM(\Delt)=c_1(0)/2=10/2=5,\qquad
 \kfib(X)=\operatorname{rk}\widetilde H(K3,\Z)=24.
\]
The scalar readouts have different sources, so no route-dependent
chiral-characteristic table is formed.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The categorical value is the Kunneth-multiplicative Euler characteristic
of the total space.  The Heisenberg value is the rank of the
Mukai--Heisenberg specialisation, not the compact total-space invariant.
The BKM value is the Borcherds weight read from the constant coefficient
of the Jacobi input.  The fibre value is the Mukai-lattice rank of the
K3 fibre.  These are different constructions, hence distinct
subscripted invariants.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Route ranks are not \texorpdfstring{$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$}{kappa-ch}]
\label{rem:rho-not-kappa}
The route-distinguishing numbers $\rhoR^{R_i}$ are generator ranks or
sector ranks.  For example, the Mukai-lattice route has rank $24$, the
Kummer/orbifold route has a twisted-sector rank, and the Heisenberg
route has rank $3$.  These numbers are not per-route values of
$\kch$.  The note therefore contains no route-by-route chiral
characteristic table.
\end{remark}

\section{Residual Proof Obligations}
\label{sec:residual-obligations}

The six-route comparison is repaired to the following exact obligation
list.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{O\arabic*.},leftmargin=2.5em]
\item Construct the completed $\Eone$-chiral route category
$\mathcal C_X^{E_1,\wedge}$ and place all six route outputs inside it.
\item Construct the six completed chain maps
$\alpha_{12},\alpha_{23},\alpha_{34},\alpha_{45},\alpha_{56},\alpha_{61}$.
\item Prove that each $\alpha_{ij}$ preserves OPEs, brackets,
coproducts where present, Mukai gradings, completions, and declared
$\kappa_\bullet$ labels.
\item Construct the cycle homotopy $H_{\mathrm{cyc}}$ in the completed
route category.
\item Build the Hall--Borcherds double package:
positive half, negative half, non-degenerate Hopf pairing after radical
quotient, completion, bracket comparison with $\Gdelta$, and centre
continuity.
\item Supply the oriented hCS-to-Hall primitive witness package
$(\theta^{(0)},\eta_{\mathrm{MC}},\lambda_{\mathrm{or}},
\eta_{\mathrm{gr}},H_{\mathrm{TS}},H_{\mathrm{fact}},Q)$ on the DWR/Ran
nerve.
\item Keep formal QME, all-orders formal tower, Borel summability,
Stage-$2$ equality, and nonperturbative completion as separate status
surfaces until each has its own proof.
\end{enumerate}
