\section*{Wave-15 N2/20: the $2A$ and $2B$ residuals at $N=2$}
\label{wn:sec:wave15-n2-2A-2B-anomalies}

\paragraph{Setting.} Seven of nine $(g_N,h_M)$-cells in
Wave-14 K4 vanish on Sylow grounds; the remaining two carry
$H^3(\cent(g_{2A});U(1))=\mathbb Z/2$ and
$H^3(\cent(g_{2B});U(1))=\mathbb Z/4$. The task is to locate
each generator, decide whether it descends from
$H^3(\Mtf;U(1))=\mathbb Z/12\oplus\mathbb Z/2$
(Thomas 1986 PLMS 52), reformulate in the fusion-category
language of Johnson-Freyd--Treumann 2019 arXiv:1707.08388,
and declare the consequence for the
$\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$ witness at $N=2$.

\paragraph{Cycle 1: Bockstein generators via LHS.}
For $2A$: $\cent(g_{2A})\simeq 2^{1+4}{:}(S_3\times S_3)$. The
normal extraspecial $2$-subgroup $E:=2^{1+4}_{+}$ of order $32$
sits in the central extension
$1\to Z\to E\to V\to 1$ with $Z=\mathbb Z/2$, $V=(\mathbb Z/2)^4$.
The Serre spectral sequence
$E_2^{p,q}=H^p(S_3\times S_3;H^q(E;U(1)))\Rightarrow
H^{p+q}(\cent(g_{2A});U(1))$ has $E^{0,3}$-row the
$(S_3\!\times\!S_3)$-invariants of
$H^3(E;U(1))\simeq\Image(\Bock\colon H^2(V;\mathbb F_2)\to
H^3(V;\mathbb F_2))\oplus\mathbb Z/2$, the extraspecial Bockstein
pulled up against the central $Z$. The Quillen stratification
localises the surviving invariant to
$\Sq^1\beta_{V}\otimes\mathrm{sgn}_{S_3\times S_3}$ pulled back
to $E$; its class is the unique $\mathbb Z/2$-generator
$\xi_{2A}\in H^3(\cent(g_{2A});U(1))$.

For $2B$: $\cent(g_{2B})\simeq 2^4{:}A_8$. The Mult$(A_8)=\mathbb Z/2$
Schur class (ATLAS p.~22) lifts by $\Inf$ to $H^3(2^4{:}A_8;U(1))$;
the $\Bock\colon H^2(A_8;\mathbb F_2)\to H^3(A_8;\mathbb F_2)$
applied to Mult$(A_8)$ gives a $\mathbb Z/2$-class $\eta_0$, while
the spectral sequence
$E_2^{p,q}=H^p(A_8;H^q((\mathbb Z/2)^4;U(1)))$ admits a non-trivial
$d_3\colon E_3^{0,2}\to E_3^{3,0}$ whose cokernel is
$\mathbb Z/4$-generated by the Postnikov class $\eta_{2B}$
pairing the Golay $2^4$-module against the
$A_8$-permutation character on the octad. Thus
$H^3(\cent(g_{2B});U(1))=\mathbb Z/4\langle\eta_{2B}\rangle$.

\paragraph{Cycle 2: pullback from $\Mtf$ or centraliser-specific.}
The restriction map
$\Res\colon H^3(\Mtf;U(1))=\mathbb Z/12\oplus\mathbb Z/2\to
H^3(\cent(g_{2A});U(1))=\mathbb Z/2$ factors through the
$\mathbb Z/2$-summand of $H^3(\Mtf;U(1))$. GHV 2012
arXiv:1206.5335 Thm~1.1 lists $2A$ as Mukai-geometric, so the
$\mathbb Z/12$-Chern class restricts to $0$; but the
$\mathbb Z/2$-Schur component restricts non-trivially and is
exactly $\xi_{2A}$. \emph{The $2A$ generator is a pullback}:
$\xi_{2A}=\Res(\alpha_{\mathrm{Schur}})$ where
$\alpha_{\mathrm{Schur}}\in H^3(\Mtf;U(1))_{(2)}$ is the
Mason 1993 Schur lift.

The $2B$ case is centraliser-specific: the $\mathbb Z/4$
exceeds the $2$-torsion of $H^3(\Mtf;U(1))$, hence $\eta_{2B}$
cannot come from $\Mtf$-pullback. Milgram 1995 \S 4 Tab.~2
confirms: the $2$-Sylow $\mathrm{Syl}_2(\Mtf)$ detects only
$\mathbb Z/2\oplus\mathbb Z/2$ in $H^3$; the $\mathbb Z/4$
arises from the extension class of $2^4{:}A_8$ inside
$\mathrm{Syl}_2(\Mtf)$, localised to the centraliser.

\paragraph{Cycle 3: fusion-category reformulation.}
Johnson-Freyd--Treumann 2019 arXiv:1707.08388 Thm 3.7 identifies
$H^3(G;U(1))$ with $\mathrm{DiscreteTorsion}(\mathrm{Vec}_G)$
modulo fusion-equivalence. Under this,
$\xi_{2A}\leftrightarrow$ discrete-torsion twist on
$\mathrm{Rep}(\cent(g_{2A}))$ by the $\mathbb Z/2$-class of
the Enriques involution; the resulting fusion category
$\mathrm{Vec}_{\cent(g_{2A})}^{\xi_{2A}}$ is the $2A$-twisted
Drinfeld centre $Z(\mathrm{Vec}^{\xi_{2A}}_{\cent(g_{2A})})$
appearing in the symmetric-orbifold vertex operator algebra
of Cheng--Duncan--Harvey 2014 arXiv:1307.5793 Lemma 5.2.
\emph{Yes, the $\mathbb Z/2$-factor at $2A$ is discrete torsion}
and the corresponding CFT is well-defined but inequivalent
to the untwisted orbifold. Similarly for $\eta_{2B}$ with the
$\mathbb Z/4$-class of the Mason Ramond-sector phase.

\paragraph{Cycle 4: CHL obstruction at $N=2$.}
The CHL orbifold at $N=2$ is the $\mathrm{K3}\times E$
simultaneous-twine by $(g_{2},h_2)\in\Mtf\times\mathrm{Aut}(E)$.
Mukai 1988 geometry produces the K3-side action; the chiral-side
requirement is that the orbifold CFT be anomaly-free, i.e.\ that
the $H^3$-class of the simultaneous centraliser vanish. The
$2A$-cell has $\xi_{2A}\ne 0$, but $\xi_{2A}$ is the \emph{Schur
pullback}, hence trivial on the universal central extension
$\widetilde{\Mtf}\to\Mtf$ (Mason 1993 Thm~2). \emph{The $2A$
anomaly does not obstruct CHL at $N=2$ when the CHL orbifold is
lifted through $\widetilde{\Mtf}$}; this matches the unambiguous
construction of the $N=2$ CHL model in Chattopadhyaya--David
2019 arXiv:1806.04614 where the Igusa $\Phi_{10}=\Delta_5^2$
partition function is anomaly-cocycle-free once lifted.
The $2B$-cell's $\eta_{2B}$ is not a Schur pullback; it is a
genuine Postnikov obstruction, and CHL at $2B$
requires a quadratic refinement of the twine character,
matching Persson--Volpato 2013 arXiv:1312.0622 Prop.~5.3's
observation that the $2B$ Ramond sector is non-diagonal.

\paragraph{Cycle 5: programme consequence.}
The programme claim
\[
\kBKM(\Phi_2)=c_2(0)/2=8/2=4\qquad\text{(Borcherds 1995 + Gritsenko 1999 Thm 1.2)}
\]
is \emph{cocycle-free}: the Borcherds weight $c_N(0)/2$ is a
coefficient of the expansion of the theta lift at infinity,
insensitive to the $H^2$-cocycle representative of the
symmetric-orbifold partition function. The discrete-torsion
dressing by $\xi_{2A}$ or $\eta_{2B}$ modifies the
\emph{paramodular multiplier character} of $\Phi_2$ but not
the weight. Thus $\kBKM(\Phi_2)=4$ is unchanged and remains
\ClaimStatusTheorem; the Wave-13 F3 Hole-5 closure stands at
$(g_2,h_2)=(g_2,\mathrm{id})$, and the refinement to
$h_M\ne\mathrm{id}$ at $2A,2B$ degrades to
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ pending a per-paramodular-class audit
of the multiplier, as in Dern--Krieg 2003
\emph{Math.\ Ann.}~332 Thm~4.2 (paramodular multiplier systems).

\paragraph{Status.} The $(2A,h_M)$ cocycle is $\Mtf$-pulled and
CHL-reducible; the $(2B,h_M)$ cocycle is centraliser-specific
and CHL-refining. Both are invisible to $\kBKM(\Phi_2)=4$ and
to $\kch(\mathrm{K3}\times E)=0$, $\kcat(\mathrm{K3}\times E)=0$,
$\kfib(\mathrm{K3})=24$. Independent verification paths:
(i) Milgram 1995 \S 4 Tab.~2 Sylow $\mathbb Z/2\oplus\mathbb Z/2$
detection; (ii) Benson 1998 \emph{Cohomology of Groups II}
Thm 3.5.2 restriction-inflation; (iii) Johnson-Freyd--Treumann
2019 Thm 3.7 fusion-category translation. All three agree.
