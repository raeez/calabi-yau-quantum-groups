\section*{Wave-19 P2 slides $\leftrightarrow$ Vol III cross-form coherence map}

The slides inscribe one GBKM superalgebra $\gDelta$ with denominator
$\Delta_5$, constructed as a Borcherds lift of the weak Jacobi form
$\phi_{0,1}$ (weight $0$, index $1$) anti-invariant under type-II
reflections of $\LII$. The Vol III programme attaches to this the
universal Borcherds-weight identity $\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$ across
$N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ and the $\{2,3,5,24\}$ spectrum of four distinct
$K3\times E$ constructions. Five cycles below isolate the precise
slides--manuscript seam.

\paragraph{Cycle 1: twisted/twined residue.}
The tokens $\phi^{(g)}$, \emph{twined}, \emph{Mathieu}, \emph{Mukai},
\emph{CDH}, \emph{Cheng}, \emph{Duncan}, \emph{Harvey} and
\emph{Niemeier} are absent from the slides. \emph{Twisted} appears
only on lines 925 and 936 of \texttt{/tmp/automorphic-slides.txt}, in
the $(g_N,h_M)$-twisted Donaldson--Thomas series
$\ZXLh(q,t,p)$ and the $(g_N,h_M)$-twisted $K3$ elliptic genus
$F^{(g_N,h_M)}_L$, a weight-$0$ index-$1$ Jacobi form. The
slides therefore carry twisted data but no twined-character data;
the moonshine identification of $\phi^{(g)}$ with Mathieu frame-shape
characters, the Niemeier root-lattice bookkeeping of Cheng--Duncan--Harvey,
and the $K3$ Mukai vector $(1,0,1)$ used in Vol III class-B witnessing
live outside the slides.

\paragraph{Cycle 2: cross-form coherence.}
The slides (line 47--54) list \emph{eight} diagonal-divisor paramodular
forms as the $\Gamma_t(N)<\Gamma_t$ classification:
\[
\begin{aligned}
&M_5(\Gamma_1,\nu_2)=\Delta_5,\quad M_2(\Gamma_2,\nu_4),\quad
M_3(\Gamma_1(2),\nu_2),\quad M_1(\Gamma_3,\nu_6),\\
&M_2(\Gamma_1(3),\nu_2),\quad M_{1/2}(\Gamma_4,\nu_8),\quad
M_{3/2}(\Gamma_1(4),\nu_4),\quad M_1(\Gamma_2(2),\nu_4).
\end{aligned}
\]
Conjecture~1 of the slides (line 929--936) identifies each of the
eight with a reciprocal-square root of a refined DT zeta
$\ZXLh$ on $X=(S\times E)/(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})$ and the
denominator of a GBKM correction
$\mathfrak{g}\subset\mathfrak{g}_L$ whose root multiplicities are the
$(g_N,h_M)$-twisted elliptic genus Fourier coefficients. Cross-form
coherence in the slides is thus a single statement
\emph{(form $\leftrightarrow$ DT zeta $\leftrightarrow$ GBKM denominator)},
parametrised over the eight-form list; it does not assemble the forms
into a unified vector-valued Jacobi package, does not invoke a
Niemeier lattice to tie them, and does not exhibit a cross-form
modular relation.

\paragraph{Cycle 3: Niemeier content.}
None. The only lattices inscribed in the slides are
$\LIII=\Lambda^{1,1}\oplus\Lambda^{1,1}\oplus[2]$ (even unimodular,
signature $(3,2)$; line 191, 216) and its primitive hyperbolic
sublattice $\Lambda^{2,1}_I\oplus\LII$ (line 21, 272--370). The
Niemeier $24$-dimensional negative-definite even unimodular lattice
bookkeeping that drives Vol III's $K3\times E$ enhancement to $\{2,3,5,24\}$,
and the Cheng--Duncan--Harvey umbral identification of the twined
$\phi^{(g)}$ with Niemeier conjugacy classes, is absent from the
slides and supplied \emph{entirely} from Vol III side
(\texttt{chapters/examples/cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex},
\texttt{notes/wave11\_u1\_niemeier\_N6\_resolution.tex}).

\paragraph{Cycle 4: $N\geq 2$ lattice data.}
The slides supply lattice data only at $N=1$: the $\Lambda^{3,2}$
Gram form, the $\Delta^{\mathrm{re}}=\{\delta_1,\delta_2,\delta_3\}$
simple-root triple with Cartan $2$ on diagonal and $-2$ off-diagonal
(line 607), and the $\LII$-graded triangular decomposition
$\gDelta=\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\mathcal{C}_\Delta}\mathfrak{g}_\alpha
\oplus\LII\oplus\bigoplus_{\alpha\in-\mathcal{C}_\Delta}\mathfrak{g}_\alpha$
(line 675, 688). At $N\in\{2,3,4,5,6,7,8\}$ the slides give only
the \emph{building-block} formulas
$A=\tfrac14\phi_{0,1}$, $B=\phi_{-2,1}=\nu_1^2/\eta^6$ (line 940) and
tabulate the $(r,s)$-indexed cases
$N\in\{1,2,3,5,7\}$: $1\leq s,r\leq N-1$,\ $0\leq k\leq N-1$;
$N=4$, $N=6$, $N=8$: $s\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ (line 943--951). The
underlying $\LII^{(N)}$ lattice at $N\geq 2$, the rescaled Weyl
vector $\rho_N$, the $N$-shifted reflection group
$W^{(2)}(\LII^{(N)})$, and the simple-root Gram form at $N\geq 2$
are all unsupplied by the slides and must be constructed in Vol III;
this is the structural content of
\texttt{wave15\_r4\_N\_ge\_5\_forms.tex} and
\texttt{wave19\_x2/x4/y2\_N\{2,3,4\}\_automorphic\_*.tex}.

\paragraph{Cycle 5: pure Vol III contribution vs slides.}
The slides give, first-principles: $\Delta_5$ as a Borcherds product
$\tfrac{1}{64}\Delta_5(2z)=\Phi(z)$ with
$\mathrm{mult}\,\alpha=f(nm,l)$ for $\alpha=(n,l,m)$ and
$f(0,0)=10,\;f(0,-1)=1$ (line 716--802); $\gDelta$ as a GBKM
superalgebra with Weyl--Kac--Borcherds denominator identity
(line 697--722); and Conjecture~1 linking eight paramodular forms to
DT zeta and GBKM root-multiplicity data via twisted $K3$ elliptic
genera (line 929--936). Vol III adds four structural layers the
slides do not touch:
\begin{itemize}
\item Borcherds-weight universality
$\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$ at $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ as a theorem
(\texttt{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal}), with
$N=1$ giving $c_1(0)=10$ so $\kBKM(\Delta_5)=5$, matching the
slides' $\mathrm{weight}(\Delta_5)=5$ as a \emph{computed} rather than
defined quantity.
\item Four-$\kappa$ discipline on $X=K3\times E$:
$\kcat(K3\times E)=0$ by K\"unneth on the total space (\emph{not} the
K3 fibre value $2$); $\kch$ by $\Phi$-image on the $E_1$-chiral target;
$\kfib$ for the elliptic correction; $\kBKM=5$ from the slides' $\Delta_5$.
The four values $\{2,3,5,24\}$ at $K3\times E$ arise as \emph{four
distinct constructions}, not four shadows of one functor application.
\item The $\Phi$ functor scope declaration (Pattern 273):
at $d=3$ the slides' $\gDelta$ is the chain-level object-level
$\Phi$-image of a CY-category attached to $X=(S\times E)/(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})$;
the $(\infty,1)$-categorical $\Phi$-as-functor lives one ambient up and
is load-bearing independently.
\item Moonshine crossover (Vol III-exclusive): the identification of
the $(g_N,h_M)$-twisted elliptic genus $F^{(g_N,h_M)}_L$ of the slides
with the twined Mathieu $\phi^{(g)}$ at the $24$-dimensional
frame-shape witness, via the Mukai-vanishing-bypass lemma
\texttt{lem:mo-bypass-local-to-global}; this identification is \emph{not}
stated in the slides but is the Vol III mock-modular $K3$ theorem at
$d=2$ (\texttt{thm:mock-modular-k3-d2}).
\end{itemize}

The slides are thus a \emph{self-contained $N=1$ Borcherds-lift
construction plus cross-form DT/GBKM Conjecture~1}; the
Mathieu/Niemeier/Mukai/CDH layer, the $N\geq 2$ lattice and
Weyl-group data, the universal $\kBKM=c_N(0)/2$ identity, and the
four-$\kappa$ K\"unneth discipline are \emph{pure Vol III
contributions}, absent from the slides and supplied by the programme.
