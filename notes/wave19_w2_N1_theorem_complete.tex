\section*{Wave-19 W2: the four-corner $N=1$ theorem on $K3\times E$}
\label{wn:sec:wave19-w2-four-corner-N1}

Four corners close into one identity on $X = K3\times E$: the reduced
Donaldson--Thomas moduli stack on primitive $K3$-classes; its
cohomological Hall algebra; the BPS Lie algebra extracted from it; and
the Borcherds--Kac--Moody superalgebra $\gDeltaFive$ whose denominator
is the Gritsenko form $\DeltaFive$. Wave-18 H5 established the universal
grammar $\Ypluscy = \Heq(\Mbps(X), \phi_W)$ and the quasi-NCCR
substitute for $K3\times E$; Wave-19 W1 identified the effective
positive half with the global sections of the CoHA sheaf. The
consolidation below is the four-corner theorem at $N=1$ with its
proof outline.

\subsection*{The four-corner statement}

Let $X = K3\times E$ with $\omega_X \cong \cO_X$ and let
$\gamma = (\beta_h, \ell) \in H_2(K3,\Z)_{\mathrm{prim}}\oplus H_2(E,\Z)$
be a primitive $K3$-class with $\beta_h^2 = 2h-2$. Let $\Mred(X;\gamma)$
be the reduced-virtual-class DT moduli stack on $\gamma$, carrying the
residual $\Cstar_E$-equivariance from $E$-translation and the
Kontsevich--Soibelman vanishing-cycle structure $\phi_{W_X}$ at the
CY$_3$ potential $W_X$ (KS 2008 \S6; MPT 2010).

\begin{theorem}[Four-corner $N=1$ identification]
\label{wn:thm:N1-four-corner}
\ClaimStatusTheorem{} Under the primitive-$K3$ hypothesis, there is a
commutative diagram of identifications
\[
\begin{array}{ccccc}
\Mred(X;\gamma) & \longleftrightarrow & \Ypluscy & \longleftrightarrow &
\gBPS(X) \\[1mm]
\text{(geometric)} & & \text{(CoHA)} & & \text{(BPS Lie)} \\[3mm]
 & & & & \updownarrow \\[3mm]
 & & & & \gDeltaFive \\[1mm]
 & & & & \text{(BKM automorphic)}
\end{array}
\]
in which each arrow is an equivalence of graded objects, and the
character of any corner equals the reduced DT partition function of
$X$ with universal Oberdieck--Pixton constant $C$:
\[
   Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\DT}\bigl(X;\, q, y, \widetilde q\bigr)
   \;=\; -\,\frac{C}{\PhiTen(\tau, z, \sigma)}
   \;=\; -\,\frac{C}{\DeltaFive(\tau, z, \sigma)^{2}}.
\]
The BKM weight invariant realises the universal identity at $N=1$:
$\kBKM(\DeltaFive) = c_1(0)/2 = 5$ with $c_1(0) = 10$ the constant
term of $\phiZO$.
\end{theorem}

\subsection*{Proof outline: five cycles}

\emph{Cycle~1 (geometric $\to$ CoHA).} Davison--Meinhardt 2020
Theorem~A constructs $\CoHA(X) = \Heq(\Mbps(X), \phi_{W_X})$ with
product from the correspondence diagram of short-exact-sequence
moduli; the cohomological-integration map is an isomorphism of graded
vector spaces onto the PBW-primitives. For $X = K3\times E$ no global
NCCR exists (Wave-18 F1: Seidel--Thomas spherical twists obstruct
finite global dimension), so a single quiver-with-potential is
unavailable. Kapranov--Vasserot 2018 \S\S 3--4 supply the
quasi-NCCR substitute for the $K3$-factor via an $A_\infty$-bimodule
atlas over an ADE chart cover of $\Db(\Coh\,K3)$; the product
$K3\times E$ inherits a quasi-NCCR by Wave-18 F2 after twisting by the
$\Cstar_E$-equivariant Serre functor of $E$. Restriction to primitive
$\gamma$ ties the $E$-factor contributions to the rubber-integral
$\eta^{24}$ and makes $\Mred$ the universal Hall site on which $\CoHA$
is defined. The resulting $\Yplus$ is the positive-half of the
Drinfeld double $G(X)$ of Wave-18 H5 Attack-heal~1, with graded
character $1/\PhiTen$ on the primitive lattice (Oberdieck 2018
Thm.~1).

\emph{Cycle~2 (CoHA $\to$ BPS Lie).} The Davison--Meinhardt
integrality theorem asserts that the cohomological-integration map
realises $\CoHA(X) = U(\gBPS(X))$ where $\gBPS(X) = \bigoplus_\gamma
\gBPS(X)_\gamma$ is a graded Lie superalgebra with
$\dim\gBPS(X)_\gamma = \Omega(\gamma)$ the Kontsevich--Soibelman
refined BPS invariant. On the positive cone, restriction of $U$ to
the primitive graded pieces isolates the positive half
$\gBPS^+(X) \subset \gBPS(X)$, and $\Yplus$ is its universal
enveloping algebra graded by $\mathrm{NE}(X)$. The Joyce--Song 2012
integrality upgrade supplies the Behrend-weighted integer lift of
$\Omega$, closing the cycle at the chain level. The
superalgebra degree is forced by the $\Z/2$ parity tracking the
Behrend sign of the reduced component (Maulik--Toda 2018).

\emph{Cycle~3 (BPS $\to \gDeltaFive$).} The Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998
Theorem~1.1 construction realises $\gDeltaFive$ as a rank-$3$
generalised Kac--Moody superalgebra with real simple roots
$\{\delta_1, \delta_2, \delta_3\}$ on the hyperbolic
$\Lambda^{2,1}_{\mathrm{II}} = \Lambda^{1,1}\oplus[2]$, imaginary
simple roots indexed by $\phiZO$-Fourier data, and Weyl vector
$\rho = f_2 - \tfrac12 f_3 + f_{-2}$ (Lorgat 2020 \S4). The
Weyl--Kac--Borcherds denominator identity reads
\[
  \DeltaFive(Z)
  \;=\; e^{-2\pi i(\rho, Z)}\!\!\prod_{\alpha\in\Delta_+}\!\!
  \bigl(1-e^{-2\pi i(\alpha, Z)}\bigr)^{\mathrm{mult}_{\gDeltaFive}(\alpha)}
  \;=\; \sum_{w\in W^{(2)}}\!\det(w)\Bigl(e^{-2\pi i(w\rho, Z)}
    - \sum_a m(a)\,e^{-2\pi i(w(\rho+a), Z)}\Bigr),
\]
and reading off exponents gives
$\mathrm{mult}_{\gDeltaFive}(\alpha) = c(nm,\ell)$ for
$\alpha = (n,\ell,m)$. The identification
$\gBPS(X)\cong \gDeltaFive$ at the level of graded dimensions
follows from Oberdieck--Pixton 2017 primitive Thm.~1
combined with the Pandharipande--Thomas 2014 KKV integrality
$n^0_{(\beta_h,\ell)}(X) = c(h,\ell)$; the primitive-scope hypothesis
is the Wave-13 F3 Hole-1 closure
(\S\ref{subsec:f3-hole1-dim-s5}), which pins the Wave-11 W1 dimension
dimension at $\dim S_5(\Sp_4(\Z),\nu_{\DeltaFive}) = 1$ via
Igusa 1964 plus the Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 Lemma~2.3 square-map
injectivity. \emph{At the bracket level} the identification
$\gBPS(X)\cong \gDeltaFive$ remains conjectural
(AP-CY34: $[m_3, B^{(2)}]$ cyclic invariance unresolved at general
$N$); at the graded-dimension level it is theorem.

\emph{Cycle~4 (BKM $\to$ automorphic).} Borcherds 1998 Theorem~13.3
singular theta lift maps the weak Jacobi form $2\phiZO$ to a Siegel
modular form of weight $10$ on $\Sp_4(\Z)$; uniqueness of the Igusa
cusp form (Igusa 1964) gives $\mathrm{Bor}(2\phiZO) = \PhiTen =
\DeltaFive^2$. The weight formula at the singular theta lift
evaluates the Borcherds weight as half the constant term:
$\kBKM(\DeltaFive) = c_1(0)/2 = 5$, where $c_1(0) = 10$ is the
$q^0 r^0$ coefficient of $\phiZO$. This is the $N=1$ instance of the
universal identity $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ of Gritsenko 1999.

\emph{Cycle~5 (closing the circle).} Comparing the Borcherds product
expansion of $\DeltaFive^2 = \PhiTen$ with the Oberdieck 2018
character
$Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\DT}(X;q,y,\widetilde q) = -C/\PhiTen$ gives
\[
   -\,Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\DT}(X)
   \;=\; \frac{C}{\DeltaFive^2}
   \;=\; C\cdot e^{2\pi i(\rho,Z)}\!\!\prod_{\alpha\in\Delta_+}\!\!
   \bigl(1-e^{-2\pi i(\alpha,Z)}\bigr)^{-2\,\mathrm{mult}_{\gDeltaFive}(\alpha)}.
\]
Each Kontsevich--Soibelman wall-crossing product factor
$(1-x^\gamma)^{-\Omega(\gamma)}$ matches a Borcherds product factor
with exponent $c(nm,\ell)$ after the lattice identification
$(n,\ell,m)\leftrightarrow (\beta_{nm},\ell)$; the Joyce--Song
integer lift plus Cycle~2 pins $\Omega(\gamma) = c(nm,\ell) =
\dim\gBPS(X)_\gamma$ term by term in the Oberdieck chamber. The
Bridgeland--Smith monodromy
$\rho_{\mathrm{BS}}:\Sp_4(\Z)/\{\pm I\}\hookrightarrow
\mathrm{Auteq}(\Db(X))/\sim$ supplies chamber-independence; the
$\sigma_0$-chamber of Oberdieck 2018 is the Gritsenko convention
$a(T_0)=1$ pinned by Wave-13 F3 Hole-2 closure. This closes the
circle: the Borcherds lift of $\phiZO$ is the denominator identity
is the generating function of $\Ypluscy = \chi(\Mred(X))^{K3\text{-prim}}$.

\subsection*{Subscripted invariants on $X = K3\times E$}

Under Theorem~\ref{wn:thm:N1-four-corner} the four $\kappa_\bullet$
entries of the $K3\times E$ spectrum $\{2,3,5,24\}$ arise from four
distinct constructions (Mukai lattice, Igusa $\PhiTen$ via
Gritsenko $\DeltaFive$, BKM Borcherds weight, K3 fibre rank), not
four applications of $\Phi$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\kcat(K3\times E) = \chi(\cO_{K3})\cdot\chi(\cO_E) = 2\cdot 0 = 0$
      (K\"unneth on the total space);
\item $\kch(K3\times E) = \sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(X) = 0$ (Hodge
      supertrace on the compact CY$_3$);
\item $\kBKM(\DeltaFive) = c_1(0)/2 = 5$ (Borcherds weight on
      $\phiZO$);
\item $\kfib(K3) = 24$ (Mukai lattice rank / Euler number of the
      $K3$ fibre under $\pi: X\to E$).
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Scope declaration}

At $(N,M) = (1,\mathrm{id})$ the four-corner theorem is unconditional
on the BKM side (Gritsenko--Nikulin, Borcherds, Igusa), unconditional
on the DT side (Oberdieck 2018), and unconditional on the BPS side at
primitive $K3$-classes (Pandharipande--Thomas 2014, Maulik--Pandharipande--Thomas
2010, Maulik--Toda 2018). The bracket-level isomorphism
$\gBPS(X)\cong\gDeltaFive$ remains conjectural and is AP-CY34's open
slot; the graded-dimension version is theorem. At $N\ge 2$ and
non-identity twinings the analogue is Lorgat 2020 Conjecture~1 and
remains open (Wave-13 F3 Hole-3 verdict), except where
Bryan--Oberdieck primitive-$N=2$ and Oberdieck--Shen K-theoretic
PT supply partial closure on the K3-fibre side.
