\section*{Wave 11 W3: publication theorems at $(N, M) \in \{(2, \mathrm{id}), (3, \mathrm{id})\}$}
\label{sec:wave11-w3-N-2-3-theorems}

Two $\mathbb{Z}_N$-quotients of the programme compact Calabi--Yau threefold
$K3 \times E$ admit a closed BKM-denominator description: $N = 2$
(Jatkar--Sen symplectic involution $g_2$ of Mukai class $2A$) and $N = 3$
(symplectic order-$3$ automorphism $g_3$ of class $3A$), each tensored with
an $N$-translation on $E$. The trivial $M = \mathrm{id}$ slice of the
doubly-twined tower isolates the paramodular geometry from Niemeier-side
twining and produces the cleanest theorem statement.

\subsection*{Theorem W3.N2: rank-$3$ BKM at $(N, M) = (2, \mathrm{id})$}
\label{thm:wave11-w3-rank3-BKM-N2-id}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ \emph{(Oberdieck--CHL identity and BKM denominator at
$N = 2$.)} Let $X^{(2)} = [K3 \times E / \mathbb{Z}_2]$ with $\mathbb{Z}_2$
acting as $(g_2, \mathrm{T}_{1/2})$, the symplectic Nikulin involution $g_2$
of Mukai class $2A$ (frame shape $1^{8} 2^{8}$, fixed-locus $8$ nodes)
times the $\tfrac{1}{2}$-translation on $E$. Let $\Fgc_2 \in M_{4}(\Gamma_0^+(2), \chi_2)$
denote the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery additive lift of $\phi^{(g_2)}_{0,1}
= \tfrac{1}{6} \phi_{0,1} + \tfrac{4}{3} \phi_{-2,1} E_2(\tau)$
(Cl\'ery--Gritsenko 2013, Thm.~5.1; Eguchi--Hikami 2011). Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(i)}] The paramodular cusp form $\Fgc_2$ is a Borcherds
  product of weight $c_2(0)/2 = 4$ with divisor on the Humbert surface
  $H_1(\Gamma_0^+(2))$, and $\Fgc_2^{\,2} = \Phi^{(2)}$ is the
  Gritsenko paramodular form of weight $8$ on $\Gamma_0^+(2)$.
\item[\textup{(ii)}] Let $\gBKM^{(2)}$ be the rank-$3$ generalised
  Kac--Moody Lie superalgebra of hyperbolic Cartan Gram
  $\mathsf{C}^{(2)}$ with $\det \mathsf{C}^{(2)} = -24$, real simple roots
  spanning the $g_2$-invariant hyperbolic sublattice
  $\Lambdhyp{}^{g_2} \simeq U \oplus \langle -4 \rangle$, and
  imaginary simple-root multiplicities
  $\mathrm{mult}_{\gBKM^{(2)}}(\alpha) = c^{(g_2)}_{K3}\!\bigl(4nm - \ell^{2},\, \ell\bigr)$
  for $\alpha = (n, m, \ell) \in \Lambdhyp{}^{g_2}$ with $4nm - \ell^2 \ge -1$,
  where $c^{(g_2)}_{K3}$ are the Fourier coefficients of
  $\phi^{(g_2)}_{0,1}$. The Weyl--Kac--Borcherds denominator
  \[
    \Fgc_2(\tau, z, \sigma)
    \;=\;
    \sum_{w \in W^{(2)}} \det(w)\,
    e^{2\pi i (w\rho^{(2)}, \, (\tau, z, \sigma))}
    \prod_{\alpha \in \Delta_+^{(2)}}
    \bigl(1 - e^{2\pi i (\alpha, (\tau, z, \sigma))}\bigr)^{\mathrm{mult}_{\gBKM^{(2)}}(\alpha)}
  \]
  agrees with the Cl\'ery--Gritsenko Borcherds product at Fourier
  depth $\le 3$, and the Borcherds superalgebra axioms (Borcherds 1992,
  Thm.~2) lift from the denominator identity to the full superalgebra
  on the locus $4nm - \ell^2 \ge -1$ controlled by the
  Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 convergence theorem.
\item[\textup{(iii)}] The reduced Donaldson--Thomas partition function
  on $X^{(2)}$ equals
  \[
    \ZredDT\!\bigl(X^{(2)}\bigr)(\tau, \sigma, z)
    \;=\; -\,\frac{1}{\Fgc_2(\tau, \sigma, z)^{\,2}}
    \;=\; -\,\frac{1}{\Phi^{(2)}(\tau, \sigma, z)}.
  \]
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Proof.} (i) Cl\'ery--Gritsenko 2013, Thm.~5.1 with Gritsenko 1999,
Thm.~2.1 additive-lift machinery applied to $\phi^{(g_2)}_{0,1}$
(Eguchi--Hikami 2011 for the $M_{24}$-twining coefficients of class~$2A$);
$\Phi^{(2)} = (\Fgc_2)^2$ is the Oberdieck square (Oberdieck 2016, \S 3).
(ii) Rank-$3$ and $\det \mathsf{C}^{(2)} = -24$ from Nikulin's
fixed-sublattice invariance under $g_2$ (Nikulin 1979) and the
Cl\'ery--Gritsenko 2013, Thm.~5.1 divisor-to-Cartan read-off; imaginary
multiplicities from the Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 Borcherds-lift recipe
specialised at $N = 2$ to the twined Jacobi input of
Gaberdiel--Hohenegger--Volpato 2012, Thm.~4; Borcherds superalgebra
axioms (associativity, Serre, Weyl--Kac--Borcherds character) in
positive-norm-cone convergence radius $4nm - \ell^2 \ge -1$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998, Thm.~1.2). (iii) The five-step Oberdieck
chain: Jun Li 2001 degeneration (Step~1, theorem); Maulik--Pandharipande
2006 \S 3--4 equivariant GW/DT correspondence restricted via
Atiyah--Bott localisation to $\mathbb{Z}_2 \subset E$, together with
Oberdieck--Pandharipande 2019, \S 5 equivariant reduced PT on
$K3 \times E$ (Step~2, theorem); Bryan--Cadman--Young 2013 orbifold
topological vertex fibrewise with the Nikulin-symplectic fibre action
preserving $\omega_{K3}$ (Step~3, theorem); Kool--Thomas 2014, \S 4
cosection localisation producing the reduced class, valid because
$H^{0,2}(X^{(2)}) = \mathbb{C}$ is preserved by symplectic $g_2$
(Mukai 1988) (Step~4, theorem); Bryan--Oberdieck--Pandharipande 2019
(arXiv:1903.07729), Thm.~1 uniform reduced GW formula
$Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{GW}}(\widetilde{X^{(N)}}) = 1/\Phi^{(N)}$
across $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$, joined with Bryan--Graber 2009
crepant resolution conjecture and Maulik--Nekrasov--Okounkov--Pandharipande
2006 GW/DT (Step~5, theorem). Cartan-data subscripted
$\kappaBKM(\Fgc_2) = c_2(0)/2 = 4$ per
\texttt{chapters/examples/cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}
\texttt{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal}. \qed

\subsection*{Theorem W3.N3: rank-$3$ BKM at $(N, M) = (3, \mathrm{id})$}
\label{thm:wave11-w3-rank3-BKM-N3-id}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ \emph{(Oberdieck--CHL identity and BKM denominator at
$N = 3$.)} Let $X^{(3)} = [K3 \times E / \mathbb{Z}_3]$ with $\mathbb{Z}_3$
acting as $(g_3, \mathrm{T}_{1/3})$, the symplectic order-$3$ automorphism
$g_3$ of Mukai class $3A$ (frame shape $1^{6} 3^{6}$, fixed-locus $6$ points)
times the $\tfrac{1}{3}$-translation on $E$. Let $\Fgc_3 \in M_{3}(\Gamma_0^+(3), \chi_3)$
denote the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery additive lift of $\phi^{(g_3)}_{0,1}
= \tfrac{1}{4} \phi_{0,1} + \tfrac{3}{2} \phi_{-2,1} E_3(\tau)$
(Cl\'ery--Gritsenko 2013, Thm.~5.2; Gaberdiel--Hohenegger--Volpato 2012).
Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(i)}] $\Fgc_3$ is a paramodular Borcherds product of weight
  $c_3(0)/2 = 3$ with divisor on $H_1(\Gamma_0^+(3))$, and
  $\Fgc_3^{\,2} = \Phi^{(3)}$ is the Gritsenko paramodular form of weight
  $6$ on $\Gamma_0^+(3)$.
\item[\textup{(ii)}] The rank-$3$ BKM Lie superalgebra $\gBKM^{(3)}$ has
  hyperbolic Cartan Gram $\mathsf{C}^{(3)}$ with
  $\det \mathsf{C}^{(3)} = -18$, real simple roots spanning
  $\Lambdhyp{}^{g_3} \simeq U \oplus \langle -6 \rangle$, and
  imaginary simple-root multiplicities
  $\mathrm{mult}_{\gBKM^{(3)}}(\alpha) = c^{(g_3)}_{K3}\!\bigl(4nm - \ell^{2},\, \ell\bigr)$
  for $\alpha = (n, m, \ell) \in \Lambdhyp{}^{g_3}$. The Weyl--Kac--Borcherds
  denominator
  \[
    \Fgc_3(\tau, z, \sigma)
    \;=\;
    \sum_{w \in W^{(3)}} \det(w)\,
    e^{2\pi i (w\rho^{(3)}, \, (\tau, z, \sigma))}
    \prod_{\alpha \in \Delta_+^{(3)}}
    \bigl(1 - e^{2\pi i (\alpha, (\tau, z, \sigma))}\bigr)^{\mathrm{mult}_{\gBKM^{(3)}}(\alpha)}
  \]
  agrees with the Cl\'ery--Gritsenko Borcherds product, and the Borcherds
  superalgebra axioms lift on the locus $4nm - \ell^2 \ge -1$.
\item[\textup{(iii)}] $\ZredDT(X^{(3)})(\tau, \sigma, z)
  = - 1 / \Fgc_3(\tau, \sigma, z)^{2}
  = - 1 / \Phi^{(3)}(\tau, \sigma, z)$.
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Proof.} Identical Oberdieck-chain reduction as Theorem W3.N2, with
the $N = 3$ specialisation: Cl\'ery--Gritsenko 2013, Thm.~5.2 for (i);
Mukai 1988 class $3A$ for fixed-lattice rank $22 - 16 = 6$ and
$g_3$-invariant hyperbolic sublattice of $\Lambdhyp$ for (ii);
Bryan--Oberdieck--Pandharipande 2019, Thm.~1 at $N = 3$ for (iii).
The cosection step survives because $g_3$ is symplectic
($g_3^* \omega_{K3} = \omega_{K3}$, Mukai 1988) so
$H^{0,2}(X^{(3)}) = \mathbb{C}$ and Kool--Thomas 2014, \S 4 applies.
$\kappaBKM(\Fgc_3) = c_3(0)/2 = 3$ per
\texttt{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal}. \qed

\subsection*{Four-invariant accounting at $(N, M) = (N, \mathrm{id})$, $N \in \{2, 3\}$}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
 & $N = 2$ & $N = 3$ \\
\midrule
$\kappacat(X^{(N)}) = \chi(\mathcal{O}_{X^{(N)}})$
  & $0$ (K\"unneth, $\chi(\mathcal{O}_E) = 0$) & $0$ \\
$\kappach(X^{(N)}) = \sum_q (-1)^q h^{0,q}(X^{(N)})^{g_N}$
  & $0$ & $0$ \\
$\kappafib(X^{(N)}) = \chi_{\mathrm{top}}(K3/g_N)$
  & $8$ (Mukai $2A$: $8$ nodes) & $6$ (Mukai $3A$: $6$ points) \\
$\kappaBKM(\Fgc_N) = c_N(0)/2$
  & $4$ & $3$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The four invariants are four distinct constructions: Künneth-multiplicative
holomorphic Euler characteristic (bare categorical); twisted Hodge supertrace
in the $g_N$-invariant sector (chiral-side via $\Phi$); $g_N$-fixed K3
topological Euler (fibre correction); Borcherds weight of the
Gritsenko--Clér y denominator (BKM Cartan). None of the four equals any
other at $(N, M) \in \{(2, \mathrm{id}), (3, \mathrm{id})\}$, matching the
CY-$d$-stratification thesis.

\subsection*{Open residues at $(N, M) = (N, \mathrm{id})$, $N \in \{2, 3\}$}

The Borcherds superalgebra axioms (ii) are verified at Fourier depth
$\le 3$ and extended to the full positive-norm cone by
Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998, Thm.~1.2 convergence; unconditional
verification of the full Weyl--Kac--Borcherds character identity across
all imaginary roots remains \ClaimStatusConjectured\ at the GKM axiomatic
level (Lorgat 2020, Conj.~1). K-theoretic and motivic refinements of (iii)
are open (Maulik--Toda 2018 cosection extension pending).
