# The Vision: CY3 Combinatorics as Generalized Root Data of Quantum Vertex Chiral Groups

## The Central Thesis

The combinatorics of a Calabi-Yau threefold X — its lattice, intersection form, BPS spectrum — should be understood as the **generalized root datum** of a **quantum vertex chiral group** G(X). This object sits at the intersection of vertex algebras, quantum groups, and CY categories, and unifies two seemingly disparate constructions:

1. **The K3 x E tower** (Lorgat 2020): CY3 = (S x E)/(Z/NZ) produces a generalized BKM superalgebra g_{Delta_5} whose denominator identity IS the Igusa cusp form Delta_5, a Siegel modular form of weight 5 for Sp_4(Z). Root multiplicities = Fourier coefficients of the K3 elliptic genus phi_{0,1}.

2. **The toric CY3 tower** (Kontsevich-Soibelman, Schiffmann-Vasserot, Rapcak-Soibelman-Yang-Zhao): For a toric CY3, the critical CoHA is the positive half of an affine super Yangian. The toric diagram determines the quiver, hence the "root datum."

Both are instances of a single construction: CY3 geometry --> generalized root datum --> quantum vertex chiral group.

## The Generalized Root Datum of a CY3

Given a CY3 threefold X, the generalized root datum R(X) consists of:

### (i) The lattice Lambda(X)
- For K3 x E: Lambda^{3,2} = Lambda^{1,1} + Lambda^{1,1} + [2], signature (3,2), from H^2(K3) and the elliptic fiber.
- For toric CY3: the lattice of the toric fan (the quiver lattice, from K-theory of the exceptional collection).
- In general: extracted from the Mukai lattice / K-theory K(X) with the Euler form.

### (ii) The hyperbolic sublattice (real roots)
- For K3 x E: Lambda^{2,1}_{II} with Gram matrix ((2,-2,-2),(-2,2,-2),(-2,-2,2)). Three real simple roots delta_1, delta_2, delta_3 forming the polyhedron P_{II} with Aut(P_{II}) = S_3.
- For toric CY3: the vertices of the toric diagram. For C^3: a single vertex. For the conifold: two vertices.
- These are the REAL EVEN simple roots Delta^{re} = P_{II,prim}.

### (iii) Root multiplicities from BPS counting
- For K3 x E: mult(alpha) = f(nm, l) where f(n,l) = Fourier coefficients of the weak Jacobi form phi_{0,1} (the K3 elliptic genus). The phi_{0,1} controls everything.
- For toric CY3: mult(alpha) = DT invariants DT_{alpha}(X). The generating function is the DT partition function.
- Even imaginary roots Delta^{im}_0: mult > 0, contribute bosonic generators (e_alpha).
- Odd imaginary roots Delta^{im}_1: mult < 0, contribute fermionic generators (f_alpha). The negative sign = superalgebra.

### (iv) The Weyl group W(X)
- For K3 x E: W^{(2)}(Lambda^{2,1}_{II}) = reflection group generated by reflections in square-2 vectors. The group O(Lambda^{2,1})_+ decomposes as W^{(2)}(Lambda^{2,1}) = W^{(2)}(Lambda^{2,1}_I) x_semi Aut(P_I) = W^{(2)}(Lambda^{2,1}_{II}) x_semi Aut(P_{II}).
- For toric CY3: permutation symmetries of the toric diagram.

### (v) The Weyl vector rho
- For K3 x E: rho in P_{II} tensor Q with (rho, delta_i) = -(delta_i, delta_i)/2 = -1 for all simple roots delta_i. Explicitly rho = f_2 - (1/2)f_3 + f_{-2}.
- Controls the exponential prefactor in the denominator identity.

### (vi) The automorphic form (denominator identity)
- For K3 x E: Delta_5 = (1/64) * Phi(z) where Phi is the Weyl-Kac-Borcherds denominator function:
  Phi = sum_{w in W^{(2)}} det(w) exp(-pi i (w(rho), z)) - sum_{a} m(a) exp(-pi i (w(rho+a), z))
     = exp(-2pi i (rho,z)) * prod_{alpha in Delta_+} (1 - exp(-2pi i (alpha, z)))^{mult(alpha)}
- For toric CY3: the DT partition function Z^{DT}(X).

## The Quantum Vertex Chiral Group G(X)

The quantum vertex chiral group G(X) associated to R(X) is the object whose:

### Algebraic structure
- **As a superalgebra**: The generalized BKM (Borcherds-Kac-Moody) Lie superalgebra g_X with triangular decomposition g_X = n_+ + h + n_-.
  - Cartan subalgebra h = Lambda^{hyp} tensor R
  - Generators h_alpha, e_alpha, f_alpha for alpha in Delta = Delta^{re} union Delta^{im}
  - Relations: [h_alpha, e_{alpha'}] = (alpha, alpha') e_{alpha'}, etc.
  - Serre relations from the Gram matrix

- **As a vertex algebra**: g_X determines a vertex algebra V(g_X) via the vacuum module of the affinization. For K3 x E: this recovers a vertex algebra whose character is related to Delta_5. The "quantum" means the full BKM, not just the underlying Kac-Moody.

- **As a quantum group**: The R-matrix of G(X) comes from the E_2-chiral structure. At the tree level (genus 0), this is the Yangian / RTT R-matrix. The braided monoidal category Rep^{E_2}(G(X)) is the representation category.

### The positive half = critical CoHA
The positive part n_+ of g_X, or more precisely its enveloping algebra U(n_+), is the critical CoHA:
- For toric CY3: this is Rapcak-Soibelman-Yang-Zhao's identification CoHA(Q_X) = Y^+(hat{g}_X)
- For K3 x E: this is the positive part of g_{Delta_5}
- The CoHA multiplication = the E_1-chiral product (associative, from the ordered sector)

### The full algebra = BKM superalgebra with automorphic correction
The passage from the "naive" algebra g (Kac-Moody of the real root Gram matrix) to the "corrected" algebra g_X (the full BKM with imaginary roots) is the AUTOMORPHIC CORRECTION:
- **In the igusa-cusp-form paper**: g has Gram matrix ((2,-2,-2),(-2,2,-2),(-2,-2,2)). The automorphic correction g_{Delta_5} adds all the imaginary roots with multiplicities from phi_{0,1}.
- **In the Vol I language**: this is the passage from the tree-level r-matrix r(z) to the full MC element Theta_A. The imaginary roots ARE the higher-arity shadow obstruction tower components.

THIS IS THE KEY IDENTIFICATION:
  **Automorphic correction = Shadow obstruction tower**

The shadow obstruction tower Theta_A^{<=r} of Vol I, projected at successive arities, recovers:
- Arity 2 (kappa): the leading coefficient = the Weyl vector contribution
- Arity 3 (cubic shadow C): the first imaginary root corrections
- Arity 4 (quartic Q): higher corrections
- Full tower Theta_A: the complete automorphic correction, whose denominator identity is the automorphic form

## Connection to the E_1/E_2 Hierarchy

### E_1 sector (tree-level, ordered)
- The CoHA is an E_1-algebra (associative, ordered)
- Its representation category is MONOIDAL (not braided)
- This is the "positive half" / "tree-level" data
- For K3 x E: the positive roots in the positive cone C(Lambda^{2,1}_{II})_+
- For toric CY3: the quiver representations with dimension vector in the positive cone

### E_2 sector (braided, quantum)
- The full quantum vertex chiral group is E_2 (braided)
- The braiding comes from the Sp_4(Z) / O(Lambda^{3,2})_+ action
- For K3 x E: the Sp_4(Z) symmetry IS the E_2 structure — the Siegel modular form transforms under Sp_4(Z), which is exactly the braided monoidal structure
- For toric CY3: the braiding is the quantum group R-matrix of the affine super Yangian

### The genus tower
- Genus 0: the tree-level root datum (real roots + first imaginary roots)
- Genus 1: the automorphic correction at one loop = kappa * lambda_1
- Genus g: the full shadow obstruction tower obs_g = kappa * lambda_g
- The Siegel modular form Delta_5 lives on H_2 (genus-2 Siegel upper half-space) — it IS a genus-2 object. The eight diagonal divisor modular forms correspond to the eight CY3 geometries X = (S x E)/(Z/NZ) for different N.

## The Two Main Examples in Detail

### Example 1: K3 x E (from igusa-cusp-form paper)

Pipeline:
1. Start: CY3 threefold X = (S x E)/(Z/NZ), S = K3, E = elliptic curve
2. Lattice: H^*(X) gives Lambda^{3,2}; the hyperbolic sublattice Lambda^{2,1}_{II}
3. Root datum: P_{II,prim} = {delta_1, delta_2, delta_3}, Gram matrix ((2,-2,-2),(-2,2,-2),(-2,-2,2))
4. BPS spectrum: DT invariants encode root multiplicities = f(nm,l) from phi_{0,1}
5. Algebra: generalized BKM superalgebra g_{Delta_5}
6. Denominator identity: (1/64) Delta_5(2Z) = Phi(z) (Theorem 3 of the paper)
7. Product formula: (1/64) Delta_5 = exp(pi i (z_1+z_2+z_3)) prod (1-exp(2pi i (nz_1+lz_2+mz_3)))^{f(nm,l)} (Theorem 4)

The quantum vertex chiral group G(K3 x E):
- Chiral algebra A_{K3 x E} whose bar complex B(A) has bar differential encoding the OPE on Lambda^{2,1}
- Shadow obstruction tower: Theta_A is the automorphic correction, Theta_{<=2} = kappa (Weyl vector contribution), higher arities add imaginary roots
- Modular characteristic: kappa(A_{K3 x E}) = weight 5 (the weight of Delta_5)
- E_2 structure: the Sp_4(Z) action on H_2 = the braided monoidal structure

Conjecture 1 of the paper: all eight dd-modular forms arise as denominator functions of generalized BKM superalgebras with root multiplicities from twisted twined elliptic genera. In our language: each of the eight CY3 geometries X_N determines a quantum vertex chiral group G(X_N), and the eight dd-modular forms are the eight denominator identities.

### Example 2: Toric CY3 (critical CoHA / affine super Yangians)

Pipeline:
1. Start: toric CY3 X determined by a toric diagram (fan)
2. Quiver: the toric diagram determines a quiver Q_X (e.g., for C^3: the Jordan quiver; for conifold: the Klebanov-Witten quiver)
3. Root datum: K(X) with Euler form provides the lattice; quiver dimension vectors are roots
4. BPS spectrum: DT invariants = critical CoHA multiplication
5. Algebra: CoHA(Q_X) = positive half of affine super Yangian Y(hat{g}_{Q_X})
6. Full algebra: the full affine super Yangian Y(hat{g}_{Q_X}) is the quantum vertex chiral group

For C^3:
- Quiver: tripled Jordan quiver (one vertex, three loops with cubic potential)
- CoHA = Y^+(hat{gl}_1) (Schiffmann-Vasserot)
- Full algebra: affine Yangian Y(hat{gl}_1), with W_{1+infty} reached through the completed Fock/evaluation representation.
- Connection to Vol I: W_{1+infty} is already in the standard landscape! Its MC4 is solved by weight stabilization. The Yangian R-matrix is DK-0.

For general toric CY3 without compact 4-cycles (RSYZ):
- Quiver: determined by the toric diagram
- CoHA = Y^+(hat{g}_{Q_X}) for an appropriate super Lie algebra g_{Q_X}
- The toric diagram encodes the "root system" of g_{Q_X}

## The Unifying Framework

The quantum vertex chiral group G(X) of a CY3 X is defined by the following data:

1. **Root datum** R(X) = (Lambda(X), Delta^{re}, Delta^{im}_0, Delta^{im}_1, W(X), rho, mult)
   extracted from the geometry of X (lattice, BPS counting, symmetries)

2. **Quantum vertex chiral algebra** A(X) = the chiral algebra on a curve C whose:
   - Bar complex B(A(X)) encodes the CY cyclic homology CC_*(Coh(X)) or CC_*(Fuk(X))
   - MC element Theta_{A(X)} is the automorphic correction
   - Shadow obstruction tower Theta^{<=r} gives the finite-arity projections
   - Denominator identity of the BKM superalgebra = the bar-complex Euler characteristic

3. **E_2-chiral enhancement**: The braided monoidal structure on Rep(A(X)) comes from:
   - For K3 x E: the Sp_4(Z) action on genus-2 moduli
   - For toric CY3: the quantum group R-matrix of the affine super Yangian
   - In general: the E_2-operad action from the CY S^2-framing (CY_3 has S^3-framing, giving E_3 -> E_2 by Dunn)

4. **The four target theorems become**:
   - CY-A: G(X) = Phi(Coh(X)) (or Fuk(X)) — the CY-to-chiral functor produces a quantum vertex chiral group
   - CY-B: Bar-cobar for G(X) is the BKM bar-cobar — automorphic correction = shadow obstruction tower
   - CY-C: Rep^{E_2}(G(X)) = the braided monoidal category of representations (quantum group reps for toric CY3, Sp_4-module category for K3 x E)
   - CY-D: kappa(G(X)) = weight of the automorphic form = CY Euler characteristic
