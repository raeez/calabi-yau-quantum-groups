\section*{Mukai fixed loci and the Borcherds weight}
\label{wn:sec:mukai-fixed-loci-borcherds-weight}

\subsection*{Holomorphic Euler trace}

A symplectic automorphism $g_N \in \mathrm{Aut}_{\symp}(\K)$ preserves
$\Omega_{\K}$ (Mukai 1988, \emph{Invent.\ Math.}~94, Thm.~0.6), hence
acts trivially on $H^0(\cO_{\K}) \oplus H^2(\cO_{\K}) \cong \mathbb{C}
\oplus \mathbb{C}\cdot\Omega$, giving
\[
  \chi_{g_N}(\cO_{\K}) \;=\; 1 + \mathrm{tr}(g_N^* \mid H^2(\cO_{\K}))
  \;=\; 1 + 1 \;=\; 2 \;=\; \chi(\cO_{\K})
\]
identically for every $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$ (Atiyah--Bott 1968,
\emph{Ann.\ of Math.}~88, Thm.~4.12 applied to the symplectic residue;
Nikulin 1979, \emph{Trans.\ Moscow Math.\ Soc.}~38, \S4).
The holomorphic Euler trace is constant on this ladder.  The varying
table
\[
  c_N(0)=(10,8,6,4,2),\qquad
  \kappa_{\BKM}(\Phi_N)=(5,4,3,2,1),
\]
is a Jacobi-coefficient table.

\subsection*{Borcherds weight theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Universal Borcherds-weight identity]
\label{wn:thm:mukai-fixed-loci-borcherds}
For every $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$ and every Gritsenko-lifted paramodular
cusp form $\Phi_N \in M_{k(N)}(\Gamma_N, \nu)$ of the
$\Delta$-tower,
\[
  \kappa_{\BKM}(\Phi_N) \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\,c_N(0),
\]
where $c_N(0) = [\zeta^0 q^0]\,\phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1}$ is the
$\zeta^0 q^0$-coefficient of the $g_N$-twined weight-$0$ index-$1$
weak Jacobi form. The explicit tuples are
$c_N(0) = (10, 8, 6, 4, 2)$ and
$\kappa_{\BKM}(\Phi_N) = (5, 4, 3, 2, 1)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Borcherds 1998, \emph{Invent.\ Math.}~132, Thm.~13.3 applies to the
weakly-holomorphic vector-valued theta lift of
$\phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1}/\eta^{24}$ to the paramodular group $\Gamma_N$
(Gritsenko 1999, arXiv:math/9906191, Thm.~3.4). The primary source is
the Borcherds weight formula, a universal theorem on singular-theta
lifts.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{What Mukai 1988 supplies}

Mukai 1988 is the \emph{classification} of finite groups of symplectic
automorphisms of K3: admissible orders are $N \in \{1,\ldots,8\}$, with
fixed-locus Euler characteristics
$\chi(\K^{g_N}) = (24, 8, 6, 4, 2)$ at $N = (1,2,3,4,6)$
(Mukai 1988 \S2 table; Nikulin 1979 \S4). This classification specifies
the symplectic K3 orders feeding the paramodular $\Gamma_N$ ladder.
The \(N\)-dependence of \(c_N(0)\) lives in the index-\(1\) Jacobi
theta-components and is controlled by Cheng--Harrison--Paquette 2014
Table~4 twined \(M_{24}\)-class data.

\subsection*{Conjecture 1 prime}

Conjecture 1 of Lorgat 2020 relates eight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery forms, DT
zeta roots, and twined elliptic genera after a row and normalisation
have been fixed.  Theorem~\ref{wn:thm:mukai-fixed-loci-borcherds} is
the CHL-ladder instance.  Its numerical engine is the universal
Borcherds identity
\[
  \kappa_{\BKM}(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2.
\]
The additive refinement is the theta-component identity
\[
  c_N(0)=T_{H^*}(g_N)-2A_N,
\]
which links the Borcherds input coefficient to the Lefschetz trace on
\(H^*(\K,\mathbb Q)\) through the index-\(1\) theta decomposition.
