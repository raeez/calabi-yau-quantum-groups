\section*{Wave-10 S2: Mukai-refinement-failure consequences}
\label{wn:sec:wave10-s2-mukai-refinement-failure-consequences}

\subsection*{The invalidation}

A symplectic automorphism $g_N \in \mathrm{Aut}_{\symp}(\K)$ preserves
$\Omega_{\K}$ (Mukai 1988, \emph{Invent.\ Math.}~94, Thm.~0.6), hence
acts trivially on $H^0(\cO_{\K}) \oplus H^2(\cO_{\K}) \cong \mathbb{C}
\oplus \mathbb{C}\cdot\Omega$, giving
\[
  \chi_{g_N}(\cO_{\K}) \;=\; 1 + \mathrm{tr}(g_N^* \mid H^2(\cO_{\K}))
  \;=\; 1 + 1 \;=\; 2 \;=\; \chi(\cO_{\K})
\]
identically for every $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$ (Atiyah--Bott 1968,
\emph{Ann.\ of Math.}~88, Thm.~4.12 applied to the symplectic residue;
Nikulin 1979, \emph{Trans.\ Moscow Math.\ Soc.}~38, \S4). Hence both
\begin{itemize}
\item Wave-7 D2's ratio
  $c_N(0)/c_1(0) = \chi_{g_N}(\cO_{\K})/\chi(\cO_{\K}) = 1$,
  which would force $c_N(0) \equiv 10$ and $\kappa_{\BKM}(\Phi_N) \equiv 5$;
\item Wave-9 O1's calibration
  $\kappa_{\BKM} = (5/2)\,\chi^{\mathrm{EOT}}_{g_N}(\cO)$ with tuple
  $(\chi^{\mathrm{EOT}}_{g_N}(\cO))_N = (2, 8/5, 6/5, 4/5, 2/5)$,
\end{itemize}
are incompatible with the correct Gritsenko--Nikulin weight table
$(5, 2, 1, 1, 1)$ and the correct constant-term table $c_N(0) =
(10, 4, 2, 2, 2)$ (Wave-9 N3, primary-source-reconciled).

\subsection*{Correct programme statement}

\begin{theorem}[Universal Borcherds-weight identity]\ClaimStatusTheorem
\label{wn:thm:wave10-s2-universal-borcherds}
For every $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$ and every Gritsenko-lifted paramodular
cusp form $\Phi_N \in M_{k(N)}(\Gamma_N, \nu)$ of the
$\Delta$-tower,
\[
  \kappa_{\BKM}(\Phi_N) \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\,c_N(0),
\]
where $c_N(0) = [\zeta^0 q^0]\,(2\phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1})$ is the
$\zeta^0 q^0$-coefficient of the $g_N$-twined weight-$0$ index-$1$
weak Jacobi form. The explicit tuples are
$c_N(0) = (10, 4, 2, 2, 2)$ and
$\kappa_{\BKM}(\Phi_N) = (5, 2, 1, 1, 1)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Borcherds 1998, \emph{Invent.\ Math.}~132, Thm.~13.3 applied to the
weakly-holomorphic vector-valued theta lift of
$\phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1}/\eta^{24}$ to the paramodular group $\Gamma_N$
(Gritsenko 1999, arXiv:math/9906191, Thm.~3.4). No Mukai-refinement
hypothesis on $\chi_{g_N}(\cO)$ is required; the primary source is the
Borcherds weight formula, a universal theorem on singular-theta lifts.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{What Mukai 1988 supplies}

Mukai 1988 is the \emph{classification} of finite groups of symplectic
automorphisms of K3: admissible orders are $N \in \{1,\ldots,8\}$, with
fixed-locus Euler characteristics
$\chi(\K^{g_N}) = (24, 8, 6, 4, 2)$ at $N = (1,2,3,4,6)$
(Mukai 1988 \S2 table; Nikulin 1979 \S4). This is \emph{input} to the
programme --- it specifies which paramodular $\Gamma_N$ exists --- but
does not produce any non-trivial ratio between $c_N(0)$ and
$\chi_{g_N}(\cO)$; the latter is constant $= 2$ precisely by symplecticity.
The non-trivial $N$-dependence of $c_N(0)$ lives in the Jacobi-form
theta-components (Wave-10 Q4, Thm.~wave10-q4-mukai-refinement-corrected)
and is controlled by Cheng--Harrison--Paquette 2014 Table~4 twined
$M_{24}$-class data.

\subsection*{Conjecture 1$'$ status}

Conjecture 1 of Lorgat 2020 (eight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery forms
$\leftrightarrow$ DT zeta roots $\leftrightarrow$ twined elliptic genera)
is \emph{not} weakened by the refinement failure. Its content is the
identification of eight-form weights with $c_N(0)/2$, which is
Theorem~\ref{wn:thm:wave10-s2-universal-borcherds} applied across the
census. The Wave-9 O1 calibration was a phantom overlay; dropping it
restores the universal Borcherds identity as the sole numerical
engine. The Wave-10 Q4 theta-component additive refinement
$c_N(0) = T_{H^*}(g_N) - 2A_N$ is the replacement ``refinement''
(linking $c_N(0)$ to the Lefschetz trace on $H^*(\K, \mathbb{Q})$ via
the index-$1$ theta decomposition), not a failure.
