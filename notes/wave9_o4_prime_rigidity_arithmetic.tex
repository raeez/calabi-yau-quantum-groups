\section{Arithmetic Numerators in the Class-\texorpdfstring{$\mathcal{S}$}{S} and CHL Comparison}
\label{wn:sec:prime-rigidity-arithmetic}

\providecommand{\kBKM}{\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}}
\providecommand{\cfd}{c_{\mathrm{4d}}}
\providecommand{\ctd}{c_{\mathrm{2d}}}
\providecommand{\PhiN}{\Phi_N}
\providecommand{\WN}{W_N}
\providecommand{\Zmod}[1]{\mathbb{Z}/#1\mathbb{Z}}

\paragraph{Class-\(\mathcal{S}\) central charge.}
The \(A_1\) class-\(\mathcal{S}\) parent on the \(24\)-punctured sphere is
\[
  \TclassS{A_1}{\Sigma_{0,24}},
\]
with twenty-four maximal regular punctures. A pants decomposition of
\(\Sigma_{0,24}\) has \(22\) trinions and \(21\) \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\)
tubes. A trinion contributes \(n_h=4\), and a tube contributes
\(n_v=3\). Thus
\[
  n_v=21\cdot 3=63,\qquad n_h=22\cdot 4=88.
\]
The Shapere--Tachikawa anomaly formula gives
\[
  \cfd
  =
  \frac{2n_v+n_h}{12}
  =
  \frac{126+88}{12}
  =
  \frac{107}{6},
  \qquad
  \ctd=-12\cfd=-214 .
\]
The integer \(107\) is the numerator of the class-\(\mathcal{S}\)
central charge. It is supplied by the marked-curve anomaly formula;
conductor data belong to a separate calculation.

\paragraph{Primitive \(K3\times E\) CHL denominator ladder.}
A primitive fixed-origin elliptic CHL construction on \(K3\times E\)
requires an origin-fixing elliptic automorphism of the same order as the
K3 symplectic automorphism. The possible elliptic orders are exactly
\[
  N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}.
\]
On this ladder, Gritsenko--Nikulin and Borcherds give the following
Borcherds input constants and weights:
\[
  \begin{array}{c|cc}
    N & c_N(0) & \kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2 \\ \hline
    1 & 10 & 5 \\
    2 & 8  & 4 \\
    3 & 6  & 3 \\
    4 & 4  & 2 \\
    6 & 2  & 1
  \end{array}
\]
These are automorphic denominator weights. They are separate from
Shapere--Tachikawa anomaly coefficients until a level-\(N\)
class-\(\mathcal{S}\) surface and its comparison map are constructed.

\paragraph{Boundary Nikulin orders.}
Nikulin symplectic K3 automorphisms also have orders \(5,7,8\). These
orders carry Borcherds singular-theta data after the twined Jacobi
input, multiplier or cover, and normalisation are fixed:
\[
  \begin{array}{c|cc}
    N & c_N(0) & \kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2 \\ \hline
    5 & 4 & 2 \\
    7 & 2 & 1 \\
    8 & 2 & 1
  \end{array}
\]
They are boundary order-indexed automorphic rows. The fixed-origin
elliptic CHL construction on \(K3\times E\) has no matching smooth
primitive quotient at these orders.

Order \(11\) is absent from Nikulin's symplectic K3 list. The
Jatkar--Sen weight-zero boundary at \(N=11\) belongs to a different
Narain or CHL automorphic tower. A K3 symplectic host for \(N=11\)
would require a new host theorem with different hypotheses.

\paragraph{Gritsenko--Cl\'ery diagonal-divisor atlas.}
The Gritsenko--Cl\'ery diagonal-divisor catalogue is triple-indexed by
\((t,N;k)\), where \(k\) is the automorphic weight:
\[
  \begin{array}{c|c}
    (t,N;k) & 2k \\ \hline
    (1,1;5) & 10 \\
    (2,1;2) & 4 \\
    (1,2;3) & 6 \\
    (3,1;1) & 2 \\
    (1,3;2) & 4 \\
    (4,1;\tfrac12) & 1 \\
    (1,4;\tfrac32) & 3 \\
    (2,2;1) & 2
  \end{array}
\]
This dd-modular atlas and the CHL ladder use different row parameters.
The common row is \((1,1;5)\), the \(\Delta_5\) row. The CHL constants
at orders \(2,3,4,6\) remain the five-row table above.

\paragraph{Atkin--Lehner action.}
Atkin--Lehner theory acts on spaces of modular forms. For a level \(N\),
the involutions \(W_d\) are indexed by exact divisors \(d\Vert N\) in the
classical \(\Gamma_0(N)\) setting; the paramodular analogue contains the
Fricke involution and level-dependent extensions. If a modular form is
an eigenform, the eigenvalue statement is
\[
  W_d F=\varepsilon_d(F)F,\qquad \varepsilon_d(F)\in\{\pm1\}.
\]
An eigenspace decomposition belongs to the ambient modular-form space
after the operators are fixed. Assigning such a decomposition to an
integer numerator requires three data:
\[
  \text{the precise modular space},\qquad
  \text{the Atkin--Lehner operators on that space},\qquad
  \text{the eigenspace decomposition of the form under study}.
\]
For the level-one \(\Delta_5\) row, the exact-divisor group supplies only
the identity operator. The numerator \(107\) therefore has no
Atkin--Lehner--Hecke eigenspace interpretation in the level-one
\(\Delta_5\) construction.

\paragraph{Formal higher-level numerator rule.}
The expression
\[
  \cfd^{(N)}
  =
  \frac{n_v^{(N)}}{2}
  +
  \frac{49\,c_N(0)}{30}
\]
produces the following formal values:
\[
  \begin{array}{c|cccc}
    N & n_v^{(N)} & c_N(0) & \cfd^{(N)} & \text{numerator} \\ \hline
    1 & 3 & 10 & 107/6 & 107 \\
    2 & 1 & 8  & 407/30 & 407=11\cdot 37 \\
    3 & 0 & 6  & 49/15 & 49=7^2 \\
    4 & 1 & 4  & 211/30 & 211 \\
    6 & 3 & 2  & 143/30 & 143=11\cdot 13
  \end{array}
\]
This is a substitution into an unproved rule. It supplies no
class-\(\mathcal{S}\) theorem, no Atkin--Lehner eigenspace theorem, and
no conductor theorem.

\paragraph{Level-\(N\) comparison datum.}
A CHL class-\(\mathcal{S}\) extension consists of
\[
  \Sigma_N,\qquad
  \text{all puncture types},\qquad
  \Zmod{N}\curvearrowright\Sigma_N,\qquad
  \text{a pants decomposition computing }(n_v,n_h).
\]
Only then does the Shapere--Tachikawa formula define a central charge
\[
  \cfd(N)=\frac{2n_v(N)+n_h(N)}{12}.
\]
For genus zero with \(n_N\) maximal regular \(A_1\)-punctures, the
first-principles count gives
\[
  n_v=3(n_N-3),\qquad n_h=4(n_N-2),
  \qquad
  \cfd(N)=\frac{5n_N-13}{6}.
\]
Fixed-point counts of symplectic K3 automorphisms are K3-side data.
Ramification indices of a Gaiotto curve are class-\(\mathcal{S}\) data.
A comparison theorem must specify the map between these two structures
before transporting arithmetic from one side to the other.

\paragraph{Arithmetic statement.}
The established identities are
\[
  \cfd\bigl(\TclassS{A_1}{\Sigma_{0,24}}\bigr)=107/6,
  \qquad
  \ctd=-214,
\]
together with the independent CHL denominator identity
\[
  \kBKM(\PhiN)=c_N(0)/2 .
\]
The first identity is a class-\(\mathcal{S}\) anomaly computation. The
second identity is a Borcherds denominator-weight computation. Prime
factorisations of formal numerators become arithmetic only after the
marked level-\(N\) surface, modular space, Atkin--Lehner operators, and
comparison map have all been constructed.
