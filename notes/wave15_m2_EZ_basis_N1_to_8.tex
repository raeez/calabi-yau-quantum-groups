\section*{Eichler--Zagier constants for the CHL ladder and the
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery catalogue}

The graded ring of weak Jacobi forms on \(\HH\times\mathbb{C}\) is
generated over classical modular forms by the Eichler--Zagier forms
\[
  \phi_{0,1}(\tau,z),\qquad \phi_{-2,1}(\tau,z),
\]
of weights \(0,-2\) and index \(1\).  With
\(r=e^{2\pi i z}\) and \(q=e^{2\pi i\tau}\),
\[
  \phi_{0,1}=(r^{-1}+10+r)
  +q(10r^{-2}-64r^{-1}+108-64r+10r^2)+O(q^2),
\]
\[
  \phi_{-2,1}=(r^{-1}-2+r)
  +q(-2r^{-2}+8r^{-1}-12+8r-2r^2)+O(q^2).
\]
The coefficient \(10=[q^0r^0]\phi_{0,1}\) is the \(N=1\) Borcherds
constant in the primitive CHL normalisation.

\subsection*{Primitive CHL denominator ladder}

For \(N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}\), let \(\Phi_N^{\mathrm{BKM}}\) be the
primitive Gritsenko--Nikulin denominator attached to the
\(g_N\)-twined K3 Jacobi input.  Borcherds' singular-theta weight
formula gives
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_N^{\mathrm{BKM}})=\frac{c_N(0)}{2},
\]
where \(c_N(0)\) is the \(\zeta^0q^0\)-coefficient in the Borcherds
input.  In the standard CHL normalisation,
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
N & \Phi_N^{\mathrm{BKM}} & c_N(0) & \kBKM(\Phi_N^{\mathrm{BKM}})\\
\hline
1 & \Delta_5 & 10 & 5\\
2 & \Delta_4^{(2)} & 8 & 4\\
3 & \Delta_3^{(3)} & 6 & 3\\
4 & \Delta_2^{(4)} & 4 & 2\\
6 & \Delta_1^{(6)} & 2 & 1
\end{array}
\]
Thus
\[
  (c_N(0))_{N=1,2,3,4,6}=(10,8,6,4,2),\qquad
  \kBKM=(5,4,3,2,1).
\]
The genus-two physical denominator is the square of the primitive
BKM denominator:
\[
  \Phi_N^{\mathrm{phys}}=(\Phi_N^{\mathrm{BKM}})^2,\qquad
  \wt(\Phi_N^{\mathrm{phys}})=c_N(0).
\]
For \(N=1\), this is the familiar identity
\(\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_5^2\).  The weight \(10\) belongs to
the squared physical form; the BKM denominator \(\Delta_5\) has
\(\kBKM=5\).

\subsection*{Separate Gritsenko--Cl\'ery dd-catalogue}

Gritsenko--Cl\'ery classify eight dd-modular Siegel forms with simplest
diagonal divisor.  The indexing is by \((t,N_{\mathrm{dd}};k)\), not by
the CHL order \(N\).  Write \(F_{t,N_{\mathrm{dd}}}\) for the row and
\[
  \kBKM(F_{t,N_{\mathrm{dd}}})=\wt(F_{t,N_{\mathrm{dd}}})
  =\frac{c^{\mathrm{dd}}_{t,N_{\mathrm{dd}}}(0)}{2}.
\]
The row data are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
\text{row} & (t,N_{\mathrm{dd}};k) & F_{t,N_{\mathrm{dd}}}
  & c^{\mathrm{dd}}_{t,N_{\mathrm{dd}}}(0)\\
\hline
1 & (1,1;5) & F_{1,1}=\Delta_5 & 10\\
2 & (2,1;2) & F_{2,1} & 4\\
3 & (1,2;3) & F_{1,2} & 6\\
4 & (3,1;1) & F_{3,1} & 2\\
5 & (1,3;2) & F_{1,3} & 4\\
6 & (4,1;\tfrac12) & F_{4,1} & 1\\
7 & (1,4;\tfrac32) & F_{1,4} & 3\\
8 & (2,2;1) & F_{2,2} & 2
\end{array}
\]
The twice-weight tuple of this catalogue is
\[
  (10,4,6,2,4,1,3,2).
\]
The primitive CHL tuple is \((10,8,6,4,2)\).  The two catalogues
coincide only at \(F_{1,1}=\Delta_5\), unless a separate row-by-row
theorem identifies another automorphic product, character, divisor, and
normalisation.

\subsection*{Orders \(5,7,8\)}

The order-indexed K3 twining lane extends the Borcherds-weight formula
to Mukai orders \(5,7,8\) with
\[
  (c_5(0),c_7(0),c_8(0))=(4,2,2),\qquad
  \kBKM=(2,1,1).
\]
These entries require new geometric hosts because no elliptic curve has
automorphism order \(5,7,\) or \(8\).  They are not ordinary
\(K3\times E\) CHL rows, and their \(\kcat\) and \(\kch\) must be
computed from the chosen host:
\[
  \kcat(X)=\chi(\cO_X),\qquad
  \kch(\Phi(D^b\mathrm{Coh}(X)))
  \quad\text{is the chiral-side invariant of the constructed }\Phi\text{-image}.
\]
Numerical agreement with a dd-row weight does not identify the order
twining with a Gritsenko--Cl\'ery row.

\subsection*{Eichler--Zagier normalisation}

The EZ expansions fix the \(N=1\) coefficient directly:
\[
  [q^0r^0]\,\phi_{0,1}=10,\qquad
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)=10/2=5.
\]
For \(N=2,3,4,6\), the constants \(8,6,4,2\) are not obtained by
substituting powers of \(\phi_{0,1}\) into an untwined polynomial.
They come from the \(g_N\)-twined K3 Jacobi inputs and the
Gritsenko--Nikulin arithmetic lift.  The EZ basis supplies the local
normalisation of coefficients; the twining supplies the actual
constant.
