\section*{Local Hirzebruch $K_{\F_n}$: positive-half dependence on $n$}
\label{sec:wave18-e4-local-fn-yplus}

The Hirzebruch surface $\F_n = \P(\cO_{\P^1} \oplus \cO_{\P^1}(-n))$
has, for every $n \in \Z_{\geq 0}$, a toric CY$_3$ canonical bundle
$K_{\F_n} = \Tot(\omega_{\F_n})$. Three questions fix the problem.
How does the toric fan encode the twist $n$? Which arrows of the
McKay quiver depend on $n$, and which do not? And does the
positive-half $Y^+(K_{\F_n})$ split by $n$ into distinct algebras,
or do all $n$ fall into one equivalence class?

\subsection*{Toric fan of $\F_n$ and of $K_{\F_n}$}

The fan $\Sigma_{\F_n} \subset \Z^2$ carries four rays
\[
 v_1 = (1, 0), \quad v_2 = (0, 1), \quad v_3 = (-1, n), \quad v_4 = (0, -1),
\]
bounding four $2$-cones arranged cyclically. The $n$-dependence sits
in the third ray: $n = 0$ gives the symmetric $\P^1 \times \P^1$
fan with $v_3 = (-1, 0)$; $n = 1$ gives the one-blowup of $\P^2$;
$n = 2$ gives the cone $\C^2/\Z_2$ partially resolved; $n \geq 3$
gives non-Fano. The CY$_3$ fan of $K_{\F_n}$ lifts the four rays
to height one and adds the CY direction,
\[
 \tilde v_i = (v_i, 1) \in \Z^3, \qquad i = 1, 2, 3, 4,
\]
so $\Sigma_{K_{\F_n}}$ has four $3$-cones, four walls, and one common
vertex at the origin. The coplanarity of $\tilde v_1, \ldots, \tilde v_4$
at height one is the CY condition. The twist $n$ is the shift of
$\tilde v_3$ along the $\F_n$-base direction; the height-one CY
component is unaffected.

\subsection*{Four-chart cover and $\C^*$-overlaps}

Label the $3$-cones $\sigma_1, \sigma_2, \sigma_3, \sigma_4$ cyclically.
Each dualises to the positive octant, so
\[
 U_i = \Spec \C[\sigma_i^\vee \cap M] \simeq \C^3,
\]
with $M = \Z^3$ the character lattice of $T = (\C^*)^3$. The pairwise
adjacent overlap along the wall $\sigma_i \cap \sigma_{i+1}$ is
$\C^* \times \C^2$; the non-adjacent overlap $U_1 \cap U_3$ and
$U_2 \cap U_4$ is $(\C^*)^2 \times \C$ because the two cones meet only
at the vertex. The CY volume form $\Omega = dx \wedge dy \wedge dz$ is
$T$-invariant on each chart; the transition Jacobian between adjacent
charts is unimodular on the wall, and the $n$-twist enters the
transition on the wall $\sigma_2 \cap \sigma_3$ as a monomial change
of variable $(x_2, y_2, z_2) \mapsto (x_3, y_3, z_3)$ with matrix
\[
 \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 0 & 0 \\ n & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
 \in \mathrm{SL}_3(\Z),
\]
the shear by $n$ in the base direction fixing the CY fibre. The
discriminant locus of the transition sees $n$; the CY volume form
does not.

\subsection*{McKay quiver from the exceptional collection}

Kapranov $1988$ \cite{Kapranov1988} gives the full strong exceptional
collection on $\F_n$,
\[
 E_\bullet(\F_n) \;=\; \bigl(\cO,\; \cO(f),\; \cO(h),\; \cO(h + f)\bigr),
\]
with $h$ the class of the zero section (self-intersection $-n$) and
$f$ the fibre class. The tilting bundle $T_n = \bigoplus E_\bullet$
produces a derived equivalence $D^b(\Coh(\F_n)) \simeq D^b(\End(T_n)
\text{-mod})$; the endomorphism algebra is the path algebra of the
four-vertex quiver $Q_{\F_n}$ with four vertices and the Ext-arrows
\begin{align*}
 &\dim \mathrm{Hom}(\cO, \cO(f)) = 2, \quad
 \dim \mathrm{Hom}(\cO(f), \cO(h)) = n + 2, \\[-2pt]
 &\dim \mathrm{Hom}(\cO(h), \cO(h + f)) = 2, \quad
 \dim \mathrm{Hom}(\cO, \cO(h)) = n + 2, \\[-2pt]
 &\dim \mathrm{Hom}(\cO(f), \cO(h + f)) = n + 2, \quad
 \dim \mathrm{Hom}(\cO, \cO(h + f)) = 2(n + 2).
\end{align*}
The lifting to $K_{\F_n}$ adds the CY reverse-arrows via Serre duality,
giving $(8 + 2n)$ forward and $(8 + 2n)$ Serre-dual arrows per
surface. The superpotential $W_{\F_n}$ is the Closset $2012$
\cite{Closset2012} cyclic-symmetric cubic-quartic combination
\[
 W_{\F_n} \;=\; \sum_{\text{triangle}} \tr(a b c)
 \;+\; n \sum_{\text{base-fibre cycle}} \tr(a^{(h)} a^{(h)} a^{(f)} a^{(f)}),
\]
with the quartic term carrying the twist-dependent multiplicity.
The Jacobi algebra $J(Q_{\F_n}, W_{\F_n})$ is the
Klebanov--Witten--Closset $\mathcal{N} = 1$ worldvolume algebra of
D$3$-branes probing $K_{\F_n}$ at the orbifold point.

\subsection*{Positive half and Nekrasov character}

Rapcak--Soibelman--Yang--Zhao $2018$ \cite{RSYZ} identifies, for
every toric CY$_3$ $X$ without compact $4$-cycles, the critical
CoHA of the associated quiver-with-potential with the positive half
of the affine super-Yangian of the McKay Cartan:
\[
 \CoHA(Q_{\F_n}, W_{\F_n}) \;\simeq\; Y^+(\widehat{\fg}_{Q_{\F_n}}),
\]
where $\widehat{\fg}_{Q_{\F_n}}$ is the Kac--Moody algebra with
$4 \times 4$ Cartan matrix read off the $Q_{\F_n}$ Euler form.
The diagonal entries are $2$; the off-diagonals are $-2$ along the
triangle and $-(n+2)$ along the base-fibre diagonal. For $n = 0$
the matrix is the symmetric affine $D_4^{(1)}$-like pattern; for
$n = 1$ it is the extended $A_3$ pattern with one arm weighted by $3$;
for $n = 2$ it is the extended $A_1^{(1)} \times A_1^{(1)}$ pattern
with one weight-$4$ arm.

The positive-half character is the Nekrasov partition function of
$K_{\F_n}$ in the topological vertex formalism,
\[
 \mathrm{ch}\bigl(Y^+(K_{\F_n})\bigr)(q, Q_h, Q_f)
 \;=\; Z_{\mathrm{top}}^{K_{\F_n}}(q, Q_h, Q_f),
\]
with $Q_h, Q_f$ the K\"ahler parameters of the base and fibre and
$q = e^{-g_s}$ the refined parameter \cite{IqbalNekrasov2004}.
The leading expansion is
\[
 Z_{\mathrm{top}}^{K_{\F_n}}
 \;=\; 1 \;+\; (2 + (n+2))\, q
 \;+\; \bigl(4 + 4(n+2) + (n+2)^2\bigr)\, q^2 \;+\; \cdots,
\]
the coefficient of $q^k$ counting coloured Young-diagram partitions
weighted by the Ext-arrow multiplicities. The $n$-dependence is
polynomial in $(n+2)$; the leading coefficient $(n + 4)$ records
the total arrow count modulo the three base-fibre symmetries.

\subsection*{$n = 0$: the product structure}

At $n = 0$, $\F_0 = \P^1 \times \P^1$ and $K_{\F_0} = K_{\P^1}
\boxtimes K_{\P^1}$ in the total-space sense; the McKay quiver
$Q_{\F_0}$ is the product of two $A_1$-McKay quivers (each the
$\P^1$-exceptional collection $\cO, \cO(1)$ tilt). The positive-half
factors,
\[
 Y^+(K_{\F_0}) \;\simeq\; Y^+(\widehat{\fsl}_2) \otimes Y^+(\widehat{\fsl}_2),
\]
the external tensor product of two rank-one affine Yangians. The
Nekrasov partition factors accordingly,
$Z_{\mathrm{top}}^{K_{\F_0}} = Z_{\mathrm{top}}^{K_{\P^1}}(Q_h) \cdot
Z_{\mathrm{top}}^{K_{\P^1}}(Q_f)$. This is Iqbal--Nekrasov's
product formula at the toric-product point.

\subsection*{$n = 2$: the exceptional $\P^1$ and toric McKay}

At $n = 2$, the base $\P^1$ has self-intersection $-2$: it is a
rational $(-2)$-curve, the exceptional divisor of the minimal
resolution of $\C^2/\Z_2$. The fan $\Sigma_{\F_2}$ is the blow-up
of the $\C^2/\Z_2$-cone at the apex, and $K_{\F_2}$ is the canonical
bundle of this resolution. The Bridgeland--King--Reid derived
equivalence \cite{BKR2001} gives
$D^b(\Coh(K_{\F_2})) \simeq D^b([\C^3/\Z_2])$,
where $\Z_2$ acts on $\C^3$ with weights $(1, 1, 0)$ (the CY
condition fixes the third weight). The McKay presentation therefore
identifies $Q_{\F_2}$ with the $\Z_2$-orbifold quiver on $\C^3$,
two vertices with three arrows each, plus the Serre CY-dual arrows;
the extra weight-$4$ arm corresponds to the length-two string of
$\Z_2$-characters appearing in the $(n+2) = 4$ base-fibre Hom.
Positive-half:
\[
 Y^+(K_{\F_2}) \;\simeq\; Y^+(\widehat{\fsl}_2|\widehat{\fsl}_2),
\]
the affine super-Yangian of rank $2$ with super-structure recording
the $\Z_2$-character grading.

\subsection*{$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$ and the twist witness}

The non-compact shadow $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(K_{\F_n})$ is the
half-Euler of the base $\F_n$,
\[
 \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(K_{\F_n})
 \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(\F_n)
 \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \;=\; 2,
\]
independent of $n$: the topological Euler number of every
Hirzebruch is $4$. The Hodge numbers split as $h^{0,0} = h^{2,2} = 1$,
$h^{1,1} = 2$; the $n$-dependence is invisible to $\chi_{\mathrm{top}}$
and therefore to $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$. The twist is witnessed at
the level of $Y^+$: the algebras $Y^+(K_{\F_n})$ for different $n$
are not isomorphic, distinguished by the Cartan matrix entry
$-(n + 2)$. The $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$ is a coarse invariant; the
positive-half structure is the fine invariant.

\subsection*{Synthesis}

Four charts cover $K_{\F_n}$; four $\C^*$-walls glue them; the
Kapranov exceptional collection provides a $4$-vertex McKay quiver
whose Ext-form is $n$-twisted on one arrow class; the
Rapcak--Soibelman--Yang--Zhao identification gives $Y^+(K_{\F_n})
\simeq Y^+(\widehat{\fg}_{Q_{\F_n}})$, with structure constants
polynomial in $(n + 2)$; the Nekrasov--Iqbal topological vertex
computes the positive-half character; $n = 0$ factors as a product,
$n = 2$ is the minimal resolution of $\C^3/\Z_2$ under
Bridgeland--King--Reid. The family $\{Y^+(K_{\F_n})\}_{n \geq 0}$
interpolates between the product affine Yangian and the orbifold
super-Yangian through a continuous family of Cartan matrices; this
is the first non-trivial $n$-family of positive halves in the
local-surface landscape.
