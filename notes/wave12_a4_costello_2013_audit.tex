\subsection{Target and scope}

The treatise (\texttt{notes/yplus\_to\_chiral\_yangian\_treatise.tex} l.~200
and Table at l.~266; \texttt{chapters/theory/quantum\_chiral\_algebras.tex}
\S4 Theorem~\texttt{thm:5d-boundary-yangian};
\texttt{chapters/theory/en\_factorization.tex} \S\S8.3, 12.1) cites
Costello~2013 \texttt{arXiv:1303.2632} as the source of five interlocking
assertions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(C1)] the twisted observable complex of $\mathcal N{=}2$ $U(1)$ pure
gauge theory in four real dimensions on $\Sigma\times\mathbb C_z$ in an
$\Omega$-background is a factorisation algebra $\Fgauge$ on the spectral
line $\mathbb C_z$;
\item[(C2)] $\Fgauge$ is a vertex algebra in the Costello--Gwilliam sense;
\item[(C3)] the affine Yangian $Y(\gghone)$ is the Lie algebra controlling
the deformation of $\Fgauge$;
\item[(C4)] the construction is a five-dimensional-to-boundary descent;
\item[(C5)] the result is \emph{full} (all orders, not merely perturbative).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Cycle 1 --- Title, abstract, ambient theory}

\ClaimStatusCorrected\ \emph{The ambient theory in Costello~2013 is
four-dimensional pure $\mathcal N{=}1$ supersymmetric gauge theory, not
$\mathcal N{=}2$.} The \texttt{arXiv:1303.2632} abstract reads, verbatim:
\emph{a twisted, deformed version of the pure $\mathcal N{=}1$
supersymmetric gauge theory is controlled by the Yangian, in the same way
that Chern--Simons theory is controlled by the quantum group.} The
treatise's four-dimensional $\mathcal N{=}2$ $U(1)$ pure gauge theory
(\texttt{yplus\_to\_chiral\_yangian\_treatise.tex}~l.~200) conflates
Costello's setup with the Nekrasov $\mathcal N{=}2$ $\Omega$-background
(Nekrasov~2002) in which the Yangian acts on equivariant instanton
cohomology (Maulik--Okounkov~2012 \texttt{arXiv:1211.1287}). These are
\emph{distinct constructions}: Costello~2013 twists pure $\mathcal N{=}1$
to a holomorphic-topological theory on $\mathbb R^2\times\mathbb C$;
Maulik--Okounkov's Yangian action is the stable-envelope $R$-matrix on
Nakajima-variety cohomology. The common denominator is $Y(\gghone)$;
the two sources are the same Lie algebra approached from perpendicular
angles.

\subsection{Cycle 2 --- What Costello~2013 actually proves (C1, C4)}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Costello~2013, main result.) The twisted,
$\Omega$-deformed pure $\mathcal N{=}1$ gauge theory in four real
dimensions is controlled, perturbatively, by the Yangian $Y(\mathfrak g)$
of the gauge Lie algebra. The control is witnessed by an exact
perturbative expectation value of a net of $n{+}m$ Wilson operators
matching the partition function of an integrable spin chain on an
$n{\times}m$ doubly-periodic lattice.

\ClaimStatusCorrected\ (C1). Costello~2013 does not state its output as
$\Fgauge$ is a factorisation algebra on $\mathbb C_z$ in the explicit
shape the treatise presents. The paper constructs the quantisation of
the twisted theory via Costello--Gwilliam factorisation-algebra
renormalisation (Costello, \emph{Renormalisation and the BV Formalism},
Ch.~5; Costello--Gwilliam \emph{Factorization Algebras in QFT} Vol.~I,
\texttt{arXiv:1610.01500}); the chiral-algebra structure on $\mathbb C_z$
follows functorially from the holomorphic-topological twist. The
factorisation-algebra statement is therefore a \emph{compound} result
of Costello~2013 plus Costello--Gwilliam~2017; Costello~2013 in
isolation does not contain it. The treatise's compound attribution
Costello~2013, CG~2017 at l.~266 is correct; the standalone Costello~2013
citation at l.~203 is attenuated.

\ClaimStatusCorrected\ (C4). The five-dimensional-to-boundary framing is
not Costello~2013's native setup. Costello~2013 works in four real
dimensions, with holomorphic-topological twist
$\mathbb R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\mathbb C^{\mathrm{hol}}_z$; the
five-dimensional lift is Costello--Yagi~2018 \texttt{arXiv:1810.01970}
Thm.~1 (four-dimensional Chern--Simons has an origin in a six-dimensional
topological-holomorphic theory $\Omega$-deformed on
$\mathbb R^2\times\Sigma\times\mathbb C$). The explicit five-dimensional
holomorphic Chern--Simons realisation whose boundary chiral algebra is
$Y(\gghone)$ is constructed in Costello--Gaiotto~2018
\texttt{arXiv:1812.09257} and Costello--Dimofte--Paquette
\texttt{arXiv:2005.00083}. The treatise citation
Costello--Paquette~2021 at \texttt{quantum\_chiral\_algebras.tex}~l.~719
for the five-dimensional-to-affine-Yangian result is attenuated as a
standalone Costello~2013 citation, accurate as a compound one folding
in CDP and CG.

\subsection{Cycle 3 --- Yangian action as deformation (C3, C5)}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Costello~2013, \S11.) The Yangian $Y(\mathfrak g)$
acts as the universal deformation on the twisted, $\Omega$-deformed
theory. Equivalently, the deformation complex of the classical BRST
observables is controlled as an $L_\infty$-algebra by $Y(\mathfrak g)$;
the $R$-matrix of $Y(\mathfrak g)$ is recovered as the OPE of line
operators.

\ClaimStatusCorrected\ (C5). The Costello~2013 result is stated in the
abstract as \emph{exact calculation, in perturbation theory}. Perturbative
in $\hbar$ is not all orders. The treatise's PROVED/Full tag at
\texttt{working\_notes.tex}~l.~4520 propagates a stronger status than
Costello~2013 earns in isolation. The non-perturbative completion
(affine-Yangian-as-VOA identification with the all-orders boundary
algebra of five-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons) is stated in
Costello--Dimofte--Paquette \texttt{arXiv:2005.00083} Thm.~1
(\emph{Boundary Chiral Algebras and Holomorphic Twists}), and even
there in a scope restricted to specific boundary conditions; the
all-orders chiral Yangian as VOA for general $\mathfrak g$ remains
\emph{partially conjectural}.

\subsection{Cycle 4 --- Vertex-algebra structure on $\Fgauge$ (C2)}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Compound.) Costello~2013 does not contain the
explicit $\Fgauge$ is a vertex algebra statement. The abstract route
is: Costello~2013 gives holomorphic-topological quantisation;
Costello--Gwilliam Vol.~I \S5.3 proves that a holomorphic factorisation
algebra on $\mathbb C$ with smooth translation-equivariant structure
maps is equivalent to a vertex algebra. The composite $\Fgauge$-as-VOA
is therefore a theorem in the compound citation Costello~2013 plus CG~2017,
not of Costello~2013 alone. The treatise's compound row at l.~267 is
accurate.

\subsection{Cycle 5 --- Relation to CG~2018 and CP~2020}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ Costello--Yagi~2018 \texttt{arXiv:1810.01970} Thm.~1:
four-dimensional Chern--Simons arises as the $\Omega$-deformation of a
six-dimensional topological-holomorphic theory, supplying the
five-dimensional lift that Costello~2013 alone does not.

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ Costello--Gaiotto~2018 \texttt{arXiv:1812.09257}
(\emph{Twisted Holography}) derives a B-model/chiral-algebra duality
where the chiral algebra is a gauged $\beta\gamma$ system; specialised
to $U(1)$, the boundary chiral algebra realises $Y(\gghone)$ as a
vertex algebra, supplying the VOA identification the treatise needs
for \texttt{thm:5d-boundary-yangian}.

\ClaimStatusConjectured\ The \emph{full} chiral-Yangian-as-VOA for
$Y(\mathfrak g)$ with $\mathfrak g$ general simple is not established
as a theorem in \texttt{1303.2632}, \texttt{1810.01970}, or
\texttt{1812.09257}; only the $\gghone$/abelian case is complete
(combining Schiffmann--Vasserot~2013 \texttt{arXiv:1202.2756},
Tsymbaliuk~2017 \texttt{arXiv:1703.04551}, and CDP 2020).

\subsection{Corrections for the treatise}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \texttt{yplus\_to\_chiral\_yangian\_treatise.tex}~l.~200:
four-dimensional $\mathcal N{=}2$ $U(1)$ pure gauge theory should be
replaced with four-dimensional pure $\mathcal N{=}1$ $U(1)$ gauge
theory, twisted to holomorphic-topological on
$\mathbb R^2\times\mathbb C_z$, in the $\Omega$-background of
Nekrasov~2002.
\item \texttt{working\_notes.tex}~l.~4520: split the row into a
four-dimensional perturbative Yangian control clause (Costello~2013)
and a five-dimensional boundary VOA identification clause
(Costello--Gaiotto 2018 plus CDP 2020), restricted to $\gghone$.
\item \texttt{en\_factorization.tex}~l.~2529: the proved tag should
attribute Costello~2013 for the four-dimensional twisted-theory side;
Costello--Gaiotto 2018 and CDP 2020 for the five-dimensional-to-boundary
VOA identification at $\gghone$.
\item \texttt{quantum\_chiral\_algebras.tex}~l.~1139: the claim that
the boundary chiral algebra is $Y(\gghone)$ should cite
Costello--Gaiotto 2018 \S6 and CDP 2020 Thm.~1 as primary; Costello~2013
as the four-dimensional perturbative antecedent.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Summary verdict}

The treatise's ultimate claim (the affine Yangian $Y(\gghone)$ is the
VOA of the boundary of a five-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons
theory, equivalent to the factorisation algebra of a twisted
$\Omega$-deformed gauge theory on $\mathbb C_z$) is supported in the
compound citation Costello~2013 plus Costello--Gwilliam~2017 plus
Costello--Yagi~2018 plus Costello--Gaiotto~2018 plus
Costello--Dimofte--Paquette~2020, and is a theorem in that compound
scope restricted to $\gghone$. Attributing the whole to Costello~2013
alone overstates what that paper proves. The $\kch(\mathbb C^3)=3/2$
and $\kcat(\mathbb C^3)=1$ shadows are unaffected. HZ verification is
satisfied by three independent arXiv abstracts plus the
Costello--Gwilliam vertex-algebra-from-factorisation-algebra theorem.
