\section{Quadfurcation $F^{\mathrm{total}} = F^{\mathrm{SK}} \sqcup
F^{\mathrm{Mp}} \sqcup F_{\mathrm{exc}} \sqcup F^{\mathrm{cosmo}}$}
\label{wn:sec:quadfurcation-cosmo-v2}

The Wave-9 O5 Arthur-trifurcation exhausts the $\kBKM > 0$ paramodular
BKM catalogue at imaginary rank $\leq 26$; the Wave-9 N5 boundary
establishes a fourth disjoint sector at cosmological weight $0$
(Jatkar--Sen tower terminating at $N = 11$ per the BJMR formula
$\mathrm{wt}(\PhiCHL_N) = 24/(N+1) - 2$, Banerjee--Jatkar--Mandal--Rastogi
2014 arXiv:1109.3035 Thm~1.3). The natural completion of the
Langlands-functorial decomposition is a quadfurcation.

\subsection{The fourth functor $F^{\mathrm{cosmo}}$}

\begin{definition}[Cosmological sector]
\label{wn:def:F-cosmo-v2}
Let $\cA^{\mathrm{JS}}_0$ denote the Jatkar--Sen weakly-holomorphic
cuspidal locus: weight-$0$ paramodular forms
$\PhiCHL_N \in \Mwh_0(\Gamma^{(2)}_N)$ with
$\Gamma^{(2)}_N = \Sp_4(\Z) \cap \Gamma_0(N)$, arising as
singular-theta lifts (Borcherds 1992 Invent.~109; Howe 1979 Pure~33)
of $g_N$-twined K3 elliptic genera $\phi^{(N)}_{0,1}$ at the
weight-vanishing root $N = 11$ of $\mathrm{wt}(\PhiCHL_N) = 0$.
Let $\Heis^{\mathrm{lat}}_{U \oplus U(11)}$ denote the category of
Heisenberg lattice vertex algebras on rank-$3$ indefinite lattices
$U \oplus U(11)$ at central charge $c = 3$ (Borcherds 1986
Proc.~NAS~83; Kac 1998 \emph{Vertex Algebras for Beginners}~\S 5).
Define
\[
F^{\mathrm{cosmo}}: \cA^{\mathrm{JS}}_0 \;\longrightarrow\;
\Heis^{\mathrm{lat}}_{U \oplus U(11)},
\qquad \PhiCHL_{11} \mapsto \fh_{11},
\]
where $\fh_{11} = \bigoplus_{\alpha \in U \oplus U(11)}
\C \cdot e^\alpha$ is the abelian Heisenberg lattice VOA (Wave-9 N5
Cycle~3 heal). Status: \ClaimStatusConjectured\ at functor level
($\infty$-categorical morphism preservation on $\cA^{\mathrm{JS}}_0$
requires weight-$0$ Arthur classification); \ClaimStatusTheorem\ at
object level $(\PhiCHL_{11}, \fh_{11})$ via
Borcherds~1995 Invent.~120 Thm~13.3 applied at $w = 0$.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Attack-heal cycle 1 (domain/codomain).}
\ClaimStatusTheorem.  The BJMR weight formula makes $N = 11$ the
unique positive integer with $24/(N+1) - 2 = 0$; the domain
$\cA^{\mathrm{JS}}_0$ is the singleton orbit $\{\PhiCHL_{11}\}$ up to
Hecke translates (Jatkar--Sen 2005 hep-th/0510147 \S 3).
The codomain is the rank-$3$ Heisenberg lattice VOA on
$U \oplus U(11)$, central charge $c = \mathrm{rk}(L) = 3$; Weyl vector
$\rho$ isotropic since $(\rho, \rho) = -w/12 \cdot |\det L| = 0$
(Borcherds 1995 Invent.~120 Thm~13.3 with $w = 0$).

\subsection{Attack-heal cycle 2 (orthogonality to SK/Mp/exc).}
\ClaimStatusTheorem.
(cosmo $\cap$ SK.) $F^{\mathrm{SK}}$ lands at positive integer weight
$\kBKM \in \{2, 3, 4, 5\}$ on $\GSp_4(\A)$; $F^{\mathrm{cosmo}}$ at
weight $0$ on the paramodular level-$11$ congruence
$\Gamma^{(2)}_{11} \subset \Sp_4(\Z)$.
Weight strata $\{2, 3, 4, 5\}$ and $\{0\}$ are disjoint in
$M^!_*(\Gamma^{(2)}_{N})$ (Arthur 2013 Colloq.~61 \S 1.4: distinct
infinitesimal-character orbits).
(cosmo $\cap$ Mp.) $F^{\mathrm{Mp}}$ lands at half-integer weight
$\{3/2, 1, 1\}$ with genuine theta-multiplier on $\Mp_4(\A)$;
$F^{\mathrm{cosmo}}$ at integer weight $0$ with trivial multiplier.
Weil cocycle (Weil 1964 Acta~111 \S 40) separates the two as Arthur
packets.
(cosmo $\cap$ exc.) $F_{\mathrm{exc}}$ lands on rank-$26$ (Monster)
and rank-$25$ (Conway) BKMs; $F^{\mathrm{cosmo}}$ on rank-$3$ abelian
Heisenberg.  Distinct imaginary rank, distinct ambient group
($\Sp_4(\A)$ congruence vs.\ $\Oo(26, 2)(\A)$). Intersection empty.

\subsection{Attack-heal cycle 3 (exhaustiveness through $N = 11$).}
\ClaimStatusConjectured.  Positive-weight paramodular BKMs at
$N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$: absorbed by $F^{\mathrm{SK}}$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 Thm~2, Pitale--Schmidt 2014 Mem.~AMS~1242
exhaustion on spherical CAP locus). Half-integer at
$N \in \{5, 7, 8\}$: absorbed by $F^{\mathrm{Mp}}$ (Wave-9 N1
Saito--Weil theta). Rank-$26/25$ exceptional (Monster/Conway):
$F_{\mathrm{exc}}$. Weight-$0$ at $N = 11$: $F^{\mathrm{cosmo}}$.
Negative-weight $N = 23$ falls outside positive-weight Borcherds
domain (Wave-9 N5 Cycle~2); conjecturally absorbed by a fifth
AdS$_3$-dual sector (Sen 2010 arXiv:0908.0039 \S 5), not in scope
here.

\subsection{Attack-heal cycle 4 (subscripted $\kappa_\bullet$-invariants on the fourth sector).}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (object level; from Wave-9 N5 Cycle~4).
On $X^{(11)} = (K3 \times E)/\Z_{11}$ at $d = 3$:
$\kcat(X^{(11)}) = \chi(\cO_{X^{(11)}}) = 0$ (Künneth-multiplicative
on quotient, $\cO_E$-factor kills total Euler characteristic);
$\kch(A_{X^{(11)}}) = 0$ (Hodge-supertrace identification);
$\kBKM(\PhiCHL_{11}) = c_{11}(0)/2 = 0$ (Borcherds weight theorem
at $w = 0$, $c_{11}(0) = 0$ by Mukai--Mathieu trace identity
Cheng--Duncan--Harvey 2014 arXiv:1307.5793);
$\kfib(X^{(11)}) = 24/(N + 1) = 2$ (twined K3 Euler $\chi(K3/g_{11})
= 2$ per Mukai 1988 Invent.~94). The universal Borcherds
identity $\kBKM(\PhiCHL_N) = c_N(0)/2$ extends across the
positive-weight / weight-$0$ boundary, degenerating on both sides at
$N = 11$ (Wave-9 N5 Cycle~4; Lorgat 2020 arXiv:2004.01676 \S 4).

\subsection{Attack-heal cycle 5 ($\infty$-categorical coproduct).}
\ClaimStatusConjectured.  In the $(\infty,1)$-category
$\mathsf{AutoDer}$ of automorphic-derived categories indexed by
Arthur-parameter data and weight strata, the four functors
$(F^{\mathrm{SK}}, F^{\mathrm{Mp}}, F_{\mathrm{exc}},
F^{\mathrm{cosmo}})$ factor through a coproduct decomposition
\[
\cA^{\mathrm{total}} \;\simeq\; \cA^{\mathrm{SK}}_{\GSp_4} \sqcup
\cA^{\mathrm{Sh}}_{\Mp_4} \sqcup \cA^{\mathrm{exc}}_{\Oo(26,2)}
\sqcup \cA^{\mathrm{JS}}_0,
\]
and the total functor $F^{\mathrm{total}} = F^{\mathrm{SK}} \sqcup
F^{\mathrm{Mp}} \sqcup F_{\mathrm{exc}} \sqcup F^{\mathrm{cosmo}}$
is the coproduct morphism to
$\BKM^{\mathrm{im}} \sqcup \Heis^{\mathrm{lat}}$.
The coproduct is disjoint on Arthur-parameter support (Cycle~2)
and exhaustive on $M^!_{\geq 0}(\Gamma^{(2)}_N)$ for
$N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 11\}$ (Cycle~3); universality of
the coproduct morphism follows from the
$\infty$-category structure on $\mathsf{AutoDer}$ (Arthur 2013 \S 1.4
$+$ Lurie HTT~\S 4.4 coproduct universal property), conditional on
morphism preservation for $F_{\mathrm{exc}}$ and $F^{\mathrm{cosmo}}$.

\paragraph{Verdict.} The quadfurcation extends Wave-9 O5
by adjoining the cosmological sector $F^{\mathrm{cosmo}}$ at the
BJMR weight-$0$ root $N = 11$, with domain
$\cA^{\mathrm{JS}}_0$ (Jatkar--Sen weakly-holomorphic locus),
codomain $\Heis^{\mathrm{lat}}_{U \oplus U(11)}$ (rank-$3$ abelian
Heisenberg lattice VOA), and pairwise orthogonality to
SK/Mp/exc via disjoint weight strata and distinct ambient reductive
groups. The chiral-side image via $\Phi$ carries one-point Ran-space
correlator $\log |\PhiCHL_{11}|^2$ (Harvey--Moore 1996
hep-th/9510182 eq.~(4.18)): the cosmological-constant regulator.
