\section{The Weight-Zero Jatkar-Sen Boundary}
\label{wn:sec:quadfurcation-cosmo}

The Jatkar-Sen genus-two CHL tower is indexed by
\[
  N^{\mathrm{JS}}\in\{1,2,3,5,7,11\}.
\]
Its Siegel modular form $\PhiCHL_N$ has weight
\[
  \mathrm{wt}(\PhiCHL_N)=\frac{24}{N+1}-2,
\]
so the weight sequence is
\[
  (10,6,4,2,1,0)_{N=1,2,3,5,7,11}.
\]
The equation $\frac{24}{N+1}-2=0$ has the unique positive solution
$N=11$. Thus $\PhiCHL_{11}$ is a weight-$0$ weakly holomorphic Siegel
modular function on the level-$11$ Jatkar-Sen boundary
$\Gamma^{(2)}_{11}\subset\Sp_4(\Z)$ (Jatkar-Sen 2005, \S 3;
David-Jatkar-Sen 2006, \S 2.2).

\subsection{Two Order-Indexed Towers}

Two different order laws appear under the symbol $\Phi_N$.

\begin{center}\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}
\begin{tabular}{c|c|c|c}
Tower & Order set & Weight or invariant & Source of the order \\
\hline
Jatkar-Sen & $\{1,2,3,5,7,11\}$ &
$\mathrm{wt}(\PhiCHL_N)=24/(N+1)-2$ &
Narain level matching \\
Primitive BKM/CHL denominator & $\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ &
$\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2=(5,4,3,2,1)$ &
K3-twined Jacobi input \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

For the primitive BKM/CHL denominator tower,
\[
  c_N(0)=(10,8,6,4,2)_{N=1,2,3,4,6},
  \qquad
  \kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2.
\]
In the primitive denominator normalization the Jatkar-Sen tower and the
primitive BKM/CHL denominator tower meet only at $N=1,2,3$; their
weight laws agree by squaring exactly at $N=1$:
\[
  \Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_5^2,\qquad
  D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5,\qquad
  c_1(0)=10,\qquad
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)=5.
\]
The K3 elliptic genus in this normalization is
$Z_{K3}=2\phi_{0,1}^{\mathrm{EZ}}$ with
$\phi_{0,1}^{\mathrm{EZ}}(\tau,z)=y^{-1}+10+y+O(q)$. The primitive
Borcherds denominator is $\Delta_5$; the full Igusa form
$\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}$ is the square-normalized object.

\subsection{The K3xE Scalar Package}

The compact $K3\times E$ row carries five named scalar slots and four
distinct numerical values:
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)=0,\qquad
  \kch^{\mathrm{compact}}(K3\times E)=0,\qquad
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)=3,
\]
\[
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)=5,\qquad
  \kfib(K3)=24.
\]
The first two values are the Kunneth multiplicative total-space
invariants
\[
  \chi(\mathcal O_{K3\times E})
  =
  \chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\chi(\mathcal O_E)
  =
  2\cdot0=0.
\]
The value $3$ is the Stage-$2$ Heisenberg reading, additive as
$2+1$ along the $K3$ and $E$ factors. The value $5$ is the
Borcherds weight of $\Delta_5$, equal to $c_1(0)/2=10/2$. The value
$24$ is the rank of the Mukai lattice of the K3 fiber. These four
numbers come from four constructions, not from one unsubscripted
scalar.

\subsection{The Gritsenko-Clery Catalogue}

The Gritsenko-Clery diagonal-divisor catalogue is a separate
triple-indexed list. Its eight rows have
\[
  \operatorname{wt}
  =
  (5,2,3,1,2,1/2,3/2,1)
\]
and Borcherds input constants
\[
  c_{\mathrm{dd}}(0)
  =
  (10,4,6,2,4,1,3,2).
\]
Thus row by row $\kBKM=c_{\mathrm{dd}}(0)/2$. The catalogue contains
the eight weights displayed above. The Jatkar-Sen order tower and the
primitive BKM/CHL denominator tower are separate indexed families; an
identification with the dd catalogue requires an explicit row map.

\subsection{The Ordinary Mathieu Table}

The ordinary Mathieu table has $26$ ATLAS conjugacy classes. It includes
the class $3B$; same-order classes retain their own ordinary twining
data and are not collapsed into the five primitive CHL denominator rows.
The frame-shape entries needed for the CHL boundary checks are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
[g] & \text{frame shape}\\
\hline
3B  & 3^8\\
4A  & 2^4 4^4\\
4B  & 1^4 2^2 4^4\\
6B  & 6^4\\
10A & 2^2 10^2\\
12A & 2^1 4^1 6^1 12^1\\
12B & 12^2
\end{array}
\]
The five primitive denominator rows are $1A,2A,3A,4B,6A$ with
$c_N(0)=(10,8,6,4,2)$. The remaining ordinary classes are Mathieu
twining classes.

\subsection{The Boundary Sector}

Let
\[
  \cA^{\mathrm{JS}}_0
  :=\{\PhiCHL_{11}\in\Mwh_0(\Gamma^{(2)}_{11})\}
\]
denote the weight-$0$ boundary locus of the Jatkar-Sen tower. The
notation $F^{\mathrm{cosmo}}$ denotes the boundary assignment of this
boundary locus to the scalar regulator
\[
  \PhiCHL_{11}\longmapsto \log |\PhiCHL_{11}|^2 .
\]
This datum is a weakly holomorphic modular function together with its
logarithmic regulator. A Borcherds denominator identity requires a
Jacobi input, a Weyl chamber, a generalized Kac-Moody algebra, and a
lattice $\VOA$. The primitive BKM denominator package has rows
$N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$; the level-$11$ Jatkar-Sen boundary belongs to the
weight-$0$ boundary sector.

The $K3\times E$ geometry has no smooth compact quotient
$(K3\times E)/\Z_{11}$. Thus $\kcat$, $\kch$, and $\kfib$ for a putative
geometric object $X^{(11)}$ await a separate derived CY$_3$ host. Its
specialized CY-to-chiral output has type $E_1$; an $E_2$ structure
belongs to a centre, double, or module category.

\subsection{Four Automorphic Sectors}

The positive-weight paramodular, genuine multiplier, exceptional
orthogonal, and weight-$0$ boundary data form a disjoint stratification:
\[
  F^{\mathrm{total}}
  =
  F^{\mathrm{SK}}
  \sqcup F^{\mathrm{Mp}}
  \sqcup F_{\mathrm{exc}}
  \sqcup F^{\mathrm{cosmo}} .
\]
This formula records disjoint labelled strata. A functorial coproduct
requires comparison functors for the metaplectic, exceptional, and
weight-$0$ boundary strata.

\begin{center}\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}
\begin{tabular}{c|c|c|c}
Sector & Ambient group or cover & Weight data & Output datum \\
\hline
$F^{\mathrm{SK}}$ &
$\GSp_4(\A)$ &
$\kBKM(\Phi_N)=(5,4,3,2,1)$ for $N=\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ &
primitive BKM denominator \\
$F^{\mathrm{Mp}}$ &
metaplectic cover of $\Sp_4(\A)$ &
genuine multiplier rows, including weights $1/2$ and $3/2$ &
metaplectic Borcherds products \\
$F_{\mathrm{exc}}$ &

\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The strata are separated by weight, multiplier, ambient group, and
denominator data. The $F^{\mathrm{SK}}$ row uses the primitive
BKM/CHL denominator constants. The $F^{\mathrm{cosmo}}$ row uses the
Jatkar-Sen weight law. The exceptional row lives on a separate
orthogonal Shimura datum.

\subsection{Cosmological Regulator}

The physics statement is heuristic. Harvey-Moore threshold corrections
place logarithms of automorphic forms in one-loop effective actions. On
the Jatkar-Sen boundary the retained scalar is
\[
  \log |\PhiCHL_{11}|^2 .
\]
It is read as a cosmological-constant regulator for the Narain
boundary. A theorem identifying this scalar with a chiral correlator
requires four additional constructions: a CY$_3$ or derived host,
its CY-to-chiral category, the protected scalar partition function, and
a comparison theorem between the chiral one-point function and the
Harvey-Moore logarithm.
