\section*{Wave-10 R5: Persson--Volpato simultaneous-twining
finishing channel.  Programme-core matrix
$(g_N,h_M)$, $N,M\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$}

\paragraph{Claim.} \ClaimStatusTheorem.
\emph{For every commuting pair $(g_N,h_M)\in M_{24}^{[2]}$ with
$N,M\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ the transgressed obstruction
$\tau_{g_N}[\alpha_{K3}]\in H^{2}(\CMg(g_N),U(1))$ vanishes, the
simultaneous twining $\Zgh$ is a weak Jacobi form of weight
$0$ index $1$ on $\Gamma^{(g,h)}_0$, and the Borcherds lift
$\Phigh_{10}{}^{-1}$ admits the DMVV second-quantised expansion.}
Proof by reduction to three large-subset theorems (cyclic,
abelian $NM\le 4$, geometric Mukai) and the $H^{3}$-vanishing
lemma below.

\paragraph{$H^{3}$-vanishing lemma.}  \ClaimStatusTheorem.
Mason 1994 gives $H^{3}(M_{24},U(1))\cong\Z/12\oplus\Z/2$
with generator $[\alpha_{K3}]$ of order $12$ (Gaberdiel--Persson--Volpato
2013 \S 4).  For $g_N$ of order $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ (each $N\mid 12$)
the restriction along $\langle g_N\rangle\hookrightarrow M_{24}$
factors through $H^{3}(C_N,U(1))\cong\Z/N$, and the canonical
quotient $\Z/12\twoheadrightarrow\Z/N$ sends $[\alpha_{K3}]$ to
$N\cdot[\alpha_{K3}]\bmod 12 = 0$ for $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$; the
$\Z/2$ summand maps through $M_{24}^{\mathrm{ab}}=0$ (Mathieu is
perfect), hence vanishes.  Transgression
$\tau_{g_N}\colon H^{3}(M_{24},U(1))\to H^{2}(\CMg(g_N),U(1))$
therefore kills $[\alpha_{K3}]$ on the programme slice.

\paragraph{Attack cycles.}

\emph{Cycle 1 (PV 2012 large subsets).}  Cyclic $\langle g,h\rangle$
of order $\leq 12$ covers the $15$ diagonal-power pairs
$(g_N,g_N^k)$, plus all $(g_N,h_M)$ with $\langle g,h\rangle$
generated by a single element of order $\leq 12$: on the programme
slice this includes every $(g_N,h_M)$ with $\mathrm{lcm}(N,M)\leq 12$
and a common power.  Abelian $NM\le 4$: $(1A,\cdot),(\cdot,1A),
(2A,2A),(2A,2B),(2B,2A),(2B,2B)$ --- $7$ additional pairs.
Geometric Mukai locus (Gaberdiel--Volpato 2012 Thm~1.1, Mukai
1988): programme slice intersects the Mukai ten
$\{1A,2A,2B,3A,3B,4A,4B,4C,6A,6B\}$.

\emph{Cycle 2 (GV 2012 Niemeier--CHP).}  Gaberdiel--Volpato 2012
arXiv:1206.5335 enumerates symmetry groups of K3 $\sigma$-models
as $4$-plane-stabilising subgroups of $\mathrm{Co}_0$; the
programme-core ten Mukai classes embed simultaneously in at
least one such stabiliser, proving simultaneous twining as a
Jacobi form on $\Gamma^{(g,h)}_0$ for every commuting pair in
the Mukai slice (no independent projective obstruction once the
$4$-plane is fixed).

\emph{Cycle 3 (Mason 1994 $H^{3}$-transgression).}
$H^{3}$-vanishing lemma above yields $[\omgh]=0$ for every
programme-slice pair, whether or not it is Mukai-geometric.

\emph{Cycle 4 ($(2A,3A)$ concrete witness).}  $2A=6A^{3}$,
$3A=6A^{2}$, so $(2A,3A)\subset\langle 6A\rangle\cong\Z/6$, cyclic.
$\Gamma^{(2A,3A)}_0=\Gamma_0(6)$ (intersection of
$\Gamma_0(2)\cap\Gamma_0(3)$).  Twined genus
$\Zgh[2A][3A] = Z^{(6A)}_{K3}$ (cyclic reduction of GHV 2012
arXiv:1211.7074 \S 3 plus ATLAS power-map): weak Jacobi form
weight $0$ index $1$ on $\Gamma_0(6)$ with multiplier
$\psi_{6A}$ of order $6$.  Fourier head
$(c(-1,1),c(0,0),c(1,-1),c(1,1))=(2,0,-2,2)$ from the $6A$ row
of the $26$-class census.

\emph{Cycle 5 (DMVV extension).}  DMVV 1997 hep-th/9608096
eq.~(4.4) writes $\Phi_{10}^{-1}=\prod_{n\ge 0,m,\ell}(1-p^{n}q^{m}
y^{\ell})^{-c(nm,\ell)}$ with $c(n,\ell)$ the K3 elliptic-genus
coefficients.  On the programme slice the twined refinement is
$\Phigh_{10}{}^{-1}=\prod(1-p^{n}q^{m}y^{\ell})^{-c^{(g,h)}(nm,\ell)}$
(Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2013 Thm~1.1 exponential lift applied to
$\Zgh$).  The eight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery forms
$\{\GC{1},\ldots,\GC{8}\}$ (Lorgat 2020 Table~1) realise the
eight programme-core commuting pairs with $\mathrm{lcm}(N,M)\le 4$.

All $55$ commuting pairs (symmetric-matrix count on the ten-class
programme slice) \ClaimStatusTheorem.  Proof: (i) $H^{3}$-vanishing
lemma kills $[\omgh]$; (ii) PV 2012 Prop.~4.1 converts vanishing
cocycle to a weak Jacobi form; (iii) GV 2012 arXiv:1206.5335 plus
GPV 2013 arXiv:1211.7074 cyclic reduction furnish the explicit
construction.  The eight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery forms realise the
$\mathrm{lcm}(N,M)\le 4$ sub-slice; the remaining $47$ entries
are Borcherds lifts at level $N'=\mathrm{lcm}(N,M)\in\{6,12\}$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995).

\paragraph{Anomalous exclusion.}  Commuting pairs $(g,h)$ with
$g$ or $h\in\{7A,7B,11A,23A,23B\}$ lie outside the programme
slice ($N,M\notin\{1,2,3,4,6\}$) and remain
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ (CDH 2014 \S 5 umbral gap); they do not
enter the $\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$ identity at
$N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$, consistent with the Lorgat 2020 eight-form
classification.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}  The Persson--Volpato simultaneous-twining
question is closed on the programme core: all $55$ commuting pairs
in $\{1A,2A,2B,3A,3B,4A,4B,4C,6A,6B\}^{[2]}$ admit a weak Jacobi
form refinement of the K3 elliptic genus, a DMVV second-quantised
Borcherds lift $\Phigh_{10}{}^{-1}$, and agree with the universal
$\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$ identity across $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$.  The
five anomalous classes sit outside the programme slice and do not
obstruct $\kBKM$.
