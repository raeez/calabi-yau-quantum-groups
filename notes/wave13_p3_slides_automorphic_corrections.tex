\section*{Automorphic Denominator of the $\Delta_5$ BKM Superalgebra}

The reflection system $W^{(2)}(\LamII)$ has real simple roots
$\delta_1,\delta_2,\delta_3$ with Gram matrix
\[
  \bigl((\delta_i,\delta_j)\bigr)=
  \begin{pmatrix}
  2 & -2 & -2\\
  -2 & 2 & -2\\
  -2 & -2 & 2
  \end{pmatrix}.
\]
The corresponding Lorentzian cone supplies the real simple roots
\[
  \Delta^{\mathrm{re}}
  =\mathcal P_{\mathrm{II},\mathrm{prim}}
  =\ZZ\delta_1\oplus\ZZ\delta_2\oplus\ZZ\delta_3.
\]
The Borcherds enlargement adds imaginary simple roots read from the
Fourier expansion of $\Delaf$.  With
\[
  a=(n-1)f_2-(l-1)\frac12f_3+(m-1)f_{-2}
  \in \frac12\LamII
\]
and Fourier coefficients $m(a)=-f(n,l,m)/64$, the imaginary part is
\[
  \Delta^{\mathrm{im}}_0
  =\{\tau(a)a:(a,a)=0,\ \tau(a)>0\},
  \qquad
  \Delta^{\mathrm{im}}_1
  =\{m(a)a:(a,a)<0,\ m(a)<0\}.
\]
The isotropic coefficient is fixed by the $\eta(z_1)^9$ factor in
$\psi_{5,1/2}$:
\[
  \tau(a)=9
\]
on the isotropic rays in
$\LamII\cap\RR_{>0}\mathcal P_{\mathrm{II}}$.  Equivalently, for an
isotropic primitive ray $a_0$,
\[
  1-\sum_{t\in\NN}m(ta_0)q^t
  =\prod_{t\in\NN}(1-q^t)^{\tau(ta_0)}.
\]

The full simple-root set is
\[
  \Delta=\Delta^{\mathrm{re}}\cup\Delta^{\mathrm{im}}.
\]
It satisfies $(\alpha,\alpha)>0$ on real roots,
$(\alpha,\alpha)\leq0$ on imaginary roots,
$(\alpha,\alpha')\leq0$ for distinct simple roots, and
$2(\alpha,\alpha')/(\alpha,\alpha)\in\ZZ$ whenever $\alpha$ is real.
The generalized BKM superalgebra $\gbkm$ is generated by
\[
  e_\alpha,\ h_\alpha,\ f_\alpha\qquad (\alpha\in\Delta),
\]
with the usual $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ relations along real roots,
orthogonality cancellations for $(\alpha,\alpha')=0$, and Serre
relations controlled by the real-root Cartan integers.  Its triangular
decomposition is
\[
  \gbkm=
  \left(\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\mathcal C_\Delta}\gbkm_\alpha\right)
  \oplus\LamII\oplus
  \left(\bigoplus_{\alpha\in-\mathcal C_\Delta}\gbkm_\alpha\right),
  \qquad
  \mathcal C_\Delta=\sum_{\alpha\in\Delta}\ZZ_{\geq0}\alpha.
\]
The supermultiplicity is
\[
  \mult_\alpha=\dim\gbkm_{\alpha,0}-\dim\gbkm_{\alpha,1}.
\]

The Weyl--Kac--Borcherds denominator is
\[
  \Phi(z)=
  \sum_{w\in W^{(2)}(\LamII)}\det(w)
  \left(
  e^{-2\pi i(w\rho,z)}
  -
  \sum_{a\in\LamII\cap\RR_{>0}\mathcal P_{\mathrm{II}}}
  m(a)e^{-2\pi i(w(\rho+a),z)}
  \right),
\]
and also
\[
  \Phi(z)=
  e^{-2\pi i(\rho,z)}
  \prod_{\alpha\in\Delta_+}
  \left(1-e^{-2\pi i(\alpha,z)}\right)^{\mult_\alpha}.
\]
The Borcherds--Gritsenko--Nikulin identity is
\[
  \frac{1}{64}\Delaf(2z)=\Phi(z).
\]

The multiplicities are read from the weak Jacobi form
$\phi_{0,1}=\phi_{12,1}/\delta_{12}$, where
\[
  \delta_{12}=q\prod_{n\geq1}(1-q^n)^{24}
\]
and
\[
  \phi_{0,1}
  =(r^{-1}+10+r)
  +q(10r^{-2}-64r^{-1}+108-64r+10r^2)+\cdots .
\]
The product formula
\[
  \frac{1}{64}\Delaf
  =e^{\pi i(z_1+z_2+z_3)}
  \prod_{\substack{n,l,m\in\ZZ\\(n,l,m)>0}}
  \left(1-e^{2\pi i(nz_1+lz_2+mz_3)}\right)^{f(nm,l)}
\]
identifies
\[
  \mult_\alpha=f(nm,l).
\]

For $N=1$, the Donaldson--Thomas partition function of $S\times E$ is
controlled by
\[
  Z^{S\times E}(q,t,p)=-\frac{C}{\Delaf^2},
\]
and the elliptic genus of $K3$ is $2\phi_{0,1}$.  The BKM weight is
\[
  \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Delaf)=\frac{c_1(0)}2=\frac{10}{2}=5.
\]
For $N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$ the CHL family is governed by the same rule
\[
  \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N)=\frac{c_N(0)}2,
\]
with CHL constants $(10,8,6,4,2)$ and weights $(5,4,3,2,1)$.
Twined elliptic genera and the extended Gritsenko--Clery catalogue
must be cited as separate inputs with their own row data.
