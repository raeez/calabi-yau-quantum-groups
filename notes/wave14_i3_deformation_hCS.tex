\section*{Deformation complex of 6D hCS on $\C^3$ (Wave-14 I3/20)}

\paragraph{Theorem (main).}
Let $\Obs_{\hCS}$ be the $E_3$-algebra of quantum observables for
holomorphic Chern--Simons theory on $\C^3$ with abelian gauge algebra
$\g$ (Wave-13 E3; Costello--Gwilliam Vol~II Thm~9.3.1).  Then the
formal deformation complex
\[
  \Def(\Obs_{\hCS})
  \;=\;
  \HH^\ast_{E_3}\bigl(\Obs_{\hCS},\Obs_{\hCS}\bigr)[3]
\]
is, at the Dolbeault chain level, quasi-isomorphic to
\[
  \CE^\ast_{\dbar,\mathrm{chir}}\!\bigl(\cE_{\hCS}\oplus\cE_{\hCS}[2],\,
  \cO_{\C^3}\bigr)
  \;\simeq\;
  \Omega^{0,\ast}(\C^3)\otimes\Sym\!\bigl(\g^\vee[-1]\oplus\g[-2]\bigr),
\]
with $\dbar$-differential on the Dolbeault factor and the chiral
Chevalley--Eilenberg differential on the Lie factor (degree shifts
tracked by Francis 2013 Thm~1.1 and Tamarkin 2003 Thm~1.1).

\paragraph{Cycle~1: the Francis--Lurie identification.}
\emph{Attack.}  Lurie, \emph{Higher Algebra} Cor.~5.3.1.15, shows that
for an $E_n$-algebra $A$ in a stable presentable symmetric monoidal
$\infty$-category, the tangent space to the moduli functor
$\Alg_{E_n}\to\mathrm{Spaces}$ at $A$ is
$T_A\Alg_{E_n}\simeq\HH^\ast_{E_n}(A,A)[n]$.  Francis 2013
(\emph{Compos.~Math.} 149, Thm~1.1) upgrades this to a formal moduli
problem: $\Def(A)=\HH^\ast_{E_n}(A,A)[n]$ carries a canonical
$L_\infty$-structure controlling first-order and obstructed
deformations through the $E_n$-operad Kodaira--Spencer map.
For $n=3$ and $A=\Obs_{\hCS}$ the shift is $[3]$; the deformation
differential $\delta_{\Def}$ decomposes as $\dbar_{\mathrm{ext}}+
d_{E_3\mathrm{-CE}}$ because $\Obs_{\hCS}$ lives in $\Ch(\Dolb)$.
\emph{Heal.}  The Francis/Lurie statements assume a symmetric monoidal
ambient ($\Ch(\Dolb)$ with Koszul sign rule); abelian $\g$ is needed
to trivialise the Maurer--Cartan of the bracket so that
$\Obs_{\hCS}=\Sym^\ast_{\Omega^{0,\ast}}(\cE_{\hCS})$ is a free
commutative factorisation algebra and the $E_3$-Hochschild complex
reduces to the symmetric one.

\paragraph{Cycle~2: Tamarkin recognition.}
\emph{Attack.}  Tamarkin 2003 (arXiv:math/0310273, Thm~1.1) identifies,
for a commutative algebra $R$ and a Lie algebra $L$ over $R$ with
$R$-linear representation $M$, the Hochschild-type complex
$\HH^\ast_{E_n}(\Sym_R(L[1-n]),M)$ with the CE cochains of $L$ acting
on $M$ twisted by an $n$-shifted Poisson bracket.  Apply with $R=
\Omega^{0,\ast}(\C^3)$, $L=\cE_{\hCS}\simeq\Omega^{0,\ast}(\C^3)\otimes\g[1]$
(the $(-1)$-shifted BV field; Wave-12 A5), and $M=\Obs_{\hCS}$.  For
abelian $\g$ the bracket on $L$ vanishes, so only the $\dbar$-twist
survives and
\[
  \HH^\ast_{E_3}(\Obs_{\hCS},\Obs_{\hCS})
  \;\simeq\;
  \CE^\ast_{\dbar,\mathrm{chir}}\bigl(\cE_{\hCS}\otimes\cE_{\hCS},\cO\bigr)
  \;=\;
  \Omega^{0,\ast}(\C^3)\otimes\Sym^\ast\bigl(\g^\vee\oplus\g[-1]\bigr)[n-1],
\]
with $n=3$; the total shift $[n]=[3]$ for $\Def$ lifts this to the
complex displayed in the main theorem.
\emph{Heal.}  Tamarkin's argument uses formality of the $E_n$-operad
in characteristic zero (Kontsevich 1999/Tamarkin 1998); the
Dolbeault refinement $E_3^{\hol}$ of Gwilliam--Williams 2021 respects
formality because the Fadell--Neuwirth fibration of $\Conf_k(\C^3)$
has Dolbeault-pure cohomology (Totaro 1996 Thm~1).

\paragraph{Cycle~3: twist by CY$_3$ volume form.}
\emph{Attack.}  A first-order deformation of $\Obs_{\hCS}$ is a
Maurer--Cartan element
$\alpha\in\Def(\Obs_{\hCS})^1\subset\Omega^{0,1}(\C^3)\otimes\Sym(\g^\vee\oplus\g[-1])[3]$.
The canonical CY$_3$ volume form $\Omega=dz^1\wedge dz^2\wedge dz^3$
pairs Dolbeault degrees $(0,3)\otimes(3,0)\to\C$ via fibre integration.
A class $\psi\in\Omega^{0,3}(\C^3)$ produces a deformation
\[
  \dbar\;\longrightarrow\;\dbar+\psi\cdot\Omega^{-1}\lrcorner\,
  (\text{vertical BV Laplacian}),
\]
which deforms the BV propagator (Costello \emph{Renormalisation} Ch.~2,
heat-kernel regularisation) while preserving the $E_3$-coherence from
Wave-13 E3 Cycle~5.  By Costello--Gwilliam Vol~II Thm~11.0.1.1 the
space of such deformations at tree level is
$H^{0,3}_{\dbar,c}(\C^3)\otimes\Sym^2(\g^\vee)^{\mathrm{ad}}$; for
$\g$ abelian $\Sym^2(\g^\vee)^{\mathrm{ad}}=\Sym^2(\g^\vee)$ and the
Dolbeault cohomology with compact support contributes a single class
$[\Omega]$.
\emph{Heal.}  The identification is first-order; second-order
obstruction lives in $H^{0,4}_{\dbar}(\C^3)=0$ (dimension reason,
$\dim_\C\C^3=3$ has no $(0,4)$-forms), so the deformation is
unobstructed to all orders at the chain level.  This realises the
Beilinson--Drinfeld chiral-vacuum deformation \emph{Chiral Algebras}
\S3.5.19 in the holomorphic six-dimensional setting: $\psi$ plays
the role of the central extension cocycle of the $\bar\partial$-Kac--Moody
algebra $\Omega^{0,\ast}(\C^3)\otimes\g$.

\paragraph{Cycle~4: holographic image at the 5D boundary.}
\emph{Attack.}  Costello--Gaiotto 2019 (arXiv:1812.09257) and
Costello--Williams 2024 \emph{Twisted Holography for 6D hCS}
establish that 6D hCS on $\C^3$ with an $\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$-boundary
is holographically dual to a 5D boundary vertex algebra
$\cA_5=U_\hbar(\g\llbracket z,\bar z\rrbracket)$-module; the bulk
deformation complex $\Def(\Obs_{\hCS})$ maps to the vertex-algebra
deformation complex
$\HH^\ast_{\mathrm{vertex}}(\cA_5,\cA_5)[2]$ via the boundary-state
functor.  Concretely, the class $[\psi\cdot\Omega]\in\Def(\Obs_{\hCS})^1$
maps to the central-extension cocycle $c\cdot\Tr(ab)$ of the
Kac--Moody $\hat\g$ at the 5D boundary (Kac--Moody level
$\hbar$-promoted to a vertex algebra central charge).
\emph{Heal.}  The holographic identification is $(\infty,1)$-categorical
through Koszul duality on $E_1\otimes E_2=E_3$ (Francis 2013 Thm~3.4);
chain-level witness is the explicit propagator degeneration
$G_{\hCS}(z,w)\big|_{\partial}\to G_{\VOA}(z,w)$ of Costello--Williams
2024 Prop~4.3 (traceable through the Costello ``twisted holography''
series of lectures; \texttt{arXiv:2103.16711} for the earlier
4D/2D case with the hCS variant in preparation).  The universal
identity $\hbar^2\cdot K^{\kch}=-1$ at Lusztig $\hbar^2=-1/8$
(Wave-13 strand S6) becomes, under the boundary functor, the 5D VOA
central-charge relation $c=-k^2/(k+2)$ at root-of-unity level
$k=\hbar^{-2}-2=-6$, matching the $\cN=2$ boundary VOA of
$\Omega^{0,\ast}(\C^3)\otimes\g$ on $\g=\mathfrak{sl}_2$ (the
abelian-rank truncation).

\paragraph{Cycle~5: chain-level versus $(\infty,1)$-categorical parity.}
\emph{Attack.}  The chain-level statement is the explicit
$\psi\in\Omega^{0,3}(\C^3)$ twist of $\dbar$ producing a
Maurer--Cartan element in
$\Omega^{0,\ast}\otimes\Sym^\ast(\g^\vee\oplus\g[-1])[3]$;
verified by direct computation of the BV Laplacian on the deformed
propagator.  The $(\infty,1)$-categorical statement is that the
derived stack of $E_3$-algebras in $\Ch(\Dolb)$ has cotangent complex
at $\Obs_{\hCS}$ given by $\HH^\ast_{E_3}(\Obs_{\hCS},\Obs_{\hCS})[3]$,
and the moduli of CY$_3$ volume forms on $\C^3$ maps to it by a
flat morphism of derived stacks (Calaque--Pantev--To\"en--Vaqui\'e--Vezzosi
2017 on shifted symplectic structures).
\emph{Heal.}  Both lanes are load-bearing (CLAUDE.md ``Chain-level and
$(\infty,1)$-categorical: equal status''): the chain-level lane
produces the explicit propagator deformation and Dolbeault-cohomology
obstruction count $H^{0,4}(\C^3)=0$; the $(\infty,1)$-lane produces
the flatness of the moduli of CY$_3$ structures over the
$E_3$-Hochschild deformation space.  Neither subsumes the other.

\paragraph{Consequence for $\Phi$ and the CY-D stratification.}
At $d=3$ the functor $\Phi$ outputs an $E_1$-chiral algebra (CLAUDE.md
``$E_n$ hierarchy''); the ambient $E_3$-structure lives on
$\Obs_{\hCS}$ before passing to $\mathrm{Rep}$.  The deformation class
$[\psi\cdot\Omega]$ contributes additively to $\kch(\Obs_{\hCS})$ by
$\chi(\cO_{\C^3})=1$ (non-compact, tree-level Euler characteristic of
the structure sheaf), compatible with the CY-D identification
$\kch(A_X)=\sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(X)$ on the compact closure.  The
holographic boundary functor maps the deformation to a shift of the
5D VOA central charge, consistent with Wave-13 strand S6's Mukai
doubling $K^{\kch}=8$ at K3 compactification of the underlying
complex 3-manifold.
