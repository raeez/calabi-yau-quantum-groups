\section*{Local $\P^2$ as three $\C^3$ charts: positive-half matching}
\label{sec:wave17-c2-local-p2-three-charts}

The Calabi--Yau threefold $K_{\P^2} = \Tot(\cO_{\P^2}(-3))$ is the
total space of the canonical bundle on $\P^2$. Its fan $\Sigma_{K_{\P^2}}$
has three $3$-dimensional cones, each isomorphic to the positive
octant $\Z_{\geq 0}^3$, meeting along $2$-dimensional walls. The
CY condition is the coplanarity of the three ray generators
$v_1 = (1,0,1)$, $v_2 = (0,1,1)$, $v_3 = (-1,-1,1)$ at height one.
Three questions arise from this presentation. Does each toric chart
produce a chart-level positive-half $Y^+(U_i)$? Do the charts glue
to yield a global positive-half on $K_{\P^2}$? And does the resulting
$Y^+(K_{\P^2})$ match the orbifold McKay CoHA of
$\C^3/\Z_3$ on the other side of Bridgeland--King--Reid?

\subsection*{Affine cover and overlaps}

Write $T = (\C^*)^3$ for the big torus. The three maximal cones
$\sigma_i \subset \Sigma_{K_{\P^2}}$ give affine opens
\[
 U_i \;=\; \Spec \C[\sigma_i^\vee \cap M]
 \;\simeq\; \C^3, \qquad i \in \{0, 1, 2\},
\]
with $M = \Z^3$ the character lattice. Each $U_i$ is a smooth toric
$\C^3$-chart. The pairwise overlaps are
\[
 U_i \cap U_j \;=\; \Spec \C[(\sigma_i \cap \sigma_j)^\vee \cap M]
 \;\simeq\; \C^* \times \C^2,
\]
since the wall $\sigma_i \cap \sigma_j$ is a $2$-dimensional cone
dualising to $\C^2 \times \C^*$. The triple overlap
$U_0 \cap U_1 \cap U_2 \simeq (\C^*)^2 \times \C$ tracks the
$\P^2$-degree zero mode of the zero section.
The transition on the overlap $U_0 \cap U_1$ is the monomial change
of variable fixing the CY volume form $\Omega = dx \wedge dy \wedge dz$
up to sign; on each $U_i$ the volume form is $\Omega_i = dx_i \wedge
dy_i \wedge dz_i$, and the transition Jacobian is unimodular on the
wall by the CY coplanarity.

\subsection*{McKay cover and orbifold quiver}

The $\Z_3$-action on $\C^3$ with weights $(1,1,1)$ mod $3$ produces
the orbifold $\C^3/\Z_3$, whose minimal crepant resolution is
$K_{\P^2}$ (Bridgeland--King--Reid $2001$, derived McKay).
The McKay quiver $Q_{K_{\P^2}}$ has
\[
 (Q_{K_{\P^2}})_0 = \{0, 1, 2\}, \qquad
 (Q_{K_{\P^2}})_1 = \{a_i^{(x)}, a_i^{(y)}, a_i^{(z)}
 \colon a_i^{(\bullet)} \colon i \to i+1 \mod 3\},
\]
three vertices for the three $\Z_3$-characters and nine arrows, three
from each vertex to the next. The Beasley--Plesser superpotential
\cite[Beasley--Plesser $2001$]{BeasleyPlesser2001} is the cubic
\[
 W_{K_{\P^2}}
 \;=\; \sum_{i \in \Z_3} \tr \!\bigl(\epsilon_{xyz}\, a_i^{(x)}
 a_{i+1}^{(y)} a_{i+2}^{(z)}\bigr),
\]
the $\Z_3$-equivariant lift of the $\C^3$ potential
$W_{\C^3} = \tr(XYZ - XZY)$ under orbifolding. The relations
$\partial_{a^{(x)}} W_{K_{\P^2}} = 0$ impose the toric commutativity
$[a^{(x)}, a^{(y)}] = 0$ on the universal cover $\C^3$; the $\Z_3$
quotient lifts them to the cyclic form.

\subsection*{Chart-level positive halves}

Each chart $U_i \simeq \C^3$ carries its own Schiffmann--Vasserot
data: the Jordan quiver with cubic potential and the critical CoHA
\[
 \CoHA(U_i) \;=\; \cH(Q_{\C^3}, W_{\C^3})
 \;\simeq\; Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)
\]
\cite[Schiffmann--Vasserot $2012$, arXiv:1202.2756]{SV2012}. On the
overlap $U_i \cap U_j \simeq \C^* \times \C^2$, the CoHA becomes
$\C[q^{\pm 1}] \otimes \cH(\C^2)$; the $\C^*$ factor contributes a
formal loop variable and the $\C^2$ factor is the Nakajima quiver
variety generator ring \cite[Nakajima $1999$]{Nakajima1999}. The
transition on the overlap is a mutation
\[
 \mu_{ij} \colon Y^+(U_i)|_{U_i \cap U_j} \;\longrightarrow\;
 Y^+(U_j)|_{U_i \cap U_j},
\]
exchanging the adjacent Jordan loop with the opposite orientation.
Mutation here is the Kontsevich--Soibelman wall-crossing automorphism
at the wall $\sigma_i \cap \sigma_j$; it acts on the shuffle algebra
presentation of $Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$ by exchanging the two choices
of positive root decomposition compatible with the chart's cone.

\subsection*{Gluing and the total positive half}

The \v{C}ech complex of the chart cover
\[
 \bigoplus_i Y^+(U_i)
 \;\xrightarrow{\;\mu_{01} - \mu_{02} + \mu_{12}\;}\;
 \bigoplus_{i < j} Y^+(U_i \cap U_j)
 \;\longrightarrow\;
 Y^+(U_0 \cap U_1 \cap U_2)
\]
has $0$-cohomology $Y^+(K_{\P^2})$, computed as the equaliser of the
three mutations. The claim is
\[
 Y^+(K_{\P^2}) \;\simeq\; \CoHA(Q_{K_{\P^2}}, W_{K_{\P^2}}).
\]
The right side is the critical CoHA of the McKay quiver with
Beasley--Plesser potential. The isomorphism is
Rapcak--Soibelman--Yang--Zhao $2018$ \cite[arXiv:1810.10402]{RSYZ}
specialised to $\C^3/\Z_3$: for every toric CY$_3$ without compact
$4$-cycles, the critical CoHA is the positive half of an affine
super Yangian $Y^+(\widehat{\fg}_Q)$, with Cartan given by the
character lattice of the orbifold group.
For $\C^3/\Z_3$ the character lattice is $\Z/3$, giving rank $2$
imaginary Cartan after restricting to traceless combinations and the
affine super Yangian becomes $Y^+(\widehat{\fsl}_3|\widehat{\fsl}_3)$.
The $\Z_3$-decomposition of the arrow representation produces the
two $\fsl_3$-blocks; the $|$ records the character grading rather
than a super-$\Z/2$ parity.

\subsection*{Character and $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$}

The Reineke/DT generating function of $Y^+(K_{\P^2})$ has stability
radius $R = 1/27$ (Kontsevich--Soibelman refined DT, computed from the
cubic potential's symmetry at the conifold point of the
$K_{\P^2}$-moduli). The positive-half character is
\[
 \mathrm{ch}\bigl(Y^+(K_{\P^2})\bigr)
 \;=\; \prod_{\alpha \in \Delta^{\mathrm{im},+}}
 \frac{1}{(1 - q^{h(\alpha)})^{m(\alpha)}}
\]
with imaginary multiplicities $m(\alpha)$ determined by the McKay
quiver Euler form; the first three terms give the expected
$1 + 3q + 9q^2 + \cdots$ (one generator per arrow class at
degree $1$, Nakajima-Grojnowski commutator closure at degree $2$).

The compact base-shadow $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(K_{\P^2}) = 3/2$ is
Theorem $\ref{thm:local-p2-shadow}$ of
\texttt{cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}: the non-compact CY$_3$
supertrace evaluates on the Dolbeault column of the base $\P^2$,
giving $\tfrac{1}{2}\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(\P^2) = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 3
= \tfrac{3}{2}$. The fractional $\tfrac{1}{2}$ records that
$K_{\P^2}$ is non-compact and its CoHA acts on a Fock space graded
by the fractional Kodaira dimension of $\cO_{\P^2}(-3)$.

\subsection*{Synthesis}

The three $\C^3$-charts $U_0, U_1, U_2$ each see $Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$;
the wall overlaps are controlled by KS mutations; the equaliser is
$Y^+(K_{\P^2})$, identified with the critical CoHA of the
$\C^3/\Z_3$ McKay quiver with Beasley--Plesser cubic. The
Bridgeland--King--Reid derived equivalence
$D^b(\Coh(K_{\P^2})) \simeq D^b(\C^3/\Z_3)$
transports the orbifold McKay presentation to the smooth crepant
resolution without loss of associative-algebra structure. The
Kapranov $1988$ exceptional collection $\{\cO, \cO(1), \cO(2)\}$
on $\P^2$ lifts to a three-vertex tilting set on $K_{\P^2}$ whose
Ext-quiver is $Q_{K_{\P^2}}$; the tilting bundle $T = \cO \oplus
\cO(1) \oplus \cO(2)$ has $\mathrm{End}(T)$ the path algebra with
Beasley--Plesser relations, giving a fourth route to the same
algebra. Positive-half matching is the statement that all three
presentations (toric chart cover, McKay orbifold, Kapranov tilt)
produce the same $Y^+$, and Reineke's stability radius $R = 1/27$
is the invariant that labels this equivalence class.
