\subsection{Borcherds Product}
\label{subsec:delta5-borcherds-product}

Let
\[
  Z=(\tau,z,\rho)\in\mathfrak H_2,\qquad
  q=e^{2\pi i\tau},\quad r=e^{2\pi iz},\quad s=e^{2\pi i\rho}.
\]
The K3 Jacobi input is the Eichler--Zagier generator
\[
  \phi_{0,1}^{K3}(\tau,z)=\sum_{n,l}f(n,l)q^nr^l
  =r^{-1}+10+r+O(q).
\]
Thus the discriminant-zero constant entering Borcherds' weight theorem
is
\[
  c_1(0)=f(0,0)=10.
\]
Borcherds' singular theta lift gives weight \(c_1(0)/2\).  Gritsenko
and Nikulin identify the resulting product with the weight-five Igusa
square root.  In the theta-leading convention
\([q^{1/2}r^{1/2}s^{1/2}]\Delta_5=64\), the monic product is
\[
  \Dfive(Z)
  =
  q^{1/2}r^{1/2}s^{1/2}
  \prod_{(n,l,m)>0}
  (1-q^nr^ls^m)^{f(nm,l)}
  =
  64^{-1}\Delta_5(Z).
\]
The denominator algebra uses the even denominator variable:
\[
  \operatorname{den}(\gDelta)
  =
  \Dfive(2Z)
  =
  64^{-1}\Delta_5(2Z).
\]
Therefore
\[
  \kBKM(\gDelta)
  =
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)
  =
  \operatorname{wt}(\Delta_5)
  =
  \frac{c_1(0)}2
  =
  5.
\]
This statement is automorphic and Lie-theoretic.  Compact Hall
correspondences, BPS operator products, Pfaffian orientations, Hopf
pairings, associators, and representation categories are extra source
data.

\subsection{Root Lattice, Weyl Chamber, and Character}
\label{subsec:delta5-root-lattice-weyl-character}

Inside the Gritsenko--Nikulin orthogonal model
\(\Lambda^{3,2}\subset\bigwedge^2\mathbb Z^4\), fix the primitive
hyperbolic sublattice
\[
  \Lambda^{2,1}
  =
  \mathbb Z f_2\oplus\mathbb Z f_3\oplus\mathbb Z f_{-2}.
\]
The type-II root sublattice \(\LII\subset\Lambda^{2,1}\) has real
simple roots
\[
  \delta_1=2f_2-f_3,\qquad
  \delta_2=2f_{-2}-f_3,\qquad
  \delta_3=f_3,
\]
and \(\LII=\mathbb Z\delta_1\oplus\mathbb Z\delta_2
\oplus\mathbb Z\delta_3\), with Gram matrix
\[
  ((\delta_i,\delta_j))_{i,j=1}^3
  =
  \begin{pmatrix}
    2 & -2 & -2\\
    -2 & 2 & -2\\
    -2 & -2 & 2
  \end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its signature is \((2,1)\).  The Weyl chamber is the cone
\[
  \cP_{\mathrm{II}}
  =
  \{x\in C(\LII)_+:(x,\delta_i)\leq0,\ i=1,2,3\},
\]
and the Weyl group \(\WII\) has simple reflections
\(s_{\delta_i}\).  The Weyl vector is the unique solution of
\((\rho_{\Delta},\delta_i)=-1\):
\[
  \rho_{\Delta}
  =
  \tfrac12(\delta_1+\delta_2+\delta_3)
  =
  f_2-\tfrac12 f_3+f_{-2}.
\]

The charge-to-root map is
\[
  \alpha(n,l,m)
  =
  2nf_2-lf_3+2mf_{-2}
  =
  n\delta_1+m\delta_2+(n+m-l)\delta_3,
\]
with character
\[
  q^nr^ls^m=\exp\!\bigl(-\pi i(\alpha(n,l,m),z_{\mathrm{ort}})\bigr).
\]
On the active support \(f(nm,l)\neq0\), the Gritsenko--Nikulin
denominator identity gives the signed root supermultiplicity
\[
  \operatorname{sdim}\gDelta_{\alpha(n,l,m)}
  =
  \dim\gDelta_{\alpha(n,l,m),\bar0}
  -
  \dim\gDelta_{\alpha(n,l,m),\bar1}
  =
  f(nm,l).
\]
Equivalently,
\[
  \operatorname{den}(\gDelta)
  =
  e^{-2\pi i(\rho_{\Delta},z_{\mathrm{ort}})}
  \prod_{\alpha\in\cR_+}
  \left(1-e^{-2\pi i(\alpha,z_{\mathrm{ort}})}\right)^{
    \operatorname{sdim}\gDelta_{\alpha}}
  =
  64^{-1}\Delta_5(2Z).
\]
The product determines signed superdimensions.  Ordinary even and odd
root-space dimensions require a parity fixture or an explicit
finite-height presentation of \(\gDelta\).

The Maass character of the square root is denoted \(\nu_{\Delta_5}\).
On the three type-II simple reflections,
\[
  \nu_{\Delta_5}(s_{\delta_i})=-1,\qquad i=1,2,3.
\]
The chamber-permuting \(S_3=\operatorname{Aut}(\cP_{\mathrm{II}})\)
has automorphic value \(+1\) on the standard Gritsenko--Nikulin
extension.  A compact Hall orientation must supply an orientation
character \(\epsilon_o\) and prove
\[
  \epsilon_o|_{\WII}=\nu_{\Delta_5}|_{\WII}.
\]
The constants \(64\), \(4096\), and the Oberdieck--Pandharipande sign
carry no reflection-character datum.

\subsection{Square Normalisation}
\label{subsec:delta5-square-normalisation}

The scalar Igusa form belongs to the squared branch.  In the
unnormalised theta convention,
\[
  \PhiTen^{\mathrm{un}}(Z)=\Delta_5(Z)^2.
\]
In the Oberdieck--Pandharipande monic convention,
\[
  \PhiTen^{\mathrm{OP}}(Z)
  =
  \Dfive(Z)^2
  =
  4096^{-1}\Delta_5(Z)^2.
\]
The primitive reduced \(K3\times E\) scalar series is
\[
  Z^{K3\times E}_{\mathrm{OP/DT}}(Z)
  =
  -(\PhiTen^{\mathrm{OP}}(Z))^{-1}
  =
  -\Dfive(Z)^{-2}
  =
  -4096\,\Delta_5(Z)^{-2}.
\]
Thus \(\Delta_5^{-2}\) is a squared scalar convention.  A positive
Hall--BKM half carries one denominator copy.  A full scalar character
requires both halves, the Hopf pairing, the trace convention, and the
normalisation above.

\subsection{The Five \texorpdfstring{$K3\times E$}{K3 x E} Invariants}
\label{subsec:k3e-five-invariants-delta5}

Let \(X=K3\times E\).  The compact total-space values are
\[
  \kcat(X)
  =
  \chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\chi(\mathcal O_E)
  =
  2\cdot0
  =
  0,
\]
and
\[
  \kch(X)
  =
  \sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(X)
  =
  1-1+1-1
  =
  0.
\]
The Heisenberg--Mukai specialisation, the Borcherds denominator, and
the K3 fibre lattice give
\[
  \kHeis(X)=3,\qquad
  \kBKM(\gDelta)=5,\qquad
  \kfib(X)=\operatorname{rk}\widetilde H(K3,\mathbb Z)=24.

The separated invariant list is
\[
  \bigl(
  \kcat,\,
  \kch,\,
  \kHeis,\,
  \kBKM,\,
  \kfib
  \bigr)(K3\times E)
  =
  (0,0,3,5,24).
\]
The value \(2=\chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\) is a fibre witness.  The
total-space invariant is \(\kcat(K3\times E)=0\).  The Borcherds
weight is read from \(c_1(0)/2\), independently of chiral Hodge
supertraces and fibre Euler characteristics.

\subsection{The Compact Hall Source Package}
\label{subsec:compact-hall-source-package-hdelta}

The notation \(\HDelta\) denotes an inverse-limit compact
Hall--Drinfeld source only after the following finite-height data have
been supplied.  Fix a stability chamber \(\sigma\) and a
downward-saturated finite Borcherds window \(W_H\subset\cR_+\).  The
positive Hall window is
\[
  \cH^{+,\le H}_{X,\sigma}
  =
  H^{\BM,\mathrm{red}}_\bullet
  \bigl(\cM^{\le H}_{X,\sigma},\varphi_{\mathrm{vir}}\bigr),
\]
graded by \(\alpha\in W_H\).  Its product is the Hall product induced
by Hecke correspondences
\[
  \cM_{\alpha,\sigma}\times\cM_{\beta,\sigma}
  \xleftarrow{\ p_-\ }
  \Hecke_{\alpha,\beta}
  \xrightarrow{\ p_+\ }
  \cM_{\alpha+\beta,\sigma},
\]
namely
\[
  m_{\alpha,\beta}
  =
  (p_+)_*
  \bigl(p_-^*(-\boxtimes-)\cap[\Hecke_{\alpha,\beta}]_{\mathrm{red}}\bigr).
\]
This is the Hall functor
\[
  \mathsf{Hall}^{\le H}_{X,\sigma}\colon
  \mathsf{Corr}^{\Hecke}_{X,\sigma,W_H}
  \longrightarrow
  \SVect,
  \qquad
  \cM_{\alpha,\sigma}\longmapsto
  H^{\BM,\mathrm{red}}_\bullet(\cM_{\alpha,\sigma},\varphi_{\mathrm{vir}}).
\]

The BKM target functor is
\[
  \mathsf{BKM}^{\le H}_{\Delta_5}\colon
  W_H\longrightarrow\SVect,
  \qquad
  \alpha\longmapsto\gDelta_{\alpha},
\]
equipped with the Borcherds--Serre bracket, parity fixture, PBW
filtration, and signed character
\(\operatorname{sdim}\gDelta_{\alpha}=f(nm,l)\) for
\(\alpha=\alpha(n,l,m)\) on the active support.  A positive comparison
is a natural transformation after primitive projection:
\[
  \Psi_H^+\colon
  \operatorname{gr}\Prim(\cH^{+,\le H}_{X,\sigma})
  \longrightarrow
  \bigoplus_{\alpha\in W_H}\gDelta_{\alpha}.
\]
It must preserve the Lorentzian charge \(\alpha\), signed Euler
character, parity, the associated graded Hall commutator, the real
Serre relations, the orthogonal imaginary super-commutativity
relations, and the no-extra primitive relation condition.

The negative half is the continuous Serre-dual Hall dual
\[
  \cH^{-,\le H}_{X,\sigma}
  =
  \left(\cH^{+,\le H}_{X,\sigma^\vee}\right)^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}},
\]
and the Cartan part is the completed group algebra of \(\LII\) modulo
the Borcherds Cartan radical.  A finite compact Hall--Drinfeld double
is then
\[
  D^{\le H}_\hbar(\cH^+_{X,\sigma})
  =
  D_\hbar
  \bigl(
    \overline{\cH}^{+,\le H}_{X,\sigma},\,
    \overline{\cH}^{-,\le H}_{X,\sigma},\,
    \mathbb C[\LII]^{\le H}
  \bigr),
\]
formed with a nondegenerate finite Euler--Hall pairing.  A full source
package also contains:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{coproduct} &
  \text{a continuous Hall coproduct compatible with the finite windows;}\\
\text{orientation} &
  \text{a Pfaffian orientation with }
  \epsilon_o|_{\WII}=\nu_{\Delta_5}|_{\WII};\\
\text{completion} &
  \text{Mittag--Leffler exact transition maps over the windows;}\\
\text{associator} &
  \text{a Siegel--Borcherds associator satisfying the pentagon;}\\
\text{braiding} &
  \text{an \(R\)-matrix satisfying the hexagon in the chosen module category;}\\
\text{comparison} &
  \text{maps \(\Theta_H:D^{\le H}_\hbar(\cH^+_{X,\sigma})
  \to U(\gDelta)^{\le H}\) preserving PBW, pairing, coproduct,
  centre, and radical.}
\end{array}
\]
The representation category is the exact inverse limit
\[
  \Rep^{\Eone}(\HDelta)
  =
  \varprojlim_H
  \Rep^{\Eone}\!\left(D^{\le H}_\hbar(\cH^+_{X,\sigma})\right),
\]
with restriction functors induced by \(W_{H+1}\to W_H\).  Associator
and braiding data act on this category after the displayed pentagon and
hexagon identities are part of the source package.

\subsection{Recognition Statement}
\label{subsec:hdelta5-recognition-statement}

Assume the compact Hall source package of
Subsection~\ref{subsec:compact-hall-source-package-hdelta}.  Assume
that the finite comparison maps \(\Theta_H\) preserve
\(\nu_{\Delta_5}\), identify the primitive signed root characters with
the coefficients \(f(nm,l)\), identify the Borcherds--Serre ideal, and
pass to an exact inverse limit.  Then
\[
  \Prim(\HDelta)|_{\hbar=0}
  \cong
  \gDelta
\]
as a \(\LII\)-graded BKM Lie superalgebra, and
\[
  \operatorname{den}(\Prim(\HDelta)|_{\hbar=0})
  =
  \Dfive(2Z),
  \qquad
  \kBKM(\Prim(\HDelta)|_{\hbar=0})=5.
\]
The full scalar character satisfies
\[
  \operatorname{ch}_{\mathrm{full}}(\HDelta)
  =
  -\Dfive^{-2}
  =
  -4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}
\]
precisely in the Oberdieck--Pandharipande square convention and after
the trace normalisation is fixed.  The denominator product alone gives
the automorphic target.  The Hall package gives the algebra.

\subsection{Adjacent Branches}
\label{subsec:adjacent-branches-hdelta}

The Hall--Drinfeld/BKM branch is controlled by
\[
  (\LII,\ \cP_{\mathrm{II}},\ \rho_{\Delta},\
   \nu_{\Delta_5},\ \Delta_5,\ \Psi_H^+,\ \Theta_H),
\]
with signed root supermultiplicities read from \(\phi_{0,1}^{K3}\).
The Yangian/Mukai branch is controlled by the Mukai lattice
\(\widetilde H(K3,\mathbb Z)\), the Mukai pairing of signature
\((4,20)\), and the abelian-at-Lie Heisenberg specialisation.  A map
between the two branches must be a specified comparison functor.

The flat local model has the same half/full distinction:
\[
  \CoHA(\mathbb C^3)=Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1).
\]
The full \(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}\) object appears after taking the
Drinfeld double, centre, completion, or evaluation appropriate to the
chosen category:
\[
  D(Y^+)\simeq\mathcal W_{1+\infty}
\]
in the standard completed \(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1\) setting.  The
\(K3\times E\) analogue starts from a compact Hall positive half and
reaches a full object through the finite Hall--Drinfeld double and the
recognition maps above.

The operadic level is fixed by dimension.  For the Calabi--Yau
threefold \(K3\times E\), the native specialised output of
\[
  \Phi_3
  =
  \mathrm{Sp}_{\Sigma_2,C}\circ\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3
\]
is \(\Eone\)-chiral.  An \(\Etwo\)-structure occurs on centre, double,
or module layers, for example
\[
  \cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}
  \bigl(\Rep^{\Eone}(\HDelta)\bigr),
\]
after those layers have been constructed.
