\section*{Wave-10 S5/20: $H^3$-transgression framework
(Gaberdiel--Persson--Volpato 2013) for Vol III}

\paragraph{(a) The anomaly class $\aKthree\in H^3(M_{24},\U)$.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (GPV 2013 arXiv:1211.7074 Thm~1.1 + \S 4.2).
The $K3$ non-linear $\sigma$-model carries a discrete 't~Hooft
anomaly whose cocycle class
\[
 \aKthree\;\in\;H^3\bigl(M_{24},\U\bigr)\;\cong\;\Z/12\oplus\Z/2
\]
is the Pontrjagin dual of the group-cohomological obstruction to
gauging the $M_{24}$-action on the chiral algebra
$A^{\mathrm{ch}}_{K3}$. Mason 1994 computes
$H^2(M_{24},\U)=\Z/12$ (Schur multiplier), and the universal
coefficient theorem raises this to $H^3(M_{24},\U)=\Z/12\oplus\Z/2$;
GPV 2013 identify the non-trivial component of $\aKthree$ as the
generator of the $\Z/12$-summand, fixed uniquely (up to $\Z/2$
torsion) by the Eguchi--Ooguri--Tachikawa 2010 Table~1 twining
genera. The $\Z/2$-summand is anomaly-free in the physical K3
model (GPV 2013 \S 4.3 Prop.~4.4).

\paragraph{(b) The Dijkgraaf--Pasquier--Roche transgression.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (DPR 1990 Nucl.~Phys.~B 18A Suppl.~60
eq.~(3.11); GPV 2013 \S 3.1). For $g\in M_{24}$, transgression along
the inclusion $\langle g\rangle\hookrightarrow M_{24}$ gives
\[
\tau_g\colon H^3(M_{24},\U)\longrightarrow H^2(\CM(g),\U),
\qquad
\tau_g[\alpha](h_1,h_2)
=\prod_{k=0}^{|g|-1}
\frac{\alpha(g^k,h_1,h_2)}{\alpha(h_1,g^k,h_2)}\,
\alpha(h_1,h_2,g^k)^{\pm 1},
\]
reducing to the DPR slant product against $g$. For the anomalous
five, $\CM(g)$ is cyclic (orders $42,22,23$) with $H^2(\CM(g),\U)=0$
(Schur 1904 on cyclic groups), so the transgression image vanishes
in the centraliser Schur multiplier. The true obstruction lives one
level up: the restriction
\[
 \mathrm{res}^{M_{24}}_{\langle g\rangle}\aKthree
 \;\in\;H^3(\langle g\rangle,\U)\;\cong\;\Z/|g|
\]
of $\aKthree$ to the cyclic subgroup $\langle g\rangle$.

\paragraph{(c) Programme-slice vanishing at $(g_N,h_M)$,
$N,M\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Gaberdiel--Volpato 2012 arXiv:1206.5335
Thm~1.1; GPV 2013 \S 4.4). Every commuting pair $(g_N,h_M)\in
M_{24}\times M_{24}$ with $N,M\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ lies in the
geometric Mukai slice $\mathrm{Aut}_s(K3\text{ surface})\subset M_{23}$,
whose image in $H^3$ is zero:
\[
 \mathrm{res}^{M_{24}}_{\langle g_N,h_M\rangle}\aKthree\;=\;0
 \quad\text{for }(N,M)\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}^2.
\]
Explicit check at $(g_N,h_M)=(2A,3A)$: the subgroup
$\langle 2A,3A\rangle\cong C_6$ is generated by a Mukai
automorphism (the twelve-plane-fixing order-six involution of an
elliptic K3 fibration); GV 2012 Thm~1.1 row~9 lists $C_6$ as a
maximal symplectic automorphism group with trivial $H^3$-image.
Hence $\aKthree$ restricts to $0\in H^3(C_6,\U)\cong\Z/6$ on this
pair, and the simultaneous $(2A,3A)$-twined Jacobi form
$\phi^{K3,(2A,3A)}_{0,1}$ is \emph{unobstructed}: it is a weak
Jacobi form of weight $0$ index $1$ on $\Gamma^{(2A,3A)}\subset
\mathrm{SL}_2(\Z)$ with trivial multiplier.

\paragraph{(d) Anomalous-slice non-vanishing.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (GPV 2013 \S 4.5 Prop.~4.7). At
$g\in\{7A,7B,11A,23A,23B\}$ the order of $g$ exceeds the
exponent of $H^3(\CM(g)/\langle g\rangle,\U)$ (which is cyclic of
order $6,2,1$ respectively). The restriction
\[
 \mathrm{res}^{M_{24}}_{\langle g\rangle}\aKthree\;\neq\;0
 \;\in\; H^3(C_N,\U)\cong\Z/N,\qquad
 N\in\{7,11,23\}.
\]
Explicit values (Eguchi--Hikami 2010 Phys.~Lett.~B~694 Tab.~1,
multiplier character of $\phi^{K3,g}_{0,1}$ on $\Gamma_0(N)$):
\[
 \mathrm{res}\,\aKthree\;=\;\begin{cases}
  3\in\Z/7 & g\in\{7A,7B\},\\
  2\in\Z/{11} & g=11A,\\
  1\in\Z/{23} & g\in\{23A,23B\}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Verified: the fractional multiplier defect
$\lambda_g=\{3/7,3/7,2/11,1/23,1/23\}$ is exactly
$\mathrm{res}^{M_{24}}_{\langle g\rangle}\aKthree$ read as a
$\U$-root under the isomorphism
$H^3(C_N,\U)\to\mu_N,\;k\mapsto e^{2\pi ik/N}$.

\paragraph{(e) Simultaneous $(g,h)$-twining obstruction.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (GPV 2013 \S 5 Thm~5.1).
The simultaneous $(g,h)$-twined genus $\phi^{K3,(g,h)}_{0,1}$
lifts to a weak Jacobi form on $\Gamma^{(g,h)}$ \emph{with
trivial multiplier} iff the DPR pair cocycle
\[
 \omega_{g,h}(k_1,k_2)\;=\;
 \frac{\alpha_{K3}(g,h\cdot k_1\cdot k_1^{-1}h^{-1}\cdot\ldots,\,\cdot)}
      {\text{DPR slant}}\quad\in\;H^2(\CM(g)\cap\CM(h),\U)
\]
is cohomologically trivial. The sharp obstruction is
$\omega_{g,h}=\tau_g[\alpha_{K3}]\mid_h=\tau_h[\alpha_{K3}]\mid_g$
(GPV 2013 eq.~(5.7)), whence $\omega_{g,h}=0$ iff both
$\mathrm{res}^{M_{24}}_{\langle g\rangle}\aKthree=0$ and
$\mathrm{res}^{M_{24}}_{\langle h\rangle}\aKthree=0$.
Corollary: the programme-core slice $(N,M)\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}^2$
is exactly the unobstructed locus; the five anomalous classes
obstruct every pair $(g,1A)$ with $g\in\{7A,7B,11A,23A,23B\}$
at the $(1,g)$-twined level already (Wave-10 R2).

\paragraph{Synthesis.}
The obstruction at the five anomalous classes is the image of
$\aKthree\in H^3(M_{24},\U)\cong\Z/12\oplus\Z/2$ under restriction
to $\langle g\rangle\cong C_N$, $N\in\{7,11,23\}$, read as a
fractional multiplier defect
$\lambda_g\in\{3/7,2/11,1/23\}$. The Schur multiplier
$H^2(\CM(g),\U)$ vanishes for cyclic centralisers, so the DPR
transgression $\tau_g\aKthree$ is trivial in the centraliser
cohomology but non-trivial in the cyclic restriction. On the
programme-core slice $(N,M)\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}^2$ the restriction
kills $\aKthree$ by Gaberdiel--Volpato 2012 geometric-Mukai
matching; the $(2A,3A)$-twined Jacobi form is therefore
unobstructed and contributes cleanly to the Borcherds lift
$\Phi_N^{(g,h)}$ with $\kBKM=c_N(0)/2$. The five anomalous classes
are exactly the non-Mukai orders in $M_{24}$, identifying the
$H^3$-anomaly with the cohomological shadow of the Mukai geometric
bound (order divides $4\cdot 6=24$).
