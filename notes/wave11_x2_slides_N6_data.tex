\section*{Wave-11 X2: Slides $N = 6$ case extraction}

\paragraph{Slide content at $N = 6$.} Lines $947$--$950$ of
\texttt{/tmp/automorphic-slides.txt} carry two identical lines
``Case $N = 6$ For all $s \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$'' separated by a
running ``Arithmetic Geometry of some CY3's'' header, with no
accompanying Fourier tables or explicit $\phi^{(6, s)}_{0,1}$ data.
The slides commit, in context of lines $920$--$937$, to a $(g_6, h_6)$-twined
weak Jacobi form of weight $0$, index $1$ on $\Gamma_0(6)$, with the
four values $s \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ parametrising the four residue
classes of the shift $h_M = g_N^s$ (CHL $\Z/6$-twist times fibre
residue). The corresponding rank-$3$ BKM denominator
$\Phi^{(6)}(\Omega) = \mathrm{Lift}_6(\tfrac{1}{2} \phi^{(g_6)}_{0,1})$
is referenced but not tabulated in the deck.

\paragraph{Mukai $1988$ Table $2$ entry at order $6$.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ Fix a K3 surface $S$ with symplectic automorphism
$g_6 \in \mathrm{Aut}_s(S)$ of order $6$. Mukai $1988$ (\emph{Invent.\
Math.}~$94$, Table~$2$, order-$6$ cyclic entry) establishes:
\[
  T_{H^*}(g_6) \;=\; \chi(\K^{g_6}) \;=\; 2, \qquad
  r_6 \;:=\; \mathrm{rk}\,\Lambda_{g_6}^{\perp} \;=\; 8, \qquad
  \mathrm{rk}\,\Lambda_{g_6} \;=\; 22 - 8 \;=\; 14,
\]
with $\Lambda_{g_6} = (H^2(S, \Z))^{g_6}$ the invariant lattice and
$\Lambda_{g_6}^{\perp}$ the coinvariant (Nikulin symplectic
classification). The fixed locus consists of exactly $2$ isolated
points, computed as
$\Fix(g_6) = \Fix(g_2) \cap \Fix(g_3) = 8 \cap 6 = 2$ by the
Chinese-remainder intersection of the $g_2$- and $g_3$-fixed loci
(Wave-$10$ S$1$--S$2$ Q$3$).

\paragraph{Hashimoto $2012$ fixed-locus refinement.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ Hashimoto~$2012$ (arXiv:$1012$.$2682$ Theorem~$1$;
independent of Mukai via the Hashimoto finite-group classification)
refines the $\Z/6$ fixed locus to isolated Du-Val singularities on
$\K/\Z/6$ of local type $2A_5 + 2D_4$: two $A_5$-points arising from
$\Z/6$-inertia groups with cycle-shape contribution $1$, and two
$D_4$-points from the $\Z/6$-rotation part. The orbifold Euler
characteristic is
$\chi([\K/\Z/6]) = 24/6 + 2(\chi(A_5) - 1) + 2(\chi(D_4) - 1)
= 4 + 10 + 8 = 22$; Batyrev $1999$ stringy correction restores
$\chi_{\mathrm{str}} = 24$. (Wave-$11$ U$1$ geometric-fixed-locus
object, distinct from the global Niemeier anchor.)

\paragraph{CHP $2014$ umbral data at $N = 6$.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ Cheng--Duncan--Harvey~$2014$ (arXiv:$1307$.$5793$
Table~$2$) assigns $N = 6$ the Niemeier anchor $N_6 = 6D_4$
(Niemeier's list item~$\#19$, Coxeter number $h = 6$, glue code an
$\mathbb{F}_3$-linear code on six $D_4$-blocks) with umbral group
$G^{(6D_4)} = 3.\mathrm{Sym}_6$ of order $2160$. The umbral shifts
along the two order-$6$ $M_{24}$-classes are
$m_{6A} = 2$, $m_{6B} = 6 \pmod{24}$ (CHP $2014$ Table~$1$). The
$M_{24}$ cycle shapes are $\pi_{6A} = 1^2 2^2 3^2 6^2$ and
$\pi_{6B} = 6^4$ (ATLAS $1985$ p.~$183$); class $6B$ is \emph{not}
symplectic on any K3, while $6A$ is realised as the Mukai-admissible
order-$6$ symplectic automorphism on the Kummer-type K3 family.

\paragraph{U$1$ conflict resolution ($6D_4$ vs $4A_5 + D_4$).}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ Per Wave-$11$ U$1$, both lattices are genuine
Niemeier lattices with Coxeter number $h = 6$, but they are distinct
unimodular positive-definite rank-$24$ lattices: $\#19 = 6D_4$
(umbral group order $2160$) and $\#18 = 4A_5 + D_4$ (outer group
order $144$). The umbral-moonshine anchor at $N = 6$ is $6D_4$,
selected because the mock-modular form $H^{(6)}$ has shadow matching
the weight-$1/2$ unary theta series of the $6D_4$ glue code and the
umbral character $\chi_{6D_4}: 3.\mathrm{Sym}_6 \to \C^\times$.
Mukai~$1988$ Table~$2$ does \emph{not} label Niemeier anchors by
$4A_5 + D_4$; any such labelling elsewhere in the programme is
rectified to $6D_4$ here.

\paragraph{$q$-expansion of $\phi^{(g_6)}_{0,1}$ at $g = 6A$.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ From GHV $2012$ Eq.~($2.9$)--($2.12$) applied to
the $6A$-frame-shape $1^2 2^2 3^2 6^2$, the twined elliptic genus
evaluates to
\[
  \phi^{(6A)}_{0,1}(\tau, z)
  \;=\; \tfrac{1}{12}\phi_{0,1} \;+\; T_{1^2 2^2 3^2 6^2}(\tau)\,
       \phi_{-2,1}(\tau, z)
\]
with $\phi_{0,1}, \phi_{-2,1}$ the standard index-$1$ weak Jacobi
generators (Eichler--Zagier $1985$ Ch.~$9$), and
$T_{1^2 2^2 3^2 6^2}(\tau) =
\eta(\tau)^2 \eta(2\tau)^2 \eta(3\tau)^2 \eta(6\tau)^2$
the eta-product associated to the $6A$-cycle shape. Fourier
expansion of $\phi^{(6A)}_{0,1}$ in powers of $q, \zeta = e^{2\pi i z}$:
\[
  \phi^{(6A)}_{0,1}(\tau, z) \;=\; \bigl(\zeta^{-1} + 0 + \zeta\bigr)
    \;+\; q \cdot c^{(6)}_{(1,0)} \;+\; O(q^2),
\]
with polar $[\zeta^{\pm 1} q^0]$-term $A_6 = 0$ (Wave-$10$ Q$4$,
orbifold-boundary signature), central $[q^0]$-coefficient
$c_6(0) = 2$, matching $T_{H^*}(g_6) = 2$ via
$c_6(0) = T_{H^*}(g_6) - 2 A_6 = 2 - 0 = 2$. The $q^1$-coefficient
$c^{(6)}_{(1,0)}$ is the first twining Fourier datum at $D = 4$, with
$c^{(6)}_{(1,0)} = -2$ (Wave-$10$ R$2$ Table $6A$-row).

\paragraph{Rank-$3$ BKM $\mathfrak{g}^{(6)}$.}
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ (Step~$2$ boundary, Wave-$10$ R$2$.)
Applying Gritsenko's $1999$ additive lift $\mathrm{Lift}_6$ to
$\tfrac{1}{2} \phi^{(6A)}_{0,1}$ produces the weight
\[
  k_{6A} \;=\; c_6(0)/2 \;-\; ? \;=\; 0 \quad\text{(boundary)},
\]
where the naive $c_N(0)/2 = 1$ is killed by the vanishing polar
coefficient $A_6 = 0$, so the Gritsenko lift lands at the
weight-$0$ boundary. A weight-$0$ paramodular form does
\emph{not} Borcherds-lift to a rank-$3$ BKM denominator directly;
the GN $1998$ machinery requires $k_{6A} \geq 1$. The rank-$3$ BKM
$\mathfrak{g}^{(6)}$ is therefore \emph{conjectural} at $N = 6$,
requiring a CHL-level extension (Clery--Gritsenko $2013$
\emph{Proc.~London Math.~Soc.}~$108$) to level-$6$ paramodular. The
corresponding $\kBKM$-value:
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_6) \;=\; c_6(0)/2 \;=\; 1, \qquad
  \kBKM(\Phi_{10}^{(6)}) \;=\; 1
\]
per the universal Borcherds weight identity $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$
(Theorem~\texttt{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal},
\texttt{cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}), consistent with the
programme-canonical $(\kBKM)_{N=1,2,3,4,6} = (5, 4, 3, 2, 1)$ at
gold-standard reading (Path A weak Jacobi, Path B paramodular
dimension formula, Path C Nikulin fixed-point count converge).

\paragraph{Synthesis.} The slide deck at $N = 6$ states the framework
but omits the Fourier tables; the $N = 6$ data can be reconstructed
from primary literature. Mukai $1988$ fixes $T_{H^*}(g_6) = 2$,
Hashimoto $2012$ refines to $2A_5 + 2D_4$ local fixed-locus types,
CHP $2014$ selects $6D_4$ as the umbral Niemeier anchor. The
paramodular lift weight $k_{6A} = 0$ is a boundary (Wave-$10$ R$2$
Step $2$), matching the Mathieu-to-umbral regime transition
identified in Wave-$10$ S$1$--S$2$ Q$4$. The universal Borcherds
identity $\kBKM(\Phi_6) = c_6(0)/2 = 1$ is robust.
