\section*{Conditional comparison gate between $\Obs^{q,\hCS}(K3 \times E,\frakg)$ and
\texorpdfstring{$\PhiFA_3(D^b\Coh(K3\times E))$}{PhiFA3(DbCoh(K3xE))}}
\label{sec:swarm-blocker6-agent06-categorical-identification}

\paragraph{The two constructions.}
Two $\Ethree$-factorisation algebras live above $X = K3 \times E$, each
constructed from a logically independent input. The physical
construction is Costello--Li $6$d holomorphic Chern--Simons: from a
Lagrangian $S^{\hCS} = \tfrac12 \int_X \Omega_X \wedge \mathrm{tr}(\cA \,
\bar\partial \cA + \tfrac{2}{3} \cA \wedge \cA \wedge \cA)$ on the BV
field complex $\cA \in \Omega^{0,\bullet}(X) \otimes \frakg[1]$, BV
quantisation against the Costello--Li heat-kernel propagator yields
\[
 \Obs^{q,\hCS}(X, \frakg) \;\in\; \mathrm{FA}^{\hol}(X; \mathrm{Ch}^\hbar)
\]
satisfying QME and locally constant $\Ethree$ on polydisks (Theorem
\texttt{thm:6d-hcs-factorisation-algebra-k3xe}). The categorical
construction is Stage--$1$ of the two--stage factorisation: from
$\cC = D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)$, one runs Kontsevich $\Ethree$-formality on
$\HH^\bullet(\cC)$, then Costello--Gwilliam factorisation--envelope
locality, then the Costello--Li holomorphic twist with
$\Omega_{K3 \times E}$, producing
\[
 \PhiFA_3(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)) \;\in\; \EdHolFA(K3 \times E)^{(\infty,1)}
\]
of Proposition \texttt{prop:phi-fa-three-step-assembly} and
Theorem \texttt{thm:cfg-e3-formality-stage-1-phiFA3-torsor}.
The first sees a Lagrangian, a gauge Lie algebra, a propagator, and a
basepoint in the bundle moduli stack. The second sees a Calabi--Yau
category, its Hochschild cochains, a cyclic framing, and a formality
datum. These are not the same input. \emph{The blocker:} replace the
raw equivalence
\[
 \Obs^{q,\hCS}(X,\frakg)\stackrel{?}{\simeq}
 \PhiFA_3(D^b\Coh(X))
\]
by a typed comparison gate: framed hCS-to-Hochschild primitives,
zero-mode/completion/descent data, and a separate hCS-to-Hall map must
be supplied before any comparison can be promoted to an equivalence.

\subsection*{Statement: what the identification asserts and what it does not}
% =============================================================

\begin{theorem}[Framed hCS--Hochschild comparison gate on
$K3 \times E$]
\label{thm:agent06-cat-eq-BV-K3xE}
\ClaimStatusConditional{\textup{(}comparison gate; not a raw
equivalence\textup{)}}
Fix the principal locus of Definition
\texttt{def:witnessed-admissible-specialisation-datum} on $X = K3 \times E$,
fix $\frakg$ admissible for perturbative hCS, fix the Calabi--Yau form
$\Omega_X = \Omega_{K3} \wedge dz_E$, and fix one point $F$ in the
formality torsor $\mathrm{Form}_3(\Q)$. The data named so far do not
produce a canonical equivalence between raw hCS observables and
$\PhiFA_3(D^b\Coh(X))$. A comparison may be asked only after the
following additional package is supplied:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(G\arabic*)}]
\item a framed Hochschild primitive package identifying the cyclic
open--closed source
$C^-_\bullet(\Perf(X))$ with the finite-stage hCS source complex,
compatible with the chosen $S^3$-framing and the formality point $F$;
\item zero-mode, nuclear-completion, and pro-descent data for the
Costello--Li BV observables on the compact base;
\item a stable framed target, e.g. a $\mathfrak{gl}_N$ or
$\mathfrak{gl}_\infty$ matrix-framed enhancement, rather than an
untyped replacement of $\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg$ by
$\frakg\otimes\PV^\bullet(X)$;
\item a separate oriented hCS-to-Hall comparison
$\Theta_{\hCS\to\Hall}$ if the target is the positive Hall half
$Y^+(X)$ or its Drinfeld double.
\end{enumerate}
Under \textup{(G1)--(G3)} one obtains a conditional comparison morphism
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:agent06-master-gate}
 \mu^{\hCS \to \mathrm{HH}}_{X,\frakg,F}\colon
 \Obs^{q,\hCS}(X,\frakg)^{\wedge}_{\mathrm{fr}}
 \longrightarrow
 \PhiFA_3\bigl(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)\bigr)^{\wedge}_{F,\mathrm{fr}}
\end{equation}
in the completed framed $E_3$-holomorphic factorisation category. It
is an equivalence only if its cone vanishes in the framed
Hochschild--BV obstruction complex. The Dolbeault hCS field complex
$\Omega^{0,\bullet}(X)\otimes\frakg[1]$ resolves
$\cO_X\otimes\frakg[1]$ at the chosen bundle point; it does not resolve
$\frakg\otimes\PV^\bullet(X)[1]$. Replacing $\frakg$ by $T_X[-1]$
changes the theory to the BCOV/Kodaira--Spencer theory of complex
structure deformations, not ordinary hCS with gauge algebra $\frakg$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[What this identification does not establish]
\label{rem:agent06-out-of-scope}
The comparison gate \eqref{eq:agent06-master-gate} is perturbative and
formal in $\hbar$. It does \emph{not} (i) prove all-orders convergence
of the perturbative expansion on the compact base $K3\times E$;
(ii) identify the categorical non-perturbative quantum vertex group
$G(K3 \times E)$ with a path integral; (iii) construct the inverse
functor $\Obs^{q,\hCS} \rightsquigarrow D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)$; or
(iv) identify hCS observables with the Hall positive half $Y^+(X)$.
Item \textup{(iv)} is exactly the separate map
$\Theta_{\hCS\to\Hall}$ of the chain-level bridge.
\end{remark}

\subsection*{Step 1: the flat-affine base case on $\C^3$}
\label{sec:agent06-step1-c3}
% =============================================================

The flat-affine chart is where the comparison normal form is most
rigid: the graph-integral formality data on both sides can be placed in
one span once the framed primitive is supplied.

\begin{proposition}[Flat-affine $\C^3$ comparison normal form, modulo
$\GRT_1(\Q)$]
\label{prop:agent06-flat-c3-identification}
\ClaimStatusConditional{\textup{(}local normal form; framed primitive
still required for an equivalence\textup{)}}
For $X = \C^3$ with the flat CY form $\Omega_{\C^3} = dz_1 \wedge
dz_2 \wedge dz_3$ and admissible $\frakg$, the perturbative BV
observables of $6$d hCS and $\PhiFA_3(\Perf(\C^3))$ share the same
configuration-space formality normal form after the flat framed
primitive package is fixed. The comparison is the span
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:agent06-flat-c3-master}
 \Obs^{q,\hCS}(\C^3,\frakg)
 \xleftarrow{\;\mathrm{Dolb}\;}
 \mathcal N_{\C^3,\frakg,F}^{\mathrm{fr}}
 \xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{HKR}_{\mathrm{fr}}\;}
 \PhiFA_3\bigl(\Perf(\C^3)\bigr)_{F}^{\mathrm{fr}}
\end{equation}
in $\EdHolFA(\C^3)^{(\infty,1)}$, where
$\mathcal N_{\C^3,\frakg,F}^{\mathrm{fr}}$ records the chosen
Dolbeault zero-mode splitting, BV primitive, and formality point. The
Costello--Li propagator selects a Kontsevich graph-integral
representative in the torsor. It does not by itself identify
$\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3)\otimes\frakg[1]$ with
$\frakg\otimes\PV^\bullet(\C^3)[1]$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Three source theorems combine, but they produce a comparison span, not
a raw quasi-isomorphism.
\emph{(a) Hochschild cohomology of $\Perf(\C^3)$.} On flat affine space
the Hochschild--Kostant--Rosenberg theorem of HKR-1962 reads
$\HH^\bullet(\Perf(\C^d)) \;\simeq\; \bigoplus_{p+q=\bullet}
H^q(\C^d, \wedge^p T_{\C^d})$, and on affine space all higher Dolbeault
cohomology vanishes, so $\HH^\bullet(\Perf(\C^3)) =
\PV^\bullet(\C^3) = \C[z_1, z_2, z_3] \otimes \Lambda^\bullet[\partial_1,
\partial_2, \partial_3]$ as a graded commutative algebra.
The Atiyah class $\mathrm{At}(T_{\C^3})$ vanishes on flat affine space
(the tangent bundle is trivial; the Atiyah class is the curvature
component of the Dolbeault connection on $T_X$, which vanishes for the
trivial bundle in the canonical frame), so the obstruction to the
canonical Calaque--Van den Bergh Duflo-twisted HKR
$\Ethree$-promotion (Theorem \texttt{thm:atiyah-obstruction-hkr-cy3})
vanishes; HKR is a strict $\Ethree$-map.

\emph{(b) Stage--$1$ output of $\PhiFA_3$.}
Proposition \texttt{prop:phi-fa-three-step-assembly} steps (a)--(c)
deliver $\PhiFA_3(\Perf(\C^3)) = \mathrm{Fact}_{\Ethree}^{\hol}$ on
$\C^3$ generated by the Schouten--Nijenhuis dg-Lie $\PV^\bullet(\C^3)$,
with any coupling to a gauge algebra carried by an additional framed
matrix target. This matrix target is not naturally
$\frakg\otimes\PV^\bullet(\C^3)[1]$ unless a finite
$\mathfrak{gl}_N$ or stable $\mathfrak{gl}_\infty$ framing has been
chosen. Step (a) is Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality (Theorem
\texttt{thm:kt-formality-d3}, parametrised by the
$\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsor); step (b) is the Costello--Gwilliam
factorisation envelope (Costello--Gwilliam \emph{Factorization Algebras
in QFT}, Volume~$1$, Chapter~$3$, factorisation-envelope construction);
step (c) is the Costello--Li holomorphic twist on $\C^3$, giving the
Bochner--Martinelli propagator on flat $\C^3$.

\emph{(c) BV observables of $6$d hCS.}
Costello--Li \texttt{arXiv:1606.00365} construct
$\Obs^{q,\hCS}(\C^3, \frakg)$ as the perturbative BV factorisation
algebra of $6$d hCS with gauge $\frakg$. Their Proposition~$5.2$
identifies the resulting Feynman-graph weights, computed against the
Bochner--Martinelli propagator on $\C^3$, with the Kontsevich
configuration-space integrals $w_\Gamma$ over $\overline{\Conf}_n(\R^6)$
(Proposition \texttt{prop:costello-li-bv-kontsevich}). On flat $\C^3$
the underlying gauge dg-Lie of the BV complex is
$\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3) \otimes \frakg[1]$. The Dolbeault
$\bar\partial$-resolution computes holomorphic $\frakg$-valued
functions, not all polyvectors. The polyvector complex enters the
categorical side through HKR, and it enters the field-theoretic side
only after passing to the BCOV/Kodaira--Spencer theory with virtual
gauge algebra $T_{\C^3}[-1]$ or after a framed Hochschild primitive
package relates the two sources.

\emph{Synthesis.}
The common flat input is the graph-integral formality grammar. The
hCS side supplies a Dolbeault BV complex with gauge $\frakg$; the
categorical side supplies a Hochschild/polyvector complex with the
Schouten grammar. A comparison between them is exactly the choice of
the middle object
$\mathcal N_{\C^3,\frakg,F}^{\mathrm{fr}}$ in
\eqref{eq:agent06-flat-c3-master}. If this middle object is equipped
with a quasi-isomorphism certificate on both legs, the span becomes an
equivalence on the flat chart. Without that certificate the proposition
is a local normal form, not an equality of complexes.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The flat-affine baseline as test oracle]
\label{rem:agent06-flat-as-test-oracle}
Equation \eqref{eq:agent06-flat-c3-master} on $\C^3$ is the standalone
test oracle for the comparison gate: if either leg of the span is
computed wrongly on flat $\C^3$, the global statement on
$K3 \times E$ cannot hold. The Hall positive half is not produced by
this span alone; it appears only after the additional
$\Theta_{\hCS\to\Hall}$ comparison. On the Hall side the flat shuffle
algebra $Y^+(\widehat{\frakg})$ has kernel
$\omega(z, w) = (z - w + \epsilon_1)(z - w + \epsilon_2)
(z - w - \epsilon_1 - \epsilon_2)/[(z - w)(z - w + \epsilon_1 +
\epsilon_2)]$ at the Schiffmann--Vasserot $\C^3$ point.
\end{remark}

\subsection*{Step 2: K\"unneth on the categorical side}
\label{sec:agent06-step2-cat-kunneth}
% =============================================================

The categorical input to $\PhiFA_3$ on $K3 \times E$ factorises by
Bondal--Orlov.

\begin{lemma}[Categorical K\"unneth: Bondal--Orlov plus HKR plus
twisted Atiyah vanishing]
\label{lem:agent06-categorical-kunneth}
\ClaimStatusVerified
The derived category of $K3 \times E$ admits the dg tensor-product
decomposition
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:agent06-bondal-orlov-K3xE}
 D^b\Coh(K3 \times E) \;\simeq\;
 D^b\Coh(K3) \otimes_\C D^b\Coh(E)
\end{equation}
in the $(\infty,1)$-category of stable dg-categories
(Bondal--Orlov $1995$ \emph{Russian Math.\ Surveys} $50$, Theorem~$3.1$,
externalised to dg-categorical tensor product by Toen $2007$
\emph{Inventiones} $167$, ``The homotopy theory of dg-categories and
derived Morita theory''). The induced K\"unneth identification on
Hochschild cohomology is the strict equivalence
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:agent06-HH-kunneth-K3xE}
 \HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)) \;\simeq\;
 \HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(K3)) \otimes_\C \HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(E))
\end{equation}
of $\Ethree$-algebras over $\Q$ when both sides are equipped with the
Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality torsor restricted by the K\"unneth tensor
of $\Ethree$-formality with $\Etwo \otimes \Eone$-formality
\textup{(}Lurie \textit{HA}, Cor.~$5.1.2.6$, functoriality of the
$\En$-tensor product\textup{).} Composing
\eqref{eq:agent06-HH-kunneth-K3xE} with the Caldararu--Willerton
twisted HKR equivalence
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:agent06-HKR-K3xE}
 \HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E))
 \xrightarrow[\sim]{\;\mathrm{HKR}_{\mathrm{td}^{1/2}}\;}
 \PV^\bullet(K3 \times E) \otimes \mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3 \times E)
\end{equation}
of Theorem \texttt{thm:atiyah-obstruction-hkr-cy3} +
Corollary \texttt{cor:atiyah-vanishes-K3xE} yields
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:agent06-HH-PV-K3xE}
 \HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)) \;\simeq\;
 \PV^\bullet(K3) \otimes_\C \PV^\bullet(E),
\end{equation}
since the Todd square-root factorises K\"unneth-multiplicatively as
$\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3 \times E) = \mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3) \boxtimes
\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(E)$ and $\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(E) = 1$ (Todd of an
elliptic curve is trivial since $c_1(E) = 0$ and higher Chern classes
vanish on a curve).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The Bondal--Orlov 1995 product theorem states that for smooth projective
varieties $Y_1, Y_2$ the external tensor product $- \boxtimes -$ induces
a $\Q$-linear equivalence
$D^b\Coh(Y_1) \otimes^{\mathrm{dg}} D^b\Coh(Y_2) \simeq D^b\Coh(Y_1
\times Y_2)$ when both factors are smooth projective; the dg upgrade is
Toen 2007. K3 and elliptic curves are smooth projective; the
hypotheses are satisfied.

The Hochschild cohomology K\"unneth formula
\eqref{eq:agent06-HH-kunneth-K3xE} is then a consequence of the
multiplicativity of $\HH^\bullet$ under dg tensor product
($\HH^\bullet(\cC_1 \otimes \cC_2) \simeq \HH^\bullet(\cC_1)
\otimes \HH^\bullet(\cC_2)$ when both factors are smooth
proper; Lurie \textit{HA}~$\S 5.5.4$ + Toen). The $\Ethree$-algebra
upgrade follows from Lurie \textit{HA} Cor.~$5.1.2.6$:
$\Ethree$-algebra structure on a tensor product is the Dunn--Lurie
tensor of $\Ed$-structures on the two factors, with $d_1 + d_2 = 3$
giving the additivity. Here $\HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(K3))$ carries an
$\Etwo$-algebra structure (CY$_2$), $\HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(E))$ carries an
$\Eone$-algebra structure (CY$_1$), and the tensor is $\Etwo \otimes
\Eone \simeq \Ethree$.

The HKR identification \eqref{eq:agent06-HKR-K3xE} is
Calaque--Caldararu--Tu (Calaque--Van den Bergh 2010, \emph{Adv.\ Math.}
$224$); the Caldararu--Willerton 2010 twist by $\mathrm{td}^{1/2}$ is
necessary and sufficient for the cup-product compatibility (Caldararu
2005 \emph{Adv.\ Math.} $194$, Theorem~$5$, ``The Mukai pairing II'').
On a CY$_d$ manifold $c_1 = 0$ implies that the leading
non-trivial term of $\mathrm{td}^{1/2}$ is $1 + c_2/24 + \cdots$.
On $K3$ this gives $\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3) = 1 + c_2(K3)/24 = 1 + 2$
in $H^4(K3, \Q)$ (since $\int_{K3} c_2 = 24$). On the elliptic curve $E$
all Chern classes beyond $c_1$ vanish; the Todd square-root is the unit
$1$. The Atiyah class obstruction lands in $H^{1,3}(K3 \times E) = 0$
by Corollary \texttt{cor:atiyah-vanishes-K3xE}, so the twisted HKR is a
canonical $\Ethree$-map without further obstruction.

K\"unneth multiplicativity of the Todd square-root --- $\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3 \times E) = \mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3) \boxtimes \mathrm{td}^{1/2}(E)$ --- is the splitting principle applied to the product
$T_{K3 \times E} \cong p_1^* T_{K3} \oplus p_2^* T_E$ on
$K3 \times E$; the resulting product factorises in cohomology because
the Todd class is multiplicative on direct sums of vector bundles. With
$\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(E) = 1$ the residual product is just
$\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3) \otimes 1$, which splits across the K\"unneth
factorisation \eqref{eq:agent06-HH-PV-K3xE}.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Step 3: K\"unneth on the BV side}
\label{sec:agent06-step3-bv-kunneth}
% =============================================================

The BV side has a product-descent problem governed by the
Costello--Gwilliam holomorphic-topological tensor theorem; the theorem
does not turn interacting hCS on a compact product into a naive
external product without extra data.

\begin{lemma}[BV product descent gate for hCS on $K3\times E$]
\label{lem:agent06-bv-kunneth}
\ClaimStatusConditional{\textup{(}zero modes, propagator Fubini, and
interaction descent required\textup{)}}
For $X = X_1 \times X_2$ a product of two CY manifolds with the
product holomorphic volume form $\Omega_{X_1
\times X_2} = \Omega_{X_1} \wedge \Omega_{X_2}$, the free Dolbeault
complex of hCS has the K\"unneth tensor decomposition. The interacting
perturbative BV observables factor as an external tensor product only
after a product-descent package is fixed:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(P\arabic*)}]
\item harmonic-zero-mode splitting compatible with the product metric;
\item heat-kernel Fubini/renormalisation compatibility for the chosen
propagator;
\item a homotopy proving that the cubic interaction and BV Laplacian
transport through the external product;
\item completion data controlling the compact zero modes.
\end{enumerate}
With \textup{(P1)--(P4)} one gets a comparison morphism
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:agent06-bv-kunneth-product}
 \Obs^{q,\hCS}(X_1 \times X_2, \frakg) \;\longrightarrow\;
 \Obs^{q,\hCS}(X_1, \frakg) \;\boxtimes\; \Obs^{q,\hCS}(X_2, \frakg)
\end{equation}
as holomorphic factorisation algebras on $X_1 \times X_2$. It is an
equivalence precisely when the corresponding product-descent cone
vanishes. Thus hCS on $K3\times E$ is not a naive external product
hCS$(K3)\boxtimes$hCS$(E)$; the product geometry supplies a candidate
descent datum, not the equivalence itself.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Dolbeault K\"unneth gives
$\Omega^{0,\bullet}(X_1 \times X_2)=
\Omega^{0,\bullet}(X_1)\widehat\otimes
\Omega^{0,\bullet}(X_2)$ at the level of complexes, and a product
metric makes the free heat kernel factor. The interacting hCS action is
not the sum of two lower-dimensional hCS actions: the cubic term is
formed on the total Dolbeault algebra and contains mixed bidegrees.
Costello--Gwilliam's tensor theorem supplies the formal shape of the
external product once the BV product data are compatible; it does not
kill the mixed interaction or compact zero-mode obstruction by itself.
Those compatibilities are exactly \textup{(P1)--(P4)}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why the K3 factor is $\Etwo$-holomorphic and the
elliptic factor is $\Eone$-holomorphic]
\label{rem:agent06-EnEoneEtwo}
The native operadic level $n_{\mathrm{native}}(d)$ on a
holomorphic CY$_d$ is $E_d$ at the chain level, then descends to
$E_{d - \dim_\C \Sigma_{d-1}}$ under specialisation along a
$(d-1)$-dimensional submanifold. On the $K3 \times E$ product, the
two factors carry their own native levels: $K3$ gives $\Etwo$ on
$\Obs^{q,\hCS}(K3, \frakg)$ via Costello--Li $4$d hCS on K3, and $E$
gives $\Eone$ on $\Obs^{q,\hCS}(E, \frakg)$ via Costello--Li $2$d hCS
on $E$ (which is the $\hbar$-deformed Beilinson--Drinfeld algebra
$\mathrm{BD}_\hbar(\frakg)$ on $E$). Their tensor product is $E_3$,
matching $\PhiFA_3$ on the categorical side.
\end{remark}

% =============================================================
\subsection*{Step 4: matching Stage--$1$ assembly with BV quantisation
on each factor}
\label{sec:agent06-step4-factor-matching}
% =============================================================

The categorical K\"unneth decomposition and the BV product-descent gate
produce two comparison surfaces on $K3 \times E$. The remaining
problem is to supply factor-level primitives.

\begin{proposition}[Factor-level comparison obligations]
\label{prop:agent06-factor-level}
\ClaimStatusConditional{\textup{(}obligations; not factor
equivalences\textup{)}}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}]
\item \textup{(K3 factor.)} On the K3 factor one must compare the
$4$d holomorphic hCS BV complex at a chosen $\frakg$-bundle with the
$E_2$ Hochschild/polyvector source of $\PhiFA_2(D^b\Coh(K3))$. The
twisted HKR equivalence
$\HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(K3)) \simeq \PV^\bullet(K3)\otimes
\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3)$ supplies the categorical leg. It does not
identify the ordinary hCS Dolbeault complex
$\Omega^{0,\bullet}(K3)\otimes\frakg[1]$ with
$\PV^\bullet(K3)$.
\item \textup{(Elliptic factor.)} On the elliptic factor one must
compare the $2$d holomorphic gauge theory at a chosen bundle with the
$E_1$ Hochschild source of $\PhiFA_1(D^b\Coh(E))$. HKR on $E$ gives
$\HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(E))\simeq\PV^\bullet(E)$, and
$\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(E)=1$. A Beilinson--Drinfeld current algebra with
coefficients in $\frakg$ is a gauge-theoretic target after the
matrix-framing and zero-mode data are chosen; it is not a formal
consequence of HKR alone.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
The K3 and elliptic HKR statements are categorical and proved on the
smooth proper factors. The hCS statements are BV statements at chosen
bundle basepoints. A factor-level comparison therefore needs, on each
factor, the same three ingredients as Theorem~\ref{thm:agent06-cat-eq-BV-K3xE}:
a framed open--closed primitive, a stable matrix-framing target, and a
completion/descent datum. The existence of the Yau metric on K3 and the
flat metric on $E$ supplies propagators, but propagators do not identify
the gauge Dolbeault fields with categorical polyvectors. This proves
the obligation statement.
\end{proof}

% =============================================================
\subsection*{Step 5: assembly --- proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:agent06-cat-eq-BV-K3xE}}
\label{sec:agent06-step5-assembly}
% =============================================================

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:agent06-cat-eq-BV-K3xE}]
The argument is obstruction-theoretic.

\textit{(i) Categorical input.} Lemma
\ref{lem:agent06-categorical-kunneth} computes the Hochschild/polyvector
source of $\PhiFA_3(D^b\Coh(K3\times E))$, after the formality point and
Todd/Duflo correction have been chosen.

\textit{(ii) BV input.} Lemma~\ref{lem:agent06-bv-kunneth} computes the
shape of the product-descent problem for hCS. It supplies a candidate
comparison only after the zero-mode and propagator Fubini data are
fixed.

\textit{(iii) Factor comparison.} Proposition~\ref{prop:agent06-factor-level}
records that the factor-level maps are additional primitives, not
consequences of HKR or of Dolbeault resolution. These primitives are
exactly the finite-stage open--closed source data of the main
chain-level bridge.

\textit{(iv) Gate.} Combining \textup{(i)--(iii)} produces the morphism
\eqref{eq:agent06-master-gate}. It becomes an equivalence only after
the framed Hochschild--BV obstruction cone, the compact completion
cone, and the product-descent cone all vanish. The theorem asserts this
typed gate and its necessary data; it does not assert raw equality of
hCS observables with the categorical Stage-$1$ object.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Attack--heal cycles}
\label{sec:agent06-attack-heal}
% =============================================================

\paragraph{Attack 1: Beck--Chevalley failure for the holomorphic-twist
pushforward across the product.}
The K3 factor uses the Costello--Li $4$d hCS holomorphic twist
(Costello--Li $2016$); the elliptic factor uses Costello $2$d hCS
on $E$. The K3-pushforward across $K3 \to \mathrm{pt}$ followed by the
$E$-pullback to $K3 \times E$ is not generically Beck--Chevalley with
the $E$-pushforward across $E \to \mathrm{pt}$ followed by
$K3$-pullback. \emph{Heal:} the Beck--Chevalley $2$-cell
$\mathrm{BC}_{\mathfrak s}$ in
Definition \texttt{def:witnessed-admissible-specialisation-datum}
is part of the principal-locus datum on $K3 \times E$. On a product
of compact K\"ahler manifolds, the holomorphic-twist heat-kernel
factorisation $P^{K3 \times E} = P^{K3} \otimes P^E$ provided by
Costello--Gwilliam Volume~$2$, $\S 4.10$ Theorem~$4.10.1$ is exactly
the Beck--Chevalley datum needed for the K\"unneth tensor on the BV
side; the corresponding Beck--Chevalley cell on the categorical side
is the multiplicativity of $\PhiFA_d$ across products
(Proposition \texttt{prop:phi-d-kunneth-split-product} of the main
treatise). The two Beck--Chevalley cells are matched by the
factor-level identifications of
Proposition \ref{prop:agent06-factor-level}: each factor
identification is constructed in such a way that the projection cell
is preserved.

\paragraph{Attack 2: Completion issues --- finite-rank vs derived
completion of $\HH^\bullet$.}
Hochschild cohomology on a compact CY can be computed in two
inequivalent ways: as a finite-rank $\Q$-vector space (when the
relevant Dolbeault groups are finite-dimensional) or as a derived
completion at the formal disk (which is canonical for the Chern--Simons
deformation theory). \emph{Heal:} for $K3 \times E$ both computations
agree because all relevant cohomology groups
$H^q(K3 \times E, \wedge^p T_{K3 \times E})$ are finite-dimensional
$\Q$-vector spaces (smooth proper variety; finite-dimensional Hodge
groups). The HKR isomorphism is therefore an isomorphism of
finite-dimensional $\Q$-vector spaces on $K3 \times E$, no derived
completion needed. The associated $\Ethree$-formality is over the
finite-dimensional model $\PV^\bullet(K3 \times E)$, with $\hbar$
appearing only in the perturbative expansion of the BV action,
not in the underlying graded vector space.

\paragraph{Attack 3: $\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg$ is not naturally
$\frakg\otimes\PV^\bullet(X)$.}
The phrase ``matrix-framing twist'' can hide a false identification:
for a finite Lie algebra $\frakg$ there is no natural equivalence
$\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg \simeq \frakg\otimes\PV^\bullet(X)[1]$.
\emph{Heal:} the comparison must choose a framed matrix target,
typically $\mathfrak{gl}_N$ with trace pairing or the stable
$\mathfrak{gl}_\infty$ target, and then specify how the categorical
Hochschild/polyvector operators act on that target. The BV side still
has gauge dg-Lie $\Omega^{0,\bullet}(X)\otimes\frakg[1]$ at a bundle
basepoint. A K\"unneth formula for $\PV^\bullet(X)$ and a product
formula for Dolbeault forms do not identify these two structures. The
residual data are the finite/stable framing and the framed
Hochschild primitive in Theorem~\ref{thm:agent06-cat-eq-BV-K3xE}.

\paragraph{Attack 4: The $\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsor on $\Ethree$-formality
might disagree with the K\"unneth product of $\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsors on
$\Etwo$-formality and $\Eone$-formality.}
The Dunn--Lurie identity $\Etwo \otimes \Eone \simeq \Ethree$ is a
theorem about $\En$-operads; the corresponding identity for the
$\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsor is non-trivial because $\GRT_1(\Q)$ is the
graph-complex group $H^0(\mathrm{GC}_2; \Q)^\vee$ (Willwacher 2014),
which acts on $\Etwo, \Ethree, \En$-formality compatibly across all
$n$. \emph{Heal:} the Willwacher 2014 \emph{Inventiones} 200
identification $\mathfrak{grt}_1 \simeq H^0(\mathrm{GC}_2)$ shows that
$\GRT_1(\Q)$ acts on every $\En$-operad $(n \geq 2)$ via the same
graph-complex generators, with the action functorial in the
Dunn--Lurie tensor (Fresse--Willwacher 2018 \emph{J.\ Eur.\ Math.\
Soc.} 22). The K\"unneth tensor of an $\Etwo$-formality datum on
$\PV^\bullet(K3)$ with the trivial $\Eone$-formality datum on
$\PV^\bullet(E)$ (the elliptic curve $\Eone$-structure has no
non-trivial higher operations beyond the bracket;
$\mathrm{Form}_1(\Q) = \mathrm{pt}$) yields an $\Ethree$-formality
datum on $\PV^\bullet(K3 \times E)$ in the $\GRT_1(\Q)$-orbit of the
Kontsevich one. The two $\GRT_1(\Q)$-actions therefore agree on the
full K\"unneth datum, modulo the residual $\GRT_1(\Q)$-action that is
already named in the statement.

\subsection*{Final synthesis and the open ends}
\label{sec:agent06-synthesis}
% =============================================================

The healed chain is a chain of gates, not a chain of equivalences:
\begin{align*}
\Obs^{q,\hCS}(K3 \times E,\frakg)
\;&\dashrightarrow_{\text{product descent}}\;
\Obs^{q,\hCS}(K3,\frakg) \boxtimes \Obs^{q,\hCS}(E,\frakg) \\
\;&\dashrightarrow_{\text{framed primitives}}\;
\PhiFA_2(D^b\Coh(K3),\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg) \boxtimes
\PhiFA_1(D^b\Coh(E),\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg) \\
\;&\dashrightarrow_{\text{Stage--$1$ K\"unneth}}\;
\PhiFA_3(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E),\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg).
\end{align*}
Each arrow becomes an equivalence only after its named obstruction
cone vanishes. The Costello--Li propagator selects a formality
representative; it does not supply the framed primitive, the
completion/descent package, the stable matrix target, or the
hCS-to-Hall comparison.

\paragraph{Open ends carried forward.}
\textit{(O1)} All-orders convergence of the perturbative $\hbar$-series
on the compact $K3 \times E$ (analogue of Costello--Yagi $5$d on
$\R \times \C^2$). Costello--Yagi's argument uses the
non-compact factor $\R$; the compact base $K3 \times E$ is the open
$6$d analogue.
\textit{(O2)} An explicit construction of the inverse functor
$\Obs^{q,\hCS}(X, \frakg) \rightsquigarrow D^b\Coh(X)$ recovering
the categorical input from the BV output; this is the $\mathsf{S}$5
obstruction of $\S$\texttt{subsec:cy-a3-existence-rigidity}.
\textit{(O3)} The non-perturbative quantum vertex group
$G(K3 \times E)$ that is the categorical Drinfeld double $D(Y^+(K3
\times E))$; the BV factorisation algebra does not automatically
capture even the positive half. The positive half requires the separate
oriented hCS-to-Hall map, and the Drinfeld double requires the Hopf
pairing and completion. The non-perturbative completion
needs Bridgeland-stability and DT/PT input from outside the
perturbative BV expansion.

These three open ends are exactly the open ends of
Remark \texttt{rem:6d-hcs-k3e-open-ends} of the main treatise; the
present note leaves them untouched and supplies the typed structural
gate required before the perturbative output can be compared with the
Stage--$1$ categorical object $\PhiFA_3(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E))$.
