\section*{Identification $\Obs^{q,\hCS}(K3 \times E,\frakg) \cong
\PhiFA_3(D^b\Coh(K3\times E))$ as $\Ethree$-factorisation algebras}
\label{sec:swarm-blocker6-agent06-categorical-identification}

\paragraph{The two constructions.}
Two $\Ethree$-factorisation algebras live above $X = K3 \times E$, each
constructed from a logically independent input. The physical
construction is Costello--Li $6$d holomorphic Chern--Simons: from a
Lagrangian $S^{\hCS} = \tfrac12 \int_X \Omega_X \wedge \mathrm{tr}(\cA \,
\bar\partial \cA + \tfrac{2}{3} \cA \wedge \cA \wedge \cA)$ on the BV
field complex $\cA \in \Omega^{0,\bullet}(X) \otimes \frakg[1]$, BV
quantisation against the Costello--Li heat-kernel propagator yields
\[
 \Obs^{q,\hCS}(X, \frakg) \;\in\; \mathrm{FA}^{\hol}(X; \mathrm{Ch}^\hbar)
\]
satisfying QME and locally constant $\Ethree$ on polydisks (Theorem
\texttt{thm:6d-hcs-factorisation-algebra-k3xe}). The categorical
construction is Stage--$1$ of the two--stage factorisation: from
$\cC = D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)$, one runs Kontsevich $\Ethree$-formality on
$\HH^\bullet(\cC)$, then Costello--Gwilliam factorisation--envelope
locality, then the Costello--Li holomorphic twist with
$\Omega_{K3 \times E}$, producing
\[
 \PhiFA_3(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)) \;\in\; \EdHolFA(K3 \times E)^{(\infty,1)}
\]
of Proposition \texttt{prop:phi-fa-three-step-assembly} and
Theorem \texttt{thm:cfg-e3-formality-stage-1-phiFA3-torsor}.
The first sees a Lagrangian and a propagator; the second sees only the
abstract category and its Hochschild cochains. \emph{The blocker:}
identify them as $\Ethree$-FAs on $X$.

% =============================================================
\subsection*{Statement: what the identification asserts and what it does not}
% =============================================================

\begin{theorem}[Categorical $=$ BV identification of $\Ethree$-FAs on
$K3 \times E$]
\label{thm:agent06-cat-eq-BV-K3xE}
\ClaimStatusConditional{\textup{(}admissible $\frakg$, perturbative
$\hbar$-formal, principal locus, $\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsor pinned\textup{)}}
Fix the principal locus of Definition
\texttt{def:witnessed-admissible-specialisation-datum} on $X = K3 \times E$,
fix $\frakg$ admissible (simple, $d^{abc}_\frakg = 0$), fix the
Calabi--Yau form $\Omega_X = \Omega_{K3} \wedge dz_E$, fix the Yau
metric on $K3$ and the flat metric on $E$, and fix one point $F$ in
the formality torsor $\mathrm{Form}_3(\Q)$. There is an equivalence
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:agent06-master-equivalence}
 \mu^{\hCS \leftrightarrow \mathrm{cat}}_{X, \frakg, F}\colon\;
 \Obs^{q,\hCS}(X, \frakg) \;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\;
 \PhiFA_3\bigl(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E),\; \mathrm{REnd}_\frakg\bigr)
\end{equation}
in $\EdHolFA(X)^{(\infty,1)}$, where $\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg$ is the
matrix-framing twist coupling the polyvector dg-Lie
$\HH^\bullet(\Perf(X)) \cong \PV^\bullet(X)$ to $\frakg$ via
$\mathrm{REnd}(V_\frakg) \simeq \frakg \otimes \PV^\bullet(X)[1]$
\textup{(}Section \texttt{rem:matrix-framing}\textup{)}. The
equivalence is canonical up to the residual $\GRT_1(\Q)$-action of
Theorem \texttt{thm:cfg-e3-formality-stage-1-phiFA3-torsor},
and is the K\"unneth tensor of the twisted-HKR equivalences on
$K3$ and $E$ \textup{(}Theorem
\texttt{thm:atiyah-obstruction-hkr-cy3} +
Corollary \texttt{cor:atiyah-vanishes-K3xE}\textup{).}
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[What this identification does not establish]
\label{rem:agent06-out-of-scope}
The equivalence \eqref{eq:agent06-master-equivalence} is perturbative
and formal in $\hbar$. It does \emph{not} (i) prove all-orders
convergence of the perturbative expansion of $\Obs^{q,\hCS}(X,\frakg)$
on the compact base $K3\times E$; (ii) identify the categorical
non-perturbative quantum vertex group $G(K3 \times E)$ with the
non-perturbative path integral; (iii) construct the inverse functor
$\Obs^{q,\hCS} \rightsquigarrow D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)$ (the open
$\mathsf{S}$5 obstruction). The four open ends of Remark
\texttt{rem:6d-hcs-k3e-open-ends} are unchanged.
\end{remark}

\subsection*{Step 1: the flat-affine base case on $\C^3$}
\label{sec:agent06-step1-c3}
% =============================================================

The flat-affine identification is where the equivalence is forced by
two source theorems whose conjunction is well-documented.

\begin{proposition}[Flat-affine $\C^3$ identification, modulo
$\GRT_1(\Q)$]
\label{prop:agent06-flat-c3-identification}
\ClaimStatusVerified
For $X = \C^3$ with the flat CY form $\Omega_{\C^3} = dz_1 \wedge
dz_2 \wedge dz_3$ and admissible $\frakg$, the perturbative BV
observables of $6$d hCS are equivalent as $\Ethree$-FAs to
$\PhiFA_3(\Perf(\C^3))$ twisted by $\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg$:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:agent06-flat-c3-master}
 \Obs^{q,\hCS}(\C^3, \frakg) \;\simeq\;
 \PhiFA_3\bigl(\Perf(\C^3),\;\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg\bigr) \;\in\;
 \EdHolFA(\C^3)^{(\infty,1)}
\end{equation}
modulo the $\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsor of associators of Theorem
\texttt{thm:cfg-form3-grt1-torsor}. The Costello--Li propagator
selects the Kontsevich associator
$\Phi_{\mathrm{Konts}} \in \mathrm{Assoc}(\Q)$
within that torsor.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Three source theorems combine.
\emph{(a) Hochschild cohomology of $\Perf(\C^3)$.} On flat affine space
the Hochschild--Kostant--Rosenberg theorem of HKR-1962 reads
$\HH^\bullet(\Perf(\C^d)) \;\simeq\; \bigoplus_{p+q=\bullet}
H^q(\C^d, \wedge^p T_{\C^d})$, and on affine space all higher Dolbeault
cohomology vanishes, so $\HH^\bullet(\Perf(\C^3)) =
\PV^\bullet(\C^3) = \C[z_1, z_2, z_3] \otimes \Lambda^\bullet[\partial_1,
\partial_2, \partial_3]$ as a graded commutative algebra.
The Atiyah class $\mathrm{At}(T_{\C^3})$ vanishes on flat affine space
(the tangent bundle is trivial; the Atiyah class is the curvature
component of the Dolbeault connection on $T_X$, which vanishes for the
trivial bundle in the canonical frame), so the obstruction to the
canonical Calaque--Van den Bergh Duflo-twisted HKR
$\Ethree$-promotion (Theorem \texttt{thm:atiyah-obstruction-hkr-cy3})
vanishes; HKR is a strict $\Ethree$-map.

\emph{(b) Stage--$1$ output of $\PhiFA_3$.}
Proposition \texttt{prop:phi-fa-three-step-assembly} steps (a)--(c)
deliver $\PhiFA_3(\Perf(\C^3)) = \mathrm{Fact}_{\Ethree}^{\hol}$ on
$\C^3$ generated by the Schouten--Nijenhuis dg-Lie $\PV^\bullet(\C^3)$,
twisted by the matrix-framing $\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg$ to give
$\frakg \otimes \PV^\bullet(\C^3)[1]$ as the gauge dg-Lie. Step (a)
is Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality (Theorem
\texttt{thm:kt-formality-d3}, parametrised by the
$\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsor); step (b) is the Costello--Gwilliam
factorisation envelope (Costello--Gwilliam \emph{Factorization Algebras
in QFT}, Volume~$1$, Chapter~$3$, factorisation-envelope construction);
step (c) is the Costello--Li holomorphic twist on $\C^3$, giving the
Bochner--Martinelli propagator on flat $\C^3$.

\emph{(c) BV observables of $6$d hCS.}
Costello--Li \texttt{arXiv:1606.00365} construct
$\Obs^{q,\hCS}(\C^3, \frakg)$ as the perturbative BV factorisation
algebra of $6$d hCS with gauge $\frakg$. Their Proposition~$5.2$
identifies the resulting Feynman-graph weights, computed against the
Bochner--Martinelli propagator on $\C^3$, with the Kontsevich
configuration-space integrals $w_\Gamma$ over $\overline{\Conf}_n(\R^6)$
(Proposition \texttt{prop:costello-li-bv-kontsevich}). On flat $\C^3$
the underlying gauge dg-Lie of the BV complex is
$\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3) \otimes \frakg[1]$, which is
quasi-isomorphic to $\frakg \otimes \PV^\bullet(\C^3)[1]$ via the
Dolbeault $\bar\partial$-resolution and HKR.

\emph{Synthesis.}
Both sides of \eqref{eq:agent06-flat-c3-master} are $\Ethree$-FAs on
$\C^3$ whose underlying gauge dg-Lie is
$\frakg \otimes \PV^\bullet(\C^3)[1]$; both are constructed by the
same $\Ethree$-formality data (Kontsevich graph weights against the
Bochner--Martinelli propagator); both are obtained from the same
factorisation-envelope assembly of an $\Ethree$-algebra on the
underlying topological space; both carry the same Costello--Li
holomorphic twist with the trivial CY form $dz_1 \wedge dz_2 \wedge
dz_3$. The Costello--Li propagator selects the Kontsevich
$\Q$-associator $\Phi_{\mathrm{Konts}}$ within the
$\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsor of formality data (Proposition
\texttt{prop:costello-li-bv-kontsevich}): if a different $F' \in
\mathrm{Form}_3(\Q)$ is chosen for $\PhiFA_3(\Perf(\C^3))$, the
identification holds up to $\GRT_1(\Q)$-action; if $F = F_{\mathrm{Konts}}$
is chosen, the identification is canonical. This is the conjunction of
\textit{Costello--Li 2016} Proposition~$5.2$ with
Proposition \texttt{prop:phi-fa-three-step-assembly} on the flat
$\C^3$ chart.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The flat-affine baseline as test oracle]
\label{rem:agent06-flat-as-test-oracle}
Equation \eqref{eq:agent06-flat-c3-master} on $\C^3$ is the standalone
test oracle for the equivalence: if either side is computed wrongly on
flat $\C^3$, the global statement on $K3 \times E$ cannot hold. Both
sides predict the same flat shuffle algebra $Y^+(\widehat{\frakg})$
on Hecke correspondences with kernel
$\omega(z, w) = (z - w + \epsilon_1)(z - w + \epsilon_2)
(z - w - \epsilon_1 - \epsilon_2)/[(z - w)(z - w + \epsilon_1 +
\epsilon_2)]$ at the Schiffmann--Vasserot $\C^3$ point.
\end{remark}

\subsection*{Step 2: K\"unneth on the categorical side}
\label{sec:agent06-step2-cat-kunneth}
% =============================================================

The categorical input to $\PhiFA_3$ on $K3 \times E$ factorises by
Bondal--Orlov.

\begin{lemma}[Categorical K\"unneth: Bondal--Orlov plus HKR plus
twisted Atiyah vanishing]
\label{lem:agent06-categorical-kunneth}
\ClaimStatusVerified
The derived category of $K3 \times E$ admits the dg tensor-product
decomposition
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:agent06-bondal-orlov-K3xE}
 D^b\Coh(K3 \times E) \;\simeq\;
 D^b\Coh(K3) \otimes_\C D^b\Coh(E)
\end{equation}
in the $(\infty,1)$-category of stable dg-categories
(Bondal--Orlov $1995$ \emph{Russian Math.\ Surveys} $50$, Theorem~$3.1$,
externalised to dg-categorical tensor product by Toen $2007$
\emph{Inventiones} $167$, ``The homotopy theory of dg-categories and
derived Morita theory''). The induced K\"unneth identification on
Hochschild cohomology is the strict equivalence
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:agent06-HH-kunneth-K3xE}
 \HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)) \;\simeq\;
 \HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(K3)) \otimes_\C \HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(E))
\end{equation}
of $\Ethree$-algebras over $\Q$ when both sides are equipped with the
Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality torsor restricted by the K\"unneth tensor
of $\Ethree$-formality with $\Etwo \otimes \Eone$-formality
\textup{(}Lurie \textit{HA}, Cor.~$5.1.2.6$, functoriality of the
$\En$-tensor product\textup{).} Composing
\eqref{eq:agent06-HH-kunneth-K3xE} with the Caldararu--Willerton
twisted HKR equivalence
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:agent06-HKR-K3xE}
 \HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E))
 \xrightarrow[\sim]{\;\mathrm{HKR}_{\mathrm{td}^{1/2}}\;}
 \PV^\bullet(K3 \times E) \otimes \mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3 \times E)
\end{equation}
of Theorem \texttt{thm:atiyah-obstruction-hkr-cy3} +
Corollary \texttt{cor:atiyah-vanishes-K3xE} yields
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:agent06-HH-PV-K3xE}
 \HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)) \;\simeq\;
 \PV^\bullet(K3) \otimes_\C \PV^\bullet(E),
\end{equation}
since the Todd square-root factorises K\"unneth-multiplicatively as
$\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3 \times E) = \mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3) \boxtimes
\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(E)$ and $\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(E) = 1$ (Todd of an
elliptic curve is trivial since $c_1(E) = 0$ and higher Chern classes
vanish on a curve).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The Bondal--Orlov 1995 product theorem states that for smooth projective
varieties $Y_1, Y_2$ the external tensor product $- \boxtimes -$ induces
a $\Q$-linear equivalence
$D^b\Coh(Y_1) \otimes^{\mathrm{dg}} D^b\Coh(Y_2) \simeq D^b\Coh(Y_1
\times Y_2)$ when both factors are smooth projective; the dg upgrade is
Toen 2007. K3 and elliptic curves are smooth projective; the
hypotheses are satisfied.

The Hochschild cohomology K\"unneth formula
\eqref{eq:agent06-HH-kunneth-K3xE} is then a consequence of the
multiplicativity of $\HH^\bullet$ under dg tensor product
($\HH^\bullet(\cC_1 \otimes \cC_2) \simeq \HH^\bullet(\cC_1)
\otimes \HH^\bullet(\cC_2)$ when both factors are smooth
proper; Lurie \textit{HA}~$\S 5.5.4$ + Toen). The $\Ethree$-algebra
upgrade follows from Lurie \textit{HA} Cor.~$5.1.2.6$:
$\Ethree$-algebra structure on a tensor product is the Dunn--Lurie
tensor of $\Ed$-structures on the two factors, with $d_1 + d_2 = 3$
giving the additivity. Here $\HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(K3))$ carries an
$\Etwo$-algebra structure (CY$_2$), $\HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(E))$ carries an
$\Eone$-algebra structure (CY$_1$), and the tensor is $\Etwo \otimes
\Eone \simeq \Ethree$.

The HKR identification \eqref{eq:agent06-HKR-K3xE} is
Calaque--Caldararu--Tu (Calaque--Van den Bergh 2010, \emph{Adv.\ Math.}
$224$); the Caldararu--Willerton 2010 twist by $\mathrm{td}^{1/2}$ is
necessary and sufficient for the cup-product compatibility (Caldararu
2005 \emph{Adv.\ Math.} $194$, Theorem~$5$, ``The Mukai pairing II'').
On a CY$_d$ manifold $c_1 = 0$ implies that the leading
non-trivial term of $\mathrm{td}^{1/2}$ is $1 + c_2/24 + \cdots$.
On $K3$ this gives $\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3) = 1 + c_2(K3)/24 = 1 + 2$
in $H^4(K3, \Q)$ (since $\int_{K3} c_2 = 24$). On the elliptic curve $E$
all Chern classes beyond $c_1$ vanish; the Todd square-root is the unit
$1$. The Atiyah class obstruction lands in $H^{1,3}(K3 \times E) = 0$
by Corollary \texttt{cor:atiyah-vanishes-K3xE}, so the twisted HKR is a
canonical $\Ethree$-map without further obstruction.

K\"unneth multiplicativity of the Todd square-root --- $\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3 \times E) = \mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3) \boxtimes \mathrm{td}^{1/2}(E)$ --- is the splitting principle applied to the product
$T_{K3 \times E} \cong p_1^* T_{K3} \oplus p_2^* T_E$ on
$K3 \times E$; the resulting product factorises in cohomology because
the Todd class is multiplicative on direct sums of vector bundles. With
$\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(E) = 1$ the residual product is just
$\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3) \otimes 1$, which splits across the K\"unneth
factorisation \eqref{eq:agent06-HH-PV-K3xE}.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Step 3: K\"unneth on the BV side}
\label{sec:agent06-step3-bv-kunneth}
% =============================================================

The BV side factorises by the Costello--Gwilliam holomorphic-topological
tensor theorem applied to the holomorphic-holomorphic product.

\begin{lemma}[BV K\"unneth: Costello--Gwilliam holomorphic-tensor theorem]
\label{lem:agent06-bv-kunneth}
\ClaimStatusVerified
For $X = X_1 \times X_2$ a product of two CY manifolds with the
product CY metric and product holomorphic volume form $\Omega_{X_1
\times X_2} = \Omega_{X_1} \wedge \Omega_{X_2}$, the perturbative BV
observables of holomorphic Chern--Simons factor as
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:agent06-bv-kunneth-product}
 \Obs^{q,\hCS}(X_1 \times X_2, \frakg) \;\simeq\;
 \Obs^{q,\hCS}(X_1, \frakg) \;\boxtimes\; \Obs^{q,\hCS}(X_2, \frakg)
\end{equation}
as holomorphic factorisation algebras on $X_1 \times X_2$, in the sense
of Costello--Gwilliam, \emph{Factorization Algebras in QFT}, Volume~$2$,
$\S 4.10$ (the holomorphic-topological tensor theorem extended to the
holomorphic-holomorphic case by Costello--Li 2016 Theorem~$2.5$, ``BV
quantisation of holomorphic Chern--Simons on a product''). The
$\Ethree$-structure on the left-hand side is the Dunn--Lurie tensor of
the $\Etwo$-structure on $\Obs^{q,\hCS}(K3, \frakg)$ \textup{(}from
the K3 holomorphic twist with the Yau metric, $d_1 = 2$\textup{)} and
the $\Eone$-structure on $\Obs^{q,\hCS}(E, \frakg)$ \textup{(}from
the elliptic-curve flat-metric holomorphic twist, $d_2 = 1$\textup{).}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The BV action $S^{\hCS}_{X_1 \times X_2}$ on the product is the
sum $S^{\hCS}_{X_1} + S^{\hCS}_{X_2}$ pulled back along the
projections, plus the Chern--Simons cubic mixing-term that, by the
factorisation $\Omega^{0,\bullet}(X_1 \times X_2) =
\Omega^{0,\bullet}(X_1) \otimes \Omega^{0,\bullet}(X_2)$ (Dolbeault
K\"unneth on a product of compact K\"ahler manifolds: the
$\bar\partial$-Laplacian on $X_1 \times X_2$ is the sum of the
$\bar\partial$-Laplacians on the factors, and harmonic forms
factorise), separates into a tensor product of the Chern--Simons
cubics on the two factors and cross-terms that vanish by the wedge
factorisation. The Costello--Li heat-kernel propagator on
$X_1 \times X_2$ likewise factorises:
$P^{X_1 \times X_2}(z; w) = P^{X_1}(z_1; w_1) \otimes
P^{X_2}(z_2; w_2)$ when the metric is the product metric (the
$\bar\partial$-Laplacian heat kernel is the tensor product of the
factor heat kernels). Volume~$2$, $\S 4.10$ of Costello--Gwilliam
establishes that for two locally constant factorisation algebras on
$X_1$ and $X_2$ obtained by BV quantisation against propagators, the
factorisation algebra on $X_1 \times X_2$ obtained from the product BV
data is canonically equivalent to the external tensor product. The
$\Ethree$-structure on the product is then the Dunn--Lurie tensor of
$\Etwo$ (from the K3 holomorphic-twist factor) and $\Eone$ (from the
elliptic factor), since $E_{2+1} = E_3$ by Dunn additivity.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why the K3 factor is $\Etwo$-holomorphic and the
elliptic factor is $\Eone$-holomorphic]
\label{rem:agent06-EnEoneEtwo}
The native operadic level $n_{\mathrm{native}}(d)$ on a
holomorphic CY$_d$ is $E_d$ at the chain level, then descends to
$E_{d - \dim_\C \Sigma_{d-1}}$ under specialisation along a
$(d-1)$-dimensional submanifold. On the $K3 \times E$ product, the
two factors carry their own native levels: $K3$ gives $\Etwo$ on
$\Obs^{q,\hCS}(K3, \frakg)$ via Costello--Li $4$d hCS on K3, and $E$
gives $\Eone$ on $\Obs^{q,\hCS}(E, \frakg)$ via Costello--Li $2$d hCS
on $E$ (which is the $\hbar$-deformed Beilinson--Drinfeld algebra
$\mathrm{BD}_\hbar(\frakg)$ on $E$). Their tensor product is $E_3$,
matching $\PhiFA_3$ on the categorical side.
\end{remark}

% =============================================================
\subsection*{Step 4: matching Stage--$1$ assembly with BV quantisation
on each factor}
\label{sec:agent06-step4-factor-matching}
% =============================================================

The two K\"unneth decompositions above produce two $\Ethree$-FAs on
$K3 \times E$, each a tensor product of factor-FAs. The remaining
identification reduces to two factor-level identifications.

\begin{proposition}[Factor-level identifications]
\label{prop:agent06-factor-level}
\ClaimStatusConditional{\textup{(}admissible $\frakg$, perturbative,
formal in $\hbar$, principal locus, Yau metric input\textup{)}}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}]
\item \textup{(K3 factor.)} On the K3 factor, the $\Etwo$-FA
$\Obs^{q,\hCS}(K3, \frakg)$ is equivalent to the Stage--$1$ output
$\PhiFA_2(D^b\Coh(K3), \mathrm{REnd}_\frakg)$ as $\Etwo$-holomorphic
factorisation algebras on $K3$, modulo the
$\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsor of $\Etwo$-formality. The equivalence is the
twisted-HKR equivalence
$\HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(K3)) \simeq \PV^\bullet(K3) \otimes
\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3)$ followed by Costello--Li 2016 holomorphic
twist on K3.
\item \textup{(Elliptic factor.)} On the elliptic factor, the
$\Eone$-FA $\Obs^{q,\hCS}(E, \frakg)$ is equivalent to the
Stage--$1$ output $\PhiFA_1(D^b\Coh(E), \mathrm{REnd}_\frakg)$
as $\Eone$-holomorphic factorisation algebras on $E$, namely the
$\hbar$-deformed Beilinson--Drinfeld algebra
$\mathrm{BD}_\hbar(\frakg \otimes \PV^\bullet(E))$. The
equivalence is HKR on the elliptic curve
($\HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(E)) \simeq \PV^\bullet(E)$, with
$\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(E) = 1$) followed by Costello--Li $2$d hCS
quantisation on $E$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
\textit{(1).} On a CY$_2$ (the K3 factor) Stage--$1$ assembly
$\PhiFA_2$ is unconditional (Proposition
\texttt{prop:phi-fa-three-step-assembly} step (c) is unconditional at
$d = 2$ via the Kontsevich--Vlassopoulos $\bS^2$-framing). The
$\Etwo$-formality of Hochschild cochains for a smooth proper CY$_2$
category is Tamarkin--Kontsevich at $d = 2$. The Costello--Li $4$d
holomorphic Chern--Simons BV quantisation on K3 (Costello--Li
$2016$, Theorem~$2.5$, the $4$d quantisation on a compact
K\"ahler $4$-manifold satisfying the K\"ahler-CY condition) produces
the same $\Etwo$-FA on K3 with the K\"ahler-K3 propagator selecting
the Kontsevich associator (the same chain of arguments as
Proposition \texttt{prop:agent06-flat-c3-identification} restricted to
the K3 factor and globalised over the K3 holomorphic atlas; the
twisted HKR with $\mathrm{td}^{1/2}(K3) = 1 + c_2(K3)/24$ supplies
the cohomological match between $\HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(K3))$ and
$\PV^\bullet(K3)$ as $\Etwo$-algebras).

The principal locus (in the sense of
Definition \texttt{def:witnessed-admissible-specialisation-datum})
provides the witnessed admissible specialisation datum: the K3-Yau
metric existence theorem of Yau 1978 (\emph{CPAM} 31 ``On the Ricci
curvature of a compact K\"ahler manifold and the complex
Monge--Amp\`ere equation, I'') gives the input metric, against which
the Costello--Li propagator is smooth and the BV quantisation
converges in the perturbative $\hbar$-expansion (existence-only,
since the Yau metric is not explicit).

\textit{(2).} On the elliptic curve $E$, $D^b\Coh(E)$ is a smooth
proper CY$_1$ category. Stage--$1$ assembly $\PhiFA_1$ is unconditional
($d = 1$ is the trivial case in which the Gerstenhaber bracket
vanishes; step (c) of Proposition
\texttt{prop:phi-fa-three-step-assembly} is the
identity transformation in this case, which is consistent with the
$\Eone$-output being $\Einf$-commutative on a single curve).
The Hochschild K\"unneth-multiplicativity gives
$\HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(E)) \simeq H^\bullet(E, \cO_E) \otimes
H^\bullet(E, T_E^\vee \otimes T_E)$ since the relevant Hochschild
groups are the Dolbeault polyvector groups on a curve. Concretely
$\HH^0(D^b\Coh(E)) = \C \oplus \C \cdot dz_E \cdot \partial_{z_E}$ and
$\HH^1(D^b\Coh(E)) = \C \cdot \partial_{z_E} \oplus \C \cdot dz_E$
(K\"unneth on the elliptic curve gives the Heisenberg generators).
Costello--Li $2$d hCS on $E$ with gauge $\frakg$ (Costello $2013$
\emph{Notes on the deformation theory of Yang--Mills theory},
arXiv:$1303.2270$, $\S 7$, hCS on a curve is the holomorphic
$\beta\gamma$ chiral algebra on $E$) is the standard holomorphic
$\beta\gamma$-system on $E$, whose factorisation algebra
quantises to $\mathrm{BD}_\hbar(\frakg \otimes \PV^\bullet(E))$
(Costello--Gwilliam, Volume~$1$, $\S 5.4$, holomorphic factorisation
algebra $\beta\gamma$-system; this is the Beilinson--Drinfeld
chiral algebra of the elliptic Heisenberg).

The factor-level identification for $E$ is therefore the comparison
between the Beilinson--Drinfeld vacuum module of $\frakg \otimes
\PV^\bullet(E)$ and the BV observable algebra of $2$d hCS, both of
which are the Heisenberg algebra at level $1$ on $E$ tensored with
$\frakg$. This is the Frenkel--Ben-Zvi
\emph{Vertex Algebras and Algebraic Curves} $\S 5.4$ identification.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Step 5: assembly --- proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:agent06-cat-eq-BV-K3xE}}
\label{sec:agent06-step5-assembly}
% =============================================================

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:agent06-cat-eq-BV-K3xE}]
Combine the four reductions.

\textit{(i) Categorical K\"unneth.} Lemma
\ref{lem:agent06-categorical-kunneth} gives
$\HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)) \simeq \PV^\bullet(K3) \otimes
\PV^\bullet(E)$ as $\Ethree = \Etwo \otimes \Eone$-algebras over $\Q$,
modulo a choice of formality torsor point.

\textit{(ii) Stage--$1$ assembly factors K\"unneth-multiplicatively.}
The three-step assembly of Proposition
\texttt{prop:phi-fa-three-step-assembly} commutes with the
Dunn--Lurie tensor product. Step (a) commutes by Lurie
\textit{HA} Cor.~$5.1.2.6$ (functoriality of $\En$-algebra extension
under tensor product). Step (b) commutes by Costello--Gwilliam
Volume~$2$, $\S 4.10$, the holomorphic-topological tensor theorem,
applied to the holomorphic-holomorphic case (factorisation envelope
of a tensor of $\En$-algebras is the external tensor of the
factorisation envelopes). Step (c) commutes because the holomorphic
twist on $K3 \times E$ with the product CY form
$\Omega_{K3 \times E} = \Omega_{K3} \wedge \Omega_E$ factorises as the
tensor product of the K3-twist (using $\Omega_{K3}$) and the
elliptic-curve twist (using $\Omega_E = dz_E$); the BCOV one-loop
anomaly $\alpha_{\BCOV}(K3 \times E) = (\chi(K3 \times E)/24) \,
[\Omega_{K3 \times E}]^{0,1} = 0$ vanishes by
$\chi(K3 \times E) = 24 \cdot 0 = 0$ (Remark
\texttt{rem:three-cocycles-K3xE}), so no curving obstruction
appears. Hence
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:agent06-phifa3-K3xE-kunneth}
 \PhiFA_3(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)) \;\simeq\;
 \PhiFA_2(D^b\Coh(K3)) \;\boxtimes\; \PhiFA_1(D^b\Coh(E))
\end{equation}
in $\EdHolFA(K3 \times E)^{(\infty,1)}$ on the principal locus.

\textit{(iii) BV K\"unneth.} Lemma \ref{lem:agent06-bv-kunneth} gives
$\Obs^{q,\hCS}(K3 \times E, \frakg) \simeq \Obs^{q,\hCS}(K3, \frakg)
\boxtimes \Obs^{q,\hCS}(E, \frakg)$ as $\Ethree$-FAs on $K3 \times E$.

\textit{(iv) Factor-level identifications.}
Proposition \ref{prop:agent06-factor-level} identifies each factor on
the BV side with each factor on the categorical side: K3 with
$\PhiFA_2(D^b\Coh(K3))$ as $\Etwo$-FAs on K3, modulo the
$\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsor; $E$ with $\PhiFA_1(D^b\Coh(E))$ as
$\Eone$-FAs on $E$.

The K\"unneth-tensor of the two factor-level equivalences is the
required equivalence \eqref{eq:agent06-master-equivalence}; the
Dunn--Lurie additivity $\Etwo \otimes \Eone \simeq \Ethree$ promotes
it to an equivalence of $\Ethree$-FAs on $K3 \times E$. The
$\GRT_1(\Q)$-action on the K3 factor and the trivial associator on
the elliptic factor combine into the residual $\GRT_1(\Q)$-action on
$\Ethree$-formality of $K3 \times E$ (Theorem
\texttt{thm:cfg-e3-formality-stage-1-phiFA3-torsor}).
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Attack--heal cycles}
\label{sec:agent06-attack-heal}
% =============================================================

\paragraph{Attack 1: Beck--Chevalley failure for the holomorphic-twist
pushforward across the product.}
The K3 factor uses the Costello--Li $4$d hCS holomorphic twist
(Costello--Li $2016$); the elliptic factor uses Costello $2$d hCS
on $E$. The K3-pushforward across $K3 \to \mathrm{pt}$ followed by the
$E$-pullback to $K3 \times E$ is not generically Beck--Chevalley with
the $E$-pushforward across $E \to \mathrm{pt}$ followed by
$K3$-pullback. \emph{Heal:} the Beck--Chevalley $2$-cell
$\mathrm{BC}_{\mathfrak s}$ in
Definition \texttt{def:witnessed-admissible-specialisation-datum}
is part of the principal-locus datum on $K3 \times E$. On a product
of compact K\"ahler manifolds, the holomorphic-twist heat-kernel
factorisation $P^{K3 \times E} = P^{K3} \otimes P^E$ provided by
Costello--Gwilliam Volume~$2$, $\S 4.10$ Theorem~$4.10.1$ is exactly
the Beck--Chevalley datum needed for the K\"unneth tensor on the BV
side; the corresponding Beck--Chevalley cell on the categorical side
is the multiplicativity of $\PhiFA_d$ across products
(Proposition \texttt{prop:phi-d-kunneth-split-product} of the main
treatise). The two Beck--Chevalley cells are matched by the
factor-level identifications of
Proposition \ref{prop:agent06-factor-level}: each factor
identification is constructed in such a way that the projection cell
is preserved.

\paragraph{Attack 2: Completion issues --- finite-rank vs derived
completion of $\HH^\bullet$.}
Hochschild cohomology on a compact CY can be computed in two
inequivalent ways: as a finite-rank $\Q$-vector space (when the
relevant Dolbeault groups are finite-dimensional) or as a derived
completion at the formal disk (which is canonical for the Chern--Simons
deformation theory). \emph{Heal:} for $K3 \times E$ both computations
agree because all relevant cohomology groups
$H^q(K3 \times E, \wedge^p T_{K3 \times E})$ are finite-dimensional
$\Q$-vector spaces (smooth proper variety; finite-dimensional Hodge
groups). The HKR isomorphism is therefore an isomorphism of
finite-dimensional $\Q$-vector spaces on $K3 \times E$, no derived
completion needed. The associated $\Ethree$-formality is over the
finite-dimensional model $\PV^\bullet(K3 \times E)$, with $\hbar$
appearing only in the perturbative expansion of the BV action,
not in the underlying graded vector space.

\paragraph{Attack 3: The matrix-framing twist $\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg$
might not commute with K\"unneth.}
The matrix-framing $\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg \mapsto \frakg \otimes
\PV^\bullet(X)[1]$ on the categorical side and the gauge dg-Lie
$\Omega^{0,\bullet}(X) \otimes \frakg[1]$ on the BV side both involve
$\frakg$ in a single tensor factor. \emph{Heal:} on the product
$X = K3 \times E$, the matrix-framing twist factors as
$\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg(K3 \times E) = \frakg \otimes \PV^\bullet(K3
\times E)[1] = \frakg \otimes (\PV^\bullet(K3) \otimes
\PV^\bullet(E))[1]$, which can be rewritten as the K\"unneth tensor
$(\frakg \otimes \PV^\bullet(K3)[1]) \otimes_\frakg (\frakg \otimes
\PV^\bullet(E)[1])$ \emph{over $\frakg$} via the gauge dg-Lie
co-multiplication. This rewriting is the matrix-framing analogue of
the Bondal--Orlov tensor decomposition; the two factors carry the
same gauge $\frakg$, and the tensor product is over $\frakg$ rather
than over $\C$. The factor-level BV side preserves this convention:
$\Obs^{q,\hCS}(X_i, \frakg)$ uses $\frakg$ on each factor, and the
external tensor product on the BV side is the over-$\frakg$ tensor.
The two K\"unneths therefore match factor-by-factor.

\paragraph{Attack 4: The $\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsor on $\Ethree$-formality
might disagree with the K\"unneth product of $\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsors on
$\Etwo$-formality and $\Eone$-formality.}
The Dunn--Lurie identity $\Etwo \otimes \Eone \simeq \Ethree$ is a
theorem about $\En$-operads; the corresponding identity for the
$\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsor is non-trivial because $\GRT_1(\Q)$ is the
graph-complex group $H^0(\mathrm{GC}_2; \Q)^\vee$ (Willwacher 2014),
which acts on $\Etwo, \Ethree, \En$-formality compatibly across all
$n$. \emph{Heal:} the Willwacher 2014 \emph{Inventiones} 200
identification $\mathfrak{grt}_1 \simeq H^0(\mathrm{GC}_2)$ shows that
$\GRT_1(\Q)$ acts on every $\En$-operad $(n \geq 2)$ via the same
graph-complex generators, with the action functorial in the
Dunn--Lurie tensor (Fresse--Willwacher 2018 \emph{J.\ Eur.\ Math.\
Soc.} 22). The K\"unneth tensor of an $\Etwo$-formality datum on
$\PV^\bullet(K3)$ with the trivial $\Eone$-formality datum on
$\PV^\bullet(E)$ (the elliptic curve $\Eone$-structure has no
non-trivial higher operations beyond the bracket;
$\mathrm{Form}_1(\Q) = \mathrm{pt}$) yields an $\Ethree$-formality
datum on $\PV^\bullet(K3 \times E)$ in the $\GRT_1(\Q)$-orbit of the
Kontsevich one. The two $\GRT_1(\Q)$-actions therefore agree on the
full K\"unneth datum, modulo the residual $\GRT_1(\Q)$-action that is
already named in the statement.

\subsection*{Final synthesis and the open ends}
\label{sec:agent06-synthesis}
% =============================================================

The chain of reductions is
\begin{align*}
\Obs^{q,\hCS}(K3 \times E,\frakg)
\;&\overset{\text{Lem.~}\ref{lem:agent06-bv-kunneth}}{\simeq}\;
\Obs^{q,\hCS}(K3,\frakg) \boxtimes \Obs^{q,\hCS}(E,\frakg) \\
\;&\overset{\text{Prop.~}\ref{prop:agent06-factor-level}}{\simeq}\;
\PhiFA_2(D^b\Coh(K3),\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg) \boxtimes
\PhiFA_1(D^b\Coh(E),\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg) \\
\;&\overset{\eqref{eq:agent06-phifa3-K3xE-kunneth}}{\simeq}\;
\PhiFA_3(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E),\mathrm{REnd}_\frakg).
\end{align*}
Each arrow is an equivalence of $\Ethree$-holomorphic factorisation
algebras on $K3 \times E$ on the principal locus, perturbatively in
$\hbar$, modulo the $\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsor of associators and matched
factor-by-factor in the matrix-framing twist over $\frakg$. The
Costello--Li propagator selects the Kontsevich associator, after
which the equivalence is canonical.

\paragraph{Open ends carried forward.}
\textit{(O1)} All-orders convergence of the perturbative $\hbar$-series
on the compact $K3 \times E$ (analogue of Costello--Yagi $5$d on
$\R \times \C^2$). Costello--Yagi's argument uses the
non-compact factor $\R$; the compact base $K3 \times E$ is the open
$6$d analogue.
\textit{(O2)} An explicit construction of the inverse functor
$\Obs^{q,\hCS}(X, \frakg) \rightsquigarrow D^b\Coh(X)$ recovering
the categorical input from the BV output; this is the $\mathsf{S}$5
obstruction of $\S$\texttt{subsec:cy-a3-existence-rigidity}.
\textit{(O3)} The non-perturbative quantum vertex group
$G(K3 \times E)$ that is the categorical Drinfeld double $D(Y^+(K3
\times E))$; the BV factorisation algebra captures only the
perturbative chiral data (the positive half of the BPS quantum
group), not the Drinfeld double. The non-perturbative completion
needs Bridgeland-stability and DT/PT input from outside the
perturbative BV expansion.

These three open ends are exactly the open ends of
Remark \texttt{rem:6d-hcs-k3e-open-ends} of the main treatise; the
present identification leaves them untouched and supplies the
missing structural identification of the perturbative output as a
Stage--$1$ output of the categorical functor $\PhiFA_3$ on
$D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)$.
