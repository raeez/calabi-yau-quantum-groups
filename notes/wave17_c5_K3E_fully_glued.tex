\section*{The compact positive half of \(K3\times E\)}
\label{sec:k3e-positive-half-gluing}

Let \(X=S\times E\), where \(S\) is an elliptic \(K3\) surface with
section and \(E\) is an elliptic curve.  The compact Hodge calculation is
\[
  \sum_{q=0}^{3}(-1)^q h^{0,q}(X)
  =
  (1+1)(1-1)=0,
\]
equivalently
\[
  \chi(\cO_X)=\chi(\cO_S)\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot 0=0.
\]
Thus the categorical Euler characteristic and the compact
CY$_3$ Hodge supertrace have the same numerical value through different
constructions:
\[
  \kcat(X)=0,\qquad \kch^{\mathrm{comp}}(X)=0.
\]
The \(K3\times E\) spectrum used by Vol III records four separated
constructions:
\[
  \kcat(X)=0,\qquad
  \kHeis(X)=3,\qquad
  \kBKM(\Delfive)=5,\qquad
  \kfib(S)=\operatorname{rk}\widetilde H(S,\mathbb Z)=24.
\]
The Borcherds weight belongs to the denominator input. The compact
Hodge supertrace, the Heisenberg--Mukai specialization, and the fibre
Mukai rank are separate constructions.  No additive law relates them.

\subsection*{Automorphic normalization}

The primitive Jacobi input is the Eichler--Zagier normalized
\(\phi_{0,1}^{\mathrm{EZ}}\), with discriminant coefficients
\[
  c_1(-1)=1,\qquad c_1(0)=10.
\]
The \(K3\) elliptic genus is
\[
  \operatorname{Ell}(K3;\tau,z)=2\,\phi_{0,1}^{\mathrm{EZ}}(\tau,z).
\]
Borcherds' weight formula gives
\[
  \kBKM(\Delfive)=\frac{c_1(0)}{2}=5.
\]
The monic denominator section and the reduced-DT square are
\[
  \Dfive=64^{-1}\Delfive,\qquad
  \PhiOP=\Dfive^2=4096^{-1}\Delfive^{\,2}.
\]
The unnormalized Igusa square is
\[
  \Phiun=\Delfive^{\,2}.
\]
The primitive denominator is \(\Delfive\); \(\Phiun\) and \(\PhiOP\)
belong to the scalar-square partition-function lane.

For the CHL-averaged paramodular denominator convention used here one has
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
N & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 6\\
\hline
c_N(0) & 10 & 8 & 6 & 4 & 2\\
\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2 & 5 & 4 & 3 & 2 & 1
\end{array}
\]
This row is a denominator row.  It is disjoint from the OP scalar square
and from the Gritsenko--Clery eight-form catalogue except where a row
map and normalization are named.

\subsection*{Local positive halves}

Let \(\mathfrak U=\{U_\alpha\to S\}\) be an admissible etale
symplectic/NCCR atlas.  Its data consist of \(2\)-Calabi--Yau
quiver-with-potential models \((\cA_\alpha,W_\alpha)\) on the charts,
derived Morita equivalences on overlaps, coherent Cech \(2\)-cells, and
orientation data for vanishing cycles.  This is an input to the compact
gluing problem.  Darboux charts alone supply local symplectic
coordinates; critical CoHA charts require the NCCR data above.

On \(U_\alpha\times E\) the positive half is the completed critical CoHA
of the \(K3\) chart tensored with the \(E\)-oscillator:
\[
  \mathcal Y_\alpha^{+}
  =
  \left(
  \bigoplus_{d}
  H_c^\bullet\bigl(\cM_d(\cA_\alpha),\varphi_{W_\alpha}\bigr)
  \right)^{\wedge}
  \widehat\otimes
  \Heis\bigl(H^1(E,\mathbb C)\bigr).
\]
The Heisenberg factor has rank \(2\) and Fock character
\[
  \chi_{\Heis(E)}(q)=\prod_{n\geq 1}(1-q^n)^{-2}.
\]
Since \(\dim_{\mathbb C}X=3\), the specialised output of \(\Phi_3\) is
\(\Eone\)-chiral.  The \(\Etwo\) structure lives on the centre, the
Hall--Drinfeld double, or the module category of the positive half.

The toric normalization is fixed by the local model
\[
  \CoHA(\mathbb C^3)=Y^{+}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1).
\]
The full \(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}\) object requires a double, centre,
completion, or evaluation construction.

\subsection*{Compact descent data}

The compact positive-half gluing uses the following conditional datum.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(H1)}] \emph{Root datum.}  The primitive BKM root lattice is
  \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{BKM}}\cong\Lambda_{\mathrm{II}}^{2,1}\), with real
  simple roots \(f_2,f_3,f_{-2}\), Gram matrix \(2(2I-J_3)\), and root
  multiplicities given by the coefficients of
  \(\phi_{0,1}^{\mathrm{EZ}}\).
\item[\textup{(H2)}] \emph{Orientation.}  The vanishing-cycle CoHAs carry
  compatible Kontsevich--Soibelman orientation data on all overlaps, and
  the reduced \(K3\) cosection orientation is fixed for the compact
  primitive sector.
\item[\textup{(H3)}] \emph{Framing.}  The local \(\Eone\)-factorisation
  structures are framed over \(\Ran(U_\alpha\times E)\), and the
  mutation cocycle preserves the BV trace used by Hall integration.
\item[\textup{(H4)}] \emph{Completion.}  All CoHAs are completed with
  respect to the effective-charge filtration.  Cech totalisation is
  taken in the complete symmetric monoidal category of
  \(\Eone\)-factorisation bialgebras, and completed tensoring with the
  fixed \(E\)-oscillator is exact for this filtration.
\item[\textup{(H5)}] \emph{Denominator recognition.}  The
  Hall--Drinfeld double of the completed positive half admits a Hopf
  pairing whose Weyl--Kac--Borcherds denominator is
  \(\Dfive(2Z)=64^{-1}\Delfive(2Z)\).
\end{enumerate}

Under \(\textup{(H1)}\)--\(\textup{(H4)}\), the compact positive half is
the completed Cech totalisation
\[
  \Ypos_X
  =
  \Tot\left(
  \prod_\alpha \mathcal Y_\alpha^{+}
  \rightrightarrows
  \prod_{\alpha\beta}\mathcal Y_{\alpha\beta}^{+}
  \rightthreetimes
  \prod_{\alpha\beta\gamma}\mathcal Y_{\alpha\beta\gamma}^{+}
  \cdots
  \right).
\]
The chart-independent \(E\)-oscillator factors through the completed
tensor product:
\[
  \Ypos_X
  \simeq
  \Tot\left(\prod_\alpha
    \CoHA(\cA_\alpha,W_\alpha)^{\wedge}\rightrightarrows\cdots
  \right)
  \widehat\otimes
  \Heis\bigl(H^1(E,\mathbb C)\bigr).
\]
This is an isomorphism in the completed factorisation category.  Its
Euler-character specialisation is obtained only after the reduced Hall
integration map is applied.

Condition \(\textup{(H5)}\) is extra structure on the double, independent
of the OP scalar theorem and the reciprocal Igusa character.

\subsection*{Reduced character}

The bare product
\[
  \eta(\tau)^{-24}\eta(\tau)^{-2}
\]
is the unreduced Fock character of the \(K3\)-Mukai oscillators and the
\(E\)-oscillator.  The compact enumerative character starts with the
reduced obstruction theory.
Oberdieck--Pixton prove the primitive Igusa formula in reduced
Gromov--Witten theory; the reduced GW/PT/DT comparison in the OP
normalization gives the DT scalar
\[
  Z_{\mathrm{DT}}^{\mathrm{red}}(K3\times E)
  =
  -(\PhiOP)^{-1}
  =
  -\Dfive^{-2}
  =
  -4096\,\Delfive^{-2}.
\]
After a Joyce--Song/Hall integration map compatible with the reduced
obstruction theory, the unsigned scalar attached to the candidate
positive half is
\[
  \chi_{\mathrm{red}}^{+}(q,\zeta,p)=(\PhiOP(q,\zeta,p))^{-1}
\]
and the Behrend sign gives \(Z_{\mathrm{DT}}^{\mathrm{red}}=-\chi_{\mathrm{red}}^{+}\).
The Hall product, coproduct, Hopf pairing, PBW theorem, and denominator
recognition remain additional data.

The Humbert residue records the rank-\(48\) Heisenberg check in the
unnormalized square convention.  For
\[
  Z=\begin{pmatrix}\tau&z\\ z&\sigma\end{pmatrix}\in\mathcal H_2,
  \qquad
  q=e^{2\pi i\tau},
\]
Gritsenko--Nikulin give
\[
  \Phiun(\tau,z,\sigma)
  =
  (2\pi iz)^2\eta(\tau)^{24}\eta(\sigma)^{24}+O(z^4)
\]
at the separating Humbert divisor.  Hence on \(\sigma=\tau\),
\[
  \left[(2\pi iz)^2\Phiun(\tau,z,\tau)^{-1}\right]_{z=0}
  =
  \eta(\tau)^{-48}
  =
  q^{-2}\prod_{n\geq 1}(1-q^n)^{-48}.
\]
The right-hand side is the conformally shifted Fock character of
\(\Heis(\Muk(S)^{\oplus 2})\), since
\(\operatorname{rk}\Muk(S)^{\oplus 2}=48\).

\subsection*{Double and denominator}

The reduced scalar \((\PhiOP)^{-1}\) supplies only the integrated
positive-half scalar.  The BKM object requires the compact
Hall--Drinfeld double, the orientation-compatible Hopf pairing, and the
denominator recognition in \(\textup{(H5)}\).  With these data the
primitive denominator is
\[
  \operatorname{den}(\mathfrak g_{\Delfive})=\Dfive(2Z),
  \qquad
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delfive})=5.
\]
The scalar \(\PhiOP=\Dfive^2\) is the OP reduced-DT square.  The
primitive BKM denominator remains the weight-\(5\) form \(\Delfive\).

\subsection*{Literature}

Oberdieck--Pixton, \texttt{arXiv:1706.10100}, prove the primitive
\(K3\times E\) reduced theory and the Igusa cusp form conjecture.
Oberdieck--Pandharipande, \texttt{arXiv:1411.1514}, formulate the
\(K3\times E\) Igusa \(\chi_{10}\) curve-counting identity and its
stable-pairs form.  Gritsenko--Nikulin, \texttt{alg-geom/9611028},
construct the \(\Delfive\) denominator and the Humbert residue.
Borcherds' automorphic-product theorem gives weight \(c(0)/2\).
Nakajima's Hilbert-scheme Heisenberg action gives the
\(\prod_{n\geq 1}(1-q^n)^{-\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(S)}\) character.
Kontsevich--Soibelman give critical CoHAs with vanishing cycles;
Davison--Meinhardt give mutation-natural coproducts after orientation
data are fixed.
