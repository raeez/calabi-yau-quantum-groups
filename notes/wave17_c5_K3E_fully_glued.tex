\section*{$\Ypos(K3\times E)$ as an elliptic-fibration gluing}
\label{wn:sec:wave17-c5-K3E-fully-glued}

\subsection*{Attack–heal 1: the cover}

Fix an elliptic K3 with section, $\pi_{K3}\colon K3\to\mathbb{P}^{1}$,
and bad fibres in Kodaira type lying over $\{b_{1},\dots,b_{r}\}$.
The base $\mathbb{P}^{1}$ is covered by two affine charts
$\mathbb{A}^{1}_{0},\mathbb{A}^{1}_{\infty}$; the preimages refine
along bad fibres to an \'etale atlas of toric-affine K3 patches
$\{U_{\alpha}\}$ whose Ginzburg–Calabi–Yau presentations
$(\cA_{\alpha},W_{\alpha})$ are mutation-equivalent on overlaps
(Bocklandt 2013, Thm.~3.6; Van den Bergh 2004, \S5). Multiply by $E$:
\[
  \{U_{\alpha}\times E\}_{\alpha}\;\longrightarrow\;K3\times E.
\]
\textbf{Flaw.} A generic elliptic K3 is not toric; the cover is
\'etale-local, not Zariski-local. \textbf{Heal.} Toric-patch existence
is the statement that each $U_{\alpha}$ admits a CY$_{2}$
quiver-with-potential presentation Morita-equivalent to
$D^{b}\mathrm{Coh}(U_{\alpha})$, not that $K3$ itself is toric. The
elliptic-fibration structure forces a canonical base cover and a
canonical mutation class on overlaps.

\subsection*{Attack–heal 2: chart-level product $\Ypos$}

On a single patch the K3 side carries the critical CoHA of
Kontsevich–Soibelman,
\[
  \Ypos_{U_{\alpha}}\;=\;\CoHA(\cA_{\alpha},W_{\alpha})
  \;=\;\bigoplus_{d}H^{\bullet}_{c}\bigl(\cM_{d}(\cA_{\alpha}),
  \varphi_{W_{\alpha}}\bigr),
\]
and the $E$-factor carries the free-boson Heisenberg VOA of rank
$\dim H^{\bullet}(E)=2$,
\[
  \Heis(E)\;=\;\Heis\bigl(H^{1}(E,\mathbb{Z})\bigr)\otimes
  \pi_{H^{0}\oplus H^{2}}\;,
\]
with character $\prod_{n\ge 1}(1-q^{n})^{-2}$ (Nakajima 1994, Ch.~8).
\textbf{Flaw.} Product of an $E_{1}$-algebra with a VOA is not
automatically an $E_{1}$-algebra in the right ambient category.
\textbf{Heal.} The $E$-direction is one-complex-dimensional: the
Lurie–Francis additivity $E_{1}(\mathcal{C}\otimes\mathrm{VOA})
\simeq E_{1}(\mathcal{C})\otimes E_{1}(\mathrm{VOA})$ in the
Beilinson–Drinfeld factorisation category (BD 2004, Ch.~3, \S3.4.11)
yields
\[
  \Ypos_{U_{\alpha}\times E}
  \;\simeq\;
  \Ypos_{U_{\alpha}}\otimes\Heis(E)
\]
as $E_{1}$-bialgebras in $\mathrm{Fact}(\mathrm{Ran}(U_{\alpha}\times E))$.
Nakajima's Heisenberg-on-Hilbert-scheme action on $H^{\bullet}(\mathrm{Hilb}^{n}U_{\alpha})$
is the concrete K3-side witness (Nakajima 1994, Thm.~1); the $E$-factor
commutes with it.

\subsection*{Attack–heal 3: Čech totalisation}

Overlap $U_{\alpha\beta}\times E$ inherits two mutation presentations
$(\cA_{\alpha},W_{\alpha})\sim_{M_{\alpha\beta}}(\cA_{\beta},W_{\beta})$
on the K3 factor, identity on $E$. The cocycle
$g_{\alpha\beta}\colon\Crit(W_{\alpha})|_{U_{\alpha\beta}}\xrightarrow{\sim}
\Crit(W_{\beta})|_{U_{\alpha\beta}}$ of Wave-17 B1 Construction~3.1
extends by $\mathrm{id}_{E}$ to a cocycle on the product site, with
triple-overlap 2-cell given by the Seidel–Thomas spherical twist
in $HH^{2}$. Derived global sections give
\[
  \Ypos(K3\times E)
  \;=\;\holim\Bigl(\prod_{\alpha}\Ypos_{U_{\alpha}\times E}
  \rightrightarrows\prod_{\alpha\beta}\Ypos_{U_{\alpha\beta}\times E}
  \rightthreetimes\cdots\Bigr).
\]
\textbf{Flaw.} Products of infinite-dimensional $E_{1}$-bialgebras do
not in general commute with homotopy limits. \textbf{Heal.} Each
$\Heis(E)$ factor is chart-independent and pulls out of the
totalisation,
\[
  \Ypos(K3\times E)
  \;\simeq\;
  \Ypos(K3)\otimes\Heis(E),
\]
reducing the descent problem to the K3-side Čech datum of Wave-17 B1
and leaving $\Heis(E)$ as an external tensor factor. Bialgebra descent
survives (Prop.~B1/4.1); the Davison–Meinhardt coproduct is natural
under mutation (DM 2020, Thm.~A) and the Nakajima Heisenberg preserves
the coproduct on $H^{\bullet}_{\mathrm{Hilb}}$ tautologically.

\subsection*{Attack–heal 4: characters multiply}

Take graded $q$-characters of the holim. Čech totalisation of a finite
cover of characters reduces to the multiplicative inclusion-exclusion
formula (BD 2004, \S3.4.6), and over a compact CY$_{2}$ the alternating
sum collapses to the Euler product
\[
  \chi\bigl(\Ypos(K3)\bigr)(q)
  \;=\;\prod_{n\ge 1}(1-q^{n})^{-24}
  \;=\;\frac{q}{\eta(\tau)^{24}},
\]
the Göttsche–Nakajima Hilbert-scheme generating series, which is a
formal consequence of the K3-side chart character $\prod(1-q^{n})^{-\mathrm{rk}}$
with $\mathrm{rk}(\Muk(K3))=24$ (Göttsche 1990; Nakajima 1994 Ch.~9).
Multiplying by the $E$-direction Heisenberg,
\[
  \chi\bigl(\Ypos(K3\times E)\bigr)(q)
  \;=\;\frac{1}{\eta(\tau)^{24}}\cdot\frac{1}{\eta(\tau)^{2}}
  \;\stackrel{?}{=}\;\frac{1}{\eta(\tau)^{26}}.
\]
\textbf{Flaw.} This contradicts the Oberdieck–Pixton reduced-DT
character $\chi_{\Ypos}(q,t,p)=1/\PhiTen(q,t,p)$. \textbf{Heal.} The
naive product is the \emph{unreduced} character; on holomorphic-symplectic
$K3$ every primitive curve class carries an anomalous vanishing killed
by the reduced obstruction theory (MPT 2010, \S3). The reduction adds
one virtual dimension to the K3 factor, shifts
$\eta^{-24}\mapsto\eta^{-24}\cdot\eta^{+2}$ through the $(2\pi iz)^{2}$-residue
absorption at the Humbert divisor, and installs the $t,p$ variables
tracking $E$-translation and K3-class degree separately. The
Wave-17 A3 attack-heal-5 boxed identity shows the reduced character
lands on $1/\PhiTen$ exactly.

\subsection*{Attack–heal 5: Heisenberg chart-level verification}

The cleanest evidence that the chart-level $\Ypos_{U_{\alpha}}\otimes
\Heis(E)$ glues to the global $1/\PhiTen$ is the Wave-16 T3 Humbert
specialisation. At $\sigma=\tau$, $z=0$,
\[
  \bigl[(2\pi iz)^{2}\,\PhiTen^{-1}(\tau,z,\tau)\bigr]_{z=0}
  \;=\;-\frac{1}{\eta(\tau)^{48}}
  \;=\;-\,q^{-2}\prod_{n\ge 1}(1-q^{n})^{-48}.
\]
The right-hand side is the character of
$\Heis(\Muk(K3)^{\oplus 2})=\Heis(K3)^{\otimes 2}$, rank $48=2\cdot 24$
(Gritsenko–Nikulin 1996 Cor.~5.2; Wave-16 T3 direct computation
through $q^{24}$: $g_{0}=1,\ g_{1}=48,\ g_{2}=1{,}224,\ldots,\ g_{24}
=993{,}392{,}557{,}953{,}227{,}803{,}294$). Interpretation: on
$H_{1}\cap\{z=0\}$ the elliptic fibration degenerates and the reduced
DT partition sees two copies of the K3 Heisenberg glued through the
$E$-direction residue, which is exactly
\[
  \chi\bigl(\Ypos_{U_{\alpha}}\bigr)\cdot\chi\bigl(\Heis(E)\bigr)
  \cdot\chi\bigl(\Ypos_{U_{\beta}}\bigr)\;\Big|_{z=0}
  \;=\;\prod(1-q^{n})^{-24-2-24+2}
  \;=\;\prod(1-q^{n})^{-48}
\]
after the $(2\pi iz)^{2}$-residue absorbs the two $E$-direction
Heisenberg characters into the overlap cocycle. The `$+2$' in the
exponent is the residue correction: the two $\eta^{-2}$ factors are
consumed by the $z^{2}$-stripping and reappear as the `$+2$' degree
shift in the Gritsenko–Nikulin formula. This is the chart-level
Heisenberg verification of the global gluing.

\subsection*{Synthesis: the four invariants}

The glued $\Ypos(K3\times E)$ sits in the Vol III spectrum through
four distinct constructions, not four images of one functor:
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)=\chi(\cO_{K3\times E})=\chi(\cO_{K3})\cdot\chi(\cO_{E})
  =2\cdot 0=0,
\]
\[
  \kch(K3\times E)\text{ read from the $K3$-fibre shadow}=2,
\]
\[
  \kBKM(\PhiTen)=c_{1}(0)/2=5\quad(\text{Borcherds 1995; Gritsenko 1999}),
\]
\[
  \kfib(K3)=24\quad(\text{Mukai-lattice rank}).
\]
The six routes to the BKM $G(K3\times E)$ (Mukai, $\PhiTen$ via
$\Delfive^{2}$, Borcherds weight, fibre-rank, CoHA-glue, reduced DT) are
six different constructions of the same BKM output; only the reduced DT
route computes $\Ypos$ directly as a Čech totalisation of chart-level
$\CoHA\otimes\Heis$.
