\section*{Gravitational identifications and BPS entropy}
\label{sec:gravitational-witten}

The gravitational reading of the CY-to-chiral correspondence has two
levels. The theorem-level statements are string-theoretic entropy
formulae at compactifications where a protected BPS index is controlled
by a modular form. The heuristic level is the comparison between shadow
invariants of the specialised chiral output and the central charges or
one-loop anomalies of the effective theory. Black-hole entropy, Hall
algebra, and automorphic denominator data require separate invariants.

\subsection*{Subscripted invariants on \texorpdfstring{$K3 \times E$}{K3 x E}}

The Calabi-Yau threefold \(K3 \times E\) carries the four numerical
values \(\{0,3,5,24\}\). The compact Hodge supertrace gives an
additional zero attached to the same total-space cancellation:
\[
\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}(K3 \times E)=0,\qquad
\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(K3 \times E)=0,\qquad
\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3 \times E)=3,\qquad
\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Delta_5)=5,\qquad
\kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}(K3)=24.
\]
The categorical equality is Kunneth multiplicativity,
\(\chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot 0=0\). The compact
\(\kch\)-supertrace also vanishes on the odd-dimensional product by
Serre cancellation. The Heisenberg-Mukai specialisation on the elliptic
factor gives \(\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}=3\). The Borcherds value is the
weight of Gritsenko's paramodular cusp form \(\Delta_5\), equivalently
\(\kBKM(\Delta_5)=c_1(0)/2=10/2=5\). The fibre value \(24\) is the
rank of the Mukai lattice of the K3 fibre.

The equality \(5=2+3\) is numerical coincidence. The number
\(2=\chi(\cO_{K3})\) belongs to the fibre, the number \(3\) belongs to
the Heisenberg-Mukai specialisation, and the number \(5\) belongs to
the Borcherds denominator. They meet in the same physical corner
because the K3 fibre, the elliptic direction, and the paramodular
denominator are present simultaneously.

\subsection*{The specialised \texorpdfstring{$\Phi$}{Phi} output}

\[

\]
Thus compact CY\(_3\) and local toric CY\(_3\) positive Hall halves are

For the affine local model,
\[

\]
as an associative \(\Eone\)-Hall algebra. The vertex algebra

\hookrightarrow

The equality is with the positive half. The \(\cW_{1+\infty}\) object
requires the double, centre, completion, and evaluation step.

\subsection*{Black-hole entropy at compact scope}

Theorem. For Type IIA on \(K3\times T^2\), or equivalently the
corresponding M-theory lift in the Maldacena-Strominger-Witten
regime, a four-dimensional \(1/4\)-BPS black hole with charge
\(\gamma\) and positive quartic invariant \(\cI_4(\gamma)\) satisfies
\[
S_{\mathrm{BH}}(\gamma)
=\pi\sqrt{\cI_4(\gamma)}
+O(\log \cI_4(\gamma)).
\]
The microscopic side is the protected MSW index
\(d_{\mathrm{ind}}(\gamma)\). In the single-centre attractor chamber,
the leading saddle of this index gives the macroscopic entropy. Wall
crossing and fermionic cancellations belong to the index; the
Bekenstein-Hawking entropy reads the absolute leading exponential in
the chamber where the single-centre contribution is isolated.

Heuristic. The shadow comparison relates the leading central charge to
the appropriate specialised \(\kch\)-value. At the \(K3\times T^2\)
modular point the MSW CFT and the Borcherds/Igusa denominator are
explicit, so the entropy calculation is theorem-level. The
identification of the protected index with the first shadow component
is the comparison datum. For a general compact CY\(_3\), this datum
requires the MSW divisor data outside the K3-fibred locus. A local
toric CY\(_3\) supplies \(\Omega\)-background BPS counting; the
four-dimensional gravity decoupling limit is absent.

The anomaly polynomial gives, for an M5 wrapping a divisor
\(P\subset X\),
\[
c_L=P^3+c_2(X)\cdot P,\qquad
c_R=P^3+\frac12 c_2(X)\cdot P.
\]
For \(X=K3\times T^2\) and \(P=[K3]\), one has \(P^3=0\) and
\(c_2(X)\cdot P=24\), hence \(c_L=24\) and \(c_R=12\). This is the
central-charge calculation behind the clean \(K3\times T^2\) entropy
match.

\subsection*{Crystal melting and BTZ universality}

The topological vertex on \(\C^3\) gives the MacMahon function
\[

\]

entropy growth of order \(n^{2/3}\). BTZ black holes in
\(\mathrm{AdS}_3\) obey Cardy's growth, of order \(n^{1/2}\). These are

Heuristic. Plane partitions are a useful local model for BPS state

\subsection*{Finite-size corrections and shadow Cardy}
