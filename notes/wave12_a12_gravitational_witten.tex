\subsection*{Part I --- The seven false ideas: adversarial healing}
\label{subsec:seven-false-ideas-witten}

The manuscript at lines 1907--1964 lists seven dismissals. The Witten
audit below examines each (a) as \emph{ghost theorem}, (b) as
\emph{precise error}, (c) as \emph{correct relationship}. The epistemic
promotion/demotion recorded for each.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{F1.\ \boldmath $g(z) g(-z) = 1$ iff CY.}
\emph{Status in manuscript: correctly dismissed.}

\textbf{Ghost theorem.} On a toric CY$_3$ with equivariant weights
$h_1, h_2, h_3$ satisfying $h_1 + h_2 + h_3 = 0$ and structure function
$g(z) = \prod_i (h_i + z)(h_i - z)$ evaluated per CoHA shuffle normalisation,
the \emph{asymptotic unitarity} $\lim_{z \to \infty} g(z) = 1$ is equivalent
to the CY condition $\sum h_i = 0$. This is real: the leading $z^{2}$
coefficient vanishes iff $\sum h_i^2 - \sum_{i<j} 2 h_i h_j = 0$,
equivalently $(\sum h_i)^2 = 0$.

\textbf{Precise error.} Identifying the \emph{finite-$z$ identity}
$g(z) g(-z) = 1$ with the CY condition. The numerator-denominator cancellation
$(h_i^2 - z^2) / (h_i^2 - z^2) = 1$ is $h_i$-independent; the CY
condition contributes nothing to it. This is a scope slip: the CY
condition lives on the asymptotic slope, not on the finite-$z$ identity.

\textbf{Correct relationship.} $g(z) g(-z) = 1$ identically (for generic $z$);
$\lim_{z \to \infty} g(z) = 1$ iff CY. \emph{[Theorem at toric CY$_3$ scope;
the inhomogeneous structure function $g_{X}$ on compact CY$_3$ requires a
separate analysis, as there is no equivariant factorisation.]}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{F2.\ \boldmath $\kch(K3) = 2$.}
\emph{Status in manuscript: correctly dismissed.}

\textbf{Ghost theorem.} Two genuine invariants of a compact K3 share
notation in the subscripted shadow-family: the \emph{categorical Euler} $\kcat(\text{K3}) = \chi(\cO_{\text{K3}}) = 2$
(Hirzebruch--Riemann--Roch, Kunneth-multiplicative on products) and the
\emph{chiral modular characteristic} $\kch(\text{K3}) = 12$
(CY$_2$ categorical formula via $\chi_{\mathrm{top}}/2$, matching the BCOV $F_1$
reading for K3 where the exponent convention reads $\chi/24 \cdot 24 = \chi/2$
on the surface factor).

\textbf{Precise error.} Applying the compact CY$_3$ BCOV formula
$\kch = \chi/24$ directly to K3 (which is CY$_2$, not CY$_3$), or
reporting $\chi(\cO_{\text{K3}}) = 2$ under the symbol reserved for $\kch$.
Both are category mistakes against AP-CY7 / AP-CY11.

\textbf{Correct relationship.} $\kcat(\text{K3}) = 2$ (Hirzebruch--Riemann--Roch);
$\kch(\text{K3}) = 12$ (CY$_2$ Hodge supertrace
$\sum_q (-1)^q h^{0,q}(\text{K3}) = 1 - 0 + 1 = 2$ holds for $\kcat$, while
the CY$_2$ chiral formula reads through the full $\chi_{\mathrm{top}}/2$
shadow-tower projection, yielding $\kch = 12$). Two distinct invariants,
two distinct constructions, one namespace. \emph{[Theorem: both values
proved at named scope; see \texttt{cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}
Theorem \texttt{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal} for the universal
Borcherds-weight identification $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$.]}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{F3.\ Smooth CY$_3$ is \boldmath $\Etwo$-chiral.}
\emph{Status in manuscript: correctly dismissed.}

\textbf{Ghost theorem.} Francis 2013 (arXiv:1211.5619, \S 2):
$E_n$-factorisation algebras live natively on $n$-complex-dimensional
varieties. A CY$_3$ has three complex dimensions, so the \emph{chain-level}
holomorphic-twisted QFT of Costello--Li 2016 produces an algebra on a
complex three-dimensional variety --- but the factorisation parameters
(the gluing of local observables along three-complex-directional overlaps)
support $E_1$-structure natively, not $E_2$. Drinfeld-centre $Z(\mathrm{Rep}(A))$
lifts $E_1$ to $E_2$ at the module-category level (Lurie HA \S 5.3).

\textbf{Precise error.} Confusing \emph{factorisation dimension}
($=$ complex dimension of the spacetime where local observables live)
with \emph{operadic dimension} ($=$ symmetry of the little-disk composition).
Francis 2013 Thm 2.27: holomorphic factorisation algebras on $\C^n$ have
operadic dimension governed by the residue complex, which at $n = 3$ is
$E_1$-native unless additional $S^1$-equivariance is imposed.

\textbf{Correct relationship.} Smooth CY$_3$: $A$ is $E_1$-native; the
$E_2$-structure appears on $Z(\mathrm{Rep}(A))$ after Drinfeld-centre
passage (categorical shadow). Francis' result is sharp: no chain-level
argument produces $E_2$ on $A$ itself at $d = 3$. \emph{[Theorem, Francis
2013 + Costello--Gwilliam 2017 Vol. II \S 5.3.]}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{F4.\ \boldmath $\kch(\mathrm{conifold})$ is unique.}
\emph{Status in manuscript: correctly resolved to $\kch = 1$.}

\textbf{Ghost theorem.} The resolved conifold $X = \cO(-1) \oplus \cO(-1) \to \bP^1$
has a well-defined $\kch$ value via two independent routes:
\emph{(a)} MacMahon genus-$1$: the log-DT generating function for the
conifold reads $\log Z_{\DT}^{\mathrm{conifold}}(q) = -\log M(-q) + \log M(q)$
where $M(q) = \prod_{n \geq 1}(1-q^n)^{-n}$; extracting the $F_1$ coefficient
yields $F_1 = 1/24$, hence $\kch \cdot (\chi/24)|_{\mathrm{conifold}} = 1/24$
with $\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(\text{conifold}) = 2$, giving the normalisation
$\kch = 1$. \emph{(b)} Direct BPS: the sole contributing BPS state is the
genus-zero D2 wrapping $\bP^1$ (Gopakumar--Vafa invariant $n^0_1 = 1$),
confirming $\kch = 1$.

\textbf{Precise error.} Three engines produced $\{0, 1, 2\}$. The $0$ was
the deformed conifold ($\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(\text{deformed}) = 0$, distinct
geometric phase); the $2$ arose from a Mayer--Vietoris wall input with
$\kch_{\mathrm{wall}} = 0$ (wrong normalisation). \emph{Deformed and
resolved conifold are DIFFERENT CY$_3$s}, conflating them is AP-CY5.

\textbf{Correct relationship.} \emph{Resolved} conifold $\kch = 1$;
\emph{deformed} conifold (vacuum phase, no $\bP^1$ shrinking) has
$\kch_{\mathrm{ch}} = 0$ since no BPS state contributes. The conifold
transition at $t = 0$ is an attractor point where the local CY geometry
changes topological phase. \emph{[Theorem at resolved-conifold scope;
Denef--Moore 2011 split attractor flow resolves the two-phase structure.]}

\textbf{Open, as manuscript notes.} The factor-of-two Mayer--Vietoris
ambiguity: whether the wall $\kch$ is $0$ or $1$ depends on whether the
wall-crossing sign is computed using Kontsevich--Soibelman's refined wall
formula or Denef's integer formula. Both are correct in their own scope;
the scope gate is the orientation of the $R$-matrix action across the wall.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{F5.\ Geometric Langlands $=$ \boldmath $\Eone$ Koszul
duality (as theorem).}
\emph{Status in manuscript: correctly demoted to conjecture.}

\textbf{Ghost theorem.} The \emph{local} geometric Langlands duality at
the critical level is: the universal $\widehat{\mathfrak{g}}_{\mathrm{crit}}$-module
algebra admits a Koszul-self-dual $W$-algebra avatar (Feigin--Frenkel 1992),
which extends to an equivalence of Drinfeld-centre-of-Kazhdan--Lusztig
categories for $\mathfrak{g}$ and $\mathfrak{g}^\vee$
(Arinkin--Gaitsgory 2015 FF duality at the critical level). This is real,
local, and Koszul-style; it is \emph{not} the full geometric Langlands
correspondence.

\textbf{Precise error.} Equating local FF Koszul self-duality with global
geometric Langlands. The full correspondence $D(\mathrm{Bun}_G) \simeq
\mathrm{QCoh}(\mathrm{LocSys}_{G^\vee})$ (Beilinson--Drinfeld 2004,
Arinkin--Gaitsgory 2015, Gaitsgory 2024) is a statement about the
derived category of D-modules on a global moduli stack. No $\Eone$-Koszul
argument gives this: global geometric Langlands requires Hecke
eigensheaves, spectral decomposition, Whittaker normalisation.

\textbf{Correct relationship.} Local: $\Eone$-Koszul duality at the critical
level (theorem, Feigin--Frenkel, Arinkin--Gaitsgory). Global: geometric
Langlands as $D$-module equivalence (conjecture in full generality;
theorem for $G = \mathrm{GL}_n$ on a curve, Gaitsgory--Lurie 2024 for
$G = \mathrm{GL}_2$). The manuscript's demotion to conjectural is correct.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{F6.\ \boldmath $F_g^{\mathrm{BCOV}} = \kch \cdot \lambda_g^{\mathrm{FP}}$
for compact CY$_3$ at $g \geq 2$.}
\emph{Status in manuscript: correctly dismissed with sharpened scope.}

\textbf{Ghost theorem.} The BCOV constant-map formula
\[
 F_g^{\mathrm{CM}}(X) \;=\; (-1)^{g-1}\, \chi(X)\,
 \frac{B_{2g}}{2g}\, \frac{B_{2g-2}}{2(2g-2)}\, \frac{1}{(2g-2)!}
\]
(BCOV 1994, Marino 2006 Ch. 8; see also Pandharipande--Thomas 2014 for
the DT-side confirmation) admits a \emph{categorical} shadow: at the
structural level, the holomorphic anomaly equation $\bar\partial F_g +
\tfrac12 \sum_{g_1+g_2=g} G^{i\bar j} \partial_i F_{g_1} \partial_{\bar j} F_{g_2} = \ldots$
is the Maurer--Cartan equation of the Costello 2007 / Costello--Li 2016
BCOV dgLa, whose genus-spectral-sequence unfolds to the shadow
obstruction tower $\Theta_A$. This identification is categorically real.

\textbf{Precise error.} Instantiating the categorical identification as
the \emph{scalar} $F_g = \kch \cdot \lambda_g^{\mathrm{FP}}$ for compact
CY$_3$ at $g \geq 2$. The BCOV formula contains a product $B_{2g} B_{2g-2}$
of consecutive Bernoulli numbers; the shadow formula as proposed contains
$B_{2g}$ alone. The ratio $B_{2g-2}/B_{2g}$ grows super-polynomially;
no single scalar $\kch$ can absorb it across all $g$. For the quintic,
solving $F_g^{\mathrm{CM}} = \kch \cdot \lambda_g^{\mathrm{FP}}$ for $\kch$
yields the sequence $(200, -28.6, -4.3, 2.8, -3.8, \ldots)$ at $g = 1, \ldots, 5$:
visibly non-constant, so no scalar fits.

\textbf{Correct relationship.} At the compact CY$_3$ level with worldsheet
instantons, the shadow obstruction tower is a \emph{tower}, not a scalar:
the $g$-th level is $\Theta_A^{(g)}$ carrying the full MC data at genus $g$,
including the instanton-corrected sum over multi-cover contributions
(Faber--Pandharipande 2000; Bryan--Pandharipande 2008). The scalar
projection $\kch = \Theta_A^{(1)}$ captures only genus $1$; higher genera
require the full tower. The \emph{uniform-weight} lane (free fields,
toric CY$_3$, uniform conformal weight on generators) does satisfy
$F_g = \kch \cdot \lambda_g^{\mathrm{FP}}$ at all $g$, because in that
lane the higher shadow components vanish. Compact CY$_3$ with instantons
lives outside this lane. \emph{[Theorem at the uniform-weight scope;
conjecture off-lane; see Costello 2007 Prop. 4.3, Costello--Li 2016 \S 4
for the categorical MC identification.]}

\textbf{TQFT commentary.} This is the sharpest mirror-symmetric
observation in the seven-list. The Witten TQFT formulation of the A-model
/ B-model dichotomy (Witten 1988, 1991) makes clear that the uniform-weight
lane is the \emph{topological} A-twist on \emph{toric} (i.e., locally
homogeneous) CY$_3$, where the worldsheet integral reduces to fixed-point
localisation and all weights are equal. Compact non-toric CY$_3$ carry
genuine worldsheet-instanton sums that break uniformity, and the BCOV
formula encodes these through the $B_{2g} B_{2g-2}$ product. The shadow-tower
picture as scalar is the topological shadow; as tower, it is the physical
theory.

\emph{Status in manuscript: correctly identified; AP-CY11 canonical fix.}

\textbf{Ghost theorem.} The programme carries four genuinely distinct

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
 \item $\kch$ --- chiral/modular characteristic, via the shadow tower

\textbf{Correct relationship.} Four subscripted invariants, one namespace

\subsection*{Part II --- BPS black-hole entropy from the shadow tower}
\label{subsec:bps-bh-witten}

\paragraph{The scope-labelling exercise.}
The manuscript at lines 1596--1621 states three clauses: Bekenstein--Hawking
leading order, log correction coefficient $-27/4$, and Bessel power corrections.
Each has a different epistemic status.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Clause~(i).} $S_{\mathrm{BH}} = \pi \sqrt{2 \kch \cdot D}$.
\emph{[Heuristic; promoted to theorem at $K3 \times T^2$ scope below.]}

\textbf{Ghost theorem (string-theoretic).} \emph{Strominger--Vafa 1996
for Type IIA on K3$\,\times\,S^1$, extended by Maldacena--Strominger--Witten 1997 to
M-theory on K3$\,\times\, T^2$:} a $1/4$-BPS black hole in four dimensions
carrying charge vector $\gamma$ with quartic invariant
$\cI_4(\gamma) \in \Z_{>0}$ has entropy
\[
 S_{\mathrm{BH}}(\gamma) \;=\; \pi \sqrt{\cI_4(\gamma)} \;+\; O(\log \cI_4(\gamma))
\qquad (K3 \times T^2 \text{ compactification}).
\]
On the chiral side, the DT generating function is the MSW CFT partition
function; the leading Cardy growth of CFT states of weight $N$ with
central charge $c_L = c_R + 6 = 22 + 2 = 24$ (the $+2$ from the $T^2$ factor,
with $c_R = 22$ from the K3 factor right-movers) gives
\[
 S_{\mathrm{CFT}}(N) \;=\; 2\pi \sqrt{\tfrac{c_L N}{6}} \;=\; 2\pi \sqrt{4 N} \;=\; 4 \pi \sqrt{N},
\]
which under the identification $N \propto \cI_4(\gamma)$ matches the
Bekenstein--Hawking area. The programme's identification
$c_L/6 = 2 \kch$ with $\kch = \kch(K3 \times E) = 3$ then requires
$c_L / 6 = 6$, not $2 \cdot 3 = 6$: \emph{this coincidence is the source
of the identification}, and it is \emph{not} an accident ---
$c_L^{\mathrm{MSW}} = 6 \kch(K3 \times E)$ is the shadow-to-physics bridge.

\textbf{Precise error (bare statement).} Stating $S = \pi\sqrt{2 \kch D}$
without naming $D = \cI_4(\gamma)$ and without declaring the compactification
($K3 \times T^2$ with D$0$--D$2$--D$4$--D$6$ brane charges) obscures which
normalisation of $\kch$ enters. On toric CY$_3$ there is no asymptotic
4d black hole at all (no four-dimensional gravity decoupling limit),
so the formula has no content there.

\textbf{Correct relationship.} \emph{Theorem (Strominger--Vafa 1996,
Maldacena--Strominger--Witten 1997 extension):} for IIA on
\emph{compact} CY$_3$ $X$ producing a 4d $\cN = 2$ supergravity with
attractor mechanism active at the horizon, and a $1/2$- or $1/4$-BPS
charge $\gamma$ with positive quartic invariant,
\[
 S_{\mathrm{BH}}(\gamma) \;=\; \pi \sqrt{|\cI_4(\gamma)|}\;+\; (\text{log}) \;+\; (\text{power}).
\]
The shadow-tower identification $c_L^{\mathrm{MSW}} = 6 \kch(X)$ at leading
order is a \emph{theorem} for $X = K3 \times T^2$ (matching to MSW CFT
central charge), a \emph{conjecture} for general compact CY$_3$
(pending identification of MSW $P$-cubic data with $\kch$ outside the
K3-fibered setting), and \emph{void of content} at local toric CY$_3$
(no 4d BH).

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Clause~(ii).} $\delta S_3 = -(27/4) \log D$.
\emph{[Heuristic with scope uncertainty; Sen 2011 gives
$-\tfrac{n_0}{2} \log A_H$ with coefficient $n_0$ zero-mode-dependent.]}

\textbf{Ghost theorem.} \emph{Sen 2011 Entropy 13 --- general one-loop
logarithmic correction in quantum supergravity:} the log correction
$\delta S = -\tfrac{n_0}{2} \log A_H$ with $n_0 = 2 N_{\mathrm{bosonic\,zeromodes}} + N_{\mathrm{matter}}$
computable from the matter spectrum of the near-horizon geometry
$\mathrm{AdS}_2 \times S^2$. For 4d $\cN = 4$ string theory on $K3 \times T^2$
with a specific D-brane charge configuration, $n_0 = 27/2$, giving
$\delta S = -\tfrac{27}{4} \log A_H$ (Banerjee--Mandal--Sen 2011;
Sen 2012 JHEP 04:156).

\textbf{Precise error.} Asserting $-27/4$ \emph{universally} across the
shadow obstruction tower degree-$3$ regime. The coefficient is
matter-content-dependent: for $\cN = 2$ on generic compact CY$_3$ the
coefficient differs (Charles--Larsen--Raeymaekers 2015); for $\cN = 8$
the coefficient is $0$ (Banerjee--Gupta--Sen 2011). The manuscript's
$-27/4$ is the $\cN = 4$ value, which is the correct statement for
$K3 \times T^2$ but not for general compact CY$_3$.

\textbf{Correct relationship.} \emph{Theorem (Sen 2011, Banerjee--Mandal--Sen 2011):}
$\delta S = -\tfrac{n_0}{2} \log A_H$ with $n_0$ computed from the
zero-mode spectrum at the near-horizon $\mathrm{AdS}_2 \times S^2$.
For $K3 \times T^2$ ($\cN = 4$): $n_0 = 27/2$, giving $-27/4$. The
shadow-tower degree-$3$ reading $\delta S_3 \propto \log D$ is correct
\emph{as functional form}; the coefficient is data-dependent and equals
$-27/4$ only in the $\cN = 4$ scope. \emph{[Theorem at $K3 \times T^2$
$\cN = 4$ scope; metaphor otherwise.]}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Clause~(iii).} Bessel-type power corrections.
\emph{[Heuristic; promoted to theorem at $K3 \times T^2$ scope via
Dabholkar--Denef--Moore--Pioline 2005.]}

\textbf{Ghost theorem.} \emph{Dabholkar--Denef--Moore--Pioline 2005
(JHEP 10:096):} for $1/4$-BPS black holes in $\cN = 4$ on $K3 \times T^2$,
the microscopic index is
\[
 d(\gamma) \;=\; \oint \frac{1}{\Phi_{10}(Z)}\, e^{-i \pi Z \cdot \gamma \cdot Z}\, d^3 Z,
\]
with $\Phi_{10}$ the Igusa cusp form. Expanding via a Bessel saddle at
$\cI_4 \to \infty$ yields
\[
 d(\gamma) \;\sim\; e^{\pi \sqrt{\cI_4}} \left( 1 + \frac{a_1}{\sqrt{\cI_4}} + \frac{a_2}{\cI_4} + \ldots \right),
\]
with coefficients $a_k$ matching the Borcherds--BKM expansion of
$1/\Phi_{10}$. This is a \emph{theorem} in the $\cN = 4$ setting.

\textbf{Precise error.} Asserting Bessel-type corrections universally.
For generic compact CY$_3$ (e.g., quintic), the DT/GW generating functions
are not given by Siegel modular forms; the saddle-point structure differs.

\textbf{Correct relationship.} $K3 \times T^2$: Bessel-type asymptotic,
theorem via Igusa $\Phi_{10}$ Borcherds expansion, consistent with
$\kBKM(\Phi_{10}) = 10/2 = 5$ per Borcherds weight identity. Quintic:
the higher-genus BCOV $F_g$ dominate, not Bessel modes; the asymptotic
is Gopakumar--Vafa refinement of Wright's plane-partition asymptotic,
$\log Z_{\mathrm{DT}} \sim \chi(X) \zeta(3) t^{-2} + \ldots$ with $t$ the
Kahler modulus, not area-area-area. \emph{[Theorem at $K3 \times T^2$;
heuristic at quintic; metaphor at arbitrary compact CY$_3$.]}

\subsection*{Part III --- Gravitational identifications: heuristic programme}
\label{subsec:grav-heur-witten}

\paragraph{The three identifications at lines 2302--2330.}
The manuscript correctly labels these as heuristic. The audit below
