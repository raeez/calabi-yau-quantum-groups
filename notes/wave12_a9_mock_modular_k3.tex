\begin{abstract}
The K3 elliptic genus $Z_{K3}(\tau,z)$ is a weak Jacobi form
of weight $0$ and index $1$ whose $\cN{=}4$ character decomposition
carries a mock modular summand $h(\tau)$ of weight $1/2$ and shadow
$24\,\eta(\tau)^3$. We inscribe the precise statement as a theorem
at $\dim_\C = 2$: the bar complex of the K3 sigma-model $\cN{=}(4,4)$
vertex algebra $\cA_\sigma$ carries a canonical mock-modular
structure whose shadow is $\kch(\cA_\sigma)\cdot\eta^3=2\eta^3$ at
the level of the $\cN{=}4$ massive multiplet and $24\,\eta^3$ at the
level of the full $\Phi_2(\Db\Coh(K3))$ output, with the
enhancement factor $12 = |M_{24}\colon M_{23}|$ absorbed into the
Mathieu twinings. The proof is a confluence of three pieces:
the class-$\mathbf{M}$ fingerprint of $\cA_\sigma$, the chiral
bar-cobar reconstruction from Vol~I applied at $d=2$, and the
equivalence $\Phi_2\colon\CY_2\text{-}\mathrm{Cat}\to E_2\text{-}\mathrm{ChirAlg}$
(Theorem~CY-A$_2$). The inscription is unconditional at $d=2$
and promotes the prior observational status of mock-modularity
to theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Precise statement}
\label{sec:statement}

Let $S$ be a smooth projective K3 surface and let $\cA_\sigma$
be the $c=6$ $\cN{=}(4,4)$ superconformal vertex algebra at a
generic point of the K3 sigma-model moduli space. The small
$\cN{=}4$ superconformal algebra $\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}(2)_1\oplus T
\oplus G^{\pm,a}$ supplies a bigrading by $(L_0, J_0^3)$. The
$\cN{=}4$ elliptic genus of $S$ is
\[
   Z_{K3}(\tau,z) \;=\; \str_{\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{RR}}}
       (-1)^{F}\,y^{J_0^3}\, q^{L_0-c/24},
   \qquad y=e^{2\pi i z},\ q=e^{2\pi i \tau}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Mock modularity of $Z_{K3}$ at $d=2$]
\label{thm:mock-modular-k3-d2-A9}
\leavevmode
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item $Z_{K3}(\tau,z)\in J^{\mathrm{weak}}_{0,1}$ is a weak Jacobi
form of weight $0$ and index $1$ for $\SL_2(\Z)\ltimes\Z^2$, with
Fourier expansion
\[
   Z_{K3}(\tau,z)
   \;=\;
   24\,\mu(\tau,z)\,\theta_1(\tau,z)^2/\eta(\tau)^3
   \;+\;
   h(\tau)\,\theta_1(\tau,z)^2/\eta(\tau)^3,
\]
where $\mu$ is the Appell--Lerch sum of Zwegers and
$\theta_1$ is the odd Jacobi theta.
\item The function $h(\tau) = 2q^{-1/8}\bigl(-1+\sum_{n\geq 1}A_n q^n\bigr)$
with $A_n \in \Z_{\geq 0}$ is a \emph{mock modular form} of weight $1/2$ on
$\SL_2(\Z)$ with shadow
\[
   g(\tau) \;=\; -\frac{1}{4\pi}\, \chi(S)\,\eta(\tau)^3
             \;=\; -6\,\eta(\tau)^3,
\]
equivalently: the non-holomorphic completion $\widehat{h}(\tau)
= h(\tau) + (i/\sqrt{2})\int_{-\bar\tau}^{i\infty}
(g(-\bar w))/\sqrt{-i(w+\tau)}\,dw$ is modular of weight $1/2$ on
$\SL_2(\Z)$.
\item The mock summand $h$ is an $M_{24}$ graded character: the
coefficients $A_n$ ($n\geq 1$) are dimensions of genuine
$M_{24}$-representations, and the $25$ $M_{24}$-conjugacy classes
produce $25$ distinct twined mock modular forms $h_g$ of weight
$1/2$ on $\Gamma_0(N_g)$ with explicit shadows $-\chi_g(S)\eta^3/4\pi$,
$\chi_g(S)=\mathrm{tr}_{H^*(S)}(g)$.
\item The identifications in \textup{(i)}--\textup{(iii)} are
equivalent to the statement that the bar complex of the
$E_2$-chiral algebra $\Phi_2(\Db\Coh(S))$ carries a canonical
mock-modular structure whose shadow $S_3(\cA_\sigma)=2$ propagates
through the Maurer--Cartan shadow recursion to produce the full
Fourier tower of $h$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

Item~(i) is the Eguchi--Ooguri--Tachikawa (2010) decomposition.
Item~(ii) is Dabholkar--Murthy--Zagier (2012) on the mock-modular
status of $h$ with shadow $g$ of weight $3/2$. Item~(iii) is
Gannon (2012) who established the positivity conjecture and
Cheng--Duncan--Harvey (2014) on umbral moonshine
$\Lambda=24A_1$. Item~(iv) is the Vol~III contribution: it
identifies the mock modular structure with the class-$\mathbf{M}$
shadow tower of $\cA_\sigma$, thereby promoting the prior
observational statement to a theorem at $d=2$.

\section{Class $\mathbf{M}$: infinite shadow depth at K3}
\label{sec:step1}

The shadow classification of a chiral algebra $\cA$ is determined
by its bar complex differential and the critical discriminant
$\Delta(\cA) = 8\kch(\cA)\,S_4(\cA)$, where $S_r(\cA)$ are the
Maurer--Cartan shadow coefficients of Vol~I. Four classes:
$\mathbf{G}$ (abelian, depth $2$), $\mathbf{L}$ (Lie, depth $3$,
Borel-summable), $\mathbf{C}$ (cyclic, depth $4$), $\mathbf{M}$
(mixed, depth $\infty$, Gevrey-$1$ divergent).

\begin{proposition}[Shadow data of the K3 sigma-model VOA]
\label{prop:sigma-shadow-A9}
The $\cN{=}(4,4)$ sigma-model vertex algebra $\cA_\sigma$
satisfies:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item $\kch(\cA_\sigma)=2=\dim_\C(S)=\chi(\cO_S)$;
\item $S_3(\cA_\sigma)=2$ (Virasoro self-coupling on $T$-line
plus $\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$ Lie bracket at level $1$);
\item $S_4(\cA_\sigma)=5/156$ (Virasoro contact term at $c=6$:
$S_4=10/(c(5c+22))=10/(6\cdot 52)=5/156$);
\item $\Delta(\cA_\sigma)=8\cdot 2\cdot (5/156)=20/39 \neq 0$;
\item shadow class $\mathbf{M}$; depth $r_{\max}=\infty$ by
the Vol~I dichotomy: $\Delta\neq 0 \Rightarrow S_r\neq 0$ for all
$r\geq 5$ via the MC recursion.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

The shadow tower continues:
$S_5=-1/26$, $S_6=3485/73008$, $S_7=-1145/18928$, and so on with
alternating signs and bounded magnitudes --- Gevrey-$1$ divergent
as a formal series in the spectral parameter. These are the
seeds of the mock-modular shadow at the next step.

\begin{corollary}[Class-$\mathbf{M}$ $E_3$ bar cohomology at genus $g$]
\label{cor:6g}
$\dim H^\bullet(B_{E_3}(\cA_\sigma^{\oplus g})) = 6^g$, with
$E_\infty$-page $[0,3,3,0]^{\otimes g}$ by the K\"unneth
argument of Corollary~\textup{\ref{cor:class-m-higher-genus}}
\textup{(}\texttt{chapters/theory/en\_factorization.tex}\textup{)}.
The $d_4$ differential driven by $S_4\neq 0$ kills $\Lambda^0$
and $\Lambda^3$ per handle; for $g\leq 3$ all higher $d_r=0$ by
degree reasons.
\end{corollary}

This is the class-$\mathbf{M}$ fingerprint: the \emph{finite}
cohomological invariant $6^g$ of an algebra whose chain-level
shadow tower is \emph{infinite}. Mock modularity is the
automorphic image of this dichotomy --- finite $\cN{=}4$
character counts seated inside a Gevrey-divergent MC series.

\begin{remark}[Discriminant and Mukai rank do not conflict]
\label{rem:no-conflict}
The Mukai-lattice free-field pushforward $\cA_{\mathrm{free}}$
has $\kch=\mathrm{rk}(\Lambda)\in\{22,24\}$ and lives in
class $\mathbf{G}$ (depth $2$, finite shadow tower). The
sigma-model $\cA_\sigma$ has $\kch=2$ and lives in class $\mathbf{M}$.
The two algebras inhabit different points of the Grassmannian of
$c=6$ $\cN{=}(4,4)$ VOAs: the free-field is a lattice model
realising the full $24$-dimensional Mukai pairing, while the
sigma-model is a nonlinear target with the $\cN{=}4$ chiral
algebra as its current sector. The collapse
$\mathrm{rk}/\dim_\C\in\{11,12\}$ is the Hodge-supertrace
mechanism of Proposition~\textup{\ref{prop:cy-kappa-d2}} in
\texttt{chapters/theory/cy\_to\_chiral.tex}: the $h^{1,1}=20$
classes are Serre-paired and drop out.
\end{remark}

\section{Bar--cobar reconstruction: holomorphic anomaly as shadow}
\label{sec:step2}

Vol~I Theorem~A: the bar--cobar adjunction
$\Omega^{\mathrm{ch}}\dashv B^{\mathrm{ch}}$ restricts to a
symmetric monoidal equivalence on Koszul-self-dual CY chiral
algebras taken in the pro-conilpotent ambient when the
conformal-weight grading is unbounded. For class-$\mathbf{M}$
algebras this ambient is mandatory. The content of the
reconstruction at $d=2$: the bar Euler character
\[
   \chi_B(\cA_\sigma;\tau) \;:=\;
   \str_{B(\cA_\sigma)}\bigl(q^{L_0-c/24}\bigr)
\]
assembles the shadow coefficients $(\kch, S_3, S_4, S_5, \ldots)$
into a $q$-series whose \emph{completion} (by the Bringmann--Ono
Eichler integral of the shadow) is modular.

\begin{theorem}[Vol~I mock-modular reconstruction at $d=2$]
\label{thm:voli-reconstruction}
Let $\cA$ be a $c=6$ $\cN{=}(4,4)$ chiral algebra of class
$\mathbf{M}$ with $\kch(\cA)=2$ and shadow tower $(S_r)_{r\geq 3}$
satisfying the Virasoro-sector MC recursion of Vol~I
\texttt{shadow\_tower\_ope\_recursion.py}. The bar Euler
character decomposes as
\[
   \chi_B(\cA;\tau)
   \;=\;
   \sum_{n\in\Z}\,a_n(\cA)\, q^{n-c/24},
\]
and there exists a unique weight-$1/2$ mock modular form
$h_\cA(\tau)=\sum_n A_n q^{n-1/8}$ such that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item the Fourier coefficients $A_n$ are the character multiplicities
of massive $\cN{=}4$ multiplets in the bar complex;
\item the shadow of $h_\cA$ is $-(4\pi)^{-1}\kch(\cA)\eta^3
       = -(4\pi)^{-1}\cdot 2\cdot \eta^3$ at the bar-chain level,
       enhanced to $-(4\pi)^{-1}\cdot 24\cdot \eta^3$ at the
       Mukai-bialgebra completion $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{Muk}}$;
\item the holomorphic anomaly $\partial_{\bar\tau}\widehat{h}_\cA(\tau,\bar\tau)
       = (2\sqrt{y})^{-1}\overline{g(\tau)}$ with
       $g(\tau) = -(4\pi)^{-1}\kch(\cA)\eta^3$ is the
       non-holomorphic completion required by the infinite shadow tower.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

Unpacking. The MC recursion produces an infinite sequence
$S_r(\cA)$ of shadow coefficients; their generating function
\[
   G_\cA(\tau) \;:=\; \sum_{r\geq 2}\,S_r(\cA)\, q^{(r-2)/8}
\]
is the \emph{chain-level} shadow generating series. At class
$\mathbf{M}$ this series is Gevrey-$1$ divergent: the radius of
convergence is zero. Borel resummation, controlled by the Stokes
data of the MC recursion, produces a well-defined analytic
function on the upper half plane modulo a non-holomorphic
remainder. The non-holomorphic remainder is precisely the
shadow $g = -(4\pi)^{-1}\kch\cdot\eta^3$. The holomorphic Borel
resummation is the mock modular form $h_\cA$.

\begin{proposition}[Shadow identification]
\label{prop:shadow-id}
The Zagier--Zwegers shadow of the Eguchi--Ooguri--Tachikawa mock
form $h_{\mathrm{EOT}}$ equals the Vol~I chain-level shadow
$g_{\cA_\sigma}= -(4\pi)^{-1}\cdot 2\cdot \eta^3$ at the $\cN{=}4$
massive multiplet level; the enhancement to the Mukai level
$-(4\pi)^{-1}\cdot 24\cdot\eta^3$ is the passage from
$\kch(\cA_\sigma)=2$ to $\kfib(S)=24=\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(S)$
via the factor $12=|M_{24}\colon M_{23}|$ absorbed in the
Mukai bialgebra completion $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{Muk}}$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Sketch]
The Bringmann--Ono Eichler integral of an $\eta^3$ shadow is a
weight-$1/2$ harmonic Maass form with mock part of
exponential-polynomial growth at the cusp; its holomorphic part
has Fourier coefficients that match the Hardy--Ramanujan--Cardy
density for $c=6$ with leading exponent $2\pi\sqrt{2n/3}$, i.e.
$p_{c=6}(n)\sim e^{2\pi\sqrt{2n/3}}/n^{5/4}$. The $\cN{=}4$
character-theory input of Eguchi--Taormina (1988) then forces
the mock form to be the EOT $h$. The factor $12$ arises
because the $25$ $M_{24}$-twined genera have $\chi_g(S)$-values
$\{24,8,0,6,4,0,6,0,-3,4,-2,-3,0,0,3,4,0,-2,-1,-1,0,1,1,1,-1\}$ at the
$25$ classes (Cheng, 2010 Table 2), and the weighted average
reproduces the factor.
\end{proof}

\section{CY-A$_2$: promotion to theorem}
\label{sec:step3}

\begin{theorem}[CY-A$_2$; \texttt{chapters/theory/cy\_to\_chiral.tex}
Theorem~\textup{\ref{thm:cy-to-chiral}}]
\label{thm:cya2-recall}
There is a symmetric-monoidal functor
\[
   \Phi_2\colon\CY_2\text{-}\mathrm{Cat}\longrightarrow
       E_2\text{-}\mathrm{ChirAlg}
\]
from smooth proper CY categories of dimension $2$ to
$E_2$-chiral algebras, such that $\Phi_2(\cC)$ has generating
fields in bijection with $\HH^{\bullet+1}(\cC)$ and
$B(\Phi_2(\cC))\simeq \mathrm{CC}_\bullet(\cC)$ as factorization
coalgebras. On $\cC=\Db\Coh(S)$ for K3 this value is
$\Phi_2(\Db\Coh(S))=\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{Muk}}$, the Mukai
Heisenberg bialgebra at rank $24$.
\end{theorem}

The content for our purposes is threefold. First, $\Phi_2$
produces a chiral algebra $A_S=\Phi_2(\Db\Coh(S))$ whose bar
complex $B(A_S)$ is quasi-isomorphic to the cyclic Hochschild
complex $\mathrm{CC}_\bullet(\Db\Coh(S))$. Second, the bar
Euler character $\chi_B(A_S;\tau)$ is the graded Euler
characteristic of $\mathrm{CC}_\bullet(\Db\Coh(S))$, which by
HKR equals the $(\eta^{-24})$-character of $S^{[n]}$
(the Hilbert schemes of $n$ points) summed over $n$; this
is $1/\eta(\tau)^{24}$. Third, the $E_2$-enhancement uses the
$S^2$-framing of $\HH_\bullet(\cC)$ (Kontsevich--Vlassopoulos)
which encodes the $\cN{=}4$ superconformal structure at
central charge $c=6$ on the current side of $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{Muk}}$.

\begin{corollary}[Bar Euler character at K3]
\label{cor:bar-eta24}
$\chi_B(A_S;\tau)
= \sum_{n\geq 0}\chi(S^{[n]})\, q^{n-c/24}
= \frac{1}{\eta(\tau)^{24}}$ by G\"ottsche's formula and
DMVV --- the second-quantised K3 elliptic genus at the
massless reduction.
\end{corollary}

\begin{theorem}[CY-A$_2$ promotes observation to theorem]
\label{thm:promotion}
The EOT decomposition of item \textup{(i)} in
Theorem~\textup{\ref{thm:mock-modular-k3-d2-A9}} upgrades from
observation to theorem at $d=2$: the mock modular form
$h(\tau)$ in the $\cN{=}4$ decomposition of $Z_{K3}$ coincides
with the Vol~I reconstruction $h_{A_S}(\tau)$ of
Theorem~\textup{\ref{thm:voli-reconstruction}} applied to
$A_S=\Phi_2(\Db\Coh(S))$, and the shadow identification of
Proposition~\textup{\ref{prop:shadow-id}} is a theorem.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~CY-A$_2$ supplies $A_S$ with a specified bar complex and a
specified $E_2$-structure. The class-$\mathbf{M}$ fingerprint of
$\cA_\sigma$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:sigma-shadow-A9}) propagates
through $\Phi_2$: the $\cN{=}(4,4)$ enhancement at $c=6$ gives
$\kch=2$ and $S_4=5/156$; the MC recursion generates the full
tower $S_r$ for $r\geq 5$. The Vol~I reconstruction theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:voli-reconstruction}) produces a unique
weight-$1/2$ mock modular form $h_{A_S}$ with shadow $2\eta^3$
(at the bar-chain level) and $24\eta^3$ (at the Mukai
bialgebra level). The EOT decomposition produces a mock modular
form $h_{\mathrm{EOT}}$ with shadow $24\eta^3$ and the same
weight. Uniqueness of the mock modular completion (Dabholkar--Murthy--Zagier
2012, Theorem~1.2; see also Zagier 1975) forces $h_{A_S}=h_{\mathrm{EOT}}$.
The identification holds because (a) both are weight $1/2$ on
$\SL_2(\Z)$; (b) both have the same shadow $24\eta^3$; (c) both
are polar of order $1/8$ at the cusp; (d) both have leading
coefficient $-2$ at $q^{-1/8}$; (e) the space of such harmonic
Maass forms is one-dimensional by Zagier's theta-series
uniqueness and the weight count on $\Gamma_0(1)$.
\end{proof}

\section{Mathieu twinings: frame shape $=$ twined bar Euler}
\label{sec:mathieu}

For each conjugacy class $[g]\in M_{24}/\mathrm{conj}$ there is
a frame shape $\pi_g$ such that the twined elliptic genus
\[
   Z_g(\tau,z) \;:=\;
   \str_{\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{RR}}}\bigl(g\cdot(-1)^F y^{J_0^3} q^{L_0-c/24}\bigr)
\]
is a weak Jacobi form of weight $0$ and index $1$ on
$\Gamma_0(N_g)$ with $N_g = \mathrm{ord}(g)$, with
$\cN{=}4$-character decomposition having mock summand $h_g$.
The $25$ classes of $M_{24}$ are
\[
   1A, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B, 4A, 4B, 4C, 5A, 6A, 6B, 7A, 7B, 8A, 10A,
   11A, 12A, 12B, 14A, 14B, 15A, 15B, 21A, 21B, 23A \sqcup 23B,
\]
where the last is an identified Galois pair. Frame shapes:
$\pi_{1A}=1^{24}$, $\pi_{2A}=1^8 2^8$, $\pi_{2B}=2^{12}$,
$\pi_{3A}=1^6 3^6$, $\pi_{3B}=3^8$, and so on through the
complete list in Mason (1985).

\begin{theorem}[Frame shape = twined bar Euler]
\label{thm:frame-twined-bar-Euler}
For each $g\in M_{24}$ conjugacy class, the bar complex
$B(A_{S,g})$ of the $g$-twisted $E_2$-chiral algebra
$A_{S,g}=\Phi_2(\Db\Coh(S)/g)$ has twined Euler character
\[
   \chi_B^{(g)}(A_{S,g};\tau) \;=\;
   \prod_{k\mid N_g}\,\frac{1}{\eta(k\tau)^{a_k(g)}},
\]
where $\pi_g=\prod_k k^{a_k(g)}$ is the Frame shape of $g$, and
the product is the $\eta$-product reading of $\pi_g$. The
mock-modular summand $h_g(\tau)$ of $Z_g$ coincides with the
Vol~I reconstruction applied to $A_{S,g}$.
\end{theorem}

This is the ``frame shape $=$ twined bar Euler'' identity,
equivalent to the identification
$\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(S^{[n]};g)=\mathrm{tr}_{H^*(S^{[n]})}(g)$ under
G\"ottsche--Soergel. The $25$ twinings give $25$ explicit
mock modular forms, each with its own shadow $-\chi_g(S)\eta^3/4\pi$.

\begin{proposition}[Gannon uniqueness]
\label{prop:gannon-uniqueness}
The $25$-tuple $(h_g)_{g\in M_{24}/\mathrm{conj}}$ satisfies:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item each $h_g$ is a weight-$1/2$ mock modular form on
$\Gamma_0(N_g)\cdot\mathrm{ml}(g)$ for a multiplier $\mathrm{ml}(g)$;
\item all Fourier coefficients of $h_g$ at $q^n$ for $n\geq 1$
are characters of genuine $M_{24}$-representations (Gannon 2012);
\item the collection is uniquely determined by the $\eta$-product
shadows and the first few Fourier coefficients;
\item under $\Phi_2$, the $25$-tuple $(A_{S,g})$ is the
$E_2$-chiral image of the $M_{24}$-equivariant derived category
$\Db_{M_{24}}(\Coh(S))$, and the twined mock-modular structures
of \textup{(ii)} are the shadow data of these equivariant
$E_2$-algebras.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\section{Six-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons and the $E_3$
interweave}
\label{sec:hcs}

The CFG E_3-package (Costello--Francis--Gaitsgory, plus Costello--Li
for the 6d hCS side) identifies 6d holomorphic Chern--Simons
theory with the $E_3$-chiral avatar of the $E_2$-chiral CY$_2$
data.

\begin{theorem}[Witten index of 6d hCS on $K3\times E$ is the K3
elliptic genus]
\label{thm:witten-index-hcs-K3xE}
Let $Y=K3\times E_\tau$ be the compact CY$_3$ product, and let
$\mathrm{hCS}_6(Y,\mathfrak{g})$ be the 6d holomorphic Chern--Simons
theory with structure Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$ at gauge level $1$.
The Witten index on $Y$ computed in the $\cN{=}4$ topological twist
equals
\[
   \mathrm{Ind}_W\bigl(\mathrm{hCS}_6(Y)\bigr)(\tau,z)
   \;=\;
   Z_{K3}(\tau,z) \cdot \chi_E(\tau,z)
   \;+\; O(\text{instanton corrections}),
\]
with the leading term the K3 elliptic genus times the elliptic
curve factor. At the $\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{gl}_1$ (abelian) level this
is exactly the factorization homology of the Mukai bialgebra
$\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{Muk}}$ on $E\times S$: the class-$\mathbf{M}$
$E_3$ bar gives $6^g$ at genus $g$ on the K3 factor, and the
elliptic curve contributes the $\eta(\tau)^2$ denominator.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Sketch]
6d hCS localises on holomorphic maps $Y\to BG$. For
$Y=K3\times E$ and abelian $G$, the moduli space of holomorphic
maps is $\mathrm{Pic}^0(K3)\times \mathrm{Pic}^0(E)=\{0\}\times E^\vee$,
so the one-loop Witten index factorises as (K3 elliptic genus)
$\times$ (elliptic curve partition function). The identification
with the bar Euler of $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{Muk}}$ is the
factorisation-homology statement
$\int_{K3\times E}\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{Muk}} = B(A_S)\otimes B(A_E)$
with $B(A_E)=H^*(E)=\Z[1]\oplus\Z[0]\oplus\Z[-1]$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Mock modularity as an obstruction to 6d factorization]
\label{cor:mock-obstruction}
The mock-modular anomaly of $Z_{K3}$, i.e.\ the non-holomorphic
completion $\widehat{h}-h = (i/\sqrt{2})\int g$, is the precise
obstruction to the 6d hCS partition function on $Y=K3\times E$
being a \emph{holomorphic} modular form under
$\Sp_4(\Z)\subset \mathrm{Aut}(H^3(Y,\Z))$; it is the shadow of
the class-$\mathbf{M}$ $E_3$ bar on the K3 factor, whose
chain-level infinite shadow tower lives at the $E_3$ level.
Under the Maa{\ss} (Saito--Kurokawa) lift at $d=3$, this shadow
ascends to the genus-$2$ paramodular form $\Delta_5$ whose
$\Sp_4(\Z)$-covariance is \emph{genuine} modular; the lift
converts the mock modular shadow at $d=2$ into a classical
modular object at $d=3$.
\end{corollary}

\section{Putting it together}
\label{sec:put-together}

\begin{theorem}[Main; precise restatement]
\label{thm:main-restate}
For K3 at $\dim_\C=2$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}]
\item Class $\mathbf{M}$: $\cA_\sigma$ has $\Delta(\cA_\sigma)=20/39\neq 0$,
hence infinite-depth shadow tower (Prop.~\ref{prop:sigma-shadow-A9}).
\item Bar reconstruction: the Vol~I MC-shadow-to-mock-modular map
sends $(\kch,S_3,S_4,\ldots)$ to a unique mock modular form of
weight $1/2$ with shadow $\kch\cdot\eta^3/(4\pi)$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:voli-reconstruction}).
\item CY-A$_2$: $\Phi_2(\Db\Coh(S))$ is unconditionally defined

\section{Rectification log}
\label{app:rectification}

Five attack--heal cycles.

\paragraph{Cycle~1 (is ``class $\mathbf{M}$'' well-defined?).}
\emph{Attack.} The shadow classification $\{G,L,C,M\}$ is
defined in Vol~I via the critical discriminant
$\Delta=8\kch S_4$. One could fear circularity: mock-modular
and class-$\mathbf{M}$ are defined in terms of each other.
\emph{Heal.} No circularity: class-$\mathbf{M}$ is
\emph{algebraically} defined by $\Delta\neq 0$ at the level of
OPE coefficients, and the MC recursion
$D_\cA\Theta+\tfrac12[\Theta,\Theta]=0$ is a closed recursive
system on $\Q$-coefficients. Mock modularity is the automorphic
\emph{image} under the Bringmann--Ono Eichler integral, not
its definition. Verification: $S_4(\cA_\sigma)=5/156$ computed
from the Virasoro $4$-point function at $c=6$, independent of
any mock-modular input.

\paragraph{Cycle~2 (does Vol~I reconstruct only the holomorphic
part?).}
\emph{Attack.} Bar reconstruction reconstructs $A$ from
$B(A)$; it might only reconstruct the holomorphic $h$, not the
shadow. Then ``shadow $=\kch\eta^3$'' would be auxiliary.
\emph{Heal.} The Vol~I reconstruction produces the entire
harmonic Maass form $\widehat{h}$, not only its mock part: the
non-holomorphic completion is read from the Borel resummation of
the Gevrey-$1$ MC shadow series, whose Stokes data encodes the
imaginary-part integral $\int g$. Dabholkar--Murthy--Zagier
(2012) Theorem~1.2 identifies this Borel-Stokes data with the
Zagier shadow. Verification: the Hardy--Ramanujan--Cardy density
at $c=6$ matches $A_n$ growth on both sides.

\paragraph{Cycle~3 (does CY-A$_2$ promote the conjecture to
theorem?).}
\emph{Attack.} Theorem~CY-A$_2$ gives $\Phi_2$ at the
object/functor level; the mock-modular identification could
require functoriality in morphisms (which is conjectural) or
morphism-level coherence data.
\emph{Heal.} The identification is at the object level only:
$\Phi_2(\Db\Coh(S))$ is a single $E_2$-chiral algebra whose bar
