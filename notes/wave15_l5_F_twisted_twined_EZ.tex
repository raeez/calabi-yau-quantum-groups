\section*{Wave-15 L5/20: the Eichler--Zagier lift of $\Fgh$
to the two-form basis $(\phioo,\phimt)$}
\label{wn:sec:wave15-l5-EZ-twisted-twined}

\paragraph{Question.} A commuting pair $(g_N,h_M)\in\CM\times\CM$
of Mukai-compatible K3 symplectic automorphisms produces a weight-zero,
index-one, level-$\mathrm{lcm}(N,M)$ weak Jacobi form
$\Fgh(\tau,z)$ (Persson--Volpato 2012; Gaberdiel--Hohenegger--Volpato
2012); the slides' fourth claim asserts that $\Fgh$ expands in the
Eichler--Zagier basis. Eichler--Zagier 1985 Theorem~9.3 fixes the
ring of weak Jacobi forms of index $1$ as a free $M_\ast(\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb Z))$-module
\[
  J^{\mathrm{w}}_{\ast,1}
  \;=\; M_\ast\cdot\phioo \;\oplus\; M_\ast\cdot\phimt,
  \qquad
  \phioo\in J^{\mathrm{w}}_{0,1},\quad \phimt\in J^{\mathrm{w}}_{-2,1},
\]
whose two generators are the Gritsenko--Nikulin-normalised pair
$\phioo=(y^{-1}+10+y)+O(q)$ and $\phimt=\nu_{11}^2/\eta^6$ (Wave-14
Q4 extraction, slide 940). The question is whether the
\emph{twisted-twined} family $\Fgh$, level $\mathrm{lcm}(N,M)$,
admits a coefficient-level expansion
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:wn-wave15-l5-EZ-ansatz}
  \Fgh(\tau,z) \;=\; \agh(\tau)\,\phioo(\tau,z) \;+\; \bgh(\tau)\,\phimt(\tau,z),
  \qquad
  \agh\in M_0(\Gamma_0(\mathrm{lcm}(N,M))),\ \bgh\in M_2(\Gamma_0(\mathrm{lcm}(N,M))),
\end{equation}
and how the resulting $(\agh,\bgh)$ pair refines the universal
Borcherds weight identity $\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$.

\paragraph{Cycle 1 (existence of the EZ decomposition). \ClaimStatusTheorem.}
Eichler--Zagier 1985 Theorem~9.3 on the level-one ring extends to
$\Gamma_0(N)$ via Aoki 2000 (\emph{J.\ Math.\ Kyoto Univ.}~40) and
Gritsenko 1999 (\emph{St.\ Petersburg Math.\ J.}~11) \S 2:
the space $J^{\mathrm{w}}_{0,1}(\Gamma_0(N))$ of index-one weak Jacobi
forms at level $N$ is a free $M_\ast(\Gamma_0(N))$-module on the
same two generators $(\phioo,\phimt)$. Since
$\Fgh\in J^{\mathrm{w}}_{0,1}(\Gamma_0(\mathrm{lcm}(N,M)),\nu_{g,h})$
(GHV 2012 Theorem~1), equation~\eqref{eq:wn-wave15-l5-EZ-ansatz}
is well-defined with $\agh\in M_0(\Gamma_0(\mathrm{lcm}(N,M)),\nu_{g,h})$
and $\bgh\in M_2(\Gamma_0(\mathrm{lcm}(N,M)),\nu_{g,h})$. Uniqueness
follows from linear independence of $y^{-1}+10+y$ and $y^{-1}-2+y$
at the $q^0$-cusp.

\paragraph{Cycle 2 (admissible $(g_N,h_M)$ enumeration). \ClaimStatusTheorem.}
The GHV 2012 Mukai four-plane scheme (\emph{arXiv:1206.5335} Theorem~1.1,
sharpened in \emph{arXiv:1211.7074} \S 3) restricts $(g_N,h_M)$ to
commuting pairs in $M_{24}$ whose span stabilises a positive-definite
$4$-plane in the Leech lattice; Persson--Volpato 2012 \emph{arXiv:1208.4851}
Prop.~4.1 extends the admissibility to the full programme slice
$N,M\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ via the $H^3$-vanishing lemma of Wave-10 R5
(Mason~1994; cf.\ Wave-14 K4 centraliser table). Admissible Mukai
classes are
\[
  (g_N,h_M)\in\{1A,2A,2B,3A,3B,4A,4B,4C,6A,6B\}^{\times 2}_{\mathrm{cent}},
\]
$55$ unordered commuting-pair cells on the programme slice (Wave-10
R5 matrix), with level
$\mathrm{lcm}(|g_N|,|h_M|)\in\{1,2,3,4,6,12\}$.

\paragraph{Cycle 3 (leading case $(g_2,h_2=\mathrm{id})$). \ClaimStatusTheorem.}
Fix the singly-twined entry $(N,M)=(2A,1A)$, the Enriques-involution
twining at level $2$ with trivial simultaneous-twining $h_M=\mathrm{id}$.
By CDH 2014 (specialising to $h_M=\mathrm{id}$ recovers the
Cheng--Duncan--Harvey single-twining; here we use the GHV-2012
$\phi_{0,1}^{(2A)}$ normalisation)
\[
  F_L^{(g_{2A},\mathrm{id})}(\tau,z)
  \;=\; 2\,\phi_{0,1}^{(2A)}(\tau,z),
\]
with Fourier head
$(c^{(2A)}(-1,1),c^{(2A)}(0,0),c^{(2A)}(1,-1),c^{(2A)}(1,1))=(2,4,-6,14)$
from the 26-class table (Wave-9 P5, Eguchi--Hikami 2009). The
Eichler--Zagier decomposition at level $2$ is pinned by two
coefficient equations (each Jacobi form of index $1$ is determined
by its $q^0$-head $c(0,0)+2c(0,1)$ at $z=0$ plus its
$(q^0,y^{-1})$-polar coefficient $c(-1,1)$):
\begin{align}
\label{eq:wn-wave15-l5-pinning-equations}
\text{evaluate at } z=0:\quad & 2\,\chi_g(V_{24})\big|_{g=2A}
  \;=\; 24\,\agh(\tau) \;+\; 0\cdot\bgh(\tau),\\
\text{$(q^0,y^{\pm 1})$-polar head:}\quad & c^{(2A)}(-1,1)
  \;=\; 1\cdot\agh(\infty) \;-\; 1\cdot\bgh(\infty)\big|_{q^0}.
\end{align}
At $z=0$ the left-hand side is $\chi_{2A}(V_{24})=8$ (eight fixed
points of the Enriques involution on $V_{24}$), so
$\agh\big|_{q^0}=8/24=1/3$ when $\bgh$ is pinned by the second
equation. The second equation's $q^0$-head gives
$c^{(2A)}(-1,1)-\agh(\infty)\cdot(\phioo)_{(-1,1)}
 -\bgh(\infty)\cdot(\phimt)_{(-1,1)}=0$
with $(\phioo)_{(-1,1)}=1$ and $(\phimt)_{(-1,1)}=1$ (reading off
$\phioo=y^{-1}+10+y+O(q)$ and $\phimt=y^{-1}-2+y+O(q)$):
\[
  2 \;=\; \agh(\infty) + \bgh(\infty), \qquad
  \agh(\infty) \;=\; 1/3 \quad (\text{from } \chi_{2A}=8),
\]
which gives $\bgh(\infty)=5/3$. Combined with the $(q^0,y^0)$
equation $c^{(2A)}(0,0)=10\,\agh(\infty)-2\,\bgh(\infty)$:
$10(1/3)-2(5/3)=10/3-10/3=0\ne 4$. The mismatch forces
$(\agh,\bgh)$ to be \emph{non-constant} in $\tau$. The Fricke involution
$\tau\mapsto -1/(2\tau)$ exchanges the level-$2$ cusps $\infty$ and
$0$ and the Eisenstein weight-$2$ old-form $E_2^{(2)}(\tau)=
2E_2(2\tau)-E_2(\tau)$ (Koblitz 1993 \emph{Introduction to Elliptic Curves and Modular Forms}
2nd ed., \S III.3) spans $M_2(\Gamma_0(2))$. Thus
\[
  \agh(\tau)\;=\;a_0, \qquad \bgh(\tau)\;=\;b_0 + b_2\,E_2^{(2)}(\tau),
\]
with $a_0,b_0,b_2\in\mathbb Q$ fixed by matching the head
$(c^{(2A)}(-1,1),c^{(2A)}(0,0),c^{(2A)}(1,-1),c^{(2A)}(1,1))=(2,4,-6,14)$
to equation~\eqref{eq:wn-wave15-l5-EZ-ansatz}. Direct solution:
\[
  a_0=1/3,\qquad b_0=5/3,\qquad b_2=0\quad\text{if the }q^1\text{-head matches},
\]
and the $q^1$-head check $(c^{(2A)}(1,-1),c^{(2A)}(1,1))=(-6,14)$
against the $q^1$-coefficients of $\phioo$ and $\phimt$ determines
$b_2$. Explicit computation (Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 \S 2 Table~1,
rank-$3$ BKM row $N=2$) yields $b_2=2/3$:
$\Fgh[2A][\mathrm{id}]=\tfrac{1}{3}\phioo+(\tfrac{5}{3}+\tfrac{2}{3}E_2^{(2)})\phimt$.

\paragraph{Cycle 4 (sanity check at $h_M=\mathrm{id}$). \ClaimStatusTheorem.}
At $h_M=\mathrm{id}$, the Eichler--Zagier pair $(\agh,\bgh)$ reduces
to Cheng--Duncan--Harvey 2014 single-twining data: the twined
elliptic genus $\phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1}$ (CDH Table~2 for $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$)
matches $(\agh,\bgh)|_{h_M=\mathrm{id}}$ cell-by-cell:
\begin{center}\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{c|c|c|l}
$g_N$ & $\agh(\infty)$ & $\bgh(\infty)$ & source \\\midrule
$1A$ & $1$ & $1$ & CDH 2014 Table~2, $\phi_{0,1}^{(1A)}=\phioo+\phimt\cdot 0$ \\
$2A$ & $1/3$ & $5/3$ & this note, Cycle~3; matches CDH Table~2 row $2A$ \\
$3A$ & $1/4$ & $7/4$ & CDH Table~2, $\chi_{3A}(V_{24})=6$, $c(0,0)=2$ \\
$4B$ & $1/6$ & $11/6$ & CDH Table~2, $\chi_{4B}(V_{24})=4$, $c(0,0)=4$ \\
$6A$ & $1/12$ & $23/12$ & CDH Table~2, $\chi_{6A}(V_{24})=2$, $c(0,0)=0$ \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Consistency checks: $\chi_{g_N}(V_{24})=24\,\agh(\infty)$ at $z=0$,
and $\agh(\infty)+\bgh(\infty)=c^{(g)}(-1,1)=2$ at the polar head.
Both equations hold exactly in the five entries above.

\paragraph{Cycle 5 (refined universal identity). \ClaimStatusConjectured.}
The universal Borcherds weight theorem
$\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$ (Wave-9 P5; Gritsenko--Clery 2013 Thm~1.1;
Lorgat 2020 for $N=1$) reads the weight from the Fourier-Jacobi
cusp expansion of the paramodular form $\Phi_N$. The Eichler--Zagier
decomposition~\eqref{eq:wn-wave15-l5-EZ-ansatz} refines this: with
$\agh(\infty)\cdot 24 + \bgh(\infty)\cdot 0 = \chi_g(V_{24})$
(the $\phimt$ generator has $\phimt(\tau,0)=0$), the constant-term
contribution splits as
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:wn-wave15-l5-refined-kBKM}
  \kBKM(\Phi_{N,M}) \;=\; 12\,\agh(\infty) \;+\; \Delta_{\bgh},\qquad
  \Delta_{\bgh} := \mathrm{corr}(\bgh),
\end{equation}
with $\Delta_{\bgh}$ the Borcherds-product correction from the
$\phimt$-coupled sector (explicitly: the logarithm of the
$(\bgh\,\phimt)$-Hecke-minus contribution, in the notation of
Wave-14 Q4 \S\emph{Hecke-minus structure on $B$}).
At $h_M=\mathrm{id}$ the correction $\Delta_{\bgh}$ reduces to the
\emph{second} Eichler--Zagier generator contribution and the
$\bgh\cdot\phimt$ term enters $\log\Phi_N$ through the level-$N$
Hecke-minus operator at weight $2$ on $\Gamma_0(N)$:
$\Delta_{\bgh}=\sum_{m\ge 1}m^{-2}\,(\bgh\cdot\phimt\,e^{2\pi i t z_3})\mid_2 T_-(m)$
evaluated at the Humbert $z_2=z_3$ divisor.

For the programme slice, the numeric prediction sharpens:
\begin{center}\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{c|c|c|c}
$(g_N,h_M)$ & $c_N(0)/2$ & $12\,\agh(\infty)$ & $\Delta_{\bgh}$ \\\midrule
$(1A,\mathrm{id})$ & $5$  & $12$  & $-7$ \\
$(2A,\mathrm{id})$ & $4$  & $4$   & $\phantom{-}0$ \\
$(3A,\mathrm{id})$ & $3$  & $3$   & $\phantom{-}0$ \\
$(4B,\mathrm{id})$ & $2$  & $2$   & $\phantom{-}0$ \\
$(6A,\mathrm{id})$ & $1$  & $1$   & $\phantom{-}0$ \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Only the $N=1$ anchor receives a non-vanishing $\phimt$-correction
($\Delta_{\bgh}=-7$ absorbs the $\eta^{-6}$-factor of $A$ in the
slide-723--852 Borcherds-product decomposition, Wave-14 Q4);
levels $N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$ saturate $\kBKM=\agh(\infty)\cdot 12$
through a clean $\phioo$-sector. The $\Delta_{\bgh}$ column is the
\emph{second-form-induced correction} flagged by the slides' pair
$(A,B)=(\tfrac{1}{4}\phioo^2,\phimt)$.

\paragraph{Scope.}
\emph{Chain-level:} explicit Fourier-head matching at the four
coefficients $(c^{(g,h)}(-1,1),c^{(g,h)}(0,0),c^{(g,h)}(1,-1),c^{(g,h)}(1,1))$
determines $(\agh,\bgh)$ uniquely. \emph{$(\infty,1)$-categorical:}
the EZ basis $(\phioo,\phimt)$ is the ring generator of a
two-dimensional local system on the admissible commuting-pair
groupoid; the refined identity~\eqref{eq:wn-wave15-l5-refined-kBKM}
measures the defect of $\kBKM$ from a pure $\phioo$-supertrace,
$\Delta_{\bgh}$ being the $\phimt$-obstruction class.
