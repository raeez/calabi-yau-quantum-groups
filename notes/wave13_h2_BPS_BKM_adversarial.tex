\section*{Wave-13 H2: Adversarial audit of
  $\gBPS \cong \gBKM$}

\subsection*{Cycle 1: dimensions settle; brackets are everything}

Oberdieck--Pandharipande 2017 and Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998
Proposition~2.5 combine (via denominator-identity uniqueness for
Siegel cusp forms of weight $5$ on $\mathrm{O}(2,3;\Z)$) to force
dim-by-dim equality
\[
  \dim \bigl(\gBPS\bigr)_\alpha
  \;=\; \dim \bigl(\gBKM\bigr)_\alpha
  \qquad
  (\alpha \in \LambdaII),
\]
under the two inputs $\alpha \mapsto \kappa_{\BKM}(\alpha) =
c_1(0)/2 = 5$ and $n^g_h(K3)$ of OP 2017. \emph{This is the only
piece that is a theorem.} Bracket compatibility --- the
Lie-superalgebra isomorphism as opposed to the graded-vector-space
isomorphism --- is untouched by denominator uniqueness. Three
Lie superalgebras can share a character and fail to be isomorphic
(Ray 2006 on lattice-Lie-superalgebra non-rigidity; the obstruction
lives in $H^2(\gBKM_0, \gBKM_0)$-cocycles and is not controlled by
root multiplicities alone). \emph{Obstruction 1 (bracket):} the
$V_{\LambdaII}$-intertwiner must realise brackets, not just
dimensions; Wave-12 C3 did not and cannot reduce to the character
side.

\subsection*{Cycle 2: Cartan-lattice identification is a jump, not
a computation}

$\gBKM$ has a \emph{rank-$3$ hyperbolic} Cartan: the even sublattice
$\LambdaII = \Lambda^{1,1} \oplus [2]$ of the rank-$3$ Lorentzian
$\Lambda^{2,1}$, Weyl group $W^{(2)}(\LambdaII)$ of index~$6$ in
$\mathrm{O}(\LambdaII)_+$, three real simple roots $\delta_1,
\delta_2, \delta_3$ with
$(\delta_i, \delta_j) = \mathrm{Gram}\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}-2 &
 2 & 2 \\ 2 & -2 & 2 \\ 2 & 2 & -2 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 \S2.4).

$\gBPS$ is \emph{a priori graded by} $H^{\mathrm{ev}}(K3 \times E,
\Z) \cong \Lambda_\Muk(K3) \oplus H^*(E, \Z)$ of rank
$24 + 2 = 26$ with signature $(5, 21)$ --- not by $\LambdaII$.
The projection
\[
  \pi : H^{\mathrm{ev}}(K3 \times E, \Z) \twoheadrightarrow
  \LambdaII
\]
is \emph{not canonical}. Inside
\S\ref{sec:fact-cn}-adjacent text (\texttt{k3e\_bkm\_chapter.tex}
lines 546--643) the identification
$\LambdaII \hookrightarrow \Lambda_\Muk$ is \emph{stated} via the
null direction $\rho$ and the BRST realisation $V_{\LambdaII}
\otimes V_{K3} \otimes \mathrm{bc}$; but the stated embedding
requires a \emph{choice} of Weyl-vector direction and of the $[2]$
summand that is not a BPS-invariant of $K3 \times E$. Two different
choices (e.g.\ two distinct $[2]$-sublattices of the discriminant
form of $\Lambda_\Muk$) give two different $\gBPS$'s. \emph{Obstruction 2
(Cartan non-canonicity):} the rank-$3$-vs-$26$ projection needs a
construction (not a citation) of a canonical $\LambdaII \hookrightarrow
H^{\mathrm{ev}}(K3 \times E)$ equivariant under the BPS grading. No
such construction is in-manuscript; a $\mathrm{O}(\LambdaII, \Z)$
ambiguity of the $W^{(2)}$-Weyl-vector choice remains a
$\mathrm{O}(3,2;\Z)/W^{(2)}$ ambiguity.

\subsection*{Cycle 3: equivariant parameters vs rigid BKM}

$\gBPS$ constructed from DT/reduced-DT on $K3 \times E$ carries a
torus-localisation $T = (\C^*)^3$-equivariant structure with
parameters $(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3)$,
$\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2 + \epsilon_3 = 0$ in the CY-3 case
(Nekrasov--Okounkov; Schiffmann--Vasserot). The chiral output
of Chapter~\ref{ch:k3-chiral-algebra} \textit{inherits} a non-trivial
$\varepsilon$-deformation (cf.\ \texttt{k3e\_bkm\_chapter.tex}
line 2587: Omega-background deformation with weight correction).

$\gBKM$ has \emph{no} such structure: its denominator formula is
rigid, $\Delta_5(Z)$ has no equivariant parameter. Setting
$\varepsilon = 0$ recovers the conformal-weight spectrum (line
2634: ``universal weight collapse at $\varepsilon = 1$'' is listed
as a check, not a proof of isomorphism at generic $\varepsilon$).
\emph{Obstruction 3 (equivariant mismatch):} the intertwiner
must land in the \emph{$T$-invariant} part. If
$(\gBPS)^T \neq \gBPS$, the identification is with only the
$T$-fixed subalgebra; Wave-12 C3 does not register this
restriction, and K3 surface geometry (no generic $T$-action)
obstructs even defining $T$ canonically on $K3 \times E$. The
manuscript's own scope-declaration (\texttt{k3e\_bkm\_chapter.tex}
line 1136, ``conjectural; the intermediary mechanism involves
equivariant localization'') is an acknowledgment of this obstruction,
not a resolution.

\subsection*{Cycle 4: Davison's construction does not apply}

Davison 2017 (arXiv:1601.02479) Theorem A constructs
$\mathfrak{g}_\BPS(Q, W)$ for a \emph{quiver with potential}
$(Q, W)$: BPS Lie algebra $=$ primitive part of the CoHA
$\mathcal{H}(Q, W)$ under the integrality isomorphism
$\mathcal{H}(Q, W) \cong \mathrm{Sym}(\mathfrak{g}_\BPS(Q,W) \otimes
H^*_T(\mathrm{pt})[u])$. The construction is \emph{not}
general --- it requires:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a quiver-with-potential presentation
  $D^b(\Coh(X)) \simeq D^b(\mathrm{mod}\,\mathrm{Jac}(Q, W))$;
\item the potential $W$ to be of finite type (or at least with
  vanishing-cycle formalism applicable).
\end{enumerate}
$K3 \times E$ has \emph{no} such presentation: it is a smooth
projective CY-3 without a tilting object (Bridgeland 2008 \emph{Duke
  Math.\ J.}\ 141, no tilting for $K3$; $K3 \times E$ inherits).
Van~den~Bergh's NCCR machinery fails (non-local, non-affine).
\emph{Obstruction 4 (construction-level):} even the \emph{input}
$\gBPS$ to the Wave-12 C3 reduction is not defined by Davison 2017
for $K3 \times E$. The manuscript's
\texttt{cy\_holographic\_datum\_master.tex} line 328 records the
symplectic-Fukaya analogue as ``conjectural --- neither (i) HMS at
CY-3 derived-stack level nor (ii) Joyce pair-of-pants on Lagrangian
moduli is proved''. The coherent side is in the same position:
$\mathcal{H}(K3 \times E)$ as a \emph{motivic} Hall algebra is
unconditional (\texttt{k3e\_bkm\_chapter.tex} line 1106); the
\emph{passage to a BPS Lie algebra} (primitive-part extraction)
requires Kontsevich--Soibelman integrality for smooth projective
CY-3, proved only for local models (toric CY-3, resolutions of
$\mathbb{C}^3/\Gamma$). For $K3 \times E$ the integrality
statement is open; Davison--Meinhardt 2020 proves it for
symmetric quivers, not for K3-fibered CY-3.

\subsection*{Cycle 5: potential counterexamples in both directions}

\textbf{BPS-side states without BKM witness.} Maulik--Thomas
2023 (\emph{Gopakumar--Vafa invariants via reduced DT} for K3) and
Oberdieck 2022 (\emph{Reduced Donaldson--Thomas}) show the reduced-DT
BPS indices $\widehat{n}^g_h$ for $K3$ in \emph{non-primitive} class
$m \cdot \beta$ ($m \geq 2$) carry \emph{multi-cover corrections}
(Pandharipande--Thomas 2014) whose BKM-side witness is not in
$c_1(0)/2 = 5$: they lie in the $N \geq 2$ sector
($N$-fold cover $\to$ $N = m$ multiplier system). The Wave-12 C3
reduction to a single $V_{\LambdaII}$-module-map is insufficient:
the full intertwiner must be a $V_{\bigoplus_N \LambdaII^{(N)}}$-module
map over the orbit structure $\{N = 1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$
(\texttt{cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}
Theorem~\texttt{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal}). A
single-$N$ intertwiner misses the $m \geq 2$ BPS sector.

\textbf{BKM-side roots without BPS witness.} $\gBKM$ has
\emph{imaginary} simple roots of norm $-2k$ for all $k \geq 1$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 Table on p.~18): the imaginary roots are
the Fourier coefficients $f(n, l, m)$ with $4nm - l^2 < 0$ and
$l$ arbitrary. The reduced-DT spectrum on $K3 \times E$ (OP 2017)
is \emph{supported} on classes
$(n, l, m)$ satisfying \emph{positivity} $n, m \geq 0$ and
$|l| \leq n + m$ (effectivity bound, Bayer--Macr\`i 2014). The
imaginary roots at $4nm - l^2 < 0$, $n + m < 0$ that appear in
$\gBKM$ via the dual-cone extension have no BPS witness in
effective-class DT.
\emph{Obstruction 5 (effective-cone mismatch):} the BKM
dual-cone extension covers $\LambdaII$ symmetrically
($\alpha \leftrightarrow -\alpha$), but reduced-DT covers only the
effective cone. Wave-12 C3 does not close this asymmetry; a formal
``negative-class'' BPS algebra must be defined, and its only known
candidate (Mochizuki 2009 wall-crossing extension) lifts the
asymmetry only up to a sign.

\subsection*{Summary of obstructions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Bracket vs character.} Denominator uniqueness controls
  root multiplicities, not Lie-bracket compatibility; the Wave-12
  C3 $V_{\LambdaII}$-intertwiner is necessary, not sufficient, for
  the algebra isomorphism.
\item \emph{Cartan projection non-canonical.} $\LambdaII
  \hookrightarrow H^{\mathrm{ev}}(K3 \times E, \Z)$ requires a
  Weyl-vector choice; a $\mathrm{O}(3, 2; \Z)/W^{(2)}$ orbit
  ambiguity persists.
\item \emph{Equivariant mismatch.} $\gBPS$ carries a torus
  $T$-grading; $\gBKM$ does not; the isomorphism can only hold on
  $(\gBPS)^T$, and $T$ itself is non-canonical on a non-toric
  CY-3.
\item \emph{Davison-inapplicability.} $K3 \times E$ is not a
  quiver-with-potential; the BPS Lie algebra is not unconditionally
  defined; Kontsevich--Soibelman integrality is open for K3-fibered
  CY-3.
\item \emph{Effective-cone asymmetry and multi-cover.} Imaginary
  BKM roots outside the effective cone have no BPS witness;
  $m \geq 2$ multi-cover BPS states lie in the $N \geq 2$ sector
  outside the $\Delta_5$/$N = 1$ denominator.
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Net verdict.} The Wave-12 C3 reduction is a necessary
condition on a subalgebra of $\gBPS^T$ against a subspace of
$\gBKM$ matching the effective cone and the single-$N$ sector.
The conjecture as stated ($\gBPS \cong \gBKM$ as Lie
superalgebras, unconditioned) fails four independent sufficiency
tests and rests on a construction (BPS Lie algebra for a smooth
projective K3-fibered CY-3) that is itself conjectural. Downgrade
recommended: state as a conjecture conditional on (a)
Kontsevich--Soibelman integrality for $K3 \times E$, (b) a
canonical $\LambdaII$-projection, (c) a $T$-invariance reduction,
and (d) an effective-cone/BKM-root matching dictionary
(each of which is itself conjectural under the present manuscript).
The one-line $V_{\LambdaII}$-intertwiner is the fifth of these,
not the only one.
