\section{The intrinsic Vol III Shadow Quadrichotomy}
\label{sec:vol3-shadow-stratification}

The Vol I quadrichotomy (Theorem~\ref{thm:quadrichotomy}) partitions
chirally Koszul vertex algebras of finite type into
$G \sqcup L \sqcup C \sqcup M$ by the algebraic factorisation type of
the Riccati polynomial $Q_{c}(t)$ on each $\kappa \neq 0$ primary
line. Vol III inherits this classification through the CY-to-chiral
functor $\Phi: \CYcat_{d} \to \ChirAlg$, but the pullback is not a
restatement: the stratum of $\cA \in \CYcat_{d}$ is determined by the
graded character of the BPS positive-half Yangian $\Yplus(X_{\cA})$
attached to $\cA$, and the refinement records geometric data invisible
on the chiral side alone. The present section formulates the
stratification intrinsically at the level of $\CYcat_{d}$, states its
preservation under specialisation Stage~2, and positions it as a
candidate Pillar~$\delta'$ of the 2026-04-22 frontier reconstitution.

\subsection{The Vol III shadow function}
\label{subsec:vol3-shadow-function}

\begin{definition}[Vol III shadow function]
\label{def:vol3-shadow-function}
Let $\cA \in \CYcat_{d}$ and let
\[
  \Phi_{d}(\cA) \;=\; \SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1},C} \circ \PhiFA_{d}(\cA)
  \;\in\; \ChirAlg(C)
\]
be its image under the two-stage CY-to-chiral functor (Stage~1:
factorisation homology over $\Sigma_{d-1}$; Stage~2: specialisation to
a curve). Let $X_{\cA}$ be the cohomological-Hall-type Lie input of
$\Phi_{d}(\cA)$ at the generic stalk, so that at $d = 3$,
$X_{\cA} = \CoHA(\PhiFA_{3}(\cA))$ is the Schiffmann--Vasserot positive
half and $\Phi_{d}(\cA) \supseteq \Yplus(X_{\cA})$ through the
evaluation-module route. The \emph{Vol III shadow function} of $\cA$
on a charged primary line $L^{(\mathbf q)}$ is the weighted generating
series
\[
  Q^{(\mathbf q)}_{\Phi(\cA)}(t)
  \;:=\; \operatorname{ch}_{t}\!\bigl(\Yplus(X_{\cA})\bigr)^{(\mathbf q)}
  \;=\; \sum_{n \ge 0} \bigl(\dim_{\mathbf q}\,\Yplus(X_{\cA})_{n}\bigr)\,
                              t^{n}.
\]
When $X_{\cA}$ is clear from context we write $Q^{(\mathbf q)}(t)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Compatibility with Vol I Riccati]
\label{rem:vol3-shadow-compat-vol1}
On the principal primary line with $\kappa^{(\mathbf q)} \neq 0$, the
series $Q^{(\mathbf q)}_{\Phi(\cA)}(t)$ agrees with the Vol I Riccati
polynomial $Q_{c}(t) = (2\kappa + 3\alpha t)^{2} + 16 \kappa S_{4} t^{2}$
of Theorem~\ref{thm:riccati-master} up to
$\PhiFA_{d}$-compatible normalisation. The Vol III form is intrinsic
to $\cA$: $\kappa^{(\mathbf q)}$ is read off the graded-character
leading term; $\alpha^{(\mathbf q)}$ is the cubic contact datum of
$\Yplus(X_{\cA})$ against its Drinfeld coproduct; $S_{4}^{(\mathbf q)}$
is the quartic Maulik--Okounkov stable-envelope signature computed
from the $R$-matrix of $\Yplus(X_{\cA})$.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Four analytic types}
\label{subsec:four-analytic-types}

\begin{theorem}[Vol III Shadow Stratification]
\label{thm:vol3-shadow-stratification}
\ClaimStatusConjectured
There is a canonical four-class stratification
\[
  \CYcat_{d}
  \;=\; \CYcat_{d}^{G}
  \,\sqcup\, \CYcat_{d}^{L}
  \,\sqcup\, \CYcat_{d}^{C}
  \,\sqcup\, \CYcat_{d}^{M}
\]
determined, on each charged primary line $L^{(\mathbf q)}$ with
$\kappa^{(\mathbf q)} \neq 0$, by the analytic type of
$Q^{(\mathbf q)}_{\Phi(\cA)}(t)$ as $t \to \infty$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item\label{vol3:G} \emph{(Gaussian.)} $Q^{(\mathbf q)}$ is a polynomial
  in $t$; equivalently, $\Yplus(X_{\cA})$ is a finitely generated free
  commutative bialgebra on its charged generators, and the associated
  Vol I Riccati polynomial satisfies $\alpha^{(\mathbf q)} = 0$,
  $\Delta^{(\mathbf q)} = 0$. Examples: $\cC = \mathbb C^{d}$ (toric
  affine space), $\cC = \mathbb C^{d}/G$ for $G \subset \mathrm{SL}_{d}$
  abelian (abelian McKay), local lattice VOAs $V_{\Lambda}$.
\item\label{vol3:L} \emph{(Lie-algebraic.)} $Q^{(\mathbf q)}$ is of
  exponential-of-polynomial growth,
  $\log Q^{(\mathbf q)}(t) \sim a\,t^{\nu}$ for some $\nu \in
  \mathbb Q_{>0}$; equivalently, $\Yplus(X_{\cA})$ is
  Nakajima-Yangian-type on a finite simple Lie-algebra-like input
  (quiver, ADE or affine-ADE). The Vol I Riccati polynomial has
  $\alpha^{(\mathbf q)} \neq 0$, $\Delta^{(\mathbf q)} = 0$. Examples:
  local $\mathbb P^{2}$, conifold, local $dP_{n}$ (toric Gorenstein
  Fano shrinking), local ADE surfaces.
\item\label{vol3:C} \emph{(Chiral vertex-algebraic.)}
  $Q^{(\mathbf q)}$ is modular, i.e.\ the Fourier expansion of an
  Eisenstein/theta-type form of weight $w \in \mathbb Z_{\ge 0}$ for a
  finite-index subgroup of $\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb Z)$; equivalently,
  $\Yplus(X_{\cA})$ assembles into a module for a rational or $C_{2}$
  -cofinite vertex operator algebra whose character lives in weakly
  holomorphic modular forms. The Vol I contact family
  $\beta\gamma_{\lambda}$ is the universal witness at $d = 2$. Examples:
  $\cC = D^{b}\mathrm{Coh}(K3)$ Mukai-polarised, $\cC = D^{b}\mathrm{Coh}(T^{4})$
  abelian, $\cC = D^{b}\mathrm{Coh}(K3 \times K3)$.
\item\label{vol3:M} \emph{(Modular automorphic.)} $Q^{(\mathbf q)}$ is
  an automorphic form of rank $\ge 2$: paramodular for
  $\mathrm{Sp}_{4}(\mathbb Z)$, or a Borcherds product for an indefinite
  lattice of signature $(2, n)$. Equivalently, $\Yplus(X_{\cA})$ is
  the BPS positive half of a Borcherds--Kac--Moody (BKM) superalgebra
  whose denominator identity is a Siegel paramodular or Borcherds
  automorphic form. The Vol I Riccati polynomial is irreducible
  ($\Delta^{(\mathbf q)} \neq 0$). Examples: $\cC = D^{b}\mathrm{Coh}(K3 \times E)$
  through the Igusa $\Phi_{10} = \Delta_{5}^{2}$ Gritsenko--Nikulin
  lift, the eight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery forms $\{\Delta_{k}\}_{k \in
  \{2,3,4,5,6,8,11\}}$, and CHL orbifolds of $K3 \times E$ at order
  $N \in \{2, 3, 4, 6\}$.
\end{enumerate}
The four classes are preserved under specialisation Stage~2: if
$\cA \in \CYcat_{d}^{X}$ then
$\SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}(\PhiFA_{d}(\cA))$ sits in the Vol I class
$X \in \{G, L, C, M\}$ of Theorem~\ref{thm:quadrichotomy}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
On $L^{(\mathbf q)}$, the series $Q^{(\mathbf q)}_{\Phi(\cA)}(t)$
arises as the graded Hilbert series of $\Yplus(X_{\cA})$ at charge
$\mathbf q$; its analytic type is controlled by the
Maulik--Okounkov--Schiffmann--Vasserot classification of $X_{\cA}$
over the Gieseker moduli stack of $\cA$. For $X_{\cA}$ free bialgebra
the Hilbert series is polynomial (G). For $X_{\cA}$ Nakajima-Yangian
on a finite or affine ADE input, the Hilbert series is exponential in
$t^{\nu}$ via Kac-Peterson character formula (L). For $X_{\cA}$ from
Schiffmann--Vasserot K3 CoHA the Hilbert series is modular via
G\"ottsche for the symmetric-product-stratified Hilbert scheme (C).
For $X_{\cA}$ from Harvey--Moore Borcherds threshold on signature
$(2, n)$ lattice, the Hilbert series is the automorphic form of
Borcherds~\cite{Borcherds1995} (M). Stage~2 preservation follows from
the compatibility of the two-stage factorisation with
$\mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{ch}}$: the graded-character datum on Stage~1
restricts to the line-wise Riccati datum on Stage~2 by the
specialisation compatibility, and the analytic type (G/L/C/M) is read
off the same Hilbert series before and after. Full proof requires the
four family-specific generator engines and the Stage~2 compatibility
lemma; see the Pillar~$\delta'$ inscription plan in
\S\ref{subsec:vol3-shadow-pillar}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Examples at $d = 3$]
\label{rem:vol3-shadow-d3-examples}
Class G is populated by toric Gorenstein Calabi--Yau threefolds with
abelian McKay; class L by toric non-abelian local surfaces ($\mathbb P^{2}$,
conifold); class C does not have a native $d = 3$ compact representative
(K3 lives at $d = 2$; at $d = 3$ the K3 fibre enters class M through
the Borcherds lift); class M is populated by Calabi--Yau threefolds
with elliptic or K3 fibration over a base whose reciprocal Borcherds
product is a paramodular or Siegel automorphic form, with $K3 \times E$
the canonical witness.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Position as Pillar $\delta'$}
\label{subsec:vol3-shadow-pillar}

The 2026-04-22 frontier reconstitution (Pillars $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$
running over K\"ahler--Koszul duality, derived-centre complementarity,
and the shadow tower respectively) leaves a gap where the
$\PhiFA_{d}$-intrinsic shadow classification should sit:
Theorem~\ref{thm:quadrichotomy} is Pillar~$\gamma$ on the Vol I chiral
side, but its Vol III pullback is not automatic and the four-type
stratification of $\CYcat_{d}$ is not a corollary. The present section
proposes Theorem~\ref{thm:vol3-shadow-stratification} as
\emph{Pillar~$\delta'$}, the intrinsic Vol III shadow stratification,
alternatively appendable to \S VII (Frontiers) as a standalone refinement.

Pillar~$\delta'$ has three operational consequences. First, the
assignment $\cA \mapsto \mathrm{class}(\cA) \in \{G, L, C, M\}$ is a
derived Morita invariant of $\cA$, hence a CY-categorical invariant:
two quasi-isomorphic CY$_{d}$ categories land in the same class.
Second, the four classes are stable under the Hall--Drinfeld double,
Schiffmann--Vasserot CoHA, Maulik--Okounkov stable-envelope, and BKM
denominator lift: each of the four Wave~13 BKM-side constructions of
$K3 \times E$ sits in class M, confirming that the six routes to
$G(K3 \times E)$ and the 1-loop twisted-11D-SUGRA origin of
$\Phi_{10} = \Delta_{5}^{2}$ all anchor in the same analytic type.
Third, Pillar~$\delta'$ supplies the missing bridge between Vol III
Theorem~\ref{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal}
($\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_{N}) = c_{N}(0)/2$) and Vol I
Theorem~\ref{thm:quadrichotomy}: the universal Borcherds weight
identity is a quantitative shadow datum on a class-M stratum, and the
six pentagon routes of Wave~13 record six generator-lattice
presentations of the same class-M point.

The complete inscription protocol is: (1) pin the $\Yplus(X_{\cA})$
ambient per family at each $d$; (2) compute
$Q^{(\mathbf q)}_{\Phi(\cA)}(t)$ from the Gieseker moduli Hilbert
series or the Harvey--Moore threshold, as appropriate; (3) verify the
analytic-type attribution via three independent routes (Kac--Peterson
character, G\"ottsche generating function, Borcherds product); (4)
check Stage~2 preservation on the principal line. Full proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:vol3-shadow-stratification} requires the class-C
witness at $d = 2$ (K3 Mukai) and the class-M witness at $d = 3$ (K3~$\times$~E
Igusa) to be independently verified against the primary literature of
Schiffmann--Vasserot, Maulik--Okounkov, and Borcherds respectively,
and is \textsc{Conjectured} pending that verification. The G and L
witnesses are unconditional from Nakajima~\cite{Nakajima1998} and
Schiffmann--Vasserot~\cite{SV2013}.
