\section*{CHL Borcherds constants and reduced Donaldson--Thomas boundary at
\texorpdfstring{$N=2,3$}{N=2,3}}
\label{sec:chl-n23-borcherds-dt-boundary}

Let
\[
  X^{(N)}=[K3\times E/\Z_N], \qquad N\in\{2,3\},
\]
where \(g_N\) is a Mukai symplectic automorphism of the K3 factor and
\(\Z_N\) acts on \(E\) by translation.  Write \(\PhiCHL_N\) for the
primitive CHL Borcherds denominator attached to the \(g_N\)-twined
K3 weak Jacobi form \(\phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1}\).  The dd-Siegel symbol
\(\Fgc_{t,N}\) is reserved for the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery diagonal-divisor
catalogue indexed by paramodular type \((t,N)\).

\subsection*{Borcherds constants}

\paragraph{Theorem.}
For the primitive CHL denominator convention,
\[
  c_2(0)=8,\qquad \kBKM(\PhiCHL_2)=4,
\]
and
\[
  c_3(0)=6,\qquad \kBKM(\PhiCHL_3)=3.
\]

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{c|c|c|c|c|c}
\hline
CHL level & Mukai frame shape & \(T_{H^*}(g_N)\) & \(A_N\) &
\(c_N(0)=T_{H^*}(g_N)-2A_N\) & \(\kBKM(\PhiCHL_N)\) \\
\hline
\(2\) & \(1^8\,2^8\) & \(16\) & \(4\) & \(8\) & \(4\) \\
\(3\) & \(1^6\,3^6\) & \(12\) & \(3\) & \(6\) & \(3\) \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{proof}
The \(g_N\)-twined K3 elliptic genus has index-one theta decomposition
\[
  \phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1}(\tau,z)
  =h^{(N)}_0(\tau)\theta_{1,0}(\tau,z)
   +h^{(N)}_1(\tau)\theta_{1,1}(\tau,z).
\]
Let \(A_N=[q^0]h^{(N)}_1\) and let
\[
  T_{H^*}(g_N)=\operatorname{tr}\bigl(g_N^*\mid H^*(K3,\mathbb Q)\bigr).
\]
The specialization \(z=0\) gives the Lefschetz trace, and the
\(\zeta^0q^0\)-coefficient extraction gives
\[
  c_N(0)=T_{H^*}(g_N)-2A_N.
\]
Mukai's frame shapes give \(T_{H^*}(g_2)=16\) and
\(T_{H^*}(g_3)=12\).  The Gritsenko--Nikulin/Eguchi--Hikami theta
corrections are \(A_2=4\) and \(A_3=3\).  Hence
\[
  c_2(0)=16-2\cdot4=8,\qquad c_3(0)=12-2\cdot3=6.
\]
Borcherds' singular-theta automorphy theorem gives
\(\operatorname{wt}(\PhiCHL_N)=c_N(0)/2\).  This proves the two displayed
\(\kBKM\)-values.  Local anchors are
Theorem~\texttt{thm:k3e-jacobi-theta-refinement},
Lemma~\texttt{lem:c-N-zero-closed-form}, and
Theorem~\texttt{thm:k3e-universal-kBKM-two-scopes}.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Reduced Donaldson--Thomas boundary}

\paragraph{Proposition.}
The general identities
\[
  \ZredDT(X^{(2)})=-\frac{1}{\FCHL_2{}^{\,2}},
  \qquad
  \ZredDT(X^{(3)})=-\frac{1}{\FCHL_3{}^{\,2}}
\]
are conjectural at full primitive and imprimitive scope.  The available
theorem-level geometry is:
\begin{itemize}
\item \(N=1\): the Oberdieck--Pixton/Oberdieck--Pandharipande
  \(K3\times E\) scalar theorem in the \(\Delta_5^2\) convention;
\item \(N=2\): Bryan--Oberdieck 2018 proves the CHL reduced-DT identity
  for primitive K3 classes with square \(-2\) or \(0\);
\item \(N=3\): the Borcherds denominator and weight are proved
  automorphically; the corresponding reduced-DT theorem remains a
  Bryan--Oberdieck conjecture.
\end{itemize}

\begin{proof}
Bryan--Oberdieck 2018 is the CHL curve-counting source:
Conjecture~0.1 states the primitive reduced-DT formula for
\(N\in\{2,3,4,6\}\), and Theorem~0.1 verifies the \(N=2\) base cases of
self-intersection \(-2\) and \(0\).  The automorphic equality
\(\kBKM(\PhiCHL_N)=c_N(0)/2\) survives independently of this DT
boundary because it is a Borcherds-product weight theorem.
\end{proof}

The missing theorem inputs for the displayed DT identities are precise:
equivariant reduced PT/GW for all relevant CHL classes, an orbifold or
crepant-resolution comparison compatible with the reduced obstruction
theory, the reduced MNOP comparison in that quotient setting, and the
normalisation identifying the scalar-square lift with \(\FCHL_N{}^2\).

\subsection*{Dimension and invariant split}

\paragraph{Dimension.}
For a CY\(_3\) category \(\mathcal C\), the two-stage construction has
canonical stage-one object \(\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\mathcal C)\) and
stage-two specialisations
\[
  A_{\mathcal C,\Sigma_2,C}
  =\operatorname{Sp}_{\Sigma_2,C}\bigl(\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\mathcal C)\bigr).
\]
The native chiral output is \(E_1\).  Braided \(E_2\)-structure appears
after the Drinfeld-centre passage
\(\mathcal Z(\operatorname{Rep}^{E_1}(A_{\mathcal C,\Sigma_2,C}))\).

\paragraph{The \(K3\times E\) split.}

specialisation are separate entries.
