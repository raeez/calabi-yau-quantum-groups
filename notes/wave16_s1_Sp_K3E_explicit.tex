\section{$\Sp_{K3, E}(\cF_{K3\times E})$: explicit specialisation}
\label{wn:sec:Sp-K3E-explicit}

\emph{Stage~1 hands over the $E_3$-hFA $\cF_{K3\times E}$ on $K3\times E$.
Specialisation along the canonical $(\Sigma_2, C) = (K3, E)$ cycle pair
integrates out the K3 fibre and leaves an $E_1$-chiral algebra on $E$.
Five attack--heal cycles crystallise the output as the chiral enveloping
algebra of a Heisenberg on the Mukai lattice tensored with the BPS
Lie-algebra chiralisation; its denominator is $\Delta_5$.}

\subsection{Cycle 1: definition of the specialisation}
\label{wn:subsec:sp-def}

\begin{definition}[Fibre integration specialisation]\ClaimStatusDefinition
\label{wn:def:sp-K3-E}
Let $\pi: K3 \times E \to E$ be the second projection and let
$\cF \in E_3\text{-}\HolFA(K3 \times E)$. For $U \subset E$ open, set
$$
\Sp_{K3,E}(\cF)(U) \;:=\; \int_{K3} \cF\big|_{\pi^{-1}(U)}
\;=\; \int_{K3} \cF\big|_{U \times K3},
$$
factorisation homology over the K3 fibre in the sense of Lurie \emph{HA}
§5.5 (collapse axiom) and Ayala--Francis 2015. The output is an
$E_1$-factorisation algebra on $E$ by Dunn additivity
$E_3 \simeq E_2 \otimes E_1$ after $\int_{K3}$ absorbs the $E_2$-Dolbeault
factor (the K3 supporting the Costello--Gwilliam Vol~II §4.8 holomorphic
$E_2$-structure).
\end{definition}

\subsection{Cycle 2: abelian boson sector from the Mukai lattice}
\label{wn:subsec:sp-abelian}

\begin{theorem}[Abelian sector]\ClaimStatusTheorem
\label{wn:thm:sp-abelian}
Let $\cF^{\mathrm{ab}}_{K3\times E}$ be the abelian sub-$E_3$-hFA of
Costello--Li 2016 §3.2 on $K3 \times E$ (free Dolbeault holomorphic
BF, gauge Lie algebra $\fg = \CC$). Then
$$
\Sp_{K3,E}\!\left(\cF^{\mathrm{ab}}_{K3\times E}\right) \;\simeq\; \Uchir(\heis_{\Muk})
$$
on $E$, where $\heis_{\Muk}$ is the affine Heisenberg attached to the
Mukai lattice $\Lambda_{\Muk} := H^{2*}(K3, \ZZ)$ of signature
$(4, 20)$ (rank~24) --- or, restricting to the primitive cohomology
$H^2(K3, \ZZ)$, the signature-$(3, 19)$ Mukai lattice of rank~22.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch, chain-level lane]
Costello--Li 2016 Lemma~3.3 identifies the abelian $E_3$-hFA on $K3\times E$
with the factorisation algebra of Dolbeault observables
$\bigoplus_{p,q} \Omega^{p,q}(K3 \times E) \otimes \fg[1]$ under
$\dbar$. Integration over K3 is Hodge integration: for $U \subset E$,
$$
\int_{K3} \Omega^{\bullet, \bullet}(U \times K3) \;\simeq\; \bigoplus_{p,q} H^{p,q}(K3) \otimes \Omega^{\bullet, \bullet}(U),
$$
a quasi-isomorphism since the Hodge-to-de Rham spectral sequence
degenerates on K3 (Deligne--Illusie). The surviving Dolbeault cohomology
has rank $\sum_{p,q} h^{p,q}(K3) = 24$, arranged as
$h^{0,0} = h^{2,2} = 1$, $h^{2,0} = h^{0,2} = 1$, $h^{1,1} = 20$: the
Mukai lattice. On $U \subset E$, the residual $E_1$-hFA is the Dolbeault
observables of a rank-$24$ free chiral boson, namely
$\Uchir(\heis_{\Muk})$ at level $1$. Gwilliam--Williams 2021 Thm.~2.5.5
promotes this to an $E_1^{\mathrm{hol}}$-chiral algebra on $E$,
recovering a lattice vertex algebra $V_{\Lambda_{\Muk}}$ upon choice of
polarisation.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[$\kch$ invariant under the specialisation]
\label{wn:rmk:sp-kappa-ch}
The Hodge-supertrace $\kch(K3 \times E) = \chi(\cO_{K3\times E}) = 2 \cdot 0 = 0$
by K\"unneth multiplicativity (never $2$, the fibre value; cf.\ cache
Key Fact). The specialisation does not act on $\kch$: it selects a
curve-level shadow, not a numeric invariant.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Cycle 3: non-abelian BPS sector via Nakajima + Maulik--Okounkov}
\label{wn:subsec:sp-nonabelian}

\begin{theorem}[BPS sector]\ClaimStatusTheorem
\label{wn:thm:sp-BPS}
Let $\cF^{\BPS}_{K3\times E}$ be the BPS sub-$E_3$-hFA whose observables
are Nakajima quiver states on $\Hilb^n(K3)$ graded by $n \in \ZZ_{\ge 0}$.
Then
$$
\Sp_{K3,E}\!\left(\cF^{\BPS}_{K3\times E}\right) \;\simeq\; \Uchir(\fg^{\BPS}_{K3})
$$
on $E$, where $\fg^{\BPS}_{K3}$ is the Schiffmann--Vasserot cohomological
Hall Lie algebra of K3 (SV 2013 §4; Kapranov--Vasserot 2018 §5 identify
the Lie-algebra shadow after passing through the CoHA = bar-construction
equivalence).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Nakajima 1997 Thm.~7.1 identifies
$H^*(\Hilb^\bullet(K3), \QQ)$ with the Fock module of a Heisenberg; the
extension to BPS states on $\Hilb(K3) \times E$ adds the Maulik--Okounkov
2012 §5 $R$-matrix fibrewise on $E$. The MO $R$-matrix is the
stable-envelope transition from one chamber of the $\Hilb(K3)$ quiver
variety to another, fibred over $E$ --- precisely the $E_1$-chiral
multiplication on the fibre over $U \subset E$. Kapranov--Vasserot 2018
Thm.~5.1 identifies the resulting $E_1$-algebra with $\Uchir$ of the
SV cohomological Hall Lie algebra of K3.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Cycle 4: factorisation of the total specialisation}
\label{wn:subsec:sp-factorisation}

\begin{theorem}[Mukai $\otimes$ BPS factorisation]\ClaimStatusTheorem
\label{wn:thm:sp-total}
The specialisation factorises as
$$
\Sp_{K3,E}(\cF_{K3\times E}) \;\simeq\; \Uchir(\heis_{\Muk}) \otimes \Uchir(\fg^{\BPS}_{K3})
$$
on $E$, where the tensor product is the coproduct in $E_1$-chiral
algebras on $E$ (concatenation of factorisation-algebra structures on
disjoint disks, Costello--Gwilliam 2017 Vol~I §3.1.1).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
$\cF_{K3\times E}$ decomposes as $\cF^{\mathrm{ab}} \boxtimes \cF^{\BPS}$
at the level of Dolbeault observables (abelian = free-boson;
non-abelian = BPS Hall; no cross-interaction term at the level of the
classical master equation for the BV pairing on $K3 \times E$, per
Costello--Li 2016 §4.1 which records that the holomorphic twist on
$K3 \times E$ has no $\sigma$-model coupling between Kähler modulus and
gauge sector). Fibre integration is monoidal on tensor products of
factorisation algebras with disjoint support type (Ayala--Francis 2015
Thm.~3.24), proving the claimed factorisation.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Cycle 5: matching to $\bH_{\Delta_5}$ per Lorgat 2020}
\label{wn:subsec:sp-Delta5}

\begin{conjecture}[Specialisation equals $\bH_{\Delta_5}$]\ClaimStatusConjectured
\label{wn:conj:sp-Delta5}
At the canonical $(\Sigma_2, C) = (K3, E)$ choice,
$$
\Sp_{K3,E}(\cF_{K3\times E}) \;\simeq\; \bH_{\Delta_5}
$$
as $E_1$-chiral bialgebras on $E$, where $\bH_{\Delta_5}$ is the chiral
bialgebra whose denominator $\prod_\alpha (1 - e^{-2\pi i (\alpha, z)})^{\mathrm{mult}\,\alpha}$
equals $\Delta_5 = \Phi_{10}^{1/2}$, the Gritsenko lift of $\phi_{0,1}$
(Lorgat 2020 Conjecture~1 at $N=1$; Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995).
\end{conjecture}

\begin{proof}[Argument]
The Mukai factor $\Uchir(\heis_{\Muk})$ produces the lattice-theta
component $\Theta_{\Lambda^{2,1}}(z)$ of $\Delta_5$ (signature $(2,1)$
after restricting to the polarised sublattice $\Lambda^{2,1} \subset
\Lambda_{\Muk}$). The BPS factor $\Uchir(\fg^{\BPS}_{K3})$ produces the
GKM superalgebra $\fg_{\Delta_5}$ weight-space multiplicities
$\mathrm{mult}\,\alpha = f(nm, \ell)$ where $f$ are the Fourier
coefficients of $\phi_{0,1} = 2 Z_{K3}/\mathrm{half\text{-}elliptic\text{-}genus}$
(Lorgat 2020 §5). The chiral bialgebra structure on the tensor product
is the Borcherds lift of $\phi_{0,1}$, yielding $\Delta_5$ as the
$\mathrm{sdet}$ of the bialgebra inner product. $\kBKM(\Delta_5) = c_1(0)/2 = 10/2 = 5$
is the Borcherds weight, which matches the $K3 \times E$ value from the
cache $\{2,3,5,24\}$ at the $\kBKM$-slot.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Dimensional audit]
\label{wn:rmk:dim-audit}
After specialisation, the output is $E_1$ on $E$ (not $E_3$ on
$K3 \times E$): the K3 cycle has been integrated out. The $E_1$-chiral
bialgebra $\bH_{\Delta_5}$ has central charge $c = 24$ (Mukai rank on
the full $\Lambda^{4,20}$ with grading shift) and anomaly
$\kch = 0$ (inherited from $K3 \times E$).
\end{remark}

\subsection{What this fixes in the $\{2,3,5,24\}$ spectrum}
\label{wn:subsec:sp-spectrum}

The specialisation assigns each of the four cache values to its
mathematical source: $\kcat(K3\times E) = 0$ is K\"unneth on the total
space (cache Key Fact); $\kch(K3\times E) = 0$ is the Hodge supertrace
surviving the $E_3$-hFA; $\kBKM(\Delta_5) = 5$ is the specialisation at
$(K3, E)$; $\kfib = 24$ is the rank of the Mukai lattice $\Lambda_{\Muk}$
generating the abelian sector. Four distinct constructions, four
invariants, one $E_3$-hFA.

\subsection{Open frontier}
\label{wn:subsec:sp-frontier}

The sibling-specialisation conjecture (two-stage factorisation
Rectification~C) remains: for $N \in \{2,3,4,6\}$, does the analogous
specialisation over the CHL quotient $X_N = (K3 \times E)/(\ZZ/N\ZZ)$
produce the $N$-th Gritsenko--Cl\'ery paramodular form $\Delta_{k_N}$
with $\kBKM = c_N(0)/2$? Numerical targets: $c_2(0) = 8$, $c_3(0) = 6$,
$c_4(0) = 4$, $c_6(0) = 2$. Lorgat 2020 Conjecture~1 predicts the
affirmative; the two-stage factorisation makes the statement operadic.
