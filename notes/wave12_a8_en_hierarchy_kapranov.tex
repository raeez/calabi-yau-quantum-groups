\begin{abstract}
The dimensional stratification of the CY-to-chiral correspondence rests on three load-bearing assertions: (i) the Dunn--Lurie additivity $\Etwo \simeq \Eone \otimes_{\Ezero} \Eone$ explains the jump $d=2 \to d=3$; (ii) the $\Omega$-background freezes one $\Eone$-factor; (iii) at $d \geq 3$ the Drinfeld centre restores an $\Etwo$-braiding on $\Rep(A)$ rather than on $A$. Each admits a natural wrong reading. Five attack-heal passes extract the precise content: Dunn additivity is an equivalence of $\infty$-operads, not of algebras; the $\Omega$-background is a toric-specific geometric input, not a universal prescription; the five routes for $\C^3$ and for $K3 \times E$ are five distinct constructions, not five applications of a single functor; the $\Eone \to \Etwo$ bifurcation at $d \geq 3$ lives on $\cZ(\Rep(A))$, NOT on $A$; the $\pi_d(B\mathrm{Sp})$ framing obstruction is an $\Eone$-grading datum, not a refinement of $\Eone$ to $\En$. Healed picture: $\Phi_d$ factors through factorization homology, $\int_{S^1}$ produces Hochschild data hence $\Eone$-chiral on curves, $\int_{S^2}$ produces genuine $\Etwo$ data only when the input category carries $\Etwo$-structure, and the CY dimension $d$ enters through the dimension of the framed sphere on which the derived trace is taken.
\end{abstract}

\section{Standing conventions}

Throughout, $\En$ denotes the little $n$-disks operad in spectra or in chain complexes over $\C$. An $\En$-algebra in $\cC$ is an algebra for $\En \otimes \cC$ when $\cC$ is a symmetric-monoidal $(\infty,1)$-category. Dunn--Lurie additivity (Lurie HA \S5.1.2):
\begin{equation}
 \Etwo \simeq \Eone \otimes_{\Ezero} \Eone
 \quad \text{as $\infty$-operads.}
 \label{eq:dunn}
\end{equation}
More generally $\En \simeq \Eone^{\otimes n}$. An $\Etwo$-algebra is equivalently an $\Eone$-algebra in $\Eone$-algebras; equivalently, an algebra with two compatible (braided) multiplications.

The CY-to-chiral assignment $\Phi_d$ attaches to a CY $d$-category $\cC$ an $\En$-algebra at $n$ determined by $d$:
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}
\begin{tabular}{c|c|l}
$d$ & $\Phi_d(\cC)$ target & primitive example \\
\hline
$1$ & $\Einf$-chiral & elliptic curve, CFT \\
$2$ & $\Etwo$-chiral & K3, Yangian MO \\
$\geq 3$ & $\Eone$-chiral & $\C^3$, $K3\times E$, quintic \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Label discipline (CLAUDE.md KEY FACT, top-15 item 3): \emph{at $d \geq 3$ the algebra $A = \Phi_d(\cC)$ is $\Eone$; any $\Etwo$-braiding lives on $\cZ(\Rep(A))$, the Drinfeld centre of its representation category, not on $A$ itself.}

\section{Attack-heal 1. Dunn additivity as an identity vs as an equivalence of $\infty$-operads}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\begin{attack}
Working-notes line 1691 writes
\[
 \Etwo \;\simeq\; \Eone \otimes_{\Ezero} \Eone
\]
as if asserting a single identity between two operads at the set-theoretic level, and then applies it (lines 1692--1694) to conclude that ``the $\Omega$-background freezes one of the two $\Eone$-factors''. The adversarial reading: the symbol $\otimes_{\Ezero}$ is not ordinary tensor of chain complexes, it is the Boardman--Vogt tensor product of $\infty$-operads (Lurie HA Def 2.2.5.3). One cannot ``freeze'' a factor of a Boardman--Vogt tensor the way one zeros out a coordinate in a product of vector spaces: the tensor does not come with a projection $\Eone \otimes \Eone \to \Eone$ onto either factor.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
Dunn--Lurie additivity $\En \simeq \Eone^{\otimes n}$ is an equivalence of $\infty$-operads (Dunn 1988, Lurie HA Thm 5.1.2.2). An $\En$-algebra in $\cC$ is equivalently an $\Eone$-algebra in the $(\infty,1)$-category of $\mathsf{E}_{n-1}$-algebras. There is a tautological forgetful functor $\mathrm{forget}\colon \mathsf{Alg}_{\En}(\cC) \to \mathsf{Alg}_{\Eone}(\cC)$ in \emph{each} of the $n$ directions.
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
The precise content of Dunn--Lurie is iterated-algebra:
\begin{equation}
 \mathsf{Alg}_{\Etwo}(\cC) \;\simeq\; \mathsf{Alg}_{\Eone}\!\big(\mathsf{Alg}_{\Eone}(\cC)\big).
 \label{eq:dunn-iterated}
\end{equation}
This is what ``freezing one factor'' can mean, and the meaning is precise: given a map of $\infty$-operads $\Eone \to \Etwo$ arising from one of the two inclusions of the little-$1$-disk into the little-$2$-disk (``stacking'' direction, or ``juxtaposition'' direction), pullback along this map sends an $\Etwo$-algebra to an $\Eone$-algebra. This is forgetful, not ``freezing''.

The $\Omega$-background does \emph{not} freeze a factor of $\Eone \otimes_{\Ezero} \Eone$ by introducing a preferred direction; rather, the $\Omega$-background \emph{specifies which of the two $\Eone$-directions survives} after equivariant reduction. Concretely, on the transverse $\C^2 \subset \C^3$ with $\Omega$-weights $\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2$ subject to the Calabi--Yau constraint $\epsilon_3 = -\epsilon_1 - \epsilon_2$, the $\mathbb T^2 = U(1)^2$-equivariant localisation collapses the $\Etwo$ factorisation structure on $\PV^\ast(\C^3)$ to the $\Eone$ factorisation structure on its $\mathbb T^2$-fixed locus, which is $0$-dimensional in the transverse directions. The surviving $\Eone$ is the remaining direction along $\C \subset \C^3$. Costello--Li 2016 arXiv:1605.01473 \S5 exhibits the explicit localisation.

\textbf{Precise statement.} Dunn--Lurie \eqref{eq:dunn-iterated} gives a pair of forgetful functors $\mathsf{Alg}_{\Etwo} \rightrightarrows \mathsf{Alg}_{\Eone}$, and the $\Omega$-background selects one of them via $\mathbb T^2$-localisation on toric $\C^3$. On a non-toric CY3 (e.g.\ the quintic) there is no $\mathbb T^2$-action, hence no $\Omega$-background, hence no ``freezing'' --- but the algebra $A = \Phi_3(\cC)$ is still $\Eone$, for the independent reason stated in \S\ref{sec:ah5} below: $\pi_3(B\mathrm{Sp}) = 0$, so the framing obstruction is trivial and no $\Etwo$-refinement is available.
\end{heal}

\section{Attack-heal 2. $\Omega$-background as universal prescription vs toric-specific geometric input}
\label{sec:ah2}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\begin{attack}
Working-notes \S\ref{subsec:e1-mechanism} treats the $\Omega$-background as the universal mechanism for the $\Etwo \to \Eone$ reduction at $d=3$. The implicit assertion: every CY3 chiral algebra arises from an $\Omega$-deformed $\Etwo$ parent by freezing one factor. But the $\Omega$-background is the Nekrasov equivariant regulator, defined only when $X$ carries a $\mathbb T$-action. Global compact CY3 (the quintic, $K3 \times E$) carry no such action --- applying the $\Omega$-background slogan to all CY3 is a category mistake of the kind cache entry \#1 flags (symbol overload with a scope hypothesis dropped).
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
The $\Omega$-background specialisation does give a genuine $\Etwo \to \Eone$ mechanism on toric CY3. The transverse $\mathbb T^2$-action with $\Omega$-weights reduces the factorisation envelope of $\PV^\ast(\C^3)$ from $\Etwo$-chiral on $\C^2$ to $\Eone$-chiral on the fixed locus. Costello--Li 2016 \S5 exhibits this.
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
State the $\Etwo \to \Eone$ mechanism at $d=3$ in two distinct scopes, never collapsing them.

\medskip

\textbf{Toric scope} ($\C^3$, local $\mathbb P^2$, resolved conifold): $X$ carries a $\mathbb T^d$-action with $d \geq 2$. Choose $\mathbb T^2 \subset \mathbb T^d$ with Calabi--Yau condition $\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2 + \epsilon_3 = 0$. The $\mathbb T^2$-equivariant factorisation envelope of polyvector fields is $\Etwo$-chiral; $\mathbb T^2$-localisation produces an $\Eone$-chiral algebra on the remaining direction. The Costello--Li chain
\[
 \text{5d NCCS on } \C^3 \;\to\; Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1) \;\simeq\; \cW_{1+\infty}
\]
is an instance.

\medskip

\textbf{Intrinsic scope} (compact CY3 $X$, e.g.\ quintic, $K3 \times E$): no $\mathbb T$-action. The algebra $A_X = \Phi_3(D^b\mathrm{Coh}(X))$ is $\Eone$ because the framing datum needed to lift $\Eone$ to $\Etwo$ is a class in $\pi_3(B\mathrm{Sp}) = 0$, which vanishes. See \S\ref{sec:ah5} below.

\medskip

The unified content: \emph{at $d=3$ the target of $\Phi_d$ is $\Eone$; the route to $\Eone$ is toric-equivariant ($\Omega$-background, when available) or intrinsic ($\pi_3(B\mathrm{Sp}) = 0$, always available).} The $\Omega$-background is not a mechanism in the non-toric scope; it is a witness of the mechanism in the toric scope.
\end{heal}

\begin{remark}[Relation to cache entry \#1]
Applying ``$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}} = \chi_{\mathrm{top}}/24$'' to local toric CY3 is a scope violation (cache \#1): the /24 reading is BCOV/compact. Applying ``$\Omega$-background'' to compact CY3 is the symmetric scope violation: $\Omega$ is toric/non-compact. Both come from eliding the hypothesis under which a formula is defined. Discipline: every reference to the $\Omega$-background must either (i) be accompanied by a toric hypothesis on $X$, or (ii) be replaced by the intrinsic $\pi_d(B\mathrm{Sp})$ argument.
\end{remark}

\section{Attack-heal 3. Five routes to $\cW_{1+\infty}$ as five constructions vs five applications of a single functor}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\begin{attack}
Working-notes \S\ref{subsec:costello-li-c3} enumerates five routes to $\cW_{1+\infty}$ and five routes to the chiral algebra of $K3 \times E$. A reader primed on the symmetric-monoidal voice of Vol III would mistake these as five applications of a single functor $\Phi$ to five presentations of one input category. Attack: this is precisely the confusion flagged by the CLAUDE.md KEY FACT ``six routes to $G(K3\times E)$ are six DIFFERENT constructions, NOT six $\Phi$ applications''. It recurs here in fivefold form, and must be attacked before it calcifies.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
There is a genuine convergence phenomenon: five independent categorical constructions, starting from different input data (critical CoHA, crystal melting, shuffle algebra, factorization envelope, $5d$ Chern--Simons), produce equivalent output algebras. The equivalences are theorems (SV 2013, Nekrasov--Okounkov 2014, Costello--Li 2016, Feigin--Tsymbaliuk 2011, Gaiotto--Rapcak 2019), each requiring its own proof.
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
Five routes to $\cW_{1+\infty}$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(R\arabic*)}]
\item \emph{Critical CoHA.} Input: the $3$-Calabi--Yau category $\mathrm{MF}(\C^3, W)$ for $W = xyz$ (or the acyclic quiver with potential). Output: the Kontsevich--Soibelman CoHA $\cH(\C^3) = Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$ via Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013. Structure: associative $\Eone$-algebra.
\item \emph{Crystal melting / MacMahon.} Input: the Fock space $\C[P^{(1)}]$ of $3d$ plane partitions with MacMahon measure. Output: the Feigin--Tsymbaliuk vertex operator algebra $\cW_{1+\infty}$ via level-one MacMahon module identification.
\item \emph{Shuffle algebra.} Input: the double shuffle ring on $\C[u^{\pm 1}]$ with structure function $\zeta(u)$. Output: $Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$ via Negut shuffle presentation.
\item \emph{Factorization envelope.} Input: the dg Lie algebra $\PV^\ast(\C^3)[2]$ with Schouten bracket. Output: the chiral envelope $U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\PV^\ast(\C^3)[2])$ via the Costello--Gwilliam factorisation envelope construction (BV II \S5).
\item \emph{$5d$ Chern--Simons.} Input: the perturbative expansion of the holomorphic-topological Chern--Simons action on $\C^3$ with gauge group $U(1)$. Output: the boundary chiral algebra, matching $\cW_{1+\infty}$ via Costello--Li 2016 \S4 Feynman-diagram expansion.
\end{enumerate}

Five \emph{distinct inputs}. Five \emph{distinct output procedures}. A posteriori theorem: all five produce equivalent algebras at $N=1$. The equivalences are:
\begin{itemize}
\item (R1) $\simeq$ (R3) via Schiffmann--Vasserot 2018 arXiv:1810.12160 (CoHA $\simeq$ shuffle).
\item (R1) $\simeq$ (R2) via Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013 Compositio 149 (CoHA $\simeq$ affine Yangian on Fock).
\item (R2) $\simeq$ (R4) via Gaiotto--Rapcak 2019 arXiv:1703.00982 (crystal $\simeq$ envelope).
\item (R4) $\simeq$ (R5) via Costello--Li 2016 \S6 (envelope $\simeq$ $5d$ CS boundary).
\end{itemize}

This is \textbf{not} ``one $\Phi$ computed five ways''. The functor $\Phi$ in Vol III is anchored on categorical input $D^b\mathrm{Coh}(X)$; routes (R1), (R2), (R3) take non-categorical input (a quiver with potential, a Fock space, a shuffle ring); routes (R4), (R5) take $L_\infty$-algebraic and perturbative-field-theoretic input. Each route is its own functor or construction; the equivalences between them are load-bearing theorems, not tautologies.

The same taxonomy applies to the five routes for $K3 \times E$ in working-notes \S\ref{subsec:five-routes-k3e}: (a) relative DT, (b) Borcherds BKM, (c) Maulik--Okounkov, (d) Vafa--Witten, (e) relative chiral algebra --- five distinct constructions, each with its own input datum, converging on the same output. The ``six routes to $G(K3\times E)$'' KEY FACT in CLAUDE.md enforces the same discipline.
\end{heal}

\begin{remark}[Against AP-CY57: construction vs narration]
A vice of exposition is to write ``we construct the chiral algebra in five ways and then observe that they agree''. This is narration. The healed voice writes ``five distinct functors $F_i$ from distinct input data $I_i$ produce five objects $A_i$; theorems $T_{ij}$ establish $A_i \simeq A_j$''. The $T_{ij}$ are the mathematics; the fivefoldness is a consequence of the state of the art in constructing chiral algebras, not of the structure of $\Phi$.
\end{remark}

\section{Attack-heal 4. The Drinfeld centre as ``averaging'' vs as right adjoint}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\begin{attack}
Working-notes line 1694 writes ``The Drinfeld center passage (Theorem~\ref{wn:thm:c3-drinfeld-center}) restores the $\Etwo$ structure by recovering the frozen factor.'' The word ``recovering'' suggests an averaging procedure: take the $\Eone$-algebra $A$, average over the lost direction, obtain an $\Etwo$-algebra. Attack: this is precisely the confusion flagged in the CLAUDE.md KEY FACT top-15 item 7 (AP-CY54 analogue): \emph{the Drinfeld centre is not averaging}. Averaging over a direction is a LEFT adjoint (induction, tensor-up); the Drinfeld centre is a RIGHT adjoint (Hochschild cochains, $\mathrm{End}$ of the forgetful functor).
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
There is a genuine passage $\Eone \leadsto \Etwo$ at $d=3$: given an $\Eone$-algebra $A$ in a symmetric-monoidal category $\cC$, the \emph{derived centre} $Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\Eone}(A) \simeq \mathrm{End}^\bullet_{A \otimes A^{\mathrm{op}}}(A)$ is an $\Etwo$-algebra. At the categorical level, this is $\cZ(\Rep(A))$, the Drinfeld centre of the representation category.
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
The Drinfeld centre is the universal $\Etwo$-enhancement of an $\Eone$-input:
\begin{equation}
 Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\Eone}(A) \;=\; \mathrm{End}^\bullet_{A\text{-bimod}}(A) \;\simeq\; \HH^\bullet(A, A).
 \label{eq:drinfeld-centre}
\end{equation}
It is a RIGHT adjoint, not left. It is the endomorphism object of the identity $\Eone$-bimodule, which inherits $\Etwo$-structure by the Deligne conjecture (proved by McClure--Smith, Kontsevich--Soibelman, Tamarkin, Hu--Kriz--Voronov, Lurie).

Characteristic diagnostic: \emph{the Drinfeld centre commutes with limits, not colimits.} $Z^{\mathrm{der}}(\lim_i A_i) \simeq \lim_i Z^{\mathrm{der}}(A_i)$ under mild hypotheses; the analogous statement for colimits fails. Averaging is a colimit, so it does not agree with the centre.

For the CoHA of $\C^3$:
\begin{equation}
 \cZ\!\big(\Rep^{\Eone}(Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1))\big) \;\simeq\; \Rep^{\Etwo}(Y(\widehat{\fgl}_1)).
 \label{eq:coha-centre}
\end{equation}
The right-hand side carries genuine $\Etwo$-braiding: the $R$-matrix of the full affine Yangian (not just its positive half) witnesses the braiding. Character: $M(q)^2 P(q) = \prod_n (1-q^n)^{-(2n+1)}$ (working-notes Thm~\ref{wn:thm:c3-drinfeld-center}).

\textbf{The braiding is not lost at chain level.} The $R$-matrix $R(u) \in \mathrm{End}(V(u) \otimes V(v))[[u^{-1}]]$ is a chain-level object: a formal Laurent series in the spectral parameter with values in $\mathrm{End}$ of a tensor product of evaluation modules. The spectral parameter comes from the loop-algebra structure on $Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$, not from a categorical centre axiom; the centre axiom supplies the \emph{hexagon equations} satisfied by $R(u)$, which are the $\Etwo$-structure witnesses. This is the Two-Derived-Centres cache entry 46--50 point: spectral parameter = evaluation modules, not centre.

\textbf{Averaging trap.} Suppose one tried to ``recover'' the second $\Eone$-factor by averaging $A$ over a hypothetical circle action $U(1) \to \mathrm{Aut}(A)$. This would produce $A^{U(1)} \simeq A \otimes_{\Einf} \C[u]$ (polynomial ring in the formal variable $u$), which is $\Eone$, not $\Etwo$. Averaging does NOT add a second multiplication; it symmetrises the first one. The centre construction adds a second multiplication by taking ENDOMORPHISMS.

See also Vol I cache AP186 / Vol III cache AP-CY54 / Vol III KEY FACT top-15 item 7 for the ``centre vs averaging'' discipline.
\end{heal}

\section{Attack-heal 5. The $\En$ stabilisation tower at $d \geq 4$: framing obstructions are $\Eone$-gradings, not $\En$-refinements}
\label{sec:ah5}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\begin{attack}
Working-notes \S\ref{subsec:en-stabilization} tabulates a Bott-periodic framing obstruction $\pi_d(B\mathrm{Sp})$ and writes the structure column as $\Eone$, $\Eone + \Z$-shifted, $\Eone + \Z_2$-graded, etc. A fast reader might infer that the $\pi_d(B\mathrm{Sp})$ class refines the $\Eone$-structure to some $\En$-structure with $n > 1$ by a homotopy-Bott argument. Attack: that inference is wrong. $\pi_d(B\mathrm{Sp})$ measures the obstruction to trivialising the normal bundle to the $d$-framed disk; it does not inject into the operadic hierarchy $\Eone \subset \Etwo \subset \cdots$.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
The framing obstruction is real. For an $\En$-factorisation algebra on a $d$-manifold $M$, the integrand datum transforms under $\mathrm{Emb}(\R^d, M)$, whose homotopy type is $\mathrm{Map}(M, B\mathrm{Diff}_c(\R^d)) \simeq \mathrm{Map}(M, B\mathrm{O}(d))$ in the topological case, $\mathrm{Map}(M, B\mathrm{Sp}(d))$ in the Calabi--Yau case. The classes $\pi_d(B\mathrm{Sp})$ enter as genuine obstructions to defining $\int_M$ of an $\En$-algebra.
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
The precise hierarchy splits into two orthogonal directions.

\medskip

\textbf{Operadic direction.} $\Eone \subset \Etwo \subset \Ethree \subset \cdots \subset \Einf$. A choice of $\En$-structure for $n > 1$ on $A$ is an ENRICHMENT of the $\Eone$-structure (iterated-algebra Dunn--Lurie \eqref{eq:dunn-iterated}); it is \emph{additional} structure on $A$.

\medskip

\textbf{Integration direction.} For a fixed $\En$-algebra $A$ and a $d$-manifold $M$ with $n$-framing (reduction of the structure group of $TM \oplus \R^{n-d}$ to $\mathrm{O}(n)$ via $M \to B\mathrm{O}(n)$), the factorization homology $\int_M A$ is defined. Obstructions to $n$-framing live in $\pi_\ast(B\mathrm{O}(n)/B\mathrm{Sp}(d))$ and friends.

\medskip

The Vol III table in working-notes \S\ref{subsec:en-stabilization} conflates these two directions. Correct reading: \emph{the output of $\Phi_d$ is always $\Eone$ at $d \geq 3$; the $\pi_d(B\mathrm{Sp})$ class is additional data carried by the CY category, which shifts / grades / twists the $\Eone$-structure via a grading, not an operadic refinement.}

Concretely:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item $d=3$: $\pi_3(B\mathrm{Sp}) = 0$. No shift. $\Phi_3(\cC) = A_X$ is $\Eone$, ungraded.
\item $d=4$: $\pi_4(B\mathrm{Sp}) = \Z$. The class pairs with an integer, producing a $\Z$-grading on $A_X$. $\Phi_4(\cC) = A_X$ is $\Eone$ with additional $\Z$-grading. It is NOT $\Etwo$.
\item $d=5$: $\pi_5(B\mathrm{Sp}) = \Z_2$. Torsion, producing a $\Z_2$-grading ($\Z_2$-super). $\Phi_5(\cC) = A_X$ is $\Z_2$-graded $\Eone$.
\item $d=6$: $\pi_6(B\mathrm{Sp}) = \Z_2$. Second $\Z_2$-grading, potentially interacting with $d=5$ via Bott.
\item $d=7$: $\pi_7(B\mathrm{Sp}) = 0$. No shift. $\Eone$, ungraded.
\item $d=8$: $\pi_8(B\mathrm{Sp}) = \Z$. Eight-fold Bott closure.
\end{enumerate}

The $8$-fold periodicity is Bott periodicity for $B\mathrm{Sp}$, NOT a recurrence to $\Etwo$ structure. At every $d \geq 3$, $\Phi_d(\cC)$ is $\Eone$ in its algebra category; the Bott data is carried by a grading/shift functor $\mathrm{gr}_d$ acting on the category of $\Eone$-algebras.

\textbf{Why $\Phi_d$ fails to refine past $\Eone$ at $d \geq 3$.} An $\Eone$-to-$\Etwo$ refinement of $\Phi_d(\cC)$ would require a coherent second multiplication arising from the CY structure. The naive candidate is ``Künneth along the CY direction'', but this lands in a symmetric-monoidal structure ($\Einf$), not in $\Etwo$. A genuine $\Etwo$-refinement requires a braiding witnessed by an $R$-matrix, and the spectral parameter for $R$ comes from evaluation modules (loop structure on $\widehat{\mathfrak g}$), which exists only when $\cC$ carries $\mathfrak g$-data. At $d=2$ (K3, $\mathfrak g = \so(4,20)$ or Mukai), this data exists. At $d \geq 3$ (compact CY3), the Mukai-type loop algebra has not been constructed; this is the CY-A$_3$ programme (AP-CY6).
\end{heal}

\section{Healing reconstruction: $\Phi_d$ via factorization homology}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

Let $\cC$ be a CY $d$-category (dualisable, $d$-Calabi--Yau in the $(\infty,1)$-sense of Kontsevich--Soibelman). Kapranov-voice reconstruction of $\Phi_d$:
\begin{equation}
 \Phi_d(\cC) \;:=\; \int_{S^{d-1}_{\mathrm{fr}}} \cC,
 \label{eq:phi-factorization-homology}
\end{equation}
where the right-hand side is the framed factorization homology of $\cC$ over the $(d-1)$-sphere. The target category depends on $d$ via the sphere factorisation:
\begin{equation}
 \int_{S^k_{\mathrm{fr}}} (-) \;\colon\; \mathsf{Alg}_{\mathsf{E}_k}(\mathsf{Cat}) \;\to\; \mathsf{Cat}.
\end{equation}
\begin{itemize}
\item $d=1$: $\int_{S^0}$ is two-point factorization, producing $\mathsf{Mod}(\cC) \otimes_{\cC \otimes \cC^{\mathrm{op}}} \mathsf{Mod}(\cC) \simeq \HH_\ast(\cC) =$ Hochschild homology. This is $\Einf$-chiral (symmetric).
\item $d=2$: $\int_{S^1}$ is the loop integral, producing Hochschild homology of the $\Etwo$-structure, which is $\Etwo$-chiral (braided).
\item $d=3$: $\int_{S^2}$ is the surface integral of the $\Ethree$-structure --- but the CY3 category is only $\Eone$-monoidal in the derived categorical sense, so the output is $\Eone$.
\end{itemize}

The dimensional stratification emerges from the interaction between the input $\En$-structure on $\mathsf{Cat}$ (natively $\Etwo$ because of symmetric monoidal on symmetric monoidal --- one tensor per direction) and the surface integral sphere dimension. For $d=1, 2$, both integrals produce the expected $\Einf$ (open string at $d=1$) or $\Etwo$ (closed string at $d=2$).

At $d \geq 3$, the naive Künneth-tensor $\cC \otimes \cC$ fails to carry a second multiplication beyond the symmetric one; the output of $\int_{S^{d-1}}$ lands in $\Eone$.

\begin{theorem}[$\Eone$-output at $d \geq 3$, conjectural via CY-A$_d$]
\label{thm:e1-output-d3}
Assuming the CY-A$_d$ programme (an $\Einf$-monoidal $(\infty,1)$-categorical structure on $\cC$ with a CY $d$-trace), the factorization homology $\int_{S^{d-1}_{\mathrm{fr}}} \cC$ is an $\Eone$-algebra. The $\Etwo$-structure on $\cZ(\mathsf{Mod}(\int_{S^{d-1}} \cC))$ is automatic by Dunn--Lurie applied to the Drinfeld centre.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Francis 2013 arXiv:1211.5619 Thm 4.1: factorization homology of a framed $n$-manifold over a variety of $\En$-algebras produces an $\Eone$-algebra when the manifold has nontrivial $\pi_1$-action (loop / sphere). Specialise to $M = S^{d-1}$, $n = d - 1$: the surface has framing obstruction $\pi_{d-1}(B\mathrm{O}(n))$, and for the CY variant $\pi_{d-1}(B\mathrm{Sp}(n))$. At $d=3$: $\pi_2(B\mathrm{Sp}) = \Z$ (symplectic $c_1$-class) --- but this class is trivialised by the Calabi--Yau condition $c_1 = 0$, so the obstruction vanishes and $\int_{S^2}$ is defined. At $d \geq 4$: the Bott periodicity table in \S\ref{sec:ah5} governs.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Relation to the five-routes framework]
Theorem~\ref{thm:e1-output-d3} is one route to $\Eone$ at $d \geq 3$; it sits alongside (R1)--(R5) from \S3 as a sixth abstract route. It is particularly clean because it reads off the $\Eone$ status from the factorization-homology target rather than from a specific construction. It is also more schematic: for concrete values of $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$ one still needs one of (R1)--(R5).
\end{remark}

Five new cache entries (AP-CY-tentative, for reviewer inclusion in the Wave 12 Kapranov append):

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{AP-CY-K\arabic*.}]
\item \textbf{Dunn additivity as identity vs as $\infty$-operad equivalence.} Writing $\Etwo \simeq \Eone \otimes \Eone$ and then ``freezing'' a factor misreads the Boardman--Vogt tensor as a cartesian product. The precise content is iterated-algebra \eqref{eq:dunn-iterated}; forgetful functors exist, no projection.

\item \textbf{$\Omega$-background as universal prescription.} The $\Omega$-background applies to toric CY3 via a $\mathbb T^2$-action. Using it as the reason for $\Eone$-output on compact (non-toric) CY3 is a scope violation. The intrinsic reason is $\pi_3(B\mathrm{Sp}) = 0$.

\item \textbf{Five routes as five $\Phi$-applications.} The ``five routes to $\cW_{1+\infty}$'' (resp.\ to $A_{K3\times E}$) are five distinct functors from distinct inputs; the equivalences between their outputs are theorems, not tautologies. Reading them as five applications of one $\Phi$ to one input is the five-fold version of the six-route CLAUDE.md KEY FACT violation for $K3\times E$.

\item \textbf{Drinfeld centre as averaging.} The centre $Z^{\mathrm{der}}(A) \simeq \HH^\bullet(A,A)$ is a right adjoint (endomorphism object), not a left adjoint (averaging / symmetrising). The $\Etwo$-structure on the centre is via the Deligne conjecture, not via averaging over a hypothetical group action. See cache \#7 (Vol III top-15) and AP-CY54 cross-programme.

\item \textbf{$\pi_d(B\mathrm{Sp})$ as $\En$-refinement.} The Bott-periodic framing obstruction at $d \geq 4$ grades / shifts the $\Eone$-structure via a $\Z$- or $\Z_2$-grading functor, not by refining $\Eone$ to $\En$. At every $d \geq 3$, $\Phi_d(\cC)$ is $\Eone$ in its algebra category.
\end{enumerate}
