\section*{Chiral Koszul Duality and Six-Dimensional hCS}

\paragraph{Local hCS complex.}
Let \(U\subset\C^3\) be a Stein polydisc, or let \(U=\C^3\) with
polynomial or compact-support boundary conditions. Let
\((\g,\langle-,-\rangle)\) be a finite-dimensional quadratic complex dg
Lie algebra. The six-real-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons local
field complex on a relatively compact open \(V\subset U\) is
\[
  L_{\hCS}(V)=\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(V,\g)[1],
  \qquad
  \ell_1=\dbar,
  \qquad
  \ell_2(\alpha,\beta)=[\alpha\wedge\beta]_\g .
\]
The shift is the BV shift. The \((0,1)\)-component is the physical gauge
field; the \((0,0)\), \((0,2)\), and \((0,3)\)-components are the ghost
and antifields. A holomorphic volume form \(dz_1dz_2dz_3\) and the
quadratic pairing give
\[
  \omega_{\BV}(\alpha,\beta)
  =
  \int_U dz_1dz_2dz_3\wedge\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle .
\]
The classical action
\[
  S_{\mathrm{cl}}(a)
  =
  \int_U dz_1dz_2dz_3\wedge
  \left\langle
    \frac12\,a\,\dbar a+\frac16\,a[a,a],
    1
  \right\rangle
\]
satisfies the classical master equation by \(\dbar^2=0\), Jacobi for
\([-,-]_\g\), and invariance of \(\langle-,-\rangle\).

\paragraph{Classical observables.}
The completed classical hCS observable algebra is the continuous
Chevalley--Eilenberg cochain algebra of the local dg Lie algebra:
\[
  \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS}(V)
  =
  C^\bullet_{\CE,\mathrm{cont}}\bigl(L_{\hCS}(V)\bigr)
  =
  \widehat{\Sym}\bigl(L_{\hCS}(V)^\vee[-1]\bigr),
\]
with differential dual to \(\dbar\) and \(\ell_2\). For pairwise
disjoint opens \(V_1,\ldots,V_m\subset V\), extension by zero gives the
factorisation product
\[
  \bigotimes_{i=1}^m \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS}(V_i)
  \longrightarrow
  \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS}(V).
\]
This is the Costello--Gwilliam classical observable factorisation
algebra for the local dg Lie algebra \(L_{\hCS}\).

\paragraph{Holomorphic \texorpdfstring{\(E_3\)}{E3} envelope.}
The factorisation operations are controlled by Dolbeault chains on
\(\Conf_m(\C^3)\) and by the boundary strata of Fulton--MacPherson
compactifications. They form an algebra over the holomorphic
little-\(3\)-disks operad in \(\Ch(\Dolb)\). An ordinary locally
constant \(\Ethree\)-algebra is obtained only after a chosen
topological transport or Dolbeault contraction. The notation
\[
  U^{\mathrm{fact}}_{\Ethree}(L_{\hCS})
\]
will mean this holomorphic factorisation envelope together with the
chosen reduction data when a locally constant \(\Ethree\)-model is used.

\paragraph{BD enveloping and Francis--Gaitsgory duality.}
Beilinson--Drinfeld define \(U^{\chir}_X(\cL)\) for a chiral Lie algebra
on a smooth curve \(X\) as the left adjoint from chiral Lie algebras to
chiral algebras, built on the Ran-space symmetric algebra and diagonal
relations. The hCS object on \(\C^3\) lives first in a higher
holomorphic factorisation category. A BD chiral enveloping algebra
appears after a curve-valued specialisation, or after the higher
factorisation object has been sent to a Ran-space model with all
specialisation data fixed.

Francis--Gaitsgory chiral Koszul duality is an
\((\infty,1)\)-categorical statement about augmented chiral algebras and
formal moduli objects under completeness and pro-nilpotence hypotheses.
It supplies the ambient Koszul-duality functor. It does not identify an
hCS BV complex with a chiral enveloping algebra until the comparison map,
augmentation, completion, and duality topology have been specified.

\paragraph{\texorpdfstring{\(E_3\)}{E3} bar construction.}
For an augmented locally constant model of
\(U^{\mathrm{fact}}_{\Ethree}(L_{\hCS})\), its Koszul-dual coalgebra is
the \(E_3\)-bar construction
\[
  \Bar_{\Ethree}
  \bigl(U^{\mathrm{fact}}_{\Ethree}(L_{\hCS})\bigr)
  \in
  \Coalg_{E_3^!}(\Ch(\Dolb)).
\]
Here \(E_3^!\) denotes the Koszul-dual cooperad. In the usual
topological formality convention this is the threefold suspension of
the \(E_3\)-cooperad, and the primitive Browder bracket has degree
\(1-3=-2\). The bar differential is the sum of the internal
Dolbeault differential and the coderivation dual to the factorisation
product and bracket.

The corresponding CE chain coalgebra is
\[
  C^{\CE}_{\bullet,\mathrm{cont}}(L_{\hCS}(V))
  =
  \widehat{\Sym}\bigl(L_{\hCS}(V)[1]\bigr)
\]
with differential induced by \(\dbar\) and \(\ell_2\). Under cofibrancy,
augmentation, conilpotence, and completion hypotheses, PBW for
\(E_3\)-enveloping algebras gives a filtered quasi-isomorphism of
coalgebras
\[
  \Psi_V:
  \Bar_{\Ethree}
  \bigl(U^{\mathrm{fact}}_{\Ethree}(L_{\hCS})\bigr)(V)
  \longrightarrow
  C^{\CE}_{\bullet,\mathrm{cont}}(L_{\hCS}(V)).
\]
On length \(n\), \(\Psi_V\) sends a bar word
\([x_1|\cdots|x_n]\) to the signed symmetric CE chain
\(x_1\cdots x_n\). The signs are the Koszul signs from moving
Dolbeault-homogeneous elements through the shifted symmetric algebra.
Stokes' formula on the configuration compactification gives
\[
  d_{\CE}\Psi_V=\Psi_V d_{\Bar}.
\]

\paragraph{Continuous dual and chiral CE cochains.}
The observable algebra is the continuous dual of the CE chain coalgebra:
\[
  \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS}(V)
  =
  C^\bullet_{\CE,\mathrm{cont}}(L_{\hCS}(V))
  =
  \Bigl(C^{\CE}_{\bullet,\mathrm{cont}}(L_{\hCS}(V))\Bigr)^\vee
\]
when the nuclear or linearly compact duality hypotheses hold. Thus
\(\Psi_V\) dualises to the chain-level comparison
\[
  \Psi_V^\vee:
  C^\bullet_{\CE,\mathrm{cont}}(L_{\hCS}(V))
  \longrightarrow
  \Bigl(
  \Bar_{\Ethree}
  \bigl(U^{\mathrm{fact}}_{\Ethree}(L_{\hCS})\bigr)(V)
  \Bigr)^\vee .
\]
After a higher-dimensional Ran or factorisation model has been fixed,
the arity \(n\) cochain piece has the schematic form
\[
  \CE^*_{\dbar,\chir}(L_{\hCS},\cO_U)(V)
  =
  \Hom_{\cD_{U^n}}
  \bigl(\Sym^n_{\chir} L_{\hCS},\Delta^{(n)}_*\cO_U\bigr)
\]
with the Dolbeault differential on both source and target. This formula
is a presentation of the chiral CE cochains inside the chosen model. The
bar coalgebra, CE chains, CE cochains, and BD chiral envelope are four
different objects connected by the displayed maps and dualities.

\paragraph{Quantum hCS package.}
A quantum hCS factorisation algebra is additional structure on the
classical object. It consists of a holomorphic volume form, an
orientation and determinant-line framing for the BV pairing, an elliptic
Costello gauge fixing \(Q^{\mathrm{GF}}\), heat kernels \(K_t\),
regularised propagators \(P_{\varepsilon,L}\), completed local
functionals, counterterms, sewing or factorisation descent, and
effective interactions \(I[L]\) satisfying the scale-\(L\) quantum
master equation
\[
  QI[L]+\frac12\{I[L],I[L]\}_L+\hb\Delta_L I[L]=0.
\]
Under this datum the renormalised observables are
\[
  \Obs^{\mathrm{q}}_{\hCS,L}(V)
  =
  \left(
    \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS}(V)[[\hb]],
    d_{\Obs}^{\mathrm{cl}}+\{I[L],-\}_L+\hb\Delta_L
  \right).
\]
The square of the displayed differential is controlled by the
obstruction classes in the completed local BV complex.

\paragraph{One-loop obstruction and Casimir counterterm.}
For a representation \(\rho:\g\to\End(W)\), set
\[
  I_4^\rho(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)
  =
  \frac1{4!}\sum_{\sigma\in S_4}
  \Tr_W\!
  \left(
    \rho(x_{\sigma(1)})\rho(x_{\sigma(2)})
    \rho(x_{\sigma(3)})\rho(x_{\sigma(4)})
  \right).
\]
In complex dimension \(3\), the one-loop gauge obstruction to the QME is
represented by
\[
  \Theta^\rho_U(\alpha_0,\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3)
  =
  \int_U
  \left[
  I_4^\rho\bigl(
    \alpha_0,\partial\alpha_1,\partial\alpha_2,\partial\alpha_3
  \bigr)
  \right]_{(3,3)}.
\]
Its class
\[
  \mathfrak a_{\hCS}(U,\g,\rho)
  =
  [\,\hb\,\Theta^\rho_U\,]
  \in
  H^1_{\mathrm{loc}}
  \bigl(L_{\hCS}(U),\cO_{\mathrm{loc}}\bigr)
\]
is the first obstruction to solving the quantum master equation. The
quadratic Casimir
\[
  C_2^\rho=\sum_a\rho(T_a)\rho(T^a)
\]
for dual bases \(\{T_a\}\), \(\{T^a\}\) belongs to two-point
field-strength renormalisation. The quartic class has arity \(4\) and
cohomological degree \(1\); the Casimir counterterm has arity \(2\) and
cohomological degree \(0\).

\paragraph{Compact global hCS.}
Let \(Y\) be a compact Calabi--Yau threefold. A global quantum hCS claim
on \(Y\) requires all of the following data: a holomorphic volume form,
orientation and determinant-line framing, a quadratic gauge dg Lie
algebra, elliptic gauge fixing, heat-kernel regulator, completed local
functional topology, counterterm scheme, factorisation or TCFT sewing
descent, a trivialisation or vanishing theorem for
\(\mathfrak a_{\hCS}(Y,\g,\rho)\), and higher-loop obstruction control.
A comparison with Hall or CY-to-chiral structures also requires a
completed comparison functor preserving augmentation, coproduct,
pairing, filtration, and specialisation data.

\paragraph{Relation to \texorpdfstring{\(\Phi_3\)}{Phi3}.}
The hCS factorisation object is a candidate model for the stage-one
object
\[
  \PhiFA_3(\mathcal C)\in E_3\text{-}\mathrm{HolFA}(X)
\]
only on a constructed comparison locus. For a specialisation datum
\((\Sigma_2,C)\), the specialised CY-to-chiral output is
\[
  A_C
  =
  \SpCh_{\Sigma_2,C}\bigl(\PhiFA_3(\mathcal C)\bigr)
  \in
  \Alg^{\chir}_{\Eone}(C).
\]
The \(d=3\) specialised output is \(\Eone\)-chiral. The \(\Etwo\) layer
is carried by centre, double, or module constructions, for example
\[
  Z\bigl(\Rep^{\Eone}(A_C)\bigr)
  \simeq
  \Rep^{\Etwo}\bigl(\Zchder(A_C)\bigr)
\]
on the constructed Drinfeld-centre locus. The \(\Ethree\)-structure
belongs to the holomorphic factorisation object before specialisation.

\paragraph{Separated invariants.}
The compact \(K3\times E\) constants belong to distinct constructions:
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \kcat(K3\times E)
    &=\chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot0=0,\\
  \kch^{\mathrm{compact}}(K3\times E)
    &=\sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(K3\times E)=1-1+1-1=0,\\
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)
    &=3,\\
  \kBKM(\Phi_N)
    &=c_N(0)/2,\\
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)
    &=10/2=5,\\
  \kfib(K3)
    &=24.
\end{aligned}
\]
The hCS CE comparison does not compute these constants by itself. It
supplies one local chain model whose global use depends on the QME,
framing, sewing, completion, and comparison hypotheses above.

\paragraph{Chain-level and \texorpdfstring{\((\infty,1)\)}{(infinity,1)} lanes.}
The chain-level lane consists of \(L_{\hCS}\), the CE complexes, the
factorisation maps, the bar map \(\Psi\), the Dolbeault signs, and the
BV obstruction classes. The \((\infty,1)\)-categorical lane consists of
Francis--Gaitsgory chiral Koszul duality, the augmented
factorisation-envelope functor, centres, module categories, and
specialisation functors. A theorem comparing the two lanes is the data
of a functorial map preserving augmentation, filtrations, completion,
and the relevant operadic structures. Both lanes are load-bearing.
