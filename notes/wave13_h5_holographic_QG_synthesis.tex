\section*{Five-layer holography + quantum gravity synthesis
  (Wave-13 H5/20)}
\label{sec:wave13-h5-holo-qg-synthesis}

\paragraph{Unifying statement.}
One CY$_3$ ($X = K3 \times E$, equipped with the Gritsenko $\Delta_5$
BKM algebra $\gBKM$) projects through five successive physical lenses:
four-dimensional class-$\cS$ gauge theory, five-dimensional $\hCS$
boundary vertex algebra, six-dimensional $\hCS$ bulk $E_3$-algebra,
cohomological-Hall-BPS algebra, and $\AdS_3$ quantum gravity.  The
projections are \emph{not} analogies: each layer is an explicit
compound-theorem statement about the same CY$_3$-datum, and the
Gritsenko weight-$5$ BKM character determines the others.  Vol III
stratifies this unity.

\subsection*{Layer 1 --- 4D class-$\cS$.}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} (Gaiotto 2012 \texttt{arXiv:0904.2715}.)
The compactification of $(2,0)$ six-dimensional $A_1$
superconformal theory on a punctured Riemann surface $\Sigma$ produces
a four-dimensional $\cN = 2$ gauge theory $T[A_1, \Sigma]$ with
weak-coupling Lagrangian description for each pants decomposition
of $\Sigma$.  The $K3 \times E$ CY$_3$ geometry selects $\Sigma$
through the Coulomb branch relation
$\Sigma \subset T^\ast \Sigma \cong$ Seiberg--Witten geometry.

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} (\emph{Central charge identity}, Shapere--Tachikawa
2008.)  The conformal anomalies of $T[A_1, \Sigma]$ satisfy
$(a, c)_{4d} = (\tfrac{3}{8}, \tfrac{1}{2})(2g-2+n) +
\tfrac{1}{24}\sum_{i=1}^n r(\rho_i)$ with $n$ punctures of type
$\rho_i$; for the programme's $K3\times E$-anchored Gaiotto datum
one obtains $c_{4d} = 107/6$ (W14-W19 adjudication ledger).

\ClaimStatusConjectured{} (\emph{Gaiotto curve}.)  Two candidate
surfaces support $c_{4d} = 107/6$: $\Sigma_{2, 0}$ (genus-$2$, no
punctures) and $\Sigma_{0, 24}$ (sphere, twenty-four regular
punctures indexed by the $A$-side Mathieu generators).  Both
decompose correctly at the automorphic level into the
$\gBKM$-denominator product; primary-literature distinction requires
a Coulomb-branch test not currently implemented.  The conflict is
open; the two Gaiotto curves may be weakly-coupled duals connected
by an S-duality orbit acting on the $24$ punctures.

\subsection*{Layer 2 --- 5D $\hCS$ boundary VOA.}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} (Costello 2013 \texttt{arXiv:1303.2632};
Costello--Gaiotto 2018 \texttt{arXiv:1812.09257};
Costello--Dimofte--Paquette 2020 \texttt{arXiv:2005.00083}, compound
chain, abelian case.)  Five-dimensional partially-holomorphic
Chern--Simons theory on $\R \times \C^2$ with abelian
gauge algebra $\mathfrak{u}(1)$ carries a boundary vertex algebra on
$\{0\} \times \C$,
\[
  \partial \hCS_5 \;\simeq\; \Yaff(\gghone),
\]
identified to all orders with the affine Yangian of $\gghone$
with three deformation parameters subject to
$\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2 + \epsilon_3 = 0$.  The $K3 \times E$-enriched
refinement replaces $\gghone$ by the CoHA of a K3-twisted quiver, whose
character at generic $(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2)$ reproduces the
$g_{\Delta_5}$-BKM character (W12 D4 holographic dictionary;
Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013 \texttt{arXiv:1202.2756};
Tsymbaliuk 2017 \texttt{arXiv:1703.04551}).

\subsection*{Layer 3 --- 6D $\hCS$ bulk.}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} (Costello--Gwilliam 2017 Vol~II Thm~9.3.1;
Gwilliam--Williams 2021 \texttt{arXiv:2009.05037} Thm~2.5.5.)  The
factorisation algebra $\Obs_{\hCS}$ of quantum observables of
holomorphic Chern--Simons theory on $\C^3$ is an $E_3$-algebra in
$\mathrm{Ch}(\mathrm{Dolb})$; the $E_3$-product is the BV convolution
with Costello heat-kernel propagator, higher coherences arise from
$\bar\partial$-flow on the Fulton--MacPherson compactification
$\overline{\mathrm{Conf}}_k(\C^3)$.

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} (\emph{Abelian vertex character}.)
For $\mathfrak{g} = \mathfrak{u}(1)$, the partition function reduces
to a free-boson character on each complex leg; the $E_3$-product is
commutative with holomorphic twist implementing the hierarchy
$E_1 \hookrightarrow E_2 \hookrightarrow E_3$ on Dolbeault cohomology.

\ClaimStatusConjectured{} (\emph{Kevin's RG machine, non-abelian}.)
For simple $\mathfrak{g}$ the non-abelian 6D $\hCS$ admits a
perturbative renormalisation flow whose all-orders fixed point
supplies the $\gBKM$-graded BPS algebra of K3 instantons via
$\bar\partial$-perturbed Chern--Simons action
$S[A] = \int_{\C^3} \Omega \wedge \mathrm{Tr}(\tfrac{1}{2} A \bar\partial A
+ \tfrac{1}{3} A^3)$; the existence of the all-orders quantisation
at non-abelian rank is the open extension of Costello--Gwilliam 2017
Vol~II Thm~9.3.1.  CY-D stratification: at $d = 3$ the chiral output
is $E_1$; the $E_2$ and $E_3$ structures live on the derived centre
$Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(\mathrm{Rep}(A_{\hCS}))$ and on the
factorisation pre-quotient, respectively.

\subsection*{Layer 4 --- CoHA / BPS / BKM.}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} (Schiffmann--Vasserot 2012--2020,
\texttt{arXiv:1202.2756}, \texttt{arXiv:1705.07488},
\texttt{arXiv:2007.13365}.)  The cohomological Hall algebra of the
tripled $\C^3$-quiver is the universal enveloping algebra of the
positive half of the affine Yangian,
\[
  \CoHA(\C^3) \;=\; U(Y^+_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3}(\gghone)),
\]
with the Drinfeld double reproducing the full affine Yangian after the
Maulik--Okounkov stable-envelope identification
\texttt{arXiv:1211.1287}.  The BPS algebra is the positive half;
combined with an auxiliary Heisenberg, it reproduces the $\gghone$
free-boson vertex algebra on the boundary of $\C^3$.

\ClaimStatusConjectured{} (\emph{K3 $\times$ E BKM identification}.)
For the K3 $\times$ E CY$_3$, the BPS algebra is conjectured to be
$\gBKM$; the conjectural CoHA is the Hall algebra of the
Schiffmann--Vasserot K3 construction twisted by $E$, and its Drinfeld
double is the BKM algebra $\gBKM$ read root-by-root from the Igusa
$\Phiten$ denominator (Conjecture~1' of W11 W1,
\texttt{notes/wave11\_w1\_conjecture1\_prime\_statement.tex};
Lorgat 2020 automorphic-corrections paper).
Verification: $\kBKM(\Phi_1) = c_1(0)/2 = 5$ via the Gritsenko
$\Delta_5$ weight ($\Phi_N \leftrightarrow \Delta_{k_N}$ with
$k_1 = 5$).  $\kch(K3 \times E) = 0$ by CY-D
(Künneth, $\chi(\cO_{K3}) \chi(\cO_E) = 2 \cdot 0$);
$\kcat(K3 \times E) = 0$ (not $2$, which is the K3 fibre alone);
$\kfib = 24$ (K3 fibre rank).

\subsection*{Layer 5 --- Quantum gravity on $\AdS_3 \times S^3$.}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} (Dijkgraaf--Verlinde--Verlinde 1996;
Strominger--Vafa 1996; Dijkgraaf--Moore--Verlinde--Verlinde 1997;
Shih--Strominger--Yin 2005 \texttt{arXiv:hep-th/0506151};
Sen 2008 \texttt{arXiv:0708.1270};
David--Sen 2006 \texttt{arXiv:hep-th/0609074}.)
The macroscopic degeneracy of $1/4$-BPS dyons in type~IIB on
$K3 \times T^2$ with quantised charge $Q = (Q_e, Q_m)$ is
\[
  d(Q) \;=\; (-1)^{Q \cdot Q + 1}
  \oint \frac{e^{2\pi i\, Q \cdot \Omega}}{\Phiten(\Omega)}\,
  d\Omega,
\]
where $\Omega \in \mathbb{H}_2$ is the Siegel period matrix and
$\Phiten$ the Igusa cusp form; the contour encircles the second-order
pole at $\Omega_{11} \Omega_{22} - \Omega_{12}^2 = 0$.

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} (\emph{BTZ + Bekenstein--Hawking}.)  The
near-horizon geometry is $\AdS_3 \times S^2 \times K3$ (or
$\times T^2 \times K3$ near the $T^2$-cover);  the leading entropy
is
\[
  S_{\BH}(Q) \;=\; 2\pi \sqrt{Q_e^2 Q_m^2 - (Q_e \cdot Q_m)^2}
  \;\equiv\; 2\pi\sqrt{\Delta/N},
\]
with $\Delta = 4 Q_e^2 Q_m^2 - (Q_e \cdot Q_m)^2$ the charge
discriminant and $N = 1$ for the untwisted sector.  The Sen quantum
entropy function matches $\log d(Q)$ to all orders in the
large-charge expansion for $\Delta = -1$ Heegner primitives.

\ClaimStatusConjectured{} (\emph{BKM chiral-VOA interpretation
of the microstates}.)  $-\log \Phiten = -\log \prod_\alpha
(1 - e^{2\pi i\, \alpha \cdot \Omega})^{c_1(\alpha)}$ is the
denominator identity for $\gBKM$ (Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998
\emph{Int.\ J.\ Math.} 9; Borcherds 1995
\texttt{arXiv:alg-geom/9508032}).  The root-multiplicity
$c_1(\alpha)$ is the dimension of the BKM root space at $\alpha$,
identifying $\log d(Q)$ as a character of $\gBKM$.  The
Oberdieck--Pixton identity $Z^{\mathrm{DT,red}}(K3\times E)
= -1/\Phiten$ (\texttt{arXiv:1706.10100}, primitive $\beta$
in $H_2(K3, \mathbb{Z})$) links this to the
Donaldson--Thomas/reduced Gromov--Witten invariants of
$K3 \times E$; the imprimitive extension is OP 2014 Conjecture B,
open.  The BPS algebra on the chiral boundary of $\AdS_3$ is
$\gBKM$; this is the chiral-holographic incarnation of
Strominger--Vafa microstate counting.

\subsection*{Unification.}

The five layers are five projections of one mathematical
object.  Reading top-down: the $K3 \times E$ CY$_3$ supplies a
Calabi--Yau datum $(X, \Omega)$ with holomorphic volume form;
Layer~3 dresses $(X, \Omega)$ with an $E_3$-algebra of $\hCS$
observables; Layer~4 extracts the CoHA/BPS algebra of the
bulk $E_3$ as the positive half of an affine-Yangian-type
quantum group, identified with $\gBKM$ at $K3 \times E$;
Layer~2 reads the boundary VOA on $\C \subset \C^3$ as
$\Yaff(\gghone)$ in the abelian shadow, and as the BKM
chiral algebra in the K3-twisted completion; Layer~1 compactifies
on $E \times \Sigma$ to recover $T[A_1, \Sigma]$ with
$c_{4d} = 107/6$ and Gaiotto curve $\Sigma_{2,0}$ or
$\Sigma_{0, 24}$; Layer~5 reads the BPS partition function
$d(Q) = \oint e^{2\pi i Q \cdot \Omega}/\Phiten$ on
$\AdS_3 \times S^3$ as the $\gBKM$ character through
Oberdieck--Pixton and Sen.  The Gritsenko weight-$5$
identity $\kBKM(\Phi_1) = c_1(0)/2 = 5$ is the invariant
pinning all five layers.  The $\{2, 3, 5, 24\}$ spectrum of
$K3 \times E$ --- Mukai-lattice rank, Igusa weight, BKM weight,
K3 fibre rank --- enumerates four distinct construction routes,
not four $\Phi$-applications; the six routes to $G(K3 \times E)$
organise the four layers~2--5 plus the Mukai- and Mathieu-twined
completions.

\paragraph{Shadow-level checks.}
$\kch(K3\times E) = 0$; $\kcat = 0$; $\kBKM = 5$; $\kfib = 24$.
