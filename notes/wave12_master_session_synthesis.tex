\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The CY-to-chiral construction factors through a canonical
$E_d^{\mathrm{hol}}$ factorisation algebra and a transverse
specialisation to an $E_1$-chiral algebra for $d \geq 3$.  The
$K3\times E$ package contains four distinct invariants
$\{0,3,5,24\}$, and the primitive CHL Borcherds constants are
\[
  c_N(0)=(10,8,6,4,2),\qquad
  \kBKM(\Phi_N)=(5,4,3,2,1),
  \qquad N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}.
\]
The Gritsenko--Clery dd-modular catalogue is a separate eight-row
family; its twice-weight tuple is $(10,4,6,2,4,1,3,2)$ and it has no
CHL-weight normalisation except at the common $\Delta_5$ row.
\end{abstract}

\section{Two-stage factorisation}

The CY-to-chiral functor decomposes as
\[
  \Phi_d
  =
  \mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ \Phifa_d .
\]
The first stage
\[
  \Phifa_d \colon \CY_d\text{-cat}\longrightarrow
  E_d\text{-}\HolFA(X)
\]
is the holomorphic factorisation algebra on the ambient $d$-fold,
constructed from Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality and
Costello--Gwilliam--Li locality.  The second stage is the
factorisation-homology specialisation along a framed transverse
$(d-1)$-cycle $\Sigma_{d-1}$ and reference curve $C$:
\[
  \mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\colon
  E_d\text{-}\HolFA(X)\longrightarrow E_1\text{-}\ChirAlg(C)
  \qquad (d\geq 3).
\]
At $d=2$ the residue is $E_2$-chiral; at $d=1$ it is
$E_\infty$ over a point.  A single CY datum therefore has one
canonical Stage-$1$ object and many specialised $E_1$-chiral shadows.

\section{Functorial Scope}

There are two separate statements about $\Phi_3$.  The object-level
chain assignment
\[
  \Phi_3^{\mathrm{obj}}\colon X\longmapsto A_X
\]
is proved through Costello--Li locality on the factorisation-algebra
complex.  The $(\infty,1)$-functor lift on morphism spaces requires
the morphism-preservation theorem and is available only up to the
$\GRT_1$-torsor of $R$-matrix gauges at the present level of proof.
Thus the proved statement is object-level construction plus
gauge-qualified functoriality, not essential surjectivity onto all
$E_1$-chiral algebras.

\section{Shift Law}

Pantev--Toen--Vaquie--Vezzosi shifted symplectic geometry and
Dunn--Lurie additivity give the table
\[
  (d,\mathrm{shift},E_n^{\mathrm{cl}})
  \in
  \{(2,-2,\Etwo),(3,-1,\Eone),(4,0,E_0),(5,+1,E_5\text{-Poisson})\}.
\]
The $d=5$ row is the natural operadic home of the Fake Monster Lie
superalgebra on $\mathrm{II}_{25,1}$.  It is not a Stage-$2$
specialisation of $\Phi_3(K3\times E)$, since the Niemeier rank $24$
exceeds $h^{1,1}(K3)=20$.

\section{Four Invariants on \texorpdfstring{$K3\times E$}{K3xE}}

The $K3\times E$ package is
\[
  \{\kcat,\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}},\kBKM,\kfib\}(K3\times E)
  =
  \{0,3,5,24\}.
\]
The entries are produced by four different constructions:
\begin{align*}
\kcat(K3\times E)
  &= \chi(\cO_{K3\times E})
   = \chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot 0=0,\\
\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)
  &= \mathrm{rk}_{\mathrm{hyp}}(\fgDel)=3,\\
\kBKM(\Delta_5)
  &= c_1(0)/2=10/2=5,\\
\kfib(K3)
  &= \mathrm{rk}\,H^*(K3,\Z)=24.
\end{align*}
The K3-fibre Euler characteristic $\chi(\cO_{K3})=2$ is a fifth,
fibre-marked datum.  It is not the total-space value of
$\kcat(K3\times E)$.  No additive identity links
$\kBKM(\Delta_5)$ to $\kch$ or to any fibre Euler characteristic.

\section{Borcherds Weight Constants}

Let $\Phi_N$ be the primitive CHL paramodular denominator for
$N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$.  The Borcherds weight theorem gives
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_N)=\mathrm{wt}(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2.
\]
The primitive CHL constants are
\[
  (c_1(0),c_2(0),c_3(0),c_4(0),c_6(0))
    =(10,8,6,4,2),
\]
and hence
\[
  (\kBKM(\Phi_1),\kBKM(\Phi_2),\kBKM(\Phi_3),
   \kBKM(\Phi_4),\kBKM(\Phi_6))
    =(5,4,3,2,1).
\]
At $N=1$, the K3 elliptic genus is $2\phi_{0,1}^{\mathrm{EZ}}$, while
the primitive $\Delta_5$ Borcherds input uses $c_1(0)=10$:
\[
  \mathrm{wt}(\Delta_5)=c_1(0)/2=5.
\]

The Gritsenko--Clery dd-modular catalogue is an independent
triple-indexed family.  Its twice-weight tuple is
\[
  (10,4,6,2,4,1,3,2),
\]
with weights
\[
  (5,2,3,1,2,\tfrac12,\tfrac32,1).
\]
This catalogue supplies consistency evidence for automorphic
neighbouring rows.  It is not the primitive CHL row
$(10,8,6,4,2)$ and it does not define CHL weights away from the
common $\Delta_5$ entry.

\section{Routes to \texorpdfstring{$G(K3\times E)$}{G(K3xE)}}

The six constructions leading to the $K3\times E$ quantum vertex
chiral group are Schiffmann--Vasserot CoHA, Maulik--Okounkov stable
envelopes, Nakajima--Heisenberg theory, the Borcherds denominator,
the Gritsenko--Nikulin automorphic lift, and the Kuga--Satake
abelian-fibre construction.  They are distinct machines acting on a
common geometric package.  Three admit direct cycle-class indexing by
$(\Sigma_2,C)$; three consume non-cycle-class data such as a Jacobi
form, a lattice, or a six-dimensional field-theoretic model.  The
route-dependent ranks $\rho^{R_i}\in\{3,12,24\}$ record generator
presentations, not distinct values of $\kch$.

\section{Faces of \texorpdfstring{$r_{\mathrm{CY}}$}{rCY}}

The seven faces of $r_{\mathrm{CY}}$ stratify into three layers:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{Stage 1} &
  \cF_{\cC}\text{ on the CY host }X,\\
\text{specialisation datum} &
  (\Sigma_{d-1},C)\text{ with framing and transverse bordism class},\\
\text{Stage 2} &
  A_X=\mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}(\cF_{\cC})
  \text{ on }C.
\end{array}
\]
The first layer carries the universal factorisation-algebra
$R$-matrix shadow.  The second layer carries CHL, Humbert,
Kuga--Satake, and defect choices.  The third layer carries the
classical $r$-matrix, Yangian structure functions, and Borcherds
denominators.

\section{CY\texorpdfstring{$_3$}{3} Existence and Rigidity}

For CY$_3$ categories, the established theorem is the existence of
$\Phifa_3$ and the $E_1$-rigidity of Stage-$2$ specialisation along a
reference curve.  The inputs are
\[
  \HH^{-2}_{E_1}(\cC)=0,\qquad \pi_3(B\Sp(2m))=0,
\]
and contractibility of the space of $E_1$ lifts through
Costello--Li locality.  Essential surjectivity onto all
$E_1$-chiral algebras is not part of the theorem.

\section{Positive Half and Vertex Algebra}

Schiffmann--Vasserot identify
\[
  \CoHA(\C^3)=Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1),
\]
the positive half of the affine Yangian.  The full
$\mathcal W_{1+\infty}$ vertex algebra enters after Drinfeld double
and evaluation:
\[
  Y^+ \hookrightarrow Y(\widehat{\fgl}_1)
  \xrightarrow{\mathrm{ev}_\lambda}
  \mathrm{End}(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}[\lambda]).
\]
The equality is with the positive half, not with the full vertex
algebra.

\section{K3 Yangian}

The K3 Lie algebra in the Mukai pairing is abelian:
\[
  \fg_{K3}\cong \C^{24}.
\]
The Hopf algebra is not abelian.  Its spectral-shift coproduct has the
form
\[
  \Delta_z(y(w))=
  y(w)\otimes 1+1\otimes y(w-z)+\hbar\, r_{K3}(w,z),
\]
where $r_{K3}$ is the $(24,24)$ Mukai structure function realised
through Maulik--Okounkov stable envelopes on $\mathrm{Hilb}^n(K3)$.
The heterotic matter central charge $24$ and the Yangian Sugawara
central charge $16$ are different data.

\section{Shadow Tower and Class \texorpdfstring{$\mathsf M$}{M}}

The CY$_3$ chiral-Hochschild obstruction tower has connected
generating coefficient
\[
  G_5^{\mathrm{conn}}=775/5184,
\]
with
\[
  m_5=-80/9,\qquad
  m_7=-24320/81,\qquad
  m_8=33157760/19683.
\]
The powers of $3$ come from cyclic triangle symmetry on the
CY$_3$ constraint $\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+\epsilon_3=0$.

For class $\mathsf M$, the $E_3$ bar complex is chain-level infinite
and has cohomology dimension $6^g$:
\[
  (3t+3t^2)^g\big|_{t=1}=6^g.
\]
The qualifier ``at cohomology'' is part of the statement.

\section{Mock Modular K3}

At $d=2$, the K3 elliptic genus through $\Phi_2$ gives the
Eguchi--Ooguri--Tachikawa mock-modular form of weight $1/2$ carrying
$M_{24}$ representations.  Twining by $g\in M_{24}$ gives
\[
  \phi_g\in J_{0,1,\chi_g}.
\]
The centraliser-gerbe cohomology vanishes on seven admissible cells;
the $2A$ and $2B$ cells carry residual $\Z/2$ and $\Z/4$ torsion,
respectively.  This torsion dresses the multiplier and does not alter
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_2)=c_2(0)/2=4.
\]
