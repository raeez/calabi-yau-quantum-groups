\section*{Completed Koszul duality for the
$\mathrm{O}(3,3)$ BKM lattice envelope}

\paragraph{Lattice envelope.}
Let $\Lat^{3,3}=U^{\oplus 3}$ be the even unimodular lattice of
signature $(3,3)$.  Fix a primitive vector $q$ with $q^2=-2$ and let
$\sigma_q(x)=x+(x,q)q$ be the reflection in $q$.  Let
$\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}$ be a Borcherds--Kac--Moody Lie algebra equipped
with:
\[
  \gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}
  =
  \cH(\Lat^{3,3})
  \oplus
  \bigoplus_{\alpha\neq 0}\gBKM^{(\alpha)},
  \qquad
  [\gBKM^{(\alpha)},\gBKM^{(\beta)}]\subset
  \gBKM^{(\alpha+\beta)} ,
\]
a root-height filtration $F_{\leq h}$ with finite-dimensional graded
pieces, a lattice $2$-cocycle $\epsilon:\Lat^{3,3}\times\Lat^{3,3}
\to\{\pm1\}$, and a $\sigma_q$-action preserving the bilinear form,
$\epsilon$, and the root-height filtration.  Assume
\[
  \bigl(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}\bigr)^{\sigma_q}
  =
  \gBKM_{\Delta_5}
\]
as a filtered Lie algebra.  This ambient denominator algebra is
additional input.  The Pfaffian lattice and the reflection supply the
lattice and stabiliser data; the Borcherds product, the root
multiplicity function, and the restriction theorem are part of the
present hypothesis.

\paragraph{Completed chiral envelope.}
Let $U^{\chir}_{\widehat F}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})$ be the completed
chiral envelope in the Dolbeault Ran category of $\C^3$, formed as a
continuous inverse limit over root-height quotients:
\[
  U^{\chir}_{\widehat F}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})
  =
  \varprojlim_h
  U^{\chir}\!\left(F_{\leq h}\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}\right).
\]
Its associated graded is the completed chiral symmetric algebra on the
filtered Lie object:
\[
  \operatorname{gr}_F U^{\chir}_{\widehat F}
  \cong
  \widehat{\Sym}^{\bullet}_{\chir}
  \bigl(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}\bigr),
\]
with differential $\dbar+d_{\gBKM}+d_{\chir}$.  The lattice vertex
algebra $V_{\Lat^{3,3}}=M(\cH)\otimes \C_\epsilon[\Lat^{3,3}]$ is the
Heisenberg-and-lattice model for the associated local fields; it is a
completion of the root-filtered chiral envelope once the Borcherds
root completion and the cocycle convention have been fixed.
The $\Ethree$-structure below is the local holomorphic factorisation
structure on $\C^3$.  The compact CY$_3$ output of $\Phi_3$ is native
$\Eone$.

\paragraph{Theorem.}
Assume the following five conditions.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The root-height completion of
    $U^{\chir}_{\widehat F}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})$ is separated,
    complete, and conilpotent for the reduced bar filtration.
  \item A $\sigma_q$-equivariant fibrant-cofibrant replacement has been
    chosen in the Dolbeault Ran model category.
  \item The bar filtration has finite-dimensional associated graded
    pieces on every root-height quotient.
  \item The inverse system of root-height quotients is Mittag--Leffler,
    so continuous duals and completed Chevalley--Eilenberg cochains
    compute the inverse-limit object.
  \item Comparisons with compact CY$_3$ hCS data require TCFT trace,
    $S^3$-framing, BV anomaly trivialisation, gauge-fixing/RG
    compatibility, and completion transition data.
\end{enumerate}
Then there is a natural quasi-isomorphism of completed
$E_3^!$-coalgebras
\[
  \Psi_{\Lat^{3,3}}:
  \Bar_{E_3}
  \!\left(U^{\chir}_{\widehat F}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})\right)
  \xrightarrow{\ \simeq\ }
  \widehat{\CE}^{\,*}_{\dbar,\chir,\mathrm{cont}}
  \!\left(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}},\C\right).
\]
With the equivariant replacement in condition \textup{(2)}, the map is
$\sigma_q$-equivariant at chain level.  For another cofibrant model,
the completed homotopy category carries the canonical equivariant
class.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Francis--Gaitsgory identify chiral Lie algebras on the Ran space with
factorisation coalgebras through chiral Koszul duality.  Fresse's
operadic bar--cobar formalism applies to a cofibrant completed
$E_3$-algebra whose bar filtration is complete and whose associated
graded pieces are controlled.  Positselski's criterion is the
completion input: a filtered morphism between separated complete
objects is a quasi-isomorphism once its associated graded morphism is a
quasi-isomorphism.

Apply these three facts first to each finite root-height quotient
$U^{\chir}(F_{\leq h}\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})$.  The PBW filtration has
associated graded the chiral symmetric algebra on
$F_{\leq h}\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}$, so the associated graded bar complex is
the cofree Koszul coalgebra on the shifted root-graded Lie object.
Passing to the completed inverse limit gives
\[
  \operatorname{gr}_F
  \Bar_{E_3}
  \!\left(U^{\chir}_{\widehat F}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})\right)
  \cong
  \widehat{\coCE}_{E_3^!}
  \bigl(\operatorname{gr}_F\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}[1]\bigr).
\]
On primitives, $E_3$-Koszul duality gives the shifted Lie coalgebra
governed by the $E_3^!$ cooperad.  The bar differential is
\[
  d_{\Bar}=\dbar+d_{\gBKM}^{\vee}+d_{\chir}^{\vee},
\]
where $d_{\gBKM}^{\vee}$ is dual to the Borcherds bracket and
$d_{\chir}^{\vee}$ is dual to the chiral diagonal operation.  The
Chevalley--Eilenberg differential on
$\widehat{\CE}^{\,*}_{\dbar,\chir,\mathrm{cont}}
(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}},\C)$ is the same sum.  Hence the associated graded
map induced by the shuffle pairing is a quasi-isomorphism on every
finite root-height quotient.  The Mittag--Leffler condition removes
derived inverse-limit error terms, and Positselski convergence promotes
these finite quotient quasi-isomorphisms to the displayed completed
quasi-isomorphism $\Psi_{\Lat^{3,3}}$.

\paragraph{Reflection and fixed points.}
The reflection $\sigma_q$ preserves the lattice bilinear form and the
chosen cocycle $\epsilon$, so it acts on
$V_{\Lat^{3,3}}=M(\cH)\otimes\C_\epsilon[\Lat^{3,3}]$ by chiral
automorphisms.  It also preserves the root-height filtration and the
Borcherds bracket; hence it acts on
$U^{\chir}_{\widehat F}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})$ by completed
$E_3$-algebra automorphisms.  The bar functor is functorial, so
$\Bar_{E_3}(\sigma_q)$ is a completed $E_3^!$-coalgebra automorphism.
The equivariant replacement in the theorem makes the shuffle map
$\Psi_{\Lat^{3,3}}$ commute with $\sigma_q$ strictly.  A changed
replacement changes the strict square by a specified homotopy.

Because $\sigma_q$ has finite order and the ground field has
characteristic zero, invariants are exact on each finite root-height
quotient.  Add the exactness condition
\[
  \left(\varprojlim_h C_h\right)^{\sigma_q}
  \cong
  \varprojlim_h C_h^{\sigma_q}
\]
for the completed bar and CE inverse systems.  Then
\[
  \left(
    \Bar_{E_3}
    U^{\chir}_{\widehat F}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})
  \right)^{\sigma_q}
  \simeq
  \Bar_{E_3}
  U^{\chir}_{\widehat F}(\gBKM_{\Delta_5}),
\]
and the fixed-point restriction of $\Psi_{\Lat^{3,3}}$ gives
\[
  \Bar_{E_3}
  U^{\chir}_{\widehat F}(\gBKM_{\Delta_5})
  \xrightarrow{\ \simeq\ }
  \widehat{\CE}^{\,*}_{\dbar,\chir,\mathrm{cont}}
  (\gBKM_{\Delta_5},\C).
\]
Thus the fixed-point statement is the finite-quotient averaging theorem
plus commutation of averaging with the separated completion.

\paragraph{Strict and homotopy Koszul morphisms.}
The phrase ``strict Koszul morphism'' means that a chosen
$\sigma_q$-equivariant cofibrant model makes the square
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
  \Bar_{E_3}U^{\chir}_{\widehat F}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})
  & \xrightarrow{\ \Psi_{\Lat^{3,3}}\ } &
  \widehat{\CE}^{\,*}_{\dbar,\chir,\mathrm{cont}}
  (\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}},\C) \\
  \big\downarrow{\scriptstyle \Bar_{E_3}(\sigma_q)}
  && \big\downarrow{\scriptstyle \CE^*(\sigma_q)} \\
  \Bar_{E_3}U^{\chir}_{\widehat F}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})
  & \xrightarrow{\ \Psi_{\Lat^{3,3}}\ } &
  \widehat{\CE}^{\,*}_{\dbar,\chir,\mathrm{cont}}
  (\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}},\C)
\end{array}
\]
commute as a square of complexes.  Francis--Gaitsgory supply the
homotopy-invariant equivalence; Fresse and Positselski allow the strict
model after cofibrant replacement and completion.  The strict statement
is model-dependent, while the completed homotopy class is canonical.

\paragraph{Borcherds weight and chiral scalar.}
Koszul duality controls the bar/CE comparison.  The Borcherds-side
invariant is an independent denominator weight:
\[
  \kBKM(\operatorname{Borch}(F))=\frac{c_F(0)}{2}.
\]
For the Igusa form $\Delta_5$ this gives
\[
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)=\frac{c_1(0)}{2}=\frac{10}{2}=5.
\]
For the primitive CHL ladder,
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
N & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 6\\ \hline
c_N(0) & 10 & 8 & 6 & 4 & 2\\
\kBKM(\Phi_N) & 5 & 4 & 3 & 2 & 1
\end{array}
\]
and $\kBKM(\Phi_N)=\operatorname{wt}(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$.
The Gritsenko--Cl\'ery simplest-divisor catalogue is a separate
eight-row list with twice-weight tuple
\[
  (10,4,6,2,4,1,3,2).
\]
The monic Igusa denominator convention is
\[
  D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5,\qquad
  \operatorname{den}(\gBKM_{\Delta_5})=D_5(2Z).
\]

On $Y=K3\times E$ the separated invariants are
\[
  \kcat(Y)=0,\qquad
  \kch(Y)=0,\qquad
  \kchHeis(Y)=3,\qquad
  \kBKM(\gBKM_{\Delta_5})=5,\qquad
  \kfiber(Y)=24.
\]
They arise respectively from K\"unneth for $\chi(\mathcal O_Y)$, the
compact Hodge supertrace, the Heisenberg--Mukai specialisation, the
Borcherds denominator, and the Mukai-lattice rank.  At $d=3$ the
native CY-to-chiral output is an $\Eone$ algebra; an $\Etwo$ structure
lives on the appropriate centre or double after its hypotheses are
constructed.

\paragraph{Boundary of the theorem.}
The theorem asserts local completed Koszul duality for the supplied
root-filtered envelope.  Compact CY$_3$ global chain-level closure
requires the all-scale hCS BV package, TCFT trace, $S^3$-framing
comparison, anomaly cancellation, gauge-fixing homotopy, overlap RG
compatibility, relative orientation, and completion transition system.
The smooth toric statement is
\[
  \mathrm{CoHA}(\C^3)=Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1),
\]
the positive Hall half; the $\mathcal W_{1+\infty}$ comparison belongs
to the Drinfeld double or centre.  The Hall--Drinfeld/BKM branch
attached to $\Delta_5$ and the K3 self-mirror Yangian branch are
separate objects.
