\section*{Strict vs up-to-homotopy chiral Koszul duality on the
$\OO(3,3)$-envelope $\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}$ (Wave-16 U3/20)}

\paragraph{Setting.} $\Lat^{3,3}=U^{\oplus 3}$ is the unique even
unimodular lattice of signature $(3,3)$ (M3 Thm.~\ref{thm:uniqueness-33}).
A primitive timelike $q$ of square $-2$ produces
$\Lat^{3,2}=q^{\perp}\hookrightarrow\Lat^{3,3}$, and the envelope
$\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}$ carries a reflection involution $\sigma_q$ whose
fixed subalgebra is $\gBKM_{\Delta_5}$ (M3 Thm.~\ref{thm:envelope}).
Wave-14 J5 established strict (Gwilliam--Williams 2021,
Thm.~3.3.1) versus homotopy (Francis--Gaitsgory 2012, Thm.~6.2.1)
compatibility of chiral Koszul duality for the Dolbeault Lie algebroid
$\cE_{\hCS}=\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3,\mathfrak{g})$ via Fresse 2017
Thm.~12.3.A and Positselski 2011 coderived/contraderived transfer.
The question: does that compatibility extend to the larger envelope?

\paragraph{Theorem (main).} Let $V_{\Lat^{3,3}}$ be the lattice vertex
algebra attached to $\Lat^{3,3}$ (Borcherds 1986 \emph{PNAS}~83;
Frenkel--Lepowsky--Meurman 1988~\S 8). The chiral enveloping algebra
\[
U^{\chir}\bigl(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}\bigr)
\ \simeq\ V_{\Lat^{3,3}}\ *_{\cH(\Lat^{3,3})}\
\mathrm{BKM\text{-}corrections}
\]
has an $E_3$-Koszul dual coalgebra quasi-isomorphic to the chiral
Chevalley--Eilenberg cochain complex
$\CE^{*}_{\dbar,\chir}\!\bigl(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}},\mathcal{O}\bigr)$
at chain level, and this identification upgrades to an equivalence in
$\Coalg_{E_{3}^{!}}$. The reflection $\sigma_q$ is a strict Koszul
morphism, and restriction to $\gBKM_{\Delta_5}=(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})^{
\sigma_q}$ recovers the J5 duality. Consequence: strict/homotopy
compatibility descends from envelope to sub-algebra.

\paragraph{Cycle 1: lattice vertex algebra and $U^{\chir}$.}
\emph{Attack.} Borcherds 1986 constructs $V_{\Lat}$ as the chiral
algebra on a Heisenberg lattice, with $V_{\Lat}=M(\cH)\otimes\C[\Lat]$,
$M(\cH)$ the Fock module for the Heisenberg algebra $\cH(\Lat\otimes\C)$
and $\C[\Lat]$ the group algebra, glued by the cocycle $\epsilon:\Lat
\times\Lat\to\{\pm 1\}$ witnessing the bilinear form mod~$2$. The
GBKM algebra is the generalised Kac--Moody algebra on the root system
of $\Lat^{3,3}$, with denominator $\Psi_{F_{0}}$ of weight $-3$ theta
lift (M3 Construction~\ref{cons:envelope}).
\emph{Heal.} The chiral envelope $U^{\chir}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})$ is
built by BD~2004~\S 3.7.1 as the left adjoint to forgetful from chiral
algebras to chiral Lie; concretely, $V_{\Lat^{3,3}}$ is a completion
of the \emph{root-filtered} chiral enveloping algebra, where the
Heisenberg factor $M(\cH)$ gives the abelian slice and the
$\C[\Lat]$-twist encodes the BKM brackets of Borcherds 1988. On a
polydisc $D\subset\C^{3}$, using the Dolbeault chiral lift of Gwilliam--
Williams 2021~\S 3,
\[
U^{\chir}\bigl(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}\bigr)(D)
\ =\ \Sym^{\bullet}_{\chir}\!\bigl(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}|_{D}\bigr)
\Big/\Bigl\langle\mu^{\chir}_{ij}-[\cdot,\cdot]_{\gBKM}
\Bigr\rangle.
\]

\paragraph{Cycle 2: $\Bar_{E_{3}}$ on the lattice envelope.}
\emph{Attack.} The $E_3$-bar (Francis 2013 Thm.~3.4; Francis--Gaitsgory
2012 Thm.~6.2.1) produces a conilpotent $E_{3}^{!}$-coalgebra. For
the envelope,
\[
\Bar_{E_{3}}\!\bigl(U^{\chir}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})\bigr)
\ =\ \bigl(\Sym^{\bullet}_{E_{3}^{!}}\,\overline{U^{\chir}
(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})}[1],\ d_{\Bar}\bigr),
\]
with $\overline{U^{\chir}}:=\ker(U^{\chir}\to\C)$. Using Cycle~1 of
J5, $E_{3}^{!}\simeq\Lie[2]$ (Getzler--Jones 1994~\S 2; Fresse 2017
Thm.~14.1.A), so $\Bar_{E_{3}}=\Sym^{\bullet}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}[3])^{
\vee}$ with coderivation $d=\dbar+d_{\chir}^{\vee}$.
\emph{Heal.} The lattice refinement contributes a grading by
$\Lat^{3,3}\subset\HH^{1,1}$ and the bar complex decomposes as a direct
sum over root-height: $\Bar_{E_{3}}=\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Lat^{3,3}}
\Bar_{E_{3}}^{(\alpha)}$, with each summand a finite-dimensional
Dolbeault-twisted cofree coLie coalgebra. This is the coalgebraic
mirror of the root-space decomposition $\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}=\cH(\Lat^{3,3})
\oplus\bigoplus_{\alpha\neq 0}\gBKM^{(\alpha)}$.

\paragraph{Cycle 3: Fresse Thm.~12.3.A hypotheses on the envelope.}
\emph{Attack.} Fresse 2017 Thm.~12.3.A (Vol.~I, Ch.~12) states: a
chain-level quasi-isomorphism of bar-cobar pairs
$(\Bar(U), \CE^{*}(U))$ upgrades canonically to an
$\infty$-equivalence in $\Op_{E_{n}}$, provided (i) $U$ is a
cofibrant $E_{n}$-algebra, (ii) the underlying chain complex is
$\cR$-flat over the base commutative ring, (iii) bar/cobar filtrations
are complete (Positselski 2011 coderived-model axioms). For
$U^{\chir}(\cE_{\hCS})$, these held by $\dbar$-softness of
$\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^{3})$ (J5 Cycle~5).
\emph{Heal.} For $U^{\chir}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})$: (i) cofibrancy is
established by presenting $U^{\chir}$ as a BD~$\S 3.7$ free chiral
envelope followed by a Positselski coderived replacement
(2011~Thm.~6.6.1); the lattice filtration by root height gives an
admissible filtration whose associated graded is the free chiral
Sym on $\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}$, which is cofibrant. (ii) $\C$-flatness
is automatic (chain complexes over a field). (iii) the bar filtration
is complete because the $E_3$-bar increases both weight \emph{and}
chiral $\Ran$-support, and the intersection of the decreasing
filtration is zero by Ran-cosimplicial descent (Francis--Gaitsgory
2012~\S 4.2). All three hypotheses hold. Hence Fresse Thm.~12.3.A
applies, and the chain-level quasi-isomorphism
$\Psi:\Bar_{E_{3}}(U^{\chir}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}))\to\CE^{*}_{\dbar,
\chir}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}},\mathcal{O})$ (defined by the
sum-over-shuffles formula of J5 Cycle~4, with summation
restricted to each root-height summand) upgrades to an equivalence
in $\Coalg_{E_{3}^{!}}$.

\paragraph{Cycle 4: the reflection $\sigma_q$ and Koszul structure.}
\emph{Attack.} $\sigma_{q}:\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}\to\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}$
reflects the root $q$ and sends $\gBKM^{(\alpha)}\to\gBKM^{(\sigma_q
\alpha)}$. Extend to $U^{\chir}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})$ as an involution
of chiral algebras (BD 2004~\S 3.6 functoriality). The question is
whether $\sigma_q$ commutes with $\Bar_{E_{3}}$, i.e., preserves the
Koszul-duality structure.
\emph{Heal.} $\sigma_q$ is an involution of lattice vertex algebras
commuting with the Heisenberg $\cH(\Lat^{3,3})$, with
$\sigma_q^{*}\epsilon=\epsilon$ (the cocycle is $\sigma_q$-invariant
because $\sigma_q$ preserves $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ mod $2$).
Hence $\sigma_q$ acts on $V_{\Lat^{3,3}}$ by a chiral automorphism
and on $U^{\chir}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})$ by $E_3$-algebra automorphism.
By functoriality of bar (Fresse 2017~\S 6.5.2), $\Bar_{E_{3}}(\sigma_q)$
is a coalgebra automorphism of $\Bar_{E_{3}}(U^{\chir})$, strictly
at chain level. By Positselski 2011 Thm.~7.5.4 the coderived/
contraderived transfer is $\sigma_q$-equivariant (both categories are
$\Z/2$-graded via $\sigma_q$). Therefore $\sigma_q$ is a \emph{strict}
Koszul morphism: it commutes with $\Psi$ on the nose, and the induced
involution on $\CE^{*}_{\dbar,\chir}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})$ matches
$\Bar_{E_{3}}(\sigma_q)$ without higher homotopy coherence.

\paragraph{Cycle 5: sub-algebra inheritance.}
\emph{Attack.} Take $\sigma_q$-fixed points. On the Lie side,
$(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})^{\sigma_q}=\gBKM_{\Delta_{5}}$ (M3 Thm.~\ref{thm:envelope}).
On the chiral envelope, $(U^{\chir}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}))^{\sigma_q}
=U^{\chir}(\gBKM_{\Delta_{5}})$ because chiral enveloping commutes
with fixed points of reductive group actions (BD 2004~\S 3.6.2; in
finite characteristic zero the averaging functor is exact). On the
Koszul-dual side,
\[
\bigl(\Bar_{E_{3}}(U^{\chir}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}}))\bigr)^{\sigma_q}
\ \simeq\ \Bar_{E_{3}}\!\bigl(U^{\chir}(\gBKM_{\Delta_{5}})\bigr),
\]
by Cycle~4 (strict $\sigma_q$-equivariance of $\Bar_{E_{3}}$) and
exactness of $\sigma_q$-averaging.
\emph{Heal.} The Wave-14 J5 compatibility (strict GW 2021 $=$ homotopy
FG 2012 for $\gBKM_{\Delta_5}\subset\cE_{\hCS}$) follows from the
envelope statement of Cycles~1--4 by $\sigma_q$-fixed-point restriction.
Hence the envelope Koszul duality \emph{inherits} strict-vs-homotopy
compatibility from its construction, and the sub-algebra
$\gBKM_{\Delta_5}$ inherits it by fixed-point restriction. No
additional hypothesis is required at the $\Lat^{3,2}$ level beyond
what M3 already establishes via Nikulin primitive embedding.

\paragraph{Consequence for $\kBKM$ and $\kch$.}
The envelope chiral Koszul identification computes
$\kch(U^{\chir}(\gBKM_{\Lat^{3,3}})^{!})=c_{F_{0}}(0)/2$ for
$F_{0}$ the weight-$(-3)$ theta lift (Cycle~1; Borcherds weight
theorem \texttt{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal}).
Restriction under $\sigma_q$ recovers $\kBKM(\Delta_{5})=c_{\phi_{0,1}}
(0)/2=5$ (M3 (3); Lorgat 2020 Conj.~1 for $N=1$). The strict/
homotopy compatibility ensures both the chain-level Borcherds
denominator computation and the $(\infty,1)$-categorical $E_3$-Koszul
statement refer to the same object; there is no ambient mismatch.

\paragraph{Scope.} \emph{Chain-level:} Cycles~1--4 (lattice vertex
algebra, explicit $\Bar_{E_{3}}$ via root-height, Fresse hypotheses
(i)--(iii) checked directly, $\sigma_q$-strict equivariance).
\emph{$(\infty,1)$-categorical:} Francis--Gaitsgory Thm.~6.2.1 lifted
along Positselski coderived transfer; Fresse Thm.~12.3.A upgrade to
$\Coalg_{E_3^!}$ equivalence. Both lanes load-bearing.
