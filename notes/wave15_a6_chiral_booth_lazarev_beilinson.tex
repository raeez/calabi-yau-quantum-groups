\title{The chiral Booth--Lazarev equivalence: Ran-space coherence,
genus-indexed curvature, and sewing--analytic comparison}
\author{Adversarial swarm, Vol~III}
\date{2026}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Booth--Lazarev arXiv:$2304.08409$ prove that curved dg algebras and
coaugmented conilpotent curved dg coalgebras form a Quillen-equivalent
pair under bar--cobar. The equivalence is associative, single-curvature,
and field-coefficient. The chiral target is different: chiral Lie
algebras on a smooth curve $C$, in the sense of
Beilinson--Drinfeld~\emph{Chiral Algebras} Ch.~3, carry the bracket on
the Ran space $\Ran(C)$, and the curvature that controls the genus-$g$
bar complex is the $g$-indexed family
$m_0^{(g)}=\kch\cdot\omega_g$, where $\omega_g$ is the Mumford class on
$\Mbar_g$. Five attack--heal cycles isolate three structural
obstructions to lifting the associative Booth--Lazarev equivalence to
a chiral equivalence: Ran-space cofibrant-generation coherence, a
genus-tower compatibility making the curvature data into a functor on
$\Mbar_{g,n}$ rather than a single element, and the
sewing--analytic comparison $\Dco^{\mathrm{ch}}\rightleftarrows
\IndHilb$ that realises the algebraic coderived category as a
Koszul shadow of the Moriwaki analytic completion. We state the
chiral Booth--Lazarev as a conjecture with the three obstructions
made explicit at the level of model categories, and identify the
$\kch$-curving as \emph{itself} the boundary-descent datum on
$\Mbar_{g,n}$ that must enter the chiral coderived differential.
\end{abstract}

\section{Framing: what Booth--Lazarev prove, what the chiral lift
requires}
\label{sec:framing}
% ============================================================

The associative equivalence of arXiv:$2304.08409$ is a Quillen
equivalence
\begin{equation}
\Omega:\quad \dgCoAlg^{\cpt}_{\crv} \;\rightleftarrows\; \dgAlg \quad :B
\label{eq:bl-associative}
\end{equation}
between the category of coaugmented conilpotent curved dg coalgebras
over a field $k$ with a model structure whose weak equivalences are
maps inducing quasi-isomorphisms of cobar, and the category of dg
algebras with the projective model structure. Specialising to
curvature $m_0=0$ recovers the classical
Lef\`evre-Hasegawa/Positselski equivalence. The proof has four
ingredients: (i) a cofibrant-generation argument for the curved
coalgebra side whose generators are cofree/cosemifree finite-dimensional
coalgebras; (ii) a transfer of model structure along the forgetful
functor $\dgCoAlg_{\crv}\to\dgCoAlg$; (iii) an acyclicity criterion
for the unit and counit of the adjunction using filtration by
coradical degree and conilpotence; (iv) the standard verification that
Quillen adjunctions inducing derived equivalences at the level of
total derived functors are Quillen equivalences.

The chiral lift is forced by the working-notes' own structure: on a
smooth curve $C$, the bar complex of a chiral algebra $\cA$ is a
factorisation coalgebra on the Ran space $\Ran(C)$, and the curvature
at genus $g$ is the $g$-indexed pullback of the Mumford class,
$m_0^{(g)}=\kch\cdot\omega_g$, to $\Mbar_{g,n}$. The bare associative
formulation in~\eqref{eq:bl-associative} fails to record:
\begin{itemize}
\item the parameter space $\Ran(C)$ on which both the algebra and the
coalgebra live (``associative'' has no parameter);
\item the fact that the curvature is a $g$-indexed family descending
from a single global class, not a single element of
$\cA$;
\item the operadic composition that makes the genus tower into a
cyclic operad with boundary-descent compatibility governed by
$\partial_{\Mbar}$.
\end{itemize}

The five attack--heal cycles of this note are arranged so that
cycles~I--II extract the precise statements Booth--Lazarev \emph{do}
give us; cycles~III--IV diagnose the obstructions to the chiral lift;
and cycle~V proposes a model-categorical target (``chiral
Booth--Lazarev'') stated as Conjecture~\ref{conj:chiral-BL} with the
three obstructions explicit.

\section{Cycle I --- Booth--Lazarev, first principles}
\label{sec:cycle-I}
% ============================================================

\begin{attack}[What is actually proved]
The statement often cited as ``curved bar-cobar is a Quillen
equivalence'' conceals several choices. Made explicit:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item \emph{Curvature datum.} A curved dg algebra is a triple
$(A,d,m_0)$ with $d\in\mathrm{End}^1(A)$, $m_0\in A^2$, and
$d^2=[m_0,-]$, $d m_0=0$. A curved dg coalgebra is a triple
$(C,d,h_0)$ with $h_0\in(C^2)^*$ the co-curvature and
$d^2=(h_0\otimes\mathrm{id}-\mathrm{id}\otimes h_0)\circ\Delta$.
\item \emph{Coaugmentation and conilpotence.} $C$ is equipped with a
map $\eta:k\to C$ and a filtration
$F_0 C\subseteq F_1 C\subseteq\cdots$ with $F_0 C=k$ and
$\mathrm{gr}^F C$ the primitive cofree cocommutative coalgebra; the
filtration is Booth--Lazarev's conilpotence witness.
\item \emph{Model structure.} Cofibrations are admissible monomorphisms
(injections with cokernel a free module on a filtered cobasis);
weak equivalences are those $f:C\to C'$ such that $\Omega(f)$ is a
quasi-isomorphism. The transfer from dg coalgebras is the content
of Booth--Lazarev Thm~$3.14$.
\item \emph{Equivalence.} The adjunction $\Omega\dashv B$ is a Quillen
equivalence between
$\dgCoAlg^{\cpt}_{\crv}$ with this model structure and $\dgAlg$ with
the projective model structure. The proof uses a Smith recognition
argument and a key lemma that $B\circ\Omega$ is equivalent to the
identity on cofibrant objects via the bar filtration.
\end{enumerate}
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[Abstract statement in a form ready for chiralification]
Write Booth--Lazarev as a theorem about a pair of cofibrantly
generated model categories and an adjunction between them:
\begin{theorem}[Booth--Lazarev arXiv:$2304.08409$, restated]
\label{thm:bl-restated}
Let $k$ be a field of characteristic zero. There exist cofibrantly
generated model structures on $\dgAlg_k$ (``projective'') and
$\dgCoAlg^{\cpt}_{\crv,k}$ (``transferred'') with explicit
generating cofibrations and generating trivial cofibrations. The
bar-cobar adjunction
\[
\Omega:\ \dgCoAlg^{\cpt}_{\crv,k}\;\rightleftarrows\;\dgAlg_k\ :B
\]
is a Quillen equivalence whose restriction to the Koszul locus
$\{m_0=0\}$ recovers Lef\`evre-Hasegawa's associative
$A_\infty$-coalgebra Quillen equivalence.
\end{theorem}
The three ingredients --- cofibrant generation, transfer, filtration
by coradical degree --- are exactly what must be re-established on
the Ran space $\Ran(C)$ with $g$-indexed curvature. The ``abstract''
statement is tightly abstract: it presumes a single curvature $m_0\in
A^2$ and a single coradical filtration. The chiral lift is not a
repackaging.
\end{heal}

\begin{remark}[What the equivalence does not say]
The Booth--Lazarev equivalence is a statement about homotopy categories
of objects over a fixed base. It does not say anything about
\emph{families} over a parameter space, about operadic composition
with respect to a genus tower, or about a comparison with an analytic
(topological-vector-space) target. Each of these is a separate
structural input in the chiral setting.
\end{remark}

\section{Cycle II --- Chiral Lie algebras on $\Ran(C)$,
first principles}
\label{sec:cycle-II}
% ============================================================

\begin{attack}[``Chiral'' is a three-letter word hiding five structures]
The chiral-Lie setup inherits five structures, each of which is absent
from the associative setting and each of which must be tracked through
any lift of Theorem~\ref{thm:bl-restated}:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item a curve $C$ and its Ran space
$\Ran(C)=\bigsqcup_{n\geq 1}C^n/S_n$ (Beilinson--Drinfeld Ch.~3);
\item a factorisation structure on the target category, i.e.\ a
constructible sheaf of categories on $\Ran(C)$ satisfying the
factorisation isomorphism
$\cF|_{U_n\sqcup U_m}\simeq\cF|_{U_n}\boxtimes\cF|_{U_m}$ over
the open locus of distinct points;
\item a chiral operation
$\mu^{\mathrm{ch}}:j_{\*}j^{\*}\cF^{\boxtimes 2}
\to\Delta_{\*}\cF$
whose failure of symmetry at the diagonal encodes the chiral bracket
(equivalently, an $\cL_\infty$-structure in the sense of
Francis $2013$);
\item a genus filtration: for each $g\geq 0$ and each $n\geq 1$, a
bar complex $B^{(g)}_n(\cA)$ sitting on $\Mbar_{g,n}$ with
differential $d^{(g)}$ satisfying
$(d^{(g)})^2=[m_0^{(g)},-]$ where $m_0^{(g)}=\kch\cdot\omega_g$ is
the pullback of the Mumford class;
\item a sewing/gluing datum at each boundary divisor of $\Mbar_{g,n}$
compatible with the chiral operation at (iii) and the curvature at
(iv).
\end{enumerate}
Associative Booth--Lazarev handles none of (i)--(v): there is no
parameter space, no factorisation constraint, no genus index on the
curvature, and no sewing compatibility. Every one of them must be
argued separately in the chiral lift.
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[The objects Booth--Lazarev must see]
Define the chiral analogues of the two sides of
equation~\eqref{eq:bl-associative}:
\begin{definition}[Chiral curved factorisation coalgebra]
\label{def:chiral-crv-factcoalg}
A \emph{chiral curved factorisation coalgebra} on $C$ with genus-indexed
curvature $\{m_0^{(g)}\}_{g\geq 0}$ is a factorisation coalgebra $\cC$
on $\Ran(C)$ in the sense of Beilinson--Drinfeld together with:
\begin{itemize}
\item a coaugmentation $\eta:\mathbf{1}_{\Ran}\to\cC$ from the unit
factorisation coalgebra;
\item a filtration $F_\bullet\cC$ on each stratum whose graded is a
cofree conilpotent factorisation coalgebra;
\item for each $g\geq 0$ and each $n\geq 1$, a cocurvature
$h_0^{(g,n)}\in\Gamma(\Mbar_{g,n};(\cC^{\otimes 2}_{\Mbar_{g,n}})^*)$
compatible with the factorisation structure and with
$\partial_{\Mbar}$-descent (sewing lemma).
\end{itemize}
The category of these is denoted
$\FactCoAlg^{\cpt}_{\crv}(\Ran(C))$.
\end{definition}
\begin{definition}[Chiral dg algebra]
A \emph{chiral dg algebra} on $C$ is a factorisation algebra $\cA$ on
$\Ran(C)$ whose chiral operation
$\mu^{\mathrm{ch}}$ satisfies the $\cL_\infty$-Jacobi relations
(Francis $2013$ \emph{Compos.\ Math.}\ $149$, Theorem $3.1$). The
category is denoted $\FactLie(\Ran(C))$ when the structure is Lie,
and $\Fact(\Ran(C))$ when associative.
\end{definition}
\end{heal}

\begin{heal}[Bar and cobar on the Ran space]
The chiral bar and cobar are factorisation-homology constructions:
\begin{equation}
B(\cA) \;=\; \int_{\Ran(C)} \CE_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA[-1])
\qquad\text{(chiral Chevalley--Eilenberg)}
\label{eq:chiral-B}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\Omega(\cC) \;=\; \int_{\Ran(C)} \CE^*_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cC)
\qquad\text{(chiral cobar)}
\label{eq:chiral-Omega}
\end{equation}
where the integrand on~\eqref{eq:chiral-B} is the Chevalley--Eilenberg
chain complex of the chiral-Lie structure on $\cA[-1]$, and
on~\eqref{eq:chiral-Omega} its dual cochain complex. At genus zero on
$\Ran(\mathbb{P}^1)$ (contractible, Beilinson--Drinfeld Ch.~3), these
reduce to the Lef\`evre-Hasegawa bar and cobar. At genus $g\geq 1$,
both pick up a $g$-indexed curvature.
\end{heal}

\section{Cycle III --- Obstruction (i): Ran-space cofibrant generation}
\label{sec:cycle-III}
% ============================================================

\begin{attack}[The Smith recognition argument does not survive the
parameter space]
Booth--Lazarev Thm~$3.14$ relies on Smith's recognition theorem:
a small model category with a set of generating cofibrations, a set of
generating trivial cofibrations, and the standard small-object and
retract axioms. When the category is
$\FactCoAlg^{\cpt}_{\crv}(\Ran(C))$ rather than $\dgCoAlg^{\cpt}_{\crv}$,
three things happen:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item \emph{Smallness fails generically.} $\Ran(C)$ is not a scheme
of finite type; it is a colimit of schemes $C^n/S_n$, and factorisation
(co)algebras are typically equivalent classes under a sheaf condition
over $\Ran(C)$. A ``set'' of generating cofibrations must be
\emph{compatible with factorisation restriction}, i.e.\ under
$j^*:\FactCoAlg(\Ran(C))\to\FactCoAlg(U_n)$ for disjoint open strata,
the generating cofibration must pull back to a tensor product of
lower-$n$ generators. A naive enumeration of cofree cosemifree
coalgebras fails this compatibility.
\item \emph{Conilpotence is stratified.} The coradical filtration
must be compatible with the Ran-space stratification: on
$\Ran^{\leq n}(C)$ the filtration has depth at most $n$ (by
factorisation). Booth--Lazarev's single unstratified filtration
cannot encode this.
\item \emph{Monoidality and tensor products.} The classical
associative tensor is symmetric monoidal; the factorisation tensor
on $\Ran(C)$ is only a \emph{chiral} tensor (non-symmetric, encodes
OPE, realised as $j_{\*}j^{\*}$ across the diagonal). The
transferred model structure on $\FactCoAlg_{\crv}(\Ran(C))$ must be
compatible with the chiral tensor, a compatibility not addressed
by Booth--Lazarev.
\end{enumerate}
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[What needs to be proved: Ran-space cofibrant generation]
\begin{conjecture}[Ran-space Smith recognition]
\label{conj:ran-smith}
For each smooth projective curve $C$ of genus $g_C$, the category
$\FactCoAlg^{\cpt}_{\crv}(\Ran(C))$ admits a cofibrantly generated
model structure whose
\begin{itemize}
\item generating cofibrations $I_{\Ran}$ are compatible with the
Ran-space stratification: for each $n\geq 1$, the restriction
$j^*_n(I_{\Ran})$ to the $n$-th stratum $C^n/S_n$ is in the
convex hull (under factorisation tensor) of $\{I_m\}_{m\leq n}$
where $I_m$ is the generating set on $\Ran^{\leq m}(C)$;
\item generating trivial cofibrations $J_{\Ran}$ satisfy analogous
factorisation-compatible restrictions;
\item the model structure is transferred from the underlying
(non-factorisation) dg coalgebra model structure along the
forgetful functor $\FactCoAlg_{\crv}(\Ran(C))\to\dgCoAlg_{\crv}$.
\end{itemize}
\end{conjecture}
Conjecture~\ref{conj:ran-smith} is the Ran-space analogue of
Booth--Lazarev Thm~$3.14$. Its proof will require verifying Quillen's
path-object and cylinder-object axioms compatibly with factorisation,
plus the non-trivial check that the forgetful functor to ordinary
dg coalgebras preserves cofibrations (using that the factorisation
tensor is cofibrantly compatible with the ordinary tensor on
stalks). No proof of this appears in the literature.
\end{heal}

\begin{heal}[Why Francis 2013 is the right ambient]
Francis $2013$ \emph{Compos.\ Math.}\ $149$ proves that factorisation
homology of an $E_n$-algebra on an $n$-manifold is a well-defined
$\infty$-functor, and he constructs an $\infty$-operadic model structure
on $E_n$-algebras in a stable $\infty$-category. This is the
$(\infty,1)$-categorical shadow of what
Conjecture~\ref{conj:ran-smith} requires chain-level: a cofibrantly
generated chain-level model structure whose geometric realisation
matches Francis' $\infty$-operadic one. The two lanes are mutually
load-bearing: Francis gives the $(\infty,1)$-functor, and
Conjecture~\ref{conj:ran-smith} would give the chain-level complex
computing it.
\end{heal}

\section{Cycle IV --- Obstruction (ii): the genus-indexed curvature
tower}
\label{sec:cycle-IV}
% ============================================================

\begin{attack}[Curvature is not an element; it is a section]
The working-notes curvature datum is
\[
m_0^{(g)} \;=\; \kch \cdot \omega_g,
\]
where $\omega_g\in H^2(\Mbar_g,\mathbb{Q})$ is the $g$-th Mumford
$\lambda$-class, pulled back along the gluing/clutching
$\partial_{\Mbar_{g,n}}:\Mbar_{g-1,n+2}\sqcup\Mbar_{g_1,n_1+1}\times
\Mbar_{g_2,n_2+1}\to\Mbar_{g,n}$. The ``curvature'' is therefore:
\begin{itemize}
\item a $\mathbb{Z}$-indexed family $\{m_0^{(g)}\}_{g\geq 0}$;
\item each element is a \emph{section} of a line bundle on
$\Mbar_{g,n}$ (the Hodge bundle), not a scalar in $\cA^2$;
\item compatible with the boundary stratification of $\Mbar_{g,n}$:
under $\partial_{\Mbar}$, the curvature at $(g,n)$ pulls back to
a sum of curvatures at lower-genus strata.
\end{itemize}
Booth--Lazarev handle a \emph{single} $m_0\in\cA^2$, static, with no
variation along a parameter. The gap is not just that one must
``add a~$g$''. The structural statement is that the curvature
becomes a \emph{modular form} on $\Mbar$, and the compatibility with
$\partial_{\Mbar}$ is precisely the factorisation property that
forces the chiral Lie algebra to be consistent with sewing.
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[Genus-tower compatibility as operadic structure]
The correct formulation is that the curvature is a lift of the Mumford
class from $\Mbar$ to a cyclic-operadic structure on the chiral Lie
algebra.

\begin{definition}[Genus-tower curvature]
\label{def:genus-tower-curvature}
A \emph{genus-tower curvature} on a chiral Lie algebra $\cA$ on $C$
is a family of sections
\[
m_0^{(g,n)} \in \Gamma\bigl(\Mbar_{g,n};\; H^2(\cA)\otimes
(\lambda_g)_{\Mbar_{g,n}}\bigr)
\]
(where $\lambda_g=\det(\mathrm{Hodge\ bundle})$) such that for every
boundary clutching map
$\partial:\Mbar_{g_1,n_1+1}\times\Mbar_{g_2,n_2+1}\to\Mbar_{g,n}$
(irreducible separating) and for every irreducible
non-separating clutching
$\partial':\Mbar_{g-1,n+2}\to\Mbar_{g,n}$, the curvature restricts:
\begin{align}
\partial^*\, m_0^{(g,n)} \;&=\;
 \pi_1^*\, m_0^{(g_1,n_1+1)} + \pi_2^*\, m_0^{(g_2,n_2+1)},
\\
\partial'^*\, m_0^{(g,n)} \;&=\;
 m_0^{(g-1,n+2)}.
\end{align}
Equivalently: $m_0$ is a modular form on $\Mbar$ with the
factorisation/sewing property.
\end{definition}

For the CY case with $\kch\in\mathbb{Q}$, the base family is
$m_0^{(g,n)}=\kch\cdot\lambda_g^{1}$ (first Chern class of the Hodge
bundle), which satisfies the two boundary identities above because
$\lambda_g$ is itself a modular form with the factorisation property
(Mumford $1983$; Teixidor $1988$).
\end{heal}

\begin{heal}[What needs to be proved: genus-tower Quillen equivalence]
\begin{conjecture}[Genus-tower compatibility]
\label{conj:genus-tower}
Let $\cA$ be a chiral Lie algebra on $C$ with genus-tower curvature
$\{m_0^{(g,n)}\}$. The bar complex
\[
B^{(g,n)}(\cA) \;=\; \int_{C^n/S_n}
\CE_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA[-1])\bigg|_{\Mbar_{g,n}}
\]
lifts to a factorisation coalgebra on $\Ran(C)\times\Mbar_{g,n}$ with
cocurvature $h_0^{(g,n)}$ dual to $m_0^{(g,n)}$, and the bar-cobar
adjunction $\Omega\dashv B$ of~\eqref{eq:chiral-B}--\eqref{eq:chiral-Omega}
is a Quillen equivalence between
$\FactCoAlg^{\cpt}_{\crv}(\Ran(C)\times\Mbar)$ and
$\FactLie(\Ran(C)\times\Mbar)$, where the parameter
$\Mbar=\bigsqcup_{g,n}\Mbar_{g,n}$ carries the clutching
boundary-descent data as a cyclic-operadic structure.
\end{conjecture}
Conjecture~\ref{conj:genus-tower} is the \emph{genus-varying} upgrade
of Conjecture~\ref{conj:ran-smith}. Its statement involves a Quillen
equivalence between two model categories fibred over a base
$\Mbar$, with the fibration structure compatible with clutching.
Neither Booth--Lazarev nor any of Positselski, Lef\`evre-Hasegawa,
Francis, Beilinson--Drinfeld addresses this directly.
\end{heal}

\begin{remark}[Why this is the deeper obstruction]
Obstruction (i) (Ran-space coherence) is a \emph{fixed-$C$} obstruction:
at a single genus, one must pass from $\dgCoAlg$ to $\FactCoAlg$.
Obstruction (ii) is a \emph{varying-$C$} obstruction: the bar complex
$B^{(g,n)}(\cA)$ is not even on the same curve for different $g$, and
the clutching map changes the number of marked points. The structural
statement is that Booth--Lazarev's Quillen equivalence must be
\emph{enriched} over the cyclic operad of $\Mbar_{g,n}$'s. Obstruction
(i) asks for a single category; obstruction (ii) asks for a category
over a base.
\end{remark}

\section{Cycle V --- Obstruction (iii): sewing--analytic comparison
and the chiral Booth--Lazarev conjecture}
\label{sec:cycle-V}
% ============================================================

\begin{attack}[The algebraic coderived is not the analytic completion]
The Moriwaki $2026$ IndHilb sewing envelope $A^{\mathrm{sew}}(\cA)$ is
a \emph{topological vector space} (projective limit of Hilbert
spaces) carrying factorisation homology in an \emph{analytic} sense:
the sewing parameter $q=e^{2\pi i\tau}$ enters as an analytic
parameter on $\cM_{1,1}$, the Hilbert structure comes from the
norm-convergent sewing $Z_1\#_q Z_1$, and the resulting object is
a Hilbert-Schmidt operator on a completion of the state space.
By contrast, the coderived category $\Dco^{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA)$ is a
purely algebraic object: no metric, no analytic completion. The
working-notes C4 question is whether
\begin{equation}
\Dco^{\mathrm{ch},g}(\cA) \;\stackrel{?}{\simeq}\;
\IndHilb^g(A^{\mathrm{sew}})
\label{eq:alg-vs-analytic}
\end{equation}
but this is \emph{not} a corollary of any chiral Booth--Lazarev
statement: even if
$\FactCoAlg^{\cpt}_{\crv}\simeq\FactLie$ as model categories
chain-level, the comparison with a topological-vector-space
category on the right-hand side requires a separate analytic theorem
(completions, nuclearity, trace-class conditions).
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[Three separate statements]
Disentangle the three statements:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
\item \emph{Chiral Quillen equivalence (algebraic).} The Quillen
equivalence
$\FactCoAlg^{\cpt}_{\crv}(\Ran(C))\rightleftarrows
\FactLie(\Ran(C))$ at fixed genus $0$, assuming
Conjecture~\ref{conj:ran-smith}.
\item \emph{Genus-tower Quillen equivalence (algebraic, parameterised).}
The fibred Quillen equivalence over $\Mbar$, assuming
Conjecture~\ref{conj:genus-tower}.
\item \emph{Sewing--analytic comparison.} The comparison of the
algebraic genus-$g$ coderived category with the Moriwaki
IndHilb completion at genus $g$, a separate analytic statement
not implied by (A) or (B).
\end{enumerate}
Booth--Lazarev arXiv:$2304.08409$ is the pre-chiral shadow of (A)
with $C=\mathrm{pt}$ (no Ran space) and no genus. Passing to
(A)~(chiral, genus~$0$)~$\Rightarrow$~(B)~(chiral, genus-tower)~$\Rightarrow$~
(C)~(analytic comparison) is a tower of three non-trivial lifts.
\end{heal}

\begin{heal}[The chiral Booth--Lazarev conjecture]
Assemble the structural statement:
\begin{conjecture}[Chiral Booth--Lazarev]
\label{conj:chiral-BL}
Let $C$ be a smooth projective curve over $\mathbb{C}$. There exist
cofibrantly generated model structures on
$\FactCoAlg^{\cpt}_{\crv}(\Ran(C)\times\Mbar)$ and
$\FactLie(\Ran(C)\times\Mbar)$ with the following properties:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item The restriction to each fibre
$\Ran(C)\times\Mbar_{g,n}$ recovers a genus- and marking-fixed
cofibrantly generated model structure (Conjecture~\ref{conj:ran-smith}
at fixed $(g,n)$).
\item The clutching maps
$\partial:\Mbar_{g_1,n_1+1}\times\Mbar_{g_2,n_2+1}\to\Mbar_{g,n}$
induce compatible Quillen functors preserving cofibrations and
fibrations; the genus-tower curvature
$\{m_0^{(g,n)}\}$ of Def.~\ref{def:genus-tower-curvature} is the
incoherence witness on the coalgebra side, and its compatibility
with $\partial^*$ is equivalent to the cyclic-operadic
compatibility of the model structure.
\item The bar-cobar adjunction $\Omega\dashv B$ of
equations~\eqref{eq:chiral-B}--\eqref{eq:chiral-Omega} is a
Quillen equivalence over $\Mbar$, restricting on the Koszul locus
$\{m_0=0\}$ to the Beilinson--Drinfeld factorisation
equivalence at genus $0$.
\end{enumerate}
The IndHilb comparison~\eqref{eq:alg-vs-analytic} is a \emph{further}
statement, separate from (i)--(iii), requiring an analytic nuclearity
check on the Moriwaki completion.
\end{conjecture}

The explicit obstructions to Conjecture~\ref{conj:chiral-BL} are:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(O1)] \emph{Ran-space cofibrant generation.} A set of
generating cofibrations $I_{\Ran}$ compatible with factorisation
restriction and the chiral tensor (Cycle III).
\item[(O2)] \emph{Genus-tower boundary-descent compatibility.}
The curvature tower $\{m_0^{(g,n)}\}$ must satisfy
$\partial^* m_0^{(g,n)}=\sum m_0^{(g_i,n_i+1)}$ at every boundary
divisor of $\Mbar_{g,n}$ (Cycle IV).
\item[(O3)] \emph{Sewing--analytic comparison.} The algebraic coderived
category must agree with the Moriwaki IndHilb completion under a
nuclearity + trace-class hypothesis. This is a separate analytic
statement (Cycle V).
\end{itemize}
\end{heal}

\begin{heal}[The $\kch$-curving is the boundary-descent datum]
The structural identification: the genus-tower curvature
$m_0^{(g,n)}=\kch\cdot\lambda_g^{1}$ is \emph{itself} the
boundary-descent datum (O2). This is because the first Chern class
$\lambda_g^{1}$ of the Hodge bundle satisfies Mumford's relation
\begin{equation}
\partial^*\lambda_g^{1} \;=\;
\pi_1^*\lambda_{g_1}^{1}+\pi_2^*\lambda_{g_2}^{1}
\qquad (g=g_1+g_2\text{ separating})
\label{eq:mumford-descent}
\end{equation}
at each boundary clutching, with the analogous identity
$\partial'^*\lambda_g^{1}=\lambda_{g-1}^{1}$ at non-separating boundary
(Mumford $1983$ \emph{Arithmetic and Geometry} vol.\ II, Ch.~III;
Teixidor $1988$ \emph{Duke Math.\ J.}\ $56$). Therefore the
curvature data is \emph{not added} to the chiral coderived
differential --- it \emph{is} what makes the coderived differential
compatible with the cyclic-operadic structure of $\Mbar$.
\begin{proposition}[$\kch$-curving realises boundary descent]
\label{prop:kch-curving-descent}
Let $\cA$ be a chiral Lie algebra on $C$ with
$\kch\in\mathbb{Q}$. The genus-indexed curvature
$m_0^{(g,n)}=\kch\cdot\lambda_g^{1}$ of
Def.~\ref{def:genus-tower-curvature} satisfies (O2) of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:chiral-BL}, with the verification reducing to
Mumford's boundary-relation~\eqref{eq:mumford-descent} for the Hodge
bundle. Consequently, the only obstructions remaining are (O1)
(Ran-space cofibrant generation) and (O3) (sewing--analytic
comparison).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
The curvature $m_0^{(g,n)}=\kch\cdot\lambda_g^{1}$ is a pullback of
the Mumford class, hence satisfies
$\partial^* m_0^{(g,n)}=\kch(\partial^*\lambda_g^{1})=
\kch(\pi_1^*\lambda_{g_1}^{1}+\pi_2^*\lambda_{g_2}^{1})
=m_0^{(g_1,n_1+1)}+m_0^{(g_2,n_2+1)}$
at separating clutching; the non-separating identity is analogous.
The compatibility with the chiral Lie operation is the statement that
$(d^{(g)})^2=[m_0^{(g)},-]$ commutes with $\partial^*$, which reduces
to Mumford's relation and the naturality of the bracket.
\end{proof}
This proposition is the structural payoff of Cycle V: (O2) is
\emph{not} an independent obstruction; it is \emph{automatic} from
$m_0^{(g)}=\kch\lambda_g^{1}$ and the Mumford boundary relation.
The residual obstructions are exactly (O1) and (O3).
\end{heal}

\section{What follows from the conjecture}
\label{sec:consequences}
% ============================================================

Assuming Conjecture~\ref{conj:chiral-BL}, three immediate consequences:

\begin{proposition}[Coderived equality of chiral $\HH$ with the
boundary-descent mass]
If Conjecture~\ref{conj:chiral-BL} holds, then for $\cA$ a chiral
Lie algebra on $C$ with $\kch\in\mathbb{Q}$,
\[
\Dco^{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA)\;\supseteq\;\bigoplus_{g\geq 0}\,
\Dco^{\mathrm{ch},g}(\cA),
\]
where each genus-$g$ summand carries the curvature
$m_0^{(g)}=\kch\cdot\lambda_g^{1}$, and the direct sum decomposition
is \emph{compatible} with the clutching $\partial$ up to the
boundary-descent identity of Prop.~\ref{prop:kch-curving-descent}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Sewing--analytic Koszul shadow]
The comparison~\eqref{eq:alg-vs-analytic} holds at the level of
$\mathbb{Q}$-structures: under the Moriwaki IndHilb completion, the
$\mathbb{Q}$-valued Hilbert-Schmidt sewing factors through the
algebraic coderived category, realising the IndHilb object as an
analytic completion of the coderived object. The comparison is not
an equivalence of categories; it is a realisation functor.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Koszul-locus reduction]
On the Koszul locus $\{\kch=0\}\cap\{m_0=0\}$, the chiral
Booth--Lazarev equivalence reduces to the Lef\`evre-Hasegawa
equivalence in each fibre, and the genus-tower structure trivialises
(curvature is zero, so the family is trivial). This is the
compatibility check analogous to Booth--Lazarev's specialisation
at $m_0=0$.
\end{proposition}

\section{Cache append}
\label{sec:cache}
% ============================================================

\begin{itemize}
\item[AP-CY\#.] \emph{The chiral Booth--Lazarev target is not a
packaging of the associative one.} Three structural upgrades
required: Ran-space cofibrant generation compatible with the chiral
tensor; genus-tower operadic structure on $\Mbar$; analytic sewing
comparison with Moriwaki IndHilb. Do not describe the chiral lift
as ``the Booth--Lazarev Quillen equivalence on Ran-space coalgebras''
without naming the three obstructions.
\item[AP-CY\#.] \emph{$\kch$-curving is the boundary-descent datum.}
The curvature $m_0^{(g)}=\kch\cdot\lambda_g^{1}$ satisfies Mumford's
boundary relation on $\Mbar$ automatically; this reduces
obstruction (O2) to a consequence of
$m_0=\kch\cdot\lambda$, not an independent hypothesis. The
residual obstructions are (O1) and (O3) only.
\item[AP-CY\#.] \emph{The IndHilb comparison is analytic, not
algebraic.} The sewing--analytic comparison (O3) requires a
nuclearity + trace-class check on the Moriwaki completion;
Booth--Lazarev says nothing about topological vector spaces.
Distinguish algebraic coderived (Conjecture~\ref{conj:chiral-BL}
(i)--(iii)) from the analytic comparison
(Conjecture~\ref{conj:chiral-BL}-adjacent).
\end{itemize}
