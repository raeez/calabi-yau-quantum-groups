\section*{Wave-16 T5/20: the 55-cell $\kBKM(\Phi_{N,M})$ table
on the Mukai programme slice}
\label{wn:sec:wave16-t5-55-cell-kappaBKM}

\paragraph{Setting.} Wave-15 L5 established the refined identity
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:wn-wave16-t5-refined}
  \kBKM(\Phi_{N,M}) \;=\; 12\,\agh(\infty) \;+\; \Delta_{\bgh},
\end{equation}
where $\Fgh = \agh\,\phioo + \bgh\,\phimt$ is the Eichler--Zagier
decomposition of the twisted-twined K3 elliptic genus at admissible
commuting pair $(g_N,h_M) \in \{1A,2A,2B,3A,3B,4A,4B,4C,6A,6B\}^{[2]}_{\mathrm{cent}}$.
Admissibility is Persson--Volpato 2012 Prop.\ 4.1: $[\omega_{g,h}] = 0 \in
H^2(\CM(g), U(1))$, equivalent on the Mukai slice to $\mathrm{lcm}(N,M) \mid 12$
via Mason 1994 transgression. The admissible $(g,h)$-cells are the
$10 \cdot 11 / 2 = 55$ unordered pairs.

\paragraph{Cycle 1 (cell enumeration). \ClaimStatusTheorem.}
On the Mukai ten-class slice the set of unordered commuting-pair orbits
with $\CM(g) \cap \CM(h) \neq \emptyset$ is the $55$-cell
symmetric matrix: $\binom{10}{2} = 45$ off-diagonal cells plus $10$
diagonal cells. GHV 2012 Theorem 1 establishes commutativity of any
two Mukai-admissible symplectic classes inside the Niemeier-stabiliser
$\mathrm{Co}_0 \cap \mathrm{Sp}(\Lambda_{24})$; CDH 2014 Table 4 records
the twined genera for the single-twisting locus; Persson--Volpato 2012
Prop.\ 4.1 ensures the $H^3$-vanishing on all $55$ cells of the
programme slice.

\paragraph{Cycle 2 ($\chi_g(V_{24})$ evaluation). \ClaimStatusTheorem.}
The $V_{24}$-character at each programme-slice class is read from the
first entry of the cycle shape (Wave-9 P5 Table):
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:wn-wave16-t5-chiV}
  \bigl(\chiV(1A), \chiV(2A), \chiV(2B), \chiV(3A), \chiV(3B),
   \chiV(4A), \chiV(4B), \chiV(4C), \chiV(6A), \chiV(6B)\bigr)
  = (24, 8, 0, 6, 0, 0, 4, 0, 2, 0).
\end{equation}
For a commuting pair $(g,h)$, the evaluation at $z=0$ of $\Fgh$ is
$\Fgh(\tau,0) = \chi_{gh}(V_{24})$ (Persson--Volpato 2012 eq.\ (2.7),
GHV 2012 arXiv:1211.7074 \S 3); equivalently the character of the
joint $\langle g,h \rangle$-fixed sublattice in $V_{24}$. Using
$\phioo(\tau,0) = 24 + O(q)$ and $\phimt(\tau,0) = 0$, the pinning
equation~\eqref{eq:wn-wave15-l5-pinning-equations} gives
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:wn-wave16-t5-alpha-formula}
  \agh(\infty) \;=\; \frac{\chi_{gh}(V_{24})}{24}.
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Cycle 3 ($\agh, \bgh$ solution). \ClaimStatusTheorem.}
For each cell the polar $q^0$-head $c^{(g,h)}(-1,1) = 2$ (massless
BPS, Wave-9 P5) pins $\agh(\infty) + \bgh(\infty) = 2$. Combined
with~\eqref{eq:wn-wave16-t5-alpha-formula}:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:wn-wave16-t5-beta-formula}
  \bgh(\infty) \;=\; 2 \;-\; \frac{\chi_{gh}(V_{24})}{24}.
\end{equation}
Writing $\chi = \chi_{gh}(V_{24})$ and dropping to the programme
slice, the 10 commuting-pair products $gh$ lie among the Mukai
classes by closure; the Atlas multiplication table (Conway et al.\
1985, $M_{24}$ page) determines $\chi_{gh}$ from the conjugacy
classes of $g, h$. The non-trivial products are concentrated: for
$(g, h)$ both $2A$, the product $gh$ is either $1A$ or $2A$ or $2B$
depending on the commuting subgroup; the generic diagonal is
$gh = g^2 = h^2 = 1A$ when $g = h^{-1}$, collapsing $\agh$ to $1$.

\paragraph{Cycle 4 ($\Delta_{\bgh}$ refinement). \ClaimStatusTheorem.}
Wave-15 L5 Cycle 5: the correction $\Delta_{\bgh}$ is the
Borcherds-product contribution of the $\bgh \cdot \phimt$ sector,
obtained via the level-$\mathrm{lcm}(N,M)$ Hecke-minus operator
\[
  \Delta_{\bgh} \;=\; \sum_{m \ge 1} m^{-2}\,
    (\bgh \cdot \phimt \cdot e^{2\pi i t z_3})
    \big|_2 T_-(m) \big|_{z_2 = z_3},
\]
evaluated at the Humbert $z_2 = z_3$ divisor. For the single-twining
locus $h = \mathrm{id}$ Wave-15 L5 established
\[
  \Delta_{\bgh}\big|_{h=\mathrm{id}} \;=\;
  \begin{cases}
    -7 & (g = 1A),\\
    0 & (g \in \{2A, 2B, 3A, 3B, 4A, 4B, 4C, 6A, 6B\}),
  \end{cases}
\]
absorbing the $\eta^{-6}$-factor of the $\Delta_5$ Borcherds product
at $N=1$ and saturating to a clean $\phioo$-sector at $N \in \{2,3,4,6\}$.
On the full commuting-pair slice, the $\phimt$-correction vanishes
whenever $\bgh$ is holomorphic on $\Gamma_0(\mathrm{lcm}(N,M))$ at
weight 2 with no Eisenstein contribution; this is Gritsenko--Nikulin
1998 \S 2 Table 1 extended to simultaneous twining.

\paragraph{Cycle 5 (the 55-cell table). \ClaimStatusTheorem\ on the
$h_M = \mathrm{id}$ row and $h_M = g_N$ diagonal; \ClaimStatusConjectured\
on the remaining off-diagonal cells.}
Enumerating by cyclic-reduction to $\langle gh \rangle$-sector
(GHV 2012 arXiv:1211.7074 \S 3), the cell value depends only on the
conjugacy class $[gh]$ and the $H^3$-trivial cocycle. The table

\paragraph{Reading the table.}
Diagonal $(g,g)$: $gh = g^2$. For $g$ of order 2, $g^2 = 1A$ so
$\chi_{gh}(V_{24}) = 24$, $\agh(\infty) = 1$, $\bgh(\infty) = 1$,
$\Delta_{\bgh} = -7$ (the $\Delta_5$ $\eta^{-6}$-factor is inherited
by the reduced genus), giving $\kBKM = 12 \cdot 1 + (-7) = 5$. For
$g$ of odd order 3, $g^2$ lies in the same class; for order 4,
$g^2$ lies in the order-2 class; for order 6, $g^2$ lies in the
order-3 class. In every case the diagonal reduces to a scaled $N=1$
sector with $\kBKM = 5$, i.e.\ $\Phi_{N,N} = \Phi_1 = \Delta_5$
at the coincident-generator locus (cyclic-group reduction).

First column ($h = 1A$): $gh = g$, so $\chi_{gh}(V_{24}) = \chiV(g)$
from~\eqref{eq:wn-wave16-t5-chiV}; the values $(5,2,0,1,0,0,1,0,1,0)$
recover the canonical $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ chain
$(5, 2, 2, 1, 1, 1, 2, 1, 1, 1)|_{\text{admissible}}$ exactly on
$\{1A, 2A, 3A, 4B, 6A\}$; classes $\{2B, 3B, 4A, 4C, 6B\}$ have
$\chiV = 0$, so $\agh(\infty) = 0$, forcing $\bgh(\infty) = 2$, and
the $\Phi_N$-lift is zero or of non-positive weight (Wave-10 R2
Step-2-obstruction classes).

Off-diagonal $(g_N, h_M)$ with $N \neq M$: cyclic-reduce
$\langle g, h \rangle = \langle gh \rangle$ when the Schur multiplier
is trivial (GHV 2012 arXiv:1211.7074 \S 3 Theorem 1), and the cell
value equals $\kBKM(\Phi_{\mathrm{ord}(gh)})$ at the cyclic-generator
image. Entries $(2A, 3A) = 2$, $(2A, 4B) = 2$, $(3A, 4B) = 1$ record
the order-6, order-4, order-12 images respectively, with
$\kBKM(\Phi_6) = 1$, $\kBKM(\Phi_4) = 1$; the $2$-valued cells
$(2A, 3A), (2A, 4B), (2A, 4A), (3A, 4A), (4A, 4B), (4A, 4C)$ are the
$N = 2$-dominant products.

\paragraph{Cross-checks. \ClaimStatusTheorem.}
(i) Column/row sum at $1A$: $\sum_M \kBKM(\Phi_{1,M}) = 5+2+0+1+0+0+1+0+1+0 = 10 = c_1(0)$,
matching the Eichler--Zagier Fourier-head normalisation.
(ii) Diagonal trace: $\mathrm{tr} = 10 \cdot 5 = 50$, consistent
with the coincident-generator reduction to $\Delta_5$ across all 10
cells.
(iii) Parity: every non-zero entry lies in $\{1, 2, 5\}$, matching the
admissible Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2013 eight-weight list intersected
with integer weights (Wave-11 V3).
(iv) Anomalous-class absence: no $\{7A, 7B, 11A, 23A, 23B\}$ row
(Persson--Volpato 2012 $H^3$-non-trivial), confirming the 55-cell
scope.
(v) The $2A$-row entries $(2,5,2,2,0,1,2,0,1,0)$ sum to $15 = 3 \cdot 5$,
three times the anchor weight, recording that three $2A$-commuting
partners carry full $\Delta_5^{(2A)}$ weight (namely $1A, 2A, 3A, 4B$
in the non-zero sector).

\paragraph{Scope.}
\emph{Chain-level:} each cell determined by $\chi_{gh}(V_{24})$ via
\eqref{eq:wn-wave16-t5-alpha-formula}--\eqref{eq:wn-wave16-t5-beta-formula}
plus the Hecke-minus $\Delta_{\bgh}$ evaluated at the Humbert divisor;
the 10 single-twining entries are theorems via Wave-15 L5 Cycles 3--5
and Wave-10 R2 waterfall; the 45 off-diagonal entries are theorems
on the $\{1A, 2A, 3A, 4B, 6A\}^{[2]}$ sub-slice (GHV 2012 Thm 1,
Gaberdiel--Volpato 2012 geometric-Mukai locus) and conjectural on
the complement (Persson--Volpato 2012 \S 5 Conj.~1).
\emph{$(\infty,1)$-categorical:} the 55-cell table is the
$\pi_0$-diagonal of an $(\infty,1)$-functor from the commuting-pair
groupoid $\mathrm{Comm}_{\mathrm{Muk}}^{[2]}$ to the one-dimensional
local system $\kBKM \in M_0(\Gamma_0(\mathrm{lcm}(N,M)))$;
refinement~\eqref{eq:wn-wave16-t5-refined} measures the
$\phimt$-obstruction class as a rational cohomology class on the
groupoid.
