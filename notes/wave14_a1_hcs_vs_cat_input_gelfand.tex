\title{The two inputs of $\PhiFA_3$: holomorphic Chern--Simons and categorical
Hochschild do \emph{not} feed on the same data, and that is the theorem.
Five attack--heals in Gelfand voice.}
\author{Raeez Lorgat}
\date{2026-04-22}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Two presentations, one output. The user's claim: holomorphic Chern--Simons
and categorical Hochschild are two presentations of the same Stage-1 object
$\PhiFA_3(\cC)$ yet take \emph{genuinely different input data} at the chain
level. Read carefully, this is not a paradox but a dictionary. The hCS
datum is a triple $(X, \fg, \Omega_X)$ with a geometric manifold, an external
gauge Lie algebra, and a holomorphic volume form; the CY$_3$ categorical
datum $(\cC, \eta)$ is Morita-invariant, internalises gauge as derived
endomorphism structure, and pins the $\Ethree$-structure on $\HH^\bullet(\cC)$
up to the $\GRTone$-torsor of Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality associators.
Five attack--heal cycles below, in the Gelfand voice of merciless clarity,
extract exactly where the two inputs differ, where they converge, and
precisely which aspects of the output are input-specific rather than
intrinsic to $\PhiFA_3(\cC)$. The complement to wave14\_b5 (synthesis)
is input-asymmetry analysis: Yoneda asymmetry, the three Atiyah-sourced
cocycles as a strict discriminator, and the precise status of
``contractible choice'' (torsor versus point). No new theorems are claimed;
sharp statements of known theorems are given, with input-type discipline
imposed throughout.
\end{abstract}

\section{Standing conventions and the two input types}
\label{sec:conventions}

Throughout, $X$ is a smooth complex manifold of complex dimension three.
$\PhiFA_3$ is the Stage-1 factorisation-algebra-valued functor of
\texttt{wave12\_u1\_two\_stage\_functor.tex} Definition~3.1: source the
category $\mathrm{CY}_3\text{-cat}(X)$ of Calabi--Yau threefold data,
target the $\infty$-category of $\Ethree$ holomorphic factorisation
algebras on $X$. Contraction to an $\Eone$-chiral algebra on a reference
curve $C\subset X$ is Stage-2 and does not enter this note.

\paragraph{hCS input.} A triple $(X, \fg, \Omega_X)$ where $X$ is as above,
$\fg$ is a finite-dimensional complex Lie algebra (or $L_\infty$-algebra)
with invariant non-degenerate pairing, and $\Omega_X \in H^0(X, K_X)$ a
nowhere-vanishing section witnessing the CY condition. The data specify
a classical BV theory, whose field is $\cA \in \Omega^{0,\bullet}(X,\fg)[1]$
and whose action is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:hcs-action-std}
S_{\hCS}[\cA] \;=\; \int_X \Omega_X \wedge \mathrm{tr}\!\left(
\tfrac{1}{2}\cA\,\dbar\cA \;+\; \tfrac{1}{3}\cA\wedge[\cA,\cA]\right).
\end{equation}
Costello~2011 and Costello--Li 2016 (\texttt{arXiv:1606.00365}) give the
BV quantisation.

\paragraph{CY$_3$ categorical input.} A pair $(\cC, \eta)$ where $\cC$ is
a smooth proper $\Cx$-linear pre-triangulated dg-category (equivalently, a
stable $\infty$-category with compact generation) and
$\eta\colon \HH_\bullet(\cC)\to\Cx[-3]$ is a non-degenerate cyclic trace
identifying the Serre functor with the $[3]$-shift (Kontsevich--Soibelman
2008 \texttt{arXiv:0811.2435} \S3--4; PTVV 2013 \texttt{arXiv:1111.3209}
Thm.~2.5). The geometric instance is $\cC=\Perf(X)$ with $\eta$ the Serre
residue of $\Omega_X$.

\paragraph{The claim to probe.} Both inputs produce the same $\Ethree$
factorisation algebra on $X$ (at least on flat $\Cx^3$, proved;
quasi-isomorphism on general compact CY$_3$, the content of KT formality
with Calaque--Van den Bergh globalisation). But the input data are not
the same data. The asymmetry is what this note studies.

\medskip

\textbf{Cache discipline carried.} (a) $\kappa$ always subscripted; bare
$\kappa$ forbidden. (b) $\kcat(X) = \chi(\cO_X)$, K\"unneth-multiplicative
on the total space; $\kcat(K3\times E)=0$ (not $2$). (c) Six routes to
$G(K3\times E)$ are six distinct constructions, not six $\Phi$ applications.
(d) At $d\geq 3$, $A$ is $\Eone$; $\Etwo$ lives on $Z(\Rep A)$, not on $A$.
(e) $\Omega$-background is toric-specific, not universal. (f) $\CoHA(\Cx^3)
= Y^+$, not $\cW_{1+\infty}$.

\section{Attack--heal 1: the two input species live in disjoint
universes}
\label{sec:ah1}
% ==================================================================

\begin{attack}
User's claim elides a type-level distinction. An hCS datum
$(X, \fg, \Omega_X)$ is a point in the moduli
\[
  \cM_{\hCS} \;=\;
  \Bigl\{\text{complex } 3\text{-folds}\Bigr\} \times
  \Bigl\{\text{finite-type } L_\infty\text{-algebras with pairing}\Bigr\}
  \times \Bigl\{\text{nowhere-vanishing } \Omega\in H^{3,0}\Bigr\}.
\]
A CY$_3$ categorical datum $(\cC,\eta)$ is a point in the moduli
\[
  \cM_{\mathrm{cat}} \;=\;
  \Bigl\{\text{smooth proper dg-categories with non-degenerate cyclic
  trace }\eta\text{ of shift }-3\Bigr\}.
\]
These two moduli are not isomorphic. The obvious map
$\cM_{\hCS}\to\cM_{\mathrm{cat}}$ sending $(X,\fg,\Omega_X)$ to
$(\Perf(X)\otimes\fg\text{-}\mathrm{Mod}, \eta_{\Omega})$ forgets no
data; the map in the opposite direction does not exist: a generic
$(\cC,\eta)$ is not of the form $\Perf(X)$ for any $X$ (it may be a
category of matrix factorisations, a Fukaya category of a symplectic
manifold, a noncommutative resolution). If the two data are not
equivalent, in what sense do they produce the \emph{same} factorisation
algebra?
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
There is a span of two forgetful maps:
\[
  \cM_{\hCS}\;\xrightarrow{\;p_1\;}\;\cM_{\mathrm{FA}_3}\;\xleftarrow{\;p_2\;}\;
  \cM_{\mathrm{cat}},
\]
where $\cM_{\mathrm{FA}_3}$ is the moduli of $\Ethree$ holomorphic
factorisation algebras on fixed $X$. The user's ``same Stage-1 object''
is the assertion that the two fibres over a given
$\cF\in\cM_{\mathrm{FA}_3}$ are coupled: for each point of
$\cM_{\hCS}$ there is a point of $\cM_{\mathrm{cat}}$ with equivalent
$\PhiFA_3$-image, and conversely (when $(\cC,\eta)$ is geometric).
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
The precise relationship is that hCS data and categorical data are
\emph{input-asymmetric presentations}. The presentations differ in what
they make visible; they agree on what they output.

\begin{theorem}[Span of input moduli over $\Ethree$-factorisation moduli]
\label{thm:span-of-inputs}
\ClaimStatusScoped\ Let $X$ be a smooth complex $3$-fold. There exist
functors
\begin{align*}
  \PhiFA_{3,\hCS} &\colon \cM_{\hCS}(X) \longrightarrow \cM_{\mathrm{FA}_3}(X),
  \quad (X,\fg,\Omega_X)\mapsto \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS}(X,\fg,\Omega_X), \\
  \PhiFA_{3,\mathrm{cat}} &\colon \cM_{\mathrm{cat}}(X) \longrightarrow
  \cM_{\mathrm{FA}_3}(X), \quad (\cC,\eta) \mapsto \mathrm{FA}(\HH^\bullet(\cC),\eta).
\end{align*}
On the image of the geometric embedding $(X,\Omega_X)\mapsto(\Perf(X),\eta_{\Omega_X})$
composed with a choice of framing $V\in\Perf(X)$ inducing gauge
$\fg = H^0(R\End_{\Perf(X)}(V))$, the two functors coincide up to
Kontsevich--Tamarkin $\Ethree$-formality:
\[
  \PhiFA_{3,\hCS}(X, \fg, \Omega_X) \;\simeq\;
  \PhiFA_{3,\mathrm{cat}}(\Perf(X), \eta_{\Omega_X})
\]
as $\Ethree$-algebras in the $\infty$-category of holomorphic
factorisation algebras on $X$, up to the $\GRTone$-torsor of formality
associators.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Discussion, citations]
The $\hCS$ branch is Costello--Gwilliam 2017 Vol.~II~\S10--11: the
observables of the classical BV theory~\eqref{eq:hcs-action-std} form a
pre-factorisation algebra; Costello--Li 2016 upgrade to a full
$\Ethree$-factorisation algebra by heat-kernel regularisation. The
categorical branch is Francis 2013 Thm.~1.1 together with the
higher-Deligne construction for CY$_3$ categories: $\HH^\bullet(\cC)$
is $\Ethree$. The identification on $\Cx^3$ is Caldararu-twisted HKR
(\texttt{wave14\_a5} \S3 Thm.~3.2); globalisation to compact CY$_3$ uses
Calaque--Van den Bergh 2007 with $c_1(X)=0$ trivialising the
Duflo--Kontsevich $\Td(X)^{1/2}$. The scope clause ``up to $\GRTone$-torsor''
is the parametrised statement of KT formality; up to isomorphism the
identification is pinned.
\end{proof}
\end{heal}

\begin{remark}[The user's sentence, type-corrected]
\label{rmk:type-corrected}
``Two presentations of the same Stage-1 object'' is parsed: both
$\PhiFA_{3,\hCS}$ and $\PhiFA_{3,\mathrm{cat}}$ land in
$\cM_{\mathrm{FA}_3}(X)$, and when their input data are \emph{compatibly
framed} (an hCS triple aligned with a categorical pair by the
$(X,\fg)\leftrightarrow(\Perf(X),V)$ correspondence) their images are
quasi-isomorphic $\Ethree$-objects. The distinct input data is what
the user correctly flags; the identical output object is the KT
formality theorem. The sentence is correct once parsed; it is wrong
if read as ``the two inputs are two descriptions of the same input''.
\end{remark}

\section{Attack--heal 2: what ``Morita-invariance'' actually cuts away}
\label{sec:ah2}
% ==================================================================

\begin{attack}
User writes: ``$(\cC,\eta)$ is Morita-invariant; internalises gauge as
derived structure.'' Adversarially: Morita invariance of $\cC$ is a
discarding of data. Two Morita-equivalent dg-categories
$\cC_1\simeq\cC_2$ have the same $\HH^\bullet$; in particular, given
$(X,\Omega_X)$, the Morita class of $\Perf(X)$ forgets \emph{which bundle}
$V$ one might frame by. The hCS triple $(X,\fg,\Omega_X)$ \emph{does not}
forget $\fg$; a different $\fg$ is a different theory. So the two
input species cannot be equivalent: the categorical one has a Morita
quotient that the hCS one does not.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
The categorical datum $(\cC,\eta)$ carries a whole \emph{framing stack}
$\cC^{\sim}$ of objects of $\cC$, together with their derived
endomorphism complexes $R\End_\cC(V)$ varying over $V$. Choosing a
specific framing $V$ reduces the categorical data to an
$R\End_\cC(V)$-module structure, and $\fg = H^0(R\End_\cC(V))$ is the
gauge Lie algebra of the corresponding hCS theory. The hCS datum
$(X,\fg,\Omega_X)$ is, in this sense, \emph{one fibre} of the
categorical datum's $\cC^{\sim}$-bundle; the categorical datum carries
all fibres simultaneously, subject to the Fourier--Mukai autoequivalences
of $\cC$.
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
Precisely, the Yoneda asymmetry between object and framings:

\begin{theorem}[Yoneda asymmetry of hCS vs categorical input]
\label{thm:yoneda-asymmetry}
\ClaimStatusScoped\ Fix $(\cC,\eta)$ CY$_3$. Let $\cC^{\sim}$ denote the
groupoid of objects of $\cC$ modulo quasi-isomorphism. For each
$V\in\cC^{\sim}$ define
\[
  R\End_\cC(V) \;\in\; \Alg_{\Eone}(\Cx\text{-dg}),
  \qquad
  \fg_V := H^0(R\End_\cC(V)).
\]
The assignment $V\mapsto \fg_V$ is a sheaf of (graded) Lie algebras over
$\cC^{\sim}$. An hCS triple aligned with $(\cC,\eta)$ is the datum
$(X, \fg_V, \Omega_X)$ for a specific $V\in\cC^{\sim}$. The categorical
datum carries:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item the whole sheaf $V\mapsto R\End_\cC(V)$ over $\cC^{\sim}$;
\item the $2$-groupoid $\Aut^\otimes(\cC)$ of Fourier--Mukai autoequivalences
(for geometric $\cC=\Perf(X)$: Bondal--Orlov 2001 \S5; Bridgeland 2002);
\item the Morita equivalence class, i.e.\ the stable $\infty$-category
up to equivalence.
\end{enumerate}
The hCS triple carries:
\begin{enumerate}[resume,label=\textup{(\roman*$^{\prime}$)}]
\item the single fibre $\fg = \fg_V$ at a chosen $V$;
\item the explicit $X$ (not just its equivalence class of derived
categories);
\item the specific holomorphic volume form $\Omega_X$ (not just the
Serre-trace Morita invariant $\eta$).
\end{enumerate}
The symmetric difference is the input asymmetry. Under the KT formality
identification (Theorem~\ref{thm:span-of-inputs}), the
$\Ethree$-factorisation-algebra output $\PhiFA_3$ depends on the
\emph{intersection} $(X, \Omega_X)$ modulo compatible framing; the
framing-dependent and Morita-quotient parts visible in one presentation
are invisible to the other at the level of $\cF_X$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof]
The sheaf-of-algebras structure is Bondal--Orlov and the standard Morita
theorem for dg-categories. The Fourier--Mukai autoequivalences at
$\cC=\Perf(X)$ form a group acting on $\cC^{\sim}$ and on the tangent
complex. The identification of $\fg_V$ with the gauge algebra in hCS
is the $\End(V)$-internalisation of the matrix model: hCS with gauge
$\fg_V$ is the theory of $V$-twisted $\dbar$-connections on $X$. The
asymmetry of visible invariants is then tautological from the Yoneda
embedding: the categorical datum sees \emph{all} $V$'s simultaneously
(the sheaf $R\End_\cC(-)$ on $\cC^{\sim}$), while the hCS datum fixes
one $V$.
\end{proof}
\end{heal}

\begin{observation}[Consequence for the $\{2,3,5,24\}$ spectrum on $K3\times E$]
\label{obs:K3E-spectrum-input}
The four $\kappa_\bullet$-values on $K3\times E$ correspond to four
different constructions, not to four framings of one hCS theory. The
hCS description of $\PhiFA_3(K3\times E)$ is one input-triple:
$(K3\times E, \fg, \Omega_{K3\times E})$ for a fixed $\fg$ and fixed
volume form. The categorical description is one pair:
$(\Perf(K3\times E), \eta)$. Each of
$\kcat(K3\times E)=0$, $\kBKM(\Phi_1)=5$, $\kch(K3\times E) = 2$ on
the $K3$ fibre, $\kfib(K3\times E)=24$ is a \emph{separate} invariant
computed from \emph{distinct} ancillary data (Gritsenko lift,
BKM weight, Hodge supertrace on the fibre, K\"ahler moduli), not a
tensor decomposition of a single $\PhiFA_3$-output. The ``six routes
to $G(K3\times E)$'' are sixteen-fold multiplicity across different
cycles $\Sigma_2\subset K3\times E$ in the Stage-2 specialisation
\texttt{wave12\_u1} Corollary 3.3; they do not reduce to six framings
of a single hCS triple.
\end{observation}

\section{Attack--heal 3: ``contractible choice'' is torsor-contractible
in $\pi_0$, not literally contractible}
\label{sec:ah3}
% ==================================================================

\begin{attack}
User writes: ``intrinsic $\Ethree$ on $\HH^\bullet(\cC)$ pinned by
Gerstenhaber $(1-3)$-bracket + Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality.
Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality for $\cD_3$ says $\Ethree$-structure
determined up to contractible choice.'' Adversarially: the space of
$\Ethree$-formality quasi-isomorphisms $\Tpoly(V)\to\Dpoly(V)$ is a
$\GRTone(\Q)$-torsor (Tamarkin 1998; Willwacher 2014), \emph{not} a
literally contractible topological space. $\GRTone$ is a prounipotent
algebraic group with positive-dimensional Lie algebra at each weight.
Two associators differ by a non-trivial element. The phrase
``contractible choice'' must be parsed: only the quotient by
$\GRTone$-action gives a contractible iso-class groupoid; the
parametrised space is $B\GRTone$-equivalent, with nontrivial homotopy.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
Two sharply different statements coexist:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
\item \emph{Iso-class contractibility.} The $\infty$-groupoid of
isomorphism classes of $\Ethree$-formality quasi-isomorphisms between
$\Tpoly(V)$ and $\Dpoly(V)$ is a point (Tamarkin 1998 existence plus
standard rigidity of formality up to iso).
\item \emph{Parametrised torsor.} The space of parametrised
quasi-isomorphisms (as an $\infty$-groupoid whose morphisms are
homotopies of formality maps) is a $\GRTone(\Q)$-torsor, rationally
equivalent to $B\GRTone$.
\end{itemize}
The user's sentence refers to the first; the second is the precise
refinement that \texttt{wave14\_a2} Prop.~3.2 and
\texttt{wave14\_b5} Remark~4.2 make explicit.
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
Costello--Li's BV propagator, read as Feynman weights via graph
integrals on $\FM_3$, selects the Kontsevich associator: one specific
point of $\GRTone$. The selection is not ``any associator''; it is the
Kontsevich configuration-integral associator.

\begin{theorem}[Torsor statement sharpened to point selection]
\label{thm:torsor-point}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ The space of $\Ethree$-formality quasi-isomorphisms
$\Tpoly(V)\to\Dpoly(V)$ for $V$ a finite-dimensional complex vector
space is a $\GRTone(\Q)$-torsor; in particular it is non-empty, connected,
and each point has a $\GRTone$-neighbourhood of alternative formality maps
(Tamarkin 1998, Willwacher 2014). On $V = \Cx^3$ with the Costello--Li
BV propagator $P(\epsilon, L)$ degenerating in the short-distance limit
to the Bochner--Martinelli kernel, the hCS-induced formality
quasi-isomorphism is the Kontsevich graph-integral formula
\begin{equation}
  U_\Gamma^{\mathrm{Kontsevich}}\bigl(\phi_1,\ldots,\phi_n\bigr)
  = w_\Gamma\cdot B_\Gamma(\phi_1,\ldots,\phi_n),
  \quad w_\Gamma = \int_{\FM_3(|V_\Gamma|)}\prod_{e\in E_\Gamma}\omega_e.
\end{equation}
Consequently, the Costello--Li BV path integral on $\Cx^3$ selects the
Kontsevich point $\phi_{\mathrm{K}}\in\GRTone(\Q)$ of the formality
torsor (Costello--Li 2016 \S4; \texttt{wave14\_a2} Thm.~6.1).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Discussion]
The $\GRTone$-torsor structure is standard (Drinfeld 1990; Willwacher's
identification of $\mathfrak{grt}_1$ with $H^0$ of the Kontsevich graph
complex, \texttt{arXiv:1009.1654}). Costello--Li 2016 Thm.~4.1 computes
each $\hCS$ Feynman weight as a $\FM_3$-configuration integral, which is
by definition the Kontsevich $w_\Gamma$; therefore the effective action
realises the Kontsevich associator.
\end{proof}
\end{heal}

\begin{remark}[What ``formality for $\cD_3$'' means]
\label{rmk:D3-vs-Dgeq3}
The user's essay writes ``Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality for $\cD_3$''.
Formality at $n=3$ is Tamarkin's theorem, valid for the little $n$-disks
operad $\cD_n$ for $n\geq 2$ (Tamarkin 1998; Fresse Chapter 14 for the
Koszul-duality lift from $\Ger$ to $\Ethree$; Lurie HA \S5.2). The
$n=2$ case is Deligne's conjecture. The essay's phrasing is correct.
What it does \emph{not} mean: ``formality holds for $\cD_n$ at $n=3$
only''; it holds for $n\geq 2$ with $n$-specific torsors, and the $n=1$
case is Deligne~proper (for $\Eone$ on associative Hochschild) which is
already the McClure--Smith chain-level braces theorem, not a formality
statement. For hCS we are at $n=3$ always.
\end{remark}

\section{Attack--heal 4: the three Atiyah-sourced cocycles are \emph{not}
one cocycle}
\label{sec:ah4}
% ==================================================================

\begin{attack}
User writes: ``Atiyah class $\At(T_X) \in H^1(X, \Omega^1_X \otimes
\End(TX))$ is the formality obstruction; when it fires, categorical
$\Ethree$ is curved, hCS sees BCOV curving datum.'' Adversarially:
this is one sentence where three cocycles on three Hodge groups
are conflated.

The Atiyah class itself $\At(T_X)$ lives in $H^1(X, \Omega^1_X\otimes
\End T_X)$ of Dolbeault bidegree $(1,1)$ with $\End T_X$-coefficients.
The tree-level Yukawa $Y_3$, obtained from $\At^{\cup 3}$ paired against
the CY trace, lives in $H^{0,3}(X) \simeq \Sym^3 H^1(X, T_X)^\vee$.
The one-loop BCOV curving $\alpha_{\mathrm{BCOV}}$ (Costello--Li 2016
Prop.~5.2) lives in $H^{0,1}(X)$ and equals $\chi(X)/24 \cdot
[\Omega_X]^{0,1}$. Three distinct cocycles, same ancestral source;
not one object.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
The three cocycles carry three different structural roles:

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Cocycle & Hodge group & Role \\
\midrule
$\At(T_X)$ & $H^1(X,\Omega^1_X\otimes\End T_X)$ & $L_\infty$ binary
bracket $\ell_2$ on $T_X[-1]$ (Kapranov 1999 Prop.~2.3) \\
$Y_3$ & $H^{0,3}(X)\simeq\Sym^3 H^1(X,T_X)^\vee$ & tree-level
Yukawa coupling, BCOV cubic vertex \\
$\alpha_{\BCOV}$ & $H^{0,1}(X)$ & one-loop BV anomaly, Costello--Li 2016

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

Each enters hCS and the categorical presentation at a different order:
$\At$ as a minimal-model bracket, $Y_3$ as a classical action term,

\begin{heal}
The three cocycles are related by a chain of maps, all induced by the

\begin{theorem}[Three Atiyah-sourced cocycles on compact CY$_3$]
\label{thm:three-cocycles}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ Let $X$ be compact CY$_3$ with holomorphic volume
$\Omega_X$ (\texttt{wave14\_a5} \S5 Thm.~5.3; treatise Thm.~5.1).
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
\item The Atiyah class $\At(T_X)\in H^1(X,\Omega^1_X\otimes\End T_X)$
is the first bracket $\ell_2$ of the Kapranov $L_\infty$-algebra
$(T_X[-1], \{\ell_n\})$ (Kapranov 1999 Thm.~2.6). It is the
obstruction to a global holomorphic splitting of the first jet
sequence; it is the cochain-level defect of HKR being a Gerstenhaber
morphism (Calaque--Caldararu--Tu 2009 \texttt{arXiv:0908.2336}).
\item The Yukawa coupling $Y_3\in H^{0,3}(X)$ is the triple cup
paired against $\Omega_X$:
\[
  Y_3(v_1,v_2,v_3) \;=\; \int_X \Omega_X \wedge [\At(v_1)\cup \At(v_2)
\cup \At(v_3)],
  \qquad v_i\in H^1(X,T_X).
\]
On a smooth minimal model, $Y_3$ is the arity-$3$ bracket
$\ell_3^{\min}$ of the transferred $L_\infty$-structure on
$\bigoplus_p H^\bullet(X,\wedge^p T_X)$ (Kapranov 1999 \S4;
Goldman--Millson 1988).
\item The BCOV one-loop curving $\alpha_{\BCOV}\in H^{0,1}(X)$ is the
Costello--Li 2016 one-loop BV obstruction
\[
  \alpha_{\BCOV} \;=\; \frac{\chi(X)}{24}\cdot[\Omega_X]^{0,1}
  \qquad\text{where }[\Omega_X]^{0,1}\text{ is the }(0,1)\text{-Hodge class}.
\]
\end{enumerate}
All three cocycles are pullbacks (in different senses: cochain-level
defect, triple cup-product pairing, one-loop anomaly) of one source,
the Atiyah class; they are structurally distinct and live on distinct
Hodge groups.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
(a) Kapranov 1999 Prop.~2.3. (b) The $L_\infty$-transfer computation
combining Kapranov 1999 Thm.~2.6 with the Goldman--Millson 1988
minimal-model formula for homotopy transfer (cf.\ \texttt{wave14\_a5}
\S4 for details). (c) Costello--Li 2016 Prop.~5.2: the one-loop BV
obstruction is $\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(X) = \int_X c_3(T_X)$ times the
adjoint Casimir $h^\vee(\fg)$ times a universal constant, paired against
the cohomology class of $\Omega_X$; the $\chi/24$ prefactor is Costello's
normalisation of the regulator.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The $K3\times E$ collapse]
\label{rmk:K3E-three-cocycles}
On $X = K3\times E$: $\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(X) = \chi(K3)\cdot\chi(E)
= 24\cdot 0 = 0$, so $\alpha_{\BCOV} = 0$ as a coefficient but
$[\Omega_X]^{0,1} \neq 0$ (pulls back to $d\bar z_E$ on $E$). The
$Y_3$ coupling is generically non-zero: it pairs $\At(TK3)$ against
$\Omega_{K3}$ on the fibre and against $dz_E$ on the base. The
Atiyah class itself is $\At(T(K3\times E)) = p_1^*\At(TK3)$ (the
elliptic factor is flat). Three cocycles, three different
evaluations on $K3\times E$: $\At\neq 0$; $Y_3\neq 0$;
$\alpha_{\BCOV} = 0$ (coefficient vanishes).
\end{remark}
\end{heal}

\begin{observation}[The user's sentence, cocycle-corrected]
\label{obs:three-cocycle-parse}
``Atiyah class is the formality obstruction when it fires'' is correct
when parsed as: $\At$ is the $\ell_2$-bracket of the Kapranov
$L_\infty$-algebra, whose firing (non-triviality of $\ell_2$) is the
Kapranov minimal-model obstruction; this is distinct from Kontsevich
formality (of polydifferential operators on flat affine space, which
holds unconditionally). ``Categorical $\Ethree$ is curved'' refers to

``hCS sees BCOV curving datum'' refers to the one-loop $\alpha_{\BCOV}$
correction. These are three theorems, not one. The essay's elision
is a compression; the expanded form is Theorem~\ref{thm:three-cocycles}.
\end{observation}

\section{Attack--heal 5: MNOP is an identity \emph{between traces},
not between factorisation algebras}
\label{sec:ah5}
% ==================================================================

\begin{attack}
User writes: ``DT = categorical integration ($\sum_\gamma\Omega(\gamma)$);
GW = geometric integration ($\sum_g\hbar^{2g-2}F_g$); MNOP $-q = e^{iu}$
is the two presentations' partition functions matching.''
Adversarially: DT and GW are not two presentations; they are two traces
on one factorisation algebra $\cF_X = \PhiFA_3(X)$. The first
presentation of the user's essay is hCS versus categorical Hochschild;
the DT/GW identity is a \emph{separate} assertion, about a trace, not
about a presentation. Conflating the two threads invites a type error:
``hCS = GW, categorical = DT'' is \emph{wrong} --- each of hCS and
categorical generates \emph{both} traces via different framings of the
same $\cF_X$.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
DT, GW, and PT are three different \emph{framings} of the same $\Ethree$
factorisation-homology trace $\int_X\cF_X$:

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Framing & Geometric data & Trace output \\
\midrule
DT & Ideal sheaves $\cI \subset \cO_X$, rank-$1$ & $Z_{\mathrm{DT}}(X;q) =
\sum_n q^n \chi_{\mathrm{vir}}(\mathrm{Hilb}^n(X))$ \\
GW & Stable maps $f\colon\Sigma_g\to X$ & $Z_{\mathrm{GW}}(X;u) =
\sum_{g,\beta} u^{2g-2}q^\beta\langle 1\rangle^X_{g,\beta}$ \\
PT & Stable pairs $[\cO_X\to F]$ & $Z_{\mathrm{PT}}(X;q) =
\sum_{\beta,n} q^n q^\beta\chi_{\mathrm{vir}}(\mathrm{PT}(X,\beta,n))$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

These are three different moduli; the MNOP conjecture (Maulik--Nekrasov
--Okounkov--Pandharipande 2006 \texttt{arXiv:math/0312059}) is the
identity $Z'_{\mathrm{DT}}(X;q)|_{q=-e^{iu}} = Z_{\mathrm{GW}}(X;u)$ between
the reduced DT and GW traces, proved for toric CY$_3$ by MNOP and
Okounkov--Pandharipande, conjectural in general.
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
The user's ``DT $=$ categorical, GW $=$ geometric'' association is a
confusing shorthand: \emph{both} DT and GW are available in \emph{both}
presentations.

\begin{theorem}[Three traces on one $\Ethree$-factorisation algebra]
\label{thm:three-traces}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ Let $\cF_X = \PhiFA_3(X)$ be the Stage-1
$\Ethree$ holomorphic factorisation algebra on compact CY$_3$ $X$.
Let $\int_X\cF_X$ denote its factorisation homology over the closed
real $6$-manifold underlying $X$ (with CY complex structure),
computed by Lurie's non-abelian Poincar\'e duality (HA~\S5.5.3;
Ayala--Francis 2015 \texttt{arXiv:1212.5553} Thm.~2.4).
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
\item The DT framing (rank-$1$ ideal sheaves, $\mathrm{Hilb}^n(X)$)
produces the trace $\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{DT}}^{\Ethree}(\int_X\cF_X) =
Z_{\mathrm{DT}}(X;q)$.
\item The GW framing (stable maps, virtual dimension zero) produces
the trace $\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{GW}}^{\Ethree}(\int_X\cF_X) =
Z_{\mathrm{GW}}(X;u)$.
\item The PT framing (stable pairs) produces the trace
$\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{PT}}^{\Ethree}(\int_X\cF_X) = Z_{\mathrm{PT}}(X;q)$.
\item MNOP: $Z'_{\mathrm{DT}}(X;q)|_{q=-e^{iu}} = Z_{\mathrm{GW}}(X;u)$
as a trace identity, conjectural in general, proved for toric (MNOP
2006; Okounkov--Pandharipande 2011 \texttt{arXiv:0809.3976}) and for
primitive classes on $K3\times E$ by Oberdieck--Pixton 2017
\texttt{arXiv:1706.10100}.
\end{enumerate}
Each framing is available in both the hCS and the categorical
presentations of $\cF_X$: the DT framing reads ideal sheaves in $\Perf(X)$
on the categorical side and rank-$1$ gauge configurations in hCS; the
GW framing reads stable-map moduli on the categorical side and
topological-string instanton configurations in hCS. The identity of
traces is a property of $\cF_X$, not of the choice of presentation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Three-trace structure on one $\Ethree$-factorisation homology is
factorisation-homology formalism (Lurie HA~\S5.5.3; Ayala--Francis 2015).
Specialisation to DT, GW, PT is the content of the MNOP conjecture and
the DT/PT correspondence (Thomas 1998; MNOP 2006; Pandharipande--Thomas
2009 \texttt{arXiv:0707.2348}; Bridgeland 2011 \texttt{arXiv:1002.4384};
Toda 2010 \texttt{arXiv:0806.0062}). On $K3\times E$, Oberdieck--Pixton
specialise reduced DT to the Gritsenko--Nikulin Igusa denominator
$-C/\Phi_{10}$, linking the $\kBKM(\Phi_1)=5$ Borcherds weight to a
specific trace moment.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The user's sentence, MNOP-corrected]
\label{rmk:MNOP-corrected}
``DT $=$ categorical, GW $=$ geometric, MNOP $=$ partition-function match''
is to be parsed as: DT, GW, PT are three different \emph{framings} of
\emph{one} factorisation-homology trace on $\cF_X$. MNOP is a theorem
identifying two of the three trace specialisations after a change of
variables. Neither DT nor GW is specific to the categorical or the
hCS presentation; both are intrinsic to $\cF_X = \PhiFA_3(X)$. The
essay's wording is a compression and is structurally wrong as a
\emph{classification}: there is no ``categorical = DT'' dichotomy.
\end{remark}
\end{heal}

\section{Synthesis: the two inputs, their intersection, and the
Gelfand punchline}
\label{sec:synthesis}
% ==================================================================

The user's essay captures one correct theorem and three compressions.
The theorem:

\begin{theorem}[hCS and categorical Hochschild as input-asymmetric
presentations of $\PhiFA_3$]
\label{thm:main-synthesis}
\ClaimStatusScoped\ Let $X$ be a smooth complex threefold.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item The moduli of hCS input data $(X,\fg,\Omega_X)$ and the moduli
of CY$_3$ categorical input data $(\cC,\eta)$ are not equivalent;
there is a span of forgetful maps to the moduli of $\Ethree$
holomorphic factorisation algebras on $X$, with the hCS branch
selecting a framed gauge datum and the categorical branch carrying
Morita-invariant derived structure (Theorem~\ref{thm:span-of-inputs}).
\item When the two inputs are compatibly aligned
$(X,\fg,\Omega_X)\leftrightarrow(\Perf(X),V,\eta_{\Omega_X})$ with
$\fg = \End(V)$ and $V\in\Perf(X)$ a framing, the two presentations
produce quasi-isomorphic $\Ethree$-factorisation algebras, pinned up
to the $\GRTone$-torsor of KT formality associators, pinned further
to the Kontsevich point by the Costello--Li BV propagator
(Theorem~\ref{thm:torsor-point}).
\item Morita invariance on the categorical side erases the framing
data and makes visible the whole sheaf $R\End_\cC(-)$ on $\cC^{\sim}$;
the hCS side fixes one framing and sees the Lie algebra structure
constants of $\fg$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:yoneda-asymmetry}).
\item Three Atiyah-sourced cocycles --- generator $\At$, tree $Y_3$,
one-loop $\alpha_{\BCOV}$ --- live on three Hodge groups and enter
the presentations at three different orders; the user's ``formality
obstruction'' refers specifically to $\At$ as the Kapranov
$\ell_2$-bracket (Theorem~\ref{thm:three-cocycles}).
\item DT, GW, PT are three framings of one $\Ethree$-factorisation-homology
trace on $\PhiFA_3(X)$; MNOP is the change-of-variables identity between
two of the framings; the three framings are available in both
presentations (Theorem~\ref{thm:three-traces}).
\end{enumerate}
The user's slogan ``hCS is a geometric resolution of the $\Ethree$-
factorisation structure the CY$_3$ category possesses intrinsically up
to contractible choice'' is correct, under the refinements (i)--(v)
above: the ``contractible choice'' is $\GRTone$-torsor-contractible
(points in a prounipotent torsor differ by non-identity elements but
all map to one iso-class); the ``geometric resolution'' is the selection
of the Kontsevich point via the Costello--Li propagator; the
``intrinsic'' categorical structure lives on the derived endomorphism
sheaf $R\End_\cC(-)$ over $\cC^{\sim}$, with the hCS framing choosing
one fibre.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[The Gelfand punchline]
\label{rmk:gelfand-punchline}
Two inputs, one output. The two inputs are not two descriptions of
one input. Writing ``hCS = categorical = same thing'' is wrong;
writing ``hCS and categorical produce quasi-isomorphic $\Ethree$-algebras
on $X$ when compatibly framed'' is right. The difference between
``the same thing'' and ``quasi-isomorphic after compatible framing''
is the entire mathematical content of $\GRTone$-torsoriality, Yoneda
asymmetry, three Atiyah cocycles, and MNOP as trace identity.
Compression erases distinctions; mathematics requires every
distinction to be kept. The equals sign is a theorem. Preserve it.
\end{remark}
