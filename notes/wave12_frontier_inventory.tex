\begin{abstract}
A structured distillation of the open research frontier surviving the
wave-12 adversarial synthesis on the CY-to-chiral functor $\Phi:
\mathrm{CY}\text{-cat}_d \to \mathrm{ChirAlg}$. Each item is stated
as mathematics, scoped to its correct dimension and ambient, and
pointed to the wave-12 note in which its detailed treatment survives.
The inventory organises the frontier along five cross-sections:
the CY-A/B/C/D/H framework, the universal Borcherds weight identity
$\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$, structural conjectures, physics interweave,
and cross-volume bridges; it closes with single-sentence targets for
the next adversarial wave.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{CY-A completion items}

\paragraph{Chain-level $\Sp^{\mathrm{ch}}_{K3, E}$ beyond the existing
proposition.} The platonic-synthesis theorem furnishes
$\Sp^{\mathrm{ch}}_{K3, E}(\Phifa_3(K3 \times E)) \simeq
U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\fheis_{\mathrm{Muk}}) \otimes U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\fg^{\mathrm{BPS}}_{K3})$
as a stratum-$\mathrm{S}_3$ derivation combining Costello--Li 6d hCS on
the toric $\C^3$-chart with Dolbeault gluing. The chain-level
specialisation beyond a single reference curve --- a factorisation-homology
equation on $\mathrm{Ran}(E)$ pulling back the K3-factor
Maulik--Okounkov stable envelope to its $\Eone$-chiral shadow on
$E$ --- has not been written. Obstruction: the $E$-factor $\Eone$-extraction
on the rank-$24$ Mukai Heisenberg via Dunn--Lurie
$\int_{K3}\Ethree \simeq \Eone$ needs the global critical-chart
hypothesis, not automatic for every compact CY$_3$. See
\texttt{notes/wave12\_f5\_programme\_meta\_audit.tex} and
\texttt{notes/wave12\_u1\_two\_stage\_functor.tex}.

\paragraph{$S^3$-framing trivialisation on the quintic.} The
$S^3$-framing obstruction for a CY$_3$ category lives in
$\pi_3(B\mathrm{Sp})$ and vanishes on every smooth projective CY$_3$
for topological reasons; no explicit chain-level trivialisation of
the framing on $\Perf(Q^5)$ is known outside the toric setting. The
question is: write the trivialising $A_\infty$-twisting datum on the
derived endomorphism algebra of the structure sheaf of the quintic.
See \texttt{notes/wave12\_a3\_cy\_a3\_equivalence.tex}
(Open question: explicit chain-level framing on the quintic).

\paragraph{Inverse direction $\Omega_3$.} Under what hypotheses on
$\mathcal{H} \in \mathrm{Alg}_{\Eone}$ does there exist a CY$_3$
category $\mathcal{C}$ with $\Phi_3(\mathcal{C}) \simeq \mathcal{H}$?
This is CY-B$_3$ in disguise: the cobar functor $\Omega_3$ must land
back in $\mathrm{CY}_3\text{-cat}$. See
\texttt{notes/wave12\_a3\_cy\_a3\_equivalence.tex}.

\paragraph{Essential-image characterisation of $\Phi_3$.} Describe the
essential image of $\Phi_3$ inside $\mathrm{Alg}_{\Eone}$. The
working hypothesis: those $\Eone$-algebras admitting a compatible
factorisation structure on every curve, plus Kontsevich--Soibelman
wall-crossing flatness along the Stokes-stratification of the
stability-condition moduli. See
\texttt{notes/wave12\_a3\_cy\_a3\_equivalence.tex}.

\paragraph{Morphism preservation on compact non-product CY$_3$.} The
functor $\Phi_3$ is established as an $(\infty, 1)$-functor at the
object level; full morphism-level coherence (action on
Fourier--Mukai kernels with cycle-class lifts) is not stated in the
non-toric, non-product setting. Six-route convergence to
$G(K3 \times E)$ and the Super-Yangian bridge both assume this.
See \texttt{notes/wave12\_a3\_cy\_a3\_equivalence.tex} and
\texttt{notes/wave12\_a12\_six\_routes\_k3\_e.tex}.

See \texttt{notes/wave12\_a12\_six\_routes\_k3\_e.tex} and
\texttt{notes/wave12\_a3\_cy\_a3\_equivalence.tex}.

\paragraph{$\mathcal{Z}(\mathrm{Rep}^{\Eone}(A_X))$ computational

\texttt{notes/wave12\_f1\_6d\_hcs\_e3\_generators.tex}

\paragraph{Super-Yangian $Y(\fgl(4|20))$ explicit coproduct and

\subsection{CY-D at odd $d$}

\paragraph{Correct chain-level $\kch$ at odd $d$.} The naive
Hodge-supertrace identification $\kch(A_X) = \sum_q (-1)^q h^{0, q}(X)$
holds at $d = 2$ (e.g.~$K3 \to 2$) but fails at $d = 3$: on
$K3 \times E$ the Serre-involution cancellation forces $\kch = 0$ as
a Hodge supertrace, while the Heisenberg-level generator count
gives $\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}} = 3$. The correct chain-level formula is
$\kch = \mathrm{str}_{F^0}(q^{L_0})$ on the Hodge-filtered piece,
conjecturally reducible at $d = 3$ to a sum of $\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}$
contributions from each $E$-transverse $\Sigma_2$-fibre; the
statement needs a chain-level proof. See
\texttt{notes/wave12\_a5\_cy\_d\_stratification.tex}.

\paragraph{Extension beyond McKay to non-toric compact cases.} The
$\kch$-atlas is complete for toric local surfaces (via McKay
quiver) and for $K3 \times E$ (via Mukai). The compact quintic has
$\kch^{\mathrm{BCOV}} = -25/3$ from the one-loop BCOV weight but no
first-principles $\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}$. Non-compact non-toric
CY$_3$ (e.g.~generic local $K3$) has no direct McKay input. See
\texttt{notes/wave12\_a5\_cy\_d\_stratification.tex} (Open
questions 1--4).

\subsection{Lorgat 2020 Conjecture 1, sharpened}

The output $\Sp^{\mathrm{ch}}_{K3, E}(\mathcal{F}_{K3 \times E})
\simeq U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\fheis_{\mathrm{Muk}}) \otimes U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\fg^{\mathrm{BPS}}_{K3})$
is currently identified structurally; the sharpened form promotes
this to a chiral-bialgebra isomorphism on $E$ with
$\mathbf{H}_{\Delta_5}$, carrying character $\Delta_5^{-2}$ and
root multiplicities equal to $g_N$-twisted-twined K3 elliptic
genera. Five adversarial holes remain unhealed: the twisted
dimension $\dim S_5(\Sp_4(\Z), \nu_{\Delta_5}) = 1$ cite is
uncorroborated; the four-derivation convergence $C_1 = C_2 = C_3 = C_4$
at $c = -214$ is normalisation-matching-open; Oberdieck--Pixton is
explicit only at $N = 1$ primitive K3-classes; Cl\'ery--Gritsenko
2013 proves automorphy, not BKM-denominator structure; and
$H^3(\Mtf, U(1)) = \Z/12 \oplus \Z/2$ is computational, not theorem.
Post-wave-12 status: $(1, \mathrm{id})$ is theorem modulo holes 1--2;
rows $N \in \{2, 3, 4, 6\}$ are conjectural. See
\texttt{notes/wave12\_d2\_conj1\_adversarial.tex} and
\texttt{notes/wave12\_f5\_programme\_meta\_audit.tex} (shortest-path
frontier, Vol III).

\subsection{Chiral volume conjecture}

The asymptotic $\lim_{N \to \infty} (1/N) \log |Z_N(X, C)|
= (1/2\pi) |\AJ_X(C)|$, conditional on CY-A$_3$, identifies the
chiral-partition-function growth rate with the Abel--Jacobi period
of the reference curve $C \subset X$; the physical interpretation is
the on-shell action of 6d hCS on $\widetilde X = \mathrm{Tot}(\Omega^{3, 0}_X)$
with $C$ as source. Test at small $N \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ on the quintic
against the Picard--Fuchs period of $\Omega_X$ is open; large-$N$
uniformity near conifold points (where $\AJ$ develops logarithmic
singularities) is open; extension to local CY$_3$ replacing $\AJ$ by
the Mordell integral is open. See
\texttt{notes/wave12\_a14\_chiral\_volume.tex}.

\subsection{Class $\mathbf{M}$ $\Ethree$ bar $= 6^g$ beyond $g = 3$}

The identity $\dim H^\bullet(B_{\Ethree}(\mathcal{A}_\sigma); \Sigma_g)
= 6^g$ is theorem at $g \leq 3$ by direct tricomplex computation, and
conjectural at $g \geq 4$ pending evaluation of higher differentials
$d_{r \geq 5}$ on the $g = 4$ tricomplex of support width $5$. The
Gevrey-$1$ Borel resummation of the shadow tower predicts $6^g$ as
the summed value; the chain-level verification at $g = 4$ requires
computing $d_5$ explicitly. An $\Mtf$-equivariant refinement at
every $g$ and a Kauffman-skein-module dictionary are both open. See
\texttt{notes/wave12\_a10\_class\_m\_e3\_bar.tex} (Open questions).

\subsection{Line and surface operators}

The 6d analogue of Costello--Witten 4d hCS, which furnishes the
affine Yangian structure from a line operator, is not written. Line
operators on 6d hCS supported on $(0, 1)$-curves transverse to
generic slicing should carry Chern--Simons--Witten-type central
charges; the Yangian target of 4d hCS line operators becomes a
Super-Yangian target at 6d. Surface operators on $S \subset X$ should
align their $\Eone$-shadow with the Maulik--Okounkov $R$-matrix of
$S$; transverse-direction comparison between OPE-level surface data
and MO-stable-envelope cohomology is open. See
\texttt{notes/wave12\_f1\_6d\_hcs\_e3\_generators.tex} (items
Q4--Q5).

\subsection{Holographic dual}

The 6d holomorphic factorisation avatar $\Phifa_d$ should sit on the
asymptotic boundary of a $(d + 1)$-real-dimensional twisted
M-theory in the sense of Costello--Gaiotto; the holographic pairing
$(\Phifa_d)_{\mathrm{bdy}} \to Z_{\mathrm{bulk}}$ at
$d = 3$ carries the Borcherds Monster $\Phi_{10}^{1/2} = \Delta_5$
through the $(1+1+1) = 3$ sector-split of the heterotic worldsheet
matter central charge $c = 24$. The AdS$_3 \times S^3 \times K3$
microstate-counting reading of $\kBKM(\Phi_1) = 5$ as a BPS symmetry
of the Strominger--Vafa black hole is conjectural; the
higher-$\lambda$ Gaberdiel--Gopakumar vector-model collapse
$\lambda \to 1$ at large $N$ is open. See
\texttt{notes/wave12\_d4\_holographic\_AdS3.tex} and
\texttt{notes/wave12\_d5\_quantum\_gravity\_BH.tex}.
