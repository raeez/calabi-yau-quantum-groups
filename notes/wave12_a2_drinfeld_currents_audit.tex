\subsection{The current theorem}

Let
\[
  \mathbb F=\mathbb C(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2),\qquad
  \epsilon_3=-\epsilon_1-\epsilon_2 .
\]
The cohomological shuffle--current theorem used here is Tsymbaliuk's
presentation of the affine Yangian of \(\gghone\), together with the
Schiffmann--Vasserot shuffle algebra.  Arbesfeld's 2018 theorem is a
\(K\)-theoretic surface-Hall presentation with wheel relations.  The
cohomological Drinfeld-current isomorphism used here is Tsymbaliuk's.

\begin{theorem}[Shuffle double and Drinfeld currents]
\label{thm:drinfeld-current-shuffle-double}
Let
\[
  \Sh^+=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}\mathbb F[z_1,\ldots,z_n]^{S_n}
\]
with shuffle product defined by the kernel
\[
  \omega(z,w)
  =
  \frac{(z-w-\epsilon_1)(z-w-\epsilon_2)(z-w-\epsilon_3)}
       {(z-w)^3}.
\]
After the standard completion and Cartan extension, the Drinfeld
double
\[
  D(\Sh^+)=\Sh^-\bowtie\Sh^0\bowtie\Sh^+
\]
is isomorphic to \(Y(\gghone)\).  In Tsymbaliuk's current
normalisation the isomorphism
\[
  \Theta:Y(\gghone)\xrightarrow{\sim}D(\Sh^+)
\]
sends the degree-one current modes to
\[
  \Theta(e_k)=z^k\in\Lam_1,\qquad
  \Theta(f_k)=z^k\in\Lam_1^{\mathrm{op}},
\]
where \(\Lam_1=\mathbb F[z]\) is the one-variable shuffle component.
The image of the Cartan mode \(\psi_k\) is the corresponding Cartan
polynomial determined by the Hopf pairing of \(\Sh^+\) and
\(\Sh^-\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Schiffmann--Vasserot construct the positive shuffle algebra and its
Cartan extension.  Tsymbaliuk identifies the completed double with the
Drinfeld-current presentation of \(Y(\gghone)\).  The assignment
\(e_k\mapsto z^k\) is in shuffle degree \(1\); the unit component
\(\Lam_0=\mathbb F\) maps to the algebra unit, not to \(e_0\).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Low current brackets}

The loop index and the shuffle degree are independent:
\[
  e_k\in Y(\gghone)\quad\longmapsto\quad z^k\in\Lam_1 .
\]
Products \(e_{k_1}\cdots e_{k_n}\) live in shuffle degree \(n\) after
multiplication.  The mode \(e_0\) is therefore the constant polynomial
in the one-variable component.  The shuffle unit lies in
\(\Lam_0=\mathbb F\).

With Tsymbaliuk's mode normalisation,
\[
  [e_i,f_j]=\psi_{i+j},\qquad [\psi_i,\psi_j]=0 .
\]
The first brackets are
\[
  [e_0,f_0]=\psi_0,\qquad [e_0,f_1]=\psi_1 .
\]
If logarithmic Cartan coordinates \(h_r\) are chosen by
\[
  \psi(z)=\exp\!\left(\sum_{r\geq0}h_r z^{-r-1}\right),
\]
then
\[
  \psi_0=h_0,\qquad
  \psi_1=h_1+\frac{1}{2}h_0^2 .
\]
On the shuffle side the same brackets are the degree-one
Drinfeld-double commutators
\[
  [1_{\Lam_1},1_{\Lam_1^{\mathrm{op}}}]=\psi_0,\qquad
  [1_{\Lam_1},z_{\Lam_1^{\mathrm{op}}}]=\psi_1 ,
\]
with the normalisation fixed by the Schiffmann--Vasserot Hopf pairing.
No unnormalised residue computation at \(z=0\) supplies this
normalisation by itself.

\subsection{Positive half, double, and chiral level}

The toric Calabi--Yau threefold statement is
\[
  \CoHA(\mathbb C^3)\simeq
  Y^+_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3}(\gghone)
\]
as a localised positive shuffle algebra.  The Chevalley involution,
negative half, Cartan half, coproduct, and \(R\)-matrix belong to the
completed double \(D(Y^+)\).  For a Calabi--Yau input of dimension
\(d\geq3\), the specialised chiral output is \(E_1\).  An
\(E_2\)-structure is present on a
constructed centre, double, or representation category with the
relevant hypotheses.

\subsection{K3 branch separation}

The theorem above concerns the toric \(\mathbb C^3\) affine-Yangian
model.  The \(K3\times E\) BKM branch is the completed Hall--Drinfeld
double
\[
  \mathcal D_\hbar(\CoHA_{K3\times E})
\]
with Borcherds denominator data from \(\Delta_5\).  The Mukai
self-mirror Yangian branch is a separate stable-envelope construction
on K3 Hilbert-scheme towers.

At the abelian Mukai-current level, positive Heisenberg generators
commute:
\[
  [J_a^+,J_b^+]_{\mathrm{Lie}}=0 .
\]
The vertex algebra generated by the full modes has the Heisenberg
\(\lambda\)-bracket
\[
  [J_a{}_\lambda J_b]=(a,b)_{\mathrm{Muk}}\lambda\,\mathbf 1 ,
\]
equivalently
\[
  [J_a(m),J_b(n)]
  =
  m\,(a,b)_{\mathrm{Muk}}\delta_{m+n,0}K .
\]
This central vertex term belongs to the Heisenberg vertex algebra.  The
imaginary-root brackets of \(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5}\) and the BKM branch
remain separate Borcherds data.

\subsection{Low-mode comparison}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{l|l}
\textbf{Drinfeld current mode} & \textbf{Shuffle image under \(\Theta\)} \\\hline
\(e_0\) & \(1\in\Lam_1\) \\
\(e_1\) & \(z\in\Lam_1\) \\
\(e_k\) & \(z^k\in\Lam_1\) \\
\(f_0\) & \(1\in\Lam_1^{\mathrm{op}}\) \\
\([e_0,f_0]\) & \(\psi_0\) \\
\([e_0,f_1]\) & \(\psi_1=h_1+\tfrac12h_0^2\) in logarithmic Cartan coordinates
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Thus the current presentation, shuffle degree, and K3 branch
separation are compatible only after the positive half, completed
double, and vertex-level central extension are kept distinct.
