\section*{Automorphic DT Data and Typed Chiral Boundaries}
\label{sec:automorphic-dt-coha-boundary}

\paragraph{Reduced DT scalar.}
Let
\[
 X_N=(S\times E)/(\Z/N\Z),
\]
where the generator acts on \(S\) by a symplectic automorphism of order
\(N\) and on \(E\) by translation by an \(N\)-torsion point.  For an
elliptically fibred K3 surface with \(N\)-torsion sections, write
\[
 \beta_h=\frac{1}{N}(s_1+\cdots+s_N+hF).
\]
The reduced enumerative series is the Behrend-weighted Hilbert-scheme
integral
\[
 Z^X(q,t,p)
 =\sum_{h,d,n}\DT^X_{n,(\beta_h,d)}\,
 q^{d-1}t^{\frac12\langle\beta_h,\beta_h\rangle}(-p)^n,
\qquad
 \DT^X_{n,(\beta_h,d)}
 =\int_{\mathrm{Hilb}^n(X,\beta_h)/E}\nu\,de .
\]
This branch ends in scalar reduced \(\DT\) invariants.  A Hall product
requires extension correspondences, orientation data for vanishing cycles,
and a completion compatible with Harder-Narasimhan filtrations.  A vertex
algebra requires a vacuum, translation, locality, and state-field
correspondence.  These structures are extra data beyond the scalar
integration above.

\paragraph{MNOP and the Igusa scalar.}
The reduced \(\GW/\DT\) comparison is the scalar \(\MNOP\) branch:
\[
 \GW^{\mathrm{red}}_{g,\beta,d}(K3\times E)
 \;\longleftrightarrow\;
 \DT^{\mathrm{red}}_{n,\beta,d}(K3\times E)
\]
after the reduced obstruction theory, the quotient by the \(E\)-action,
and the usual partition-function change of variables.  In the
Oberdieck-Pixton and Oberdieck-Pandharipande normalization,
\[
 Z^{K3\times E}_{\mathrm{OP/DT}}
 =-D_5^{-2}
 =-4096\,\Delta_5^{-2},
\qquad
 D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5 .
\]
The factor \(64\) is the theta-leading-coefficient conversion from the
monic Borcherds product to the Igusa square-root normalization.

\paragraph{Borcherds and Igusa denominator data.}
Use the Eichler-Zagier normalization
\[
 \phi_{0,1}^{K3}(\tau,z)=r^{-1}+10+r+O(q).
\]
Its discriminant-zero constant is
\[
 c_1(0)=[q^0r^0]\phi_{0,1}^{K3}=10 .
\]
Borcherds' singular theta lift and the Gritsenko-Nikulin denominator give
\[
 \phi_{0,1}^{K3}
 \xrightarrow{\operatorname{Bor}}
 \Delta_5,\qquad
 \kBKM(\Delta_5)=\mathrm{wt}(\Delta_5)=\frac{c_1(0)}{2}=5 .
\]
The Igusa square convention is
\[
 \Phi_{10}=\Delta_5^2 .
\]
The primitive CHL denominator row is
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
N&1&2&3&4&6\\ \hline
c_N(0)&10&8&6&4&2\\
\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2&5&4&3&2&1
\end{array}
\]
with the level-one entry \(\Phi_1=\Delta_5\).  The row is a Borcherds
weight statement.  A sum relation with a chiral invariant or a fibre
Euler characteristic would require a natural transformation between
different constructions; the displayed maps supply none.

\paragraph{\texorpdfstring{$K3\times E$}{K3 x E} numerical layers.}
For \(X=K3\times E\), K\"unneth gives
\[
 \kcat(X)=\chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot0=0.
\]
The compact Hodge column is \(h^{0,\bullet}(X)=(1,1,1,1)\), hence
\[
 \kch(A_X)=\sum_{q=0}^{3}(-1)^qh^{0,q}(X)=1-1+1-1=0.
\]
The Heisenberg-Mukai specialization and the Borcherds denominator read
\[
 \kHeis(X)=2+1=3,\qquad
 \kBKM(\Delta_5)=5,\qquad
 \kfib(X)=\operatorname{rk}\widetilde H(K3,\Z)=24 .
\]
Thus
\[
 \{\kcat(X),\kch(A_X),\kHeis(X),
   \kBKM(\Delta_5),\kfib(X)\}
 =
 \{0,0,3,5,24\},
\]
with the two zeroes distinguished by construction.  The K3 fibre value
\(\chi(\cO_{K3})=2\) belongs to the fibre computation; the compact
total-space rows have value zero.

\paragraph{Class-\texorpdfstring{$\cS$}{S} Schur corner.}
The relevant four-dimensional parent is
\[
 \cT_{24}:=\cT[A_1,\Sigma_{0,24}],
\]
where \(\Sigma_{0,24}\) is a sphere with twenty-four maximal regular
\(\mathfrak{su}(2)\)-punctures.  A pants decomposition has \(22\)
trinions and \(21\) gauged \(SU(2)\) tubes, so
\[
 (n_v,n_h)=(63,88),\qquad
 c_{4d}=\frac{2n_v+n_h}{12}=\frac{107}{6},\qquad
 c_{2d}=-12c_{4d}=-214 .
\]
The closed genus-two curve gives the separate datum
\[
 \Sigma_{2,0}:\qquad (n_v,n_h)=(9,8),\qquad c_{4d}=\frac{13}{6}.
\]
The Schur-to-Igusa comparison has the typed form
\[
 \chi(\cT_{24})
 \longrightarrow
 \cI_{\mathrm{Schur}}(\cT_{24})
 \xrightarrow{\Pi_{M_{24}}}
 \phi_{0,1}^{K3}
 \xrightarrow{\operatorname{Bor}}
 \Delta_5
 \rightsquigarrow
 \fg_{\Delta_5}.
\]
Here \(\Pi_{M_{24}}\) denotes the \(M_{24}\)-equivariant projection after
marking the twenty-four punctures by the K3 fibre labels.  The first
arrow is the Beem-Lemos-Liendo-Peelaers-Rastelli-van Rees Schur functor
from a four-dimensional \(\mathcal N=2\) theory to its vertex algebra and
protected character.  The final arrow is the Borcherds denominator
recognition of the generalized Borcherds-Kac-Moody superalgebra.  A
VOA-level isomorphism between \(\chi(\cT_{24})\) and a denominator
algebra requires an explicit BRST or centre reduction, matching fields,
OPEs, and stress tensor.

\paragraph{Local Hall half and evaluation layer.}
For the one-vertex three-loop quiver with potential
\[
 W=\operatorname{tr}(X[Y,Z]),
\]
the critical algebra has Jacobi algebra
\[
 \C\langle X,Y,Z\rangle/([X,Y],[Y,Z],[Z,X])\cong\C[X,Y,Z].
\]
Over the Calabi-Yau equivariant torus
\[
 \epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+\epsilon_3=0,
\]
Schiffmann-Vasserot identify the toric critical CoHA with the positive
half of the affine Yangian:
\[
 \CoHA_{T^{\mathrm{CY}}}(\C^3)\cong Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1).
\]
The vertex-algebra layer is reached through the completed double and an
evaluation representation:
\[
 Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
 \hookrightarrow
 D_{\mathrm{Drin}}\bigl(Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)\bigr)
 =Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{ev}_\lambda}
\operatorname{End}\bigl(\Winf[\lambda]\text{-}\mathrm{vac}\bigr).
\]
Equivalently, after the Hall pairing and completion, the centre layer is
\[
 Z\bigl(\mathrm{Rep}^{E_1}(Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1))\bigr)
 \simeq
 \mathrm{Rep}^{E_2}\bigl(Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)\bigr).
\]
The positive Hall half supplies the creation current sector.  The
negative half, Cartan currents, completion, central character, vacuum
module, and state-field correspondence supply the full
\(\Winf[\lambda]\) environment.

\paragraph{\texorpdfstring{$\Phi_d$}{Phi-d} output level.}
The CY-to-chiral construction is parameterized by a specialization datum:
\[
 \Phi_d^{(\Sigma_{d-1},C)}
 =
 \Sp_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ\Phi_d^{\mathrm{FA}}.
\]
The native operadic level is
\[
 n_{\mathrm{native}}(d)=
 \begin{cases}
 \infty,& d=1,\\
 2,& d=2,\\
 1,& d\geq3.
 \end{cases}
\]
For \(d\geq3\), the output \(A_X=\Phi_d^{(\Sigma_{d-1},C)}(X)\) is
\(E_1\)-chiral.  Braiding appears on named centre, double, or module
layers such as
\[
 Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A_X),\qquad
 D(\CoHA),\qquad
 Z(\mathrm{Rep}^{E_1}(A_X)).
\]

\paragraph{Two branch sources.}
The \(K3\times E\) Borcherds branch has source
\[
 \CoHA(K3\times E)^+
 \longrightarrow
 \mathrm{Prim}
 \longrightarrow
 \fg_{\Delta_5}
 \longrightarrow
 U_{\mathrm{ch}}(\fg_{\Delta_5}),
\]
after the compact positive Hall half, orientation data, primitive
projection, radical quotient, PBW comparison, nondegenerate Hall pairing,
and completion have been supplied.  The phrase K3 Yangian is reserved for
the separate Mukai self-mirror branch governed by the rank-\(24\) Mukai
current algebra and by stable-envelope/Yangian comparison data on the K3
surface side.

\paragraph{Compact Hall-Borcherds recognition data.}
The bridge from reduced \(K3\times E\) scalars to a compact Hall-Borcherds
object requires the following mathematical inputs:
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep2pt
  \item an extension-closed compact Hall or critical-CoHA sector for the
  reduced \(E\)-quotient;
  \item oriented critical extension correspondences and vanishing-cycle
  coefficient systems;
  \item Harder-Narasimhan finite completions and protected integration
  compatible with the completed Hall product and wall crossing;
  \item a primitive projection with
  \(\mathrm{sdim}\,\mathrm{Prim}_{(n,l,m)}=f(nm,l)\);
  \item the Borcherds-Gritsenko-Nikulin bracket, radical quotient,
  no-extra-relations check, and PBW comparison;
  \item a nondegenerate Hall pairing producing the completed
  Hall-Drinfeld double;
  \item centre, completion, vacuum, and evaluation data before a
  \(\Winf\)-type vertex algebra is asserted.
\end{enumerate}
