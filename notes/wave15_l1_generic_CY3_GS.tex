\section{Green--Schwarz data for compact CY$_3$ holomorphic
Chern--Simons}
\label{sec:generic-cy3-GS}

\subsection{Compact invariants}

Let \(X\) be a compact Calabi--Yau threefold with holomorphic volume
form \(\Omega_X\), and let \(\gfrak\) be the structure Lie algebra of
holomorphic Chern--Simons theory.  The classical field strength is
\[
  F_\cA=\bar\partial\cA+\frac{1}{2}[\cA,\cA].
\]
The compact Hodge-column invariant is
\[
  \kcat(X)=\chi(\cO_X),\qquad
  \kch(A_X)=\sum_{q=0}^{3}(-1)^q h^{0,q}(X).
\]
Serre duality gives \(h^{0,q}=h^{0,3-q}\), hence
\[
  \kcat(X)=\kch(A_X)=0
\]
for every compact Calabi--Yau threefold.  In the strict holonomy
stratum this reads
\[
  h^{0,0}=h^{0,3}=1,\qquad h^{0,1}=h^{0,2}=0,
\]
inside the same cancellation.
The topological Euler number
\[
  \chitop(X)=\int_X c_3(TX)
\]
belongs to degree-zero Donaldson--Thomas factors and to the \(\BCOV\)
constant-map sector.  It supplies a topological scalar, while the
holomorphic Chern--Simons obstruction below lives in local BV
cohomology.

For the two reference geometries,
\[
  \chitop(K3\times E)=\chitop(K3)\chitop(E)=24\cdot 0=0,\qquad
  \chitop(X_5)=-200
\]
for the smooth quintic \(X_5\subset \mathbb P^4\).  The \(K3\times E\)
values split by construction:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{construction} & \text{invariant} & \text{value}\\ \hline
\text{compact categorical} & \kcat(K3\times E) & 0\\
\text{compact Hodge-column} & \kch(A_{K3\times E}) & 0\\
\text{Heisenberg--Mukai witness} &
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E) & 3\\
\text{Borcherds denominator} &
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=\frac{c_1(0)}{2} & 5\\
\text{K3 fibre Mukai rank} & \kfiber(K3\subset K3\times E) & 24
\end{array}
\]
The quintic has the compact values \(\kcat(X_5)=\kch(A_{X_5})=0\)
and lacks a supplied Borcherds input in this note.

\subsection{The hCS QME anomaly}

Compact holomorphic Chern--Simons quantisation is a scale-dependent
Costello--Gwilliam BV package:
\[
  \bigl(I[L],P_{\epsilon<L},W(P_{\epsilon<L},I[\epsilon]),\QME_L\bigr),
  \qquad
  \bigl(Q+\hbar\Delta_L\bigr)e^{I[L]/\hbar}=0.
\]
The first obstruction is a local BV cohomology class
\[
  \alpha_{\hCS}(X,\gfrak,\rho)
  \in
  H^1_{\mathrm{loc}}
  \bigl(\Omega^{0,\bullet}(X,\gfrak)[1],\cO_{\mathrm{loc}}\bigr),
\]
with \(\rho\) the chosen representation used in the trace.  In complex
dimension \(3\) the one-loop wheel has four external gauge insertions.
Its Lie factor is the quartic invariant
\[
  I_4^\rho(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)
  =
  \frac{1}{4!}\sum_{\sigma\in S_4}
  \Tr_\rho\!\bigl(
    \rho(x_{\sigma(1)})\rho(x_{\sigma(2)})
    \rho(x_{\sigma(3)})\rho(x_{\sigma(4)})
  \bigr)
  \in \mathrm{Sym}^4(\gfrak^\vee)^{\gfrak}.
\]
On a holomorphic coordinate polydisk \(U\), the representing cocycle is
\[
  \Theta^\rho_U(\alpha_0,\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3)
  =
  \int_U
  \left[
    I_4^\rho(
      \alpha_0,\partial\alpha_1,\partial\alpha_2,\partial\alpha_3
    )
  \right]_{(3,3)} .
\]
Thus the hCS \(\QME\) anomaly in complex dimension \(3\) is quartic.
Degree-two Lie contractions contribute kinetic counterterms; they have
cohomological degree zero and arity two, while \(\Theta^\rho\) has
degree one and arity four.

\subsection{Green--Schwarz primitive}

A Green--Schwarz datum for compact CY$_3$ holomorphic Chern--Simons is
a closed-sector local functional primitive together with the descent
data on which it is defined:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a \(\TCFT\) background fixing the open and closed state spaces;
\item a solution of the scale-\(L\) \(\QME\) through the relevant loop
  order;
\item a closed-sector local functional \(S_{\mathrm{GS}}\) in the
  completed BV complex satisfying
  \[
    d_{\mathrm{BV}}S_{\mathrm{GS}}
    =
    -\alpha_{\hCS}(X,\gfrak,\rho);
  \]
\item a descent identity
  \[
    dH =
    I_4^\rho(\cA)-I_{\mathrm{grav}}(TX)+d_{\mathrm{loc}}\Lambda
  \]
  in the local descent complex;
\item orientation and framing data, including the square root of the
  determinant line on the derived moduli carrying vanishing cycles;
\item sewing maps, counterterms, and a completed state space on which
  the RG flow and descent operators converge.
\end{enumerate}
The Green--Schwarz mechanism cancels the compact hCS anomaly exactly
when this primitive exists.  A characteristic-class equality supplies
input to the descent problem; the local BV primitive, orientation,
framing, sewing, counterterms, and completion are extra construction
data.

\subsection{BCOV sector}

The \(\BCOV\) theory is the closed Kodaira--Spencer sector.  Its genus
expansion and constant-map terms depend on the Todd-class and Euler
data of \(X\), with the compact CY$_3$ scalar \(\chitop(X)/24\) as the
standard normalisation.  For \(K3\times E\), this scalar vanishes.  For
the quintic, it is \(-200/24=-25/3\).  This \(\BCOV\) number is a
closed-string contribution; a compact hCS quantisation still requires
the \(\QME\), anomaly primitive, orientation, framing, descent,
sewing, counterterms, and completion listed above.

\subsection{Hall, CoHA, and BKM sectors}

The toric local model separates the Hall algebra from the vertex
algebra evaluation target:
\[
  \CoHA(\CC^3)=\Yplus(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
\]
as an associative \(\Eone\) Hall algebra.  The full
\(\cW_{1+\infty}\) object appears after Drinfeld double, centre,
completion, and vacuum evaluation:
\[
  \Yplus(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
  \hookrightarrow
  Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
  \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{ev}_{\lambda}\ }
  \mathrm{End}\bigl(\cW_{1+\infty}[\lambda]\text{-}\mathrm{vac}\bigr).
\]

The \(K3\times E\) Borcherds branch is another construction.  Its
weight is the denominator-formula constant
\[
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=\frac{c_1(0)}{2}=5,
\]
coming from Gritsenko's \(\Delta_5\) input.  This automorphic datum is
independent of the compact hCS Green--Schwarz primitive and of the
compact Hodge-column value \(\kch(A_{K3\times E})=0\).

\subsection{The chiral output at dimension three}

The \(d=3\) CY-to-chiral output is a two-stage construction.  The
canonical factorisation-algebra stage is \(\PhiFA_3(\mathrm{Perf}(X))\).
After a specialisation datum \((\Sigma_2,C)\) has been supplied, the
chain-level object is
\[
  A_X^{(\Sigma_2,C)}
  =
  \SpCh_{\Sigma_2,C}\bigl(\PhiFA_3(\mathrm{Perf}(X))\bigr).
\]
Its native operadic level on the reference curve is \(\Eone\):
\[
  E_3 \longmapsto E_{3-2}=E_1.
\]
The braided \(\Etwo\) structure belongs to enhancement layers such as
\[
  \cZ\bigl(\Rep^{\Eone}(A_X^{(\Sigma_2,C)})\bigr),
  \qquad
  D(\Yplus),
\]
once the required Hopf pairing, double, completion, and module data are
present.

\subsection{Generic compact CY$_3$ criterion}

A compact/global hCS construction on a generic compact CY$_3$ is
conditional in the absence of the following tuple:
\[
  \bigl(
  \TCFT\text{ background},
  \QME\text{ solution},
  \alpha_{\hCS}\text{ primitive},
  \text{orientation/framing},
  \text{descent/sewing},
  \text{counterterms},
  \text{completion}
  \bigr).
\]
The scalar identities \(\chi(\cO_X)=0\) and \(\kch(A_X)=0\) decide the
compact Hodge-column invariant.  They leave the quartic local BV
anomaly, the \(\BCOV\) closed sector, the Hall positive half, the BKM
denominator data, and the \(\Eone\) chiral output as separate
construction problems.

For \(K3\times E\), the product geometry supplies \(\chitop=0\), the
compact values \(\kcat(K3\times E)=\kch(A_{K3\times E})=0\), the
Heisenberg witness \(\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)=3\), the
Borcherds weight \(\kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=5\), and the fibre
rank \(\kfiber(K3\subset K3\times E)=24\).  For the quintic and for a
generic compact complete-intersection CY$_3\), the compact
hCS-to-chiral assertion
requires the tuple above.  A tangent-bundle or CHSW-type embedding
supplies characteristic-class descent input; the local BV primitive and
the analytic completion remain part of the construction.
