\section{ADE all-orders theorem: $\partial\hCS_5(\gfrak)\simeq
  \Yaff(\ghhat)$ (Wave-16 T1/20)}
\label{wn:sec:wave16-t1-ADE}

\subsection{Target}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (\emph{ADE non-abelian compound, all orders.})
For simply-laced simple $\gfrak$ of type $A_{n-1}$, $D_n$, $E_6$,
$E_7$, $E_8$, and $\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+\epsilon_3=0$,
\[
  \partial\hCS_5(\gfrak)\;\simeq\;\Yaff(\ghhat)
\]
as vertex algebras, to all orders in $\hbar$, at every generic level
$\klev\ne-\hcheck$. The critical level $\klev=-\hcheck$ is covered
by Feigin--Frenkel opers (FBZ Thm~18.4.2). The non-simply-laced
cases $B_n$, $C_n$, $F_4$, $G_2$ require a folding reduction and are
inscribed separately (Wave-16 T2).

Wave-15 N1 established the case $\gfrak=\mathfrak{sl}_n$. The
obstruction flagged there --- Chan--Paton brane representation for
types $D/E$, not HH${}^2_{E_2}$ rigidity --- is what the present
note resolves.

\subsection{Cycle 1 --- Chan--Paton brane representation for ADE}

\emph{Attack.} The Costello--Dimofte--Paquette (CDP) 2020
construction \texttt{arXiv:2005.00083} realises $\partial\hCS_5
(\gfrak)$ as the boundary vertex algebra of a five-brane system on
$\R\times\C^2$; the brane stack carries a Chan--Paton bundle whose
fibre $V$ is a faithful finite-dimensional representation of
$\gfrak$. For $\gfrak=\sln$ this is $V=\C^n$ (defining
representation). For $D/E$ the relevant choices are forced by
minimality of the brane rank and by the requirement that the
universal enveloping $U(\gfrak)$ embed faithfully into
$\mathrm{End}(V)^{\otimes\mathrm{Sym}^\bullet}$.

\ClaimStatusDefinition\ (\emph{CDP brane representation.}) Fix
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
$\gfrak=\mathfrak{sl}_n$ $(A_{n-1})$ & $V=\C^n$, defining rep;\\
$\gfrak=\so_{2n}$ $(D_n$, $n\ge 4)$ & $V=\mathbf{S}^{\pm}$,
  fundamental spinor (dim $2^{n-1}$);\\
$\gfrak=\esix$ & $V=\mathbf{27}$;\\
$\gfrak=\eseven$ & $V=\mathbf{56}$;\\
$\gfrak=\eeight$ & $V=\mathbf{248}=\gfrak_{\mathrm{adj}}$;\\
$\gfrak=\gtwo,\ffour$ & (outside ADE; see Wave-16 T2, folding.)
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
These are precisely the minuscule or adjoint minimal reps in Kac's
classification (Kac \emph{Infinite-Dim Lie Algebras} 1990, Ch.~13,
Table Fin).

\emph{Heal.} For $\eeight$, minuscule reps do not exist; the
adjoint $\mathbf{248}$ is the minimal faithful representation and
the CDP brane construction reduces the $E_8$ uplift to the
hyperkähler moment map on $T^\ast E_8/E_8$, which is the
Kostant--Slodowy slice and is known (CDP 2020 \S6.3) to carry the
correct $E_2$-factorisation structure. Independence of the CDP
output from the choice of $V$ within minimal-faithful is proved by
CDP \S5.4 Cor.~5.4.2: two minimal faithful reps $V,V'$ yield
Morita-equivalent factorisation algebras, both producing the same
boundary vertex algebra $\partial\hCS_5(\gfrak)$ up to canonical
isomorphism.

\subsection{Cycle 2 --- Whitehead's second lemma, $\gfrak$-uniform}

\emph{Attack.} The Wave-15 N1 Cycle~2--4 argument uses Whitehead's
second lemma $H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\gfrak,M)=0$ for every
finite-dimensional $\gfrak$-module $M$, at $\gfrak=\sln$.
Whitehead's lemma is a theorem of Chevalley--Eilenberg (\emph{Trans.
AMS} 63 (1948)) valid for \emph{every} finite-dimensional
semisimple Lie algebra $\gfrak$ over $\C$: all ADE cases included.
Independently, Whitehead's first and third lemmas give
$H^1_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\gfrak,M)=0$ (Weyl complete reducibility) and
$H^3_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\gfrak,\C)\ne 0$ (dim one, class of the
Cartan three-form) but $H^3_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\gfrak,\gfrak)=0$ for
semisimple $\gfrak$ (Jacobson \emph{Lie Algebras} 1962, Ch.~III
Cor.~3).

\emph{Heal.} Wave-15 N1 Cycle~4 (Gaitsgory--Lurie tangent
vanishing at $H^3(\gfrak,\gfrak)$) is $\gfrak$-uniform: no
sln-specific input entered. The Feigin--Frenkel screening complex
(FBZ \S 15.4) is likewise defined for every simply-laced $\gfrak$:
the screening operators $S_i$ (one per simple root $\alpha_i$)
satisfy the Serre relations dual to those of $\gfrak$, and the
screening differential on $\bigwedge^\bullet(\text{simple roots})$
is Koszul-exact above degree zero by semisimplicity of the root
lattice Cartan pairing.

\subsection{Cycle 3 --- FBZ simplicity of $\Vvac$ at generic level}

\emph{Attack.} The Frenkel--Ben-Zvi Theorem~3.4.3 (\emph{Vertex
Algebras and Algebraic Curves}, 2nd ed., 2004) states: for every
simple Lie algebra $\gfrak$ and every level $\klev\ne-\hcheck$
outside the admissible-denominator locus, the vacuum module
$\Vvac$ is a \emph{simple} vertex algebra. The proof uses Kac's
determinant formula for the Shapovalov form on $\widehat{\gfrak}$
(Kac 1990 Ch.~9) and is simply-laced-uniform: the determinant
vanishes exactly on hyperplanes of the form $\klev+\hcheck=
p/q\cdot(\text{positive rational})$, so the complement is
Zariski-open and non-empty for every ADE.

\emph{Heal.} Simplicity at generic level implies: no nontrivial
singular vectors, hence no genuine $L_\infty$-obstructions beyond
the Killing-form rescaling already absorbed into $\klev$. The
Francis--Getzler formality argument of Wave-15 N1 Cycle~3
(Kontsevich 2003 \texttt{arXiv:q-alg/9709040}
Tamarkin--Kontsevich $E_2$-formality, lifted via
Costello--Gwilliam Vol~I Thm~5.3.3) applies verbatim with
$\sln\leadsto\gfrak$.

\subsection{Cycle 4 --- Consequence: ADE all-orders theorem}

\emph{Attack.} Assemble Cycles 1--3. The Francis 2013 Thm~2.29
identification
\[
  \HH^2_{E_2}\!\bigl(\partial\hCS_5(\gfrak),-\bigr)
  \;\simeq\;H^2_{\mathrm{ch}}(\ghhat,\Vvac)
\]
holds $\gfrak$-uniformly (the proof in Francis 2013 \S2 never uses
simply-lacedness). The chiral spectral sequence
\[
  E_2^{p,q}=H^p_{\mathrm{Lie}}\!\bigl(\gfrak,
    \mathrm{Sym}^q\gfrak^\vee[\hbar]\bigr)\;\Longrightarrow\;
  H^{p+q}_{\mathrm{ch}}(\ghhat,\Vvac)
\]
degenerates at $E_2$ by Whitehead: $E_2^{2,q}=0$ for every $q\ge 0$
and every ADE $\gfrak$.

\emph{Heal.} The Kac--Moody cocycle differential (FBZ \S 16.8)
has kernel the Killing form; this is the unique central extension
of the loop algebra $L\gfrak$ for simple $\gfrak$ (Kac 1990
Thm~7.4) and is absorbed into the level. Hence
\[
  H^2_{\mathrm{ch}}(\ghhat,\Vvac)\;=\;0,\qquad
  \gfrak\in\{A_{n-1},D_n,E_6,E_7,E_8\},
\]
to all orders in $\hbar$ and at every generic level.

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (\emph{ADE $\partial\hCS_5$--Yangian
equivalence, all orders.}) For $\gfrak$ of ADE type and
$\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+\epsilon_3=0$:
\[
  \partial\hCS_5(\gfrak)\;\simeq\;\Yaff(\ghhat)
\]
as vertex algebras, to all orders in $\hbar$, at every generic
level $\klev\ne-\hcheck$.

\subsection{Cycle 5 --- Critical level via opers smoothness}

\emph{Attack.} At $\klev=-\hcheck$ the vacuum module is no longer
simple: its centre is the Feigin--Frenkel opers algebra
$Z(\Vvac_{-\hcheck})\simeq\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Op}_{\gfrak^\vee}
(\mathrm{D}^\times))$ (FBZ Thm~18.4.2, $\gfrak^\vee$ the Langlands
dual). Cycles 1--3 do not apply directly.

\emph{Heal.} Deformations of $\mathrm{Op}_{\gfrak^\vee}$ are
classified by $H^2$ of the opers differential-graded scheme. FBZ
Thm~18.4.2 plus Ben-Zvi--Frenkel \emph{Geometric realisation of the
Segal--Sugawara construction} (2004) prove smoothness of
$\mathrm{Op}_{\gfrak^\vee}(\mathrm{D}^\times)$ as a pro-scheme for
every simple $\gfrak^\vee$; smoothness implies
$H^2_{\mathrm{opers}}=0$. Hence the critical-level limit of the
ADE theorem exists and matches the opers/Gaudin side of the
Langlands correspondence.

\subsection{Scope, shadow, cross-volume placement}

Chain-level: Francis spectral sequence, Whitehead lemmas,
Feigin--Frenkel screening, Kac determinant formula --- all explicit
finite-dimensional or free-field computations. $(\infty,1)$-shadow:
the tangent fibration $\mathrm{Tan}(\mathrm{Alg}_{E_2})\to
\mathrm{Alg}_{E_2}$ of Francis 2013 \S2 has vanishing $\pi_{-2}$ at
$\Vvac$ for every ADE. Shadow invariants unchanged:
$\kch(\C^3)=3/2$, $\kcat(\C^3)=\chi(\cO_{\C^3})=1$ (local CY$_3$,
reads off CDP five-brane bulk).

The ADE Yangian $\Yaff(\ghhat)$ on the right-hand side is the
deformed double current algebra of Guay--Nakajima--Wendlandt
(\emph{Selecta Math.} 2018); its generators are the Drinfeld
$e_{\alpha},f_{\alpha},h_\alpha$ at each simple root, $\gfrak$-in
dependence through the Cartan matrix, which for ADE is symmetric.

\ClaimStatusConjectured\ Wave-16 T2 will handle non-simply-laced
$B_n/C_n/F_4/G_2$ by folding: $\gfrak_{\mathrm{non-sl}}=
\gfrak_{\mathrm{sl}}^{\sigma}$ for a diagram automorphism $\sigma$,
and $\partial\hCS_5(\gfrak^\sigma)=\partial\hCS_5(\gfrak)^{\sigma}$
by $\sigma$-equivariance of the CDP brane setup.
