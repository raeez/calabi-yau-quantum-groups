\section*{Lie-Bracket Compatibility for \texorpdfstring{$K3\times E$}{K3 x E}
and \texorpdfstring{$\Delta_5$}{Delta5}}

\subsection*{Objects and invariants}

Let \(X=K3\times E\).  The \(d=3\) Calabi-Yau input has specialised
chiral output of type \(E_1\).  An \(E_2\)-structure enters through a
constructed centre, a completed double, or a representation category
equipped with its Hall pairing and completion.

The scalar invariants attached to \(X\) occupy four different
constructions:
\[
  \{\kcat,\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}},\kBKM,\kfib\}(K3\times E)
  =
  \{0,3,5,24\}.
\]
Here \(\kcat(K3\times E)=\chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\chi(\mathcal O_E)=0\),
the total-space Hodge supertrace \(\kch(K3\times E)=0\),
\(\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}=3\) belongs to the Heisenberg-Mukai
specialisation, \(\kBKM(\gDel)=c_1(0)/2=10/2=5\) is the
Gritsenko-Nikulin-Borcherds weight of \(\Delta_5\), and
\(\kfib=24\) is the K3 Mukai-lattice rank.  The Igusa square
\(\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_5^2\) has weight \(10\); the
primitive BKM denominator has weight \(5\).

The \(K3\times E\) BKM branch is the compact Hall-Borcherds branch:
when the compact datum exists, it is built from the completed
Hall-Drinfeld double of the positive Hall half
\[
  \mathcal D^\wedge_\hbar(\CoHA_X).
\]
The Mukai self-mirror Yangian branch is the rank-\(24\)
Mukai-Heisenberg stable-envelope construction on K3 Hilbert-scheme
towers.  The two branches share the Mukai lattice and have different
brackets.

\subsection*{The primitive Hall bracket}

Fix a stability chamber and an orientation datum on the derived
Calabi-Yau threefold stack of objects.  Work under the compact critical
CoHA hypotheses needed for Davison-Meinhardt PBW: an oriented critical
CoHA \(\Hall^+_X\), a BPS sheaf, purity, and the perverse filtration.
The charge lattice is
\[
  \Gamma_X=H^{\mathrm{ev}}(X,\mathbb Z)/\mathrm{tors}.
\]
For \(\alpha,\beta\in\Gamma_X\), let
\(\Mmod_{\alpha,\beta}^{\mathrm{ses}}\) be the stack of short exact
sequences with subobject of class \(\alpha\), quotient of class
\(\beta\), and middle term of class \(\alpha+\beta\).  The Hall
correspondence is
\[
  \Mmod_\alpha\times\Mmod_\beta
  \xleftarrow{\ (p_1,p_2)\ }
  \Mmod_{\alpha,\beta}^{\mathrm{ses}}
  \xrightarrow{\ p_3\ }
  \Mmod_{\alpha+\beta}.
\]
The product is
\[
  x *_\mathrm{Hall} y
  =
  (p_3)_*
  \bigl((p_1,p_2)^*(x\boxtimes y)\otimes\phi_{\mathrm{or}}\bigr),
\]
where \(\phi_{\mathrm{or}}\) is the chosen orientation line on the
extension correspondence.

The orientation datum gives a quadratic refinement of the Euler form,
hence a bimultiplicative sign
\[
  \varepsilon_{\mathrm{or}}(\alpha,\beta)
  \in\{\pm1\},\qquad
  \varepsilon_{\mathrm{or}}(\alpha,\beta)
  \varepsilon_{\mathrm{or}}(\beta,\alpha)^{-1}
  =
  (-1)^{\chi(\alpha,\beta)} .
\]
For homogeneous \(x,y\), set
\[
  \varepsilon_{\mathrm{Hall}}(\alpha,\beta;x,y)
  =
  (-1)^{|x||y|}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{or}}(\alpha,\beta).
\]
Under the PBW hypotheses the primitive BPS Lie piece is
\[
  \gBPS(X)_\gamma
  =
  H^\bullet(\BPS_\gamma)
  \cong
  \mathrm{gr}^{\mathfrak p}_1\Hall^+_{X,\gamma}.
\]
Its bracket is the primitive projection of the Hall supercommutator:
\[
  m^{\mathrm{BPS}}_{\alpha,\beta}(x,y)
  =
  \operatorname{pr}_{\mathfrak p=1}
  \bigl(
    x *_\mathrm{Hall} y
    -
    \varepsilon_{\mathrm{Hall}}(\alpha,\beta;x,y)\,
    y *_\mathrm{Hall} x
  \bigr).
\]
The associative Hall product gives Jacobi after the primitive
projection has been proved to close.  Davison-Meinhardt integrality
supplies this primitive Lie algebra for the admissible critical-CoHA
input; identification with the Borcherds target requires the separate
Hall-Borcherds bracket datum.

\subsection*{The Borcherds zero-mode bracket}

Let \(\LamII\) be the type-II Lorentzian lattice in the
Gritsenko-Nikulin realisation of the \(\Delta_5\) denominator.  The
Borcherds algebra \(\gDel\) is realised through BRST cohomology of the
lattice vertex algebra:
\[
  \gDel_\delta
  =
  H^1_{\BRST}(V_{\LamII})_\delta .
\]
Its bracket is the BRST zero-mode bracket
\[
  m^{\Delta_5}_{a,b}(u,v)
  =
  [u,v]_{\gDel}
  =
  u_{(0)}v ,
  \qquad
  u\in(\gDel)_a,\ v\in(\gDel)_b .
\]
The lattice two-cocycle \(\epsII\) fixes the signs.  For exponential
vectors,
\[
  Y(e^a,z)e^b
  =
  \epsII(a,b)\,z^{(a,b)_{\LamII}}e^{a+b}+\text{higher modes}.
\]
When \((a,b)_{\LamII}=-1\), the zero-mode residue gives
\[
  [e^a,e^b]_{\gDel}=\epsII(a,b)e^{a+b}.
\]
For general imaginary-root vectors the zero-mode formula is the
definition of the bracket; the exponential residue is the rank-one
calculation inside it.

\subsection*{The type-II projection}

A Hall-to-BKM comparison begins with a type-II projection
\[
  \piII:\Gamma_X^{\mathrm{eff}}\longrightarrow\LamII
\]
with four properties:
\[
\begin{gathered}
  \piII(\alpha+\beta)=\piII(\alpha)+\piII(\beta),\\
  D_X(\gamma)=D_{\Delta_5}(\piII(\gamma)),\\
  \mathrm{ht}_X(\gamma)=\mathrm{ht}_{\Delta_5}(\piII(\gamma)),\\
  \varepsilon_{\mathrm{or}}(\alpha,\beta)
  =
  \eta(\alpha+\beta)\eta(\alpha)^{-1}\eta(\beta)^{-1}
  \epsII(\piII(\alpha),\piII(\beta))
\end{gathered}
\]
for a coboundary \(\eta:\Gamma_X^{\mathrm{eff}}\to\{\pm1\}\).  The
first line preserves charge addition, the second preserves the
discriminant feeding the Jacobi coefficient \(c(D)\), the third
preserves finite-height truncations, and the fourth identifies the
Hall orientation sign with the lattice-VOA cocycle up to change of
orientation trivialisation.

The denominator theorem gives the signed target character
\[
  \operatorname{sdim}(\gDel)_\delta=c(D_{\Delta_5}(\delta)).
\]
After the BPS sheaf and orientation data have been constructed, the
compact Hall source gives signed primitive Euler characteristics.
Ordinary dimensions and parity splits require the finite Hall-Borcherds
recognition data.

\subsection*{Bracket compatibility}

A bracket comparison is the following datum.  For every
\(\delta\in\LamII\), construct a graded linear map
\[
  \rho_\delta:
  \bigoplus_{\piII(\gamma)=\delta}\gBPS(X)_\gamma
  \longrightarrow
  (\gDel)_\delta .
\]
It must satisfy, for all homogeneous \(x\in\gBPS(X)_\alpha\) and
\(y\in\gBPS(X)_\beta\),
\[
  \rho_{\piII(\alpha+\beta)}
  \bigl(m^{\mathrm{BPS}}_{\alpha,\beta}(x,y)\bigr)
  =
  m^{\Delta_5}_{\piII(\alpha),\piII(\beta)}
  \bigl(\rho_{\piII(\alpha)}(x),\rho_{\piII(\beta)}(y)\bigr).
\]
If \(\piII\) is injective on the chamber, the direct sums collapse to
single charge spaces.  If \(\piII\) has nontrivial fibres, the direct
sum is part of the comparison: the BKM root space sees the aggregate
of all Hall charges with the same type-II image.

Character equality, wall-crossing, and PBW existence are strictly
weaker than this displayed identity.  The bracket identity requires a
map preserving Hall extension correspondences, primitive projection,
orientation signs, type-II charge addition, and BRST zero modes.

\subsection*{Wall crossing and primitive brackets}

Kontsevich-Soibelman wall crossing lives in the pro-nilpotent group
\[
  \widehat G_{\mathrm{mot}}=\exp(\widehat{\mathfrak g}_{\mathrm{mot}})
\]
of the motivic quantum torus.  Its torus multiplication and bracket are
\[
  e_\alpha e_\beta
  =
  \mathbb L^{\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle/2}e_{\alpha+\beta},
  \qquad
  [e_\alpha,e_\beta]_{\mathrm{mot}}
  =
  \left(
    \mathbb L^{\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle/2}
    -
    \mathbb L^{-\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle/2}
  \right)e_{\alpha+\beta}.
\]
Its scope is ordered transport of wall factors.  The primitive
Hall bracket \(m^{\mathrm{BPS}}_{\alpha,\beta}\) is extracted from the
Davison-Meinhardt primitive part of the Hall commutator.  The BKM
bracket is the BRST zero-mode bracket.  A Hall-Borcherds intertwiner is
the additional structure relating the two.

\subsection*{Mukai currents and vertex central extension}

The Mukai self-mirror branch has commuting positive currents:
\[
  [J_a^+,J_b^+]_{\mathrm{Lie}}=0 .
\]
The full Heisenberg-Mukai vertex algebra has modes satisfying
\[
  [J_a(m),J_b(n)]
  =
  \hbar\,m\,(a,b)_{\mathrm{Muk}}\delta_{m+n,0}\mathbf c,
\]
equivalently
\[
  [J_a{}_\lambda J_b]=\hbar\,(a,b)_{\mathrm{Muk}}\lambda\,\mathbf 1 .
\]
This is a central vertex extension of the Mukai-Heisenberg branch.  The
BKM imaginary-root bracket in \(\gDel\) is the lattice-VOA zero-mode
bracket governed by \(\epsII\).  The branch separation is algebraic:
Mukai positive currents, full Heisenberg modes, Hall-Drinfeld doubles,
and Borcherds BRST zero modes are four different structures.

\subsection*{Sheaf-level intertwiner}

The exact proof obligation is a sheaf-level map.  In the constructible
or monodromic mixed-Hodge sheaf category carrying the BPS sheaves,
construct
\[
  \mathcal I_\delta:
  \bigoplus_{\piII(\gamma)=\delta}\BPS_\gamma
  \longrightarrow
  \mathcal H^1_{\BRST}(V_{\LamII})_\delta
\]
whose cohomology is \(\rho_\delta\).  For
\(\delta_i=\piII(\alpha_i)\), it must intertwine the extension
correspondence with the lattice-VOA residue:
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \mathcal I_{\delta_1+\delta_2}\circ
  \operatorname{pr}_{\mathfrak p=1}\circ
  (p_3)_*
  \bigl((p_1,p_2)^*(-)\otimes\phi_{\mathrm{or}}\bigr)
  &=
  \operatorname{Res}_{z=0}
  Y(-,z)(-)\circ
  (\mathcal I_{\delta_1}\boxtimes\mathcal I_{\delta_2})
\end{aligned}
\]
after applying the type-II projection to charges.  The same equality
for reversed inputs must reproduce the supercommutator sign
\(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{Hall}}\) and the lattice cocycle
\(\epsII\) through the coboundary \(\eta\) above.

Thus the character-level Hall-Borcherds comparison and the
\(\Delta_5\) denominator identify the target and the signed root
multiplicities.  The Lie-bracket comparison is conditional on the
intertwiner \(\mathcal I_\delta\), sign compatibility, parity fixture,
radical quotient, and completion compatibility.  These data are
additional to denominator work.

\subsection*{Primary anchors}

Kontsevich-Soibelman construct the motivic Hall and wall-crossing
torus.  Davison-Meinhardt give the PBW and BPS-sheaf extraction under
critical-CoHA hypotheses.  Borcherds and Gritsenko-Nikulin construct
the BKM denominator and \(\Delta_5\) root-character target.  The
lattice-VOA zero-mode calculation is the Frenkel-Kac and
Frenkel-Lepowsky-Meurman mechanism, in the Lorentzian BRST form used by
Borcherds.
