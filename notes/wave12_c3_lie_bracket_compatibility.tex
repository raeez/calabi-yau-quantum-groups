\section*{C3/20. Lie-bracket compatibility: what is open, what it would take}

\subsection*{The statement, narrowed to its Beilinson minimum}

Fix the charge lattice $\Gamma = H^{\mathrm{ev}}(K3 \times E,\Z)/\mathrm{tors}$ with
the Mukai--Mukai pairing twisted by the $E$-Kodaira--Spencer form.  The numerical
BPS count is governed by the Oberdieck--Pixton denominator
$Z^{K3 \times E}_{\mathrm{num}} = -C/\Delta_{10}$, and dimension equality of the
two graded spaces
\[
  \gBPS(K3 \times E)_{\alpha}
  \stackrel{?}{=}
  (\gDel)_{\alpha}, \qquad \alpha \in \LamII \cap \overline{\R_{>0}\mathcal P_{II}},
\]
is a consequence of (a) OP 2017 \emph{Geom.\ Topol.}~22 (denominator identity), (b)
Davison cohomological integrality \emph{Invent.}~2022 (the BPS sheaf has Euler
characteristic $|c(D(\alpha))|$), and (c) the Gritsenko--Nikulin 1997/98 uniqueness
of the GKM whose Weyl--Kac--Borcherds denominator is $\Delta_5$.  What is open, per
\S 3.3, is not the grading: it is the \emph{bracket}
\[
  [-,-]_{\gBPS} \stackrel{?}{=} [-,-]_{\gDel}
  \quad\text{as bilinear maps on}\quad
  \bigoplus_{\alpha} (\gBPS)_\alpha \otimes (\gBPS)_\beta \to (\gBPS)_{\alpha+\beta}.
\]

\subsection*{Cycle 1. The CoHA side: where the bracket actually lives}

Schiffmann--Vasserot constructs $\CoHA(K3 \times E)$ as a graded associative algebra
via Joyce's convolution on $\Mmod^{\mathrm{ses}}$ (stack of short exact sequences of
stable coherent sheaves), pulled back along source/target and pushed forward along
middle.  The \emph{commutator} $[x,y] = xy - yx$ is then a bilinear map of graded
vector spaces, not obviously a Lie bracket.

Davison--Meinhardt 2020 (\emph{Invent.}~221, Theorem~A) provides the crucial refinement:
the perverse filtration $\Pfilt^{\leq p}\CoHA(X)$ on a CY$_3$ Hall algebra splits,
and the \emph{primitive} part
\[
  \gBPS(X)_\alpha := \mathrm{gr}^{\mathfrak p}_1 \CoHA(X)_\alpha \;\subset\; \CoHA(X)_\alpha
\]
closes under the commutator: $[\gBPS,\gBPS] \subset \gBPS$.  The bracket on
$\gBPS(X)$ is the restriction of the CoHA commutator to primitives, and it is a
\emph{Lie bracket} (Jacobi) because the associative law on $\CoHA$ gives the
commutator Jacobi identity automatically.  Skew-symmetry is manifest.

\emph{First concrete form.} On extension classes at $(\alpha,\beta) \in \Gamma^2$
with $\alpha + \beta$ primitive, the bracket is
\[
  [x_\alpha, y_\beta]
  = \sum_{[E \hookrightarrow F \twoheadrightarrow G]}
  (-1)^{\chi_{\mathrm{Eu}}(\alpha,\beta)}
  \, \chi\bigl(\mathrm{Ext}^1(G,E)\bigr) \cdot z_{\alpha+\beta}
  \;-\; (\alpha \leftrightarrow \beta),
\]
with $\chi_{\mathrm{Eu}}$ the Euler pairing on $\Gamma$.  The sign is the Joyce
sign, not a free parameter.

\subsection*{Cycle 2. The GKM side: Borcherds 1992 axioms on imaginary simples}

$\gDel$ is defined by Borcherds 1992 (\emph{Invent.}~109, Thms~1--2) from the
Cartan data $(\LamII, m(\alpha))$: a real simple generates $\sl_2$-relations; two
imaginary simples $\alpha_{\mathrm{im}}^{(1)}, \alpha_{\mathrm{im}}^{(2)}$ (norm $\leq 0$)
satisfy \emph{no Serre relation among themselves} when $(\alpha^{(1)},\alpha^{(2)}) \leq 0$.
The bracket of two imaginary simple-root generators is free of obstruction except for
Jacobi; the \emph{nontrivial content} of $\gDel$ is that the Euler--Poincar\'e
character of $\fn_+ \subset \gDel$ equals $\Delta_5^{-1}$ (denominator identity).

\subsection*{Cycle 3. The lattice identification}

The CY-3 charge lattice $H^{\mathrm{ev}}(K3 \times E)/\mathrm{tors} \cong \Lambda_{K3} \oplus H^{\mathrm{ev}}(E) \cong U^{\oplus 4} \oplus E_8(-1)^{\oplus 2} \oplus U$ has signature $(4, 20)$; OP 2017 \S3 reduces the imaginary-root locus, via the transfer to the $\mathrm{O}(3,2)$ Narain lattice of signature $(3,2)$, to the Gritsenko--Nikulin lattice $\LamII$ of signature $(2,1)$ (type-II restriction, cf.\ Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 \emph{Duke}~119, Lemma~2.1).  The identification
\[
  \mathrm{deg}_{\LamII}\bigl(\gBPS(K3 \times E)\bigr)
  \;\cong\;
  \LamII \cap \overline{\R_{>0}\mathcal P_{II}}
\]
is \emph{proved} (OP~2017 Thm~1.1 + Davison 2022 Thm~A); no open obstruction here.

\subsection*{Cycle 4. Does KS motivic WCF determine brackets?}

Kontsevich--Soibelman 2008 (\emph{Motivic DT invariants and motivic WCF}, arXiv:0811.2435)
presents the motivic WCF as an identity in the pro-nilpotent group
$\widehat G_{\mathrm{mot}} = \exp(\widehat{\mathfrak g}_{\mathrm{mot}})$ over the motivic
quantum torus $\widehat T_{\mathrm{mot}}$.  The WCF determines
$\Omega^{\mathrm{mot}}(\gamma; \sigma)$ as functions of the stability $\sigma$, but
the \emph{Lie bracket} on $\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{mot}}$ is the \emph{motivic quantum
torus commutator}
\[
  [e_\alpha, e_\beta]_{\mathrm{mot}}
  = (\mathbb L^{\langle \alpha, \beta \rangle/2} - \mathbb L^{-\langle \alpha, \beta \rangle/2})
  \, e_{\alpha + \beta}
\]
\emph{before} passing to the classical limit.  This is the \emph{structure-constant
Lie bracket on the motivic torus}, not the Lie bracket on $\gBPS$ itself.

\emph{Key distinction.}  KS gives the bracket on the \emph{exponentiated} object (the
motivic enveloping algebra $U(\gBPS) \otimes \widehat T_{\mathrm{mot}}$).  Davison--Meinhardt 2020 Theorem~A extracts $\gBPS$ as the \emph{primitive sub-Lie algebra}
via the perverse filtration, and equips it with the commutator bracket.  KS alone
does not determine $[-,-]_{\gBPS}$ beyond the structure constants of $U(\gBPS)$; the
Lie bracket refinement is Davison--Meinhardt content.

Thus: dimension equality on each root space $\alpha$ comes from OP 2017 + GN
uniqueness.  What KS WCF does \emph{not} control is whether the extracted Lie
bracket on primitives, pulled from the CoHA commutator, \emph{matches} the
Borcherds bracket extracted from the GKM axioms.  Matching structure constants of
universal enveloping algebras does not force matching of Lie brackets, since
$U(\mathfrak g)$ as a graded associative algebra does not uniquely determine the PBW
primitive filtration unless the deformation is quadratic--Koszul.

\subsection*{Cycle 5. What would reduce bracket compatibility to a theorem}

Three candidate reductions, in increasing strength:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{(Koszul rigidity.)}  Chiral Koszulity of $U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\gBPS(K3 \times E))$
  holds in the ambient sense of the CY-to-chiral programme (Vol~I Koszul reflection
  theorem); what is \emph{not yet} available is a generator-relation Priddy--Manin
  presentation identifying the quadratic dual as $U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\gDel)^!$ on the
  nose.  The Davison--Meinhardt PBW filtration on CoHA$(K3 \times E)$ gives the
  primitive decomposition, but the quadratic--Koszul refinement that would force
  Lie isomorphism from enveloping-algebra isomorphism requires a presentation-level
  comparison not yet in the literature.
  \item \textbf{(Motivic integration / comparison of Chow groups.)}  Compute the
  bracket structure constant $c_{\alpha,\beta}^{\alpha+\beta}$ on primitives by
  motivic integration over the moduli of short exact sequences with $[E]=\alpha$,
  $[G]=\beta$ (Behrend--Dhillon--Joyce style), and compare with the explicit
  Borcherds formula $[e_\alpha,e_\beta] = \epsilon(\alpha,\beta) e_{\alpha+\beta}$
  ($\epsilon$ the 2-cocycle of the lattice VOA).  This is a finite computation for
  $|\alpha|, |\beta| \leq $ first few levels (D=0,3,4,7,8); success through level
  $D=8$ would be strong evidence, not proof.
  \item \textbf{(Lattice VOA intertwiner.)}  The Frenkel--Lepowsky--Meurman lattice-VOA
  realization $V_{\LamII}$ acts on the K3 Fock space via BRST; bracket compatibility
  is equivalent to \emph{intertwining} $V_{\LamII}$-module structure on the Davison
  BPS sheaf $\mathcal{BPS}_\gamma$.  Reducing the open problem to this intertwiner
  compatibility is the sharp reformulation: the BRST cohomology $H^1_{\mathrm{BRST}}$
  identification of $\gDel$ (Borcherds 1992 \S 6) meets the Davison BPS sheaf through
  a \emph{sheaf-level intertwiner}, provably a Lie algebra map once it is shown to
  be a $V_{\LamII}$-module map.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Report.}

Dimension-per-root equality is proved (OP 2017 + Davison 2022 + GN uniqueness).
Bracket equality is open because: (i) the Joyce--Davison commutator on CoHA primitives
produces a Lie bracket whose structure constants involve motivic integration on
$\Mmod^{\mathrm{ses}}$; (ii) the Borcherds bracket on $\gDel$ is given by the
lattice VOA 2-cocycle on $\LamII$; and (iii) KS motivic WCF controls only the
universal-enveloping-algebra character, not the primitive-level bracket.  The
cleanest reduction is route~(3): bracket compatibility is \emph{equivalent} to the
Davison BPS sheaf $\mathcal{BPS}_\gamma$ admitting a $V_{\LamII}$-module-map
intertwiner with the Borcherds BRST realization of $\gDel$.  Status
\texttt{\textbackslash ClaimStatusConjectured} is correct; upgrade requires the
intertwiner, not further denominator work.
