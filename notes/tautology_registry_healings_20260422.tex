\subsection*{Entry~3: Costello TCFT $\{b, B^{(2)}\} = 0$ reframed to
TCFT-vs-naive disambiguation}
\label{wn:sec:tautology-healing-entry3}

\emph{Prior concern.} Theorem~\ref{thm:total-ainf-compat} asserted
$\{b, B^{(2)}\} = 0$ on $C_\bullet(A)$ for cyclic $\Ainf$-algebras of
CY dimension $d$.  The engine
\texttt{chain\_level\_m2\_b2\_cancellation.py} verified that the naive
chain-level contraction $B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{naive}}$ does \emph{not}
satisfy cross-arity cancellation: $\{b_2, B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{naive}}\}$
maps arity~$5$ to arity~$2$, whilst
$\{b_3, B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{naive}}\}$ maps arity~$5$ to arity~$1$; the
two land in different graded pieces and cannot cancel directly.  The
theorem and the engine refutation appeared to contradict.

\emph{2026-04-17 disambiguation.} Two operators, one theorem, one
open problem.  The remark \texttt{rem:tcft-vs-naive-b2} in
\texttt{chapters/theory/m3\_b2\_obstruction.tex}~L605--656 installs
the distinction:
\begin{itemize}
 \item $B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{TCFT}}$: the operator on $C_\bullet(A)$
  defined by Costello's open-closed identification with the
  genus-change operation on the moduli operad
  $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0, n}^{\mathrm{open}}$.  At the moduli-operad
  chain level, $\{b, B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{TCFT}}\} = 0$ holds by
  $\partial^2 = 0$.  This is the statement of
  Theorem~\ref{thm:total-ainf-compat}.
 \item $B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{naive}}$: the direct bar-complex Connes-hierarchy
  contraction $[a_0 | \cdots | a_n] \mapsto \sum_{i, j} \pm
  \langle a_i, a_j \rangle [a_0 | \cdots \hat{a}_i \cdots \hat{a}_j
  \cdots]$.  Cross-arity cancellation fails at the strict chain level.
 \item \emph{Chain-level identification} $B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{TCFT}}
  \overset{?}{\simeq} B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{naive}}$: this is the open
  chain-level frontier of CY-A$_3$.  The identification holds for
  formal $\Ainf$-algebras by Tradler's strictification
  (arXiv:math/0108027); for non-formal $\Ainf$-algebras, lifting the
  operadic-TCFT identity to a strict chain-level identity requires
  either (a) chain-level rectification of the cyclic $\Ainf$-structure,
  or (b) an explicit chain homotopy $h$ with
  $B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{TCFT}} - B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{naive}} = [d_{\mathrm{tot}}, h]$.
  Neither is constructed in general.
\end{itemize}

\emph{Status.} \ClaimStatusHealed{}  Theorem~\ref{thm:total-ainf-compat}
retains \texttt{\textbackslash ClaimStatusProvedHere} at the stated
scope (TCFT-derived operator at the moduli-operad level).  The
chain-level identification with the naive operator on the bar complex
is conjectural for non-formal $\Ainf$-algebras and is listed as the
open chain-level frontier.  Cross-reference: AP-CY62
(geometric-vs-algebraic Hochschild model distinction) frames the
same family of model-dependent identifications.

\label{wn:sec:tautology-healing-entry5}

\emph{2026-04-22 structural rewrite via the two-stage factorisation.}

C} \circ \PhiFA_d$ of Definition~\ref{def:two-stage-phi} separates two
orthogonal axes that the prior reading collapsed onto one column:
\begin{itemize}
 \item \emph{Stage~$1$ (canonical).}  $\PhiFA_d \colon
  \CY_d\text{-cat}(X) \to E_d\text{-HolFA}(X)$ produces the canonical
  $E_d$ holomorphic factorisation algebra on $X$ itself, pinned up to
  contractible choice by Kontsevich--Tamarkin $E_d$-formality plus
  Costello--Gwilliam locality; for $d = 3$ on $K3 \times E$, by
  Costello--Li holomorphic Chern--Simons.
 \item \emph{Stage~$2$ (choice-dependent).}  $\mathrm{Sp}_{\Sigma_{d-1},
  C} \colon E_d\text{-HolFA}(X) \to \Eone\text{-ChirAlg}(C)$ specialises
  along a $(d - 1)$-cycle $\Sigma_{d - 1} \subset X$ and a reference
  curve $C \subset X$ via factorisation homology
  $\int_{\Sigma_{d - 1}}(-)|_C$.  The output depends on the pair
  $(\Sigma_{d - 1}, C)$.
\end{itemize}
At $d = 3$ on $X = K3 \times E$ with $C = E$ the reference curve, the
specialisation moduli
\[
\mathrm{Cyc}_2(K3 \times E) \times \mathrm{Curves}(K3 \times E)
\]
