\section*{Nekrasov characters and Hall positive halves}
\label{wn:nek-char-yplus}

Let $X$ be a Calabi--Yau target equipped with an effective critical
Hall datum
\[
  \mathcal H_X=(Q_X,W_X,\theta,o_X,T,\mathsf I_X):
\]
a BPS quiver or critical atlas, a stability chamber, orientation data
for vanishing cycles, an equivariance group, and a cohomological
integration map. Let \(\mathfrak M_\gamma(X,\theta)\) denote the
corresponding effective critical stack. In a quiver chart it is the
GIT semistable critical stack
\[
  \mathfrak M_\gamma(X,\theta)
  =
  \left[
    \Rep_\gamma(Q_X)^{\theta\text{-ss}}/G_\gamma
  \right],
  \qquad
  \varphi_{\gamma}=\varphi_{\mathrm{Tr}(W_X)}\otimes o_X .
\]
The Hall positive half attached to the datum is
\[
  \Yp(X;\mathcal H_X)
  :=
  \bigoplus_{\gamma\in \mathbb Z_{\geq 0}^{Q_X^0}}
  H^{\mathrm{BM}}_{T,c}\!\left(
    \mathfrak M_\gamma(X,\theta),
    \varphi_{\gamma}
  \right),
\]
with multiplication given by the Kontsevich--Soibelman extension
correspondence. The orientation datum lives in the sheaf. The
integration map \(\mathsf I_X\) turns the graded Hall object into a
refined Donaldson--Thomas character.

When this virtual integral is the chosen Nekrasov, Donaldson--Thomas,
or reduced Donaldson--Thomas series in a chamber, it has the form
\[
  Z_X(q,t,Q_\bullet)
  =
  \sum_{\gamma} q^{\gamma_0}Q^\gamma
  \chi_T\!\left(
    \Meff(X,\theta)_\gamma,
    \varphi_{\gamma}
  \right).
\]
Here the word Nekrasov denotes the equivariant virtual character at
this partition-function layer. The Hall statement begins at the point
where the same virtual integral has been placed in the Hall datum.
When \(\mathsf I_X\) is defined and compatible with the Hall product,
\[
  Z_X(q,t,Q_\bullet)
  =
  \chq\!\bigl(\Yp(X;\mathcal H_X)\bigr)
  =
  \chi_T\!\left(\Meff(X,\theta),\varphi_{\mathrm{Tr}(W_X)}\right)
\]
in the completed character ring. This equation is a statement about
graded characters and virtual integrals. Full Yangians, vertex
algebras, and braided representation categories require the negative
half, Cartan part, Hopf pairing, completion, Drinfeld double or centre,
and a chosen representation, with a radical quotient when the pairing
is degenerate.

For \(d\geq 3\) the specialised CY-to-chiral output \(A_X\) is native
\(\Eone\). Any \(\Etwo\)-braiding at this layer belongs to
\(\mathcal Z(\Rep^{\Eone}(A_X))\), to a completed Drinfeld double, or
to a module category after the pairing and completion data have been
supplied.

\subsection*{$\mathbb C^3$}

For the one-vertex three-loop quiver with potential
\[
  W_3=x[y,z],
\]
the critical CoHA of \(0\)-dimensional sheaves on \(\mathbb C^3\) is
\[
  \CoHA(\mathbb C^3)
  =
  \bigoplus_{n\geq 0}
  H^{\mathrm{BM}}_{T,c}\!\left(
    \left[\Rep_n(\mathbb C\langle x,y,z\rangle)/GL_n\right],
    \varphi_{\mathrm{Tr}(x[y,z])}
  \right)
  \cong
  \Yp(\gghone).
\]
Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013, Theorem~$1.1$, identifies this algebra
with the positive half of the affine Yangian of \(\gghone\) in the
shuffle normalisation. The full affine Yangian is obtained from this
positive half by adjoining the Cartan and negative half and taking the
completed Drinfeld double. The \(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}\) object appears
at the evaluation or vacuum-representation layer of the completed
doubled algebra.

The $T$-fixed points of $\Hilb^n(\mathbb C^3)$ are plane partitions.
The unrefined toric DT crystal character is
\[
  Z_{\mathrm{DT}}(\mathbb C^3;q)
  =
  \sum_{\pi\ \mathrm{plane\ partition}} q^{|\pi|}
  =
  M(q)
  =
  \prod_{k\geq 1}(1-q^k)^{-k}.
\]
Equivariant refinements retain the \(T\)-weights in the completed
character ring; the displayed product is the scalar \(q\)-character.
The Schiffmann--Vasserot shuffle basis gives the same character:
\[
  \chq\!\bigl(\Yp(\gghone)\bigr)=M(q).
\]
The topological vertex evaluates the empty three-leg vertex to the
same product. Plane partitions, the shuffle PBW basis, and the
topological vertex therefore compute the same positive-half character.
The hCS-to-Hall factorisation map is additional structure.

\subsection*{Resolved conifold}

Let
\[
  \widetilde Y=\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-1)\oplus
  \mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-1)
\]
and let $(Q_{\mathrm{con}},W_{\mathrm{con}})$ be the conifold quiver
with two vertices, four arrows, and potential
\[
  W_{\mathrm{con}}
  =
  \mathrm{tr}(a_1b_1a_2b_2-a_1b_2a_2b_1).
\]
For a fixed stability chamber \(\theta\),
\[
  \Yp_\theta(\widetilde Y)
  =
  \CoHA(Q_{\mathrm{con}},W_{\mathrm{con}})_\theta .
\]
Szendr\H{o}i 2008, Theorem~$2.7.1$, gives the noncommutative-chamber
character, and Morrison--Mozgovoy--Nagao--Szendr\H{o}i 2011,
Theorem~$1.1$, gives its wall-crossing form:
\[
  Z_{\mathrm{DT}}^{\mathrm{nc}}(\widetilde Y;q,Q)
  =
  M(-q)^2
  \prod_{k\geq 1}
  (1-(-q)^kQ)^k(1-(-q)^kQ^{-1})^k .
\]
The \(Q^{-1}\)-factor records the noncommutative chamber. After
Kontsevich--Soibelman wall-crossing, the MNOP change
\(q_{\mathrm{DT}}=-q_{\mathrm{GV}}\), and removal of the degree-zero
factor \(M(-q)^2\), the large-volume reduced Gopakumar--Vafa product is
\[
  Z_{\mathrm{red}}^{\mathrm{LV}}(\widetilde Y;q,Q)
  =
  \prod_{k\geq 1}(1-q^kQ)^k .
\]
The conifold is the curve-local CY$_3$ chart
$\mathrm{Tot}(\mathcal O(-1)^{\oplus 2})_{\mathbb P^1}$ with two-node
Hall quiver. The Vol III local chart invariant is
\[
  \kch(A_{\widetilde Y})=1,
\]
computed by the direct McKay/conifold-quiver route.

\subsection*{$K3\times E$}

The compact product $K3\times E$ uses reduced Donaldson--Thomas
theory on primitive curve classes in place of a toric
\(\Omega\)-background. Oberdieck--Pixton 2017, Theorem~$1$, gives the
scalar reduced-DT partition function in their convention:
\[
  Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{DT}}(K3\times E)
  =
  -\IgusaOP^{-1}
  =
  -\Df^{-2}
  =
  -4096\,\Deltaf^{-2},
\qquad
  \Df=64^{-1}\Deltaf,\quad \IgusaOP=\Df^2 .
\]
Equivalently, the unnormalised scalar square is
\(\Igusaun=\Deltaf^2\). Hall multiplication and compact orientation
monodromy are additional data. A Hall-character theorem for the
primitive sector requires an oriented compact critical Hall atlas or an
equivalent Serre-compatible quasi-NCCR atlas. With that datum fixed,
the character identity below specialises to
\[
  \chq\!\bigl(\Yp(K3\times E;\mathcal H_{K3\times E})\bigr)
  \big|_{\mathrm{prim},\beta}
  =
  \bigl[-\IgusaOP^{-1}\bigr]_{\beta}
  =
  \bigl[Z_{\mathrm{DT}}^{\mathrm{red}}(K3\times E)\bigr]_{\beta}.
\]

The Borcherds denominator invariant is attached to the primitive
square root. Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995, Part~II, Theorem~$2.1$, gives
the additive lift, and Borcherds 1998, Theorem~$13.3$, gives the
weight formula
\[
  \kBKM(\Deltaf)=\frac{c_1(0)}{2}=\frac{10}{2}=5.
\]
The scalar square \(\IgusaOP=\Df^2\) has weight \(10\); the Vol III
\(K3\times E\) BKM characteristic is the primitive denominator weight
\(5\). The total-space categorical and compact chiral invariants are
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)
  =
  \chi(\mathcal O_{K3\times E})
  =
  \chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\chi(\mathcal O_E)
  =
  2\cdot 0
  =
  0,
\]
\[
  \kch(A_{K3\times E})
  =
  \sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(K3\times E)
  =
  1-1+1-1
  =
  0.
\]
The Heisenberg specialisation and fibre readings are distinct:
\[
  \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)=3,\qquad
  \kfib(K3\times E)=\dim H^\bullet(K3)=24.
\]
The compact $K3\times E$ measurements are
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)=0,\qquad
  \kch(A_{K3\times E})=0,\qquad
  \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)=3,\qquad
  \kBKM(\Deltaf)=5,\qquad
  \kfib(K3\times E)=24,
\]
with each value supplied by its own construction.

\subsection*{Borcherds constants}

For the primitive CHL denominator ladder \(N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}\),
Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995, Part~II, Theorem~$2.1$, and Borcherds 1998,
Theorem~$13.3$, give
\[
  (c_1(0),c_2(0),c_3(0),c_4(0),c_6(0))
  =
  (10,8,6,4,2),
\]
\[
  \bigl(
    \kBKM(\Phi_1),
    \kBKM(\Phi_2),
    \kBKM(\Phi_3),
    \kBKM(\Phi_4),
    \kBKM(\Phi_6)
  \bigr)
  =
  (5,4,3,2,1).
\]
Here \(\Phi_1=\Deltaf\) in the primitive denominator convention. The
identity
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2
\]
is the Borcherds denominator-weight formula. Its input is the zeroth
Borcherds coefficient, independent of $\kch$ and fibre Euler data.

Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008, Theorem~$1.4$, gives the separate
triple-indexed dd-modular catalogue. Theorem~$3.1$ gives the row-wise
Borcherds product weight. Its eight weights are
\[
  (5,2,3,1,2,\tfrac12,\tfrac32,1),
\]
with twice-weight tuple
\[
  (10,4,6,2,4,1,3,2).
\]
Its overlap with the CHL ladder is the common \(\Deltaf\) row.

\subsection*{Character identity}

\begin{theorem}[Character identity with Hall data fixed]
\label{wn:thm:nek-char-hall-data}
Let $X$ be a Calabi--Yau target with an effective critical Hall datum
$\mathcal H_X=(Q_X,W_X,\theta,o_X,T,\mathsf I_X)$ as above. Assume
that the cohomological integration map \(\mathsf I_X\) is defined on
the chosen vanishing-cycle Borel--Moore theory, is compatible with Hall
multiplication, and lands in a completed character ring \(R_X\). Define
the integrated Hall character by applying \(\mathsf I_X\) degree by
degree:
\[
  Z_{\mathcal H_X}
  :=
  \sum_\gamma q^{\gamma_0}Q^\gamma\,
  \mathsf I_X\!\left(
    H^{\mathrm{BM}}_{T,c}
    (\mathfrak M_\gamma(X,\theta),\varphi_\gamma)
  \right)
  \in R_X .
\]
Then
\[
  \chq\!\bigl(\Yp(X;\mathcal H_X)\bigr)
  =
  Z_{\mathcal H_X}
  =
  \chi_T\!\left(\Meff(X,\theta),\varphi_{\gamma}\right)
  \quad\text{in }R_X.
\]
If an external theorem identifies this same virtual integral with a
Nekrasov, reduced-DT, or Oh--Thomas series in the chosen chamber, then
\[
  Z_X(q,t,Q_\bullet)=\chq\!\bigl(\Yp(X;\mathcal H_X)\bigr).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Kontsevich--Soibelman define the Hall product by convolution along
the stack of short exact sequences, on vanishing-cycle
Borel--Moore homology of the critical representation stacks.
The direct sum defining \(\Yp(X;\mathcal H_X)\) is graded by
\(\gamma\). Applying \(\mathsf I_X\) to each graded piece gives the
displayed element \(Z_{\mathcal H_X}\); compatibility with Hall
multiplication says that this assignment is a character of the Hall
positive half, beyond a graded vector-space count.

Davison--Meinhardt 2020, Theorem~A, identifies the Poincar\'e series
of an oriented critical CoHA with the refined DT series after the
orientation data have been fixed. In toric CY$_3$ chambers the same
virtual integral is computed by equivariant localisation on the
torus-fixed Hilbert-scheme or stable-pair locus. In the resolved
conifold it is the Szendr\H{o}i and Morrison--Mozgovoy--Nagao--
Szendr\H{o}i chamber character transported by wall-crossing. For
\(K3\times E\), Oberdieck--Pixton supply the reduced-DT scalar series;
the equality with a Hall character is exactly the additional compact
Hall-datum hypothesis above. For DT$_4$ targets, Oh--Thomas integration
enters as part of \(\mathcal H_X\) when the DT$_4$ orientation and
integration theory are supplied. The compact Hall atlas remains an
external proof obligation.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Full doubles and representations}

The positive half \(\Yp(X;\mathcal H_X)\) is the algebra controlled by
critical Hall correspondences. A full Yangian-type algebra is obtained
from \(\Yp\) by adding the Cartan part, negative half, Hopf pairing,
radical quotient if necessary, and completion. A vertex algebra or
\(\mathcal W\)-object is then obtained from a representation, usually
a Fock or vacuum module. For \(\mathbb C^3\) the chain is
\[
  \Yp(\gghone)
  \hookrightarrow
  \Dh\!\bigl(\Yp(\gghone)\bigr)
  \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Fock}_\lambda}
  \mathrm{End}\!\left(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}[\lambda]\text{-vacuum}\right).
\]
For \(K3\times E\), once the compact orientation, pairing, and
completion data are supplied, the Hall--Drinfeld/BKM branch is the
double of the oriented Hall positive half with primitive denominator
\(\Deltaf\). The K3 self-mirror Yangian is a separate Mukai-lattice
branch.

Any map from quantised hCS factorisation observables to the oriented
critical Hall positive half must respect vanishing cycles, Hall
integration, the BV bracket, and equivariant normalisation. The
character theorem above uses such a map as part of the specified Hall
datum.
