\section*{Reduced DT and the positive effective geometry of $K3\times E$}

\subsection*{Reduced virtual class}

Let \(S=K3\) and \(X=S\times E\).  On the K3 curve-counting moduli
attached to \(S\), the holomorphic symplectic form
\(\sigma_S\in H^0(S,\Omega^2_S)\) gives a Serre-dual cosection
\[
  \sigma_S^\vee:\mathrm{Ob}\longrightarrow \mathcal O.
\]
The ordinary virtual class vanishes in nonzero curve class.  The
Maulik--Pandharipande--Thomas reduced obstruction theory replaces
\(\mathrm{Ob}\) by
\[
  \mathrm{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}=\ker(\sigma_S^\vee)
\]
and raises the virtual dimension by \(1\).  For a K3-primitive class
\(\beta\in H_2(S,\mathbb Z)\) with \(\beta^2=2h-2\), the reduced class
\[
  \virred\in
  A_{\mathrm{vd}+1}\bigl(\Mred_{\mathrm{DT}}(S,\beta,n)\bigr)
\]
is the class integrated by the Kawai--Yoshioka and KKV series.

For \(X=S\times E\), write
\[
  \gamma=(\beta,d)
  =
  (\iota_S)_*\beta+(\iota_E)_*(d[E])\in H_2(X,\mathbb Z),
\]
where \(\iota_S:S\hookrightarrow X\) and \(\iota_E:E\hookrightarrow X\)
are fibres of the two projections.  The product Calabi--Yau form is
\(\sigma_S\wedge dz_E\), but the cosection is the K3 two-form pulled
back from \(S\).  It removes the symplectic obstruction line and leaves
a reduced virtual class of dimension \(1\) in the reduced
Gromov--Witten theory.  Oberdieck--Pixton integrate the insertion
\[
  \mathrm{ev}_1^*
  \bigl(\pi_1^*(\beta^\vee)\cup\pi_2^*([\mathrm{pt}])\bigr),
  \qquad
  \langle \beta,\beta^\vee\rangle=1,
\]
which kills the residual dimension and counts maps modulo translation
in the elliptic factor.

For reduced pairs, \(E\) acts on \(P_n(X,\gamma)\) by translation with
finite stabilisers.  The reduced PT invariant is the Behrend-weighted
orbifold Euler characteristic
\[
  \mathsf P_{n,\gamma}
  =
  \int_{P_n(X,\gamma)/E}\nu\,de .
\]
The reduced GW/PT correspondence is read with the stable-pair Euler
variable \(\mathsf y=-e^{iu}\):
\[
  \sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}\mathsf P_{n,h,d}\mathsf y^n
  =
  \sum_g \mathsf N_{g,h,d}u^{2g-2}.
\]
Here \(Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\DT}\) denotes the resulting reduced DT/PT scalar
partition function in the primitive K3 sector.  With the quotient
Behrend sign convention used in Oberdieck's reduced DT/PT comparison,
\[
  Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{DT}}(K3\times E)
  = -(\PhiOP)^{-1}.
\]

\subsection*{Elliptic quotient}

The continuous symmetry on \(X\) is translation by \(E\).  A literal
\(\mathbb C^\times\)-action on \(E\) is absent.  Translation moves
fibre-supported stable pairs in \(E\)-families, so the reduced invariant
is computed on the quotient by \(E\).  The elliptic variable records
the degree/Jacobi variable of the quotient theory, separate from
one-parameter subgroup characters of \(E\).  Atiyah--Bott fixed-point
localisation requires fixed loci; the translation direction on
\(K3\times E\) is free.

\subsection*{Finite K3 automorphisms and CHL quotients}

Let \(\Auts(K3)\) denote a finite symplectic automorphism group of the
K3 factor in Mukai's sense.  For CHL level \(N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}\), take
\(g\in\Auts(K3)\) of order \(N\) and an exact \(N\)-torsion translation
\(\tau\in E[N]\).  For \(N>1\), every non-trivial power of \(\tau\) acts
freely on \(E\), so the diagonal action generated by
\((g,\tau)\) is free in the smooth CHL cases and gives the quotient
threefold; \(N=1\) is the unquotiented \(K3\times E\) case.
The primitive CHL denominator constants are
\[
  (c_N(0))_{N=1,2,3,4,6}=(10,8,6,4,2),
  \qquad
  \bigl(\kBKM(\Phi_N)\bigr)_{N=1,2,3,4,6}=(5,4,3,2,1).
\]
The Gritsenko--Cl\'ery diagonal-divisor catalogue is a separate
eight-row system with twice-weight tuple
\[
  (10,4,6,2,4,1,3,2);
\]
it shares the \(\Deltafive\) row with the \(N=1\) CHL entry and then
follows its own paramodular row data.

At \(N=1\), Oberdieck--Pixton prove the primitive reduced GW
Igusa-scalar theorem; Oberdieck's reduced DT/PT comparison and the
Oberdieck--Pandharipande GW/PT change of variables identify the same
Laurent expansion with the reduced DT/PT scalar:
\[
  Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{DT}}
  (K3\times E;\gamma\ \mathrm{primitive\ on\ }K3)(p,q,\tilde q)
  =
  -\frac{1}{\PhiOP(p,q,\tilde q)}
  =
  -\Dfive(p,q,\tilde q)^{-2}
  =
  -4096\,\Deltafive(p,q,\tilde q)^{-2},
\]
where Oberdieck--Pixton write the scalar as \(\chiten\), and the Vol III
monic convention writes \(\chiten=\PhiOP\) with
\[
  \Dfive=64^{-1}\Deltafive,
  \qquad
  \PhiOP=\Dfive^2=4096^{-1}\Deltafive^2.
\]
Higher CHL levels require their own twined denominator forms, finite
equivariance data, and orientation conventions.  The \(N=1\) primitive
theorem supplies neither imprimitive multiplicities nor higher-level
bracket comparisons.

\subsection*{Toric contrast}

For toric CY$_3$ targets such as \(\mathbb C^3\), local
\(\mathbb P^2\), and the resolved conifold, a torus action with isolated
fixed strata reduces DT integration to topological-vertex lattice sums.
In the affine case this positive half is
\(\CoHA(\mathbb C^3)=Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)\).
For \(K3\times E\), \(H^0(K3,T_{K3})=0\), hence the identity component
of the K3 holomorphic automorphism group is trivial, and the elliptic
translation direction is free.  The reduced sequence is
\[
  \mathrm{cosection\ reduction}
  \longrightarrow
  E\text{-quotient residual integration}
  \longrightarrow
  \Auts(K3)\text{-equivariance}.
\]
The Igusa scalar \(\PhiOP\) is the scalar generating-function
consequence of this reduced geometry.

\subsection*{Positive effective geometry}

Reduced DT supplies a scalar character for a positive effective sector.
A compact Hall realisation, if constructed with the required orientation
and support conditions, would have graded vector space
\[
  \CoHAredpos(K3\times E)
  :=
  \bigoplus_{\gamma,n}
  H^{\bullet}_{\Auts(K3)}
    \bigl(P_n(K3\times E,\gamma)/E,\varphi_{\mathrm{red}}\bigr),
\]
where \(\varphi_{\mathrm{red}}\) denotes the reduced vanishing-cycle or
Behrend orientation datum.  If this compact Hall object is equipped with
Hall product and orientation data, its primitive K3 sector has unsigned
character
\[
  \chi_{\CoHAredpos}(p,q,\tilde q)
  =
  (\PhiOP(p,q,\tilde q))^{-1}.
\]
The Behrend sign gives
\(Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\DT}=-\chi_{\CoHAredpos}\) in Oberdieck's quotient
Behrend convention.  This is only a positive-half character statement.
A BKM-side quantum object requires a
Hall product, coproduct, non-degenerate Hopf pairing, completion, and
Hall--Drinfeld double.  That branch is separate from the Mukai
self-mirror Yangian branch.

The primitive Borcherds denominator is \(\Deltafive\).  The reduced-DT
scalar is the monic square \(\PhiOP=\Dfive^2\):
\[
  \Dfive=64^{-1}\Deltafive,
  \qquad
  \PhiOP=\Dfive^2=4096^{-1}\Deltafive^2,
  \qquad
  \kBKM(\Deltafive)=c_1(0)/2=5.
\]
The negative half, Hopf pairing, Hall coproduct, centre compatibility,
completion, and Borcherds bracket comparison are additional data after
the scalar reciprocal-Siegel identity.

\subsection*{$\kappa_{\bullet}$ values}

The \(K3\times E\) invariants come from distinct constructions:
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)
  =
  \chi(\mathcal O_{K3\times E})
  =
  \chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\chi(\mathcal O_E)
  =
  2\cdot0
  =
  0,
\]
\[
  \kch^{\mathrm{cpt}}(K3\times E)
  =
  \sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(K3\times E)
  =
  1-1+1-1
  =
  0,
  \qquad
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)=3,
  \qquad
  \kBKM(\Deltafive)=5,
  \qquad
  \kfib(K3)=\operatorname{rank}\widetilde H(K3,\mathbb Z)=24.
\]
Thus
\[
  \{\kcat,\kch^{\mathrm{cpt}},\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}},\kBKM,\kfib\}
  (K3\times E)
  =
  \{0,0,3,5,24\}.
\]
The number \(2=\chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\) is the K3 fibre
structure-sheaf Euler characteristic, separate from the total-space
categorical invariant.  The number \(24\) is the Mukai-lattice
topological fibre witness, separate from \(\chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\).
The identity
\(\kBKM=\kch+\chi(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{fiber}})\) is excluded here:
at \(N=1\) it would give \(5=0+0\) on the total space.  The correct
Borcherds formula is \(\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2\).  The \(d=3\)
specialisation
\(\Phi_3^{(K3,E)}\) lands in an \(E_1\)-chiral algebra on the elliptic
base; braided \(E_2\) structure belongs to the Drinfeld centre of the
representation layer or to the double.

\subsection*{Primitive and nonprimitive scope}

The displayed reduced-DT identity is the \(N=1\) theorem in primitive
K3 class and in the OP scalar normalisation.  Imprimitive roots use the
Borcherds divisor-sum correction and the Oberdieck--Shen imprimitive
multiple-cover input; the primitive coefficient \(c_{K3}(h,\ell)\)
alone is insufficient.  For CHL levels \(N\in\{2,3,4,6\}\), finite
equivariance and orientation data must be fixed level by level before
comparison with the corresponding twined denominator.  The scalar
identity, the positive-half character statement, the compact CoHA
construction, and the Hall--Drinfeld double comparison are separate
claims.
