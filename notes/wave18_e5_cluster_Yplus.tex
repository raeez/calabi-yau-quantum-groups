\section*{Wave-18 E5/20: cluster categories and the $\Yplus$-gluing cocycle}
\label{wn:sec:cluster-Yplus-gluing}

The gluing cocycle of Wave-17 B4 attaches a CoHA-automorphism
$g_{UV}:\CoHA(A_{U},W_{U})\to\CoHA(A_{V},W_{V})$ to every overlap of
quiver-affine charts on $\mathrm{Stab}(X)$.  The claim of this section
is sharper: $g_{UV}$ is not merely an automorphism compatible with
wall-crossing, it is the CoHA avatar of a cluster mutation.  Amiot's
cluster category organises all quiver presentations of a fixed CY$_{3}$
category into a single orbit under mutation; Keller--Yang lift each
mutation to a derived equivalence; pushing that equivalence through the
CoHA functor is the overlap datum.

\subsection*{Cycle 1: the cluster category of a CY$_{3}$ Jacobi algebra.}

Let $(Q,W)$ be a Jacobi-finite quiver with potential and $A_{Q} =
\Jac(Q,W)$ its Ginzburg CY$_{3}$ dg algebra (Ginzburg 2006,
Theorem~3.6.4).  Write $\per(A_{Q})$ for the perfect derived category
and $\sD_{\fd}(A_{Q})$ for the full subcategory of finite-dimensional
dg modules.  Amiot 2009, Theorem~2.1, defines
\[
  \sC(Q,W)\;:=\;\per(A_{Q})\big/\sD_{\fd}(A_{Q}),
\]
the \emph{generalised cluster category}.  For $Q$ of finite
representation type $(ADE)$ and $W = 0$ this recovers the
Buan--Marsh--Reineke--Reiten--Todorov cluster category of
Fomin--Zelevinsky type (arXiv:math/0208229);  Amiot's theorem shows
$\sC(Q,W)$ is Hom-finite, $2$-Calabi--Yau as a triangulated category,
and admits the canonical object $\pi(A_{Q})$ as a cluster tilting
object.  The functor $\CoHA:(Q,W)\mapsto\CoHA(Q,W)$ factors through
$\sC(Q,W)$ at the triangulated level because CoHA cohomology depends
only on the stack of representations up to derived equivalence
(Kontsevich--Soibelman 2010, Corollary~6.3).

\subsection*{Cycle 2: cluster tilting classifies quiver presentations.}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ \emph{(Iyama--Yoshino 2008, Theorem~5.3.)}  In the
$2$-CY triangulated category $\sC(Q,W)$ the cluster tilting objects
$T$ --- maximal rigid objects with $\Ext^{1}_{\sC}(T,T) = 0$ and
$|T| = n$ summands --- are precisely the basic tilting objects whose
endomorphism algebras
\[
  A_{T}\;:=\;\End_{\sC(Q,W)}(T)
\]
are Jacobi algebras of quivers with potential $(Q_{T},W_{T})$ on the
same vertex set as $Q$, satisfying $\sC(Q_{T},W_{T})\simeq\sC(Q,W)$.
Two cluster tilting objects $T,T'$ in the same cluster category give
two quiver presentations $A_{T}, A_{T'}$ of the \emph{same} CY$_{3}$
category.  The collection of cluster tilting objects carries a free
action of the mutation groupoid with $n$ generators
$\mu_{1},\ldots,\mu_{n}$, one per vertex.  Translating to the CoHA side:
a quiver-affine chart $U$ on $\mathrm{Stab}(X)$ records the choice of
cluster tilting object $T_{U}\in\sC(Q,W)$, and the mutation groupoid
acts on charts.

\subsection*{Cycle 3: mutation as derived equivalence.}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ \emph{(Keller--Yang 2011, Theorem~3.2.)}  The
mutation $\mu_{k}T = T'$ lifts to a pair of triangle equivalences
\[
  \mu_{k}^{+}:\per(A_{Q})\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\per(A_{Q'}),\qquad
  \mu_{k}^{-}:\per(A_{Q'})\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\per(A_{Q}),
\]
restricting to equivalences $\sD_{\fd}(A_{Q})\simeq\sD_{\fd}(A_{Q'})$,
hence descending to $\sC(Q,W)\simeq\sC(Q',W')$.  Explicitly, $\mu_{k}^{+}$
is the left-twist along the spherical twist generated by the simple
$S_{k}$ --- a derived tilt in the sense of Bondal--Kapranov --- and the
potential transforms by the Derksen--Weyman--Zelevinsky mutation rule
(arXiv:0704.0649, Definition~5.5).  Jacobi-finiteness is preserved
when the pre-mutation contains no $2$-cycles, the regularity condition
Keller--Yang impose throughout.

\subsection*{Cycle 4: the induced CoHA map is a cohomological
isomorphism.}

\ClaimStatusDerivation\ Derived equivalence of CY$_{3}$ Ginzburg algebras
is preserved by the CoHA construction at the cohomological level: the
critical cohomology $H^{\bullet}(\sM(A_{Q},W_{Q}),\varphi_{\tr W_{Q}})$
and its $\mu_{k}^{+}$-twisted counterpart are related by the Saito--Li
thimble-base-change identity (Kontsevich--Soibelman 2010, Theorem~6.4),
which yields a graded algebra isomorphism
\[
  \mu_{k}^{*}:\CoHA(Q,W)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\CoHA(Q',W').
\]
The isomorphism is not canonical --- it depends on a framing of the
spherical twist on critical loci --- but is canonical up to the
Picard--Lefschetz monodromy, which acts on $\CoHA$ by inner
automorphism.  Restricting to the positive half $\Yplus(Q,W)\subset
\CoHA(Q,W)$ (the subalgebra of stable/semistable classes of positive
degree, Soibelman 2014, \S 3) gives
\[
  \mu_{k}^{*}\big|_{\Yplus}:\Yplus(Q,W)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\Yplus(Q',W'),
\]
a positive-half CoHA automorphism attached to each mutation step.
Composing mutations along a walking path $T_{U}\to T_{V}$ in the
cluster groupoid produces the overlap automorphism
$g_{UV}:\Yplus(A_{U})\to\Yplus(A_{V})$ of Wave-17 B4; path
independence up to inner automorphism is the cocycle condition.

\subsection*{Cycle 5: conifold and local $\bP^{2}$ as cluster seeds.}

\ClaimStatusDerivation\ The two examples of Wave-17 are explicit
cluster-seed transitions.  The resolved conifold $\widetilde{Y} =
\cO(-1)^{\oplus 2}\to\bP^{1}$ admits the Klebanov--Witten quiver
$(Q_{\mathrm{KW}},W_{\mathrm{KW}})$ with two vertices, four arrows, and
superpotential $W_{\mathrm{KW}} = a_{1}b_{1}a_{2}b_{2} -
a_{1}b_{2}a_{2}b_{1}$.  The flop $\widetilde{Y}\to\widetilde{Y}^{+}$
(Bridgeland 2002) is precisely the Iyama--Yoshino mutation
$\mu_{1}$ at the first vertex; Nagao 2013, Theorem~5.2, identifies the
induced derived equivalence with the flop functor, and the CoHA transfer
is the conifold wall-crossing operator
$\CoHA(\widetilde{Y}) \to \CoHA(\widetilde{Y}^{+})$ of
Morrison--Mozgovoy--Nagao--Szendr\H{o}i (arXiv:1107.5017).  Local $\bP^{2}$
carries the three-vertex Beilinson quiver $(Q_{\bP^{2}},W_{\bP^{2}})$
with nine arrows and cubic potential; the three charts of Wave-17 C2
correspond to the three cluster seeds in the orbit
$\mu_{1}\mu_{2}\mu_{3}$, the minimal cycle of the affine $\widehat{A}_{2}$
cluster algebra (Fomin--Zelevinsky 2003, Figure~4 bottom row).  The
positive-half gluing of Wave-17 C2 is thus the orbit cocycle of the
cluster-$\widehat{A}_{2}$ mutation sequence acting on
$\Yplus(\mathrm{Tot}(\omega_{\bP^{2}}))$.

\subsection*{Upshot.}

\ClaimStatusDerivation\ The positive-half gluing cocycle $\{g_{UV}\}$
on $\mathrm{Stab}(X)$ is the CoHA image of the cluster mutation
cocycle on $\sC(Q,W)$: to each path in the cluster groupoid between
cluster tilting objects is attached a $\Yplus$-automorphism, and
path-independence-up-to-inner-automorphism is precisely the Ext-group
cocycle condition of Iyama--Yoshino.  $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(X)$ is
invariant along the orbit because $\mu_{k}^{*}$ is a graded algebra
isomorphism preserving the Euler supertrace.  Global $\Yplus(X)$ is the descent
of local $\Yplus(Q_{T},W_{T})$ along the cluster groupoid, with
conifold and local $\bP^{2}$ as explicit two- and three-seed
descent data.
