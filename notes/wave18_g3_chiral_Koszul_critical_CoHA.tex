\section{Critical CoHA and Chiral Koszul Primitives}
\label{sec:fg-ks-chiral-critical}

A $3$-Calabi--Yau quiver with potential produces a critical Hall algebra. A
factorisation algebra on a Ran space has a chiral Koszul primitive complex. The
two constructions meet after a third datum is supplied: an oriented
Hall-to-factorisation comparison whose collision product is the Hall
correspondence. This is an $\Eone$ positive-half statement. Full Yangians,
vertex algebras, and braided $\Etwo$ categories require Drinfeld doubling,
centres, completions, or evaluation modules.

\subsection{Chiral Koszul primitives}
\label{subsec:fg-chiral-koszul}

Let $X$ be a smooth affine complex variety and let $\Ff$ be a cofibrant
factorisation algebra on $\Ran(X)$ in the Francis--Gaitsgory model category.
Francis--Gaitsgory chiral Koszul duality~\cite[\S 6]{FG2012} gives the
bar-cobar Quillen adjunction between chiral Lie algebras and conilpotent
chiral coalgebras. On a chosen holomorphic $E_d$ local model it is written
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:fg-quillen-equivalence}
\Bar^{E_d} :
\Chiral_{\Lie}(X)
\rightleftarrows
\coLie_d\text{-}\Fact(X)
: \coBar_{E_d}.
\end{equation}
The primitive complex extracted from this adjunction is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:fg-primitive-complex}
K_{\mathrm{FG}}(\Ff)
:=
\Prim_{\coLie_d}\!\left(\Bar^{E_d}(\Ff)\right).
\end{equation}
The complex \eqref{eq:fg-primitive-complex} is Koszul data. A Hall positive
half appears when $\Ff$ is the factorisation object supplied by a critical Hall
theory and the comparison map identifies its collision operation with Hall
convolution. At $d=3$, the native CY-to-chiral output is $\Eone$-chiral. If
$A$ denotes this $\Eone$ algebra, the $\Etwo$ layer is the centre
\[
 \cZ\!\left(\Rep^{\Eone}(A)\right)
\]
or, equivalently on constructed Hopf loci, the representation category of the
Drinfeld double.

\subsection{Critical Hall algebra}
\label{subsec:ks-critical-coha}

Let $(Q,W)$ be a quiver with potential, let
\[
 A_{Q,W}:=\C Q/(\partial_a W)_{a\in Q_1}
\]
be its Jacobi algebra, and assume that the associated Ginzburg dg algebra is
$3$-Calabi--Yau. For a dimension vector
$\mathbf d\in \Z_{\geq 0}^{Q_0}$, write $\Mm_{\mathbf d}(Q)$ for the stack of
$Q$-representations and
\[
 W_{\mathbf d}\colon \Mm_{\mathbf d}(Q)\longrightarrow \C
\]
for the trace of the potential. The critical locus is the stack of
$A_{Q,W}$-modules of dimension $\mathbf d$. With orientation data and the
standard cohomological shifts fixed, the Kontsevich--Soibelman critical
cohomological Hall algebra is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ks-critical-coha}
\CoHA(Q,W)
:=
\bigoplus_{\mathbf d}
H^{\BM,\bullet}\!\left(\Mm_{\mathbf d}(Q),\phiW_{\mathbf d}\right).
\end{equation}
Its product is the pull-push operation along the stack of short exact sequences,
with Thom-Sebastiani compatibility for vanishing cycles supplying the
multiplicativity of $\phiW$. This is an associative $\Eone$ algebra.

Davison--Meinhardt~\cite{DM2020} refine the Kontsevich--Soibelman integrality
theorem to a PBW statement. For a generic stability condition and fixed slope,
the perverse filtration on $\CoHA(Q,W)$ has associated graded algebra
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:dm-pbw}
\operatorname{gr}_{P}\CoHA(Q,W)
\cong
\operatorname{Sym}\!\left(
\BPS(Q,W)\otimes H^\bullet(\mathrm{B}\C^\times)
\right),
\end{equation}
and $\BPS(Q,W)$ carries the BPS Lie algebra structure. Thus the primitive
piece is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:dm-primitives}
\Prim\!\left(\operatorname{gr}_{P}\CoHA(Q,W)\right)
\cong
\BPS(Q,W)\otimes H^\bullet(\mathrm{B}\C^\times).
\end{equation}
Here $H^\bullet(\mathrm{B}\C^\times)=\QQ[u]$ with $|u|=2$, in the
standard cohomological normalisation.
The positive half of the Yangian-type quantum group is the Hall algebra
\eqref{eq:ks-critical-coha}, filtered by \eqref{eq:dm-pbw}; the full doubled
object needs a negative half, a Cartan part, a nondegenerate Hall pairing, and a
completion.

\subsection{Comparison theorem}
\label{subsec:comparison-theorem}

An oriented Hall factorisation datum for $(Q,W)$ consists of:
\[
\left(
\Hh_{Q,W},\;
\Theta_{\mathrm{hCS}\to\mathrm{Hall}},\;
\mathrm{or}_{Q,W},\;
P_\bullet
\right).
\]
Here $\Hh_{Q,W}$ is a factorisation object on the chosen Ran or configuration
base whose fibres are the vanishing-cycle groups in \eqref{eq:ks-critical-coha};
$\Theta_{\mathrm{hCS}\to\mathrm{Hall}}$ is an isomorphism of correspondence
diagrams over the two-point collision stratum, compatible with
Thom--Sebastiani for vanishing cycles and with the Hall flag stack;
$\mathrm{or}_{Q,W}$ is the square-root orientation datum for the virtual
canonical line; and $P_\bullet$ is the Davison--Meinhardt perverse filtration,
with Hall multiplication filtered by $P_\bullet$.

\begin{proposition}[Koszul-Hall primitive comparison]
\label{prop:koszul-hall-primitive-comparison}
Assume the oriented Hall factorisation datum above, purity of the
vanishing-cycle Hodge modules, finite type on each dimension-vector stratum,
and filtered compatibility of $\Theta_{\mathrm{hCS}\to\mathrm{Hall}}$ with the
bar and perverse filtrations. Then the bar filtration on
$K_{\mathrm{FG}}(\Hh_{Q,W})$ and the Davison--Meinhardt filtration on
$\CoHA(Q,W)$ give an associated-graded quasi-isomorphism of primitive complexes
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:koszul-hall-comparison}
\operatorname{gr}_{P}K_{\mathrm{FG}}(\Hh_{Q,W})
\xrightarrow{\;\simeq\;}
\BPS(Q,W)\otimes H^\bullet(\mathrm{B}\C^\times),
\end{equation}
and the induced associative algebra on the enveloping side is the positive half
\[
 \Yplus_{\mathrm{KS}}(Q,W):=\CoHA(Q,W).
\]
The comparison lives at the $\Eone$ Hall layer.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The factorisation product of $\Hh_{Q,W}$ over disjoint configurations is direct
sum of representations. Collision is identified by
$\Theta_{\mathrm{hCS}\to\mathrm{Hall}}$ with the flag correspondence defining
Hall multiplication. Give the Francis--Gaitsgory bar complex and the critical
Hall algebra their Rees filtrations. Filtered compatibility of
$\Theta_{\mathrm{hCS}\to\mathrm{Hall}}$ identifies the Rees differential and
the binary collision product with the Rees Hall pull-push product on the
associated graded. The bar construction sends $\Hh_{Q,W}$ to a $\coLie_3$
coalgebra, and primitives extract the indecomposable collision classes.
Purity makes the perverse filtration split on the associated graded.
Davison--Meinhardt PBW then identifies those indecomposables with
$\BPS(Q,W)\otimes H^\bullet(\mathrm{B}\C^\times)$, which is
\eqref{eq:dm-primitives}. The product statement follows from the equality of
the two pull-push correspondences under $\Theta_{\mathrm{hCS}\to\mathrm{Hall}}$.
\end{proof}

Francis--Gaitsgory duality supplies the Koszul primitive complex.
Kontsevich--Soibelman Hall theory supplies the critical Hall algebra. The
comparison requires the orientation datum and the collision-to-Hall
identification. The proposition is an associated-graded primitive comparison
and a filtered positive-Hall reconstruction. A presentation theorem for a
Drinfeld double, centre, BKM algebra, or vertex algebra has stronger
hypotheses: PBW completeness, relation preservation, a nondegenerate Hall
pairing, completion, and a recognition map preserving defining relations.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{$\C^3$}{C3}}
\label{subsec:C3}

The toric chart $\C^3$ is controlled by the one-vertex quiver with three loops
$x,y,z$ and cubic potential
\[
 W_{\C^3}=\operatorname{Tr}(x[y,z]).
\]
For dimension $n$, $W_{\C^3,n}$ is the function
$\operatorname{Tr}(X[Y,Z])$ on $\operatorname{Mat}_n(\C)^3/\GL_n$, and
$\Crit(W_{\C^3,n})$ is the commuting-triple stack. The Jacobi algebra is
\[
 \Jac(Q_{\C^3},W_{\C^3})\cong \C[x,y,z].
\]
The equivariant critical CoHA is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:c3-critical-coha}
\CoHA(\C^3)
=
\bigoplus_{n\geq 0}
H^{\BM,T}_*\!\left(
\operatorname{Mat}_n(\C)^3/\GL_n,\phiW_{\C^3,n}
\right),
\qquad
T=(\C^\times)^3,\quad
\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+\epsilon_3=0 .
\end{equation}

Schiffmann--Vasserot identify this algebra with the positive half of the affine
Yangian of $\mathfrak{gl}_1$:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sv-C3}
\CoHA(\C^3)
\cong
\Yplus(\gghat).
\end{equation}
In the shuffle model, the degree-$n$ piece is a space of symmetric rational
functions in $z_1,\ldots,z_n$, and multiplication is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:c3-shuffle-product}
(f*g)(z_1,\ldots,z_{m+n})
=
\operatorname{Sym}\!\left[
f(z_1,\ldots,z_m)g(z_{m+1},\ldots,z_{m+n})
\prod_{\substack{1\leq i\leq m\\m<j\leq m+n}}
\omega_{\C^3}(z_i-z_j)
\right].
\end{equation}
In the Jordan-quiver normalisation one writes
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:c3-shuffle-kernel}
\omega_{\C^3}(u)
=
\frac{(u+\epsilon_1)(u+\epsilon_2)(u+\epsilon_3)}
{u^3},
\qquad u=z-w.
\end{equation}
Equivalently, the numerator is the $S_3$-symmetric structure polynomial
\[
G_{\C^3}(u)=\prod_{i=1}^3(u+\epsilon_i),
\qquad
\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+\epsilon_3=0 .
\]
The generators $e_r$ in dimension $1$ map to the positive Drinfeld currents of
$\Yplus(\gghat)$. The algebra presentation is the shuffle-recognition theorem
of Schiffmann--Vasserot, in the toric CoHA form used by
Rapcak--Soibelman--Yang--Zhao~\cite{SV2013,RSYZ2020}. The graded character
supplies a character constraint; defining relations come from the shuffle
recognition theorem.

The full affine Yangian and the $\cW_{1+\infty}$ vertex layer sit beyond
\eqref{eq:sv-C3}:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:c3-layer-chain}
\CoHA(\C^3)=\Yplus(\gghat)
\longrightarrow
\DDrin^\wedge(\Yplus(\gghat))
 =Y(\gghat)=\Yplus\bowtie Y^0\bowtie Y^-
\xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{ev}_{\lambda}\ }
\operatorname{End}\!\left(\cW_{1+\infty}[\lambda]\text{-vac}\right).
\end{equation}
The first arrow is Drinfeld doubling with completion and Cartan part; the
second is evaluation on a vacuum module. The native Hall algebra remains the
positive half. Its
braided $\Etwo$ structure is read on
$\cZ(\Rep^{\Eone}(\Yplus(\gghat)))$ or on
$\Rep^{\Etwo}(Y(\gghat))$ after the double.

The toric noncompact modular characteristic recorded in the Vol III
stratification is
\[
 \kch(\C^3)=1.
\]
This value belongs to the local chain-level Hall/BCOV lane. The compact Hodge
supertrace formula is reserved for compact Calabi--Yau manifolds.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{$K3\times E$}{K3 x E} boundary data}
\label{subsec:k3e-boundary-data}

The local comparison above is a toric positive-half statement. A compact claim
for the Gritsenko $\Delta_5$ branch is a different theorem: it requires an
oriented compact Hall/CoHA atlas, a compact Hall pairing, the completed
Hall--Drinfeld double, radical descent, finite comparison maps, and a
relation-preservation theorem for the recognition map.

The constants measure different objects:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:k3e-four-kappas}
\kcat(K3\times E)=0,\qquad
\kch(K3\times E)=0,\qquad
\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)=3,\qquad
\kBKM(\Delta_5)=5,\qquad
\kfib(K3)=24.
\end{equation}
Here $\kcat(K3\times E)=\chi(\Oo_{K3})\chi(\Oo_E)=2\cdot 0$ and
\[
\kch(K3\times E)
=h^{0,0}-h^{0,1}+h^{0,2}-h^{0,3}
=1-1+1-1=0 .
\]
The value $\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}=3$ belongs to the Heisenberg--Mukai
defect specialisation, $\kBKM(\Delta_5)=5$ is the Borcherds weight
$c_1(0)/2=10/2$ of the denominator form
\cite{Borcherds1998,Gritsenko1999,GN1998}, and $\kfib=24$ is the Mukai
lattice rank of the K3 fibre. No additive law relates these four invariants.
The monic Oberdieck--Pandharipande scalar is~\cite{OP2016}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:D5-normalisation}
D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5,\qquad
\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}=D_5^2=4096^{-1}\Delta_5^2,\qquad
Z_{\mathrm{OP}}=-(\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}})^{-1}
=-4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}.
\end{equation}
For the primitive CHL rows
\[
N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\},
\qquad
c_N(0)=(10,8,6,4,2),
\]
the Borcherds weights are~\cite{Borcherds1998,Gritsenko1999,GN1998}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:primitive-chl-kappaBKM}
\kBKM(\Phi_N)=\frac{c_N(0)}{2}=(5,4,3,2,1).
\end{equation}
The Nikulin non-CHL extension rows $N\in\{5,7,8\}$ belong to a separate
paramodular atlas. In the same Borcherds-weight convention they have
\[
c_N(0)=(4,2,2),\qquad
\kBKM(\Phi_N)=(2,1,1),
\]
and therefore lie outside the primitive $K3\times E$ CHL quotient rows. The
Gritsenko--Clery catalogue is triple-indexed by $(t,N;k)$ and requires its own
row map~\cite{CleryGritsenko2011}.

The Hall--Drinfeld/BKM branch and the Mukai self-mirror branch are separate
constructions. Character agreement, a common K3 fibre, and the local
$\CoHA(\C^3)$ chart supply consistency evidence. The compact theorem requires
orientation, pairing, completion, radical descent, finite comparison maps, and
recognition inputs preserving defining relations.
