\section*{Wave-16 U5/20: Miki $\Sthree$ on the coalgebra dual of $\Yplus = \CoHA(\C^{3})$}

\subsection*{Attack-heal 1 --- $\Sthree$ on the Drinfeld double $\Ydouble$}

The affine Yangian triangular decomposition
$\Ydouble(\gghone) \cong \Yplus \otimes \Yzero \otimes \Yminus$
with $\Yzero = \C[\psi_{k}]_{k \geq 0}$ the Cartan generating function
and $\Yminus = \Yplus^{\mathrm{op}}$ via Chevalley involution
(Feigin--Jimbo--Miwa--Mukhin 2017; Tsymbaliuk 2017
\texttt{arXiv:1703.04551}) is built from structure constants depending
only on $(\epsilon_{1}, \epsilon_{2}, \epsilon_{3})$ subject to
$\epsilon_{1} + \epsilon_{2} + \epsilon_{3} = 0$.  Every
structural datum of $\Ydouble$ --- the shuffle kernel, the
double pairing $\langle -, - \rangle : \Yplus \otimes \Yminus \to
\Yzero$, the coproduct on $\Yplus$ --- is a rational function symmetric
in the $\epsilon_{i}$.  Hence $\Sthree$ acts on $\Ydouble$ by Hopf
algebra automorphisms.  On $\Yplus$ alone the action is by associative
algebra automorphisms; on $\Yzero$ by permutation of Cartan generating
series; on $\Yminus$ diagonally.  This is one action, extending
compatibly across all three factors.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 2 --- Shuffle coalgebra and $\Sthree$-equivariance}

The positive half $\Yplus$ admits the Feigin--Odesskii shuffle
presentation: $\Yplus \hookrightarrow \Sh(V) = \bigoplus_{n \geq 0}
V^{\otimes n}$ with $V = \C[\epsilon_{i}]$, where the shuffle product is
\[
  (f \star g)(x_{1}, \dots, x_{m+n})
   = \sum_{\sigma \in \mathrm{Sh}(m, n)}
       \sigma \cdot \bigl(
         f(x_{1}, \dots, x_{m}) g(x_{m+1}, \dots, x_{m+n})
         \cdot \omega(x_{i}, x_{j})
       \bigr)
\]
with kernel
\[
  \omega(x, y)
   = \frac{(x - y + \epsilon_{1})(x - y + \epsilon_{2})
           (x - y + \epsilon_{3})}
          {(x - y)^{3}}
\]
(Negut 2014 \texttt{arXiv:1302.6202}; Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013
\texttt{arXiv:1202.2756} \S4 for the CoHA presentation on $\C^{2}$
extended to $\C^{3}$ via the tripled potential of Kontsevich--Soibelman
2008).  The kernel $\omega$ is manifestly
$\Sthree$-symmetric in $(\epsilon_{1}, \epsilon_{2}, \epsilon_{3})$; so
is its reciprocal; so is the embedding $\Yplus \hookrightarrow \Sh(V)$.
Therefore $\Sthree$ acts on $\Sh(V)$ by algebra automorphisms preserving
the shuffle product and restricting to automorphisms of $\Yplus$.

The coproduct $\Delta: \Yplus \to \Yplus \hat{\otimes} \Yplus$
(the ``topological'' Drinfeld coproduct, \texttt{1703.04551} \S3) is
specified by $\Delta(e(z)) = e(z) \otimes 1 +
\psi^{-}(z) \otimes e(z)$ on the current $e(z) = \sum_{k} e_{k} z^{-k-1}$,
with $\psi^{-}(z)$ the Cartan Gauss factor.  The $\Sthree$-action on
$\Yplus$ permutes $\epsilon_{i}$ inside both $e(z)$ (via its
shuffle-algebra representation) and $\psi^{-}(z)$, compatibly.
Hence $\Delta$ is $\Sthree$-equivariant: $\Sthree$ preserves the
coalgebra structure of $\Yplus$.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 3 --- The coalgebra dual and its factorisation
structure}

Let $\Yplus{}^{\vee}$ denote the graded dual with the dualised coalgebra
structure (an associative algebra under $\Delta^{\vee}$) and the
dualised shuffle product (a coalgebra under $\star^{\vee}$).  The shuffle
algebra $\Sh(V)$ is precisely the cofree cocommutative coalgebra on $V$
equipped with a deformed product; its dual is a commutative algebra
on a dual space of distributions, isomorphic to the Hopf algebra of
``quasi-shuffle polynomial functions'' on $\mathrm{Sym}(V[z^{\pm 1}])$
(Feigin--Odesskii 1998; Enriquez 2003 \texttt{arXiv:math/0212384}).

The factorisation-algebra incarnation: $\Sh(V)^{\vee}$ is the global
sections of a constructible factorisation algebra on $\Ran(\C)$ whose
fibre at a configuration $\{x_{1}, \dots, x_{n}\}$ is
$V^{\otimes n}$ twisted by $\prod_{i < j} \omega(x_{i}, x_{j})$
(Beilinson--Drinfeld 2004 \emph{Chiral Algebras} Ch.~3.4 for the
general Ran-space setup; Gaitsgory--Lurie 2014 for the
$(\infty, 1)$-factorisation-algebra reformulation).  The factorisation
structure maps are the fusion of colliding pairs $x_{i} \to x_{j}$,
governed by $\omega$ acquiring a pole.  Under this dictionary
\[
  \Yplus{}^{\vee}
   \;\simeq\;
   \Fact_{\Ran(\C)}\bigl(V, \omega\bigr),
\]
the $\omega$-twisted chiral bialgebra on $\C$ in the Beilinson--Drinfeld
sense, chain-level (the ``quasi-coherent shuffle factorisation
algebra'').  $\Sthree$ acts on $V = \C[\epsilon_{i}]$ by permutation of
generators; $\omega$ is $\Sthree$-symmetric; hence $\Sthree$ descends to
an action on $\Fact_{\Ran(\C)}(V, \omega)$ by automorphisms of
factorisation algebras --- i.e.\ commuting with the fusion maps on
$\Ran(\C)$.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 4 --- Match to Wave-13 F5's compound chiral-Yangian}

Wave-13 F5 identifies the abelian 5D holomorphic Chern--Simons boundary
vertex algebra with $\Yaff(\gghone)$ via the five-link chain
\{Costello 2013; Costello--Gwilliam 2017 Thm 5.3.3;
Costello--Yagi 2018; Costello--Gaiotto 2018; Costello--Dimofte--Paquette
2020\}.  The $\Sthree$ present in the boundary VOA of 5D hCS is the
permutation of the three transverse $\C$-legs
$(\C_{\epsilon_{1}}, \C_{\epsilon_{2}}, \C_{\epsilon_{3}})$ under the
CY constraint $\sum \epsilon_{i} = 0$.  Under the boundary-VOA
identification, this permutation is exactly the $\Sthree$ on $\Yplus$
of Attack-heal 2, restricted to the boundary of a single $\C$-leg;
under the Costello--Gwilliam factorisation-algebra identification, it
becomes the $\Sthree$ on $\Fact_{\Ran(\C)}(V, \omega)$ of
Attack-heal 3.  The diagram
\[
  \begin{array}{ccc}
     \Yplus & \hookrightarrow & \Ydouble \\
     \Big\downarrow & & \Big\downarrow \mathrm{ev}_{\lambda} \\
     \Fact_{\Ran(\C)}(V, \omega) & \leftrightsquigarrow &
       \Winf[\lambda]
  \end{array}
\]
commutes $\Sthree$-equivariantly: the horizontal right is
Miki's 2007 automorphism on $\Winf[\lambda]$
(J.\,Math.\,Phys.~48); the horizontal left is the chain-level shuffle-to-
factorisation identification; the vertical right is the evaluation
map of Wave-15 N4; the vertical left is the coalgebra-dual embedding.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 5 --- Consequence and scope}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ ($\Sthree$-equivariance of the shuffle presentation:
elementary from the $\Sthree$-symmetry of $\omega$; the shuffle
presentation itself is theorem in Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013 and
Feigin--Odesskii 1998.)

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ ($\Sthree$-action on $\Yplus = \CoHA(\C^{3})$ by
Hopf algebra automorphisms: combine the shuffle presentation, the
Drinfeld coproduct of Tsymbaliuk 2017, and the manifest
$\Sthree$-symmetry of the coefficients.)

\ClaimStatusConjectured\ ($\Sthree$-equivariance of the compound
chiral-Yangian realisation of Wave-13 F5 at the level of factorisation
algebras on $\Ran(\C)$: the individual links are each $\Sthree$-compatible
in their own scope, but the all-orders Costello--Dimofte--Paquette 2020
identification at Link~5 is established only in the abelian case, and
its $\Sthree$-equivariance follows from the rotational symmetry of the
three $\C$-legs in 11D M-theory before twisting; the full statement
--- ``$\Sthree$ acts on $\partial \mathrm{hCS}_{5} \simeq \Yaff(\gghone)$
as a factorisation-algebra automorphism'' --- inherits the non-abelian
bottleneck of Wave-13 F5.)

Miki triality is structural for $\Yplus$-factorisation data: it acts
(i) on the Drinfeld double $\Ydouble$ by Hopf-algebra automorphisms;
(ii) on $\Yplus$ alone by shuffle-algebra automorphisms preserving
the coproduct; (iii) on the coalgebra dual
$\Fact_{\Ran(\C)}(V, \omega)$ by factorisation-algebra automorphisms.
The $\Winf[\lambda]$ triality is the
$\mathrm{ev}_{\lambda}$-image and therefore a shadow; the CoHA-level
triality is the ambient datum.  The cache rule
$\CoHA(\C^{3}) = \Yplus \neq \Winf$ stands: triality acts on the
positive half; the $\Winf[\lambda]$-quotient inherits it.

\emph{Shadow checks.} $\kchir(\C^{3}) = 3/2$ (Wave-13 F5 closing
