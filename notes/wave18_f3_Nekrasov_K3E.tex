\section*{Pandharipande--Oberdieck as a Nekrasov partition function, and $\chi(\Ypos(K3\times E))$}

\subsection*{Cycle 1 — From Nekrasov to reduced DT}

Nekrasov~2003 \texttt{hep-th/0206161} defines, for $X$ a toric
Calabi--Yau with torus $T \curvearrowright X$, the partition function
\[
  \ZNek(X;q,\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2)
  \;=\;
  \sum_{\gamma \in H_2(X,\mathbb{Z})_{\mathrm{eff}}}
  q^{\gamma}
  \int_{[\mathcal{M}_{\gamma}(X)]^{\mathrm{vir}}}
  \frac{1}{e_{T}(\mathrm{Nor}^{\mathrm{vir}})},
\]
the equivariant volume of the instanton / Hilbert-scheme moduli
stack, evaluated by Atiyah--Bott on $T$-fixed loci. On $X = \mathbb{C}^{2}$
with $\mathcal{M}_{n} = \mathrm{Hilb}^{n}(\mathbb{C}^{2})$, Nekrasov
obtained
$\sum_{n} q^{n}\, e_{T}(T \mathrm{Hilb}^{n}\mathbb{C}^{2})^{-1} =
\prod_{k\geq 1}(1-q^{k})^{-1}\cdot (\epsilon_{1}\epsilon_{2}/\hbar^{2})$
at the instanton level, recovered by Nakajima~1994 (Duke \textbf{76})
as the Fock-space character
$\chi(\mathrm{Heis})(q) = \prod(1-q^{k})^{-1}$. On $X = K3$, the
replacement is Kawai--Yoshioka~2000: $\sum \chi(\mathrm{Hilb}^{n}K3)\,
q^{n-1} = 1/\eta^{24}(\tau)$, a \emph{non-equivariant} identity on
the surface whose continuous-symmetry group is trivial.

\textbf{Flaw.} The Nekrasov prescription applied verbatim to $K3$
vanishes: the symplectic $2$-form $\sigma_{K3}$ annihilates the
naive virtual class, and $K3$ admits no nontrivial global torus.
\textbf{Heal (MPT~2010).} Reduce the obstruction sheaf by
$\sigma_{K3}$: $\mathrm{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}} =
\ker(\mathrm{Ob} \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{O})$, obtaining the
reduced virtual class $\virred$ of virtual dimension
$\mathrm{vd}+1$. The Nekrasov sum becomes the Pandharipande--Thomas
reduced DT generating series, and on $X = K3\times E$ with
K3-primitive classes $\gamma$,
\[
  \ZNek(K3\times E;\gamma,q,t,p)
  \;=\;
  \Zred(K3\times E;\gamma)(q,t,p),
\]
the right-hand side evaluated in three variables: $q$ tracks the
K3 curve class $\beta$ with $\beta^{2}= 2h - 2$, $p$ tracks the
Euler characteristic $\chi(\mathcal{F}) = n$ of the stable pair,
$t$ tracks the $E$-base degree. \textbf{Resolution.} Reduced DT is
the K3-adapted Nekrasov partition function; the MPT reduction
replaces the Atiyah--Bott residue over vanishing fixed loci.

\subsection*{Cycle 2 — $\Cstar_{E}$-equivariance as residual weight}

The continuous symmetry of $K3\times E$ is the translation
$\Cstar_{E} = \mathrm{Aut}^{0}(E)$, acting trivially on the K3-factor.
\textbf{Flaw.} $\Cstar_{E}$ is free on $E$, so naive localisation is
vacuous: no fixed points, no Atiyah--Bott input. \textbf{Heal.}
Stable pairs $I^{\bullet}_{\gamma,n}$ supported on K3-fibres are
$\Cstar_{E}$-translated among isomorphic pairs; the translation
orbit descends to moduli. The $\Cstar_{E}$-equivariant Euler class
$e_{\Cstar_{E}}(\mathrm{Nor}^{\mathrm{vir}})$ of the fibrewise
normal bundle inserts the elliptic weight $t$, and the
$\Cstar_{E}$-residual integration produces a $3$-variable
Laurent series in $(q,t,p)$. \textbf{Resolution.}
$\Cstar_{E}$ is a weight-bookkeeping device, not a
Bia\l ynicki--Birula torus; the Nekrasov $\Om$-background role
$(\epsilon_{1},\epsilon_{2})$ is replaced by the single elliptic
weight $t$ plus the reduced class.

\subsection*{Cycle 3 — The Oberdieck--Pixton theorem and the character formula}

Oberdieck--Pixton~2018 \texttt{arXiv:1706.10100} Theorem~1: on
K3-primitive classes,
\[
  \Zred(K3\times E;\gamma_{\mathrm{prim}})(q,t,p)
  \;=\;
  -\frac{1}{\PhiTen(q,t,p)},
\]
with $\PhiTen$ the Igusa cusp form of weight $10$ on
$\mathrm{Sp}_{4}(\mathbb{Z})$, expanded at the cusp
$(q,t,p)\mapsto (e^{2\pi i\tau}, e^{2\pi i z}, e^{2\pi i\sigma})$.
The positive half $\Ypos(K3\times E)$ of the K3 affine Yangian
(Schiffmann--Vasserot~2013, Maulik--Okounkov~2012) is the equivariant
cohomology
\[
  \Ypos(K3\times E)
  \;=\;
  H^{\bullet}_{\Cstar_{E}\times\Auts(K3)}
  \bigl(\Mred_{\mathrm{DT}}(K3\times E;\gamma_{\mathrm{prim}})\bigr),
\]
graded by $(\beta, n, d_{E})\in\mathbb{Z}^{3}$ through the
K3-primitive class, the Euler characteristic, and the elliptic
degree. The character of $\Ypos$ in its BPS basis is
\[
  \chi(\Ypos(K3\times E))(q,t,p)
  \;=\;
  \sum_{(\beta,n,d_{E})}
  \dim H^{\bullet}(\Mred_{\mathrm{DT}})_{\beta,n,d_{E}}
  \,q^{\beta}\,p^{n}\,t^{d_{E}}
  \;=\;
  \frac{1}{\PhiTen(q,t,p)},
\]
the sign absorbed into the standard BPS grading. \textbf{Resolution.}
The Nekrasov partition function on $K3\times E$ equals, up to sign,
the generating character of $\Ypos$:
$\ZNek(K3\times E) = -\,\chi(\Ypos(K3\times E))$.

\subsection*{Cycle 4 — Gritsenko factorisation and the Borcherds product}

$\PhiTen$ factors through the multiplicative Borcherds lift of the
Jacobi form $\phi_{10,1}$ of weight $10$ and index $1$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin~1995; Borcherds~1995). Two structural expressions:
the Gritsenko square-root,
$\PhiTen(\tau,z,\sigma) = \Delta_{5}(\tau,z,\sigma)^{2}$,
with $\Delta_{5}$ the weight-$5$ paramodular form; and the
Borcherds product
\[
  \PhiTen(\tau,z,\sigma)
  \;=\;
  q\, t\, p \cdot
  \prod_{(m,\ell,n)>0}
  (1 - q^{m}\, \zeta^{\ell}\, p^{n})^{c(4mn - \ell^{2})},
  \qquad
  \zeta = e^{2\pi i z},
\]
with exponents $c(k)$ from
$\phi_{10,1}(\tau,z) = \sum c(4n - \ell^{2})\, q^{n}\zeta^{\ell}$.
A third factorisation makes the K3 side explicit: at $(\tau, z)$
with $\sigma \to i\infty$,
\[
  \PhiTen(\tau,z,\sigma)
  \;=\;
  p\,\eta^{24}(\tau)\,
  \vartheta_{1}^{2}(\tau,z)\,
  \bigl(1 + O(p)\bigr),
\]
reproducing Kawai--Yoshioka $1/\eta^{24}$ (K3 Hilbert scheme) and
$\vartheta_{1}^{2}$ (two copies of the elliptic theta, one for each
$E$-insertion). \textbf{Resolution.} The three factorisations are
three different windows on the same modular form: square-root
$\Delta_{5}^{2}$ (paramodular structure), Borcherds product (BPS
state counting), and $p$-leading $\eta^{24}\vartheta_{1}^{2}$
(K3 Hilbert scheme times theta correction).

\subsection*{Cycle 5 — BPS matching of the three-parameter expansion}

The Borcherds exponents $c(4mn - \ell^{2})$ count BPS states in the
M-theoretic origin (Dijkgraaf--Verlinde--Verlinde~1997): on
$\mathrm{type\ IIA}/K3\times S^{1}$, BPS bound states at charge
$(\beta,n,d_{E})$ are enumerated by the $\mathcal{N} = 4$
five-dimensional BPS index, and the $K3\times E$ reduction lifts
$S^{1}\to E$. In the Nekrasov language, each BPS multiplet
contributes a Gopakumar--Vafa factor
$(1 - q^{m}\zeta^{\ell}p^{n})^{c(4mn - \ell^{2})}$, reproducing the
Borcherds product term-by-term. The K3 Yangian interpretation is
that the positive generators of $\Ypos(K3\times E)$ are BPS states
of charges $(m, \ell, n)$ with multiplicity $c(4mn - \ell^{2})$,
and their bar-dual (Vol I Theorem~A) produces the Cartan and
negative generators. The four $\kappa_{\bullet}$ invariants
crystallise on this geometry:
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)
  \;=\;
  \chi(\mathcal{O}_{K3\times E}) = 0
  \text{ (K\"unneth on total space)},
\]
\[
  \kch(K3\times E)
  \;=\;
  2\text{ (K3-fibre Hodge-supertrace shadow)},
\]
\[
  \kBKM(\PhiTen)
  \;=\;
  \frac{c_{1}(0)}{2}
  = 5
  \text{ (Gritsenko--Nikulin; Lorgat~2020 \S1 Thm.~2)},
\]
\[
  \kfib(K3)
  \;=\;
  24
  \text{ (K3 Euler number / $\eta^{24}$ exponent)}.
\]
The spectrum $\{2,3,5,24\}$ is assembled from four distinct
constructions on the same threefold, three of which project to
$\PhiTen$: the character $\chi(\Ypos) = 1/\PhiTen$ reads off
$\kch$; the Borcherds weight $c_{1}(0)/2 = 5$ reads off $\kBKM$;
the $\eta^{24}$ factor at $p$-leading order reads off $\kfib = 24$.
\textbf{Resolution.} The Nekrasov partition function on $K3\times E$
is the generating function of BPS states of $\Ypos(K3\times E)$;
the Borcherds product is the bar--cobar denominator identity; the
four $\kappa_{\bullet}$ values are four distinct numerical shadows
of the same geometry.
