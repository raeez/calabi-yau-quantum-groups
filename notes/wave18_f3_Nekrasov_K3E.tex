\section*{The reduced Nekrasov character of $K3\times E$}

Let $S$ be a nonsingular projective K3 surface, let $E$ be an elliptic
curve, and put $X=S\times E$.  The toric Nekrasov partition function is
an equivariant integral over an instanton or sheaf moduli space in an
$\Omega$-background.  On $S\times E$ the holomorphic symplectic form on
$S$ gives a cosection of the ordinary obstruction sheaf, so the ordinary
virtual class is replaced by the Maulik--Pandharipande--Thomas reduced
class.  The free translation orbit of $E$ is divided out before
integration.

For a primitive class $\beta\in H_2(S,\mathbb Z)$ with
$\beta^2=2h-2$, elliptic degree $d$, and stable-pair Euler
characteristic $n$, define
\[
  \mathrm{PT}^{\mathrm{red}}_{h,d,n}(S\times E)
  =
  \int_{[P_n(S\times E,\beta+d[E])/E]^{\mathrm{red}}}1 .
\]
The three variables record the three primitive charges:
$q^{h-1}$ records the K3 norm, $\zeta^n$ records the stable-pair Euler
characteristic, and $p^d$ records the elliptic degree.  The reduced
Nekrasov character of $S\times E$ is
\[
  \ZNek^{\mathrm{red}}(S\times E)
  =
  \Zred(S\times E)
  =
  \sum_{h,d,n}
  \mathrm{PT}^{\mathrm{red}}_{h,d,n}(S\times E)\,
  q^{h-1}\zeta^n p^d .
\]

\paragraph{Scalar theorem.}
In the primitive quotient-by-$E$ Oberdieck--Pandharipande convention,
\[
  \Zred(S\times E;q,\zeta,p)
  =
  -\bigl(\PhiOP(q,\zeta,p)\bigr)^{-1}.
\]
The automorphic normalisations are
\[
  \Dfive=64^{-1}\DeltaFive,\qquad
  \PhiOP=\Dfive^2=4096^{-1}\DeltaFive^2,\qquad
  \Phiun=\DeltaFive^2 .
\]
Therefore
\[
  \boxed{\;
  \Zred(S\times E)
  =
  -(\PhiOP)^{-1}
  =
  -\Dfive^{-2}
  =
  -4096\,\DeltaFive^{-2}.
  \;}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
Oberdieck--Pandharipande compute the reduced stable-pair series on
$K3\times E$ as the inverse of the monic Igusa product, with the
primitive quotient by the elliptic translation factor.  Oberdieck--
Pixton prove the corresponding primitive reduced Gromov--Witten Igusa
cusp-form theorem; the reduced GW/PT comparison preserves the displayed
normalisation.  Gritsenko--Nikulin's theta-normalised square root
$\DeltaFive$ has leading theta coefficient $64$, while $\Dfive$ is the
monic Borcherds product.  Hence $\Dfive=64^{-1}\DeltaFive$ and
$\PhiOP=\Dfive^2=4096^{-1}\DeltaFive^2$.  Inverting the OP product and
including the stable-pair sign gives the boxed formula.

\subsection*{Positive Hall character}

Assume compact orientation data for the reduced critical CoHA of
$S\times E$: an orientation line, proper Hall correspondences on the
completed support, a coproduct, a non-degenerate Hopf pairing, a
closed radical quotient, PBW compatibility, and centre/Fock evaluation
data.  The positive Hall half is then
\[
  \Ypos_{S\times E}
  =
  H^\bullet_{\mathrm{red}}
  \bigl(P_\bullet(S\times E,\beta+d[E])/E,\varphi_{\mathrm{DT}}\bigr)
\]
with Kontsevich--Soibelman convolution and K3 cosection-reduced
cohomology of vanishing cycles.  Its signed Euler character is
\[
  \chi_{\mathrm{sgn}}\bigl(\Ypos_{S\times E}\bigr)(q,\zeta,p)
  =
  \Zred(S\times E;q,\zeta,p)
  =
  -(\PhiOP)^{-1}.
\]
This statement is a character identity.  The Hall bracket, the
negative half, the Hopf pairing, and the completed Hall--Drinfeld
double are the additional algebraic recognition data carried by the
orientation package.

The affine toric comparison fixes the layer separation:
\[
  \CoHA(\mathbb C^3)\cong \Ypos(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1),
\]
the Schiffmann--Vasserot positive half.  The full affine Yangian and
the $\mathcal W_{1+\infty}$ vacuum module arise from Drinfeld doubling,
derived-centre passage, completion, and evaluation.  For compact
$K3\times E$, the enumerative layer is the Hall positive half; the BKM
layer is the completed Hall--Drinfeld double.  The Mukai self-mirror
Yangian branch is a separate K3 construction.

\subsection*{Borcherds denominator}

Let
\[
  \phi_{0,1}^{K3}(\tau,z)
  =
  \sum_{n,\ell}c_1(4n-\ell^2)q^n\zeta^\ell
\]
be the K3 weak Jacobi input.  Its constant term is
\[
  [q^0\zeta^0]\phi_{0,1}^{K3}=c_1(0)=10.
\]
The Gritsenko--Nikulin product attached to this input is the
weight-$5$ Siegel form
\[
  \DeltaFive=\mathrm{Grit}(\eta^9\vartheta_1).
\]
The monic Borcherds product is
\[
  \Dfive(Z)
  =
  q^{1/2}\zeta^{1/2}p^{1/2}
  \prod_{(m,\ell,n)>0}
  \bigl(1-q^m\zeta^\ell p^n\bigr)^{c_1(4mn-\ell^2)},
  \qquad
  Z=(\tau,z,\sigma),
\]
and $\DeltaFive=64\Dfive$.  The denominator of the primitive BKM
superalgebra $\mathfrak g_{\DeltaFive}$ is the single product
$\Dfive$; in Weyl-character variables the same denominator is often
written $\Dfive(2Z)$ because the exponent convention changes from
$e^{-\pi i(\alpha,Z)}$ to $e^{-2\pi i(\alpha,Z)}$.

Borcherds' singular theta lift gives the product weight as one half of
the constant coefficient of the weight-zero Jacobi input.  Hence
\[
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\DeltaFive})
  =
  \frac{c_1(0)}{2}
  =
  5 .
\]
The Igusa square $\Phiun=\DeltaFive^2$ and the OP scalar
$\PhiOP=\Dfive^2$ are square-level enumerative forms.  The primitive
BKM denominator is the square root $\Dfive$.

For the primitive CHL denominator rows,
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
N & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 6\\
\hline
c_N(0) & 10 & 8 & 6 & 4 & 2\\
\kBKM(\Phi_N) & 5 & 4 & 3 & 2 & 1
\end{array}
\]
and in every row
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_N)=\frac{c_N(0)}{2}.
\]
The Nikulin-order rows $N\in\{5,7,8\}$ and the Gritsenko--Clery
diagonal-divisor rows belong to separate automorphic catalogues with
their own cover-group and recognition data; they are separate from the
primitive compact CHL ladder above.

\subsection*{Subscripted invariants on $K3\times E$}

The compact chiral supertrace is the Hodge supertrace:
\[
  \kch(S\times E)
  =
  \sum_{q=0}^3(-1)^q h^{0,q}(S\times E).
\]
By Kunneth,
\[
  H^{0,\bullet}(S\times E)
  =
  H^{0,\bullet}(S)\otimes H^{0,\bullet}(E),
  \qquad
  (h^{0,0},h^{0,1},h^{0,2},h^{0,3})=(1,1,1,1).
\]
Thus
\[
  \kch(S\times E)=1-1+1-1=0 .
\]
The categorical Euler invariant is the holomorphic Euler
characteristic of the total space:
\[
  \kcat(S\times E)
  =
  \chi(\mathcal O_{S\times E})
  =
  \chi(\mathcal O_S)\chi(\mathcal O_E)
  =
  2\cdot0
  =
  0 .
\]
The Heisenberg--Mukai specialisation uses a different chiral trace:
the Mukai/Nakajima component contributes $2$ and the elliptic current
contributes $1$, so
\[
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(S\times E)=2+1=3.
\]
Since $X$ has Calabi--Yau dimension $3$, the native object
$A_{S\times E}$ is $E_1$-chiral.  Braided $E_2$ structure lives on
$Z(\Rep(A_{S\times E}))$, on the completed double, or on the associated
module-centre layer.

The BKM invariant is the automorphic weight of the primitive
denominator:
\[
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\DeltaFive})=\frac{10}{2}=5.
\]
The fibre invariant is the Mukai-lattice rank of the K3 fibre:
\[
  \kfib(S)=\operatorname{rk}\widetilde H(S,\mathbb Z)=1+22+1=24.
\]
The four constructions therefore give
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|l}
\text{object} & \text{invariant} & \text{value}\\
\hline
S\times E & \kcat & 0\\
S\times E & \kch & 0\\
S\times E & \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}} & 3\\
\mathfrak g_{\DeltaFive} & \kBKM & 5\\
S & \kfib & 24
\end{array}
\]
with numerical spectrum $\{0,3,5,24\}$.  The fibre number
$\chi(\mathcal O_S)=2$ is the holomorphic Euler characteristic of the
K3 fibre; it is absent from the compact total-space values
$\kcat(S\times E)$ and $\kch(S\times E)$.

At $N=1$ the proposed additive formula is contradicted by the two sides:
\[
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\DeltaFive})
  =
  5,
  \qquad
  \kch(S\times E)+\chi(\mathcal O_E)
  =
  0+0 .
\]
The equality governing the BKM side is the Borcherds formula
$\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$.

\subsection*{Primary anchors}

Nekrasov's instanton partition function supplies the equivariant
$\Omega$-background model for toric localization.  Nakajima's
Heisenberg action on Hilbert schemes and Kawai--Yoshioka's K3
Hilbert-scheme formula supply the surface Fock-space input.
Maulik--Pandharipande--Thomas give the K3 reduced obstruction theory.
Oberdieck--Pandharipande compute the reduced $K3\times E$ pairs
partition function in the Igusa cusp-form normalisation, and
Oberdieck--Pixton prove the primitive reduced Gromov--Witten Igusa
theorem.  Gritsenko--Nikulin construct the primitive denominator
$\DeltaFive$ and the monic product $\Dfive=64^{-1}\DeltaFive$; Gritsenko
gives the primitive CHL constants.  Schiffmann--Vasserot identify
$\CoHA(\mathbb C^3)$ with the positive half
$\Ypos(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$.  The compact $S\times E$ scalar
character uses these source results together with the compact
orientation and Hall-recognition data stated above.
