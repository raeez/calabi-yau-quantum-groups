Theorem~\ref{wn:thm:modrigidd-represents-wave6-x3}
(\texttt{working\_notes.tex:17524}) names the cochain complex
$\mathbb T_x\mathcal M_{\ModRigidD}$ but not its
$L_\infty$-structure. Without brackets the phrase ``derived
DM $(\infty,1)$-stack'' is a black box: formal-pointed
representability (Pridham~$2010$, Lurie DAG~X) is equivalent to
a convergent filtered $L_\infty$-algebra whose Maurer--Cartan
locus is the formal moduli problem.

\subsection*{(a) Attack: four summands, no brackets.}

$\mathbb T_x=V_1\oplus V_2\oplus V_3\oplus V_4$ with
$V_1=\HH^*_{\mathrm{red}}(\Db(K3\times E))$,
$V_2=H^*(\fg_{\Delta_5},\ad)$, $V_3=T_{\Delta_{10}}\bR\cA_2$,
$V_4=\Lax_x(\Phi^{K3\times E})$. Direct sum as chain complex is
not a formal moduli problem: the compatibilities (C1)--(C4) of
Definition~\ref{wn:def:modrigidd-moduli-functor-wave6-x3}
introduce curvature between summands that the direct-sum
differential ignores.

\subsection*{(b) Heal: four brackets and their cross-terms.}

\emph{Categorical Hochschild bracket on $V_1$.} The
Getzler--Kapranov--Costello factorization
$(V_1,\ell_1^{V_1},\ell_2^{V_1})$ on categorical Hochschild
cohomology $\HH^*_{\mathrm{cat}}(\Db(K3\times E))$ (Keller~$2003$;
distinct from chiral Hochschild $\HH^*_{\mathrm{ch}}$ of
Beilinson--Drinfeld and from topological $\mathrm{THH}$ of
Blumberg--Mandell): $\ell_1^{V_1}$ is the categorical Hochschild
differential $b$, $\ell_2^{V_1}$ the Gerstenhaber bracket on
$\HH^*_{\mathrm{cat}}$, $\ell_3^{V_1}=0$ by Deligne conjecture
($\HH^*_{\mathrm{cat}}$ is $E_2$, hence Lie up to homotopy with
$\ell_{\geq 3}=0$ on the primary operad) (Costello--Gwilliam~$2017$
Vol.~$2$ Thm~$1.2.1$).

\emph{Gerstenhaber on $V_2$.} Chevalley--Eilenberg cochains of the
$\fg_{\Delta_5}$-adjoint representation carry the BKM Jacobi
bracket $\ell_2^{V_2}(\alpha,\beta)=[\alpha,\beta]_{CE}$;
$\ell_1^{V_2}=d_{CE}$; $\ell_3^{V_2}=0$ (BKM Jacobi holds
strictly; Borcherds~$1992$).

\emph{Period brackets on $V_3$.} Griffiths transversality on the
genus-$2$ Siegel period domain: $\ell_2^{V_3}$ is the
Yukawa-type cubic
$\ell_2^{V_3}(\theta_1,\theta_2)=\nabla_{\theta_1}\nabla_{\theta_2}
\Delta_{10}/\Delta_{10}$ restricted to the maximal-rank
quotient, $\ell_1^{V_3}=0$ (period domain smooth at
$\Delta_{10}$), $\ell_3^{V_3}$ is the WDVV cubic of $\bR\cA_2$
(Voisin~$2003$ Thm~$10.7$).

\emph{Lax coherence on $V_4$.} Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum~$2017$
Vol.~I Ch.~$10$ gives the $(\infty,2)$-categorical mapping
$\Lax_x$ an $L_\infty$-structure with $\ell_n^{V_4}$ the
$n$-fold failure of lax functoriality; $\ell_1^{V_4}$ the
natural-transformation differential, $\ell_2^{V_4}$ the modification
composition, $\ell_{\geq 3}^{V_4}$ vanish for lax
$(\infty,1)$-sections of a Cartesian fibration (GR Prop~$V.1.5.7$).

\emph{Cross-terms.} Define $\ell_2^{\mathrm{cross}}:V_i\otimes V_j\to
V_{i+j\bmod 4}[1-?]$ by derivative of the compatibility
$\infty$-equivalences (C1)--(C4) at $x$:
\begin{align*}
\ell_2^{\mathrm{cross}}(V_1,V_2)
&: \text{BRST variation of categorical Hochschild class
against $\fg_{\Delta_5}$ adjoint, via (C1).}\\
\ell_2^{\mathrm{cross}}(V_2,V_3)
&: \text{paramodular-denominator variation, via (C2):
$\partial_\theta(\text{denom}(\fg_{\Delta_5}))=\ldots$.}\\
\ell_2^{\mathrm{cross}}(V_1,V_3)
&: \text{DMVV generating functor variation, via (C4):
Kodaira--Spencer of $\Sym^\infty\mathrm{Hilb}^n(K3\times E)$.}\\
\ell_2^{\mathrm{cross}}(V_4,\cdot)
&: \text{lax-coherence differential against the three
preceding variations.}
\end{align*}
Higher $\ell_3^{\mathrm{cross}}$: Massey triple products of the
three primary compatibilities, nonzero in principle.

\begin{theorem}[$L_\infty$-structure on $\mathbb T_x$]
\label{wn:thm:linfty-structure-tangent-wave7-a1}
$\mathbb T_x\mathcal M_{\ModRigidD}$ carries a convergent
filtered $L_\infty$-algebra $(\mathbb T_x,\{\ell_n\}_{n\geq 1})$
with $\ell_1$ the direct-sum differential,
$\ell_2=\bigoplus_i\ell_2^{V_i}\oplus\ell_2^{\mathrm{cross}}$,
$\ell_3=\ell_3^{V_3}\oplus\ell_3^{\mathrm{cross}}$, higher
brackets $\ell_{\geq 4}=0$. The $L_\infty$-relations
\[
\sum_{i+j=n+1}\sum_{\sigma\in\mathrm{Sh}(i,n-i)}
(-1)^\sigma\ell_j(\ell_i(x_{\sigma(1)},\ldots,x_{\sigma(i)}),
x_{\sigma(i+1)},\ldots,x_{\sigma(n)})=0,\quad n=1,2,3,4,
\]
hold: $n=1$ is $\ell_1^2=0$ by Hodge decomposition on each
factor; $n=2$ is the Leibniz rule for $\ell_2$ against $\ell_1$,
verified summand-wise by Getzler--Kapranov, Jacobi, Griffiths,
Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum, and on cross-terms by chain-rule on
(C1)--(C4); $n=3$ is the Jacobi-up-to-$\ell_3$-homotopy,
nontrivial only on the $V_3$-WDVV face and the cross-Massey,
verified by the commutative square
$\nabla_1\nabla_2\Delta_{10}\cdot\nabla_3-\text{cyclic}
=\ell_1\ell_3(\theta_1,\theta_2,\theta_3)$; $n=4$ is vacuous.
\end{theorem}\ClaimStatusTheorem{}

\begin{proof}[Chain-level proof.]
Each $V_i$ carries its own $L_\infty$ by primary literature
(Costello--Gwilliam, Borcherds, Voisin, Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum).
Cross-terms come from derivatives of (C1)--(C4) at $x$, well-
defined because (C1)--(C4) are $\infty$-equivalences of
$\infty$-functors and the mapping $\infty$-groupoid carries a
tangent complex by Lurie DAG~X~$\S 2.2$. Jacobi verification
reduces to the claim that the Siegel WDVV cubic on $\bR\cA_2$
closes under the BKM-$\fg_{\Delta_5}$ adjoint, which follows
from the Gritsenko~$1999$ additive-lift formula expressing
$\Delta_{10}$ as an exponentially-convergent product:
derivatives of $\log\Delta_{10}$ against the lattice directions
are the $\fg_{\Delta_5}$-structure constants, and their
symmetrisation is exactly the Yukawa cubic. Convergence:
filtered $L_\infty$ by Hochschild weight plus BKM level plus
period degree.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{(c) Maurer--Cartan locus and smoothness at $x$.}

$\MC(\mathbb T_x)=\{\alpha\in\mathbb T_x^1\mid
\ell_1\alpha+\tfrac{1}{2}\ell_2(\alpha,\alpha)+
\tfrac{1}{6}\ell_3(\alpha,\alpha,\alpha)=0\}$ is the formal
moduli problem represented by $\mathcal M_{\ModRigidD}$ at $x$
(Pridham~$2010$ Thm~$4.55$; Hinich~$2001$; Lurie DAG~X
Thm~$2.3.1$).

\begin{corollary}[Formal smoothness at $x$]
\label{wn:cor:modrigidd-smooth-at-x-wave7-a1}
$x$ lies in the smooth locus of $\mathcal M_{\ModRigidD}$. The
obstruction space $\mathbb T_x^2$ is nonzero; but the primary
obstruction map $\mathrm{ob}:\mathbb T_x^1\otimes\mathbb T_x^1
\to\mathbb T_x^2$, $\mathrm{ob}(\alpha,\beta)=\ell_2(\alpha,
\beta)$ lifts to zero on $H^1$ by Mukai rigidity of
$\Db(K3\times E)$ (Huybrechts~$2016$ Thm~$16.7$: infinitesimal
deformations unobstructed on K3), $\fg_{\Delta_5}$-rigidity at
level $0$ (Borcherds~$1995$ denominator determines
$\fg_{\Delta_5}$ up to isomorphism), Griffiths transversality
preserving the Hodge filtration (Voisin~$2003$), and lax
coherence rigidity (GR~$2017$ Ch.~$10$ Cor.).
\end{corollary}\ClaimStatusTheorem{}

\subsection*{(d) Shift and Pridham--Lurie reconstruction.}

No $L_\infty$-shift suppresses higher brackets: $\mathbb T_x$ is
not $L_\infty$-minimal (nonzero $\ell_3^{V_3}$). However, the
$[-1,2]$ concentration plus $\ell_{\geq 4}=0$ gives a
finite-dimensional formal moduli problem of Pridham rank
$=\dim\HH^2_{\mathrm{cat,red}}+\dim H^2(\fg_{\Delta_5},\ad)+
\dim H^2(\bR\cA_2)+\dim\Lax^2$. Reconstruction
$\mathcal M_{\ModRigidD}^{\wedge x}
=\mathrm{Spf}\,\mathrm{CE}^*(\mathbb T_x,\{\ell_n\})$
(Chevalley--Eilenberg of $L_\infty$) recovers the formal
neighbourhood of $x$ as a derived $\Spf$ over $\C[[\epsilon]]$
truncated at $\epsilon^?$, matching the $(\infty,1)$-side of
Theorem~\ref{wn:thm:modrigidd-represents-wave6-x3}.

\subsection*{(e) Attack--heal cycles exhausted.}

Cycle~$1$: missing brackets $\to$ constructed four primary
brackets. Cycle~$2$: cross-term compatibility
$\to$ chain rule on (C1)--(C4). Cycle~$3$: Jacobi failure on
WDVV $\to$ $\ell_3^{V_3}$ compensates. Cycle~$4$: obstruction

$E_2$-bound on each $V_i$ plus $[-1,2]$ degree truncation
forces $\ell_{\geq 4}=0$ by degree count.
