\section{Seven arithmetic witnesses for $\rCY$ on $K3\times E$}
\label{rcy:sec:seven-arithmetic-witnesses}

Let $X=K3\times E$.  A face of $\rCY$ in this note is a typed
witness:
\[
(\hbox{construction},\ \hbox{input data},\ \hbox{invariant},\
\hbox{justification}).
\]
The common symbol $X$ supplies the geometric host.  Each row names the
construction that produces its invariant.

\subsection{Constants fixed by the source}
\label{rcy:subsec:constants}

The source constants are
\[
\kcat(X)=0,\qquad
\kch(\cA_X)=0,\qquad
\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(X)=3,\qquad
\kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=5,\qquad
\kfib(X)=24.
\]
The two zeros come from different constructions: $\kcat(X)$ is the
holomorphic Euler characteristic of the total space, and
$\kch(\cA_X)$ is the compact Hodge supertrace.  The value
$\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}=3$ is a chiral Heisenberg--Mukai
specialisation.  The value $\kBKM=5$ is the Borcherds weight of the
primitive denominator $\Delta_5$.  The value $\kfib=24$ is the rank of
the Mukai lattice of the K3 fibre.

For the primitive CHL ladder
\[
N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\},\qquad
c_N(0)=(10,8,6,4,2),
\]
the Borcherds characteristic is
\[
\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2=(5,4,3,2,1).
\]
The normalisations used in the $N=1$ reduced-DT comparison are
\[
D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5,\qquad
\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_5^2,\qquad
\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}=D_5^2=4096^{-1}\Delta_5^2.
\]
Thus $\Delta_5$ is the primitive BKM denominator; the OP scalar is the
square of the monic product.

\subsection{Specialisation scope}
\label{rcy:subsec:specialisation-scope}

Only faces (E), (B), and (T) contain a concrete
$(\Sigma_2,C)$ specialisation input.  Face (C) is intrinsic to the total
CY$_3$.  Face (M) is fibre-lattice data.  Face (H) is boundary
monodromy in the genus-$2$ automorphic parameter space.  Face (N) is a
lattice atlas.

For $d=3$, the output of the CY-to-chiral construction after
specialisation is $\Eone$-chiral.  Any $\Etwo$ structure belongs to a
derived centre, a representation category, or a $d\leq2$ construction;
it lives away from the native $d=3$ algebra $A$.

\subsection{Separate formulas}
\label{rcy:subsec:separate-formulas}

The Borcherds weight is read from the principal-part constant:
\[
\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2.
\]
The expression $c_N(0)/2$ is the complete formula for this row.  At
$N=1$, the Borcherds side gives $5$; the compact total-space
supertrace and the elliptic fibre Euler characteristic both vanish.
The K3 fibre value $\kcat(K3)=2$ and the Heisenberg--Mukai
specialisation value $\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}=3$ belong to separate
constructions.

\subsection{Two quantum-group sources}
\label{rcy:subsec:quantum-group-sources}

The compact Hall branch has source
\[
\mathbf H_{\Delta_5}\quad\hbox{is the conditional Hall--Drinfeld
target attached to }K3\times E,
\]
and the Mukai branch has source the rank-$24$ Mukai--Heisenberg current
algebra on K3.  A strict finite-rank Yangian presentation requires
Kac--Moody input and a split Manin triple.  The BKM algebra
$\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5}$ has imaginary simple roots, and the
Hall--Borcherds target is governed by a Manin-pair completion.

The toric comparison obeys the same type separation:
\[
\mathrm{CoHA}(\mathbb C^3)=Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1).
\]
The full affine Yangian and the $\mathcal W_{1+\infty}$ vertex
algebra enter only after the Drinfeld double, Cartan currents, negative
half, and evaluation data are supplied.

\subsection{Six routes and seven witnesses}
\label{rcy:subsec:six-routes-seven-witnesses}

The six routes to $G(K3\times E)$ are six different constructions:
Schiffmann--Vasserot CoHA, Maulik--Okounkov stable envelopes,
Gritsenko--Nikulin Borcherds lift, Costello--Paquette holography,
Bridgeland--Smith autoequivalence input, and Fourier--Jacobi
genus escalation.  Each route has its own input construction and
comparison map.

The seven witnesses above are an arithmetic slicing of $\rCY$ on
$K3\times E$.  They share a geometric host.  A single theorem emerges
only after the Hall source, nearby-cycle, and Niemeier row-map
constructions are supplied.
