\section{Holomorphic Chern--Simons Boundary Algebras}

\subsection{3d holomorphic CS $\to$ Kac--Moody (Costello--Gwilliam)}

The boundary of holomorphic Chern--Simons theory on $\C \times \R$
is the affine vertex algebra $V_k(\frakg)$. In the Beilinson--Drinfeld
language this is $E_\infty$-chiral along the curve; the ordered
boundary refinement used in Vol~II is $E_1$ in the transverse real
direction. Proposition~\ref{prop:hcs-as-ce-cochains} identifies
Chevalley--Eilenberg cochains with bulk observables. At critical level
the Feigin--Frenkel center is $\Fun(\Op_{G^L}(D))$.

\subsection{5d holomorphic CS $\to$ affine Yangian (Costello 2013)}

Theorem~\ref{thm:5d-boundary-yangian} gives the boundary affine Yangian
in the $\Omega$-background with parameters
$h_1,h_2,h_3$ satisfying $h_1+h_2+h_3=0$. Its structure function is
\[
 g(u)=\prod_i(u-h_i)\big/\prod_i(u+h_i).
\]
The $\C^2$ factorization algebra is $E_2$-chiral, while projection to a
curve is $E_1$-chiral. The associative positive half is
$Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)=\CoHA(\C^3)$; the full Yangian is obtained by
Drinfeld doubling.

\subsection{Proch\'azka--Rap\v{c}\'ak: $Y(\widehat{\fgl}_1) \simeq W_{1+\infty}$}

The Proch\'azka--Rap\v{c}\'ak identification sends the full affine
Yangian to $W_{1+\infty}$ at $c=1$. The vacuum module contributes
$P(q)$, and the Drinfeld double character is
$M(q)^2P(q)$ as in Theorem~\ref{thm:drinfeld-center-coha}(iii).

\subsection{Drinfeld center passage}

Theorem~\ref{thm:drinfeld-center-coha} identifies
\[
 \cZ(\Rep^{E_1}(Y^+))\simeq \Rep^{E_2}(Y(\widehat{\fgl}_1)).
\]
Thus the CY3 algebra remains $E_1$ at the positive-half level, and the
$E_2$ braiding enters through the center/double. The half-braiding on
charge-$1$ and charge-$2$ Fock modules gives the universal $R$-matrix,
with unitarity $R_{12}(u)R_{21}(-u)=1$ and classical limit
$r(u)=-\sigma_2/u$.

\subsection{Maulik--Okounkov $R$-matrix comparison}

Theorem~\ref{thm:mo-chiral-rmatrix} compares the chiral $R$-matrix with
the Maulik--Okounkov stable-envelope $R$-matrix. The diagonal
eigenvalues have the form
\[
 \prod_{s\in\lambda}g(u+c(s)),
\]
and the comparison is checked by the Hilbert-scheme calculation,
unitarity, the Yang--Baxter equation, crossing symmetry, the classical
$r$-matrix, and the Schiffmann--Vasserot specialization. The toric
$\C^3$ case and the $K3\times E$ case via $\Hilb^n(K3)$ are the two
current geometric witnesses.

\subsection{$E_1$-chiral bialgebra axioms (Section 7 of e1\_chiral\_algebras.tex)}

An $E_1$-chiral Hopf algebra
$(A,\mu,\Delta_z,\epsilon,\eta,S)$ consists of an augmented
$E_1$-chiral algebra in $\mathsf{M}^{E_1}_C$, a $z$-parametrized
coalgebra with deconcatenation, bialgebra compatibility of
$\Delta_z$, spectral coassociativity
\[
(\Delta_w\times\id)\Delta_{z+w}=(\id\times\Delta_z)\Delta_w,
\]
and the Hopf axiom
\[
\mu(S\times\id)\Delta_0=\eta\epsilon=\mu(\id\times S)\Delta_0.
\]
Spectral coassociativity encodes the Yang--Baxter equation, and the
Hopf axiom determines $S$ on connected algebras. The averaging functor
$\mathrm{av}\colon E_1\text{-}\mathrm{ChirHopf}\to
E_\infty\text{-}\mathrm{ChirCoalg}$ forgets the $R$-matrix, the
antipode, and the $z$-dependence.

\subsection{Chiral coproduct spin-2}

The spin-$2$ chiral coproduct
\[
 \Delta_{\mathrm{ch}}\colon A^!\to A^!\widehat{\otimes}A^!
\]
deviates from primitivity by the Dimofte $K$-matrix $K_A(z)$. The
complexity of $K_A(z)$ is the diagnostic distinguishing the
$\mathsf{G}/\mathsf{L}/\mathsf{C}/\mathsf{M}$ shadow classes.

\subsection{$E_n$ hierarchy from holomorphic CS}

The holomorphic Chern--Simons pipeline is
\[
 \mathrm{holCS}(\C^n)\longrightarrow A\longrightarrow B(A)
 \longrightarrow A^!\longrightarrow \Rep^{E_2}(A^!).
\]
The accompanying $E_n$ tower supplies $E_1$ stabilization, Bott
periodicity obstructions in dimensions $1,\ldots,16$, the $d=4$
Pontryagin obstruction, and the $d=5$ $\Z/2$ symplectic refinement.

\subsection{Universal bar cohomology formula $(1+t)^{3g}$ for classes L, C}

For the $E_3$ bar complex of class $\geq\mathsf{L}$ chiral algebras
with $g$ generators per direction,
\[
 H^\bullet(B_{E_3}(A))=(1+t)^{3g}=H^\bullet(T^{3g}),
 \qquad \dim H^\bullet=2^{3g}.
\]
Class $\mathsf{G}$ is the formal Heisenberg boundary case with
$H^\bullet=P(q)^3$, class $\mathsf{L}$ has
$(1+t)^3=H^\bullet(T^3)$, and class $\mathsf{C}$ has
$(1+t)^6=H^\bullet(T^6)$. The three $E_3$ differentials
$d_1,d_2,d_3$ replace one direction at a time by its Koszul dual.

\subsection{K3 Yangian degree-24 structure function}

The K3 Yangian $Y(\frakg_{K3})$ has structure function
\[
 g_{K3}(z)=\prod_{i=1}^{24}\frac{z-h_i}{z+h_i},
\]
with Mukai-lattice parameters $h_i$ satisfying the CY$_2$ constraint
$\sum_i h_i=0$. The Mukai lattice has signature $(4,20)$ and
$\Lambda_{\mathrm{Muk}}=U^3+E_8(-1)^2$. A Kummer parametrization takes
$h_i=\epsilon$ for $i=1,\ldots,4$ and $h_i=-\epsilon/5$ for
$i=5,\ldots,24$, giving Mukai norm $16/5$. The structure function
satisfies $g(z)g(-z)=1$; the diagonal $R$-matrix entries are
$R_{ii}(z)=(z-h_i)/(z+h_i)$ and the classical limit is
$r_{ii}=-2h_i$. Degree $24$ reflects the full Mukai lattice rank.

\subsection{The Kummer route}

Conjecture~\ref{conj:kummer-route} computes the K3 chiral algebra by
equivariant factorization homology on $T^4/\Z_2$. The five pieces are:
the rank-$4$ Heisenberg algebra $H_4$ on $T^4$ with eight generators,
the $\Z_2$ orbifold $H_4^{\Z_2}$ with sixteen twisted sectors, the
singular Kummer orbifold algebra
\[
 \frac{1}{\prod(1-q^n)^8}+16q^{1/2}\frac{1}{\prod(1-q^n)^2},
\]
the $A_1$ resolution of sixteen singularities
$40-16=24=\dim H^\bullet(K3,\C)$, and the resolved algebra
$A_{K3}^{\mathrm{Kum}}=H_8+16R_i/\mathrm{gluing}$.
The orbifold value $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(A^{\mathrm{orb}})=2$
already matches the smooth K3 categorical value.

\subsection{Center-hocolim obstruction}

Proposition~\ref{prop:center-hocolim} states that the Drinfeld center
does not commute with homotopy colimits. For a CY$_3$ atlas
$\{(Q_\alpha,W_\alpha)\}$,
\[
 \cZ\!\left(\hocolim_\alpha \Rep^{E_1}(\CoHA_\alpha)\right)
 \not\simeq
 \hocolim_\alpha \cZ(\Rep^{E_1}(\CoHA_\alpha)).
\]
The obstruction is $0$ for $\C^3$, since there is a single chart; it is
$2$ for the conifold, where the global center has dimension $1$ and
the chartwise hocolim has dimension $3$; it is nonzero for local
$\mathbb{P}^2$; and for $K3\times E$ it is controlled by the full BKM
superalgebra. Hence the $E_2$-braided category is a global center/double
construction, not a chartwise assembly.

\subsection{MO stable envelopes bypass center-hocolim}

The Maulik--Okounkov stable-envelope $R$-matrix of
Theorems~\ref{thm:mo-chiral-rmatrix} and~\ref{thm:k3e-mo-rmatrix}
gives an independent route to $E_2$ braiding. It constructs $R(u)$
directly from stable envelopes on $\Hilb^n(X)$, using equivariant
geometry of the Nakajima variety instead of the Drinfeld center of
local CoHA charts. For toric CY$_3$ the MO $R$-matrix matches the
chiral $R$-matrix. For $K3\times E$, stable envelopes on
$\Hilb^n(K3)$ give the degree-$24$ K3 Yangian structure function.

\section{Conjectural Six-Dimensional Structures}

\subsection{6d holomorphic theory $\to$ $E_3$ factorization}

Conjecture~\ref{conj:topological-e3-comparison} concerns a
non-Lagrangian six-dimensional holomorphic theory on $\C^3$. It is
not standard holomorphic Chern--Simons theory: the expression
$\Omega\wedge A\wedge\bar\partial A$ has type $(3,2)$ and is top
degree on a real five-dimensional slice rather than on $\R^6$.
The available constructions are the holomorphic twist of the 6d
$(2,0)$ SCFT, Costello--Li perturbative BCOV theory, and
Costello--Francis--Gwilliam factorization homology of $E_3$ local
observables. The algebraic $E_3$ structure from Deligne's theorem on
$\HH^\bullet(A,A)$ and the topological $E_3$ structure from framed
little $3$-disks agree rationally under Kontsevich formality; the
integral comparison remains a torsion question.

\subsection{Quantum toroidal from $E_3$ projection}

Conjecture~\ref{conj:6d-boundary-toroidal} predicts that projecting the
$E_3$ theory on $\C^3$ to an $E_1$ curve algebra gives
$U_{q,t}(\widehat{\widehat{\fgl}}_1)$ with
$q=e^{2\pi i h_1/h_3}$ and $t=e^{-2\pi i h_2/h_3}$. The first
affinization comes from the $E_2$ projection to $\C^2$, which is the
affine Yangian of the 5d theorem; the second affinization is the
additional $E_3$ direction.

\subsection{Borcherds lift = resummation of perturbative series}

In `k3_times_e.tex`, the perturbative factorization homology
$\int_{K3}\mathcal F$ is computed order-by-order in $\sigma_3$. At tree
level $(\sigma_3=0)$ one obtains the lattice VOA
$V_{\Lambda_{K3}}$ of class $\mathsf{G}$. At one loop
$(\sigma_3^1)$ the cup-product correction gives class $\mathsf{L}$ of
depth $3$. At two loops $(\sigma_3^2)$ the second Fourier--Jacobi
coefficient $\phi_2$ of $\Delta_5$ enters. The all-loop limit is the
Borcherds product for $\Delta_5$, producing class $\mathsf{M}$. Thus
the Borcherds multiplicative lift is the non-perturbative resummation
of the perturbative chiral bar-complex expansion. K3 formality
$(m_k=0$ for $k\geq3)$ leaves the cup product $m_2$ as the operation
controlling all loop orders.

\subsection{Class M = mock modular forms}

For $K3\times E$, shadow class $\mathsf{M}$ means
$r_{\max}=\infty$. The BKM root multiplicities $c(D)$ are nonzero for
infinitely many discriminants and satisfy the Rademacher asymptotic
$|c(D)|\sim e^{4\pi\sqrt D}$. The $\frac14$-BPS counting function
$h(\tau)$ is a mock modular form of weight $1/2$ with shadow
$24\eta^3=\chi(K3)\eta^3$. Mock modularity records wall crossing:
the failure of modularity measures chamber-dependence of the BPS
spectrum. The shadow obstruction tower is the chamber-independent
algebraic completion; the infinite BKM tower is the chiral-algebraic
shadow of the infinite Borcherds product.

\subsection{Categorical $S$-matrix $\to$ $\mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z)$}

The categorical $S$-matrix is the trace of double braiding,
\[
 S_{V,W}=\Tr_W(R_{V,W}R_{W,V}).
\]
At charge $1$, CY inversion $g(z)g(-z)=1$ gives the trivial value
$S=1$. For charge-$2$ diagonal Fock sectors,
\[
 S_{(2)}(u)=g(u+h_2)g(h_2-u),\qquad
 S_{(1,1)}(u)=g(u+h_1)g(h_1-u),
\]
and $S(u)=S(-u)$. The conditional $E_3$ factorization ansatz is
\[
 S^{E_3}(u,v)=S(u)S(v)S(u-v),
\]
even in both variables. The expected pipeline is
\[
 E_3\text{-braiding}\longrightarrow \text{categorical }S\text{-matrix}
 \longrightarrow \mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z)\text{-action}
 \longrightarrow \Phi_{10}\text{ as partition function}.
\]
The $\mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z)$ term arises from the $K3\times E$ lattice
$\Lambda^{3,2}$, with
$\mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z)/\{\pm I_4\}\simeq O(\Lambda^{3,2})_+/\{\pm I_5\}$.
The identification of $\Phi_{10}$ through CY-A$_3$ remains
conditional.

\subsection{$K3 \times E$ via factorization homology}

Conjecture~\ref{conj:fact-hom-k3xe} predicts
\[
 \int_{K3}\mathcal F|_{K3}
\]
as a chiral algebra on $E$ with character governed by $\Phi_{10}$, and
\[
 \int_E\left(\int_{K3}\mathcal F\right)=\Phi_{10}(\tau)
\]
as the partition function. The intermediate object is a toroidal
Kac--Moody enveloping chiral algebra on $E$. Remark~\ref{rem:6d-adds-to-k3xe}
compares three routes to $A_{K3\times E}$: the CY-to-chiral functor
$\Phi$, the toroidal presentation, and the six-dimensional holomorphic
theory. Their agreement is conjectural. The four K3$\times E$
invariants are $\{0,3,5,24\}$, coming from distinct constructions.

\section{Six-Dimensional Open Constructions}

\subsection{Zamolodchikov tetrahedron equation for $U_{q,t}$}

The $E_3$ braiding on $\C^3$ predicts the Zamolodchikov tetrahedron
equation
\[
 R_{123}R_{145}R_{246}R_{356}
 =
 R_{356}R_{246}R_{145}R_{123},
\]
the $3$-simplex analogue of the $E_2$ Yang--Baxter equation. For
$U_{q,t}$, the tetrahedron $R$-matrix should act on triple tensor
products of Fock spaces and should admit a Kapranov--Voevodsky
2-categorical interpretation. The proof obligation is an explicit
charge-$2$ operator satisfying the displayed equation.

\subsection{Spin-3 chiral coproduct}

At spin $3$, the $W_3$ current gives the first nonlinear OPE
contribution. The coproduct $\Delta_{\mathrm{ch}}(W_3)$ involves
structure constants of $W_{1+\infty}$ beyond the Heisenberg sector and
requires the full Yangian $R$-matrix at charge $3$. The expected
$K$-matrix correction distinguishes class $\mathsf{L}$ from class
$\mathsf{C}$.

\subsection{Two-parameter $R$-matrix $R(u,v)$}

The 6d $E_3$ structure predicts a two-parameter $R$-matrix $R(u,v)$,
where the parameters come from the two transverse directions beyond
the Wilson line. Conjecture~\ref{conj:chiral-qg-c3}(ii) requires a
parametric Yang--Baxter equation in $(u,v)$. The Maulik--Okounkov
construction gives the one-parameter $R(u)$ from $\C^2$; the second
parameter $v$ comes from the third $\C^3$ direction. At $v=0$ the
matrix reduces to the Yangian $R$-matrix of
Theorem~\ref{thm:yang-r-matrix-ybe}.

\subsection{$\tau_3(n)$ as $E_3$ bar complex dimensions}

The heuristic from `holomorphic_cs_chiral_engine.py` is that the arity
$n$ piece of the $E_3$ bar complex of $W_{1+\infty}$ is governed by
the three-dimensional divisor function
\[
 \tau_3(n)=\#\{(a,b,c):abc=n\}.
\]
The pattern is $E_1$ bar dimensions like partition numbers $p(n)$,
$E_2$ bar dimensions like divisor numbers $d(n)$, and $E_3$ bar
dimensions like $\tau_3(n)$. This remains a conjectural dimension
formula; the proof obligation is to identify the spectral sequence
whose Dirichlet series is $1/\zeta(s)^3$.

\subsection{K3 double current algebra quantization}
The chiral algebra $\int_{K3}\mathcal F|_{K3}$ on $E$ in
Conjecture~\ref{conj:fact-hom-k3xe}(iii) should be a double current
algebra: currents from the rank-$24$ K3 lattice $\Lambda_{K3}$ with two
loop parameters, $\tau$ from $E$ and $\sigma$ from K3 moduli. The
$\Omega$-background on the K3 directions supplies the deformation
parameter. The classical limit is the lattice VOA $V_\Lambda$; at
generic deformation one expects a vertex algebra on $E$ whose character
is the Fourier--Jacobi expansion of $\Phi_{10}$. The definition is
conditional on CY-A$_3$.

\section{The Bottleneck: Chain-Level $S^3$-Framing for CY-A$_3$}

\subsection{The obstruction}
CY-A$_2$ uses the $S^2$-framing of $\HH_\bullet(\cC)$ for a CY$_2$
category to produce an $E_2$-chiral object. CY-A$_3$ requires an
$S^3$-framing. Theorem~\ref{thm:e1-stabilization-cy} gives
$\mathrm{Obs}_{\mathrm{eff}}(3)=0$, since
$\pi_3(BU)=0$ and $\pi_3(BSp)=0$. The obstruction group vanishes, but
the explicit chain-level $S^3$-framing has not been constructed.

\subsection{Existing approaches and the MO bypass}

There are five routes. The direct route is to construct the
$S^3$-framing on $\HH_\bullet(\cC)$ for a CY$_3$ dg category; the
missing object is an explicit model for this framing. The CoHA route
uses SVYZ $\CoHA$ as an $E_1$ algebra and then passes to the Drinfeld
center to obtain $E_2$ braiding; Proposition~\ref{prop:center-hocolim}
shows that this route is global rather than chartwise. The
Maulik--Okounkov route constructs $E_2$ braiding directly from stable
envelopes on $\Hilb^n(X)$: it works for toric CY$_3$ and for
$K3\times E$ through $\Hilb^n(K3)$, but it requires a torus action or a
Hilbert-scheme description. The holomorphic-field-theory route starts
with the six-dimensional $E_3$ theory on $\C^3$ and projects to an
$E_1$ curve algebra; for compact manifolds this framework is still
conjectural. The relative chiral-algebra route for $K3\times E$ uses
the elliptic fibration $\pi:S\to\mathbb{P}^1$ to build
$A_{K3,\mathrm{rel}}$ directly. Thus MO stable envelopes and the
relative route give concrete $E_2$ access for $K3\times E$, while the
general CY$_3$ $S^3$-framing remains open.

\section{Open Problems}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Chain-level $S^3$-framing for CY-A$_3$.}
  Construct the functor $\Phi$ at $d=3$. All of Vol III downstream of CY-A depends on this. Impact: unlocks CY-B, CY-D, and the entire $K3 \times E$ programme. The MO stable envelope and relative chiral algebra routes bypass this for specific targets but not in general.

\item \textbf{6d algebraic framework for compact manifolds.}
  Extend the CFG factorization homology from $\C^3$ to $K3 \times E$. Would provide an alternative to Problem 1. Requires non-Lagrangian algebraic QFT on compact complex 3-folds.

\item \textbf{$E_3$ Koszul duality (Conj~\ref{conj:e3-koszul-duality}).}
  Prove $B_{E_3}$ has three commuting differentials and coproducts, and that $\cF^!$ has inverted parameters. The $(1+t)^{3g}$ formula for classes L, C provides strong evidence.

\item \textbf{Zamolodchikov tetrahedron equation for $U_{q,t}$.}
  Construct the tetrahedron $R$-matrix and verify the 3-simplex equation computationally at charge 2. First genuine $E_3$ invariant beyond the YBE.

\item \textbf{Two-parameter $R$-matrix $R(u,v)$.}
  Construct and verify the parametric YBE for the $E_3$ $R$-matrix on Fock spaces. Relates MO stable envelopes to the quantum toroidal braiding.

\item \textbf{Spin-3 chiral coproduct.}
  Extend the spin-2 scaffold (23 tests) to spin 3. First test of nonlinear OPE in the coproduct. Discriminates shadow class L from class C.

\item \textbf{$\int_{K3} \cF$ computation.}
  Compute the perturbative factorization homology over K3 and match to the Fourier--Jacobi expansion of $\Phi_{10}$. The Borcherds resummation result confirms the all-loop answer; extend to the non-perturbative regime.

\item \textbf{Categorical $S$-matrix to $\Phi_{10}$.}
  Close the gap between the charge-$2$ categorical $S$-matrix and the full $\mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z)$ modularity. Identify $\Phi_{10}$ as the partition function of the $E_3$ braided category.

\item \textbf{Algebraic vs topological $E_3$ over $\Z$.}
  Determine whether the two $E_3$ structures (Deligne conjecture vs configuration space) agree integrally or only rationally. Torsion discrepancy would affect the quantum toroidal algebra at roots of unity.

\item \textbf{Quantum group realization CY-C.}
  Construct $C(\frakg,q)$ as a chiral quantum group. Currently \texttt{\textbackslash begin\{conjecture\}} only. Requires resolution of Problems 1 or 2 as input.
\end{enumerate}
