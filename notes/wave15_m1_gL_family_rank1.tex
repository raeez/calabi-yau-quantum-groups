\section*{The lattice-polarised GBKM family $\gfrak_L$: rank-1 construction}

\subsection*{Nikulin polarisation data}

Fix a K3 surface $X$ with N\'eron--Severi lattice $\Pic(X) \supset L$, where $L$ is an
even non-degenerate lattice of signature $(1, t)$, $t = \rk(L) - 1 \geq 0$,
primitively embedded in the K3 lattice
$\Lambda_{K3} = 2 E_8(-1) \oplus 3 U$ of signature $(3, 19)$. Orthogonal complement
$L^\perp = T \subset \Lambda_{K3}$ carries signature $(2, 19 - t)$ and is the
transcendental lattice of the generic $L$-polarised K3. The period domain
\[
 \Om_L = \{ [\omega] \in \mathbb{P}(T \otimes \C) : \langle \omega, \omega \rangle = 0,\ \langle \omega, \bar\omega \rangle > 0 \}^+
\]
is a type IV Hermitian symmetric domain of dimension $19 - t$; the modular group
$\Oneg(T) \subset \mathrm{O}(T)$ fixing a component acts properly discontinuously, with
$\mathcal{M}_L = \Om_L / \Oneg(T)$ the coarse moduli of $L$-polarised K3s
(Nikulin 1979, \S1; Dolgachev 1996, Thm.~3.1). Nikulin-admissibility requires
$L$ primitive even hyperbolic with $L^\perp$ containing a hyperbolic plane $U$
so that Dolgachev mirror symmetry applies: $L^\perp = U \oplus \check L$ identifies
the mirror polarisation $\check L$ of rank $20 - t$
(Dolgachev 1996, \S6; the rank-2 condition ensures the existence of the mirror
family $\check X$ with $\Pic(\check X) \supset \check L$).

\subsection*{Rank-1 polarisation $L = \langle 2d \rangle$}

Set $L = \Z h$ with $\langle h, h \rangle = 2d$, $d \geq 1$. Primitivity in
$\Lambda_{K3}$ holds for all $d$; admissibility holds because
$\langle 2d \rangle^\perp \supset U \oplus U \oplus 2 E_8(-1)$ contains a hyperbolic
plane. The mirror lattice $\check L = U \oplus 2 E_8(-1) \oplus \langle -2d \rangle$
has rank $19$, signature $(1, 18)$: the mirror family $\mathcal{M}_{\check L}$
parametrises K3s of mirror-Picard $\check L$. The coarse moduli $\mathcal{M}_{2d}$
has dimension $19$ (Mukai 1988; Gritsenko--Hulek--Sankaran 2007 Thm.~2.1) and maps
to the $\Sig(2, 19)$ period domain $\Om_{2d}$. Generic degree $2d$ K3s are
realised as complete intersections in weighted projective spaces for
$d \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ (Mukai 1988; quartic, degree 4 complete intersection
in $\mathbb{P}^5$, etc.).

\subsection*{Twisted elliptic genus}

The $L$-polarised K3 carries an elliptic genus $\phi_L(\tau, z)$: for rank-1
$L = \langle 2d \rangle$ with K3 fibre, $\phi_L = \phi_{0,1}$ is independent of
$d$ up to normalisation, but the Borcherds input changes via the Jacobi form
degree-2 lift. Explicitly, $\phi_{0,1}(\tau, z) \in \Jac_{0,1}(1)$ is the weak
Jacobi form of weight $0$ index $1$ (Eichler--Zagier Thm.~9.3), with
Fourier expansion $\phi_{0,1} = 2y + 20 + 2y^{-1} + q(\ldots)$; integration over the K3
fibre of the $L$-polarised family preserves this Jacobi datum
(Libgober 2000; Gritsenko 1999 \S3).

\subsection*{Borcherds lift}

Apply Borcherds (1995, Thm.~10.1; Gritsenko 1999, Thm.~2.1): the additive lift
from weak Jacobi forms of weight $0$ index $d$ on the paramodular
$\Gamma_d = \mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z)$-commensurate group to paramodular forms of weight
$k_d$ on $\HH_2$ produces, starting from $\phi_{0,1}$ at index $d$,
the \emph{Gritsenko--Cl\'ery paramodular form} $\Phi_L = \Phi_{2d}$ of weight
$k_d = 5$ at $d = 1$ (recovering $\Delta_5$), with Fourier--Jacobi expansion
\[
 \Phi_L(\tau, z, \tau') = \sum_{m \geq 1} \psi_{L, m}(\tau, z) q'^m,
\]
where $\psi_{L, m}$ are weak Jacobi forms of weight $k_d$ and index $m \cdot d$
determined by the Hecke--Maass descent of $\phi_{0,1}$ (Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008,
\S2--4). At $d = 1$ this is Gritsenko's $\Delta_5$; at $d = 2$ one recovers
the Igusa--Gritsenko form $\Phi_2$ of weight $2$; at $d = 3$ the paramodular
weight drops to $1$ (Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008, Thm.~3). The Borcherds product
\[
 \Phi_L(Z) = q^a y^b q'^c \prod_{(n,\ell,m) > 0} (1 - q^n y^\ell q'^m)^{c_L(4nm - \ell^2)},
\]
with multiplicities $c_L(D)$ read off from the Fourier expansion of $\phi_{0,1}$
via Borcherds' singular theta correspondence, converges on a Weyl chamber of
$\Om_L$ (Borcherds 1995 \S7).

\subsection*{GBKM $\gfrak_L$}

The Borcherds denominator identity
\[
 \Phi_L(Z) = \sum_{w \in W_L} \det(w)\, e^{w(\rho_L)} \prod_{\alpha \in \Delta_+^L} (1 - e^{\alpha})^{\mathrm{mult}(\alpha)}
\]
exhibits $\Phi_L$ as the denominator of a generalised Kac--Moody (BKM)
superalgebra $\gfrak_L$ (Borcherds 1995, Thm.~10.1; Gritsenko--Nikulin 1997,
\S2): Cartan subalgebra $\hfrak_L = (L \oplus U) \otimes \C$, root lattice
$L \oplus U$, simple roots read from the Fourier coefficients of
$\phi_{0,1}$ lifted to index $d$, Weyl group $W_L \subset \Oneg(L \oplus U)$ the
reflection group generated by real simple roots, and multiplicities of
imaginary roots governed by $c_L(D)$. The Cartan type is thus the hyperbolic
signature-$(2, t+1)$ lattice $L \oplus U$, and $\gfrak_L$ is Nikulin-Lorentzian
in the sense of Gritsenko--Nikulin 2002.

For $L = \langle 2 \rangle$ (degree 2 K3): $\gfrak_L = \gfrak_{\Delta_5}$ is
the Gritsenko--Nikulin algebra with denominator $\Delta_5$ (Gritsenko--Nikulin
1997, \S3; compatible with the user's 2020 Lorgat GKM construction).
The Cartan is $\langle 2 \rangle \oplus U$, rank $3$, signature $(2, 1)$;
three real simple roots $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3$ with Cartan matrix
$A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -2 & -2 \\ -2 & 2 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1997 Thm.~3.1), and imaginary simple roots $\delta_n$
at multiplicities $c_1(n^2) / 2$.

\subsection*{Inclusion $\gfrak_{\Delta_5} \hookrightarrow \gfrak_L$}

A primitive lattice extension $L \hookrightarrow L'$ of rank-1 polarisations
corresponds to $L = \langle 2 \rangle$, $L' = \langle 2d \rangle$ via
$h \mapsto d \cdot h'$ for $d | d'$ not directly but through index-scaling
embeddings $\langle 2 \rangle \hookrightarrow \langle 2d \rangle \otimes \Q$
intersecting $\langle 2d \rangle$ in $d \cdot \Z h'$. The induced embedding
$\langle 2 \rangle \oplus U \hookrightarrow \langle 2d \rangle \oplus U$ on
root lattices extends to $\gfrak_{\Delta_5} \hookrightarrow \gfrak_L$ as a
subalgebra of BKM type when the Fourier lift is compatible, i.e. when
$\phi_{0,1}|_{\langle 2 \rangle} = \phi_{0,1}|_{\langle 2d \rangle}$ reduces
by the Hecke--Maass relation at $d$
(Gritsenko 1999 Prop.~4.2). This is conjectural in full generality and
proved for $d = 2$ via the Igusa $\chi_{10} \to \Phi_2$ descent
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1997 Thm.~3.3); for higher $d$ it awaits verification of
primitive root transfer (Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008 Rem.~4.5).

\subsection*{$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\gfrak_L)$}

By the universal Borcherds weight identity $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_L) = c_L(0) / 2$
(Borcherds 1995 Thm.~10.1; Gritsenko 1999): at $d = 1$, $c_1(0) = 10$,
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\gfrak_{\Delta_5}) = 5$. At $d = 2$, $c_2(0) = 4$,
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\gfrak_{\langle 4 \rangle}) = 2$. At $d = 3$, the paramodular
weight is $1$ and $c_3(0) = 2$, giving $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}} = 1$
(Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008 Table 1). The sequence $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\gfrak_L)$
for $L = \langle 2d \rangle$ thus traces the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery weight descent;
it equals the mirror Mukai vector length $\sqrt{2d}$ correction in the paramodular
Hodge bundle (Gritsenko--Hulek 2014 \S5).
