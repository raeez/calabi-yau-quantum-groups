\section*{Slides cross-reference to ancillary Oberdieck-family papers}

\subsection*{Scope}

The slides cache \texttt{/tmp/automorphic-slides.txt} (953 lines,
\textit{Arithmetic Geometry of some CY3's} self-quotient deck) is
interrogated for any surface contact with the Oberdieck-ancillary
corpus beyond Oberdieck--Pixton 2017: Oberdieck 2018 (twisted DT),
Bryan--Oberdieck 2018, Pandharipande--Thomas 2014 (stable pairs),
Oberdieck--Shen 2020.  Five parallel grep passes across the deck and
a line-context read of the only DT block (\S{}\texttt{856--952})
decide the question.

\subsection*{What is in the slides}

The deck carries exactly one DT-geometric statement, concentrated in
the closing eight-slide block \texttt{L.856--952}.  The geometry is
the $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$ self-quotient $X = (S \times E)/(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})$
of an elliptically fibred K3 $S$ against an elliptic curve $E$ with an
$N$-torsion point, the acting diagonal shift $(s,e) \mapsto (g \cdot s,
e + e_0)$ preserving the CY form.  The generating series
\[
   Z^X(q,t,p) = \sum_{h \geq 0} \sum_{d \geq 0} \sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}}
      \mathrm{DT}_{n,(\beta_h,d)} \, q^{\tfrac{1}{2}\langle \beta_h,
      \beta_h\rangle \cdot d - 1}\,(-p)^n
\]
is defined through $E$-equivariant Behrend-weighted Euler integrals on
$\mathrm{Hilb}^n(X,\beta_h)/E$.  At $N=1$ the deck states
$Z^{S \times E} = -C/(\Delta_5)^2$ and records that the K3 elliptic genus
is $2\varphi_{0,1}$.  The $L$-$(h_M = g_N^s)$-twisted series
$Z^X_{L,h_M}(q,t,p)$ is defined and conjectured to furnish, up to
constant, all eight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery diagonal-divisor Siegel forms
with a GBKM correction $\mathfrak{g}_{(g_N,h_M)} \subset
\mathfrak{g}_L$ whose twisted-twined weight-zero index-one Jacobi form
$F^{(g_N,h_M)}_L$ reads off the root-multiplicity data.

\subsection*{The single bridge that does appear}

At line 912 the deck asserts, without proof or citation-track,
\[
   Z^{S \times E}(q,t,p) = -\frac{C}{\Delta_5^2}\,,
\]
this being the Oberdieck--Pixton Igusa cusp form conjecture (now a
theorem of Oberdieck, Pixton, and joint work; the deck's author
himself in 2020 cites Oberdieck--Pixton here) at $N=1$, $X = S
\times E$.  This is the \emph{only} lattice-arithmetic-to-DT bridge
printed in the deck.  The bridge is stated at the level of the
generating series; it is not unpacked through the reduced theory,
the GW/DT correspondence, stable pairs, or the KKV formula --- none
of those machines appear as named objects.

\subsection*{Cross-reference against memory}

The memory entry
\texttt{reference\_lorgat\_2020\_automorphic\_corrections.md}
confirms the deck's scope.  Conjecture 1 is the author's: all eight
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery diagonal-divisor paramodular forms arise as
reciprocal-square roots of refined/motivic DT zeta
$Z^X_{L,h_M}$ and simultaneously as GBKM denominators with root
multiplicities equal to twisted-twined K3 elliptic genera.  The
$N = 1$ reduction to Oberdieck--Pixton is the only fully-proved
instance; $N \in \{2,3,4,5,6,7,8\}$ are the conjectural-extension
targets.  No ancillary Oberdieck-family paper is cited in the deck
or in the memory entry's cited-works line, which names
\emph{Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995, Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008, Borcherds
1998, Oberdieck--Pixton 2017} as the four pillars.

\subsection*{Finding, sharp}

The deck carries \emph{no ancillary Oberdieck-family content}.  The
only Oberdieck reference in the deck is the implicit Oberdieck--Pixton
$N=1$ identity $Z^{S \times E} = -C/\Delta_5^2$, taken as a black box.
Oberdieck 2018 twisted DT, Bryan--Oberdieck 2018, Pandharipande--Thomas
2014, and Oberdieck--Shen 2020 are not engaged either by direct
citation or by implicit use of their technology.  Wave-17~D1's
conclusion (zero gluing content in the deck) extends to: zero
DT-ancillary machinery content.

\subsection*{Consequence for Vol III frontier}

The bridge the programme actually wants --- between
lattice-arithmetic $(\Lambda^{3,2},\ \Delta_5,\ \mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5})$
and DT geometry $\bigl(\mathrm{Hilb}^n(X,\beta_h),\ \nu_{\mathrm{Behrend}},\
Z^X\bigr)$ --- is the $N=1$ Oberdieck--Pixton identity in its
$1/\Delta_5^2$ form, extended conjecturally to $N \leq 8$ and
$g_N$-twisted analogues.  The ancillary Oberdieck-family machines that
would be expected to deliver the $N \geq 2$ extensions, i.e.\
Oberdieck 2018 $g$-twisted DT for K3 surfaces, Bryan--Oberdieck 2018
on $K3 \times E$ curve-counting of higher-genus class,
Pandharipande--Thomas 2014 stable pairs, and Oberdieck--Shen 2020
Nekrasov-type pairs on CY3, are \emph{absent from the 2020 deck}.
That absence is itself content: the author's route in 2020 was
arithmetic (Borcherds--Gritsenko--Nikulin denominator calculus) and
not geometric; the geometric ancillary would be supplied only by
later work.

For the Vol III Part V / Part VI programme this has three
consequences.
\emph{(i)} The 2020 deck cannot be used to cite $g_N$-twisted DT
invariants at $N \geq 2$; primary source there remains
Oberdieck 2018 \texttt{arXiv:1705.10672} and subsequent extensions,
not the deck.
\emph{(ii)} The universal weight identity
$\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ (Borcherds 1995; Gritsenko 1999) is
independently verified in the deck through the
$(\Delta_5,\ \varphi_{0,1})$ Fourier-Jacobi data at $N=1$: root
super-multiplicities read off $\varphi_{0,1}$, constant coefficient
$c_1(0) = 10$, $\kBKM = 5$.  The deck carries no analogous direct
Fourier data at $N \geq 2$.
\emph{(iii)} Conjecture 1 (deck, L.929--936) is the natural
statement of a $\Phi$-to-DT correspondence at the generating-function
level.  In the Vol III vocabulary it says
\[
   -1\,\big/\,Z^X_{L,h_M}(q,t,p)\ =\ \Phi_{N}(q,t,p)
   \quad
   \text{(up to constant)},
\]
with $\Phi_N$ a diagonal-divisor paramodular form of weight
$c_N(0)/2$; and the GBKM correction
$\mathfrak{g}_{(g_N,h_M)} \subset \mathfrak{g}_L$ has root multiplicities
equal to $F^{(g_N,h_M)}_L$.  This is precisely the frontier
statement ancillary Oberdieck-family work would interface with.
The deck does not close that interface; it states it.
