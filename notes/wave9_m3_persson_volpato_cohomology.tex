\section*{Persson and Volpato Constraints for Twisted-Twined K3 Genera}

\paragraph{Ordinary twining.}
For a geometric symmetry \(h\) of a K3 sigma model, the twined elliptic
genus is the Ramond trace
\[
  \phi^{K3,(1,h)}_{0,1}(\tau,z)
  =
  \Tr_{\mathcal H_{\mathrm{RR}}}
  \left(h(-1)^{F+\widetilde F}q^{L_0-c/24}
  \bar q^{\widetilde L_0-\widetilde c/24}y^{J_0}\right).
\]
For an arbitrary conjugacy class \([h]\subset M_{24}\), the Mathieu
moonshine genus is the character of the Gannon \(M_{24}\)-module in the
K3 elliptic-genus decomposition.  Gaberdiel, Hohenegger, and Volpato
give the resulting weak Jacobi form of weight \(0\) and index \(1\) for
every \(M_{24}\)-class, with multiplier fixed by the Frame shape.
This one-variable datum supplies a twining genus.  A simultaneous
twisted-twined genus also requires a commuting pair and the projective
centralizer action on the twisted sector.

\paragraph{Dijkgraaf-Witten transgression.}
Let \(g,h\in M_{24}\) commute.  The twisted-twined genus has the form
\[
  \Zgh(\tau,z)
  =
  \Tr_{\mathcal H_g}
  \left(\rho_g(h)(-1)^{F+\widetilde F}q^{L_0-c/24}
  \bar q^{\widetilde L_0-\widetilde c/24}y^{J_0}\right),
\]
where \(\rho_g\) is a projective representation of \(\CMg(g)\) on the
\(g\)-twisted sector.  Generalised Mathieu moonshine fixes a primitive
class
\[
  [\alpha]\in H^3(M_{24},\Uone)\cong \mathbb Z/12
\]
from the ordinary twining multiplier, and the projective \(2\)-cocycle
on \(\CMg(g)\) is the Dijkgraaf-Witten transgression
\[
  c_g(x,y)
  =
  \frac{\alpha(g,x,y)\,
        \alpha(x,y,(xy)^{-1}g(xy))}
       {\alpha(x,x^{-1}gx,y)},
  \qquad x,y\in \CMg(g).
\]
Thus \([c_g]\in H^2(\CMg(g),\Uone)\) is part of the input data.  A table
indexed only by the two conjugacy classes of \(g\) and \(h\) loses the
centralizer cocycle.

\paragraph{Regularity.}
If \(g,h,k\) commute, the conjugation law gives
\[
  \Zgh=\xi_{g,h}(k)\Zgh,
  \qquad
  \xi_{g,h}(k)=c_g(h,k)c_g(k,h)^{-1}.
\]
The genus vanishes whenever \(\xi_{g,h}(k)\neq 1\).  Equivalently,
\(h\) is \(c_g\)-regular in the projective character theory of
\(\CMg(g)\) precisely when \(c_g(h,k)=c_g(k,h)\) for every commuting
\(k\in \CMg(g)\).  This is the first obstruction of Gaberdiel,
Persson, Ronellenfitsch, and Volpato.

\paragraph{Multiplier split.}
Persson and Volpato split cyclic generators \(g\) by the restriction of
\([\alpha]\) to \(H^3(\langle g\rangle,\Uone)\).  The trivial-multiplier
case is exactly the \(M_{23}\)-contained list
\[
  2A,\ 3A,\ 4B,\ 5A,\ 6A,\ 7A,\ 7B,\ 8A,\ 11A,\ 14A,\ 14B,\
  15A,\ 15B,\ 23A,\ 23B.
\]
The nontrivial-multiplier case is
\[
  2B,\ 3B,\ 4A,\ 4C,\ 6B,\ 10A,\ 12A,\ 12B,\ 21A,\ 21B.
\]
The five large-prime classes often isolated in K3 twining tables belong
to the first list:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
[g] & \text{Frame shape on }24 & \Tr_{24}(g) & \Tr_{24}(g)/\ord(g)\\
\hline
7A,7B & 1^3\,7^3 & 3 & 3/7\\
11A & 1^2\,11^2 & 2 & 2/11\\
23A,23B & 1\,23 & 1 & 1/23
\end{array}
\]
These fractions are fixed-point trace ratios in the ordinary twining
table.  The obstruction test is the cocycle regularity condition above.

\paragraph{Second-quantized product.}
Persson and Volpato attach to each commuting pair a product
\[
  \Phi_{g,h}(\sigma,\tau,z)
  =
  p\,q^{1/(N\lambda)}y
  \prod_{(d,m,\ell)>0}\prod_{t=0}^{M-1}
  \left(1-e^{2\pi i t/M}q^{m/(N\lambda)}y^\ell p^d\right)^{
    \widehat c_{g,h}(d,m,\ell,t)},
\]
where \(N=\ord(g)\), \(\lambda\) is the shortest-cycle length of \(g\)
in the \(24\)-point permutation representation, \(M=\ord(h_\alpha)\)
in the central extension, and the exponents are simultaneous
eigenspace superdimensions.  This product is a Siegel modular form
after the stated Hodge prefactor.  A Borcherds-Kac-Moody
denominator interpretation additionally requires a lattice, Weyl
chamber, Weyl vector, parity convention, and root-multiplicity
identification.

\paragraph{\(K3\times E\) normalization.}
On the compact threefold \(K3\times E\), the invariants used in Vol III
are construction-distinct:
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)=0,\qquad
  \kch(K3\times E)=0,\qquad
  \kchHeis(K3\times E)=3,\qquad
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=5,\qquad
  \kfiber=24.
\]
The equality \(\kcat(K3\times E)=\chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\chi(\mathcal
O_E)=2\cdot0=0\) is K\"unneth multiplicativity on the total space.  Since
\(\dim_{\mathbb C}(K3\times E)=3\), the native output of \(\Phi_3\) is
\(E_1\).  The BKM branch is the Hall-Drinfeld/Borcherds branch; the K3
self-mirror Yangian is a separate Mukai-lattice branch.

\paragraph{CHL denominator slice.}
For the primitive CHL denominator inputs
\[
  N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\},
\]
the Borcherds-input constants and weights are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
N & c_N^{\mathrm{CHL}}(0) & \kBKM(\PhiN^{\mathrm{CHL}})\\
\hline
1 & 10 & 5\\
2 & 8 & 4\\
3 & 6 & 3\\
4 & 4 & 2\\
6 & 2 & 1
\end{array}
\]
Borcherds' weight theorem gives
\[
  \kBKM(\PhiN^{\mathrm{CHL}})
  =
  \operatorname{wt}(\PhiN^{\mathrm{CHL}})
  =
  c_N^{\mathrm{CHL}}(0)/2.
\]
At \(N=1\), the monic Gritsenko-Nikulin denominator section is
\[
  \Dfive=64^{-1}\Delta_5,
  \qquad
  \Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}=\Dfive^2,\qquad
  \Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_5^2.
\]

\paragraph{Enriques constraint.}
An Enriques quotient \(S=K3/\iota\) is produced by a fixed-point-free
anti-symplectic involution: \(\iota^*\omega_{K3}=-\omega_{K3}\), and
\(K_S\) is nontrivial \(2\)-torsion.  The symplectic Mathieu class
\(2A\) acts trivially on \(H^{2,0}(K3)\).  The Enriques automorphic
branch therefore uses the Enriques lattice and its own multiplier data.
Any comparison with the \(M_{24}\) symplectic-twining table requires an
explicit lattice and multiplier comparison theorem.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
A twisted-twined K3 genus is specified by
\[
  (g,h),\quad \CMg(g),\quad [\alpha],\quad [c_g],\quad
  \xi_{g,h},\quad \Gamma^{(g,h)},\quad \nu_{g,h}.
\]
A BKM denominator row is specified by the lattice, chamber, Weyl vector,
character or cover, parity convention, and product exponents.  These two
lists meet only after the Borcherds product data and the projective
Mathieu data are matched row by row.

\paragraph{Proof anchors.}
Gannon 2016 proves the \(M_{24}\)-module behind Mathieu moonshine.
Gaberdiel, Hohenegger, and Volpato 2010, Eq.~(3.8) and Table~1, give
the ordinary twining genera and multipliers.  Gaberdiel, Persson,

twisted-twined cocycle, regularity obstruction, and multiplier split.
Persson and Volpato 2014, \S3.3.2, Eqs.~(3.44)-(3.54), give the
second-quantized product.  Borcherds 1998, Theorem~13.3, gives the
weight \(c(0)/2\).  Gritsenko-Clery 2013, Theorems~1.4 and~3.1, give
the eight simplest-divisor rows.  The Vol III CY-dimension
stratification chapter fixes the \(K3\times E\), CHL, and
Gritsenko-Clery normalizations used here.
