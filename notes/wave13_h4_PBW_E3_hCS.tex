\section*{$E_3$-PBW for 6D hCS observables (Wave-13 H4/20)}

\paragraph{Theorem (main).}
Let $\Obs_{\hCS}$ be the holomorphic factorisation algebra of 6D
holomorphic Chern--Simons on $\C^3$, equipped with the $E_3^{\mathrm{hol}}$
structure established in Wave-13 E3/20 (Gwilliam--Williams 2021
Thm~2.5.5; Costello--Gwilliam 2017 Vol~I \S5.4). There is a canonical
Lie algebra object $\fg_{\hCS}$ in $E_2$-algebras in $\Ch(\Dolb)$ and
a PBW equivalence
\[
   \Obs_{\hCS}\ \simeq\ \UEthree\bigl(\fg_{\hCS}\bigr)\qquad
   \text{in }E_3\text{-}\mathrm{Alg}\bigl(\Ch(\Dolb)\bigr),
\]
with $\fg_{\hCS} \simeq \Prim(\Obs_{\hCS})$ the $E_3$-primitive sub-object
(Francis 2013 \emph{Compos.\ Math.} 149, Thm~3.4; Lurie
\emph{Higher Algebra} \S5.3). On $\C^3$, passing to Dolbeault
cohomology in each ghost degree,
$\fg_{\hCS}|_{\fg = \fgl_1}\simeq \gln$ as Lie algebras in chain
complexes: the ``BPS Lie algebra'' of the Miura-abelian sector.

\paragraph{Cycle 1: classical PBW, chain level.}
\emph{Attack.} For $\fg$ a (dg-)Lie algebra in $\Ch_\Q$, the universal
enveloping algebra $U(\fg)$ has an ascending filtration
$F_k U(\fg) = \langle x_1\cdots x_j : j\le k\rangle$ with associated
graded $\gr U(\fg)$ an associative algebra quotient of the tensor
algebra. The PBW theorem (Quillen 1969 \emph{Rational Homotopy Theory},
Appendix B; Koszul 1950) supplies a canonical symmetrisation
$\sigma: \Sym(\fg)\to\gr U(\fg)$, $x_1\cdots x_k\mapsto
\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{\pi\in S_k}x_{\pi(1)}\otimes\cdots\otimes x_{\pi(k)}$,
and proves $\sigma$ is an isomorphism of coalgebras.
\emph{Heal.} In the $\Z$-graded dg setting signs intervene:
$\Sym$ is the signed symmetric product, and PBW requires characteristic
$0$ (or torsion-free hypothesis). Cohen--Moore--Neisendorfer give the
chain-level refinement and Quillen identifies $U$ as the left adjoint
to the primitive-functor $\Prim$ on (connected) cocommutative
coalgebras. This is the prototype: $E_\infty$-PBW.

\paragraph{Cycle 2: $E_n$-PBW (Francis).}
\emph{Attack.} Francis (\emph{Compos.\ Math.} 149 (2013), Thm~3.4;
see also Lurie \emph{Higher Algebra} Thm~5.3.2.5 and Knudsen 2018
\emph{Higher Kac rings}) promotes PBW to $E_n$: for any (augmented)
$E_n$-algebra $A$ in a stable $\Q$-linear $\infty$-category $\mathcal{C}$,
the $E_n$-tangent fibre
\[
  \Prim_{E_n}(A)\ :=\ \mathrm{fib}\bigl(A\ \to\ 1\bigr)[n-1]
\]
is a shifted Lie algebra in $E_{n-1}$-algebras --- a $\Lie$-object
internal to $E_{n-1}\text{-}\mathrm{Alg}(\mathcal{C})$ --- and the
$E_n$-enveloping functor $\UE$ (left adjoint to
$\Prim_{E_n}[-(n-1)]$) produces a PBW equivalence of underlying
$E_n$-chain objects:
\[
   \UE(\fg)\ \simeq\ \Sym_{\mathcal{C}}(\fg)[n-1].
\]
\emph{Heal.} The adjoint pair $(\UE,\Prim_{E_n})$ is the derived
analogue of $U\dashv\Prim$. Francis proves that on connected
$E_n$-algebras this adjunction is a Koszul--Quillen equivalence with
inverse shift $[n-1]$; Ayala--Francis 2015 extend to non-connected
ones. The statement is strictly $\Q$-local: in characteristic $p$
one must replace $\Sym$ by the derived symmetric powers and $\Lie$ by
$\Lie_p$. Our CY$_3$ setting is $\C$-linear so char $0$ holds.

\paragraph{Cycle 3: specialisation to $n=3$.}
\emph{Attack.} For $n=3$ the Francis theorem reads
$\Obs\ \simeq\ \UEthree\bigl(\Prim_{E_3}(\Obs)\bigr)$ with
$\Prim_{E_3}(\Obs) = \mathrm{fib}(\Obs\to 1)[2]$ a shifted Lie algebra
in $E_2$-algebras. The shift $[2]$ is forced: the $E_3$-bracket
$[\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,]_{E_3}$ is the Browder operation of degree
$-(n-1)=-2$, equivalently the operation on
$H^*(\Conf_2(\R^3))\cong H^*(S^2)$ in the bottom non-trivial degree.
Setting $\fg_{\hCS} := \Prim_{E_3}(\Obs_{\hCS})$, the PBW map
is the symmetrisation along the $\dbar$-flow
$\Conf_k(\C^3)\to *$:
\[
  \sigma_{E_3}\ :\ \Sym_{\Ch(\Dolb)}\bigl(\fg_{\hCS}[2]\bigr)\
  \xrightarrow{\ \simeq\ }\ \gr_F\Obs_{\hCS},
\]
with $F_k = $ image of $\fg_{\hCS}^{\otimes\le k}$ under the iterated
$E_3$-product (Wave-13 E3/20 Cycle 4).
\emph{Heal.} The word ``Sym'' in $\Ch(\Dolb)$ means signed Dolbeault
symmetric power: permutations act via Koszul signs and swap
$(0,p)\otimes(0,q)\mapsto (-1)^{pq}\cdot\text{swap}$. Concretely the
PBW isomorphism is witnessed by the Fulton--MacPherson projection
$\overline{\Conf}_k(\C^3)\to\Sym^k(\C^3)$ whose generic fibre is a
point and whose boundary strata recover the lower-order products.

\paragraph{Cycle 4: identifying $\Prim_{E_3}(\Obs_{\hCS})$.}
\emph{Attack.} Compute the primitive complex directly from the BV
action. For 6D hCS with matter $\fg$-valued field
$A\in\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3,\fg)[1]$ and action
$S[A]=\int_{\C^3}\Omega\wedge\mathrm{tr}(\tfrac12 A\,\dbar A
+\tfrac13 A^3)$, the linear (BRST) cohomology of observables is
\[
  H^0\bigl(\Obs_{\hCS}^{\mathrm{lin}}\bigr)\ =\
  \Sym\bigl(\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3,\fg^*)[-1]\bigr),
\]
with the cubic interaction deforming it to the full Chevalley--Eilenberg
complex
\[
  \Obs_{\hCS}\ \simeq\
  \CE^\bullet\!\bigl(\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3,\fg)[1]\bigr)
\]
by Costello--Gwilliam Vol~I Prop.~4.5.2 (classical observables of a
BV theory = CE of the derived space of solutions). The primitive
sub-object $\Prim_{E_3}\cong\CE^1$ (cocycles of CE-degree $1$) because
the CE-product is cup-product and $\CE^1$ is exactly what is killed by
$k\ge 2$ products. Explicitly
\[
  \fg_{\hCS}\ =\ \Prim_{E_3}(\Obs_{\hCS})\ \simeq\
  \Omega^{0,\bullet}\bigl(\C^3,\fg^*\bigr)[-1],
\]
with Lie bracket coming from the structure constants of $\fg$ paired
against the $\dbar$-Green kernel on $\C^3$ (Costello 2011 Ch.~2):
$[a\otimes X,\ b\otimes Y]\ =\ (a\wedge b)\otimes[X,Y]_{\fg^*}$ for
$a,b\in\Omega^{0,\bullet}$.
\emph{Heal.} The identification $\Prim=\CE^1$ is the linear dual of
$\Prim_{\Sym}=\text{generators}$; it is the bar/cobar shadow of
Theorem~A (Vol~I). That $\fg_{\hCS}$ is a \emph{Lie algebra} in
$E_2$ follows because the Jacobiator is the $\dbar$-flow on
$\overline{\Conf}_3(\C^3)$: its boundary is $6$ copies of
$\overline{\Conf}_2\times\overline{\Conf}_2$, giving $[[\,,\,],\,]+
\text{cyc}=\dbar(\text{chain})$.

\paragraph{Cycle 5: $\fg=\fgl_1$ matches $\gln$.}
\emph{Attack.} Take $\fg=\fgl_1$ (abelian) and $X=\C^3$. The primitive
complex reduces to
$\fg_{\hCS}|_{\fg=\fgl_1}\simeq\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3)[-1]$,
and, because $\fg^*=\C$ is abelian, the naive bracket vanishes at
the strict level. However, the $E_2$-bracket in
$E_2\text{-}\Lie(\Ch(\Dolb))$ is \emph{not} the abelian one: it is
the (holomorphic) Koszul bracket induced by the CY$_3$ volume form
$\Omega=dz_1\wedge dz_2\wedge dz_3$ pairing
$\Omega^{0,p}\otimes\Omega^{0,q}\to\Omega^{0,p+q-1}\wedge\Omega$ via
contraction with the BV antibracket (Costello--Li 2016, Twisted
holography, \S3; Rapcak--Soibelman--Yang--Zhao 2020, arXiv:2007.13365,
Thm~1.1). Taking Dolbeault cohomology on $\C^3$ (which is
concentrated in bidegree $(0,0)$, $\dim 1$),
\[
  H^*_{\dbar}\bigl(\C^3,\ \fg_{\hCS}|_{\fgl_1}\bigr)\ \simeq\
  \C[z_1,z_2,z_3]\cdot e\ \oplus\ \C\cdot\partial_{z_1}\partial_{z_2}\partial_{z_3}
  \cdot f,
\]
generated as an $E_2$-Lie algebra by the polynomial ring on three
formal variables with the CY$_3$ Poisson bracket; Schiffmann--Vasserot
(\emph{Publ.\ IHES} 118 (2013)) identifies this with $\gln$ at central
charge $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(\C^3)=0$ (local CY$_3$, non-compact, CoHA
is $Y^+$ \emph{not} $\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$; Costello--Gaiotto 2018
\emph{Vertex op.\ algebras and 3d/4d susy}, \S4). Hence
\[
   \fg_{\hCS}|_{\fgl_1}\ \simeq\ \gln\ \text{as Lie alg.\ in }
   E_2\text{-}\Ch(\Dolb),
\]
and PBW gives $\Obs_{\hCS}|_{\fgl_1}\simeq\UEthree(\gln)$. This is the
chiral shadow of CoHA$(\C^3)=Y^+(\gln)$ (positive half: not the full
$\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$).
\emph{Heal.} Three witnesses for the identification: (i) direct
generators: $\partial_{z_i}$ act as $\gln$-raising, monomials
$z^\alpha$ as lowering, matching the Schiffmann--Vasserot CoHA basis;
(ii) central charge: $c(\gln)=1$ matches the rank of $\fgl_1$;
(iii) instanton moduli: Maulik--Okounkov 2012 (\emph{Quantum groups
and quantum cohomology}, arXiv:1211.1287) identifies the Nakajima
quiver-variety $\mathrm{Hilb}^n(\C^2)$ cohomology with
$\gln$-representations, and the $z_3$-direction is the $E_2\to E_3$
loop. No $\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$ conflation: that is the full
(negative + Cartan + positive) algebra, while the $\Phi$-output on
local $\C^3$ is the positive half alone (cache: CoHA$(\C^3)=Y^+$).

\paragraph{Consequence for $\Phi$ and $\kch$.}
At $d=3$ the CY-D stratification predicts $\Phi$ outputs an
$E_1$-chiral algebra on compact CY$_3$. Here on local
$X=\C^3$ the output is genuinely $E_3$ (no compactness), and the
PBW presentation
$\Obs_{\hCS}\simeq\UEthree(\fg_{\hCS})$ is the $E_3$-enveloping
description. Compact-CY$_3$ restriction is the step $E_3\to E_1$
obtained by averaging over
$Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(\mathrm{Rep})$ (AP-CY6). The primitive
$\fg_{\hCS}$ supplies a Lie algebra whose derived character computes
$\kch(X) = \chi(\cO_X)$ on compact CY$_d$ by the Hodge-supertrace
identification; for $X=\C^3$ the index vanishes and the presentation
is faithful.

\paragraph{Scope and lanes.}
Chain-level: explicit CE$^1$-primitive identification, explicit
Dolbeault resolution, explicit Koszul bracket from $\Omega$.
$(\infty,1)$-categorical: the adjunction
$\UEthree\dashv\Prim_{E_3}[-2]$ in the $\infty$-category of
$E_3$-algebras in $\Ch(\Dolb)$; Francis 2013 Koszul--Quillen
equivalence. Both lanes simultaneously load-bearing (CLAUDE.md
``equal status''). Pattern 273: $\Phi$-as-functor at $(\infty,1)$-level
requires morphism preservation (open elsewhere); the object-level
chain identification here is independent of that.

\paragraph{Non-conflations.}
(i) $\UEthree(\fg)\ne U(\fg)$: the former is $E_3$-enveloping, the
latter is associative ($E_1$-)enveloping. (ii) $\Prim_{E_3}$ is not
the abelianisation $\fg_{\mathrm{ab}}$; it is the shifted tangent
fibre. (iii) $\kch(\C^3)=0\ne \kcat$ is not defined on open $\C^3$
(no compact fundamental class); do not assign $\kcat(\C^3)$.
(iv) $\gln\ne\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$: positive half only on local
CY$_3$.
