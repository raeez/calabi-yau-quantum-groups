\section*{$E_3$ PBW for Six-Dimensional Holomorphic Chern-Simons Observables}

\paragraph{Theorem.}
Let \(U\subset \C^3\) be a Stein polydisc or \(U=\C^3\) with polynomial
boundary conditions, and let \((\fg,\langle-,-\rangle)\) be a finite
dimensional quadratic dg Lie algebra. Put
\[
  \mathscr L_{\hCS}(U)=\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(U,\fg),
  \qquad
  d=\dbar,
  \qquad
  [\alpha,\beta]=[\alpha\wedge\beta]_{\fg}.
\]
The classical holomorphic Chern-Simons factorisation algebra generated by
\(\mathscr L_{\hCS}\) is the \(E_3^{\mathrm{hol}}\) enveloping
factorisation algebra. After choosing a holomorphic translation invariant
little-disks model on \(\C^3\), its locally constant reduction has a PBW
filtration \(F_\bullet\) with
\[
  \operatorname{gr}_F\,
  \mathrm U^{\mathrm{fact}}_{E_3}(\mathscr L_{\hCS})
  \simeq
  \Sym_{\Ch(\Dolb)}\!\bigl(\mathscr L_{\hCS}[2]\bigr)
\]
as an \(E_3\) factorisation coalgebra. Equivalently, the completed
classical observables are the continuous Chevalley-Eilenberg cochains
\[
  \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS}(U)
  =
  C^\bullet_{\CE,\mathrm{cont}}\bigl(\mathscr L_{\hCS}(U)[1]\bigr)
  =
  \widehat{\Sym}\bigl(\mathscr L_{\hCS}(U)[1]^\vee[-1]\bigr),
\]
with differential \(d_{\dbar}+d_{\mathrm{CE}}\), and their associated
graded is the completed signed symmetric algebra on the linear local
fields. The PBW statement is filtered and local; a quantum equality of
compact global observables requires the BV package stated below.

\paragraph{PBW mechanism.}
For an \(E_n\)-enveloping algebra in a stable \(\Q\)-linear category,
Francis' \(E_n\) PBW theorem identifies the associated graded of the
enveloping filtration with the shifted symmetric algebra on the
generating Lie object. For \(n=3\), the shift is \([2]\). It is the
degree of the Browder operation associated to
\[
  H^\bullet(\Conf_2(\R^3))\cong H^\bullet(S^2),
\]
and it is the same shift appearing in the factorisation envelope of the
local dg Lie algebra \(\mathscr L_{\hCS}\).

The symmetrisation map is computed by Fulton-MacPherson configurations:
\[
  \sigma_{E_3}\colon
  \Sym_{\Ch(\Dolb)}\bigl(\mathscr L_{\hCS}[2]\bigr)
  \longrightarrow
  \operatorname{gr}_F\,
  \mathrm U^{\mathrm{fact}}_{E_3}(\mathscr L_{\hCS}).
\]
The fibre over a configuration of \(k\) distinct points is the ordinary
signed symmetrisation. Boundary strata encode iterated brackets, and
Stokes' formula on the compactified configuration space is the Jacobi
identity for \(\mathscr L_{\hCS}\). The word \(\Sym\) means the signed
Dolbeault symmetric power: a transposition of \((0,p)\) and \((0,q)\)
forms contributes the Koszul sign \((-1)^{pq}\).

\paragraph{Primitives and observables.}
The primitive generator of the enveloping factorisation algebra is
\(\mathscr L_{\hCS}[2]\). The primitive linear functional in the
completed observable algebra is the continuous dual
\[
  \mathscr L_{\hCS}(U)[1]^\vee[-1]
  \simeq
  \Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(U,\fg)^\vee[-2].
\]
These two objects are dual presentations of the same linear BV datum. A
Milnor-Moore style reconstruction
\(\Obs_{\hCS}\simeq \UEthree(\Prim_{E_3}\Obs_{\hCS})\) requires the
augmentation, completion, conilpotence, and duality hypotheses that make
that formula meaningful.

For polynomial boundary conditions on \(\C^3\),
\[
  H^\bullet_{\dbar}(\C^3,\fg)=\C[z_1,z_2,z_3]\otimes\fg
\]
in degree \(0\). The standard affine Dolbeault contraction transfers the
strict dg Lie algebra to
\[
  \ell_1^{\min}=0,\qquad
  \ell_2^{\min}(f\otimes x,g\otimes y)=fg\otimes[x,y]_\fg,\qquad
  \ell_m^{\min}=0\quad(m\geq3).
\]
With \(L^2\) boundary conditions the harmonic subspace is zero, and this
polynomial minimal model disappears. The boundary condition is therefore
part of the theorem.

\paragraph{The \(\mathfrak{gl}_1\) sector and the toric CoHA.}
For \(\fg=\fgl_1\), the strict hCS dg Lie bracket on
\(\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3,\fgl_1)\) is zero, and the polynomial minimal
model is the abelian Lie algebra \(\C[z_1,z_2,z_3]\). The affine Yangian
appears after passing to the toric BPS Hall algebra. The precise
comparison is
\[
  \CoHA(\C^3)\simeq Y^+(\gln),
\]
the positive half identified by Schiffmann and Vasserot for the critical
CoHA of the tripled Jordan quiver. The full affine Yangian is the
Drinfeld double
\[
  D\bigl(Y^+(\gln)\bigr)=\widehat Y(\gln),
\]
and the \(\cW_{1+\infty}\) vertex algebra appears after the corresponding
double, centre, completion, and evaluation operations. Thus the PBW
filtration on hCS observables and the PBW filtration on \(Y^+(\gln)\)
match only on the constructed toric comparison locus.

\paragraph{BV quantisation and compact CY3s.}
A compact Calabi-Yau threefold statement for hCS observables requires a
holomorphic volume form, an orientation and determinant-line framing for
the BV pairing, a quadratic gauge dg Lie algebra, a Costello elliptic
gauge fixing, a heat-kernel regulator, completed local functionals,
renormalisation counterterms, factorisation or TCFT sewing data, and a
solution of the scale \(L\) quantum master equation. The first open
one-loop obstruction in complex dimension \(3\) is the quartic local
class
\[
  \Theta^\rho_U(\alpha_0,\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3)
  =
  \int_U
  \left[
  I_4^\rho\bigl(
    \alpha_0,\partial\alpha_1,\partial\alpha_2,\partial\alpha_3
  \bigr)
  \right]_{(3,3)},
  \qquad
  I_4^\rho\in\Sym^4(\fg^\vee)^\fg .
\]
Its cohomology class is \(\kanom(X,\fg,\rho)\). Vanishing or explicit
trivialisation of this class permits the first quantum correction. The
quadratic Casimir
\[
  C_2^\rho=\sum_a \rho(T_a)\rho(T^a)
\]
belongs to the two-point field-strength counterterm and to field
renormalisation. It occupies degree \(0\) and arity \(2\), while
\(\Theta^\rho\) occupies degree \(1\) and arity \(4\).

\paragraph{Relation to \(\Phi_3\).}
The object controlled by the PBW theorem is the middle factorisation
object \(\PhiFA_3\) on the local hCS or Hochschild comparison locus. The
specialised CY-to-chiral output is
\[
  A_C=
  \SpCh_{\Sigma_2,C}\bigl(\PhiFA_3(\mathcal C)\bigr)
  \in \mathrm{Alg}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{E_1}(C).
\]
For \(d\geq3\), the native output \(A_C\) is \(E_1\)-chiral. The braided
\(E_2\) structure belongs to the centre, double, or module layer, for
example
\[
  Z\bigl(\Rep^{E_1}(A_C)\bigr)
  \simeq
  \Rep^{E_2}\bigl(\Zchder(A_C)\bigr)
\]
on the verified Drinfeld-centre locus. The \(E_3\) structure in the PBW
theorem is the stage-one factorisation structure before specialisation.

\paragraph{Five separated algebraic objects.}
For an \(E_1\)-chiral algebra \(A\), the following objects have different
definitions and different functorial roles:
\[
  A,\qquad B(A),\qquad A^i,\qquad A^!,\qquad \Zchder(A).
\]
The identity \(\Omega B(A)\simeq A\) is bar-cobar inversion. The object
\(A^i\) is the Koszul dual coalgebra. The object \(A^!\) is the Verdier
or Koszul dual algebra, depending on the duality context. The bulk or
braided layer at \(d\geq3\) is \(\Zchder(A)\), equivalently the derived
chiral centre in the constructed cases.

\paragraph{Numerical invariants.}
The hCS obstruction invariant \(\kanom(X,\fg,\rho)\) is independent of
the construction-specific Calabi-Yau invariants used elsewhere. For
\(X=K3\times E\),
\[
  \kcat(X)=0,\qquad
  \kch^{\mathrm{compact}}(X)=0,\qquad
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(X)=3,\qquad
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=5,\qquad
  \kfib(X)=24.
\]
These values come from distinct constructions: total-space
\(\chi(\cO_X)\), compact Hodge supertrace, Heisenberg-Mukai
specialisation, Gritsenko's \(\Delta_5\) Borcherds denominator, and the
K3 Mukai lattice rank. The hCS quartic anomaly calculation reaches these
equalities only after an additional CY-to-chiral comparison.
