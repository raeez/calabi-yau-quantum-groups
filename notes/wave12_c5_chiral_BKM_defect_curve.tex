\section*{Chiral-BKM construction: three candidate defect curves}
\label{sec:wave12-c5-defect-curves}

Fix the compact CY$_3$ target $X = K3 \times E$. We ask: on what
complex curve $\Sigma$ does the chiral-BKM sit, and how does each
candidate curve reproduce the Igusa denominator $\Delta_5 =
\mathrm{Borch}(\phi_{0,1})$ of the GKM superalgebra $\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$?
Three candidates: $\Sigma = E$ (elliptic factor), $\Sigma = \mathbb{C}$
(HT-twist spectral plane), $\Sigma = \Sigma_{2,0} = \Sigma_{0,24}$
(Gaiotto class-S curve).

\subsection*{1.\ $\Sigma = E$: Kodaira--Spencer boundary VOA}

Reduce Type IIB on $X$ in the HT twist, contract the K3 topological
directions (Costello--Li 2016). One obtains a chiral algebra $A_E$
on $E$ whose generators are $H^*(K3; \mathbb{C})$, giving the central
charge $c(A_E) = \chi(K3) = 24$; $\kch(A_E) = 24$ (Construction
\ref{constr:costello-li-k3xe}). The OPE level is $\delta^{ab}$,
\emph{not} the Mukai pairing (Warning \ref{warn:ope-level-mukai}).
Vacuum character on $E$ is then $\eta^{-24}$ (twenty-four free bosons,
$\mathrm{Vir}$ at $c=24$), whose genus-one partition function is a
weight-$-12$ modular form. The automorphic covariance is inherited
from $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ acting on the modulus of $E$; the
Igusa lift to $\mathrm{Sp}_4(\mathbb{Z})$ requires fibration data
(second torus) that $\Sigma = E$ alone does not see. This construction
delivers the character $1/\eta^{24}$, not $1/\Delta_5$; $\Delta_5$ is
invisible unless one also couples in an elliptic-genus insertion.

\subsection*{2.\ $\Sigma = \mathbb{C}$: holomorphic twist / Costello--Paquette}

The HT twist of a 4D $\mathcal{N}=2$ theory wrapped on $\Sigma$
localises observables onto holomorphic sections. For $X = K3 \times E$
viewed as an M-theory background, placing the M5 on $E$ and further
localising after HT twist produces a spectral plane $\Sigma = \mathbb{C}$
(Costello 2013, Costello--Paquette 2020: on flat $\mathbb{C}^2$
transverse directions the twist produces a 2D chiral algebra on one
holomorphic plane, the \emph{twistor-line} slice). Here ``$\mathbb{C}$''
is \emph{not} a submanifold of the compact $X$; it is the reduced
spectral parameter obtained after the compact K3 and $E$ directions
are integrated out. The VOA is the $\Omega$-deformed KS algebra,
effectively the CoHA-side input $Y^+(\mathfrak{g}_{K3})$ at the level
of modes. $\Delta_5$ does \emph{not} appear as a vacuum character of
this VOA: $\Delta_5$ is an Igusa form, requiring $(g, m) \in \mathbb{H}_2$,
which $\Sigma = \mathbb{C}$ has thrown away. The $\mathbb{C}$ lens is
faithful to the $E_1$-chiral scope at $d=3$, but blind to the two-variable
automorphic refinement.

\subsection*{3.\ $\Sigma = \Sigma_{0,24}$: Gaiotto class-S}

The 6D $(2,0)$ $A_1$ theory on a punctured Riemann surface
$\Sigma_{g,n}$ with maximal-regular $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ punctures
produces a 4D $\mathcal{N}=2$ SCFT $\mathcal{T}[A_1, \Sigma_{g,n}]$
(Gaiotto 2012). Its Schur sector is a 2D VOA
$\chi[\mathcal{T}] = \mathrm{VOA}(\Sigma_{g,n})$
(Beem--Lemos--Liendo--Peelaers--Rastelli--van Rees 2015,
Beem--Rastelli 2015) with
\[
c_{2d}(A_1, \Sigma_{g,n}) = -12 \, c_{4d}, \qquad
c_{4d}(A_1, \Sigma_{0,n}, \mathrm{max\, reg}) = \frac{5n-13}{6}.
\]
At $(g, n) = (0, 24)$, preserving the Steiner system $S(5, 8, 24)$
(Conway--Sloane 1988), this gives $c_{4d} = 107/6$, $c_{2d} = -214$,
Coulomb rank $21$, flavour $\mathfrak{su}(2)^{24}$. The Schur index,
$M_{24}$-averaged over flavour fugacities, equals
\[
\phi_{0,1}(z_1, z_2) = 2 \cdot \ZK(z_1, z_2),
\]
whose Borcherds lift $\mathrm{Borch}(\phi_{0,1}) = \Delta_5$ is the
denominator of $\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$ (Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995,
Borcherds 1998, Lorgat 2020). This curve has the $M_{24}$
moonshine symmetry, sees the Mukai $\widetilde{H}(K3)$ rank $24$
directly in the puncture count, and is \emph{the} channel through
which the Igusa $\Delta_5$ is manufactured.

\subsection*{4.\ Comparison and unification}

The three defect curves are distinct constructions.
\begin{center}
\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{tabular}{llll}
Curve & VOA & Vacuum character & $\kch$ \\
\hline
$\Sigma = E$ & KS boundary $A_E$ & $\eta(\tau)^{-24}$ & $24$ \\
$\Sigma = \mathbb{C}$ & $\Omega$-KS / CoHA & formal $Y^+$ char. & $\kch(K3 \times E) = 0$ \\
$\Sigma = \Sigma_{0,24}$ & $\chi[\mathcal{T}[A_1, \Sigma_{0,24}]]$ & $\phi_{0,1} \mapsto \Delta_5^{-1}$ & $\kch[c_{2d}= -214]$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
There is no canonical defect curve: each curve witnesses a different
slice of $\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$. $E$ gives the modular measure;
$\mathbb{C}$ gives the CoHA generators; $\Sigma_{0,24}$ gives the
Igusa denominator identity. The unifying datum is the \emph{triple}
$(E, \mathbb{C}, \Sigma_{0,24})$ equipped with a Schur-averaging
surjection $\chi[\mathcal{T}[A_1, \Sigma_{0,24}]] \twoheadrightarrow
A_E$ factoring through the $M_{24}$-orbifold and a Borcherds-lift
identification with a shift of $A_\mathbb{C}$ (see
Section~\ref{sec:cy-c-six-routes}). These are six different
constructions of $G(K3 \times E)$ in the sense of the AP150 cache:
NOT six applications of $\Phifun$.

\subsection*{5.\ Which construction delivers $\Delta_5^{-1}$?}

Only $\Sigma = \Sigma_{0,24}$. The Schur index of
$\mathcal{T}[A_1, \Sigma_{0,24}]$, under $M_{24}$-flavour averaging,
equals $\phi_{0,1}$ (class-S Schur index $=$ weight-$0$ index-$1$
weak Jacobi form at puncture count $24$). Borcherds lift sends
$\phi_{0,1} \mapsto \Delta_5$ (Lorgat 2020, Eq.\ (3.1); $c_1(0) = 10$,
$\kBKM(\Delta_5) = 5$). Hence
\[
\chi_{\mathrm{vac}}\big(\chi[\mathcal{T}[A_1, \Sigma_{0,24}]]\big)
\overset{M_{24}\text{-avg}}{\longrightarrow}
\phi_{0,1}
\overset{\mathrm{Borch}}{\longmapsto}
\Delta_5
\]
and the chiral-BKM denominator is the Borcherds--Gritsenko lift of
the $M_{24}$-averaged class-S Schur index. The other two curves do
not carry the Igusa directly.
