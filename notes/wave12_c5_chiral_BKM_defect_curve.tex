\section*{Defect Curves for the \(K3\times E\) Chiral BKM}
\label{sec:k3e-defect-curves}

Let \(X=K3\times E\). The \(\Delta_5\)-BKM construction has three
curve-level inputs, each with its own functorial role:
\[
  E,\qquad \mathbb C,\qquad \Sigma_{0,24}.
\]
The elliptic curve \(E\) supplies the Heisenberg-Mukai chiral
specialisation; the plane \(\mathbb C\) supplies the local
\(\Omega\)-deformed CoHA/shuffle coordinate; the punctured sphere
\(\Sigma_{0,24}\) supplies the class-\(\mathcal S\) Schur sector whose
\(M_{24}\)-averaged Jacobi-form projection enters the Borcherds lift.
The genus-two Siegel variable belongs to the automorphic target of the
lift.

\subsection*{The Elliptic Curve \(E\)}

The \(K3\)-fibration specialisation is the Stage-2 object
\[
  A_E
  =
  \SpCh_{K3,E}\bigl(\PhiFA_3(D^b\mathrm{Coh}(K3\times E))\bigr).
\]
Since \(X\) has Calabi-Yau dimension \(3\), \(A_E\) is an
\(E_1\)-chiral algebra on the elliptic factor. The \(K3\) fibre
contributes the Mukai lattice
\[
  \widetilde H(K3,\mathbb Z)
  =
  H^0(K3,\mathbb Z)\oplus H^2(K3,\mathbb Z)\oplus H^4(K3,\mathbb Z),
  \qquad
  \operatorname{rk}\widetilde H(K3,\mathbb Z)=24.
\]
The associated lattice-Heisenberg character has the genus-one form
\[
  \eta(\tau)^{-24}.
\]
This rank-\(24\) oscillator count is the fibre invariant
\(\kfib(K3\times E)=24\). The compact CY\(_3\) Hodge supertrace is
\[
  \kch(K3\times E)
  =
  \sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(K3\times E)
  =
  1-1+1-1
  =
  0.
\]
The categorical Euler invariant is the same total-space value by
K\"unneth:
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)
  =
  \chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\,\chi(\mathcal O_E)
  =
  2\cdot 0
  =
  0.
\]
The Heisenberg-Mukai Stage-2 modular characteristic is the separate
rank-additive value
\[
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)
  =
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3)+\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(E)
  =
  2+1
  =
  3.
\]

\subsection*{The Spectral Plane \(\mathbb C\)}

The local holomorphic twist of the \(6d\) hCS theory on \(\mathbb C^3\)
gives a local factorisation algebra of classical observables. After
choosing a holomorphic line and applying the \(\Omega\)-deformation, the
defect observables form a chiral algebra on a spectral plane
\(\mathbb C\). This plane is an output coordinate after compact
directions have been integrated out.

The algebraic output belongs to the positive-half CoHA lane:
\[
  \mathrm{CoHA}(\mathbb C^3)=Y^+,
\]
where \(Y^+\) is the positive half of the affine
\(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1\) Yangian/shuffle algebra. The double or
centre of this positive half is the object compared with
\(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}\). For \(d=3\), the native CY-to-chiral output
is \(E_1\)-chiral; \(E_2\) structure is carried by
\[
  Z(\mathrm{Rep}(A_E)),\qquad
  D(Y^+),\qquad
  \text{or a specified braided module category}.
\]
The genus-two variables of \(\Delta_5\) belong to the automorphic
Borcherds target, outside the local plane.

\subsection*{The Class-\(\mathcal S\) Curve \(\Sigma_{0,24}\)}

The class-\(\mathcal S\) parent is
\[
  \mathcal T[A_1,\Sigma_{0,24}],
\]
where \(\Sigma_{0,24}\) is a sphere with twenty-four maximal regular
\(\mathfrak{su}(2)\) punctures. A pants decomposition has \(22\)
trinions \(T_2\) and \(21\) \(\mathfrak{su}(2)\) gauge tubes. Since
each \(T_2\) contributes \((n_v,n_h)=(0,4)\) and each tube contributes
\((3,0)\),
\[
  (n_v,n_h)
  =
  21(3,0)+22(0,4)
  =
  (63,88).
\]
The Shapere-Tachikawa normalisation gives
\[
  c_{4d}
  =
  \frac{2n_v+n_h}{12}
  =
  \frac{107}{6},
  \qquad
  c_{2d}=-12c_{4d}=-214.
\]
Thus the Schur chiral algebra attached to
\(\mathcal T[A_1,\Sigma_{0,24}]\) has central charge \(-214\). Its
Cardy leading term is governed by this \(2d\) central charge, while the
automorphic denominator \(\Delta_5\) has weight \(5\). The bridge to
\(\Delta_5\) is therefore the composite datum
\[
  \mathcal I_{\mathrm{Schur}}\bigl(\mathcal T[A_1,\Sigma_{0,24}]\bigr)
  \xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{av}_{M_{24}}\;}
  \phi_{0,1}^{K3}
  \xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{Bor}\;}
  \Delta_5 .
\]
The first arrow is an \(M_{24}\)-equivariant flavour-fugacity averaging
map to the K3 weak Jacobi form. The second arrow is the
Gritsenko-Nikulin/Borcherds multiplicative lift.

\subsection*{The \(\Delta_5\) Normalisation}

Use the Eichler-Zagier normalisation
\[
  \phi_{0,1}(\tau,z)\big|_{q^0}
  =
  \zeta^{-1}+10+\zeta,
  \qquad
  c_{\phi_{0,1}}(0,0)=10.
\]
Borcherds' singular-theta weight formula gives
\[
  \operatorname{wt}\operatorname{Bor}(\phi_{0,1})
  =
  \frac{c_{\phi_{0,1}}(0,0)}{2}
  =
  5.
\]
In the primitive BKM denominator convention, the monic denominator
section is
\[
  D_5(2Z)
  =
  64^{-1}\Delta_5(2Z)
  =
  \operatorname{Bor}(\phi_{0,1})(Z),
  \qquad
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})
  =
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)
  =
  c_1(0)/2
  =
  10/2
  =
  5.
\]
The Igusa square convention uses the weight-\(10\) form
\[
  \Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_5^2.
\]
The primitive BKM denominator and the reduced-DT/Igusa square are two
normalisations of the same genus-two automorphic package.

\subsection*{The Genus-Two Siegel Target}

The genus-two object belongs to the target of the Borcherds lift. The
Siegel upper half-space \(\mathbb H_2\), the Humbert divisor \(H_1\),
and the Jacobian of a genus-two curve organize the automorphic form
\(\Delta_5\). The \(A_1\) class-\(\mathcal S\) curve with
\[
  c_{4d}=107/6,\qquad c_{2d}=-214
\]
is \(\Sigma_{0,24}\). A closed genus-two curve has different
four-dimensional anomaly arithmetic.

\subsection*{Hall-Drinfeld and Yangian Branches}

The BKM branch attached to \(K3\times E\) is the completed
Hall-Drinfeld double of the compact Hall positive half after the
Borcherds denominator comparison. Its root supercharacter is controlled
by the Fourier coefficients of \(\phi_{0,1}\), and its primitive
denominator is \(\Delta_5\).

The historical K3 Yangian branch is the Mukai self-mirror/RTT branch
at the K3 surface. It has its own Drinfeld-current presentation and
Maulik-Okounkov stable-envelope origin. The Hall-Drinfeld/BKM branch
and the K3 Yangian branch share K3 input data, while their algebraic
endpoints, completions, and proof obligations are distinct.

\subsection*{Boundary and Defect Quantisation Data}

A compact hCS boundary construction of the \(K3\times E\) BKM object
requires the following data:
\[
\begin{gathered}
  \text{an oriented reduced hCS/Ran datum},\qquad
  \text{a compact Hall positive half},\\
  \text{a Serre-dual negative half},\qquad
  \text{a non-degenerate Hopf pairing},\\

the \(E_1\)-chiral output.

\subsection*{Recognition Boundary}

A theorem identifying the curve-level constructions as one compact BKM
object must supply:
\[
  \mathrm{av}_{M_{24}},\qquad
  c_{\phi_{0,1}}(0,0)=10,\qquad
  \mathrm{CoHA}(\mathbb C^3)=Y^+,\qquad
  D_{\mathrm{Hall}}\bigl(Y^+_{K3\times E}\bigr),\qquad
  Z(\mathrm{Rep}(A_E)).
\]
The first two entries are automorphic and Schur-index data; the third is
the local positive-half comparison; the last two are the double/centre
data needed for the full Hall-Drinfeld/BKM object and its \(E_2\)-level
module theory. The curve roles and constants are fixed on the stated
local anchors. The compact Hall-Drinfeld recognition remains a
construction-level obligation.
