\section*{$\chi(\Yplus(\Ytil_{\mathrm{conifold}}))$ in MacMahon form}

\subsection*{Datum}

Let $\Ytil$ be a small resolution of the conifold $\{xy - zw = 0\} \subset
\mathbb{A}^4$. Two flops $\Ytil_{\pm}$ exist; each contains a single
exceptional $\mathbb{P}^1$ of class $\beta$ with normal bundle
$\mathcal{O}(-1) \oplus \mathcal{O}(-1)$. The Gopakumar--Vafa invariants
(Gopakumar--Vafa 1998; Iqbal--Kashani-Poor--Vafa 2003) read
\[
  \GV_0(\Ytil, d\beta) = 0 \quad (d \geq 1), \qquad
  \GV_1(\Ytil, \beta) = 1, \qquad \GV_g(\Ytil, d\beta) = 0 \;\;(g \geq 1 \text{ or } d \geq 2).
\]
Szendr\H{o}i's noncommutative Donaldson--Thomas partition function for
the resolved conifold (Szendr\H{o}i 2008, Theorem~5.2; Nagao--Nakajima
2011 for the wall-crossing) carries the chiral image
\[
  \Yplus(\Ytil) \;=\; U(\mathfrak n_+^{\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_2}) \otimes \Lambda,
  \qquad \kch(\Yplus(\Ytil)) = 1,
\]
as inscribed in Wave-17 C1. The graded character factorises into an
abelian factor (MacMahon, from the $\widetilde Y$-internal $\mathbb{C}^3$
contributions along the zero-dimensional stratum) and a
curve-class factor (Gopakumar--Vafa contribution along $\beta$).

\subsection*{Szendr\H{o}i--Nekrasov--Okounkov form}

\begin{proposition}[Szendr\H{o}i 2008, \S 5]\label{prop:szendroi-character-conifold}
On the Calabi--Yau threefold $\Ytil$ with $\chtop(\Ytil) = 2$ and $\GV_1
= 1$,
\[
  \chi(\Yplus(\Ytil))(q)
  \;=\;
  \MacM(q)^{\chtop(\Ytil)}
  \cdot
  \prod_{n \geq 1} \bigl(1 - (-q)^n\bigr)^{n \cdot \GV_1(\Ytil)}
  \;=\;
  \MacM(q)^{2} \cdot \prod_{n \geq 1}(1 - (-q)^n)^{n},
\]
where $\MacM(q) = \prod_{n \geq 1}(1-q^n)^{-n}$ is MacMahon's generating
function for plane partitions (MacMahon 1916, \S\,IX; also
Okounkov--Reshetikhin--Vafa 2006).
\end{proposition}

\subsection*{Explicit expansion}

Write $\MacM(q)^{2} = \prod_{n \geq 1}(1-q^n)^{-2n}$ and
$S(q) := \prod_{n \geq 1}(1-(-q)^n)^{n}$.

\emph{MacMahon squared.} Logarithmic expansion:
\[
  \log \MacM(q)^{2}
  \;=\;
  2 \sum_{n \geq 1} n \sum_{k \geq 1} \frac{q^{nk}}{k}
  \;=\;
  2q + 5 q^{2} + \frac{26}{3} q^{3} + \frac{59}{4} q^{4} + \cdots,
\]
yielding, by exponentiation,
\[
  \MacM(q)^{2}
  \;=\;
  1 + 2q + 9 q^{2} + 30 q^{3} + 89 q^{4} + 234 q^{5} + 578 q^{6} + \cdots.
\]

\emph{Sign factor.} Split parity: $S(q) = \prod_{m \geq 1}(1 - q^{2m})^{2m}
\cdot \prod_{m \geq 0}(1 + q^{2m+1})^{2m+1}$. Take logs:
\[
  \log S(q)
  \;=\;
  \sum_{n \geq 1} n \log(1 - (-q)^n)
  \;=\;
  - \sum_{n \geq 1} n \sum_{k \geq 1} \frac{(-q)^{nk}}{k}.
\]
Collecting by total degree $d = nk$,
\[
  [q^{d}] \log S(q) \;=\; - (-1)^{d} \sigma_{1}(d),
  \qquad
  \sigma_1(d) := \sum_{n \mid d} n \cdot \frac{d}{n \cdot (d/n)} = \sum_{n \mid d} 1 \cdot n = \sigma_1(d),
\]
so $\log S(q) = -q + \tfrac{3}{2} q^{2} - \tfrac{4}{3} q^{3} + \tfrac{7}{4} q^{4}
- \tfrac{6}{5} q^{5} + \cdots$. Exponentiation gives
\[
  S(q) = 1 - q + 2 q^{2} - 4 q^{3} + 8 q^{4} - 15 q^{5} + 28 q^{6} - \cdots.
\]

\emph{Product.} Let $a_d := [q^d] \MacM(q)^{2}$ and $b_d := [q^d] S(q)$.
Then $[q^d] \chi(\Yplus(\Ytil)) = \sum_{k=0}^{d} a_k b_{d-k}$:
\begin{align*}
  [q^{0}] &= 1 \cdot 1 = 1, \\
  [q^{1}] &= 1\cdot(-1) + 2\cdot 1 = 1, \\
  [q^{2}] &= 1\cdot 2 + 2\cdot(-1) + 9\cdot 1 = 9, \\
  [q^{3}] &= 1\cdot(-4) + 2\cdot 2 + 9\cdot(-1) + 30\cdot 1 = 21, \\
  [q^{4}] &= 1\cdot 8 + 2\cdot(-4) + 9\cdot 2 + 30\cdot(-1) + 89\cdot 1 = 77.
\end{align*}

Hence
\[
  \boxed{\;\chi(\Yplus(\Ytil_{\mathrm{conifold}}))(q)
  \;=\;
  1 + q + 9 q^{2} + 21 q^{3} + 77 q^{4} + \cdots.\;}
\]

\begin{remark}
The leading $1 + q$ isolates the single Gopakumar--Vafa exceptional
class: the $n=1$ factor $(1+q)^{1}$ cancels one unit of the $2q$ coming
from $\MacM^{2}$, leaving the genus-zero curve count $1$ at $q^{1}$.
The apparent heuristic expansion $1 + q + 3q^{2} + 6q^{3} + \cdots$
tracks the reduced DT partition function
$Z_{\mathrm{DT}}^{\mathrm{red}}(\Ytil) = S(q)$ after the MacMahon
$\MacM(q)^{2}$ factor is stripped (Behrend--Bryan--Szendr\H{o}i 2013 for
the motivic refinement), \emph{not} the full graded character of
$\Yplus$. The explicit product $\MacM^{2} \cdot S$ is the graded
dimension of the positive-half Yangian, by Szendr\H{o}i's
identification at chain level.
\end{remark}

\subsection*{Verification}

Three independent paths:

\emph{(i) Direct expansion.} The coefficient arithmetic above uses only
the two primary identities $\MacM(q) = \prod(1-q^n)^{-n}$
(MacMahon 1916) and $\GV_1 = 1$ (Gopakumar--Vafa 1998, \S 5;
Iqbal--Kashani-Poor--Vafa 2003, \S 3).

\emph{(ii) Nekrasov--Okounkov compatibility.} On a CY threefold with
divisor classes only at genus zero and one curve class with $\GV_1 = 1$,
the Nekrasov--Okounkov formula collapses to
$Z(q, Q) = \MacM(q)^{\chtop} \cdot M(-Q, q)^{-1} \cdot M(-Q^{-1}, q)^{-1}$
(Maulik--Nekrasov--Okounkov--Pandharipande 2006); setting $Q = 1$ and
extracting the $\beta$-class contribution recovers the factor $S(q)$ up
to the known flop sign (Nagao--Nakajima 2011).

\emph{(iii) Conifold topological vertex.} The refined topological vertex
computation of Iqbal--Kashani-Poor--Vafa gives
$Z_{\mathrm{top}}(\Ytil) = \prod(1 - Q q^{n})^{n}$ at $Q = -1$, which is
exactly $S(q)$. Combined with $\MacM(q)^{\chtop(\Ytil)} = \MacM(q)^2$
for the constant-map contribution (Faber--Pandharipande 2000), the
product is $\MacM(q)^{2} S(q)$.

All three paths converge on the same explicit series. $\kch(\Yplus(\Ytil))
= 1$ is the Hodge supertrace of $\widetilde Y$'s small resolution
structure sheaf: $\chi(\mathcal O_{\Ytil}) = 1 - 0 + 0 - 0 = 1$ since
$h^{0,0} = 1$ and $h^{0,q} = 0$ for $q \geq 1$ on the non-compact
resolved conifold (Calabi--Yau + simply connected + local model).
