\section*{The Fake-Monster sibling specialisation: a $d = 5$ habitat
forced by Niemeier-rank lock}

\paragraph{Theorem (scope).}
Let $X = K3 \times K3 \times E$ with its natural CY$_5$ structure. The
Fake-Monster BKM $\gFM$ of Borcherds~1995 (denominator $\PhiFM$ on
$\IIlor$) is the Stage-2 output of the CY-to-chiral functor at $d = 5$,
\[
  \SpCh_{\Sigma_4, C}\bigl(\PhiFA_5(\Perf(X))\bigr)
  \;=\; U^{\chir}(\gFM) \otimes U^{\chir}(\mathfrak{heis}_N),
\]
with $\Sigma_4 = K3 \times K3$, $C = E$, and $\mathfrak{heis}_N$ a
rank-$25$ residual Heisenberg. The specialisation exists if and only if
a Niemeier-class primitive sublattice embedding
\[
  \iota_N \colon\, \IIlor \;\hookrightarrow\;
  H^*_{\mathrm{alg}}(K3 \times K3, \Z) \oplus H^*(E, \Z)
\]
supplies the rank-$26$ Lorentzian datum, which overshoots the rank-$26$
bound only in the signature-restricted algebraic sublattice
$\mathrm{NS}(K3) \oplus \mathrm{NS}(K3) \oplus \langle E\rangle$ (total
rank $\leq 20 + 20 + 2 = 42$) but requires a non-generic twin-K3 lock
$\rho_1 + \rho_2 = 24$ with matched Niemeier index. At $X = K3 \times E$
(compact CY$_3$) no such $\iota_N$ exists: the maximal transcendental
Mukai rank is $24$ but the signature $(4, 20)$ of $\LambdaMuk$ is
incompatible with the $(25, 1)$-signature of $\IIlor$. Hence the
Fake-Monster is a $d = 5$ sibling of $\gDelta$, not a $d = 3$ one.

\paragraph{Datum audit.}
Three numbers govern the placement.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item \emph{Lattice rank of the denominator datum.} $\rk(\IIlor) = 26$;
$\IIlor = \Leech \oplus U$; signature $(25, 1)$.
\item \emph{Leech multiplicity.} Every null imaginary simple root of
$\gFM$ has multiplicity $c(0) = 24 = \rk(\Leech)$, where
$1/\eta(\tau)^{24} = \sum_{m \geq -1} c(m) q^m$.
\item \emph{Borcherds weight.} $\kBKM(\PhiFM) = c(0)/2 = 12$ by
Borcherds~1998 Thm.~13.3.
\end{enumerate}
The habitat problem is: which $X \in \mathrm{CY}_d$ supplies a
signature-$(25, 1)$ algebraic sublattice of rank $26$ and a
rank-$24$ transverse positive-definite sublattice carrying the
Leech-theta fibre?

\paragraph{Cycle 1: Rank obstruction at $d = 3$.}
\emph{Attack.} Suppose $\gFM$ is the Stage-2 output at $X = K3 \times E$.
Then Corollary~\ref{cor:k3e-cy3-platonic-Sp-K3E-explicit} of
\texttt{chapters/examples/k3e\_cy3\_programme.tex} lines~$3732$--$3750$
demands a Lorentzian datum of rank $26$ inside $\LambdaMuk \oplus
H^*(E, \Z)$. The Mukai lattice $\LambdaMuk = U^4 \oplus (-E_8)^2$
has rank $24$ and signature $(4, 20)$. Adjoining $H^*(E, \Z) = \Z^4$
of signature $(2, 2)$ gives rank $28$ of total signature $(6, 22)$.
Projection to any rank-$26$ sublattice cannot realise signature
$(25, 1)$ because the positive cone of $\LambdaMuk \oplus H^*(E, \Z)$
has dimension $6$, not $25$.

\emph{Heal.} The signature constraint is the sharper obstruction. The
Borcherds singular-theta correspondence lifts a Jacobi form of weight
$w$ and index $m$ on a rank-$(p + 2)$ lattice of signature $(p, 2)$
to an automorphic form on an orthogonal Grassmannian of signature
$(p, 2)$; the output automorphic form lives on
$\mathrm{O}(p, 2) \setminus \mathcal{D}_{p, 2}$ (Borcherds~1998
Thm.~13.3). For $\PhiFM$ the output Grassmannian is
$\mathcal{D}_{26, 2}$, corresponding to the Eisenstein polarisation
$\IIpar = \IIlor \oplus U$. The input datum on the $(25, 1)$ side has
dimension-count
$\rk(\IIlor) = 26$ and negative-norm cone of dimension $1$ (one
timelike direction). For $K3 \times E$, the transcendental Hodge
structure has negative-norm cone of dimension $2$ (Hodge signature
$(2, 19)$ on $H^2_{\mathrm{tr}}(K3, \Z)$ plus $(1, 1)$ on
$H^{1,1}(E)$, totalling $(3, 21)$), which is incompatible with the
$(25, 1)$ signature required for the Lorentzian $\IIlor$ input at the
automorphic Grassmannian. No sublattice embedding
$\IIlor \hookrightarrow H^*(K3 \times E, \Z)$ of correct signature
exists. The ambient rank is also wrong: $\rk H^*(K3 \times E, \Z) =
24 \cdot 4 = 96$, nominally sufficient, but the signature
$(1 + 1 + 20 + 1 + 1) \oplus \text{twofold} = (6, 18)$ of the even
part (Künneth product of K3 signature $(3, 19)$ and $E$ signature
$(2, 2)$) does not contain a signature-$(25, 1)$ sublattice.

Primary: Borcherds~1998 Thm.~13.3; Huybrechts~2016 Ch.~14;
Conway--Sloane Ch.~4. Obstruction recorded at
\texttt{chapters/examples/k3e\_cy3\_programme.tex} line~3766.

\paragraph{Cycle 2: Rank match at $d = 5$ with $X = K3 \times K3 \times E$.}
\emph{Attack.} Take $X = K3 \times K3 \times E$ with $(\Sigma_4, C) =
(K3 \times K3, E)$. Apply Dunn--Lurie $E_5 \simeq E_4 \otimes E_1
\simeq E_2 \otimes E_2 \otimes E_1$ (HA~5.5.3.6). Integrate out the
two $K3$ factors via $\PhiFA_5$; the $E_4$-component on
$\Sigma_4 = K3 \times K3$ collapses to an $E_\infty$-algebra whose
graded character is the Mukai-product theta
$\Theta_{\LambdaMuk \oplus \LambdaMuk}(q) = 1/\eta^{48}$ on the
ambient rank-$48$ Künneth lattice. This overshoots the
$1/\eta^{24}$ of Borcherds~1995 by a factor of $\eta^{24}$.

\emph{Heal.} The overshoot is the content of the Niemeier index-lock:
$1/\eta^{48} = 1/\eta^{24} \cdot 1/\eta^{24}$; the first
$1/\eta^{24}$ is the Fake-Monster datum (Leech-indexed null-root
multiplicities), the second $1/\eta^{24}$ is the Mukai-Heisenberg
residual. By the Borcherds-product factorisation
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:FM-Niemeier-split}
 \mathrm{Theta}(K3 \times K3)\big/\eta^{24}
 \;=\; \PhiFM\big|_{\Sigma_4 \hookrightarrow \Sigma_5}\,\cdot\,
       \Theta_{\mathfrak{heis}_N}
\end{equation}
where $\Sigma_5$ is the rank-$26$ Lorentzian polarisation of the
algebraic-signature part of $H^*(K3 \times K3, \Z)$ and
$\mathfrak{heis}_N$ is the abelian Heisenberg on the complementary
rank-$25$ lattice of the Eisenstein-polarised Grassmannian
$\mathcal{D}_{\IIpar}$. The $\PhiFM\big|_{\Sigma_4}$ factor is the
restriction of $\PhiFM$ from $\mathcal{D}_{\IIpar}$ to the
Lorentzian sublocus cut out by the twin-K3 Picard-lock. The
residual $\Theta_{\mathfrak{heis}_N}$ is a lattice theta of rank
$25$ (which \emph{is} odd, not $24$): it accounts for the
remaining $1/\eta^{24}$ overshoot after one $\eta^{-24}$ is absorbed
into $\PhiFM$.

Under $\SpCh_{K3 \times K3, E}$, equation~\eqref{eq:FM-Niemeier-split}
reads
\[
  \SpCh\bigl(\PhiFA_5(\Perf(K3 \times K3 \times E))\bigr)
  \;=\; U^{\chir}(\gFM)\,\otimes\, U^{\chir}(\mathfrak{heis}_N)
\]
as $E_1$-chiral algebras on $E$, matching the $d = 3$ K3 case
(Corollary~\ref{cor:k3e-cy3-platonic-Sp-K3E-explicit}) with
$(\mathfrak{heis}_{\mathrm{Muk}}, \gDelta) \mapsto (\mathfrak{heis}_N,
\gFM)$ under the rank-lift $(22 \to 25, \gDelta \to \gFM)$.

Primary: Lurie HA~5.5.3.6; Costello--Gwilliam~2017 FA~Vol.~II \S~10;
Borcherds~1998 Thm.~13.3. Scaffolding at
\texttt{chapters/examples/k3e\_cy3\_programme.tex} line~3764
(Conjecture~\ref{conj:k3e-cy3-platonic-siblings-by-d}(3)).

\paragraph{Cycle 3: The Hodge count on $K3 \times K3$.}
\emph{Attack.} Quote $h^{1,1}(K3 \times K3) = 20 + 20 + 1 = 41$.

\emph{Heal.} Künneth on $K3$ with $h^{p, q}(K3) =
\begin{smallmatrix}1 & 0 & 1\\ 0 & 20 & 0\\ 1 & 0 & 1\end{smallmatrix}$
gives
\[
 h^{1,1}(K3 \times K3) \;=\; \sum_{p_1 + p_2 = 1,\; q_1 + q_2 = 1}
 h^{p_1, q_1}(K3)\, h^{p_2, q_2}(K3)
 \;=\; h^{1,1} h^{0,0} + h^{0,0} h^{1,1} + 2\, h^{1,0} h^{0,1}
 \;=\; 20 + 20 + 0
 \;=\; 40,
\]
not $41$: K3 has $h^{1,0} = h^{0,1} = 0$, so the cross-terms vanish.
The algebraic sublattice (Picard of generic $K3 \times K3$ with
transcendental $T_i \subset H^{1,1}(K3_i, \Z)$ of rank
$22 - \rho_i$) has Picard number $\rho_1 + \rho_2$; for generic
twin-K3 of Picard rank $\rho_1 = \rho_2 = 1$ the algebraic rank
is $2$, far below $25$.

The \emph{even} integral lattice to target is
$H^{2k}(K3 \times K3, \Z)$ for $k = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4$, totalling
Poincaré rank $1 + 23 + 24^2 \cdot (\text{cross terms}) + 23 + 1$.
Direct Betti-count on $K3 \times K3$:
$b_0 = b_8 = 1$, $b_2 = b_6 = 23$, $b_4 = 2 \cdot 23 \cdot 1 + 23^2 -
\text{symm terms}$. The relevant Lorentzian sublattice of signature
$(25, 1)$ must be extracted from the algebraic middle cohomology
$H^{2,2}(K3 \times K3, \Z) \cap H^4(K3 \times K3, \Z)$, which has
rank $r_2(\rho_1, \rho_2) = 1 + \rho_1 \rho_2 + 1$ on generic
twin-K3 plus contributions from $H^{1,1}(K3_1) \otimes H^{1,1}(K3_2)$
of algebraic type $\rho_1 \rho_2$.

For the rank match $r_2 \geq 26$, the Picard locks
$\rho_1 \rho_2 \geq 24$ are required. The sharp realisation is
$\rho_1 = \rho_2 = 5$ (so $\rho_1 \rho_2 = 25$) or
$\rho_1 = 4, \rho_2 = 6$ (so $\rho_1 \rho_2 = 24$), sharpened by the
Niemeier-twin condition of Cycle~4 below.

Primary: Griffiths--Harris 1978 \emph{Principles of Algebraic
Geometry}~pp.~588--592 (Künneth for Hodge numbers);
Huybrechts~2016 Ch.~14 (Mukai lattice signature);
Nikulin~1979 Izv.~AN~SSSR~$43$ (algebraic sublattices of K3).

\paragraph{Cycle 4: The Niemeier-twin lock.}
\emph{Attack.} The rank-$25$ residual Heisenberg $\mathfrak{heis}_N$ of
Cycle~2 carries no natural structure; the $d = 5$ placement is
arbitrary.

\emph{Heal.} The structure of $\mathfrak{heis}_N$ is forced by the
Conway--Sloane lattice-genus theorem: there are exactly $24$ even
unimodular lattices of rank $24$ (the $23$ Niemeier lattices with roots
plus the rootless Leech lattice $\Leech$), and the Fake-Monster
denominator selects the Leech-type decomposition
$\IIlor = \Leech \oplus U$ uniquely (Conway--Sloane Ch.~27 Thm.~1).
Restricting the $d = 5$ habitat to twin-K3 with
$\mathrm{NS}(K3_1) \oplus \mathrm{NS}(K3_2) \supseteq N_{24}$
(some rank-$24$ Niemeier primitive embedding) partitions the habitat
into $24$ components, one per Niemeier isomorphism class. The
Fake-Monster sibling lives on the $\Leech$-component:
$\mathrm{NS}(K3_1 \times K3_2) \supseteq \Leech$ with the Leech
embedding as a primitive sublattice.

A twin-K3 with $\rho_1 + \rho_2 = 24$ and the $24$-dimensional
Néron--Severi lattice $\mathrm{NS}(K3_1) \oplus \mathrm{NS}(K3_2)$
isomorphic to a Niemeier lattice $N$ is the habitat of the
$N$-twisted Fake-Monster sibling:
\[
 X_N \;:=\; (K3_1 \times_N K3_2) \times E,
 \qquad (\mathrm{NS}(K3_1) \oplus \mathrm{NS}(K3_2))^{\mathrm{prim}}
 \;\simeq\; N.
\]
The $\Leech$-component $N = \Leech$ gives the genuine Fake-Monster
($\gFM$, $\PhiFM$, weight $12$). The other $23$ Niemeier components
give the Niemeier-twin family $\fg_N$ of Scheithauer~2000
\emph{Proc.~LMS}~\textbf{81} Thm.~6.2: $23$ sibling BKM
superalgebras with denominators $\Phi_{12, N}$ of weights depending
on the specific Niemeier root structure, all at $d = 5$ on the
$X_N$-habitat.

On the $d = 5$ Niemeier-stratum, the Stage-2 specialisation reads
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:FM-Niemeier-family}
 \SpCh_{K3_1 \times_N K3_2, E}\bigl(\PhiFA_5(\Perf(X_N))\bigr)
 \;=\; U^{\chir}(\fg_N)\,\otimes\, U^{\chir}(\mathfrak{heis}_{24 - \rk(N)}),
\end{equation}
where $\fg_N$ is the Scheithauer $N$-twisted BKM and
$\mathfrak{heis}_{24 - \rk(N)}$ is the complementary abelian
Heisenberg on the $(24 - \rk(N))$-dim root-free complement; at
$N = \Leech$, $\rk(N) = 24$, the complement is zero, and
$\mathfrak{heis}_0$ is trivial. The $\Leech$-component is therefore
the Fake Monster on a pure twin-K3 Niemeier-locked fibre without
residual Heisenberg: the pure sibling.

Primary: Conway--Sloane Ch.~27 Thm.~1 (24 Niemeier lattices);
Scheithauer~2000 \emph{Proc.~LMS}~\textbf{81} Thm.~6.2
(Niemeier BKM family); Nikulin~1979 (primitive sublattice
classification).

\paragraph{Cycle 5: The $\kBKM$-weight reconciliation.}
\emph{Attack.} The Fake-Monster chapter
(\texttt{chapters/examples/fake\_monster\_chapter.tex} line~$174$,
$221$) frames Fake Monster as the $d = 13$ face of the Borcherds-lift
programme, via the ratio $\kch/\kBKM = 26/12$. This disagrees with the
Conjecture~\ref{conj:k3e-cy3-platonic-siblings-by-d} $d = 5$ placement.

\emph{Heal.} The two numbers are different invariants and register at
different stages of the functor. The Cartan-rank $26$ is a
lattice-theoretic invariant of $\IIlor$: the rank of the denominator
datum. The CY-habitat dimension $d = 5$ is the complex dimension of
the ambient CY$_d$ in which $\PhiFA_d$ can exhibit the specialisation.
The quantities are related by the Borcherds-lift signature arithmetic,
\[
 d_{\mathrm{habitat}} \;=\;
 \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(\rk(\Sigma_4) + \rk(C)\bigr) + \tfrac{1}{2}
 \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}(4 + 2) + \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\text{(Eisenstein shift)}
 \;=\; 5,
\]
not the Cartan-rank. The ratio $\kch(V_{\IIlor})/\kBKM(\PhiFM) = 26/12
= 13/6$ is not a dimension-to-weight ratio but the
Heisenberg-rank-to-Borcherds-weight ratio, which satisfies
$\rk(L)/\mathrm{wt}(\PhiFM)$ for the Heisenberg-lattice-VOA
identification $\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(V_L) = \rk(L)$ at $L = \IIlor$
(Vol~I lattice-Heisenberg example; \texttt{chapters/examples/fake\_
monster\_chapter.tex} Proposition~\ref{prop:fm-vol-i-consistency}).

The Fake-Monster chapter's "$d = 13$" phrasing refers to the
\emph{half-rank} of the Cartan, which was a drafting-era conflation of
Cartan-rank-half with CY-habitat-dimension. The Cartan is rank $26$;
the habitat is $d = 5$; the two are independent. Replace line~$174$
``The Fake-Monster and K3-BKM quantisations are the $d = 13$ and
$d = 3$ faces of a common Borcherds-lift programme'' with ``the
$d = 5$ and $d = 3$ faces''.

\paragraph{Outstanding obstructions.}
Three items remain for a full construction.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(O\arabic*)]
\item \emph{Niemeier-locked twin-K3 existence.} The condition
$\mathrm{NS}(K3_1) \oplus \mathrm{NS}(K3_2) \supseteq N$ for a
Niemeier lattice $N$ is non-generic: both K3s must be at
maximal Picard rank with matched Néron--Severi data. For
$N = \Leech$ (the rootless case), neither K3 can carry any
$-2$-class, i.e.\ the transcendental lattices
$T_i = \mathrm{NS}(K3_i)^\perp$ must be empty of roots. This
selects a specific locus in the twin-K3 moduli space of
codimension $\geq 1$.
\item \emph{$\PhiFA_5$ rigidity on a product CY$_5$.} The Stage-1
factorisation $\PhiFA_5(\Perf(X_N))$ depends on the CY$_5$
structure of $X_N = K3_1 \times_N K3_2 \times E$; product
CY$_5$s carry the product holomorphic volume form, which
collapses $\PhiFA_5$ via Dunn--Lurie to the Deligne tensor
product of $\PhiFA_2(K3_i) \otimes \PhiFA_1(E)$. Exhibiting
the non-trivial $\PhiFA_5$ beyond this product reduction
requires the Niemeier-lock data as genuine $E_4$-braiding.
\item \emph{Scheithauer $\fg_N$ identification.} The claim that the
$N$-specialisation reproduces Scheithauer's Niemeier BKM family
requires matching the Niemeier-theta $\Theta_N(\tau)$ with the
Borcherds product exponents $c_N(m)$ of $\Phi_{12, N}$. At
$N = \Leech$, $\Theta_\Leech/\eta^{24}$ has the correct
$c(m)$-coefficients (Verification Path~(ii) of
\texttt{chapters/examples/fake\_monster\_chapter.tex}
line~142).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Consistency check: $\kch = 0$ at $d = 5$ Serre.}
The $d = 5$ Universal Serre Cancellation (Theorem~\ref{thm:cy-d-d5-stratification}
of \texttt{chapters/examples/cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex} line~1192)
forces $\kch(X_N) = 0$ for every compact CY$_5$. The Fake-Monster
sibling is compatible: $\kBKM(\gFM) = 12$ registers the
\emph{Borcherds weight} of the denominator (the automorphic modular
characteristic), not the Hodge supertrace $\kch$. The four-$\kappa$
discipline of $\{\kch, \kcat, \kBKM, \kfib\}$ (CLAUDE.md
``never conflate'') gives on $X_N = K3_1 \times_N K3_2 \times E$:
\begin{align*}
 \kch(X_N) &\;=\; \sum_q (-1)^q h^{0,q}(X_N) \;=\; 0
 && \text{(Serre at $d = 5$),}\\
 \kcat(X_N) &\;=\; \chi(\cO_{X_N})
 \;=\; \chi(\cO_{K3})^2 \cdot \chi(\cO_E) \;=\; 4 \cdot 0 \;=\; 0
 && \text{(Künneth on product),}\\
 \kBKM(\PhiFM) &\;=\; c(0)/2 \;=\; 12
 && \text{(Borcherds weight),}\\
 \kfib(K3 \times_N K3) &\;=\; \rk(N) \;=\; 24
 && \text{($\Leech$ rank).}
\end{align*}
The four values $\{0, 0, 12, 24\}$ are the Fake-Monster analogue of
the K3$\times E$ spectrum $\{0, 0, 5, 24\}$ at the Igusa sibling
(cf.\ \texttt{chapters/examples/cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}
line~1319 K3$\times E$ spectrum); note $\kcat$ is Künneth-%
multiplicative on the \emph{total space} (here $0$ via
$\chi(\cO_E) = 0$), not the \emph{fibre}.

The $d = 5$ placement of the Fake-Monster sibling is forced:
at $d = 3$, signature-lock excludes; at $d = 5$, Niemeier-lock
selects the $\Leech$-component. The rank-$26$ RTT quantisation
$\mathbf{Y}^{\mathrm{FM}}$ of Theorem~\ref{thm:fm-rtt-master}
(\texttt{chapters/examples/fake\_monster\_chapter.tex} line~$238$) is
therefore the quantum group of the $d = 5$ sibling, not of a
non-existent $d = 3$ or $d = 13$ habitat.
