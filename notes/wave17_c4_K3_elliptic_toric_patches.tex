\section{Elliptic K3 surfaces}

Let $S$ be a projective K3 surface. A smooth complete toric variety
has $H^{p,0}=0$ for $p>0$ by the Danilov-Demazure cohomology
calculation, while $h^{2,0}(S)=1$. Hence a compact K3 surface admits
only non-toric smooth complete presentations. Toric geometry enters
through formal charts and ADE surface singularities obtained from local
contractions.

An elliptic fibration on $S$ is a morphism
\[
 \pi\colon S \longrightarrow \PP^1
\]
whose general fibre is a smooth genus-one curve. A primitive nef
isotropic class $F\in\NS(S)$ with $F^2=0$ gives a genus-one fibration
whose fibre class is $F$; a class $O$ with $O\cdot F=1$ supplies a
section. If $\rho(S)\geq 5$,
the lattice $\NS(S)$ is indefinite of rank at least five, so Meyer's
theorem gives an integral isotropic vector, and the K3 Weyl group
moves an effective representative into the nef cone on the elliptic
locus. This is the Piatetski-Shapiro-Shafarevich elliptic-fibration
criterion in lattice form.

For every elliptic K3 surface,
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{top}}(S)=c_2(S)=24
   = \sum_{b\in\Delta_\pi} e(F_b),
\]
where $F_b=\pi^{-1}(b)$ and $\Delta_\pi\subset\PP^1$ is the Kodaira
discriminant. A generic elliptic K3 with section has twenty-four
fibres of type $I_1$. Special members collect these contributions
into higher Kodaira fibres.

\section{Smooth-fibre tubes}

Let $U\subset\PP^1$ be a small simply connected disc with
$U\cap\Delta_\pi=\emptyset$, and set $W=\pi^{-1}(U)$. The map
$W\to U$ is a holomorphic family of elliptic curves. After choosing a
section and a holomorphic period ratio $\tau(t)$ on $U$, it has the
analytic presentation
\[
 W \simeq (\CC_z\times U_t)/
 \bigl(z\sim z+m+n\tau(t)\bigr),\qquad m,n\in\ZZ.
\]
Ehresmann gives $C^\infty$ local triviality. Holomorphic triviality is
equivalent to constant $j$-invariant on $U$.

The elliptic fibre acts on itself by translations. This is an abelian
variety action; the algebraic-torus input used in Hall calculations is
formal: at a point
$p\in W$,
\[
 \widehat{\cO}_{W,p}\simeq \CC[[x,t]],
\]
and the completed bidisc carries the formal two-torus weights
$(x,t)\mapsto(\lambda_1x,\lambda_2t)$. Nakajima creation operators on
$\bigoplus_n H^*(\Hilb^n(W))$ restrict to this formal surface chart and
give the positive Heisenberg half generated by $H^*(F_t)$ on the
smooth-fibre tube. The Schiffmann-Vasserot shuffle formulas apply to
the formal $\CC^2$ model; the compact elliptic fibre is recovered only
after quotienting by the period lattice and imposing monodromy.

\section{Kodaira fibres and ADE root lattices}

Assume $\pi$ has a section $O$. For a singular fibre $F_b$, let
$\Root_b$ be the negative-definite root lattice generated by the
irreducible components of $F_b$ disjoint from $O$. Kodaira's
classification gives the following local root data.
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{Kodaira type} & e(F_b) & \Root_b \\
\hline
I_1 & 1 & 0 \\
I_n,\ n\geq 2 & n & A_{n-1} \\
II & 2 & 0 \\
III & 3 & A_1 \\
IV & 4 & A_2 \\
I_n^{*},\ n\geq 0 & n+6 & D_{n+4} \\
IV^{*} & 8 & E_6 \\
III^{*} & 9 & E_7 \\
II^{*} & 10 & E_8
\end{array}
\]
The sum of the middle column over all singular fibres is $24$.
The root lattice column records real ADE roots. The generic
twenty-four $I_1$ fibres contribute vanishing cycles and
Picard-Lefschetz monodromy; their root-lattice entry is zero.

For $\Root_b\neq 0$, Grauert contraction of the components generating
$\Root_b$ produces a rational double point of the same ADE type, and
the original neighbourhood is its minimal resolution near the
exceptional configuration. Nakajima quiver varieties attached to
$\Root_b$ carry the corresponding geometric representation of
$\frakg_{\Root_b}$, and the Maulik-Okounkov stable envelope supplies
the local positive Yangian half $\Ybold^+_\hbar(\frakg_{\Root_b})$
after equivariant localisation. Type $I_1$ and type $II$ fibres lie in
the vanishing-cycle Heisenberg sector instead of the ADE real-root
sector.

\section{\v{C}ech cover}

\begin{construction}[\v{C}ech cover of an elliptic K3 surface]
\label{constr:k3-elliptic-cover}
Fix $\pi\colon S\to\PP^1$ with section. Enumerate
$\Delta_\pi=\{b_1,\ldots,b_r\}$. Choose pairwise disjoint discs
$D_i\subset\PP^1$ with $b_i\in D_i$, and set $V_i=\pi^{-1}(D_i)$.
Cover $\PP^1\setminus\bigcup_i D_i$ by finitely many simply connected
discs $U_\alpha$ avoiding $\Delta_\pi$, and set
$W_\alpha=\pi^{-1}(U_\alpha)$. Then
\[
 \mathfrak U_\pi=\{W_\alpha\}_\alpha\cup\{V_i\}_{i=1}^r
\]
is an analytic open cover of $S$.
\end{construction}

On a smooth tube $W_\alpha$ the local positive half is the Heisenberg
algebra generated by the cohomology of the smooth fibre. On a singular
tube $V_i$ the positive half has two pieces: the vanishing-cycle
Heisenberg part from local Picard-Lefschetz monodromy, and the ADE
Yangian half $\Ybold^+_\hbar(\frakg_{\Root_{b_i}})$ when
$\Root_{b_i}\neq 0$. On overlaps $W_\alpha\cap V_i$, the restriction
map is conjugation by the local monodromy operator
$M_{b_i}\in SL_2(\ZZ)$ on $H_1(F_t,\ZZ)$, together with the induced
reflection on the root lattice $\Root_{b_i}$ when the fibre is
reducible.

\section{Positive half before double and centre}

\begin{proposition}[Patch positive halves]
\label{prop:patch-positive-halves}
Let $\mathfrak U_\pi$ be the cover of
Construction~\ref{constr:k3-elliptic-cover}. There is a diagram
$\mathcal H^+_\pi$ of local algebras on the \v{C}ech nerve of
$\mathfrak U_\pi$ with
\[
 \mathcal H^+_\pi(W_\alpha)=H^+(H^*(F_t)),\qquad
 \mathcal H^+_\pi(V_i)=
 H^+_{\mathrm{van}}(b_i)\otimes
 \Ybold^+_\hbar(\frakg_{\Root_{b_i}})
\]
where the Yangian factor is omitted when $\Root_{b_i}=0$. The
restriction maps are induced by Picard-Lefschetz monodromy and by
the ADE Weyl action on the contracted rational double point.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Surface positive half by descent]
\label{thm:k3-surface-positive-half-descent}
Assume the Kapranov-Vasserot surface CoHA descent comparison for the
analytic cover $\mathfrak U_\pi$, the Nakajima-Maulik-Okounkov
identification on every ADE contraction, and monodromy equivariance
of the local stable envelopes. Then the \v{C}ech derived sections of
the patch diagram identify the generated positive half of the K3
surface Hall algebra:
\[
 \mathbb R\Gamma(\mathfrak U_\pi,\mathcal H^+_\pi)
 \;\simeq\;
 \CoHA^+_{\mathrm{KV}}(S)_{\pi}.
\]
The subscript $\pi$ records dependence on the chosen elliptic
fibration and its root lattice decomposition. The isomorphism is on
the algebra generated by smooth-fibre Heisenberg classes, vanishing
cycles, and ADE real-root generators.
\end{theorem}

The descent statement stops at the positive half. The following
promotions are separate constructions:
\[
 \CoHA^+(S)
 \longrightarrow
 \mathcal D_\hbar(\CoHA^+(S))
 \longrightarrow
 Z(\Rep(\mathcal D_\hbar(\CoHA^+(S))))
 \longrightarrow
 \widehat{\mathcal D}_\hbar(\CoHA^+(S)).
\]
Thus the elliptic K3 cover supplies local generators and monodromy
relations for $\CoHA^+(S)$. A Hall-Drinfeld double, a braided centre,
and a completed BKM denominator object require extra dual, centre,
and completion data.

\section{Surface invariants and the product boundary}

For the K3 surface itself,
\[
 \kcat(S)=\chi(\cO_S)=h^{0,0}(S)-h^{0,1}(S)+h^{0,2}(S)=2,
\]
and the CY-to-chiral surface supertrace gives
\[
 \kch(S)=\sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(S)=1+1=2.
\]
The Mukai lattice has rank
\[
 \operatorname{rk}\widetilde H(S,\ZZ)
 =\operatorname{rk}\bigl(H^0(S,\ZZ)\oplus H^2(S,\ZZ)\oplus H^4(S,\ZZ)\bigr)
 =24.
\]
This rank is the K3 fibre invariant used by the compact
$S\times E$ constructions.

Let $Y=S\times E$ with $E$ an elliptic curve. The compact
Calabi-Yau threefold invariants are
\[
 \kcat(Y)=\chi(\cO_S)\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot0=0,
\]
\[
 \kch(Y)=\sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(Y)
 =1-1+1-1=0,
\]
\[
 \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}(Y)=2+1=3,\qquad
 \kBKM(\Delta_5)=\frac{c_1(0)}2=5,\qquad
 \kfiber(S)=24.
\]
These entries keep five readings separated. The four named
$S\times E$ spectrum values are $\{0,3,5,24\}$; the compact Hodge
supertrace shares the value $0$ with $\kcat(Y)$. The constructions are
Kunneth structure-sheaf Euler characteristic on the total space,
compact Hodge supertrace, Heisenberg specialisation along the K3 fibre
and elliptic curve, Borcherds weight of the Gritsenko-Nikulin
$\Delta_5$ denominator, and Mukai-lattice rank on the K3 fibre. The
elliptic surface cover of $S$ accounts only for the surface
positive-half input. Reduced Hall and BKM data for $S\times E$ belong
to the compact CY$_3$ product geometry.

For $d=3$, the two-stage $\Phi_3$ specialisation along
$\Sigma_2=S$ and reference curve $E$ yields an $E_1$-chiral output on
$E$. The associated $E_2$ braided structure lives on the Drinfeld
centre of the $E_1$ representation category.
