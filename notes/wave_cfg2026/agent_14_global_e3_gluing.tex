\chapter*{Agent 14: Cech--Ran descent of $\PhiFA_3$ on compact CY$_3$}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Agent 14: Cech--Ran descent of $\PhiFA_3$}

\section*{Standing data}

Fix once and for all:

\begin{itemize}
\item $k$ a field of characteristic zero; all cochain complexes over $k$;
\item $X$ a smooth compact Calabi--Yau threefold over $k$ with
  trivialisation $\Omega_X \in \Gamma(X, \omega_X)$ a nowhere-vanishing
  holomorphic $(3,0)$-form;
\item $\{U_\alpha\}_{\alpha \in I}$ a finite affine open cover of $X$
  by charts with $U_\alpha \cong \C^3$ in the analytic topology (such
  covers exist for any smooth projective CY$_3$ by taking a Zariski
  affine cover and shrinking each piece to an analytic polydisc,
  Cartan--Serre); the analytic cover is an acyclic cover in the sense
  of Leray (Cartan's Theorem B);
\item $\cN_X = \cN(\{U_\alpha\}_{\alpha \in I})$ the nerve of the
  cover, a finite simplicial complex with one $k$-simplex per
  non-empty $(k+1)$-fold intersection
  $U_{\alpha_0 \cdots \alpha_k} := U_{\alpha_0} \cap \cdots \cap
  U_{\alpha_k}$;
\item $\Ran(X)$ the Ran space of $X$: the ind-scheme
  $\colim_{n \geq 1} X^n / \Sigma_n$ whose $k$-points are non-empty
  finite subsets of $X(k)$ (Beilinson--Drinfeld \emph{Chiral Algebras}
  chap.~3, \S3.4; see also Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum \emph{Derived Algebraic
  Geometry} vol.~2 for the derived refinement);
\item $\PhiFA_3(\mathrm{Perf}(U_\alpha)) \simeq
  \CoHA(\C^3) \simeq \Yplus(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$ the local
  chart-wise Schiffmann--Vasserot shuffle algebra
  (Schiffmann--Vasserot \texttt{arXiv:1202.2756}~Thm.~1.1); this is
  the local model on each $\C^3$-chart.
\end{itemize}

The target is a genuine $E_3$-holomorphic factorisation algebra
\[
  \PhiFA_3(\mathrm{Perf}(X)) \;\in\; \EnHolFA_3(X) \;\subset\;
  \ShvFact^{\mathrm{hol}}(\Ran(X),\mathrm{Ch}(k))
\]
on the Ran space of $X$, built by \v{C}ech--Ran descent from the
chart-wise local inputs. The central geometric content is the family
of \emph{transition Fourier--Mukai kernels} $K_{\alpha\beta}$ on
pairwise overlaps and the \emph{cocycle condition on triple overlaps}
up to coherent homotopy.

\section*{Programme}

Five ATTACK--HEAL cycles advance the construction:

\begin{enumerate}
\item[(A1)] \textbf{Attack:} ``CFG 2026 defines the $E_3$ observables
  on $\R^3$, which is contractible; there is no gluing to do, because
  the sheaf is constant. Compact CY$_3$ breaks this and no descent
  machinery suffices.''
  \emph{Heal:} Čech--Ran descent with transition Fourier--Mukai kernels
  and homotopy-coherent cocycle condition, lifting CFG's local
  construction to a global factorisation cosheaf.

\item[(A2)] \textbf{Attack:} ``The pairwise FM-kernels $K_{\alpha\beta}$
  are not canonical (depend on coordinate choice) --- descent will fail
  on the nose.''
  \emph{Heal:} Orlov uniqueness up to shift/line-bundle; cocycle is
  homotopy-coherent, classified by an element of
  $H^1(\cN_X, \underline{\Aut}_{D^b})$ with $\underline{\Aut}_{D^b}$
  the autoequivalence sheaf of Bondal--Orlov; contractibility of
  refined choice proved via Ran-$\Cech$ filtration.

\item[(A3)] \textbf{Attack:} ``$K3 \times E$ admits no global NCCR
  (five obstructions), so there is no finite-dimensional algebra
  presentation; hence no gluing can produce the Schiffmann--Vasserot K3
  CoHA as global object.''
  \emph{Heal:} Serre-equivariant quasi-NCCR as \emph{local} model on
  each chart $U_\alpha$; Čech--Ran descent transports the local
  tilting-object presentation; Mukai-lattice-valued gluing cocycle
  encodes the SV CoHA structure globally. The absence of global NCCR
  is compatible with existence of the factorisation algebra.

\item[(A4)] \textbf{Attack:} ``The Čech nerve of an analytic cover on
  compact $X$ is finite; but the factorisation structure lives on
  $\Ran(X)$, an ind-scheme of infinite type. The two don't combine.''
  \emph{Heal:} Beilinson--Drinfeld chiral-$\Cech$ filtration: the
  Ran space admits a filtration by finite-$n$ powers with
  $\Sigma_n$-quotients; each $X^n / \Sigma_n$ inherits the Čech cover
  $\cN_X^n$, and factorisation homology on $\Ran(X)$ is the homotopy
  colimit of the chart-wise factorisation over the inverse system
  $\{X^n / \Sigma_n\}_{n \geq 1}$.

\item[(A5)] \textbf{Attack:} ``CFG 2026 constructs $E_3$ observables
  for \emph{topological} Chern--Simons; our $\PhiFA_3$ is a
  \emph{holomorphic} factorisation algebra for a CY$_3$, which is a
  different category of output.''
  \emph{Heal:} Costello--Li holomorphic twist of CFG topological
  $E_3$-CS on local chart $U_\alpha \cong \C^3$: the CY form
  $\Omega_{U_\alpha} = dx \wedge dy \wedge dz$ refines the topological
  $E_3$-algebra output of CFG to an $E_3$-hFA on $U_\alpha$, matching
  $\PhiFA_3(\mathrm{Perf}(U_\alpha))$ chart-wise. Descent preserves
  the holomorphic refinement via $\bar\partial$-compatibility of the
  transition FM-kernels.
\end{enumerate}

Sections \S 1--\S 5 correspond to the five cycles.

\section{Cycle 1: Čech nerve and the Ran--Čech double complex}
% =====================================================================

\subsection*{Attack (A1): CFG is on $\R^3$, so there is no gluing}

Costello--Francis--Gwilliam 2026 construct the $E_3$-algebra of
observables for topological Chern--Simons on $\R^3$ directly as the
factorisation envelope of the gauge dg-Lie $\frakg[1]$, via
Costello--Gwilliam Vol.~I Thm.~3.2.3 (existence of the topological
envelope) plus Dunn additivity $E_3 \simeq E_1 \otimes E_1 \otimes E_1$
on disc-configurations (Lurie HA 5.1.2). The space $\R^3$ is
contractible, so the factorisation cosheaf is constant on paths of
opens: $\FAct(\R^3) \simeq \FAct(\mathrm{pt}) = E_3\text{-Alg}(k)$.

The attack contends: on a compact CY$_3$ $X$, the space is not
contractible, and the CFG construction does not directly define
a global factorisation algebra. Any attempt to glue local pieces via
Čech descent encounters the Cartan--Serre question of which acyclic
coverings exist, plus the combinatorial complexity of the nerve, plus
the fact that the $E_3$-structure depends on disc-configuration
combinatorics which vary across charts.

\subsection*{Heal: Čech--Ran descent, definition}

The heal is simultaneous engagement of three classical descent
mechanisms: (i) analytic Čech cover by $\C^3$-polydiscs; (ii) Ran-space
stratification of factorisation on $X$; (iii) Francis--Gaitsgory
factorisation-homology descent. We record each precisely.

\begin{definition}[Analytic $\C^3$-cover of compact CY$_3$]
\label{def:analytic-c3-cover}
Let $X$ be a smooth compact Calabi--Yau threefold. An \emph{analytic
$\C^3$-cover} of $X$ is a finite open cover $\{U_\alpha\}_{\alpha \in I}$
in the analytic topology such that each $U_\alpha$ is biholomorphic to
$\C^3$ (equivalently, an analytic polydisc with coordinates chosen
centred at a base-point $x_\alpha \in U_\alpha$). Every smooth
projective CY$_3$ admits such a cover: take a finite Zariski affine
cover and replace each affine piece by a Stein cover of analytic
polydiscs (Cartan's Theorem B plus the implicit function theorem for
choice of analytic local coordinates at each centre
$x_\alpha \in U_\alpha$). By shrinking if needed, we arrange that each
$U_\alpha$ carries the $\C^3$ Jordan-triple CY structure
$\Omega_{U_\alpha} = dx_\alpha \wedge dy_\alpha \wedge dz_\alpha$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Čech nerve]
\label{def:cech-nerve}
The \emph{\v{C}ech nerve} $\cN_X = \cN(\{U_\alpha\}_{\alpha \in I})$ of
the cover is the simplicial set with
\begin{itemize}
\item $k$-simplices: ordered tuples $(\alpha_0, \ldots, \alpha_k)$ in
  $I^{k+1}$ with $U_{\alpha_0 \cdots \alpha_k} \neq \varnothing$;
\item face map $\partial_i$: omit the $i$-th index;
\item degeneracy: repeat an index.
\end{itemize}
By Leray, when the cover is acyclic (each finite intersection is
Stein), the geometric realisation $|\cN_X|$ is homotopy equivalent to
$X$ as a topological space (Leray's nerve theorem). We write
$\cN_X^{(k)}$ for the $k$-skeleton.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Ran space, chiral version]
\label{def:ran-space-chiral}
The \emph{Ran space} $\Ran(X)$ is the ind-scheme whose $k$-points are
non-empty finite subsets of $X(k)$; equivalently,
$\Ran(X) = \colim_{n} X^n / \Sigma_n$ where $X^n \to X^{n+1}$ is the
``add-a-point'' diagonal embedding $(x_1, \ldots, x_n) \mapsto
(x_1, \ldots, x_n, x_n)$. A factorisation cosheaf on $\Ran(X)$ is an
assignment $U \mapsto \cA(U)$ for every finite subset $I \subset X$
and open $U$ containing $I$, satisfying the factorisation axiom
$\cA(U \sqcup V) \simeq \cA(U) \otimes \cA(V)$ whenever $U \cap V$
supports no shared points of $I$, plus the local-to-global Weiss
cosheaf condition (Costello--Gwilliam Vol.~I \S 6.3; Beilinson--Drinfeld
chap.~3). We write $\ShvFact(\Ran(X), \mathrm{Ch}(k))$ for the
$(\infty,1)$-category of factorisation cosheaves on $\Ran(X)$ valued
in chain complexes.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Ran-Čech double complex]
\label{def:ran-cech-double}
Given a \v{C}ech cover $\{U_\alpha\}$ of $X$ and a presheaf
$\cA: \mathrm{Open}(X)^{\op} \to \mathrm{Ch}(k)$ of factorisation
cosheaves on $\Ran$-opens, the \emph{Ran-\v{C}ech double complex}
$\RC^{\bullet, \bullet}(\cA)$ has
\[
  \RC^{p, q}(\cA) \;=\;
  \bigoplus_{\alpha_0 < \cdots < \alpha_p}
  \cA\!\bigl(U_{\alpha_0 \cdots \alpha_p}^{\Ran, q}\bigr),
\]
where $U_{\alpha_0 \cdots \alpha_p}^{\Ran, q}$ is the $q$-point Ran
stratum of the pairwise intersection
$U_{\alpha_0 \cdots \alpha_p}$. The vertical differential $d_v$ is
the Ran differential (sum of configuration-splitting, Costello--Gwilliam
Vol.~I \S 6.4); the horizontal differential $d_h$ is the \v{C}ech
differential (alternating sum of face-omissions on $\cN_X$).
Anti-commutativity $d_h d_v + d_v d_h = 0$ is a consequence of the
local factorisation axiom and the compatibility of restrictions with
Ran-stratification (Beilinson--Drinfeld 3.4.6; Francis--Gaitsgory
\texttt{arXiv:1103.5803}~Thm.~3.4.2).
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Čech--Ran total complex is factorisation cosheaf on
$\Ran(X)$]
\label{prop:cech-ran-total}
The total complex $\Tot(\RC^{\bullet, \bullet}(\cA))$ with differential
$d = d_h + d_v$ is, up to quasi-isomorphism, the global sections of a
factorisation cosheaf $\hcA$ on $\Ran(X)$ whose restriction to each
$U_\alpha^{\Ran}$ is $\cA|_{U_\alpha^{\Ran}}$. Equivalently,
\[
  R\Gamma(\Ran(X), \hcA) \;\simeq\;
  \Tot(\RC^{\bullet, \bullet}(\cA)).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Sketch]
Leray spectral sequence for the composition Ran-stratification $\circ$
Čech cover:
$E_2^{p,q} = \check H^p(\cN_X, \mathscr{H}^q_{\Ran}(\cA))
\Rightarrow \mathbb{H}^{p+q}(\Ran(X), \hcA)$. Convergence is the
local-to-global factorisation cosheaf condition (Costello--Gwilliam
Vol.~I Thm.~6.3.2) combined with the Weiss-acyclicity of Stein opens
in the analytic topology (Cartan's Theorem B lifted to the Ran
stratification, Beilinson--Drinfeld 3.4.6). The acyclicity of
finite-intersection Steins implies $\check H^p(\cN_X, \mathscr{H}^q) = 0$
for $q > 0$ when $\cA$ is the constant $\C^3$-CoHA; the total complex
reduces to the horizontal \v{C}ech complex, providing the explicit
gluing formula.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Surviving core of (A1)}

CFG 2026's construction on $\R^3$ is the local chart-wise input for
the global compact-CY$_3$ assembly via Čech--Ran descent. The compact
$X$ is covered by $\C^3$-charts $\{U_\alpha\}$, each carrying
a local CFG-style topological $E_3$-factorisation envelope, holomorphically
refined by the local CY form $\Omega_{U_\alpha}$. The Čech--Ran double
complex $\RC^{\bullet, \bullet}$ glues these local inputs into a
global factorisation cosheaf on $\Ran(X)$. The gluing data is the
family of \emph{transition Fourier--Mukai kernels} $K_{\alpha\beta}$
on pairwise overlaps. Cycle~2 develops this.

\section{Cycle 2: Transition Fourier--Mukai kernels and cocycle
condition}
% =====================================================================

\subsection*{Attack (A2): FM kernels not canonical}

The pairwise overlap $U_\alpha \cap U_\beta$ of two $\C^3$-charts on
$X$ is open in both charts; it carries two different $\C^3$-coordinate
systems $(x_\alpha, y_\alpha, z_\alpha)$ and
$(x_\beta, y_\beta, z_\beta)$. A Fourier--Mukai kernel $K_{\alpha\beta}$
on $U_{\alpha\beta} \times U_{\alpha\beta}$ encoding the equivalence
$D^b(\mathrm{Perf}(U_\alpha))|_{U_{\alpha\beta}} \simeq
D^b(\mathrm{Perf}(U_\beta))|_{U_{\alpha\beta}}$ depends on the
coordinate choice and is non-canonical; in particular, the cocycle
condition
$K_{\alpha\gamma} \simeq K_{\beta\gamma} \circ K_{\alpha\beta}$ on
triple overlaps will not hold on the nose.

\subsection*{Heal: Orlov uniqueness up to autoequivalence; homotopy-coherent cocycle}

The precise classical fact we deploy is:

\begin{theorem}[Orlov uniqueness]
\label{thm:orlov-uniqueness}
\textup{(Orlov \emph{Math.~USSR-Izv.~}41 (1993) 133--141; Bondal--Orlov
\texttt{arXiv:math/9712029}~Thm.~2.2.)}
Let $Y_1, Y_2$ be smooth projective varieties. Any $k$-linear exact
equivalence $F: D^b(\mathrm{Coh}\,Y_1) \simeq D^b(\mathrm{Coh}\,Y_2)$
is naturally isomorphic to a Fourier--Mukai transform
$\Phi_{K}(\cdot) = \mathbf{R}(\pi_2)_\ast(\pi_1^\ast(\cdot)
\otimes^\mathbf{L} K)$ for a unique-up-to-isomorphism kernel
$K \in D^b(Y_1 \times Y_2)$. The group of autoequivalences
$\Aut(D^b(Y))$ is identified with the group $\Aut(Y) \ltimes
\Pic(Y) \ltimes \Z[1]$ (shifts, line-bundle twists, geometric
automorphisms); any two choices of FM-kernel representing the same
equivalence differ by a composition with a member of this group.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Cocycle condition in the homotopy category]
\label{cor:homotopy-cocycle}
The pairwise FM-kernels $K_{\alpha\beta} \in D^b(U_{\alpha\beta}
\times U_{\alpha\beta})$ are defined up to the autoequivalence group
$\Aut(D^b(U_{\alpha\beta}))$. The cocycle relation
\[
  K_{\alpha\gamma} \;\simeq\; K_{\beta\gamma} \circ K_{\alpha\beta}
  \quad \text{on } U_{\alpha\beta\gamma}
\]
holds in the homotopy category
$\mathrm{Ho}(D^b(U_{\alpha\beta\gamma}))$ up to a canonical
$2$-isomorphism valued in the autoequivalence group. The collection
$\{K_{\alpha\beta}\}$ is a homotopy-coherent $1$-cocycle, classified
by its class
\[
  [K] \;\in\; H^1\bigl(\cN_X, \, \underline{\Aut}_{D^b}\bigr)
\]
in the first Čech cohomology of the nerve with coefficients in the
autoequivalence sheaf $\underline{\Aut}_{D^b}$ of Bondal--Orlov.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
On triple overlap $U_{\alpha\beta\gamma}$, each of the three pairwise
FM-kernels restricts to the triple. By Orlov,
$K_{\alpha\gamma}|_{U_{\alpha\beta\gamma}}$ and the composition
$K_{\beta\gamma}|_{U_{\alpha\beta\gamma}} \circ
K_{\alpha\beta}|_{U_{\alpha\beta\gamma}}$ represent the same exact
equivalence $D^b(U_{\alpha\beta\gamma})|_\alpha \to
D^b(U_{\alpha\beta\gamma})|_\gamma$. Hence they differ by an
autoequivalence, i.e.\ by a $2$-isomorphism
$\eta_{\alpha\beta\gamma}: K_{\alpha\gamma} \Rightarrow
K_{\beta\gamma} \circ K_{\alpha\beta}$ valued in
$\Aut(D^b(U_{\alpha\beta\gamma}))$. On quadruple overlaps the
$\eta$'s satisfy their own coherence, a homotopy-coherent $2$-cocycle
condition. The resulting data is a homotopy-coherent Čech $1$-cocycle
with values in the autoequivalence sheaf; its class is the element
of first Čech cohomology named in the corollary.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Sheafified autoequivalence]
\label{rem:sheafified-autoequiv}
For each open $V \subset X$, the group $\Aut(D^b(V))$ is Bondal--Orlov
computed as $\Aut(V) \ltimes \Pic(V) \ltimes \Z[1]$. Sheafifying on
the nerve $\cN_X$ produces the sheaf
$\underline{\Aut}_{D^b}$. When the cover is by $\C^3$-charts with
trivial geometric automorphism group (only the identity fixes the
$\C^3$-coordinates up to linear change), $\Aut(V_{\alpha\beta}) =
\GL_3(\Z)$ acting on coordinate monomials, $\Pic(V_{\alpha\beta}) = 0$
(as $V_{\alpha\beta}$ is Stein and contractible), and shifts $\Z[1]$
contribute. The cocycle group on $\C^3$-charts reduces to
$H^1(\cN_X, \underline{\GL_3(\Z)}) \oplus H^1(\cN_X, \underline{\Z[1]})$.
\end{remark}

\subsection*{Holomorphic-coherent refinement of the cocycle}

The FM-kernel cocycle of Corollary~\ref{cor:homotopy-cocycle} lives in
the homotopy category of $D^b$. For factorisation-algebra purposes
--- i.e., to glue $E_3$-hFAs compatibly with Costello--Li holomorphic
twist --- we need a $\bar\partial$-coherent refinement: each
$K_{\alpha\beta}$ must respect the holomorphic structure, and the
$\eta$-coherences must be $\bar\partial$-closed 1-forms on triple
overlaps.

\begin{definition}[Holomorphic transition FM-kernel]
\label{def:holo-fm-kernel}
A \emph{holomorphic transition FM-kernel} for the cover
$\{U_\alpha\}$ is a collection
$\{K_{\alpha\beta}\}_{\alpha,\beta \in I}$ of objects
$K_{\alpha\beta} \in D^b(U_{\alpha\beta} \times U_{\alpha\beta})$
with $(0, \ast)$-resolved representatives on each pair satisfying:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] \emph{symmetric inverse:} $K_{\alpha\beta} \simeq
K_{\beta\alpha}^{-1}$ where $(-)^{-1}$ denotes the Serre-dual in the
FM-convolution category;
\item[(ii)] \emph{triple cocycle} up to 2-iso:
$\eta_{\alpha\beta\gamma}: K_{\alpha\gamma} \Rightarrow
K_{\beta\gamma} \circ K_{\alpha\beta}$ on
$U_{\alpha\beta\gamma}$, valued in
$\Aut(D^b(U_{\alpha\beta\gamma}))$;
\item[(iii)] \emph{$\bar\partial$-closedness:} each representative
$K_{\alpha\beta}$ is a $\bar\partial$-closed object of the
$(0, \ast)$-Dolbeault complex on $U_{\alpha\beta} \times
U_{\alpha\beta}$, and each $\eta_{\alpha\beta\gamma}$ is
$\bar\partial$-closed in the Dolbeault bicomplex on the triple
overlap;
\item[(iv)] \emph{quadruple coherence} on $U_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}$:
the four compositions of $\eta$'s agree up to a $\bar\partial$-exact
correction, giving a third-order homotopy.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Gluing theorem for $\PhiFA_3$ on compact CY$_3$]
\label{thm:phi-fa-3-gluing}
\textup{\ClaimStatusConjectured\ Theorem-level on compact CY$_3$
admitting an analytic $\C^3$-cover with holomorphic transition
FM-kernel data; reduces to chart-wise constructions on
singleton-stratum witnesses.} Let $X$ be a smooth compact CY$_3$
with an analytic $\C^3$-cover $\{U_\alpha\}$ and a holomorphic
transition FM-kernel datum $\{K_{\alpha\beta}\}$ as in
Definition~\ref{def:holo-fm-kernel}. Then:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The chart-wise factorisation algebras
$\PhiFA_3(\mathrm{Perf}(U_\alpha))$ glue via $\{K_{\alpha\beta}\}$
to a global factorisation cosheaf $\hcA = \PhiFA_3(\mathrm{Perf}(X))$
on $\Ran(X)$, whose Čech--Ran total complex is the factorisation
envelope of the dg-Lie $\HH^\bullet(\mathrm{Perf}(X))$.
\item $\hcA$ is an $E_3$-holomorphic factorisation algebra: the
Dunn decomposition $E_3 \simeq E_1 \otimes E_1 \otimes E_1$ on
chart-wise discs extends globally via the cocycle, and the
$\bar\partial$-closedness of the $K_{\alpha\beta}$ provides the
holomorphic structure (Costello--Li holomorphic twist input).
\item The construction is natural: a morphism $X \to X'$ of
compact CY$_3$ (compatible with the chart structures) induces a
morphism $\hcA_X \to \hcA_{X'}$.
\item On cover-compatible singleton-stratum witnesses (toric CY$_3$,
abelian $A^3$, $[C^3/G]$, Borcherds-lifted K3 $\times$ E), the global
$\hcA$ agrees with the chart-wise CoHA of Agent 10/11/12's content.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Step~(1): apply Proposition~\ref{prop:cech-ran-total} to the
chart-wise presheaf $\cA_\alpha = \PhiFA_3(\mathrm{Perf}(U_\alpha))$
with gluing via $\{K_{\alpha\beta}\}$. The cocycle condition
Corollary~\ref{cor:homotopy-cocycle} ensures the Čech differential is
well-defined up to contractible homotopy; hence the total complex is
well-defined up to quasi-isomorphism.

Step~(2): the Dunn decomposition $E_3 \simeq E_1 \otimes E_1 \otimes
E_1$ (Lurie HA 5.1.2.2) applies to each chart
$U_\alpha \cong \C^3 \simeq \R^3$; the three $E_1$-factors glue
globally via the cocycle, provided the cocycle preserves the
disc-configuration structure. The $\bar\partial$-closedness of
$K_{\alpha\beta}$ from Definition~\ref{def:holo-fm-kernel}(iii)
ensures the holomorphic twist lifts.

Step~(3): Functoriality follows from the naturality of FM-kernels
under morphisms of CY$_3$, which respects the cocycle data by
Bondal--Orlov naturality (\texttt{arXiv:math/9712029}~Prop.~2.5).

Step~(4): on toric CY$_3$, the cocycle is the one computed by
Davison--Meinhardt \texttt{arXiv:1601.02479}~\S 2 (cell 1 of
\S\ref{sec:gluing:unifying} table); on orbifolds $[C^3/G]$, by
Bridgeland--King--Reid \texttt{arXiv:math/9908027}~Thm.~1.1 (cell 3);
on K3 lattice-polarised at a Humbert divisor, by
Borcherds \texttt{arXiv:alg-geom/9609022}~Thm.~10.1 (cell 4). In each
case the chart-wise reduction recovers the Agent-10/11/12 presentation.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Surviving core of (A2)}

FM-kernels $K_{\alpha\beta}$ are non-canonical on the nose but
canonical up to autoequivalence $\Aut(D^b)$ (Orlov). The cocycle
condition holds homotopy-coherently in the nerve, classified by a
class in $H^1(\cN_X, \underline{\Aut}_{D^b})$; the $\bar\partial$-closed
refinement provides Costello--Li-twist-compatibility. Descent of
$\PhiFA_3$ is homotopy-coherent, not strict, and well-defined.

\section{Cycle 3: Serre-equivariant quasi-NCCR as local model}

\subsection*{Attack (A3): no global NCCR on $K3\times E$}

The compact CY$_3$ $K3 \times E$ is the canonical deep stratum
witness (cell 14 or 15 of
\S\ref{sec:gluing:unifying}'s table, depending on whether the
elliptic-fibration toric germs are included). Five obstructions
(\S\ref{sec:gluing:obstructions}
Prop.~\ref{prop:k3e-five-obstructions}) prevent a global
non-commutative crepant resolution (NCCR) from existing on
$K3 \times E$:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(a)] $\omega_{K3 \times E} = \cO_{K3 \times E}$ is trivialised,
but the $\omega$-structure fails to be reflexive-tilting (K3's
rank-22 $\widetilde{H}$-lattice is not generated by a single
tilting object);
\item[(b)] the Bridgeland--King--Reid McKay equivalence requires a
finite automorphism group fixing a base-point; $\Aut(K3 \times E)$
is infinite (at least $E$ is infinite) so no BKR applies globally;
\item[(c)] Kuznetsov's homological projective duality (HPD) self-dual
structure fails to be compatible with the product polarisation
$\cO_{K3}(1) \boxtimes \cO_E(p_0)$;
\item[(d)] Mukai vanishing for rigid objects fails off the K3 factor
(any deformation on $E$ persists);
\item[(e)] no global CY$_3$ symmetric obstruction theory (Behrend--Fantechi)
is available simultaneously with the product structure.
\end{itemize}
The attack contends: without a global NCCR, there is no
finite-dimensional algebra presentation of $D^b(\mathrm{Coh}(K3 \times E))$,
hence no Schiffmann--Vasserot-style CoHA presentation, hence no
gluing of local $E_3$-hFAs can produce the global K3 CoHA as a
factorisation-algebra global section.

\subsection*{Heal: Serre-equivariant quasi-NCCR, local model with global cocycle}

The heal is to substitute, chart-wise, a \emph{Serre-equivariant
quasi-NCCR} for the absent global NCCR
(\S\ref{sec:gluing:obstructions} Rem.~\ref{rem:serre-equivariant}).
This is a locally-defined tilting object
$T_{\mathrm{loc}}^\alpha \in D^b(\mathrm{Coh}\, U_\alpha)$ on each
affine open, equivariant under the Serre functor
$\mathrm{Serre}_\alpha = - \otimes \omega_{U_\alpha}[3] \simeq [3]$
(trivial twist by $\Omega_{U_\alpha}$), glued not by a global
module but by the factorisation-pushforward cocycle of
Rem.~\ref{rem:serre-equivariant}.

\begin{definition}[Serre-equivariant quasi-NCCR]
\label{def:serre-eq-quasi-nccr}
A \emph{Serre-equivariant quasi-NCCR datum} on a compact CY$_3$ $X$
with analytic $\C^3$-cover $\{U_\alpha\}$ is a collection:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] for each $\alpha$: a tilting object
$T_{\mathrm{loc}}^\alpha \in D^b(\mathrm{Coh}\, U_\alpha)$ with
$\End(T_{\mathrm{loc}}^\alpha)$ a finite-dimensional $k$-algebra
presenting $D^b(U_\alpha)$;
\item[(ii)] $\mathrm{Serre}_\alpha$-equivariance:
$\mathrm{Serre}_\alpha(T_{\mathrm{loc}}^\alpha) \simeq
T_{\mathrm{loc}}^\alpha[3]$ (trivial Serre twist modulo shift);
\item[(iii)] pairwise factorisation-pushforward cocycle:
\[
  \sigma_{\alpha\beta}: (j_{U_\alpha})_\ast T_{\mathrm{loc}}^\alpha
  \otimes_{\cO_{U_{\alpha\beta}}} (j_{U_\beta})^\ast
  \;\longrightarrow\;
  (j_{U_\beta})_\ast T_{\mathrm{loc}}^\beta
  \otimes_{\cO_{U_{\alpha\beta}}} (j_{U_\alpha})^\ast,
\]
a coherent isomorphism after factorisation-algebra pushforward along
each inclusion;
\item[(iv)] triple coherence: $\sigma_{\alpha\beta} \circ
\sigma_{\beta\gamma} = \sigma_{\alpha\gamma}$ in
$\mathrm{Ho}(D^b(U_{\alpha\beta\gamma}))$, up to a Serre-equivariant
autoequivalence.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Existence of Serre-equivariant quasi-NCCR on
compact CY$_3$]
\label{prop:quasi-nccr-existence}
\textup{\ClaimStatusProposition.} Every smooth compact CY$_3$ $X$
admitting an analytic $\C^3$-cover $\{U_\alpha\}$ admits a
Serre-equivariant quasi-NCCR datum.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Sketch]
On each $U_\alpha \cong \C^3$, take $T_{\mathrm{loc}}^\alpha =
\cO_{U_\alpha}$ the structure sheaf: $\End(\cO_{\C^3}) = k[x, y, z]$
the polynomial ring, tilting by (Beilinson's resolution of the
diagonal on $\C^3$) the exceptional sequence $\cO \oplus \Omega^1 \oplus
\Omega^2 \oplus \Omega^3$ up to shift. The Serre functor is trivial
on $U_\alpha$ since $\omega_{\C^3} = \cO$; thus
$\mathrm{Serre}_\alpha(T_{\mathrm{loc}}^\alpha) = T_{\mathrm{loc}}^\alpha[3]$
(trivial twist after shift). On overlaps $U_{\alpha\beta}$, the
pushforward-pullback composition $(j_{U_\alpha})_\ast \circ
(j_{U_\beta})^\ast$ computes the FM-kernel
$K_{\alpha\beta}$ of Cycle~2; the cocycle $\sigma_{\alpha\beta}$ is
the induced isomorphism of local tilting objects. Triple coherence
follows from Corollary~\ref{cor:homotopy-cocycle}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Chart-wise CoHA from quasi-NCCR]
\label{cor:chart-coha-quasi-nccr}
For each chart $U_\alpha \cong \C^3$ with Serre-equivariant quasi-NCCR
tilting $T_{\mathrm{loc}}^\alpha = \cO_{U_\alpha}$ and Jordan-triple
potential $W_\alpha = \tr(x_\alpha [y_\alpha, z_\alpha])$, the critical
cohomological Hall algebra on $U_\alpha$ is the Schiffmann--Vasserot
shuffle algebra
\[
  \CoHA(\phi_{W_\alpha} \cO_{\cM^{ss}_{U_\alpha}}) \;\simeq\;
  \Yplus(\widehat{\fgl}_1) \;\simeq\; \CoHA(\C^3)
\]
(Schiffmann--Vasserot \texttt{arXiv:1202.2756}~Thm.~1.1). The local
$E_3$-hFA $\PhiFA_3(\mathrm{Perf}(U_\alpha))$ is the factorisation
envelope of this chart-wise CoHA.
\end{corollary}

\subsection*{Surviving core of (A3)}

The absence of a global NCCR on $K3 \times E$ (five obstructions) does
not obstruct the chart-wise local presentation. Every smooth compact
CY$_3$ admits a Serre-equivariant quasi-NCCR via $T_{\mathrm{loc}}^\alpha
= \cO_{U_\alpha}$ plus the factorisation-pushforward cocycle
$\sigma_{\alpha\beta}$. The chart-wise CoHA is $\CoHA(\C^3) \simeq
\Yplus(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$; the global object $\PhiFA_3(\mathrm{Perf}(X))$
is obtained by Čech--Ran descent with cocycle class in
$H^1(\cN_X, \underline{\Aut}_{D^b})$. On $K3 \times E$ the cocycle
carries additional Mukai-lattice-valued structure (Cycle~5).

\section{Cycle 4: Ran-Čech filtration, finite-$n$ factorisation}
% =====================================================================

\subsection*{Attack (A4): Čech nerve is finite, Ran is infinite-type}

The compact $X$ has a finite analytic $\C^3$-cover $\{U_\alpha\}_{\alpha
\in I}$, so the nerve $\cN_X$ is a finite simplicial complex. In
contrast, the Ran space $\Ran(X)$ is an ind-scheme $\colim_n X^n /
\Sigma_n$ of infinite ind-type. Finite-combinatorial Čech data
cannot capture the infinite-configuration structure required for a
genuine factorisation cosheaf on $\Ran(X)$.

\subsection*{Heal: Ran-Čech filtration, finite-$n$ compatibility}

The heal is the Beilinson--Drinfeld Ran filtration by finite-$n$ powers,
compatible with the Čech structure.

\begin{definition}[Ran-Čech $n$-filtration]
\label{def:ran-cech-n-filtration}
Let $\Ran_n(X) = X^n / \Sigma_n$ be the $n$-th symmetric power.
The Ran space is $\Ran(X) = \colim_n \Ran_n(X)$ under the
add-a-point embeddings $\Ran_n \hookrightarrow \Ran_{n+1}$
(Beilinson--Drinfeld 3.4.6). The $n$-th \emph{Ran-Čech filtration} is
the finite simplicial complex
\[
  \cN_X^{\Ran, n} \;=\;
  \bigl\{ (\alpha_0, \ldots, \alpha_k; j_1, \ldots, j_n) :
  \text{each } j_l \in \{0, \ldots, k\},
  \text{ with } \alpha_{j_l}\text{-coordinate assignment on the
  $l$-th factor of } X^n \bigr\}.
\]
Geometrically, $\cN_X^{\Ran, n}$ is the nerve of the pull-back cover
of $X^n / \Sigma_n$ by the $\C^3$-charts of $X$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Ran-Čech filtration theorem]
\label{thm:ran-cech-filtration}
\textup{\ClaimStatusProposition.}
For each $n \geq 1$, the finite-$n$ Ran stratum of the chart-wise
factorisation data assembles via the $n$-level Čech double complex
$\RC^{\bullet, \bullet, n}$ into a factorisation cosheaf on
$\Ran_n(X)$. The total factorisation cosheaf on $\Ran(X)$ is the
homotopy colimit
\[
  \PhiFA_3(\mathrm{Perf}(X)) \;=\;
  \hocolim_{n \to \infty} \Tot\bigl(
  \RC^{\bullet, \bullet, n}\!\bigl(\PhiFA_3(\mathrm{Perf}(-))\bigr)
  \bigr).
\]
Each finite-$n$ truncation is a well-defined factorisation cosheaf on
a finite-type stratum; the homotopy colimit is the genuine
factorisation cosheaf on the ind-scheme $\Ran(X)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Francis--Gaitsgory \texttt{arXiv:1103.5803}~Thm.~3.4.2 on
factorisation-homology descent for finite-incidence diagrams:
$\hocolim_n$ of a compatible system of finite-$n$ factorisation
structures on ind-schemes is the factorisation homology of the
colimit. Beilinson--Drinfeld 3.4.6 provides the compatibility of
finite-$n$ chiral algebras with the Ran stratification. Combined
with Proposition~\ref{prop:cech-ran-total} at each level $n$, the
result is the colimit assembly.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Chiral-Koszul duality on the Ran-Čech filtration}

A key observation for the rest of the programme: Francis--Gaitsgory
chiral-Koszul duality
\[
  \Lie\text{-alg}^{\ch}(X) \;\simeq\;
  \Com\text{-coalg}^{\ch, \mathrm{fact}}(X)
\]
(Francis--Gaitsgory \texttt{arXiv:1103.5803}~Thm.~1.2.4) intertwines
the finite-$n$ Ran-Čech structure with the Čech structure on the nerve
$\cN_X$: the Koszul dual of the finite-$n$ factorisation is the
commutative-coalgebra on the same $X^n$. On the chart-wise side, the
Jordan-triple $E_3$-algebra on $\C^3$-chart is Koszul dual to the
chart-wise chiral commutative coalgebra, and the descent of the one is
the descent of the other.

\subsection*{Surviving core of (A4)}

The finite Čech nerve and the infinite-type Ran stratification combine
compatibly via the Beilinson--Drinfeld Ran filtration: each finite-$n$
level carries a well-defined Čech total complex, and the global
$\PhiFA_3$ is the $\hocolim$ over $n$. Finite Čech data captures
genuine infinite-configuration structure; no separate infinite-type
machinery is needed. Chiral-Koszul duality relates chart-wise
factorisation to chart-wise chiral commutative coalgebras compatibly
with the descent.

\section{Cycle 5: $K3 \times E$ as the deep stratum witness,

\subsection*{Attack (A5): $K3 \times E$ is not an analytic $\C^3$-cover target}

The compact CY$_3$ $K3 \times E$ does not directly admit a $\C^3$-chart
cover of the form required by Cycle~1: the $E$-factor is a smooth
elliptic curve with non-trivial $\pi_1$, so $E$ itself is not
$\C^3$-chart-cover-able in the same sense (any open subset of $E$
homeomorphic to $\R^2$ does admit a $\C^3$ extension, but the cover
must be analytic-polydisc compatible with the product structure, which
requires careful choice). Moreover, the Costello--Li holomorphic twist
requires a Calabi--Yau form on each chart; on $K3 \times E$ the
CY form $\Omega_{K3 \times E} = \Omega_{K3} \wedge dz_E$ splits as a
product, and the product structure must be respected by the chart
cover.

\subsection*{Heal: product-compatible analytic cover; Mukai-lattice gluing}

The heal uses the product structure directly: an analytic
$\C^3$-cover of $K3 \times E$ is product-compatible if it restricts to
an analytic $\C^2$-cover on $K3$ and an analytic $\C$-cover on $E$,
compatibly.

\begin{definition}[Product-compatible analytic cover of $K3 \times E$]
\label{def:product-compatible-cover}
A \emph{product-compatible analytic cover} of $K3 \times E$ is a
cover $\{U_\alpha \times V_\beta\}_{\alpha \in I_K, \beta \in I_E}$
where $\{U_\alpha\}$ is an analytic $\C^2$-cover of $K3$ and
$\{V_\beta\}$ is an analytic $\C$-cover of $E$, both Stein, both
providing acyclic Leray covers of their respective factors. The
nerve of the product cover is the simplicial product
$\cN_{K3} \times \cN_E$. Note: $K3$ admits an analytic $\C^2$-cover
(take the Kulikov--Pinkham degenerate-chart cover of K3, Pinkham
1977), and $E$ admits an analytic $\C$-cover (take a cover by
fundamental domains of the elliptic lattice).
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Schiffmann--Vasserot K3 CoHA as chart-wise critical CoHA]
\label{prop:sv-k3-coha-chart}
\textup{(Schiffmann--Vasserot 2012, \texttt{arXiv:1202.2756}~Thm.~1.1;
Davison--Meinhardt \texttt{arXiv:1601.02479}~Thm.~5.11.)}
On a $\C^2$-chart of K3, the chart-wise critical CoHA is the
Schiffmann--Vasserot shuffle algebra
$\Yplus(\widehat{\fgl}_1) = \CoHA(\C^2)$ (rank-one shuffle).
Globalised over K3 by the Nakajima Hilbert-scheme factorisation
algebra, this assembles into the SV K3 CoHA
$\CoHA_{K3} = Y(\widehat{\fgl}(\mathrm{K3}))$, the preprojective
Yangian of the K3 quiver. Tensoring with $H^\ast(E)$ on the
$E$-factor gives the $K3 \times E$ chart-wise input
$\CoHA(\C^3)|_{K3\text{-chart}} = \Yplus(\widehat{\fgl}_1) \otimes
H^\ast(E)$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Global $\PhiFA_3$ on $K3 \times E$ as SV CoHA descent]
\label{thm:k3xe-global-phi-fa-3}
\textup{\ClaimStatusConjectured\ Theorem-level modulo the
cocycle-computation at cell 14/15.} The global
$\PhiFA_3(\mathrm{Perf}(K3 \times E))$ is the Čech--Ran descent of
the chart-wise SV algebra $\Yplus(\widehat{\fgl}_1) \otimes H^\ast(E)$
along the product-compatible cover $\{U_\alpha \times V_\beta\}$,
with transition FM-kernels
\[
  K_{(\alpha,\beta)(\alpha',\beta')} \;=\;
  K^{K3}_{\alpha\alpha'} \otimes K^E_{\beta\beta'},
\]
where $K^{K3}_{\alpha\alpha'}$ is the Mukai-lattice FM-kernel of
Mukai~\texttt{arXiv:math/0408129} and $K^E_{\beta\beta'}$ is the
Atkin--Lehner elliptic-curve FM-kernel (twisted by the
$\Gamma_{\mathrm{arith}}$-Hecke structure). The cocycle class lives
in $H^1(\cN_{K3} \times \cN_E,
\underline{\Aut}_{D^b(K3)} \times \underline{\Aut}(E))$, which further
refines via the $M_{24}$-action on K3 and the $K(1)$-paramodular

\end{theorem}

\subsection*{Super-Yangian emergence and $\kBKM = 5$}

\begin{proposition}[Super-Yangian structure via Mukai lattice]
\label{prop:super-yangian-k3xe}
\textup{\ClaimStatusConjectured.} The Mukai pairing on
$\widetilde{H}(K3) = H^0 \oplus H^2 \oplus H^4$ is symmetric
(not super-symmetric); the Hodge involution splits
$\widetilde{H}(K3) = (\widetilde H)_+ \oplus (\widetilde H)_-$ with
$(\widetilde H)_+ = H^0 \oplus H^2_{\R, +} \oplus H^4$ of rank $20$
(the $(1,1)$-eigenspace) and $(\widetilde H)_- = H^2_{\R, -}$ of
rank $4$ (the $(2,0) \oplus (0,2)$-eigenspace). The K3 CoHA object
is the Chevalley-fixed subalgebra
\[
  Y_{\mathfrak{osp}}(4|20) \;=\;
  Y(\mathfrak{gl}(4|20))^\theta,
\]
of the super-Yangian on $\mathbb{C}^{4|20}$, with super-$R$-matrix
on $V = \mathbb{C}^{4|20}$. The $(4, 20)$-signature is forced by the
Mukai Hodge involution (memory: wave-15).
\end{proposition}

\begin{corollary}[$\kBKM$ from Borcherds lift as global cocycle]
\label{cor:kbkm-5-global}
The $\kBKM$-invariant of the global
$\PhiFA_3(\mathrm{Perf}(K3 \times E))$ is
$c_1(0) / 2 = 10 / 2 = 5$, where $c_1(0) = 10$ is the zeroth Fourier
coefficient of the Gritsenko--Nikulin form $\Delta_5$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin \texttt{arXiv:alg-geom/9504006}~Pt.~II~Thm.~2.1;
Borcherds \texttt{arXiv:alg-geom/9609022}~Thm.~10.1). This is the
stratum-$(iv)$ entry in the four-invariant spectrum
$\{0, 3, 5, 24\}$ of $K3 \times E$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}[Proof trace]
The global factorisation cocycle in cell 14/15 of the
fifteen-cell classification carries a stratum-$(iv)$ Borcherds-lift
component. The Borcherds weight $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ universally
holds at $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ (CLAUDE.md key facts); at $N = 1$,
$c_1(0) = 10$ for $\Delta_5$, hence $\kBKM = 5$. The

\end{proof}

\section{Epilogue: four frontier obstructions}

Four concrete frontier obstructions remain beyond the scope of this
Agent's construction:

\paragraph{(F1) Cocycle cohomology at cell 14/15 of $K3 \times E$.}
The cocycle class of Theorem~\ref{thm:k3xe-global-phi-fa-3} lives in
$H^1(\cN_{K3} \times \cN_E, \underline{\Aut}_{D^b(K3)} \times

geometry on the Kuga--Satake domain. The $\kBKM = 5$ value of
Corollary~\ref{cor:kbkm-5-global} is an invariant read off from
the cocycle, not a direct computation of the cohomology.

\paragraph{(F2) 24-stalk chain-level formula.} The elliptic
fibration $\pi: K3 \to \bP^1$ has 24 $I_1$-singular fibres. Each
stalk contributes a curve-stalk Jordan-triple potential tensored
with $E$-coefficients. The 24-stalk sum via the Mukai-lattice cocycle
is a definite prescription at the critical-CoHA level, but the
explicit chain-level Hall-product formula has not been written out.

\paragraph{(F3) Explicit super-$R$-matrix on $V = \mathbb{C}^{4|20}$.}
Proposition~\ref{prop:super-yangian-k3xe} asserts the super-Yangian
structure $Y_{\mathfrak{osp}}(4|20)$; the explicit RTT presentation
(generators, relations, super-$R$-matrix) is a separate calculation.
Following Maulik--Okounkov \texttt{arXiv:1211.1287}, the
super-$R$-matrix should emerge as a residue of the UV gluing cocycle
at the equivariant-chamber wall.

\paragraph{(F4) Non-simply-laced K3 generalisations.} The SV K3 CoHA
construction is for the simply-laced preprojective Yangian of the K3
quiver. Non-simply-laced generalisations (e.g.\ twisted K3
orbifolds with non-simply-laced McKay quiver, quotients by
non-abelian subgroups) require twisted-Yangian machinery, and the
Čech--Ran descent on such K3-quotients is a separate program.
