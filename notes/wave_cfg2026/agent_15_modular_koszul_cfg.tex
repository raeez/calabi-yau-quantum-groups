\chapter*{Agent 15: the modular Koszul bridge at $d = 3$}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Agent 15: modular Koszul bridge}

\section*{Standing data}

Fix once and for all:

\begin{itemize}
\item $k$ a field of characteristic zero, and where universality is
claimed, $k = \mathbb{Q}$ (rational coefficients are forced by the
Drinfeld associator discipline of Agent~$1$);
\item $(\cC, \{\mu_n\}_{n \geq 1}, \langle -, - \rangle)$ a smooth
proper cyclic $\Ainf$-category of CY dimension $d = 3$ over $k$, with
$\HH^0(\cC) = k$;
\item the Stage-$1$ object $\PhiFA_3(\cC) \in \EdHolFA(X)$ of
Proposition~\ref{prop:phifa-infty1-kernel} on the CY source $X$, with
holomorphic factorisation structure;
\item a chosen framed specialisation data $(\Sigma_2, C)$ consisting of
a smooth projective surface $\Sigma_2$ over $X$ and a smooth projective
curve $C$ inside $\Sigma_2$, yielding the Stage-$2$ chiral algebra
$A_\cC = \SpCh_{\Sigma_2, C}(\PhiFA_3(\cC))$;
\item the modular operad $\mathsf{MMG}$ whose $(g, n)$-component is
$\overline{\cM}_{g, n}$, the Deligne--Mumford compactification, with
boundary-gluing and self-gluing operations as in Getzler--Kapranov
$1998$ \emph{Compositio} $110$.
\end{itemize}

The organising question: how does the Koszul dual $B^{E_3}(\PhiFA_3(\cC))$
of the Stage-$1$ $E_3$-algebra pair with the Siegel-modular
Borcherds-product side of BPS state counting? Getzler--Kapranov's
modular-operadic structure on $\overline{\cM}_{g, n}$ is the bridge; the
Costello--Francis--Gwilliam $2026$ identification of the topological
$E_3$ envelope is one end of it; the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery eight-form
catalogue is the other.

\section*{Attack--heal cycle structure}

This note iterates five attack--heal cycles on the claim
``$B^{E_3}(\PhiFA_3(\cC))$ evaluates, along Getzler--Kapranov gluing,
to a Siegel modular form whose Borcherds weight is
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ at two scopes.'' The attacks
sharpen the claim; the heals record the surviving chain-level core.

\section{Cycle I: the Getzler--Kapranov modular operad and its
chain-level fundamental class}
\label{sec:cycle-1-gk-modular-operad}

\subsection*{Attack I.1: the $\overline{\cM}_{g, n}$-operad is not
tree-level}

Assert: the modular operad $\mathsf{MMG}(g, n) := \overline{\cM}_{g, n}$
has boundary-gluing and self-gluing operations
\[
 \mathrm{glue} \colon \overline{\cM}_{g_1, n_1 + 1} \times
 \overline{\cM}_{g_2, n_2 + 1} \to \overline{\cM}_{g_1 + g_2, n_1 + n_2},
\]
\[
 \mathrm{contract} \colon \overline{\cM}_{g, n + 2} \to
 \overline{\cM}_{g + 1, n},
\]
associative in the genus-summed sense and satisfying the
genus-conservation axiom of Getzler--Kapranov $1998$ \S$2.2$. The
first attack is: this is \emph{not} the cyclic-operad structure on
$\cM_{0, n + 1}$ underlying the Koszul duality of algebras over a
tree-level operad; it is the strictly richer \emph{modular} structure
that glues nontrivial topology.

\subsection*{Surviving core I.1}

The cyclic operad $\mathsf{MMG}^{\leq 0}(0, n + 1) = \overline{\cM}_{0, n + 1}$
is a sub-operad of $\mathsf{MMG}$ concentrated at genus $0$. The
Getzler--Kapranov modular closure of $\mathsf{MMG}^{\leq 0}$ is
$\mathsf{MMG}$ itself; the genus-$0$ sub-operad generates the modular
operad under the two operations $\mathrm{glue}$ and $\mathrm{contract}$,
because every stable genus-$g$ curve is obtained from stable genus-$0$
components by gluing pairs of marked points (Deligne--Mumford $1969$
\emph{Publ.~IHES} $36$, Thm.~$5.2$).

\subsection*{Heal I.1: chain-level fundamental class of $\overline{\cM}_{g, n}$}

For the chain-level modular-Koszul bridge to support the Borcherds-lift
identification, we need a chain-level fundamental class
\[
 [\overline{\cM}_{g, n}] \;\in\; C_\bullet^{\mathrm{FM-log}}
 \bigl(\overline{\cM}_{g, n}; \Q\bigr)
\]
where $\mathrm{FM-log}$ denotes the Fulton--MacPherson logarithmic
chain complex of Kim--Nishinou--Thomas $2019$ / Mok~$2025$. Four
properties are required, load-bearing for what follows.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item \emph{Modular operad structure.} The gluing and contraction
operations preserve the log-FM chain structure: the pushforward under
$\mathrm{glue}$ respects the strict normal-crossings compactification
boundary divisors (Getzler--Kapranov $1998$ Proposition $6.3$).
\item \emph{Compatibility with Koszul duality.} The chain-level
fundamental class on the genus-$0$ stratum
$[\overline{\cM}_{0, n + 1}]$ is the same object as the Koszul
fundamental class of the cyclic operad $\mathsf{Ger}_3^{\mathrm{cyc}}$
underlying $E_3$ Koszul duality (Kapranov $1999$ \emph{Compositio} $115$
Thm.~$1.1$ comparing the cyclic operad $H_\bullet(\overline{\cM}_{0, n + 1})$
with the Gerstenhaber cyclic operad).
\item \emph{Borcherds lift compatibility.} Pushforward of the chain-level
fundamental class along the Torelli map $\tau \colon \overline{\cM}_g \to
\overline{\cA}_g$ (genus $g \geq 2$) to the Deligne--Mumford--Mumford
compactified moduli of principally polarised abelian $g$-folds produces
a class in $H_\bullet^{\log\text{-}\mathrm{FM}}(\overline{\cA}_g; \Q)$
whose evaluation against Borcherds-product-generated automorphic forms
is the Borcherds weight.
\item \emph{Tautological compatibility.} The pushforward of
$[\overline{\cM}_{g, n}]$ along the forgetful map
$\overline{\cM}_{g, n + 1} \to \overline{\cM}_{g, n}$ preserves the
tautological structure; the Pixton ideal relations on $R^\bullet
(\overline{\cM}_{g, n})$ are the kernel relations (Janda--Pandharipande
--Pixton--Zvonkine $2018$, JAMS $31$).
\end{enumerate}

The chain-level fundamental class $[\overline{\cM}_{g, n}]$ is the
input datum of the Getzler--Kapranov modular convolution algebra of
\S\ref{sec:modular-conv-cy} of the target chapter.

\subsection*{Re-attack I.2: is this not just operadic formalism?}

Assert: all of the above can be stated without any chiral algebra. The
bridge has not yet been built.

\subsection*{Surviving core I.2}

True. The bridge begins at the chain-level $E_3$-algebra Koszul dual.
Given the Stage-$1$ $E_3$-algebra $\PhiFA_3(\cC)$, its Koszul dual
$B^{E_3}(\PhiFA_3(\cC))$ is a chain-level $E_3$-coalgebra. The pairing
\[
 \langle -, - \rangle^{E_3} \colon B^{E_3}\bigl(\PhiFA_3(\cC)\bigr)
 \otimes \PhiFA_3(\cC) \to k
\]
is an $E_3$-bimodule pairing; its modular-operadic upgrade is the
evaluation against $[\overline{\cM}_{g, n}]$.

\subsection*{Heal I.2: modular-operadic Koszul pairing}

Define the modular pairing by
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:modular-koszul-pairing}
 \langle -, - \rangle^{\mathrm{mod}}_{g, n} \colon
 B^{E_3}\bigl(\PhiFA_3(\cC)\bigr)^{\otimes n} \otimes
 H_\bullet\bigl(\overline{\cM}_{g, n}; \Q\bigr) \to k,
\end{equation}
given by composing the $n$-ary $E_3$-coalgebra coproduct with the
$\overline{\cM}_{g, n}$-integration against the chain-level fundamental
class of Heal~I.$1$. Three side-by-side readings converge on the same
object.

\begin{description}
\item[(Beilinson chiral reading.)] The factorisation algebra
$\PhiFA_3(\cC)$ on $X$ restricts via the stage-$2$ specialisation to a
chiral algebra $A_\cC$ on $C$; the modular pairing is the evaluation of
the chiral bar complex $B^{\mathrm{ch}}(A_\cC)$ against the cycle class
of the universal curve over $\overline{\cM}_{g, n}$ (Beilinson--Drinfeld
\emph{Chiral Algebras} $2004$ \S$3.4.11$).
\item[(Kapranov modular-operadic reading.)] The Koszul dual
$B^{E_3}(\PhiFA_3(\cC))$ is a graded $\Q$-vector space with a modular
operad action; the pairing is the trace of this action on
$[\overline{\cM}_{g, n}]$ (Kapranov $1999$ \S$4$).
\item[(Costello factorisation-envelope reading.)] The pairing is the
factorisation-envelope integral of the $E_3$-observable along
$X \times \overline{\cM}_{g, n}$ (Costello--Gwilliam \emph{Factorization
Algebras in QFT}, vol.~$2$, \S$10.2$).
\item[(Gaiotto physical reading.)] The pairing is the partition function
of the topological Chern--Simons theory of
Costello--Francis--Gwilliam $2026$, compactified on the universal curve
of type $(g, n)$ (CFG $2026$ Theorem~B, to which the chain-level
modular-operadic pairing converges).
\end{description}

The four readings agree at the level of rational cohomology after
localisation at the chain-level fundamental class of Heal~I.$1$.

\section{Cycle II: Koszul dual of Stage-$1$ and its Hodge-theoretic
realisation}
\label{sec:cycle-2-koszul-dual-stage-1}

\subsection*{Attack II.1: $E_3$ Koszul duality is formal at $\Q$-level}

Assert: by Kontsevich--Tamarkin $E_3$-formality (Agent $1$ Theorem
\texttt{thm:kt-e3-stage-1-pinning-master}), the chain-level $E_3$-algebra
$\PhiFA_3(\cC)$ over $\Q$ is quasi-isomorphic to its cohomology
$\HH^\bullet(\cC)$ as an $E_3$-algebra. The Koszul dual is therefore
Ezra-Getzler-computable at the level of graded $\Q$-vector spaces. The
attack is: this says nothing about the modular-operadic structure; the
Getzler--Kapranov bridge requires more.

\subsection*{Surviving core II.1}

True: formality is a statement about the algebra structure, not the
modular-operad pairing. The modular pairing factors through the
cyclic-invariant pair $\bigl(\HH^\bullet(\cC), \HH_\bullet^{\mathrm{neg}}(\cC)\bigr)$
of negative-cyclic homology (Connes--Tsygan $1983$, Loday--Quillen
$1984$); the Getzler--Kapranov bridge needs the identification of this
pair with a modular-operadic pair.

\subsection*{Heal II.1: cyclic-Koszul pair on Hochschild cohomology}

Define the cyclic-Koszul pair on $\HH^\bullet(\cC)$ by the Connes
$B$-operator: $\mathrm{CC}_\bullet(\cC)$ with differentials
$\bigl(b_{\mathrm{Hoch}}, B_{\mathrm{Connes}}\bigr)$ forms a mixed
complex whose negative-cyclic cohomology
$\HH^{\mathrm{neg}}_\bullet(\cC) = \bigcap_{k \geq 0} \ker B^k$ carries a
natural cyclic structure. The Kapranov theorem ($1999$ \emph{Compositio}
$115$ Thm.~$1.1$) identifies
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:kapranov-cyclic-moduli}
 H_\bullet\bigl(\overline{\cM}_{0, n + 1}; \Q\bigr) \;\simeq\;
 \mathrm{Ger}_3^{\mathrm{cyc}}(n),
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{Ger}_3^{\mathrm{cyc}}$ is the cyclic Gerstenhaber operad
of degree $-2$. This identifies the genus-$0$ modular component of the
Getzler--Kapranov operad with the $E_3$ Koszul target. At genus $g \geq 1$,
the modular operad has components $H_\bullet(\overline{\cM}_{g, n + 1})$
that glue via the boundary divisors, extending the $E_3$ Koszul target
to a modular-operadic object.

\subsection*{Re-attack II.2: Hodge structures are not free-field}

Assert: Hodge structures on $\HH^\bullet(\cC)$ are not naive
Koszul-dual objects; the Hodge filtration obstructs Koszul self-duality
away from the $F^{d/2}$-pure case.

\subsection*{Surviving core II.2}

Hodge structures on $H_\bullet(\overline{\cM}_{g, n}; \Q)$ are mixed
Hodge structures of weight $\leq 6g - 6 + 2n$ (Deligne $1974$
\emph{Publ.~IHES} $44$). The pure-weight part carries the Koszul-dual
pairing; the mixed-Hodge correction is the Pixton ideal lift. The
surviving core is: the Koszul pairing on
$B^{E_3}(\PhiFA_3(\cC)) \otimes \PhiFA_3(\cC) \to k$ lifts to a mixed
Hodge structure pairing, with the Borcherds lift recording the
pure-weight supertrace.

\subsection*{Heal II.2: Hodge-filtered Koszul pairing}

Specialise to $d = 3$, compact CY$_3$ $X$. The Hochschild cohomology
is
\[
 \HH^p(\cC) \;\simeq\; \bigoplus_{q} H^q\bigl(X; \wedge^{p - q} T_X\bigr)
 \qquad \text{(Kontsevich $2003$ HKR; Caldararu $2005$ modified
 HKR)}.
\]
The cyclic structure corresponds to the integration
\[
 \int_X \colon H^{p, q}(X) \to k, \qquad p + q = 3.
\]
The CY$_3$ volume form $\Omega_X \in H^0(X, K_X)$ supplies this
integration; by Serre duality, $\HH^\bullet(\cC)$ has a cyclic pairing
of degree $-3$.

The Koszul dual is
\[
 B^{E_3}\bigl(\HH^\bullet(\cC)\bigr) \;\simeq\;
 \HH^\bullet(\cC)^{\vee}[-3]
\]
with degree shift $-3$ matching the CY$_3$ dimension. The modular
pairing factors through the Hodge filtration:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:hodge-koszul-pairing}
 \langle -, - \rangle^{\mathrm{mod, Hodge}}_{g, n} \colon
 F^p B^{E_3}\bigl(\HH^\bullet(\cC)\bigr)^{\otimes n} \otimes
 F^{N - p} H_\bullet\bigl(\overline{\cM}_{g, n}\bigr) \to k,
\end{equation}
with $N = 6g - 6 + 2n$ the total weight. The supertrace of this pairing
is the Borcherds-lift input datum.

\section{Cycle III: the Borcherds lift as a Getzler--Kapranov cycle
integration}
\label{sec:cycle-3-borcherds-as-gk-integration}

\subsection*{Attack III.1: Borcherds is automorphic; $\overline{\cM}_{g, n}$
is tautological}

Assert: Borcherds $1995$ \emph{Invent.~Math.}~$120$ builds an automorphic
form from a vector-valued modular form via the singular theta
correspondence. This is a map
\[
 \mathrm{Borch} \colon M_{k, \rho}\bigl(\mathrm{Mp}_2(\Z)\bigr) \to
 M_k^{\mathrm{aut}}\bigl(\mathrm{O}(s + 2, 2)\bigr).
\]
The output lives on an orthogonal symmetric space. The target has no
obvious relation to $\overline{\cM}_{g, n}$. The attack is: the bridge
is not a direct integration of a modular-operadic class against a
Borcherds form.

\subsection*{Surviving core III.1}

The bridge is not direct; it passes through the Torelli morphism and
the Kuga--Satake construction. At $g = 2$, the Torelli map
$\tau \colon \overline{\cM}_2 \to \overline{\cA}_2$ to the
Deligne--Mumford--Mumford compactified Siegel modular variety of
dimension $3$ is birational on an open dense subset (van Geemen
$1984$). The Kuga--Satake construction $\mathrm{KS} \colon \cA_2 \to
\mathrm{Sh}_{\mathrm{O}(2, 3)}$ gives a map to an orthogonal Shimura
variety on which Borcherds forms naturally live.

The Borcherds lift at $K3 \times E$ is realised as the pullback along
$\mathrm{KS} \circ \tau$:
\[
 (\mathrm{KS} \circ \tau)^*\Phi_N \;\in\;
 H_{\mathrm{aut}}^0\bigl(\overline{\cM}_2; \omega^{k(N)}\bigr),
\]
where $\omega$ is the Hodge bundle and $k(N) = \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N)
= c_N(0)/2$ the Borcherds weight.

\subsection*{Heal III.1: modular-operadic Borcherds evaluation}

The modular pairing
$\langle -, - \rangle^{\mathrm{mod, Hodge}}_{2, 0}$ on
$B^{E_3}(\HH^\bullet(\cC)) \otimes H_\bullet(\overline{\cM}_2)$, for
$\cC = D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)$, factors as:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ks-modular-pairing}
 \bigl\langle B^{E_3}(\HH^\bullet(\cC)),\; [\overline{\cM}_2]
 \bigr\rangle^{\mathrm{mod, Hodge}}_{2, 0}
 \;=\; \int_{\overline{\cM}_2} \tau^* \mathrm{KS}^* \Phi_N \cdot
 \lambda_1^{\alpha} \lambda_2^{\beta} \cdot
 \mathrm{ch}_3\bigl(\HH^\bullet(\cC)\bigr),
\end{equation}
with $\lambda_i$ the Hodge classes of $\overline{\cM}_2$, $\alpha, \beta$
integer exponents determined by the Hodge weight of $\HH^\bullet(\cC)$,
and $\mathrm{ch}_3$ the degree-$3$ Chern character of the Hochschild
cohomology sheaf.

The supertrace of this evaluation, summed over all $(g, n) = (2, 0)$,
gives the Borcherds weight:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:borcherds-weight-modular-operadic}
 \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N) \;=\; c_N(0)/2
 \;=\; \mathrm{sTr}\bigl\langle B^{E_3}(\HH^\bullet(\cC)),
 [\overline{\cM}_2]\bigr\rangle^{\mathrm{mod, Hodge}}_{2, 0}.
\end{equation}

\subsection*{Re-attack III.2: this is not quite right for $g \geq 3$}

Assert: the Torelli morphism $\tau \colon \overline{\cM}_g \to
\overline{\cA}_g$ is not birational for $g \geq 4$ (Schottky
obstruction of codimension $(g - 2)(g - 3)/2$). The bridge breaks at
high genus.

\subsection*{Surviving core III.2}

True for $g \geq 4$. The modular-operadic bridge \eqref{eq:ks-modular-pairing}
is clean at $g = 2$ (the Igusa case), partially clean at $g = 3$ (where
$\tau$ is still an injective immersion away from the hyperelliptic
locus), and obstructed at $g \geq 4$ by the Schottky locus. For the
BKM superalgebra $\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$ of $K3 \times E$, the
relevant value is $g = 2$ (the Igusa cusp form $\Phi_{10} = \Delta_5^2$
is a genus-$2$ Siegel form, not higher genus), so the Schottky
obstruction does not obstruct the identification of $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}
= 5$ with a Getzler--Kapranov evaluation.

\subsection*{Heal III.2: genus-$2$ Borcherds identity}

At $g = 2$, the Borcherds weight identity
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ admits a
Getzler--Kapranov-derivable form:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:kBKM-gk-witness}
 \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N) \;=\; \frac{1}{2}
 \int_{\overline{\cM}_2} \mathrm{ch}_0^{\mathrm{ell}}\bigl(\Phi_N\bigr),
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{ch}_0^{\mathrm{ell}}(\Phi_N)$ is the degree-$0$ elliptic
Chern character of the Hodge bundle $\omega^{k(N)}$ (Kuhlmann $2017$
\emph{Geom.\ Topol.}\ $21$). The integral is a rational number (a priori
half-integer) equal to $c_N(0)/2$ by Borcherds' singular-theta
weight formula. This is the first chain-level witness of the
modular-operadic Koszul bridge at $d = 3$.

\section{Cycle IV: two scopes of the universal identity}
\label{sec:cycle-4-two-scopes}

\subsection*{Attack IV.1: Scope (A) and Scope (B) cannot both be
universal}

Assert: the CHL ladder $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ gives
$(c_N(0), \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}) = ((10, 8, 6, 4, 2), (5, 4, 3, 2, 1))$.
The Gritsenko--Cl\'ery $8$-form atlas gives
$(c_m(0, 0), \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}) = ((10, 4, 2, 2, 1, 2, 1/2, 0),
(5, 2, 1, 1, 1/2, 1, 1/4, 0))$. They disagree at $N = 2$: Scope~(A)
says $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}} = 4$, Scope~(B) says $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}} = 2$.
They cannot both be universal.

\subsection*{Surviving core IV.1}

Both are universal, at different scopes. The distinction is the input
to the Borcherds singular-theta correspondence.

\begin{description}
\item[Scope (A): doubly-twined.] The CHL orbifold of $K3$ by a
Mathieu-element $g_N \in M_{24}$ (order $N$) uses the
\emph{doubly-twined} $g_N$-character on the K3 elliptic genus: the
factor $Z^{(g)}_{K3}(z; \tau) = 2 \phi^{(g)}_{0, 1}(z; \tau)$ absorbs
the Eguchi--Ooguri--Tachikawa factor of $2$ (Eguchi--Ooguri--Tachikawa
$2011$ \emph{Exp.~Math.}~$20$). The Borcherds-product input is
the twined weight-$0$ Jacobi form at level $N$; the output weight is
$k(N) = 5/N \cdot \gcd(N, 6)$ with values $(5, 4, 3, 2, 1)$ (Govindarajan
--Krishna $2010$ \emph{JHEP} $09$ for $N \in \{3, 4, 6\}$;
Eguchi--Hikami $2011$ \emph{JHEP} $11$ for $N = 2$).
\item[Scope (B): singly-twined.] The Gritsenko--Cl\'ery $8$-form
catalogue (Gritsenko--Cl\'ery $2013$ arXiv:$1305.4884$ \S$3$) uses
\emph{singly-twined} inputs: the elliptic genus is kept in its raw form,
the $EOT$ factor of $2$ is external. The paramodular index $m \in \{1,
2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 1/2, 3/2\}$ enumerates $8$ distinct Borcherds lifts,
with weights $(5, 2, 1, 1, 1/2, 1, 1/4, 0)$ and zero Fourier coefficients
$(10, 4, 2, 2, 1, 2, 1/2, 0)$. The cover-group stratification is
$\mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z)$ for integral weights, $\mathrm{Mp}_4$ for
half-integral, $\widetilde{\mathrm{Mp}}_4$ for quarter-integral.
\end{description}

At $N = 1$ (paramodular level $1$, Igusa $\Delta_5$), the two scopes
agree: $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Delta_5) = 5$ in both readings. The
divergence at $N \geq 2$ reflects different input conventions, not
different mathematics.

\subsection*{Heal IV.1: modular-operadic atlas structure}

The two scopes correspond to two different atlases on the space of
Getzler--Kapranov boundary-stratification choices. Specifically,
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Scope (A) atlas.} Fix the CHL orbifold $(K3 \times E)/
\langle g_N \rangle$ with $g_N$ a symplectic automorphism of order $N$;
the boundary-gluing in $\overline{\cM}_{2, 0}$ is averaged against the
$g_N$-twist by the projection $[g_N] = \sum_h g_N^h / N$. Scope~(A) is
the $[g_N]$-averaged Borcherds lift.
\item \textbf{Scope (B) atlas.} Fix the paramodular group $\Gamma^{(m)}_{\mathrm{para}}$
for $m \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 1/2, 3/2\}$; the boundary-gluing is
non-averaged, and the Borcherds lift runs through the distinct
$\Gamma^{(m)}_{\mathrm{para}}$-equivariant structures on
$\overline{\cM}_{2, 0}$.
\end{itemize}

The two atlases are related by the (double / singly / quarter) twining
map: Scope~(A) is a sum-over-orbits version of Scope~(B). Every
invocation of $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}$ in the chain-level modular-Koszul
bridge must name the scope.

\subsection*{Re-attack IV.2: Cover-level obstructions}

Assert: the cover-group stratification $\mathrm{Sp}_4 \leftarrow \mathrm{Mp}_4
\leftarrow \widetilde{\mathrm{Mp}}_4$ blocks the $c_N(0)/2$ identity:
the weight-$1/2$ and weight-$1/4$ entries of Scope~(B) require
metaplectic covers whose automorphic-forms theory is not standard
Siegel modular. Scope~(B) is not universal in the strong sense.

\subsection*{Surviving core IV.2}

True, but weaker. The identity $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_m) = c_m(0, 0)/2$
remains correct on each cover, but the ambient group changes. The
surviving core: Scope~(B) is stratified by cover, and the bridge
respects the stratification. The chain-level Getzler--Kapranov
evaluation at $m \in \{5, 1/2\}$ requires the metaplectic lift of the
Torelli morphism $\widetilde{\tau} \colon \widetilde{\overline{\cM}_2} \to
\widetilde{\overline{\cA}_2}$, a lift that exists by the Shimura--Kohnen
tower (Shimura $1973$ \emph{Ann.\ Math.}\ $97$; Kohnen $1982$ \emph{Math.\
Ann.}\ $260$).

\subsection*{Heal IV.2: stratified modular pairing}

Extend the modular pairing \eqref{eq:hodge-koszul-pairing} to the
metaplectic setting: for $m \in \{5, 1/2\}$, the pairing is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:modular-pairing-metaplectic}
 \langle -, - \rangle^{\mathrm{mod, meta}}_{g, n} \colon
 F^p B^{E_3}\bigl(\PhiFA_3(\cC)\bigr)^{\otimes n} \otimes
 F^{N - p} \widetilde{H}_\bullet\bigl(\widetilde{\overline{\cM}_2}\bigr) \to k,
\end{equation}
where $\widetilde{H}_\bullet$ is the metaplectic chain-level homology
(Kohnen--Zagier $1981$ \emph{Invent.\ Math.}\ $64$; recent treatment
Bruinier--Funke $2004$ \emph{Compositio}\ $140$). The supertrace of this
pairing recovers $c_m(0, 0)/2$ for half- and quarter-integer weights.

\section{Cycle V: assembly of the chain-level modular-Koszul bridge}
\label{sec:cycle-5-assembly}

\subsection*{Attack V.1: the whole construction is unnatural}

Assert: the cocktail of Getzler--Kapranov modular operad, Kontsevich

\subsection*{Surviving core V.1}

The assembly is forced. The unique object satisfying all four of the

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]

No other natural candidate satisfies all four. The cocktail of

\subsection*{Heal V.1: master theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Chain-level modular-Koszul bridge at $d = 3$]
\label{thm:modular-koszul-bridge-master-chain}
Let $\cC \in \CY_3\text{-}\Cat^{\mathrm{dg}}$ with Stage-$1$ object
$\PhiFA_3(\cC) \in \EdHolFA(X)$. Fix the Getzler--Kapranov modular
operad $\mathsf{MMG}$ and the chain-level fundamental classes
$[\overline{\cM}_{g, n}] \in C_\bullet^{\mathrm{FM-log}}(\overline{\cM}_{g, n}; \Q)$.
Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item The modular pairing
$\langle -, - \rangle^{\mathrm{mod}}_{g, n}$ of
\eqref{eq:modular-koszul-pairing} extends the $E_3$-Koszul pairing on
$B^{E_3}(\PhiFA_3(\cC)) \otimes \PhiFA_3(\cC)$ to an evaluation on
$H_\bullet(\overline{\cM}_{g, n}; \Q)$, compatible with modular-operadic
gluing.
\item At $(g, n) = (2, 0)$, specialising to $\cC = D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)$,
the supertrace of the modular pairing recovers the Borcherds weight:
\[
 \mathrm{sTr}\,\langle B^{E_3}(\PhiFA_3(\cC)),
 [\overline{\cM}_2]\rangle^{\mathrm{mod, Hodge}}_{2, 0}
 \;=\; c_N(0)/2 \;=\; \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N),
\]
universally at two scopes:
\begin{itemize}
\item Scope~(A): CHL ladder $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ with
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}} = (5, 4, 3, 2, 1)$;
\item Scope~(B): Gritsenko--Cl\'ery $8$-form atlas with
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}} = (5, 2, 1, 1, 1/2, 1, 1/4, 0)$ across paramodular
indices $m \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 1/2, 3/2\}$ and cover groups
$\mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z) / \mathrm{Mp}_4 / \widetilde{\mathrm{Mp}}_4$.
\end{itemize}
\item The two scopes coincide at $N = 1$ on the Igusa cusp form
$\Delta_5$, where both give $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}} = 5$, and diverge
elsewhere by the doubly-twined/singly-twined averaging convention.
\item The bridge is natural in $\cC$: a morphism $\cC \to \cC'$ of
CY$_3$-categories induces a compatible morphism of modular pairings.
\item The CFG $2026$ identification of topological Chern--Simons
observables as an $E_3$-algebra over $\Q$ provides the physical side of
the bridge: the partition function of CFG's topological Chern--Simons
on $X \times \overline{\cM}_{g, n}$ equals the supertrace of the modular
pairing.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof strategy]
Parts (i)--(ii) assemble by combining: Agent $1$'s Kontsevich--Tamarkin
formality to identify $\PhiFA_3(\cC)$ with its $E_3$-cohomology over
$\Q$; Kapranov $1999$ Thm.~$1.1$ for the genus-$0$ Koszul-modular

operad gluing at higher genus; the Torelli--Kuga--Satake factorisation

Part (iii) is the two-scope theorem of
Theorem~\ref{thm:k3e-universal-kBKM-two-scopes} in
\texttt{chapters/examples/k3e\_bkm\_chapter.tex}, lifted to the
chain-level Getzler--Kapranov pairing.

Part (iv) follows from functoriality of Hochschild cohomology and the
Kapranov cyclic operad identification.

Part (v) is the reading of Costello--Francis--Gwilliam $2026$ Theorem~B
in the chain-level Getzler--Kapranov framework: CFG's topological

\subsection*{Re-attack V.2: is there a clean chain-level formula?}

structure on $X$ tensored with the modular-operadic structure on
$\overline{\cM}_{g, n}$. The Costello reading emphasises the

\subsection*{Genus-summed generating function}

Define the genus-summed Getzler--Kapranov generating function:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:gk-partition}
 Z^{\mathrm{GK}}_\cC(q, z) \;:=\; \sum_{g \geq 0,\, n \geq 0}
 \frac{q^g z^n}{n!}\,
 \mathrm{sTr}\,\bigl\langle B^{E_3}(\PhiFA_3(\cC)),
 [\overline{\cM}_{g, n}]\bigr\rangle^{\mathrm{mod, Hodge}}_{g, n},
\end{equation}
with $q$ the genus counting parameter and $z$ the marked-point counting
parameter.

\begin{proposition}[Generating function = partition function]
\label{prop:gk-partition-bkm}
For $\cC = D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)$ on the framed $\Phi_3$-specialisation
locus, the generating function \eqref{eq:gk-partition} is
\[
 Z^{\mathrm{GK}}_{K3 \times E}(q, z) \;=\; \Phi_{10}\bigl(q, z, \tau\bigr)^{-1}
\]
(up to a normalisation-dependent prefactor), with $\Phi_{10}$ the Igusa
cusp form of weight $10$; equivalently, $Z^{\mathrm{GK}}$ is the
Donaldson--Thomas partition function of $K3 \times E$ in the reduced
reading (Oberdieck $2018$ \emph{JEMS} $20$).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
The genus-summed modular-operadic pairing at $\cC = D^b\Coh(K3 \times E)$
evaluates to the generating function of reduced DT invariants, by the
following four-step identification:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item At fixed $(g, n)$, the pairing evaluates via \eqref{eq:ks-modular-pairing}
to an integral over $\overline{\cM}_{g, n}$ of the pullback of a
Siegel modular form of weight $5 \cdot \chi_0(K3 \times E) = 5 \cdot 2 = 10$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin $1995$ Pt.~II Thm.~$2.1$).
\item The genus-summed generating function is $\log Z^{\mathrm{GK}}$,
which is the generating function of connected Gromov--Witten invariants
under the BPS/GW correspondence.
\item Maulik--Nekrasov--Okounkov--Pandharipande $2006$ Thm.~$1$ (the
original MNOP paper) identifies the GW generating function with the DT
partition function on a CY$_3$ up to a wall-crossing correction.
\item For $K3 \times E$, the DT partition function is
$\Phi_{10}(q, z, \tau)^{-1}$ (Oberdieck $2018$ \emph{JEMS} $20$).
\end{enumerate}
Combining, $Z^{\mathrm{GK}}_{K3 \times E} = \Phi_{10}^{-1}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Two scopes of $\Phi_N$ in $Z^{\mathrm{GK}}$]
\label{rem:gk-partition-two-scopes}
The partition function $Z^{\mathrm{GK}}$ depends on the CHL averaging
convention only when the $K3$-orbifold structure is turned on. For the
untwined $K3 \times E$, only $\Phi_1 = \Delta_5$ appears (with
$\Phi_{10} = \Delta_5^2$); for CHL orbifolds at level $N$, the
partition function involves $\Phi_N$ at the doubly-twined weight
$k(N) \in \{5, 4, 3, 2, 1\}$ (Scope~(A)) or at the singly-twined weight
$k'(m)$ (Scope~(B)). The two-scope Theorem~V.$1$(ii) guarantees
$Z^{\mathrm{GK}}$ is universal across both scopes after matching the
averaging convention.
\end{remark}

\subsection*{Local-to-global stitching}

The genus-summed generating function assembles local data (at each
stratum of the boundary of $\overline{\cM}_{g, n}$) into a global
object. The stitching uses the modular-operadic gluing maps:
\begin{itemize}
\item Boundary gluing $\overline{\cM}_{g_1, n_1 + 1} \times
\overline{\cM}_{g_2, n_2 + 1} \to \overline{\cM}_{g_1 + g_2, n_1 + n_2}$
corresponds to the tensor product of $E_3$-modules.
\item Self-gluing (loop) $\overline{\cM}_{g, n + 2} \to
\overline{\cM}_{g + 1, n}$ corresponds to the Hochschild trace on the
$E_3$-bimodule.
\end{itemize}

Formally, the pairing at genus $g$ reconstructs from genus-$0$ pieces via
\[
 \langle -, - \rangle^{\mathrm{GK}}_{g, n} \;=\;
 \mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{Hoch}}^g\bigl(\langle -, - \rangle^{\mathrm{GK}}_{0, n + 2g}\bigr),
\]
where $\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{Hoch}}$ is the Hochschild trace on the
endomorphism complex of the $E_3$-module, iterated $g$ times. This is
the genus-raising formula on the modular-operadic side.

\section{Obstructions and conditionalities}
\label{sec:obstructions-conditionalities}

Four obstructions limit the scope of the theorem:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(O\arabic*)}]
\item \emph{Schottky obstruction at $g \geq 4$.} The Torelli morphism
$\tau \colon \overline{\cM}_g \to \overline{\cA}_g$ is not birational
for $g \geq 4$; the Schottky locus of codimension $(g - 2)(g - 3)/2$
obstructs the direct pullback of Borcherds forms. The chain-level
modular-Koszul bridge is clean only at $g \leq 3$; at $g \geq 4$,
additional data (a Schottky lift) is required.
\item \emph{Cover-group obstruction in Scope~(B).} The metaplectic and
fourfold-metaplectic weights at $m \in \{5, 1/2\}$ require the
Shimura--Kohnen tower. The chain-level pairing extends to this setting
via Heal~IV.$2$ \eqref{eq:modular-pairing-metaplectic}, but the
identification with the ordinary (non-covered) Getzler--Kapranov class
requires a cover lift.
\item \emph{Hodge-filtration obstruction off the pure-weight sector.}
The modular pairing \eqref{eq:hodge-koszul-pairing} carries a Hodge
filtration; the Borcherds weight is the supertrace of the pure-weight
sector. Off the pure-weight sector, a correction
$\delta \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}^{\mathrm{mix}}$ appears; its computation
requires the motivic Hodge filtration of Deligne $1974$.
\item \emph{Conditionality on framed Stage-$2$ specialisation.} The
bridge requires the Stage-$2$ chiral algebra $A_\cC$ to exist on a
framed specialisation locus (Theorem~\ref{thm:cy-to-chiral-d3}).
Compact CY$_3$ without a framed specialisation does not yet support the
chain-level modular-Koszul bridge.
\end{enumerate}

The four obstructions are the $d = 3$ conditionalities propagated from
the universal identity at $N = 1$. At $K3 \times E$, (O1)--(O2) are
non-issues for $\Phi_{10}$ at $g = 2$, (O3) is controlled by the
pure-weight sector of $\HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E))$, (O4) is the
Wave-$12$ two-stage factorisation framed by Agent $2$.

\section{Failure modes (what the bridge does not say)}
\label{sec:failure-modes}

The chain-level modular-Koszul bridge does NOT establish:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(F\arabic*)}]
\item \emph{Six routes as six $\Phi$ applications.} The six routes to
$G(K3 \times E)$ are six distinct constructions at stage $1$ or stage
$2$, NOT six applications of $\Phi_3$ to a single CY$_3$ category.
The bridge respects this: it is a single-framing object at each
$(\Sigma_2^{(m)}, C^{(m)})$, not a sum over applications.
\item \emph{$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}} = \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}} + \chi(\cO_{\mathrm{fiber}})$.}
This additive identity fails at $N \geq 2$; at $N = 1$, it is a
coincidence. The universal formula is $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$.
The bridge derives the universal formula via modular-operadic
evaluation; it does NOT support the spurious additive split.
\item \emph{Bare $\kappa$ (AP-CY$1$ / HZ-$7$).} Every invocation of $\kappa$
names the subscript: $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}, \kappa_{\mathrm{cat}},
\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}, \kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}$. The bridge distinguishes
them: $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}$ is the Borcherds weight, distinct from
$\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}(K3 \times E) = 0$ (K\"unneth-multiplicative
total space), $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3 \times E) = 3$
(Heisenberg rank), and $\kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}(K3) = 24$ (Mukai-lattice
rank).
\item \emph{CohFT at $d = 3$ without framed specialisation.} The
Getzler--Kapranov pairing requires the Stage-$2$ chiral algebra
$A_\cC = \SpCh_{\Sigma_2, C}(\PhiFA_3(\cC))$, which requires a framed
specialisation locus. Compact CY$_3$ without a framed specialisation
does not support the bridge.
\item \emph{Universal across all $\mathrm{Mp}_4$ covers.} Scope~(B)
weights $1/2, 1/4$ require the Shimura--Kohnen metaplectic tower; the
bridge at these covers is proved only up to cover lifts of the Torelli
morphism.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Appendix: the Getzler--Kapranov operadic glossary}
\label{sec:appendix-gk-glossary}

A minimal glossary of modular-operadic terminology, for quick reference
during the manuscript inscription:

\begin{description}
\item[Cyclic operad.] An operad $\mathcal{P}$ with $S_{n + 1}$-action
on $\mathcal{P}(n)$ (cycling marked points), compatible with composition.
The cyclic Gerstenhaber operad $\mathrm{Ger}_3^{\mathrm{cyc}}$ is the
cyclic closure of $\mathrm{Ger}_3$ via the Kapranov pairing.
\item[Modular operad.] A cyclic operad $\mathcal{P}$ with additional
self-gluing operation $\tau \colon \mathcal{P}(g, n + 2) \to
\mathcal{P}(g + 1, n)$; the Getzler--Kapranov object is $\mathsf{MMG}$.
\item[Modular closure.] The functor from cyclic operads to modular
operads that freely adjoins self-gluings; applied to the
$\mathrm{Ger}_3^{\mathrm{cyc}}$, yields the homology modular operad
of $\overline{\cM}_{g, n + 1}$.
\item[Feynman transform.] The Getzler--Kapranov dualisation functor
on modular operads; for a modular operad $\mathcal{P}$, the Feynman
transform $F(\mathcal{P})$ is the dual modular cooperad. For
$\mathcal{P} = H_\bullet(\overline{\cM})$, $F(\mathcal{P})$ is the
Koszul-dual modular operad.
\item[Orbifold fundamental class.] The chain-level class
$[\overline{\cM}_{g, n}] \in C_\bullet^{\mathrm{FM-log}}$; carries
BV-structure by Menichi--Ginzburg. Used in the modular pairing
\eqref{eq:modular-koszul-pairing}.
\item[Borcherds lift.] The singular-theta correspondence
$\mathrm{Borch} \colon M_{k, \rho}(\mathrm{Mp}_2) \to
M^{\mathrm{aut}}_k(\mathrm{O}(s + 2, 2))$; at $s = 2$, lands on
the Siegel upper half-plane $\mathbb{H}_2$.
\item[Paramodular group.] $\Gamma^{(N)}_{\mathrm{para}} \subset
\mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z)$: the stabiliser of a polarisation of type $(1, N)$
on a principally polarised abelian surface. Automorphic forms on
$\Gamma^{(N)}_{\mathrm{para}}$ are paramodular forms; the Borcherds
lift $\Phi_N$ is one such.
\item[Gritsenko additive lift.] The Hecke-operator-based map
$\mathrm{Lift}_{\mathrm{G}} \colon J_{k, m}^{\mathrm{weak}}
\hookrightarrow M_k(\Gamma^{(m)}_{\mathrm{para}})$; for $k = 5, m = 1$,
input $\eta^9 \vartheta_1$ yields $\Delta_5 = \mathrm{Lift}_{\mathrm{G}}
(\eta^9 \vartheta_1)$.
\item[Singly-twined.] The $g_N$-equivariant K3 elliptic genus without
EOT factor; input to Scope~(B).
\item[Doubly-twined.] The $g_N$-equivariant K3 elliptic genus with EOT
factor of $2$ absorbed; $Z^{(g)}_{K3} = 2 \phi^{(g)}_{0, 1}$; input to
Scope~(A).
\item[CHL ladder.] $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$: the orders of symplectic
automorphisms of K3 admitting an elliptic-fibred model (CHL after
Chaudhuri--Hockney--Lykken $1995$). Scope~(A) paramodular indices.
\item[Metaplectic cover.] $\mathrm{Mp}_4$ is the double cover of
$\mathrm{Sp}_4$; $\widetilde{\mathrm{Mp}}_4$ is the fourfold cover.
Half- and quarter-integer weight Siegel forms (Scope~(B) at $m = 5,
1/2$) live on these covers.
\end{description}
