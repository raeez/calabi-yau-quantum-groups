\section*{$N=3$ CHL effective positive half and BPS Lie algebra
$\fg^{(3)}_{\BPS}$}
\label{sec:N3-positive-half-BPS}

\paragraph{The quotient.} Fix a symplectic K3 automorphism
$g_3\in\Auts(K3)$ of order $3$, Mathieu class $3A$, frame shape
$1^6 3^6$. Nikulin's fixed-point formula gives
$|\Fix(g_3)|=6$. The trace on $H^2(K3,\bQ)$ is $4$, hence
$H^2(K3,\bQ)^{g_3}$ has rank $10$. The surface quotient
$K3/\langle g_3\rangle$ has six rational double points of type $A_2$;
their exceptional curves on the minimal resolution form
$A_2^{\oplus 6}$. Fix a translation $t_3\in E[3]$ of exact order $3$ and put
$\sigma_3=(g_3,t_3)$. For $a=1,2$, the translation $t_3^a$ is
fixed-point-free on $E$. Hence $\sigma_3^a$ is fixed-point-free on
$K3\times E$,
independently of the fixed points of $g_3^a$ on the K3 factor. The
quotient
\[
  X_3 \;=\; (K3 \times E)\big/\langle\sigma_3\rangle,
\]
is a smooth projective Calabi-Yau threefold. The quotient map
$K3\times E\to X_3$ is finite etale of degree $3$, and the holomorphic
form $\omega_{K3}\wedge dz_E$ descends because $g_3$ is symplectic and
translations preserve $dz_E$.

\paragraph{The elliptic fibration.} Projection to the elliptic factor
descends to a smooth isotrivial fibration
\[
  \pi\colon X_3\longrightarrow E/\langle t_3\rangle=:E'.
\]
The map $E\to E'$ is a degree-three isogeny, hence etale. The fibration
is etale over all of $E'$. The surface quotient
$K3/\langle g_3\rangle$ and its $A_2$ singularities belong to the
K3-factor orbifold calculation; every
geometric fibre of the smooth threefold $X_3\to E'$ is a K3 surface.

\paragraph{The primitive CHL denominator.} The order-three primitive
CHL Borcherds input is the CHL-averaged weight-zero, index-one Jacobi
form
\[
  \varphi_3(\tau,z)=
  \sum_{n,\ell}c^{\mathrm{CHL}}_3(4n-\ell^2,\ell)q^n\zeta^\ell .
\]
Its constant coefficient is
\[
  c_3(0)=c^{\mathrm{CHL}}_3(0,0)=6.
\]
Equivalently, the Mathieu frame trace of the $3A$ row is
$T_{24}(3A)=12$, the theta-component correction is $A_3=3$, and
\[
  c_3(0)=T_{24}(3A)-2A_3=12-6=6.
\]
Borcherds' singular-theta weight theorem gives
\[
  \kBKM(\DeltaCHLThree)
  =\mathrm{wt}(\DeltaCHLThree)
  =\frac{c_3(0)}2
  =3.
\]
The fixed-locus number $|\Fix(g_3)|=6$ is a separate Lefschetz
calculation on the K3 fibre. Its equality with the CHL constant is a
coincidence of the order-three row; the definition of $c_3(0)$ is the
zeroth Fourier coefficient of the Borcherds input.
This is the third entry of the primitive CHL ladder
\[
  (c_N(0))_{N=(1,2,3,4,6)}=(10,8,6,4,2),\qquad
  (\kBKM(\Phi_N))_{N=(1,2,3,4,6)}=(5,4,3,2,1).
\]
The Gritsenko-Cl\'ery diagonal-divisor catalogue is a separate
triple-indexed family. Its nearby forms
\[
  \DeltaDDThree\in M_1(\Gamma_3,\chi_6),\qquad
  \NablaThree\in M_2(\Gamma_0^{(2)}(3),\chi_2)
\]
have weights $1$ and $2$ respectively, and belong to the rows
$(3,1;1)$ and $(1,3;2)$ of the diagonal-divisor catalogue. The
primitive CHL value remains $\kBKM(\DeltaCHLThree)=3$.

\paragraph{Reduced DT scalar.} The K3 holomorphic two-form induces the
standard reduced cosection on the obstruction theory for curves on
$K3\times E$, and the cosection is $\sigma_3$-equivariant because
$g_3$ preserves $\omega_{K3}$ and $t_3$ is a translation. The
equivariant reduced moduli problem is represented on the cover by
\[
  \cM^{\red,\sigma_3}_{\DT}(X_3;\gamma)
  \;=\;
  \bigl(\cM^{\red}_{\DT}(K3\times E;\tilde\gamma)\bigr)^{\sigma_3},
\]
where $\tilde\gamma$ is a lift of the curve class
$\gamma\in H_2(X_3,\bZ)$ to the invariant part of the cover.
Bryan-Oberdieck's primitive order-three reduced-DT conjecture fixes the
scalar denominator, after the chamber and variables are chosen, as the
square of the primitive CHL denominator:
\[
  \Phi^{\mathrm{scal}}_3(\tau,\sigma,z)
  =
  (\DeltaCHLThree(\tau,\sigma,z))^2,
  \qquad
  \DTred(X_3;\tau,\sigma,z)
  \;=\;
  -\,\mathcal C^{\mathrm{BO}}_3(\tau,z)\,
  \bigl(\Phi^{\mathrm{scal}}_3(\tau,\sigma,z)\bigr)^{-1}.
\]
The prefactor $\mathcal C^{\mathrm{BO}}_3$ is fixed by the
Bryan-Oberdieck scalar normalisation and boundary terms. The square is
the protected scalar denominator. It records signed characters after
the Bryan-Oberdieck chamber and variables are fixed. This scalar branch
is the CHL analogue of the OP scalar at $N=1$ and is separate from the
positive Hall half below. Hall brackets and Weyl anti-invariant signs
enter through orientation, parity, pairing, and BKM recognition.

\paragraph{Effective positive-half moduli $\cM^+_\eff(X_3)$.} The
CHL positive half is
\[
  \cM^+_\eff(X_3) \;=\; \coprod_{\gamma\in C^+_3}
  \cM^{\red,\sigma_3}_{\DT}(X_3;\gamma),
\]
with $C^+_3\subset H_2(X_3;\bZ)$ the effective cone of classes with
$\beta_{K3}\in\mathrm{NS}(K3)^{g_3}_+$ (non-negative on the invariant
ample class) and non-negative degree on the elliptic base. The Hall
product on extensions gives the associative positive half after the
equivariant orientation line, compact-support convention, and Behrend
integration map have been fixed. This is the $E_1$ Hall half native to a
CY$_3$ input. Braided $E_2$ structure belongs to a Drinfeld centre,
Drinfeld double, or representation category built from this Hall half.

\paragraph{BPS Lie algebra $\fg^{(3)}_{\BPS}$.}
Davison-Meinhardt construct the BPS sheaf and the Hall-commutator Lie
algebra for critical CoHAs under purity and orientation hypotheses. In
the CHL quotient these hypotheses define the primitive positive Hall
Lie algebra
\[
  \fg^{(3)}_{\BPS}
  \;=\;
  H^*\bigl(\mathrm{BPS}^{\red}_{K3\times E}\bigr)^{\sigma_3}
  \;=\;
  \bigoplus_{\gamma\in C^+_3}
  \bH^{\BPS}_\gamma(X_3),
\]
a $\bZ_{\geq0}^{\oplus}$-graded Lie algebra on the Davison perverse
filtration. The Borcherds recognition target for this positive Hall
algebra has denominator
\[
  \prod_{\alpha\in\Delta^+}
  (1 - e^{-\alpha})^{\mathrm{mult}(\alpha)}
  \;=\; \DeltaCHLThree(\tau,\sigma,z),
  \qquad
  \mathrm{mult}(\alpha)
  \;=\;
  c^{\mathrm{CHL}}_3(4nm-\ell^2,\ell).
\]
The compatible BKM algebra is the generalized Kac-Moody superalgebra
$\fg_{\Delta_3^{(3)}}$ with denominator $\DeltaCHLThree$. The scalar
denominator supplies the signed root supermultiplicities. A Lie-algebra
isomorphism
$\fg^{(3)}_{\BPS}\cong\fg_{\Delta_3^{(3)}}$ additionally requires
orientation signs, a non-degenerate Hall pairing, a radical quotient,
PBW compatibility, and equality of brackets under the
Kontsevich-Soibelman product.

\paragraph{Borcherds product.}
Borcherds' weight theorem and the Gritsenko-Nikulin CHL construction
give the order-three primitive product
\[
  \DeltaCHLThree(\tau,\sigma,z)
  =
  q^A\zeta^B p^C
  \prod_{\substack{(n,\ell,m)\in\bZ^3\\(n,\ell,m)>0}}
  \left(1-q^n\zeta^\ell p^m\right)^{
    c^{\mathrm{CHL}}_3(4nm-\ell^2,\ell)} ,
\]
with Weyl-vector monomial $q^A\zeta^B p^C$. The coefficient
$c_3(0)=6$ fixes the weight. The remaining Fourier coefficients of
the same CHL Jacobi input fix the product exponents and hence the
Borcherds root multiplicities.

\paragraph{Four invariants on $X_3$.}
\[
  \kcat(X_3)=\chi(\cO_{X_3})=0,\qquad
  \kch(X_3)=\sum_q(-1)^qh^{0,q}(X_3)=0.
\]
Indeed
$H^q(X_3,\cO_{X_3})=H^q(K3\times E,\cO_{K3\times E})^{\sigma_3}$, and
the symplectic K3 action and elliptic translation act trivially on the
four factors in the Hodge column, so
$h^{0,\bullet}(X_3)=(1,1,1,1)$. The twined fibre Lefschetz trace is
\[
  \mathrm{Tr}\bigl(g_3\mid H^*(K3,\bQ)\bigr)=|\Fix(g_3)|=6.
\]
The untwisted \(K3\times E\) fibre invariant remains the Mukai-rank
value \(\kfib(K3)=24\). The order-three BKM invariant is
\[
  \kBKM(\DeltaCHLThree)=c_3(0)/2=3.
\]
At the untwisted \(K3\times E\) anchor,
\[
  \{\kcat,\kch,\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}},
  \kBKM(\Delta_5),\kfib\}(K3\times E)
  =
  \{0,0,3,5,24\}.
\]

\paragraph{Boundary.} The smooth CHL quotient, the etale fibration over
$E'$, the Hodge-supertrace computation, and the Borcherds weight
$\kBKM(\DeltaCHLThree)=3$ are proved from the cited geometry and
Borcherds-product theorem. The full order-three primitive reduced-DT
scalar remains the Bryan-Oberdieck conjecture, with proved boundary
coefficients. The compact BPS Lie algebra comparison with
$\fg_{\Delta_3^{(3)}}$ is a recognition problem: scalar coefficients
must be lifted to an oriented Hall positive half and then to a bracket
comparison. The analogous local separation is
$\CoHA(\bC^3)=Y^+$: the CoHA is a positive $E_1$ Hall half. The OP
scalar, CHL scalar, positive Hall half, Hall-Drinfeld recognition
target, and vertex-algebra envelope are separate objects. The
self-mirror current branch requires its own Yangian data.
