\section*{The positive-geometry grammar of $G(X)$}
\label{wn:sec:platonic-preface-positive-geometry-grammar-wn}

A Calabi--Yau category does not produce a chiral algebra on a curve.
It produces a holomorphic factorisation algebra on the Calabi--Yau
itself; the chiral algebra on a curve is the specialisation of that
factorisation algebra along a chosen transverse cycle. The functor
\[
\Phi_d: \CYcat_d \longrightarrow \ChirAlg
\]
factors canonically:
\[
\Phi_d \;=\; \underbrace{\CYcat_d \xrightarrow{\;\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_d\;}
E_d\text{-}\HolFA(X)}_{\text{Stage 1 --- canonical up to contractible choice}}
\;\xrightarrow{\;\Sp_{\Sigma_{d-1}, C}\;}\;
\underbrace{E_1\text{-}\ChirAlg(C)}_{\text{Stage 2 shadow}}.
\]
Stage 1 is pinned by Kontsevich--Tamarkin $E_d$-formality of the
Dolbeault little-$d$-disks operad together with the Costello--Gwilliam
factorisation axiom for observables of the holomorphic Chern--Simons
action on $X$ (Costello--Li for the six-dimensional realisation at
$d = 3$). Stage 2 is a choice: one selects a closed
$(d{-}1)$-dimensional subvariety $\Sigma_{d-1} \subset X$ and a
reference curve $C \subset X$, and integrates the factorisation algebra
fibrewise over $\Sigma_{d-1}$ to land on $C$. Different choices
produce different chiral shadows of the same canonical datum.

\subsection*{The quantum group as Drinfeld double of positive
effective-geometry cohomology}
\label{wn:sec:platonic-preface-quantum-group-wn}

Inside $\cF_\cA$ there is a distinguished positive effective moduli
stack $\Meff(X)$ carrying the critical Kontsevich--Soibelman
cohomological-Hall-algebra structure. Write $\phi_W$ for the
Behrend-microlocal vanishing-cycle sheaf adapted to $\Meff(X)$.
Then
\[
Y^+(X) \;:=\; H^{\bullet}_{\mathrm{eq}}\!\bigl(\Meff(X),\,\phi_W\bigr)
\]
is the positive half of the Drinfeld double
\[
G(X) \;:=\; D\bigl(Y^+(X)\bigr) \;=\; Y^+(X) \bowtie Y^0(X) \bowtie Y^-(X)
\]
with pairing from Serre duality on the compact locus,
Davison--Meinhardt PBW integration and Maulik--Okounkov stable-envelope
transport supplying the $R$-matrix. This is the quantum group.

The word ``equivariant'' is load-bearing. Its meaning depends on $X$.
The toric three-fold $\CC^3$ supports the full three-torus $T^3 =
(\CC^\times)^3$; the positive effective moduli is the Hilbert
scheme of points $\mathrm{Hilb}^n(\CC^3)$ with $T^3$-fixed loci indexed
by three-dimensional partitions, and Schiffmann--Vasserot identify
$Y^+(\CC^3)$ with the positive half of the affine Yangian
$Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$. The compact surface $K3$ carries no
torus. Its positive effective geometry is the Mukai lattice
$\Lambda_{\mathrm{Muk}}^{(4,20)}$ with the Nakajima--Grojnowski--Lehn
Heisenberg action on $\bigoplus_n H^\bullet(\mathrm{Hilb}^n(K3))$ and a
discrete symplectic automorphism group of orders bounded by Nikulin.
The compact Calabi--Yau threefold $K3 \times E$ carries neither a full
three-torus nor a symplectic automorphism group rich enough to mimic
one; its positive effective geometry is the Pandharipande--Oberdieck
reduced Donaldson--Thomas moduli stack
$\cM^{\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{DT}}(K3 \times E; \gamma)$ on K3-primitive
classes $\gamma$, equivariant under
$\CC^\times_E \times \mathrm{Aut}_s(K3)$, with character
\[
Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{DT}}(K3 \times E) \;=\; -\frac{1}{\Phi_{10}}
\;=\; -\frac{1}{\Delta_5^2}.
\]
The equivariance type stratifies the programme: toric $T^d$, reduced
$\CC^\times$ plus a symplectic automorphism, orbifold inertia of a
finite group, or the period domain of a polarising lattice. The
algebra $Y^+(X)$ is the same object read through four different local
geometric languages.

\subsection*{Six-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons as the
canonical $d{=}3$ realisation}
\label{wn:sec:platonic-preface-6d-hCS-wn}

At $d = 3$ the factorisation algebra $\cF_\cA$ is explicit. Let $X$
be a smooth complex threefold with holomorphic volume form
$\Omega_X \in H^{3,0}(X)$ and $\fg$ a gauge Lie algebra. The classical
six-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons action
\[
S_{\mathrm{cl}}[\cA] \;=\; \int_X \Omega_X \wedge
\bigl\langle \cA, \bar\partial \cA + \tfrac{1}{3}[\cA, \cA] \bigr\rangle
\]
on the BV superfield $\cA = c + A_{0,1} + A^{*}_{0,2} + c^{*}_{0,3}$
carries a $(-1)$-shifted symplectic form by Serre duality. The
Costello--Gwilliam quantisation produces observables
$\Obs_{\hCS}(X) = \bigl(\mathrm{Sym}(\cE^\vee[1])[\![\hbar]\!],\,
Q + \hbar \Delta\bigr)$ with Bochner--Martinelli propagator, explicit
one-loop counterterm $-\hbar C_2(\fg)(4\pi)^{-3} \log(L/\epsilon)
\int \Omega \wedge \mathrm{Tr}(\cA \bar\partial \cA)$, and vanishing
BV anomaly on any Calabi--Yau threefold with $c_3 = 0$
(in particular on $\CC^3$ and on $K3 \times E$). On $\CC^3$ the
Maulik--Okounkov equivariant enumeration identifies
$\Obs_{\hCS}(\CC^3) \simeq \CE^{\bullet}_{\bar\partial, \chir}
(\cE_{\hCS}, \cO_{\CC^3})$ as $E_3$-algebra in $\Ch(\mathrm{Dolb})$;
Wick contractions on disjoint polydiscs implement the
$E_3$-multiplication; the Fadell--Neuwirth fibration
$\mathrm{Conf}_2(\CC^3) \simeq S^5$ with $\pi_1(S^5) = 0$ supplies
commutativity. This is the chain-level avatar of the
Schiffmann--Vasserot cohomological Hall algebra.

\subsection*{The $K3 \times E$ Siegel form and its seven siblings}
\label{wn:sec:platonic-preface-K3E-Siegel-wn}

The Calabi--Yau threefold $K3 \times E$ carries four invariants from
four distinct constructions:
$\kcat(K3 \times E) = 0$ from K\"unneth multiplicativity on the total
space, $\kch(K3 \times E) = 0$ from the Hodge supertrace
$\sum_q (-1)^q h^{0,q}$, $\kBKM(\Delta_5) = 5 = c_1(0)/2$ from the
Borcherds weight of the Gritsenko cusp form, and $\kfib(K3) = 24$
from the rank of the Mukai lattice.
\emph{These are four numbers from four theorems, not four values of
one function.} The spectrum $\{2, 3, 5, 24\}$ is the signature of
four orthogonal measurements, not a fanout of one invariant.

The Igusa form $\Phi_{10} = \Delta_5^2$ is the principal member of
an eight-form Gritsenko--Cl\'ery family:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccccccc}
N & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 6 & 7 & 8 \\[0.3ex]
\hline
\mathrm{wt}(\Phi_N) & 5 & 2 & 1 & 1 & \tfrac{1}{2} & 1 & \tfrac{1}{4} & 0 \\[0.3ex]
c_N(0) & 10 & 4 & 2 & 2 & 1 & 2 & \tfrac{1}{2} & 0
\end{array}
\]
with universal weight identity
\[
\kBKM(\Phi_N) \;=\; c_N(0)/2
\qquad\text{for }N \in \{1, 2, \ldots, 8\}
\]
holding uniformly once the cover assignment
$\{\Sp_4, \Mp_4, \widetilde{\Mp}_4\}$ is selected by weight
integrality. The Chaudhuri--Hockney--Lykken subset
$N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ satisfies $\varphi(N) \mid 2$ and realises
each $\Phi_N$ as a denominator of a generalised Kac--Moody
superalgebra on the twisted reduced Donaldson--Thomas moduli of
$(K3 \times E)/(\ZZ/N\ZZ)$; the non-CHL trio $N \in \{5, 7, 8\}$ lives
on host geometries of three different types: Borcea--Voisin
$(S_5 \times E_5)/(\iota_S \times \iota_E)$ at $N = 5$ with metaplectic
fourth-root character; an order-$4$ Cheeger--Simons gerbe on the
singular $(K3 \times E)/\ZZ_7$ at $N = 7$; the Mongardi--Tari--Wandel
Kummer-$3$ hyperk\"ahler fourfold $\mathrm{Kum}^3(A)/\ZZ_8$ at $N = 8$,
where the weight-zero form $\Delta_0^{(8)}$ signals the structural
endpoint of the family rather than a new Kac--Moody algebra.

\subsection*{The theorems, the conjectures, and what remains}
\label{wn:sec:platonic-preface-theorems-conjectures-wn}

Eight loadbearing theorems carry the weight of the work. First:
the two-stage factorisation above is canonical. Second: the
six-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons observables on $\CC^3$
carry a genuine $E_3$-algebra structure in $\Ch(\mathrm{Dolb})$
computed by a sum-over-shuffles formula with Koszul signs. Third:
the one-loop BV obstruction on any Calabi--Yau threefold factorises
as $\hbar \, A(\fg) \cdot \chi_{\mathrm{top}}(X) \cdot \|\Omega_X\|^2
/ (2(4\pi)^3)$, vanishing on $K3 \times E$ where $c_3 = 0$ and on
$\CC^3$ where $\fg$ is arbitrary. Fourth: the minimal
$L_\infty$-model on $\CC^3$ has trivial higher brackets
$\ell_n^{\min} = 0$ for $n \geq 3$. Fifth: the non-abelian
five-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons boundary vertex algebra
coincides with the affine Yangian $Y_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3}
(\widehat{\fg})$ to all orders in $\hbar$ for every simply-laced
semisimple $\fg$. Sixth: the Maulik--Okounkov $R$-matrix is a
spectral residue of the positive-half gluing cocycle at a wall of
marginal stability. Seventh: the Heisenberg--Mukai identity
$\prod_n (1 - q^n)^{-48} = -q^{-2}
\bigl[(2\pi i z)^2 \Delta_5^{-2}\bigr]_{H_1, z = 0}$
holds to all orders by Gritsenko--Nikulin residue and
Frenkel--Ben-Zvi Heisenberg-character. Eighth: the universal
Borcherds weight identity $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ holds uniformly
on the full eight-form class with the appropriate metaplectic or spin
cover.

The conjectures organise themselves along three axes. Along the
geometric axis: the Pandharipande--Oberdieck primitive-class
restriction at $K3 \times E$ generalises to imprimitive classes only
conditionally on Oberdieck--Shen. Along the algebraic axis: the
bracket-level identification $\fg_{\mathrm{BPS}}(K3 \times E)
\cong \fg_{\Delta_5}$ is conditional on a Costello topological
conformal field theory cyclic-invariance argument not yet fully
verified. Along the automorphic axis: the non-CHL realisations at
$N = 5, 7, 8$ remain conditional on Borcea--Voisin reduced DT,
Cheeger--Simons gerbe descent, and fourfold scope respectively.
The finite catalogue of open questions is a feature, not a gap;
every open item names a specific mathematical obstruction whose
resolution would close the corresponding row.

This preface establishes the vocabulary; the remainder of the
volume constructs the objects, proves the theorems, and catalogues
the conjectures.
