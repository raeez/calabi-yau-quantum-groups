\section{Attack--Heal \#1: Costello--Francis--Gwilliam $E_3$ ambiguity}
\label{sec:ah1-cfg}

\paragraph*{Ghost theorem.} \textit{The CFG factorisation-homology programme assigns a single $\Ethree$-algebra to every theory of either $3$-real-dimensional topological type or holomorphic-topological type, and this $\Ethree$-algebra is the same object across Vol~II ($3$D HT QFT bulk) and Vol~III ($6$D holomorphic on $\C^3$).}

\paragraph*{Precise error.} \textit{False as stated.} There are \emph{two} different $\Ethree$-structures, and they live on different objects:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Vol~II's $\Ethree$: \emph{topological} factorisation algebra of bulk observables on the $\R$-direction of a 3D HT theory on $C \times \R$, with the chiral direction $C$ forgotten. Reference: \texttt{chiral-bar-cobar-vol2/chapters/connections/brace.tex}:180--188 (``$\Ethree$-rigidity'' in two regimes), \texttt{bar-cobar-review.tex}:4540 (``bulk $\Ethree$-topological factorisation algebra''), \texttt{celestial\_holography.tex}:2018--2028.
\item Vol~III's $\Ethree$: \emph{holomorphic-chiral} factorisation algebra on a $3$-complex-dimensional Calabi--Yau, each complex direction contributing one chiral level. Reference: \texttt{calabi-yau-quantum-groups/chapters/theory/en\_factorization.tex}:449--500, \texttt{quantum\_chiral\_algebras.tex}:227--243.
\end{enumerate}

The shared scope qualifier from Vol~III \texttt{en\_factorization.tex}:449 is explicit: ``The $\Eone$ stabilization theorem\dots concerns the CY-to-chiral correspondence programme $\{\Phi_d\}$ and shows that $d \geq 3$ produces at most $\Eone$-chiral algebras. A distinct source of higher $\En$ structure exists: the holomorphic Chern--Simons programme of Costello and Costello--Francis--Gwilliam, where the $\En$ level is set by the \emph{complex dimension of the ambient space}, not by the CY dimension of the category.''

\paragraph*{Correct relationship.} The two $\Ethree$-structures \emph{both live on categories of observables, not on the chiral algebra $\cA_X$ itself}:
\begin{itemize}
\item Vol~II's $\Ethree$ acts on $\ChirHoch^\bullet(\cA)$ (higher Deligne), and records the topologisation ladder reaching $\Ethree$ from $N=2$ inner stress tensors (Vol~II CLAUDE.md line 32).
\item Vol~III's $\Ethree$ acts on $\mathrm{Obs}(X)$ for $X = \C^3$ (complex-dimensional observables), and restricts to the $\Eone$-chiral $\Phi_3(\cC)$ only through the $\Ethree \to \Eone$ boundary-to-bulk projection.
\end{itemize}

\noindent They are compatible via the bulk-boundary correspondence: Vol~I Thm~H (derived-centre/Hochschild) and Vol~II bulk--boundary duality connect observables-side $\Ethree$ with algebra-side $\Eone$ (Vol~III \texttt{en\_factorization.tex}:449 final clause). Neither subsumes the other.

\paragraph*{Resolution.} No edit to \texttt{working\_notes.tex} is required; \S1731 is already careful (``Swiss-cheese structure $\mathsf{SC}^{\mathrm{ch,top}}$ provides the operadic framework for the $\Etwo$ bar complex''). The potential tension is only at the level of labelling: the Vol~III $E_3$ passage at $\C^3$ is the CFG 6D-holomorphic construction, not the Vol~II 3D HT construction; both coexist with explicit scope qualifier.

Flag minor drift: \S19770 writes ``K3-fibre-rank entry of $\{2, 3, 5, 24\}$'' (correct attribution). No surface conflict against Vol~I, provided the Vol~III reader reads $\{2,3,5,24\}$ as ``four-constructions'' and not as ``five-archetype ceiling.''

No edit needed, but the text would gain from the clarifying parenthetical ``three graded pieces of the two-coloured dioperad $\mathsf{SC}^{\mathrm{ch,top}}$''; pure bookkeeping, logged not-acted.

\section{Attack--Heal \#6 (bonus): K\"unneth multiplicativity on $\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}$}

\paragraph*{Status.} Vol~III flags this as AP-CY-5 (forbidden K\"unneth-additive conflation). Coherent.
