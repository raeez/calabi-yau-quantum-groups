\section*{Simultaneous $(g_N,h_M)$-twining of the K3 elliptic genus:
status matrix and extension path}

\paragraph{Claim under audit.}
For $(g,\mathrm{id})\in M_{24}$, the twined weak Jacobi form
$\phiKg(\tau,z)$ of weight $0$ index $1$ on the congruence subgroup
$\Gamma^{(g)}\subset \mathrm{SL}_2(\Z)$ is constructed for all $26$
conjugacy classes of $M_{24}$.  For commuting pairs
$(g,h)\in M_{24}\times M_{24}$ with $gh=hg$, the simultaneous twining
$\phiKgh$ is a conjectural weak Jacobi form on
$\Gamma^{(g,h)}\subset \mathrm{SL}_2(\Z)$ of weight $0$ index $1$
(Persson--Volpato 2012 arXiv:1208.4851, established on explicit
large subsets, conjectural in full $M_{24}$ generality).

\paragraph{Extension path GHV $\Longrightarrow$ full Persson--Volpato.}
The passage from single-twist $(g,\mathrm{id})$ to simultaneous
$(g,h)$ twining is controlled by a sequence of four named
constructions with explicit primary-literature anchors.

\emph{Step 1 (Hilbert-space orbifold: $g$-sector).}
The $(g,\mathrm{id})$ genus is the graded trace
$\phiKg = \mathrm{tr}_{\mathcal{H}_{K3}}(g\,(-1)^{F+\bar F}q^{L_0-c/24}
\bar q^{\bar L_0-\bar c/24}y^{J_0})$ over the unorbifolded K3
sigma-model Hilbert space (EOT 2011 eq.~(2.5); GHV 2012
arXiv:1106.5218 Thm~1).  The weak-Jacobi property at each of the $26$
classes is GHV's main theorem: $\phiKg\in J_{0,1}(\Gamma^{(g)},\psi_g)$
with multiplier $\psi_g$ of order $\mathrm{ord}(g)$.

\emph{Step 2 (twisted sector construction: $h$-twist).}
For $h\in\CM(g)$, define the $g$-twisted sector $\mathcal{H}^{h}_{K3}$
as the $h$-module generated by $g$-twisted ground states.
The double trace
$\phiKgh = \mathrm{tr}_{\mathcal{H}^{h}_{K3}}(g\,(-1)^{F+\bar F}
q^{L_0-c/24}\bar q^{\bar L_0-\bar c/24}y^{J_0})$
is a priori a formal $q$-series; its modular completion is
obstructed by a projective cocycle
$\omega_{g,h}\in H^2(\CM(g),\mathrm{U}(1))$
(Dijkgraaf--Vafa--Verlinde--Verlinde 1989; Persson--Volpato 2012 \S 3).

\emph{Step 3 (cocycle vanishing: the hidden observation).}
\emph{Simultaneous twining $(g,h)$ is a $2$-cocycle on the centraliser
$\CM(g)$; its class
$[\omega_{g,h}]\in H^2(\CM(g),\mathrm{U}(1))$
is the obstruction to lifting $\phiKgh$ to a weak Jacobi form on
$\Gamma^{(g,h)}\subset\mathrm{SL}_2(\Z)$.}  When $[\omega_{g,h}]=0$ the
simultaneous twining exists as a true weak Jacobi form
(Persson--Volpato 2012 Prop.~4.1 with GHV 2012 arXiv:1211.7074 \S 3
cyclic-subgroup reduction); when $[\omega_{g,h}]\neq 0$ the formal
series $\phiKgh$ survives only as a Jacobi form for a projective
multiplier on $\Gamma^{(g,h)}$, and the Gritsenko lift of \S 4 below
picks up a phase-twisted cocycle that obstructs the rank-$3$ BKM
denominator at the anomalous classes $\{7A, 7B, 11A, 23A, 23B\}$
(CDH 2014 \S 5; \texttt{chapters/theory/cy\_to\_chiral.tex}
l.~4518).

\emph{Step 4 (umbral extension beyond $M_{24}$).}
For Niemeier lattices $\Lambda$ other than $A_1^{24}$, CDH 2014
arXiv:1204.2779 constructs $23$ umbral twining genera in the
single-twist $(g,\mathrm{id})$ channel only; the simultaneous
$(g,h)$-refinement at the umbral level is the content of
Paquette--Persson--Volpato 2016 and remains
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ for all Niemeier root systems except
$A_1^{24}$.

\paragraph{Gritsenko lift.}
The rank-$3$ Borcherds denominator
$\Delta_5^{(g,h)}\in M_5(\Gamma^{(g,h)}_0,\chi_{g,h})$ is the
multiplicative Borcherds lift of $\phiKgh$ in the sense of
Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995 / Gritsenko 1999 \S 6.  The lift exists as
a meromorphic Siegel modular form whenever Step~3 produces a true
weak Jacobi form; the lift itself is \ClaimStatusTheorem\ at every
$(g,h)$ with $[\omega_{g,h}]=0$, and \ClaimStatusConjectured\ at
$[\omega_{g,h}]\neq 0$ (Gritsenko 1999 does not cover the
projective-multiplier case).  For $g=\mathrm{id}$, $h=\mathrm{id}$
the lift recovers the Igusa cusp form $\Delta_5$ with Borcherds
weight $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Delta_5) = c_1(0)/2 = 5$
(Lorgat 2020; Oberdieck--Pixton 2018).

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The GHV 2012 theorem covers $(g,\mathrm{id})$ at all $26$ classes
with the weak-Jacobi property; Persson--Volpato 2012 upgrade to
$(g,h)$ is proved on the union of the cyclic-$\langle g,h\rangle$
locus (GHV 2012 arXiv:1211.7074), the low-order abelian locus
(PV 2012 Thm~4.2), and the geometric Mukai locus (Gaberdiel--Volpato
2012 arXiv:1206.5335), and is conjectural on the complement.  The
obstruction is the class
$[\omega_{g,h}]\in H^2(\CM(g),\mathrm{U}(1))$, precisely the Schur
multiplier obstruction that controls
$\widetilde{M}_{24}\to M_{24}$ at
\texttt{chapters/theory/en\_factorization.tex} l.~2698 and
\texttt{chapters/examples/fukaya\_categories.tex} l.~975.  The
Gritsenko lift to $\Delta_5^{(g,h)}$ inherits the same dichotomy.
