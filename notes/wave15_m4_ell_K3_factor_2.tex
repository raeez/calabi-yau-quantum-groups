\section*{The Two Normalisations of \(\phi_{0,1}\)}

\paragraph{Eichler--Zagier normalisation.}
Let \(\phi_{0,1}^{\EZ}\) denote the Eichler--Zagier generator of
\(J^{\mathrm{weak}}_{0,1}\), normalized by
\[
  \phi_{0,1}^{\EZ}(\tau,z)
  =
  \zeta^{-1}+10+\zeta
  +
  q(10\zeta^{-2}-64\zeta^{-1}+108-64\zeta+10\zeta^2)
  +O(q^2),
  \qquad
  \zeta=e^{2\pi iz}.
\]
At \(z=0\),
\[
  \phi_{0,1}^{\EZ}(\tau,0)=12.
\]
The coefficient entering the primitive \(\Delta_5\) Borcherds product is
\[
  c_{\EZ}(0)=10.
\]

\paragraph{K3 elliptic genus normalisation.}
The K3 elliptic genus is the doubled form
\[
  \Ell(\Kthree;\tau,z)=2\,\phi_{0,1}^{\EZ}(\tau,z).
\]
Hence
\[
  \Ell(\Kthree;\tau,0)=24,
\]
as required by the Witten-index specialization
\(\Ell(\Kthree;\tau,0)=\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(\Kthree)\). Its \(q^0\) term is
\[
  2\zeta^{-1}+20+2\zeta .
\]
Thus the same Jacobi object appears in two scalar conventions:
\[
  \phi_{0,1}^{K3}:=\Ell(\Kthree)=2\phi_{0,1}^{\EZ}.
\]

\paragraph{Primitive denominator versus square.}
Borcherds' product theorem applied in the primitive
Eichler--Zagier--Gritsenko convention gives
\[
  D_5=64^{-1}\DeltaFive
  =
  \mathrm{Bor}\bigl(\phi_{0,1}^{\EZ}\bigr),
  \qquad
  \kBKM(\DeltaFive)
  =
  \operatorname{wt}(\DeltaFive)
  =
  c_{\EZ}(0)/2
  =
  5 .
\]
Applying the same lift to the K3 elliptic genus doubles the input:
\[
  \mathrm{Bor}\bigl(2\phi_{0,1}^{\EZ}\bigr)=D_5^2,
  \qquad
  \PhiTen=\DeltaFive^2,
  \qquad
  \operatorname{wt}(\PhiTen)=10 .
\]
The factor of \(2\) therefore separates the primitive BKM denominator
\(\Delta_5\) from the Igusa square \(\Delta_5^2\). Substituting the
K3-normalised constant \(20\) into the primitive formula computes
the square's weight \(10\). The primitive denominator has weight \(5\).

\paragraph{CHL convention.}
On the primitive CHL denominator ladder
\[
  N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\},
\]
the constants are taken in the Borcherds-input normalization:
\[
  c_N(0)=(10,8,6,4,2),
  \qquad
  \kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2=(5,4,3,2,1).
\]
The \(N=1\) entry uses \(c_1(0)=10\), the
\(\phi_{0,1}^{\EZ}\) constant. The K3 elliptic genus \(2\phi_{0,1}^{\EZ}\)
belongs to the squared Igusa/DVV normalization.

\paragraph{Discipline.}
Any formula sensitive to this scalar must name its convention:
\[
  \phi_{0,1}^{\EZ}
  \quad\hbox{for the primitive \(\Delta_5\) denominator,}
  \qquad
  \phi_{0,1}^{K3}=2\phi_{0,1}^{\EZ}
  \quad\hbox{for the K3 elliptic genus.}
\]
Root exponents of \(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5}\) use the primitive
coefficients of \(\phi_{0,1}^{\EZ}\). Reduced-DT and Igusa-square
formulas use the doubled K3 elliptic genus and land on
\(\Delta_5^2\) or its normalized square \(D_5^2\).
