\section*{Chiral bar--cobar for local 6d hCS factorisation algebras}

\paragraph{Complete local category.}
Let $\Ch_{\Ran}^{\dbar}(\C^3)$ denote the Dolbeault model of complete
filtered complexes on $\Ran(\C^3)$ with chiral tensor product along
disjoint finite subsets.  The arity filtration is
\[
  F^p M = \prod_{n \geq p} M^{(n)}
\]
and completion means
\[
  M \;\simeq\; \varprojlim_p M/F^pM,
  \qquad
  \bigcap_p F^pM = 0 .
\]
Connected means $M^{(0)}=0$.  Complete connected objects are
conilpotent for the arity coalgebra structure; this is the hypothesis
which places the bar and cobar constructions in Positselski's
coderived and contraderived framework.

\paragraph{Local hCS Lie object.}
Let
\[
  \cL_{\hCS}=\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3,\g)
\]
be the local Dolbeault dg Lie algebra of holomorphic Chern--Simons
theory, with differential $\dbar$ and pointwise bracket.  The bracket
defines a chiral operation by extension from disjoint configurations
and distributional restriction along partial diagonals of $\Ran(\C^3)$.
It preserves the arity filtration and has no arity-zero part.

\paragraph{Theorem.}
In the complete connected subcategory of
$\Ch_{\Ran}^{\dbar}(\C^3)$ there is an adjunction
\[
  \Bar^{\chir}:\Lie^{\chir}_{\mathrm{conn}}
       \bigl(\Ch_{\Ran}^{\dbar}(\C^3)\bigr)
  \;\rightleftarrows\;
  \coLie^{\chir}_{\mathrm{conn}}
       \bigl(\Ch_{\Ran}^{\dbar}(\C^3)\bigr):\Cob^{\chir}.
\]
For every fibrant-cofibrant complete connected chiral Lie object whose
associated graded is pro-nilpotent, the unit and counit are
quasi-isomorphisms after separated completion.  Applied to
$\cL_{\hCS}$ this gives
\[
  \Bar^{\chir}(\cL_{\hCS})
   \;\simeq\;
  \CE^*_{\dbar,\chir}(\cL_{\hCS},\C),
  \qquad
  \Cob^{\chir}\CE^*_{\dbar,\chir}(\cL_{\hCS},\C)
   \;\simeq\;
  \cL_{\hCS}
\]
inside the completed homotopy category of connected chiral Lie
objects.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Francis--Gaitsgory prove chiral Koszul duality on Ran-space
$\mathcal D$-modules.  The proof input used here is the filtered
operadic Koszul statement for the Lie operad in a complete symmetric
monoidal dg category: the bar construction sends a chiral Lie object
$\cF$ to the completed chiral Chevalley coalgebra
\[
  \Bar^{\chir}(\cF)
  =
  \left(
    \prod_{n\geq 0}
    j_{n*}j_n^*
    \Sym^n_{\chir}(\cF[1])^\vee,\,
    d_{\cF}+\dbar+d_{\chir}
  \right),
\]
where $j_n:\operatorname{Conf}_n(\C^3)\hookrightarrow(\C^3)^n$ is the
open configuration embedding and $d_{\chir}$ is induced by the chiral
bracket along partial diagonals.  The cobar construction sends a
connected chiral coalgebra $\cC$ to the completed free chiral Lie object
on the desuspension:
\[
  \Cob^{\chir}(\cC)
  =
  \left(
    \prod_{n\geq 1}
    j_{n*}j_n^*
    \Sym^n_{\chir}(\cC[-1]),\,
    \partial_{\cC}+\partial_{\chir}
  \right),
\]
where $\partial_{\chir}$ is dual to the chiral cobracket.

The associated graded of the arity filtration forgets collision
corrections and reduces the unit and counit to the ordinary Koszul
resolution for the Lie--coLie pair.  The Lie operad is Koszul, hence
the associated graded maps are quasi-isomorphisms.  Positselski's
complete filtered criterion lifts these quasi-isomorphisms from the
associated graded category to the completed coderived and
contraderived categories.  For $X=\C^3$, the Dolbeault complex
$\Omega^{0,\bullet}_{\C^3}$ is a soft resolution of
$\cO_{\C^3}^{\mathrm{hol}}$, and Gwilliam--Williams identify the
current algebra $\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3,\g)$ as a holomorphic
factorisation Lie algebra.  Substituting $\cF=\cL_{\hCS}$ in the bar
formula gives
\[
  \Bar^{\chir}(\cL_{\hCS})
  =
  \CE^*_{\dbar,\chir}(\cL_{\hCS},\C),
\]
and the completed counit gives the displayed recovery of
$\cL_{\hCS}$.

\paragraph{Five objects.}
For a specialised CY-to-chiral output $A$ in the $d=3$ lane the
following symbols have fixed types:
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
  A & \Eone\text{-chiral algebra produced by }\Phi_3 \\
  B(A) & \text{conilpotent bar coalgebra of }A \\
  A^i=H^\bullet(B(A)) & \text{bar-cohomology coalgebra} \\
  A^!=\mathbb{D}_{\Ran}(A^i) &
    \text{Verdier/Koszul dual algebra under finite-type or continuity hypotheses} \\
  Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)
    = C^\bullet_{\mathrm{ch}}(A,A)
    \simeq \RHom_{A\otimes A^{\mathrm{op}}}(A,A) &
    \text{Hochschild derived chiral centre}
\end{array}
\]
The equivalence $\Omega B(A)\simeq A$ is the bar--cobar counit.  The
construction of $A^!$ passes through $A^i$ and Verdier or continuous
finite duality.  The centre is the Hochschild cochain object controlling
bulk and centre phenomena.

\paragraph{hCS observables and coefficients.}
The local quantum observable factorisation algebra is
\[
  \Obs_{\hCS}(\C^3)
  \;\simeq\;
  \CE^*_{\dbar,\chir}(\cL_{\hCS},\cO_{\C^3})
\]
after Costello--Gwilliam BV data, a gauge-fixing parametrix, local
counterterms, and a solution of the quantum master equation have been
chosen.  The coefficient-free bar object is related by base change:
\[
  \CE^*_{\dbar,\chir}(\cL_{\hCS},\cO_{\C^3})
  \;\simeq\;
  \Bar^{\chir}(\cL_{\hCS})\widehat{\otimes}_{\C}\cO_{\C^3}.
\]
This is a local equality.  A compact CY$_3$ theorem requires a
global BV theory satisfying the quantum master equation, anomaly
cancellation, orientation and framing data, descent and sewing for
the factorisation algebra, compatible counterterms, and a separated
completion transition system.

\paragraph{Dimension of the specialised output.}
The local factorisation algebra $\Obs_{\hCS}(\C^3)$ carries the
holomorphic $\Ethree$ structure of a three-complex-dimensional local
field theory.  The specialised CY-to-chiral output at $d=3$ is an
$\Eone$ algebra $A$.  The first $\Etwo$ layer attached to $A$ is a
centre or double layer, for example
\[
  \Rep^{\Etwo}\!\bigl(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)\bigr)
  \quad\text{or}\quad
  \mathcal Z(\Rep^{\Eone}(A)),
\]
under the hypotheses ensuring these centres exist.

\paragraph{Chain and categorical formulations.}
The chain model is the displayed arity-completed Dolbeault complex with
the bracket differential $d_{\chir}$ and the cobracket differential
$\partial_{\chir}$.  The $(\infty,1)$-categorical model is the same
adjunction in complete filtered chiral Lie and coLie objects on the Ran
space.  The chain calculation proves the filtered unit and counit; the
categorical formulation records invariance under filtered
quasi-equivalences and supplies functoriality for morphisms preserving
augmentation, completion, Dolbeault differential, and chiral
operations.

\paragraph{Toric positive half.}
For the toric input $\MF(\C^3,0)$ the cohomological Hall computation
gives
\[
  \CoHA(\C^3)\simeq Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1).
\]
The algebra $\mathcal W_{1+\infty}$ enters after Drinfeld double,
derived centre, completion, or evaluation.

\paragraph{\texorpdfstring{$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$}{chiral characteristic} scalar.}
For a compact CY$_3$ object with a constructed $\Phi_3$ output in the
Hodge-supertrace regime,
\[
  \kch^{\mathrm{Hodge}}(A_X)
  =
  \sum_q (-1)^q h^{0,q}(X).
\]
For a smooth quintic threefold $X_5$ the column is
$(h^{0,0},h^{0,1},h^{0,2},h^{0,3})=(1,0,0,1)$, hence
\[
  \kch^{\mathrm{Hodge}}(A_{X_5})=1-1=0.
\]
The BCOV value $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{BCOV}}(A_{X_5})
=\chi(X_5)/24=-25/3$ is a different invariant attached to the
worldsheet/BCOV construction.  The Hodge-supertrace scalar above is
\(\kch^{\mathrm{Hodge}}\).

\paragraph{\texorpdfstring{$K3 \times E$}{K3 x E} invariants.}
For the compact product $X=K3\times E$ the invariants used in this note
are
\[
  \kcat(X)=0,\qquad
  \kch^{\mathrm{Hodge}}(A_X)=0,\qquad
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(A_X)=3,\qquad
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=5,\qquad
  \kfiber(K3)=24.
\]
The scalar $\chi(\cO_{K3})=2$ belongs to the K3 fibre categorical Euler
characteristic.  The compact total-space value is
$\chi(\cO_{K3\times E})=0$ by K\"unneth multiplicativity.

\paragraph{Global proof obligations.}
The local theorem gives the bar--cobar recovery of
$\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3,\g)$ and the coefficient comparison for local
observables.  A compact global hCS/factorisation theorem additionally
requires: a solution of the quantum master equation, cancellation of
one-loop and higher anomalies, orientation and framing choices,
sewing-compatible descent maps, renormalisation counterterms compatible
with overlaps, and completion maps whose inverse limits are separated.
The $\Etwo$ centre layer requires the existence of the derived chiral
centre or the Drinfeld centre of the $\Eone$ representation category.
