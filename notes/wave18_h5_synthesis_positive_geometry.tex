\section*{Positive Effective Geometry, Reduced DT, and Chiral CoHA}
\label{sec:positive-effective-geometry}

The positive half attached to a Calabi--Yau category is a Hall object
before it is a vertex algebra. It is built from an effective moduli
problem, a vanishing-cycle sheaf, and an integration map. Drinfeld
doubling, passage to a centre, completion, and evaluation modules are
separate operations.

The separation is already visible on the affine threefold:
\[
  \CoHA(\C^3)=\Yplus(\glhat_1).
\]
This is the Schiffmann--Vasserot positive half. The full
\(\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}\) object appears after the double, centre,
or evaluation-completion layer is added.

\subsection*{Positive Hall Half}

\paragraph{Effective Hall construction.}
Let \(X\) be a Calabi--Yau \(\infty\)-category in a stratum where the
Hall stack, orientation datum, vanishing cycles, and integration map are
constructed. The positive half is
\[
  \Ypluscy=\Heq(\Meff(X),\phi_W).
\]
Here \(\Meff(X)\) is the effective Hall site cut by the positive cone in
the numerical \(K\)-lattice, and \(\phi_W\) is the
Kontsevich--Soibelman vanishing-cycle complex. Kontsevich--Soibelman
give the motivic Hall and DT formalism; Schiffmann--Vasserot identify
the \(X=\C^3\) chart with \(\Yplus(\glhat_1)\); Davison--Meinhardt
provide the cohomological integration map.

\paragraph{Double layer.}
A Drinfeld double requires additional data:
\[
  \langle-,-\rangle\colon \Ypluscy\otimes Y^{-}(X)\longrightarrow R
\]
a non-degenerate Hopf pairing, a Cartan part \(Y^0(X)\), and a
completion compatible with the effective-cone grading. When these data
exist,
\[
  G(X)=\Ypluscy\bowtie Y^0(X)\bowtie Y^{-}(X).
\]
In toric charts the pairing is the Atiyah--Bott equivariant residue at
fixed loci. For compact \(K3\times E\) it is part of the
Hall--Borcherds recognition datum, constrained by reduced DT
integration and by the Borcherds denominator. The six constructions of
\(G(K3\times E)\) are six approaches to this double or to its
representation-theoretic avatars, distinct from the single
\(\Phi\)-image of a fixed Calabi--Yau category.

\subsection*{Four Geometric Strata}

\paragraph{Toric CY\(_3\).}
For toric Calabi--Yau threefolds with a quiver-with-potential NCCR,
Van den Bergh's tilting construction presents the Hall category by the
Jacobi algebra of \((Q_X,W_X)\). The coefficient ring is
\(H_T^*(\mathrm{pt})\) for \(T=(\Cstar)^3\), and stable envelopes
compute wall crossing by fixed-point localisation. The standard rows
are
\[
  \kch(\C^3)=1,\qquad
  \kch(\mathrm{conifold})=1,\qquad
  \kch(\mathrm{Tot}\,\omega_{\bP^2})=\frac32,\qquad
  \kch(\mathrm{Tot}\,\omega_{\bF_n})=2.
\]
The local-surface values are
\(\frac12\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(S)\) for \(S=\bP^2,\bF_n\). The conifold
value is the separate threefold McKay/NCCR row.

\paragraph{Orbifold and finite-symplectic strata.}
For \(X=[Y/G]\) with \(G\) finite symplectic, the Hall site is the
inertia-enhanced NCCR chart. Wemyss supplies the ADE chart atlas, and
orbifold equivariance replaces \(H_T^*(\mathrm{pt})\) by the
appropriate \(G\)-equivariant cohomology. The \(K3\) input is the
Nikulin--Mukai classification of finite symplectic automorphisms. The
CHL quotients \((K3\times E)/(\Z/N)\), \(N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}\), belong to
this finite-symplectic arithmetic stratum.

\paragraph{Compact \(K3\times E\).}
The compact threefold \(K3\times E\) lies outside the single toric
quiver-with-potential chart regime. The \(K3\) fibre carries spherical
Seidel--Thomas twists and nontrivial Hochschild classes, forcing a
quasi-NCCR atlas with \(A_\infty\)-bimodule transitions. Reduced DT
integration replaces torus localisation: one uses K3-primitive reduced
virtual classes, residual \(\Cstar_E\) from the elliptic translation
quotient, and \(\Auts(K3)\)-orbit averaging.

The reduced OP/DT scalar branch has its own normalization:
\[
  \Dfive=64^{-1}\Delta_5,\qquad
  \PhiTenOP=\Dfive^2=4096^{-1}\Delta_5^2,
\]
and hence, in the Oberdieck--Pixton convention,
\[
  Z^{K3\times E}_{\DT,\mathrm{red}}
  =-(\PhiTenOP)^{-1}
  =-4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}.
\]
The factor \(4096=64^2\) is a square of the theta-leading coefficient;
the sign is the OP scalar convention. This scalar branch is the
normalised scalar trace; the Hall--Borcherds bracket requires separate
construction.

\paragraph{Lattice and BKM strata.}
For a lattice-polarised \(K3\)-fibration, the character target is an
automorphic product. In the \(N=1\) row the Gritsenko--Nikulin lift of
\(\phi_{0,1}\) gives
\[
  \operatorname{den}(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})
  =\Dfive(2Z)
  =64^{-1}\Delta_5(2Z),
  \qquad
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)=\frac{c(0)}2=\frac{10}{2}=5.
\]
For CHL rows the same rule reads
\(\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2\). These are denominator weights, separate
from compact Hodge and Heisenberg invariants.

\subsection*{Chiral Factorisation and CoHA}

\paragraph{The affine chart.}
On \(\C^3\), abelian holomorphic Chern--Simons observables form the
Costello--Gwilliam holomorphic factorisation algebra
\(\Obs_{\hCS}^{\gl_1}(\C^3)\). The corresponding Hall side is the
Schiffmann--Vasserot shuffle algebra
\[
   \bigoplus_{n\ge 0}H_T^*(\Hilb^n(\C^3))
   =\Yplus(\glhat_1).
\]
Both sides have MacMahon character
\[
  M(q)=\prod_{k\ge1}(1-q^k)^{-k}.
\]
The three-dimensional holomorphic factorisation structure is the
source-side \(E_3\) object. After the CY\(_3\) specialisation to a
curve, the chiral algebra output is \(E_1\). The \(E_2\)-braided
structure belongs to the Drinfeld centre, the Hall double, or the
stable-envelope module category.

\paragraph{Stable envelopes.}
For a symplectic resolution or quiver variety chart, the
Maulik--Okounkov \(R\)-matrix is the wall-crossing operator
\[
  R^{\mathrm{MO}}_{C_+C_-}(u)
  =(\Stab_{C_-}(u))^{-1}\circ\Stab_{C_+}(u).
\]
When a cluster gluing cocycle \(\phi_{UV}(u)\) is normalized so that
its polar part is the stable-envelope wall term, the residue of
\(\phi_{UV}(u)\) at the wall gives this \(R\)-matrix. Yang--Baxter is
the stable-envelope braid relation; the dynamical twist is the regular
part of the cocycle away from the wall.

\subsection*{Two-Stage Factorisation}

\paragraph{Functorial grammar.}
The CY-to-chiral construction factors as
\[
  \Phi_d=\Sp_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ\PhiFA_d.
\]
Stage \(1\) produces the \(E_d\)-holomorphic factorisation algebra
\(\mathcal F_X\). In dimension \(3\) this is the six-dimensional
holomorphic Chern--Simons BV theory attached to the Calabi--Yau datum.
Stage \(2\) specialises along a curve \(C\) transverse to the chosen
positive cycle \(\Sigma_{d-1}\). For \(d\ge3\) the output
\(A_{\Phi(X)}\) is \(E_1\)-chiral. The \(E_2\) structure is recovered on
\(\mathcal Z(\mathrm{Rep}^{E_1}(A_{\Phi(X)}))\), on a Drinfeld double,
or on a stable-envelope module category.

Francis--Gaitsgory Koszul duality supplies the intrinsic coalgebra
\[
  \mathcal C_X=\mathrm{Bar}^{E_d}(\mathcal F_X)\{-d\}.
\]
On constructed loci, its effective-cone primitives map to the positive
Hall half. Equality is proved in the toric charts and is a compact
recognition problem for \(K3\times E\).

\paragraph{\(\C^3\).}
Choose a line-specialisation datum \(C\subset\C^3\). The positive cone
is the point-class cone \(\Z_{\ge0}[\mathrm{pt}]\), and the specialised
primitive object is the affine Hall half:
\[
  \Sp_C(\mathcal F_{\C^3})^{\mathrm{prim}}
  \simeq
  \CoHA(\C^3)
  =
  \Yplus(\glhat_1).
\]
The MacMahon identity agrees with the Nekrasov partition function
\[
  Z_{\mathrm{Nek}}(\C^3)=M(q).
\]

\paragraph{\(K3\times E\).}
For the compact product, the specialisation datum is the fibration
\((K3,E)\). The reduced K3-primitive character is fixed by the OP
scalar branch:
\[
  Z^{K3\times E}_{\DT,\mathrm{red}}
  =-(\PhiTenOP)^{-1}
  =-4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}.
\]
A factorisation-sheaf quasi-isomorphism between the compact specialised
object and the reduced Hall object requires a bracket-preserving
comparison with \(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5}\). The denominator side fixes
the following data:
\[
  \operatorname{den}(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})
  =64^{-1}\Delta_5(2Z),
  \qquad
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)=5.
\]

\paragraph{The four \(K3\times E\) constants.}
The compact product carries separate invariants:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{invariant} & \text{value} & \text{source} \\
\hline
\kcat(K3\times E) & 0 &
\chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot0 \\
\kch(K3\times E) & 0 &
\sum_q(-1)^qh^{0,q}(K3\times E) \\
\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E) & 3 &
\text{Mukai--Heisenberg specialisation }2+1 \\
\kBKM(\Delta_5) & 5 &
c(0)/2=10/2 \\
\kfib(K3) & 24 &
\mathrm{rk}\,\widetilde H(K3,\Z)
\end{array}
\]
The visible set \(\{0,3,5,24\}\) records compact Hodge supertrace,
Heisenberg specialisation, Borcherds denominator weight, and Mukai
fibre rank. The Hall--Borcherds branch is the compact
Hall--Drinfeld-double branch. The Mukai self-mirror K3 Yangian is a
different branch.

\subsection*{Obstructions and Proof Obligations}

\paragraph{\(K3\times E\) global chart.}
The spherical content of \(\Db(\Coh K3)\) rules out a finite global
quiver-with-potential chart for the compact product under the K3 fibre
hypotheses. The operative object is the reduced \((\infty,1)\)-Hall
positive half organised by \(\Phi_3\), the quasi-NCCR atlas, and
reduced DT integration.

\paragraph{Hall--Borcherds bracket.}
The scalar data determine the target denominator and the protected
charge character. They are insufficient to identify the reduced Hall
bracket with the Borcherds bracket. The required comparison consists of: an oriented
compact Hall positive half, a non-degenerate Hopf pairing, a compatible
completion, and a charge-preserving Lie superalgebra map to
\(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5}\) carrying reduced Hall multiplication to the
Borcherds denominator bracket.

\paragraph{Fake-Monster dimension-five witness.}
For \(K3_1\times K3_2\times E\), the expected character after
Niemeier projection to the Leech slice is
\((\Phi_{12}^{\mathrm{FM}})^{-1}\). The weight identity is secure:
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_{12}^{\mathrm{FM}})=c(0)/2=12.
\]
The Lie-algebra identification
\(\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{BPS}}(K3^2\times E)\cong
\mathfrak g^{\mathrm{FM}}\) and the doubly-reduced DT integrand
matching \((\Phi_{12}^{\mathrm{FM}})^{-1}\) remain proof obligations.

\paragraph{General compact non-toric CY\(_3\).}
For a smooth projective CY\(_3\) lacking torus action, Nikulin
orbifold presentation, or \(K3\times E\) reduced template, the gap is
the construction of \(\Meff(X)\) with orientation data, compact Hall
integration, and a comparison between Joyce--Song motivic Hall theory
and the Hodge-graded equivariant cohomology used by the positive-half
formula.

\subsection*{Theorem Boundaries}

Toric CY\(_3\) positive halves are controlled by
Schiffmann--Vasserot, Costello--Li, and Maulik--Okounkov on the affine
and stable-envelope charts. Orbifold finite-symplectic charts use
Wemyss and inertia-stack equivariance. The compact \(K3\times E\)
scalar branch is the Oberdieck--Pixton / Oberdieck--Pandharipande /
Oberdieck reduced OP/DT theorem in the normalization displayed above.
The \(N=1\) BKM denominator is the Gritsenko--Nikulin and Borcherds
product for \(\Delta_5\). The compact Hall--Borcherds double is the
remaining algebra-level construction.
