\section*{Summary (\(\leq 600\) words)}

The audit surveys the fifteen Vol~III open frontiers enumerated in
\texttt{FRONTIER.md} and cross-referenced at
\texttt{notes/CoHA\_to\_W\_infty\_treatise.tex:61--68} against every
\texttt{.tex} chapter of \(\sim\)\texttt{chiral-bar-cobar} (Vol~I) and
\(\sim\)\texttt{chiral-bar-cobar-vol2} (Vol~II).  The finding is uniform:
\emph{zero semantic contradictions between the three volumes at any
frontier item}.  Every frontier is either (a) scoped consistently across
all three volumes at the same level of openness, or (b) anchored in Vol~I
or Vol~II at a weaker special case from which the Vol~III full frontier
is the natural generalisation.

frontier on the other: \(\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2\) (Vol~I \texttt{programme\_overview\_platonic.tex:447},
Vol~II \texttt{introduction.tex:2023}, Vol~I \texttt{bar\_cobar\_adjunction\_curved.tex:7505};
