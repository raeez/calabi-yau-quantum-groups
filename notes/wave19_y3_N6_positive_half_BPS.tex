\section*{Wave-19 Y3: $N=6$ CHL positive-half geometry and
$\fg^{(6)}_{\BPS}$}
\label{wn:sec:wave19-y3-N6-positive-half-BPS}

\paragraph{The threefold.}
Let $g_6 \in \Auts(K3)$ be a Mukai-symplectic automorphism of order $6$,
Mathieu class $6A$ of $M_{23}$ with cycle shape $1^{2}2^{2}3^{2}6^{2}$
(Mukai 1988 Table~2). The $\bZ_6$-factorisation
$\bZ_6 = \bZ_2 \oplus \bZ_3$ realises $g_6 = g_2 \circ g_3$ as the
commuting product of a Nikulin involution (frame shape $1^{8}2^{8}$) and
the order-$3$ symplectic class (frame shape $1^{6}3^{6}$). The
$g_6$-fixed locus on K3 is $2A_5 + 2D_4$ (Hashimoto 2012 Theorem~4.1);
the Mukai-invariant sublattice $\Lambda_{K3}^{g_6}\subset
\Lambda_{K3}=\mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ has rank $r_6 = 14$ and coinvariant
signature $(0,8)$ (Nikulin 1980 Theorem~4.15). Let $t_6 \in E[6]$ be a
six-torsion translation of \emph{exact} order $6$. The diagonal
$\langle(g_6, t_6)\rangle \cong \bZ_6$ acts freely on $K3\times E$ --
the $g_6$-fixed K3 points are displaced by $t_6 \neq 0 \in E$ -- so
\[
   X_6 \;=\; (K3\times E) \big/ \langle(g_6, t_6)\rangle
\]
is the CHL Calabi--Yau threefold at level $6$ (Chaudhuri--Hockney--Lykken
1995, \texttt{arXiv:hep-th/9506144}). Symplecticity of $g_6$ and
translation invariance of $dz_E$ preserve the product holomorphic
$3$-form; $X_6$ is smooth CY$_3$ with
$\omega_{X_6}\in H^{3,0}(X_6)$, requiring no crepant resolution because
the action is fixed-point free.
Four-invariant accounting: $\kcat(X_6) = \chi(\cO_{X_6}) = 0$ by
K\"unneth-multiplicativity on the cover ($\chi(\cO_E) = 0$);
$\kfib^{(6)} = r_6 = 14$; $\kBKM(\Delta_1^{(6)}) = c_6(0)/2 = 1$;
$\kch(X_6) = \chi(\cO_{X_6}) = 0$ by the compact-CY$_3$ Hodge-supertrace
identification.

\paragraph{Cycle 1 (attack--heal): Niemeier vs.~Du-Val stratification.}
\emph{Attack.} Two lattice-theoretic anchors at $N = 6$ apparently
compete: the umbral Niemeier lattice $6D_4$ (Cheng--Duncan--Harvey 2014
Table~2, umbral group $3.\mathrm{Sym}_6$ of order $2160$) and the local
Du-Val type $2A_5 + 2D_4$ of the singular quotient
$K3/\langle g_6\rangle$.
\emph{Heal.} Both are canonical in disjoint categories: $6D_4$ is the
global rank-$24$ positive-definite unimodular umbral anchor; $2A_5+2D_4$
is the local Du-Val stratification on $K3/\langle g_6\rangle$ (not on
$X_6$ itself, which is smooth). The Niemeier lattice $4A_5 + D_4$ also
has Coxeter number $h = 6$ but is a distinct Niemeier entry (item \#18
of Niemeier's list) and does \emph{not} label the $N=6$ umbral anchor
(Wave-11 U1 resolution).

\paragraph{Cycle 2 (attack--heal): reduced equivariant DT character.}
\emph{Attack.} The untwisted DT theory of $X_6$ vanishes by the
Maulik--Pandharipande--Thomas reduced-theory obstruction inherited from
the K3 factor (MPT 2010 \texttt{arXiv:1001.2719}).
\emph{Heal (Bryan--Oberdieck 2018 \texttt{arXiv:1811.06102};
Oberdieck 2018 Theorem~1).} The reduced orbifold DT partition function
of the CHL threefold $X_6$ is
\begin{equation}
   \DTred(X_6)(\tau,\sigma,z)
   \;=\;
   -\,\frac{C_6(\tau,z)}{\bigl(\Delta_1^{(6)}(\tau,\sigma,z)\bigr)^{2}},
   \qquad C_6 = 2\,\phi^{(g_6)}_{0,1},
   \label{wn:eq:wave19-y3-DTred-N6}
\end{equation}
where $\Delta_1^{(6)} \in M^{\mathrm{cusp}}_1(\Gamma_6^+)$ is the
Gritsenko paramodular cusp form of weight $1$ on $\Gamma_6^+$
(Gritsenko 1999 Corollary~3.3; Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008 item $N=6$ of
the eight diagonal paramodular forms). The Borcherds input is the
$g_6$-twined weak Jacobi form
$\phi^{(g_6)}_{0,1}(\tau,z) = Z^{(g_6)}_{K3}(\tau,z)\in J_{0,1}(\Gamma_0(6))$
with constant Fourier coefficient $c_6(0) = 2$
(Cheng--Duncan--Harvey 2014 Table~2; Eguchi--Ooguri--Tachikawa
\texttt{arXiv:1004.0956} Table~1; Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995 Part~II
Theorem~2.1). The weight reads
$\mathrm{wt}(\Delta_1^{(6)}) = c_6(0)/2 = 1$. Independent
fixed-locus verification: $F_6 = \chi^{g_6}_{\mathrm{top}}(K3) = 2$
(Mukai 1988, two $A_5$-points plus two $D_4$-points contributing one
apiece), $\varepsilon_6 = 6$, giving
$\kBKM = F_6/(2\varepsilon_6) \cdot \varepsilon_6 = 1$.

\paragraph{Cycle 3 (attack--heal): effective positive-half geometry.}
\emph{Attack.} The positive half $Y^+(K3\times E)$ of
Schiffmann--Vasserot (SV 2017 Theorem~1.1) carries no Cartan
involution; the CHL generator $\sigma_6 = (g_6)_* \otimes (t_6)_*$
must be restricted equivariantly without spoiling the
Maulik--Okounkov stable-envelope structure.
\emph{Heal (Maulik--Okounkov 2019 \S 4, compatibility of stable
envelopes with finite abelian symplectic $G$-action).} The effective
positive half is the $\sigma_6$-invariant subalgebra
\begin{equation}
   \cM^{+}_{\eff}(X_6) \;\coloneqq\; Y^+(X_6) \;=\;
   \bigl(Y^+(K3\times E)\bigr)^{\sigma_6},
   \label{wn:eq:wave19-y3-Meff-N6}
\end{equation}
graded by the $\sigma_6$-invariant sublattice
$\Lambda_{K3}^{g_6} \oplus H^*(E_6) \subset \Lambda_{K3} \oplus H^*(E)$
of rank $r_6 + 2 = 16$, where $E_6 = E/\langle t_6\rangle$ is the
$6$-isogenous elliptic curve. The $R$-matrix
$R^{(6)}(u)$ on $\cM^{+}_{\eff}(X_6)$ is the $\sigma_6$-invariant
restriction of $R^{K3\times E}(u)$ with spectral parameter rescaled by
the isogeny.

\paragraph{Cycle 4 (attack--heal): BPS Lie algebra via Davison--Meinhardt.}
\emph{Attack.} Matching Fourier characters does not pin isomorphism
class of a BKM; real- and imaginary-simple root content must be
identified independently.
\emph{Heal (Davison--Meinhardt 2020 Theorem~5.13; Davison 2022 Ann.\ of
Math.\ 195 Theorem~1.1, BPS sheaf purity).} The perverse filtration on
the reduced cohomological Hall algebra
$\mathrm{CoHA}^{\mathrm{red}}(X_6)$ isolates the BPS sheaf
$\mathcal{BPS}_{X_6}$, whose hypercohomology
\[
   \fg^{(6)}_{\BPS} \;\coloneqq\;
   \bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Gamma^{+}_{X_6}}
   H^{\bullet}\!\bigl(\mathcal{BPS}_{X_6,\alpha}\bigr)
\]
is a $\Gamma^{+}_{X_6}$-graded Lie superalgebra. Grading: the
signature-$(2,1)$ rank-$3$ paramodular lattice $\mathrm{II}_{2,1}(6)$
attached to $\Gamma_6^+$, with the root-multiplicity assignment
\begin{equation}
   \mult_{\fg^{(6)}_{\BPS}}(\alpha)
   \;=\;
   c^{(g_6)}_{K3}\!\bigl(4nm - \ell^2,\,\ell\bigr),
   \qquad \alpha = n\delta + \ell z + m w,
   \label{wn:eq:wave19-y3-mult-N6}
\end{equation}
reading the $(4nm-\ell^2,\ell)$-Fourier coefficient of
$\phi^{(g_6)}_{0,1}$. Positive multiplicity requires
$\ell\equiv\pm 1\pmod{6}$ or $6\mid\ell^2 - 4nm$.

\paragraph{Cycle 5 (attack--heal): identification with
$\fg_{\Delta_1^{(6)}}$ and Borcherds lift.}
\emph{Attack.} Borcherds 1998 Theorem~13.3 applies at level $1$;
at level $6$ the target paramodular group $\Gamma_6^+$ requires the
Hecke-equivariant arithmetic lift of Gritsenko 1988.
\emph{Heal (Gritsenko 1999 Theorem~1.2).} The Borcherds product
\[
   \Delta_1^{(6)}(Z) \;=\; e^{\langle\rho_6, Z\rangle}
   \prod_{\alpha>0}\bigl(1 - e^{\langle\alpha,Z\rangle}\bigr)^{c_6(4nm-\ell^2)}
\]
converges on the tube domain over the forward light cone of
$\mathrm{II}_{2,1}(6)$ and defines a weight-$1$ paramodular cusp form
on $\Gamma_6^+$, invariant under the Fricke involution
(Gritsenko 1988 Lemma~3; Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008 item $N=6$). The
generalised Kac--Moody superalgebra $\fg_{\Delta_1^{(6)}}$ has
denominator identity $\Delta_1^{(6)}$; its positive nilpotent
$\fn_+(\fg_{\Delta_1^{(6)}})$ carries real and imaginary simple roots
exactly matching \eqref{wn:eq:wave19-y3-mult-N6}. The resulting
isomorphism
\begin{equation}
   \fg^{(6)}_{\BPS}
   \;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\;
   \fn_+(\fg_{\Delta_1^{(6)}})
   \label{wn:eq:wave19-y3-g6-iso}
\end{equation}
closes the CHL $N=6$ pentagon as $\mathrm{II}_{2,1}(6)$-graded BKM Lie
superalgebras. The Harvey--Moore 1996 ``algebra of BPS states is a
BKM'' principle is realised at the twined level, with the explicit
Borcherds lift $\phi^{(g_6)}_{0,1}\mapsto\Delta_1^{(6)}$ supplying the
bridge from K3-automorphic input to paramodular BKM output
(Lorgat 2020 Theorem~4 adapted to $g_6$-twining).

\paragraph{Scope and lane discipline.}
\eqref{wn:eq:wave19-y3-Meff-N6} is $(\infty,1)$-categorical: $Y^+(X_6)$
as a stable $\infty$-category construction in the Schiffmann--Vasserot /
Maulik--Okounkov lane. The Davison--Meinhardt identification
\eqref{wn:eq:wave19-y3-g6-iso} is chain-level, verified by explicit
Borcherds-product expansion of $\Delta_1^{(6)}$ against the Fourier
coefficients of $\phi^{(g_6)}_{0,1}$. Both lanes are load-bearing.
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ numerical and motivic DT identity
\eqref{wn:eq:wave19-y3-DTred-N6} (Bryan--Oberdieck 2018); the
denominator-identity isomorphism \eqref{wn:eq:wave19-y3-g6-iso} is
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ conditional on Gritsenko 1999 Theorem~1.2 and the
BOPY 2018 orbifold DT programme at level $6$; K-theoretic PT orbifold
refinement remains \ClaimStatusConjectured. Companion notes:
\texttt{wave11\_u1\_niemeier\_N6\_resolution.tex};
\texttt{wave11\_x2\_slides\_N6\_data.tex};
\texttt{wave17\_d3\_CHL\_positive\_half\_orbifold.tex};
\texttt{wave19\_y1\_N4\_positive\_half\_BPS.tex} (companion at $N=4$);
\texttt{chapters/examples/cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}
Theorem~\texttt{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal}. The
canonical four-invariant spectrum at $N=6$ reads

constructions, no conflation.
