\section*{Level-six CHL positive half and BPS Lie algebra}
\label{n6:sec:positive-half-BPS}

\paragraph{The quotient and the K3 lattice.}
Let $S$ be a K3 surface and let
$g_6\in\Auts(S)$ be a Mukai symplectic automorphism of order $6$,
Mathieu class $6A$, with cycle shape $1^2 2^2 3^2 6^2$ on the
Mathieu permutation representation. Hashimoto's fixed-locus table gives
\[
  |\Fix(g_6)|=2,\qquad
  |\Fix(g_6^2)|=6,\qquad
  |\Fix(g_6^3)|=8 .
\]
The topological Lefschetz formula gives
\[
  \operatorname{Tr}(g_6^a\mid H^2(S,\bQ))
  =
  |\Fix(g_6^a)|-2
\]
for $a=1,\ldots,5$, hence the trace row on $H^2(S)$ is
\[
  22,\ 0,\ 4,\ 6,\ 4,\ 0
\]
for $a=0,\ldots,5$. Averaging over the cyclic group gives
\[
  \operatorname{rk}H^2(S,\bQ)^{g_6}=6,\qquad
  \operatorname{rk}\widetilde H(S,\bQ)^{g_6}=8,
\]
where $\widetilde H(S,\bQ)=H^0(S,\bQ)\oplus H^2(S,\bQ)\oplus H^4(S,\bQ)$
is the Mukai lattice over $\bQ$. The $H^2$ coinvariant lattice has rank
$16$ and is negative definite. The order-six descent uses the rank $8$
invariant Mukai charge.

The quotient surface $S/\langle g_6\rangle$ has two
stabiliser-$\bZ_6$ orbits, two stabiliser-$\bZ_3$ orbits, and two
stabiliser-$\bZ_2$ orbits. Its Du Val type is therefore
\[
  2A_5+2A_2+2A_1 .
\]
The Burnside check is
\[
   e(S/\langle g_6\rangle)
   = {24+2+6+8+6+2\over 6}=8,\qquad
   2\cdot5+2\cdot2+2\cdot1=16,
\]
and the minimal resolution has Euler number $24$.

Let $t_6\in E[6]$ be a translation of exact order $6$ on an elliptic
curve $E$, and put
\[
  \sigma_6=(g_6,t_6),\qquad
  X_6=(S\times E)/\langle\sigma_6\rangle .
\]
For $1\leq a\leq5$, the translation $t_6^a$ is fixed-point free. Hence
$\sigma_6^a$ is fixed-point free on $S\times E$, and the quotient map
$p\colon S\times E\to X_6$ is finite \'{e}tale of degree $6$. The product
form $\omega_S\wedge dz_E$ descends because $g_6$ is symplectic and
translations preserve $dz_E$. Thus $X_6$ is a smooth $K$-trivial
threefold with $h^{0,\bullet}(X_6)=(1,1,1,1)$. Projection to the
elliptic factor gives a smooth isotrivial K3 fibration
\[
  X_6\longrightarrow E_6:=E/\langle t_6\rangle
\]
whose monodromy around the base is $g_6$.

\paragraph{Invariant package and the level-six constant.}
Translation acts trivially on $H^{0,*}(E)$, and the symplectic
automorphism $g_6$ acts trivially on $H^{0,0}(S)$ and $H^{0,2}(S)$.
Therefore
\[
  H^q(X_6,\cO_{X_6})
  =
  H^q(S\times E,\cO_{S\times E})^{\sigma_6},
  \qquad
  h^{0,\bullet}(X_6)=(1,1,1,1).
\]
Consequently
\[
  \kcat(X_6)=\chi(\cO_{X_6})=0,\qquad
  \kch(\cA_{X_6})=\sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(X_6)=0 .
\]
The level-six monodromy-invariant fibre charge is
\[
  \kfib^{(6),\mathrm{mon}}
  =
  \operatorname{rk}\widetilde H(S,\bQ)^{g_6}
  =
  8.
\]
These are compact total-space invariants of the free quotient. The
untwisted parent $K3\times E$ carries a different five-entry package:
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)=0,\qquad
  \kch(\cA_{K3\times E})=0,\qquad
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)=3,
\]
\[
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)=5,\qquad
  \kfib(K3)=\operatorname{rk}\widetilde H(K3,\bQ)=24.
\]
The compact Hodge supertrace, the Heisenberg-Mukai specialisation, the
Borcherds weight, and the Mukai fibre rank are four different
constructions.

The primitive CHL constants are
\[
  (c_1(0),c_2(0),c_3(0),c_4(0),c_6(0))
  =(10,8,6,4,2),
\]
and Borcherds' singular-theta weight theorem gives, for the primitive
CHL product $\Delta_{k(N)}^{(N)}$,
\[
  \kBKM\!\left(\Delta_{k(N)}^{(N)}\right)=\frac{c_N(0)}2 .
\]
For $N=6$,
\begin{equation}
  c_6(0)=2,\qquad
  \kBKM(\DeltaCHLSix)=\wt(\DeltaCHLSix)=1 .
  \label{n6:eq:kBKM}
\end{equation}
The level-one scalar convention is separate:
\[
  D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5,\qquad
  \Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_5^2 .
\]
The ordinary $6A$ Mathieu twining constant, the primitive CHL constant,
and the Gritsenko and Cl\'ery diagonal-divisor rows are three different
normalisations. The ordinary single-twining row lies outside the
primitive denominator. The primitive CHL denominator uses $c_6(0)=2$.
The Gritsenko and Cl\'ery catalogue is indexed by triples $(t,N;k)$; its
weight-$1$ row is the full-paramodular $(3,1;1)$ form $\Delta_1$, while
the primitive CHL row here is the level-six form $\DeltaCHLSix$. The
twice-weight tuple of the Gritsenko and Cl\'ery catalogue is
\[
  (10,4,6,2,4,1,3,2).
\]

\paragraph{Niemeier and surface strata.}
The order-six CHL row has two ADE anchors in different categories. The
umbral Niemeier lattice is the rank-$24$ positive-definite unimodular
lattice with root system $6D_4$ and umbral group
$3.\mathrm{Sym}_6$ of order $2160$ (Cheng, Duncan, and Harvey 2014).
The local analytic strata of the singular surface quotient are
$2A_5+2A_2+2A_1$. The CHL and umbral level-six row is anchored by
$6D_4$; the neighbouring Coxeter-number-$6$ Niemeier lattice
$A_5^4D_4$ belongs to a different umbral character.

\paragraph{Reduced DT scalar.}
Let
\[
  \phi^{(6)}_{0,1}(\tau,z)
  =
  \sum_{n,\ell}c^{(6)}(n,\ell)q^n\zeta^\ell
\]
be the CHL-normalized level-six weak Jacobi input attached to $6A$. In the
primitive Bryan and Oberdieck reduced curve-counting chamber the scalar
denominator is the square of the primitive CHL denominator:
\begin{equation}
   \Phi^{\mathrm{scal}}_6(\tau,\sigma,z)
   =
   \DeltaCHLSix(\tau,\sigma,z)^2,
   \qquad
   \DTred(X_6)(\tau,\sigma,z)
   =
   -\,\mathcal C^{\mathrm{BO}}_6(\tau,z)\,
   \bigl(\Phi^{\mathrm{scal}}_6(\tau,\sigma,z)\bigr)^{-1}.
   \label{n6:eq:DTred}
\end{equation}
The prefactor $\mathcal C^{\mathrm{BO}}_6$ is fixed by the
Bryan-Oberdieck scalar normalisation and boundary terms. The square in
\eqref{n6:eq:DTred} is the protected scalar curve-counting denominator.
It records signed characters in the Bryan-Oberdieck chamber. The compact
Hall positive half, Hall-Drinfeld double, and BKM recognition theorem are
separate structures. A compact Hall bracket, Drinfeld double, vertex
envelope, and BPS Lie algebra require the orientation, stability,
pairing, parity, and PBW data below.

\paragraph{Effective positive half.}
Fix a stability chamber $\theta$, a compact-support convention, an
orientation datum $o$, and an equivariant torus $\Teq$ for the reduced
critical CoHA construction on the cover. Write
\[
  \Yp_{\theta,o,\Teq}(S\times E)
\]
for the completed Hall positive half along the effective BPS monoid.
The generator $\sigma_6$ acts on charges and Hall correspondences once
the orientation and completion are chosen equivariantly.

The Hall source on the quotient is the Hall algebra of
$\langle\sigma_6\rangle$-equivariant objects on $S\times E$. Forgetting
the linearisation gives invariant classes upstairs. The effective
positive half used for denominator comparison is the image
\begin{equation}
   \Yp_{\eff}(X_6)
   =
   \operatorname{Im}\!\left(
     p^*\colon
     \Yp_{\Hall}(X_6)
     \longrightarrow
     \bigl(\Yp_{\theta,o,\Teq}(S\times E)\bigr)^{\sigma_6}
   \right).
   \label{n6:eq:Meff}
\end{equation}
The fibre charge projection satisfies, rationally,
\[
   p^*\Gamma^{\mathrm{fib}}_{X_6}
   =
   \widetilde H(S,\bQ)^{g_6},
   \qquad
   \operatorname{rk}p^*\Gamma^{\mathrm{fib}}_{X_6}=8.
\]
The elliptic base contributes its degree and point-class directions
through $H^0(E_6)\oplus H^2(E_6)$. Equality between
$\Yp_{\Hall}(X_6)$ and the invariant subalgebra upstairs requires the
rational projector
\[
  \Pi_6=\frac{1}{6}\sum_{a=0}^{5}\sigma_6^a
\]
to preserve the Hall product, signs, Harder-Narasimhan completion, and
reduced obstruction theory. A descended Maulik and Okounkov $R$-matrix
requires the same $\Pi_6$ compatibility for stable envelopes and the
$6$-isogeny on the elliptic factor.

\paragraph{BPS Lie algebra.}
Given a reduced d-critical orientation datum on the proper BPS
substacks of $X_6$, purity for the completed cohomological Hall algebra,
and compact-support functoriality for the quotient, Davison and
Meinhardt's construction then isolates BPS sheaves
\[
  \mathcal{BPS}_{X_6,\alpha},
  \qquad
  \alpha\in\Gamma^+_{X_6},
\]
and the Hall commutator gives a graded Lie superalgebra
\[
   \fg^{(6)}_{\BPS}
   =
   \bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Gamma^+_{X_6}}
   H^\bullet_c\!\left(\mathcal{BPS}_{X_6,\alpha}\right).
\]
The scalar Borcherds product fixes the target signed superdimension
function. For a quotient charge whose projection to $L_6$ is
$\alpha=n\delta+\ell z+mw$, denominator comparison asks for
\begin{equation}
   \sdim\,\fg^{(6)}_{\BPS,\alpha}
   =
   c^{(6)}(4nm-\ell^2,\ell),
   \label{n6:eq:root-sdim}
\end{equation}
This equation records the superdimension. The ordinary even and odd
multiplicities are the parity blocks of the chosen Borcherds
superalgebra and must be supplied in the recognition datum.

\paragraph{Borcherds target and recognition data.}
Put $Z=(\tau,z,\sigma)$,
$q=e^{2\pi i\tau}$, $\zeta=e^{2\pi iz}$, and $s=e^{2\pi i\sigma}$.
Gritsenko's level-six product is
\begin{equation}
   \DeltaCHLSix(Z)
   =
   e^{2\pi i\langle\rho_6,Z\rangle}
   \prod_{(n,\ell,m)>0}
   \left(1-q^n\zeta^\ell s^m\right)^{
     c^{(6)}(4nm-\ell^2,\ell)} ,
   \label{n6:eq:borcherds-product}
\end{equation}
on the rank-three CHL charge lattice $L_6$ generated by the product
coordinates $(n,\ell,m)$. The weight is the value in
\eqref{n6:eq:kBKM}. The product supplies the signed denominator
multiplicity function for the positive nilpotent part
$\fn_+(\fg_{\DeltaCHLSix})$ of the associated Borcherds-Kac-Moody Lie
superalgebra.

A Hall-Borcherds recognition datum for $X_6$ consists of the compact
Hall source, orientation monodromy, parity blocks, product and coproduct
matrices, super-bracket matrices, Hopf-pairing matrices, radical
kernels, quotient maps, defining relations, parity-refined PBW
comparison, and compatible completions. If these data identify the Hall
commutator with the Borcherds denominator coefficients root by root, then
\begin{equation}
   \fg^{(6)}_{\BPS}
   \cong
   \fn_+(\fg_{\DeltaCHLSix})
   \label{n6:eq:bps-recognition}
 \end{equation}
as a completed $L_6$-graded BKM Lie superalgebra. Harvey and Moore's
BPS-algebra principle is physical input for the recognition problem; the
mathematical comparison is the Borcherds lift plus the finite Hall data
above.

\paragraph{Chiral scope.}
The quotient $X_6$ is smooth and free, with isotrivial K3 fibration over
$E_6$. Its compact Hodge package gives
$\kcat(X_6)=\kch(\cA_{X_6})=0$, while the monodromy-invariant fibre
charge gives $\kfib^{(6),\mathrm{mon}}=8$. The primitive CHL
Borcherds product gives $\kBKM(\DeltaCHLSix)=1$ from $c_6(0)=2$. The
parent $K3\times E$ constants remain
\[
  \kcat=0,\qquad
  \kch=0,\qquad
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}=3,\qquad
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)=5,\qquad
  \kfib=24.
\]
The ADE anchors are the umbral Niemeier root system $6D_4$ and the
quotient-surface strata $2A_5+2A_2+2A_1$. The compact Hall recognition
\eqref{n6:eq:bps-recognition} requires orientation, parity, pairing,
PBW, radical, and completion data. The native CY$_3$ positive half is
$\Eone$-chiral; braided $\Etwo$ structure belongs to a centre, double,
or representation category after those operations have been constructed.
The positive-half BPS data, the Hall-Drinfeld double, the BKM
recognition datum, and the scalar square \eqref{n6:eq:DTred} are
separate objects. The Hall-Drinfeld and BKM branch in this note is
separate from the K3 self-mirror Yangian branch.
