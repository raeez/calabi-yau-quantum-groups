\section*{Wave-18 F1: NCCR obstruction for $K3\times E$}
\label{wn:sec:wave18-f1-K3E-NCCR}

\subsection*{The Van den Bergh frame}

A non-commutative crepant resolution of a Gorenstein normal variety
$X$ is a reflexive sheaf $\cE$ on $X$ such that
$\Lambda := \End_{\cO_X}(\cE)$ is Cohen--Macaulay and has finite
global dimension (Van den Bergh 2004, Def.~4.1, arXiv:math/0211064).
When $X$ is smooth projective there is nothing to resolve: any
tilting bundle gives an equivalence
$\Db(\Coh X) \simeq \Db(\mathrm{mod}\text{-}\Lambda)$.
The NCCR question transposes to: does $\cO_X$ itself, or a reflexive
cousin, admit an endomorphism ring that is \emph{quiver-presented}
(finitely generated projective over its centre by a quiver with
relations)? This is the question Wave-17 B2 answered negatively for
generic compact CY$_3$; we ask whether the $K3\times E$ factorisation
buys us a special case.

\subsection*{Cycle~1. The K3 factor}

\begin{proposition}[No global tilting bundle on smooth K3]
\ClaimStatusTheorem
\label{wn:prop:no-global-tilting-K3}
A smooth projective K3 surface $S$ carries no tilting bundle: there
is no vector bundle $T$ on $S$ with $\Ext^i_S(T,T) = 0$ for $i>0$
whose endomorphism algebra has finite global dimension and generates
$\Db(\Coh S)$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
By Serre duality $\Ext^2_S(T,T) \cong \Hom_S(T,T)^\vee \neq 0$
because $\omega_S \cong \cO_S$. Hence no nonzero $T$ is tilting in
the strong sense. Bridgeland 2008 (\emph{Stability conditions on K3
surfaces}, Duke Math.~J.~141, arXiv:math/0307164) removes any residual
loophole: every heart in the stability-condition space of
$\Db(\Coh S)$ is an infinite-rank tilt of $\Coh S$, none equivalent
to $\mathrm{mod}\text{-}\Lambda$ for finite-dimensional $\Lambda$.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textit{Heal.} The Kapranov exceptional collection exists
only on Fano varieties; K3 is strictly Calabi--Yau, not Fano. The
$24$-vertex Mukai ``quiver'' is a \emph{lattice} datum on
$\widetilde{H}(S,\mathbb{Z}) \cong U^4 \oplus E_8(-1)^2$, not a
quiver datum on $\Db(\Coh S)$. The Cheng--Duncan moonshine quiver
(if one is fixed) is a quiver on the symmetry groupoid of K3
sigma-model vacua, not a tilting quiver in the Van den Bergh sense.
Heal: restrict to ADE-singular K3s, where crepant resolutions of
ADE singularities carry NCCRs (Wemyss 2018); for smooth K3 we must
use spherical-twist generation (Seidel--Thomas~2001) which is not of
NCCR form.

\subsection*{Cycle~2. The elliptic factor}

\begin{proposition}[FM self-duality of $\Db(\Coh E)$]
\ClaimStatusTheorem
\label{wn:prop:FM-elliptic}
For a smooth elliptic curve $E$, the Poincar\'e bundle $\cP$ on
$E \times \widehat{E}$ induces Mukai's Fourier transform
(Mukai 1981, Nagoya J.~81),
$\mathrm{FM}_\cP: \Db(\Coh E) \xrightarrow{\sim} \Db(\Coh \widehat{E})$,
with $\widehat{E}\cong E$. The Atiyah classification of indecomposable
bundles on $E$ by rank--degree pairs makes the moduli of simples a
copy of $E$ itself, not a zero-dimensional quiver variety; no tilting
bundle on $E$ presents $\Db(\Coh E)$ as $\mathrm{mod}\text{-}\Lambda$.
\end{proposition}

\noindent\textit{Heal.} $E$ has $\omega_E \cong \cO_E$, so the
same Serre-duality obstruction applies in one dimension lower:
no global tilting bundle exists on $E$. The FM self-duality is a
genuine piece of non-commutative geometry --- an autoequivalence
of $\Db(\Coh E)$ generating $\mathrm{SL}_2(\bZ)$ together with
tensor by a line bundle (Mukai 1981; Polishchuk, \emph{Abelian
Varieties, Theta Functions and the Fourier Transform}, Cambridge
2003, Ch.~11) --- but it is not a tilting equivalence.

\subsection*{Cycle~3. Tensor of derived categories}

The external tensor product gives the Bondal--Orlov--Kuznetsov
identification
\[
  \Db(\Coh(K3 \times E))
  \simeq
  \Db(\Coh \K) \boxtimes \Db(\Coh E),
\]
where $\boxtimes$ is the $\mathbb{C}$-linear Deligne tensor product
of $k$-linear triangulated categories with a Lurie dg enhancement.

\begin{proposition}[Tensor product of non-tilted categories is not tilted]
\ClaimStatusTheorem
\label{wn:prop:tensor-not-tilted}
If $\cA$ and $\cB$ are dg-enhanced triangulated categories neither
of which admits a tilting generator, then $\cA \boxtimes \cB$ admits
a tilting generator only if each factor does. In particular,
$\Db(\Coh(K3 \times E))$ admits no global tilting bundle.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof]
A tilting generator $T$ on $\cA\boxtimes\cB$ splits as
$T = T_\cA \boxtimes T_\cB$ up to summands by the K\"unneth formula
for Ext-groups; vanishing of $\Ext^i(T,T)$ for $i>0$ implies
vanishing in each factor, returning a tilting bundle on each, which
contradicts Proposition~\ref{wn:prop:no-global-tilting-K3}.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Cycle~4. Tensor of quivers with potential}

The naive guess $Q_{K3\times E} = Q_\K \times Q_E$ fails at two
points. First, neither factor has a global quiver with potential
(Cycles~1 and~2). Second, even for local tilting charts the tensor
product of quivers with potential
$(Q_1\times Q_2, W_1 + W_2)$ computes a CoHA at CY-dimension
$2+1=3$ only when both factors are toric CY$_2$ (conifold
degenerations, $A_n$ surface singularities). K3 is compact and
non-toric; $E$ is compact and non-toric. The Kontsevich--Soibelman
critical-CoHA framework (KS 2011, arXiv:1006.2706) uses quivers
with potential as the \emph{input}, not as the output of some
non-existent reduction functor.

\subsection*{Cycle~5. Partial NCCR by stratification}

\begin{theorem}[Local ADE-chart atlas, no global NCCR]
\ClaimStatusTheorem
\label{wn:thm:local-ADE-no-global}
The following hold for $X = K3 \times E$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(i)}] Around each ADE singularity
$p \in K3$ (in the orbifold or degenerate limit), the formal
neighbourhood $\widehat{X}_{(p,q)}$ for $q \in E$ admits an NCCR
of the form $\End(\cE_{\text{ADE}}) \otimes \cO_E$: an ADE McKay
endomorphism algebra stretched along $E$.
\item[\textup{(ii)}] The smooth locus of $K3 \times E$ admits no
NCCR because the global Serre functor
$\Sigma = [3] \otimes \omega_X = [3]$ has no finite-dimensional
tilting resolution (K\"ahler--Einstein + $c_1 = 0$).
\item[\textup{(iii)}] There is no Cech descent of the ADE NCCRs
in (i) to a global NCCR: the transition $A_\infty$-bimodules
between two ADE charts on K3 include spherical twists around
$(-2)$-classes whose endomorphism algebra is
$\cO_\K[-2]\oplus\cO_\K$, which has global dimension $\infty$ in
any $\mathrm{mod}\text{-}\Lambda$ presentation.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof]
(i) is Wemyss 2018 for the K3 ADE factor tensored with the trivial
bundle on $E$. (ii) is Proposition~\ref{wn:prop:no-global-tilting-K3}
times $E$ through Cycle~3. (iii) follows from
Seidel--Thomas~2001: the spherical twist
$T_\cO: \Db(\Coh \K) \to \Db(\Coh \K)$ has
$T_\cO(\cO) \in \Db(\Coh \K)\smallsetminus\cO^{\oplus\ast}$, and
the induced bimodule on overlapping ADE charts is not a finite
projective over the transition endomorphism algebra.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Obstruction, stated}

\begin{corollary}[NCCR obstruction for $K3\times E$]
\ClaimStatusTheorem
\label{wn:cor:NCCR-obstruction-K3E}
The compact Calabi--Yau threefold $X = K3\times E$ does not admit a
non-commutative crepant resolution in the sense of Van den Bergh.
The obstruction is the $(-2)$-spherical content of $\Db(\Coh \K)$:
the spherical-twist group (Seidel--Thomas 2001, Duke Math.~J.~108,
arXiv:math/0001043) acts on $\Db(\Coh \K)$ with no finite-rank
tilting fixed point, and does not present $\Db(\Coh \K)$ as
$\mathrm{mod}\text{-}\Lambda$ for any finite-dimensional $\Lambda$.
An ADE-chart atlas with $A_\infty$-bimodule transitions exists,
but the global quiver-with-potential single-chart reduction fails.
This matches Wave-17 B2: compact non-toric CY$_3$ has no global
QP; $K3\times E$ is compact non-toric despite the elliptic
fibration, because the K3 base is non-toric.
\end{corollary}

\subsection*{Consequence for the $\kappa_\bullet$-spectrum}

The four constructions yielding $\kappa_{\bullet}(K3\times E) \in
\{2,3,5,24\}$ never use an NCCR. $\kappa_\cat = 0$ is K\"unneth on
$\chi(\cO_X)$. $\kappa_\ch$ is the Hodge supertrace identification
of the $\Phi$-output on the smooth compact CY$_3$.
$\kappa_{\BKM}(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ is the Borcherds weight
(Gritsenko series). $\kappa_\fib = 24$ is the Mukai lattice rank.
None of these needs a tilting bundle; the CoHA side
(Davison integrality on the derived category of $K3\times E$)
survives without one, which is the operational content of
Corollary~\ref{wn:cor:NCCR-obstruction-K3E}: the non-commutative
data of $K3 \times E$ is organised by an
$(\infty,1)$-categorical $\Phi$-functor, not by a quiver with
potential.
