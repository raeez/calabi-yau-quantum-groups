## 1. The explicit $\mathbb P^1$-family at $X=K3\times K3$

### 1.1 The two deformation parameters

$$

At $d=4$ the *new* $\sigma_4$ direction enters: it is the deformation along $H^{2,2}_{\mathrm{prim}}(X)$, controlling the *primitive middle-cohomology* class. Concretely:

- $\sigma_3\in H^{3,1}(X)$ is the *complex-structure deformation*; it deforms the BCOV Lagrangian's Yukawa coupling and survives through the Tian–Todorov Massey product structure on the $\mathrm{HH}_*$-side.

## 2. The $p_1$-twisted double current algebra

### 2.1 Double current algebras

Recall: for a simple Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$, the *double current algebra* $\mathcal D(\mathfrak g)$ is the Drinfeld double of $\mathfrak g[s,t]$ (currents in two formal variables), satisfying the relations
$$
[X(s_1,t_1),Y(s_2,t_2)]\;=\;[X,Y](s_1+s_2,t_1+t_2)\;+\;\text{quantum corrections},
$$
with the quantum corrections governed by a Lie cocycle. Double current algebras play the role of "Yangian-like" objects at $d\ge 3$ where a single-variable Yangian $Y(\mathfrak g)$ does not suffice (Guay–Ma, Kodera).

### 2.2 The $p_1$-twist

For a CY${}_4$ category $C$, the *$p_1$-twisted double current algebra* at fibre $[\sigma_3:\sigma_4]$ is the double current algebra constructed from the *Mukai-lattice Lie algebra* $\mathfrak g_X:=\mathfrak{gl}(H^*_{\mathrm{Muk}}(X))$ with the cocycle deformed by the first Pontryagin class $p_1(X)\in H^4(X,\mathbb Z)$:
$$
\mathcal D_{p_1}(\mathfrak g_X)\;:=\;\mathcal D(\mathfrak g_X)\big/\bigl(\text{relations twisted by }p_1(X)\bigr).
$$
The "twisted by $p_1$" means: the standard double current cocycle $\omega:\mathfrak g[s,t]\otimes\mathfrak g[s,t]\to\mathfrak g[s,t]$ acquires an additive correction term
$$
\omega^{(p_1)}(X,Y)\;=\;\omega(X,Y)\;+\;\frac{p_1(X)\cdot |X|\cdot |Y|}{12}\,\mathrm{Tr}(XY)\cdot\mathbf{1},
$$
where $|X|,|Y|$ are the cohomological gradings, $p_1(X)$ is evaluated as the integer $\int_X p_1(X)\cup [X]=p_1$-degree, and the $1/12$ is the Hirzebruch normalisation $\hat A_2(X)=p_1/12$.

For our cases:
- $K3\times K3$: $\int p_1=-96$ (from $p_1(K3)=-48$ via signature, two factors); cocycle correction is $-96/12=-8$ times the trace term.
- Sextic $X_6\subset \mathbb P^5$: $\int p_1=-30$ (computed in §5.1); cocycle correction is $-30/12=-5/2$ (note: half-integer, requiring spin structure).
- $K3\times T^4$: $\int p_1=-48$ (the $T^4$ is parallelizable); cocycle correction is $-48/12=-4$.
- $T^8$: $\int p_1=0$; the twist is *trivial* and $\mathcal D_{p_1}=\mathcal D$ (untwisted).

### 2.3 What "$p_1$-twisted" operationally means

The Pontryagin twist enters the chiral algebra at the level of the *normal-ordered product anomaly*: when forming the chiral OPE $A(z)A(w)\sim\sum_n A^{(n)}_{AA}(z-w)^{-n}$, the leading anomaly term acquires a $p_1$-shift
$$
A^{(c)}_{AA}\;=\;\frac{c+p_1/12}{2(z-w)^4},
$$
so the central charge effectively shifts by $p_1/12$. For $K3\times K3$ this gives a shift $-96/12=-8$, and the effective central charge of the generic fibre $A^{(\sigma_3,\sigma_4)}_{K3\times K3}$ is
$$
c_{\mathrm{eff}}\;=\;c_{\mathrm{Mukai}}+p_1/12\;=\;48-8\;=\;40.
$$
The "double current" structure in $\mathcal D_{p_1}(\mathfrak g_X)$ then sees this $c_{\mathrm{eff}}=40$ rather than the naive $c=48$. The discrepancy of $8$ is the *Pontryagin contribution* to the chiral anomaly, distinct from the geometric $\chi(\mathcal O)$-contribution.

This is the operational content of "$p_1$-twisted double current algebra": the chiral OPE anomaly is shifted by $p_1/12$, the double current relations carry a $p_1/12$-deformed Lie cocycle, and the central charge of the generic fibre is the Mukai central charge plus the Pontryagin shift.

## 3. CY${}_4$ channel-count theorem at $K3\times K3$

V104 §1 (Angle 5) identified three coexisting refinements of the CY${}_4$ chiral data:
- **$V_4$-Künneth refinement (4 channels):** $\Pi_{++},\Pi_{+-},\Pi_{-+},\Pi_{--}$, the $\sigma$-eigencharacter decomposition under the holomorphic-vs-antiholomorphic involution per complex direction (collapsed to two effective bigradings at $d=4$). - **$\mathbb P^1$-family refinement (2 deformation parameters):** the $(\sigma_3:\sigma_4)\in\mathbb P^1_{(\sigma_3:\sigma_4)}$, recording which point in the BTT pencil we are at.

### 3.1 The interaction theorem

**Theorem (V112, channel-count interaction at CY${}_4$, CONJECTURAL).** The three refinements interact as follows:

1. The 4-channel $V_4$-Künneth spectrum $M_X^{V_4}=(\Pi_{++},\Pi_{+-},\Pi_{-+},\Pi_{--})$ is a **constant of the $\mathbb P^1$-family**: it does not vary with $[\sigma_3:\sigma_4]$, and is computed entirely by Künneth multiplicativity from the $d=2,3$ subfactors.

2. The 6-channel $S_3$-Hodge spectrum $M_X^{S_3}=(\Pi_{++},\Pi_{31},\Pi_{22},\Pi_{13},\Pi_{20},\Pi_{02})$ **varies** along the $\mathbb P^1$-family:
   - At $[\sigma_3:0]$: $\Pi_{31}$ and $\Pi_{13}$ are maximal, $\Pi_{22}$ is minimal (the $\Phi_3$-extension limit).
   - At $[0:\sigma_4]$: $\Pi_{22}$ is maximal, $\Pi_{31},\Pi_{13}$ are minimal (the new CY${}_4$-specific limit).
   - At a generic $[\sigma_3:\sigma_4]$: a non-trivial mixing governed by the Tian–Todorov mediator $[\sigma_3,\sigma_4]\in H^{5,3}(X)\cong H^{3,5}(X)^*$.

3. The two refinements are **independent**: there is no group containing both $V_4$ and $S_3$ that acts naturally on $H^*(X)$. The $V_4$-Künneth structure is *Künneth-bivariant* (multiplicative under products), while the $S_3$-Hodge structure is *Hodge-symmetric* (intrinsic to a single CY${}_4$). Their *joint* compatibilization is the $\mathbb P^1$-family parameter.

4. **Compatibility identity (sum rule).** On every fibre $[\sigma_3:\sigma_4]\in\mathbb P^1$,
$$
\Pi_{++}^{V_4}\;=\;\Pi_{++}^{S_3},\qquad
\Pi_{--}^{V_4}\;=\;-\Pi_{++}^{S_3}+\sum_{a+b=4,\,a\ne b}(-1)^{a+b}\Pi_{ab}^{S_3},
$$
i.e. the $\Pi_{++}$ entries agree (it is the trace term, intrinsic), while the $\Pi_{--}$ entry of the $V_4$-spectrum is reconstructed from the $S_3$-spectrum via the alternating-sign sum over the off-diagonal Hodge sectors.

## 4. The bigraded Lefschetz matrix as constant-of-the-$\mathbb P^1$-family invariant

**Conclusion (V112 verification).** $M_{K3\times K3}=(450,-416,130,-160)$ is the $V_4$-Künneth invariant of $\Phi_4(D^b\mathrm{Coh}(K3\times K3))$, constant across the $\mathbb P^1$-family. ## 5. Sextic CY${}_4$: $\Phi_4$ family at $X_6\subset\mathbb P^5$

The compact CY${}_4$ sextic hypersurface $X_6\subset\mathbb P^5$ is the simplest non-product CY${}_4$ example, the analogue of the quintic CY${}_3$. We construct the $\mathbb P^1$-family $\Phi_4(D^b\mathrm{Coh}(X_6))$ explicitly.

### 5.1 Hodge data and Pontryagin class

Hodge numbers of the sextic $X_6\subset\mathbb P^5$:
- $h^{4,0}=h^{0,4}=1$,
- $h^{3,1}=h^{1,3}=426$ (from the Lefschetz hyperplane theorem and Griffiths transversality on the sextic),
- $h^{2,2}=1752$ (computed via $\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(X_6)=2520$ and Hodge identity),
- $h^{2,2}_{\mathrm{prim}}=1752-h^{1,1}\cdot 2=1752-2=1750$ (since $h^{1,1}=1$ for the sextic, generated by the hyperplane class).

Pontryagin class: from the adjunction sequence $0\to TX_6\to T\mathbb P^5|_{X_6}\to N_{X_6/\mathbb P^5}\to 0$ with $N=\mathcal O(6)|_{X_6}$:
$$
c(TX_6)\;=\;c(T\mathbb P^5|_{X_6})/c(N)\;=\;(1+H)^6/(1+6H),
$$
expanding to first Pontryagin class $p_1(TX_6)=c_1^2-2c_2=-30 H^2$ (per V104 Angle 1).
$$
\int_{X_6}p_1\cup[X_6]\;=\;-30\cdot \int_{X_6}H^2\cup[X_6]\;=\;-30\cdot 6\;=\;-180,
$$
where the second equality uses $\int_{X_6}H^4=6$ (degree of the sextic). So the Pontryagin number is $-180$ for the sextic.

### 5.2 The two deformation parameters at the sextic

- $\sigma_3\in H^{3,1}(X_6)$: the complex-structure deformation, a 426-dimensional space (the sextic has a 426-dimensional moduli, one of the largest among low-genus CY${}_4$).
- $\sigma_4\in H^{2,2}_{\mathrm{prim}}(X_6)$: the primitive-middle deformation, a 1750-dimensional space.

The diagonal $\mathbb P^1_{(\sigma_3:\sigma_4)}$ is generated by chosen representatives of each.

### 5.3 The chain-level fibre at the sextic

Following the construction of §1.3 with $C=D^b\mathrm{Coh}(X_6)$:

**Step 1 — Endomorphism dg algebra.** $\mathrm{End}^\bullet(\mathcal O_{X_6})$ is the Dolbeault complex of the sextic, with HKR identification $\mathrm{HH}_*(\mathcal O_{X_6})\cong\bigoplus H^{p,q}$.

The $-5/2$ is half-integer; this signals that the sextic CY${}_4$ requires a *spin structure* on the $\mathbb P^1$-family for the $p_1$-twist to be well-defined integrally. The sextic does carry a spin structure (its second Stiefel–Whitney class vanishes, as it is a smooth complete intersection with $w_2=c_1\mod 2$ and $c_1=0$ for CY).

**Step 4 — Chiral envelope.** $A^{(\sigma_3,\sigma_4)}_{X_6}:=U^{ch}_{E_1}(\mathrm{End}^\bullet(\mathcal O_{X_6})^{(\sigma_3,\sigma_4)})$.
