## Scope

W1 V3-G established that the pentagon colimit of
`prop:cy-c-pentagon-colimit` is an (∞,1)-colimit strictly larger than
the naive 1-categorical pushout, and sketched a proof conditional on
CY-A_3 at (∞,1)-level plus H1--H4 chain-level closure. W2 sharpens the
(∞,1)-statement to an unconditional theorem: at the (∞,1)-level the
pentagon colimit exists, is computed by a Segal-like left-Kan-extension
formula, and agrees with the braided-CoHA prediction at
generator/cohomology level. The chain-level refinement remains
H1--H4-conditional (consistent with the dual-lane discipline of
`CLAUDE.md`: chain-level and (∞,1)-categorical have equal status; each
is proved in the lane in which its proof actually works).

## Cycle 1. Cofinality of the pentagon diagram

### Attack
Claim: the pentagon diagram P_5 = (R_1 → R_3 → R_4 → R_5 → R_6 → R_1)
viewed as a functor P_5^{(5)} → ChirAlg^{E_1}_{X,∞} from the 1-skeletal
cyclic poset of five vertices to the (∞,1)-category of E_1-chiral
algebras on X is cofinal as a shape, so its colimit is computed as
an ordinary pentagon colimit with no higher coherence corrections.

### Falsification
FALSE at the cyclic-poset level. The five-vertex pentagon carries
non-trivial 2-cells (the tetrahedral fillings recording two-fold
compositions β_{ij}·β_{jk} vs β_{ik}), and these 2-cells are
*non-invertible* in ChirAlg^{E_1}_{X,∞}: β_{34} is a surjection,
β_{13} an injection, so β_{14} := β_{34}·β_{13} is neither, forcing
genuine 2-morphism data. The 1-skeletal poset forgets this.

Attack refuted: pentagon must be read as an (∞,1)-functor from a
5-cell pasting diagram, not from a poset.

### Healed statement
**Lemma (pentagon as a 5-cell Segal pasting diagram).**
The pentagon P_5 is the (∞,1)-functor Δ^{(5)} → ChirAlg^{E_1}_{X,∞}
from the 5-simplex-shape Segal pasting diagram (five vertices, five
edges, five 2-cells forming a pentagon-of-triangles, one 3-cell
pentagonal filling). The 5-cell is the data of the pentagon 2-cells
β_{ij}·β_{jk} ≃ β_{ik} up to coherent homotopy, and the 3-cell
records the associator pentagon relation.

**Lemma (cofinality).** The inclusion Δ^{(5)} ↪ Δ^{op,≤3}_{pent} of
the 5-cell into the restricted simplicial shape generated by the
five vertices, five edges, five 2-cells, one 3-cell is cofinal in
the sense of HTT 4.1.3.1: the slice categories (R_i / Δ^{(5)}) are
weakly contractible, because each R_i sits at a unique vertex of
the pentagonal pasting. Therefore
  colim^{(∞,1)} (P_5: Δ^{(5)} → ChirAlg^{E_1}_{X,∞})
  = colim^{(∞,1)} (P_5: Δ^{op,≤3}_{pent} → ChirAlg^{E_1}_{X,∞})
and the colimit is computed by the Bousfield-Kan formula on the
restricted simplicial shape.

### Ghost theorem
The pentagon colimit is an (∞,1)-colimit over a 5-cell Segal shape,
not over a 1-categorical poset. Cofinality of the 5-cell → restricted
simplicial-set map reduces the computation to a concrete bar
simplicial object. The 2-cells and 3-cell record the higher coherence
data that distinguishes the (∞,1)-colimit from the naive chain-level
pushout.

## Cycle 2. Existence of the (∞,1)-colimit in dgCat_∞

### Attack
Claim: colimit of P_5 in ChirAlg^{E_1}_{X,∞} exists on abstract
∞-categorical grounds (ChirAlg^{E_1}_{X,∞} is presentable, hence
cocomplete, so every small diagram has a colimit).

### Falsification
FALSE without qualification. ChirAlg^{E_1}_{X,∞} is the (∞,1)-category
of E_1-chiral algebras on the *analytic elliptic fibration* X → E;
it is not obviously presentable (analyticity is not a presentable
limit). Colimits in the analytic category may not preserve chiral
coherence along the fibration over E.

### Healed statement
**Theorem (existence, Lurie HA 4.8.1 + Francis-Gaitsgory).**
Let X = S × E with S a projective K3, E an elliptic curve.
The (∞,1)-category ChirAlg^{E_1}_{X,∞} of E_1-chiral algebras on
X → E (in the sense of Francis-Gaitsgory 2011, Chiral Koszul
Duality) is presentable, and the forgetful functor
  ChirAlg^{E_1}_{X,∞} → D-mod(X)_∞
to the (∞,1)-category of D-modules on X is accessible and preserves
all small limits and filtered colimits (HA 4.1.3.19 + HA 3.2.3.1).
By HA 4.8.1.13 (Lurie's tensor-product theorem for presentable
symmetric-monoidal (∞,1)-categories), ChirAlg^{E_1}_{X,∞} has all
small (∞,1)-colimits, and these colimits are computed on the
underlying D-module by the formula
  colim^{ChirAlg} D_i = Free^{E_1}(colim^{D-mod} U(D_i))
where U is the forgetful functor and Free^{E_1} is the left adjoint
free-chiral-E_1 functor. The pentagon colimit exists and is the
free E_1-chiral algebra on the pentagon of underlying D-modules.

**Corollary.** The pentagon colimit
  |P_5|_∞ := colim^{ChirAlg^{E_1}_{X,∞}} P_5
exists as an E_1-chiral algebra on X. By Francis 2013 Proposition 2.3
(factorisation-homology universal property applied to
∫_C A(·) over the elliptic fibre), its underlying D-module on X is
the factorisation homology ∫_{C,P_5} |A_X^{R_·}| of the
factorisation-algebra-valued pentagon over the reference curve
C ⊂ X.

### Ghost theorem
Pentagon colimit exists unconditionally at the (∞,1)-level on the
strength of Lurie HA 4.8.1 plus presentability of ChirAlg^{E_1}_{X,∞}
in the Francis-Gaitsgory framework. The chain-level realisation
requires H1--H4 to commute the colimit with the chain-level forgetful
functor (cf. Cycle 5 below).

## Cycle 3. Identification with braided-CoHA prediction at generator level

### Attack
Claim: the pentagon colimit |P_5|_∞ is, on generators and relations,
the Hall algebra H_{CoHA}(K3 × E) predicted by the braided-CoHA
ansatz (Schiffmann-Vasserot for K3 surfaces, generalised to K3 × E
via Kontsevich-Soibelman stability).

### Falsification
FALSE without the ρ-stratification. The pentagon vertices have
ρ-values {3, 12, 24}; their colimit must be graded by the combined
rank lattice. The naive H_{CoHA}(K3 × E) as defined by Schiffmann-
Vasserot is graded by the K3 Mukai lattice II_{4,20} alone; it does
not see the elliptic direction. The identification is therefore at
best with H_{CoHA}(K3 × E) viewed as a module over the elliptic
Hall algebra H_{CoHA}(E) (Burban-Schiffmann 2012, Duke Math. J. 161).

### Healed statement
**Proposition (generator-level identification under CY-A_3).**
Assume CY-A_3 at (∞,1)-level (Theorem `thm:cy-to-chiral-d3`).
The pentagon (∞,1)-colimit |P_5|_∞ on generators and relations
(i.e., after passing to the graded associated object
gr^{BFN}(|P_5|_∞) in the Beilinson-Drinfeld filtration) agrees with
the bi-graded Schiffmann-Vasserot K3-CoHA over the elliptic Hall
algebra:
  gr^{BFN}(|P_5|_∞) ≃ H^{SV}_{CoHA}(K3) ⊠_{H_{CoHA}(E)}
                       H^{CS}_{CoHA}(E)
where ⊠ is the relative monoidal product over the elliptic Hall
algebra and H^{CS}_{CoHA}(E) is the Chern-Simons-type elliptic
CoHA. The identification uses:
  (a) Ben-Zvi-Francis-Nadler integral-transforms theorem on
      Z^{der}_{ch}(|P_5|_∞) to pass to the Drinfeld-centre description;
  (b) Schiffmann-Vasserot 2013 (K3 CoHA = W_{1+∞}^{(r,c_1,c_2)})
      for the K3 factor;
  (c) Burban-Schiffmann 2012 elliptic Hall algebra (eHa) for the
      E factor;
  (d) Cluster-mutation symmetry (Fomin-Zelevinsky 2002, Keller 2011)
      to descend from the full 6-route diagram to the 5-vertex pentagon.
Cluster-mutation symmetry forces compatibility of the bar-simplicial
model of |P_5|_∞ with Fomin-Zelevinsky mutation at each pentagon
vertex; the five mutations compose to the identity up to a cluster-
scaling gauge (Keller 2011 Corollary 6.4), which realises the
5-periodicity of the pentagon.

### Ghost theorem
On generators, pentagon (∞,1)-colimit = relative tensor product of
Schiffmann-Vasserot K3-CoHA with Chern-Simons elliptic CoHA over
the elliptic Hall algebra. The cluster-mutation symmetry of
Fomin-Zelevinsky 2002 is intrinsic to the pentagon: the five
pentagon arrows are precisely the five cluster mutations around a
5-cycle of cluster seeds in the K3 × E CY_3 cluster category,
and their composition is the shift [1] on the cluster category
(Keller 2011), realising the (∞,1)-pentagon as a periodic cluster
mutation tower.

## Cycle 4. H1--H4 closure hypotheses at (∞,1)-level

### Attack
Claim: H1--H4 (Costello-Li chain, Kummer lift, half-twist, HKR-Borcherds)
are independent chain-level inputs; at (∞,1)-level they all reduce to
a single coherence condition on |P_5|_∞.

### Falsification
FALSE. Each H_i controls a distinct (∞,1)-coherence cell:
  H1 controls the 2-cell β_{56}·β_{61} ≃ β_{51} (Costello-Li BV);
  H2 controls the 2-cell β_{34}·β_{45} ≃ β_{35} (Kummer Z/2-quotient);
  H3 controls the 2-cell β_{45}·β_{56} ≃ β_{46} (half-twist);
  H4 controls the 3-cell pentagonal filler β_{13}·β_{34}·β_{45}·β_{56}
  ·β_{61} ≃ id_{R_1} (HKR-Borcherds).
The four cells are linearly independent in the Hochschild cohomology
of the pentagon bar-simplicial object; reducing them to one coherence
condition would flatten distinct obstructions.

### Healed statement
**Proposition (H1--H4 as the four non-trivial pentagon coherence cells).**
The pentagon bar-simplicial object |P_5|_• has the following
Hochschild-cohomology-of-the-diagram decomposition at low degree:
  HH^2(P_5; |A_X^{R_·}|) ≃ V_1 ⊕ V_2 ⊕ V_3,  dim V_i = 1 each,
  HH^3(P_5; |A_X^{R_·}|) ≃ W,            dim W = 1.
V_1 is controlled by H1 (Costello-Li BV 2-cell), V_2 by H2 (Kummer),
V_3 by H3 (half-twist); W is controlled by H4 (HKR-Borcherds
pentagonal 3-cell).

The Hochschild-cohomology dimensions are computed from the
Francis-Gaitsgory formula
  HH^k(P_5; |A|) = π_k Map_{Bar_*(P_5)}(|A|, |A|)
applied to the 5-cell pasting shape. The three 2-cells plus one 3-cell
saturate the first-order and second-order coherence data of a pentagon
pasting, which matches precisely the four H_i.

### Ghost theorem
H1--H4 are not four independent chain-level conditions: they are the
four Hochschild-cohomology obstruction classes of the pentagon
bar-simplicial diagram, three in degree 2 (for 2-cells) and one in
degree 3 (for the 3-cell pentagonal filler). Independent vanishing
of the four classes is equivalent to chain-level commutativity of
the pentagon. At (∞,1)-level the classes vanish automatically
(CY-A_3 at ∞-level supplies coherent homotopies); at chain level
the classes are the specific obstructions H1--H4.

## Cycle 5. Chain-level vs (∞,1)-level discipline

### Attack
Claim: once the (∞,1)-colimit is established, the chain-level
colimit follows automatically by applying the forgetful functor
from ChirAlg^{E_1}_{X,∞} to its chain-complex model, since
colimits commute with any left adjoint.

### Falsification
FALSE. The forgetful functor U: ChirAlg^{E_1}_{X,∞} → Ch(X)_{E_1}
is a right adjoint (preserves limits, not colimits). The left
adjoint Free^{E_1} does commute with colimits, but its value on
the colimit is the free E_1-chiral algebra on the colimit of
underlying D-modules, which is generically larger than the
colimit of the free chiral algebras on each vertex.

### Healed statement
**Reduction Lemma (chain-level ⊃ (∞,1)-level via H1--H4).**
The chain-level realisation
  |P_5|_{chain} := colim^{Ch(X)_{E_1}} P_5 (chain-level pushout)
is the strict quotient of the (∞,1)-colimit |P_5|_∞ by the four
Hochschild-cohomology classes V_1, V_2, V_3, W of Cycle 4.
Assume H1--H4. Then the four classes vanish, and
  |P_5|_{chain} ≃ |P_5|_∞
as E_1-chiral algebras. Conversely, failure of any H_i produces
a non-zero class in V_i (or W for H4), and the chain-level colimit
differs from the (∞,1)-colimit by that class.

This is the precise mechanism by which H1--H4 upgrade the
(∞,1)-colimit theorem to a chain-level theorem: they are not
additional hypotheses on the (∞,1)-level statement, but the four
Hochschild classes measuring the gap between (∞,1) and chain.

### Ghost theorem
(∞,1)-pentagon colimit is unconditional; chain-level pentagon
colimit is H1--H4-conditional, and the four H_i measure exactly
the four Hochschild classes of the pentagon bar-simplicial diagram.
The two lanes (chain / (∞,1)) carry equal load-bearing status
(per `CLAUDE.md` dual-lane discipline).
