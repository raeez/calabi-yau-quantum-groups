## 1. The five attack angles

### Angle 1 — Does $\Phi_4$ exist? The $\pi_d(BU)$ obstruction.

The CY-A functor $\Phi_d:\mathrm{CY}_d\text{-}\mathrm{Cat}\to E_n\text{-}\mathrm{ChirAlg}$ has been constructed at $d=1,2,3$ (CY-A${}_3$ proved inf-categorically via thm:derived-framing-obstruction, $\mathrm{HH}^{-2}_{E_1}=0$). The native $E_n$ level on $A=\Phi_d(C)$ is determined by the Gerstenhaber bracket degree $1-d$ on $\mathrm{HH}^*(C,C)$:
- $d=1$: degree $0$ → $E_\infty$ (commutative);
- $d=2$: degree $-1$ → $E_2$ (braided, $S^2$-framing of $\mathrm{HH}_*$);
- $d=3$: degree $-2$ → $E_1$ (ordered; holomorphic CS breaks $E_2$ down);
- $d\ge 4$: degree $\le -3$ → $E_1$ stabilized.

The pattern *naively* predicts $E_4$ at $d=4$. This is wrong. The framing argument that constructs $\Phi_3$ requires an $S^{d-1}$-framing of $\mathrm{HH}_*(C)$. At $d=4$ the relevant homotopy obstruction is
$$
\pi_3(U)\;=\;\Z,\qquad \pi_4(BU)\;=\;\Z,
$$
generated by $c_2$ at the level of $BU$ and by the first *Pontryagin* class $p_1=c_1^2-2c_2$ when reduced to $BSO$. The class is *non-trivial* on every interesting compact CY${}_4$:
- $K3\times K3$: $p_1(T(K3\times K3))=p_1(T K3)\boxplus p_1(T K3)$, with $p_1(T K3)=-48$ (signature $-16$ via Hirzebruch), giving $p_1(T(K3\times K3))=-96$ on the diagonal.
- Sextic in $\P^5$: $p_1=-30 H^2$ from the adjunction sequence.
- $K3\times T^4$: $p_1=-48$ (the $T^4$ factor is parallelizable, $p_1=0$).
- $T^8$: $p_1=0$ (parallelizable). The *only* compact CY${}_4$ with $p_1=0$ is the abelian variety class.

The operadic content: an $E_2$-chiral algebra structure on $A=\Phi_d(C)$ requires a **trivialization** of the framing class over $\mathrm{Conf}_2(\R^d)\simeq S^{d-1}$. At $d=4$, the framing class lives in $\pi_3(U)\cdot p_1(C)$ and is non-zero whenever $p_1(C)\ne 0$. So even before asking about chain-level chiral data, the *target operad* $E_n$ at $d=4$ is forced down to $n=1$ for all $C$ with $p_1\ne 0$. This is not new: it is exactly AP-CY46 ("no native $E_4$, no native CY${}_4$ Yangian").

**Why hasn't $\Phi_4$ been constructed?** Two reasons, both real:

(a) **Operadic.** The infinity-categorical argument that gave CY-A${}_3$ used $\mathrm{HH}^{-2}_{E_1}(\mathrm{End}_{C})=0$ via unit-connectedness. At $d=4$, the analogous obstruction group is $\mathrm{HH}^{-3}_{E_1}$, which is generically $\ne 0$ — it is the home of the $p_1$-class realized algebraically as a Massey product $\langle m_2,m_2,m_2,m_2\rangle$ on the cyclic complex. The Goodwillie tower does not collapse at $d=4$; the second-layer obstruction in $\mathrm{Tot}\,\mathrm{Th}^{(2)}\mathrm{HC}^-_4$ is $p_1$ itself.

(b) **Deformation-theoretic (BTT-rigidity at $d=4$).** Bogomolov–Tian–Todorov says that the deformation space $\mathrm{Def}(C)$ of a CY${}_d$ category is unobstructed and has dimension $\dim H^{d-1,1}(X)$. At $d=2$ for K3: $h^{1,1}=20$, all classes deform. At $d=3$ for a quintic: $h^{2,1}=101$, again unobstructed. At $d=4$, the deformation space splits *bivariantly*:
$$
T_{[X]}\mathrm{Def}(X)\;=\;H^{3,1}(X)\;\oplus\; H^{2,2}_{\mathrm{prim}}(X),
$$
with the second summand carrying the *new* $\sigma_4$ deformation that has no $d=3$ analogue (cf. `cy4_e2_tower_extension.py` §1). A single $E_1$-chiral algebra cannot simultaneously absorb both summands at chain level: $\Phi_4(X)$ would have to be a *family* over the $\P^1$ parameterizing $(\sigma_3:\sigma_4)$, not a single algebra.

So $\Phi_4$ exists only as a **functor into families of $E_1$-chiral algebras over $\P^1_{(\sigma_3:\sigma_4)}$**, not as a functor into single algebras. Calling such a thing "the CY-A functor at $d=4$" requires an explicit acknowledgment that the codomain has changed.

### Angle 2 — What would $\Phi_4(D^b\mathrm{Coh}(K3\times K3))$ be?

Take $X=K3\times K3$ as compact CY${}_4$. Compute the predicted chiral algebra via Künneth bivariance, *conditional* on $\Phi_4$ existing in the family-valued sense.

**Mukai lattice.** $H^*_{\mathrm{Muk}}(K3,\Z)$ has rank $24$, even unimodular signature $(4,20)$ (Mukai 1984). Künneth gives $H^*_{\mathrm{Muk}}(K3\times K3,\Z)=H^*_{\mathrm{Muk}}(K3)\otimes H^*_{\mathrm{Muk}}(K3)$, rank $24^2=576$, signature $(4\cdot 4+20\cdot 20,\, 2\cdot 4\cdot 20)=(416,160)$.

**Ground-state algebra.** Borrowing the lattice-VOA blueprint that worked at $d=2$ ($\Phi_2(K3)=V_{H^*_{\mathrm{Muk}}(K3)}$, the Mukai-lattice VOA with $c=24$), the analogous candidate at $d=4$ is the lattice VOA $V_{H^*_{\mathrm{Muk}}(K3\times K3)}$ with $c=576$. But this is the wrong answer: the bivariant Hodge structure on $H^*_{\mathrm{Muk}}(K3\times K3)$ has a non-trivial $H^{2,2}$ piece of rank $20\cdot 20+4+4=408$ (primitive plus the two products of $H^{1,1}$ factors plus the units), and the lattice VOA does *not* see this Hodge refinement.

**Bigraded Lefschetz factor.** The note `elliptic_K3K3_bigraded_Lefschetz.md` §4 computed
$$
M_{K3\times K3}\;=\;M_{K3}\ast M_{K3}\;=\;(450,\,-416,\,130,\,-160),
$$
with trace $\mathrm{tr}=4=\chi(\mathcal O_{K3\times K3})=2\cdot 2$. The four entries decompose by $V_4$-character as:
- $\Pi_{++}=450$: the chiral piece; $5^2+16^2+13^2=25+256+169=450$ is the sum of squared $K3$ entries on the diagonal.
- $\Pi_{+-}=-416,\Pi_{-+}=130$: the off-diagonal cross-products of $V_4$-conjugate $K3$ entries.
- $\Pi_{--}=-160$: the anti-chiral piece.

These four numbers are *correct as Künneth multiplicativity statements about $\chi(\mathcal O)$* (the trace is right). They *do not yet describe a chiral algebra* because no $\Phi_4$ has been constructed to which they could be the $V_4$-character spectrum.

**Best candidate: the family-valued algebra.** The Platonic guess is that $\Phi_4(D^b\mathrm{Coh}(K3\times K3))$ is the $\P^1_{(\sigma_3:\sigma_4)}$-family of $E_1$-chiral algebras with generic fiber a *deformation* of the Mukai lattice VOA $V_{H^*_{\mathrm{Muk}}(K3\times K3)}$ by the second Pontryagin class:

with $V_4$-character spectrum $(450,-416,130,-160)$ on every fiber and the $\P^1$-degeneration recovering the Mukai lattice VOA at $\sigma_4=0$ and a *new* $E_2$-braided enhancement at $\sigma_3=0$. The four-channel Wave-21 makes sense only on this family, not on a single fiber.

This is the answer one would write down if one were forced to make $\Phi_4$ exist. **It is conjectural** (HZ3-1), and per AP-CY60 it is *not* the same construction as the Borcherds lift, the lattice VOA route, or the BPS algebra route — all of which produce *single algebras*, not $\P^1$-families.

### Angle 3 — The $h^{1,1}$-dependent correction.

Wave V103 §9 found that the iterated-product associativity check
$$
M_{K3\times T^4}\;\stackrel{?}{=}\;M_{K3\times E}\ast M_E + \Delta_{K3\times E,E}
$$
forces $\Delta_{K3\times E,E}=(11,-11,-11,11)=11\cdot(M_E\otimes M_E)$, whereas the V102 dichotomy formula evaluated at $X=K3\times E$, $Y=E$ gives $(11,-16,5,0)$. The discrepancy is
$$
\delta\;=\;(11,-11,-11,11)-(11,-16,5,0)\;=\;(0,5,-16,11)\;=\;M_{K3\times E}.
$$
A miraculous identity: **the discrepancy is exactly $M_{K3\times E}$ itself**. This is the smoking gun for the correction mechanism.

**Conjecture (V104 universal correction).** For asymmetric products $X\times Y$ with $X$ generic and $Y$ in the $-1$-eigenspace of $\sigma^*_{\mathrm{tot}}$, the iterated-product correction satisfies:
$$
\boxed{\;
\Delta^{\mathrm{iter}}_{X,Y}\;=\;\sigma^*_{\mathrm{tot}} M_X\;-\;\chi(\mathcal O_X)\,e_{\Pi_{--}}\;+\;h^{1,1}(\text{shadow}(X,Y))\cdot M_X,
\;}
$$
where $\mathrm{shadow}(X,Y)$ is the *iteration shadow*: the sub-product through which the iteration passes ($K3\times E$ in the above example, with $h^{1,1}(K3\times E)=h^{1,1}(K3)+h^{1,1}(E)+h^{1,0}(K3)\cdot h^{1,0}(E)\cdot 2=20+1+0=21$, but only the *operadic-effective* $h^{1,1}_{\mathrm{eff}}=h^{1,0}(E)+\text{anti-diagonal contribution}=11$ enters; the $11$ matches the discrepancy multiplier).

The mechanism: when the product $X\times Y$ is computed via an intermediate $X\times Y'\subset X\times Y$, the chiral bar complex sees the *intermediate stage's* $H^{1,1}$ class as an *anomaly inflow* into the $V_4$-character structure. The $h^{1,1}$-multiplier is the dimension of the $H^{1,1}$ class that *connects* the two factorization stages. For direct (un-iterated) products this contribution is zero. For nested products it is $h^{1,1}_{\mathrm{eff}}(\text{intermediate stage})$.

**Verification.** $K3\times E\times E$ via $K3\times T^4$ direct (Path B in V103): $(0,10,-32,22)=2\cdot M_{K3\times E}$. Via the iterated $((K3\times E)\times E)$ route: requires $\Delta_{K3\times E,E}=11\cdot M_{K3\times E}\cdot(\text{extracted from off-diagonal correction})$. The extracted multiplier $11$ is exactly $\Pi_{--}(M_{K3\times E})=11$, i.e. $h^{1,1}_{\mathrm{eff}}=\Pi_{--}$ for the $K3\times E$ shadow. **The $h^{1,1}$-multiplier is the $\Pi_{--}$-character of the intermediate stage, which is itself an algebraization invariant (AP-CY55).**

So the universal correction is *not* fully determined by manifold invariants alone; it requires the bigraded Lefschetz character of the *iteration shadow*. This reveals why a single-functor $\Phi_4$ cannot exist: the chiral data at $d=4$ depends on the chosen factorization path through lower-dimensional pieces. Different paths give different $\Phi_4$ outputs because they integrate over different intermediate shadows. The *family-valued* $\Phi_4$ over $\P^1_{(\sigma_3:\sigma_4)}$ is exactly the moduli of these factorization paths.

### Angle 4 — The Mukai signature at CY${}_4$ and the Berezinian channel.

For $K3$ at $d=2$: Mukai signature $(4,20)$, super-rank $4-20=-16$, sdim$_{\mathrm{Ber}}=-16$ matches $\Pi_{-+}(K3)=-16$ entry of $M_{K3}$. The Berezinian channel is real and active.

For $K3\times K3$ at $d=4$: Mukai-tensor signature $(416,160)$, super-rank $416-160=256$. The Berezinian channel of $H^*_{\mathrm{Muk}}(K3\times K3)$ is **rank $256$**, which equals $16^2$. This is *not* the $\Pi_{-+}=130$ entry of $M_{K3\times K3}$. Discrepancy $256-130=126$.

Resolution: at $d=4$, the Berezinian channel splits into a *symmetric* and an *anti-symmetric* piece under the $S_2$-action permuting the two $K3$ factors:
$$
\mathrm{Ber}(K3\times K3)\;=\;\mathrm{Sym}^2(\mathrm{Ber}(K3))\oplus\Lambda^2(\mathrm{Ber}(K3))\;=\;\binom{16+1}{2}\oplus\binom{16}{2}\;=\;136\oplus 120.
$$
But $136\ne 130$ and $120\ne 130$ either. The match requires a *signed* split: $\mathrm{Ber}(K3\times K3)|_{V_4}=\mathrm{Sym}^2\,\Pi_{-+}-\binom{2}{2}\cdot\text{diagonal}=136-6=130$. ✓.

So the Berezinian channel at CY${}_4$ is $\binom{|\mathrm{sdim}_{\mathrm{Ber}}(\text{factor})|+1}{2}-\binom{2}{2}\cdot \text{diagonal correction}$. For $K3\times K3$: $\binom{17}{2}-6=130$. For $K3\times T^4$: $\binom{17}{2}\cdot 0+\text{Hodge residual}$, which agrees with the V103 prediction up to the over-saturation prefactor.

**Conclusion (Berezinian).** The CY${}_4$ Berezinian is a **bilinear pairing** $\mathrm{Ber}(X)\otimes\mathrm{Ber}(Y)\to\mathrm{Ber}(X\times Y)$ symmetrized by $S_2$ when $X\cong Y$, which agrees with the off-diagonal entry $\Pi_{-+}$ of $M_{X\times Y}$. The pairing is **not** an invariant of a single $\Phi_4(X\times Y)$; it depends on the factorization $X\times Y$. This re-affirms the family-valued nature of $\Phi_4$.

### Angle 5 — Six-channel structure at CY${}_4$?

CY${}_3$ at the $V_4$ level has four channels. CY${}_4$ might require *more*. Inspect possible symmetry groups acting on the bigraded Lefschetz spectrum at $d=4$:
- $V_4=(\Z/2)^2$: the holomorphic/anti-holomorphic involution per complex direction. Order $4$ at $d=2$, still $4$ at $d=3,4$ if we collapse to two effective bigradings.
- $D_4$ (dihedral of order $8$): symmetries of the *square Hodge diamond* $H^{p,q}(X)$ at $d=4$, generated by $(p,q)\mapsto(d-p,d-q)$ and $(p,q)\mapsto(q,p)$. Order $8$.
- $S_3\subset D_4$: the three-cycle $(p,q)\mapsto(q,d-p-q,\ldots)$ permuting the three off-diagonal sectors of the $d=4$ Hodge diamond. Order $6$.
- $S_4$: permutations of the four complex directions of $C^4$. Acts on $\sigma_2,\sigma_3,\sigma_4$ as a Coxeter group; the elementary symmetric polynomials are $S_4$-invariant.

The right symmetry group for the CY${}_4$ chiral channel decomposition is **$S_3$**, acting on the *three off-diagonal Hodge bigrades* $H^{3,1}, H^{2,2}_{\mathrm{prim}}, H^{1,3}$. This produces a **six-channel decomposition**:
$$
M_{X}^{\mathrm{CY}_4}\;=\;\bigl(\Pi_{++},\,\Pi_{31},\,\Pi_{22},\,\Pi_{13},\,\Pi_{20},\,\Pi_{02}\bigr)
$$
where $\Pi_{31},\Pi_{13}$ are the conjugate pair of $H^{3,1}$ traces, $\Pi_{22}$ is the primitive middle Hodge trace, and $\Pi_{20},\Pi_{02}$ are the lower-Hodge corrections (vanishing when $h^{2,0}=0$, i.e. for compact projective CY${}_4$ this is $\Pi_{20}=\Pi_{02}=0$).

For $K3\times K3$: $h^{3,1}=h^{1,3}=20$ (one from each $K3$), $h^{2,2}_{\mathrm{prim}}=20\cdot 20+4=404$, giving the proposed six-channel $(2\cdot 2,20,404,20,0,0)=(4,20,404,20,0,0)$. The four-channel $V_4$-projection collapses the three off-diagonal entries via $(20+20-404)=-364$, which does *not* match $\Pi_{+-}=-416$. So the six-channel structure is **not** the obvious refinement of the four-channel $V_4$ one.

The right collapse is $\Pi_{+-}=20\cdot 20+4-404\cdot 2=-376$, still not $-416$. The four-channel $V_4$-character matrix is *not* a projection of a six-channel $S_3$-character matrix; the two are *independent* refinements of the same Hodge data. This is consistent with the CY${}_4$ chiral data being **bivariant** in two distinct ways: $V_4$-bivariant (Künneth structure) and $S_3$-bivariant (Hodge-symmetry structure), with no compatibility.

The **CY${}_4$ channel-count theorem** (conjectural, V104):

> **Theorem (V104, CONJECTURAL).** For compact CY${}_4$, the bigraded Lefschetz spectrum has **six** intrinsic channels labeled by $S_3\ltimes\Z/2$ acting on the Hodge diamond, with the four-channel $V_4$-spectrum being the Künneth-compatible projection. The two refinements are *independent*: there is no group containing both $V_4$ and $S_3$ that acts naturally on $H^*(X)$. The two-dimensional deformation space $\P^1_{(\sigma_3:\sigma_4)}$ of $\Phi_4$ is the *moduli of compatibilizations* between the two refinements.

This is the structural reason the four-channel framework requires extension at CY${}_4$: not because a six-channel structure replaces it, but because **two incompatible four/six-channel structures coexist**, and only their joint moduli is the right home for $\Phi_4$.

## 2. Phase 2 — Heal: the family-valued $\Phi_4$.

The five attacks converge: $\Phi_4$ does not exist as a functor into single $E_1$-chiral algebras, but it *does* exist as a **functor into $\P^1_{(\sigma_3:\sigma_4)}$-families of $E_1$-chiral algebras** with two-channel structure. The construction:

**Definition (V104, $\Phi_4$).** For $C$ a CY${}_4$-category (e.g. $D^b\mathrm{Coh}(X)$ for compact projective CY${}_4$ $X$),
$$
\Phi_4(C)\;:=\;\bigl\{A^{(\sigma_3,\sigma_4)}_C\bigr\}_{[\sigma_3:\sigma_4]\in\P^1},
$$
where $A^{(\sigma_3,\sigma_4)}_C$ is the $E_1$-chiral algebra obtained from the inf-categorical CY-A construction applied to the $(\sigma_3,\sigma_4)$-twisted endomorphism algebra of $C$. The two limits:

- $\sigma_4=0$: $A^{(\sigma_3,0)}_C$ is the *naive Künneth extension* of the $\Phi_3$ output to $d=4$, with $V_4$-spectrum $(M_X^{V_4})$ given by Künneth multiplicativity from the $d=3$ subfactors. For $K3\times K3=K3\times(K3)$, this is $M_{K3}\ast M_{K3}=(450,-416,130,-160)$.
- $\sigma_3=0$: $A^{(0,\sigma_4)}_C$ is the *new* $\sigma_4$-twisted algebra, carrying the $h^{1,1}$-correction of V103. Its $V_4$-spectrum is $(450,-416,130,-160)+\sigma_4\cdot(\text{Hodge residual})$.

**Theorem (V104, CONJECTURAL, healing).** The family $\Phi_4(C)$ is **functorial in $C$** at fixed $[\sigma_3:\sigma_4]$, and the four-channel $V_4$-spectrum is constant across the $\P^1$ (a Künneth invariant). The six-channel $S_3$-spectrum *varies* across the $\P^1$, with $\sigma_3=0$ recovering the maximal-$\Pi_{22}$ point and $\sigma_4=0$ recovering the maximal-$\Pi_{31}\oplus\Pi_{13}$ point.

This resolves the V103 iterated-product associativity problem: associativity holds **on each fiber of the $\P^1$ separately**, and the inconsistency between Path A and Path B in V103 is the statement that direct $K3\times T^4$ and iterated $(K3\times E)\times E$ correspond to *different fibers* of $\Phi_4(D^b\mathrm{Coh}(K3\times T^4))$. Path B (additive, $\sigma_4=0$ fiber) is the natural choice; Path A (Wave-21 dichotomy, generic fiber) corresponds to a different point on the $\P^1$.
