\section*{Compact CoHA Scope for $K3\times E$}

\paragraph{The quiver theorem.}
Davison and Meinhardt construct the cohomological Hall algebra
$\CoHA_{Q,W,\theta}$ attached to a quiver with potential $(Q,W)$, a
King stability parameter $\theta$, and a fixed slope. The input is the
Jacobi algebra $\Jac(Q,W)$ and the stack
$\Mod^{\theta\text{-ss}}_{\Jac}$ of semistable representations. Their
PBW theorem gives a canonical associated-graded description in mixed
Hodge modules,
\[
\operatorname{gr}\CoHA^{\theta,\mu}
  \cong
  \operatorname{Sym}\!\left(
    \gBPS^{\theta,\mu}\otimes H^*(\mathrm{B}\mathbb{G}_m)
  \right),
\]
where $\gBPS^{\theta,\mu}$ is the BPS sheaf on the coarse moduli of
polystable objects. This is a global quiver-with-potential critical-stack
theorem: the stack is presented as $\operatorname{crit}(W)/G$ for a
regular function $W$, and the PBW filtration is built from the
semisimplification map and its perverse filtration. Compact sheaf moduli
on $\K\times E$ require a separate critical-Hall construction before the
quiver theorem has an object on which to act.

\paragraph{The compact $K3\times E$ obstruction.}
The derived moduli stack of sheaves on a smooth CY$_3$ has a
$(-1)$-shifted symplectic structure by Pantev, To\"en, Vaqui\'e, and
Vezzosi, and the Brav, Bussi, Dupont, Joyce, and Szendr\H{o}i theory
supplies local d-critical charts. A compact critical CoHA on
$\K\times E$ is a stronger datum than this local atlas. It requires:
\[
\begin{gathered}
\text{a finite-type stability chamber on }\Db\Coh(\K\times E),\\
\text{algebraic orientation data for the d-critical moduli stack},\\
\text{vanishing-cycle sheaves }\phW\text{ compatible with Hall correspondences},\\
\text{a primitive BPS Lie bracket compatible with Serre duality},\\
\text{a negative half, Hopf pairing, completion, and double.}
\end{gathered}
\]
Bridgeland stability on the K3 factor is supplied by Bridgeland, Bayer,
and Macr\`i. The product threefold still needs compatibility with
the critical-Hall package above. Serre duality on $\Db\Coh(\K\times E)$
follows from $\omega_{\K\times E}\simeq\cO$; the PBW theorem for compact
sheaf moduli begins only after this categorical duality is promoted to
Hall correspondences and primitive brackets.

\paragraph{The translation direction.}
For a generic K3 surface, $H^0(T_{\K})=0$, while
$H^0(T_E)=\mathbb{C}$. K\"unneth gives
\[
H^0(T_{\K\times E})
 =
H^0(T_\K)\otimes H^0(\cO_E)
\oplus
H^0(\cO_\K)\otimes H^0(T_E)
\cong \mathbb{C}.
\]
This vector field generates the elliptic-translation direction in the
moduli problem. The symmetric obstruction theory is still governed by
$\operatorname{ext}^1(\mathcal E,\mathcal E)-
\operatorname{ext}^2(\mathcal E,\mathcal E)$. The translation direction
belongs to the ambient action of $E$ on the support. The reduced
Donaldson-Thomas theory of Oberdieck and Pandharipande removes this
trivial factor or treats it equivariantly.

\paragraph{What is available.}
Davison's preprojective-stack theorem identifies the BPS algebra of
$S\times\mathbb{C}$, for $S$ a smooth quasi-projective surface, with the
K-theoretic CoHA on $S$. This covers local $K3\times\mathbb{C}$-type
geometry. Compact $\K\times E$ passes through the oriented 3-CY
categorical CoHA framework of Kinjo, Park, and Safronov. Conditional on
strong orientation data and Hall-compatible vanishing cycles, this
framework defines the compact positive Hall half. The filtered PBW
identification $\operatorname{gr}\CoHA\simeq
\operatorname{Sym}(\gBPS\otimes H^*(\mathrm{B}\mathbb G_m))$ for that
compact CoHA remains a proof obligation. The stronger equality
$\CoHA\simeq U(\gBPS)$ requires the corresponding primitive Lie algebra
and its no-extra-relations theorem.

\paragraph{Native $E_1$ output.}
The CY-to-chiral output at $d=3$ is native $E_1$ on the reference curve.
For $X=\K\times E$, the Stage-2 specialisation integrates over the K3
factor and leaves an $E_1$-chiral algebra on $E$:
\[
 \int_{\K}^{\mathrm{hol}} E_3\text{-HolFA}(X)
   \longrightarrow E_1\text{-HolFA}(E).
\]
The braided $E_2$ structure lives on the Drinfeld centre
$\cZ(\Rep^{E_1}(A_E))$ of the resulting $E_1$ algebra. This centre
construction is separate from the positive compact Hall half and from the
Hall-Drinfeld double.

\paragraph{Hall-Drinfeld and Yangian branches.}
The BKM-side object attached to $K3\times E$ is the Hall-Drinfeld double
of the compact positive Hall half,
\[
  \cD_\hbar\bigl(\CoHA^+_{\K\times E}\bigr),
\]
after the negative half, Hopf pairing, completion, and finite recognition
data have been supplied. The K3 self-mirror Yangian
$Y(\fg_{\K})$ lives on the Mukai lattice
$\Lambda_{\mathrm{Muk}}\cong \mathrm{II}_{4,20}$ and belongs to the
ADE/Kummer stable-envelope branch. The two branches share K3 lattice
input and diverge at construction: compact CY$_3$ Hall double on one
side, Mukai self-mirror Yangian on the other.

\paragraph{Four invariants.}
The four numerical values attached to $K3\times E$ occupy four
construction slots:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{slot} & \text{definition} & \text{value}\\ \hline
\kcat(\K\times E) &
  \chi(\cO_{\K\times E})=\chi(\cO_\K)\chi(\cO_E) &
  2\cdot 0 = 0\\
\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(\K\times E) &
  \text{rank of the Heisenberg-Cartan }E_1\text{ shadow on }E &
  3\\
\kBKM(\Delta_5) &
  \text{Borcherds weight of the Gritsenko denominator} &
  c_1(0)/2=10/2=5\\
\kfib(\K) &
  \operatorname{rk} H^\bullet(\K,\mathbb Z) &
  24
\end{array}
\]
The compact total-space Hodge supertrace also vanishes:
\[
 \sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(\K\times E)
 = (1+1)(1-1)=0.
\]
The value $3$ is the Heisenberg specialisation on the residual elliptic
curve, the value $5$ is an automorphic weight, and the value $24$ is the
Mukai rank of the K3 fibre.

\paragraph{Automorphic scalar.}
The Borcherds scalar
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_1)=c_1(0)/2=10/2=5
\]
comes from the Gritsenko and Nikulin weak Jacobi input and Borcherds's
weight formula for the denominator form $\Delta_5=\Phi_1$. In the
physics Igusa convention, $\chi_{10}=(\Delta_5)^2$ has weight $10$; the
primitive BKM denominator uses the square root $\Delta_5$ of weight $5$.
On the primitive CHL ladder
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
N & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 6\\ \hline
c_N(0) & 10 & 8 & 6 & 4 & 2\\
\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2 & 5 & 4 & 3 & 2 & 1
\end{array}
\]
This table has primitive CHL scope. The Gritsenko-Cl\'ery eight-form
catalogue has its own levels, multiplier systems, and constants.

A compact Hall-to-Borcherds theorem for $\K\times E$ must identify the
primitive BPS multiplicities, Serre signs, brackets, coproduct, and
completion with the root data of $\fg_{\Delta_5}$. The scalar equality
$\kBKM(\Delta_5)=5$ supplies the automorphic normalisation alone.
