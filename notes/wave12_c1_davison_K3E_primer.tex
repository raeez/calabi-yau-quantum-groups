\section*{Cycle 1 --- What Davison 2015 / DM 2020 covers}

The paper cited in \S3.2 of the treatise as ``Davison 2017 \texttt{arXiv:1601.02010}'' is a typo for Davison--Meinhardt \texttt{arXiv:1601.02479}, to appear Invent. Math.\ 221 (2020). The scope declared in the abstract and body is \emph{narrow}: it constructs and studies the cohomological Hall algebra $\CoHA_{Q,W,\theta}$ of a \emph{quiver with potential} $(Q,W)$, stability $\theta$, and slope class. Its geometric input is the Jacobi algebra $\Jac(Q,W)$ and its semistable representation stack $\Mod^{\theta\text{-ss}}_{\Jac}$. The paper does \emph{not} construct a CoHA on moduli of sheaves on a proper CY$_3$ such as $\K \times E$. K3$\times$E is nowhere in the hypotheses. The PBW isomorphism stated there is an isomorphism of bigraded mixed Hodge modules
\[
\CoHA^{\theta,\mu} \,\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\, \mathrm{Sym}\!\left(\gBPS^{\theta,\mu}\otimes H^*(\mathrm{B}\mathbb{G}_m)\right)
\]
with $\gBPS^{\theta,\mu}$ a mixed Hodge module concentrated on the coarse moduli of polystable objects; the associativity of $\CoHA$ degenerates to the free supercommutative algebra on $\gBPS\otimes H^*(\mathrm{B}\mathbb{G}_m)$.

\section*{Cycle 2 --- Stability condition / t-structure requirement}

DM 2020 requires a \emph{King-type generic stability} $\theta$ and a fixed slope $\mu$. The ``hidden properness'' of the semisimplification map $\Mod^{\theta\text{-ss}}\!\to\!\Mod^{\theta\text{-pst}}$ is what produces the perverse filtration. On $\K\times E$, Bayer--Macr\`i (Invent. Math.\ 198, 2014) and Bridgeland (Ann.\ Math.\ 166, 2008) guarantee existence of Bridgeland stability on $D^b\K$; the product stability on $D^b(\K\times E)$ is supplied by Bayer--Macr\`i base-change plus Toda pullback arguments. Whether a Bridgeland stability admits a \emph{global critical-locus presentation} of $\Mod^{\mathrm{st}}(\K\times E)$ as a Jacobi stack is the primary obstruction: the DM 2020 setup demands $\Mod=\mathrm{crit}(W)/G$ with $W$ a \emph{regular} function, not a local Kuranishi patch. On $\K\times E$, the Brav--Bussi--Dupont--Joyce--Szendr\H{o}i d-critical structure (Publ. IHES 129, 2019) supplies this only locally analytically; globalisation to the DM 2020 regular-function hypothesis requires orientation data \emph{\`a la} Joyce--Upmeier 2021, whose existence on $\Mod^{\mathrm{st}}(\K\times E)$ is established but not algebraic in the DM sense.

\section*{Cycle 3 --- PBW $\CoHA = U(\gBPS)$ and primitive elements}

DM 2020 Theorem~A gives $\CoHA \simeq U(\gBPS)$ as \emph{filtered} algebras, with $\gBPS$ a Lie algebra in mixed Hodge modules, canonically attached to the primitive part of the perverse filtration. For quivers this is canonical. On $\K\times E$, the analogous statement requires three inputs none of which is in DM 2020: (i) a moduli stack $\Mod(\K\times E,v)$ with a global d-critical chart and orientation data; (ii) a sheaf $\phW\cdot\mathrm{IC}_{\Mod}$ with DT-perverse structure (Brav--Bussi--Dupont--Joyce--Szendr\H{o}i); (iii) Serre self-duality of the category $\mathrm{Coh}(\K\times E)$ compatibly promoting the CoHA product to the bialgebra structure on which Joyce's Lie bracket on $\gBPS$ is defined. Serre duality on $D^b(\K\times E)$ is canonical via $\omega_{\K\times E}\simeq\mathcal{O}$ (since $c_1=0$ and the canonical bundle is trivial); but the Serre-self-dual \emph{categorification} required for the DM 2020 PBW isomorphism is a statement about moduli stacks, not the base variety, and has been proved in this generality only for quiver moduli and for 2-CY categories satisfying a ``totally negative'' hypothesis (Davison--Hennecart--Schlegel Mejia \texttt{arXiv:2212.07668}), which $\mathrm{Coh}(\K)$ does \emph{not} satisfy (Mukai lattice $\mathrm{II}_{4,20}$ has positive-rank subspace).

\section*{Cycle 4 --- Virtual dimension and $H^0(T_{\K\times E})$}

For a CY$_3$ $X$ with trivial canonical bundle, the virtual dimension at $[\mathcal{E}]\in\Mod^{\mathrm{st}}(X,v)$ is $\mathrm{ext}^1(\mathcal{E},\mathcal{E})-\mathrm{ext}^2(\mathcal{E},\mathcal{E})$. On $\K\times E$ this is correct: the shifted symplectic structure at degree $-1$ exists by PTVV, and the symmetric obstruction theory is produced by Brav--Bussi--Joyce. The concern ``$H^0(T_{\K\times E})\ne0$ spoils virtual dimension'' is misplaced at the virtual-dimension level: $H^0(T_X)$ controls $\mathrm{Aut}(X)$, not $\mathrm{Ext}^i(\mathcal{E},\mathcal{E})$. On generic $\K$, $H^0(T_\K)=0$ (Mukai); on $E$, $H^0(T_E)=\mathbb{C}$ (the translation vector field). K\"unneth gives $H^0(T_{\K\times E})=H^0(T_\K)\otimes H^0(\mathcal{O}_E)\oplus H^0(\mathcal{O}_\K)\otimes H^0(T_E)=0\oplus\mathbb{C}=\mathbb{C}$ (consistent with \texttt{chapters/theory/drinfeld\_center.tex:1483}). This non-vanishing produces a $1$-parameter $E$-translation action on $\Mod^{\mathrm{st}}(\K\times E,v)$, which must be quotiented (or used equivariantly) to obtain finite-type reduced moduli, and is the reason the programme uses $E[N]$-equivariant cohomology (\texttt{CoHA\_to\_W\_infty\_treatise.tex:517}). It does \emph{not} break the virtual class; it shifts the expected dimension by $1$ along the $E$-translation direction, and the Oberdieck--Pandharipande ``reduced'' DT theory on $\K\times E$ (arXiv:1411.1514) is precisely the removal of this trivial factor.

\section*{Cycle 5 --- Davison 2023 refinement and what is actually available}

Davison \texttt{arXiv:1602.02110} (J. Reine Angew. Math.\ 804, 2023) generalises DM 2020 to preprojective stacks and proves the integrality conjecture for them. Its Corollary~5.7 gives an identification: for $S$ a smooth quasi-projective surface, the BPS algebra of $S\times\mathbb{C}$ (CY$_3$ of local type) equals the K-theoretic CoHA on $S$. This \emph{does not} cover compact $\K\times E$, only local $\K\times\mathbb{C}$. The \emph{compact-fibre} case $\K\times E$ remains outside the strict DM-2020 plus Davison-2023 framework. The status-of-the-art on compact CY$_3$ CoHA as of Kinjo--Park--Safronov \texttt{arXiv:2406.12838} (2024) establishes CoHA for 3-CY categories \emph{admitting strong orientation data}; the existence of strong orientation data on $D^b(\K\times E)$ is a theorem of Joyce--Upmeier (Mem.\ AMS, 2021) in complex-analytic setting, and Bojko--Kuhn--Liu (2024+) for algebraic-geometric orientation. Combining these gives a CoHA construction on $\K\times E$ \emph{conditional on algebraic orientation data}; the PBW identification $\CoHA=U(\gBPS)$ for this CoHA has not been proven in the literature. \S3.2 of the treatise must therefore cite the chain (DM 2020) $+$ (Davison 2023) $+$ (Joyce--Upmeier 2021) $+$ (KPS 2024) as the multi-input route to $\CoHA(\K\times E)$, not a single-citation Davison-2017 black box. The numerical identity $\dim\gBPS_{\gamma}(\K\times E)=\mathrm{mult}_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\alpha_\gamma)=c_1(0)/2\cdot[\text{Gritsenko}]$ at $N{=}1$ remains the Oberdieck--Pixton theorem (Invent.\ Math.\ 222, 2020), independent of the Lie-level identification.

\section*{Verdict: applicability audit}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Davison--Meinhardt 2020 \emph{does not directly apply} to $\K\times E$. Its theorem is a quiver-with-potential PBW; the moduli input on a compact CY$_3$ is not in scope.
\item Bridgeland stability on $D^b(\K\times E)$ \emph{exists} (Bayer--Macr\`i + Toda). A global regular-function critical presentation is \emph{not} available; only a d-critical atlas (BBDJS).
\item The PBW $\CoHA=U(\gBPS)$ statement on $\K\times E$ is a conjecture with three independent prerequisites: algebraic orientation data (Bojko--Kuhn--Liu, conditional), strong orientation data on the 3-CY category (KPS 2024), and Serre-self-dual categorification in the DM sense (open on compact CY$_3$).
\item The $H^0(T_{\K\times E})=\mathbb{C}$ non-vanishing is \emph{not} a virtual-dimension obstruction; it is exactly the $E$-translation direction removed by the Oberdieck--Pandharipande reduced theory.
\item Davison 2023 (preprojective stacks) and DHSM 2022 (totally-negative 2-CY) refine DM 2020 but leave $\K\times E$ compact-CY$_3$ outside strict hypotheses.
\end{enumerate}

The treatise citation ``Davison 2017'' at \texttt{chapters/theory/cy\_categories.tex:286--291} and \texttt{chapters/examples/cy\_c\_beyond\_k3e\_existence\_obstruction.tex:318,\,755,\,4009} should be rectified to read the multi-input chain, and the arXiv id 1601.02010 should be replaced with 1601.02479 throughout. The claim $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_{10})=5$ is Gritsenko--Nikulin plus Borcherds weight $c_1(0)/2$; it does not require the PBW theorem to be valid on $\K\times E$.
