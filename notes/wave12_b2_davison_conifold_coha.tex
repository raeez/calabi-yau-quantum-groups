\section*{Conifold Critical CoHA and Its BPS Lie Algebra}
\label{sec:davison-conifold-coha}

\paragraph{Conifold Jacobi algebra.}
Let
\[
  X_{\mathrm{con}}=\mathrm{Tot}\bigl(\cO_{\bP^1}(-1)\oplus
  \cO_{\bP^1}(-1)\bigr)
\]
be the resolved conifold, the small resolution of the ordinary double
point. The affine contraction
\[
  \Spec \C[x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4]/(x_1x_2-x_3x_4)
\]
has a noncommutative crepant resolution given by the Jacobi algebra of
the Klebanov--Witten quiver \(Q\) with vertices \(Q_0=\{0,1\}\), arrows
\[
  a_1,a_2\colon 0\to 1,\qquad b_1,b_2\colon 1\to 0,
\]
and potential
\[
  W=\tr(a_1 b_1 a_2 b_2-a_1 b_2 a_2 b_1).
\]
The algebra
\[
  J_{\mathrm{con}}=\Jac(Q,W)
  =\C Q/\bigl(\partial_{a_i}W,\partial_{b_i}W: i=1,2\bigr)
\]
has centre
\(\C[x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4]/(x_1x_2-x_3x_4)\), the ordinary double
point. The compact exceptional locus of \(X_{\mathrm{con}}\) is the
curve \(\bP^1\), and the normal bundle has rank two. Formulas for
\(\mathrm{Tot}(K_S)\) over a Fano surface \(S\) belong to a separate
toric family.

\paragraph{Critical CoHA.}
For a dimension vector \(\gamma=(\gamma_0,\gamma_1)\in\mathbb N^2\), let
\[
  \Rep_\gamma(Q)=
  \bigoplus_{a\in Q_1}\mathrm{Hom}\bigl(\C^{\gamma_{s(a)}},
  \C^{\gamma_{t(a)}}\bigr),\qquad
  G_\gamma=\mathrm{GL}_{\gamma_0}\times\mathrm{GL}_{\gamma_1}.
\]
The trace of the potential gives a \(G_\gamma\)-invariant function
\(\tr(W)_\gamma\) on \(\Rep_\gamma(Q)\). The conifold critical
cohomological Hall algebra is
\[
  \cH_{Q,W}
  =\bigoplus_{\gamma\in\mathbb N^2}
  H^{*}_{c,G_\gamma}\bigl(\Rep_\gamma(Q),
  \varphi_{\tr(W)_\gamma}\bigr)^\vee ,
\]
with the Kontsevich--Soibelman Hall product. Davison's critical-CoHA
construction gives the localized coproduct for \(\cH_{Q,W}\), and the
Davison--Meinhardt PBW theorem identifies the primitive mixed Hodge
structure with the BPS Lie algebra \(\fg_{\BPS}^{Q,W}\).

\paragraph{Stability chambers.}
The same quiver supports three different structures. The algebra
\(\cH_{Q,W}\) is the unframed critical CoHA. Szendr\H{o}i's
non-commutative DT partition function counts framed cyclic
\(J_{\mathrm{con}}\)-modules in the chamber attached to the
non-commutative resolution; its torus-fixed basis is the pyramid
partition basis. The Morrison--Mozgovoy--Nagao--Szendr\H{o}i chamber
formula computes the motivic DT series for every generic stability
vector \(\zeta\). These chamber series extract refined BPS
multiplicities; the CoHA multiplication remains separate data.

\paragraph{Root data.}
The positive roots are the affine \(A_1^{(1)}\) roots
\[
  \Delta_+^{\mathrm{re}}
  =\{(n+1,n),(n,n+1):n\geq 0\},\qquad
  \Delta_+^{\mathrm{im}}=\{(n,n):n\geq 1\}.
\]
In the numerical convention used here, the primitive BPS supertraces are
\[
  \DT(n+1,n)=\DT(n,n+1)=1,\qquad
  \DT(n,n)=-2.
\]
Each real-root primitive sector is one-dimensional. The imaginary sector
contributes two Tate summands; after the Davison cohomological shift its
numerical supertrace is \(-2\). The two-dimensional imaginary sector is
visible already at charge \((1,1)\).

\paragraph{Low charges.}
At the two simple real roots one has
\[
  \BPS_{(1,0)}=\C\langle e_0\rangle,\qquad
  \BPS_{(0,1)}=\C\langle f_0\rangle .
\]
At the first imaginary root, dimensional reduction to the compact curve
stratum gives, before the global shift, the two-generator vector space
\[
  \BPS_{(1,1)}
  \cong H^*(\bP^1)
  =\C\langle h_1^{\mathrm{pt}}\rangle
  \oplus \C\langle h_1^{\mathrm{fund}}\rangle .
\]
The first primitive bracket lands in this sector:
\[
  [e_0,f_0]_{\fg_{\BPS}}\in \BPS_{(1,1)}.
\]
The Davison shift accounts for the sign in \(\DT(1,1)=-2\). After
parity-forgetting and traceless projection, the bracket gives the usual
Cartan direction \(h_1^{\mathrm{pt}}-h_1^{\mathrm{fund}}\). The conifold
BPS mixed Hodge structure retains both imaginary generators before that
projection.

\paragraph{Affine and super forms.}
The affine \(A_1^{(1)}\) root diagram records the real-root strings
\((n+1,n)\) and \((n,n+1)\). The literal traceless
\(\widehat{\fsl}_2\) quotient records only one Cartan direction at each
positive imaginary level. The conifold CoHA primitive structure before
this quotient has the two-dimensional imaginary sector above. Its
low-charge comparison target is therefore a
\(\widehat{\fgl}(1|1)\)-type super form. A Yangian-type or
vertex-algebra comparison requires a Hall pairing, completion, and
double or centre construction.

\paragraph{Comparison with \(\C^3\).}
The identity
\[
  \CoHA(\C^3)=Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)
\]
belongs to the one-vertex, three-loop affine chart. The conifold uses
the two-vertex Jacobi algebra \(J_{\mathrm{con}}\) and its own critical
CoHA. The associative conifold CoHA itself is the Hall-side input.
A \(\mathcal W\)-algebra realisation requires a Drinfeld double, derived
centre, or representation-theoretic construction.

\paragraph{Chiral modular characteristic.}
The invariant computed by this conifold CoHA calculation is
\[
  \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(X_{\mathrm{con}})
  =\DT(1,0)=1.
\]
The direct McKay/Jacobi-algebra route gives the same number: the simple
root \((1,0)\) contributes one primitive generator.
The categorical invariant \(\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}\), the Borcherds
weight invariant \(\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}=c_N(0)/2\), and the fibre
invariant \(\kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}\) are different constructions and
are absent from this local conifold computation. When the
CY-to-chiral functor is specialised at \(d=3\), its output is \(E_1\);
any \(E_2\) structure belongs to a centre, double, or module layer.
