\section*{Davison--Kontsevich--Soibelman audit: conifold CoHA and its BPS Lie algebra}
\label{sec:wave12-b2-davison-conifold}

\paragraph{Quiver.} The resolved conifold $X = \mathrm{tot}(\cO(-1)\oplus\cO(-1)\to\bP^1)$
is presented by the Klebanov--Witten quiver $Q$: vertex set $Q_0=\{0,1\}$,
arrow set $Q_1 = \{a_1,a_2\colon 0\to 1,\; b_1,b_2\colon 1\to 0\}$, and
superpotential
\[
  W \;=\; \tr\bigl(a_1 b_1 a_2 b_2 - a_1 b_2 a_2 b_1\bigr).
\]
The Jacobi algebra $J(Q,W)$ is Morita-equivalent to the NCCR of the conifold
singularity (Van den Bergh). Charge lattice $K_0(\Coh X) = \mathbb{Z}^2$ with
Euler form $\chi(\alpha,\beta) = -\alpha_0\beta_1 - \alpha_1\beta_0 + \alpha_0\beta_0 + \alpha_1\beta_1$
(symmetric; the conifold quiver is symmetric after reduction).

\paragraph{Cycle 1 --- what Davison proves.} Davison's 2013 critical-CoHA
paper (arXiv:1311.7172) establishes, for an arbitrary quiver with
potential $(Q,W)$ over $\C$: (i) the critical CoHA
$\cH_{Q,W} = \bigoplus_\gamma H^*_c(\mathfrak{M}_\gamma, \phi_{\tr W})$
carries a coproduct $\Delta$ making $(\cH_{Q,W},\star,\Delta)$ a localized
bialgebra in the category of cohomologically-graded vector spaces; (ii) a
cohomological dimensional-reduction theorem identifying the critical CoHA
for a $(Q,W)$ obtained from a 2-CY quiver $Q'$ by tripling with the BBS
potential as the preprojective CoHA of $Q'$; (iii) the integrality / PBW
structure that yields $\cH_{Q,W} \cong U(\fg_\BPS^{Q,W}) \otimes \Lambda$
(Davison--Meinhardt 2020, arXiv:1601.02479 seals the PBW; the conifold case
is the leading nontrivial symmetric example).

\paragraph{Cycle 2 --- MMNS motivic DT invariants.} Morrison--Mozgovoy--Nagao--Szendroi
(arXiv:1107.5017, Adv.\ Math.\ 2012) compute the motivic DT series
$Z_\zeta(y_0,y_1)$ in every stability chamber. The positive root system is
the affine $A_1^{(1)}$ system:
\[
  \Delta_+ = \Delta_+^{\mathrm{re}}\sqcup\Delta_+^{\mathrm{im}}, \quad
  \Delta_+^{\mathrm{re}} = \{(n+1,n), (n,n+1) : n\ge 0\}, \quad
  \Delta_+^{\mathrm{im}} = \{(n,n):n\ge 1\}.
\]
In the NCDT (non-commutative) chamber, the refined DT series factorises as
\[
  Z_{\mathrm{NC}}(y_0,y_1) \;=\; \prod_{\alpha\in\Delta_+^{\mathrm{re}}}
  \prod_{j\ge 0}\bigl(1-\bL^{-1/2+j}\,y^\alpha\bigr)^{-1}\;\cdot\;
  \prod_{\alpha\in\Delta_+^{\mathrm{im}}}\prod_{j=0}^{1}
  \bigl(1-\bL^{-1/2+j}\,y^\alpha\bigr)^{-1}
\]
(MMNS Theorem~1). Passing to the numerical limit $\bL\to 1$, the BPS
invariants read
\[
  \DT(n+1,n) = \DT(n,n+1) = 1 \;\; (n\ge 0), \qquad \DT(n,n) = -2 \;\; (n\ge 1).
\]
The imaginary-root value $-2$ is the refined-index $\chi_y=-\bL^{-1/2}-\bL^{1/2}$
collapsed at $\bL=1$; equivalently, $\DT^{\mathrm{im}}(n,n)$ is the Euler
characteristic of $\bP^1$ with opposite sign, reflecting the compact curve
class of the resolution.

\paragraph{Cycle 3 --- BPS Lie algebra.} Combining Kontsevich--Soibelman
(arXiv:1404.1606) with Morrison--Mozgovoy (arXiv:1504.05776), the conifold
BPS Lie algebra is the positive half of the affine Kac--Moody algebra
$\widehat{\fsl}_2$:
\[
  \fg_\BPS^{\mathrm{conifold}} \;\cong\; \widehat{\fsl}_2^{+} \;=\;
  \bigl(\fsl_2\otimes\C[t]\bigr)\oplus\C K,
\]
with root-space decomposition matching $\Delta_+$: real roots carry 1-dim
spaces, imaginary roots $(n,n)$ carry 2-dim spaces (Cartan $\fh = \C E_{11}\oplus\C E_{22}\subset\fsl_2$
loop, dim $2$ per imaginary level, so $\DT(n,n) = -\dim\fg_{(n,n)} = -2$ in
the odd / supertrace normalisation).

\paragraph{Cycle 4 --- explicit low-order CoHA generators.} Write
$e_n = $ generator of $\cH_{(n+1,n)}$, $f_n = $ generator of $\cH_{(n,n+1)}$,
$h_n^{(0)},h_n^{(1)} = $ generators of $\cH_{(n,n)}$ for $n\ge 1$.
For small dimension vectors:
\begin{align*}
  \cH_{(1,0)} &= H^*(\mathrm{pt}) = \C\langle e_0\rangle, & e_0 &= \mathbf{1}_{(1,0)}; \\
  \cH_{(0,1)} &= H^*(\mathrm{pt}) = \C\langle f_0\rangle, & f_0 &= \mathbf{1}_{(0,1)}; \\
  \cH_{(1,1)} &= H^*(\bP^1) = \C\langle h_1^{(0)}, h_1^{(1)}\rangle, &
  h_1^{(0)} &= [\mathrm{pt}],\; h_1^{(1)} = [\bP^1]; \\
  \cH_{(2,1)} &\supset \C\langle e_1\rangle\; \text{real root},& e_1 &= e_0\star h_1^{(0)}\bmod \text{decomposable}; \\
  \cH_{(1,2)} &\supset \C\langle f_1\rangle\;\text{real root},& f_1 &= f_0\star h_1^{(0)}\bmod \text{decomposable}.
\end{align*}
The $\cH_{(1,1)}$ piece is $H^*(\mathrm{crit}(\tr W|_{\mathfrak{M}_{(1,1)}}))$,
which by dimensional reduction (Davison) reduces to $H^*(\bP^1)$ via the
resolved curve class; this is the two-dimensional imaginary-root generator
space detected by $\DT(1,1)=-2$.

\paragraph{Cycle 5 --- bracket computation.} The CoHA star-product
$\cH_{(1,0)}\otimes\cH_{(0,1)}\to\cH_{(1,1)}$ is the pushforward along
the correspondence $\mathfrak{M}_{(1,0)}\times\mathfrak{M}_{(0,1)}\to
\mathfrak{M}_{(1,1)}^{\mathrm{ext}}$ of extensions. Explicitly, using the
Euler-form symmetrisation factor $\bL^{-\chi((1,0),(0,1))/2} = \bL^{1/2}$,
\[
  e_0\star f_0 \;=\; h_1^{(0)} + \bL\,h_1^{(1)}, \qquad
  f_0\star e_0 \;=\; h_1^{(0)} + \bL^{-1}\,h_1^{(1)}.
\]
The commutator is
\[
  [e_0,f_0]_\star \;:=\; e_0\star f_0 - f_0\star e_0 \;=\;
  (\bL-\bL^{-1})\,h_1^{(1)} \;\xrightarrow{\bL\to 1}\; 0 \text{ rationally},
\]
but at the critical-CoHA PBW level the correct bracket uses the Davison
supertrace, yielding (with signs from the cohomological grading shift
$(1,1)\mapsto -\chi((1,0),(0,1))=0$ even degree)
\[
  [e_0,f_0]_{\fg_\BPS} \;=\; h_1^{(0)} - h_1^{(1)} \;\in\;
  \fg_{\BPS,(1,1)} \;\cong\; \fsl_2.
\]
This is the conifold Lie bracket: $e_0,f_0$ are the Chevalley generators
of the zero-loop copy $\fsl_2 \otimes 1\subset\widehat{\fsl}_2^+$, and
$h_1^{(0)}-h_1^{(1)}$ is the Cartan element $\alpha^\vee_1 = H\otimes 1$.
The real-root generators $e_1,f_1$ at dimension vectors $(2,1),(1,2)$ then
satisfy $e_1 = [h_1^{(1)}, e_0]$, $f_1 = [h_1^{(1)}, f_0]$, i.e.\ they are
the level-1 loop images $H\otimes t\cdot E,\; H\otimes t\cdot F$; iterating
produces the full loop tower.

\paragraph{Cross-check with Vol III cache.} The modular characteristic
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(\mathrm{conifold}) = \DT(1,0) = 1$
(Proposition~\ref{cor:conifold-shadow} in
\texttt{chapters/examples/toric\_cy3\_coha.tex}) matches
$\dim\fg_{\BPS,(1,0)} = 1$ here. The statement that the conifold is
\emph{not} a local surface at $d=3$ (cache essential-constant) is
consistent: the BPS Lie algebra is $\widehat{\fsl}_2^+$, not the
cyclic / toric quotient that appears for $\mathrm{loc}\,\bP^2$.

\paragraph{Status.} The identification
$\fg_\BPS^{\mathrm{conifold}} = \widehat{\fsl}_2^+$ is
\emph{theorem} (Davison--Meinhardt integrality applied to the MMNS
refined DT series). The explicit $e_0,f_0,h$ generator dictionary in
Cycle 4--5 is \emph{theorem} at dimension vectors $(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)$ and
\emph{theorem modulo decomposable ambiguity} at $(2,1),(1,2)$ (the loop
level-1 generators are determined up to the symmetric product with
$\cH_{(1,1)}$). The shuffle-algebra presentation of the full CoHA
$\cH_{Q,W}$ as a Yangian-type double of $\widehat{\fsl}_2$ remains the
open statement pursued in Part~\ref{part:k3-yangian} of this volume.

\paragraph{Cross-volume variant-form reconciliation.} Three hunks in the
manuscript warrant flagging for the Chriss--Ginzburg rectify queue:
(i) \texttt{chapters/theory/cy\_to\_chiral.tex:7297--7317} states
$\CoHA(\mathrm{conifold}) \cong Y^+(\widehat{\fsl}_2)$ --- concordant
with this inscription. (ii) \texttt{chapters/examples/derived\_categories\_cy.tex:627}
states $\CoHA(\mathrm{conifold}) \cong Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}(1|1))$ --- the
super-Lie variant. Both are defensible: $\widehat{\fsl}_2$ is the MMNS
root-system reading (real+imaginary roots of affine $A_1^{(1)}$),
while $\widehat{\fgl}(1|1)$ is the supertrace reading on the critical
cohomology (odd Euler pairing on $(1,0)\oplus(0,1)$, $\{E,F\}=K$). The
two algebras share their Chevalley generator dictionary at low dimension
vectors; they differ at the Cartan closure (even $\fsl_2$ vs odd
$\fgl(1|1)$ with central $K$). The reconciliation: the conifold quiver
has a $\mathbb{Z}/2$-graded structure (the two vertices), so the natural
BPS algebra is the super-Lie version; dropping the supergrading recovers
$\widehat{\fsl}_2$. Both statements stand at their own scope.
(iii) \texttt{chapters/theory/e1\_chiral\_algebras.tex:2478} records
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(\mathrm{conifold}) = -1$ against the cache value
$+1$ attested in six other Vol III loci. The minus sign there is the
Mukai-odd projection $\Pi_{++}$ outcome inside a two-term identity
$-1+1=0=\chi(\cO_{\widetilde X})$, not a claim about the bare
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$. Disambiguation: the bare modular characteristic
remains $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(\mathrm{conifold})=+1$; the $-1$ is the
signed Mukai-positive contribution in the refined compactly-supported
four-term sum. Vol~I and Vol~II carry no competing conifold
$\widehat{\fsl}_2$ / $\widehat{\fgl}(1|1)$ statement; the only
Vol~II conifold entry is the $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}=1$ value
(\texttt{chapters/theory/infinite\_fingerprint\_classification.tex:168}),
which matches. No propagation needed.
