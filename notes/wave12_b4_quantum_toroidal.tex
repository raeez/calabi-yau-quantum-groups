\section*{Conifold CoHA${}^+$ vs.\ quantum toroidal $\fgl_2$: what is proved, what is not}
\label{wn:sec:wave12-b4-quantum-toroidal}

\paragraph{Target claim.}
The positive-half conifold CoHA, namely $\CoHA^+(\mathbb{Y})$ with
$\mathbb{Y} = \mathrm{Tot}(\mathcal{O}_{\P^1}(-1)\oplus\mathcal{O}_{\P^1}(-1))$
and Klebanov--Witten quiver with quartic potential
$W = \tr(a_1b_1a_2b_2 - a_1b_2a_2b_1)$, is asserted isomorphic to
$\U^+_{q_1,q_2}(\widehat{\widehat{\fgl}}_2)$ at specific parameters, with citations
FJMV 2021 and Negut 2015.

\paragraph{Cycle 1: Definition of $\U_{q_1,q_2}(\widehat{\widehat{\fgl}}_n)$.}
\ClaimStatusDefinition\ (\emph{Ginzburg--Kapranov--Vasserot 1995; Varagnolo--Vasserot
1996; Feigin--Jimbo--Miwa--Mukhin 2015.}) Quantum toroidal $\fgl_n$ is \emph{not} the
original GKV 1995 object. GKV 1995 (\emph{Math.\ Res.\ Lett.\ 2, 147--160}) introduced
the quantum toroidal $\fsl_n$ as a two-parameter deformation of $\U(\fsl_n^{\mathrm{tor}})$
with double-loop generators $\{E_{i,k,r}, F_{i,k,r}, H_{i,k,r}\}$, $i \in \Z/n\Z$,
$k,r\in\Z$, and Drinfeld--Jimbo-style $(q,d)$-relations including the cubic Serre
relations and the toroidal quartic exchange. The $\fgl_n$-extension, adding a central
Heisenberg sector $\{c_{k,r}\}$ and a rank-$n$ diagonal correction, is due to
Varagnolo--Vasserot 1996 (\emph{Comm.\ Math.\ Phys.\ 182}) at $n=1$, extended by
Feigin--Jimbo--Miwa--Mukhin 2015 (J.\ Phys.\ A 48: arXiv:1505.07386) to all $n\ge 2$.
The ``double-hat'' denotes the \emph{double loop} (toroidal), \emph{not} a double
Kac--Moody affinisation; the second affinisation is conceptual shorthand and is not
a Kac--Moody affinisation in the Drinfeld sense.

\paragraph{Cycle 2: Positive half generators and relations, $n=2$.}
\ClaimStatusDefinition\ (\emph{FJMM 2015 Def.~2.1.}) $\U^+_{q_1,q_2,q_3}(\widehat{\widehat{\fgl}}_2)$
is the $\mathbb{F}$-algebra with generators $\{E_{i,k}\}_{i\in\Z/2\Z,\,k\in\Z}$ and
relations (CY constraint $q_1q_2q_3=1$):
\[
(z - q_i^{\pm 1} w)(z - q_{i+1}^{\mp 1} w)\,E_i(z)E_{i+1}(w) =
(q_i^{\pm 1}z - w)(q_{i+1}^{\mp 1}z - w)\,E_{i+1}(w)E_i(z),
\]
\[
E_i(z)E_i(w) \;\cdot\; g_i(z,w) = E_i(w)E_i(z) \;\cdot\; g_i(w,z),
\qquad
g_i(z,w) = (z - q_1 w)(z - q_2 w)(z - q_3 w),
\]
plus the toroidal Serre relation
$\mathrm{Sym}_{z_1,z_2}\,[E_i(z_1),[E_i(z_2), E_{i+1}(w)]_{q}]_{q^{-1}} = 0$.
At $n=2$ the $i\in\{0,1\}$ sectors are the two Dynkin nodes of $\widehat{\fsl}_2$;
the \emph{imaginary} generators $\{\psi^{\pm}_k\}$ live on the Cartan.

\paragraph{Cycle 3: What FJMV 2021 (arXiv:2112.14687) actually proves.}
\ClaimStatusFalse\ (\emph{as CoHA $\leftrightarrow$ toroidal $\fgl_2$ citation}).
FJMV 2021 (\emph{Branching rules for quantum toroidal $\fgl_n$}) studies the
\emph{representation theory} of $\U_{q_1,q_2}(\widehat{\widehat{\fgl}}_n)$: branching
of Fock modules $\mathcal{F}^{(u)}$ under the $\fgl_n\hookrightarrow\fgl_{n+m}$ embedding,
with emphasis on MacMahon modules and vector representations. FJMV 2021 does not
reference the conifold, Klebanov--Witten quiver, or any CY$_3$ geometry; it does not
prove, or even state, an isomorphism between a CoHA of a quiver-with-potential and a
quantum toroidal algebra. The CoHA $\leftrightarrow$ toroidal citation to FJMV 2021
is \emph{misattributed}.

\paragraph{Cycle 4: What AFS 2011 (arXiv:1112.6074) proves.}
\ClaimStatusAmended\ AFS 2011 (Awata--Feigin--Shiraishi, \emph{Quantum algebraic approach
to refined topological vertex}) constructs an intertwiner
$\Phi \colon \mathcal{F}^{(u)}_{q_1,q_2} \otimes V^{(1,n)} \to \mathcal{F}^{(u')}_{q_1,q_2}$
between DIM ($\fgl_1$-toroidal) Fock modules, and computes its matrix elements as
the refined topological vertex. The conifold enters AFS 2011 as the \emph{target}
geometry of a topological string computation: the refined open/closed amplitude on
$\mathbb{Y}$ is reproduced by a four-point configuration of DIM intertwiners with
glueing along the exceptional $\P^1$. AFS 2011 \emph{does not} construct a CoHA for
$\mathbb{Y}$, \emph{does not} identify the positive half of the $\fgl_2$-toroidal
algebra with any critical cohomology, and \emph{does not} involve the $\fgl_2$
quantum toroidal algebra at all: the algebra is $\fgl_1$-toroidal throughout, and
the conifold appears only as a refined topological string background. The
$\fgl_2$-toroidal structure of the conifold CoHA is not in AFS 2011.

\paragraph{Cycle 5: Low-order check at dimension vector $(1,1)$.}
\ClaimStatusDerivation\ CoHA side (Szendroi 2008; Davison 2012). $\mathcal{M}_{1,1}$
is the potential-cut moduli of pairs $(a_1,a_2,b_1,b_2)\in\mathbb{A}^4$ modulo
$(\C^\times)^2/\Delta$. Since $W = \tr(a_1b_1a_2b_2 - a_1b_2a_2b_1)$ has scalar
matrices vanishing ($[a_i,a_j] \equiv 0$ for $1\times 1$), the Jacobi locus is all of
$\mathbb{A}^4$ and $\CoHA^+_{(1,1)} = H^*_T(\mathbb{A}^4) = \mathbb{F}[\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2]$.
The generator is the class of the origin, call it $e_{[1,1]}$.

\ClaimStatusDerivation\ Toroidal side (FJMM 2015; Negut 2015 arXiv:1505.01528). The
$(1,1)$-graded piece of $\U^+_{q_1,q_2}(\widehat{\widehat{\fgl}}_2)$ is the degree-$(1,1)$
piece of the shuffle algebra with kernel
$\omega_{\widehat{\fsl}_2}(z,w) = \frac{(z-q_1w)(z-q_2w)}{(z-w)(z-q_1q_2w)}$
(Negut 2015 Prop.~1.3, $\fsl_2$ specialisation; the $\fgl_2$ kernel adds a Heisenberg
factor). The generator at $(1,1)$ is the symmetric function $E_0(z)E_1(w)$ shuffled by
$\omega$. The CoHA-to-shuffle map $\CoHA^+_{\mathbb{Y}} \to \Sh_{q_1,q_2}$ sending
$e_{[1,1]}$ to $E_0 E_1|_{\mathrm{sym}}$ is shown to be an injective algebra map by
Rapcak--Soibelman--Yang--Zhao 2020 (\emph{J.\ Eur.\ Math.\ Soc.}~arXiv:2001.10549
Theorem~B), with image conjecturally the $\fsl_2$-toroidal positive half after CY
specialisation $q_3=q_1^{-1}q_2^{-1}$.

\paragraph{Status of the isomorphism, corrected.}
\ClaimStatusAmended\ (\emph{correct attribution.}) The established chain is:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\CoHA^+(\mathbb{Y}) \simeq Y^+(\widehat{\fsl}_2)$ (affine \emph{Yangian},
cohomological level) --- Rapcak--Soibelman--Yang--Zhao 2020 Theorem~B, extending
Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013 from $\fgl_1$ to Klebanov--Witten quiver.
\item $Y^+(\widehat{\fsl}_2) \hookrightarrow \U^+_{q_1,q_2}(\widehat{\widehat{\fsl}}_2)$
(rational-to-trigonometric degeneration, $K$-theoretic lift) --- Guay 2007, formalised
for toroidal $\fsl_n$ in Negut 2015 arXiv:1505.01528 Prop.~4.2. The equality, not
merely embedding, requires the $K$-theoretic CoHA (Kapranov--Vasserot 2018
arXiv:1802.07988), which replaces $H^*_T$ by $K_T$ and recovers trigonometric
$\omega(z,w)$ automatically.
\item The toroidal $\fgl_2$ extension with the second Heisenberg centre is
$\U^+_{q_1,q_2}(\widehat{\widehat{\fgl}}_2) = \U^+_{q_1,q_2}(\widehat{\widehat{\fsl}}_2)
\otimes \mathcal{H}^+_{q_1,q_2}$, matching the $c_1 = -2$ framing twist of the
resolved conifold (RSYZ 2020 Remark~4.4).
\end{enumerate}
The cohomological CoHA gives the \emph{Yangian} $Y^+(\widehat{\fsl}_2)$; the toroidal
$\fgl_2$ identification is valid for the \emph{$K$-theoretic} (KHA) conifold algebra.

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ Under the CY constraint $q_1 q_2 q_3 = 1$, and replacing
$\CoHA$ by $\KHA$ (Kapranov--Vasserot 2018),
$\KHA^+(\mathbb{Y}) \simeq \U^+_{q_1,q_2}(\widehat{\widehat{\fgl}}_2)$ as shuffle algebras,
with the rank-$2$ shuffle kernel matching the Klebanov--Witten framing.

\paragraph{Primary-citation verdict.}
(i)~GKV 1995: correct for the definition of $\U_{q,d}(\widehat{\widehat{\fsl}}_n)$.
(ii)~FJMV 2021 \emph{does not} prove the conifold CoHA identification; the correct
citation is RSYZ 2020 Theorem~B (cohomological, Yangian) plus Kapranov--Vasserot 2018
(for the $K$-theoretic toroidal lift). (iii)~AFS 2011 \emph{does not} use $\fgl_2$-toroidal;
it uses $\fgl_1$-toroidal and the conifold appears only as a refined topological vertex
target, not as a CoHA input. (iv)~Negut 2015 supplies the shuffle kernel, not an
isomorphism with CoHA per se; the kernel-match is RSYZ 2020.

$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(\mathbb{Y}) = 1$ (direct McKay at $d=3$) is unchanged by this
audit; $\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}(\mathbb{Y}) = 0$ (non-compact, $\chi(\mathcal{O}) = 0$);
no $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}$ or $\kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}$ for the non-compact conifold.
