## A1 -- ATTACK 1: "$\sigma^{\mathrm{SYZ}}$" is a misnomer; the involution is hyperKahler rotation

### A1.0 Statement of the Wave-10 claim

### A1.1 ATTACK: K3 is *not* SYZ-fibred in the literal sense

The Strominger-Yau-Zaslow conjecture (SYZ 1996, *Nucl. Phys.* B 479, hep-th/9606040) postulates that
a Calabi-Yau $X$ admits a special-Lagrangian torus fibration $T^n \to X \to B^n$ over a real
$n$-dimensional base, and that mirror symmetry is fibrewise T-duality on the torus fibres. For
this to hold for K3:

- Need a *special-Lagrangian* $T^2$-fibration $T^2 \to K3 \to S^2$.
- "Special Lagrangian" = Lagrangian with respect to the Kahler form $\omega_K$ AND
  $\mathrm{Im}(\Omega)|_L = 0$ where $\Omega$ is the holomorphic 2-form.

For a *generic* algebraic K3, NO special Lagrangian $T^2$-fibration exists -- the K3 elliptic
fibration $\pi: K3 \to \mathbb{P}^1$ is HOLOMORPHIC (the fibres $\pi^{-1}(p)$ are holomorphic
elliptic curves $E_\tau$, NOT special Lagrangian tori). The 24 singular fibres are nodal cubics
(Kodaira type $I_1$), with monodromy in $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ around each node.

**SYZ fibrations on K3 exist only at LARGE-COMPLEX-STRUCTURE LIMITS** (Gross-Wilson 2000
*J. Diff. Geom.* 55, math/9908088). At a generic point of moduli space, K3 is NOT SYZ-fibred.
What it IS is *hyperKahler*: there is an $S^2$ family of complex structures
$I, J, K$ with $I^2 = J^2 = K^2 = IJK = -1$.

**The K3 mirror symmetry is NOT SYZ in the naive sense.** The actual K3 mirror map is the
**Aspinwall-Morrison / Dolgachev mirror** (Dolgachev 1996 alg-geom/9502005; Aspinwall-Morrison
1994 *J. Diff. Geom.* 44; Aspinwall 1996 hep-th/9611137 §3): K3 mirror symmetry is the involution
$M \leftrightarrow M^\vee$ on the orthogonal sublattices of the Mukai $\Lambda^{4,20}$ at a marked
point of moduli, where $M$ is the Picard lattice of the algebraic K3 and $M^\vee$ is its perpendicular.

In the special case $M = U \oplus E_8(-1)^2$ (the maximally supersingular K3 / Atkin-Lehner-flat
case), $M^\vee = U \oplus E_8(-1)^2 = M$, so K3 IS its own Dolgachev mirror -- but this is ONE point
of moduli, not the whole moduli stack.

## A3 -- ATTACK 3: "projective ribbon" vs "modular tensor" -- the actual obstruction

### A3.1 ATTACK: "modular tensor category" requires *finite* simple objects

A modular tensor category in the Bakalov-Kirillov sense (BK 2001 *Lectures on Tensor Categories
and Modular Functors* §3) requires:
- (M0) Finitely many simple objects (= "finite" = "rational" CFT condition).
- (M1) Non-degenerate $S$-matrix on the simple objects.
- (M2) Ribbon structure with twist $\theta_X$ on each simple $X$.
- (M3) Pivotal duality with positive quantum dimension.

For $\mathbf{H}_{\Delta_5}$: this is the EK quantisation of an INFINITE-DIMENSIONAL Borcherds-Kac-Moody
Lie superalgebra, with infinitely many real and imaginary simple roots. The **representation theory of
$\mathbf{H}_{\Delta_5}$ has INFINITELY MANY simples** -- highest-weight modules indexed by elements
of the Cartan, which is rank-22 (for $\Lambda^{2,1}_{II} \subset \Gamma^{3,19}$) or rank-24 (for full
Mukai).

**So $\mathrm{Rep}(\mathbf{H}_{\Delta_5})$ is NOT a modular tensor category in the BK sense** -- it
fails (M0). It is at best a **ribbon braided tensor category** (potentially infinite, similar to
Lyubashenko 1995 *Comm. Math. Phys.* 172 setup for non-semisimple modular categories, or De Renzi-Geer-Patureau-Mirand
2018 arXiv:1809.04341 "modified modular" categories).

### A3.2 First-principles: what KIND of category is $\mathrm{Rep}(\mathbf{H}_{\Delta_5})$?

Following Lyubashenko-Majid 1994 *J. Algebra* 166 and modern non-semisimple framework:

(i) $\mathrm{Rep}(\mathbf{H}_{\Delta_5})$ is a **braided tensor category** (from the EK R-matrix).
(ii) $\mathrm{Rep}(\mathbf{H}_{\Delta_5})$ is a **ribbon category** (from the ribbon element
    $\nu \in \mathbf{H}_{\Delta_5}$ with $\nu^2 = uS(u)$ for $u = m \cdot (S \otimes \mathrm{id}) R$).
(iii) $\mathrm{Rep}(\mathbf{H}_{\Delta_5})$ is **NOT semisimple** (in general -- has indecomposable but

(iv) $\mathrm{Rep}(\mathbf{H}_{\Delta_5})$ has a **continuous family** of simple objects parametrised

The correct categorical framework is **non-semisimple modular tensor category**, in the De Renzi-Geer-Patureau-Mirand
2018 / Costantino-Geer-Patureau-Mirand 2014 arXiv:1202.3553 sense. Specifically: a "modified modular"
category equipped with a **modified trace** $\mathfrak{t}$ on the projective objects, satisfying an
$S$-matrix non-degeneracy condition on a *sublattice* of simples (not all simples).
