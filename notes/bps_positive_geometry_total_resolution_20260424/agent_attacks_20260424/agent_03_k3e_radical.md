# Agent 3: raw K3 x E Hall-BKM radical lane

## claim attacked

The attacked claim is the raw unquotiented `K3 x E` Hall-Borcherds lane in the current platonic-closure layer:

1. The quotient theorem is stated as
   `CoHA^{Mot,o}_{crit}(K3 x E)^wedge_S / Rad_Aut = Uhat(g_Delta^+)`,
   where `Rad_Aut` is the closed Hall ideal invisible to the automorphic
   denominator pairing and orientation character.
2. The raw theorem is then encoded in the residual solution stack as
   `ker G_Aut = 0`, with `G_Aut` called the finite automorphic Gram
   pairing.
3. The executable model uses a finite rational matrix and certifies the
   radical by nullity alone; the current `k3e_raw_radical` point uses the
   diagonal matrix `diag(2,3,5)`.

## strongest failure mode

The strongest failure mode is not numerical.  It is categorical:

`ker G_Aut = 0` is a necessary finite test for one linear pairing after a
Hall-Borcherds comparison datum has already produced the correct completed
pairing.  It is not, by itself, the raw unquotiented Hall-Borcherds
theorem.

A finite Gram matrix can be nondegenerate while the Hall object still
fails every structure which makes the denominator algebra into a raw
Hall-BKM algebra:

- the primitive Hall commutator may be zero or have wrong structure
  constants;
- the three real simple Hall primitives may not exist;
- the real Serre, imaginary-root, parity, and Weyl-vector relations may
  fail;
- the orientation character may not equal `nu_Delta5`;
- the protected integration may have the correct signed dimensions but
  wrong even/odd root spaces;
- the Hopf pairing may not be continuous for the completion;
- the negative half, Cartan part, centre, and Drinfeld double may not be
  constructed;
- the denominator product may match `Delta_5` while only controlling
  signed Euler characteristics.

Concrete countermodel at finite level: take a three-dimensional vector
space with Gram matrix `diag(2,3,5)` and zero bracket.  The current
`radical_non_degeneracy_certificate` passes with nullity zero, but the
object is not the positive half of `g_Delta5`, because the Borcherds real
root and imaginary-root commutator relations are absent.  This proves that
finite Gram nondegeneracy cannot be the full radical-zero condition unless
the word `Gram` denotes the Gram matrix of an already constructed
completed oriented Hall-Borcherds comparison datum.

## fatal/nonfatal verdict

Fatal for the compressed equivalence:

```text
raw K3 x E Hall-BKM theorem  <=>  ker G_Aut = 0
```

when `G_Aut` is only the finite rational pairing currently present in the
oracle.

Nonfatal for the stronger quotient theorem:

```text
raw K3 x E Hall-BKM theorem  <=>  Rad_Aut,<=N,<=R = 0 for all finite quotients
```

provided `Rad_Aut,<=N,<=R` is defined as the full closed
Hall-Borcherds radical, not merely the kernel of one Gram matrix.  The
manuscript already has the correct seed at the quotient-theorem level:
`Rad_Aut` is generated by Hall classes invisible to denominator
supertrace, automorphic pairing, and orientation character.  The later
derived-stack compression to `ker G_Aut` is the weak point.

## exact repair/heal theorem statement

**Finite full-radical theorem.**  Fix a truncation `(N,R)`.  Let
`H_{<=N,<=R}` be a finite oriented compact critical Hall algebra for
`K3 x E`, completed with respect to the support-property cone and equipped
with:

1. a protected primitive subspace `Prim_prot(H)`;
2. a charge-preserving Hall commutator
   `[ , ]_Hall: Prim_gamma x Prim_eta -> Prim_{gamma+eta}`;
3. an orientation character `eps_o`;
4. a protected integration `I_Aut`;
5. a continuous Hopf pairing with negative half and Cartan part;
6. a denominator comparison to the Borcherds positive half whose
   root supermultiplicities are `f(nm,l)` from `phi_{0,1}`;
7. real simple root classes `delta_1,delta_2,delta_3`, with the
   Gritsenko-Nikulin/Borcherds Serre, imaginary-root, parity, and
   Weyl-vector relations.

Define `Rad_Aut,<=N,<=R` to be the largest closed homogeneous
Hopf-Lie ideal in `Prim_prot(H_{<=N,<=R})`, and the closed Hall ideal it
generates in the completed Hall algebra, lying in the simultaneous kernel
of:

- the protected integration `I_Aut`;
- the denominator comparison map;
- the orientation character test `eps_o = nu_Delta5`;
- the continuous Hopf pairing against the negative half;
- the primitive bracket comparison with `g_Delta5`;
- the coproduct, centre, and completion compatibility maps needed for
  the Hall-Drinfeld double.

Then the finite raw Hall-Borcherds theorem at `(N,R)` is equivalent to
`Rad_Aut,<=N,<=R = 0`.  The completed raw theorem is equivalent to
`Rad_Aut,<=N,<=R = 0` for every `(N,R)` together with separatedness of
the inverse-limit completion:

```text
cap_{N,R} ker(H^wedge -> H_{<=N,<=R}) = 0.
```

The finite Gram nondegeneracy test is the pairing-coordinate of this
theorem.  It becomes sufficient only after the remaining coordinates of
the comparison datum have already been imposed.  The healed zero-fiber
coordinate should therefore be

```text
o_rad = (o_pair, o_or, o_int, o_br, o_Serre, o_copair, o_comp)
```

not just `ker G_Aut`.
