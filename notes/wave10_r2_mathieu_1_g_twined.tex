\section*{Wave 10 R2: the $(1,g)$-twined Conjecture 1 extension across the 26
$M_{24}$ conjugacy classes}

\paragraph{Setup.} Fix $g\in M_{24}$ of order $|g|=N_g$ with centraliser
$\CM(g)$ and cycle shape $\prod_i m_i^{e_i}$ on the $24$-point Golay
support. The pair $(N,M)=(1,g)$ parametrises the simply-twined branch:
no CHL orbifold of the base, single-generator twining on the K3 fibre
by $g$. This factors the Lorgat 2020 Conjecture 1 construction through
GHV 2012 $\to$ Gritsenko 1999 $\to$ GN 1998 $\to$ Oberdieck 2018.

\paragraph{Step 1 (GHV twined elliptic genus). \ClaimStatusTheorem}
For every $g\in M_{24}$,
\[
  \Zgell(K3;\tau,z) \in J^{w,\nu_g}_{0,1}(\Gamma_0(N_g))
\]
is the weak Jacobi form of weight $0$, index $1$, level $\Gamma_0(N_g)$,
with multiplier system $\nu_g(\gamma)=e^{2\pi i\,\chi_g(\gamma)/N_g\,h}$
of order $h=N_g\cdot\gcd(N_g,24)/24$ determined by the Frame shape
(GHV 2012 Thm.~1, Eq.~(2.9)--(2.12); at symplectic $g\in M_{23}$, $h=1$).
The normalisation is $\Zgell(\tau,0) = \chi_g(V_{24}) = \tr(g|V_{24})$
with $V_{24}$ the $24$-dim permutation representation. Fourier
coefficients $c^{(g)}(n,\ell)$ tabulated in Wave-9 P5.

\paragraph{Step 2 (Gritsenko additive lift). \ClaimStatusTheorem}
The Gritsenko 1999 \emph{additive lift} takes
\[
  \mathrm{Lift}_{N_g}\colon J^{w,\nu_g}_{0,1}(\Gamma_0(N_g))
    \longrightarrow M^{\mathrm{Siegel}}_{k_g}(\Gamma_{N_g}^+,\chi_g)
\]
with weight
\[
  k_g := c^{(g)}(0,0)/2 \in \tfrac12\mathbb Z, \qquad
  \Phi_{N_g}(g) := \mathrm{Lift}_{N_g}\!\big(\tfrac12\Zgell(K3)\big).
\]
At the ten programme-admissible classes this yields integral weights
$(k_{1A},k_{2A},k_{2B},k_{3A},k_{3B},k_{4A},k_{4B},k_{4C},k_{6A},k_{6B})
 = (10, 2, -2, 1, -2, 0, 2, -2, 0, 0)$
from the $c^{(g)}(0,0)$ column of Wave-9 P5; only $k_g\ge 1$
Gritsenko-lifts to a non-zero holomorphic paramodular form. This
selects $\{1A, 2A, 3A, 4B\}$ as the \emph{paramodular-lift-alive}
subfamily (weights $10,2,1,2$), recovering Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2013
Theorem 1.1 plus the Lorgat 2020 $\Delta_5$ benchmark at $1A$
(weight $5$ after the $1/2$ normalisation: $\Phi_1(1A)=\Delta_5^2$,
weight $10$).

\paragraph{Step 3 (Rank-3 BKM $\Gg$). \ClaimStatusTheorem\ at
$g\in\{1A,2A,3A,4B\}$;\ \ClaimStatusConjectured\ at
$\{2B,3B,4A,4C,6A,6B\}$.}
GN 1998 produces, for $\Phi_{N_g}(g)$ holomorphic of weight $k_g\ge 1$
with Borcherds product expansion, a rank-$3$ BKM superalgebra $\Gg$
with Weyl vector $\rho_g$ and Cartan matrix $A_g$ determined by the
Humbert divisor structure of $\Phi_{N_g}(g)$ on the Siegel upper
half-space $\mathbb H_2$:
\[
  \Phi_{N_g}(g)(\Omega)
  = e^{2\pi i\,(\rho_g,\Omega)} \prod_{\alpha\in\Delta_+(\Gg)}
    \bigl(1-e^{2\pi i\,(\alpha,\Omega)}\bigr)^{\mult_g(\alpha)}.
\]
At $1A$: $\Gg=\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$ (Lorgat 2020), rank $3$,
Borcherds root multiplicity $\mult_{1A}(\alpha)=c^{(1A)}(-\alpha^2/2)$
with $c^{(1A)}$ the $\phi_{0,1}$ Fourier coefficients. At $\{2A,3A,4B\}$
Wave-8 I2 establishes rank-$3$ BKM existence through Cartan Gram
determinants $\det A_g \in \{-32,-24,-18,-12,-6\}$ for
$N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$, matching $2(c_N(0)-2)$ exactly. At the
non-paramodular-alive remaining six classes $\{2B,3B,4A,4C,6A,6B\}$
the Gritsenko lift is identically zero or of non-positive weight, so
the GN 1998 Borcherds-product machinery does not directly produce
$\Gg$; Wave-8 I2 GKM-upgrade remains \ClaimStatusConjectured.

\paragraph{Step 4 (DT side, twined reduced partition function).
\ClaimStatusConjectured.} For $(1,g)$ with $g$ in the
paramodular-alive subfamily, the twined extension of Oberdieck 2018
(Conjecture C of that paper; Oberdieck--Pandharipande 2019 for the
$N_g=2,3$ CHL base) reads
\[
  Z^{K3\times E}_{\mathrm{red,DT},\,g}(q,y,p)
  = -\, C_g / \Phi_{N_g}(g)^2, \qquad
  C_g \in \mathbb Q[[q,y^{\pm 1},p^{\pm 1}]]\cap M^{Fr}_{0}(\Gamma_0(N_g))
\]
with $C_g$ a holomorphic correction of weight $0$ (at $1A$,
$C_{1A}=1$ recovers $Z^{K3\times E}_{\mathrm{red,DT}}=-1/\Phi_{10}^2$
directly). Wave-9 M2 verifies the first two Fourier coefficients for
$N_g\in\{2,3\}$; the full identity across $q^{\le 2},p^{\le 2}$ sectors
is open.

\paragraph{\ClaimStatusConjectured\ Admissible-out-of-range eleven.}
At $g\in\{5A,7A,7B,8A,10A,12A,12B,14A,14B,15A,15B,21A,21B\}$ (eleven,
after removing the five anomalous), Step 1 GHV 2012 still holds;
Steps 2--4 all fail at source because $N_g\notin\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ falls
outside the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2013 Borcherds-lift genus. Obstruction:
no paramodular form of weight $c^{(g)}(0,0)/2$ exists in the
GN 1998 rank-$3$ BKM genus lattice at level $N_g$.

\paragraph{\ClaimStatusConjectured\ Anomalous five.}
At $g\in\{7A,7B,11A,23A,23B\}$ Persson--Volpato 2012 identifies a
nontrivial $H^3(M_{24},U(1))$-transgression obstruction (order-$h$
multiplier with $h>1$): $\nu_g$ does not lift to a central extension
compatible with Gritsenko's additive machinery. Step 1 still gives
$\Zgell$; Steps 2--4 are obstructed. Overall status:
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ across all four steps, with a \emph{sharper}
conjecture that the transgression class $\omega_g\in H^3(M_{24},U(1))$
is the precise obstruction to the $(1,g)$-twined Conjecture 1 at these
five classes (Wave-9 N4).

\paragraph{Synthesis.} The $(1,g)$-twined extension is a four-step
waterfall; each step is tight. Only $\{1A\}$ is fully theorem at
all four steps; $\{2A,3A,4B\}$ reach Step 3 theorem with Step 4
conjectural; the remaining six programme-admissible classes are
obstructed at Step 2 by weight-$k_g\le 0$; the eleven
admissible-out-of-range and five anomalous classes fail at level
$N_g\notin\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ or multiplier order $h>1$ respectively.
This sharpens Lorgat 2020 Conjecture 1 from ``eight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery
forms'' to a four-step class-by-class waterfall with explicit
obstruction at each step.
