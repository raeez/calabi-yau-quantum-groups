\subsection*{The Local Calabi--Yau Threefold}

Let
\[
  \bY=\mathrm{Tot}\bigl(\cO_{\mathbb P^1}(-1)\oplus
  \cO_{\mathbb P^1}(-1)\bigr).
\]
With $H=c_1(\cO_{\mathbb P^1}(1))$, the adjunction identity
\[
  c_1(\cO(-1)\oplus\cO(-1))=-2H=c_1(K_{\mathbb P^1})
\]
trivialises the canonical bundle of $\bY$. Cover $\mathbb P^1$ by
$z=z_1/z_0$ and $\tilde z=z_0/z_1=z^{-1}$, and write the induced affine
charts as
\[
  U_+=\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb C[z,u_+,v_+],\qquad
  U_-=\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb C[\tilde z,u_-,v_-].
\]
On $U_+\cap U_-=\Gm\times\mathbb C^2$ the transition is
\[
  \tilde z=z^{-1},\qquad u_-=zu_+,\qquad v_-=zv_+.
\]
The holomorphic volume forms satisfy
\[
  d\tilde z\wedge du_-\wedge dv_-=-dz\wedge du_+\wedge dv_+,
\]
so the Calabi--Yau form glues up to the harmless orientation sign.

\subsection*{Affine GIT Charts and the Global NCCR}

Each affine chart is $\mathbb C^3$. Its Jacobi algebra is the
one-vertex three-loop algebra
\[
  Q_{\mathbb C^3}:\quad x,y,z:\bullet\to\bullet,\qquad
  W_{\mathbb C^3}=\mathrm{tr}\,x[y,z].
\]
On $U_+$ use $(x,y,z)=(z,u_+,v_+)$; on $U_-$ use
$(x,y,z)=(\tilde z,u_-,v_-)$. These one-vertex algebras record the
formal affine pieces of the resolved threefold. The global tilting
algebra is the Szendroi two-vertex conifold quiver with arrows
\[
  a_1,a_2:\circ\to\bullet,\qquad b_1,b_2:\bullet\to\circ,
\]
and potential
\[
  W_{\mathrm{con}}=a_1b_1a_2b_2-a_1b_2a_2b_1
\]
presents the usual noncommutative crepant resolution of
$\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb C[X,Y,U,V]/(XY-UV)$. The central functions are
\[
  X=a_1b_1,\qquad Y=a_2b_2,\qquad U=a_1b_2,\qquad V=a_2b_1,
  \qquad XY=UV .
\]
For dimension vector $(1,1)$ and large-radius stability the locus
$(a_1,a_2)\neq(0,0)$ modulo the diagonal gauge group is exactly
$\bY$. On the chart $a_1\neq0$,
\[
  z=\frac{a_2}{a_1},\qquad u_+=a_1b_1,\qquad v_+=a_1b_2,
\]
and on the chart $a_2\neq0$,
\[
  \tilde z=\frac{a_1}{a_2},\qquad u_-=a_2b_1,\qquad v_-=a_2b_2.
\]
The formulas give $\tilde z=z^{-1}$, $u_-=zu_+$, and $v_-=zv_+$.
Thus the two affine $\mathbb C^3$ quivers are GIT localisations of the
two-vertex Jacobi algebra at the two base sections. Van den Bergh's
tilting bundle $T=\cO_{\bY}\oplus\cO_{\bY}(1)$ gives, up to the
left/right module opposite convention,
\[
  \mathrm{End}_{\bY}(T)
  \simeq J(Q_{\mathrm{con}},W_{\mathrm{con}}),
\]
and hence a derived Morita model for $D^b(\bY)$. Szendroi's
noncommutative Donaldson--Thomas chamber is the stability chamber for
modules over this Jacobi algebra.

\subsection*{Ginzburg dg Algebras}

For a quiver with potential $(Q,W)$, the Ginzburg dg algebra
$\Gamma(Q,W)$ is generated by the arrows of $Q$ in degree $0$, dual
arrows $a^*$ in degree $-1$, and loops $t_i$ in degree $-2$, with
\[
  da=0,\qquad da^*=\partial_a W,\qquad
  dt_i=\sum_{s(a)=i}aa^*-\sum_{t(a)=i}a^*a .
\]
For $U_+$ this gives generators
\[
  z,u_+,v_+,\qquad z^*,u_+^*,v_+^*,\qquad t_+,
\]
and
\[
  dz^*=[u_+,v_+],\qquad du_+^*=[v_+,z],\qquad
  dv_+^*=[z,u_+],\qquad dt_+=\sum_{a\in\{z,u_+,v_+\}}[a,a^*].
\]
The same formulas on $U_-$ use $(\tilde z,u_-,v_-)$. Denote the two
Ginzburg dg algebras by $\Gamma_+$ and $\Gamma_-$. By Ginzburg and
Keller, each is a Calabi--Yau dg model for $\mathrm{Perf}(U_\pm)$.

\subsection*{HKR and the Local \texorpdfstring{$E_3$}{E3} Factorisation Algebra}

The algebraic Hochschild cochains of $\Gamma_\pm$ identify with
polyvector fields:
\[
  \HH^\bullet_{\mathrm{alg}}(\Gamma_\pm)
  \cong
  \PV^\bullet(U_\pm)
  =
  \bigoplus_{p+q=\bullet}
  \Gamma(U_\pm,\Lambda^pT_{U_\pm}\otimes\Omega_{U_\pm}^{0,q}).
\]
The HKR map is the Kontsevich--Caldararu map with the
$\sqrt{\mathrm{td}}$ twist. On each affine chart the Todd class is
trivial; globally the HKR comparison is glued through the coordinate
transition and the corresponding characteristic-class correction. The
local observables of holomorphic Chern--Simons theory on a complex
threefold carry the factorisation product of little three-discs, hence
an $\Ethree$ structure before chiral specialization. After
specialization to a curve or to the chiral output of $\Phi_3$, the
resulting chiral algebra is $\Eone$; any $\Etwo$ structure belongs to a
centre or completed double equipped with its pairing and completion
data.

Costello--Li holomorphic Chern--Simons theory assigns to each chart a
holomorphic factorisation algebra
\[
  \cF_\pm=\PhiFA_3(\Gamma_\pm)
\]
whose cohomology on a polydisc is the corresponding polyvector-field
complex.

\subsection*{The Overlap Transition}

On $U_+\cap U_-$ the atlas transition is the change of trivialisation
\[
  \tau_{+-}(z,u_+,v_+)=(\tilde z,u_-,v_-)
  =(z^{-1},zu_+,zv_+).
\]
It identifies the two presentations of $\mathrm{Perf}(U_+\cap U_-)$;
the line-bundle factor $\cO(-1)$ is multiplication by the invertible
function $z$ on $\Gm$. The push-forward on vector fields is
\[
  T_{+-,*}(\partial_z)
  =
  -\tilde z^2\partial_{\tilde z}
  +\tilde z u_-\partial_{u_-}
  +\tilde z v_-\partial_{v_-},
  \qquad
  T_{+-,*}(\partial_{u_+})=\tilde z^{-1}\partial_{u_-},
  \qquad
  T_{+-,*}(\partial_{v_+})=\tilde z^{-1}\partial_{v_-}.
\]
In particular
\[
  T_{+-,*}(\partial_z\wedge\partial_{u_+}\wedge\partial_{v_+})
  =
  -\partial_{\tilde z}\wedge\partial_{u_-}\wedge\partial_{v_-},
\]
matching the sign in the volume-form transition. The Schouten bracket
is natural under the biholomorphism $\tau_{+-}$, so $T_{+-}$ is an
isomorphism of the chart factorisation algebras on the overlap. The
Bridgeland--Chen flop kernel is a global equivalence between the two
small resolutions; on the non-exceptional locus it restricts to the
same coordinate transition.

\subsection*{\v{C}ech Descent}

For $\cU=\{U_+,U_-\}$ define $\cF_{\bY}$ by the homotopy equaliser
diagram on every open $V\subset\bY$:
\[
  \cF_{\bY}(V)=
  \mathrm{holim}\Bigl(
  \cF_+(V\cap U_+)
  \xrightarrow{\,T_{+-}\circ\mathrm{res}\,}
  \cF_-(V\cap U_+\cap U_-)
  \xleftarrow{\,\mathrm{res}\,}
  \cF_-(V\cap U_-)
  \Bigr).
\]
The cover has only two charts, so the triple-overlap cocycle condition
is exactly $T_{-+}T_{+-}=1$. Since $U_\pm$ and $U_+\cap U_-$ are Stein,
Cartan's theorem B identifies the \v{C}ech complex with derived global
sections. The resulting spectral sequence has
\[
  E_1^{p,q}=\check H^p(\cU,H^q(\cF_\bullet)),
  \qquad p\in\{0,1\},
\]
and computes
\[
  H^\bullet(\bY,\cF_{\bY})\cong\PV^\bullet(\bY).
\]
Thus the chart construction recovers the global HKR complex of
\(\mathrm{Perf}(\bY)\).

\begin{theorem}[Resolved conifold factorisation algebra]
\label{thm:conifold-E3-gluing}
The data $(\Gamma_+,\Gamma_-,T_{+-})$ above glue by \v{C}ech descent
to a holomorphic $\Ethree$ factorisation algebra $\cF_{\bY}$ on the
resolved conifold. Its derived global sections compute the global
polyvector fields:
\[
  H^\bullet R\Gamma(\bY,\cF_{\bY})\cong H^\bullet R\Gamma(\bY,\PV^\bullet_{\bY}).
\]
The specialized chiral output attached to the Calabi--Yau threefold is
$\Eone$. A braided $\Etwo$ structure belongs to
$Z(\mathrm{Rep}(\Phi_3(\mathrm{Perf}(\bY))))$ or to a completed
Hall--Drinfeld double after the finiteness, non-degenerate Hall pairing,
PBW, and completion hypotheses are supplied.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The Ginzburg construction gives Calabi--Yau dg models for the two
affine charts. HKR identifies their Hochschild cochains with
polyvectors, and Costello--Li gives the local holomorphic
factorisation algebras. The transition $T_{+-}$ is induced by the
analytic coordinate change and its Jacobian, so it preserves the wedge
product, Schouten bracket, and hCS volume pairing on the overlap. The
two-chart \v{C}ech complex has cocycle condition $T_{-+}T_{+-}=1$.
Cartan's theorem B computes derived sections on the Stein chart and
overlap, giving the global polyvector-field complex.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Donaldson--Thomas and Gromov--Witten Invariants}

The Euler characteristic is recovered by Mayer--Vietoris:
\[
  \chi(\bY)=\chi(U_+)+\chi(U_-)-\chi(U_+\cap U_-)
  =1+1-0=2.
\]
Consequently the degree-zero Donaldson--Thomas series is
\[
  Z^{\mathrm{DT}}_{\beta=0}(\bY;q)
  =M(-q)^{\chi(\bY)}
  =M(-q)^2,
  \qquad M(q)=\prod_{k\ge1}(1-q^k)^{-k}.
\]
In the large-radius chamber the reduced curve-class factor is the
standard conifold product
\[
  Z^{\mathrm{DT}}_{\mathrm{red}}(\bY;q,Q)
  =
  \prod_{k\ge1}(1-(-q)^kQ)^k,
\]
while Szendroi's noncommutative chamber is the NCCR chamber attached
to $J(Q_{\mathrm{con}},W_{\mathrm{con}})$. The GW side is the
Gopakumar--Vafa/topological-vertex free energy
\[
  F^{\mathrm{GW}}(\bY;g_s,Q)
  =
  \sum_{g\ge0}g_s^{2g-2}F_g(\bY,Q),
  \qquad
  F_0=\mathrm{Li}_3(Q),
\]
and, for $g\ge2$,
\[
  F_g(\bY,Q)=
  \frac{|B_{2g}|}{2g(2g-2)!}\,
  \mathrm{Li}_{3-2g}(Q).
\]
These formulae identify the local toric construction with the standard
DT/GW invariants. A chiral Yangian presentation begins only after the
Hall pairing, completion, and evaluation data below are fixed.

\subsection*{Critical CoHA and Positive Half}

The chart $\mathbb C^3$ CoHA is
\[
  \CoHA(\mathbb C^3)=Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1),
\]
the positive half of the affine Yangian, on each affine chart.

For the resolved conifold the Hall input is the two-vertex Jacobi
algebra
\[
  A_{\mathrm{con}}=J(Q_{\mathrm{con}},W_{\mathrm{con}}).
\]
For $\mathbf d=(d_\circ,d_\bullet)\in\mathbb N^2$ set
$G_{\mathbf d}=GL_{d_\circ}\times GL_{d_\bullet}$ and
\[
  \mathfrak M_{\mathbf d}
  =
  \mathrm{Rep}_{\mathbf d}(Q_{\mathrm{con}})/G_{\mathbf d},
  \qquad
  \mathrm{Tr}(W_{\mathrm{con}}):
  \mathrm{Rep}_{\mathbf d}(Q_{\mathrm{con}})\to\mathbb C .
\]
The critical CoHA is
\[
  \CoHA_{\mathrm{con},\mathbf d}
  =
  H^{\bullet}_{G_{\mathbf d},c}
  \bigl(\mathrm{Rep}_{\mathbf d}(Q_{\mathrm{con}}),
  \phi_{\mathrm{Tr}(W_{\mathrm{con}})}\mathbb Q\bigr)
  \otimes \mathbb L^{\chi_Q(\mathbf d,\mathbf d)/2},
  \qquad
  \CoHA_{\mathrm{con}}=\bigoplus_{\mathbf d\in\mathbb N^2}
  \CoHA_{\mathrm{con},\mathbf d}.
\]
Multiplication is the vanishing-cycle Hall correspondence for short
exact sequences of $A_{\mathrm{con}}$-modules, with orientation data
chosen compatibly with the $3$-Calabi--Yau form. After equivariant
localization for the torus preserving the holomorphic volume form and
after completing in the positive cone $\mathbb N^2$, the product has a
shuffle presentation with conifold kernel
\[
  K_{ij}(u)=
  \frac{\prod_{\alpha:i\to j}(u+\mathrm{wt}(\alpha))}
       {\prod_{\alpha:j\to i}(u-\mathrm{wt}(\alpha))},
  \qquad i,j\in\{\circ,\bullet\},
\]
subject to the two F-term relations from $W_{\mathrm{con}}$ and the
Calabi--Yau weight constraint. Rapcak--Soibelman--Yang--Zhao and
Kapranov--Vasserot identify this localized positive half with the
quantum-toroidal positive half attached to the conifold quiver. The
passage to a full Yangian-type or $\mathcal W_{1+\infty}$-type vertex
object uses a Hall--Drinfeld double or centre, a non-degenerate pairing,
a PBW/no-extra-relations theorem, a topological completion, and a
vacuum-module evaluation.

\subsection*{The Conifold Chiral Invariant}

The invariant used here is the CY$_3$ toric/McKay scalar attached to
the compact curve sector. The conifold is the rank-two bundle
$\cO(-1)\oplus\cO(-1)$ over the curve $\mathbb P^1$; formulas for
$\mathrm{Tot}(\omega_S)$ over a Fano twofold belong to the local-surface
geometry.

The direct conifold McKay calculation gives
\[
  \kch\bigl(\Phi_3(\mathrm{Perf}(\bY))\bigr)=1.
\]
There are three compatible readings. First, $\bY$ deformation-retracts
to its compact core $\mathbb P^1$, hence $\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(\bY)=2$
and the conifold CY$_3$ normalization gives $\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(\bY)/2
=1$. Second, the GIT McKay lattice has one compact curve generator,
the class of $\mathbb P^1$. Third, the two-vertex Jacobi algebra has a
single compact root after imposing the F-term relations and the
large-radius stability chamber. This paragraph concerns only $\kch$.
The invariants $\kcat$, $\kBKM=c_N(0)/2$, and $\kfib$ require their own
constructions.
Compact non-toric CY$_3$ examples, such as the quintic, require a
separate atlas, anomaly analysis, orientation data, and analytic
completion.

\subsection*{Defect-Curve Vertex Algebra}

For a smooth defect curve $i:C\hookrightarrow\bY$, the chiral algebra
is obtained from the restricted factorisation algebra $i^!\cF_{\bY}$
after choosing a state-field functor on formal discs of $C$. A
conifold chiral Yangian presentation consists of the following data:
\[
  \bigl(\CoHA_{\mathrm{con}},\ \langle-,-\rangle_{\mathrm{Hall}},\
  \widehat{\mathcal D}_{\mathrm{Hall}}(\CoHA_{\mathrm{con}}),\
  \mathsf{ev}_{\mathrm{vac}},\ C\bigr).
\]
Here $\langle-,-\rangle_{\mathrm{Hall}}$ is a non-degenerate critical
Hall pairing, $\widehat{\mathcal D}_{\mathrm{Hall}}$ is the completed
Hall--Drinfeld double with PBW filtration and checked extra-relation
ideal, and $\mathsf{ev}_{\mathrm{vac}}$ is the vacuum-module evaluation
that turns the completed double into fields on $C$. Under these
hypotheses the positive half is the conifold critical CoHA above, the
OPE kernel is the conifold shuffle kernel, and the braided layer is the
centre or completed double. The \v{C}ech theorem supplies the local
CY$_3$ hCS factorisation algebra; the Hall data supplies generators,
OPEs, coproduct, and vacuum representation.
