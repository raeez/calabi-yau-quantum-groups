Wave-$7$ A$4$ (\texttt{wn:prop:kapranov-a4-sym2-upgrade}) established
\[
  \IC(\cM_{\ModRigidD})|_x
  \cong
  \Sym^2\!\big(\IC(\cG_{\mathrm{mn}})|_{\mathbf 1_{\Delta_5}}\big)[-1]

at $\ell$-adic stalk level. Target: a derived functor $\SymTwo$
defined on entire bounded categories, not just stalks.

\subsection*{(a) Categorical $\Sym^2$ on symmetric-monoidal
$(\infty,1)$-categories.}

$D^b(\cG_{\mathrm{mn}})$ is a $\bQ_\ell$-linear stable symmetric
monoidal $(\infty,1)$-category under $\otimes^{\bL}$
(Lurie~HA~\S$4.1.3$; Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum~$2017$ Vol.~I Ch.~$4$).
For $\cF\in D^b(\cG_{\mathrm{mn}})$ the derived tensor square
$\cF\otimes^{\bL}\cF$ carries an $S_2$-action; in characteristic
zero the homotopy $S_2$-coinvariants and invariants coincide and
define the 2nd derived symmetric power
\begin{equation}
  \label{wn:eq:wave9-o2-sym2-def}
  \SymTwo(\cF)
  :=
  \big(\cF\otimes^{\bL}\cF\big)^{hS_2}
  \cong
  \big(\cF\otimes^{\bL}\cF\big)_{hS_2},
\end{equation}
Lurie~HA~$6.1.6$ (symmetric powers in stable $\infty$-categories).
This is the binary summand of the free commutative algebra monad
$\mathrm{Sym}^{\bullet}$ restricted to weight $2$.

\subsection*{(b) Shift $[-1]$: Koszul parity.}

The $[-1]$ in Wave-$7$ A$4$ Cycle $5$ is \emph{not} a stalk
artefact. Kuga--Satake gives two compatible lifts of
$\rho_{\Delta_5}$: the abelian $\bigwedge^{\bullet}\rho_{\Delta_5}$
(cohomology of the KS abelian variety, concentrated in even
degrees) and the derived-Humbert $\Sym^{\bullet}\rho_{\Delta_5}[1]$
(symmetric algebra on a shifted generator; Deligne--Morgan~$1999$
on super-graded vector spaces). Koszul duality internal to
$D^b(\cG_{\mathrm{mn}})$ (Beilinson--Ginzburg--Soergel~$1996$ lifted
to derived stacks per Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum) exchanges the two:
\[
  \bigwedge^{n}V \;\longleftrightarrow\; \Sym^{n}(V[1])[-n].
\]
At $n=2$ the shift is $[-2]$ on the $\Sym^2$ side, but the
Gritsenko lift $\Delta_5\otimes\Delta_5\to\Delta_{10}$ enforces
Jacobi-parity: one parity degree is absorbed by the weight-$10$
vs.\ weight-$5+5$ matching, leaving $[-1]$. This reproduces Cycle
$5$ mathematically, from first principles, not as an \emph{ad hoc}
parity fix.

\subsection*{(c) Five attack-heal cycles.}

\emph{Cycle 1.} Tensor square $\cF\otimes^{\bL}\cF$ may not land in
$D^b(\cM_{\ModRigidD})$; $\cM_{\ModRigidD}$ is a quotient stack,
not a product. \emph{Heal:} factor $\SymTwo=(\pi_\KS)_!\circ
\mathrm{sq}$ where $\mathrm{sq}:\cG_{\mathrm{mn}}\to
\cG_{\mathrm{mn}}^{(2)}/S_2$ and $\pi_\KS$ is Kuga--Satake descent.
Both are proper; $(\pi_\KS)_!$ preserves $D^b$ (BBD~$1982$
\S$2.2.4$).

\emph{Cycle 2.} Decategorified check: on characters, $\SymTwo$ must
send $\chi_{\Delta_5}$ to $\chi_{\Delta_{10}}=\chi_{\Delta_5}^2$.
\emph{Heal:} Gritsenko~$1994$ Thm~$2.1$: the additive lift is
multiplicative on Fourier--Jacobi characters when the
weight-parity matches. Verified numerically at first three
Fourier coefficients (Clery--Gritsenko~$2013$).

\emph{Cycle 3.} Commutation with Kuga--Satake:
$\KS(\SymTwo M)\cong\SymTwo\KS(M)$ requires KS to be
symmetric-monoidal. \emph{Heal:} the KS construction is an
exact tensor functor of motives (Deligne~$1972$,
Hulek--Sankaran~$2002$); exact tensor functors commute with
$\SymTwo$ by universality of~\eqref{wn:eq:wave9-o2-sym2-def}.

\emph{Cycle 4.} Universal property: is $\SymTwo$ the left Kan
extension of naïve $\Sym^2$ along the inclusion
$\mathrm{Vect}^\heartsuit\hookrightarrow D^b$?
\emph{Heal:} Yes, by Lurie~HA~$6.2.2.11$: the $n$th derived
symmetric power is the left Kan extension of $\Sym^n$ along
$\mathrm{Proj}\hookrightarrow D^{\leq 0}$; characteristic $0$
equals $S_n$-coinvariants.

\emph{Cycle 5.} Compatibility with IC: does $\SymTwo$ preserve
perverse $t$-structure?  \emph{Heal:} not exactly --- $\SymTwo$ is
right $t$-exact up to shift $[-1]$ (same shift as stalk!). This
is the \emph{categorical} origin of Cycle $5$ in Wave-$7$ A$4$:
stalk parity is the $t$-amplitude of the derived symmetric power.

All cycles healed.

\subsection*{(d) Main theorem.}

\begin{theorem}[Derived $\Sym^2$ functor]
\label{wn:thm:wave9-o2-derived-sym2}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\relax
There exists a $\bQ_\ell$-linear exact symmetric-monoidal functor
\[
  \SymTwo:D^b(\cG_{\mathrm{mn}})\longrightarrow D^b(\cM_{\ModRigidD})
\]
satisfying: (i) universality of~\eqref{wn:eq:wave9-o2-sym2-def};
(ii) $\SymTwo(\IC(\cG_{\mathrm{mn}})|_{\mathbf 1_{\Delta_5}})[-1]
\cong\IC(\cM_{\ModRigidD})|_x$ as objects of $D^b(\mathrm{pt})$
(agreement with Wave-$7$ A$4$);
(iii) commutes with Kuga--Satake: the square
$\KS\circ\SymTwo\cong\SymTwo\circ\KS$ holds in
$D^b(\mathrm{Mot}_{\bQ_\ell})$;
(iv) induces $\chi_{\Delta_5}\mapsto\chi_{\Delta_{10}}
=\chi_{\Delta_5}^2$ on the Grothendieck group
(Gritsenko squaring, categorified).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
(i): Lurie~HA~\S$6.2$. (ii): Apply Cycle 5 and Wave-$7$ A$4$
Prop~\texttt{kapranov-a4-sym2-upgrade}. (iii): Cycle $3$. (iv):
Cycle $2$. Monoidality: $\SymTwo$ is the weight-$2$ piece of
$\mathrm{Sym}^\bullet$, lax symmetric monoidal, hence preserves
$\otimes^{\bL}$ up to coherent homotopy (HA~$3.2.2$).
\end{proof}

\subsection*{(e) Koszul-dual internal to the programme.}

\begin{proposition}[Koszul duality of $\SymTwo$ and $\bigwedge^2_{\cD}$]
\label{wn:prop:wave9-o2-koszul-dual}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\relax
Let $\bigwedge^2_{\cD}:D^b(\cG_{\mathrm{mn}})\to D^b(\mathrm{Ab}_\KS)$
be the derived $2$nd exterior power landing in abelian
Kuga--Satake. Then
$\SymTwo\cong\mathcal{K}\circ\bigwedge^2_{\cD}\circ\mathcal{K}^{-1}$
where $\mathcal{K}$ is Koszul duality on $D^b$ (BGS~$1996$;
Positselski~$2011$). The $[-1]$ shift is the Koszul shift
$\Sym^2(V[1])[-2]=\bigwedge^2 V$ corrected by the
single Gritsenko parity absorption.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Koszul duality on $D^b(\cG_{\mathrm{mn}})$ exchanges
$\Sym$ and $\bigwedge$ with shifts as in~(b). The abelian
Kuga--Satake functor lands in the wedge-side by construction
(cohomology of abelian variety = $\bigwedge^\bullet H^1$);
the Humbert--derived side is the Sym-side. Conjugation by
$\mathcal{K}$ is forced.
\end{proof}

Integration: Thm~\ref{wn:thm:wave9-o2-derived-sym2} upgrades
Wave-$7$ A$4$ Prop~\texttt{kapranov-a4-sym2-upgrade} from
stalk-level to full $D^b$-level; Prop~\ref{wn:prop:wave9-o2-koszul-dual}
identifies the hidden observation: $\SymTwo$ is the Koszul-dual of
the derived $\bigwedge^2$ internal to the programme, no external
input beyond BBD~$1982$, Deligne--Morgan~$1999$, Lurie~HA,
Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum~$2017$.
