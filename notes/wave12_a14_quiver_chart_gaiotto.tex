\begin{abstract}
The quiver-chart gluing problem separates into three obstruction
objects. The structural obstruction records the Serre-parity source of
the native $\Eone$ output in CY$_3$. The \v{C}ech
obstruction records whether the ordered local $\Eone$ charts glue. The
deformation obstruction records whether the $\Omega$-background preserves
the higher braiding locus. Vol~II's Swiss-cheese operad supplies a
one-sided bulk-to-boundary comparison map; the Vol~III $\Etwo$ bar
complex remains the closed-sector input. The class-$\mathcal S$ parent
attached to the K3$\times E$ boundary shadow is
$\cT[A_1,\Sigma_{0,24}]$, with central charges fixed by pants
decomposition. The CY$_4$ prediction is a typed conjecture about a
2-category of CY$_4$ data mapping to a Swiss-cheese $\Etwo$ boundary
theory.
\end{abstract}

\section{Three Obstruction Objects}

For a toric Calabi--Yau threefold chart system $\mathfrak U$ and its
factorisation-algebra output $A_X$, the question ``why does CY$_3$
specialise to $\Eone$?'' has three separate answers:
\[
  \mathcal O_1^{\mathrm{str}}(X)\in\Z_{\ge0},\qquad
  \mathcal O_2^{\mathrm{Cech}}(X)\in
  E_2^{2,*}(\mathfrak U;\cH^{\Eone}),\qquad
  \mathcal O_3^{\mathrm{def}}(X)\subset V_{\mathrm{def}}(X).
\]
The first object measures the deficit of a braided $R$-matrix on the
native output. The second measures the obstruction to gluing ordered
$\Eone$ charts. The third is the deformation locus where the
$\Omega$-background preserves a higher braiding.

\begin{proposition}[Typing of the CY$_3$ obstruction triple]
\label{prop:three-obstruction-typing}
The three objects
\[
(\mathcal O_1^{\mathrm{str}},
 \mathcal O_2^{\mathrm{Cech}},
 \mathcal O_3^{\mathrm{def}})
\]
live in distinct targets. Their simultaneous vanishing is meaningful,
and a single numerical comparison between them is type-incoherent.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Serre duality on a CY$_3$ category makes the Euler form skew:
\[
  \chi(E,F)=-\chi(F,E).
\]
This is the structural source of the ordered $\Eone$ floor and defines
$\mathcal O_1^{\mathrm{str}}$. Descent over an affine toric cover is a
\v{C}ech question for the local $\Eone$ factorisation algebras, giving
the spectral-sequence class $\mathcal O_2^{\mathrm{Cech}}$. The
$\Omega$-background has its own parameter space
\[
  V_{\mathrm{def}}=\mathrm{Spec}\,
  \Q[\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2,\varepsilon_3]/
  (\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2+\varepsilon_3),
\]
and the higher-braiding locus is a subvariety of that space. The three
targets are respectively numerical, cohomological, and geometric.
\end{proof}

\begin{example}[$\C^3$, conifold, local $\bP^2$]
For $\C^3$, all three obstructions vanish at the undeformed chart. For
the resolved conifold, $\mathcal O_2^{\mathrm{Cech}}$ vanishes on the
two-chart cover, while $\mathcal O_1^{\mathrm{str}}$ records the
rank-one centre/double deficit and $\mathcal O_3^{\mathrm{def}}$
selects the conifold deformation locus. For local $\bP^2$, the
\v{C}ech obstruction is governed by the three-chart toric cover and the
nontrivial transition around the compact curve class.
\end{example}

\section{Corner Vertex Algebras}

The Gaiotto--Rapcak corner algebra $\cY_{N_1,N_2,N_3}$ sees the same
triple. The structural obstruction is the defect of the $R$-matrix
between modules attached to different legs of the corner. The \v{C}ech
obstruction is the trivalent gluing defect for chart vertex algebras.
The deformation obstruction is the defect of the reduction
\[
  \cY_{N_1,N_2,N_3}\longrightarrow
  \cW_{N_1+N_2+N_3}
\]
to preserve the higher braiding away from the self-dual
$\Omega$-background locus.

\section{Class-\texorpdfstring{$\mathcal S$}{S} Parent}

The protected four-dimensional parent used in the K3$\times E$ boundary
comparison is the Gaiotto theory
\[
  \cT[A_1,\Sigma_{0,24}]
\]
with twenty-four maximal regular $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ punctures. A pants
decomposition of $\Sigma_{g,n}$ contains $2g-2+n$ trinions and
$3g-3+n$ tubes. For $(g,n)=(0,24)$ this gives $22$ copies of the
$A_1$ trinion $T_2$ and $21$ $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ gauge tubes. In the
Shapere--Tachikawa normalisation,
\[
  T_2:\ (n_v,n_h)=(0,4),\qquad
  \mathfrak{su}(2)\text{ tube}:\ (n_v,n_h)=(3,0),
\]
hence
\[
  (n_v,n_h)=(21\cdot3,22\cdot4)=(63,88).
\]
The central charges are therefore
\[
  c_{4d}=\frac{2n_v+n_h}{12}
        =\frac{2\cdot63+88}{12}
        =\frac{107}{6},
  \qquad
  a_{4d}=\frac{5n_v+n_h}{24}
        =\frac{5\cdot63+88}{24}
        =\frac{403}{24}.
\]
The Beem--Lemos--Liendo--Peelaers--Rastelli--van Rees correspondence
then gives the protected two-dimensional central charge
\[
  c_{2d}=-12c_{4d}=-214.
\]

\begin{proposition}[The supported class-\texorpdfstring{$\mathcal S$}{S} label]
\label{prop:class-s-sigma024}
The class-$\mathcal S$ parent compatible with the twenty-four K3
boundary points, the $\mathfrak{su}(2)^{24}$ flavour symmetry, and the
Beem--Rastelli central charge is $\cT[A_1,\Sigma_{0,24}]$. Replacing
the label by $\Sigma_{2,0}$ changes the theory: the same pants count
gives two trinions, three gauge tubes,
\[
  (n_v,n_h)=(9,8),\qquad
  c_{4d}=\frac{13}{6},\qquad
  a_{4d}=\frac{53}{24},\qquad
  c_{2d}=-26,
\]
and the puncture permutation symmetry disappears.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For $A_1$ class-$\mathcal S$, the Lagrangian pants decomposition
computes the effective multiplet counts: every trinion is $T_2$ and
every internal edge gauges an $\mathfrak{su}(2)$. At $(g,n)=(0,24)$
the counts above give $(63,88)$ and the displayed central charges.
This agrees with the Chacaltana--Distler all-maximal formula
$c_{4d}(A_1,\Sigma_{0,n})=(5n-13)/6$ at $n=24$, with the
Shapere--Tachikawa formula for $a_{4d}$, and with the Beem--Rastelli
$4d/2d$ relation. At $(g,n)=(2,0)$ the pants counts are $2$ and $3$,
which yields the second displayed line. These invariants determine
different class-$\mathcal S$ theories.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[K3\texorpdfstring{$\times$}{x}E invariants and modular lanes]
\label{prop:k3e-invariant-separation}
The K3$\times E$ construction spectrum is
\[
  \bigl\{\kcat(K3\times E),\,
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}},\,
  \kbkm(\Delta_5),\,
  \kfiber\bigr\}
  =
  \{0,3,5,24\}.
\]
Here
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)=
  \chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot0=0,\qquad
  \kbkm(\Delta_5)=c_1(0)/2=10/2=5,
\]
and $\kfiber=24$ is the Mukai-lattice rank of K3. These four entries
belong to categorical, Heisenberg-specialisation, Borcherds-weight,
and fibre-lattice constructions respectively. CHL quotients and the
Gritsenko--Clery catalogue contribute Borcherds weights in their own
rows; the class-$\mathcal S$ central charges above remain
four-dimensional and protected-sector data.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
K\"unneth multiplicativity gives the categorical value because
$\chi(\cO_E)=0$. The Heisenberg value records the rank-three
specialised chiral branch. The Borcherds value is the weight of the
Gritsenko $\Delta_5$ product, equivalently $c_1(0)/2$. The fibre value
is the rank of the K3 Mukai lattice. The construction labels differ,
so no arithmetic operation among the four entries is part of the
definition.
\end{proof}

\section{Swiss-Cheese Comparison}

Let $A\in\Etwo\text{-}\ChirAlg$. The Swiss-cheese operad
$\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{ch,top}}$ is a two-coloured operad: it contains a
closed $\Etwo$ algebra and an open $\Eone$ module over it. The
$\Etwo$ bar complex of $A$ uses the closed sector alone. The
Swiss-cheese structure enters after an open-sector module is chosen.

\begin{proposition}[Swiss-cheese comparison map]
\label{prop:swiss-cheese-comparison}
Let $A\in\Etwo\text{-}\ChirAlg$, and let $(A,A_{\mathrm{reg}})$ be the
Swiss-cheese pair with regular open-sector module. There is a natural
comparison morphism
\[
  B_{\Etwo}(A)\longrightarrow
  \HH_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{SC}}(A,A_{\mathrm{reg}}).
\]
Closed-sector injectivity and a cokernel description on open-bulk
transition strata form the remaining chain-level proof obligation.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The closed operations of the Swiss-cheese operad restrict to the usual
$\Etwo$ operations. The open operations add the $\Eone$ module
structure. Applying chiral Hochschild cochains gives a map from the
closed bar complex into the two-coloured Hochschild complex. The
additional open-sector operations are precisely the terms whose
homological support remains to be computed.
\end{proof}

\section{The Completed Modular Koszul Datum}

The eightfold open-closed datum is a stratified object with entries in
three ambient categories. Write
\[
\Pi_X^{\mathrm{oc}}(A)=
\bigl(
\cF_X(A),\overline B_X(A),\Theta_A,L_A,
(V^{\mathrm{br}},T^{\mathrm{br}}),
R_4^{\mathrm{mod}},
\cZ_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{der}}(A),
\Tr_A
\bigr).
\]
The first four entries and the trace live on $A$. The braided vacuum
data and the modular $R$-matrix live on $\Rep(A)$ or its completion.
The derived centre is a categorical object. This stratification is the
typing that prevents confusion between $A$, $\Rep(A)$, and
$\cZ_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{der}}(A)$.

\begin{theorem}[Typed open-closed datum]
\label{thm:typed-open-closed-datum}
The datum $\Pi_X^{\mathrm{oc}}(A)$ is well formed as an object of the
fibre product
\[
  \mathsf{Alg}_{\Eone}
  \times_{\mathsf{Cat}}
  \mathsf{RepCat}
  \times_{\mathsf{Cat}}
  \mathsf{Cent}_{\Etwo},
\]
where the maps are induced by representation category and derived-centre
functors. The ordinary product in $\ChirAlg$ is type-incoherent for
this datum.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The trace, bar complex, and modular deformation are operations on the
algebra $A$. The braiding and $R$-matrix require modules, hence live on
$\Rep(A)$. The centre is a braided category. A fibre product over the
underlying categorical data is the minimal typed container for all
entries.
\end{proof}

\section{The CY$_4$ Prediction}

Dunn additivity gives
\[
  \Etwo\simeq \Eone\otimes\Eone
\]
at the level of operads. A CY$_4$ input has two independent
two-dimensional transverse directions only after a polarisation,
framing, and Swiss-cheese boundary condition are chosen. The natural
CY$_4$ prediction is therefore categorical.

\begin{conjecture}[CY$_4$ Swiss-cheese boundary functor]
\label{conj:cy4-swiss-cheese-boundary}
There is a 2-category $\mathsf{CY}_4^{\mathrm{fr,oc}}$ of framed
open-closed CY$_4$ data and a functor
\[
  \Phi_4^{\mathrm{SC}}:
  \mathsf{CY}_4^{\mathrm{fr,oc}}
  \longrightarrow
  \mathrm{Alg}_{\Etwo}(\ChirAlg)
\]
whose closed-sector restriction is an $\Etwo$ chiral algebra and whose
boundary restriction is the Swiss-cheese open sector. Under a product
polarisation by two oriented surfaces, the local model is governed by
Dunn additivity.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}[Relation to the CY$_3$ rule]
For $d\ge3$, the specialised Vol~III output $A$ is $\Eone$-chiral.
Braided $\Etwo$ structure belongs to the Drinfeld centre, a completed
double, or a separately constructed CY$_4$ closed sector. The CY$_4$
conjecture above supplies the latter route while the CY$_3$ output
retains its $\Eone$ type.
\end{remark}

\section{Concrete Chart Consequences}

\begin{proposition}[Local $\bP^2$]
For $X=\mathrm{Tot}(\cO_{\bP^2}(-3))$, the three toric charts are
$\C^3$ charts glued by the canonical bundle transition. The local chart
CoHAs are positive halves; the compact curve class enters through the
\v{C}ech transition and the corresponding DT vertex. Any full
$\cW$-algebra statement requires the completed double and evaluation
functor.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Resolved conifold]
For
\[
  X=\mathrm{Tot}(\cO_{\bP^1}(-1)\oplus\cO_{\bP^1}(-1)),
\]
the two affine $\C^3$ charts glue by the transition
$\tilde z=z^{-1}$, $u_-=zu_+$, $v_-=zv_+$. The chart CoHAs are
$Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$ objects; the conifold shuffle kernel
builds the positive half attached to the two-vertex conifold quiver.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Quintic boundary]
For the compact quintic threefold $X_5$, the Hodge supertrace gives
\[
  \kch^{\mathrm{Hodge}}(A_{X_5})=
  \sum_q(-1)^qh^{0,q}(X_5)=1-1=0.
\]
The BCOV scalar
\[
  \frac{\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(X_5)}{24}=-\frac{200}{24}=-\frac{25}{3}
\]
is a different invariant. A strict compact chain-level chiral model
requires TCFT trace, framing, anomaly cancellation, and analytic
completion.
\end{proposition}

\section{Primary Sources}

The operadic input is Dunn additivity and Voronov's Swiss-cheese
operad, with the holomorphic-topological form used in Vol~II. The
corner-vertex-algebra input is Gaiotto--Rapcak and
Prochazka--Rapcak. The chart CoHA input is Schiffmann--Vasserot for
$\C^3$ and Rapcak--Soibelman--Yang--Zhao for toric CY$_3$ quivers.
The class-$\mathcal S$ arithmetic uses Gaiotto's construction,
Chacaltana--Distler's pants-decomposition central charges,
Shapere--Tachikawa's anomaly normalisation, and the
Beem--Lemos--Liendo--Peelaers--Rastelli--van Rees $4d/2d$
correspondence. The compact CY$_3$ boundary statements use
Costello--Li, Costello--Gwilliam, and the BCOV/TCFT formalism.
