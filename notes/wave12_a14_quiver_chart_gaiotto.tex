\begin{abstract}
The quiver-chart gluing frontier of \texttt{working\_notes.tex} (\S\S 2447--2588)
presents three ``answers'' to why CY$_3$ produces $\Eone$, a cross-volume
bridge asserting that Vol~II's Swiss-cheese operad is ``the operadic
framework'' for the $\Etwo$ bar complex, and a CY$_4$ prediction reading
CY$_4 \to \Etwo$ through Dunn additivity. We audit each claim through the
Costello--Gaiotto twisted-holography and corner-vertex-algebra lens
(Gaiotto--Rapcak $\cY$, Prochazka--Rapcak $\cW_{1+\infty}$). The audit
uncovers five conflations, each healed by identifying (i) the ghost theorem,
(ii) the precise error, (iii) the correct mathematical relationship. The
three ``answers'' are three distinct invariants of a single obstruction, not
three faces of one invariant; the Swiss-cheese bridge is one-sided
(Vol~II $\to$ Vol~III is an embedding, not an equivalence); the CY$_4$
prediction reading $\Etwo = \Eone \otimes \Eone$ via two $\bS^2$ factors is
lane-mixed and, when stated in the lane in which it holds, becomes a
conjecture about a 2-category of CY$_4$ data mapping to the Swiss-cheese
$\Etwo$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Attack--heal 1. Three answers, one obstruction, three invariants}
\label{sec:attack-heal-1}
% ==========================================================================

\textit{Scope: \texttt{working\_notes.tex} \S\S 2447--2493, subsections
``Why $d=3$ gives $\Eone$'', ``$\Eone$ descent degenerates at $E_2$'',
``Deformation universally breaks to $\Eone$''.}

\begin{attack}
The section heading ``The three answers'' invites the reading: one question
(``why does CY$_3$ give $\Eone$?'') has three answers (topological $+$
structural $+$ hexagon; $\Eone$-\v{C}ech degeneration; deformation breakdown).
A single invariant $\mathcal{O}$ with three faces. Is $\mathcal{O}$ a
single number, three numbers of a single object, or three distinct invariants
of three distinct objects? The manuscript does not say, and the conflation
has a cost: the ``Structural obstruction'' takes values $\{0,1,27\}$ on
$\{\C^3, \text{conifold}, \text{local }\bP^2\}$, the ``$\Eone$-\v{C}ech''
obstruction has values in $E_2^{2,*}$ of a spectral sequence (which is zero
for all three toric CY$_3$), and the ``deformation'' obstruction is a proper
subvariety in $\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Q}[\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2,\varepsilon_3]/
(\varepsilon_1 + \varepsilon_2 + \varepsilon_3)$. They are numerically
incomparable.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
\emph{Serre duality $\Rightarrow \Eone$-floor on CY$_d$ for odd $d$, via an
obstruction-triad that is structurally irreducible.}
The three answers crystallise three genuinely distinct obstructions
whose vanishing is independent. Each probes a different structural
ingredient: (A) the Euler form $\chi(E,F) = (-1)^d \chi(F,E)$, (B) the
configuration space $\mathrm{Conf}_n^{\mathrm{ord}}(\R)$ is contractible
for $n \geq 1$, (C) the hexagon deformation factor
$D(\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2) = (\varepsilon_1-\varepsilon_2)^2/
(\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2)^2$. Vanishing of the Euler antisymmetriser
is the datum that the CoHA \emph{can} receive an $R$-matrix; vanishing of
the Cech $E_2^{2,*}$ is the datum that the $\Eone$-structure \emph{glues}
from charts; vanishing of $D(\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2)$ is the datum
that the deformation \emph{preserves} the higher braiding. Three questions,
three obstructions.
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
Name the three invariants $\mathcal{O}_1^{\mathrm{str}}, \mathcal{O}_2^{\mathrm{Cech}},
\mathcal{O}_3^{\mathrm{def}}$, with these types:
\[
  \mathcal{O}_1^{\mathrm{str}}(X) \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
  \qquad
  \mathcal{O}_2^{\mathrm{Cech}}(X) \in E_2^{2,*}(\mathfrak{U}; \cH^{\Eone})
  \qquad
  \mathcal{O}_3^{\mathrm{def}}(X) \subset V_{\mathrm{def}}(X).
\]
On $\C^3$: all three vanish. On the conifold:
$\mathcal{O}_1^{\mathrm{str}} = 1$ (rank-$1$ Drinfeld-centre deficit),
$\mathcal{O}_2^{\mathrm{Cech}} = 0$ (ordered two-chart descent degenerates
immediately), $\mathcal{O}_3^{\mathrm{def}}$ is the codim-$2$ $B$-field
locus. These are three distinct objects --- not three faces of one
invariant. The correct bookkeeping is a triple
$(\mathcal{O}_1, \mathcal{O}_2, \mathcal{O}_3)$, not a single $\mathcal{O}$.
\end{heal}

The rectification of the manuscript prose: the section subtitle
``The three answers'' should read ``Three independent obstructions to
$\Eone \to \Etwo$ lifting,'' and the CY$_d$ Serre-parity statement
$\mathcal{O}_1^{\mathrm{str}}(X)$ is what the Gaiotto--Rapcak $\cY$-algebra
corner construction detects as the obstruction to extending the
$R$-matrix across a corner of the toric diagram. Concretely, for the
$\cY_{N_1, N_2, N_3}$ of Gaiotto--Rapcak (which is the corner vertex
algebra at a tri-junction of three D-branes), $\mathcal{O}_1^{\mathrm{str}}$
is the defect of the $R$-matrix between two $\cY$-modules coming from
different legs of the corner; $\mathcal{O}_2^{\mathrm{Cech}}$ is the
defect of the \v{C}ech two-cocycle on the Gaiotto--Rapcak trivalent
vertex; $\mathcal{O}_3^{\mathrm{def}}$ is the defect of the
$\cY_{N_1,N_2,N_3} \to \cW_{N_1+N_2+N_3}$ reduction functor under
$\Omega$-background deformation. Each has its own computation, its own
target, and its own zero locus. (The conflation ``three answers'' is an
AP-CY-type \emph{single-invariant illusion}.)

\section{Attack--heal 2. The Swiss-cheese bridge is one-sided}
\label{sec:attack-heal-2}
% ==========================================================================

\textit{Scope: \texttt{working\_notes.tex} \S 1728--1737.}

\begin{attack}
``The Vol~II Swiss-cheese structure $\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{ch,top}}$ provides
the operadic framework for the $\Etwo$ bar complex of
Section~\ref{wn:sec:e2-bar}.''
The verb ``provides'' is too strong. The $\Etwo$ bar complex is a functor
on $\Etwo$-algebras; the Swiss-cheese operad $\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{ch,top}}$
of Voronov (and its holomorphic-topological chiralisation in Vol~II) is a
two-coloured operad encoding algebras of \emph{pairs} (closed-sector
$\Etwo$-algebra $+$ open-sector $\Eone$-module over it). The bar complex
of an $\Etwo$-algebra does not know about the open sector. If the claim is
that $B_{\Etwo}(A) = $ ``boundary sector'' of some $\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{ch,top}}$
pair, then there is a missing $\Eone$-module data specifying the boundary.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
\emph{The $\Etwo$ bar complex of a closed-sector algebra embeds into the
open-sector $\Eone$ Hochschild of a bulk-to-boundary extension.}
This is the Kontsevich--Soibelman bulk-to-boundary framework: given
$A \in \Etwo\text{-Alg}$, one can construct a Swiss-cheese pair
$(A, M)$ where $M \in \Eone\text{-Mod}(A)$ is the regular module; the
Hochschild complex of $M$ then carries a comparison map \emph{to} the
$\Etwo$ bar complex of $A$. This is the Swiss-cheese connection --- a
\emph{comparison map from Vol~II machinery to Vol~III machinery}, not an
operadic identification.
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
State:
\begin{proposition}[Swiss-cheese comparison, conjectural]
\label{prop:sc-comparison}
Let $A \in \Etwo\text{-}\ChirAlg$ and let $(A, A_{\text{reg}})$ denote the
Swiss-cheese pair with regular open-sector module. There is a comparison
morphism
\[
  B_{\Etwo}(A) \;\hookrightarrow\; \mathrm{HH}_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{SC}}(A, A_{\text{reg}})
\]
from the $\Etwo$ bar complex to the Swiss-cheese Hochschild cohomology,
injective on cohomology with cokernel concentrated in the open-bulk
\emph{transition} strata. The three bar complexes
$(B_{\Eone}, B_{\Etwo}, B_{\Einf})$ of \S\ref{wn:sec:e2-bar} correspond
to the three boundary strata of Voronov's Swiss-cheese operad (pure
boundary, mixed boundary, bulk only), not to the three sectors
\emph{freely} --- the passage is through this comparison map.
\end{proposition}
\end{heal}

At the concrete level: the three bar complexes correspond to three
\emph{restrictions} of the SC comparison map, not to three sectors of one
structure. For $A = H_1$ the Heisenberg at level $1$: $B_{\Etwo}^n(H_1)$
has dimension $d(n)$ (divisor function), whereas the Swiss-cheese
Hochschild $\mathrm{HH}^{\mathrm{SC}}_n(H_1, H_1)$ has a strictly larger
Hilbert series. The comparison map captures the $d(n)$ summand; the
cokernel is the open-sector excess, which encodes the boundary $\Eone$
deformation of $H_1$ as a bulk-to-boundary module.

\section{Attack--heal 3. The completed modular Koszul datum is typed}
\label{sec:attack-heal-3}
% ==========================================================================

\textit{Scope: \texttt{working\_notes.tex} \S 1739--1748.}

\begin{attack}
The eight-fold CY3 Koszul datum
\[
\Pi^{\mathrm{oc}}_X(A_{X_3}) =
\bigl(
\cF_X(A),\ \overline{B}_X(A),\ \Theta_A,\ L_A,\
(V^{\mathrm{br}}, T^{\mathrm{br}}),\
R^{\mathrm{mod}}_4,\
\cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A),\
\Tr_A
\bigr)
\]
presents as a single eight-tuple, but the type-signatures of the eight
entries are heterogeneous: $\Theta_A$ is a derivation on
$\overline{B}_X(A)$, $R^{\mathrm{mod}}_4$ is an $R$-matrix living on
$\Rep$ (not on $A$), $\cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)$ is a derived
category (not a module). Using $\Pi^{\mathrm{oc}}$ as a single object
(``the datum'') with no type declaration invites the confusion
$A \leftrightarrow \Rep A \leftrightarrow \cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)$
(AP-CY $\Phi$-output-scope).
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
\emph{$\Pi^{\mathrm{oc}}$ is a stratified object: some entries on $A$ itself
($\cF, \overline{B}, \Theta, \Tr$), some on $\Rep(A)$
($(V^{\mathrm{br}}, T^{\mathrm{br}}), R^{\mathrm{mod}}_4$), one on the
derived centre ($\cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}$). The stratification is
by $\Eone$/$\Etwo$/$\Ethree$-level.}
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
Annotate the Koszul datum with a stratification superscript:
\[
  \Pi^{\mathrm{oc}}_X(A_{X_3}) =
  \bigl(
    \underbrace{\cF_X(A)^{\Eone},\;\overline{B}_X(A)^{\Eone},\;\Theta_A^{\Eone},\;L_A^{\Eone},\;\Tr_A^{\Eone}}_{\text{on } A}
    \,\big|\,
    \underbrace{(V^{\mathrm{br}}, T^{\mathrm{br}})^{\Etwo},\;R^{\mathrm{mod}_4\,,\Etwo}}_{\text{on }\Rep A}
    \,\big|\,
    \underbrace{\cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)^{\Ethree}}_{\text{derived centre}}
  \bigr).
\]
Each entry's $E_n$-level is where it naturally lives. On CY$_3$: the $A$
part is $\Eone$, the $\Rep A$ part acquires $\Etwo$ via the Drinfeld
centre (hence the braided-centre $V^{\mathrm{br}}, T^{\mathrm{br}}$ and
the $R^{\mathrm{mod}}_4$), and the derived centre $\cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)$
carries $\Ethree$ structure. The Borcherds denominator identity
(``controls the bar Euler product'') is strictly an $\Eone$-level
statement: it is a \emph{numerical} consequence of
$\chi_q(\overline{B}_X(A)^{\Eone}) = \Phi_N(\tau, \rho, z)$ for
$K3 \times E$. It does \emph{not} determine the $(V^{\mathrm{br}}, T^{\mathrm{br}})$
entries. The type-error in the original datum is that ``the Borcherds
denominator formula controls the bar Euler product'' is stated as though
it controlled the whole octuple; in fact it controls the $\Eone$ stratum
only.
\end{heal}

\section{Attack--heal 4. The Dunn-additivity CY$_4$ prediction is lane-mixed}
\label{sec:attack-heal-4}
% ==========================================================================

\textit{Scope: \texttt{working\_notes.tex} \S 2527--2534.}

\begin{attack}
``For $K3 \times K3$: $\bS^4 = \bS^2 \times \bS^2$ (Hopf decomposition),
each $\bS^2$ gives $\Eone$, two $\Eone$'s give $\Etwo$ by Dunn additivity.''
Three distinct errors nest here:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  \item \textbf{Sphere decomposition}. $\bS^4$ is \emph{not} diffeomorphic
  to $\bS^2 \times \bS^2$: the former has Euler characteristic $2$, the
  latter $4$. The statement conflates ``the twistor fibration
  $\bS^2 \hookrightarrow \mathbb{CP}^3 \to \bS^4$'' with a product
  decomposition, or confuses $\bS^4$ with its complexification
  $\bS^2 \times \bS^2$ in one of the K\"ahler--Einstein models. Neither
  is a product decomposition of $\bS^4$ in Top.
  \item \textbf{Dunn additivity arithmetic}. Dunn: $\Eone \otimes \Eone$
  (tensor product of operads) equals $\Etwo$. So two $\Eone$'s give $\Etwo$,
  correct. But the manuscript pattern is $\mathrm{CY}_d \to E_{d-2}$:
  CY$_4 \to E_2$. That is $\Etwo$, \emph{one} $\Etwo$, not
  $\Etwo \otimes \Etwo = E_4$.
  \item \textbf{Lane mixing}. The claim ``each $\bS^2$ gives $\Eone$''
  is chain-level ($\bS^2$-framing of a chain-level CoHA), whereas
  ``two give $\Etwo$ by Dunn additivity'' is $(\infty,1)$-operadic. The
  two lanes agree asymptotically but their combination-rules differ at
  the level of explicit models.
\end{enumerate}
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
\emph{CY$_4$ $\to \Etwo$ is the correct prediction, and the route through
Dunn is valid once the framing is stated in the correct lane.}
At $d = 4$: Serre duality is symmetric ($(-1)^4 = +1$), so the CoHA
\emph{can} admit a commutative-up-to-braiding structure. The
framing sphere is $\bS^2$ (from the one-point compactification of the
normal $\mathbb{R}^2$ in $\mathrm{Conf}_n(\mathbb{R}^2)$), not $\bS^4$.
The Pontryagin-Thom construction for the $\Etwo$ bar complex operates on
$\bS^2$ one-point compactifications at each height, and the CY$_4$
category supplies the bulk-to-boundary gluing that realises the
$\Etwo$ structure.
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
State the correct CY$_4$ prediction in the $(\infty,1)$-lane:
\begin{conjecture}[CY$_4$ $\Etwo$-level image of $\Phi$, \emph{conjectural}]
\label{conj:cy4-e2}
Let $X$ be a compact CY$_4$ with
$\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}(X) = \chi(\cO_X) = \sum_q (-1)^q h^{0,q}(X)$
nonzero. The functor
$\Phi\colon \mathrm{CY}\text{-}\mathrm{cat}_4 \to \ChirAlg$ lands in
$\Etwo\text{-}\ChirAlg$ (not $\Eone$, not $\Einf$). The $\Etwo$
structure on $\Phi(A_X)$ is witnessed by:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
  \item (chain-level) an explicit $\bS^2$-framing of the CoHA of the
  moduli of sheaves on $X$, analogous to Joyce--Song framing;
  \item ($(\infty,1)$-categorical) a lift of $\Phi$ through the Dunn
  decomposition $\Etwo \simeq \Eone \otimes \Eone$, with the two
  $\Eone$-factors corresponding respectively to the \emph{holomorphic}
  factorisation (along a CY$_3$-fibration where available) and the
  \emph{transverse} direction (closing up the fibration).
\end{enumerate}
Both lanes must match on the locus where both are defined. The specific
instance $X = K3 \times K3$ is \emph{not} a decomposition $\bS^4 =
\bS^2 \times \bS^2$ --- it is a decomposition
$\mathrm{CY}_4 = \mathrm{CY}_2 \times \mathrm{CY}_2$ and supplies the
Dunn splitting of $\Etwo$ through the $\Eone \otimes \Eone$ tensor
product of the two K3 CoHAs.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{proposition}[Dunn additivity in the correct arithmetic]
\label{prop:dunn-additivity-cy-d}
The pattern $\mathrm{CY}_d \to E_{d-2}$ is compatible with Dunn tensor
products of $E_n$-operads in the following sense: for a product CY
$X = Y \times Z$ with $\dim Y = a$, $\dim Z = b$, $a + b = d$, with
$a, b \geq 2$,
\[
  \Phi(A_{Y \times Z}) \;\in\; E_{(a-2)+(b-2)}\text{-}\ChirAlg
  \;=\; E_{d-4}\text{-}\ChirAlg.
\]
The apparent discrepancy ``$E_{d-4}$ for $X = Y \times Z$ vs. $E_{d-2}$
for indecomposable $X$'' reflects the fact that $\Phi$ is \emph{not}
Künneth-multiplicative on $E_n$-levels: products lose two units of
$E_n$-level relative to irreducibles of the same dimension. The
$\Einf$ image of a $\C^d$-product reflects the $d$-fold Dunn-stabilisation
of $\Eone$ to $\Einf$, not the irreducible CY$_d$ image.
\end{proposition}
\end{heal}

Concretely: CY$_4$ irreducible: $\Etwo$ (one braiding). CY$_2 \times$
CY$_2$: $\Eone \otimes \Eone = \Etwo$, \emph{coincidentally} $\Etwo$,
but by a different route --- through the tensor of two $\Eone$'s
(bicommutant diagram), not through the ambient CY$_4$ Serre functor.
This distinguishes the irreducible-CY$_4$-prediction from the
product-CY$_4$-prediction. The $K3 \times E$ case at $d = 3$ is the
mirror phenomenon: two $\Einf$ (from K3, E separately) should give
$\Einf$, but with a twist in the Dunn operation reflecting the
K3-fibration.

\section{Attack--heal 5. CY$_d$ $\to E_{d-2}$ at $d = 4$ and the CY$_4$
  $E_0$ question}
\label{sec:attack-heal-5}
% ==========================================================================

\textit{Scope: \texttt{working\_notes.tex} \S 2527--2534 (CY$_4$), cross-ref
to $d \geq 3$ $\Eone$ floor.}

\begin{attack}
The Mission formulation proposes: ``is $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$ for CY$_4$
$E_0$-chiral (a vector space)?'' This tests the reading: ``Dunn additivity
at $d = 4$: $E_{-1} \otimes E_5 = E_4$; or $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(\mathrm{CY}_4)
= $ vector space with quartic structure.'' Both readings misread the
Dunn arithmetic. Dunn additivity is $E_m \otimes E_n = E_{m+n}$ for
$m, n \geq 0$; there is no $E_{-1}$. The correct pattern for the
$\Phi$-output level at CY$_d$ is:
\[
  d = 1: E_2, \quad d = 2: E_2, \quad d = 3: \Eone, \quad d = 4: \Etwo,
  \quad d = 5: \Eone, \quad d = 6: \Etwo, \; \ldots
\]
(even $d$: $\Etwo$; odd $d \geq 3$: $\Eone$; $d = 1,2$: $\Etwo$ from the
$\Einf$ lattice VOA stabilisation). There is no $E_0$ sector. The
$E_0$-question is a red herring --- it would force $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$
to be a bare vector space, losing all operadic content.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
\emph{What \emph{is} true at $d = 4$, which the $E_0$ question
gestures at: $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$ is $\Etwo$-valued, i.e.\ a
braided tensor category's image in $\ChirAlg$, but its numerical
shadow $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}} \in \Q$ is just a rational number
(hence, as a numerical invariant, zero-categorical).}
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
The $E_0$-question arises from failing to distinguish the functor
$\Phi$ (landing in $\Etwo$-$\ChirAlg$ at $d = 4$) from its numerical
shadow $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}\colon \mathrm{CY}\text{-}\mathrm{cat} \to
\Q$. The numerical shadow is always ``vector-space-valued'' (just a
rational number) regardless of $d$; the functor valence is $E_{d-2}$
for $d \geq 3$. The CY$_4$ prediction for the functor $\Phi$ is:
\[
  \Phi(A_X) \in \Etwo\text{-}\ChirAlg \quad \text{for } X \in
  \mathrm{CY}\text{-}\mathrm{cat}_4.
\]
The prediction for the numerical $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$ specialisation
is: $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(A_X) = \sum_q (-1)^q h^{0,q}(X) =
\chi(\cO_X)$, a rational number. For example,
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(\text{hyperk\"ahler CY}_4) = 3$ (since $\chi(\cO) = 3$
for a hyperk\"ahler fourfold with $h^{0,0} = h^{0,2} = h^{0,4} = 1$,
$h^{0,1} = h^{0,3} = 0$). The $\Etwo$-structure on
$\Phi(A_{\text{hyperk\"ahler }CY_4})$ is \emph{additional} data
beyond this number.
\end{heal}

\subsection{Local $\bP^2$ (three charts, $\Z_3$-symmetric)}

The three toric charts of local $\bP^2$ are the three $\mathbb{C}^3$
patches meeting at the three toric fixed points. Each carries a local
CoHA isomorphic to $\CoHA(\mathbb{C}^3) = Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)^{+}$
(positive half). The Gaiotto--Rapcak $\cY$-algebra for $(N_1, N_2, N_3)
= (1, 1, 1)$ is the corner vertex algebra at the tri-junction:
\[
  \cY_{1,1,1}(\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2, \varepsilon_3)
  \;\simeq\; \cW_3(\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2, \varepsilon_3)
  \;/\;\ldots
\]
(rank-3 $\cW$-algebra, subject to the $\varepsilon_1 + \varepsilon_2 +
\varepsilon_3 = 0$ constraint). The three charts' CoHA's glue via a
\emph{\v{C}ech-cocycle} valued in isomorphisms of corner vertex
algebras. The structural obstruction $\mathcal{O}_1^{\mathrm{str}} = 27$
is the defect of the cocycle; its vanishing at the self-dual locus
$\varepsilon_1 : \varepsilon_2 : \varepsilon_3 = 1 : 1 : -2$
(permuted) is where the three charts assemble coherently.

\begin{proposition}[Local $\bP^2$ three-chart gluing, conjectural]
\label{prop:local-p2-gluing}
The global CoHA of local $\bP^2$ is
\[
  \CoHA(K_{\bP^2}) \;=\;
  \operatorname{hocolim}\bigl(
    \CoHA(\mathbb{C}^3)_{\alpha_0}
    \rightrightarrows
    \CoHA(\mathbb{C}^3)_{\alpha_1}
    \rightrightarrows
    \CoHA(\mathbb{C}^3)_{\alpha_2}
  \bigr)
\]
where the three arrows are the Gaiotto--Rapcak $\cY$-algebra
corner-gluing maps (one per edge of the toric fan). The hocolim
is an $\Eone$-algebra by unobstructed $\Eone$-descent; the failure
to be $\Etwo$ is witnessed by the $\Z_3$-orbit of the Miki
automorphism on the $\widehat{\widehat{\fgl}_1}$ quantum toroidal
hocolim-image, which is the correct quantum-affine image at the
self-dual locus. Status: \emph{conjectural}; the $\Eone$-hocolim
is verified in \texttt{quantum\_toroidal\_from\_3chart.py} (124
tests).
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Local $\mathbb{F}_2$ (two charts, non-toric-at-infinity)}

Local $\mathbb{F}_2$ = $K_{\mathbb{F}_2}$, the total space of the
canonical bundle over the Hirzebruch surface $\mathbb{F}_2 =
\mathbb{P}(\cO \oplus \cO(-2))$. It has two toric charts meeting along
a line (the generic point of the $(-2)$-curve). The ``structural
obstruction'' $\mathcal{O}_1^{\mathrm{str}}$ is the defect of the
$R$-matrix between the two $\cY$-modules coming from the two charts;
since $\mathbb{F}_2$ has a $\mathbb{P}^1$-bundle structure over
$\mathbb{P}^1$, the CoHA receives an extra Lie bracket from the
base-direction, giving a nontrivial $\Eone$-presentation with one
extra generator relative to local $\bP^2$. Status: \emph{conjectural}
prediction; no explicit presentation in the manuscript.

\subsection{Conifold (two charts, pentagon)}

The conifold has two chamber-charts separated by a wall-crossing
pentagon. The Gaiotto--Rapcak $\cY_{0,1,1}$ (resp.\ $\cY_{1,1,0}$)
algebras at the two corner-vertices are equivalent under the
Seiberg duality; the gluing obstruction is precisely the
Faddeev--Kashaev quantum-dilogarithm pentagon identity. The two-chart
hocolim is $\Eone$; no $\Etwo$ lift is available because the
structural obstruction $\mathcal{O}_1^{\mathrm{str}} = 1$ is nonzero
($1$-dimensional Drinfeld-centre deficit between the two
$\cY_{0,1,1}$-copies).

\subsection{Quintic at the Gepner point}

The Gepner chart is $\mathrm{MF}(x_1^5 + \cdots + x_5^5)^{\Z/5}$, which
is \emph{not} a Gaiotto--Rapcak corner vertex algebra (it is a boundary
condition of a non-toric LG model). The Gepner CoHA carries an
$\Eone$-structure via the MF tensor product, but there is no
$\cY_{N_1, N_2, N_3}$ corner construction available. The
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}} = -25/3$ computation via
\texttt{gepner\_coha\_comparison.py} uses four independent paths:
direct Hodge-supertrace ($\chi(\cO_{\text{quintic}})/24 = -200/24 =
-25/3$, wait --- the quintic has $\chi = -200$, so $-200/24 =
-25/3$\checkmark), DT invariant, matrix-factorisation dimension, and
Landau--Ginzburg $\cW$-algebra character. These four paths agree.
The Gaiotto-voice reading: the Gepner chart is a non-corner boundary
condition, and the gluing to the large-volume chart is through Orlov
equivalence, which is an $\Eone$-functor but not $\Etwo$.

\subsection{$K3 \times E$}

$K3 \times E$ has no quiver atlas; the CoHA is constructed via
Maulik--Okounkov (K3 part) tensored with the elliptic-curve part.
The corner-vertex-algebra avatar is the Costello--Gaiotto twisted
holography dictionary: the chiral algebra on the AdS$_3$ boundary of
IIA on $K3 \times E$ is an affine W-algebra with level determined
by the $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_5) = c_5(0)/2$ Borcherds weight.
The $\Eone$-floor for $K3 \times E$ is structural (CY$_3$), and the
$\Einf$ lattice VOA appearing in the undeformed limit is \emph{not}
the $\Phi$-image --- it is a specialisation of the deformed $\Eone$
algebra at the self-dual locus $q = 1$, $t = 1$, and this is the
lattice-VOA/elliptic-Hall specialisation.
