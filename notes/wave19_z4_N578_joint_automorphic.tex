\section*{Wave-19 Z4: joint automorphic structure of the three non-CHL
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery forms at $N\in\{5,7,8\}$}
\label{wn:sec:wave19-z4-N578-joint-automorphic}

Three simplest-divisor Siegel forms at paramodular level $N\in\{5,7,8\}$
carry weights outside $\Z_{\ge 1}$: $\Delfnm{1/2}{5}$, $\Delfnm{1/4}{7}$,
$\Delfnm{0}{8}$. They are the three non-CHL entries in the
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008 census (Thm~1.1), sitting outside the Nikulin
admissibility $\varphi(N)\mid 2$ of the BKM-denominator theorem of
Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 Part~II. Five attack--heal cycles consolidate
them into a single joint automorphic object, handled through the
correct metaplectic/spin cover in each slot.

\subsection*{Cycle 1: metaplectic lift at $N=5$, weight $1/2$}

\emph{Attack.} The Borcherds multiplicative lift of
\cite{Borcherds1995} produces integer-weight Siegel forms from weak
Jacobi input with integer $c(0)$. At $N=5$, the GC~2008 census
identifies $\Delfnm{1/2}{5}\in M_{1/2}(K(5),\chi_5)$ with half-integer
weight, forcing $c_5(0)=1$ odd --- outside the original Borcherds
framework.

\emph{Heal.} Bruinier 2002 \emph{LNM}~1780 Thm~3.22 extends
Borcherds' lift to $O(2,\ell)$ on the metaplectic double cover
$\Mp_2(\Z)\to\SL_2(\Z)$: the Weil representation
$\rho_L\colon\Mp_2(\Z)\to\mathrm{GL}(\C[A_L])$ on the discriminant
group $A_L=L^\vee/L$ admits vector-valued weak Jacobi forms
$f(\tau)=\sum_\gamma f_\gamma(\tau)\mathfrak e_\gamma$ of half-integer
weight $k\in\tfrac 12+\Z$, and the singular-theta lift
$\Phi_L(f)$ is a meromorphic modular form on
$O(L)\backslash\HH^{2,\ell}$ of weight $k$ (Bruinier 2002 Prop.~5.1).
At $L=L^{(5)}=U\oplus U(5)\oplus\langle -10\rangle$ with
$\mathrm{disc}(L^{(5)})=\Z/10$ the relevant vector-valued input is the
level-$5$ half-integer weak Jacobi form
\[
   \psiJ{1/2}{5}(\tau,z)
   \;=\; \vartheta_1(\tau,z)\,\eta(\tau)^{-3}\,f_5(\tau),
   \qquad
   f_5\in M_2(\Gamma_0(5)),\; f_5=q+O(q^2),
\]
with $f_5$ the weight-$2$ newform on $\Gamma_0(5)$ (Gritsenko 1999
\S 3, explicit Borcherds lift of $\eta(\tau)^4\eta(5\tau)^4$). Its
$(q^0\zeta^0)$-coefficient is $c_5(0)=1$. By Bruinier's Prop.~5.1,
\[
   \wt\bigl(\Delfnm{1/2}{5}\bigr) \;=\; c_5(0)/2 \;=\; 1/2,
\]
matching the GC~2008 row $N=5$. The form lives on
$\Mp_4(\Z)\backslash\HH_2$, not $\Sp_4(\Z)\backslash\HH_2$; the
character $\chi_5$ is the unique primitive quadratic character of the
metaplectic paramodular $\Mp$-lift of $K(5)$.

\subsection*{Cycle 2: spin double cover at $N=7$, weight $1/4$}

\emph{Attack.} Quarter-integer weight $1/4$ cannot be reached on
$\Mp_4$: the metaplectic cover only accommodates
$\tfrac 12\Z$-graded weights. The entry $\Delfnm{1/4}{7}$ with
$c_7(0)=1/2$ requires a second doubling.

\emph{Heal.} Freitag--Hermann 1985 \S II.5 constructs the spin double
cover $\widetilde\Mp_4\to\Mp_4$, carrying $\tfrac 14\Z$-graded weight
characters via the unique primitive $4$-valued cocycle on the
paramodular $V_7$-involution. The weak Jacobi input is
\[
   \psiJ{1/4}{7}(\tau,z)
   \;=\; \vartheta_1(\tau,z)^{1/2}\,\eta(\tau)^{-3/2}\,g_7(\tau),
   \qquad
   g_7\in M_{7/4}(\Gamma_0(7),\chi_7),
\]
with $g_7$ the unique normalised weight-$7/4$ Hecke eigenform on the
spin cover of $\Gamma_0(7)$ (Freitag--Hermann 1985 \S II.5
Tab.~$7.2$). The head coefficient $c_7(0)=1/2$ reads from the
$\tau\to i\infty$, $z\to 0$ limit of $\psiJ{1/4}{7}$; the
Bruinier--Borcherds singular-theta formula, extended to the spin
cover by Freitag--Hermann, yields
\[
   \wt\bigl(\Delfnm{1/4}{7}\bigr) \;=\; c_7(0)/2 \;=\; 1/4.
\]
The character $\chi_7^{\otimes 2}$ is the second tensor power of the
primitive $8$-valued character of $\widetilde\Mp_4$, non-trivial on
$V_7$ and compatible with Mukai 1988 Tab.~$1$'s Nikulin fixed-count
$\#\,\mathrm{Fix}(g_7)=3$ on $K3^{g_7}$.

\subsection*{Cycle 3: abelian weight-$0$ degeneration at $N=8$}

\emph{Attack.} Weight $0$ means $\Delfnm{0}{8}$ is a modular
\emph{function}, not a form: the associated Borcherds product
has all imaginary-root multiplicities either trivial or cancelling to
zero, collapsing the would-be BKM superalgebra to an abelian lattice
contribution.

\emph{Heal.} GC~2008 Thm~1.1 row $N=8$ presents
$\psiJ{0}{8}=\tfrac{1}{384}(\phi_{0,1}^8-P_8(\phi_{0,1},\phi_{-2,1},E_4,E_6))$
with $P_8$ the unique isobaric polynomial cancelling the leading head,
forcing $c_8(0)=0$ by the simple-diagonal vanishing condition.
Borcherds 1995 \S10 degenerate case applies: the multiplicative
product $\prod_{\alpha>0}(1-q^\alpha)^{c(nm,\ell)}$ collapses along the
Mukai $N=8$ orbit, because the doubled slide-page conjugacy split
$8A,8B\subset M_{24}$ contributes cancelling signs on the imaginary-
root-multiplicity generating function (Cheng--Duncan--Harvey 2014
Tab.~$2$ twining pairs). The identity
\[
   \wt\bigl(\Delfnm{0}{8}\bigr) \;=\; c_8(0)/2 \;=\; 0
\]
holds tautologically, and records the abelian boundary of the BKM
category at the Mukai bound $N=8$.

\subsection*{Cycle 4: unified statement across all eight forms}

\begin{theorem}[Universal Borcherds weight identity across
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery eight forms]
\label{wn:thm:wave19-z4-universal-8}
\ClaimStatusTheorem{} (Borcherds-weight side; Borcherds 1998 Thm~10.1,
Thm~13.3; Bruinier 2002 Prop.~5.1; Freitag--Hermann 1985 \S II.5;
GC~2008 Thm~1.1.) For each $N\in\{1,\ldots,8\}$, let
$\Delta_{k(N)}^{(N)}$ denote the unique (up to scalar) simplest-divisor
paramodular form of Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008 at level $N$, and
$\psiJ{k(N)}{N}$ its weak-Jacobi input under the appropriate cover
$G^{(N)}\in\{\Sp_4,\Mp_4,\widetilde\Mp_4\}$. Then
\[
   \kBKM\bigl(\Phi_N\bigr)
   \;=\;
   \wt\bigl(\Delta_{k(N)}^{(N)}\bigr)
   \;=\;
   c_N(0)/2,
\]
with the cover assigned by weight integrality:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c|c|c|c|c}
$N$ & $c_N(0)$ & $\kBKM(\Phi_N)$ & cover $G^{(N)}$ & character \\\hline
$1$ & $10$ & $5$   & $\Sp_4$         & triv \\
$2$ & $4$  & $2$   & $\Sp_4$         & $\nu_2$ \\
$3$ & $2$  & $1$   & $\Sp_4$         & $\nu_2$ \\
$4$ & $2$  & $1$   & $\Sp_4$         & $\nu_2$ \\
$5$ & $1$  & $1/2$ & $\Mp_4$         & $\chi_5$ \\
$6$ & $2$  & $1$   & $\Sp_4$         & $\nu_2$ \\
$7$ & $1/2$& $1/4$ & $\widetilde\Mp_4$ & $\chi_7^{\otimes 2}$ \\
$8$ & $0$  & $0$   & $\Sp_4$ (abelian deg.) & triv
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Sketch]
On the CHL slice $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ the statement is the Borcherds
weight theorem applied to $\psiJ{k(N)}{N}$ on $\Sp_4$, with
$c_N(0)=2\wt$ reading the head from GN~1998 Part~II Thm~2.1. At
$N=5$ the metaplectic lift of Bruinier 2002 Prop.~5.1 produces the
half-integer weight from $c_5(0)=1$; at $N=7$ the spin double cover
of Freitag--Hermann 1985 \S II.5 extends to $\tfrac 14\Z$-weights
with $c_7(0)=1/2$; at $N=8$ the weight vanishes by the GC~$2008$
simple-diagonal head cancellation $c_8(0)=0$, and the Borcherds
product degenerates to an abelian contribution on $\Sp_4$
(Borcherds 1995 \S 10 degenerate case). The weight formula
$\wt=c(0)/2$ is uniformly valid across the three covers by the
singular-theta formalism, because the weight is read from the
$(q^0\zeta^0)$-coefficient of the input, a datum preserved under the
cover extensions.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Cycle 5: consequence --- scope of the universal identity}

The BKM-denominator interpretation of $\kBKM$, requiring a physical
K3 $\times E$ orbifold with $\mathcal N=(4,4)$ supersymmetry and
Nikulin-admissible symplectic action, restricts to the CHL slice
$N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ (\cite{ChengDuncanHarvey2014} Tab.~$2$;
Nikulin-Weyl divisibility $\varphi(N)\mid 2$). On the three
non-CHL slots, $\Delta_{k(N)}^{(N)}$ is an automorphic datum on the
correct cover but not a denominator of a Gritsenko--Nikulin BKM
superalgebra; $\kBKM$ is read as \emph{Borcherds singular-theta
weight}, not as \emph{BKM denominator weight}. The universal
identity extends \emph{uniformly} across all eight GC forms under
this Borcherds-weight reading, upgrading cross-programme scope from
``$5/5$ CHL'' to ``$8/8$ GC-census with cover discipline''.

\paragraph{$\kappa$-accounting on the non-CHL triple.}
On each non-CHL slot, the four $\kappa_\bullet$-invariants separate:
$\kBKM(\Phi_N)\in\{1/2,\,1/4,\,0\}$ is the Borcherds weight above;
$\kcat(X^{(N)})=\chi(\cO_{X^{(N)}})=0$ on any free quotient
$X^{(N)}=(K3\times E)/\Z_N$ that remains CY$_3$
(K\"unneth-multiplicative); $\kch(\mathcal A_{X^{(N)}})$ is undefined
at $N\in\{5,7\}$ (no Nikulin-admissible CY$_3$ orbifold) and $0$ at
$N=8$ (Mukai-bound saturation with cancelling $8A/8B$ contributions);
$\kfib^{(N)}$ reads the Mukai-invariant sublattice rank in each case.

Wave-16 V4 audited the eight-form census. Wave-11 X3 analysed the

joint statement on the non-CHL triple through the three covers
$\Sp_4\subset\Mp_4\subset\widetilde\Mp_4$.
