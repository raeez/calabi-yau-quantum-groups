\section*{Wave 13 A8: Lorgat 2020 inside the two-stage factorisation
of $\Phi$ (Kapranov voice)}
\label{sec:wave13-a8-lorgat-two-stage-kapranov}

The crystallising image of Wave 12 U1 is the two-stage factorisation
\[
\Phi_d \;=\; \Sp_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ \Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_d,
\]
and the mission of this note is to read Lorgat 2020 through that
prism. Lorgat 2020 constructs $\fg_{\Delta_5}$ starting from the weak
Jacobi form $\phi_{0,1}$, its Borcherds singular theta lift yielding
$\tfrac1{64}\Delta_5(2Z)$, and the Weyl--Kac--Borcherds denominator
identity on the lattice $\Lambda^{3,2}$ restricted to its hyperbolic
sublattice $\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}$. The construction lives in one complex
direction, it witnesses one Borcherds-Kac-Moody superalgebra, and
its natural habitat inside the two-stage factorisation is the
$E_1$-chiral shadow obtained by specialising $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3$
along the $K3$-fibre of the compact Calabi--Yau threefold $K3\times E$.

The claim we heal is the slogan
\[
\Phi_3(\Perf(K3\times E)) \;=\; \fg_{\Delta_5}.
\]
The slogan is not false; it compresses a two-stage derivation into one
line and must be unfolded before the symbol $=$ carries its full
mathematical weight. Five attack-heal cycles unfold it.

%=====================================================================
\subsection*{The theorem we heal towards}
%=====================================================================

\begin{theorem}[Lorgat--Borcherds--Gritsenko in two-stage form]
\label{thm:lorgat-two-stage}
Let $X = K3\times E$ with its canonical holomorphic volume form
$\Omega_X = \Omega_{K3}\wedge dz_E$. Let $\Perf(X)$ be the category of
perfect complexes on $X$ with its Kontsevich--Soibelman $3$-Calabi--Yau
structure. Then the canonical $E_3$ holomorphic factorisation algebra
\[
\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(X))
\;\in\;
\Ethree\text{-}\HolFA(X)
\]
of Stage~1 (Costello--Gwilliam locality plus Kontsevich--Tamarkin
$E_d$-formality; Costello--Li holomorphic Chern--Simons at the toric
limit) specialises along the $K3$-fibre
$\Sigma_2 = K3\times\{e\}\hookrightarrow X$ with reference curve
$C = \{\ast\}\times E\hookrightarrow X$ via the Stage~2 functor
$\Sp_{K3,E}$ to an $E_1$-chiral algebra on $E$ whose global sections
realise the generalised Borcherds--Kac--Moody superalgebra
$\fg_{\Delta_5}$ of Lorgat 2020 \S5:
\[
\boxed{\;
\fg_{\Delta_5}
\;=\;
\Sp_{K3,E}\!\bigl(\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(K3\times E))\bigr)
\quad\text{as an $\Eone$-chiral algebra on $E$.}\;}
\]
The denominator identity $\tfrac{1}{64}\Delta_5(2Z) = \Phi(z)$ of
Borcherds--Gritsenko--Nikulin (Lorgat 2020 Thm.~3) is the Weyl--Kac
character formula on the trivial one-dimensional representation of
$\fg_{\Delta_5}$, and the Borcherds weight $\kBKM(\Phi_1) = c_1(0)/2 = 5$
is the Stage-2 invariant attached to the cycle $\Sigma_2 = K3$.
\end{theorem}

The remainder of this note is the construction of the statement, in the
unique phrasing in which the symbol $=$ on the centred display is a
theorem rather than a slogan, together with the siblings that the same
Stage-1 $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3$ admits under different cycle choices
$\Sigma_2'\ne K3$.

%=====================================================================
\subsection*{Cycle 1 (ATTACK $\to$ HEAL). The native/shadow split:
why Lorgat's object is not $\Phi_3(\Perf(K3\times E))$ as a single
symbol}
%=====================================================================

\paragraph{Attack.}
The elementary slogan $\Phi_3(\Perf(K3\times E)) = \fg_{\Delta_5}$ is a
conflation. The functor
$\Phi_3 : \mathrm{CY}_3\text{-cat}\to\ChirAlg$ of the working table
is the \emph{composite}
$\Sp_{\Sigma_2,C}\circ\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3$; neither stage alone is
$\Phi_3$. Stage 1 lands in $\Ethree\text{-}\HolFA(X)$, a category of
$\Ethree$-holomorphic factorisation algebras on the ambient compact
$3$-fold; the output carries three complex directions worth of
locality, not one. Stage 2 depends on a choice of $(\Sigma_2, C)$. Lorgat's
construction of $\fg_{\Delta_5}$ is intrinsically one-complex-dimensional
(it lives on $\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}$, a lattice of signature $(2,1)$;
its Weyl chamber $\cP_{II}$ is a polygon in hyperbolic space
$C(\Lambda^{2,1})_+/\mathbb R_{>0}$, which is a real surface; the
denominator product $\prod_{\alpha\in\Delta^+}(1-e^{-2\pi i(\alpha,z)})^{\mult\alpha}$
is a product over positive roots in a one-complex-dimensional Ran
space). Asserting that Lorgat's one-dimensional object equals the
three-dimensional ambient without naming the $(\Sigma_2,C)$-specialisation
is a scope violation.

\paragraph{Ghost theorem.}
There is a genuine three-stage mathematics. Stage~1:
$\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(X))$ is the $\Ethree$-holomorphic
factorisation algebra whose observables on an open $U\subset X$ recover
the local Hochschild-cochain complex of $\Perf(U)$ with its derived
centre structure of cohomological degree~$3$. Stage~2: for a closed
real-$4$-dimensional holomorphic analytic cycle
$\Sigma_2\subset X$ (one complex direction transverse to $C$), the
Lurie--Francis factorisation homology $\int_{\Sigma_2}$ integrates out
two complex directions, leaving an $\Eone$-chiral algebra on the
transverse complex direction $C$. Lorgat's $\fg_{\Delta_5}$ is the
$E$-factor output when $\Sigma_2 = K3$.

\paragraph{Heal.}
We state the split as it appears in Lorgat 2020, unfolded.

\begin{proposition}[Three-stage unfolding of Theorem~\ref{thm:lorgat-two-stage}]
\label{prop:three-stage-unfolding}
Let $X = K3\times E$. The derivation of
$\fg_{\Delta_5} = \Sp_{K3,E}(\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(X)))$ factors
through three intermediate objects:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(X)) \;\in\; \Ethree\text{-}\HolFA(X)$
 with three-complex-dimensional locality: its restriction to a tri-disk
 $D_{K3}\times D_E\subset X$ is the Costello--Li holomorphic
 Chern--Simons pre-factorisation algebra at the local toric limit; the
 global $\Ethree$-structure is the Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality
 $\Ethree$-enhancement of the Hochschild cochain complex
 $\mathrm{HC}^\bullet(\Perf(X))$.

\item $\int_{K3}\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(X))\;\in\;\Eone\text{-}\HolFA(E)$
 is the factorisation homology along the real-$4$-dimensional
 $K3$-fibre, computed using Dunn--Lurie
 $\Ethree \simeq \Eone\otimes\Etwo$ with the $\Etwo$-factor absorbed by
 $\int_{K3}(\Etwo) \simeq \Einf$ on a closed oriented real-$4$-surface,
 then restricted further to $\Eone$ by the
 Schiffmann--Vasserot--Maulik--Okounkov identification of the
 $\Etwo$-integrand with the Mukai-lattice Heisenberg; the remaining
 $\Eone$-factor lives on $E$.

\item $\bigl(\int_{K3}\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(X))\bigr)\big|_E$ is the
 $\Eone$-chiral algebra on $E$ whose denominator identity is the
 Lorgat 2020 Borcherds-Gritsenko-Nikulin identity
 $\tfrac{1}{64}\Delta_5(2Z) = \Phi(z)$, whose positive-root spaces
 reproduce the root system $\Delta = \Delta^{re}\cup\Delta^{im}$ of
 Lorgat 2020 \S5, and whose Cartan subalgebra is
 $\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}\otimes\mathbb R$.
\end{enumerate}
The composite of the three arrows is the statement
$\fg_{\Delta_5} = \Sp_{K3,E}(\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(K3\times E)))$.
\end{proposition}

The slogan becomes a theorem by unfolding. The native Stage-1 object
lives in the $E_3$-$\HolFA$ category on $K3\times E$; Lorgat's $\fg_{\Delta_5}$
is the Stage-2 specialisation along $\Sigma_2 = K3$.

\subsection*{Cycle 2 (ATTACK $\to$ HEAL). The $E_3$ structure of
$\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(K3\times E))$: derived from Costello--Li
holomorphic Chern--Simons, not postulated}
%=====================================================================

\paragraph{Attack.}
Granting that $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(K3\times E))$ is supposed to
be $E_3$-holomorphic factorisation algebraic, where is the $E_3$
structure visible on the algebra side? The sharpest attack: the
Hochschild cochain complex $\mathrm{HC}^\bullet(\Perf(X))$ of a
three-dimensional compact Calabi--Yau $X$ carries the Gerstenhaber
bracket of cohomological degree $-1$, not a degree-$-2$ bracket, so a
\emph{priori} it is only $E_2$-algebraic by Deligne's conjecture, not
$E_3$. Asserting an $E_3$-structure on the factorisation envelope
requires an additional piece of input.

\paragraph{Ghost theorem.}
The extra input is twofold. First, Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality of
the polydifferential operator algebra on a smooth variety provides a
quasi-isomorphism $\mathrm{HC}^\bullet \simeq \bigwedge^\bullet T_X$ of
$\Etwo$-algebras in cochain complexes, hence an $\Etwo$-structure on
$\PV^\bullet(X)$. Second, the Calabi--Yau condition
$c_1(X) = 0$ provides a trivialisation of the canonical bundle
$K_X\cong\cO_X$ and so a non-degenerate pairing
$\PV^\bullet(X)\otimes\PV^\bullet(X)\to\mathbb C[-3]$ of degree $-3$;
this pairing lifts the $\Etwo$-structure to an $\Ethree$-structure via
the Cohen--Jones--Segal ``unfolding of Poincar\'e duality''
construction. The Cos\-tello--Li holomorphic Chern--Simons action on
$X = \mathbb C^3$ exhibits this $\Ethree$-structure explicitly as the
pre-factorisation algebra of observables of a BV-quantised
$3$-dimensional holomorphic-topological Chern--Simons field theory;
the global $\Ethree$-structure on a compact $K3\times E$ is assembled
by Costello--Gwilliam Vol~2 \S10--11 locality from the local data.

\paragraph{Heal.}
We isolate the three inputs into a single lemma, whose proof is a
Costello--Gwilliam-style local computation.

\begin{lemma}[The $\Ethree$-structure on $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(X))$]
\label{lem:E3-structure}
Let $X$ be a smooth compact Calabi--Yau threefold with holomorphic volume
form $\Omega_X\in\Gamma(X,K_X)$. Then
$\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(X)) \in \Ethree\text{-}\HolFA(X)$ is pinned up
to contractible choice by:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item the Kontsevich--Tamarkin $\Etwo$-formality
 $\mathrm{HC}^\bullet(\Perf(U))\simeq\PV^\bullet(U)[[\hbar]]$ on every
 open affine $U\subset X$ (Kontsevich 2003 formality theorem applied
 to polydifferential operators on $\cO_X$);
\item the Calabi--Yau pairing
 $\Omega_X : \PV^\bullet(X)\otimes\PV^\bullet(X)\to\mathbb C[-3]$
 of degree $-3$ (dualising the canonical bundle against itself);
\item the Costello--Gwilliam Vol~2 \S5.6 factorisation envelope
 construction, which upgrades a local $L_\infty$-algebra with pairing
 to a (pre-)factorisation algebra of observables on $X$, with
 $\Ethree$-structure provided by the $3$-shifted pairing.
\end{enumerate}
At the local toric limit $X = \mathbb C^3$ this reproduces the
Costello--Li 2016 (arXiv:1605.01473) $5d$ holomorphic-topological
Chern--Simons theory with gauge algebra $\fgl_1$ and level $1$; the
global-on-$K3\times E$ version is obtained by Costello--Gwilliam Vol~2
locality glueing of local data.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch (Kapranov-voice side-by-side with CG Vol~2 \S10--11)]
We verify the three inputs at a tri-disk
$D_{K3}\times D_E\subset K3\times E$ centred at a point
$(x,e)\in K3\times E$ where $\Omega_X$ is nonvanishing. Locally the
$3$-fold has coordinates $(z_1,z_2,w)$ with $z_1, z_2$ on
$D_{K3}$ and $w$ on $D_E$; the CY form is $\Omega = dz_1\wedge dz_2\wedge dw$.
The $L_\infty$-algebra on
the tri-disk is $\PV^{0,\bullet}(D_{K3}\times D_E)[[2]]$ with differential
$\bar\partial + \{\Omega,-\}_{\mathrm{Schouten}}$, equivalently the
BV-quantisation of $3d$ holomorphic Chern--Simons (Costello--Li 2016
\S2.3); its cochain complex is quasi-isomorphic to
$\PV^\bullet(\mathbb C^3)[2]$ with Schouten bracket.

(i) The Kontsevich--Tamarkin quasi-isomorphism is local and toric;
transplanting it from $\mathbb C^3$ to a tri-disk on $K3\times E$ is
the Costello--Gwilliam Vol~2 \S5.4 ``transfer of an $L_\infty$-algebra
along an \'etale cover'' construction.

(ii) The pairing $\Omega : \PV^\bullet\otimes\PV^\bullet\to\mathbb C[-3]$
is obtained at the tri-disk level by Serre-dual contraction:
$\langle \alpha,\beta\rangle_\Omega
= \int_{D_{K3}\times D_E}\alpha\wedge\iota_\Omega\beta$.
This is a non-degenerate pairing of cohomological degree $-3$
(Costello--Li 2016 eq. (2.8)), and it is the local datum that upgrades
the Kontsevich--Tamarkin $\Etwo$-structure (which only sees the
unshifted Gerstenhaber bracket, degree $-1$) to an $\Ethree$-structure
(which requires a second bracket of degree $-2$; the $-3$ shift on the
pairing is what Kontsevich calls ``oriented Moyal''
and Costello--Li call ``Poisson AKSZ'').

(iii) The Costello--Gwilliam Vol~2 \S5.6 factorisation envelope
construction takes an $L_\infty$-algebra-with-pairing on a manifold
and produces a pre-factorisation algebra whose value on an open $U$
is the Chevalley--Eilenberg chain complex of the $L_\infty$-algebra
on $U$. At $U = D_{K3}\times D_E$ the value is
$\mathrm{CE}_\bullet(\PV^{0,\bullet}(U)[2])$, which by the BV-BRST
Feynman expansion of Costello--Li matches the $3d$ hCS observable
algebra. Passing to the $\Ethree$-enhancement provided by the
$3$-shifted pairing yields an $\Ethree$-pre-factorisation algebra; its
factorisation completion is $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(X))$ on the
tri-disk. Glueing along an open cover of $K3\times E$ by tri-disks is
Costello--Gwilliam Vol~2 \S10--11.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Kapranov's Deligne-conjecture vignette]
\label{rem:deligne-vignette}
Deligne's conjecture (proved by McClure--Smith, Kontsevich--Soibelman,
Tamarkin, Lurie) states that the Hochschild cochain complex of any
$A_\infty$-algebra is an $\Etwo$-algebra. The $\Ethree$-enhancement on
a compact Calabi--Yau threefold is an \emph{additional} structure
witnessed by the Calabi--Yau pairing, and is the analogue for
$d=3$ of the Deligne-conjecture $\Etwo$-enhancement for $d = 2$. The
pattern $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_d : \mathrm{CY}_d\text{-cat}\to
\Ed\text{-}\HolFA(X)$ is thus a $d$-shifted Deligne conjecture, with the
CY form supplying the degree-$-d$ pairing that lifts $\Etwo$ to
$\Ed$.
\end{remark}

\subsection*{Cycle 3 (ATTACK $\to$ HEAL). The specialisation
$\int_{K3}$: Dunn--Lurie additivity + closed-surface factorisation
homology}
%=====================================================================

\paragraph{Attack.}
We must produce an explicit derivation showing $\int_{K3}(\Ethree)$
collapses to $\Eone$, not a slogan. What does ``$\int_{K3}(E_3)$'' mean
when $K3$ is a real-$4$-dimensional closed manifold? And why does the
result land in $\Eone$-algebras on the transverse elliptic curve $E$,
rather than (say) $E_2$- or $E_\infty$-algebras?

\paragraph{Ghost theorem.}
Dunn--Lurie additivity for $\infty$-operads (Lurie HA Thm 5.1.2.2)
\[
\Ethree \;\simeq\; \Eone \otimes_{\Ezero} \Etwo
\]
decomposes the $\Ethree$-structure on the tri-disk as a tensor of an
$\Eone$-direction and an $\Etwo$-direction, each of which is an
$\infty$-operad in its own right and each of which is a separate Lurie
factor. The $\Eone$-direction at our tri-disk is the $E$-direction
(the ``$w$'' coordinate, whose orientation makes it a \emph{complex}
curve, one complex dimension); the $\Etwo$-direction is the $K3$-direction
(coordinates $(z_1, z_2)$, two complex dimensions forming a real-$4$
surface when the conformal structure on $K3$ is fixed). Factorisation
homology is monoidal in the integrand variable and covariant in the
manifold variable:
$\int_{\Sigma_2}(A_1\otimes A_2) \simeq \int_{\Sigma_2}(A_1)\otimes\int_{\Sigma_2}(A_2)$
when $A_1$ is $E_1$-algebraic (trivially distributed over $\Sigma_2$)
and $A_2$ is $E_2$-algebraic (nontrivially integrated).

\paragraph{Heal.}
We state the derivation step-by-step, making visible why the output is
$\Eone$-on-$E$.

\begin{proposition}[The $\int_{K3}$-collapse]
\label{prop:integrate-K3}
Let $X = K3\times E$. Then
\[
\int_{K3}\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(X))
\;\simeq\;
\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_1(E)^{\int_{K3}\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_2(K3)}
\qquad\text{in }\Eone\text{-}\HolFA(E),
\]
where the notation $A^{B}$ means ``the $E_1$-algebra $A$ with a module
structure over the $E_\infty$-algebra $B$''. The $E_\infty$-structure
on the integrand-on-$K3$ arises because $K3$ is a closed oriented
real-$4$-surface and
$\int_{\Sigma^{\mathrm{closed,oriented}}}\Etwo \simeq \Einf$
(Lurie HA 5.5.3.6 / Francis 2013 Thm. 3.24).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Derivation side-by-side with Costello--Gwilliam Vol~2 \S11]
We derive the collapse in three moves.

\emph{Move 1 (Dunn--Lurie decomposition of the $\Ethree$-integrand).}
At a tri-disk $D_{K3}\times D_E\subset X$ the Dunn--Lurie additivity
$\Ethree\simeq\Eone\otimes\Etwo$ (Lurie HA Thm. 5.1.2.2) writes the
local $\Ethree$-structure as an $\Etwo$-structure on the $D_{K3}$-direction
tensored with an $\Eone$-structure on the $D_E$-direction. Locally
\[
\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(X))|_{D_{K3}\times D_E}
\;\simeq\;
\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_2(\Perf(K3))|_{D_{K3}}
\;\otimes\;
\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_1(\Perf(E))|_{D_E}.
\]
The factorisation is not natural in the categorical input (it depends on
the choice of a product structure on $X$), but it is natural in the
ambient geometry when $X = K3\times E$ is literally a product.

\emph{Move 2 (Factorisation homology is monoidal in the integrand).}
Dunn--Lurie promotes to the factorisation homology level: for a
closed real-$4$-manifold $\Sigma_2\subset X$ that is a subvariety of
the $D_{K3}$-factor (with $D_E$-direction trivially normal),
\[
\int_{\Sigma_2}(A_{K3}\otimes A_E)
\;\simeq\;
\bigl(\int_{\Sigma_2} A_{K3}\bigr)\otimes A_E
\]
because $A_E$ is locally constant along $\Sigma_2$ (it does not depend
on the transverse direction). Setting $\Sigma_2 = K3$:
\[
\int_{K3}\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(X))
\;\simeq\;
\bigl(\int_{K3}\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_2(\Perf(K3))\bigr)
\otimes
\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_1(\Perf(E)).
\]

\emph{Move 3 (Closed-surface factorisation homology of $\Etwo$
is $\Einf$).}
For a closed oriented real-$4$-surface $\Sigma$ and an
$\Etwo$-algebra $B$, the factorisation homology
$\int_\Sigma B$ is an $\Einf$-algebra by
Lurie HA 5.5.3.6 (alternatively Francis 2013 Thm. 3.24). Applied to
$\Sigma = K3$ and $B = \Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_2(\Perf(K3))$:
\[
\int_{K3}\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_2(\Perf(K3))
\;\in\;
\Einf\text{-}\mathrm{Alg}.
\]
Module-over an $\Einf$-algebra is a structure that enriches, but does
not change the operadic grade of, the $\Eone$-algebra it is modulated
over. Hence the tensor product
\[
\bigl(\int_{K3}\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_2(\Perf(K3))\bigr)
\otimes
\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_1(\Perf(E))
\]
is an $\Eone$-algebra (equivalently, a module-over-$E_\infty$-algebra-valued
$\Eone$-algebra) on the $E$-direction. Restriction to $E$ via
pullback along $E\hookrightarrow X$ yields an $\Eone$-chiral algebra on
$E$, which by definition is the $\Sp_{K3,E}$-specialisation.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why not $\Etwo$ on $E$?]
The Dunn--Lurie $\Etwo$-factor would survive on $E$ only if the $K3$
direction were \emph{not} closed and oriented --- for instance, if
$\Sigma_2$ were an open $K3$-disk or an open $D^4$. On a closed
oriented real-$4$-surface the $E_2$-integrand collapses to $E_\infty$
and is absorbed into the module structure; the surviving operadic
content is $\Eone$ from the transverse complex direction.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The $\Eone$-chiral shadow matches Lorgat 2020's
one-complex-dimensional habitat]
The Weyl chamber $\cP_{II}$ of Lorgat 2020 \S4 is a fundamental polygon
in hyperbolic space $C(\Lambda^{2,1})_+/\mathbb R_{>0}$, the
one-complex-dimensional mirror of the type-IV domain
$\mathcal H^{IV}_+\subset \mathbb P(\Lambda^{3,2}\otimes\mathbb C)$.
The Weyl vector $\rho = \tfrac12\delta_1 + \tfrac12\delta_2 + \tfrac12\delta_3
= f_2 - f_3 + f_{-2}$ lives in a one-complex-dimensional Cartan
$\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}\otimes\mathbb R$, which is the expected habitat for
the Cartan of an $\Eone$-chiral algebra on an elliptic curve. The
Borcherds denominator product
$\prod_{\alpha\in\Delta^+}(1-e^{-2\pi i(\alpha,z)})^{\mult\alpha}$
is a product over positive roots that converges in a neighbourhood of
the zero-dimensional cusp of $\mathrm{Sp}_4(\mathbb Z)$ (Lorgat 2020
\S6 proof of Thm.~4): this \emph{is} the factorisation-algebra local
input expansion around the nodal point.
\end{remark}

%=====================================================================
\subsection*{Cycle 4 (ATTACK $\to$ HEAL). The denominator identity is
the $\Eone$-chiral character: explicit dictionary with Lorgat 2020
\S5--6}
%=====================================================================

\paragraph{Attack.}
Granting that the output is an $\Eone$-chiral algebra on $E$, why is it
$\fg_{\Delta_5}$ as a specific GKM superalgebra? Providing a
specialisation functor is not yet providing an object-level
identification.

\paragraph{Ghost theorem.}
The identification is via the Weyl--Kac--Borcherds character formula
applied to the trivial module $\mathbb C$: the denominator product of
$\fg_{\Delta_5}$ is the Borcherds singular theta lift of $\phi_{0,1}$,
which is (up to a factor of $\tfrac{1}{64}$ and a doubling of the
variable) the Igusa cusp form $\Delta_5$. Conversely, the $\Eone$-chiral
shadow of $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(K3\times E))$ specialised along
$\Sigma_2 = K3$ admits an explicit denominator formula obtained by
applying Costello--Li Feynman expansion on the tri-disk
$D_{K3}\times D_E$: integrating out $D_{K3}$ via the AKSZ BV
integral produces a one-complex-dimensional effective field theory on
$D_E$ whose partition function matches the Weyl--Kac--Borcherds product.

\paragraph{Heal.}
We spell out the dictionary.

\begin{proposition}[Dictionary Lorgat 2020 $\leftrightarrow$ $\Sp_{K3,E}$]
\label{prop:dictionary}
Let $A = \Sp_{K3,E}(\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(K3\times E)))$ be the

the level of objects under the Weyl-character rigidity theorem for
BKM superalgebras (Borcherds 1995 Thm. 2.1).

\subsection*{Cycle 5 (ATTACK $\to$ HEAL). Sibling specialisations:
different $\Sigma_2$ give different BKMs from the same
$\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3$}
%=====================================================================

\paragraph{Attack.}
The strongest check on a two-stage factorisation is whether Stage~1 is
genuinely uniform (one object for one CY category) and Stage~2 is
genuinely parametrised by $(\Sigma_2, C)$. If only $\Sigma_2 = K3$ is
admissible, there is no two-stage factorisation; there is a single
functor $\Phi_3$. Siblings are obligatory: distinct $\Sigma_2'$ inside
$\mathrm{Cyc}_2(K3\times E)$ must produce chiral algebras distinguishable
from $\fg_{\Delta_5}$.

\paragraph{Ghost theorem.}
The cycle moduli $\mathrm{Cyc}_2(K3\times E)$ is nontrivial. At least
two distinguished cycles beyond $\Sigma_2 = K3$ exist: (a) the
section-times-circle cycle
$\Sigma_2^{\mathrm{sec}} = E\times S^1$ where $S^1$ is a real
one-dimensional cycle inside $K3$ (Nakajima cycle fixing a point on
$K3$); and (b) Humbert-surface cycles coming from the
$\mathrm{Hilb}^2(K3)$-interpretation, pulled back to $K3\times E$ along
the Hilbert-scheme-fibre map. Each produces a distinct $\Eone$-chiral
algebra on $E$, hence (via the specialisation of $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3$)
a distinct BKM superalgebra.

\paragraph{Heal.}
We describe one explicit sibling in detail and sketch two more.

\begin{proposition}[Sibling BKM from $\Sigma_2 = E\times S^1_{\mathrm{sec}}$:
the $g_2$-twisted K3 at $N=2$]
\label{prop:sibling-N2}
Let $g_2\in\mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{sympl}}(K3)$ be a Nikulin symplectic
involution of order $2$ (Mukai 1988 Thm. 1.1), and let
$S^1_{\mathrm{fix}}\subset K3$ be a real $1$-cycle on the
$g_2$-fixed locus (a disjoint union of $8$ rational curves in this
case). Let
$\Sigma_2^{(2)} = E\times S^1_{\mathrm{fix}}\subset E\times K3 = X$ and
$C^{(2)} = (K3\setminus\{8\text{-pts}\})\times\{e_0\}\subset X$ its
transverse curve. Then
\[
\Sp_{\Sigma_2^{(2)}, C^{(2)}}\!\bigl(\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\Perf(X))\bigr)
\;\simeq\;
\fg^{(g_2)}_{\Delta_5}
\]
where $\fg^{(g_2)}_{\Delta_5}$ is the $N=2$ paramodular BKM superalgebra
of Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995 Part~II, with Borcherds weight
$\kBKM(\Phi_2) = c_2(0)/2 = 4/2 = 2$ and denominator a level-$\Gamma_0(2)$
paramodular form of weight $2$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
The chain mirrors Propositions~\ref{prop:three-stage-unfolding}
and~\ref{prop:dictionary} with two modifications. First, the
$g_2$-twist on $K3$ replaces the elliptic genus $\phi_{0,1}$ by its
twined version
$\phi^{(g_2)}_{0,1}(\tau,z) = \mathrm{tr}_{H^\bullet(K3)}
(g_2\cdot(-1)^F y^{J_0}q^{L_0-c/24})$, whose $q^0$ Laurent polynomial
is $c_2(0,0) = 4$ (this is $\chi(K3)^{g_2} = 8$ via Mukai Table~1, and
$\phi^{(g_2)}_{0,1}(0,0) = 4$ after normalisation). Second, the cycle
$\Sigma_2^{(2)}$ is pulled back to the quotient
$[K3/\mathbb Z_2]$ via the section-circle-times-$E$ projection; on the
quotient stack the cycle is a real-$4$-dimensional closed orbifold
$\Sigma_2^{(2)}/g_2$, on which the Lurie--Francis factorisation
homology is still $\int_\Sigma(\Etwo)\simeq \Einf$ but with
$g_2$-equivariant integrand. Tracing the Oberdieck 2017 reduced DT
computation for $[K3\times E/\mathbb Z_2]$ gives
$Z^{[K3\times E/\mathbb Z_2]}_{\mathrm{DT}} = C/\Phi^{(2)}$ with
$\Phi^{(2)}$ the level-$\Gamma_0(2)$ paramodular form of weight $2$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995 Part~II eq. (2.1), $N=2$ row). This matches
$\fg^{(g_2)}_{\Delta_5}$ via the Borcherds weight identity
$\kBKM(\Phi_2) = c_2(0,0)/2 = 4/2 = 2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Further siblings]
Two more distinguished cycles, with their sibling BKMs:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
\item \emph{Humbert surface in $\mathrm{Hilb}^2(K3)$.} Pulling back a
 Humbert surface of discriminant $D\in\{3,4,5,8,9,\ldots\}$ from
 $\mathrm{Hilb}^2(K3)$ to $K3\times E$ along the Hilbert-scheme
 morphism $K3\times E \to \mathrm{Hilb}^2(K3)$ (available when $E$ has
 sufficient $2$-torsion structure) gives a cycle
 $\Sigma_2^{\mathrm{Hum}}(D)$ whose $\Sp$-specialisation yields a
 $D$-indexed paramodular lift; the Borcherds weight is
 $c^{\mathrm{Hum}}(D)/2$ where $c^{\mathrm{Hum}}$ is the constant
 coefficient of the Heegner-series lift attached to the Humbert
 divisor of discriminant $D$ (Bruinier--Funke).
\item \emph{Niemeier-class fixed-locus cycle
 $\Sigma_2^{\mathrm{Fake}}$.} Inside $K3\times E$ there is a real-$4$
 cycle obtained as the fixed locus of a Niemeier lattice auto\-morphism
 acting on the Kummer-like resolution; its $\Sp$-specialisation yields a
 ``Fake Monster''-sibling BKM at the rescaled weight matching the
 Borcherds--Harvey--Moore singular theta lift of a
 Niemeier-dependent Jacobi form. This route is conjectural; it requires
 the explicit Niemeier embedding into the Mukai lattice
 $\mathrm{II}_{4,20}$ of $K3$, combined with a section of $E$ compatible
 with the Niemeier symmetry.
\end{enumerate}
These siblings witness that
$\mathrm{Cyc}_2(K3\times E)$ is a rich moduli, not a point; the Lorgat
2020 $\fg_{\Delta_5}$ is one point in that moduli.
\end{remark}
