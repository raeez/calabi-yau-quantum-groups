\section*{McKay quiver of $\CC^{3}/\ZZ_{3}$ and the positive half of local $\PP^{2}$}

\subsection*{Attack--heal 1: the quiver}

Let $\ZZ_{3} = \langle \zeta \rangle$ act on $\CC^{3}$ by
$\zeta \cdot (x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}) = (\zeta x_{1}, \zeta x_{2}, \zeta x_{3})$,
$\zeta = e^{2\pi i/3}$. The action is diagonal with the same weight on
every coordinate, so the canonical class $\omega_{\CC^{3}} = dx_{1}\wedge
dx_{2}\wedge dx_{3}$ transforms by $\zeta^{3}=1$: the quotient is
Gorenstein Calabi--Yau of dimension three. Naively one writes down the
quiver with three vertices $i \in \ZZ_{3}$ (one per irreducible
character $\rho_{i}$) and three arrows per pair, and the resulting
path algebra sees no obstruction to being the right homological model.
\textbf{Flaw.} A bare path algebra on this quiver has Hochschild
dimension one; it cannot model a three-dimensional Calabi--Yau
singularity, whose NCCR is necessarily $3$-Calabi--Yau in the sense of
Ginzburg. \textbf{Heal} (King 1994; Ginzburg 2006, arXiv:math/0612139).
The McKay quiver $Q$ of $(\CC^{3},\ZZ_{3})$ has vertex set
$Q_{0} = \{0,1,2\}$ and three arrows
$a_{j}^{(i)}: i \to i+1 \pmod{3}$, $j \in \{1,2,3\}$, one per coordinate
$x_{j}$; the adjacency encodes
$\CC^{3}\otimes\rho_{i} = \rho_{i+1}^{\oplus 3}$. Ginzburg's construction
demands a cubic superpotential to lift $\CC Q$ to a $3$-CY dg-algebra.

\subsection*{Attack--heal 2: the superpotential}

A first guess is $W = \sum_{i} a_{1}^{(i+2)}a_{2}^{(i+1)}a_{3}^{(i)}$.
\textbf{Flaw.} This is not cyclically symmetric under the $S_{3}$
permutation of coordinate labels; the Jacobi relations
$\partial_{a} W = 0$ would pick out an ordered triple and break the
$\mathrm{SL}(3,\CC)$-covariance that the Calabi--Yau structure on
$\CC^{3}$ enjoys. \textbf{Heal} (Beasley--Plesser 2001, hep-th/0104100;
Feng--Hanany--He 2001, hep-th/0104259; Cachazo--Katz--Vafa 2001).
Totally antisymmetrise:
\[
  W \;=\; \sum_{i\in\ZZ_{3}} \;\sum_{(j,k,l)\in S_{3}}
    \mathrm{sgn}(jkl)\, a_{k}^{(i+2)}\, a_{j}^{(i+1)}\, a_{l}^{(i)}
  \;=\; \sum_{i\in\ZZ_{3}}
    \epsilon^{jkl}\, a_{k}^{(i+2)}\, a_{j}^{(i+1)}\, a_{l}^{(i)} .
\]
The Jacobian relations
$[a_{j}^{(i+1)}, a_{k}^{(i)}]_{\epsilon} := \epsilon^{jkl} a_{l}^{(i+1)}
a_{k}^{(i)} - \cdots = 0$ reproduce the commutation ideal defining
$\CC[x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}]$ as a module over each vertex algebra, i.e.\
$\Jac(Q,W) = \mathrm{End}_{\CC^{3}/\ZZ_{3}}(\bigoplus_{i}\rho_{i})$,
the skew group algebra $\CC[\CC^{3}]\rtimes\ZZ_{3}$
(Bocklandt 2008 Theorem~3.2, arXiv:math/0603558).

\subsection*{Attack--heal 3: the CoHA as positive half}

\textbf{Attack.} Build $\CoHA(Q,W)$ by summing critical cohomology of
the stack of representations of $(Q,W)$, graded by dimension vector
$\mathbf{d}\in\ZZ_{\ge 0}^{3}$:
\[
  \CoHA(Q,W) \;=\; \bigoplus_{\mathbf{d}}
    H^{\bullet,\mathrm{crit}}\!\bigl(\mathrm{Rep}(Q,\mathbf{d}),\,
    \varphi_{\mathrm{Tr}\,W}\bigr) .
\]
\textbf{Flaw.} The raw CoHA is doubled: Davison--Meinhardt 2015
(arXiv:1512.08898) construct not only the positive piece but also a
Cartan of BPS states graded by the orbifold Chern character. Writing
$\CoHA(Q,W) = Y_{\CC^{3}/\ZZ_{3}}$ without further decomposition
conflates the positive and negative halves of the BPS Lie algebra.
\textbf{Heal} (Kontsevich--Soibelman 2011 arXiv:1006.2706; Davison
2016 arXiv:1603.00416). The positive half is precisely the
critical-cohomology piece generated by effective classes
$\mathbf{d}\succeq 0$,
\[
  \Ypos_{\CC^{3}/\ZZ_{3}} \;=\; \CoHA(Q,W)^{\succeq 0}
    \;\subset\; \CoHA(Q,W) .
\]
Under the coproduct this is a sub-bialgebra. The integrality conjecture
(Kontsevich--Soibelman, proved by Davison--Meinhardt) identifies its
graded dimensions with BPS invariants
$\Omega(\mathbf{d})\in\ZZ_{\ge 0}[q^{\pm 1/2}]$ of the orbifold.

\subsection*{Attack--heal 4: BKR and the equivalence}

\textbf{Attack.} The singular quotient $\CC^{3}/\ZZ_{3}$ admits a
crepant resolution $Y \to \CC^{3}/\ZZ_{3}$ by blowing up the origin;
identify $Y = K_{\PP^{2}}$ (total space of the canonical bundle of
$\PP^{2}$) and declare $\Db(\coh\,Y) \simeq \Db(A_{Q})$.
\textbf{Flaw.} Existence of a crepant resolution does not produce an
equivalence of derived categories; the Fourier--Mukai kernel must be
constructed. For a generic abelian $G\subset\mathrm{SL}(3,\CC)$ no such
equivalence was available before 2001. \textbf{Heal}
(Bridgeland--King--Reid 2001, arXiv:math/9908027, Theorem~1.2). The
$G$-Hilbert scheme $\GHilb(\CC^{3})$ is a crepant resolution, and the
universal family $\mathcal{Z}\subset \GHilb(\CC^{3})\times \CC^{3}$
provides a Fourier--Mukai kernel
\[
  \Phi_{\mathrm{BKR}} \;:\; \Db(\GHilb(\CC^{3}))
    \xrightarrow{\;\simeq\;} \Db_{\ZZ_{3}}(\CC^{3})
    = \Db\bigl(\CC[x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}]\rtimes\ZZ_{3}\bigr) .
\]
The identification $\GHilb_{\ZZ_{3}}(\CC^{3}) = K_{\PP^{2}}$ is
Ito--Nakajima 1999 (arXiv:math/9803120); together with BKR this gives
$\Db(\coh K_{\PP^{2}}) \simeq \Db(A_{Q})$ where $A_{Q} = \Jac(Q,W)$.

\subsection*{Attack--heal 5: compatibility with local $\PP^{2}$}

\textbf{Attack.} From Wave-17 C2 one reads off
$\Ypos(K_{\PP^{2}})$ from ADHM / instanton geometry on $\PP^{2}$; the
present quiver construction reads off $\Ypos_{\CC^{3}/\ZZ_{3}}$ from
McKay; assert these agree on the nose. \textbf{Flaw.} BKR is an
equivalence of bounded derived categories of coherent sheaves; it does
not a priori respect the critical-cohomology grading, the vanishing
cycle functor, or the Davison--Meinhardt positive-cone decomposition
that enters the CoHA definition. \textbf{Heal} (Szendroi 2008
arXiv:0705.3419 for the conifold; Nagao--Nakajima 2011 arXiv:0809.2992
and Davison 2013 arXiv:1311.7172 for the $\ZZ_{n}$ orbifolds).
BKR lifts to an equivalence of $3$-Calabi--Yau dg-categories
preserving the shifted symplectic structure (Brav--Bussi--Dupont--Joyce--Meinhardt
2015 arXiv:1504.00690); the vanishing cycle sheaves
$\varphi_{\mathrm{Tr}\,W}$ and $\varphi_{\mathrm{Chern--Simons}_{K_{\PP^{2}}}}$
are identified as perverse sheaves on the matched moduli stacks. Hence
\[
  \Ypos(K_{\PP^{2}}) \;=\; \Ypos_{\CC^{3}/\ZZ_{3}} ,
\]
with graded character
$\mathrm{ch}\,\Ypos = \prod_{\mathbf{d}\succ 0}
(1-q^{\mathbf{d}})^{-\Omega(\mathbf{d})}$,
matching the Nakajima--Yoshioka local $\PP^{2}$ partition function
(Nakajima--Yoshioka 2005, arXiv:math/0505553) chamber-by-chamber via
Craw--Ishii 2004 (arXiv:math/0211360) GIT variation.

\subsection*{Numerics}

Four invariants, subscripted: $\kcat(\CC^{3}/\ZZ_{3})$ is ill-defined
(non-compact, no $\chi(\mathcal{O}_{X})$); on the crepant resolution
$\kcat(K_{\PP^{2}})$ is likewise non-compact. $\kch(K_{\PP^{2}}) = 3/2$
(cache key fact, from McKay / shadow at $d=3$). $\kBKM$ is
inapplicable (no automorphic lift for the local surface).
$\kfib$ is absent in the non-fibred local geometry. The conifold is
not a local surface at $d=3$; $\kch = 1$ via direct McKay on the
$A_{1}$ quiver, a genuinely different construction, not a perturbation
of the present $\ZZ_{3}$ datum.
