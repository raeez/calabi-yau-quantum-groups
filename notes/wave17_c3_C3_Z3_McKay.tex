\section*{McKay quiver of $\CC^{3}/\ZZ_{3}$ and the positive half of local $\PP^{2}$}

\subsection*{The McKay quiver}

Let $\ZZ_{3} = \langle \zeta \rangle$ act on $\CC^{3}$ by
\[
  \zeta \cdot (x_{1},x_{2},x_{3})
  =(\zeta x_{1},\zeta x_{2},\zeta x_{3}),
  \qquad \zeta=e^{2\pi i/3}.
\]
The canonical form
$dx_{1}\wedge dx_{2}\wedge dx_{3}$ transforms by $\zeta^{3}=1$; hence
$\CC^3/\ZZ_3$ is Gorenstein Calabi--Yau of dimension three. The quiver
with vertices alone is too small: a bare path algebra has homological
dimension one. The three-dimensional Calabi--Yau model is the Jacobian
algebra of the McKay quiver with its cubic potential (King 1994;
Ginzburg 2006, arXiv:math/0612139).

The McKay quiver $Q$ of $(\CC^{3},\ZZ_{3})$ has vertex set
$Q_{0}=\{0,1,2\}$ and three arrows
\[
  a_{j}^{(i)}: i\longrightarrow i+1 \pmod 3,
  \qquad j\in\{1,2,3\}.
\]
The adjacency records
$\CC^{3}\otimes\rho_i\simeq \rho_{i+1}^{\oplus 3}$ for the irreducible
characters $\rho_i$ of $\ZZ_3$.

\subsection*{The superpotential}

A single ordered monomial breaks coordinate symmetry. The Calabi--Yau
potential is the $\mathrm{SL}(3,\CC)$-covariant antisymmetrisation
\[
  W
  =\sum_{i\in\ZZ_{3}}\sum_{j,k,l=1}^{3}
    \epsilon^{jkl}\,
    a_{k}^{(i+2)}a_{j}^{(i+1)}a_{l}^{(i)}
\]
The cyclic derivatives give the commutation relations among the three
coordinate arrows:
\[
  \partial_{a_r^{(i)}}W
  =
  \sum_{j,k=1}^{3}\epsilon^{rjk}a_k^{(i+2)}a_j^{(i+1)}
  =0 .
\]
Equivalently, after relabelling the source vertex,
\[
  a_p^{(i+1)}a_q^{(i)}=a_q^{(i+1)}a_p^{(i)}
  \qquad (p,q\in\{1,2,3\}).
\]
Consequently
\[
  \Jac(Q,W)
  \simeq \CC[x_1,x_2,x_3]\rtimes \ZZ_3 .
\]
Equivalently, this skew-group algebra is
$\mathrm{End}_{\CC[x_1,x_2,x_3]^{\ZZ_3}}(T)$ for the McKay tilting
module
\[
  T=\bigoplus_{i\in\ZZ_3}
    \left(\CC[x_1,x_2,x_3]\otimes\rho_i\right)^{\ZZ_3},
\]
hence it is the NCCR of $\CC[x_1,x_2,x_3]^{\ZZ_3}$
(Bocklandt 2008 Theorem~3.2, arXiv:math/0603558).

\subsection*{The CoHA positive half}

For a dimension vector $\mathbf d=(d_0,d_1,d_2)$ put
$G_{\mathbf d}=\prod_i\GL_{d_i}$. The critical CoHA of the Jacobian
quiver is the Hall algebra over effective dimension vectors:
\[
  \mathcal{H}_{Q,W}
  =
  \bigoplus_{\mathbf{d}\in\ZZ_{\ge 0}^{3}}
  H^{\bullet,\mathrm{crit}}_{G_{\mathbf d}}\!\left(
    \mathrm{Rep}(Q,\mathbf{d}),
    \varphi_{\mathrm{Tr}\,W}
  \right).
\]
The product is defined by the usual Hecke correspondence for short
exact sequences of quiver representations, with vanishing cycles pulled
through the trace of $W$.
This is the positive Hall half attached to the orbifold effective cone:
\[
  \Ypos_{\CC^{3}/\ZZ_{3}} := \mathcal{H}_{Q,W}.
\]
A full quantum group requires a negative half, a Cartan part, a
non-degenerate Hopf pairing, and a completed Drinfeld double. A
$\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$-type object requires those double or centre
data. The Kontsevich--Soibelman critical Hall product, together with
the standard localized coproduct/completion, gives the positive BPS
bialgebra (Kontsevich--Soibelman 2011, arXiv:1006.2706; Davison 2016,
arXiv:1603.00416). Davison--Meinhardt integrality identifies the BPS
cohomology whose graded dimensions are the orbifold invariants
$\Omega(\mathbf{d})\in\ZZ_{\ge 0}[q^{\pm 1/2}]$.

The ambient Calabi--Yau dimension is three. The $\Phi$-side object
therefore has $E_1$-chiral scope. Braided $E_2$ structure enters through
the Drinfeld centre of the $E_1$ representation category on constructed
loci.

\subsection*{The Bridgeland--King--Reid equivalence}

The quotient $\CC^3/\ZZ_3$ has a crepant resolution
$Y\to \CC^3/\ZZ_3$, and here $Y=K_{\PP^2}$ is the total space of the
canonical bundle of $\PP^2$. Bridgeland--King--Reid supplies the
categorical equivalence. For
$G=\ZZ_3\subset\mathrm{SL}(3,\CC)$, the $G$-Hilbert scheme
$\GHilb(\CC^3)$ is a crepant resolution and its universal family
$\mathcal{Z}\subset \GHilb(\CC^3)\times\CC^3$ gives the Fourier--Mukai
kernel
\[
  \Phi_{\mathrm{BKR}}:
  \Db(\coh\,\GHilb_{\ZZ_3}(\CC^3))
  \xrightarrow{\ \simeq\ }
  \Db_{\ZZ_3}(\coh\,\CC^3)
  =
  \Db\!\left((\CC[x_1,x_2,x_3]\rtimes\ZZ_3)\text{-}\mathrm{mod}\right)
\]
(Bridgeland--King--Reid 2001, arXiv:math/9908027, Theorem~1.2). The
identification $\GHilb_{\ZZ_3}(\CC^3)=K_{\PP^2}$ is Ito--Nakajima 1999
(arXiv:math/9803120), hence
\[
  \Db(\coh K_{\PP^2}) \simeq \Db(A_Q\text{-}\mathrm{mod}),
  \qquad A_Q=\Jac(Q,W).
\]

\subsection*{Compatibility with local $\PP^{2}$}

BKR identifies the derived categories. The CoHA comparison also needs
the induced identification of moduli stacks, effective cones,
orientation data, vanishing cycles, and Hall correspondences. For a
stability chamber $\theta$ on $K_{\PP^2}$ and the corresponding orbifold
chamber $\theta'$, the oriented crepant-resolution/NCCR comparison gives
the chamberwise positive-half equivalence
\[
  \Ypos(K_{\PP^2},\theta)
  \simeq_{E_1}
  \Ypos_{\CC^3/\ZZ_3,\theta'} .
\]
The chamber dependence is the Craw--Ishii variation of GIT stability,
and the DT wall-crossing statement is the $\ZZ_n$-orbifold comparison
of Nagao--Nakajima and Davison (Craw--Ishii 2004, arXiv:math/0211360;
Nagao--Nakajima 2011, arXiv:0809.2992; Davison 2013,
arXiv:1311.7172). At the character level the comparison is
\[
  \mathrm{ch}\,\Ypos(K_{\PP^2},\theta)
  =
  \prod_{\mathbf{d}\succ 0}(1-q^{\mathbf{d}})^{-\Omega(\mathbf{d})},
\]
matching the Nakajima--Yoshioka local $\PP^2$ partition function
(Nakajima--Yoshioka 2005, arXiv:math/0505553) after the
Chern-character and stability-chamber transformation.

\subsection*{Numerics}

The numerical data split by construction. For the non-compact
threefolds $X=\CC^3/\ZZ_3$ and $Y=K_{\PP^2}$, the compact Calabi--Yau
total-space invariant $\kcat=\chi(\mathcal O_X)$ is outside the
non-compact input. The local-surface compact-core convention records
\[
  \kcat^{\mathrm{core}}(Y)=\chi(\mathcal O_{\PP^2})=1,
  \qquad
  \kfib^{\mathrm{core}}(Y)=\operatorname{rk}H_2(\PP^2,\ZZ)=1 .
\]
These are invariants of the compact core $\PP^2$. The chiral invariant
of the local Calabi--Yau threefold is the Vol III odd-$d$ chain-level
value
\[
  \kch(K_{\PP^2})
  =
  \frac{1}{2}\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(\PP^2)
  =
  \frac{3}{2}.
\]
The same value is obtained from the three toric charts, from the
MacMahon exponent, and from the genus-one local-surface extraction.

$\kBKM(K_{\PP^2})$ is undefined: the local surface data stop before the
hyperbolic Mukai lattice, weak Jacobi input, and Borcherds denominator
product required for a BKM invariant. The compact $K3\times E$ spectrum
$\{0,3,5,24\}$ belongs to the K3-fibred Borcherds construction.
The resolved conifold belongs to a different McKay datum; its
$\kch$-value is
\[
  \kch(\mathrm{conifold})=1,
\]
from the conifold quiver. The $\ZZ_3$ local-$\PP^2$ quiver gives the
separate value $\kch(K_{\PP^2})=3/2$.
