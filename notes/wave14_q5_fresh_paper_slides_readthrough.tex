\section*{Automorphic Corrections for $K3 \times E$}

\subsection*{Normalised Borcherds weights}

Let $\Phi_N$ denote the CHL paramodular Borcherds product attached to
orders
\[
  N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}.
\]
With $c_N(0)$ the $q^0 r^0$ coefficient of the corresponding twined
weak Jacobi input, the Borcherds weight is
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_N)=\frac{c_N(0)}{2}.
\]
The CHL five-row table is
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
N & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 6\\
\hline
c_N(0) & 10 & 8 & 6 & 4 & 2\\
\kBKM(\Phi_N) & 5 & 4 & 3 & 2 & 1
\end{array}
\]
The Gritsenko--Cl\'ery diagonal-divisor catalogue is a separate eight-row
atlas with its own indices, cover groups, and multiplier systems. Its
row-by-row identity is also weight $=c(0)/2$; comparison with the CHL
five-row table requires an explicit row map.

At $N=6$, the umbral Niemeier anchor is the root system $6D_4$.  The
surface quotient by the order-six symplectic automorphism has Du Val
type
\[
  2A_5+2A_2+2A_1 .
\]
The Niemeier anchor and the quotient-singularity type are different
invariants of the same order-six class.

\subsection*{Subscripted $K3 \times E$ invariants}

For the compact threefold $X=K3\times E$,
\[
  \kcat(X)=0,\qquad
  \kch(\Phi_3(X))=0,\qquad
  \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}(X)=3,\qquad
  \kBKM(\Delta_{5})=5,\qquad
  \kfib(K3)=24 .
\]
The first equality is K\"unneth multiplicativity for
$\chi(\cO_{K3\times E})=\chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot 0=0$.
The second is the compact Hodge supertrace in odd Calabi--Yau dimension.
The value $3$ belongs to the Heisenberg--Mukai specialisation on the
elliptic curve, the value $5$ to the Borcherds weight of the
Gritsenko--Nikulin square root $\Delta_{5}$, and the value $24$ to the
Mukai-lattice rank of the K3 fibre.  These values occupy distinct
construction lanes, with no additive formula joining them.

\subsection*{K3 elliptic genus}

The standard weak Jacobi form has expansion
\[
  \phioo(\tau,z)=(r^{-1}+10+r)+O(q),
  \qquad r=e^{2\pi iz}.
\]
The K3 elliptic genus is
\[
  \operatorname{Ell}(K3;\tau,z)=2\,\phioo(\tau,z).
\]
Thus the $q^0r^0$ coefficient of $\operatorname{Ell}(K3)$ is $20$,
while $\operatorname{Ell}(K3;\tau,0)=24$.  The Borcherds input
coefficient used in the $\Delta_{5}$ weight formula is the coefficient
$c_1(0)=10$ of $\phioo$, and therefore
\[
  \kBKM(\Delta_{5})=\frac{10}{2}=5 .
\]
The doubled Jacobi input gives the Igusa scalar square
$\Phi_{\!10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_{5}^{\,2}$; the primitive BKM
denominator uses the single weak Jacobi form $\phioo$.

\subsection*{Reduced Donaldson--Thomas sign}

In the reduced $K3\times E$ Donaldson--Thomas variables with summand
$(-p)^n$, the $N=1$ partition function has the form
\[
  Z^{K3\times E}_{\mathrm{red}}(q,t,p)=-\,\frac{C}{\Delta_{5}(q,t,p)^2}.
\]
If the sign is absorbed into $C$ or into a change of the $p$-variable,
the displayed formula changes notation while preserving content.  A theorem
statement must therefore specify the sign convention for $C$, the
$p$-normalisation, and the square-root convention
$\Phi_{\!10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_{5}^{\,2}$.

\subsection*{Lattice-polarised GBKM family}

Let $L$ be a Nikulin-admissible lattice polarisation of a K3 surface and
let $(g_N,h_M)$ be a compatible pair of finite-order symplectic
automorphisms.  The automorphic input is a twisted-twined Jacobi form
\[
  F_L^{(g_N,h_M)}(\tau,z)\in J^{\mathrm{weak}}_{0,1}(\Gamma_{N,M}),
\]
and the associated denominator problem asks for a generalized
Borcherds--Kac--Moody superalgebra
\[
  \gBKM_{\Delta_{5}}\subset \gBKM_L
\]
whose imaginary-root multiplicities are the Fourier coefficients of
$F_L^{(g_N,h_M)}$.  The assignment
$L\mapsto\gBKM_L$ becomes a functor after construction of the morphism
rule for lattice-polarised K3 isometries and proof that Borcherds
products, Weyl chambers, and parity signs are transported compatibly.

\subsection*{Jacobi-form basis}

The weak Jacobi forms of index $1$ are generated by
\[
  \phioo \in J^{\mathrm{weak}}_{0,1},
  \qquad
  \phimt \in J^{\mathrm{weak}}_{-2,1},
  \qquad
  \phimt=\frac{\nu_{11}^2}{\eta^6}
\]
up to the sign convention for the odd theta function.  In the usual
Mathieu/CHL notation one sets
\[
  A=\frac14\phioo,\qquad B=\phimt .
\]
For a level-$N$ group, a weight-$0$ index-$1$ twisted elliptic genus has
the form
\[
  F_L^{(g_N,h_M)}(\tau,z)
  =\alpha_{N,M,L}(\tau)\,\phioo(\tau,z)
  +\beta_{N,M,L}(\tau)\,\phimt(\tau,z),
\]
with $\alpha_{N,M,L}$ of weight $0$ and $\beta_{N,M,L}$ of weight $2$
for the relevant congruence group.  At level $1$ the second summand
vanishes because $M_2(\mathrm{SL}_2(\ZZ))=0$; at higher level it is a
genuine CHL datum.  This is the second test surface for the constants
$c_N(0)$, independent of the scalar use of $\phioo$ alone.

\subsection*{Pfaffian lattice restriction}

Let $\Lat^4$ be the rank-four symplectic lattice and let
$\wedge^2\Lat^4$ carry the pfaffian pairing.  This lattice has signature
$(3,3)$ and is isometric to $U^{\oplus 3}$.  For
\[
  q=e_1\wedge e_3+e_2\wedge e_4
\]
normalise the pfaffian form so that $q^2=-2$.  The orthogonal complement
\[
  q^\perp\subset \wedge^2\Lat^4
\]
has signature $(3,2)$.  The exceptional map
$\mathrm{Sp}_4(\ZZ)\to \mathrm{O}^+(\Lat^{3,2})$ is obtained by this
restriction.  A $\mathrm{GL}_4$ trace formula for
$\kBKM(\Delta_{5})=5$ requires more than this restriction: it requires
an explicit trace computation on the Borcherds input or on the
regularised theta lift.

\subsection*{Positive-half and chiral-output separations}

For $d\geq 3$, the native output of the CY-to-chiral construction is
$E_1$-chiral.  Any $E_2$ operation belongs to a derived centre, a
Drinfeld double, or a representation category after the relevant
hypotheses are imposed; the specialised algebra itself remains
$E_1$-chiral.

The local toric base case is
\[
  \CoHA(\mathbb{C}^3)=\Yplus(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1).
\]
The full $\mathcal W_{1+\infty}$ object is obtained through Drinfeld
doubling and a Fock/evaluation realisation.  Positive half, double, and
vertex-algebra image are different objects.

The six routes leading to $G(K3\times E)$ are distinct construction
types.  The count records one Stage-$1$ functor with distinct
specialisation and comparison data.

\subsection*{Proof obligations}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Fix the reduced-DT convention: $C$, the $p$-variable, and the
square root $\Delta_{5}$ must be normalised in the same formula.
\item Construct the morphisms in the lattice-polarised assignment
$L\mapsto\gBKM_L$ before using functorial language.
\item Compute the coefficients $\alpha_{N,M,L}$ and $\beta_{N,M,L}$ in
the $(\phioo,\phimt)$ expansion for the CHL cases $N=4,6,8$.
\item Reconstruct the vector-valued weak Maass input and regularised
theta kernel whose Borcherds lift gives $\Delta_{5}$.
\item Prove any proposed $\mathrm{GL}_4$ trace presentation of
$\kBKM(\Delta_{5})=5$ through an actual trace calculation; the
signature-$(3,3)$ to signature-$(3,2)$ restriction supplies only the
lattice embedding.
\item For any identification of the six constructions of
$G(K3\times E)$, supply comparison maps, parity data, PBW/no-extra
relations checks, and completion or realisation hypotheses.
\end{enumerate}
