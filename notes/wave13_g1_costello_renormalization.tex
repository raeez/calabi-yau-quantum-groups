\section{Costello's renormalization machine on 6D hCS}
\label{sec:wave13-g1-costello-renorm-6dhcs}

The theory. Six-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons on a Calabi--Yau
threefold $X$: fields $A \in \Omega^{0,\bullet}(X, \mathfrak{g})[1]$,
classical action
\[
  S_{\mathrm{cl}}[A] \;=\; \int_X \Omega_X \wedge \mathrm{tr}\!\left(
  \tfrac{1}{2}\, A \,\bar\partial A \;+\; \tfrac{1}{6}\, A \,[A, A]
  \right),
\]
with $\Omega_X$ the CY3 holomorphic volume form. BV antibracket
$\bracket{-}{-}_{\mathrm{BV}}$ from the degree-$(-1)$ odd symplectic
pairing induced by $\Omega_X$ on $\Omega^{0,\bullet}\otimes\mathfrak{g}$.

\paragraph{Cycle 1: scale-dependent effective action.}
Costello 2011 (Ch.~2, Def.~2.1.1) defines a family
$\{\Seff[\Lambda]\}_{\Lambda > 0}$ of functionals on fields, indexed by
a UV cutoff scale $\Lambda$, satisfying the renormalization-group
equation
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:costello-rg}
  \hbar\, \partial_\Lambda \Seff[\Lambda]
  \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\, \bracket{\Seff[\Lambda]}{\Seff[\Lambda]}_\Lambda
  \;-\; \hbar\, \Delta_\Lambda\, \Seff[\Lambda],
\end{equation}
where $\{-,-\}_\Lambda$ is the regularized BV bracket and $\Delta_\Lambda$
the regularized BV Laplacian (Costello 2011 Ch.~5, Lem.~5.3.2). For
6D~$\hCS$ the propagator is the $\bar\partial$-Green kernel on $X$
regularized by heat-kernel cutoff $e^{-\Lambda \bar\partial^*\bar\partial}$.

\paragraph{Cycle 2: Feynman-graph expansion.}
Costello 2011 (Thm.~2.3.0.1) expands the effective action as a sum over
connected Feynman graphs $\Gamma$:
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:costello-feynman-expansion}
  \Seff[\Lambda]
  \;=\; \sum_{\Gamma \text{ connected}}
  \frac{\hbar^{L(\Gamma)}}{|\Aut(\Gamma)|}\,
  \int_{\Gamma} \mathcal{A}_\Lambda(\Gamma),
\end{equation}
with $L(\Gamma) = E(\Gamma) - V(\Gamma) + 1$ the first Betti number and
$\mathcal{A}_\Lambda(\Gamma)$ the amplitude: tensor product of vertex
weights (Taylor coefficients of $S_{\mathrm{cl}}$) contracted against
edge weights (the $\Lambda$-regulated propagator $P_\Lambda$). For
$\hCS$ the only interaction vertex is cubic ($\tfrac{1}{6}\mathrm{tr}\,A[A,A]$),
so $\Gamma$ ranges over trivalent connected graphs.

\paragraph{Cycle 3: counterterms.}
Let $P_{L,\Lambda} = P_0 - P_\Lambda$ be the UV-subtracted propagator.
Costello 2011 (Ch.~3, Thm.~3.0.0.1 --- the \emph{renormalization theorem})
asserts: at each loop order $L \geq 1$, the naive $\Lambda \to 0$ limit
of $\mathcal{A}_\Lambda(\Gamma)$ is divergent; divergences are
cancelled by adding local counterterms
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:costello-ct}
  \CT_L \;\in\; \Loc(\Fields),
  \qquad
  \Seff^{\mathrm{ren}}[\Lambda]
  \;=\; \Seff[\Lambda] \;-\; \sum_{L \geq 1} \hbar^L \CT_L.
\end{equation}
Locality is crucial: $\CT_L$ is a \emph{local} functional (polynomial
in fields and their jets, supported at a point), not a general
distribution; this is the content of Costello's locality axiom
(Costello--Gwilliam 2017 Vol.~I, Def.~3.5.1).

\paragraph{Cycle 4: BV consistency (quantum master equation).}
The renormalized $\Seff^{\mathrm{ren}}$ is required to satisfy the
scale-$\Lambda$ quantum master equation
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:qme}
  \bracket{\Seff^{\mathrm{ren}}}{\Seff^{\mathrm{ren}}}_\Lambda
  \;+\; 2\hbar\, \Delta_\Lambda \Seff^{\mathrm{ren}}
  \;=\; 0.
\end{equation}
Expanding modulo $\hbar^2$, the 1-loop anomaly
$\tfrac{1}{2}\bracket{S_{\mathrm{cl}}}{\CT_1}_{\mathrm{BV}} - \Delta_0 S_{\mathrm{cl}}$
obstructs \eqref{eq:qme}; its class in $H^1$ of the local
BV complex is the Costello anomaly
$\kBV \in H^1(\Loc(\Fields), d_{\mathrm{BV}})$. Quantization exists
iff $\kBV = 0$; then $\CT$ is unique up to BV-exact shifts
(Costello 2011 Thm.~5.0.0.1, the \emph{uniqueness-up-to-BV-homotopy}
statement).

\paragraph{Cycle 5: 6D hCS specifics.}
\emph{Abelian case} $\mathfrak{g} = \mathfrak{u}(1)$. The bracket
$[A,A]$ vanishes identically, so the cubic vertex is zero and
$S_{\mathrm{cl}}[A] = \tfrac{1}{2}\int_X \Omega_X \wedge A\,\bar\partial A$
is Gaussian. Only the empty graph (one-loop determinant) contributes;
no interaction vertex means no loop graph with $L \geq 1$ has nonzero
amplitude beyond the free-field normalization. Hence
\[
  \Seff^{\mathrm{ren}}[\Lambda] \;=\; S_{\mathrm{cl}}
  \qquad \forall\,\Lambda > 0,
  \qquad \CT_L \equiv 0 \;\; (L \geq 1).
\]
The anomaly $\kBV$ vanishes trivially. Renormalization is trivial;
no counterterm is needed.

\emph{Nonabelian case.} The cubic vertex generates one-loop triangle
and bubble diagrams. Costello's general analysis (Costello 2011
Ch.~4, analysis of Chern--Simons-type theories; Costello--Li 2015 for
the 4D twisted supersymmetric variant) shows the only local 1-loop
divergence has external-field dependence $\int_X \Omega_X \wedge
\mathrm{tr}(A\,\bar\partial A)$, multiplied by the quadratic Casimir
$C_2(\mathfrak{g})$. Explicitly, the 1-loop counterterm takes the form
\[
  \CT_1 \;=\; \kct \cdot C_2(\mathfrak{g}) \cdot
  \int_X \Omega_X \wedge \mathrm{tr}(A\,\bar\partial A),
\]
with $\kct$ a rational number depending only on the regularization
scheme (heat kernel, dimensional, etc.; scheme-dependent, absorbed
into the classical action by a finite wavefunction renormalization).

The 1-loop BV anomaly for $\hCS$ on a CY3: in the Costello--Li
treatment, the anomaly cocycle pulls back from the fundamental
anomaly class on the local operators, and the CY3 condition
($c_1(X) = 0$) is precisely what makes the box-graph anomaly cohomologous
to zero. Thus on CY3, the nonabelian 6D $\hCS$ quantization exists
at 1-loop, and by Costello's inductive step (2011 Thm.~5.4.0.3) extends
to all orders with $\CT_L$ determined uniquely up to BV-homotopy.

\paragraph{Output.} For 6D $\hCS$ on $\mathbb{C}^3$ with
$\mathfrak{g} = \mathfrak{u}(1)$: renormalization is trivial, the
effective action equals the classical action at every scale, all
counterterms vanish, and the quantization is exact at tree level.
This is the content of the statement that abelian 6D $\hCS$ is a
free BV theory on CY3.

For $\mathfrak{g}$ simple nonabelian: the 1-loop counterterm
$\CT_1$ exists, is proportional to $C_2(\mathfrak{g})$, and is
uniquely determined (up to BV-homotopy) by Costello's renormalization
theorem. Higher-loop counterterms $\CT_L$ for $L \geq 2$ are
controlled by the same inductive obstruction theory and vanish
under the CY3 condition $c_1(X) = 0$, matching the Costello--Gwilliam
result that 6D $\hCS$ on CY3 lifts to a quantum factorization algebra
of observables --- the chiral-algebra image of $\Phi$.
