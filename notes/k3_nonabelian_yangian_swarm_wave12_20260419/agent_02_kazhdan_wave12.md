## Attack-heal cycle 1 — Is "Howe theta" the right vocabulary at all?

### 1.A ATTACK — Borcherds singular theta is NOT a Howe-Weil theta lift

**Attack (primary).** Wave 11 said the construction is "Kudla-Rallis regularised theta at the boundary of the stable range, $p + q = 2n + 3 = 5$". This is **half-true and misleading**. Here is why.

**Kudla-Rallis 1994** "*A regularized Siegel-Weil formula*" (Ann. of Math. 140) regularises the **Siegel-Weil theta integral**
$$I(s, g, \varphi) = E^{\mathrm{Sp}_{2n}}_s(g, \Phi_\varphi) - \int_{[\mathrm{O}(V)]} \theta(g, h, \varphi) dh$$
in the range where the cuspidal theta $\int \theta(g,h,\varphi) dh$ diverges. The regularisation is via analytic continuation in $s$ of the Eisenstein series, and the Siegel-Weil identity becomes $I(s, g, \varphi) = $ meromorphic in $s$ with pole at $s = s_0 = (n + 1 - q)/2$ whose residue is the regularised theta.

**Borcherds 1998** "*Automorphic forms with singularities on Grassmannians*" (Invent. Math. 132) constructs the lift **directly from a vector-valued weakly holomorphic modular form** $F$ of weight $k = (2-n)/2$ on $\widetilde{\mathrm{SL}}_2$:
$$\Phi(F)(Z) = \int^{\mathrm{reg}}_\mathcal{F} F(\tau) \overline{\Theta_\Lambda(\tau, Z)} \cdot y^{-s} dx\, dy \bigg|_{s = ?}$$
The regularisation is **Harvey-Moore Mellin** (Borcherds §6): extract the finite part of the Laurent expansion in $s$ around $s = 0$. This is not the same regularisation as Kudla-Rallis.

**The difference:**

| | Kudla-Rallis | Borcherds |
|---|---|---|
| Input | cuspidal/holomorphic $\varphi$ on $\widetilde{\mathrm{Sp}}_{2n}$ | **weakly holomorphic** (polar at cusp) $F$ on $\widetilde{\mathrm{SL}}_2$ |
| Output | residue of regularised Siegel-Weil | **meromorphic** automorphic form with **prescribed singularities** on rational quadratic divisors |
| Pole locus of output | holomorphic | **poles along $\lambda^\perp$ for $\lambda \in \Lambda$ with $(\lambda,\lambda) < 0$** |
| Regularisation | $s$-continuation of Eisenstein | Harvey-Moore Mellin |
| Role in theta tower | stable-range-boundary | *outside* the classical theta tower |

**The Borcherds lift is fundamentally a "theta lift of a weakly holomorphic form with polar principal part"** — this polar input is what makes the output have singularities, and is **incompatible** with the cuspidal-stable-range Howe-Weil theta framework. The two constructions are related (Bruinier 2002 "*Borcherds products on O(2,l) and Chern classes of Heegner divisors*" shows Borcherds theta = Kudla-Millson theta tower analytic continuation), but they are not the same object.

**So calling Borcherds $\Phi: \phi_{0,1} \to \Delta_5$ a "Howe theta" or "dual-pair theta" is a category error.** There is no proper dual pair in the sense of Howe 1979. What there is: a metaplectic-SL_2 input (weakly holomorphic form of half-integral weight), an orthogonal-O(p,q) target (grassmannian), and a Mellin-regularised integral.
