\subsection{Four $\kappa$-invariants, never conflated}

Throughout: $\kBKM$ denotes the Borcherds--Kac--Moody weight,
$c_N(0)/2$;
$\kch$ denotes the chiral-side supertrace of the $\Phi$-image;
$\kcat$ denotes the categorical Euler characteristic
$\chi(\mathcal O_X)$;
$\kfib$ denotes the fibre/lattice correction. Bare $\kappa$ is
forbidden.

\begin{lemma}[Four-$\kappa$ disjointness on $K3 \times E$]
\label{lem:four-kappa-disjoint}
On $X = K3 \times E$ the four invariants take four distinct values:
\[
  \kcat(X) = 0, \quad
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(X) = 3, \quad
  \kBKM(\gDelta) = 5, \quad
  \kfib(X) = 24.
\]
$\kcat(X) = 0$ is K\"unneth-multiplicative on the total space:
$\chi(\mathcal O_{K3 \times E}) = \chi(\mathcal O_{K3}) \cdot
\chi(\mathcal O_E) = 2 \cdot 0 = 0$.
$\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(X) = 3$ is the chiral Heisenberg--Mukai
specialisation.
$\kBKM(\gDelta) = 5$ is the Gritsenko 1999 paramodular weight.
$\kfib(X) = 24$ is the Mukai-lattice rank
$\mathrm{rk}\,\widetilde H^*(K3, \mathbb Z) = 24$.
Four different constructions; the constructions share no input datum
beyond $X$ itself.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The four calculations are contained in
\texttt{cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex} Theorems
\texttt{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal} ($\kBKM$),
\texttt{thm:heisenberg-mukai-kappa-ch} ($\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}$), and
the K\"unneth identity for $\kcat$. $\kfib = 24$ is
$\mathrm{rk}\,\Lambda_{K3} = 3 + 19 + 2 \cdot 1 = 24$.
\end{proof}
