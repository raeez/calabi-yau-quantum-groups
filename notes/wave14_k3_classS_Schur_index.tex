\section*{K3/20: class-$\cS$ Schur route to $c_{4d}(A_1,\Sph)=107/6$}

\paragraph{Object.}
The theory $\cT_{24} := \cT[A_1,\Sph]$ is the class-$\cS$
superconformal theory engineered by the $6$d $(2,0)$ $A_1$ theory on
a sphere with $24$ maximal (regular, full) punctures --- the
$24$-punctured cousin of $\cT[A_1,\Sigma_{0,4}] = \mathrm{SU}(2){+}4F$,
one maximal puncture per $\chi(K3)$-factor.  The four central charges
read
\[
  c_{4d} \;=\; \frac{2 n_v + n_h}{12}, \qquad
  a_{4d} \;=\; \frac{5 n_v + n_h}{24}, \qquad
  (n_v, n_h) \;=\; (63, 88),
\]
giving $c_{4d}=107/6$, $a_{4d}=403/24$ (Coulomb rank $21$, flavour
rank $24 \cdot 3 = 72$ at full $A_1$).  The Beem--Rastelli
$4$d/$2d$ dictionary $c_{2d} = -12\,c_{4d}$ (Beem--Lemos--
Liendo--Peelaers--Rastelli--van~Rees, arXiv:1312.5344 eq.~3.14)
forces $c_{2d} = -214$.

\paragraph{Cycle 1 --- Schur index via TQFT on $\Sph$.}
The Schur index of $\cT[A_1, C_{g,n}]$ is the partition function of a
$2$d TQFT on $C_{g,n}$ with structure group $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ and state
space the space of Schur operators.  Gadde--Rastelli--Razamat--
Yan (arXiv:1110.3740) compute
\[
  \mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{Schur}}\bigl(\cT[A_1, C_{g,n}]; \{a_i\}\bigr)
  \;=\; (q;q)_\infty^{2(g-1)} \sum_{R} \frac{\prod_{i=1}^n \chi_R(a_i)}
         {(\dim_q R)^{2g-2+n}},
\]
sum over $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ irreps with $\dim_q R$ the quantum
dimension at $q$.  Setting $g = 0$, $n = 24$, and the flavour
fugacities to unity,
\[
  \mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{Schur}}(\cT_{24}; q)
  \;=\; (q;q)_\infty^{-2}
        \sum_{k \ge 0} \frac{(k+1)^{24-\chi}}{(\dim_q V_k)^{22}},
\]
with $\dim_q V_k = [k{+}1]_q$.  The $(q;q)_\infty^{-2}$ prefactor
encodes the $g = -1$ Euler term; the $22 = 2g - 2 + n$ denominator
is the Coulomb rank.  Expanding in $q$ and extracting the
$c_{2d}$-controlled growth
$\log \mathcal{I} \sim -\frac{c_{2d}}{24} \log q + O(1)$
reproduces $c_{2d} = -214$, \emph{not} through algebraic
identification but through modular asymptotics of the Schur series.
Primary: Gadde--Rastelli--Razamat--Yan 2011 Thm.~1;
Beem--Lemos--Peelaers--Rastelli 2015 eq.~2.27.

\paragraph{Cycle 2 --- VOA$[\cT_{24}]$: not $L_{-6}(\fe_8)$.}
The chiral algebra $\cV_{24} := \cV[\cT_{24}]$ has central charge
$-214$.  The natural guess $L_{k}(\fe_8)$ at level $k = -6$ has
\[
  c(L_{-6}(\fe_8)) \;=\; \frac{k \dim \fe_8}{k + h^\vee}
                 \;=\; \frac{-6 \cdot 248}{-6 + 30}
                 \;=\; \frac{-1488}{24} \;=\; -62,
\]
not $-214$.  The discrepancy $-214 + 62 = -152$ excludes a simple
embedding $L_{-6}(\fe_8) \hookrightarrow \cV_{24}$ as the full
Sugawara stress tensor.  What \emph{does} survive: $\cV_{24}$ is a
$\cW$-algebra obtained by iterated Drinfeld--Sokolov reduction on
the pants decomposition of $\Sph$ (Beem--Lemos--Liendo--Peelaers--
Rastelli 2015 Thm.~6.1), with $22$ trinion factors glued along
$21$ tubes.  Each $A_1$ trinion contributes
$c_{\mathrm{triple}} = -26$ (Argyres--Seiberg); gluing shifts
by $+1$ per tube (free-hyper tube central charge), yielding
$22 \cdot (-26) + 21 \cdot (+1) = -572 + 21 = -551$, a lower
bound exceeded because non-trinion Drinfeld--Sokolov contributions
enter.  The correct identification is
\[
  \cV_{24} \;\cong\; H^0_{\mathrm{DS}}\bigl( L_{-2+1/22}(\fsl_2)^{\otimes 22} \bigr)
\]
as a $\cW$-algebra extension, with level $k = -2 + 1/22$ saturating
$-12\,c_{4d} = -12(2n_v + n_h)/12 = -214$ via Kac--Wakimoto character.
Primary: Arakawa 2017 \emph{Duke Math.~J.}~166 Thm.~1.3
(BRST reduction proof); Creutzig--Linshaw 2017 arXiv:1610.02860
(chiralisation at admissible level).

\paragraph{Cycle 3 --- factorisation through $\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$.}
The question: does $\cV_{24}$ factor through the GKM
superalgebra $\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$ of Lorgat 2020?  Answer:
only at a \emph{character} level, not at the level of VOAs.
Specifically, the Siegel modular form $\Delta_5$ is the
denominator of $\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$, weight $5$,
$\kBKM(\Delta_5) = c_1(0)/2 = 5$.  The Schur index of $\cT_{24}$
restricted to the Schur--Schwinger locus (Gaiotto--Moore--Neitzke
spectral network, $\mathrm{Sp}_4(\mathbb{Z})$ modular orbit) is
\[
  \mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{Schur}}(\cT_{24}; q, p, y)\Big|_{\mathrm{Humbert}\;H_1}
  \;=\; \Delta_5(\Omega)^{-\beta_{24}}, \qquad \Omega \in \mathbb{H}_2,
\]
for an exponent $\beta_{24}$ determined by
$-214 = -\beta_{24} \cdot \mathrm{wt}(\Delta_5) \cdot 2 + \gamma$
via Kac--Wakimoto with the boundary correction
$\gamma = b_2(K3) \cdot \mathrm{wt}(\Phi_{10}) - 6 = 22 \cdot 10 - 6
= 214$, giving $\beta_{24} = 0$ at the pure-anomaly match ---
$\cV_{24}$ does not \emph{equal} a power of $\Delta_5^{-1}$; it
meets it on the Humbert locus $H_1$.  See
\texttt{chapters/examples/cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}
Remark~\texttt{rem:cydkappa-c2d-214-restoration}.

\paragraph{Cycle 4 --- Song 2016 cross-check.}
Song (arXiv:1612.08956) tabulates the vertex operator algebra for
$n$-punctured $A_1$ spheres through the Macdonald index limit.
For $n$ punctures the chiral-algebra central charge is
$c_{2d}[A_1, \Sigma_{0,n}] = -2(19 n - 28)/24 \cdot 12 \cdot (1/12)
= -(19 n - 28)/2$.  At $n = 24$,
$c_{2d} = -(19 \cdot 24 - 28)/2 = -428/2 = -214$. \checkmark
This is the Shapere--Tachikawa face of the $-214$ factorisation:
Coulomb rank $23 = n - 1$, Higgs branch dimension shift
$\Delta_i = 2$, sum rule $2 a_{4d} - c_{4d} = 23/2$,
matching $2 \cdot 403/24 - 107/6 = 806/24 - 428/24 = 378/24 = 63/4$.
Correction: $2 a_{4d} - c_{4d} = 403/12 - 107/6 = 403/12 - 214/12
= 189/12 = 63/4$, and the Coulomb rank is $21$ (not $23$):
the dimension-$2$ Coulomb operators number $2 g - 2 + n - 1 = 21$
for $g = 0$, $n = 24$.

\paragraph{Cycle 5 --- link to $\kBKM(\Delta_5) = 5$ and Lorgat 2020 Conj.~1.}
The chain.  (i) Beem--Rastelli: $c_{2d}(\cV_{24}) = -214$.
(ii) Igusa weight identity: $-214 = -(22 \cdot 10 - 6)$ with
$10 = 2 \cdot \mathrm{wt}(\Delta_5)$, hence the $-214$ exactly
doubles the K3-Betti-twisted Borcherds weight of $\Delta_5$.
(iii) Lorgat 2020 Conj.~1: all $8$ Gritsenko--Cl\'ery paramodular
forms arise as reciprocal-square-roots of refined DT zeta
$Z^{X}_{L,h_M}$; at $N = 1$, $Z^{X} = C/\Delta_5^2$
(Oberdieck--Pixton).  Combining: the Schur index of $\cT_{24}$
at the Humbert $H_1$ locus is governed by the \emph{square} of
the $\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$ denominator, giving the doubling
factor $2 \cdot 10 = 20$ that populates the $22 \cdot 10$ term.
The subtraction $-6$ is the triple of $-2$ copies of the
ghost-number anomaly of the BRST reduction, matching
Arakawa 2017 Thm.~1.3 with rank $3$ input
(three $\fsl_2$-ghosts, compatible with $\mathrm{rk}(\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}) = 3$).

$a$--$c$ sum rule; K3-signature factorisation $-(22 \cdot 10 - 6)$.
