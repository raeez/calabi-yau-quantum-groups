\section*{Class-\texorpdfstring{$\cS$}{S} Schur Index and the Igusa Denominator for \texorpdfstring{$K3\times E$}{K3xE}}

\paragraph{The Gaiotto curve.}
Let
\[
  \Sph=(\PP^1;p_1,\ldots,p_{24})
\]
be the \(A_1\) class-\(\cS\) curve with twenty-four maximal regular
\(\fsu(2)\)-punctures, and set
\[
  \cT_{24}:=\cT[A_1,\Sph].
\]
A pants decomposition of \(\Sigma_{0,n}\) has \(n-2\) trinions \(T_2\)
and \(n-3\) gauged \(SU(2)\) tubes. The \(A_1\) trinion is the
trifundamental half-hypermultiplet system with effective
\((n_v,n_h)=(0,4)\), while an \(SU(2)\) vector multiplet contributes
\((n_v,n_h)=(3,0)\). Hence
\[
  (n_v,n_h)\bigl(\cT[A_1,\Sigma_{0,n}]\bigr)
  =
  (3(n-3),4(n-2)).
\]
At \(n=24\),
\[
  (n_v,n_h)(\cT_{24})=(63,88).
\]
The Shapere-Tachikawa normalization gives
\[
  c_{4d}=\frac{2n_v+n_h}{12},
  \qquad
  a_{4d}=\frac{5n_v+n_h}{24}.
\]
The four-dimensional to two-dimensional Schur dictionary of Beem,
Lemos, Liendo, Peelaers, Rastelli, and van Rees gives
\[
  c_{2d}=-12c_{4d}.
\]
Therefore
\[
  c_{4d}(\cT_{24})=\frac{2\cdot63+88}{12}=\frac{107}{6},
  \qquad
  a_{4d}(\cT_{24})=\frac{403}{24},
  \qquad
  c_{2d}\bigl(\chi(\cT_{24})\bigr)=-214.
\]
The Coulomb rank is \(21\), and the flavour algebra is
\(\fsu(2)^{24}\).

\paragraph{The genus-two curve.}
For the closed genus-two curve \(\Sigma_{2,0}\), the same pants count
has \(2g-2=2\) trinions and \(3g-3=3\) gauged tubes:
\[
  (n_v,n_h)=2(0,4)+3(3,0)=(9,8).
\]
Consequently
\[
  c_{4d}\bigl(\cT[A_1,\Sigma_{2,0}]\bigr)=\frac{2\cdot9+8}{12}
  =\frac{13}{6},
  \qquad
  c_{2d}=-26.
\]
The curve carrying the \(107/6\) Schur parent is \(\Sph\). The
genus-two variable belongs to the Siegel target of the Borcherds lift,
through \(Z=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}\tau&z\\z&\sigma\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\in\HH_2\)
and the Humbert divisor \(H_1\subset\mathcal A_2\).

\paragraph{Schur character and Jacobi projection.}
The Schur construction assigns to a four-dimensional \(\mathcal N=2\)
SCFT \(\cT\) a vertex algebra \(\chi(\cT)\). Its graded character is
\[
  \cI_{\mathrm{Schur}}(\cT;q,\mathbf{x})
  =
  \operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal H_{\mathrm{Schur}}}
  (-1)^Fq^{E-R}\mathbf{x}^{\mathbf f}.
\]
For class-\(\cS\) theories, Gadde, Rastelli, Razamat, and Yan identify
this character with the correlator of the Schur topological field
theory on the Gaiotto curve:
\[
  \cI_{\mathrm{Schur}}\bigl(\cT[A_1,C_{g,n}];q,\mathbf{x}\bigr)
  =
  \sum_{\lambda\geq0}
  C_{\lambda}(q)^{\,2g-2+n}
  \prod_{i=1}^{n}\psi_{\lambda}(x_i;q).
\]
The \(24\) variables of \(\cT_{24}\) are attached to the \(24\) maximal
punctures. After those punctures are labelled by the singular fibres of
an elliptic \(K3\), the Mathieu average gives a Jacobi-form projection
\[
  \Pi_{M_{24}}\colon
  \cI_{\mathrm{Schur}}(\cT_{24})
  \longrightarrow
  \phi_{0,1}^{K3}(\tau,z).
\]
The Gritsenko-Nikulin normalization is
\[
  \phi_{0,1}^{K3}(\tau,z)=y^{-1}+10+y+O(q),
  \qquad
  c_1(0)=[q^0y^0]\phi_{0,1}^{K3}=10.
\]
The geometric \(K3\) elliptic genus is \(2\phi_{0,1}^{K3}\); the
primitive Borcherds denominator uses the displayed half-normalized
Jacobi form.

\paragraph{Igusa normalization.}
Let \(\Delta_5\) denote the weight-five Igusa square root in theta
normalization. Its first product coefficient fixes
\[
  [q^{1/2}r^{1/2}s^{1/2}]\Delta_5=64,
  \qquad
  D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5.
\]
The monic denominator of the rank-three Borcherds-Kac-Moody
superalgebra \(\fg_{\Delta_5}\) is
\[
  \operatorname{den}(\fg_{\Delta_5})=D_5(2Z).
\]
Borcherds' weight formula gives
\[
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)=\frac{c_1(0)}{2}=\frac{10}{2}=5.
\]
The square normalization is
\[
  \Phi_{10}^{\theta}=\Delta_5^2,
\]
while the Oberdieck-Pandharipande monic scalar convention is
\[
  \Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}=D_5^2=4096^{-1}\Delta_5^2.
\]

\paragraph{Typed comparison.}
The comparison from the class-\(\cS\) Schur sector to the Igusa
denominator has the form
\[
  \cT[A_1,\Sph]
  \xrightarrow{\ \chi\ }
  \chi(\cT_{24})
  \xrightarrow{\ \operatorname{ch}\ }
  \cI_{\mathrm{Schur}}(\cT_{24})
  \xrightarrow{\ \Pi_{M_{24}}\ }
  \phi_{0,1}^{K3}
  \xrightarrow{\ \Borch\ }
  \Delta_5
  \rightsquigarrow
  \fg_{\Delta_5}.
\]
The first arrow is the protected chiral-algebra construction. The
second takes the Schur character. The third uses the chosen
Mathieu-equivariant labelling of the punctures. The fourth is the
Gritsenko-Nikulin Borcherds lift. The final arrow records the
denominator superalgebra whose imaginary-root supermultiplicities are
read from the Fourier coefficients of \(\phi_{0,1}^{K3}\). Each arrow
requires its displayed source and target; the character, the Jacobi
form, the Siegel form, the class-\(\cS\) curve, and the Hall data are
different mathematical objects.

\paragraph{\texorpdfstring{$K3\times E$}{K3xE} invariants.}
Let \(X=K3\times E\). The compact Hodge column is
\[
  h^{0,\bullet}(X)=(1,1,1,1),
\]
so the compact chiral Hodge supertrace is
\[
  \kch(X)=\sum_{q=0}^{3}(-1)^qh^{0,q}(X)=1-1+1-1=0.
\]
The categorical Euler invariant is K\"unneth-multiplicative:
\[
  \kcat(X)=\chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot0=0.
\]
The Heisenberg-Mukai specialization gives
\[
  \kHeis(X)=3,
\]
and the K3 fibre supplies the Mukai-lattice rank
\[
  \kfib(X)=\operatorname{rk}\widetilde H(K3,\Z)=1+22+1=24.
\]
Together with the Borcherds weight,
\[
  \kcat(X)=0,\qquad
  \kch(X)=0,\qquad
  \kHeis(X)=3,\qquad
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)=5,\qquad
  \kfib(X)=24.
\]
The two zeros are distinguished by construction. An identity joining
these quantities would require an additional morphism connecting the
Hodge, Hall, Jacobi, and fibre layers; the displayed comparison contains
only the typed arrows above.

\paragraph{Native threefold output.}
For \(d\geq3\), the native CY-to-chiral output is \(E_1\)-chiral. In
the present case,
\[
  A_X=
  \SpCh_{K3,E}\bigl(\PhiFA_3(\Perf(K3\times E))\bigr)
  \in E_1\text{-}\ChirAlg(E).
\]
Two-dimensional braided structure belongs to a named centre, double,
module category, or external Schur layer, for example
\[
  Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A_X),
  \qquad
  D(\CoHA(X)),
  \qquad
  Z(\operatorname{Rep}(A_X)),
  \qquad
  \chi(\cT_{24}).
\]
The compact threefold output is \(A_X\).

\paragraph{Primary anchors.}
The class-\(\cS\) central-charge arithmetic uses Gaiotto 2012,
Chacaltana and Distler 2010, and Shapere and Tachikawa 2008. The
Schur VOA relation \(c_{2d}=-12c_{4d}\) is the Beem, Lemos, Liendo,
Peelaers, Rastelli, and van Rees dictionary. The Schur TQFT character
formula is due to Gadde, Rastelli, Razamat, and Yan. The
\(\Delta_5\) normalization and weight use Gritsenko and Nikulin's
Borcherds product and Borcherds' singular-theta weight formula. The
reduced \(K3\times E\) Igusa square convention is the
Oberdieck-Pandharipande convention.
