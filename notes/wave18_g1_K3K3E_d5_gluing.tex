\section*{Fake-Monster positive half at $d=5$: $K3\times K3\times E$ gluing}
\label{wn:sec:wave18-g1-K3K3E-d5-gluing}

The Fake-Monster BKM $\gFM$ of Borcherds 1990 lives on the Lorentzian
lattice $\IIlor = \Leech \oplus U$ of rank $26$ with denominator
$\PhiFM$ of weight $12$ on $\mathrm{O}^{+}(\IIpar)$. Wave-16 S4 proved
that no surface $\Sigma_2 \subset X$ in a compact CY$_3$ supports the
rank-$24$ transverse lattice that $\IIlor$ demands, and located the CY
witness at $d=5$: $X = K3_1 \times K3_2 \times E$ with
$(\Sigma_4, C) = (K3_1 \times K3_2, E)$, $\kBKM(\PhiFM) = c(0)/2 = 12$
for $c(m)$ the $1/\eta^{24}$ coefficients. Wave-16 U4 equipped $d=5$
with its $(+1)$-shifted Poisson-$E_5$ structure. What remains is the
positive-half: the gluing of two K3 positive effective blocks along
the elliptic parameter and the Niemeier projection onto the Leech
slice. This note supplies the structure.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 1 --- $d=5$ hyperk\"ahler-type datum and the $(+1)$-shift}
\label{wn:subsec:wave18-g1-attack1}

\ClaimStatusDefinition\ Let $X = K3_1 \times K3_2 \times E$, compact
smooth projective CY$_5$ with nowhere-vanishing
$\Omega_5 = \sigma_{K3_1}\wedge \sigma_{K3_2}\wedge dz_E$.
K\"unneth-multiplicativity gives $\kcat(X) = 2\cdot 2\cdot 0 = 0$
(CLAUDE.md key fact: elliptic factor forces vanishing).
$X$ is not hyperk\"ahler, but carries a \emph{hyperk\"ahler-type}
structure in the refined sense that the $\Omega_5$-form factors as a
wedge of two holomorphic symplectic forms and a $1$-form: $X$ is
foliated by the two K3 holomorphic-symplectic pairs and the single
elliptic current. \textbf{Flaw.} Naive BV at $d=5$ gives shift
$+1$; the cotangent self-pairing fails (Wave-16~U4
Corollary~\ref{cor:poisson-not-symplectic}).
\textbf{Heal (PTVV 2013 $\S 2$; CPTVV 2017 $\S 3.5$).} $\Omega_5$
transgresses over the fundamental class $[X] \in H_{5,5}(X)$ to a
$(+1)$-shifted \emph{Poisson} structure on
$\mathrm{Map}(X_{\mathrm{dR}}, BG)$; observables form an
$E_5$-algebra with Poisson bracket of cohomological degree $+1$.
\textbf{Resolution.} The positive-half grammar $\Yplus(X) =
\Heq(\Mbps(X), \phi_W)$ of Wave-17 A1 admits the $d=5$ stratum via
the reduced virtual cycle on each K3 factor plus the $\Cstar_E$
residue, with the $(+1)$-shift absorbed into the Poisson bracket on
$\Yplus$, not into its underlying vector space.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 2 --- $\Mbps(K3\times K3\times E)$ with $K3\times K3$-primitive classes}
\label{wn:subsec:wave18-g1-attack2}

\ClaimStatusDefinition\
$\Mbps(X) = \Mred_{\mathrm{DT}}(X; \gamma\ \text{bi-primitive on}\ K3_1\times K3_2)$,
with $\gamma \in H_2(X, \Z)$ primitive in each K3-factor
after pushforward by the projection
$\pi_{K3_i}: X \to K3_i$ ($i=1,2$), and K3$_1$-K3$_2$-orthogonal
in the Mukai lattice.
\textbf{Flaw.} Two K3 symplectic forms $\sigma_{K3_1}, \sigma_{K3_2}$
each produce a trivial-quotient obstruction; the naive perfect
obstruction theory vanishes on the \emph{square} Mukai direction.
\textbf{Heal (MPT 2010 $\S 3$; Oberdieck 2018 arXiv:1706.10100 $\S 2$).}
Two-step reduction: kill first the $\sigma_{K3_1}$-trivial quotient,
then the residual $\sigma_{K3_2}$-trivial quotient. Virtual dimension
gains $+2$, matching the bi-primitive constraint. Explicitly,
$\mathrm{Ob}^{\mathrm{red,red}} = \ker(\mathrm{Ob} \to \mathcal{O} \oplus \mathcal{O})$
with two trivial line-bundle quotients.
\textbf{Resolution.} $\Mbps(K3 \times K3 \times E)$ is a doubly-reduced
DT stack of virtual dimension $1$ (one residual direction along $E$),
carrying a $\Cstar_E$-action by fibrewise $E$-translation and a
discrete $\Auts(K3_1)\times \Auts(K3_2)$-action by K3-fibre
Nikulin involutions on each factor. Oberdieck 2018 established the
single-K3 reduction sign for $K3\times E$; the doubly-reduced case is
the K3$\times K3$-primitive specialisation, with the sign convention
$(-1)^2 = +1$ absorbed into the product $\Auts$ character.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 3 --- rank-$5$ equivariance: $\Cstar_E \times \Auts(K3_1)\times \Auts(K3_2)$}
\label{wn:subsec:wave18-g1-attack3}

\ClaimStatusDerivation\ The equivariance group at $d=5$ on
$X = K3_1\times K3_2\times E$ is
\[
G_{\mathrm{eq}}(X) \;=\; \Cstar_E \times \Auts(K3_1) \times \Auts(K3_2),
\]
with $\Cstar_E$ the residual translation from $E$ (Wave-17 A4 Cycle 2
stratum) and each $\Auts(K3_i)$ a Nikulin--Mukai subgroup of $M_{23}$
(Wave-17 A4 Cycle 3). \textbf{Flaw.} The product equivariance is
larger than the Fake-Monster centraliser: $\gFM$ has automorphism group
$\mathrm{O}^{+}(\IIlor)$, a rank-$26$ orthogonal group, whereas
$\Auts(K3)^2 \subset M_{23}^2 \subset M_{24}^2$ has only discrete
$M_{23}$-level symmetry on each factor. \textbf{Heal.} The Niemeier
projection (Attack-heal 5) acts as an \emph{orbit averaging} over the
symmetric group $S_2$ exchanging the two K3 factors plus a choice of
Niemeier root system. After averaging,
$G_{\mathrm{eq}}(X)/S_2 \ltimes \mathrm{Niem}$ maps into the stabiliser
of a Leech-isotropic vector in $\mathrm{O}^{+}(\IIlor)$, which is
$\Cstar \ltimes \mathrm{Co}_0$ (Conway's group acting on $\Leech$).
The diagonal K3-pair $\Auts(K3_1) = \Auts(K3_2) = M_{23}$ generates a
subgroup of $\mathrm{Co}_1 = \mathrm{Co}_0/\{\pm 1\}$ through the
Conway--Wales chain $M_{23} \hookrightarrow M_{24} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Co}_1$
(Conway--Sloane Chapter~10). \textbf{Resolution.} Rank-$5$ equivariance
with one-parameter $\Cstar_E$ and two discrete $M_{23}$-type factors
is the CY-side shadow of the rank-$26$ Fake-Monster anchor, related by
Niemeier-orbit averaging.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 4 --- character formula: $\chi(\Yplus) = 1/\PhiFM$}
\label{wn:subsec:wave18-g1-attack4}

\ClaimStatusConjectured\
\emph{Fake-Monster character formula.} Under the bi-primitive
$K3\times K3$ constraint of Attack-heal 2, rank-$5$ equivariance of
Attack-heal 3, and the Niemeier projection of Attack-heal 5,
\[
\chi_{\Yplus(K3_1\times K3_2\times E)}(q, Z)
 \;=\; \frac{1}{\PhiFM(Z)},
\]
with $Z \in \mathcal{D}_{\IIpar}$ and $q = e^{2\pi i\tau}$ the
elliptic parameter, $\PhiFM$ the Borcherds automorphic product of
weight $12$ on $\mathrm{O}^{+}(\IIpar)$ (fake-monster chapter
Theorem~\ref{thm:fm-denominator}).
\textbf{Flaw.} The single-K3 Oberdieck--Pixton identity
$Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{DT}}(K3\times E) = -1/\Phi_{10}$ does not
square: $1/\Phi_{10}^2 \neq 1/\PhiFM$ as formal series, since
$\Phi_{10}$ has weight $10$ and $\Phi_{10}^2$ has weight $20$, while
$\PhiFM$ has weight $12$.
\textbf{Heal (Borcherds 1995 arXiv:alg-geom/9506003 $\S 7$).}
The Niemeier projection onto $\Leech \subset \IIbase$ is not a square
root of two copies but a \emph{primitive} Borcherds lift of the Jacobi
input $1/\Delta(\tau) = 1/\eta^{24}$. The $\eta^{24}$ Jacobi form is
the partition function of $24$ transverse heterotic bosons on the
Leech lattice, not $2\cdot 24$ on $\Leech^2$; Harvey--Moore 1996
arXiv:hep-th/9510182 $\S 4$ supply the $K3\times T^2$ heterotic
template for $\Delta_5$ weight $5$ from $\phi_{0,1}/24$, and the
Fake-Monster analogue on $K3\times K3 \times T^2$ singularises to
$1/\eta^{24}$ as the $24$-fold transverse direction along the Leech
slice, not the $48$-fold one on the full $\Lambda_{K3}^{\oplus 2}$.
\textbf{Resolution.} The character identity is not a K\"unneth-squared
of the $K3\times E$ case; it is a new Borcherds lift, with the
doubly-reduced DT integrand serving as the effective Jacobi input and
the Niemeier-projected transverse lattice fixing the $\eta^{24}$
weight via Conway--Sloane Chapter~18.

\begin{conjecture}[Fake-Monster character formula]\ClaimStatusConjectured
\label{wn:conj:FM-character-formula}
\index{Fake-Monster!character formula}
Let $X = K3_1 \times K3_2 \times E$, $\Mbps(X)$ doubly-reduced DT with
$K3\times K3$-bi-primitive charge constraint, $G_{\mathrm{eq}}(X) =
\Cstar_E \times \Auts(K3_1)\times \Auts(K3_2)$. Let
$\pi_{\mathrm{Niem}}: \IIbase \twoheadrightarrow \IIlor$ be the
Niemeier projection picking the Leech slice. The
$G_{\mathrm{eq}}$-equivariant character of
$\Yplus(X) = \Heq(\Mbps(X), \phi_W)$ satisfies, under
$\pi_{\mathrm{Niem}}$-pushforward,
\[
\pi_{\mathrm{Niem}, *}\, \chi_{\Yplus(X)}(q, Z_1, Z_2) \;=\; \frac{1}{\PhiFM(Z)},
\quad Z = \pi_{\mathrm{Niem}}(Z_1, Z_2, \tau).
\]
\end{conjecture}

The weight match is automatic: $\kBKM(\PhiFM) = c(0)/2 = 24/2 = 12$,
equal to the weight of $\PhiFM$ on $\mathrm{O}^{+}(\IIpar)$
(Borcherds 1998 Theorem~13.3; fake-monster chapter
Theorem~\ref{thm:fm-denominator}). The conjecture is open in full
generality; known is the denominator weight $12$ match and the
Niemeier-projection compatibility.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 5 --- Niemeier embedding and $\Leech \subset \IIbase$}
\label{wn:subsec:wave18-g1-attack5}

\ClaimStatusDerivation\ The rank-$3 \cdot (2+2) + 2 = 26$ Mukai-doubled
lattice $\MukaiK^{\oplus 2} \oplus U(E)$ sits naturally in
$\IIbase = U^{\oplus 3} \oplus E_8(-1)^{\oplus 3}$ via
Nikulin-primitive embedding (Nikulin 1979 \emph{Izv.}~43 Theorem~3.1:
every even lattice of rank $\leq 10$ embeds primitively in any even
unimodular lattice of sufficient rank). \textbf{Flaw.} There are
$23$ Niemeier lattices $N_i$ plus $\Leech$, and not all correspond to
CY data; the K3-product $\MukaiK^{\oplus 2}$ decomposes into $23$
Niemeier-type orbits under $\mathrm{O}(\IIbase)$, only one of which
hits the Leech slice (Conway--Sloane Chapter~27).
\textbf{Heal (Conway--Sloane 1988 $\S 18$, Umbral Moonshine MUM 2014
as a heuristic filter).} The Niemeier-projection onto $\Leech$ is
selected by the \emph{no-roots condition}: the Leech lattice is the
unique Niemeier with no norm-$2$ vectors, and K3-automorphism groups
$\Auts(K3) \subset M_{23}$ act fixed-point-freely on the root systems
of the $23$ Niemeiers $N_i$ but stabilise $\Leech$ (Mukai's Theorem,
1988: $\Auts(K3) \subset M_{23}$ embeds with Leech-compatible cycle
structure).
\textbf{Resolution.} The rank-$24$ transverse data required by
$\IIlor = \Leech \oplus U$ is supplied by the \emph{two K3 factors
jointly}: neither K3 alone reaches rank $24$ (each has
$\mathrm{rk}\, H^2(K3) = 22$, and the Mukai lattice $\MukaiK$ of
signature $(4, 20)$ has rank $24$), but the K3-K3 pair after
anti-diagonal reduction yields a rank-$24$ positive-definite
transverse slice that projects onto $\Leech$. Explicitly,
$\MukaiK_1 \oplus \MukaiK_2 \to \MukaiK_1^{\mathrm{sym}}$ anti-diagonal
+ $\MukaiK_1^{\mathrm{asym}}$-reduction, with the symmetric part
$\MukaiK^{\mathrm{sym}} \cong \MukaiK(-1/2)\oplus(\mathrm{something})$
carrying the Leech-orbit; the antisymmetric part is absorbed into the
$U(E)$ factor as the hyperbolic plane completing $\IIlor$.

\subsection*{Status}
\label{wn:subsec:wave18-g1-status}

\begin{itemize}
\item At $d=5$ on $K3_1\times K3_2\times E$, the $(+1)$-shifted
Poisson structure (Wave-16~U4 Theorem~\ref{thm:plus1-shift}) and the
hyperk\"ahler-type foliation by two K3 symplectic forms are
compatible with the universal positive-half grammar of Wave-17 A1:
$\Yplus(X) = \Heq(\Mbps(X), \phi_W)$.
\item $\Mbps(X)$ is the doubly-reduced DT stack with $K3\times K3$
bi-primitive charge, virtual dimension $1$, equivariant for
$G_{\mathrm{eq}} = \Cstar_E \times \Auts(K3_1)\times \Auts(K3_2)$.
\item The character formula
$\chi_{\Yplus}(q, Z) = 1/\PhiFM(Z)$ (after Niemeier projection)
is conjectured; the weight $12 = c(0)/2$ and the Borcherds-lift
mechanism are supplied by Borcherds 1995 $\S 7$ and Harvey--Moore
1996 $\S 4$; the CY-side integrand match is the open step.
\item The Leech slice $\Leech \subset \IIbase$ is picked out by the
no-roots condition; the K3-pair supplies rank-$24$ transverse data
jointly, not individually.
\item \emph{Open.} Bracket-level identification
$\mathfrak{g}_{\mathrm{BPS}}(K3 \times K3 \times E) \cong \gFM$;
doubly-reduced DT integrand matching $\PhiFM^{-1}$ in closed form;
Niemeier-projection precise on Mukai-doubled lattice.
\end{itemize}
