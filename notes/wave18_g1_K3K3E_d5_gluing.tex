\section*{Dimension-five Fake-Monster comparison}
\label{sec:k3k3e-d5-gluing}

The Fake-Monster BKM algebra $\gFM$ is the Borcherds algebra attached to
the Lorentzian lattice
\[
  \IIlor=\Leech\oplus U,
  \qquad \operatorname{rk}\IIlor=26 .
\]
Its denominator is the Borcherds product $\PhiFM$ of weight $12$ on
$\mathrm O^{+}(\IIpar)$. The weight is fixed by the input
\[
  \eta(\tau)^{-24}=q^{-1}+24+324q+\cdots
\]
and Borcherds' weight formula:
\[
  \kBKM(\PhiFM)
  =\operatorname{wt}(\PhiFM)
  =\frac{c_{\eta^{-24}}(0)}{2}
  =\frac{24}{2}
  =12 .
\]
This is a denominator invariant of the orthogonal Borcherds lift. It is
separate from compact Hodge supertraces, categorical Euler
characteristics, K3 fibre ranks, and OP/Igusa scalar normalisations.

\subsection*{The compact \texorpdfstring{$K3\times E$}{K3 x E} reference}
\label{subsec:k3e-reference-constants}

Let $Y=K3\times E$. K\"unneth gives
\[
  H^{0,\bullet}(Y)
  =(H^{0,0}(K3)\oplus H^{0,2}(K3))
   \otimes (H^{0,0}(E)\oplus H^{0,1}(E)),
\]
therefore
\[
  \kch(Y)=\sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(Y)
  =(1+1)(1-1)=0 .
\]
The categorical Euler invariant is the same total-space K\"unneth
number:
\[
  \kcat(Y)=\chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\chi(\mathcal O_E)=2\cdot0=0 .
\]
The Heisenberg specialisation is a different construction:
\[
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(Y)=3 .
\]
The primitive Borcherds denominator for the K3 elliptic-genus lift has
\[
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)=\frac{c_1(0)}2=\frac{10}{2}=5,
\]
and the K3 fibre-rank invariant is
\[
  \kfib(K3)=\operatorname{rk}\widetilde H(K3,\Z)=24 .
\]
Thus the compact $K3\times E$ spectrum is
\[
  \bigl(\kcat,\kch,\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}},\kBKM,\kfib\bigr)
  =(0,0,3,5,24),
\]
with the first two entries recording the compact total space and the
last three entries recording distinct specialisations.

The Igusa and OP scalar conventions occupy another layer. The
Gritsenko--Nikulin theta-normalised form $\Delta_5$ has leading
coefficient $64$ in the cusp product, hence the monic product is
\[
  \Dfive=64^{-1}\Delta_5 .
\]
The unnormalised Igusa square and the OP square are
\[
  \Phiun=\Delta_5^2,\qquad
  \PhiOP=\Dfive^2=4096^{-1}\Delta_5^2 .
\]
For primitive reduced $K3\times E$ DT theory in the OP chamber,
\[
  \ZDTred(K3\times E)
  =-(\PhiOP)^{-1}
  =-\Dfive^{-2}
  =-4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}.
\]
The factors $64$, $4096=64^2$, and the OP minus sign are scalar
normalisations. A Hall orientation character, compact Hall source, and
BKM algebra require additional construction.

\subsection*{The dimension-five product}
\label{subsec:k3k3e-product-datum}

Set
\[
  X=K3_1\times K3_2\times E,
  \qquad
  (\Sigma_4,C)=(K3_1\times K3_2,E).
\]
Then $X$ is a compact smooth CY$_5$ with holomorphic volume form
\[
  \Omega_5=\sigma_{K3_1}\wedge\sigma_{K3_2}\wedge dz_E .
\]
K\"unneth gives
\[
  \kcat(X)
  =\chi(\mathcal O_{K3_1})\chi(\mathcal O_{K3_2})\chi(\mathcal O_E)
  =2\cdot2\cdot0=0,
\]
and the same elliptic factor forces the compact Hodge supertrace
$\kch(X)=0$.

The product is a comparison datum. A construction of $\gFM$ requires
more structure than the product geometry supplies. The two K3 factors
give a rank-$48$ Mukai lattice
$\MukaiK^{\oplus2}$ before any quotient. The Fake-Monster denominator
requires the rank-$24$ rootless Leech lattice $\Leech$, hence a proved
comparison must include a primitive rootless lattice operation
\[
  \pi_{\mathrm{Leech}}\colon
  \MukaiK^{\oplus2}\oplus U(E)\dashrightarrow \Leech\oplus U .
\]
This operation is additional to the product geometry: it is a choice or
theorem selecting the unique rootless Niemeier summand.

Since $d=5\geq3$, the specialised chiral output is $E_1$-chiral. An
$E_2$ structure requires passage to a centre, a Drinfeld double, or a
module category with the necessary centre data. The product itself
supplies none of these upgrades.

\subsection*{The positive half}
\label{subsec:k3k3e-positive-half}

Let $\gamma\in H_2(X,\Z)$ be primitive after pushforward to each K3
factor and orthogonal for the two Mukai pairings. The candidate positive
half has the form
\[
  \Yplus(X)=\Heq\bigl(\Mbps(X),\phi_W\bigr),
\]
where the effective moduli problem is the following doubly reduced DT or
Hall stack once the double cosection construction is supplied:
\[
  \Mbps(X)=
  \Mred_{\mathrm{DT}}\bigl(X;\gamma\ \text{bi-primitive on }
  K3_1\times K3_2\bigr).
\]
The two holomorphic symplectic forms give two cosection quotients of the
obstruction sheaf:
\[
  \mathrm{Ob}^{\mathrm{red,red}}
  =
  \ker\!\left(\mathrm{Ob}\longrightarrow
  \mathcal O\oplus\mathcal O\right).
\]
The single K3 reduction is the Maulik--Pandharipande--Thomas and
Oberdieck--Pixton mechanism for $K3\times E$. The double reduction on
$K3_1\times K3_2\times E$ is an open construction: it requires the
doubly reduced virtual class, orientation, elliptic residue, and
compatibility with Hall correspondences.

The visible equivariance is
\[
  G_{\mathrm{eq}}(X)=
  \Cstar_E\times\Auts(K3_1)\times\Auts(K3_2),
\]
with $\Cstar_E$ the formal equivariant parameter at the elliptic cusp
and $\Auts(K3_i)$ finite symplectic K3 automorphism groups of Mukai
type. This visible group is smaller than $\mathrm O^{+}(\IIlor)$.
Orthogonal Fake-Monster symmetry requires the Leech lattice operation
and Borcherds denominator recognition.

\subsection*{Borcherds recognition}
\label{subsec:k3k3e-borcherds-recognition}

The primitive CHL ladder for $K3\times E$ has
\[
  N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\},\qquad
  (c_N(0))=(10,8,6,4,2),
\]
and therefore
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_N)=\frac{c_N(0)}2=(5,4,3,2,1).
\]
These numbers are Borcherds weights. An additive expression in compact
Hodge supertraces and fibre Euler terms is excluded. At $N=1$ the
asserted additive formula gives $5=0+0$, since
$\kch(K3\times E)=0$ and $\chi(\mathcal O_E)=0$.

The Fake-Monster lift has a different input:
\[
  \eta^{-24}\quad\leadsto\quad \PhiFM,\qquad
  \kBKM(\PhiFM)=12 .
\]
It is the Fake-Monster denominator, distinct from the K3 denominator
$\Delta_5$, the Igusa square $\Phiun=\Delta_5^2$, and the OP scalar
square $\PhiOP=\Dfive^2$.

\begin{conjecture}[Fake-Monster character comparison]
\label{conj:fm-character-comparison}
Let $X=K3_1\times K3_2\times E$. Assume the following data.
\begin{enumerate}
\item A doubly reduced DT or Hall positive half $\Yplus(X)$ for
bi-primitive charges.
\item A primitive rootless Leech operation
$\pi_{\mathrm{Leech}}\colon\MukaiK^{\oplus2}\oplus U(E)\dashrightarrow
\IIlor$.
\item A Hall-to-Borcherds recognition theorem identifying the projected
Euler character with the Borcherds product of the $\eta^{-24}$ input.
\end{enumerate}
Then
\[
  \pi_{\mathrm{Leech},*}\,
  \chi_{\Yplus(X)}(q,Z_1,Z_2)
  =
  \frac{1}{\PhiFM(Z)},
  \qquad
  Z=\pi_{\mathrm{Leech}}(Z_1,Z_2,\tau).
\]
\end{conjecture}

The weight check is already determined:
\[
  \operatorname{wt}(\PhiFM^{-1})=-12 .
\]
This differs from the OP scalar inverse
\[
  (\PhiOP)^{-1}=4096\,\Delta_5^{-2},
  \qquad \operatorname{wt}((\PhiOP)^{-1})=-10,
\]
and from the raw square $(\Phiun)^{-1}=\Delta_5^{-2}$. A product of two
$K3\times E$ scalar characters are separated from the Fake-Monster
character by weight, input, lattice, and denominator data.

\subsection*{Open constructions}
\label{subsec:k3k3e-open-constructions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Construct the doubly reduced virtual class on
$K3_1\times K3_2\times E$, including orientation and elliptic residue.
\item Produce a primitive rootless Leech operation from
$\MukaiK^{\oplus2}\oplus U(E)$, or prove that the product Mukai pairing
obstructs it.
\item Prove a Hall or DT denominator comparison whose output is
$\PhiFM^{-1}$, with K3 paramodular scalar characters kept in their
separate source package.
\item Identify the bracket-level BPS algebra, if it exists, with
$\gFM$ on $\IIlor$ after finite Hall--Borcherds recognition and
completion.
\end{enumerate}
