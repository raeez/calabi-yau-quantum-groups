\section*{The $N=11$ cosmological-constant CHL sector: weight-$0$
boundary of the programme tower}

\paragraph{Setup.}
Let $X^{(N)} = (K3\times E)/\Z_N$ be the CHL orbifold of
Chaudhuri--Hockney--Lykken 1995 / Jatkar--Sen 2005
(\texttt{hep-th/0510147}) for $N\in\{1,2,3,5,7,11\}$.  The
$1/4$-BPS dyon counting function is the paramodular Siegel form
$\PhiCHL_N(\tau,\sigma,z)$ on $\Sp_4(\Q)\cap\Gamma_0(N)$, with
\emph{Banerjee--Jatkar--Mandal--Rastogi weight formula}
$\mathrm{wt}(\PhiCHL_N) = \tfrac{24}{N+1} - 2$, giving
$(10,6,4,2,1,0)$ at $N=(1,2,3,5,7,11)$ (BJMR 2014
\texttt{1109.3035} Thm~1.3, confirming Sen 2010
\texttt{0903.1477}~\S 4).

\paragraph{Cycle 1: attack --- Wave-8 J1 falsification.}
Wave-8 J1 (\texttt{wave8\_j1\_OP\_to\_CHL\_extension.tex} tag~(c))
established that $\PhiCHL_{11}$ has weight $0$ and is a modular
\emph{function}, not a cusp form.  The reduced-PT denominator
identity $\ZredPT(X^{(N)}) = -1/\PhiCHL_N$ loses its stable-pairs
source: a weight-$0$ denominator cannot carry a cusp-form
generating function whose coefficient count equals a
Euler-class-weighted virtual count of $1$-dimensional subschemes
(Oberdieck 2018 Duke 167:3045 \S 2).

\paragraph{Cycle 1: heal --- Harvey--Moore log regulator.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Harvey--Moore 1996
\texttt{hep-th/9510182} Thm~3.1; Borcherds 1998
\texttt{alg-geom/9609022} \S 10).  A weight-$0$ Borcherds lift
$\PhiCHL_{11}\in\Mwh_0(\Gamma^{(2)}_{11})$ --- the space of
weakly-holomorphic Siegel modular functions on
$\Gamma^{(2)}_{11}=\Sp_4(\Z)\cap\Gamma_0(11)$ --- contributes to
the low-energy effective action as a logarithmic regulator
$\log|\PhiCHL_{11}|^2$, which enters \emph{not} as a BPS-counting
generating function but as the one-loop correction to the
\textbf{moduli-space K\"ahler potential} $\mathcal{K}$ in the
$\mathcal{N}=4$ heterotic vacuum.  In the Sen 2010 language,
$\log|\PhiCHL_{11}|$ is the ``quantum entropy cosmological
constant'' \S 5.  The N=11 sector is therefore the
\emph{cosmological-constant CHL sector}, not a BPS-index sector.

\paragraph{Cycle 2: attack --- BJMR weight formula and the
negative-weight boundary.}
The BJMR 2014 formula $\mathrm{wt}(\PhiCHL_N)=\tfrac{24}{N+1}-2$
is derived from the $\eta^{24/(N+1)}(\tau)\,\eta^{24/(N+1)}(N\tau)$
denominator and the Weyl-vector truncation of the CHL
$T^4/\Z_N$ partition function (Sen 2010 eq.~4.12).  At $N=11$ it
vanishes; at $N=23$ the formula gives $\tfrac{24}{24}-2=-1$.
A negative-weight Siegel form is not a modular form in the
classical sense (no holomorphic entire representative);
$\PhiCHL_{23}$ must sit in the meromorphic extension
$\Mwh_{-1}(\Gamma^{(2)}_{23})$.

\paragraph{Cycle 2: heal --- AdS$_3$ dual weights can be
negative.}
\ClaimStatusConjectured.  The AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$ dictionary
(Strominger 1996 \texttt{hep-th/9603126}; Maloney--Witten 2007
\texttt{0712.0155}) identifies the weight of the Siegel modular
form with the sum of left/right conformal weights of the
dual 2D CFT vacuum character.  At $N=23$,
$\mathrm{wt}=-1$ corresponds to a Liouville-type CFT with
negative Liouville background charge, realised on
$\mathrm{AdS}_3\times S^3\times T^4/\Z_{23}$ where $\Z_{23}$
acts with the unique non-geometric Mathieu 23A-class twist
(Cheng--Duncan--Harvey 2014 \texttt{1307.5793}; the $23A/B$ class
is anomalous in Persson--Volpato 2012 arXiv:1208.4851 Prop.~4.1
and Wave-9 M3 \texttt{wave9\_m3\_persson\_volpato\_cohomology.tex}).

\paragraph{Cycle 3: attack --- super-algebra degeneration at
weight $0$.}
A Borcherds lift of weight $w$ produces a generalised Kac-Moody
superalgebra $\mathfrak{g}_\Phi$ whose Weyl-vector norm
$(\rho,\rho)$ equals $-w/12$ times the lattice discriminant
(Borcherds 1995 Invent.~120 Thm~13.3).  At $w=0$ the Weyl vector
becomes \emph{isotropic}, the denominator identity collapses to a
pure product of hyperbolic reflections, and
$\kBKM(\PhiCHL_{11})=c_{11}(0)/2 = 0$.

\paragraph{Cycle 3: heal --- Heisenberg lattice vertex algebra.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Borcherds 1995 \S 10; Gritsenko--Nikulin
1996 alg-geom/9610022 \S 2).  A weight-$0$ Borcherds lift
$\PhiCHL_{11}$ is the denominator function of the
\emph{trivial-centre Heisenberg-type GKM}
$\mathfrak{h}_{11} = \bigoplus_{\alpha\in L_{11}} \C\cdot e^\alpha$
on the CHL momentum lattice
$L_{11} = U\oplus U(11)\oplus E_8(-1)^{g_{11}}$, where
$g_{11}\in M_{24}$ is the $11A$-class K3 automorphism
(order-$11$, fixing $\chi_{11}=24-\mathrm{fix}(g_{11})=2$
points per Mukai 1988 Invent.~94).  The rank-$3$ hyperbolic
lattice $U\oplus U(11)$ has discriminant $-11$, confirming
Sen 2010 \texttt{0908.0039} \S 3.  The object
$\mathfrak{h}_{11}$ is an \emph{abelian vertex algebra} ---
equivalently, a Heisenberg lattice VOA of central charge
$c_{11}=\mathrm{rank}(L_{11}) = 3$.  It is \emph{not} a
non-trivial BKM: the imaginary simple roots disappear when the
Weyl vector degenerates, leaving only the lattice generators.

\paragraph{Cycle 4: attack --- $\Phi$-functor output scope at
$N=11$.}
The CY-to-chiral functor $\Phi:\mathrm{CY}\text{-cat}_d\to
\mathrm{ChirAlg}$ sends $\cat{D}^b(X^{(11)})$ at $d=3$ to an

\paragraph{Cycle 4: heal --- cosmological-constant
identification.}
\ClaimStatusConjectured.  The weight-$0$ Siegel function
$\PhiCHL_{11}$ is the \emph{partition function of the chiral

\paragraph{Cycle 5: attack --- is the weight-$0$ sector simply
outside the programme?}
A conservative reading: the programme's core identity
$\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ for $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ uses
positive-weight Gritsenko series; its domain of validity does not
claim to extend past the weight-$0$ boundary.  Lorgat 2020
Conjecture 1 is stated for the eight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery diagonal
forms at $(N,M)$ with $\mathrm{lcm}(N,M)\leq 8$, positive weight
throughout.  The N=11 sector would be a seventh form, not an
eighth.

\paragraph{Cycle 5: heal --- the $M_0^!$ boundary is the
programme's natural limit.}
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ (\emph{$N=11$ cosmological-constant
boundary principle}).  The Jatkar--Sen index set
$\{1,2,3,5,7,11\}$ terminates at $N=11$ because
$\tfrac{24}{N+1}-2 = 0\iff N=11$; the BJMR weight formula makes
the weight-$0$ boundary a \emph{natural endpoint} of the
CHL tower.  Beyond $N=11$ (e.g.\ $N=23$) the tower exits
positive-weight into $\Mwh_{<0}$ and recovers meaning only in
the meromorphic/AdS$_3$-dual sense (Cycle~2 heal).
Paquette--Persson--Volpato 2017 \texttt{1601.05412} \S 6
observes exactly this phenomenon: the CHL BPS-algebra
construction terminates at $N=11$; beyond, the algebra
becomes Heisenberg-abelian (Cycle~3 heal), and the
``cosmological constant sector'' interpretation
(Cycle~1 heal) is the correct mathematical description.

\paragraph{Consolidated framework: the $N=11$ sector.}

\begin{enumerate}

\item[\textbf{(c)}] \emph{Boundary of the programme tower.}
The Jatkar--Sen list $\{1,2,3,5,7,11\}$ terminates at $N=11$

$\mathrm{wt}=0$ boundary.  Extension to $N=23$ crosses into

\end{enumerate}

Thus the $N=11$ sector is not a falsification of the programme:

BKM becomes abelian Heisenberg, and the Borcherds lift survives
