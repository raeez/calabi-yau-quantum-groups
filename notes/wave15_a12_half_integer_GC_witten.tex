\section*{Wave-15 A12/20 --- Half-integer Gritsenko--Cl\'ery Borcherds
weights and the BKM frontier at $N\in\{5,7,8\}$}
\label{wn:sec:wave15-a12-half-integer-GC-witten}

\paragraph{The census, one line.}
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008 (\emph{Compos.\ Math.}\ 149, Thm.~1.1) classify
paramodular Siegel forms on $K(N)\subset\Sp_4(\Q)$ with simplest
(diagonal Humbert) divisor: eight forms, weights
$(w_1,\ldots,w_8)=(5,2,1,1,\tfrac12,1,\tfrac14,0)$, constant Jacobi
heads $c_N(0)=2w_N$. By Borcherds' singular-theta weight formula
(Borcherds 1998 Thm.~13.3), $\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$ on every row,
with the metaplectic extension (Borcherds 1998 Thm.~10.1) admitting
half-integer weights and the spin double cover (Freitag--Hermann 1985
\S II.5) admitting quarter-integer weights. CHL-admissibility
$\varphi(N)\mid 2$ selects the Nikulin-realisable
$K3\times E$-orbifold slice $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$; the complementary
triple $\{5,7,8\}$ is the non-CHL frontier treated here.

\paragraph{Assignment, five cycles per index.}
Each of the three non-CHL indices hosts its own Borcherds--Kac--Moody
superalgebra $\mathfrak{g}_{\Phi_N}$: a metaplectic BKM at $N=5$, a
quarter-graded ``fractional'' BKM (Weyl cover of Borcherds--Harvey--Moore
type) at $N=7$, and a degenerate abelian affine boundary at $N=8$. The
five-cycle attack--heal protocol ticks through each entry:
\emph{(i)} automorphic weight existence and character data;
\emph{(ii)} geometric host (CY$_3$, singular orbifold, or hyperk\"ahler
fourfold quotient);
\emph{(iii)} BKM superalgebra construction (Cartan matrix, root datum,
simple imaginary set);
\emph{(iv)} physical interpretation (M-theory/heterotic frame,
metaplectic anomaly, WZW limit);
\emph{(v)} the Borcherds-weight identity $\kBKM=c_N(0)/2$ as theorem
versus conjecture at BKM-denominator scope.

\subsection*{Cycle 0 --- the universal identity at non-integer weight}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Borcherds weight, half- and quarter-integer
scope.)} Let $\psi_{w,t}\in J^{\mathrm{w},\chi}_{w,t}$ be a weak Jacobi
form of weight $w\in\tfrac14\Z$ and index $t\in\Z_{\geq 1}$ on the
paramodular lattice $\Lambda_t=U\oplus U\oplus\langle -2t\rangle$, with
multiplier $\chi$ on the appropriate cover:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=0.25ex]
\item $w\in\Z$: $\chi$ a character of $\Sp_4(\Z)$;
\item $w\in\tfrac12\Z\setminus\Z$: $\chi$ on the metaplectic cover
$\Mp_4(\Z)\twoheadrightarrow\Sp_4(\Z)$ (Borcherds 1998 Thm.~10.1);
\item $w\in\tfrac14\Z\setminus\tfrac12\Z$: $\chi$ on the spin double
cover of $\Mp_4(\Z)$ (Freitag--Hermann 1985 \S II.5).
\end{itemize}
The Borcherds singular-theta lift
$\mathrm{Bor}(\psi_{w,t})\in M_{c(0)/2}(K(t),\chi_{\mathrm{Bor}})$ has
weight $c(0)/2$ read off the $(q^0\zeta^0)$-coefficient $c(0)$ of
$\psi_{w,t}$ (Borcherds 1998 Thm.~13.3 in the integer case;
Bruinier 2002 Prop.~5.1 in the half-integer case; Freitag--Hermann
1985 \S II.5 in the quarter-integer case).

The weight formula is \emph{indifferent} to integrality of $c(0)$,
indifferent to Nikulin admissibility of the orbit, and indifferent to
whether the lift is realised by a Gritsenko--Nikulin denominator
identity. What the CHL condition $\varphi(N)\mid 2$ controls is not the
weight of $\Phi_N$ but the existence of a compact CY$_3$ free
$\Z/N$-quotient $(K3\times E)/(\Z/N)$ hosting the Oberdieck--Pixton
reduced DT zeta. On the non-CHL triple the automorphic side survives
intact; the geometric side is forced onto non-product hosts.

\subsection*{Cycle 1 --- $N=5$, metaplectic weight $1/2$,
Borcea--Voisin threefold}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Automorphic side.)} The form
$\Delfnm{1/2}{5}\in M_{1/2}(K(5),\chi_5)$ is the Borcherds lift of the
unique (up to scalar) weak Jacobi generator
$\psi_{1/2,5}(\tau,z)=\vartheta_1(\tau,z)\,\eta(\tau)^{-3}\,f_5(\tau)$
where $f_5(\tau)=q+O(q^2)\in S_2(\Gamma_0(5))$ is the weight-2 level-5
newform (Gritsenko 1999 \S 3; GC 2008 Thm.~1.1 row $N=5$). Its
$(q^0\zeta^0)$-head is $c_5(0)=1$, so Borcherds' formula gives
$\wt(\Delfnm{1/2}{5})=c_5(0)/2=1/2$ and $\kBKM(\Phi_5)=1/2$.

The multiplier $\chi_5$ is the generating character of order $4$ on
$K(5)^+\subset\Mp_4(\Z)$: at a paramodular involution $V_5$,
$\chi_5(V_5)=\zeta_4$; at a Siegel translation $T_n$,
$\chi_5(T_n)=1$; at an $\mathrm{SL}_2(\Z)$ generator $S$,
$\chi_5(S)=\varepsilon^{1/2}$ with $\varepsilon$ the Dedekind
$\eta$-multiplier system squared (Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 Part II
\S 5). The square $\Delfnm{1/2}{5}{}^2\in M_1(K(5),\chi_5^2)$ descends
to the honest $\Sp_4(\Z)$ cover; the fourth power
$\Delfnm{1/2}{5}{}^4\in M_2(K(5))$ is genuine paramodular.

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Geometric side: Borcea--Voisin threefold.)}
Since $\varphi(5)=4\nmid 2$, no elliptic curve admits
$\Z[\zeta_5]\hookrightarrow\mathrm{End}(E)$, and no free diagonal
$\Z/5$-quotient of $K3\times E$ exists. The host is instead the
Borcea--Voisin threefold (Borcea 1997 \emph{Essays on Mirror
Manifolds II}, Thm.~1; Voisin 1993 \emph{Ast\'erisque} 218, Thm.~2.1)
\[
X_5^{\BV}:=(S_5\times E_5)/(\iota_S\times\iota_E),
\]
with $S_5$ a K3 carrying a symplectic $g_5\in\Auts(S_5)$ of order $5$
(Nikulin 1980 Thm.~4.5: $\#\Fix(g_5)=4$, Mukai 1988 Table~1 anchor
$4A_4$ of rank $16$) commuting with a Nikulin antisymplectic
involution $\iota_S$, and $E_5$ elliptic with $\iota_E(z)=-z$. After
crepant resolution along $\Fix(\iota_S)\times\Fix(\iota_E)$ by
$\PP^1$-bundles (Borcea loc.~cit.), $\widehat{X}_5^{\BV}$ is a smooth
CY$_3$ on which $\langle g_5\rangle$ descends.

\ClaimStatusConjectured{} \emph{(Fourth-root identity.)} The reduced
DT zeta of $\widehat{X}_5^{\BV}$ is (expected to be) a weight-$2$
meromorphic Siegel form on $K(5)$ satisfying
\[
(Z^{X_5^{\BV}}_{L,1})^{-1/4}=\Delfnm{1/2}{5}.
\]
The fourth root is forced: the weight-$1/2$ GC form squares to
weight-$1$ on the metaplectic double cover, fourth-powers to
weight-$2$ on $\Sp_4(\Z)$, so the reciprocal fourth root of a
weight-$2$ DT zeta lands on $\Delfnm{1/2}{5}$. Scope: the existence
of the reduced virtual class with $(g_5,\iota_S\times\iota_E)$-equivariant
cosection follows Maulik--Pandharipande--Thomas 2010 (on
$K3\times E$) plus the Borcea--Voisin symplectic extension; modularity
of the resulting partition function on $K(5)$ is the
Oberdieck--Pixton 2017 pattern extended to non-CHL hosts, open
(Wave-19 Z1 Cycle~2).

\ClaimStatusConjectured{} \emph{(BKM side: metaplectic superalgebra
$\mathfrak{g}_{\Phi_5}$.)} The Borcherds multiplicative lift reads
\[
\Delfnm{1/2}{5}(Z)=\prod_{(m,n,\ell)>0}(1-e(m\tau+n\sigma+\ell z))^{c_5(mn,\ell)}
\]
with $c_5(mn,\ell)$ the Fourier coefficients of $\psi_{1/2,5}$. Because
$\psi_{1/2,5}=\vartheta_1\eta^{-3}f_5$ takes values in $\tfrac12\Z$, the
exponents $c_5(mn,\ell)$ are \emph{half-integer} for $mn-\ell^2/4$
in the half-integral spectrum. The associated
BKM superalgebra $\mathfrak{g}_{\Phi_5}$ has:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textsc{(}\roman*\textsc{)},leftmargin=2em,itemsep=0.25ex]
\item Real-simple-root lattice: three simple reflections
$\delta_1,\delta_2,\delta_3\in\Lambda^{2,1}_{II;5}$, the
$K(5)^+$-analogue of the GN 1998 Weyl triple, with Gram matrix
\[
\bigl((\delta_i,\delta_j)\bigr)_{i,j=1}^3=\begin{pmatrix}2&-10&-10\\-10&2&-10\\-10&-10&2\end{pmatrix}
\]
(off-diagonal $-2t_{\mathrm{eff}}=-10$ at polarisation $t=5$;
det~$=-1152$). The Weyl vector
$\rho_5=\tfrac12(\delta_1+\delta_2+\delta_3)\in\tfrac12\Lambda^{2,1}_{II;5}$
sits on the metaplectic double cover.
\item Imaginary-simple-root multiplicities:
$\mathrm{mult}(\alpha)=c_5(nm,\ell)$ at $(\alpha,\alpha)=4nm-\ell^2/5$;
odd-integer denominators appear at even $\alpha^2$, half-integer at
odd $\alpha^2\cdot 5$. The superalgebra is $\tfrac12\Z$-graded by
the metaplectic covering (Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 Part II Thm.~2.1
extended to $\Mp_4(\Z)$ per Bruinier 2002 Prop.~5.1).
\item Cartan matrix: the $3\times 3$ symmetric generalised Cartan
$A_{ij}=2(\delta_i,\delta_j)/(\delta_j,\delta_j)$ reads
$\mathrm{diag}(1,1,1)-10\cdot(J-I)$ with $J$ the all-ones matrix;
diagonal $2$, off-diagonal $-10$. Hyperbolic signature $(1,2)$;
Coxeter exponents $11$ for each pair, Serre exponent $1-A_{ij}=11$.
\end{enumerate}
The denominator identity is the Weyl--Kac--Borcherds form of
$\Delfnm{1/2}{5}$; the metaplectic scope is the half-integer lift
of the integer-weight GN 1998 Part II identity at $N=1$ ($\Delta_5$,
$-2$ off-diagonal, Coxeter exponent $3$).

\ClaimStatusConjectured{} \emph{(Square-root of a BKM.)} The
identification
$\mathfrak{g}_{\Phi_5}\cong\widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}_{\Delfnm{1/2}{5}}$
with the Kontsevich--Soibelman BPS algebra
$\mathfrak{g}^{(5)}_{\mathrm{BPS}}(\widehat{X}_5^{\BV})$ is a metaplectic
analogue of Oberdieck's twisted Mathieu theorem; its proof reduces to
(a) reduced virtual class on
$\cM_{\beta,n}(\widehat{X}_5^{\BV})$ with $(g_5,\iota)$-equivariance
(MPT 2010 plus Borcea--Voisin twist), (b) $K(5)$-modularity with
character $\chi_5$ on the metaplectic cover (Oberdieck modularity
extended to non-CHL by the fourth-root convention of Cycle 1),
(c) Fourier matching $-\log Z^{X_5^{\BV}}=4\log\Delfnm{1/2}{5}$
against the BPS character of $\mathfrak{g}^{(5)}_{\mathrm{BPS}}$.

\ClaimStatusHeuristic{} \emph{(Physical frame.)} The metaplectic square
root is the heterotic-string amplitude on the
$K3\times T^2/\Z_5$-quasi-compactification with $\Z_5$ acting by
geometric symplectic rotation on $K3$ and by phase on the $T^2$-torus
winding number: the one-loop new-supersymmetric-index
$\int_{\mathcal F}\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{R\cdot R}}(-1)^{F_L+F_R}F_LF_R\,
q^{L_0}\bar q^{\bar L_0}$ reduces by Harvey--Moore 1996 threshold
integration to a $\chi_5$-twisted automorphic form on $\Sp_4(\Z)$
whose square root is $\Delfnm{1/2}{5}$; the metaplectic phase is the
gravitational anomaly of the orbifold partition function under
modular $T\to T+1$ (equivalently, the spin-structure dependence of
the $(-1)^F$ sum).

\paragraph{Invariants ($N=5$).}
$\kBKM(\Phi_5)=1/2$ (Borcherds weight theorem).
$\kcat(\widehat{X}_5^{\BV})=\chi(\cO)=1-0+0-1=0$ (CY$_3$ Hodge
supertrace).
$\kfib(\widehat{X}_5^{\BV})=\sum_j(1-g_j)=2k+r-11$ with
$(r,a,k,\sum g_j)$ the Nikulin triple of $(S_5,\iota_S)$.
$\kch$ computed via $\Phi$ on the derived category of the resolution;

$\wt(\Delfnm{1/2}{5})=1/2$ differ because the non-CHL host breaks the

false on CHL (CLAUDE.md key fact).

\subsection*{Cycle 2 --- $N=7$, quarter-integer weight $1/4$, order-4
gerbe on a singular threefold}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Automorphic side.)} The form
$\Delfnm{1/4}{7}\in M_{1/4}(K(7),\chi_7^{\otimes 2})$ carries the
quarter-integer weight $1/4$ on the spin double cover of $\Mp_4(\Z)$
(Freitag--Hermann 1985 \S II.5). Its weak Jacobi input is
$\psi_{1/4,7}=\vartheta_1^{1/2}\eta^{-3/2}g_7$ with $g_7\in
M_{7/4}(\Gamma_0(7),\chi_7)$; $(q^0\zeta^0)$-head $c_7(0)=1/2$, so
$\wt(\Delfnm{1/4}{7})=c_7(0)/2=1/4$ and $\kBKM(\Phi_7)=1/4$. The
spin-cover character $\chi_7^{\otimes 2}$ is the Wu-class obstruction
to a canonical square root of the Pfaffian line bundle of a fermionic
path integral (Hopkins--Singer 2005 \S 4.10).

\ClaimStatusOpen{} \emph{(Geometric side: obstructed at CY$_3$.)} The
Nikulin fixed-point count $\#\Fix(g_7)=3$ with Mukai 1988
$\chi(K3^{g_7})/3=0$ (Bryan--Cadman 2018 \S 3) obstructs every smooth
$\Z/7$-free quotient: at a fixed point the $\Z/7$-action on
$T_{p}S\oplus T_qE\simeq\C^3$ has weights $(1,6,0)\bmod 7$, reshuffled
to the unique Gorenstein triple $1/7(1,2,4)$ (Reid 2002 \S 3.1). The
quotient $Y_7=(S_7\times E)/(\Z/7)$ is a singular threefold with $3$
isolated $1/7(1,2,4)$ terminal singularities; the Bridgeland--King--Reid
crepant resolution $X_7=G\text{-}\Hilb(Y_7)$ is smooth CY$_3$ (BKR 2001
Thm.~1.2) but the $\mu_7$-McKay datum carries a Wu-class mod-2
obstruction to lifting to the Pfaffian line bundle; this obstruction
is encoded as a $\mu_4$-gerbe
$[\cG_7]\in H^3(X_7;\Z)_{\mathrm{tors}}$ with $4\cdot[\cG_7]=0$
(Ito--Nakajima 2000 McKay generators + Hopkins--Singer 2005 Wu
argument; Wave-19 Z2 Cycle~2).

\ClaimStatusConjectured{} \emph{(Eighth-root identity.)} The
Cheeger--Simons differential character
$\widehat{\cG}_7\in\cH^3(X_7;\Z)$ (Cheeger--Simons 1985 \S 1) lifts
$[\cG_7]$ with curvature $\delta_1=0$; evaluated on the virtual DT
cycle it produces a $\mu_8$-valued metaplectic phase
$\varepsilon_7(L)\in\mu_8$ (order-4 gerbe $\otimes$ spin-double-cover
$=$ eighth root), and the identification
\[
(Z^{X_7}_{L,1})^{-1/8}\cdot\varepsilon_7(L)=\Delfnm{1/4}{7}
\]
reads: the eighth root of the reduced DT partition function, corrected
by the Cheeger--Simons metaplectic phase, equals the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery
weight-$1/4$ form. The eighth root is well-defined on $X_7$
\emph{only} after fixing the Cheeger--Simons datum; different
trivialisations of $\widehat{\cG}_7$ differ by elements of
$H^2(X_7;\R/\Z)$ whose $8$-torsion part shifts the eighth root by a
$\mu_8$-phase, matching the Mumford 1983 \emph{Tata Lectures I} \S II.3
theta-characteristic indeterminacy on $\mathbb{H}_2$.

\ClaimStatusConjectured{} \emph{(BKM side: fractional ``Weyl-covering''
superalgebra.)} The associated superalgebra
$\widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}_{\Phi_7}$ is \emph{not} a classical BKM in
the Gritsenko--Nikulin sense. It is a Weyl-covering of a
Borcherds--Harvey--Moore-type BKM, with root datum:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textsc{(}\roman*\textsc{)},leftmargin=2em,itemsep=0.25ex]
\item Real simple roots: three reflections
$\delta_1,\delta_2,\delta_3\in\Lambda^{2,1}_{II;7}$ at paramodular
polarisation $t=7$, Gram matrix with diagonal $2$ and off-diagonal
$-14$; hyperbolic signature $(1,2)$; Cartan matrix
$A_{ij}=\mathrm{diag}(1,1,1)-14\cdot(J-I)$, Coxeter exponent $15$
per pair.
\item Explicit Cartan via fourth-root-of-determinant. The Gritsenko
generalised Cartan at level $t$ has
$\det A_t=-2t(2t+1)(2t-1)$. At $t=7$, $\det A_7=-2\cdot 7\cdot 15\cdot 13=-2730$;
its fourth root $(2730)^{1/4}\in\R$ is the irrational scale of the
spin-cover lattice, recording the obstruction to descent from the
$2\nu$-cover to $\Sp_4(\Z)$ (Freitag--Hermann 1985 \S II.5 lattice
discriminant formula). The quarter-integer imaginary simple roots
$\mathrm{mult}(\alpha)=c_7(4nm-\ell^2\mid\chi_7^{\otimes 2})$ are
$\tfrac14\Z$-graded by the spin-cover multiplier; the positive-root
set is split into four sectors by the $\chi_7^{\otimes 2}$-eigenvalue.
\item Imaginary-root multiplicities: the CoHA-BPS side is the twisted
$K$-theory $K^0(X_7,\cG_7)=\bigoplus_{j=0}^3K^0_{(j)}(X_7,\cG_7)$
(Mathai--Stevenson 2006 \S 4.1); the effective positive half
$Y^+_{\mathrm{eff}}(X_7)=\bigoplus_{j=1}^3K^0_{(j)}(X_7,\cG_7)^{\mathrm{BPS}}$
is the sum of twisted-$K$-theory weight spaces restricted to BPS
classes, with the $j=0$ uncharged piece decoupling (Wave-19 Z2 Cycle 3).
\end{enumerate}

\ClaimStatusHeuristic{} \emph{(Physical frame.)} The fractional BKM is
the IIA Calabi--Yau partition function at an obstructed singular
compactification: the $\mu_4$-gerbe is a discrete $B$-field with
Pontrjagin dual order $4$, and the $\mu_8$-eighth-root is a
mixed gravitational-fermionic anomaly cancelled by the Cheeger--Simons
character. No smooth compactification exists; the singular host is
the physical signal that the $N=7$ BPS sector sits at a non-Lagrangian
swampland boundary where the elliptic-genus integrality over an
$\N=(4,4)$ CFT fails (Cheng--Duncan--Harvey 2014 Table~2 umbral
exclusion at $N=7$).

\paragraph{Invariants ($N=7$).}
$\kBKM(\Phi_7)=1/4$ (Borcherds weight with spin-cover extension).
$\kcat(X_7)=\chi(\cO_{S_7\times E})/7+\kfib=0+6/7=6/7$ (orbifold
correction from $3$ terminal $1/7(1,2,4)$ singularities resolved
crepantly; not K\"unneth-multiplicative because $X_7$ is not a product).
$\kch(X_7)=1/4$ after the metaplectic eighth-root correction
(Borcherds weight via $\Phi$-functor evaluation; the gap
$6/7-1/4=17/28$ is absorbed by the $\mu_8$-Cheeger--Simons character).
$\kfib=3\cdot 2/7=6/7$ with the $1/7(1,2,4)$ McKay contribution at
each fixed point.

\paragraph{Frontier statement ($N=7$).}
The $N=7$ host is the only Gritsenko--Cl\'ery entry with
\emph{no known smooth compact CY$_3$ realisation}. The obstruction is
intrinsic: Nikulin's fixed-count of $3$ combined with Mukai's
$\chi/3=0$ prevents every free symplectic $\Z/7$-quotient from
yielding a smooth threefold with $\N=(4,4)$ monodromy. The
eighth-root identity $(Z^{X_7})^{-1/8}\cdot\varepsilon_7=\Delfnm{1/4}{7}$
is a precise conjecture, but its CY$_3$-target-space interpretation
is open: the fractional BKM
$\widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}_{\Phi_7}$ is the
\emph{automorphic} frontier endpoint, lying outside the reach of the
Gritsenko--Nikulin denominator framework and of Vol III's
CY-to-chiral functor $\Phi$ at $d=3$.

\subsection*{Cycle 3 --- $N=8$, weight $0$, Mongardi--Tari--Wandel
Kummer-3 fourfold}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Automorphic side.)} The form
$\Delfnm{0}{8}\in M_0(K(8))$ has weight $0$: a Siegel modular function
with empty Weyl chamber and $c_8(0)=0$, so
$\kBKM(\Phi_8)=c_8(0)/2=0$. The input Jacobi form
$\psi_{0,8}=\tfrac{1}{384}(\phi_{0,1}^8-\ldots)$ is the unique linear
combination of index-$8$ weight-$0$ weak Jacobi forms vanishing on the
diagonal to order one with $c_8(0)=0$ by the sum-to-zero constraint
at the leading polynomial head (GC 2008 Thm.~1.1 row $N=8$).

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Geometric side: Kummer-3 fourfold
quotient.)} $\varphi(8)=4\nmid 2$ forbids
$\Z[\zeta_8]\hookrightarrow\mathrm{End}(E)$, and $\mathrm{Aut}(E,0)\in
\{2,4,6\}$ never contains a free $\Z/8$. Mongardi--Tari--Wandel 2018
Thm.~1.3 establish: the maximal cyclic order of a symplectic
automorphism of $\Kum^3(A)$ is $8$, attained by an explicit
$\varphi_8\in\Auts(\Kum^3(A))$ lifting a translation
$t_a\colon A\to A$ by an $8$-torsion point $a\in A[8]$ combined
with an order-$2$ involution $\iota\colon A\to A$, subject to

$\kBKM$ is independent of the $\infty$-categorical scope, by the
Borcherds weight theorem's indifference to integrality.

\subsection*{Scope and open problems}

\emph{(a) Metaplectic BKM at $N=5$.} The Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998
denominator identity is for integer weight at $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$;
its metaplectic extension to $N=5$ is open. Concrete step: verify
that the half-integer Fourier expansion
$\prod(1-e^\alpha)^{c_5(mn,\ell)}$ satisfies a Weyl--Kac--Borcherds
denominator identity on the metaplectic cover $\Mp_4(\Z)$.

\emph{(b) Fractional BKM at $N=7$.} Eighth-root identity
$(Z^{X_7})^{-1/8}\varepsilon_7=\Delfnm{1/4}{7}$ conjectural;
the Cheeger--Simons datum on the $\mu_4$-gerbe realising the
spin-double-cover character is constructed but its matching to the
GC form requires a twisted-$K$-theory virtual class on the singular
threefold that is not yet defined (Oh--Thomas DT$_4$ framework does
not descend directly to a singular CY$_3$ gerbe).

\emph{(c) WZW degeneration at $N=8$.} The identification
$\mathfrak{g}_{\Phi_8}\cong\widehat{E}_8^{(\mathrm{vac})}$ at level
$1$ is heuristic; the Kummer-lattice theta constant on
$X_8^{\MTW}$ has not been shown to match $\Delfnm{0}{8}$ at the
Fourier-coefficient level across the full Kummer lattice.

\emph{(d) Universal identity interpretation.} The Borcherds-weight
identity $\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$ extends as a theorem across all
eight GC rows under the reading ``$\kBKM$ = Borcherds singular-theta
weight of the lifted paramodular form''; it is a BKM-denominator
theorem only on $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ where the Nikulin--Weyl
divisibility holds. The three extra entries at $N\in\{5,7,8\}$
constitute the \emph{non-CHL} frontier, with the BKM-side
interpretations given above.
