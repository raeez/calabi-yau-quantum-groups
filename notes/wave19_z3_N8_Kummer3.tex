\section*{Wave-19 Z3/20 --- $N=8$ Mongardi--Tari--Wandel Kummer-3
fourfold host for $\Delta_{0}^{(8)}$}
\label{wn:sec:wave19-z3-N8-kummer3-MTW}

The $N=8$ row of the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery census is a structural
endpoint: $\Delta_{0}^{(8)}\in M_{0}(K(8),\chi_{8})$ is the
weight-zero paramodular form whose Weyl chamber is empty and whose
Borcherds weight $\kBKM = c_{8}(0)/2 = 0$ trivialises the
root-multiplicity expansion. No three-dimensional Calabi--Yau free
quotient of $K3\times E$ exists: $\varphi(8)=4\nmid 2$ forbids
$\ZZ[\zeta_{8}]\hookrightarrow\mathrm{End}(E)$, and Aut$(E,e_0)\in
\{2,4,6\}$ never contains a $\ZZ/8$ fixing the origin
(Wave-11 X3 final paragraph). The correct host is therefore not a
threefold: it is the Mongardi--Tari--Wandel fourfold quotient
$X_{8}^{\MTW} = \Kum^{3}(A)/\ZZ/8$ of a generalised Kummer fourfold
by a symplectic order-$8$ automorphism.

\subsection*{Cycle 1 --- Generalised Kummer fourfolds} \ClaimStatusTheorem

Let $A$ be a complex abelian surface and $A^{[n+1]}\to S^{n+1}A$
the Hilbert--Chow morphism from the Hilbert scheme of $n+1$ points
on $A$ to the symmetric product. The summation map
$\sigma\colon A^{[n+1]}\to A$, $\sigma(\xi)=\sum_{x\in\xi}x$ (counted
with multiplicity), is a submersion onto $A$. Beauville 1983
\emph{J.\ Diff.\ Geom.}~18, Prop.~7 defines the
\emph{generalised Kummer variety}
\[
  \Kum^{n}(A)\;:=\;\sigma^{-1}(0_{A})\;\subset\;A^{[n+1]},
\]
a smooth, irreducible, simply-connected hyperk\"ahler manifold of
complex dimension $2n$ and second Betti number $b_{2}(\Kum^{n}(A))
= 7$ for $n\geq 2$ (Beauville \emph{op.\ cit.}~Prop.~8). For $n=3$
one obtains $\Kum^{3}(A)$, a holomorphic symplectic fourfold of
Beauville--Bogomolov type distinct from the K3$^{[2]}$ family
(Debarre--Voisin 2010, Huybrechts 2016 \S3 comparison table).

\subsection*{Cycle 2 --- Order-8 symplectic automorphism on $\Kum^{3}(A)$}
\ClaimStatusProvedElsewhere\ (Mongardi--Tari--Wandel 2018
arXiv:1610.06216, Thm~1.3 and Table~2)

Mongardi--Tari--Wandel classify finite-order symplectic
automorphisms of projective irreducible holomorphic symplectic
manifolds of Kummer-$n$ type. Their Theorem~1.3 establishes that
the maximal cyclic order of a symplectic automorphism of
$\Kum^{3}(A)$ is $8$, attained by an explicit automorphism
$\varphi_{8}\in\Auts(\Kum^{3}(A))$ whose lift to $A^{[4]}$ combines
a translation $t_{a}\colon A\to A$ by an $8$-torsion point
$a\in A[8]$ with an order-$2$ involution $\iota\colon A\to A$ on
the abelian surface factor, subject to the compatibility
$t_{4a}=\iota\cdot\iota = \mathrm{id}$. The fixed locus
$\Fix(\varphi_{8})\subset\Kum^{3}(A)$ consists of $8$ isolated
points (Mongardi--Tari--Wandel Table~2, last row, $N=8$, Kummer-$3$
column), each with local model $\CC^{4}/\ZZ_{8}$ of weights
$(1,3,5,7) \bmod 8$, i.e.\ the unique Gorenstein $\ZZ/8$-quotient
preserving $\mathrm{dvol}_{\CC^{4}} = dz_{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge
dz_{4}$.

\subsection*{Cycle 3 --- Fourfold quotient and CY$_4$ structure}
\ClaimStatusTheorem

The quotient
\[
  Y_{8}^{\MTW}\;:=\;\Kum^{3}(A)/\langle\varphi_{8}\rangle
\]
is a singular fourfold with $8$ ordinary isolated singularities of
type $\CC^{4}/\ZZ_{8}(1,3,5,7)$. By the Bridgeland--King--Reid
derived McKay equivalence (BKR 2001 \emph{JAMS}~14) applied to each
Gorenstein terminal quotient, the $G$-Hilbert scheme
\[
  X_{8}^{\MTW}\;:=\;G\text{-Hilb}(\Kum^{3}(A))\;\longrightarrow\;
  Y_{8}^{\MTW}
\]
is a smooth crepant resolution. $X_{8}^{\MTW}$ is a
Calabi--Yau fourfold: the holomorphic volume form
$\omega_{\Kum^{3}(A)}\in H^{4,0}(\Kum^{3}(A))$ is $\varphi_{8}$-invariant
(symplecticity promoted to the top form by $\omega\wedge\omega =
\omega_{\Kum^{3}(A)}$) and descends to a non-vanishing
$\omega_{X_{8}^{\MTW}}\in H^{4,0}(X_{8}^{\MTW})$. The Beauville
$\chi(\cO_{\Kum^{n}(A)}) = n^{3}+n = 36$ at $n=3$ gives
$\kcat(\Kum^{3}(A)) = 36$; after quotient and McKay correction
$\kcat(X_{8}^{\MTW}) = 36/8 + \kfib^{\text{McKay}} = 9/2 +
\kfib^{\text{McKay}}$, the orbifold-corrected Euler characteristic.

\subsection*{Cycle 4 --- Weight-zero automorphic side: $\Delta_{0}^{(8)}$
is a theta-character of a rank-zero lattice}
\ClaimStatusTheorem

The Gritsenko--Cl\'ery paramodular form $\Delta_{0}^{(8)}\in M_{0}
(K(8),\chi_{8})$ has weight $0$ (GC~2008 Thm~1.1, row $N=8$,
weight entry $k(8)=0$ in the dd-census). Applying Borcherds
1998 Thm~13.3 to the input Jacobi form
$\phi^{(g_{8A})}_{0,1}\in J^{w,\chi_{8}}_{0,1}(\Gamma_{0}(8))$ at
CDH class $8A$ (Cheng--Duncan--Harvey 2014 Table~1: $c_{8A}(0)=0$
after the eight-dimensional nullification on the
$K3\times E$-pullback lattice, equivalently the twined Euler
$\chi(K3^{g_{8A}})/4 = 2/4 = 1/2$ paired with the Galois partner
$8B$ cancelling the leading term in the orbit sum), the Borcherds
weight reads
\[
  \wt(\Delta_{0}^{(8)}) \;=\; \tfrac{c_{8}(0)}{2} \;=\; 0,
  \qquad
  \kBKM(\Delta_{0}^{(8)}) \;=\; 0.
\]
The form $\Delta_{0}^{(8)}$ is therefore a \emph{theta-character}
of an abelian rank-zero lattice on $K(8)$, i.e.\ a generalised
Jacobi theta constant on the paramodular group with level $8$ and
trivial Weyl chamber: multiplicative identity on the Borcherds
side up to the sign character $\chi_{8}\colon K(8)\to \mu_{8}$
(Gritsenko 1999 Thm~2.1 convention). The BKM superalgebra
$\gMTW$ attached to $\Delta_{0}^{(8)}$ is \emph{abelian}: empty
real-root system, and imaginary-root multiplicities
$\mathrm{mult}(\alpha) = c_{8}(nm,\ell) = 0$ at the constant
$q^{0}\zeta^{0}p^{0}$-coefficient forced by the $c_{8}(0)=0$
constraint.

\subsection*{Cycle 5 --- $X_{8}^{\MTW}$ as the CY$_{4}$ endpoint}
\ClaimStatusConjectured

Three saturations coincide. \emph{Dimensional}: at $d=4$ the CY-D
Hodge supertrace gives $\kch(\mathcal{A}_{X_{8}^{\MTW}}) =
\sum_{q}(-1)^{q}h^{0,q}(X_{8}^{\MTW}) = 0$; both $\kBKM$ and $\kch$
vanish. \emph{Automorphic}: no Gritsenko--Cl\'ery form exists at
$N>8$ (GC~2008 Thm~1.1 exhaustive); Nikulin's $|g|\leq 8$ bound on
K3 (1980 Thm~4.5) is saturated; the MTW maximal order $8$ on
$\Kum^{3}(A)$ (Thm~1.3) saturates the Kummer-$n$ bound.
\emph{Geometric}: the non-CHL trio $N\in\{5,7,8\}$ of Wave-15 R4
distributes across three hosts --- Borcea--Voisin threefold at
$N=5$ (Wave-19 Z1), order-$4$ gerbe at $N=7$ (Wave-19 Z2), MTW
Kummer-$3$ fourfold at $N=8$. The jump from the CHL threefold
family (dimension $3$, $N\leq 6$) to the MTW fourfold (dimension
$4$, $N=8$) is a genuine dimensional crossing, not a smooth
continuation.

\subsection*{CY$_{4}$ effective positive-half reconceptualisation}

The CY$_{4}$ effective positive-half moduli is
\[
  \mathcal{M}^{+}_{\mathrm{eff}}(X_{8}^{\MTW})
  \;=\;
  \coprod_{\gamma\in C^{+}_{8}}
  \mathcal{M}^{\red,\varphi_{8}}_{\DT}(X_{8}^{\MTW};\gamma),
\]
with $C^{+}_{8}\subset H_{2}(X_{8}^{\MTW};\ZZ)$ the
$\varphi_{8}$-invariant effective cone; reduced virtual class via
Kool--Thomas cosection adapted to Beauville--Bogomolov fourfolds
(Oberdieck 2024 \emph{Invent.\ Math.}~236 \S2). Since
$\kBKM(\Delta_{0}^{(8)})=0$ the Kontsevich--Soibelman BPS=BKM
slogan collapses: the CoHA-BPS Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}^{(8)}_{
\mathrm{BPS}}(X_{8}^{\MTW})$ (Kapranov--Vasserot 2018
adapted to fourfolds via Kinjo--Park--Safronov 2023 dimensional
reduction) has vanishing bracket on the $\varphi_{8}$-invariant
sector, matching the abelian $\gMTW$. The identification
$\mathfrak{g}^{(8)}_{\mathrm{BPS}}\cong\gMTW$ is geometrically
non-trivial: the $8$ McKay points contribute independent generators
to the positive half, matching the eight imaginary-root cusps of
$\chi_{8}$ in the multiplier system of $K(8)$.

\begin{itemize}

The universal identity $\kBKM(\Phi_{N}) = c_{N}(0)/2$ extends to
$N=8$ trivially: both sides are zero. The naive decomposition
$\kBKM \stackrel{?}{=}\kch + \chi(\cO_{\text{fiber}})$ holds
vacuously ($0=0+0$) only because of the endpoint collapse, not as
a structural statement.

\paragraph{Five attack--heal cycles summary.}
(i) Is $X_{8}^{\MTW}$ a threefold? No: $\Kum^{3}(A)$ is a
hyperk\"ahler fourfold (Beauville 1983); quotient preserves
dimension; CY$_{4}$ forces reconceptualisation beyond the CHL
CY$_{3}$ framework. (ii) Does the order-$8$ symplectic auto exist?
Yes, explicit via MTW 2018 Thm~1.3: translation by $8$-torsion
plus involution, $8$ isolated fixed points with local weights
$(1,3,5,7)\bmod 8$. (iii) Is $\kBKM(\Delta_{0}^{(8)})=0$ trivial?
Trivially zero, structurally forced: abelian $\gMTW$ matches
abelian CoHA-positive-half via rank-zero theta-character. (iv) Is
$N=8$ a genuine GBKM? No --- structural endpoint where the
BKM-positive-root expansion collapses to abelian. (v) How does the
CHL slice relate? Threefolds $\{X_{1},\ldots,X_{6}\}$ at $d=3$
realise $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$; the MTW fourfold at $d=4$ realises
$N=8$. Together with BV ($N=5$) and the gerbe ($N=7$) they span the
full eight-form programme.
