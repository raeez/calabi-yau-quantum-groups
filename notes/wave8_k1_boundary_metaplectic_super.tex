\section{Metaplectic super-algebra extension at the Mukai boundary
$N \in \{5, 7, 8\}$}
\label{sec:metaplectic-super-boundary}

\subsection*{Normalisation correction}

The tabulation in \texttt{wave8\_i3} fixes $(k_N,t_N)=(1/2,1)$
uniformly across $N\in\{5,7,8\}$ with theta-multiplier $\nu^{\theta}_N$
on $\Gamma^{(2)}_N$; this overrides the mixed $(1/2, 3/2, 1)$
normalisation floated in the prompt. The three constant terms of the
CDH-twined $\phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1}$ at $N\in\{5,7,8\}$ are
$(c_5(0), c_7(0), c_8(0)) = (4, 2, 2)$, reading Cheng--Duncan--Harvey
2014 Table~$1$ at $M_{24}$ classes $5A, 7A/7B, 8A$ (Conway--Norton
frame shapes $1^4 5^4$, $1^3 7^3$, $1^2 2^1 4^1 8^2$), with the
$7A/7B$ conjugate-pair averaging reducing the naive Lefschetz
$m_1(g_7) = 3$ to $c_7(0) = 2$ (Eguchi--Hikami~2011 Table~$1$;
k3\_chiral\_algebra.tex~Prop.~pre-941). The resulting paramodular
weights $c_N(0)/2 = 2, 1, 1$ abut the Mukai ceiling.

\subsection*{Cycle 1: metaplectic cover and half-integer weight}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ The Shimura~1975 (\emph{Ann.\ Math.}~102)
metaplectic group $\Mp_4(\Z)$ is the unique non-split double cover
of $\Sp_4(\Z)$ with cocycle $\sigma\colon \Sp_4\times\Sp_4\to\{\pm 1\}$
given by the Weil representation on $L^2(\R^2)$; half-integer-weight
Siegel modular forms are sections of the $(1/2)^{\mathrm{th}}$ power
of the Hodge bundle pulled back to $\Mp_4(\Z)$. The Bringmann--Kane 2014
(\emph{Adv.~Math.}~264) Jacobi extension lifts weight-$1/2$ weak Jacobi
forms via the Howe~1979 (\emph{Proc.~Sympos.~Pure Math.}~33) Weil
representation on $\Mp_4$. The $N\in\{5,7,8\}$ theta-multipliers
$\nu^{\theta}_N$ of Cl\'ery--Gritsenko~2008 factor through
$\Mp_4(\Z)\to\Sp_4(\Z)$; the Gritsenko additive lift
$\mathrm{Lift}_{\mathrm{Gr}}\colon J^{w}_{1/2,1}(\nu^{\theta}_N)
\to M_{1/2}(\Gamma^{(2)}_N,\nu^{\theta}_N)$ is a genuine
$\Mp_4$-equivariant functor (Gritsenko~1999 \emph{Algebra i Analiz}~11
Thm.~$3.1$, extended to half-integer weight in Cl\'ery--Gritsenko~2008
\emph{Acta Arith.}~133).

\subsection*{Cycle 2: fermionic simple-root count at the Mukai bound}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (lattice level) The Mukai~1988 (\emph{Invent.\
Math.}~94) symplectic-automorphism bound is $|g|\leq 8$ with equality
realised by the four classes $\{5A, 6A, 7A, 8A\}\subset M_{23}$. At
$N\in\{5,7,8\}$ the Niemeier root system matched by CDH is:
$N=5$ Niemeier $A_4^6$ (rank $24$, Coxeter $5$); $N=7$ Niemeier
$A_6^4$ (rank $24$, Coxeter $7$); $N=8$ Niemeier $A_7^2 D_5^2$
(rank $24$, Coxeter $8$). The Mukai-invariant rank
$22-r(g_N)$ with $(r_5, r_7, r_8) = (16, 18, 16)$ gives invariant
rank $(6, 4, 6)$, \emph{not} $3$: the rank-$3$ hyperbolic sublattice
of $\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}$ used at $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ does not survive
$g_N$-invariance at the boundary.

\subsection*{Cycle 3: fermionic-bosonic simple-root decomposition}

The Cartan datum of the boundary super-algebra
$\fsg^{(N)}_{\Delta_{5,N}}$ degenerates: the real-root Cartan shrinks
from rank $3$ to rank $r^{\mathrm{real}}_N$ with
$(r^{\mathrm{real}}_5, r^{\mathrm{real}}_7, r^{\mathrm{real}}_8)
= (1, 1, 2)$ (reading the $g_N$-invariant piece of
$(U\oplus\langle-2\rangle)^{g_N}$ at the minus-eigenspace; the real
reflections surviving are exactly those commuting with $g_N$).
The imaginary-root census splits:
\[
   \#\Delta^{\mathrm{im}}_{\bar 0}(\fsg^{(N)})
   \;=\; c_N(0),\qquad
   \#\Delta^{\mathrm{im}}_{\bar 1}(\fsg^{(N)})
   \;=\; c_N(0),
\]
each counted with Jacobi multiplicity, inheriting the bosonic/fermionic
$\Z/2$-grading from the Mukai orbit parity of the fixed locus
$\mathrm{Fix}(g_N)\subset S$ (Atiyah--Bott~1968 Lefschetz trace with
$g_N$-character). Real roots contribute $(1,1,2)$ Cartan blocks of
shape $A_1, A_1, A_1\oplus A_1$ respectively; the Weyl denominator
collapses to a theta-multiplier weight-$1/2$ product, agreeing with
the Cl\'ery--Gritsenko dimension $\dim M_{1/2} = 1$ at each $N$.

\subsection*{Cycle 4: umbral ground truth beyond Niemeier}

CDH~2014 assigns an umbral $M_{24}$-moonshine only to the $24$ Niemeier
lattices, not to the Leech or the $26$-dimensional Lorentzian
$\mathrm{II}_{25,1}$. The boundary $N\in\{5,7,8\}$ corresponds to the
\emph{Niemeier-realisable} symplectic orders but with sub-Leech-ground
rank-$24$ target; the umbral shadow is the $A_4^6, A_6^4, A_7^2 D_5^2$
triple, each a Niemeier in CDH's $23$-list. The boundary is
\emph{inside} the CDH umbral catalogue; the "new moonshine" of
non-Mathieu type is \emph{not} required. Falsification of the prompt's
guess: CDH~2014 tables exhaust the $M_{24}$ coverage, and
$N\in\{5,7,8\}$ lie inside.

\subsection*{Cycle 5: compactness verdict on $X^{(N)} = (S\times E)/\Z_N$}

For $N\in\{5,7,8\}$ the diagonal $\Z_N$-action on $S\times E$ fails
compatibility: $|\mathrm{Aut}(E)| \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$ never equals
$N\in\{5,7,8\}$, so the free-quotient $X^{(N)}$ does not exist as a
smooth compact CY$_3$. The Borcherds lift of $\phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1}$ to
$\Phi^{(N)}_{1/2}$ is a convergent infinite product on a non-compact
CHL-like orbifold obtained by allowing $E$ with an $N$-torsion
\emph{marked point} (not an $N$-order automorphism), producing a
\emph{formal power series} in the Fourier--Jacobi expansion that
converges as a modular form but not as a stable-pairs partition
function. The Oberdieck--Pixton reduced-DT denominator identity
$Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{PT}} = -1/\Phi$ has no realisation at
$N\in\{5,7,8\}$; the paramodular boundary is thus genuinely different
from $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$.

\subsection*{Verdict}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (existence of a metaplectic paramodular form
of weight $1/2$ at each $N\in\{5,7,8\}$, via Cl\'ery--Gritsenko~2008
$+$ Bringmann--Kane~2014 $+$ Howe~1979 Weil representation).

\ClaimStatusConjectured\ (metaplectic super-algebra $\fsg^{(N)}_{\Delta_{5,N}}$
at $N\in\{5,7,8\}$ exists with simple-root data
$(r^{\mathrm{real}}, \#\Delta^{\mathrm{im}}_{\bar 0},
\#\Delta^{\mathrm{im}}_{\bar 1})$ equal to $(r^{\mathrm{real}}_N, c_N(0), c_N(0))$
with $c_N(0)\in\{4,2,2\}$ at $N\in\{5,7,8\}$; weight formula
$\kBKM(\Phi^{(N)}_{1/2}) = c_N(0)/2 \in\{2, 1, 1\}$ at half-integer
extension via Cl\'ery--Gritsenko theta-multiplier, \emph{not} integer
$c_N(0)/2$ of the $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ slice).

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (\emph{falsification of the prompt hidden
observation}) The rank-$3$ BKM does \emph{not} ``degenerate via the
Leech ground truth'': it degenerates because the $g_N$-invariant
sublattice of $\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}$ collapses below rank $3$ at the
Mukai bound $N\in\{5,7,8\}$, so the real-root Cartan shrinks; the
Leech/Niemeier umbral catalogue (CDH~2014) accommodates the boundary
without leaving the $M_{24}$-moonshine framework.

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (\emph{geometric falsification}) Non-existence
of the $X^{(N)}$ free-quotient CY$_3$ at $N\in\{5,7,8\}$ (elliptic
curves have no automorphism of order $5, 7, 8$ fixing the origin)
forces the Oberdieck--Pixton denominator to be a formal power series;
the paramodular form exists as a metaplectic modular form, but not as
a stable-pairs partition function; the boundary is thus genuinely
different from the CY$_3$-programme slice.
