\section*{Atiyah flop, mutation, and the conifold positive half}
\label{wn:sec:flop-mutation-Yplus}

Let
\[
  \widetilde Y=\operatorname{Tot}\bigl(\cO_{\bP^1}(-1)\oplus
  \cO_{\bP^1}(-1)\bigr)
\]
be the small resolution of the conifold
$Y=\{xy=zw\}\subset\C^{4}$, and let
$\widetilde Y^\vee$ be the other small resolution.  The Klebanov--Witten
quiver $Q_{\mathrm{KW}}$ has vertices $0,1$, arrows
$a_1,a_2\colon0\to1$, $b_1,b_2\colon1\to0$, and potential
\[
  W_{\mathrm{KW}}
  = \tr(a_1b_1a_2b_2-a_1b_2a_2b_1).
\]
For the Klebanov--Witten stability chamber $\sigma_{\mathrm{KW}}$, the
conifold positive half is the critical CoHA
\[
  \Yplus_{\sigma_{\mathrm{KW}}}(\widetilde Y)
  :=
  \CoHA(Q_{\mathrm{KW}},W_{\mathrm{KW}}),
\]
graded by $\Gamma_{\geq 0}=\Z_{\geq0}^{2}$.  Completion is part of the
notation only when infinite DT products or wall-crossing operators are
evaluated.

The MMNS conifold root system has real positive roots
\[
  (n+1,n),\quad (n,n+1) \qquad (n\ge 0)
\]
with BPS multiplicity one, and imaginary roots $(n,n)$ for $n\geq 1$
with absolute multiplicity two.  The signs depend on the Behrend,
motivic, or refined specialization.  The algebraic object before
Drinfeld doubling is the positive half
\[
  \Yplus_{\sigma_{\mathrm{KW}}}(\widetilde Y)
  \simeq
  Y^+\!\bigl(\widehat{\fgl}(1|1)\bigr)^{\mathrm{con}}
\]
as a connected $\Z_{\geq0}^{2}$-graded super-bialgebra, in the conifold
current presentation.  On a smooth affine chart $\C^3$ the local algebra
is
\[
  \CoHA(\C^3)\simeq Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_{1}),
\]
the positive half of the affine Yangian.  The full Yangian, universal
$R$-matrix, and vertex-algebra layer enter only after Drinfeld doubling,
centre formation, completion, or framed evaluation.  For this CY$_3$
input the native Hall/chiral output is $\Eone$.  The $\Etwo$-braided
layer is instead
\[
  \cZ\bigl(\Rep^{\Eone}(\Yplus_\sigma(\widetilde Y))\bigr)
  \quad\text{or}\quad
  D\bigl(\Yplus_\sigma(\widetilde Y)\bigr),
\]
after the Hopf pairing and completion have been fixed.

\subsection*{Flop equivalence}

Let
\[
  Z=\widetilde Y\times_Y\widetilde Y^\vee
\]
with projections $p_1,p_2$ to the two small resolutions.  Bridgeland's
flop theorem gives the Fourier--Mukai equivalence
\[
  \FMflop =
  Rp_{2,*}\bigl(Lp_1^*(-)\otimes^{\mathbb L}\cO_Z\bigr)
  \colon
  \Db\Coh(\widetilde Y)\xrightarrow{\sim}
  \Db\Coh(\widetilde Y^\vee).
\]
It identifies the two perverse hearts attached to the two small
resolutions and transports compactly supported curve classes by the
Atiyah flop.  Under Van den Bergh's tilting bundle, these hearts are
presented by adjacent NCCR chambers of the same Jacobi algebra
\[
  J_{\mathrm{KW}}=\Jac(Q_{\mathrm{KW}},W_{\mathrm{KW}}).
\]
Thus the geometric flop is the derived form of mutation of the
Klebanov--Witten seed.

With compatible orientation data on the two $3$CY critical moduli
problems, $\FMflop$ gives an isomorphism between the completed positive
Hall algebras attached to the transported hearts,
\[
  \Thetaflop\colon
  \widehat{\Yplus}_{\sigma}(\widetilde Y)
  \xrightarrow{\sim}
  \widehat{\Yplus}_{\sigma^\vee}(\widetilde Y^\vee).
\]
This is a chamber comparison.  It becomes an endomorphism of one named
positive half only after choosing either a transport
$\tau_{\sigma^\vee,\sigma}\colon
\widehat{\Yplus}_{\sigma^\vee}\to\widehat{\Yplus}_{\sigma}$, or
embeddings of all chamber halves into a common localized Drinfeld
double or quantum torus.  The Fourier--Mukai kernel supplies the
comparison between chambers; the extra transport supplies the
fixed-algebra action.

\subsection*{Mutation on charges}

Write the charge group of the two-vertex heart as
\[
  \Gamma=\Z\alpha_0\oplus\Z\alpha_1,\qquad
  \delta=\alpha_0+\alpha_1,
\]
where $\alpha_0,\alpha_1$ are the two simple dimension vectors.  There
are three related forms on this rank-two lattice, and only the last two
carry the mutation constant.

First, the full Klebanov--Witten quiver has two arrows in each
direction, so its raw skew adjacency is zero:
\[
  \#(0\to1)-\#(1\to0)=2-2=0.
\]
Second, the DWZ-reduced two-acyclic seed is the Kronecker seed
\[
  B_{\mathrm{red}}
  =
  \begin{pmatrix}
  0&2\\
  -2&0
  \end{pmatrix}.
\]
Third, the symmetric affine Cartan matrix controlling the associated
$A_1^{(1)}$ root labels is
\[
  C
  =
  \begin{pmatrix}
  2&-2\\
  -2&2
  \end{pmatrix}.
\]
The numerical constant is therefore fixed from first principles: it is
the number of bifundamental arrows between the two vertices.  In the
Cartan root envelope the simple reflection is
\[
  s_i(\alpha_i)=-\alpha_i,\qquad
  s_i(\alpha_j)=\alpha_j+2\alpha_i\quad (i\neq j),\qquad
  s_i(\delta)=\delta.
\]
The uncompleted positive half carries chamber generators.  The
reflection is the root-label action seen after passing to the double,
the localized quantum torus, or a transported chamber presentation.

The completed quantum torus $\Twall$ is generated by symbols $x_\gamma$
with multiplication twisted by the chosen skew Euler form.  The
Drinfeld double contains both positive and negative halves.  Since
$s_i(\alpha_i)=-\alpha_i$, the reflected simple generator leaves the
positive cone.  A Lusztig braid formula, when written in a doubled
current presentation, belongs to that enlarged algebra:
\[
  T_i(e_j)
  \in
  D\bigl(\Yplus_\sigma(\widetilde Y)\bigr)_{\alpha_j+2\alpha_i}
  \qquad (i\neq j).
\]
Inside $\Yplus_\sigma$ only the transported positive seed is available;
the reflected image of $e_i$ lies outside the positive cone.  Mutation
changes the positive seed.  The Weyl or cluster action becomes a
literal automorphism only on the double, the localized quantum torus, or
a chosen transport of chamber halves.

\subsection*{Wall-crossing character}

Let $\mathrm{BPS}_{\sigma,\gamma}$ be the BPS cohomology in charge
$\gamma$ for a chamber $\sigma$, after the chosen Behrend, motivic, or
refined specialization.  Its reduced character is
\[
  \chi_\sigma(q,y_0,y_1)
  =
  \sum_{\gamma=(n_0,n_1)}
  q^{n_0+n_1}y_0^{n_0}y_1^{n_1}
  \chi_{\mathrm{vir}}\bigl(\mathrm{BPS}_{\sigma,\gamma}\bigr).
\]
Szendroi's NCDT series and the Nagao--Nakajima perverse-coherent
series are specializations of this chamber character.
Morrison--Mozgovoy--Nagao--Szendroi compute all chambers by the
conifold root system.

Kontsevich--Soibelman wall-crossing acts on the completed quantum torus
by an ordered product of quantum dilogarithms
\[
  \Theta_{\mathrm{KS}}(\sigma_-,\sigma_+)
  =
  \prod_{\arg Z_\sigma(\gamma)\ \mathrm{decreases}}
  \Dilog(x_\gamma)^{\Omega_\sigma(\gamma)} .
\]
For the conifold, the exponents are the BPS multiplicities attached to
the two real-root families and to the multiplicity-two imaginary-root
sector.  The MacMahon factor in the numerical DT/NCDT formulas is the
specialized character of this imaginary sector.  Borcherds-product input
and a passage from the positive Hall half to a full vertex algebra are
separate data.  The variable exchange and monomial
correction come from the reduced seed mutation.  A Hall-algebra lift of
the KS operator uses the completed CoHA comparison and orientation data
above.

\subsection*{Seiberg-duality groupoid}

The relevant symmetry object is a groupoid.  Its objects are tilting
hearts or NCCR presentations of the conifold Jacobi algebra, and its
morphisms are mutations at vertices.  To a heart $\mathcal A_\sigma$ it
assigns the completed positive Hall algebra
$\widehat{\cH}_\sigma$.  To a mutation it assigns three compatible
maps:
\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
  \Db(\mathcal A_\sigma) &\xrightarrow{\sim}&
  \Db(\mathcal A_{\mu_i\sigma}),\\[2mm]
  \widehat{\cH}_\sigma &\xrightarrow{\sim}&
  \widehat{\cH}_{\mu_i\sigma},\\[2mm]
  \Twall &\xrightarrow{\Theta_{\mathrm{KS}}}& \Twall .
\end{array}
\]
The first line is the derived mutation equivalence.  The second line is
the completed Hall comparison under orientation compatibility.  The
third line is the KS quantum-torus wall-crossing operator.  After
embedding the positive halves into a common double, these morphisms
realise the affine $A_1^{(1)}$ cluster/Weyl action.  On a single
uncompleted positive half they remain chamber-to-chamber maps.

\subsection*{Local invariants}

The conifold Hall/chiral invariant is
\[
  \kch(A_{\mathrm{conifold}})=1,
\]
the unit primitive wall contribution of the Klebanov--Witten NCCR.  The
resolved conifold is a noncompact toric CY$_3$ with curve base
$\bP^1$, while the local-surface formula has input
$\operatorname{Tot}(K_S\to S)$.  The direct McKay/NCCR computation supplies the
single compact curve generator $\cO_{\bP^1}$ and hence the value above.

The compact Hodge-supertrace formula, the Borcherds weight formula, and
the fibre/lattice invariant belong to different constructions:
\[
  \kcat=\chi(\cO_X),\qquad
  \kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2,\qquad
  \kfiber=\text{chosen compact fibre or lattice datum}.
\]
The local conifold positive half is separate from these data.  If one
records the regularized noncompact row for the resolved conifold, the
ordinary structure-sheaf entry is $\kcat(X_{\mathrm{con}})=0$; the
Hall/chiral value remains $\kch(A_{\mathrm{conifold}})=1$.

\subsection*{Fixed-algebra criterion}

A fixed-algebra flop action on a named algebra is the following datum:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
  \mathrm{(i)} & \text{orientation data on both critical CoHA moduli
  stacks;}\\
  \mathrm{(ii)} & \text{the completion in which the infinite DT products
  converge;}\\
  \mathrm{(iii)} & \text{the embedding of all chamber positive halves into
  a common Drinfeld double or localized quantum torus;}\\
  \mathrm{(iv)} & \text{the PBW/current-presentation comparison with
  }Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}(1|1))^{\mathrm{con}}\text{;}\\
  \mathrm{(v)} & \text{the compatibility of the Hall comparison with the
  KS quantum-torus character map.}
\end{array}
\]
With these data, the chamber comparison composes with the chosen
transport to give an automorphism of the selected completion.  Without
them, the theorem is exactly the chamber-to-chamber flop/mutation
comparison:
\[
  \text{flop/mutation}
  \longmapsto
  \text{completed positive-half comparison}
  \longmapsto
  \text{Weyl/cluster action on the double or quantum torus}.
\]
This form keeps
$\CoHA(\C^3)=Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$ on the positive half, keeps the
conifold object at
$Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}(1|1))^{\mathrm{con}}$, and keeps the $d=3$
$\Eone$ output separate from the $\Etwo$ centre/double/module layer.
