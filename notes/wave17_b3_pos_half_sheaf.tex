\section*{The positive half sheaf $\cY^{+}_{X}$ on a CY$_3$}

Let $X$ be a smooth quasi-projective CY$_3$ equipped with an effective
compact-support category $\cC_X^{+}$.  A positive Hall sheaf on $X$ is
the descent, when it exists, of the local Kontsevich--Soibelman
positive halves attached to oriented critical charts.  The topology is
carried by the cover; the algebra is carried by the extension
correspondence.

\subsection*{Critical Hall charts}

A critical Hall chart is a quadruple
\[
  \alpha=(U_{\alpha},Q_{\alpha},W_{\alpha},\theta_{\alpha}),
  \qquad U_{\alpha}=\Spec R_{\alpha},
\]
where $(Q_{\alpha},W_{\alpha})$ is a $3$-Calabi-Yau Jacobi model for the
chosen local category of compactly supported effective objects, and
$\theta_{\alpha}$ is an orientation, equivalently a square root of the
virtual canonical line, on the associated $d$-critical moduli stack.  For
a dimension vector $\mathbf d$ put
\[
  \cM_{\alpha,\mathbf d}
  =
  \Crit\!\left(\Tr(W_{\alpha,\mathbf d})\right)/
  G_{\mathbf d},
  \qquad
  G_{\mathbf d}=\prod_{i\in Q_{\alpha,0}}\GL_{d_i}.
\]
The local positive Hall algebra is
\[
  \cH_{\alpha}^{+}
  =
  \CoHA(Q_{\alpha},W_{\alpha})
  =
  \bigoplus_{\mathbf d\in\bZ_{\geq 0}^{Q_{\alpha,0}}}
  H^{\BM}_{G_{\mathbf d}}\!
  \left(
    \Crit(\Tr(W_{\alpha,\mathbf d})),
    \varphi_{\Tr(W_{\alpha,\mathbf d})}\bQ\otimes\theta_{\alpha,\mathbf d}
  \right).
\]
The Hall product is the convolution attached to the correspondence
\[
  \cM_{\alpha,\mathbf d_1}\times\cM_{\alpha,\mathbf d_2}
  \xleftarrow{\ p\ }
  \cE_{\alpha,\mathbf d_1,\mathbf d_2}
  \xrightarrow{\ q\ }
  \cM_{\alpha,\mathbf d_1+\mathbf d_2},
\]
where $\cE_{\alpha,\mathbf d_1,\mathbf d_2}$ is the stack of short exact
sequences with subobject of class $\mathbf d_1$ and quotient of class
$\mathbf d_2$.  The map $p$ records the two endpoints, the map $q$
records the middle term, and the Thom--Sebastiani isomorphism identifies
the vanishing-cycle coefficients.  Hence $a*b=q_{!}p^{*}(a\boxtimes b)$
is associative by the two-step flag correspondence.  The algebra
$\cH_{\alpha}^{+}$ is therefore an $E_1$ Hall algebra graded by the
effective cone.  The opposite half, Cartan factor, Hopf pairing,
coproduct, and half-braiding begin after additional double or centre
data have been supplied.

\subsection*{Restriction data}

A restriction map between Hall charts is a functorial critical square
over an inclusion of open sets.  For $V\subset U$ in a chosen chart
system one needs an exact restriction functor
\[
  r_{UV}\colon \cC_{U}^{+}\longrightarrow \cC_{V}^{+}
\]
preserving compact support, effective classes, orientation lines, and
the Hall correspondence of extensions.  In a toric localization
$R\to R[f^{-1}]$ this operation is derived base change of the Jacobi
algebra.  Deleting vertices from a quiver gives the correct map exactly
when the localized category is represented by that full subquiver and
the potential restricts compatibly.

Under these hypotheses the critical stacks form Cartesian diagrams over
the representation stacks, with compatible functions and orientation
lines.  Base change for vanishing cycles gives
\[
  \rho_{UV}\colon \cH_{U}^{+}\longrightarrow \cH_{V}^{+}.
\]
Compatibility of the restriction square with the short-exact-sequence
stack gives
\[
  \rho_{UV}\bigl(a*b\bigr)=\rho_{UV}(a)*\rho_{UV}(b),
\]
so $\rho_{UV}$ is a morphism of graded $E_1$ algebras.  For
$W\subset V\subset U$, the equality
$\rho_{VW}\rho_{UV}=\rho_{UW}$ is exactly the equality of the three
restriction functors on critical stacks with their orientations.

\subsection*{Morita descent}

An oriented critical Hall atlas $\mathfrak A$ on $X$ consists of
critical Hall charts $\{\alpha\}$, the restriction maps specified above,
and transition kernels on overlaps.  On
$U_{\alpha\beta}=U_{\alpha}\cap U_{\beta}$ a transition kernel is a
$3$-Calabi-Yau Morita equivalence
\[
  T_{\alpha\beta}\colon
  \Db(\mathrm{Jac}(Q_{\alpha},W_{\alpha})|_{U_{\alpha\beta}}\Mod)
  \xrightarrow{\sim}
  \Db(\mathrm{Jac}(Q_{\beta},W_{\beta})|_{U_{\alpha\beta}}\Mod)
\]
whose induced map on moduli identifies the $(-1)$-shifted symplectic
derived critical stacks, the functions $\Tr(W_{\alpha})$ and
$\Tr(W_{\beta})$ up to right-equivalence, the orientation lines, and the
extension correspondences.  A derived Morita equivalence of the
underlying algebras supplies the algebraic equivalence component of
this datum; the potential, orientation, and Hall correspondence must
also be preserved.

Such a kernel induces an algebra isomorphism
\[
  \phi_{\alpha\beta}\colon
  \cH_{\alpha}^{+}|_{U_{\alpha\beta}}
  \xrightarrow{\sim}
  \cH_{\beta}^{+}|_{U_{\alpha\beta}}.
\]
On triple overlaps the descent condition is the specified
$2$-isomorphism
\[
  T_{\beta\gamma}\otimes^{L}T_{\alpha\beta}
  \cong
  T_{\alpha\gamma}
\]
in the oriented Morita $(2,1)$-category.  Applying vanishing-cycle
cohomology and Hall convolution yields
\[
  \phi_{\beta\gamma}\circ\phi_{\alpha\beta}
  =
  \phi_{\alpha\gamma}.
\]
The descent datum $\{\cH_{\alpha}^{+},\phi_{\alpha\beta}\}$ then defines
a sheaf of graded $E_1$ algebras on the topology in which
$\mathfrak A$ is defined:
\[
  \cY^{+}_{X,\mathfrak A}.
\]
The subscript $\mathfrak A$ is part of the notation until atlas
independence has been proved by compatible Morita descent.

\subsection*{Derived global sections}

Define
\[
  Y^{+}_{\mathrm{crit}}(X,\mathfrak A)
  :=
  R\Gamma\!\left(X,\cY^{+}_{X,\mathfrak A}\right)
  \simeq
  \Tot\!
  \left[
    \prod_{\alpha}\cH_{\alpha}^{+}
    \longrightarrow
    \prod_{\alpha,\beta}\cH_{\alpha\beta}^{+}
    \longrightarrow
    \prod_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}\cH_{\alpha\beta\gamma}^{+}
    \longrightarrow\cdots
  \right].
\]
The object on the right is the positive half attached to the atlas.
Ordinary global sections compute it precisely when this \v{C}ech complex is
acyclic above degree $0$.  Freeness of each local CoHA over its
equivariant polynomial ring is a statement about the local module;
acyclicity remains the descent assertion for the overlap system.

For $X=\bC^{3}$ there is one chart, and the \v{C}ech differential is
absent:
\[
  Y^{+}_{\mathrm{crit}}(\bC^{3})
  =
  \CoHA(\bC^{3})
  =
  Y^{+}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_{1}).
\]
This is the Schiffmann--Vasserot positive half.  The full affine Yangian
is the Drinfeld double obtained by adjoining the opposite half and
Cartan part; the vertex-algebra or
$\mathcal W_{1+\infty}$ object belongs to the centre, completion, or
evaluation layer.

\subsection*{Scalar character sheaves}

The character of a positive Hall sheaf is a weaker object than the sheaf
itself.  Given a grading and finite-dimensional graded pieces, one may
form
\[
  \operatorname{ch}\cY^{+}_{X,\mathfrak A}(U)
  =
  \sum_{\gamma}\dim\!\left(\cY^{+}_{X,\mathfrak A}(U)_{\gamma}\right)q^\gamma .
\]
This scalar character sheaf records dimensions, Behrend-weighted
Donaldson--Thomas numbers, or automorphic coefficients after the chosen
specialisation.  It forgets multiplication, extensions, orientations,
coproducts, and pairings.  A character sheaf determines a sheaf of
algebras in the presence of a separate reconstruction or recognition
theorem supplying the algebra structure and compatibility with descent.

\subsection*{Boundary with doubles, centres, and $\Phi_{3}$}

For every atlas $\mathfrak A$ satisfying the hypotheses above,
$Y^{+}_{\mathrm{crit}}(X,\mathfrak A)$ is an $E_1$ positive Hall
algebra.  A Hall-Drinfeld double requires the additional data
\[
  \left(
    Y^{-}_{\mathrm{crit}}(X),\,
    Y^{0}_{X},\,
    \Delta_{+},\,
    \langle-,-\rangle,\,
    \widehat{\phantom{Y}}
  \right),
\]
namely a completed opposite half, Cartan part, continuous coproduct,
nondegenerate Hopf pairing, and completion.  Under the usual finite or
completed topological Hopf hypotheses,
\[
  D_{\hbar}\!\left(Y^{+}_{\mathrm{crit}}(X)\right)
  =
  Y^{-}_{\mathrm{crit}}(X)
  \widehat\otimes
  Y^{0}_{X}
  \widehat\otimes
  Y^{+}_{\mathrm{crit}}(X),
  \qquad
  \cZ\!\left(\Rep^{E_1}(Y^{+}_{\mathrm{crit}}(X))\right)
  \simeq
  \Rep^{E_2}\!\left(D_{\hbar}(Y^{+}_{\mathrm{crit}}(X))\right).
\]
$E_2$ structure is a property of the centre, double, or representation
layer.  The native $d=3$ output of the CY-to-chiral functor $\Phi_{3}$
is $E_1$-chiral.

For $K3\times E$, a positive Hall sheaf supplies the positive input to
the compact BKM comparison.  Recognition of the Hall-BKM object needs
an oriented compact critical CoHA, compact-support realisation, the
completed double data listed above, PBW control, and a bracket-preserving
comparison map to the Borcherds denominator.  The numerical invariants
belong to distinct constructions:
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)
  =
  \chi(\cO_{K3\times E})
  =
  \chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)
  =
  2\cdot 0
  =
  0.
\]
The compact Hodge-supertrace value is likewise
\[
  \kch(K3\times E)=0.
\]
The Heisenberg--Mukai specialisation and the fibre lattice carry their
own scalars,
\[
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)=3,
  \qquad
  \kfiber(K3\times E)=24,
\]
while the Borcherds weight of the $\Delta_{5}$ denominator is
\[
  \kBKM(\Delta_{5})=\frac{c_{1}(0)}{2}=5.
\]
These equations are independent layer statements.  The equality for
$\kBKM$ belongs to the denominator-recognition layer; the positive half
sheaf is one input to that layer.

\subsection*{Exact hypotheses for the general CY$_3$ case}

The construction has four independent hypotheses.  First, $X$ carries an
oriented critical Hall atlas whose charts have quiver-with-potential or
derived critical models for the chosen effective compact-support
category.  Second, every overlap kernel preserves the $3$-Calabi-Yau
pairing, potential, orientation line, effective cone, and extension
correspondence.  Third, the triple-overlap kernels satisfy the oriented
Morita cocycle condition.  Fourth, reduction of
$R\Gamma(X,\cY^{+}_{X,\mathfrak A})$ to ordinary
$\Gamma(X,\cY^{+}_{X,\mathfrak A})$ requires acyclicity of the descent
complex.

With these hypotheses the sheaf $\cY^{+}_{X,\mathfrak A}$ and its
derived positive half are constructed.  The compact double, centre, and
BKM comparison require the additional boundary data displayed above.
