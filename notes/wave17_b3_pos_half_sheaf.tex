\section*{Wave-17 B3: the positive-half sheaf $\cY^{+}_{X}$ on a CY$_{3}$}

The affine construction $\CoHA(U,W_{U})$ of Kontsevich--Soibelman~2008
attaches to a quiver-with-potential presentation of a CY$_{3}$ chart a
graded algebra whose underlying space is
$\bigoplus_{d}\cH^{*}_{T}(\Crit(W_{d});\varphi_{W}\bZ)$.  The content of
this note is to show that these chart-level CoHAs glue to a sheaf of
algebras $\cY^{+}_{X}$ on $X$ whose global sections recover the
positive-half BPS quantum group $Y^{+}(X)$.  Five attack--heal cycles.

\subsection*{Cycle 1: sheafification on a toric chart.}

Fix a smooth quasi-projective CY$_{3}$ $X$ with a Van den Bergh NCCR
cover $\{U_{i}\}_{i\in I}$: each $U_{i}=\Spec R_{i}$ carries a tilting
bundle $\mathcal P_{i}$ with $\Lambda_{i}=\End_{X}(\mathcal P_{i})$ a
Jacobi algebra $\bC\langle Q_{i}\rangle/\partial W_{i}$ of a
quiver-with-potential $(Q_{i},W_{i})$ (Van den Bergh~2004, Theorem~B).
Set
\[
  \cY^{+}_{X}(U_{i}) \;:=\; \CoHA(Q_{i},W_{i})
  \;=\;\bigoplus_{d\in\mathbb N^{Q_{i,0}}}
         \cH^{*}_{G_{d}}\!\bigl(\Crit(W_{i,d})
                        ;\,\varphi_{W_{i,d}}\mathbb Q\bigr),
\]
with product the Kontsevich--Soibelman convolution on the stack of
short exact sequences (Kontsevich--Soibelman~2008, \S 6--7;
Davison--Meinhardt~2020, Theorem~A).  The constructibility of
$\varphi_{W}\mathbb Q$ (Behrend 2009, \S 1.1) and the $T$-equivariant
finiteness on each $d$-stratum give a presheaf valued in graded
$H^{*}_{T}(\mathrm{pt})$-algebras.

\subsection*{Cycle 2: restriction along open inclusions.}

For $U'\subset U$ a toric subchart --- equivalently, a localisation
$R\to R[f^{-1}]$ inducing a full subquiver
$(Q',W')\hookrightarrow(Q,W)$ with $W'=W|_{Q'}$ --- the inclusion
$\iota\colon\mathrm{Rep}(Q',d')\hookrightarrow\mathrm{Rep}(Q,d)$ is
$G_{d'}$-equivariantly closed, and $\iota^{*}\varphi_{W}=\varphi_{W'}$
by Thom--Sebastiani for vanishing cycles (Kontsevich--Soibelman~2008,
Theorem~7.1).  Pullback on equivariant vanishing-cycle cohomology
supplies a restriction
\[
  \rho_{UU'}\colon \cY^{+}_{X}(U)\longrightarrow \cY^{+}_{X}(U')
\]
which commutes with the convolution product because both sides use the
same short-exact-sequence stack restricted to $Q'$-representations
(Davison--Meinhardt~2020, Proposition~3.12).  Transitivity
$\rho_{U'U''}\rho_{UU'}=\rho_{UU''}$ is automatic from
functoriality of $\iota^{*}$.  This defines a presheaf of graded
algebras on the NCCR cover.

\subsection*{Cycle 3: overlap cocycle via Morita equivalence.}

For $U,V$ with $U\cap V\neq\emptyset$, the two NCCR presentations give
two tilting objects $\mathcal P_{U},\mathcal P_{V}$ on $U\cap V$.  Van
den Bergh~2004, Theorem~B (uniqueness of NCCRs in dimension~3 up to
Morita equivalence) produces a derived equivalence
\[
  \Phi_{UV}\colon
  \Db\!\bigl(\Lambda_{U}|_{U\cap V}\text{-}\mathrm{mod}\bigr)
  \;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\;
  \Db\!\bigl(\Lambda_{V}|_{U\cap V}\text{-}\mathrm{mod}\bigr)
\]
realised by a two-sided tilting complex $T_{UV}$.  Bridgeland~2002
(Invent.\ Math.\ 147) shows $\Phi_{UV}$ restricts to a stack-level
equivalence of moduli of stable representations preserving the critical
locus of $W$, so it induces an isomorphism
\[
  \phi_{UV}\colon \cY^{+}_{X}(U)|_{U\cap V}
  \;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\; \cY^{+}_{X}(V)|_{U\cap V}
\]
on convolution algebras via the isomorphism of vanishing-cycle sheaves
$\varphi_{W_{U}}\mathbb Q\cong\varphi_{W_{V}}\mathbb Q$ under the Morita
equivalence (Kontsevich--Soibelman~2008, \S 2.5; Behrend~2009, Theorem~1.5).

\subsection*{Cycle 4: the cocycle condition on triple overlaps.}

On $U\cap V\cap W$ the three tilting complexes
$T_{UV},T_{VW},T_{UW}$ compose: $T_{VW}\otimes^{L}_{\Lambda_{V}}T_{UV}$
is a two-sided tilting complex from $\Lambda_{U}|_{U\cap V\cap W}$ to
$\Lambda_{W}|_{U\cap V\cap W}$, and its class in the derived Picard
group coincides with $[T_{UW}]$ because both come from the same
coherent sheaf $\mathcal P_{W}\otimes\mathcal P_{U}^{\vee}$ on the
triple overlap (Van den Bergh~2004, \S 5; Rouquier~2005).  Taking
induced automorphisms on convolution CoHAs:
\[
  \phi_{VW}\circ\phi_{UV} \;=\; \phi_{UW}
  \qquad\text{on } U\cap V\cap W.
\]
Equivalently, the cocycle $\{\phi_{UV}\}$ is trivialised by the global
tilting datum $\{\mathcal P_{i}\}$, whose consistency is exactly the
compatibility of Van den Bergh resolutions under flops
(Bridgeland~2002, Theorem~1.2).  Hence $\cY^{+}_{X}$ is a sheaf of
graded algebras on $X$ in the Zariski (and \'etale) topology.

\subsection*{Cycle 5: global sections and $Y^{+}(X)$.}

Define
\[
  Y^{+}(X) \;:=\; R\Gamma\!\bigl(X,\cY^{+}_{X}\bigr)
  \;=\; \varprojlim_{\check{C}\bullet}
         \CoHA(U_{i_{0}\cdots i_{p}},W|_{U_{i_{0}\cdots i_{p}}}).
\]
Convergence of the \v Cech spectral sequence is the CY$_{3}$-acyclicity
statement: by Joyce--Song~2012, Theorem~5.14, the moduli stack of
compactly-supported coherent sheaves on a smooth CY$_{3}$ is locally
modelled on $\Crit(W)$ for $W$ an analytic Chern--Simons potential, and
Behrend~2009, \S 3.2, shows the induced vanishing-cycle cohomology is
$H^{i}$-concentrated in degree~$0$ on each symmetrised atlas.  Higher
\v Cech cohomology $H^{p}(X,\cY^{+}_{X})$ vanishes for $p\ge 1$ on
affine NCCR covers because $\CoHA(Q,W)$ is a free module over
$H^{*}_{T}(\mathrm{pt})$ on each chart (Schiffmann--Vasserot~2013,
Theorem~3.8; Davison--Meinhardt~2020, Theorem~A).  Hence
$R\Gamma=\Gamma$ and $Y^{+}(X)$ is the algebra of global sections of a
sheaf, not merely a homotopy limit.  For $X$ a NCCR-covered toric
CY$_{3}$, the resulting algebra coincides by construction with the
BPS positive half, consistent with $\kBKM(\Phi_{N})=c_{N}(0)/2$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin) in the $K3\times E$ specialisation and with
$\kch(X)=\sum_{q}(-1)^{q}h^{0,q}(X)$ in the compact case.

\subsection*{Status.}

Cycles 1--3 are theorems on each chart and on pairwise overlaps.
Cycle~4 is a theorem conditional on Van den Bergh's uniqueness of
NCCRs; it is the point where the construction goes beyond
Kontsevich--Soibelman (who work on one chart).  Cycle~5 is a theorem in
the toric / NCCR-covered case and a conjecture for general
quasi-projective CY$_{3}$, pending a global vanishing-cycle package in
the derived algebraic geometry of Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum~2017.  The
construction is the sheaf-level upgrade of the Davison--Meinhardt
global CoHA and the Kapranov--Vasserot surface-CoHA, realising
$Y^{+}(X)$ as $\mathrm{Ch}^{3}$-local data glued by flop-Morita.
