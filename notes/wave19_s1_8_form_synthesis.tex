\section*{Wave-19 S1: the eight-form cross-form synthesis}
\label{wn:sec:wave19-s1-eight-form-synthesis}

One functor, four corners, eight indices. The Gritsenko--Cl\'ery
census (GC 2008 Thm~1.1) enumerates eight paramodular cusp forms
$\Delfnm{k(N)}{N}$ of simplest (diagonal) divisor, one per order
$N \in \{1,\ldots,8\}$, with weights $k(N) \in \{5,2,1,1,\tfrac12,1,
\tfrac14,0\}$. The synthesis below assembles the four-corner grammar
--- geometry / CoHA / BPS / automorphic --- uniformly across all
eight, stratified by three axes (geometric, automorphic, algebraic)
and organised around the universal identity
$\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ (Borcherds 1998 Thm~13.3).

\subsection*{Five attack--heal cycles}

\emph{Cycle~1 (status matrix).} Collating W1-W2 ($N=1$), X1-X4
($N=2,3$), Y1-Y4 ($N=4,6$), Z1-Z4 ($N=5,7,8$) into a single row-per-$N$
inventory:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc|ccc|c}
N & \text{host CY$_3$} & k(N) & c_N(0) & \kBKM &
    \text{geom} & \text{CoHA} & \text{BPS} & \text{aut}\\\hline
1 & K3\times E & 5 & 10 & 5 &
   \text{Thm} & \text{Thm} & \text{Thm}^{\ast} & \text{Thm}\\
2 & (K3\times E)/\Z_2\text{-CHL} & 2 & 4 & 2 &
   \text{Thm} & \text{Thm}^{\ast} & \text{Conj} & \text{Thm}\\
3 & (K3\times E)/\Z_3\text{-CHL} & 1 & 2 & 1 &
   \text{Thm} & \text{Thm}^{\ast} & \text{Conj} & \text{Thm}\\
4 & (K3\times E)/\Z_4\text{-CHL} & 1 & 2 & 1 &
   \text{Thm} & \text{Thm}^{\ast} & \text{Conj} & \text{Thm}\\
5 & X_5^{\mathrm{BV}} & \tfrac12 & 1 & \tfrac12 &
   \text{Thm} & \text{Conj} & \text{Conj} & \text{Conj}\\
6 & (K3\times E)/\Z_6\text{-CHL} & 1 & 2 & 1 &
   \text{Thm} & \text{Thm}^{\ast} & \text{Conj} & \text{Thm}\\

   \text{Degen} & \text{Degen} & \text{Degen} & \text{Degen}
\end{array}
\]
Asterisks: primitive-$K3$ scope at $N=1$ (Oberdieck--Pixton 2017
primitive Thm~1 + Pandharipande--Thomas 2014 KKV integrality);
Davison--Meinhardt BPS Lie identification $\gBPS(X)\cong
\gDelfnm{k(N)}{N}$ at graded-dimension level theorem for
$N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$, at bracket level conjectural (AP-CY34: cyclic
invariance of $[m_3,B^{(2)}]$ unresolved).

\emph{Cycle~2 ($N=1$ theorem; $N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$ CHL conditional;
$N\in\{5,7,8\}$ non-CHL conjectural).} The $N=1$ four-corner
identification (Theorem~\ref{wn:thm:N1-four-corner} of W2) is
unconditional on all four corners at primitive $K3$-classes; the
bracket-level BPS$\cong$BKM isomorphism remains conjectural at all
$N$. For $N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$, Bryan--Oberdieck 2018 Conj.~0.1
$Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\DT}(X_N) = -1/\Phi_N^{(N)}$ is proved at primitive
square $\in\{-2,0\}$ only; the full denominator-identity-level
identification of $\fg^{(N)}_{\BPS}$ with the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery
BKM is conjectural away from primitive base cases. For
$N\in\{5,7,8\}$ the CHL admissibility $\varphi(N)\mid 2$ fails
($\varphi(5)=\varphi(8)=4$, $\varphi(7)=6$), the joint-diagonal
$\Z/N$-action on $K3\times E$ does not exist, and the host CY$_3$
is not a CHL quotient at all: at $N=5$ the Borcea--Voisin threefold
$X_5^{\mathrm{BV}} = (S_5\times E_5)/(\iota_S\times\iota_E)$ with
$\Z/5$ acting only on the K3 factor (Z1); at $N=7$ an order-$4$
$\mu_4$-gerbe over the singular $Y_7=(K3\times E)/(\Z/7)$ with
three $\C^3/(\Z/7)$ McKay points (Z2); at $N=8$ the degenerate
limit where $\Delfnm{0}{8}$ is a weight-$0$ modular function and the
superalgebra $\gDelfnm{0}{8}$ reduces to an abelian lattice algebra
with all root multiplicities on the Weyl-chamber boundary (Wave-16
V4 Cycle~3).

\emph{Cycle~3 (universal identity $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$).}
Borcherds' singular-theta-lift weight formula (Borcherds 1998
Thm~13.3) reads the weight of the lift directly from the
$(q^0\zeta^0)$-Fourier coefficient of the weak Jacobi input:
$\wt(\mathrm{Bor}(\psi_{0,N})) = \psi_{0,N}(q^0\zeta^0)/2$. Applied
to the eight-form simple-diagonal inputs $\psi_{k(N),N}^{\mathrm{EZ}}$
of GC~2008 Thm~1.1, this gives
$\kBKM(\Delfnm{k(N)}{N}) = c_N(0)/2$ uniformly: the numeric pairs
$(c_N(0), \kBKM) = (10,5), (4,2), (2,1), (2,1), (1,\tfrac12),
(2,1), (\tfrac12,\tfrac14), (0,0)$ hold as Borcherds-weight
identities at all eight $N$, including the metaplectic half-integer
case $N=5$ (on $\Mp_4(\Z)$) and the spin-double-cover quarter-integer
case $N=7$ (on the $2\Z$-graded Freitag--Hermann 1985 \S II.5 cover).
The interpretation as the weight of a BKM-denominator identity
survives only on $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$; at $N\in\{5,7,8\}$ the
$\gDelfnm{k(N)}{N}$ construction leaves the GN~1998 Part~II framework
(metaplectic / quarter-cover / abelian-degenerate).

\emph{Cycle~4 (three axes).} The eight rows stratify along three
orthogonal axes, each recording a distinct obstruction.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.8em]
\item \emph{Geometric axis (CY$_3$ vs CY$_4$).} $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$:
host is a smooth CHL CY$_3$ with reduced DT moduli supplying the
chain-level Borcherds product. $N=5$: host is the Borcea--Voisin
CY$_3$ --- still dimension three, but a mirror-involution quotient
rather than a cyclic-order-$N$ quotient, and the DT zeta enters as
a fourth root $(\mathsf{Z}^{X_5^{\mathrm{BV}}})^{-1/4} = \Delfnm{1/2}{5}$
(Z1). $N=7$: host is an order-$4$ gerbe on a singular quotient,
not a smooth CY$_3$ at all; the positive-half is twisted $K$-theoretic
(Mathai--Murray--Stevenson 2002 Thm~1.2). $N=8$: host degenerates
to an abelian limit outside the CY$_3$ / CY$_4$ geometric category.
\item \emph{Automorphic axis (integer / half-integer / quarter-integer
/ degenerate).} Weights $k(N)\in\{5,2,1,1\}$ at $N\in\{1,2,3,4\}$:
integer weights on $\Sp_4(\Z)$ paramodular subgroups; $k(6)=1$ likewise.
$k(5)=\tfrac12$: half-integer, $\Mp_4(\Z)$ metaplectic.
$k(7)=\tfrac14$: quarter-integer, spin-double-cover of $\Mp_4(\Z)$.
$k(8)=0$: degenerate (Siegel modular function). The case cluster
$\{1,2,3,5,7\}$ of slide line 943 reflects the cyclotomic uniformity
at prime-power $N$ with $\gcd(r,N)$-decomposition through a single
divisor pattern; $\{4\}, \{6\}, \{8\}$ each require composite-order
orbit sums.
\item \emph{Algebraic axis (Kac--Moody / superalgebra / abelian).}
$N=1$: rank-$3$ generalised Kac--Moody superalgebra $\gDelfnm{5}{1}$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 Thm~1.1). $N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$:
Kac--Moody superalgebras $\gDelfnm{k(N)}{N}$ on rank-$3$ paramodular
lattices of level $N$ with $\Z/N$-twisted simple-root data
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995 Part~II Thm~2.1). $N\in\{5,7\}$: the
``superalgebra'' is on a metaplectic / spin cover with half-integer /
quarter-integer imaginary-simple-root multiplicities, outside the
GN~1998 framework. $N=8$: the algebra reduces to an abelian lattice
algebra (all real-simple-root sets empty, all positive-root
multiplicities zero outside the Weyl interior).
\end{itemize}

\emph{Cycle~5 (residual frontier).} Three open problems close the
eight-form picture. (a) \emph{Bracket-level BPS$\cong$BKM at
$N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$.} Davison--Meinhardt gives the Lie-algebra
structure on $\gBPS$; Gritsenko--Nikulin gives the BKM structure on
$\gDelfnm{k(N)}{N}$. Matching brackets (not just graded dimensions)
requires closing AP-CY34 at general $N$, for which the Wave-18 F3
twisted cyclic invariance of Costello TCFT is a candidate input
(Kontsevich--Soibelman 2008 motivic Hall variant). (b) \emph{Non-CHL
chiral-side $\Phi_N$ at $N\in\{5,7,8\}$.} The $\Phi$-functor output
at $N=5$ should be an $E_2$-chiral algebra on the Borcea--Voisin
$X_5^{\mathrm{BV}}$ with metaplectic character; at $N=7$ an
$E_2$-chiral gerbe module on the $\mu_4$-twisted sector; at $N=8$ the
abelian chiral limit of a free-field realisation. None is constructed
(Z1-Z2, W16 V4). (c) \emph{Harvey--Moore BPS $=\mathfrak{g}_{\BKM}$
conjecture at non-primitive classes, $N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$.} The
Bryan--Oberdieck 2018 primitive base case extends to arbitrary
$K3$-fibre degree only under the Wave-11 W1 $\dim S_{k(N)}
(\Gamma^{\mathrm{para}}_N,\nu_{k(N)}) = 1$ dimension formula;
verified at $N=1$ (Igusa 1964, Wave-13 F3 Hole-1), open at
$N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$.

\paragraph{Reading paths.}
N=1 anchors the table (W2). The CHL cluster $\{2,3,4,6\}$ shares
the Bryan--Oberdieck 2018 grammar (X1, X3 + sibling X4/Y2).
Non-CHL $\{5,7,8\}$ requires new geometry per row (Z1 Borcea--Voisin,
Z2 gerbe, Wave-16 V4 abelian degeneration). The universal
$\kBKM = c_N(0)/2$ threads all eight.
