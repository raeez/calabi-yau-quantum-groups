\section*{The shuffle algebra of the toric \texorpdfstring{$\mathbb{C}^3$}{C3} CoHA}
\label{wn:sec:shuffle-first-principles-c3}

\paragraph{The product.}
Let
\[
 \mathbb{F}=\mathbb{Q}(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3),
 \qquad
 \omega(z,w)
 =
 \frac{(z-w-\epsilon_1)(z-w-\epsilon_2)(z-w-\epsilon_3)}
      {(z-w)^3}.
\]
Put $\Sh_n=\mathbb{F}(x_1,\ldots,x_n)^{S_n}$, with poles allowed only on
the partial diagonals. For
$F\in \Sh_m$ and $G\in \Sh_n$, Schiffmann--Vasserot's rank-one shuffle
product is
\[
(F\star G)(x_1,\ldots,x_{m+n})
=
\frac{1}{m!\,n!}
\sum_{\sigma\in S_{m+n}}
\sigma\!\left[
F(x_1,\ldots,x_m)G(x_{m+1},\ldots,x_{m+n})
\prod_{\substack{1\le i\le m\\m<j\le m+n}}\omega(x_j,x_i)
\right].
\]
The factor $1/(m!\,n!)$ divides only the internal symmetries of the two
input blocks. For $m=n=1$ there are two shuffle terms and no extra
factor $1/2$ in this convention.

\paragraph{Associativity.}
For block sizes $n_1,\ldots,n_r$ and symmetric functions
$F_a\in\Sh_{n_a}$, set $N=\sum_a n_a$ and let
$I_a=\{n_1+\cdots+n_{a-1}+1,\ldots,n_1+\cdots+n_a\}$. The $r$-fold
shuffle expression is
\[
\mu_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}(F_1,\ldots,F_r)
=
\frac{1}{\prod_a n_a!}
\sum_{\sigma\in S_N}
\sigma\!\left[
\prod_{a=1}^r F_a(x_{I_a})
\prod_{1\le a<b\le r}\prod_{\substack{i\in I_a\\j\in I_b}}
\omega(x_j,x_i)
\right].
\]
This formula is independent of parentheses. Indeed, in
$(F\star G)\star H$ the inner $S_{m+n}$-sum and the outer
$S_{m+n+p}$-sum compose to the same $S_{m+n+p}$-orbit as the one in
$F\star(G\star H)$; each final permutation is counted exactly
$(m+n)!$ times on the left before the factor
$((m+n)!p!m!n!)^{-1}$ is applied, leaving
$(m!n!p!)^{-1}$. The cross-pair factor is, in both parenthesisations,
\[
\prod_{\substack{i\in I_F\\j\in I_G}}\omega(x_j,x_i)
\prod_{\substack{i\in I_F\\j\in I_H}}\omega(x_j,x_i)
\prod_{\substack{i\in I_G\\j\in I_H}}\omega(x_j,x_i).
\]
No symmetry of $\omega$ is used. Associativity is the set-theoretic
associativity of refining ordered blocks together with commutativity of
the scalar cross-factors. The unit is the element $1\in\Sh_0=\mathbb{F}$,
because the cross-product over an empty block is $1$.

\paragraph{The degree-one square.}
Let $p_1(z)=z$ and write
\[
s_1=\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+\epsilon_3,\quad
s_2=\epsilon_1\epsilon_2+\epsilon_1\epsilon_3+\epsilon_2\epsilon_3,\quad
s_3=\epsilon_1\epsilon_2\epsilon_3.
\]
For $u=x_1-x_2$,
\[
\omega(x_1,x_2)=1-\frac{s_1}{u}+\frac{s_2}{u^2}-\frac{s_3}{u^3},
\qquad
\omega(x_2,x_1)=1+\frac{s_1}{u}+\frac{s_2}{u^2}+\frac{s_3}{u^3}.
\]
Hence the odd terms cancel under the two shuffle terms:
\[
\omega(x_1,x_2)+\omega(x_2,x_1)
=2+\frac{2s_2}{(x_1-x_2)^2}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{\;
(p_1\star p_1)(x_1,x_2)
=
2x_1x_2+\frac{2s_2\,x_1x_2}{(x_1-x_2)^2}.
\;}
\]
The Calabi--Yau specialisation $s_1=0$ is irrelevant for this
cancellation: the simple and triple poles cancel by the two-term
$S_2$-orbit average, while the double pole is the even part of the
kernel.

\paragraph{The degree-one cube.}
Iterating the preceding product with three singleton blocks gives
\[
p_1^{\star 3}(x_1,x_2,x_3)
=
x_1x_2x_3
\sum_{\sigma\in S_3}
\sigma\!\left[
\omega(x_2,x_1)\omega(x_3,x_1)\omega(x_3,x_2)
\right].
\]
The expression is $S_3$-symmetric because it is the orbit sum of one
ordered-block cross-factor. Its associativity follows from the
three-block formula above, not from a Yang--Baxter identity and not from
the Calabi--Yau equation $s_1=0$.

\paragraph{Positive half and vertex algebra.}
Schiffmann--Vasserot identify the critical cohomological Hall algebra of
the Jordan quiver with cubic potential, equivalently the toric
$\mathbb{C}^3$ CoHA, with the positive half
$Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$. Under this identification the
degree-one shuffle classes $z^r$ are the positive current generators.
Thus $p_1\star p_1$ is an element of $Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$,
represented by the rational shuffle function displayed above. The
diagonal double pole is part of the Hall multiplication in the shuffle
presentation.

The full affine Yangian or vertex-algebra object is a different
construction:
\[
\CoHA(\mathbb{C}^3)
=Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
\hookrightarrow
D\bigl(Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)\bigr)
=Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
\xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{ev}_\lambda\ }
\End\bigl(\Winf[\lambda]\text{-}\mathrm{vac}\bigr).
\]
The first term is an associative $\Eone$ Hall algebra. The
$\Etwo$-braided structure belongs to the Drinfeld centre of the
$\Eone$ representation category, once the double, pairing, and
completion are supplied. The vertex algebra $\Winf[\lambda]$ is obtained
through the evaluation representation of the double. The positive half
is the Hall-side input to that construction.

\paragraph{The \texorpdfstring{$d=3$}{d=3} CY-to-chiral scope.}
For the toric object over $\mathbb{C}^3$, the constructed Hall-side
output is the positive $\Eone$ algebra
$Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$. The calculation of
$p_1\star p_1$ proves a low-degree Hall multiplication formula. The
compact Hodge supertrace and the four invariants
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}},\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}},
\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}},\kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}$ are separate construction
data. Those invariants belong
to their own construction layers: $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$ to the chiral
shadow, $\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}=\chi(\mathcal{O}_X)$ to the categorical
Euler characteristic, $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}=c(0)/2$ to a Borcherds
product, and $\kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}$ to the chosen fibering datum.
