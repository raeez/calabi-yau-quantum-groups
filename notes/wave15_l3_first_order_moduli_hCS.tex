\section*{First-order moduli $H^{0,3}_{\dbar,c}(\C^3)\otimes\Sym^2(\g^\vee)^{\inv}$ (Wave-15 L3/20)}

\paragraph{Theorem (main).}
For holomorphic Chern--Simons on $\C^3$ with semisimple gauge algebra
$\g$, the tangent space to the formal moduli problem
$\Def(\Obs_{\hCS})=\HH^\ast_{E_3}(\Obs_{\hCS},\Obs_{\hCS})[3]$ in
cohomological degree zero is
\[
  T_{\Obs_{\hCS}}\Def
  \;\simeq\;
  H^{0,3}_{\dbar,c}(\C^3)\otimes\Sym^2(\g^\vee)^{\inv}
  \;\simeq\;
  \C\cdot\Omega^\vee\otimes\C\cdot\Kil
  \;\simeq\;
  \C,
\]
and the one-dimensional class is the Killing-form deformation
$\epsilon\cdot\int_{\C^3}\Omega\wedge\langle\cA,[\cA,\cA]_\epsilon\rangle$
which matches the Yangian deformation $Y_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,
\epsilon_3}(\g)$ at Costello--Gaiotto's equivariant parameter loop
$\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+\epsilon_3=0$.

\paragraph{Cycle~1: Bochner--Martinelli Serre duality on $\C^3$.}
\emph{Attack.}  The compactly-supported Dolbeault cohomology of $\C^3$
is, by Serre duality for the noncompact Stein manifold $\C^3$ paired
with the trivial holomorphic bundle,
\[
  H^{0,q}_{\dbar,c}(\C^3)
  \;\simeq\;
  H^{3-q}(\C^3,\Omega^3)^\vee,
  \qquad
  \Omega^3=\cO\cdot dz^1\!\wedge dz^2\!\wedge dz^3
  \text{ (Griffiths--Harris Ch.~3).}
\]
Since $\C^3$ is Stein and $\Omega^3$ is coherent, $H^p(\C^3,\Omega^3)=0$
for $p>0$ (Cartan's Theorem B) and $H^0(\C^3,\Omega^3)=\cO(\C^3)\cdot
dz^1\!\wedge dz^2\!\wedge dz^3$.  The compactly-supported pairing
isolates the subspace of functions whose Bochner--Martinelli residue
at infinity is nontrivial; the integration pairing
$\alpha\mapsto\int_{\C^3}\alpha\wedge\Omega$ selects a one-dimensional
quotient.  Thus
$H^{0,3}_{\dbar,c}(\C^3)\simeq\C\cdot\Omega^\vee$, dual to the
holomorphic volume form $\Omega=dz^1\wedge dz^2\wedge dz^3$.
\emph{Heal.}  The noncompactness of $\C^3$ forces the compact-support
condition; without it $H^{0,3}_{\dbar}(\C^3)=0$ (the Dolbeault lemma).
The Bochner--Martinelli kernel \emph{explicitly} realises the
residue pairing (Griffiths--Harris Ch.~3 \S~5), so the duality is
chain-level, not merely categorical.

\paragraph{Cycle~2: the $\Sym^2(\g^\vee)^{\inv}$ factor.}
\emph{Attack.}  The Francis--Tamarkin identification (Wave-14 I3)
gives, for semisimple $\g$, the degree-zero piece of the $E_3$
Hochschild complex as
\[
  \HH^0_{E_3}(\Obs_{\hCS},\Obs_{\hCS})[3]
  \;\simeq\;
  H^{0,3}_{\dbar,c}(\C^3)\otimes\Sym^\bullet(\g^\vee)^{\inv}\big|_{\text{wt 2}},
\]
where the weight-2 restriction arises because the $E_3$-operad
quadratic in $\g$ (the bracket-of-bracket piece) is the first
non-linear contribution after the linear $\dbar$-term.  Chevalley's
classical result ($\Sym^\bullet(\g^\vee)^\g$ is a polynomial ring on
$\mathrm{rk}\,\g$ generators for $\g$ semisimple; Chevalley 1955) gives
$\dim\Sym^2(\g^\vee)^{\inv}=1$ at weight~2 for simple $\g$, spanned
by the Killing form $\Kil(X,Y)=\Tr_{\mathrm{ad}}(XY)$.
\emph{Heal.}  For $\g=\g_1\oplus\cdots\oplus\g_r$ with simple
factors, $\dim\Sym^2(\g^\vee)^{\inv}=r$ (one invariant form per
simple factor).  The single-factor case $\g$ simple is the canonical
one and gives the one-dimensional moduli stated.

\paragraph{Cycle~3: the Yangian deformation.}
\emph{Attack.}  Costello 2013 (\S~5 of arXiv:1303.2632) and
Costello--Gaiotto 2018 (\S~3 of arXiv:1810.01970) construct a
deformation of the Chern--Simons action $S_{\hCS}=\frac{1}{2}\int_{\C^3}
\Omega\wedge\Tr(\cA\,\dbar\cA+\tfrac{2}{3}\cA^3)$ by adding the
Killing-twisted cubic
\[
  \delta S_\epsilon
  \;=\;
  \epsilon\int_{\C^3}\Omega\wedge\Kil\!\bigl(\cA,[\cA,\cA]_\epsilon\bigr),
\]
where $[\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,]_\epsilon$ is the deformed Lie bracket
controlled by the Miura shift on the Yangian side.  This is exactly
the Maurer--Cartan element in
$H^{0,3}_{\dbar,c}(\C^3)\otimes\Sym^2(\g^\vee)^{\inv}$ under the
identification of Cycle~2: the $\Omega$-factor is the generator of
$H^{0,3}_{\dbar,c}(\C^3)$, the $\Kil$-factor is the generator of
$\Sym^2(\g^\vee)^{\inv}$, and the tensor product pairs them into the
unique first-order deformation.  Gaiotto--Rap\v{c}\'ak 2017
(arXiv:1703.00982 \S~2) recognise the resulting deformed $E_3$
algebra as the equivariant Yangian $Y_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,
\epsilon_3}(\g)$ modulo the Calabi--Yau trace condition
$\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+\epsilon_3=0$.
\emph{Heal.}  The trace condition is not optional: it is the
statement that $\Omega$ is $\mathrm{SL}_3$-invariant (not merely
$\mathrm{GL}_3$-invariant), i.e.\ $\C^3$ is Calabi--Yau of dimension
$3$.  The Yangian family $Y_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3}$
nominally has three parameters; the CY condition cuts it to the
two-parameter family and the remaining tangent direction at
$\epsilon=0$ is the one-dimensional moduli computed above.

\paragraph{Cycle~4: higher-order obstructions vanish.}
\emph{Attack.}  The obstruction to extending a first-order deformation
$\mu_1\in H^{0,3}_{\dbar,c}(\C^3)\otimes\Sym^2(\g^\vee)^{\inv}$ to
second order lives in $\HH^4_{E_3}(\Obs_{\hCS},\Obs_{\hCS})[3]\simeq
H^{0,4}_{\dbar,c}(\C^3)\otimes\Sym^3(\g^\vee)^{\inv}$.  But $\C^3$
has complex dimension $3$, hence $\Omega^{0,4}(\C^3)=0$ as a bundle,
hence $H^{0,4}_{\dbar,c}(\C^3)=0$ identically.  All higher-order
obstructions likewise vanish: $H^{0,q}_{\dbar,c}(\C^3)=0$ for $q>3$.
Thus the first-order deformation extends uniquely to all orders
(Francis 2013 Thm~1.1 combined with Kodaira--Spencer unobstructedness).
\emph{Heal.}  The acyclicity $H^{0,q>3}=0$ on $\C^3$ is the
\emph{nilpotent filtration}: the graded pieces of the
Hodge-to-de~Rham spectral sequence truncate at the complex dimension,
so the formal moduli problem is smooth at $\Obs_{\hCS}$ and the
moduli space is unobstructed.  This is the $(\infty,1)$-categorical
shadow of the chain-level statement $\Omega^{0,q}(\C^3)=0$ for $q>3$.

\paragraph{Cycle~5: moduli of 6D hCS-like theories.}
\emph{Attack.}  Combining Cycles~1--4: for fixed semisimple $\g$, the
moduli space $\mathcal{M}_{\hCS}(\C^3,\g)$ of 6D holomorphic
Chern--Simons-type theories with gauge algebra $\g$ has tangent space
\[
  T_{\Obs_{\hCS}}\mathcal{M}_{\hCS}(\C^3,\g)
  \;=\;
  \C\cdot\Kil
  \;\simeq\;
  \C,
\]
unobstructed to all orders, and the deformation family is exactly the
Costello--Gaiotto Yangian $Y_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3}(\g)$
at the Calabi--Yau slice $\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+\epsilon_3=0$.  The
$\kch$-invariant of the deformed $E_3$ algebra varies linearly with
$\epsilon$; at $\epsilon=0$ it recovers $\kch(\Obs_{\hCS}^{(0)})=
\chi(\cO_{\C^3})=1$ by the Hodge-supertrace identification on
non-compact CY$_3$ (restricted to the Stein locus).
\emph{Heal.}  For non-simple $\g=\bigoplus_i\g_i$ the tangent space is
$r$-dimensional with one Killing deformation per simple factor; the
corresponding moduli space is $\prod_i\mathcal{M}_{\hCS}(\C^3,\g_i)$
and the Yangian factorises as $\bigotimes_i Y_{\epsilon^{(i)}}(\g_i)$.
The Calabi--Yau slice condition applies per simple factor.

\paragraph{Concordance.}
Wave-14 I3 computed the abelian case where $\Sym^2(\g^\vee)^{\inv}=
\Sym^2(\g^\vee)$ is full (no invariance) and the deformation complex
reduces to the $\dbar$-twisted symmetric algebra.  The present
computation is the nonabelian refinement: invariance cuts the
symmetric square to the one-dimensional Killing line, matching the
Costello--Gaiotto Yangian uniqueness.  The two statements together
exhaust the semisimple $\g$ first-order deformation theory of
$\Obs_{\hCS}(\C^3)$.  Both lanes are load-bearing: the chain-level
Bochner--Martinelli realisation gives the explicit Maurer--Cartan
element $\Omega\otimes\Kil$, the $(\infty,1)$-categorical
Francis--Tamarkin identification gives the tangent space of the
formal moduli problem.
