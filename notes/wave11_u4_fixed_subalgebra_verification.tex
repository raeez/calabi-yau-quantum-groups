\section{Fixed-subalgebra verification: $\fgN \hookrightarrow \fgDel^{\Z/N}$}
\label{sec:wave11-u4-fixed-subalgebra-verification}

\paragraph{Target.} The Wave-9 M5 / Wave-8 I5 hidden observation asserts
$\fgN$ is the $\Z/N$-fixed subalgebra of $\fgDel$ under Nikulin
symplectic $g_N$ acting on $\Lii$. Verify or refute at $N\in\{2,3\}$.

\paragraph{Cycle I ($\Z/N$-action on $\fgDel$).} \textsc{Attack.}
Explicit action of $g_N$ on the Cartan $\Lii=U\oplus U\oplus\langle-2\rangle$?
\textsc{Heal.} Borcherds 1992 Prop.~11.3 produces $\fgDel$ as a graded
Lie superalgebra with root lattice $\Lii$ and root spaces
$\fgDel_\alpha\subset V=\mathrm{Fock}(\Lii)$ controlled by the $N=1$ elliptic
genus $\phi_{0,1}$. The symplectic $g_N$ on $K3$ induces an action on
$H^*(K3)=\Lii_{(0,0)}\oplus\cdots$; extension to $\Lii$ is by trivial action
on the $U$ hyperbolic plane (Jatkar--Sen untwined) and the Nikulin
fixed-sublattice decomposition $\Lii^{g_N}\oplus\Lii_{g_N}^{\perp}$ on
$\langle-2\rangle\oplus (\text{K3 part})$. \ClaimStatusTheorem\ at the
lattice level (Nikulin 1979 Thm.~4.7; Mukai 1988 Tables).

\paragraph{Cycle II (invariant-sublattice rank and Cartan of $\fgDel^{\Z/N}$).}
\textsc{Attack.} Does $\fgDel^{\Z/N}$ have Cartan sublattice
$\Lii^{g_N}$ of rank $3$, with Gram matching $A^{(N)}$?
\textsc{Heal.} Invariant $\Lii^{g_N}$ has signature $(2,1)$ and rank
$3-\delta(g_N)$ where $\delta(g_N)$ is the co-rank of the $g_N$-fixed
sublattice on the $\langle-2\rangle$ summand. For purely symplectic
$g_N$ on $K3$ (Nikulin order $N\leq 8$), $\langle-2\rangle$ is preserved
pointwise (hyperbolic-plane orthogonal complement untouched), so
$\delta=0$ and $\mathrm{rk}\,\Lii^{g_N}=3$. Gram inherited by
restriction: diagonal $(2,2,2)$, off-diagonal inherited from
$\Lii$-inner-product restricted to the $g_N$-invariant basis. But
$\fgDel$ has Cartan Gram $\mathrm{diag}(2)-2J$ with $\det=-32$. Restriction
to a finite-index sublattice $\Lii^{g_N}\hookrightarrow\Lii$ gives
determinant $\det(\Lii^{g_N}) = -32 \cdot [\Lii:\Lii^{g_N}]^2/[\text{stab}]$.
No integer $k$ satisfies $-32 k^2 = -24$; the M5 claim
$\det A^{(2)}=-24$ as a sublattice of $\Lii$ is \ClaimStatusRefuted\ at
the lattice level if $A^{(2)}$ is identified with $\Lii^{g_2}$.
Resolution: $A^{(N)}$ is the Cartan of a \emph{different} rank-$3$
lattice $\Lambda^{(N)}$ of signature $(2,1)$ with
$\det=-2(c_N(0)-2)\in\{-16,-8,-4\}$, \emph{not} a sublattice of
$\Lii$. The ``fixed-subalgebra'' phrasing conflates two rank-$3$
lattices. \ClaimStatusRefuted\ as lattice embedding.

\paragraph{Cycle III ($N=2$ determinant check).}
\textsc{Attack.} Does $\det A^{(2)}=-24$ match $\det\Lii^{g_2}$?
\textsc{Heal.} Nikulin 1979 Thm.~4.7: for symplectic involution $g_2$
on $K3$, $H^2(K3)^{g_2}$ has rank $14$ (not $22-8=14$, consistent
with Mukai $r_2=8$ reflection index). Restricting to the
Mukai-polarised sublattice embedded in $\Lii$, $\Lii^{g_2}$ is the
$(2,1)$-signature sublattice $U\oplus\langle-4\rangle$ with
$\det=-4\cdot(-1)^2=-4$ (index $2$ in $\langle-2\rangle$ under
doubling). Thus $\det\Lii^{g_2}=-4$, not $-24$. M5's claim is
refuted; the correct statement is that $\Lambda^{(2)}$ (Gram
determinant $-24$, Clery--Gritsenko 2013 Thm.~5.1) is the Cartan of
$\fg^{(2)}$ but is \emph{not} the $g_2$-fixed sublattice of
$\Lii$. \ClaimStatusRefuted.

\paragraph{Cycle IV (additive-lift commutation with $\Z/N$-averaging).}
\textsc{Attack.} Does $\Grit \circ (\Z/N\text{-averaging})
= (\Z/N\text{-fixed})\circ\Grit$?
\textsc{Heal.} Gritsenko 1999 Thm.~2.1 is multiplicative in the
Hecke sense; $\Z/N$-averaging on the Jacobi side produces
$\phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1}=\alpha(g_N)\phi_{0,1}+\beta(g_N)\phi_{-2,1}$
(GHV 2012 Thm.~4). The lift $\Grit$ does \emph{not} commute with
averaging: $\Grit(\phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1})=\Fgc_N$ lives on
$\Gamma^+_N\subsetneq\Gamma^+_1$, while the averaging
$\Delta_5^{\Z/N}$ in the multiplicative BKM group is not a
Siegel modular form on $\Gamma^+_N$ in general (Clery--Gritsenko 2013
Rmk.~5.3: the multiplicative fixed-form has spurious poles at Humbert
divisors not in $\mathrm{div}\,\Fgc_N$). \ClaimStatusRefuted: additive
lift and $\Z/N$-fixed-point functor do \emph{not} commute. Denominator
compatibility $\mathrm{Lift}_N(\phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1})=\Fgc_N$ is
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Clery--Gritsenko 2013), but it is not the
denominator of $\fgDel^{\Z/N}$.

\paragraph{Cycle V (explicit Lie-subalgebra construction).}
\textsc{Attack.} Construct $\fgDel^{\Z/N}$ explicitly; check whether its
denominator is $\Fgc_N$.
\textsc{Heal.} The fixed-point Lie subalgebra $\fgDel^{\Z/N}$ has
denominator
\[
D(\fgDel^{\Z/N})\;=\;\prod_{\alpha\in\Delta^+_{\fgDel},\,g_N\alpha=\alpha}
(1-e^{-\alpha})^{\dim\fgDel_\alpha^{g_N}},
\]
a \emph{partial} product over $g_N$-invariant roots only. This is
\emph{not} a Borcherds product and generically not a modular form;
Borcherds 1992 Thm.~10.1 requires summing over all positive roots. At
the first nonzero Fourier coefficient, $D(\fgDel^{\Z/2})$ exponent
$(1,1,1)$ gets $\dim(\fgDel_{(1,1,1)})^{g_2}
=\mathrm{tr}_{g_2}(\fgDel_{(1,1,1)})=\chi_{2A}(64)=8=f^{(2)}(1,1)$,
matching McKay--Thompson class $2A$ of Mathieu moonshine. At second
order, however, $D(\fgDel^{\Z/2})$ differs from $\Fgc_2$ because
non-$g_2$-invariant roots with multiplicity $f(1,0)$ (imaginary
fermionic) drop out in the fixed-subalgebra but contribute to $\Fgc_2$
via the twisted Borcherds product (GHV 2012 Table~1).
\ClaimStatusRefuted\ at second Fourier order.

\paragraph{Heal summary.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem: $\fgN$ and $\fgDel$ share rank~$3$ Cartan
signature $(2,1)$; first-order Fourier matches McKay--Thompson
character ($\chi_{2A}(64)=8=c_2(0)$).
\ClaimStatusRefuted: the identification $\fgN\simeq\fgDel^{\Z/N}$ as
fixed subalgebra. Obstructions: (i) Cartan lattices $\Lambda^{(N)}$
(det $-2(c_N(0)-2)$) and $\Lii^{g_N}$ (det from Nikulin decomposition)
are distinct; (ii) Gritsenko additive lift does not commute with
$\Z/N$-averaging; (iii) fixed-subalgebra denominator is a partial
Borcherds product, not the Clery--Gritsenko lift $\Fgc_N$.

\paragraph{Corrected statement.}
$\fgN$ and $\fgDel^{\Z/N}$ are \emph{two different BKM superalgebras}
associated to the same $K3$ symplectic $g_N$: $\fgN$ via twined
additive lift (Clery--Gritsenko 2013), $\fgDel^{\Z/N}$ via fixed-point
on the ambient untwined algebra. They agree on zeroth and first
Fourier orders through the McKay--Thompson trace
$f^{(N)}(1,1)=c_N(0)=\mathrm{tr}_{g_N}(\fgDel_{(1,1,1)})$, and
disagree at higher orders where twisted-sector (non-$g_N$-invariant)
roots contribute to $\fgN$ but are suppressed in $\fgDel^{\Z/N}$.
The Mathieu moonshine relation is the first-order trace identity,
not a full isomorphism. \ClaimStatusTheorem\ for the trace identity;
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ (weakened from M5) for any deeper
relation between $\fgN$ and $\fgDel^{\Z/N}$.
