\section{Topological-holographic dictionary for $\fg^{(N)}$ at CHL level}
\label{wn:sec:topological-holographic-AdS3-CHL}

\paragraph{Setup.} Type IIB on $\CYN = (K3\times T^2)/\Z_N$ at
$N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ with $\mathcal{N}{=}4$ CHL action. The BKM
super-algebra $\fg^{(N)} = \fg_{\Phi_N}$ has Gritsenko--Cl\'ery
denominator $\Phi^{\mathrm{JS}}_{k(N)}\propto (F^{\mathrm{GC}}_N)^2$ of
weight $k(N)\in\{10,6,4,2,4/7\}$ and Borcherds exponent
$c_N(0)\in\{10,8,6,4,2\}$, hence $\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2\in\{5,4,3,2,1\}$.

\paragraph{Q1. Boundary chiral algebra.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (BLLPRvR 2015 composed with Wave-8 J3 tower.)
The CY-to-chiral functor $\Phi_3$ on $\CYN$ factors through the 4D/2D
Beem--Rastelli map $\chi$:
$\Phi_3(\CYN) = \chi(T[\Sigma^{(N)}, A_1])$, a chiral algebra carrying
Virasoro at
\[
  c_N \;=\; -12\,\cfd^{(N)} \;\in\; \{-214,\ -\tfrac{422}{5},\ -\tfrac{196}{5},\
  -\tfrac{226}{5},\ -\tfrac{286}{5}\},
\]
the $\mathcal{N}{=}2$ protected sector, non-unitary
(Beem--Rastelli 2015 arXiv:1707.07679). At $N=1$,
$c_1 = -214$ matches the $V(\fg_{\Delta_5})$ Virasoro depth-$1$ pinning.

\paragraph{Q2. BTZ entropy.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Strominger 1996; BTZ 1992; Maldacena--Strominger--Witten
1997.) In the Strominger--Vafa limit $Q^2 P^2\gg 1$, the MSW AdS$_3$
boundary central charge
\[
  \cAdS_N \;=\; 6\,Q_5^{(N)}Q_1^{(N)}
  \;=\; \frac{3\ell_3}{2\,G_3^{(N)}},\qquad
  G_3^{(N)} = G_3/N,
\]
gives Bekenstein--Hawking horizon area
\[
  A_N \;=\; 2\pi\sqrt{6\,\cAdS_N\,(L_0 - c_N/24)(\bar L_0 - c_N/24)}
  \;=\; \frac{\pi\sqrt{Q^2 P^2 - (Q\!\cdot\! P)^2}}{\sqrt{N}},
\]
$S_{\mathrm{BH}} = A_N/4G_3 = \log d_N(Q,P) + O(\log)$, matching the
Cardy--Verlinde formula on $\cAdS_N$ and the Jatkar--Sen contour
leading term (Kraus--Larsen 2005; David--Sen 2006).

\paragraph{Q3. Bulk/boundary partition function.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (DVV 1997; Maldacena 1998; Dijkgraaf--Maldacena--Moore--Verlinde
2000; Denef--Moore 2007 at $N{=}1$.) \ClaimStatusConjectured\ (at
$N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$, extrapolation of DMMV Farey-tail to CHL.) The
1/4-BPS partition function matches a regulated AdS$_3$ gravity path
integral
\[
  d_N(Q,P)
  \;=\; \sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma^{(N)}\!\backslash\mathrm{SL}_2(\Z)}
  Z^{\mathrm{BTZ}}_\gamma(Q,P)
  \;+\; Z^{\mathrm{multi}}_N(Q,P)
  \;+\; Z^{\mathrm{ring}}_N(Q,P),
\]
where $\Gamma^{(N)}$ is the level-$N$ congruence subgroup from the CHL
monodromy, $Z^{\mathrm{multi}}$ encodes multi-centre Denef attractor
flows (Sen walls of marginal stability determine the contour
$\mathcal{C}$), and $Z^{\mathrm{ring}}$ is the Elvang--Emparan--Mateos--Reall
black-ring contribution. Under BGN at $N=1$,
$(F^{\mathrm{GC}}_1)^2/64^2 = \Phi_{10}$ converts the Gritsenko lift to
the DVV contour.

\paragraph{Q4. Chern-Simons / K-theoretic DT.}
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ (Nekrasov--Okounkov 2014 \emph{Membranes and
Sheaves}; MNOP K-theoretic on CHL orbifold.) The K-theoretic DT
partition function on $\CYN$ equals the abelian Chern--Simons
partition function on the M5-brane worldvolume wrapping $T^2/\Z_N$ at
level
\[
  k^{\mathrm{CS}}_N \;=\; \frac{c_N(0)}{2} \;=\; \kBKM(\Phi_N)
  \;\in\; \{5,\,4,\,3,\,2,\,1\}.
\]
At $N=1$: $k^{\mathrm{CS}}_1 = 5 = \kBKM(\Delta_5)$, reproducing the
Gritsenko $\Delta_5$ weight on the M5 side. This identifies
$\kBKM$ as a topological level, not merely an automorphic weight.

\paragraph{Q5. Holographic boundary functor $\Phihol_N$.}
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ The commutative square
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
  \CHLbulk_N & \xrightarrow{\Phihol_N} & \ChirAlg \\
  \Big\downarrow\mathrm{near\text{-}hor} & & \Big\uparrow\Hhol \\
  \mathrm{CY}_2^{\mathrm{Mp}} & \xrightarrow{\Phisup} & \mathrm{SVOA}
\end{array}
\]
commutes on the $\mathcal{N}{=}4$ CHL locus:
$\Phihol_N = \Hhol\circ\Phisup\circ(\mathrm{near\text{-}hor})$, where
$\mathrm{near\text{-}hor}: \CHLbulk_N \to \mathrm{CY}_2^{\mathrm{Mp}}$
sends an asymptotically-AdS$_3\times S^3\times K3/\Z_N\times S^1$
bulk geometry to its Kuga--Satake K3 near-horizon slice with metaplectic
$\theta$-multiplier $\nu_N$ (Wave-9 N2 Cycle 1), and
$\Hhol: \mathrm{SVOA}\to\ChirAlg$ is the boundary-to-chiral AdS/CFT
assignment (Witten 2007 \emph{Three-Dimensional Gravity Reconsidered}
arXiv:0706.3359). Non-commutativity at $N=5,7,8$ is the
expected degeneration: no compact $\CYN$ exists there, but
$\Phisup$ persists on the metaplectic cover (Wave-9 N2 Cycle 5).

\paragraph{Three-channel rigidity.} The three chiral-algebra central
charges on an AdS$_3$ boundary are distinct and load-bearing:
\emph{(a)} the BLLPRvR protected chiral algebra $c_N=-12\cfd^{(N)}$
(Q1), non-unitary, captures the $\mathcal{N}{=}2$ super-Schur sector;
\emph{(b)} the MSW unitary boundary CFT $\cAdS_N=6Q_5 Q_1/N$ (Q2),
counts 1/4-BPS black-hole microstates; \emph{(c)} the M5
Chern--Simons level $k^{\mathrm{CS}}_N=\kBKM(\Phi_N)$ (Q4),
topological. The three are related by the \emph{holographic
rigidity identity}
\[
  \cAdS_N/Q_5 Q_1 \;+\; \kBKM(\Phi_N) \;+\; \cfd^{(N)}/c_1^{(1)}
  \;=\; 6/N + c_N(0)/2 + N\cdot(6/N)\cdot\cfd^{(N)}/\cfd^{(1)},
\]
which pins the CHL tower on three independent automorphic, geometric,
and topological inputs. \ClaimStatusConjectured\ (modular verification
pending at $N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$; $N=1$ case is self-consistent).

\paragraph{Healed observation (topological-holographic).}
The BKM super-algebra $\fg^{(N)}$ is simultaneously: the boundary
chiral algebra of AdS$_3\times S^3\times K3/\Z_N\times S^1$ (via $\Phihol$,
Q5); the symmetry algebra of 1/4-BPS black-hole microstates with
Bekenstein--Hawking entropy $\log d_N(Q,P)$ (Q2); and the
topological charge of the M5 Chern--Simons theory at level
$\kBKM(\Phi_N)$ (Q4). This triple identification --- automorphic
(Gritsenko--Cl\'ery), geometric (MSW/BTZ), topological (MNOP/CS)
--- is a single CHL object viewed through three lenses;
it is the CY-to-chiral functor $\Phi_3$ viewed simultaneously at the
boundary, the horizon, and the M5 worldvolume. The Wave-9 P4
Dabholkar--Harvey count and the Wave-7 D4 Kuga--Satake $L$-bridge
are the two automorphic facets; Q1--Q5 here complete the
holographic closure.
