\section{Topological holography for \(\fg^{(N)}\) at CHL level}
\label{wn:sec:topological-holographic-AdS3-CHL}

\paragraph{Primitive CHL denominator.}
Let
\[
  \CYN=(K3\times T^2)/\Z_N,\qquad N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\},
\]
with the \(\mathcal N=4\) CHL action. The primitive Borcherds product
\(F_N^{\mathrm{BKM}}\) has constant term and BKM weight
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
N & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 6\\ \hline
c_N(0) & 10 & 8 & 6 & 4 & 2\\
\kBKM(F_N^{\mathrm{BKM}})=c_N(0)/2 & 5 & 4 & 3 & 2 & 1
\end{array}
\]
by Borcherds' weight theorem applied to the Gritsenko and Nikulin
paramodular denominator tower. For \(N=1\),
\[
  F_1^{\mathrm{BKM}}=\Delta_5
  =\mathrm{Grit}(\eta^9\vartheta_1)\in S_5(K(1)),
  \qquad
  \fg_{\Delta_5}=\mathrm{Borch}(F_3,\phi_{0,1}^{K3}),
\]
and root multiplicities are the K3 elliptic-genus Fourier coefficients
in the \(\phi_{0,1}\) convention.

\paragraph{\(K3\times E\) normalisation.}
For \(Y=K3\times E\),
\[
  \kch(\mathcal A_Y)=
  \sum_{q=0}^{3}(-1)^q h^{0,q}(Y)
  =1-1+1-1=0,
\]
and
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|l}
\text{invariant} & \text{value} & \text{construction}\\ \hline
\kcat(Y) & 0 & \chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\chi(\mathcal O_E)=2\cdot0\\
\kHeis(Y) & 3 & \text{Heisenberg Mukai specialisation}\\
\kBKM(\Delta_5) & 5 & c_1(0)/2=10/2=\mathrm{wt}(\Delta_5)\\
\kfiber(Y) & 24 & \mathrm{rk}\,\widetilde H(K3,\Z)=1+22+1
\end{array}
\]
The compact Hodge supertrace \(0\), the Heisenberg reading \(3\), the
Borcherds weight \(5\), the Mukai fibre rank \(24\), and the categorical
Euler value \(0\) are different constructions. No additive identity
\(\kBKM=\kch+\chi(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{fiber}})\) is available; at
\(N=1\) the right side is \(0+0\) for the elliptic fibre and \(0+2\)
for the K3 fibre, while \(\kBKM(\Delta_5)=5\).

\paragraph{Physical square.}
The \(N=1\) physical reduced DT and dyon denominator is the square of
the primitive form:
\[
  \Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_5^2,\qquad
  D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5,\qquad
  \Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}=D_5^2.
\]
Oberdieck and Pandharipande's reduced DT normalisation gives
\[
  Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{DT}}(K3\times E)
  =
  -(\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}})^{-1}
  =
  -4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}.
\]
The square doubles the modular weight and the reciprocal exponent. The
primitive BKM algebra remains attached to \(\Delta_5\), not to
\(\Delta_5^2\).

\paragraph{Gritsenko and Clery diagonal divisor catalogue.}
The CHL ladder above is not the Gritsenko and Clery diagonal divisor
catalogue. The diagonal divisor list has eight rows
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
(t,N) & \mathrm{wt}(F_{t,N})\\ \hline
(1,1) & 5\\
(2,1) & 2\\
(1,2) & 3\\
(3,1) & 1\\
(1,3) & 2\\
(4,1) & 1/2\\
(1,4) & 3/2\\
(2,2) & 1
\end{array}
\]
with twice-weight constants
\[
  (10,4,6,2,4,1,3,2).
\]
It coincides with the CHL denominator ladder only at
\((t,N)=(1,1)\), where \(F_{1,1}=\Delta_5\). Every other row has its
own paramodular group, divisor branch, multiplier, and Borcherds input.

\paragraph{Mathieu table and CHL rows.}
The ordinary \(M_{24}\) twining table has \(26\) conjugacy classes. The
five CHL denominator rows are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
N & [g] & \text{frame shape} & c_N(0)\\ \hline
1 & 1A & 1^{24} & 10\\
2 & 2A & 1^8 2^8 & 8\\
3 & 3A & 1^6 3^6 & 6\\
4 & 4B & 1^4 2^2 4^4 & 4\\
6 & 6A & 1^2 2^2 3^2 6^2 & 2
\end{array}
\]
Same-order ordinary Mathieu rows remain separate. In particular
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
3B & 3^8\\
4A & 2^4 4^4\\
4B & 1^4 2^2 4^4\\
6B & 6^4\\
10A & 2^2 10^2\\
12A & 2^1 4^1 6^1 12^1\\
12B & 12^2
\end{array}
\]
are the frame shapes used by the ordinary table.

\paragraph{Protected vertex algebra.}
At the untwisted \(N=1\) class \(\mathcal S\) point, the
\(A_1\)-theory on \(\Sigma_{0,24}\) has
\[
  c_{4d}=\frac{107}{6},\qquad
  c_{2d}=-12c_{4d}=-214,
\]
and the Beem and Rastelli map supplies the protected vertex algebra
\(\chi(\mathcal T[A_1,\Sigma_{0,24}])\). This is a 4d/2d protected
sector. A theorem comparing it with the \(d=3\) CY output must name the
two-stage specialisation
\[
  \Phi_3(Y)\in E_1\text{-}\ChirAlg
\]
and construct the boundary map from that \(E_1\)-chiral object to the
protected vertex algebra. An \(E_2\)-structure belongs to a centre or a
Drinfeld double after the Hall hypotheses are supplied.

\paragraph{MSW and BTZ comparison datum.}
The universal Brown and Henneaux formula is
\[
  c_{\mathrm{BH}}=\frac{3\ell_3}{2G_3}.
\]
For the CHL quotient one often writes the large-charge MSW normalisation
\[
  \cAdS_N=\frac{6Q_5Q_1}{N},
\]
with \(Q_1,Q_5\) measured in the selected CHL charge lattice. A theorem
identifying this CFT with a BKM or Hall object has the following proof
obligation: specify the charge lattice \(L_N\), the index
\[
  d_N(Q,P)=[e^{2\pi i(Q,P)\cdot Z}]
  \bigl(\Phi_N^{\mathrm{phys}}(Z)\bigr)^{-1},
\]
the contour \(\mathcal C_N\), the wall-crossing chamber, the boundary
chiral algebra, and the comparison theorem linking the Cardy saddle to
that index. Without those five data the AdS\(_3\) statement is a
physical interpretation of the automorphic index, not a proved algebra
isomorphism.

\paragraph{Partition functions.}
At \(N=1\), the proved modular identity is the DVV and DMMV denominator
identity together with the Oberdieck and Pandharipande normalisation
\[
  Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{DT}}(K3\times E)
  =
  -4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}.
\]
For \(N\in\{2,3,4,6\}\), a theorem-level CHL Farey-tail equality must
fix the Jatkar and Sen congruence subgroup, Fourier-Jacobi
normalisation, charge chamber, and multi-centre subtraction. Its target
shape is
\[
  d_N(Q,P)=
  \sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma^{(N)}\backslash\mathrm{SL}_2(\Z)}
  Z^{\mathrm{BTZ}}_\gamma(Q,P)
  +Z^{\mathrm{multi}}_N(Q,P)
  +Z^{\mathrm{ring}}_N(Q,P),
\]
where \(Z^{\mathrm{multi}}_N\) records Denef multi-centre attractor
flows and \(Z^{\mathrm{ring}}_N\) records the Elvang, Emparan, Mateos,
and Reall black-ring sector.

\paragraph{Chern-Simons scalar.}
The scalar available to a Chern-Simons comparison is the primitive BKM
weight
\[
  k_N^{\mathrm{BKM}}
  =
  \kBKM(F_N^{\mathrm{BKM}})
  =
  \frac{c_N(0)}2
  \in\{5,4,3,2,1\}.
\]
At \(N=1\), \(k_1^{\mathrm{BKM}}=\kBKM(\Delta_5)=5\). A theorem that
identifies \(k_N^{\mathrm{BKM}}\) with a worldvolume Chern-Simons level
must specify the M5 worldvolume theory, gauge group, line bundle
normalisation, anomaly inflow term, and comparison from the
Borcherds weight to the Chern-Simons action.

\paragraph{Boundary square.}
The precise holographic comparison theorem has source and target
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
  \CHLbulk_N & \xrightarrow{\Phihol_N} & E_1\text{-}\ChirAlg\\
  \Big\downarrow\mathrm{near\text{-}hor} & &
  \Big\uparrow\Hhol\\
  \CY_2^{\Mp} & \xrightarrow{\Phisup} & \SVOA .
\end{array}
\]
Its proof obligation is the construction of the near-horizon map, the
metaplectic multiplier \(\nu_N\), the super-VOA
\(\Phisup(K3,\nu_N)\), the boundary functor \(\Hhol\), and a natural
isomorphism of the two composites. The compact CY\(_3\) CHL levels are
\(N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}\). Metaplectic Gritsenko and Clery rows outside
that list belong to the \(\CY_2^{\Mp}\) period-side construction.

\paragraph{Separated channels.}
The primitive BKM algebra \(\fg^{(N)}\) records the automorphic
symmetry of \(F_N^{\mathrm{BKM}}\). The MSW CFT records the
large-charge black-hole index after charge lattice, contour, and
chamber are fixed. The Chern-Simons comparison records the scalar
\(\kBKM(F_N^{\mathrm{BKM}})\) only after a worldvolume action and
normalisation are specified. The common label \(N\) does not identify
these three objects.
