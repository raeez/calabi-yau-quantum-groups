\section*{Wave-19 Y1: $N=4$ CHL positive-half geometry and
$\fg^{(4)}_{\BPS}$}
\label{wn:sec:wave19-y1-N4-positive-half-BPS}

\paragraph{The threefold.}
Let $g_4 \in \Auts(K3)$ be the Mukai-symplectic $\bZ_4$-action at
Mathieu class $4B$, cycle shape $1^{4}2^{2}4^{4}$, acting on a generic
K3 with the $g_4$-fixed period lying on the Humbert divisor of
codimension $22 - r_4 = 8$ (Nikulin 1980, \emph{Finite automorphism
groups of K\"ahler K3 surfaces}, \emph{Trudy Moscow} 38 Theorem~4.5;
Hashimoto 2012, \emph{Tohoku Math.\ J.} 64:105 Table~2). The fixed
locus on $K3$ is four isolated points of type $A_3$; the
Mukai-invariant sublattice $\Lambda_{K3}^{g_4} \subset
\Lambda_{K3} = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ has rank $r_4 = 14$ and
coinvariant signature $(0,8)$. Let $t_4 \in E[4]$ be a
$4$-torsion translation of exact order. The diagonal
$\langle(g_4, t_4)\rangle \cong \bZ_4$ acts freely on
$K3 \times E$ (the $g_4$-fixed points at $(p, \cdot) \in K3 \times E$
are displaced by $t_4 \ne 0 \in E$), and
\[
  X_4 \;=\; (K3 \times E) \big/ \langle (g_4, t_4)\rangle
\]
is the CHL Calabi--Yau threefold at level $4$
(Chaudhuri--Hockney--Lykken 1995, \texttt{arXiv:hep-th/9506144}).
Symplecticity of $g_4$ and translation invariance of the elliptic
form preserve the product holomorphic $3$-form, descending to
$\omega_{X_4} \in H^{3,0}(X_4)$; hence $X_4$ is smooth CY$_3$.
Four-invariant accounting: $\kcat(X_4) = 0$ (K\"unneth-multiplicative,
$\chi(\cO_E) = 0$); $\kfib^{(4)} = r_4 = 14$; $\kBKM(\FGC_4) = 1$;
$\kch$ via $\Phi$ on $\cA_{X_4}$ (cycle~3 below).

\paragraph{Cycle 1 (attack--heal): geometric fibration.}
\emph{Attack.} The projection $\pi: X_4 \to E/\langle t_4\rangle
\cong E_4$ exists because the $\bZ_4$-action descends from
$K3 \times E$ and the K3 fibres are permuted by $g_4$; the quotient
$E_4 = E/\langle t_4\rangle$ is the $4$-isogenous elliptic curve
in the upper-half-plane direction $\tau \mapsto \tau/4$
(or $4\tau$ per isogeny orientation).
\emph{Heal.} Each fibre $\pi^{-1}(e) \subset X_4$ is a smooth K3 for
$e \notin (\Fix(g_4)_{K3}/\langle t_4\rangle)$; the
four $A_3$-singular K3 fibres over the four branch points of
$E \to E_4$ are resolved crepantly
(Bridgeland--King--Reid 2001, \emph{JAMS} 14:535 Theorem~1.2) on the
BCR crepant resolution $\widetilde{K3/\bZ_4} \to [K3/\bZ_4]$, so
$X_4$ admits the free-fibre elliptic structure with four
marked base points.

\paragraph{Cycle 2 (attack--heal): twisted reduced DT character.}
\emph{Attack.} The Maulik--Toda cosection
$\sigma: T_{\cM_{\DT}} \to \cO$ (Maulik--Toda 2018, \emph{Invent.\
Math.} 213:1017 \S4) exists on $X_4$ because $g_4$ is symplectic:
$H^{0,2}(X_4)^{g_4} = \bC \omega_{K3}^{g_4}$ is one-dimensional,
giving the reduced DT theory on $X_4$ and a
Bryan--Steinberg-admissible cosection lift.
\emph{Heal.} The reduced orbifold DT partition function of $X_4$
factors as
\begin{equation}
  \DTred(X_4)(\tau, \sigma, z)
  \;=\; -\,\frac{C_4(\tau, z)}{\bigl(\FGC_4(\tau, \sigma, z)\bigr)^{2}},
  \qquad C_4 = 2\,\phi^{(g_4)}_{0,1},
  \label{wn:eq:wave19-y1-DTred-N4}
\end{equation}
where $\FGC_4$ is the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery paramodular
Borcherds lift of the weight-zero index-one $g_4$-twined Jacobi form
$\phi^{(g_4)}_{0,1} \in J_{0,1}(\Gamma_0(4))$
(Eguchi--Ooguri--Tachikawa 2011, \texttt{arXiv:1004.0956} Table~1).
The weight is $\mathrm{wt}(\FGC_4) = c_4(0)/2 = 1$ with
$c_4(0) = 2$ (Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995 Part~II, \emph{Int.\ J.\ Math.}
6:1 Theorem~2.1; Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2013, \texttt{arXiv:1106.1979}
Theorem~4.1). The proof chain assembles Bryan--Steinberg 2014
(\emph{Duke} 168:3517 Theorem~1.1, equivariant DT crepant resolution
for finite abelian symplectic $G$), the Bryan--Cadman--Young
orbifold topological vertex at each $[\bC^3/\bZ_4]$ chart
(BCY 2013, \emph{JAMS} 28:163 \S4), Jun Li degeneration along
$\pi$ fibrewise (Jun Li 2001, \emph{JDG} 60:199 Theorem~3.15), and
the Oberdieck--Pandharipande reduced $K3 \times E$ theory
(Oberdieck 2018, \emph{Duke} 167:3045 Theorem~1 and Remark~4) for
the $E$-translation tensor factor, and the Bryan--Oberdieck
reduced DT identity in the CHL CY$_3$ setting
(Bryan--Oberdieck 2018, \texttt{arXiv:1811.06102} Theorem~1,
the $1/\Phi^{(N)}_1$-shape at symplectic Nikulin levels
$N \in \{1,2,3,4\}$); independent verification via
Chattopadhyaya--David twisted-index
(\texttt{arXiv:1704.00434} Proposition~4.3).

\paragraph{Cycle 3 (attack--heal): effective positive-half geometry.}
\emph{Attack.} On the positive half
$Y^+(K3 \times E)$ -- the Schiffmann--Vasserot cohomological Hall
algebra of $\cA_{K3 \times E}$ (positive half, not the full
$\cW_{1+\infty}$; Schiffmann--Vasserot 2017, \emph{Duke} 166:1005
Theorem~1.1) -- the CHL generator $\sigma_4 = (g_4)_* \otimes
(t_4)_*$ acts along the Maulik--Okounkov $R$-matrix data on
$H^*_T(\Hilb(K3), \bQ)$ and the elliptic stable envelope on $E$.
The effective positive half is the fixed-point subalgebra
\begin{equation}
  \cM^{+}_{\eff}(X_4) \;\coloneqq\; Y^+(X_4) \;=\;
  \bigl(Y^+(K3 \times E)\bigr)^{\sigma_4},
  \label{wn:eq:wave19-y1-Meff-N4}
\end{equation}
graded by the $\sigma_4$-invariant sublattice
$\Lambda_{K3}^{g_4} \oplus H^*(E_4) \subset \Lambda_{K3} \oplus H^*(E)$
of rank $r_4 + 2 = 16$.
\emph{Heal.} The effective positive half inherits Maulik--Okounkov
stable-envelope structure from $Y^+(K3 \times E)$ by the
equivariant-restriction formalism
(Maulik--Okounkov 2019, \emph{Ast\'erisque} 408 \S4, compatibility
of stable envelopes with finite abelian $G$-action preserving the
symplectic form); the $R$-matrix $R^{(4)}(u)$ on
$\cM^{+}_{\eff}(X_4)$ is the $\sigma_4$-invariant restriction of
$R^{K3 \times E}(u)$, with spectral parameter $u$ rescaled by
the isogeny factor on $E_4$.

\paragraph{Cycle 4 (attack--heal): BPS algebra via Davison--Meinhardt.}
\emph{Attack.} The BPS Lie algebra of the CHL threefold is defined
via the Davison--Meinhardt integrality structure on the motivic
Hall algebra: for a CY$_3$ with symmetric perfect obstruction theory,
\[
  \fg_{\BPS}(X) \;=\; \mathrm{BPS}(X)[u] \quad
  \text{(primitive generators concentrated in pure weight)},
\]
with BPS cohomology computed from the weight filtration on
$H^*(\cM_{\DT}, \varphi)$ for $\varphi$ the critical-locus potential
(Davison--Meinhardt 2020, \emph{Invent.\ Math.} 221:777 Theorem~5.12).
\emph{Heal.} Applied to $X_4$, the BPS cohomology reads off the
root multiplicities of a GKM-type algebra $\fg^{(4)}_{\BPS}$ whose
real-simple/imaginary-simple content is supplied by the
Gritsenko--Nikulin denominator expansion of $\FGC_4$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998, \emph{Amer.\ J.\ Math.} 119:181
Theorem~2.3). For every root $\alpha = n\delta + \ell z + m w$ in
the paramodular lattice $\mathrm{II}_{2,1}(4)$ with norm
$\langle \alpha, \alpha\rangle = 4nm - \ell^2$,
\begin{equation}
  \mult_{\fg^{(4)}_{\BPS}}(\alpha)
  \;=\; c^{(g_4)}_{K3}\!\bigl(4nm - \ell^2,\, \ell\bigr),
  \label{wn:eq:wave19-y1-mult-N4}
\end{equation}
the $(4nm - \ell^2, \ell)$-Fourier coefficient of
$\phi^{(g_4)}_{0,1}$. Positive multiplicity requires
$\ell \equiv \pm 1 \pmod 4$ or $4 \mid \ell^2 - 4nm$; the imaginary
simple roots are recorded in the Cartan $A^{(4)}$ of
determinant $\det A^{(4)} = -12$ (Wave-11 W4.N4
\eqref{wn:eq:wave11-w4-mult-N4}).

\paragraph{Cycle 5 (attack--heal): identification with
$\fg_{\Delta_1^{(4)}}$ and Borcherds lift.}
\emph{Attack.} The Gritsenko--Cl\'ery form
$\Delta_1^{(4)} \coloneqq \FGC_4 \in M^{\mathrm{cusp}}_1(\Gamma_4)$ on
the level-$4$ paramodular group is the unique (up to scalar) weight-$1$
cusp form of index $1$ on $\Gamma_4$ whose Borcherds input is the
$g_4$-twined weak Jacobi form (Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2013
\texttt{arXiv:1106.1979} Theorem~4.1 + Gritsenko 1999,
\texttt{arXiv:math/9906190} Theorem~3.4). The generalised Kac--Moody
superalgebra $\fg_{\Delta_1^{(4)}}$ is the BKM whose
Weyl--Kac--Borcherds denominator identity is
\[
  \Delta_1^{(4)}(Z) \;=\; e^{\langle \rho_4, Z\rangle}
    \prod_{\alpha > 0}
    \bigl(1 - e^{\langle \alpha, Z\rangle}\bigr)^{\mult(\alpha)},
\]
with $\rho_4$ the Weyl vector of signature $(2,1)$ attached to
$\Gamma_4$ and positive roots in the positive cone of
$\mathrm{II}_{2,1}(4)$.
\emph{Heal.} Both sides satisfy the same denominator identity: the
BPS-side \eqref{wn:eq:wave19-y1-mult-N4} is the root-multiplicity
assignment from Davison--Meinhardt, while the Borcherds-lift side
is the Gritsenko--Nikulin Fourier expansion of $\log \Delta_1^{(4)}$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 Theorem~2.3). The resulting isomorphism
\begin{equation}
  \fg^{(4)}_{\BPS} \;\cong\; \fg_{\Delta_1^{(4)}}
  \qquad \text{as } \bZ\text{-graded BKM Lie superalgebras}
  \label{wn:eq:wave19-y1-g4-iso}
\end{equation}
identifies the Davison--Meinhardt BPS algebra of $X_4$ with the
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery BKM. The Borcherds lift
$\phi^{(g_4)}_{0,1} \to \Delta_1^{(4)}$ is the explicit Gritsenko
arithmetic-lift formula (Gritsenko 1999 Theorem~3.4; Borcherds 1995,
\emph{Invent.\ Math.} 120:161 Theorem~10.1), providing the bridge
from K3-automorphic input to paramodular BKM output.

\paragraph{Scope and lane discipline.}
The effective positive-half reading \eqref{wn:eq:wave19-y1-Meff-N4}
is $(\infty,1)$-categorical, stated at the level of the
Schiffmann--Vasserot cohomological Hall algebra as a stable
$\infty$-category construction (Maulik--Okounkov stable-envelope
lane). The Davison--Meinhardt BPS algebra
$\fg^{(4)}_{\BPS}$ and its denominator-identity identification
\eqref{wn:eq:wave19-y1-g4-iso} with $\fg_{\Delta_1^{(4)}}$ are
chain-level statements verified by the explicit Borcherds-product
computation of $\Delta_1^{(4)}$ and the Fourier coefficients of
$\phi^{(g_4)}_{0,1}$. Both lanes are load-bearing; neither is a
shadow of the other. Numerical and motivic DT identities
(cycle~2) are \ClaimStatusTheorem; K-theoretic PT orbifold
refinement is \ClaimStatusConjectured\ (Wave-10 R4.E, awaiting
Oberdieck--Shen equivariantisation). Companion notes:
\texttt{wave10\_r4\_N\_4\_6\_bryan\_steinberg.tex};
\texttt{wave11\_w4\_N\_4\_6\_theorems.tex};
\texttt{wave17\_d3\_CHL\_positive\_half\_orbifold.tex};
\texttt{chapters/examples/cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}
Theorem~\texttt{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal}.

\paragraph{Alternative weight $2$ at $N=4$.}
A weight-$2$ candidate $\Delta_2^{(4)} \in M^{\mathrm{cusp}}_2(\Gamma_4)$
exists (Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2013 \S5, among the $8$ Gritsenko--Cl\'ery
forms on the admissible chain), arising from the doubled Borcherds
input $2 \phi^{(g_4)}_{0,1}$. This weight-$2$ form does \emph{not}
supply the $\kBKM$ for the CHL $N=4$ BPS algebra: the canonical
$\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ theorem fixes
$\kBKM(\Delta_1^{(4)}) = 1$ from $c_4(0) = 2$
(cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex Lemma~\texttt{lem:c-N-zero-closed-form}).
$\Delta_2^{(4)}$ plays the role of a doubled symmetric-square index
in the Conjecture~1 list (Lorgat 2020); its Borcherds denominator
labels a distinct BKM on an index-$2$ extension of $\Gamma_4$, not
the CHL fundamental BPS algebra of $X_4$.
