\section*{Level-four CHL positive-half geometry and the BPS denominator}
\label{wn:sec:N4-positive-half-BPS}

\paragraph{The free quotient.}
Let $S$ be a K3 surface and let
$g_4 \in \Auts(S)$ be a Mukai symplectic automorphism of order $4$,
Mathieu class $4B$, with frame shape $1^{4}2^{2}4^{4}$.  Let
$\Lambda_S=H^2(S,\bZ)$ and
$\widetilde\Lambda_S=H^*(S,\bZ)$ with the Mukai pairing.  Nikulin and
Mukai give
\[
  \#S^{g_4}=4,\qquad
  \#S^{g_4^2}=8 .
\]
The topological Lefschetz formula gives
$\mathrm{Tr}(g_4\mid H^2(S))=2$ and
$\mathrm{Tr}(g_4^2\mid H^2(S))=6$.  If the eigenvalue multiplicities
of $g_4$ on $H^2(S,\bC)$ are
$(a,b,c,c)$ for $(1,-1,i,-i)$, then
$a+b+2c=22$, $a-b=2$, and $a+b-2c=6$.  Hence
\[
  \mathrm{rk}\,\Lambda_S^{g_4}=8,\qquad
  \mathrm{rk}\,\widetilde\Lambda_S^{g_4}=10,\qquad
  \mathrm{rk}\,(\Lambda_S^{g_4})^\perp=14 .
\]
The invariant real subspace contains $H^{2,0}(S)\oplus H^{0,2}(S)$
and a $g_4$-invariant K\"ahler class.  Its orthogonal coinvariant
lattice is negative definite, so
$\mathrm{sign}((\Lambda_S^{g_4})^\perp)=(0,14)$.
The surface quotient $S/\langle g_4\rangle$ carries Du Val stabilizer
data.  The CHL threefold uses the free diagonal action.  Choose
$t_4\in E[4]$ of exact order on an elliptic curve $E$ and set
\[
  X_4=(S\times E)/\langle(g_4,t_4)\rangle .
\]
The translation $t_4$ acts freely on $E$, so the diagonal action is free.
The quotient is smooth, and the holomorphic form
$\omega_S\wedge dz_E$ descends because $g_4$ is symplectic and
translation preserves $dz_E$.  Thus $X_4$ is a smooth $K$-trivial
threefold in the CY$_3$ source convention used here.

The projection to $E_4=E/\langle t_4\rangle$ is a smooth isotrivial
K3 fibration:
\[
  S\times E \longrightarrow X_4 \longrightarrow E_4 .
\]
The cover $E\to E_4$ is an unramified isogeny.  For each
$[e]\in E_4$, choosing a lift $e\in E$ identifies the fibre with $S$;
changing the lift composes the identification with $g_4$.  After
pullback along $E\to E_4$ the fibration becomes the product
$S\times E$.

\paragraph{Four invariants and the level-four constant.}
Translation acts trivially on $H^{0,*}(E)$ and $g_4$ acts trivially on
$H^{0,0}(S)$ and $H^{0,2}(S)$.  Hence
\[
  h^{0,q}(X_4)=(1,1,1,1),\qquad
  \kcat(X_4)=\chi(\cO_{X_4})=1-1+1-1=0 .
\]
The compact Hodge-supertrace value is likewise
$\kch(\cA_{X_4})=0$ in the compact CY$_3$ convention.  The invariant
K3-fibre rank for the level-four CHL quotient is
\[
  \kfib^{(4)}=\mathrm{rk}\,\widetilde\Lambda_S^{g_4}=10.
\]
The unquotiented parent $S\times E$ carries the separate constants
\[
  \kcat(S\times E)=0,\qquad
  \kch(\cA_{S\times E})=0,\qquad
  \kchHeis(S\times E)=3,\qquad
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)=5,\qquad
  \kfib(S)=24 .
\]
The integer $14$ is the rank of the negative-definite coinvariant
lattice $(\Lambda_S^{g_4})^\perp$ inside $H^2(S,\bZ)$.

The primitive CHL Borcherds constants are
\[
  (c_1(0),c_2(0),c_3(0),c_4(0),c_6(0))
  =(10,8,6,4,2),
\]
with
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_N)=\frac{c_N(0)}{2}.
\]
For $N=4$ this gives
\begin{equation}
  c_4(0)=4,\qquad
  \kBKM(\PhiFour)=2 .
  \label{wn:eq:N4-kBKM}
\end{equation}
Equivalently, the primitive level-four denominator is the weight-two
Gritsenko--Nikulin CHL product
\[
  \PhiFour=\Delta^{(4)}_2 .
\]
The square appearing in reduced DT scalar formulae has weight $4$.
At level one, $D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5$ and
$\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_5^2$ express the same distinction
between primitive denominator and scalar square.

Borcherds' automorphic-product weight formula (Borcherds 1998,
Theorem 13.3), the Gritsenko--Nikulin CHL paramodular tower
(arXiv:alg-geom/9611028), Gritsenko's arithmetic lift
(arXiv:math/9906190), and the normalization
$\kBKM(\Phi_N)=\mathrm{wt}(\Phi_N)$ give \eqref{wn:eq:N4-kBKM}.
The tuple above belongs to the primitive CHL tower.  Non-CHL Nikulin
quotients and the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery diagonal-divisor catalogue require
their own row-map data.

\paragraph{Reduced DT character.}
Let $\phi^{(4)}_{0,1}=\phi^{(g_4)}_{0,1}$ be the $4B$-twined K3 weak
Jacobi form of weight $0$ and index $1$, written
\[
  \phi^{(4)}_{0,1}(\tau,z)=
  \sum_{n,\ell} c^{(4)}(n,\ell)q^n y^\ell .
\]
The Bryan--Oberdieck CHL reduced-DT conjecture fixes the scalar
denominator, after the chamber and variables are chosen, as the square
of the primitive level-four denominator:
\begin{equation}
  \Phi^{\mathrm{scal}}_4(\tau,\sigma,z)
  =
  \PhiFour(\tau,\sigma,z)^2,
  \qquad
  \DTred(X_4)(\tau,\sigma,z)
  =
  -\,\mathcal C^{\mathrm{BO}}_4(\tau,z)\,
  \bigl(\Phi^{\mathrm{scal}}_4(\tau,\sigma,z)\bigr)^{-1}.
  \label{wn:eq:N4-DTred}
\end{equation}
The prefactor $\mathcal C^{\mathrm{BO}}_4$ is fixed by the
Bryan--Oberdieck scalar normalisation and boundary terms.  This is OP/DT
scalar data.  The scalar partition function uses the squared
automorphic product; the primitive denominator remains $\PhiFour$ with
exponents $c^{(4)}(4nm-\ell^2,\ell)$.  The compact Hall source, BPS Lie
bracket, Hall pairing, and Drinfeld double require orientation,
stability, completion, and finite recognition data.

\paragraph{The effective positive half.}
The finite \'etale cover $p:S\times E\to X_4$ gives the descent
equivalence
\[
  \Coh(X_4)\simeq \Coh_{\langle\gamma_4\rangle}(S\times E),
  \qquad \gamma_4=(g_4,t_4).
\]
The positive half attached to $X_4$ is therefore the
$\langle\gamma_4\rangle$-equivariant oriented critical CoHA positive
half of the cover.  For a stability condition $\vartheta$, effective
support monoid $\Gamma_{\eff}$, orientation datum $o$, and equivariant
torus $\Teq$, write
\[
  Y^+_{\vartheta,\Gamma_{\eff},o,\Teq}(S\times E)
\]
for the completed Hall positive half along the effective BPS monoid.
The action
$\gamma_{4,*}=(g_4)_*\otimes(t_4)_*$ on Hall correspondences is defined
once the equivariant orientation and completion are fixed.  The
level-four effective positive half is
\begin{equation}
  Y^+_{\eff}(X_4)
  :=
  \widehat{
    Y^{+,\langle\gamma_4\rangle}_{\vartheta,\Gamma_{\eff},o,\Teq}
    (S\times E)}
  _{\Gamma_{\eff}^{\gamma_4}},
  \label{wn:eq:N4-positive-half}
\end{equation}
with fibre charge component controlled by
\[
  \widetilde\Lambda_S^{g_4}\subset\widetilde\Lambda_S,
  \qquad \mathrm{rk}\,\widetilde\Lambda_S^{g_4}=10 .
\]
The $H^2$ part has rank $8$; the $H^0(S)\oplus H^4(S)$ summand
contributes the remaining two invariant Mukai directions.
Equation \eqref{wn:eq:N4-positive-half} is positive-half Hall data.
A full quantum-group or vertex object requires the negative half,
Cartan, Hopf pairing, completion, and centre or double comparison.  For
$\bC^3$,
\[
  \CoHA(\bC^3)=Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
\]
is the positive affine-Yangian half; the $\cW_{1+\infty}$ vertex
algebra appears after the Drinfeld-double, centre, completion, and
evaluation operations.

\paragraph{Root-superdimension convention.}
The primitive Borcherds product has denominator expansion
\begin{equation}
  \PhiFour(Z)
  =e^{(\rho_4,Z)}
   \prod_{\alpha>0}
   \bigl(1-e^{(\alpha,Z)}\bigr)^{
     c^{(4)}(4nm-\ell^2,\ell)}
  \label{wn:eq:N4-denominator}
\end{equation}
for $\alpha=n\delta+\ell z+mw$ in the positive cone of the
level-four paramodular lattice, with norm convention
\[
  (\alpha,\alpha)=4nm-\ell^2 .
\]
The exponent in \eqref{wn:eq:N4-denominator} is the signed
superdimension:
\begin{equation}
  \sdim\,\fg_{\PhiFour,\alpha}
  =
  \dim(\fg_{\PhiFour,\alpha}^{\bar 0})
  -
  \dim(\fg_{\PhiFour,\alpha}^{\bar 1})
  =
  c^{(4)}(4nm-\ell^2,\ell).
  \label{wn:eq:N4-root-sdim}
\end{equation}
Recovering ordinary even and odd multiplicities from
\eqref{wn:eq:N4-root-sdim} requires the parity convention of the chosen
Borcherds superalgebra.  The denominator fixes signed
superdimensions; a parity split is extra datum.

\paragraph{Conditional BPS recognition.}
The automorphic BKM target is
\[
  \fg_{\PhiFour}
\]
whose denominator is \eqref{wn:eq:N4-denominator} and whose
modular characteristic is \eqref{wn:eq:N4-kBKM}.  The BPS Lie algebra
of the compact CHL threefold is identified with this target under the
following finite recognition datum:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item an equivariant oriented critical-CoHA construction for
  $X_4$, including the orientation line and the reduced obstruction
  theory compatible with the free diagonal quotient;
  \item the equivariant positive half \eqref{wn:eq:N4-positive-half}, with
  stability chamber, support monoid, and completion preserved by
  $\gamma_{4,*}$;
  \item a compact Hall pairing and Drinfeld double whose Cartan and
  negative half are compatible with the invariant fibre charge lattice
  $\widetilde\Lambda_S^{g_4}$ and the elliptic descent along
  $E\to E_4$;
  \item PBW checks and relation preservation identifying the primitive
  BPS bracket beyond equality of scalar characters;
  \item a Hall-Borcherds denominator comparison sending the primitive
  BPS signed root spaces to the coefficients
  $c^{(4)}(4nm-\ell^2,\ell)$ of $\phi^{(4)}_{0,1}$.
\end{enumerate}
Under these hypotheses there is an isomorphism of
$\bZ$-graded BKM Lie superalgebras
\begin{equation}
  \fg_{\BPS}^{(4)}(X_4)\cong \fg_{\PhiFour},
  \label{wn:eq:N4-conditional-BPS-BKM}
\end{equation}
and the root spaces satisfy
\[
  \sdim\,\fg_{\BPS,\alpha}^{(4)}
  =
  c^{(4)}(4nm-\ell^2,\ell).
\]
Before this recognition datum is supplied, the established data are the
smooth free quotient, the CHL constant \eqref{wn:eq:N4-kBKM}, the
automorphic denominator \eqref{wn:eq:N4-denominator}, and the scalar
Bryan--Oberdieck input \eqref{wn:eq:N4-DTred} on its stated
normalization.

\paragraph{Dimensional and branch scope.}
The CY$_3$ output of the CY-to-chiral construction is natively
$\Eone$-chiral.  Braided $\Etwo$ structure belongs to the centre,
Drinfeld double, or module category after construction of the
corresponding data.  The BKM/Hall-Drinfeld branch considered here is
separate from the Mukai self-mirror Yangian branch.  The
level-four CHL target is a Borcherds-denominator object with
\[
  \kBKM(\PhiFour)=2.
\]
Compact Hodge data give $\kcat(X_4)=\kch(\cA_{X_4})=0$.
These invariants come from separate constructions.
