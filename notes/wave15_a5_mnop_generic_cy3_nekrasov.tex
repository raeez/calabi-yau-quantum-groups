\subsection{Partition functions and theorem loci}

Let \(X\) be a smooth projective Calabi--Yau threefold.  The degree-zero
Donaldson--Thomas factor is
\begin{equation}
  Z^{\DT}_{0}(X;q)=M(-q)^{\chitop(X)}.
  \label{eq:generic-mnop-degree-zero}
\end{equation}
The reduced DT series is
\[
  Z^{\DT\prime}(X;q,v)
  =Z^{\DT}(X;q,v)/M(-q)^{\chitop(X)}.
\]
The MNOP correspondence asks for the formal identity
\begin{equation}
  Z^{\GW\prime}(X;u,v)=Z^{\DT\prime}(X;-q,v),
  \qquad -q=e^{iu},
  \label{eq:generic-mnop-formula}
\end{equation}
after the standard change of variables and the removal of unstable
degree-zero factors.

The degree-zero formula \eqref{eq:generic-mnop-degree-zero} is a
theorem for every smooth projective threefold.  The DT/PT comparison is
a Hall-algebra theorem for projective Calabi--Yau threefolds in the
Bridgeland--Toda form.  The GW/DT or GW/PT comparison has narrower
theorem loci: smooth toric threefolds by the topological vertex of
Maulik--Nekrasov--Okounkov--Pandharipande and
Maulik--Oblomkov--Okounkov--Pandharipande; \(K3\times E\) in the
primitive reduced theory by Oberdieck--Pandharipande, Oberdieck--Pixton,
and Oberdieck's stable-pairs comparison; and the quintic in the
Gromov--Witten/Pairs theorem of Pandharipande--Pixton.  A generic
compact Calabi--Yau threefold remains conjectural.

For the quintic \(X_5\subset\mathbb P^4\),
\[
  \chitop(X_5)=-200,\qquad
  h^{1,1}(X_5)=1,\qquad
  h^{2,1}(X_5)=101,
\]
so
\begin{equation}
  Z^{\DT}_{0}(X_5;q)=M(-q)^{-200}.
  \label{eq:quintic-degree-zero}
\end{equation}
The first genus-zero BPS numbers are
\[
  n^0_H=2875,\quad
  n^0_{2H}=609250,\quad
  n^0_{3H}=317206375.
\]
They give the first terms of the Gopakumar--Vafa reorganisation; the
Pandharipande--Pixton theorem supplies the named quintic GW/Pairs
comparison, and DT/PT supplies the corresponding DT comparison.

The BCOV holomorphic-anomaly equation governs higher-genus amplitudes.
Its genus-one anomaly scalar on the quintic is
\[
  \alpha_{\mathrm{BCOV}}(X_5)=\frac{\chitop(X_5)}{24}=-\frac{25}{3}.
\]
This scalar belongs to the BCOV recursion.  The Hodge supertrace
\(\kch(A_{X_5})\) and the categorical Euler characteristic
\(\kcat(X_5)\) are computed separately in
\S\ref{subsec:generic-cy3-invariants}.

\subsection{Hypotheses for a generic compact threefold}

The generic compact threefold statement needs independent hypotheses
beyond the theorem loci above.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Vanishing cycles and orientation.}
  The derived Hilbert, stable-pairs, and stable-maps moduli carry
  BBDJM vanishing-cycle sheaves, proper or completed supports,
  orientation data, and cyclic traces compatible with the
  \((-1)\)-shifted symplectic structures.
\item \textbf{DT/PT normalisation.}
  The Bridgeland--Toda Hall product fixes the reduced DT/PT comparison
  and the MacMahon factor \(M(-q)^{\chitop(X)}\) in the same
  variable convention used by \eqref{eq:generic-mnop-formula}.
\item \textbf{GW/PT comparison and GV/KKV reorganisation.}
  The genus-refined BPS invariants \(n^g_\beta(X)\in\ZZ\) exist and
  reorganise the GW and PT potentials as completed formal power series.
\item \textbf{Trace dualisability and completion.}
  The modules
  \[
    M_\DT=\Coh(\Hilb_X),\qquad
    M_\PT=\Coh(P_n(X,\beta)),\qquad
    M_\GW=\Coh(\overline{\cM}_g(X,\beta))
  \]
  are proper and dualisable over the completed \(\Ethree\)-monoidal
  factorisation algebra
  \(\cF_X=\Obs^q_{\hCS}(X)\).  Their cyclic traces land in a completed
  \(\Etwo\)-centre of the module category.  The specialised threefold
  \(\Phi_3\)-output remains \(\Eone\)-chiral.
\item \textbf{Compact TCFT input.}
  The compact holomorphic Chern--Simons or BCOV theory satisfies the
  quantum master equation and supplies anomaly cancellation,
  counterterms, descent, sewing, framing, orientation line, completed
  state space, and comparison maps to the DT, PT, and GW enumerative
  theories.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Conditional theorem}

Let \(\cT_X\) be the completed cyclic trace target produced by the
compact TCFT package, and let
\[
  \tau_\DT,\tau_\PT,\tau_\GW \colon
  \Tr^{\Ethree}(M_\bullet)\longrightarrow \cT_X
\]
be the corresponding comparison maps.  Under the five hypotheses above,
the trace formulation of MNOP reads
\begin{equation}
  \tau_\DT\bigl(\Tr^{\Ethree}(M_\DT)\bigr)
  =
  \tau_\PT\bigl(\Tr^{\Ethree}(M_\PT)\bigr)\cdot M(-q)^{\chitop(X)}
  =
  \sigma^{-1}\tau_\GW\bigl(\Tr^{\Ethree}(M_\GW)\bigr),
  \label{eq:generic-three-trace}
\end{equation}
where \(\sigma\) implements the change of variables \(-q=e^{iu}\) and
acts on \(\cT_X\).  Equation
\eqref{eq:generic-three-trace} is equivalent to
\eqref{eq:generic-mnop-formula} after dividing by
\eqref{eq:generic-mnop-degree-zero} and applying the enumerative
character.  No equality of the three raw trace objects is asserted
before the maps \(\tau_\bullet\) and the completion \(\cT_X\) are
constructed.  The five hypotheses are part of the conditional
statement; omitting any one of them leaves the generic MNOP conjecture.

\subsection{Toric and \(K3\times E\) special features}

The toric proof uses a \(T^3\)-action, isolated fixed points on
\(\Hilb(\CC^3)\)-type charts, and explicit topological-vertex
gluing.  A generic quintic has no positive-dimensional
algebraic torus action, so the residue computation has no global
fixed-point basis.

The \(K3\times E\) primitive reduced proof uses three special facts:
\[
  \chitop(K3\times E)=0,\qquad
  H^{2,0}(K3)\neq 0,\qquad
  \text{paramodular rigidity of the }\chi_{10}\text{ target}.
\]
A generic quintic has \(\chitop(X_5)=-200\), \(H^{2,0}(X_5)=0\), and no
\(\chi_{10}\)-rigidity.  The \(K3\times E\) proof gives a theorem
locus for the trace architecture.  The generic compact threefold
construction still requires the five hypotheses above.

\subsection{Four \(\kappa_\bullet\) constants}
\label{subsec:generic-cy3-invariants}

For \(K3\times E\), the constants remain distinct:
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)=\chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot 0=0,
  \qquad
  \kch(A_{K3\times E})=0,
\]
\[
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)=3,\qquad
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=\frac{c_1(0)}{2}=5,\qquad
  \kfib(K3)=24.
\]
The OP reduced curve-counting partition function uses the Igusa cusp
form \(\chi_{10}\).  In the Vol III primitive-denominator
normalisation, \(\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_5^2\) has scalar-square
weight \(10\), and the BKM denominator algebra uses \(\Delta_5\) with
\(\kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=5\).

For a compact Calabi--Yau threefold such as the quintic,
\[
  \chi(\cO_X)=1-0+0-1=0,\qquad
  \kcat(X)=0,\qquad
  \kch(A_X)=0.
\]
A \(\kBKM\) value requires a Borcherds product with Jacobi input and
constant term \(c(0)\).  MNOP and BCOV data on a quintic supply
curve-counting and one-loop determinant data; a Borcherds denominator
identity is separate input.

\subsection{CoHA and \(G(X)\)}

The MNOP categorical CoHA, on loci where it is constructed, is an
\(\Eone\) Hall or vanishing-cycle algebra.  In the affine toric model
\(\CC^3\) one has the positive-half identification
\[
  \CoHA(\CC^3)\simeq Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1).
\]
The passage to \(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}\) requires the negative half,
Cartan factor, Hopf pairing, Drinfeld double or centre, completion, and
evaluation data.  For a generic compact threefold, CY-C and the global
object \(G(X)\) remain conjectural.

\subsection{Generic compact threefold inputs}

For a generic compact threefold, a proof requires:
\begin{enumerate}
\item construct orientation data for the relevant vanishing-cycle
  sheaves and derived moduli, with cyclic trace data;
\item fix the DT/PT Hall comparison and MacMahon normalisation;
\item prove the required GW/PT comparison map;
\item prove GV/KKV integrality and formal convergence for all
  genera and curve classes;
\item establish proper dualisability of the three modules over the
  completed \(\Ethree\)-factorisation algebra;
\item build the compact TCFT, QME, anomaly-cancellation, counterterm,
  descent, sewing, framing, and completion package for the chain-level
  \(\Phi_3\) output;
\item when invoking \(G(X)\), construct the doubled quantum group
  separately from the positive-half CoHA.
\end{enumerate}
