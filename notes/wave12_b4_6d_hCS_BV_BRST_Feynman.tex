\begin{abstract}
Six-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons $\hCS(\CC^3,\gfrak)$ is constructed
BV-first as a free $E_3$-cotangent theory on the Dolbeault complex
$\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\CC^3,\gfrak)[1]$, quantised via Costello
heat-kernel renormalization at scales $(\varepsilon,L)$. The propagator
is the Bochner--Martinelli kernel regularised by $P_{\varepsilon,L} =
\int_{\varepsilon}^{L}(\dbar^{*}\otimes\mathrm{id})K_t\,dt$. The one-loop
anomaly for non-abelian $\gfrak$ is computed: the $\dbar$-bubble diagram is
cohomologically trivial, yielding no obstruction in strict six dimensions,
confirming the Costello--Williams theorem and extending it to the exact
coefficient count. The quantum observables $\Obs^q$ form a factorization
algebra equivalent to the $E_3$-enveloping of the $L_\infty$-algebra
$(\gfrak[1]\otimes\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(\CC^3),\dbar)$. The note is organised
as five ATTACK$\leftrightarrow$HEAL pairs: propagator
dimension-counting, abelian-vs-non-abelian gap, level parameter, two-loop
master equation, chiral avatar via factorization.
\end{abstract}

\section{Ghost theorem and first-principles protocol}
%==============================================================

\subsection*{Ghost theorem}

The mathematical content Costello's renormalization programme extracts from
6d holomorphic Chern--Simons is not a ``CS-like level shift'' but a precise
statement about the {\em cohomological triviality} of the one-loop anomaly
cocycle on $\CC^3$. The ``ghost theorem'' behind every 3d analogy invoked
below is:
\begin{quote}
For each Calabi--Yau $n$-fold $X$, the obstruction to quantising the
BV classical action $S_{\mathrm{cl}}[\cA] = \int_X \Omega\wedge\tr(\tfrac12
\cA\dbar\cA + \tfrac16\cA[\cA,\cA])$ lies in the local cohomology
$H^1_{\mathrm{loc}}(X,\mathrm{BV})$; at $n=3$ this cohomology vanishes
(Pontryagin degree-$6$ forms built from $\dbar$-Laplacian heat kernels
contract to zero on the off-diagonal divisor), and at $n=2$ (Kodaira--Spencer
on $CY_2$) it does not, yielding the BCOV anomaly. The 3d CS analogy
is {\em dimensional}: in real 3d, the anomaly $k\to k+\hv$ arises from
the degree-$3$ Chern--Simons secondary class; in complex 3d (real 6d) the
parallel class has degree $6$ but its heat-kernel integrand vanishes on
$\CC^3$ by a symmetry of the Bochner--Martinelli kernel (Proposition
\ref{prop:one-loop-vanish} below).
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Precise error to avoid}

The naive expectation that six-dimensional hCS carries a level parameter
$k$ that shifts $k\to k+\hv$ at one loop fails by a dimensionality count:
the three-dimensional CS level lives in $H^4(BG,\Z) \cong \Z$; the six-
dimensional hCS analogous class lives in
$H^7(BG,\Q)$ which is torsion for finite-dimensional $\g$ and vanishes
after one rationalisation (Chern--Weil). There is no additive level
renormalization; instead, the one-loop Feynman bubble diagram must either
(i) vanish cohomologically (for our $X=\CC^3$, it does) or (ii) require
a specific counterterm (for $X$ with nonzero $c_2(X)$, it does not vanish
and yields an $L_\infty$-deformation of the BV action; this is the
$c_2$-anomaly detected by Costello--Williams).

\subsection*{Correct relationship}

$\hCS(\CC^3,\gfrak)$ quantises to an $E_3$ prefactorization algebra on
$\CC^3$ whose value on a small ball is the universal enveloping
$U_{E_3}(\gfrak[\![\dbar]\!])$ of the $\dbar$-deformed $\g$-sheaf of
$L_\infty$-algebras. The level parameter is hidden in the pairing
normalisation $\hbar\cdot\tr$ and does {\em not} shift at one loop.
The quantum-corrected scattering amplitudes at two loops do receive
contributions from planar and non-planar graphs, but these are
cohomologically absorbed into redefinitions of the pairing, not into
a new coupling. This is the Costello--Williams picture made explicit
here at Feynman-coefficient level.

\section{BV--BRST complex: field content and classical action}
%==============================================================

Let $X=\CC^3$ with coordinates $(z_1,z_2,z_3)$ and holomorphic volume form
$\Omega = dz_1\wedge dz_2\wedge dz_3$. Fix $\gfrak$ a finite-dimensional
Lie algebra with non-degenerate invariant pairing $\tr$. The BV graded
vector space is a four-term Dolbeault complex:

\begin{definition}[BV field content]\label{def:BV-fields}
The BV fields form the graded algebra
\[
\mathcal{E}^{\BV} \;=\; \Omega^{0,\bullet}(\CC^3,\gfrak[1])\oplus
\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\CC^3,\gfrak^{*}[-2])
\]
with cohomological degrees
\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathrm{gh}=+1$: ghosts $c\in\Omega^{0,0}(\CC^3,\gfrak)$;
\item $\mathrm{gh}=0$: fields $\cA\in\Omega^{0,1}(\CC^3,\gfrak)$;
\item $\mathrm{gh}=-1$: antifields
$\cA^{\dagger}\in\Omega^{0,2}(\CC^3,\gfrak)$;
\item $\mathrm{gh}=-2$: antighosts
$c^{\dagger}\in\Omega^{0,3}(\CC^3,\gfrak)$.
\end{itemize}
Total degree combines ghost number with Dolbeault degree so that
$\cA\in\mathcal{E}^{\BV,0}$, $c\in\mathcal{E}^{\BV,0}$,
$\cA^{\dagger}\in\mathcal{E}^{\BV,1}$,
$c^{\dagger}\in\mathcal{E}^{\BV,1}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[BV symplectic pairing]\label{def:BV-pairing}
Degree $-1$ symplectic form on $\mathcal{E}^{\BV}$:
\[
\omega(\alpha,\beta) \;=\; \int_{\CC^3}\Omega\wedge\tr(\alpha\wedge\beta),
\qquad
\omega:\mathcal{E}^{\BV,k}\otimes\mathcal{E}^{\BV,1-k}\to \CC.
\]
The pairing of a degree-$k$ Dolbeault form against the $(3-k)$-form
antifield produces a top form on $\CC^3$; integration against $\Omega$
gives the number. Non-degeneracy is the standard Dolbeault duality.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Classical BV action]\label{def:BV-action}
\[
S_{\mathrm{cl}}[\Phi] \;=\; \int_{\CC^3}\Omega\wedge\tr\!\Bigl(
\tfrac12\,\cA\wedge\dbar\cA + \tfrac16\,\cA\wedge[\cA,\cA]
+ \cA^{\dagger}\wedge\dbar c + \cA^{\dagger}\wedge[\cA,c]
+ \tfrac12\,c^{\dagger}\wedge[c,c]\Bigr).
\]
The BV bracket $\{S_{\mathrm{cl}},S_{\mathrm{cl}}\}=0$ is the classical
master equation. The BRST operator $Q=\{S_{\mathrm{cl}},-\}$ recovers
gauge transformations: $Qc=\tfrac12[c,c]$, $Q\cA=\dbar c+[\cA,c]$,
$Q\cA^{\dagger}=\dbar\cA+\tfrac12[\cA,\cA]+[\cA^{\dagger},c]$,
$Qc^{\dagger}=\dbar\cA^{\dagger}+[\cA,\cA^{\dagger}]+[c,c^{\dagger}]$.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Classical master equation]\label{prop:CME}
$\{S_{\mathrm{cl}},S_{\mathrm{cl}}\}=0$ holds on $\CC^3$ for any
$\gfrak$ with invariant pairing.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Expand $\{S_{\mathrm{cl}},S_{\mathrm{cl}}\}$ using the pairing;
each contribution is a six-form in Dolbeault type $(3,3)$ integrated
against $\Omega$. The three classes are:
(i) $\dbar^2=0$ on Dolbeault complex;
(ii) Jacobi identity for $[-,-]$;
(iii) $\dbar$-Leibniz rule against $[-,-]$, which is the standard
$\dbar$-holomorphic compatibility of the Lie bracket.
\end{proof}

\section{Heat-kernel propagator (ATTACK 1 $\leftrightarrow$ HEAL 1)}
%==============================================================

\begin{attack}[Propagator dimension count]\label{atk:1}
\emph{``For $\dbar$ on $\CC^3$, the Bochner--Kodaira Green's function
behaves as $K(z,w)\sim C/|z-w|^4$ by dimension count.''}

This is {\em wrong}. Real dimension of $\CC^3$ is $6$; the standard
Green's function for the Laplacian on $\RR^6$ scales as $|x-y|^{-4}$,
but we want the Green's function for $\dbar^{*}\dbar$ acting on
$\Omega^{0,1}$. The Bochner--Martinelli formula gives a $(0,n-1)$-form
in $z$ of type $|z-w|^{-2(n-1)-1} = |z-w|^{-5}$ in $n=3$.
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[Bochner--Martinelli propagator with heat-kernel
regularization]\label{heal:1}
Fix a Hermitian metric $h$ on $\CC^3$ (Euclidean for $\CC^3$, arbitrary
K\"ahler for general CY3). The $\dbar$-Laplacian is
$\Delta_{\dbar} = \dbar\dbar^{*} + \dbar^{*}\dbar$. The heat kernel
$K_t(z,w)$ for $\Delta_{\dbar}$ on $\Omega^{0,\bullet}$ satisfies
$(\partial_t + \Delta_{\dbar})K_t = 0$ with $K_0 = \mathrm{id}\cdot\delta$.

\emph{The regularized propagator} (Costello, MAP \S2, heat-kernel
renormalization) is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:P-regularized}
P_{\varepsilon,L}(z,w)\;=\;\int_{\varepsilon}^{L}(\dbar^{*}\otimes 1)K_t(z,w)\,dt
\;\in\;\Omega^{0,0}(\CC^3)\otimes\Omega^{0,1}(\CC^3),
\end{equation}
a $(0,0)$-form in $z$ times a $(0,1)$-form in $w$ (or vice versa).
For $\CC^3$ the heat kernel in Euclidean metric is
\begin{equation}
K_t(z,w) = \frac{1}{(4\pi t)^3}e^{-\|z-w\|^2/(4t)}\cdot\mathrm{Proj}_{(0,\bullet)}(z,w),
\end{equation}
a scalar Gaussian times the projection onto Dolbeault type. The
$(\dbar^{*}\otimes 1)$ of the heat kernel produces a factor of
$(\bar z_k - \bar w_k)/(2t)$ in each component.

The $L\to\infty$, $\varepsilon\to 0$ limit of \eqref{eq:P-regularized}
gives the Bochner--Martinelli kernel
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BM-kernel}
P_{0,\infty}(z,w) \;=\; \frac{2!}{(2\pi i)^3}\sum_{k=1}^{3}(-1)^{k-1}
\frac{\overline{(z_k-w_k)}}{\|z-w\|^6}\,\widehat{d\bar z_k}\wedge\Omega_w
\end{equation}
where $\widehat{d\bar z_k} = d\bar z_j\wedge d\bar z_l$ with $(j,k,l)$
cyclic, and $\Omega_w = dw_1\wedge dw_2\wedge dw_3$. The singularity is
$\|z-w\|^{-5}$ on the diagonal, consistent with the $(2n-1)$ Bochner--
Martinelli scaling at $n=3$. Attack 1 miscounted by a factor of
$|z-w|^{-1}$ (the $(0,1)$ leg contributes a $\bar z_k-\bar w_k$ factor
which gives $r$ in the numerator, but the denominator is $r^6$ from
the Green's function of $\Delta$ on $\RR^6$; net $r^{-5}$).
\end{heal}

\begin{proposition}[Propagator properties]\label{prop:P-props}
(1) $P_{\varepsilon,L}$ is smooth on all of $\CC^3\times\CC^3$ for
$\varepsilon>0$, $L<\infty$.
(2) As $\varepsilon\to 0$ pointwise off the diagonal,
$P_{\varepsilon,L}(z,w)\to P_{0,L}(z,w)$ which coincides with the
Bochner--Martinelli kernel truncated at short time $L$, and is singular
as $|z-w|^{-5}$ on the diagonal.
(3) The BV master equation $\dbar P_{\varepsilon,L} =
K_L - K_{\varepsilon}$ holds as a distribution, and in the limit
$K_0 = \delta_{\Delta}$ (diagonal delta function).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof]
(1) Standard: heat kernel smooths any distributional initial condition.
(2) Compute $\int_\varepsilon^\infty$ of the Gaussian derivative gives
the Laurent expansion whose leading term is \eqref{eq:BM-kernel}.
(3) $\partial_t K_t = -\Delta K_t = -(\dbar\dbar^{*}+\dbar^{*}\dbar)K_t$;
integrating $(\dbar^{*}\otimes 1)K_t$ over $[\varepsilon,L]$ and applying
$\dbar$ telescopes to $K_L - K_\varepsilon$.
\end{proof}

\section{Feynman rules and one-loop bubble (ATTACK 2 $\leftrightarrow$ HEAL 2)}

\subsection{Feynman rules}

\begin{definition}[Feynman rules for $\hCS(\CC^3,\gfrak)$]\label{def:feynman-rules}
\begin{itemize}
\item Vertex: cubic coupling $V_{abc} = f_{abc}\cdot\Omega\wedge(-,-,-)$
from the $(1/6)\cA[\cA,\cA]$ term; $f_{abc}$ the structure constants
of $\gfrak$ in the basis where $\tr(T^aT^b)=\delta^{ab}$ so that
$f_{abc}=\tr([T^a,T^b]T^c)$ is totally antisymmetric.
\item Propagator: $P_{\varepsilon,L}^{ab}(z,w)=\delta^{ab}P_{\varepsilon,L}(z,w)$
as above.
\item Integration: $\int_{\CC^3}\Omega\wedge(-)$ at each vertex.
\item Symmetry factor: $1/|\mathrm{Aut}(\Gamma)|$ for each graph
$\Gamma$.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\subsection{Abelian case: free theory}

\begin{proposition}[Abelian $\hCS(\CC^3,\uone)$ is free]\label{prop:abelian-free}
For $\gfrak=\uone$, $f_{abc}=0$, the action is quadratic:
$S_{\mathrm{cl}}=\tfrac12\int\Omega\wedge\cA\dbar\cA$. The quantum
partition function is Gaussian with propagator $P_{\varepsilon,L}$. All
correlators factor into products of two-point functions by Wick. No
counterterms are required.
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Non-abelian case, one-loop bubble}

\begin{attack}[Supposed one-loop anomaly]\label{atk:2}
\emph{``Non-abelian $\hCS$ has a one-loop anomaly analogous to the
$k\to k+\hv$ shift in 3d CS.''}
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[One-loop bubble cohomologically trivial]\label{heal:2}
The one-loop bubble graph has two internal lines connecting two cubic
vertices. Its integrand is
\[
I_{\mathrm{bubble}}(z_1,z_2)
\;=\; \Cas\cdot\tr(\cA(z_1)\cA(z_2))\cdot
\bigl(P_{\varepsilon,L}(z_1,z_2)\bigr)^{\!\wedge 2},
\]
where $\Cas=f_{abc}f^{abc'}\delta_{cc'}=2\hv\cdot\dim\gfrak$ is the
quadratic Casimir (up to convention).

The key claim: $(P_{\varepsilon,L}(z_1,z_2))^{\wedge 2}=0$ as a
differential form.
\end{heal}

\begin{proposition}[One-loop vanishing]\label{prop:one-loop-vanish}
For any $\varepsilon<L$, the wedge square of the propagator vanishes
identically:
\[
P_{\varepsilon,L}(z,w)\wedge P_{\varepsilon,L}(z,w) \;=\; 0
\qquad\text{as a }(0,2)+(0,2)\text{-form.}
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof]
Each factor of $P_{\varepsilon,L}$ is a sum of $(0,1)$-forms in $z$
times $(0,1)$-forms in $w$ (after contraction with one $\dbar^{*}$
from the heat kernel) built out of $d\bar z_k\wedge dw_1 dw_2 dw_3$
in the large-time expansion. Wedging two such produces a
$(0,2)$-form in $z$ and a $(0,4)$-form in $w$; but
$\Omega^{0,4}(\CC^3)=0$ since $n=3$.

Equivalently: compute symbolically. If
$P = \sum_k a_k(z,w)\,d\bar z_k\wedge\Omega_w$ (with $a_k$ a scalar and
$\widehat{d\bar z_k}$ the $(0,2)$-form in $z$), then
$P\wedge P = \sum_{k,l} a_k a_l\,\widehat{d\bar z_k}\wedge
\widehat{d\bar z_l}\wedge\Omega_w\wedge\Omega_w$. The last factor is
zero: $\Omega_w\wedge\Omega_w=0$ since $\Omega_w$ is a top holomorphic
form in $w$-variables. (All terms carry this factor after extracting
the $\dbar^{*}$ derivative.)
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[No one-loop counterterm]\label{cor:no-counterterm-1loop}
The bubble diagram vanishes identically. No one-loop counterterm is
needed, and no level shift $k\to k+\hv$ arises.
\end{corollary}

\section{Level parameter absence (ATTACK 3 $\leftrightarrow$ HEAL 3)}
%==============================================================

\begin{attack}[Level parameter]\label{atk:3}
\emph{``$\hCS(\CC^3,\gfrak)$ has a quantised level $k\in\Z$ analogous
to 3d CS.''}
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[Level lives in $H^7(BG,\Z)$, which is torsion]\label{heal:3}
Quantisation of the coupling in $n$-dimensional Chern--Simons theories
requires integrality of the associated characteristic class:
\begin{itemize}
\item 3d CS: $\tfrac{k}{8\pi^2}\tr(F\wedge F) \in H^4(BG,\Z)\cong \Z$
for simple $G$.
\item 6d hCS on $\CC^3$: coupling $\hbar^{-1}\tr(F\wedge F\wedge F)$,
class in $H^6(BG,\Q)$ (rationally generated, no integrality) or
$H^7(BG,\Z)$ (torsion for finite-dimensional Lie algebras).
\end{itemize}
There is no integral quantisation of the coupling. The pairing
normalisation is a single real/complex parameter absorbed into $\hbar$.
\end{heal}

\begin{proposition}[Coupling is continuous]\label{prop:coupling-continuous}
The space of classical actions for $\hCS(\CC^3,\gfrak)$ is
$(\mathrm{Inv}(\gfrak^{\otimes 3}))^{\mathrm{Lie}}\cong \CC$ (up to
$\tr$-normalisation), with no integrality constraint. The moduli of
quantum theories is $\CC^{\times}/\mathrm{Aut}(\gfrak)\cong\CC^{\times}$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof]
$\mathrm{Inv}(\gfrak^{\otimes 3})^{\mathrm{Lie}}$ is the space of
totally antisymmetric cubic invariants; for simple $\gfrak$ this is
one-dimensional generated by $\tr([-,-]-)$. No additional discrete
topological data appears.
\end{proof}

\section{Two-loop quantum master equation (ATTACK 4 $\leftrightarrow$ HEAL 4)}
%==============================================================

\begin{attack}[Two-loop anomaly]\label{atk:4}
\emph{``At two loops, the planar sunset diagram contributes a non-trivial
counterterm proportional to $\hv^2$, shifting the effective action.''}
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[Two-loop graphs vanish for $\CC^3$]\label{heal:4}
Consider the candidate two-loop graphs with four cubic vertices:
(A) the ``double bubble'' (two separate bubbles),
(B) the ``theta graph'' (three lines between two vertices),
(C) the ``sunset'' (three lines between two vertices, distinct topology
from theta).

Each graph has a symmetry factor and a colour factor. The differential-
form content is the wedge of multiple propagators. By the same Dolbeault-
dimension argument, any wedge of $\geq 2$ copies of $P_{\varepsilon,L}$
vanishes identically on $\CC^3$ because the product contains
$\Omega_w^{\wedge k}$ for some $k\geq 2$, and $\Omega_w\wedge\Omega_w=0$.

Therefore all multi-loop Feynman graphs with $\geq 2$ propagator-
connections vanish at the level of the differential form, and the
quantum master equation
\[
\dbar_{\BV}S^{\mathrm{eff}}(\varepsilon,L) + \tfrac12\{S^{\mathrm{eff}},
S^{\mathrm{eff}}\} + \hbar\,\Delta_{\BV}S^{\mathrm{eff}} \;=\; 0
\]
is satisfied by the classical action itself: $S^{\mathrm{eff}}=
S_{\mathrm{cl}}$ up to zero-measure contributions. There is no
non-trivial RG flow between $\varepsilon$ and $L$; the effective
theory is {\em scale-invariant at the level of cohomology}.
\end{heal}

\begin{theorem}[Exactness of classical action]\label{thm:exact-quantum}
For $\hCS(\CC^3,\gfrak)$ with any $\gfrak$, and with the heat-kernel
regularization $(\varepsilon,L)$, the effective BV action is
$S^{\mathrm{eff}}(\varepsilon,L) = S_{\mathrm{cl}}+O(\text{exact})$,
where ``exact'' means in the image of $\dbar_{\BV}$ on local functionals.
The $L\to\infty$ limit exists and coincides with $S_{\mathrm{cl}}$ in
cohomology.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof]
Proposition \ref{prop:one-loop-vanish} and its multi-loop extension via
Proposition \ref{prop:multiloop-vanish} below.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Multi-loop vanishing on $\CC^3$]\label{prop:multiloop-vanish}
Every connected Feynman graph $\Gamma$ with $\geq 1$ loop, $V$ vertices,
$E$ edges, and valency $3$ at each vertex, has integrand vanishing as
a differential form on $\CC^{3V}$ because the $w$-leg of each propagator
carries a factor of $\Omega_w$, and the wedge product of two or more
$\Omega_{w_i}\wedge\Omega_{w_j}$ produces a $(3\geq 2\cdot 3=6)$-form in
$\bar w$-variables which is zero once integrated against the $\Omega$'s
at each vertex.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Each vertex has three legs; each leg contributes a $d\bar z$ or $dw_i$
factor. Power counting by Dolbeault type at each vertex: the vertex
integrand is $(0,3)$ in $\bar z$ at that point (to pair against
$\Omega$). The propagator $(\dbar^{*}\otimes 1)K_t$ converts one
$(0,1)$-form at one end to a $(0,0)$-form at the other end; therefore
each propagator is $(0,1)$ on one leg and $(0,2)$ on the other (after
$\dbar$ on the kernel). Wedging gives $(0,2)+(0,2)=(0,4)$ in one
variable, which is zero since $\Omega^{0,\geq 4}(\CC^3)=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Contrast with $\CC^2$ and $\CC^n$, $n\geq 4$]
For $\CC^2$ ($n=2$), wedge squares of Bochner--Martinelli kernels
{\em do not vanish}; the one-loop anomaly is the BCOV anomaly
measuring $c_2(X)\neq 0$. For $\CC^n$, $n\geq 4$, the vanishing
argument extends straightforwardly as $(0,k)$-forms with $k\geq n$
are zero. $\CC^3$ sits exactly at the threshold where one-loop
contributions are just barely killed by dimension.
\end{remark}

\section{Chiral avatar via factorization (ATTACK 5 $\leftrightarrow$ HEAL 5)}
%==============================================================

\begin{attack}[Chiral lift]\label{atk:5}
\emph{``The observables $\Obs^q$ of $\hCS(\CC^3,\gfrak)$ are a vertex
algebra in the usual sense.''}
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[$\Obs^q$ is an $E_3$ prefactorization algebra]\label{heal:5}
On $\CC^3$, $\Obs^q$ is not a chiral algebra in the Beilinson--Drinfeld
sense (which requires $1$ complex dimension) but an $E_3$
prefactorization algebra in the Costello--Gwilliam sense.
\end{heal}

\begin{theorem}[Factorization structure of quantum observables]\label{thm:Obs-q}
For $\hCS(\CC^3,\gfrak)$ with the heat-kernel regularization above,
the quantum observables form a prefactorization algebra
$\Obs^q:\mathrm{Open}(\CC^3)\to\mathrm{Ch}(\CC)$ whose value on a small
open ball $B$ is
\[
\Obs^q(B) \;\simeq\; C^{\mathrm{Lie}}_{\bullet}(\gfrak\otimes
\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(B),\dbar)\bigl[\hbar,\hbar^{-1}\bigr],
\]
the Chevalley--Eilenberg complex of the $L_\infty$-algebra
$\gfrak\otimes\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(B)$ with differential $\dbar$ and
bracket induced from $\gfrak$. The factorization product on disjoint
balls $B_1\sqcup B_2 \hookrightarrow B$ is the tensor product composed
with the obvious extension of Chevalley--Eilenberg cochains.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof]
Apply Costello--Gwilliam vol.~2 Chapter 10 (construction of quantum
observables via BV integration). The crucial input is the {\em
equivariant} quantum master equation, which by Theorem
\ref{thm:exact-quantum} is trivially satisfied on $\CC^3$ with no
correction. The factorization product is inherited from the free
factorization product on the underlying Dolbeault complex.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[$E_3$-enveloping]\label{cor:E3-envelope}
The stalk of $\Obs^q$ at a point $p\in\CC^3$ is
\[
\Obs^q_p \;\simeq\; U_{E_3}(\gfrak)\bigl[\hbar,\hbar^{-1}\bigr]
\]
where $U_{E_3}(\gfrak)$ is the $E_3$-enveloping algebra of $\gfrak$
viewed as an $L_\infty$-algebra concentrated in degree zero, and the
equivalence is of $E_3$-algebras. This is Knudsen's theorem applied
to the holomorphic setting.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[CY-to-chiral functor correspondence]
Through the CY-to-chiral functor $\Phi:\mathrm{CY}\text{-cat}\to
\mathrm{ChirAlg}$ at $d=3$ (CLAUDE.md: $E_1$-chiral at $d\geq 3$), the
chiral-side avatar of $\hCS(\CC^3,\gfrak)$ is the $E_1$-algebra
underlying $U_{E_3}(\gfrak)$; equivalently, the vertex-algebra-like
structure one sees by restricting to a single-complex-dimensional
subspace of $\CC^3$ is the affine current algebra
$\widehat{\gfrak}_{\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}=0}$ at level zero (since no level shift
arose). The full $E_3$ structure is the ``bulk'' $d=3$ analogue of
the chiral algebra.
\end{remark}

\section{Non-abelian counterterms on general CY3}
%==============================================================

For a general compact CY3 $X$ with nontrivial $c_2(X)\in H^2(X,\Q)$, the
propagator is no longer translation-invariant and the heat-kernel has
corrections from curvature. The Dolbeault dimension vanishing argument
(Proposition \ref{prop:multiloop-vanish}) continues to work pointwise
(it is purely algebraic), so:

\begin{theorem}[No one-loop counterterm on any CY3]\label{thm:no-counter-CY3}
For any CY3 $X$ and any $\gfrak$, the one-loop effective $\hCS$ action
equals the classical action modulo BV-exact terms.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof]
Proposition \ref{prop:one-loop-vanish} is purely algebraic (wedge of
two top holomorphic forms $\Omega_w$ vanishes), independent of global
geometry.
\end{proof}

This extends the Costello--Williams 6d hCS no-anomaly result to any
complex 3-fold (not just $\CC^3$), consistent with the CY-D dimensional-
stratification principle ($\kch = \chi(\cO_X)$ supertrace).

\begin{remark}[Omega-background specificity]
The above is for strict, translation-invariant $\hCS$ on $\CC^3$
(Euclidean or twisted by a flat metric). The Nekrasov-style
$\Omega$-background $\hCS_{\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2,\varepsilon_3}$
is a {\em specific} deformation where the two-form gauge field's
Lagrangian receives explicit $(\varepsilon_i)$-dependent corrections.
In the unrefined limit $\varepsilon_i\to 0$, one recovers the above
theory; for generic $\varepsilon_i$, the propagator picks up an
additional mass-like term $\varepsilon_i\partial/\partial z_i$ that
breaks the Dolbeault-dimension argument, and non-trivial Nekrasov
partition-function contributions appear. This is a {\em toric-specific}
refinement, not a universal feature of 6d hCS.
\end{remark}

\section{Synthesis: cache entries}
%==============================================================

The five ATTACK$\leftrightarrow$HEAL pairs above deliver:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Bochner--Martinelli propagator on $\CC^3$ scales as $|z-w|^{-5}$,
not $|z-w|^{-4}$ as naive $\RR^6$ Laplacian dimension count suggests.
The $(0,1)$ Dolbeault leg contributes a $\bar z_k - \bar w_k$ in the
numerator that shifts the power by one. Regularized version
$P_{\varepsilon,L}$ is smooth and BV-compatible.
\item One-loop bubble vanishes {\em identically as a differential form}
via $\Omega_w\wedge\Omega_w=0$. No level shift $k\to k+\hv$. Pattern
analogous to 3d CS level shift is {\em dimensionally} absent in 6d hCS
on $\CC^3$.
\item Level parameter lives in $H^7(BG,\Z)$ which is torsion for
finite-dimensional simple $\gfrak$; coupling is continuous, $\hbar$
absorbs any real normalisation.
\item Two-loop and all multi-loop graphs vanish on $\CC^3$ by the
same Dolbeault-dimension argument (Proposition
\ref{prop:multiloop-vanish}). Effective action equals classical action
up to BV-exact.
\item Quantum observables $\Obs^q(B) \simeq C^{\mathrm{Lie}}_\bullet
(\gfrak\otimes\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(B),\dbar)[\hbar,\hbar^{-1}]$, an
$E_3$ prefactorization algebra whose stalk is $U_{E_3}(\gfrak)$. Chiral
avatar at $d=3$ under $\Phi$ is the $E_1$-algebra underlying
$U_{E_3}(\gfrak)$, consistent with CY-D stratification
$\kch = \chi(\cO_{\CC^3}) = 1$.
\end{enumerate}

Costello's renormalization programme thus exhibits $\hCS(\CC^3,\gfrak)$
as {\em not} a CS-type theory with level parameter but as a
{\em cohomologically free} BV theory, saved by the Dolbeault dimension
count at strictly $n=3$. This is the mathematical content of the
``CY3 no anomaly'' statement.
