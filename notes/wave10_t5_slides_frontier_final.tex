\subsection*{Frontier item F1. $N=8$ appears twice --- orbit-sector splitting.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (slide lines 951, 953; Mukai~1988 Theorem~0.3).
The slide deck dedicates two pages to $N=8$, not one. Mukai's bound
$N\leq 8$ for symplectic $K3$-automorphisms is saturated at $N=8$;
within $M_{23}\supset$ all finite-order symplectic K3 autos, the
$N=8$ conjugacy class $8A$ decomposes on $H^*(K3,\ZZ)\otimes\CC$ into
\emph{two} distinct orbit-sectors under the commutant
$C_{M_{24}}(8A)\simeq 8\cdot 2 \subset M_{24}$ (ATLAS). The Jacobi-form
input $F^{(g_8)}_{0,1}$ is a linear combination of \emph{two} index-1
weight-0 Jacobi forms at level $\Gamma_0(8)$, not one, and the
associated twisted $Z^X_{L, h_M}$ generating series splits into two
summands corresponding to $M_{24}$-sectors $(8A\mid 8A)$ and
$(8A\mid 8B)$ (Cheng--Duncan--Harvey~2014 twining tables). The
doubling is \emph{not} a redundant slide --- it encodes the Mukai
boundary case where the commutant ceases to be cyclic.

\subsection*{Frontier item F2. $A=\tfrac14\phi_{0,1}^2$ is an index-$2$ building block.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (slide lines 938--942).
The decomposition $A=\tfrac14\phi_{0,1}^2$, $B=\phi_{-2,1}
=\nu_{11}^2/\eta^6$ assigns $A$ to weight~$0$, \emph{index~$2$}
(because $\phi_{0,1}$ at index~1 squares to index~2), and $B$ to
weight~$-2$, index~$1$. The pair $(A, B)$ generates the bigraded ring
of weak Jacobi forms on $\mathrm{SL}_2(\ZZ)\ltimes\ZZ^2$ as a free
module over $M_*(\mathrm{SL}_2(\ZZ))$ with generators in index~$1$
(Eichler--Zagier~1985, \emph{The Theory of Jacobi Forms},
Theorem~$9.3$). The slide's specific choice $A,B$ at those weights is
the Gritsenko seed for the additive lift producing $\Delta_5$: the
Gritsenko lift $\mathrm{Lift}_1\colon J^{\mathrm{weak}}_{0,1}\to
M^{\mathrm{para}}_{10}(\Gamma_2)$ followed by Borcherds multiplicative
lift $\mathrm{Lift}_2\colon J^{\mathrm{weak}}_{0,1}\to
M^{\mathrm{para}}_{5}(\Gamma_1,\nu_{\Delta_5})$ requires \emph{both}
$A$ and $B$ as seeds at index~$1$ (Gritsenko--Nikulin~1998
Theorem~$1.4$). The index-$2$ role of $A=\tfrac14\phi_{0,1}^2$ is the
\emph{second-quantization footprint}: it records the
Hilbert-scheme-$n=2$ sector of the DMVV product
$\prod_{n}\Hilb^n(K3)$ at its most compressed.

\subsection*{Frontier item F3. Ambient lattice-polarized BKM $\mathfrak{g}_L$.}
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ (slide lines 922--936; Lorgat~2020
Conjecture~$1$). For $(S, g_N, h_M)$ with $S$ lattice-polarized by
$L\subset\Pic(S)$ of signature $(1,\rho-1)$, the ambient BKM
$\mathfrak{g}_L$ of signature $(2, \rho)$ is the Borcherds algebra
whose simple roots are the $W^{(2)}(L)$-translates of a single Weyl
vector $\rho_L$. The $(g_N, h_M)$-corrected subalgebra
$\mathfrak{g}_{(g_N, h_M)}\subset\mathfrak{g}_L$ is the fixed
subalgebra under the joint $\langle g_N, h_M\rangle$-action lifted to
$\mathfrak{g}_L$ via the $\ZZ/N$-orbifold Borcherds automorphism
(Borcherds~1992 \S 11; Gritsenko--Nikulin~1998 \S 5). The conjecture
that \emph{all} eight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery diagonal-divisor modular
forms arise as $-1/Z^X_{L, h_M}$ is equivalent to the conjecture that
the eight-element Gritsenko--Cl\'ery set of dd-forms is in bijection
with the eight orbits of $\Aut(K3, L)$-conjugacy classes of pairs
$(g_N, h_M)$ with $N, M\leq 8$ and $g_N h_M = h_M g_N$.
