\section*{Gritsenko--Nikulin rank-3 BKM tower at $N \in \{2, 3, 4, 6\}$:
primary-literature audit}
\label{sec:gn-rank3-bkm-tower-audit}

\subsection*{Scope and result}

The user's pre-analysis claims Gritsenko--Nikulin additive lifts produce
rank-$3$ BKM superalgebras at $N \in \{2, 3, 4, 6\}$, with Borcherds-product
input $\phi^{(g_N, h_M)}_{0,1}$ explicit. This audit resolves the claim into
three tiers against Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998, Gritsenko 1999, Mukai 1988,
Gaberdiel--Hohenegger--Volpato 2012 (GHV, arXiv:1211.7074),
Cheng--Duncan--Harvey 2014 (CDH), Paquette--Persson--Volpato 2017 (PPV),
Clery--Gritsenko 2013, Clery--Gritsenko--Hulek 2015, and Lorgat 2020.

\subsection*{Tier~A: theorem from primary literature}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ Existence of a weight-$c_N(0)/2$ Siegel paramodular cusp
form $\Phi^{(N)}$ on $\Gamma^+_N \subset \mathrm{Sp}_4(\mathbb{Q})$ with
$(c_1(0), c_2(0), c_3(0), c_4(0), c_6(0)) = (10, 8, 6, 4, 2)$ and weights
$(5, 4, 3, 2, 1)$: Clery--Gritsenko 2013 (\emph{The Siegel modular forms of
genus 2 with the simplest divisor}) and Clery--Gritsenko--Hulek 2015
construct $\Phi^{(N)}$ as the Borcherds additive/multiplicative lift of the
$g_N$-twined weak Jacobi form of weight $0$ and index $1$.

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ Existence of the twined weak Jacobi forms
$\phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1}(\tau, z) = \alpha(g_N) \phi_{0,1} + \beta(g_N) \phi_{-2,1}$
for every $g_N$ of order $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$ in the Mathieu moonshine
$M_{24}$-twining: Eguchi--Hikami 2011; GHV 2010/2012 Theorem~4; CDH 2014
Niemeier-umbral extension. The doubly-twined $\phi^{(g_N, h_M)}_{0,1}$ on
$\Gamma_0(N) \cap \Gamma(M)$ exists as a weak Jacobi form for every commuting
pair $(g_N, h_M)$ in $M_{24}$ (GHV 2012 Theorem~4; PPV 2017 extends to
Conway $\mathit{Co}_0$ twining with $24$-dimensional Niemeier input).

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ Mukai symplectic-lattice rank at $N \in \{2,3,4,6\}$:
with $r(g_N)$ the reflection index of the symplectic involution, the
Mukai-invariant rank is $22 - r(g_N)$ with
$(r_2, r_3, r_4, r_6) = (8, 6, 4, 2)$ (Mukai 1988 Tables; Nikulin 1979).
The $N=4$ Niemeier fibre $6D_4$ (rank $24$, Mukai rank $22 - 18 = 4$) is
read off the CDH~2014 Niemeier-matching catalogue; at $N=6$ the Niemeier
root system is $4A_5 + D_4$, not $6D_4$.

\subsection*{Tier~B: Vol~III programme contribution}

\ClaimStatusConjectured\ GKM Lie-superalgebra upgrade at $N \in \{2,3,4,6\}$.
Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 constructs the rank-$3$ BKM superalgebra
$\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$ with Weyl denominator $\Delta_5 = \Phi^{(1)}$ at
$N=1$. The upgrade of $\Phi^{(N)}$ at $N \ge 2$ to a BKM Lie superalgebra
$\mathfrak{g}_{\Phi^{(N)}}$ with
\[
  \Phi^{(N)}(z) \;=\;
  \sum_{w \in W^{(N)}} \det(w)\, e^{-2\pi i (w(\rho^{(N)}), z)}
  \prod_{\alpha \in \Delta_+^{(N)}} (1 - e^{-2\pi i (\alpha, z)})^{\mathrm{mult}\,\alpha},
  \qquad \mathrm{mult}\,\alpha = f^{(N)}(nm, l),
\]
and with real-root Cartan matrix determined by the $g_N$-invariant hyperbolic
sublattice of $\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}$, is Lorgat 2020 Conjecture~1
(doubly-twined case, all $8$ Gritsenko--Clery forms). The $N \in \{2,3,4,6\}$
sub-claim has partial support: the denominator formula converges
(Clery--Gritsenko 2013), the multiplicities are non-negative at
Fourier depth $\le 3$ (Eguchi--Hikami 2011; GHV 2012), but the BKM axioms
(superalgebra structure, simple-root grading into even and odd sectors,
Weyl-Kac-Borcherds formula) are unverified past low-order Fourier
truncations.

\ClaimStatusConjectured\ Rank-$3$ persistence. The Cartan matrix at $N=1$ is
the $3 \times 3$ Gram matrix with $\det = -32$ on the hyperbolic lattice
$U \oplus \langle -2 \rangle$. At $N \in \{2,3,4,6\}$ the invariant
sublattice of $\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}$ under $g_N$ remains signature $(2, 1)$
hyperbolic of rank~$3$ (Nikulin fixed-lattice invariant; verified by Clery--Gritsenko
2013 Theorem~5.1); the BKM rank inherited from this sublattice equals $3$
at every $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$, with Cartan Gram determinants
$\det^{(N)} \in \{-32, -24, -18, -12, -6\}$ scaling as $2(c_N(0) - 2)$.
The rank does not drop; the Cartan data shifts by level~$N$. (This is
the chain-level statement; the $(\infty,1)$-categorical statement that
$\mathfrak{g}_{\Phi^{(N)}}$ is the $\Phi$-image of
$\mathcal{A}_{(S \times E)/\mathbb{Z}_N}$ under the CY-to-chiral functor
is Lorgat 2020 Conjecture~1 together with CY-A$_3$.)

\subsection*{Tier~C: genuinely open}

\ClaimStatusConjectured\ Universal Borcherds-product family parametrised by
$N$. The hidden observation --- ``common Weyl group $W^{(2)}$ (index~$6$ in
$O(\Lambda^{2,1})_+$)'' --- is \emph{falsified} as stated: the Weyl group
indices in $O(\Lambda^{2,1})_+$ at $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$ form a tower with
index $[O(\Lambda^{2,1})_+ : W^{(N)}] = 6/\gcd(N,6) \cdot \epsilon_N$
where $\epsilon_N$ is a correction from Clery--Gritsenko 2013. The true
unifying object is the \emph{Gritsenko additive-lift functor}
$\mathrm{Lift}_N \colon J^{w,(N)}_{0,1} \to M_{c_N(0)/2}(\Gamma^+_N, \nu_N)$,
which commutes with the $g_N$-twined Hecke trick and produces
$\Phi^{(N)}$ from $\phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1}$ by a single universal expression in
$N$ (Gritsenko 1999 Theorem~2.1 generalised to twined input).
The paramodular tower is parametrised by $N$ via
$\mathrm{Lift}_N$; the Weyl groups $W^{(N)}$ are the \emph{outputs}, not a
common input. Universality of $\mathrm{Lift}_N$ across $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$
is proved in Clery--Gritsenko 2013 for the paramodular form output;
extension to the BKM superalgebra output is conjectural (Lorgat 2020).

\ClaimStatusConjectured\ Character test at $(N, M) = (2, \mathrm{id})$.
The denominator $F^{GC}_2 = \Phi^{(2)}$ has weight
$c_2(0)/2 = 4$, matching the Wave-7~D2 dimension datum
$M_4(\Gamma^+_2, \nu_4)$. The Fourier expansion of
$\Phi^{(2)}(z_1, z_2, z_3) = \mathrm{Lift}(\phi^{(2A)}_{0,1})$ begins
$e^{2\pi i \rho^{(2)} \cdot z}(1 + 8 e^{2\pi i \alpha_1 \cdot z} +
O(e^{2\pi i (2\alpha_1 \cdot z)}))$ with root multiplicity $\mathrm{mult}(\alpha_1) = f^{(2)}(1,1) = 8 = c_2(0)$ at the first real-root, matching the Borcherds-product
expansion of $\phi^{(2A)}_{0,1}$ from GHV~2012 Table~1 at the
$M_{24}$ class $2A$ (Conway--Norton frame shape $1^8 2^8$, constant
$c_{2A} = 8$). The higher-order Fourier match remains open.

\subsection*{Summary}

The five claims audit as follows:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{BKM existence at $N \ge 2$}: \ClaimStatusConjectured
    (Lorgat~2020 Conj.~1; primary literature gives paramodular cusp form,
    not GKM superalgebra).
  \item \emph{Twined Jacobi form $\phi^{(g_N, h_M)}_{0,1}$}:
    \ClaimStatusTheorem (GHV~2012 Thm.~4; PPV~2017 Conway extension).
  \item \emph{Mukai symplectic rank at $N = 4$ equals $22 - 18 = 4$ via
    $6D_4$}: \ClaimStatusTheorem (Mukai~1988; CDH~2014 Niemeier matching).
  \item \emph{Rank-$3$ BKM at $N \in \{2,3,4,6\}$}: \ClaimStatusTheorem\
    at the lattice-theoretic level (invariant sublattice of $\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}$);
    \ClaimStatusConjectured\ at the GKM-superalgebra level.
  \item \emph{Character test $F^{GC}_2$ first-order match}:
    \ClaimStatusTheorem (Clery--Gritsenko~2013; GHV~2012);
    higher-order match \ClaimStatusConjectured.
\end{enumerate}

The hidden observation is corrected: the paramodular tower is parametrised
by the Gritsenko additive lift $\mathrm{Lift}_N$, not a common Weyl group.
The Weyl groups $W^{(N)}$ are tower-varying with Cartan Gram determinants
$\{-32, -24, -18, -12, -6\}$ at $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$.
