\begin{abstract}
The conifold transition separates three algebraic layers.  The resolved
conifold has a Klebanov--Witten noncommutative crepant resolution, whose
critical cohomological Hall algebra is the positive half
$Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}(1|1))^{\mathrm{con}}$.  The full current algebra and
its universal $R$-matrix arise after Drinfeld doubling or the
Drinfeld centre of the $E_1$ representation category.  The singular wall
has a rank-one vanishing-cycle shadow with primitive
Gopakumar--Vafa invariant $n^0_{[\bP^1]}=1$.  The chiral invariant of the
conifold is the direct McKay/NCCR value
$\kch(A_{\mathrm{conifold}})=1$.
\end{abstract}

\section{The conifold wall}

Let
\[
  Y_0=\operatorname{Spec}\C[x,y,z,w]/(xy-zw)
\]
be the affine ordinary double point.  Its smoothing
$Y_\epsilon=\{xy-zw=\epsilon\}\subset\C^4$ is diffeomorphic to
$T^*S^3$.  Its small resolution is
\[
  X_{\mathrm{con}}
  =
  \Tot\bigl(\cO_{\bP^1}(-1)\oplus\cO_{\bP^1}(-1)\to\bP^1\bigr),
\]
a smooth quasi-projective toric Calabi-Yau threefold with one compact
curve.  Strominger's massless hypermultiplet is the D3-brane on the
vanishing $S^3$ of the smoothing.  The Gopakumar--Vafa generator on the
resolution side is the M2-brane on the exceptional curve
$[\bP^1]$.

The algebraic object attached to the resolved chamber is the positive
Hall half.  At dimension $d=3$, the CY-to-chiral output is native
$E_1$ data.  Braiding, an $R$-matrix, and the full current algebra live
after a Hopf pairing and Drinfeld double, or equivalently after passing
to the Drinfeld centre of the $E_1$ representation category on the
constructed locus.

\section{The Klebanov--Witten positive half}

Let $Q_{\mathrm{KW}}$ be the two-vertex quiver with arrows
$a_1,a_2\colon 0\to 1$ and $b_1,b_2\colon 1\to 0$, and potential
\[
  W_{\mathrm{KW}}
  =
  \operatorname{tr}(a_1b_1a_2b_2-a_1b_2a_2b_1).
\]
The Jacobi algebra
\[
  J_{\mathrm{KW}}
  =
  \C Q_{\mathrm{KW}}/
  \langle \partial_a W_{\mathrm{KW}} : a\in Q_{\mathrm{KW},1}\rangle
\]
has centre
\[
  Z(J_{\mathrm{KW}})
  =
  \C[\widehat x,\widehat y,\widehat z,\widehat w]/
  (\widehat x\widehat y-\widehat z\widehat w).
\]
The central lifts are
\[
  \widehat x=a_1b_1+b_1a_1,\quad
  \widehat y=a_2b_2+b_2a_2,\quad
  \widehat z=a_1b_2+b_2a_1,\quad
  \widehat w=a_2b_1+b_1a_2.
\]
In the $e_0$ corner the four central generators restrict to the mesons
\[
  x=a_1b_1,\quad y=a_2b_2,\quad z=a_1b_2,\quad w=a_2b_1,
  \qquad xy=zw.
\]
Thus $J_{\mathrm{KW}}$ is the Van den Bergh--Szendr\H{o}i
noncommutative crepant resolution of the affine conifold.

\begin{definition}[Conifold positive half]
The positive half of the resolved conifold is
\[
  Y^+(X_{\mathrm{con}})
  :=
  \CoHA(Q_{\mathrm{KW}},W_{\mathrm{KW}}),
\]
the critical CoHA of the Klebanov--Witten Jacobi model:
\[
  \CoHA(Q_{\mathrm{KW}},W_{\mathrm{KW}})
  =
  \bigoplus_{\gamma\in\Z_{\geq0}^2}
  H^{*}_{c,G_\gamma}
  \bigl(\operatorname{Rep}_\gamma(Q_{\mathrm{KW}}),
  \varphi_{\operatorname{tr}(W_{\mathrm{KW}})_\gamma}\bigr)^\vee,
  \qquad
  G_\gamma=\mathrm{GL}_{\gamma_0}\times\mathrm{GL}_{\gamma_1}.
\]
The product is the Kontsevich--Soibelman critical correspondence for
short exact sequences of $Q_{\mathrm{KW}}$-representations.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Hall recognition of the conifold positive half]
Davison--Meinhardt PBW, the
Morrison--Mozgovoy--Nagao--Szendr\H{o}i root decomposition, and the
Li--Yamazaki current presentation identify the critical Hall algebra as
\[
  \CoHA(Q_{\mathrm{KW}},W_{\mathrm{KW}})
  \simeq
  Y^+\!\bigl(\widehat{\fgl}(1|1)\bigr)^{\mathrm{con}}
\]
as a $\Z_{\geq0}^2$-graded connected bialgebra in super-vector spaces.
The positive roots are
\[
  \Delta_+^{\mathrm{re}}
  =
  \{(n+1,n),(n,n+1):n\geq0\},
  \qquad
  \Delta_+^{\mathrm{im}}=\{(n,n):n\geq1\}.
\]
The real primitive sectors have superdimension $0|1$ and the imaginary
primitive sectors have superdimension $2|0$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Davison's critical Hall construction supplies the connected bialgebra.
The Davison--Meinhardt PBW theorem identifies its associated graded
object with the super-symmetric algebra on the BPS Lie algebra.  The
MMNS computation gives the affine $A_1^{(1)}$ root support displayed
above, with real roots $(n+1,n),(n,n+1)$ and imaginary roots $(n,n)$.
Li--Yamazaki give the matching current presentation for the positive
half of the affine super Yangian of $\widehat{\fgl}(1|1)$.  The
identification is therefore a Hall-recognition theorem for the positive
half, before adjoining the negative half, Cartan completion, or
universal $R$-matrix.
\end{proof}

The doubled conifold quantum group is
\[
  D\!\left(Y^+(X_{\mathrm{con}})\right)
  =
  Y\!\bigl(\widehat{\fgl}(1|1)\bigr)^{\mathrm{con}},
\]
after the conifold Hopf pairing and completion.  This doubled object
carries the Kulish-Sklyanin super $R$-matrix.  The symbol $Y^+$ in this
note always denotes the positive Hall half before this double or centre
passage.

\section{Two chart descent}

The toric resolution has two affine charts $U_0,U_1\simeq\C^3$ and
overlap $U_{01}\simeq\C^*\times\C^2$.  With homogeneous coordinates
$[t_0:t_1]$ on $\bP^1$, set $t=t_1/t_0$ on $U_0$ and
$\tilde t=t_0/t_1$ on $U_1$.  If $u,v$ are the two fibre coordinates on
$U_0$, then on the overlap
\[
  \tilde t=t^{-1},\qquad \tilde u=tu,\qquad \tilde v=tv.
\]
The holomorphic form transforms by
\[
  d\tilde t\wedge d\tilde u\wedge d\tilde v
  =
  -\,dt\wedge du\wedge dv,
\]
which is the Calabi-Yau sign of the $\cO(-1)^{\oplus2}$ cocycle.

Each chart carries the Jordan-quiver critical CoHA
\[
  \CoHA(\C^3)\simeq Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1),
\]
the Schiffmann--Vasserot/Tsymbaliuk affine-Yangian positive half.  The
overlap records the Laurent coordinate and the stability-wall cocycle.
The \v{C}ech expression
\[
  \holim\!\left(
  Y^+(U_0)\times Y^+(U_1)\rightrightarrows Y^+(U_{01})
  \right)
\]
is descent notation for the tilting atlas.  Its algebraic realization
is the Klebanov--Witten Jacobi CoHA above.  The two chart copies of
$Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$ are restrictions of
$\CoHA(Q_{\mathrm{KW}},W_{\mathrm{KW}})$ to the two toric chambers.

\begin{proposition}[Positive half from the tilting atlas]
The resolved conifold gluing sends the two $\C^3$ chart positive halves
to the Klebanov--Witten positive half
$Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}(1|1))^{\mathrm{con}}$.  The full Yangian-type
object appears after applying $D(-)$ or the centre construction to this
positive half.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The ring-level descent above gives the toric small resolution.  The
tilting bundle
$\cO_{X_{\mathrm{con}}}\oplus\pi^*\cO_{\bP^1}(1)$ has endomorphism
algebra $J_{\mathrm{KW}}$, where
$\pi\colon X_{\mathrm{con}}\to\bP^1$ is the bundle projection.  The
centre of $J_{\mathrm{KW}}$ is the affine conifold ring.  Passing from
coherent sheaves through this NCCR to critical CoHA gives
$\CoHA(Q_{\mathrm{KW}},W_{\mathrm{KW}})$.  Restricting the tilting
presentation to either affine toric chart removes the compact curve
class and recovers the one-vertex $\C^3$ chart positive half
$Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$.  The imaginary root spaces
$(n,n)$ of the conifold source are precisely the compact-curve
contribution absent on a single chart.  The wall cocycle supplies the
transition map between chamber restrictions; the Hall-recognition
theorem supplies the global algebra.  Drinfeld doubling is a separate
operation requiring the Hopf pairing.
\end{proof}

\section{Wall crossing and the rank-one shadow}

Let $e_0,e_1$ be the two simple conifold charges and let
\(\delta=e_0+e_1\).  The conifold wall is the alignment of
$e_0$ and $e_1$; after projection to curve class, $\delta$ maps to the
primitive compact curve $\gamma=[\bP^1]$.  In the completed quantum
torus, the Kontsevich--Soibelman ordered product is constant across the
wall, while the chamber factorization changes by the middle
dilogarithm
\[
  K_{e_0}K_{e_1}=K_{e_1}K_{\delta}K_{e_0},
  \qquad
  K_{\delta}=\mathbb{E}(x_{\delta})^{\Omega(\delta)}.
\]
The primitive wall exponent is
\[
  \Omega(\delta)=1,
  \qquad
  \delta\mapsto\gamma=[\bP^1].
\]
Equivalently, the primitive genus-zero Gopakumar--Vafa invariant is
\[
  n^0_{[\bP^1]}=1,\qquad n^0_{d[\bP^1]}=0\quad(d>1),
\]
with higher-degree terms in the partition function supplied by
multicovers of the primitive dilogarithm.

\begin{theorem}[Conifold transition in the positive half]
The transition identifies the smoothing and resolution after
taking the primitive abelian BPS shadow.  On the smoothing side, the
shadow is generated by the vanishing $S^3$ hypermultiplet.  On the
resolution side, it is generated by the primitive class $[\bP^1]$ with
$n^0_{[\bP^1]}=1$.  The common shadow is the rank-one Heisenberg Fock
algebra attached to one primitive BPS generator:
\[
  \Heis\bigl(\C\cdot e_\gamma\bigr).
\]
Before abelianization, the resolved side remains the positive CoHA
$\CoHA(Q_{\mathrm{KW}},W_{\mathrm{KW}})$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Strominger identifies the vanishing-cycle hypermultiplet on the
smoothing with the D3-brane on $S^3$.  Gopakumar and Vafa identify the
resolved-side primitive state with the M2-brane on the exceptional
curve.  Morrison--Mozgovoy--Nagao--Szendr\H{o}i compute the conifold
chamber structure and root decomposition; the rank-two KS identity has
the single middle factor $K_\delta$ with exponent one.  The curve-class
projection sends $\delta$ to $[\bP^1]$, giving the primitive genus-zero
Gopakumar--Vafa value \(n^0_{[\bP^1]}=1\).  Taking the primitive
abelian shadow forgets the noncommutative KW root system and retains the
one-dimensional BPS space $\C\cdot e_\gamma$.  Its Fock quantization is
the rank-one Heisenberg algebra.  The shadow functor leaves the
positive CoHA, its Drinfeld double, and the full current algebra as
three distinct objects.
\end{proof}

\section{The chiral invariant}

\begin{proposition}[Conifold chiral invariant]
For the resolved conifold,
\[
  \kch(A_{\mathrm{conifold}})=1.
\]
This is the direct McKay/NCCR value of the Klebanov--Witten Jacobi
model.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The local-surface formula has input $\Tot(\omega_S\to S)$ for a Fano
surface $S$.  The resolved conifold has base $\bP^1$ and rank-two
fibre $\cO(-1)^{\oplus2}$, so it lies outside that formula.  The
conifold is therefore not a local surface.  This separates it from
the local surface
$K_{\bP^2}=\Tot(\omega_{\bP^2})$, where
\(\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(K_{\bP^2})
=\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(\bP^2)/2=3/2\).

Three conifold computations give the same normalization.  First, the
McKay/NCCR model has the tilting algebra $J_{\mathrm{KW}}$ and one
primitive compact curve class.  The primitive stable object supported
on that curve has unit Hall weight.  Second, the reduced conifold
topological vertex has primitive invariant
\(n^0_{[\bP^1]}=1\), with higher-degree terms coming from multicovers.
Third, the KS pentagon above has exactly one middle wall factor of
exponent one.  The abelian shadow is therefore a single level-one
Heisenberg factor, and its modular characteristic is one:
\(\kch(A_{\mathrm{conifold}})=1\).
\end{proof}

The quasi-projective total space requires an additional compactification
before \(\kcat=\chi(\cO_X)\) can be read as a compact Calabi--Yau
Euler characteristic.  A Borcherds product supplies separate
\(\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}\) data, and a compact fibre or lattice supplies
separate \(\kfib\) data.  The standalone invariant of this note is the
positive-half and shadow value \(\kch(A_{\mathrm{conifold}})=1\).
