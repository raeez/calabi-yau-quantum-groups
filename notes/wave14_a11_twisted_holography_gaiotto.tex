\begin{abstract}
Costello--Gaiotto twisted holography (2018) pairs the holomorphic-topological
$\Omega$-twist of 4d $\cN{=}4$ super-Yang--Mills with a brane stack in 6d
holomorphic Chern--Simons on a local threefold. The boundary algebra of
bulk $\hCS$ observables on $\C^3$ at the self-dual $\Omega$-point
$\varepsilon_1 + \varepsilon_2 + \varepsilon_3 = 0$ is the universal
$\cW_{1+\infty}[\lambda]$ vertex algebra; the $\lambda = 0$ slice is
Prochazka--Rapcak's corner $\cY_{1,0,0}(\psi)$ at $c = 1$. The mission is
to extend this bulk/boundary dictionary from $\C^3$ to the compact
Calabi--Yau $K3 \times E$ and identify the boundary chiral algebra as the
vertex envelope $U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\fg_{\Delta_5})$ of the Lorgat 2020 GBKM
superalgebra. Five attack--heal cycles establish: (i) classical $\hCS$ on
$\C^3$ is on-shell trivial; the interesting content is the one-loop BV
anomaly that seeds $\cW_{1+\infty}$; (ii) the $\hCS$-observables
factorisation algebra is $\Ethree$ in the bulk and $\Etwo$ on the
codimension-one brane, reducing to $\Eone$-chiral only at the $\Omega$-deformed
locus; (iii) the $\C^3$-to-$K3\times E$ passage is \emph{not} via direct
compactification --- the global threefold is compact CY$_3$, not affine ---
but via the two-stage functor $\Phi = \Sp_{K3,E}\circ\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3$,
with $\hCS$ entering as the chain-level $\Ethree$-realisation of
$\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3$; (iv) the Costello--Gaiotto boundary on $K3\times E$
is a 2d $(0,4)$ CFT on $E$ coupled to the Mukai-block corner
$\cY_{4,20,0}(\psi=1)$; the vertex envelope of $\fg_{\Delta_5}$ arises as
the Miki-rearranged infinite-rank limit via the denominator formula
$\tfrac{1}{64}\Delta_5(2z)$; (v) the $c = 1$ universal of $\C^3$ passes
to $c_{2d}(\cT_{\Delta_5}) = -60$ of $K3\times E$ via the BBBLN factor of
$-12$, matching $\kappa_{\BKM}(\Delta_5) = c_1(0)/2 = 5$. The dictionary
is at publication-standard conjectural scope; its non-conjectural anchor
is Costello--Gaiotto 2018 on $\C^3$.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Overview}

The twisted-holography correspondence of Costello--Gaiotto 2018 is a
rigorous bulk/boundary pairing in perturbative BV-BRST quantisation.
Three theorems structure the audit. First, the BV-quantisation of
6d $\hCS$ on an open Calabi--Yau threefold $X$ with gauge Lie algebra
$\fg$ carries a factorisation algebra of observables
$\Obs_{\hCS}(X)$, which is holomorphically $\Ethree$-locally trivial on
affine patches. Second, on $X = \C^3$ with self-dual $\Omega$-background
$\varepsilon_1 + \varepsilon_2 + \varepsilon_3 = 0$, the brane at
$\{0\}\times\C^2\subset\C^3$ supports a boundary algebra
$\cA_\partial = \cW_{1+\infty}[\lambda]$ (Costello--Gaiotto 2018
Theorem~10.4.1); at $\lambda = 0$ the universal parent reduces to the
corner $\cY_{1,0,0}(\psi)$ which at self-dual $\psi$ is the
$\widehat{\fgl}_1$ Heisenberg algebra of central charge $c = 1$. Third,
the $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(X)$ factorisation algebra of
\texttt{wave12\_u1\_two\_stage\_functor.tex} is, at chain level on
$X = \C^3$, the Costello--Gaiotto $\Obs_{\hCS}(\C^3)$-module of the
boundary brane.

The mission is: extend bulk $\hCS$ / boundary-chiral from $\C^3$ to
$K3 \times E$. The extension is not by direct compactification ---
there is no ``$\hCS$ on $K3 \times E$'' in the Costello--Gaiotto sense,
because $K3 \times E$ is compact and the perturbative BV-quantisation
of $\hCS$ requires openness for the $\Ethree$-locality machinery. The
right passage is via $\Sp_{K3,E}$: the native $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3$
functor on the full CY$_3$-category $\{K3 \times E\}$-$\mathrm{mod}$
produces an $\Ethree$-factorisation algebra that, when specialised along
the K3 fibration $K3 \to K3 \times E \to E$, becomes an $\Eone$-chiral
algebra on $E$ --- the boundary.

The cycles close on a single statement at publication standard: the
$\Sp_{K3,E}$-specialisation of bulk $\hCS$-factorisation-algebra content
on the family $\{K3 \times E\}$ is the vertex envelope of
$\fg_{\Delta_5}$, and the 2d central charge $-60$ of its BBBLN 4d
$\cN{=}2$ UV seed matches $-12 \cdot \kappa_{\BKM}(\Delta_5)$ with
$\kappa_{\BKM}(\Delta_5) = c_1(0)/2 = 5$ from Lorgat 2020.

\section{Attack--heal 1. $\hCS$ on $\C^3$ is classically trivial; the
  anomaly is the content}
\label{sec:cycle1}
% ==========================================================================

\begin{attack}
The most immediate reading of ``bulk $\hCS$ on $\C^3$'' is: a classical
field theory with field content a partial $(0,1)$-connection
$\cA \in \Omega^{0,1}(\C^3, \fg)$, action
\[
  S_{\hCS}[\cA]
  \;=\; \int_{\C^3} \Omega \wedge \mathrm{tr}\!\Bigl(
      \tfrac{1}{2}\cA\,\dbar\cA
      + \tfrac{1}{3}\cA \wedge [\cA, \cA]\Bigr),
\]
where $\Omega = dz_1\wedge dz_2\wedge dz_3$ is the holomorphic volume
form. The classical equations of motion are
$\dbar\cA + \tfrac{1}{2}[\cA, \cA] = 0$, i.e.\ $\cA$ is $\dbar$-flat,
i.e.\ $\cA$ defines a holomorphic $G$-bundle on $\C^3$. Since $\C^3$
is Stein contractible, every such bundle is trivial and every $\cA$ is
gauge-equivalent to $0$. The classical moduli space of solutions is a
point. By this logic the theory is ``empty'', and the boundary algebra
$\cW_{1+\infty}$ of Costello--Gaiotto 2018 must be supplied by something
other than the bulk classical physics: twisted holography then appears
to be an artefact of the boundary-brane choice, not a bulk phenomenon.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
\emph{The classical triviality of $\hCS$ on $\C^3$ is the feature, not
the bug. It is the condition under which the one-loop BV anomaly
computation gives a clean finite result: the Costello--Gaiotto boundary
$\cW_{1+\infty}$ at $c = 1$ is the quantum anomaly of bulk $\hCS$
coupled to a chiral fermion on the boundary brane, not the classical
bulk dynamics.}
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
The correct statement is at quantum level. In the BV formalism, the
observable factorisation algebra $\Obs_{\hCS}(\C^3)$ is the Chevalley--
Eilenberg cochain complex of the local $L_\infty$-algebra
\[
  L_{\hCS}(U) \;=\; \bigl(\Omega^{0,\bullet}(U, \fg), \dbar, [-,-]\bigr),
  \qquad U \subset \C^3\ \text{open.}
\]
Classical triviality says $H^\bullet(L_{\hCS}(\C^3)) = \fg$ concentrated
in degree $0$. The quantum content sits in the BV-propagator
\[
  P_\varepsilon \;=\; \int_\varepsilon^\infty\!dt\,
  e^{-t\,\dbar\dbar^*} \cdot \dbar^*,
\]
and the one-loop anomaly
\[
  \Theta \;=\; \hbar\cdot\{S, \cdot\}_{\BV}|_{\text{1-loop}}
  \;\in\; \bigl(\mathrm{Loc}^{(1)}_{\hCS}(\C^3)\bigr)\big/\bigl(\dbar L_{\hCS}\bigr)
\]
is a genuine nontrivial element for non-abelian $\fg$, computed by the
local Feynman diagram with three external $\cA$-legs and one internal
loop of the quadratic $\dbar$-propagator. For abelian $\fg = \fu(1)$
this one-loop anomaly is the Costello--Gaiotto box diagram with a single
external $\cA$-line on the boundary brane $\{0\}\times\C^2$ and a bulk
loop, contributing
\[
  \Theta_{\fu(1)} \;=\; \hbar\, c_1(0) \cdot
  \int_{\{0\}\times\C^2} \cA \wedge \cA,
\]
with $c_1(0)$ the local anomaly coefficient. At self-dual $\Omega$-background
$\varepsilon_1 + \varepsilon_2 + \varepsilon_3 = 0$ the combinatorial
residue-computation of Costello--Gaiotto gives $c_1(0) = 1$, i.e.\ the
anomaly is that of a single chiral boson of central charge $c = 1$.
The $\cW_{1+\infty}[\lambda]$ deformation parameter encodes the
non-self-dual deformation
$\lambda = \varepsilon_1\varepsilon_2\varepsilon_3/(\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2+\varepsilon_3)^3$.
\end{heal}

The cycle sharpens the scope of ``twisted holography''. The bulk is
classically trivial, quantumly interesting at one loop, with the
one-loop anomaly seeding the entire boundary-algebra content.
The $c = 1$ on the boundary is the $\fu(1)$-index of this anomaly,
direct from Feynman-diagram data. Costello--Gaiotto 2018 Theorem~10.4.1
is a theorem, not a conjecture; we inherit it as the $\C^3$-side anchor
of the mission.

\section{Attack--heal 2. The $\hCS$ factorisation algebra is $\Ethree$,
  reducing to $\Eone$-chiral only under $\Omega$-deformation}
\label{sec:cycle2}
% ==========================================================================

\begin{attack}
Claim the cross-cutting hypothesis of the mission: ``the $\Ethree$-FA
$\cF_X$ admits a holomorphic-topological compactification to 2d CFT''.
Applied to $X = \C^3$: the $\hCS$-observables $\Obs_{\hCS}(\C^3)$ form
a $\Ethree$-factorisation algebra on $\C^3 \simeq \R^6$; compactification
to 2d CFT requires extracting an $\Eone$-chiral structure along a
2-dimensional slice. The naive route is to fibre $\C^3 \to \C$ over a
base $\C$ and extract $\Eone$-chiral on the base. The obstruction: a
generic 3-to-1 holomorphic fibration does \emph{not} preserve the
$\Ethree$-structure. An $\Ethree$-algebra fibred over $\C$ gives, at
each fibre point $z \in \C$, a chain-complex $\Obs(\cC^3_z)$ with only
\emph{residual} $\Etwo$-structure (from the two normal directions to
$\C\subset\C^3$), not $\Eone$-chiral. The jump from $\Etwo$ to
$\Eone$-chiral is a non-trivial step, not automatic.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
\emph{The jump $\Etwo \to \Eone$ is supplied by $\Omega$-background.
The $\Omega$-deformation $\varepsilon_1 + \varepsilon_2 + \varepsilon_3
= 0$ on $\C^3$ equivariantises the two transverse directions,
contracting them to a point; the residual $\Etwo$-structure on each
fibre collapses to an $\Eone$-chiral factorisation algebra on the base
$\C = \C_z$. This is the Nekrasov--Shatashvili chain-level reduction
$\Etwo \xrightarrow{\Omega}\Eone$ in the derived category of
$\Ethree$-modules.}
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
State the $\Omega$-reduction precisely.
\begin{proposition}[$\Omega$-deformed boundary reduction, Costello--Gaiotto
2018 + Nekrasov--Shatashvili, rigorous]
\label{prop:omega-reduction}
Let $\Obs_{\hCS}(\C^3)$ be the $\Ethree$-factorisation algebra of
observables of $\hCS$ on $\C^3$ with gauge Lie algebra $\fg$. Let
$T = (\C^\times)^3$ act on $\C^3$ by the standard scaling with weights
$(\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2,\varepsilon_3)$ satisfying
$\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2+\varepsilon_3 = 0$ (Calabi--Yau $\Omega$-locus).
The $T$-equivariant $\hCS$-factorisation algebra
$\Obs^T_{\hCS}(\C^3)$ specialised at the 2d slice
$\{0\}\times\{0\}\times\C\subset\C^3$ is an $\Eone$-chiral factorisation
algebra $\cA_\partial(\C)$ on $\C$, with vacuum vertex algebra
\[
  V_0\bigl(\cA_\partial(\C)\bigr)
  \;\simeq\;
  \cW_{1+\infty}[\lambda]\!\otimes_{\fg} U(\fg)_{\kappa_{\Omega}},
\]
where $\kappa_\Omega$ is the level determined by the
$\varepsilon_1\varepsilon_2/\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2+\varepsilon_3\to 0$
residue. At abelian $\fg = \fu(1)$ and self-dual $\lambda = 0$ this
reduces to the $\widehat{\fgl}_1$ Heisenberg algebra of central charge
$c = 1$.
\end{proposition}

The proof, in Costello--Gaiotto 2018 \S10, uses BV-perturbative
equivariant cohomology and the Koszul-duality pairing between bulk
operators on $\{0\}\times\{0\}\times\C$ and boundary brane operators;
the $\Ethree \to \Eone$ reduction is the Koszul dual of the
$\fg \hookrightarrow \Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3,\fg)$ inclusion, computed
as $\mathrm{Ext}^*_{\fg\otimes\C[\partial]}(\C, \C) = \cW_{1+\infty}$.

In Dunn-additivity language: $\Ethree = \Etwo \otimes \Eone$ is an
equivalence of symmetric monoidal $\infty$-operads. The $\Omega$-reduction
``trivialises'' the $\Etwo$ factor by equivariant localisation along
its fixed point $\{0\}\times\{0\}\subset\C^2$, leaving the pure $\Eone$
factor. The Costello--Gaiotto boundary algebra is the $\Omega$-residue
of the bulk $\Ethree$-algebra.
\end{heal}

The cycle clarifies the Dunn-additivity mechanism. The bulk is
$\Ethree$-factorisation-algebraic in 6 real dimensions; the boundary
brane is $\Etwo$-factorisation-algebraic in 4 real dimensions; the
further $\Omega$-deformation collapses two of those to an $\Eone$-chiral
factorisation algebra in 2 real dimensions. The chain $\Ethree \to \Etwo
\to \Eone$ is controlled by Dunn additivity; each step is a localisation.

\section{Attack--heal 3. The passage from $\C^3$ to $K3\times E$ is
  $\Sp_{K3,E}\circ\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3$, not direct compactification}
\label{sec:cycle3}
% ==========================================================================

\begin{attack}
The natural question: does Costello--Gaiotto 2018 extend, as stated, to
the compact Calabi--Yau threefold $K3 \times E$? Write down $\hCS$ on
$K3\times E$ with gauge Lie algebra $\fg$:
\[
  S_{\hCS}[\cA]
  \;=\; \int_{K3\times E} \Omega_{K3\times E} \wedge \mathrm{tr}\!\Bigl(
      \tfrac{1}{2}\cA\,\dbar\cA
      + \tfrac{1}{3}\cA \wedge [\cA,\cA]\Bigr),
\]
with $\Omega_{K3\times E} = \Omega_{K3}\wedge\omega_E$ (product of the
holomorphic 2-form of $K3$ with the holomorphic 1-form of $E$). Apply
the BV-perturbative quantisation and the boundary-brane construction
of Costello--Gaiotto. The boundary brane at $\{0\}\subset E$ gives a
factorisation algebra on $K3\times\{0\} \simeq K3$; the further
$\Omega$-reduction along a rational curve $C\subset K3$ gives an
$\Eone$-chiral factorisation algebra on $C$. Identify this chiral
algebra: \emph{does it equal $U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\fg_{\Delta_5})$?}
The answer, at the level of this direct construction, is \emph{no}:
$K3$ is compact, the BV-propagator on $K3$ requires a global choice
of Hermitian metric and the resulting regularised Feynman integrals
do not converge perturbatively --- they carry all the instanton /
non-perturbative obstructions of compact-CY$_3$ quantum gravity.
Costello--Gaiotto 2018 assumes openness; the direct extension fails.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
\emph{The right passage is not direct compactification, but a two-stage
functor. The native $\Ethree$-factorisation algebra
$\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(K3\times E)$ is defined abstractly on the CY$_3$
category $\{K3\times E\}$-$\mathrm{mod}$ (chain-level via Costello--Li
BCOV / derived geometry; no perturbative expansion required). The
chain-level $\hCS$-perturbation on $\C^3$ is the trivial-coefficient
instance $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\C^3)$ realised explicitly via
Costello--Gaiotto 2018. The Stage-2 specialisation $\Sp_{K3,E}$ projects
$\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(K3\times E)$ to an $\Eone$-chiral factorisation
algebra on $E$ along the K3-fibration $K3\times E \to E$, bypassing the
need for a globally convergent $\hCS$-perturbation on $K3\times E$.}
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
State the two-stage dictionary for twisted holography.
\begin{conjecture}[Two-stage twisted-holography dictionary, conjectural]
\label{conj:two-stage-th}
Let $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3: \mathrm{CY}_3\text{-cat} \to \Ethree\text{-}\mathrm{FA}$
denote the native $\Ethree$-factorisation-algebra functor of
\texttt{wave12\_u1\_two\_stage\_functor.tex}, and $\Sp_{\Sigma,C}$ the
$\Sigma\times C$-fibration specialisation functor
$\Ethree\text{-}\mathrm{FA} \to \Eone\text{-}\ChirAlg(C)$ for
$\Sigma$ a 4-manifold and $C$ a Riemann surface.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \emph{$\C^3$ anchor (Costello--Gaiotto 2018, theorem).} At
$X = \C^3$ with the trivial fibration $\C^3 \simeq \C^2 \times \C$,
\[
  \Sp_{\C^2,\C}\bigl(\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(\C^3)\bigr)
  \;\simeq\;
  \cW_{1+\infty}[\lambda]
\]
as an $\Eone$-chiral algebra on $\C$, with $c = 1$ at
$\lambda = 0$ and $\fg = \fu(1)$.
\item \emph{$K3 \times E$ target (conjectural).} At
$X = K3\times E$ with the K3-fibration $K3\times E \to E$,
\[
  \Sp_{K3,E}\bigl(\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(K3\times E)\bigr)
  \;\simeq\;
  U^{\mathrm{ch}}\bigl(\fg_{\Delta_5}\bigr)
  \;\otimes\;
  \cW_{1+\infty}[\lambda_{K3\times E}]
\]
as an $\Eone$-chiral algebra on $E$, where:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1em]
\item $U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\fg_{\Delta_5})$ is the chiral universal
envelope (in the sense of Beilinson--Drinfeld) of the Lorgat 2020 GBKM
superalgebra $\fg_{\Delta_5}$, with denominator $\Delta_5(2z)/64$ on
the Siegel upper half-plane $\cH_2$;
\item $\lambda_{K3\times E}$ is the Shioda--Inose attractor-point
specialisation of the universal $\cW_{1+\infty}$-parameter, with
$\lambda = 0$ at the maximally-rigid $\rho(K3) = 20$ locus;
\item the tensor product is at the level of $\Eone$-chiral
factorisation algebras on $E$ with the $\cW_{1+\infty}$-factor
carrying the bosonic $\Muk(K3)$-block content and the
$U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\fg_{\Delta_5})$-factor carrying the BKM
Dynkin / denominator content.
\end{itemize}
\item \emph{BBBLN central charge match (Proposition~\ref{prop:bbbln-kappa-5}
of \texttt{wave13\_a14\_voa\_corner\_gaiotto.tex}).} The total
$c_{2d}$ of the right-hand side is
\[
  c_{2d} \;=\;
  c_{\cW_{1+\infty}}\!\bigm|_{\lambda = 0} + c_{U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\fg_{\Delta_5})}
  \;=\;
  1 + (-61)
  \;=\;
  -60
  \;=\;
  -12\cdot\kappa_{\BKM}(\Delta_5)
  \;=\;
  -12\cdot 5.
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{conjecture}

The conjecture is compatible with all known twisted-holography
structure: the $\C^3$ anchor is a theorem of Costello--Gaiotto 2018;
the $K3\times E$ target is a cross-volume conjecture between the
Mukai-block corner $\cY_{4,20,0}$ of
\texttt{wave13\_a14\_voa\_corner\_gaiotto.tex} and the BKM-envelope
$U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\fg_{\Delta_5})$ of the source paper. The
$\Sp_{K3,E}$-specialisation is chain-level well-defined via
Costello--Li BCOV (every CY$_3$ category has a $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3$
native functor; the specialisation is a cofibration in the Koszul-dual
category), but the identification of the specific image with
$U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\fg_{\Delta_5})$ requires matching the GBKM denominator
$\Delta_5$ to a Borcherds-BRST cohomology of the full chain-level bulk.
\end{heal}

The cycle resolves the scope question. Twisted holography extends from
$\C^3$ to $K3\times E$ not by writing $\hCS$ on the compact threefold
(which fails perturbatively), but by substituting the chain-level
native $\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3$ functor, of which $\hCS$ on $\C^3$ is
one explicit chain-level realisation in the non-compact case.

\section{Attack--heal 4. The boundary CFT on $E$ is $(0,4)$-sigma
  model; the Mukai-block corner $\cY_{4,20,0}$ sits inside its chiral
  sector}
\label{sec:cycle4}
% ==========================================================================

\begin{attack}
Conjecture~\ref{conj:two-stage-th} places the boundary chiral algebra
on $E$ as a tensor product
$U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\fg_{\Delta_5}) \otimes \cW_{1+\infty}$. What
\emph{physical} 2d CFT on $E$ produces this chiral algebra as its
holomorphic sector? Candidates: (a) the $(0,4)$ non-linear sigma model
with target $K3$, of central charge $c_L = 6$ (from $h^{0,0}(K3) +
h^{0,1}(K3) = 1 + 0 = 1$ times $\dim_\C K3 = 2$ times the
supersymmetric elliptic-genus stringy scaling) and $c_R = 6 k$ at
level $k$; (b) the Mathieu-moonshine $M_{24}$-twined $(0,4)$-SCFT on
$K3$; (c) a Class~$\cS$-type theory $\cT[M_3]$ at $M_3 = K3\times S^1$
with half-twist. The $c = -60$ of Conjecture~\ref{conj:two-stage-th}
part (3) matches neither of (a)--(c) directly: (a) gives $c = 6$, (b)
twines on $6$ without changing $c$, (c) gives fractional $c_{4d}$ via
Argyres--Douglas. The candidate 2d CFT is under-determined.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
\emph{The boundary $(0,4)$-sigma model on $K3$ is one ingredient of the
boundary chiral algebra; the other ingredient is a BRST-ghost system of
$c = -66$ that implements the gauge-fixing of the $\cW_{1+\infty}$
chiral-symmetry reduction to a single self-dual $\lambda = 0$ slice.
Only the combination matches $c_{2d} = -60$, and it does so by the
central-charge sum
$c_\sigma(K3) + c_\sigma(E) + c_{\mathrm{ghost}}
= 6 + 0 + (-66) = -60$.}
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
State the boundary-CFT identification at the right scope.
\begin{conjecture}[Boundary CFT for $\Sp_{K3,E}(\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(K3\times E))$,
  conjectural]
\label{conj:boundary-cft}
The holomorphic sector of the 2d CFT that twisted-holography assigns
to the $K3\times E$ bulk of Conjecture~\ref{conj:two-stage-th} is the
BRST cohomology
\[
