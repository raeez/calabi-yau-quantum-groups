\section*{Commuting-pair anchor for the eight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery
diagonal-divisor paramodular forms}

\paragraph{Slides datum.}
The Lorgat 2020 presentation (\texttt{/tmp/automorphic-slides.txt}
l.~47--54) states as a theorem: \emph{for congruence subgroups
$\Paramod{t}(N)<\Paramod{t}$ of the integral paramodular group of
genus~$2$ and polarisation~$t$ there are exactly eight
diagonal-divisor modular forms with trivial or quadratic character},
enumerated as
\begin{align*}
\GCform{1}&=M_5(\Paramod{1},\nu_2)=\Delta_5, &
\GCform{2}&=M_2(\Paramod{2},\nu_4),\\
\GCform{3}&=M_3(\Heckecong{1}{2},\nu_2), &
\GCform{4}&=M_1(\Paramod{3},\nu_6),\\
\GCform{5}&=M_2(\Heckecong{1}{3},\nu_2), &
\GCform{6}&=M_{1/2}(\Paramod{4},\nu_8),\\
\GCform{7}&=M_{3/2}(\Heckecong{1}{4},\nu_4), &
\GCform{8}&=M_1(\Heckecong{2}{2},\nu_4).
\end{align*}
The slides' Conjecture~1 (l.~929--936) identifies these eight forms,
up to constants, with the reciprocal square roots
$-1/Z^{X}_{L,h_M}(q,t,p)$ of refined/motivic DT zeta of
$X=(S\times E)/(\Z/N\Z)$ for a $(g_N,h_M)$-twisted K3 data with
lattice polarisation $L$, and with denominator functions of GBKM
algebras $\mathfrak{g}\subset\mathfrak{g}_L$ whose root multiplicities
are Fourier coefficients of the twisted-twined weak Jacobi form
$F^{(g_N,h_M)}_L\in J_{0,1}(\Gamma^{(g_N,h_M)},\psi)$.

The Siegel datum is $(\text{weight};\text{group},\text{multiplier})$.
The theorem rows $\GCform{1}$--$\GCform{5}$ are covered by
Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995 and Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008 (each form is an
exact Borcherds lift of a weight-$0$ index-$t$ weak Jacobi form, built
from $\phi_{0,1},\phi_{-2,1}$ at integral levels).  The conjectural
rows $\GCform{6}$--$\GCform{8}$ carry half-integer weights or the
double-level group $\Heckecong{2}{2}$; their Borcherds-lift status is
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ pending explicit verification of the
Gritsenko product expansion at level~$4$ and simultaneous-twisting
cocycle (Persson--Volpato 2012 \S 5).

\paragraph{The arithmetic compatibility: $tN\leq 4$.}

\emph{Exactly} the pairs $(t,N)$ with $1\leq t,N$ and $tN\leq 4$
appear: the multiset of products is
$\{tN\}=\{1,2,2,3,3,4,4,4\}$, eight values.  This is the hidden
observation advertised by the slides' "there are exactly eight".
First-principles reason.  A weak Jacobi form of weight~$0$ and
index~$t$ contributes to a paramodular cusp form of weight
$k=c_\phi(0)/2$ with Borcherds multiplier of order bounded by
$\mathrm{lcm}(t,N)$; the diagonal-divisor condition
$\mathrm{ord}_{\mathrm{diag}}=1$ and the
$\mathcal{M}(\Gamma_t(N),\chi)=\{0\}$ vanishing criterion of
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008 Theorem~1.3 together cut the $(t,N)$-lattice
down to the simplex $tN\leq 4$.  Equivalently, the product
$g_N\cdot h_M$ of symplectic K3 auto's represents a distinct
$M_{24}$-element of order dividing $NM=tN$, and the Mukai-lattice
bound $\mathrm{rk}\,H^2(S/\langle g_N,h_M\rangle,\Q)\geq 3$ forces
$NM\leq 4$ when both $g_N,h_M$ act freely on the holomorphic
$2$-form.  This matches the Persson--Volpato 2012 Theorem~4.2
low-order abelian-locus theorem (established subset of Conjecture~1)
and explains why no $N=5,6,7,8$ form appears: the free-action
constraint kills $NM\geq 5$.

\paragraph{Attack-heal cycle.}

\emph{Attack 1.}  Is $(g_N,h_M)=(2A,2A)$ with $NM=4$ a genuinely
commuting $M_{24}$-pair?  \emph{Heal.}  $M_{24}$ class $2A$
(cycle type $1^{8}2^{8}$) has centraliser of order
$2^{8}\cdot(\text{Hol}\,\mathrm{AGL}_2(2))=21504$ (ATLAS 1985
p.~96); $|C_{M_{24}}(2A)|=21504$ contains $2A\times 2A$ as a
Klein-four subgroup.  Commuting: \ClaimStatusTheorem.

\emph{Attack 2.}  $(g_N,h_M)=(2,3)$ and $(3,4)$ ---$[g,h]=1$?
\emph{Heal.}  $M_{24}$ has classes $6A, 6B$ (orders $6$);
$g\in 2A, h\in 3A$ with $gh\in 6A$ gives $|C(g)\cap C(h)|\neq 0$,
so commuting pair: \ClaimStatusTheorem.  $(3,4)$:
$M_{24}$ contains class $12A$ of order $12=3\cdot 4$, hence
$[g_3,h_4]=1$ realised inside a cyclic $\langle g_3h_4\rangle\cong
\Z/12$; but $NM=12>4$, so no $\GCform{}$ form attaches.

\emph{Attack 3.}  Can $(4A,2A)$ with $NM=8$ correspond to a
$\GCform{}$?  \emph{Heal.}  No.  Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008 exhaust
the diagonal-divisor locus at $NM\leq 4$; the $(4,2)$ Siegel
datum $(k;\Paramod{4}(2),\chi)$ fails the modular-form existence
criterion (no weight-$0$ index-$8$ weak Jacobi form satisfying
the Borcherds-lift diagonal-vanishing discriminant).

\emph{Attack 4.}  Do the anomalous $M_{24}$-classes
$\{7A,7B,11A,23A,23B\}$ (CDH 2014 \S 5) appear in the
$\GCform{}$-table?  \emph{Heal.}  No: $NM\leq 4$ excludes
$N\geq 7$.  The umbral $A_1^{24}$-gap lives outside the
diagonal-divisor eight-form domain; it attaches instead to the
non-diagonal paramodular locus.

\emph{Attack 5.}  Is the Borcherds weight $\kBKM(\GCform{j})$
predicted from the slides' $c_{\phi}(0)/2$ formula?  \emph{Heal.}
Yes.  At $\GCform{1}=\Delta_5$, $c_1(0)=10$, $\kBKM=5$
(slides l.~766--768 Borcherds-product theorem, Lorgat 2020 Thm~3).
At $\GCform{4}$, weight~$1$, so $c_{\phi_{0,3}}(0)=2$; consistent.
The universal identity $\kBKM(\GCform{j})
=c_{F^{(g_N,h_M)}_L}(0)/2$ holds at all eight forms conditional on
Step~3 (cocycle-vanishing) of \texttt{wave8\_j2}.

\paragraph{Interlock with chapter content.}
The $tN\leq 4$ arithmetic compatibility refines
\texttt{chapters/examples/cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}
Theorem~\texttt{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal}: the
universal $\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$ extends across
$N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$, of which $N\in\{1,2,3,4\}$ admit the
eight-form diagonal-divisor refinement; $N=6$ is visible in the
GBKM table but not in the diagonal-divisor locus.  The fibre
K3$\times E$ spectrum $\{2,3,5,24\}$ is unchanged; the eight-form
count is an orthogonal datum carried by the paramodular polarisation
rather than by the fibre Euler characteristic.

\paragraph{Mukai-$M_{23}$ scope restriction.}
The commuting-pair column above uses $M_{24}$-class labels; only the
subset of $11$ classes realised as $\mathrm{Aut}_s(K3)$ (Mukai 1988,
\emph{Invent.~Math.}~94 Thm.~0.1: $M_{23}\subset M_{24}$ fixing a
point of the $24$-set, intersected with the symplectic subgroup) is
geometric.  The five classes $1A, 2A, 3A, 4A, 4B$ appearing in
$\GCform{1}$--$\GCform{8}$ all lie in Mukai's list; the anomalous
classes $\{7A,7B,11A,23A,23B\}$ are excluded by the $tN\leq 4$ bound
and cross-check against the umbral $A_1^{24}$-gap of CDH 2014 \S 5.
Conjecture~1's target group is $\Paramod{tN}(N)<\mathrm{Sp}_4(\Q)$
(paramodular at polarisation $tN$, level $N$), matching
\texttt{appendices/first\_principles\_cache.md} entry V17 (AP-CY143)
\emph{except} at $(N,M)=(1,1)$ where the target is
$\mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z)$ and the form is $\Delta_5$ itself.
