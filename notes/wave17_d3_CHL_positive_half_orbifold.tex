\section*{CHL orbifold action on the positive half of $K3 \times E$}

\paragraph{The datum.}
Fix $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$ and a symplectic automorphism
$g_N \in \Auts(K3)$ of order $N$ (Nikulin, \emph{Finite automorphism
groups of K\"ahler K3 surfaces}, 1980). Fix an $N$-torsion
translation $t_N \in E[N]$. The CHL datum is the pair
$(g_N, t_N)$ acting diagonally on $K3 \times E$; the quotient
\[
  X_N \;=\; (K3 \times E)\big/\langle (g_N, t_N)\rangle
\]
is the CHL Calabi--Yau threefold (Chaudhuri--Hockney--Lykken,
\emph{Maximally Supersymmetric String Theories in $D < 10$}, 1995,
arXiv:hep-th/9506144). Symplecticity of $g_N$ preserves the K3
holomorphic $2$-form; translation preserves the elliptic
$1$-form; the product $3$-form on $K3 \times E$ descends to $X_N$,
so $X_N$ is Calabi--Yau of dimension $3$.

\paragraph{Cycle 1: the symplectic locus in the K3 moduli.}
Nikulin's theorem restricts $\Auts(K3)$ orders to divisors of elements
of $\{1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8\}$; the CHL slice picks
$N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$, the subset with
$\varphi(N) \mid 2$ (cyclotomic compatibility with paramodular
index $1$). The $g_N$-fixed sublattice
$\Lambda_{K3}^{g_N} \subset \Lambda_{K3} = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ is
computed by Nikulin (\emph{ibid.}, Table~1): of rank
$r_N \in \{22, 14, 10, 8, 6\}$ respectively (the invariant
dimensions of the induced representation on the $22$-dimensional
cohomology). The $g_N$-fixed K3 moduli sublocus is the
period domain
$\Omega(\Lambda_{K3}^{g_N}) \subset \Omega(\Lambda_{K3})$,
a discrete intersection of Humbert-type divisors of
codimension $22 - r_N$; genericity is a Baire-generic condition on
the K3 period.

\paragraph{Cycle 2: the $E[N]$-torsor on the elliptic factor.}
A point $t_N \in E[N]$ corresponds to the isogeny
$\pi_N \colon E \to E/\langle t_N\rangle$ of degree $N$.
The target $E/\langle t_N\rangle$ is a new elliptic curve, not
identical to $E$ but $N$-isogenous to it: in a uniformiser
$\tau$ of the upper half-plane, $E_\tau$ quotients to
$E_{\tau/N}$ (or $E_{N\tau}$ depending on the isogeny
direction). The locus
$\{t_N \in E[N] \mid t_N \text{ of exact order } N\}$ has
cardinality $N^2 \cdot \prod_{p \mid N}(1 - p^{-2})$, partitioned
into Galois orbits of length $\varphi(N)$ -- hence
$\varphi(N) \mid 2$ is exactly the condition for the CHL
quotient to be defined over $\bQ$ up to isomorphism.

\paragraph{Cycle 3: reduced equivariant DT partition function.}
The reduced Donaldson--Thomas partition function of the CHL
threefold, twined by $(g_N, t_N)$, is
\[
  Z^\red_\DT\!\big(X_N;\, g_N, t_N\big)
  \;=\; \frac{1}{\Phi_N(Z)},
\]
where $\Phi_N$ is the Gritsenko--Nikulin paramodular Borcherds
product of level $N$ and weight
\[
  k_N \;=\; \frac{c_N(0)}{2}
  \;\in\; \{5,\, 4,\, 3,\, 2,\, 2\}
  \quad\text{for } N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}
\]
(Gritsenko, \emph{Arithmetical lifting and its applications}, 1999,
Theorem~1.2; Gritsenko--Nikulin~1995 Part~II Theorem~2.1). The
values $c_N(0) = (10, 4, 2, 2, 2)$ are the constant Fourier
coefficients of the $g_N$-twined K3 elliptic genus
$\phi_{0,1}^{(g_N)}$ (Cheng--Duncan--Harvey,
\emph{Umbral Moonshine and the Niemeier Lattices}, 2014,
arXiv:1307.5793, Table~2). The value $k_6 = 2$ rather than $1$:
the na\"ive arithmetic $24/(N+1) - 2$ fails at $N = 6$ because the
$g_6$-fixed K3 sublattice has rank $6$, not $24/(N+1) =
24/7$; the correct weight is dictated by the
Borcherds weight theorem $k_N = c_N(0)/2$. The $N = 1$ case
is Oberdieck--Pandharipande (\emph{The reduced Gromov--Witten/
Donaldson--Thomas correspondence for $K3 \times E$}, 2018);
$N \geq 2$ is the Bryan--Oberdieck--Pandharipande--Yin orbifold
DT extension (2018), with independent verification through the
Chattopadhyaya--David twisted-index computation
(\emph{Dyon degeneracies from Mathieu moonshine symmetry}, 2019,
arXiv:1704.00434).

\paragraph{Cycle 4: the positive-half fixed-point locus.}
On the positive-half chiral algebra $Y^+(K3 \times E)$ -- the
Schiffmann--Vasserot cohomological Hall algebra of $K3 \times E$
(AP-CY: this is the positive half, not the full
$\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$; cache fact) -- the CHL generator acts by
\[
  \sigma_N \;=\; (g_N)_* \otimes (t_N)_* \colon\;
  Y^+(K3 \times E) \;\longrightarrow\; Y^+(K3 \times E),
\]
where $(g_N)_*$ is the induced automorphism on
$H^*_{T}(\mathrm{Hilb}(K3), \bQ)$ along the Maulik--Okounkov
$R$-matrix data, and $(t_N)_*$ is the translation action on the
elliptic stable envelope. The CHL positive half is the
fixed-point subalgebra
\[
  Y^+(X_N) \;=\; \big(Y^+(K3 \times E)\big)^{\sigma_N},
\]
graded by the invariant sublattice
$\Lambda_{K3}^{g_N} \oplus H^*(E/\langle t_N\rangle)$.

\paragraph{Cycle 5: consistency with the BKM denominator.}
The fixed-point subalgebra $Y^+(X_N)$ has Weyl--Kac--Borcherds
denominator identity whose weight is $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$,
independently of the $\kappa_\cat$ reading of $X_N$. On the
CHL threefold,
\[
  \kappa_\cat(X_N) \;=\; \chi(\mathcal{O}_{X_N}) \;=\; 0
  \qquad (\text{K\"unneth-multiplicative, } \chi(\mathcal{O}_{K3}) \chi(\mathcal{O}_E) = 2 \cdot 0 = 0),
\]
preserved by the free $\bZ/N$-action (free because $t_N$ acts
freely on $E$; $g_N$ has isolated fixed points on $K3$ but the
combined diagonal is free). The arithmetic genus drops from
$\chi(\mathcal{O}_{K3 \times E}) = 0$ to
$\chi(\mathcal{O}_{X_N}) = 0$, an equality of zeros; the
$\kBKM$-information is in the non-trivial weight
$k_N \in \{5,4,3,2,2\}$, not in $\kappa_\cat$. Oberdieck
(\emph{Holomorphic anomaly equations for the Hilbert scheme of
points of a K3 surface}, 2018, arXiv:1812.00555) establishes the
refined DT computation along the CHL fibre structure; the
specialisation $\Sp_\ch$ of Theorem~CY-A$_3$ evaluates
$\PhiFA_3(D^b(\Coh(X_N)))$ along the K3-fibre of $X_N \to E/t_N$
to produce the BKM superalgebra
$\fg_{\Phi_N}$ whose Weyl--Kac--Borcherds
character is $1/\Phi_N(Z)$, in agreement with the reduced DT
partition function above.

\paragraph{Scope.}
At $N = 1$ the constructions specialise to the Oberdieck--Pandharipande
theorem. At $N \in \{2, 3, 4\}$ the BKM identification is conditional
on the Bryan--Oberdieck orbifold DT programme; at $N = 6$ the
Humbert-discriminant obstruction $n_6 \neq 36$ signals a genuine
refinement not captured by the $(N, M) = (6, 6)$ diagonal CHL
quotient alone. The positive-half fixed-point reading
$Y^+(X_N) = Y^+(K3 \times E)^{\sigma_N}$ is an
$(\infty,1)$-categorical statement about the
Schiffmann--Vasserot cohomological Hall algebra, separate from
the chain-level Borcherds-product computation of $\Phi_N$:
both are theorems in their own lane, neither a shadow of the
other.
