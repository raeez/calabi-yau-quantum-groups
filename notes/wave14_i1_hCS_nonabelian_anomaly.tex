\section{Nonabelian Holomorphic Chern--Simons Anomaly in Complex Dimension Three}
\label{sec:hcs-nonabelian-anomaly}

\subsection{The BV Complex}
\label{subsec:hcs-bv-complex}

Let \(X\) be a Calabi--Yau threefold with holomorphic volume form
\(\Omega_X\), and let \(\fg\) be a finite-dimensional metric complex Lie
algebra with invariant pairing \(\langle-,-\rangle\). For
\(U\subset X\),
\[
  \mathfrak L_U=\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(U,\fg)[1],
  \qquad
  \ell_1=\bar\partial,
  \qquad
  \ell_2(\alpha,\beta)=[\alpha\wedge\beta]_\fg .
\]
The classical local observables are continuous Chevalley--Eilenberg
cochains
\[
  \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS}(U)
  =
  C^\bullet_{\CE,\mathrm{cont}}(\mathfrak L_U)
  =
  \widehat{\Sym}\bigl(\mathfrak L_U^\vee[-1]\bigr),
  \qquad
  d_{\Obs}^{\mathrm{cl}}=d_{\bar\partial}+d_{\ell_2}.
\]
The holomorphic Chern--Simons interaction is
\[
  I_{\hCS}(\cA)
  =
  \int_U \Omega_X\wedge
  \left(
    \frac12\langle\cA,\bar\partial\cA\rangle
    +\frac16\langle\cA,[\cA,\cA]\rangle
  \right).
\]
A Costello renormalisation datum \((I[L],\Delta_L,W_{\Gamma,L})\)
defines quantum observables
\[
  \Obs^q_{\hCS,L}(U)
  =
  \left(
  \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS}(U)[[\hbar]],
  d_{\Obs}^{\mathrm{cl}}+\{I[L],-\}_{L}+\hbar\Delta_L
  \right)
\]
when the renormalised interaction satisfies the quantum master equation
\[
  QI[L]+\frac12\{I[L],I[L]\}_{L}+\hbar\Delta_L I[L]=0 .
\]
The first obstruction lies in degree \(1\) in the Costello complex of
local BV functionals.

\subsection{The Quartic Descent Class}
\label{subsec:hcs-quartic-descent-class}

The universal adjoint colour tensor is the symmetrised quartic trace
\[
  \theta_{\fg}(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)
  =
  \frac{1}{4!}\sum_{\sigma\in S_4}
  \Tr_{\fg}\!\left(
    \operatorname{ad}_{x_{\sigma(1)}}
    \operatorname{ad}_{x_{\sigma(2)}}
    \operatorname{ad}_{x_{\sigma(3)}}
    \operatorname{ad}_{x_{\sigma(4)}}
  \right),
  \qquad
  \theta_{\fg}\in(\Sym^4\fg^\vee)^\fg .
\]
For a representation \(\rho:\fg\to\End(V)\), the corresponding tensor is
\[
  I_4^\rho(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)
  =
  \frac{1}{4!}\sum_{\sigma\in S_4}
  \Tr_V\!\left(
    \rho(x_{\sigma(1)})\rho(x_{\sigma(2)})
    \rho(x_{\sigma(3)})\rho(x_{\sigma(4)})
  \right),
  \qquad
  I_4^\rho\in(\Sym^4\fg^\vee)^\fg .
\]
Write \(P_4\) for either \(\theta_{\fg}\), \(I_4^\rho\), or the signed
sum of such tensors for a coupled field system. The holomorphic descent
representative on \(U\) is
\[
  \Theta^{P_4}_U(\alpha_0,\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3)
  =
  \int_U
  \Omega_X\wedge
  P_4\bigl(\alpha_0,\partial\alpha_1,
  \partial\alpha_2,\partial\alpha_3\bigr),
  \qquad
  \alpha_i\in\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(U,\fg),
\]
with the Koszul signs fixed by the CE convention. The three
holomorphic derivatives supply the \((3,0)\)-density required in
complex dimension \(3\). The one-loop gauge obstruction is the class
\[
  \alpha_{\mathrm{QME}}(X,\fg,P_4)
  =
  [\,\hbar\,\Theta^{P_4}\,]
  \in
  H^1\!\left(
    \mathrm{Loc}_{X}(\Omega^{0,\bullet}(-,\fg)[1]),
    d_{\mathrm{BV}}
  \right).
\]
The invariant of degree \(3\) belongs to the complex-dimension-two
descent slot. Complex dimension \(3\) uses \(P_4\).

\subsection{Cancellation and Counterterms}
\label{subsec:hcs-cancellation-counterterms}

The local QME closes at one loop precisely when
\[
  \alpha_{\mathrm{QME}}(X,\fg,P_4)=0 .
\]
Two concrete mechanisms give this equality.
\[
  P_4=0
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \Theta^{P_4}=0,
\]
and
\[
  \exists\,S_{\mathrm{ct}}^{(0)}\in
  \mathrm{Loc}_{X}(\Omega^{0,\bullet}(-,\fg)[1])^0
  \quad
  d_{\mathrm{BV}}S_{\mathrm{ct}}^{(0)}
  =
  -\,\hbar\,\Theta^{P_4}.
\]
The second equation must hold in the same local functional complex,
with the same regularisation, trace normalisation, and descent
representative. A Green--Schwarz type construction is an enlarged BV
theory: it adds a compensating field and replaces the displayed
equation by a coupled QME. Pure nonabelian \(\hCS\) receives no compact
quantisation theorem from a scalar topological number alone.

\subsection{Two-Point Renormalisation}
\label{subsec:hcs-two-point-renormalisation}

The logarithmic two-point graph has colour coefficient \(C_2^\rho(\fg)\)
and contributes the degree-zero local functional
\[
  S_{\mathrm{ct}}^{(2)}
  =
  -\,\hbar\,\frac{C_2^\rho(\fg)}{(4\pi)^3}
  \log(L/\varepsilon)
  \int_X\Omega_X\wedge\Tr_\rho(\cA\bar\partial\cA).
\]
It rescales the kinetic pairing and the field \(\cA\). The QME
obstruction above lives in degree \(1\) and is represented by the
quartic descent class.

\subsection{Flat and Compact Threefolds}
\label{subsec:hcs-flat-compact-threefolds}

On \(X=\CC^3\), translation invariance and the Stein contraction give
the local perturbative model. The possible gauge obstruction is still
\(\alpha_{\mathrm{QME}}(\CC^3,\fg,P_4)\), represented by the same
quartic descent cocycle. A flat-space vanishing statement therefore
requires one of the cancellation mechanisms in
Subsection~\ref{subsec:hcs-cancellation-counterterms}; the two-point
counterterm supplies field-strength renormalisation alone.

On a compact Calabi--Yau threefold, a global quantum \(\hCS\) theory
requires the following data:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the local QME for the chosen \(P_4\);
\item a vanishing proof for \(\alpha_{\mathrm{QME}}\) or compatible
local primitives \(S_{\mathrm{ct}}^{(0)}\);
\item a Calabi--Yau orientation and framing datum for the BV pairing;
\item determinant-line and BCOV or TCFT background data;
\item descent and sewing for the resulting factorisation algebra;
\item analytic OPE completion and filtration compatible with RG flow.
\end{enumerate}
These hypotheses are independent. The vanishing of an Euler coefficient
addresses only the BCOV scalar part of this list.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{\(K3\times E\)}{K3 x E}}
\label{subsec:hcs-k3e-separation}

For \(X=K3\times E\),
\[
  \chi_{\mathrm{top}}(K3\times E)
  =
  \chi_{\mathrm{top}}(K3)\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(E)
  =
  24\cdot0=0.
\]
Thus the Euler-characteristic contribution to the compact BCOV
coefficient vanishes. The representation-dependent class
\(\alpha_{\mathrm{QME}}(K3\times E,\fg,P_4)\) remains a separate
quartic descent problem until \(P_4\) is specified and trivialised.

The numerical invariants attached to \(K3\times E\) occupy distinct
construction layers:
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)=\chi(\cO_{K3\times E})=0,
  \qquad
  \kch(K3\times E)=\sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(K3\times E)=0,
\]
\[
  \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)=3,
  \qquad
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)=5,
  \qquad
  \kfib(K3)=24.
\]
The first two equalities follow from
\[
  \chi(\cO_{K3\times E})
  =
  \chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)
  =
  2\cdot0=0,
  \qquad
  \sum_q(-1)^qh^{0,q}(K3\times E)=(1-1)(1+1)=0.
\]
The Heisenberg, Borcherds, and fibre numbers come from other
constructions and do not cancel or create the local BV class.

\subsection{Boundary and Positive-Half Output}
\label{subsec:hcs-boundary-positive-half}

Let \(C\subset X\) be a holomorphic curve and assume the compact or
local quantum \(\hCS\) factorisation algebra has been constructed. The
curve specialisation is
\[
  \mathcal A_C=\int_{N_C/C}\Obs^q_{\hCS}
  \in
  \operatorname{Alg}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Eone}(C)
\]
in complex dimension \(3\). The higher braiding belongs to centre,
double, module, or enhancement layers, for example
\[
  Z\bigl(\Rep^{\Eone}(\mathcal A_C)\bigr)
  \simeq
  \Rep^{\Etwo}\bigl(
    C^\bullet_{\CE,\mathrm{ch}}(\mathcal A_C,\mathcal A_C)
  \bigr)
\]
when the chiral BZFN hypotheses hold.

For the affine local model,
\[
  \operatorname{CoHA}(\CC^3)=Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1).
\]
The full Yangian, the vacuum representation of
\(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}\), and their evaluation parameters enter after
Drinfeld double, centre, completion, and representation-theoretic
evaluation.

\subsection{Conclusions}
\label{subsec:hcs-nonabelian-anomaly-conclusions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Classical hCS observables are CE cochains of
\(\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(X,\fg)[1]\).
\item Quantum hCS observables require a Costello RG datum satisfying
the QME.
\item In complex dimension \(3\), the local gauge obstruction is
controlled by
\[
  \theta_{\fg}\in(\Sym^4\fg^\vee)^\fg
  \quad\hbox{or}\quad
  I_4^\rho\in(\Sym^4\fg^\vee)^\fg .
\]
\item The coefficient \(C_2^\rho(\fg)\) controls the logarithmic
two-point counterterm.
\item Compact nonabelian closure requires anomaly trivialisation,
orientation, framing, determinant-line or BCOV/TCFT data, sewing,
completion, and RG compatibility.
\item For \(d=3\), the specialised chiral output is \(\Eone\). The
\(\Etwo\)-structure belongs to the centre or double under the stated
hypotheses.
\end{enumerate}
