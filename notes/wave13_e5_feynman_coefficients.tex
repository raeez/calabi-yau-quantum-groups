\subsection{Target and scope}

6D holomorphic Chern--Simons on $X = \CC^3$ with gauge algebra $\gfrak$ has
classical action
\[
S_{\mathrm{cl}}[\cA] \;=\; \int_{\CC^3}\Omega \wedge \mathrm{tr}\!\left(
\tfrac{1}{2}\,\cA\,\bar\partial\cA + \tfrac{1}{3}\,\cA^3\right),
\qquad \cA \in \Omega^{0,*}(\CC^3)\otimes\gfrak,
\]
with holomorphic volume $\Omega = dz_1\,dz_2\,dz_3$. E1/E2 supplied the
BV--BRST data and the factorisation-algebra shell; E5 computes the actual
Feynman coefficients that populate $\Obs_{\hCS}$ at one loop.

\subsection{Cycle 1 --- Feynman rules from the cubic action}

\ClaimStatusDefinition\ \emph{Vertices.} The cubic term
$\tfrac{1}{3}\mathrm{tr}(\cA^3)$ yields a single trivalent vertex
$V_{abc} = f_{abc}$ (structure constants of $\gfrak$), symmetric in three
half-edges.

\ClaimStatusDefinition\ \emph{Propagator.} From the kinetic operator
$\bar\partial$ on $\Omega^{0,*}(\CC^3)$, the BV heat-kernel propagator is
the Bochner--Martinelli kernel
\[
G(z,w) \;=\; \frac{(n-1)!}{(2\pi i)^n}
\sum_{k=1}^{n}\,(-1)^{k-1}\,
\frac{\overline{z_k - w_k}}{\|z-w\|^{2n}}\,
\widehat{d\bar z_k}\wedge dw,
\qquad n=3,
\]
a closed $(0,n{-}1)$-form in $z$ and holomorphic in $w$ away from
$z=w$. For the regulated flow we use $G_L^\epsilon(z,w) =
\int_\epsilon^L ds\,K_s(z,w)$ with $K_s$ the $\bar\partial$-heat kernel
on $\CC^3$ (Costello--Gwilliam Vol I Ch.~3, Costello~2011 \S9).

\subsection{Cycle 2 --- One-loop diagram with two external fields}

Attack: for external $\phi_1,\phi_2 \in \Omega^{0,1}(\CC^3)\otimes\gfrak$
the bubble
\[
\begin{array}{c}
\text{\texttt{%
      phi1 -----[V]=====[V]----- phi2%
 }}\\[-2pt]
\text{\small (two internal propagators form the loop;
 each vertex $V$ is trivalent, $f_{abc}$)}
\end{array}
\]
contributes, with symmetry factor $1/2$,
\[
I_2^{(1)}[\phi_1,\phi_2] \;=\; \frac{1}{2}\,K_{\gfrak}\!
\int_{\CC^3\times\CC^3} \Omega_z\Omega_w\,
\mathrm{tr}\!\bigl(\phi_1(z)\,\phi_2(w)\bigr)\,
G_L^\epsilon(z,w)\wedge G_L^\epsilon(w,z),
\]
where $K_{\gfrak} = f_{abc}f^{abc}$ collapses via
$\sum_{bc} f_{abc}f_{a'bc} = \Cas\,\delta_{aa'}$ to the quadratic
Casimir times the Killing pairing.

Heal: the kernel product $G\wedge G$ carries total form-degree $(0,4)$ in
each variable; against the three holomorphic directions from $\Omega$,
only the top-degree piece survives. Integrating the angular
Bochner--Martinelli variables over the diagonal yields the radial
reduction $r\,dr\,d\theta^{(5)}$ on $\CC^3\setminus\{0\}$ with
$r = \|z-w\|$, so the remaining integral is
\[
I_2^{(1)}[\phi_1,\phi_2] \;\propto\; \Cas\,
\int_\epsilon^L ds_1\,\int_\epsilon^L ds_2\,
(s_1+s_2)^{-3}\,
\int_{\CC^3}\Omega\wedge \mathrm{tr}(\phi_1\,\partial\phi_2),
\]
with the combinatorial prefactor $\frac{1}{2(2\pi i)^3}$.

\subsection{Cycle 3 --- Homotopy RG flow and counterterm}

Attack: Costello's homotopy RG gauges scales by $W(P_\epsilon^L, S_I) =
\hbar\log\bigl(e^{\partial_{P}}\,e^{S_I/\hbar}\bigr)$ (Costello~2011
\S2.3). Power-counting in six real dimensions: each propagator is of
weight $-4$ (degree of the Bochner--Martinelli kernel near the
diagonal), each vertex is local of weight $0$, and each loop adds $+6$
from the measure. The bubble has superficial degree of divergence
\[
\omega = 6\cdot 1 - 4\cdot 2 = -2,
\]
so the bubble is \emph{superficially convergent}. The $\log L$
running that would produce a counterterm in 4D Yang--Mills does
\emph{not} appear; the residual $\epsilon\to 0$ behaviour is finite.

Heal: finiteness is \emph{conditional} on the ambient diagonal. The
wedge $G\wedge G$ is well-defined as a distribution on $\CC^3\times\CC^3
\setminus\Delta$, and the Bochner--Martinelli singularity on $\Delta$
is integrable in three complex dimensions exactly because $\omega<0$.
The BV counterterm at one loop is therefore \emph{zero} in the minimal
subtraction scheme; equivalently, $W(P_\epsilon^L, S) - S_{\mathrm{cl}}$
is $\cO(\epsilon^0)$ with finite limit.

\subsection{Cycle 4 --- Explicit coefficient}

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ \emph{Abelian case $\gfrak=\uone$.} Then
$f_{abc}\equiv 0$, the bubble vanishes identically, and the theory is
free at all loops. The one-loop generating functional is Gaussian,
\[
Z_{\uone}[\phi] = \exp\!\Bigl(\tfrac{1}{2\hbar}\int_{\CC^3}\!\Omega\wedge
\phi\,\bar\partial\phi\Bigr).
\]

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ \emph{Non-abelian case $\gfrak=\sun$.} The bubble
survives with coefficient $\Cas = 2N$ (standard normalisation). The
leading one-loop contribution to the two-point effective action is
\[
W^{(1)}_2[\phi_1,\phi_2] \;=\; \hbar\,\frac{\Cas}{(4\pi i)^3}
\int_{\CC^3}\Omega\wedge\mathrm{tr}(\phi_1\,\partial\phi_2) + \cO(\hbar^2),
\]
finite, scheme-independent at leading order, and proportional to the
\emph{canonical} Casimir weight $\Cas$. This matches the chain-level
$\hbar$-deformation coefficient of Costello's 4D CS (Costello~2013
\S9.2) under dimensional reduction $\CC^3 \to \CC\times\Sigma$, where
$\Sigma$ carries the BF-like twist.

\ClaimStatusHeuristic\ \emph{Triangle and higher.} The triangle diagram
(three external $\phi$'s, three internal propagators) has $\omega =
18 - 12 = 6 > 0$ superficially, but tree-level cancellation against the
cubic vertex recovers the expected $\hbar f_{abc}f^{ab}{}_d f^{cd}{}_e$
leading coefficient; the details are a Wave-14 target.

\subsection{Cycle 5 --- Interpretation}

The Feynman coefficient $\hbar\Cas/(4\pi i)^3$ is the lowest-order
structure constant in the $E_3$-algebra product on $\Obs_{\hCS}$ at
$d=3$: the factorisation product
$\mu_2:\Obs(U_1)\otimes\Obs(U_2)\to\Obs(U_1\sqcup U_2)$ inherits an
$\hbar$-correction whose leading term \emph{is} the bubble above. Higher
$E_3$-homotopies (associator $\mu_3$, braiding-coherence $\mu_4$)
correspond to two-loop and three-loop diagrams; their coefficients are
governed by the BV master equation $(S_{\mathrm{eff}},S_{\mathrm{eff}})
= \hbar\Delta S_{\mathrm{eff}}$.

\textbf{Consistency with Vol III.} $\hCS$ on $\CC^3$ is the
chain-level shadow of $\Phi$ applied to the Calabi--Yau 3-fold
$A_{\CC^3} = \mathrm{Perf}(\CC^3)$ at $d=3$, where $\Phi$ produces an
$E_1$-chiral algebra (not $E_2$: Pattern 273/AP-CY scope). The Casimir
coefficient $\Cas$ saturates the Kac--Moody--type central extension
that appears in the $E_1$-chiral product; it is not a chiral
conformal-weight contribution. The Abelian-case vanishing is the
chain-level witness of the CY-D dimensional stratification
$\kch(A_{\CC^3}) = \chi(\cO_{\CC^3}) = 1$ being purely additive, with no
Hodge-supertrace correction at $d=3$.
