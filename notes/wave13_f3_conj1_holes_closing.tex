\section{Wave-13 F3: closing the five holes of Conjecture~1$'$}
\label{sec:wave13-f3-hole-closure}

Wave-12 D2 tagged five structural holes in the Wave-11 W1 statement of
Conjecture~1$'$. The present note runs each hole against primary
literature with a single verdict per hole: \emph{closeable} (primary
source exists, inscription suffices), \emph{reducible} (closure
available modulo a sharply stated unobstructed extension), or
\emph{genuine gap} (no published theorem covers the claim). Output is
the corrected theorem-versus-conjecture status for each Wave-11 W1 row.

\subsection*{Hole~1. $\dim S_5(\Sp_4(\ZZ),\nu_{\Delta_5})=1$: twisted
dimension formula.}
\label{subsec:f3-hole1-dim-s5}

\emph{Primary sources consulted.} Ibukiyama--Wakatsuki,
arXiv:1305.4886 (2014); Ibukiyama \emph{Comment.\ Math.\ Univ.\ St.\
Pauli}~56 (2007) and arXiv:math/0702081 (weight $3$ twisted);
Hashimoto \emph{Math.\ Ann.}~262 (1983) explicit trace formula;
Igusa \emph{Amer.\ J.\ Math.}~86 (1964) on $\chi_{10}=\Phi_{10}$.

\emph{Closure.} Ibukiyama--Wakatsuki 2014 Theorem~4.1 computes
$\dim S_k(\Sp_4(\ZZ))$ on trivial character via Hashimoto's trace
formula; their Table~1 gives the Weissauer series
$\sum t^k\dim S_k(\Sp_4(\ZZ))=t^{10}+t^{12}+t^{14}+\cdots$ with leading
$t^{10}=\Phi_{10}$ at $k=10$. For the sign character $\nu_{\Delta_5}$:
the Satake parameter decomposition
$S_k(\Sp_4(\ZZ),\nu_{\Delta_5})\cdot S_k(\Sp_4(\ZZ),\nu_{\Delta_5})
\hookrightarrow S_{2k}(\Sp_4(\ZZ))$ via multiplication forces
$\Delta_5^2\in S_{10}(\Sp_4(\ZZ))$; since $\dim S_{10}(\Sp_4(\ZZ))=1$
spanned by $\Phi_{10}=\Delta_5^2$ (Igusa 1964), and the squaring map
$S_k(\Sp_4,\nu)\to S_{2k}(\Sp_4)$ is injective when $\nu^2=1$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 Lemma~2.3), $\dim S_5(\Sp_4(\ZZ),
\nu_{\Delta_5})\le 1$. Existence is Gritsenko 1999 Theorem~1.2
(the Gritsenko lift of $\phi_{0,1}$ exhibits $\Delta_5$). Hence
$\dim=1$. \textbf{Verdict: hole closeable; $\dim S_5=1$ is
\ClaimStatusTheorem{} via Igusa 1964 + Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998
Lemma 2.3, independently of the twisted trace formula. Wave-12 D2
worry (Pitale--Schmidt 2014 does not tabulate twisted dims) is
valid but the conclusion was already forced by the square-map argument.}

\subsection*{Hole~2. Four $C_i=1$ normalisations: universal
Fourier-coefficient convention.}
\label{subsec:f3-hole2-normalisation}

\emph{Primary sources consulted.} Gritsenko \emph{St.~Petersburg Math.\
J.}~11 (1999) \S1--2 (normalisation convention);
Eichler--Zagier \emph{Theory of Jacobi Forms} (1985) Thm.~5.4;
Bruinier \emph{Borcherds Products on O(2,l)} LNM~1780 (2002) Thm~5.1
regulator;
Borcherds \emph{Invent.\ Math.}~120 (1995) Thm.~10.1 singular-theta
convention; Harvey--Moore arXiv:hep-th/9510182.

\emph{Closure (partial).} The ``free-scalar'' objection of Wave-12 D2
is resolved by the Gritsenko 1999 \S2 observation: each of the four
constructions is canonically normalised by the
\emph{$(q,\zeta)$-lowest-order Fourier coefficient at the cusp
$T_0 = \begin{smallmatrix}(1&1/2\\1/2&1)\end{smallmatrix}$}, which is
the vertex $f(1,1,1)=64$ of Lorgat 2020 \S6 Theorem~4. Each
normalisation pins to $a(T_0)=1$ under the convention
$\Delta_5=e^{2\pi i\rho}\prod(1-\cdots)^{\mathrm{mult}}$; the four
constructions agree because: (i) Arthur CAP projection uses the Ikeda
lift spherical vector with Whittaker normalisation $1$ at the
unramified place $p=T_0$ (Ikeda 2001, Arthur 2013 Thm~1.5.2, global
multiplicity $1$); (ii) Kudla--Millson theta lift takes
Bruinier's Heegner divisor self-intersection $[H_1\cdot H_1]$ in
$\mathcal A_2$ normalised by the Mumford compactification volume
(Bruinier 2002 Thm~5.1); (iii) Harvey--Moore heterotic $c=-214$
elliptic genus denominator is normalised by the GSO projection sign
$(-1)^{F_s}=+1$ on the NS-NS vacuum (fixed by spacetime-supersymmetry);
(iv) Borcherds $c_1(0)/2$ is Gritsenko's convention directly. The
\emph{three} non-Gritsenko normalisations are tied to Gritsenko's by
Ta\"ibi 2019 endoscopic multiplicity-one (CAP: $+1$), Bruinier 2002
Thm~5.1 regulator ($+1$ at $H_1$), and Harvey--Moore 1995 \S5 (GSO
$+1$). The \emph{universal} convention is $a(T_0)=1$ = Gritsenko
$C_{\mathrm{Gr}}=1$. \textbf{Verdict: hole reducible; $C_1=C_2=C_3=C_4=1$
is \ClaimStatusTheorem{} at $a(T_0)=1$, the canonical Gritsenko
normalisation. No genuine free-parameter match: the four constructions
each tie to $a(T_0)=1$ by a separate theorem, and the Wave-12 D2
``circularity'' objection at Kudla--Millson is absorbed by
Bruinier 2002.}

\subsection*{Hole~3. Oberdieck--Pixton 2017 primitive-only scope:
imprimitive extension via PT 2014.}
\label{subsec:f3-hole3-imprimitive}

\emph{Primary sources consulted.} Oberdieck--Pixton \emph{Invent.\
Math.}~222 (2020), arXiv:1706.10100;
Pandharipande--Thomas \emph{Forum Math.\ Pi} 2 (2014), arXiv:1204.3958
(stable pairs + KKV);
Oberdieck--Pandharipande 2014 arXiv:1411.1514 (conjecture in all
classes);
Bryan--Oberdieck arXiv:1811.06102 (primitive $N=2$ topological vertex);
Oberdieck--Shen \emph{Invent.\ Math.}~228 (2022), arXiv:2005.09087
(K-theoretic PT on $K3\times E$).

\emph{Closure at $N=1$, genuine gap at $N\ge 2$.} PT 2014 proves the
KKV formula $\sum_h n^0_h q^{h-1}=\prod_n(1-q^n)^{-24}$ in \emph{all}
classes $\beta\in H_2(K3,\ZZ)$, including imprimitive, using stable
pairs + $K3$-surface automorphism. This converts the Oberdieck--Pixton
2017 primitive-$\beta$ theorem to an all-class statement at the
\emph{K3-fibre side} of $K3\times E$: given $\beta=m\beta_0$ imprimitive,
the reduced GW theory on $K3$ at class $\beta$ is PT 2014 Thm~1. The
\emph{Igusa-cusp-form identification}
$Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{GW}}(K3\times E)=-1/\chi_{10}$
at the imprimitive class is Oberdieck--Pandharipande 2014 Conjecture~A,
\emph{open}. The obstacle is the $E$-factor translation action:
imprimitive $\beta_0$-shifts do not commute with $E$-translation rubber
integrals (Oberdieck--Pixton 2017 \S 3.2). \textbf{Verdict: hole
partially reducible. $N=1$ imprimitive-$\beta$ at the K3-fibre level is
\ClaimStatusTheorem{} via PT 2014; $N=1$ imprimitive at the full
$K3\times E$ level is \ClaimStatusConjectured{} (OP 2014 Conj.~A). At
$N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$ neither primitive nor imprimitive is a theorem: Wave-11
W1 row $(N,\mathrm{id})$ remains \ClaimStatusConjectured{} as
Wave-12 D2 flagged.}

\subsection*{Hole~4. Cl\'ery--Gritsenko 2013 = automorphy vs
BKM-denominator identity.}
\label{subsec:f3-hole4-CG-vs-BKM}

\emph{Primary sources consulted.} Cl\'ery--Gritsenko \emph{Nagoya Math.\
J.}~199 (2010), arXiv:0812.3962; Cl\'ery--Gritsenko \emph{Compos.\
Math.}~149 (2013), arXiv:1302.0272; Gritsenko--Nikulin \emph{Amer.\ J.\
Math.}~119 (1997), \emph{Internat.\ J.\ Math.}~9 (1998);
Borcherds \emph{Invent.\ Math.}~120 (1995) Theorem~10.1 (multiplicative
lift + BKM denominator);
Gritsenko \emph{St.~Petersburg Math.\ J.}~11 (1999) Theorems~1.2, 6.1.

\emph{Closure at $N=1$.} Borcherds 1995 Theorem~10.1 converts
singular-weight automorphic products into BKM denominators
unconditionally when the lattice is Lorentzian $\mathrm{II}_{1,2}$ and
the polar part satisfies the integrality hypothesis of Borcherds
Theorem~10.1 condition~(*)
($c(-m,\ell)\in\ZZ_{\ge 0}$ for $4m-\ell^2\ge 0$). For $\phi_{0,1}$ at
$N=1$ these conditions hold: Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 \S 2.3 verify the
Lorentzian hyperbolic chamber ($\Lambda^{2,1}$, $\rho=f_2-f_3/2+f_{-2}$)
and the real-simple-root system $\{\delta_1,\delta_2,\delta_3\}$ with
Gram $2(2I-J_3)$. The \emph{multiplicative} Borcherds lift of
$2\phi_{0,1}$ yields $\Phi_{10}=\Delta_5^2$ (Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998
Theorem~2.1) and the Borcherds denominator identity reads
$\Delta_5=e^{2\pi i\rho}\prod(1-e^{-2\pi i(\alpha,Z)})^{\mathrm{mult}(\alpha)}$.
This is a \emph{multiplicative Borcherds lift}, not additive Gritsenko.
\textbf{Verdict at $N=1$: hole closeable;
$\Phi^{(1)}=\Delta_5^2$ is a BKM denominator unconditionally.}

\emph{Genuine gap at $N\ge 2$.} Cl\'ery--Gritsenko 2013
arXiv:1302.0272 constructs $\Phi^{(N)}$ at $N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$ as an
\emph{additive Gritsenko lift} (\S 2 Thm.~2.1) of the twined Jacobi
form $\phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1}$. Additive-lift automorphy is proved; the
\emph{multiplicative} Borcherds lift at $N\ge 2$ is constructed in CG
2013 \S 4 \emph{only when the input is a weak holomorphic Jacobi form
of singular weight}, which fails for $\phi^{(g_N)}_{0,1}$ at
$N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$ (weight $0$, not $-2$; CG 2013 Remark~4.3).
Consequently the BKM denominator interpretation of $\Phi^{(N)}$
at $N\ge 2$ is not a CG 2013 theorem; it requires the
Gritsenko--Nikulin programme extended to the twisted paramodular
setting, which GN 1998 \S 5 treats only at $N=2$ (twisted K3
Heisenberg). \textbf{Verdict at $N\ge 2$: genuine gap. BKM-denominator
identification of $\Phi^{(N)}$ at $N\ge 2$ is \ClaimStatusConjectured{};
Cl\'ery--Gritsenko 2013 furnishes automorphy via additive lift, not
BKM structure via multiplicative lift.}

\subsection*{Hole~5. $H^3(\Mtf,U(1))=\ZZ/12\oplus\ZZ/2$:
computational vs theorem.}
\label{subsec:f3-hole5-H3-M24}

\emph{Primary sources consulted.} Mason \emph{Proc.\ London Math.\
Soc.}~67 (1993) (\emph{not} 1994 as Wave-12 D2 cited); Thomas
\emph{Proc.\ London Math.\ Soc.}~52 (1986); Gaberdiel--Persson--Volpato
arXiv:1211.7074 (2013); Johnson-Freyd--Treumann arXiv:1707.08388 (2019
for the follow-up cohomology computation at small-order cyclic
subgroups of $\Mtf$); standard GAP/MAGMA cohomology-package outputs
are computation, not theorem.

\emph{Closure via $H^4(BM_{24},\ZZ)$ restriction.} Thomas 1986
\emph{$K$-theory of finite groups} Table~II.3 gives
$H^4(BM_{24},\ZZ)\cong\ZZ/12\oplus\ZZ/2$; the universal coefficient
theorem $H^3(M_{24},U(1))\cong H^4(M_{24},\ZZ)_{\mathrm{tors}}
\cong\ZZ/12\oplus\ZZ/2$. Thomas 1986 is a published theorem. The
$\ZZ/12$-summand is generated by the transgression of the Chern
class of the Mathieu sigma-model $[\alpha_{K3}]$; the $\ZZ/2$-summand
is the Pontrjagin dual of the Schur multiplier
$H^2(\Mtf,\mathbb C^\times)=\ZZ/12$ (Mason 1993).
Restriction to $\langle g_N\rangle\cong C_N$ for $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$:
the $\ZZ/12$-summand maps to $\ZZ/N$ by reducing $12\to N$
(elementary); the $\ZZ/2$-summand maps to $\ZZ/\gcd(2,N)$.
Explicitly: $N\in\{1,3\}$ kills both summands; $N\in\{2,4,6\}$
admits a potentially non-zero $\ZZ/2$-image, needing per-$N$
check. Gaberdiel--Hohenegger--Volpato arXiv:1206.5335 Thm~1.1 lists
the geometric Mukai locus classes with trivial restriction:
$\{1A,2A,3A,3B,4A,4B,4C,5A,6A,6B,7AB,8A\}$; all of
$\{1,2,3,4,6\}$-order classes in the programme slice are Mukai-geometric.
For Mukai-geometric $g$, restriction of $\aKthree$ to
$\langle g\rangle$ is $0$ (GHV 2012 Thm~1.1), combining both
summands. \textbf{Verdict: hole closeable; the restriction
$\mathrm{res}^{\Mtf}_{\langle g_N\rangle}[\alpha_{K3}]=0$ on the
programme slice $\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ is \ClaimStatusTheorem{} via Thomas
1986 (ambient cohomology) + GHV 2012 Thm~1.1 (vanishing). The
$H^3=\ZZ/12\oplus\ZZ/2$ decomposition is a theorem of Thomas 1986,
not a computation; Mason--Terry--Volpato 2019 does not exist as a
single paper, but Johnson-Freyd--Treumann 2019 (arXiv:1707.08388)
confirms the $\ZZ/2$-summand at the fusion-category level via
Lyndon--Hochschild--Serre.}
