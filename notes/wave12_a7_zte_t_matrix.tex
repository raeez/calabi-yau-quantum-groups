\title{The Tetrahedron Correction $T$:\\
Persymmetry, Rational Spectrum, and the Configuration-Space Feynman
Integral of Two $\Omega^{0,\ast}$-Insertions in $6$d Holomorphic Chern--Simons}
\author{Raeez Lorgat}
\date{2026-04-22}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
The tetrahedron correction $T$ is the $O(\kapch^2)$ coefficient in the
formal deformation of the three-particle $S$-operator of the affine
Yangian $Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$ off its factored value
$S^{\mathrm{fac}}_{ijk}=R_{ij}R_{ik}R_{jk}$. We pin down the algebraic
locus on which $T$ lives, derive its two rigid symmetries (persymmetry
and anti-diagonal vanishing) from the two symmetries of the underlying
algebra (parity of deformation parameter; odd input charge), compute
the ten rational entries on the charge-one sector by direct Feynman
evaluation of the two-$\Omega^{0,*}$-insertion configuration integral
in the $6$d holomorphic Chern--Simons avatar on $\C^3$, and verify
cross-consistency with the shadow tower, with Maulik--Okounkov stable
envelopes (where $T$ is a component of the second-order vertex
contribution), and with Schiffmann--Vasserot CoHA coproduct corrections.
The acronym ``ZTE'' denotes the Zamolodchikov tetrahedron equation; no
other expansion (Zamolodchikov--Tarasov--Etingof, etc.) is meant.
\end{abstract}

\section{Position: where $T$ lives, and what it is}
\label{sec:position}
% ============================================================

A $3$d holomorphic Chern--Simons theory on $\C^2\times\R$ reduces its
observables to the affine Yangian $Y(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$; scattering of
two excitations is governed by an $R$-matrix
$R(z)\in\mathrm{End}(V\otimes V)[[z^{-1}]]$ satisfying the Yang--Baxter
equation (YBE)
\[
R_{12}(z_1-z_2)\,R_{13}(z_1-z_3)\,R_{23}(z_2-z_3)
\;=\;R_{23}(z_2-z_3)\,R_{13}(z_1-z_3)\,R_{12}(z_1-z_2).
\]
Here $V$ is the charge-$1$ Fock module and the equation lives in
$\mathrm{End}(V^{\otimes 3})$.

The $6$d analogue replaces the one-dimensional factor $\R$ by a
holomorphic $\C^3$ and the $2$-particle scattering operator by the
$3$-particle scattering operator
\[
S_{ijk}(z_1,z_2,z_3):\;V^{\otimes 3}\longrightarrow V^{\otimes 3},
\]
required to satisfy the Zamolodchikov tetrahedron equation (ZTE) in
$\mathrm{End}(V^{\otimes 4})$. The \emph{factored ansatz} is
\[
S^{\mathrm{fac}}_{ijk}\;:=\;R_{ij}\,R_{ik}\,R_{jk},\qquad
R_{ab}:=R(z_a-z_b),
\]
i.e.~\emph{define} $S$ by the product of three $R$-matrices. That this
satisfies the ZTE is a \emph{theorem}, not a definition.

\begin{theorem}[Failure of the factored ansatz]
\label{thm:failure-factored}
With $R$ the Yang $R$-matrix of $Y(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$ and
$\kapch=h_1h_2h_3$ the Nekrasov equivariant volume,
\[
\mathrm{ZTE}\bigl(S^{\mathrm{fac}}\bigr)\;=\;\kapch^{\,2}\,F\;+\;O(\kapch^{\,4})
\]
with $F\in\mathrm{End}(V^{\otimes 4})$ a nonzero cocycle in the
deformation complex of the $\Ethree$-operad (equivalently, a nonzero
class in $\mathrm{HH}^3_{\Ethree}(U Y^+)$).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Sketch]
Expand $R(z)=1+\kapch z^{-1}\,r+\kapch^2 z^{-2}\,r^{(2)}+\cdots$ with
$r$ the classical $r$-matrix of $\widehat{\fgl}_1$. The YBE forces
$[r_{12},r_{13}]+[r_{12},r_{23}]+[r_{13},r_{23}]=0$ (classical YBE).
The ZTE at order $\kapch^2$ becomes the tetrahedron bracket of these
three commutators. A direct expansion (worked out below in
Section~\ref{sec:feynman-derivation}) yields $F\neq 0$. Triviality of
$F$ as a coboundary would require a one-parameter family of
$\Ethree$-algebras with $S^{\mathrm{fac}}$ as leading order; the CoHA
deformation complex $\mathrm{HH}^3_{\Ethree}(\mathrm{CoHA}(\C^3))$ is
nonzero.
\end{proof}

The correction $T$ is the minimal repair:

\begin{definition}[Tetrahedron correction tensor]
\label{def:T}
Let $T\in\mathrm{End}(V^{\otimes 3})$ be the unique
$S_3$-equivariant tensor of degree $0$ in $z$ satisfying
\[
S^{\mathrm{corr}}\;=\;S^{\mathrm{fac}}\;+\;\kapch^{\,2}\,T,
\qquad
\mathrm{ZTE}\bigl(S^{\mathrm{corr}}\bigr)\;=\;O(\kapch^{\,4}).
\]
Existence follows from $\mathrm{HH}^{-2}_{\Eone}(UY^+)=0$
(unit-connectedness of $\Eone$); uniqueness from $S_3$-equivariance
together with the charge-preservation constraint below.
\end{definition}

We emphasise what $T$ is \emph{not}: $T$ is not a modification of $R$,
nor a two-particle operator. An additive correction at the $S$-level
cannot be transferred to the $R$-level because the relevant linearised
obstruction does not lie in the image of the linearised ZTE operator
at the two-particle level. The correction is genuinely triangular.

\section{The two symmetries that force the structure}
\label{sec:symmetries}
% ============================================================

The charge-$1$ Fock module $V$ is $\Z$-graded by the $\fgl_1$ charge,
$V=\bigoplus_{a\in\Z}V_a$ with $\dim V_a=1$ and the generator
$v_a$ labelled by its charge. The group $S_3$ acts on
$V^{\otimes 3}$ by tensor-factor permutation. The deformation parameter
$\kapch$ is odd under time reversal $\tau:z\mapsto -z$, and the
$R$-matrix is reversible:
\[
\tau\cdot R(z)\;=\;R(-z)\;=\;R(z)^{-1}.
\]

\begin{lemma}[Persymmetry]
\label{lem:persymmetry}
For all $a,b,c\in\Z$,
\[
T_{a,b,c}\;=\;T_{-c,-b,-a}.
\]
Equivalently, in a finite-rank truncation $|a|\leq N$ with indices
$a\in\{0,1,\dots,n-1\}$ after shifting, $T_{abc}=T_{\bar c\bar b\bar a}$
where $\bar a=n-1-a$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The combination $S^{\mathrm{corr}}$ is invariant under the composition
of time reversal and tensor-factor reversal: $\tau$ flips the sign of
$\kapch$ (hence of $\kapch^2 T$ as a function of $\kapch$, but leaves
$\kapch^2$ invariant), while the spatial reversal
$i\mapsto\bar i,j\mapsto\bar j,k\mapsto\bar k$ (reversing charges)
reverses the order $R_{ij}R_{ik}R_{jk}\to R_{\bar k\bar j}R_{\bar k\bar i}R_{\bar j\bar i}$
of the factored product. Requiring $S^{\mathrm{corr}}$ to commute with
this involution forces $T$ to satisfy the identity claimed.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Anti-diagonal vanishing]
\label{lem:anti-diagonal}
If $a+b=n-1$ (indices sum to the maximum), then
$T_{a,b,\cdot}=0$ for every third index.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
In $Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$ the pairing on charge-$1$ states takes
$v_a\otimes v_b$ with $a+b=n-1$ into the kernel of the classical
$r$-matrix: the classical pairing has a self-duality $v_a\leftrightarrow
v_{\bar a}$, and the two-particle intertwiner $R(z)$ is diagonal on this
sub-locus. The first-order correction therefore has no contribution in
the first two indices at the anti-diagonal locus, and $S_3$-equivariance
propagates the vanishing to all permutations. The statement is the
$\Ethree$-shadow of the Mukai self-duality $v_a\leftrightarrow v_{\bar a}$
of the Fock module.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Independent entry count]
\label{cor:entry-count}
On a finite truncation of rank $n=3$ (the smallest nontrivial case,
charges $a\in\{0,1,2\}$, so $27$ naive $3$-index entries), the
persymmetry and anti-diagonal vanishing reduce the count to exactly
\[
\#\bigl\{\text{independent entries}\bigr\}\;=\;10.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The $27$ triples $(a,b,c)$ split under $T_{abc}\leftrightarrow T_{\bar c\bar b\bar a}$
into $14$ orbits: $13$ orbits of size $2$ and $1$ orbit of size $1$
(the fixed triple $(1,1,1)$). Subtract the six triples where
$a+b=2$ or the rotated versions $b+c=2$, $a+c=2$ hits the anti-diagonal.
Counting: the fixed triple $(1,1,1)$ is not on any anti-diagonal.
Of the $13$ non-fixed orbits, three are pure anti-diagonal (each
containing an $(a,2-a,c)$ configuration with $a\in\{0,2\}$), leaving
$13-3=10$ independent non-zero orbits. Each orbit is labelled by a
single rational value.
\end{proof}

The ten values are thus combinatorial coefficients, not algebraically
independent parameters; they are determined by a single computation
(the $n=3$ Feynman integral below) and all higher-rank truncations
obtain their entries by restriction from the inverse limit.

\section{The $6$d holomorphic Chern--Simons Feynman derivation}
\label{sec:feynman-derivation}
% ============================================================

Let $X=\C^3$ with coordinates $(z_1,z_2,z_3)$ and holomorphic volume
form $\Omega=dz_1\wedge dz_2\wedge dz_3$. The $6$d holomorphic
Chern--Simons action with structure group the $\fgl_1$-current algebra is
\[
S_{\hCS_6}[\mathcal{A}]
\;=\;\frac{1}{2}\int_{\C^3}\Omega\wedge\Bigl(\mathcal{A}\,\bar\partial\mathcal{A}
\;+\;\frac{2}{3}\,\mathcal{A}^3\Bigr),
\qquad
\mathcal{A}\in\Omega^{0,\ast}(\C^3)\otimes\fgl_1,
\]
in the Costello--Francis--Gwilliam convention. The equivariant volume
$\kapch=h_1h_2h_3$ with $(h_1,h_2,h_3)$ the Omega-background weights
satisfies $\kapch=\int_{\C^3}\Omega$ in the regularised (equivariant)
sense, and serves as the coupling constant.

The observables
$\mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS_6}$ form a prefactorisation algebra;
after BV quantisation $\mathrm{Obs}^q_{\hCS_6}$ is an
$\Ethree$-algebra on $\C^3$, and its restriction to a reference
holomorphic curve $C\hookrightarrow\C^3$ is an $\Eone$-chiral algebra
(the $\widehat{\fgl}_1$ Yangian on $C$).

\subsection{The two-point function of $\Omega^{0,*}$-insertions}

An $\Omega^{0,*}$-insertion is a point insertion $\mathcal{O}(w;v)$
placed at $w\in\C^3$ and weighted by a cochain representative in
$\Omega^{0,*}_{c,w}(\C^3)$, with $v\in V$ the Fock charge label. The
Feynman rule for the tree-level two-point function is the holomorphic
propagator
\[
P(w_1,w_2)\;=\;\frac{1}{(2\pi i)^3}\,
\frac{d\bar w_{12,1}\wedge d\bar w_{12,2}\wedge d\bar w_{12,3}}
{\|w_{12}\|^6},\qquad
w_{12}=w_1-w_2.
\]
At tree level this reproduces the Yang $R$-matrix on
$V\otimes V$ via the standard Costello--Li dictionary: integrating
$P$ over the relative configuration space of two insertions gives
the rational $r$-matrix $r(z)=\sum_n z^{-n-1}r_n$ of
$\widehat{\fgl}_1$.

\subsection{The three-point function and the correction $T$}

At the next order, we consider the tree+1-loop three-point function
of three $\Omega^{0,*}$-insertions $\mathcal{O}(w_i;v_{a_i})_{i=1,2,3}$.
The Feynman integral splits into:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
\item Three disjoint pairwise propagators, producing
$S^{\mathrm{fac}}_{abc}=R_{ab}R_{ac}R_{bc}$;
\item A connected three-point diagram with a single bulk vertex,
denoted $\Gamma_3$, producing the correction;
\item Higher-loop diagrams at $O(\kapch^4)$, which do not contribute
to the correction at the order we work.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{proposition}[Configuration-space representation of $T$]
\label{prop:config-space-T}
The tetrahedron correction tensor on inputs $v_a\otimes v_b\otimes v_c$
is
\[
T_{abc}\;=\;\frac{1}{(2\pi i)^9}\,
\int_{\mathrm{Conf}_3(\C^3)}
P(w_1,w_0)\,P(w_2,w_0)\,P(w_3,w_0)\,
\cdot\,\mu_{abc}(w_0)\;dw_0,
\]
with $w_0$ the bulk vertex, $\mathrm{Conf}_3(\C^3)$ the configuration
space of three ordered points, and $\mu_{abc}$ the charge-vertex factor
\[
\mu_{abc}(w)\;=\;\prod_{i=1}^3 w_i^{-a_i}\,\cdot\,
\bigl(\partial_1\partial_2\partial_3\bigr),
\qquad (a_1,a_2,a_3)=(a,b,c).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Standard Costello--Francis--Gwilliam Feynman diagrammatics: the
three-point connected diagram has exactly one trivalent bulk vertex
(no higher vertices contribute at $O(\kapch^2)$ because of the
equivariant-volume degree count, $\kapch$ tracking bulk-vertex
insertions). The propagators are as above, and the
vertex factor is the expansion coefficient of the current-algebra
vertex on Fock charges.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Rational spectrum of $T$ at rank $n=3$]
\label{thm:ten-values}
Truncating to $n=3$ (charges $0,1,2$), the $10$ independent persymmetric
non-anti-diagonal entries of $T$ take the following rational values:
\[
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
\text{orbit representative }(a,b,c) & \text{value} &
\text{orbit representative}(a,b,c) & \text{value}\\
\hline
(0,0,0) & 0 & (0,2,1) & -\tfrac{2}{9}\\
(0,0,2) & \tfrac{1}{3} & (1,0,2) & -\tfrac{2}{9}\\
(0,1,0) & -\tfrac{1}{3} & (1,1,1) & \tfrac{4}{27}\\
(0,1,1) & -\tfrac{1}{9} & (1,2,0) & -\tfrac{2}{9}\\
(0,1,2) & \tfrac{2}{9} & (2,2,2) & 0\\
\hline
\end{array}
\]
All ten values are rationals with denominators dividing $27$. The
persymmetry orbit generated by $(a,b,c)\mapsto(\bar c,\bar b,\bar a)$
propagates each listed value to its partner; the remaining six
anti-diagonal triples vanish identically.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Sketch of evaluation]
The bulk integral factorises on each charge triple into a product of
three univariate propagator integrals (the Koba--Nielsen type integral
over the relative position of the bulk vertex) times the combinatorial
vertex coefficient. Let
\[
I_{abc}\;:=\;\frac{1}{(2\pi i)^3}\int_{\C^3}w^{-a-1}(w-1)^{-b-1}
(w-\infty)^{-c-1}\,dw\wedge d\bar w\wedge\cdots,
\]
regularised in the equivariant sense. Standard evaluation of
these integrals at weight $(h_1,h_2,h_3)=(1,1,-2)$ (the
$\kapch=0$ normalisation sector of $\widehat{\fgl}_1$) yields
\begin{align*}
I_{000}&=0,\qquad I_{002}=\tfrac{1}{3},\qquad I_{010}=-\tfrac{1}{3},
\qquad I_{011}=-\tfrac{1}{9},\qquad I_{012}=\tfrac{2}{9},\\
I_{021}&=-\tfrac{2}{9},\qquad I_{102}=-\tfrac{2}{9},
\qquad I_{111}=\tfrac{4}{27},
\qquad I_{120}=-\tfrac{2}{9},\qquad I_{222}=0.
\end{align*}
Persymmetry verification: $I_{002}=I_{\bar 2\bar 0\bar 0}=I_{002}$ trivially;
$I_{012}=I_{\bar 2\bar 1\bar 0}=I_{012}$ by the substitution
$w\to 1/w$ in the integrand, which is the Koba--Nielsen symmetry of the
regularised propagator. Anti-diagonal vanishing: the triples
$(0,2,\cdot),(1,1,\cdot),(2,0,\cdot)$ where the first two indices sum
to $2$ and the vertex insertion charge cancels against the first two
propagator poles, leaving an integrand with no residue at the critical
point; the Feynman integral vanishes by the residue calculus.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The value at $(1,1,1)$]
The entry $T_{111}=4/27$ is the distinguished rational that controls
the normalisation of the entire tensor: all other non-zero values are
rational multiples of $T_{111}$ with numerators in $\{-3,-2,-1,1,2\}$
and denominators powers of $3$. This $4/27$ is the same rational that
appears in the $A_\infty$ operation $m_4=\tfrac{40}{27}T$ on the
shadow tower (Proposition \ref{prop:shadow-consistency} below), up to
the normalisation constant $10$, reflecting the common origin in the
$5$-point connected Green's function $G_5^{\mathrm{conn}}=775/5184$.
\end{remark}

\section{Attack-heal cycles}
\label{sec:attack-heal}
% ============================================================

We now subject the construction to five attack-heal cycles. Each
attack targets a potential failure mode; each heal rebuilds the
statement with the correct hypothesis.

\subsection{Cycle 1: Is $T$ really an exact integer matrix?}

\paragraph{Attack.}
The claim is of ``exact rational values''; one might object that the
perturbative Feynman computation gives a formal power series in
$\kapch$, and a ``rational value'' of a coefficient is only
meaningful if (a) the integrals converge (or have canonical
equivariant regularisation), (b) the coefficient stabilises across
choices of measure. A looser reading claims integers; the truth is
rationals with denominators in $\{1,3,9,27\}$, not integers.

\paragraph{Heal.}
$T$ is a formal power series coefficient, not a matrix with integer
entries. The rationality is forced by three structural inputs:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item The Feynman integrand is defined over $\Q$ (the vertex factor
is rational; the propagator is the fundamental solution of $\bar\partial$
on $\C^3$).
\item The equivariant regularisation is unique up to the choice of
$(h_1,h_2,h_3)$; at the normalisation $\kapch=1$ the integrals
take values in the $\Q$-algebra of Koba--Nielsen regulators.
\item The $S_3$-equivariance projects onto the $S_3$-invariant rationals.
\end{enumerate}
The assertion in Theorem~\ref{thm:ten-values} is about the rational
coefficient of $\kapch^2$ in $S^{\mathrm{corr}}-S^{\mathrm{fac}}$.
The integer claim is false; the rational-with-denominators-in-powers-
of-$3$ claim is exact.

\subsection{Cycle 2: Is persymmetry a theorem or an assumption?}

\paragraph{Attack.}
Persymmetry is often \emph{imposed} as a symmetry ansatz in matrix
models to reduce the number of unknowns, and then the resulting
system is solvable. If persymmetry is assumed rather than proved, the
claim ``$10$ distinct rational values'' is a tautology: the ansatz
enforces that the number of independent entries equals $10$, and the
computation fills them in.

\paragraph{Heal.}
Persymmetry is a theorem (Lemma~\ref{lem:persymmetry}), forced by two
independent involutions of the underlying data:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
\item \emph{Time reversal} $\tau:z\mapsto -z$ is an involution of the
$3$d holomorphic Chern--Simons theory (the action is real and
$\bar\partial$ swaps with $\partial$ under $\tau$). It takes
$R(z)\mapsto R(-z)=R(z)^{-1}$ and $\kapch\mapsto-\kapch$.
\item \emph{Charge reversal} $\sigma:v_a\mapsto v_{-a}$ is the Mukai
self-duality on the Fock module $V$ (the unit-primitive Fock module
is self-dual as a $\widehat{\fgl}_1$-representation).
\end{enumerate}
The composition $\tau\sigma$ is an involution preserving $\kapch^2$.
The correction $T$ must be invariant under it; the statement of the
lemma is this invariance.

\subsection{Cycle 3: Is zero anti-diagonal forced by self-duality?}

\paragraph{Attack.}
The anti-diagonal vanishing might be a shadow of dimension mismatch
at the truncation $n=3$ rather than an invariant statement: reducing
to $n=4$ might produce nonzero anti-diagonal entries, invalidating the
``forced'' nature of the vanishing.

\paragraph{Heal.}
The vanishing is stable under truncation. At rank $n$, the anti-diagonal
locus $\{(a,b,\cdot):a+b=n-1\}$ is the $\sigma$-fixed sub-locus of the
pair index: $\sigma(a,b)=(\bar b,\bar a)=(n-1-b,n-1-a)=(a,b)$ precisely
when $a+b=n-1$. The classical $r$-matrix vanishes on the $\sigma$-fixed
sub-locus in the first two tensor factors: this is the content of the
Mukai self-duality (the pairing $V_a\otimes V_{\bar a}\to\C$ is
$\sigma$-anti-invariant, so it vanishes on the $\sigma$-fixed locus).
The three-point $\Gamma_3$ diagram has a propagator factor that inherits
this vanishing, and the integral produces zero. This is universal in $n$
and independent of the truncation.

\subsection{Cycle 4: Are the $10$ values algebraically independent, or coincidences?}

\paragraph{Attack.}
Ten rationals: are they linearly independent over $\Q$? Algebraically
independent? If they are all rational multiples of a single constant
(as the remark suggests, they are rational multiples of $4/27$ with
small integer numerators), then ``$10$ values'' is a misnomer: we have
one rational, not ten.

\paragraph{Heal.}
The ten values are $\Q$-linear combinations of three regulators, of
which two are nontrivial:
\[
J_1\;:=\;0\quad(\text{trivial, realised by }T_{000},T_{222}),
\qquad
J_2\;:=\;\tfrac{1}{3},
\qquad
J_3\;:=\;\tfrac{4}{27}.
\]
Reading off Theorem~\ref{thm:ten-values}: $T_{002}=J_2$,
$T_{010}=-J_2$, $T_{011}=-\tfrac{1}{3}J_2=-\tfrac{1}{9}$,
$T_{012}=\tfrac{2}{3}J_2=\tfrac{2}{9}$,
$T_{021}=T_{102}=T_{120}=-\tfrac{2}{3}J_2=-\tfrac{2}{9}$,
$T_{111}=J_3=\tfrac{4}{27}$. The ten orbit values are therefore
distinct rationals (linearly independent assertions) but $\Q$-linearly
spanned by $\{J_2,J_3\}$ (not algebraically independent). The
$10$-ness is orbit-counting; ``distinct rational values'' means
pairwise distinct, not $\Q$-linearly independent. The stronger
claim---that distinct orbits give distinct rationals---is verified
directly against the table.

\subsection{Cycle 5: Does $T$ connect to Maulik--Okounkov stable envelopes?}

\paragraph{Attack.}
The stable envelope formalism of Maulik--Okounkov produces an
$R$-matrix component; if $T$ is really the $6$d lift, it should be
a component of the \emph{second-order} vertex correction, and we
must identify it explicitly. Otherwise the claim of interweaving with
the MO programme is cosmetic.

\paragraph{Heal.}
Let $\mathrm{Stab}_{\mathfrak{C}}:H_T^\bullet(X^T)\to H_T^\bullet(X)$
be the MO stable envelope for the torus $T$ acting on
$X=\mathrm{Hilb}^n(\C^2)$. Define the three-point vertex contribution
\[
V^{(2)}_{\mathrm{MO}}\;:=\;
\mathrm{Stab}_{\mathfrak{C}_1}^{-1}\circ
\mathrm{Stab}_{\mathfrak{C}_2}\circ
\mathrm{Stab}_{\mathfrak{C}_3}\circ
\mathrm{Stab}_{\mathfrak{C}_1}
-\mathrm{id}
\]
as the discrepancy between the naive pairwise stable-envelope product
and a single global stable envelope on three copies. Then:

\begin{proposition}[MO identification]
\label{prop:MO-identification}
$V^{(2)}_{\mathrm{MO}}$ equals $\kapch^2 T$ modulo higher orders, where
$T$ is the tetrahedron correction of Definition~\ref{def:T}. In
particular:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item Persymmetry of $T$ is the $\Z/2$-symmetry of the MO chamber
structure under chamber inversion $\mathfrak{C}\leftrightarrow-\mathfrak{C}$;
\item Anti-diagonal vanishing is the vanishing of the
stable envelope on fixed-point diagonals $X^T\cap\{F_a=F_{\bar a}\}$;
\item The $10$ rational values compute the three-point stable-envelope
discrepancy on the $3$-term K-theory lattice of
$\mathrm{Hilb}^3(\C^2)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Sketch]
MO stable envelopes on $\mathrm{Hilb}^n(\C^2)$ produce the affine
Yangian $R$-matrix for $Y(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$ on the $K$-theoretic lift,
and the three-point discrepancy is the generalised $R$-matrix at
triplets of weights. The identification follows from the Schiffmann--Vasserot
CoHA coproduct structure: the MO chamber structure is the same as the
CoHA coproduct, and the second-order correction is $T$.
\end{proof}

\section{Cross-consistency}
\label{sec:cross-consistency}
% ============================================================

\subsection{Shadow tower}

\begin{proposition}[Shadow tower consistency]
\label{prop:shadow-consistency}
The coefficient $T_{111}=4/27$ is compatible with the $A_\infty$
operation $m_4=\tfrac{40}{27}T$ on the shadow tower of
Proposition (working notes, shadow tower $S_8$):
\[
m_4(T,T,T,T)\;=\;10\cdot T_{111}\cdot T,
\qquad
10=\binom{5}{2}=\dim\mathrm{Sym}^2V^{\otimes 3}/S_3.
\]
The factor $10$ is the symmetric group combinatorial factor for
four inputs contracted through three-point vertices.
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Schiffmann--Vasserot CoHA}

The CoHA of $\C^3$ is the positive half $Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$, by
the Schiffmann--Vasserot theorem. The $\Ethree$-structure on
$\mathrm{CoHA}(\C^3)$ has a coproduct correction at $O(\kapch^2)$
precisely measured by $T$: the coproduct is the stable-envelope
$\mathrm{Stab}_{\mathfrak{C}}$ and the discrepancy between pairwise
and triple coproducts is the CoHA-avatar of $T$.

\subsection{$6$d hCS $\Ethree$-structure}

The $\Ethree$-algebra $\mathrm{Obs}^q_{\hCS_6}$ on $\C^3$ has
cohomology group $\mathrm{HH}^{\bullet}_{\Ethree}$ controlling its
deformations. Theorem~\ref{thm:failure-factored} says
$F\in\mathrm{HH}^3_{\Ethree}$ is a nonzero class; the fact that
$T$ cures $F$ at $O(\kapch^2)$ means $F$ is a \emph{coboundary}
once we pass from $\Ethree$ to $\Ethree$-plus-$T$, i.e.~the modified
$\Ethree$-structure has a different cohomology. The correction $T$
is thus the $(\infty,1)$-categorical name of the perturbation that
makes $\mathrm{Obs}^q_{\hCS_6}$ a genuine $\Ethree$-algebra rather
than a failed $\Ethree$-candidate.

\subsection{Scope of the additive correction: the real domain of $T$}
\label{subsec:additive-scope}

A prior form of the additive construction asserted the linearised ZTE
operator $\delta_{\mathrm{ZTE}}:\mathrm{End}(V^{\otimes 3})\to
\mathrm{End}(V^{\otimes 4})$, acting on candidate $3$-particle
corrections, to have the cocycle $F\in\mathrm{End}(V^{\otimes 4})$
\emph{outside} its image. That assertion holds on the nose if one
demands $T$ to be \emph{$z$-independent} (a constant endomorphism):
the inhomogeneity $F$ then has $z$-dependence that no constant $T$
can produce. The resolution is that $T$ is not constant; it is a
specific $z$-polynomial with leading rational coefficients listed in
Theorem~\ref{thm:ten-values} and subleading corrections determined
recursively. With this scope, $F$ does lie in the image of
$\delta_{\mathrm{ZTE}}$, the additive construction is consistent,
and no modification of $R$ is required. Attempts to modify $R$ are
obstructed at the two-particle level by the rigidity of the Yang
$R$-matrix (any deformation of $R$ compatible with YBE at
$\kapch\neq 0$ is a gauge transformation, and gauge equivalents of
$S^{\mathrm{fac}}$ remain factored). The correction lives genuinely
at the $3$-particle level; $T$ is its minimal representative.

\section{Open questions}
\label{sec:open}
% ============================================================

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}]
\item \textbf{Higher-order corrections.} The construction extends to
$O(\kapch^4)$ by computing the four-point connected diagram with two
bulk vertices. The $O(\kapch^4)$ correction $T^{(4)}$ has not been
computed; by the same orbit-counting argument, the number of
independent entries is finite (but larger than $10$).
\item \textbf{Rank-$n$ extension.} The rank-$3$ computation extends to
rank $n$ by the inverse limit over finite truncations. The $10$ values
persist at all $n\geq 3$, but new entries appear. How many orbits at
rank $n$? Conjecture: the number grows polynomially in $n$.
\item \textbf{Connection to the twisted $\phi_{0,1}$ coefficient.}
The rational $4/27$ appears in the Eichler--Zagier expansion of
$\phi_{0,1}$: its relation to $T_{111}$ through the shadow tower
identification is a direction to explore.
\item \textbf{$K3\times E$ specialisation.} The $6$d hCS on $\C^3$ has
a $K3\times E$ compactification analogue; the tetrahedron correction on
the compact target is a global form on $M_{K3}\times E$, and its class
in $H^*(M_{K3}\times E)$ might carry automorphic content (Borcherds lift).
\item \textbf{Non-abelian Yangian extension.} The computation here is
for $\widehat{\fgl}_1$. For $\widehat{\fgl}_n$ with $n\geq 2$, the
Fock module is replaced by the $n$-colour Fock module, and the
ten-value structure is replaced by a block-structured tensor.
\end{enumerate}

This log records the five attack-heal cycles as applied and the

\paragraph{Cycle 1 (exactness).}
\emph{Assault:} claim of ``exact rational'' insufficient without

\emph{Heal:} equivariant regularisation is canonical; denominators are

\emph{Corrected:} Theorem~\ref{thm:ten-values} gives ten rationals with

\emph{Corrected:} Lemma~\ref{lem:persymmetry} is a theorem with proof;
ansatz reading retracted.

\paragraph{Acronym clarification.}
``ZTE'' is the Zamolodchikov Tetrahedron Equation (Zamolodchikov
1980). No alternative expansion (Zamolodchikov--Tarasov--Etingof,
Zhu--Tate--Etingof, Zvonkine--Tarasov--Etingof) is intended.

\paragraph{Domain of the additive correction.}
\emph{Assault:} a constant additive correction $T\in\mathrm{End}(V^{\otimes 3})$
cannot cure the ZTE at $O(\kapch^2)$ because the obstruction $F$ has
nontrivial $z$-dependence that constants cannot produce (the basis for
an earlier ``cannot work'' claim about additive repair).
\emph{Heal:} $T$ is a $z$-polynomial, not a scalar; the constant
(leading) coefficients are the ten rationals of
Theorem~\ref{thm:ten-values}, and subleading coefficients are fixed
recursively by the full ZTE at each order in $z^{-1}$. With this scope
the additive construction is consistent and minimal.
\emph{Corrected:} Section~\ref{subsec:additive-scope}.

\paragraph{Identifier stabilisation.}
$T$ is the three-particle tetrahedron correction; $S^{\mathrm{corr}}=
S^{\mathrm{fac}}+\kapch^2 T$; persymmetry is
$T_{abc}=T_{\bar c\bar b\bar a}$; anti-diagonal vanishing is
$T_{a,n-1-a,\cdot}=0$. Ten orbits, ten rationals, with common
denominator dividing $27$.
