\section*{Higher-loop QME obstruction audit for $6$D hCS on $K3 \times E$ with admissible
$\frakg$}

\paragraph{Conditional scope.}
Let $X = K3 \times E$ with the Calabi--Yau product metric. Let $\frakg$
be a simple complex Lie algebra with vanishing totally symmetric cubic
Casimir, $d^{abc}_\frakg = 0$ (the admissible Cartan types: $A_1$,
$B_n$, $C_n$, $D_n$, $E_6$, $E_7$, $E_8$, $F_4$, $G_2$). The
$6$-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons action
\[
  S_{\mathrm{cl}}[A] = \int_X \Omega_X \wedge
  \tr\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2}A \wedge \dbar A
  + \tfrac{1}{6} A \wedge [A, A]\right),
  \qquad A \in \Omega^{0, 1}(X) \otimes \frakg,
\]
admits a renormalised quantum action
\(S_{\mathrm{ren}}[A] = S_{\mathrm{cl}}[A] + \sum_{n \geq 1} \hbar^n
\Theta_n[A]\)
satisfying the quantum master equation to all orders only if the
Costello--Li anomaly class in the CY$_3$ quartic slot
\[
  P_4 \in \Sym^4(\frakg^\vee)^\frakg
\]
is killed by the chosen counterterm package and the all-scale
renormalisation data satisfy Costello's QME recursion. The condition
$d^{abc}_\frakg=0$ is retained as the one-loop/cubic-descendant
admissibility input. It does not annihilate all cubic hCS colour
factors, does not decide the quartic $P_4$ obstruction, and does not
by itself prove a higher-loop QME.

\paragraph{Notation.}
Write $L_X := \Omega^{0, \bullet}(X) \otimes \frakg$ for the
$L_\infty$-algebra controlling deformations of the trivial $\frakg$-bundle
on $X$, with bracket $[-, -]_L$ inherited from $\frakg$ and differential
$\dbar$. The classical BV action $S_{\mathrm{cl}}$ is the Chevalley--Eilenberg
$\hbar$-formal cubic of $L_X$ with values in
$\Omega^{6, *}(X) \cap \ker \dbar$. The Costello--Li propagator
$P_X^{[\epsilon, L]}$ regularises the inverse of $\dbar$ via the heat-kernel
on $\Omega^{0, *}$, smeared between scales $\epsilon$ and $L$. The space
of local functionals on $L_X$ is denoted $\Loc(L_X)$; its cohomology
under $[-, S_{\mathrm{cl}}]_\BV$ is the local-cohomology obstruction theory
of Costello~2011 Ch.~5.

%--------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Cycle 1: Two-loop graph enumeration on the cubic theory.}
\emph{Attack.}
At one loop the QME-obstruction is the wheel with one cubic vertex
closed by the propagator. At two loops the cubic theory
$S_{\mathrm{cl}} = (1/6) \int \Omega_X \wedge \tr(A^3)$ was said to
produce three connected trivalent vacuum topologies with four vertices:
theta, dumbbell, sunrise. That count is wrong.

\emph{Heal.}
For a connected trivalent vacuum graph, $3V=2E$ and
$L(\Gamma)=E-V+1$. At $L=2$ this gives
\[
  2=E-V+1=V/2+1,\qquad V=2,\qquad E=3.
\]
Thus the tadpole-free connected trivalent vacuum graph is the theta
graph: two vertices joined by three parallel edges. If tadpoles are
allowed by the gauge-fixing convention, the second multigraph is the
dumbbell: two one-vertex tadpole loops joined by one bridge edge; its
colour factor contains $f^{aab}$ and vanishes by antisymmetry. The
sunrise/melon graph is not a connected trivalent vacuum graph of the
cubic theory at $L=2$; it belongs to two-point self-energy or
quartic-induced bookkeeping after external legs or counterterms have
been specified.

\[
  \Theta_2[A] \;=\; \Theta_2^{(\theta)}[A]
  \quad \text{plus tadpole terms only in schemes that admit them.}
\]

\emph{$\frakg$-tensorial decoration.}
The cubic vertex carries the structure constant $f^{abc}$ of $\frakg$;
joining such constants by propagator contractions produces invariant
tensors. The condition $d^{abc}_\frakg=0$ kills the totally symmetric
cubic anomaly tensor. It does not imply that the colour factor of every
cubic graph vanishes. For example,
\[
  T^{(\theta)}_2 = f^{abc} f^{ade} f^{bdf} f^{cef}, \qquad
  T^{(\bowtie)}_2 = f^{aab} f^{ccb}=0.
\]
Two-point/self-energy contractions reduce through
$f^{abc}f^{abd}=2h^\vee_\frakg\delta^{cd}$ to quadratic Casimir
wave-function terms. Quartic local functionals, when produced by
external fields or counterterm expansion, project to the invariant
polynomial slot
\[
  P_4\in\Sym^4(\frakg^\vee)^\frakg .
\]
This $P_4$ slot is the Costello--Li CY$_3$ anomaly gate. It is not
decided by $d^{abc}_\frakg=0$.

Primary: Costello~2011 Ch.~5 (graph enumeration, Euler character);
Costello-Li~2016 \S~4 (BV graph integrals on hCS).

%--------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Cycle 2: The theta graph lands in the quartic gate.}
\emph{Attack.}
The theta-graph contribution may contain a local functional of
quartic type, schematically
$\Omega_X \wedge P_4(A,\partial A,\partial A,\partial A)$, where
$P_4\in\Sym^4(\frakg^\vee)^\frakg$. It is not enough to invoke
Calabi--Yau triviality, Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality, or
Whitehead's lemma for $H^1(\frakg,\frakg)$.

\emph{Heal.}
The correct obstruction group is the Costello--Li local Lie-algebra
cohomology slot for holomorphic Chern--Simons in complex dimension
three. In the manuscript anchor
\texttt{chapters/theory/cy3\_chain\_level\_bridge.tex},
Proposition~\texttt{prop:cy3-hcs-quartic-anomaly-slot} states that
the local one-loop obstruction for a holomorphic theory on a complex
$d$-fold is governed by
\[
  P_{d+1}\in\Sym^{d+1}(\frakg^\vee)^\frakg .
\]
For $d=3$ this is $P_4$. The cubic class is the $d=2$ or $5$d
descendant slot; it cannot be transported unchanged into the CY$_3$
hCS bridge.

The geometric vector-field factor was also misread. For
$X=K3\times E$,
\[
  H^0(X,T_X)
  \cong H^0(K3,T_{K3})\oplus H^0(E,T_E)
  \cong 0\oplus\C
  \cong \C,
\]
coming from translations on the elliptic factor. Thus $H^0(T_X)$ is
not zero. Even if the relevant Whitehead group
$H^1(\frakg,\frakg)$ vanishes for simple $\frakg$, that is the wrong
cohomology group for the CY$_3$ anomaly gate: the obstruction is an
invariant-polynomial local functional, not an outer-derivation class.

\emph{Conclusion (Cycle 2).}
The theta contribution is not proved BV-trivial in this note. The
correct repaired statement is conditional:
\[
  [\Theta_2^{(\theta)}]=0
  \quad \text{only after the } P_4\text{-class is killed by a chosen
  Costello--Li counterterm package.}
\]
The dumbbell tadpole term vanishes by $f^{aab}=0$ when it is present.
Two-point/self-energy terms reduce to quadratic Casimir
wave-function counterterms. The quartic CY$_3$ anomaly class remains
the genuine gate.

Primary: Costello~2011 Ch.~5 (BV obstruction recursion);
Costello--Li local Lie-algebra cohomology for holomorphic theories;
\texttt{chapters/theory/cy3\_chain\_level\_bridge.tex},
Proposition~\texttt{prop:cy3-hcs-quartic-anomaly-slot}.

\paragraph{Cycle 3: Recursive higher-loop closure via local cohomology.}
\emph{Attack.}
At each loop order $L \geq 2$, new graph topologies appear (number of
trivalent graphs at $L$ loops grows factorially). The QME obstruction
is a class in $H^1(\Loc(L_X)[[\hbar]], [-, -]_\BV)$ at order $\hbar^L$.
A priori, distinct higher-loop classes need not vanish even when each
single-loop class does; the full QME is an inductive condition.

\emph{Heal.}
Costello~2011 Theorem~5.0.1 (the obstruction theorem, cited as
``Theorem A'' in Costello~2007 first preprint, finalised in the
Cambridge book~2011) states: assume $S_{\mathrm{cl}}$ satisfies the
classical master equation, and let
$\Theta_1, \dots, \Theta_n$ be a tower of counterterms cancelling the
QME through order $\hbar^n$. The order-$\hbar^{n+1}$ obstruction is a
local functional $O_{n+1}[A] \in \Loc(L_X)$ whose cohomology class
\[
  [O_{n+1}] \;\in\;
  H^1\bigl(\Loc(L_X), [-, S_{\mathrm{cl}}]_\BV\bigr)
\]
is well-defined (independent of the choice of $\Theta_1, \dots,
\Theta_n$). If $[O_{n+1}] = 0$, the QME extends to order $\hbar^{n+1}$
by addition of a counterterm $\Theta_{n+1}$ trivialising
$O_{n+1}$. The induction closes when $[O_n] = 0$ for all $n \geq 1$.

The false step is to replace this obstruction group by the cubic slot
alone. For holomorphic Chern--Simons in complex dimension three, the
local anomaly gate is the Costello--Li invariant-polynomial class
\[
  P_4\in\Sym^4(\frakg^\vee)^\frakg .
\]
Thus $d^{abc}_\frakg=0$ proves only that the cubic descendant anomaly
is absent. It does not prove
\[
  H^1\bigl(\Loc(L_X),[-,S_{\mathrm{cl}}]_\BV\bigr)/(\text{BV-trivial})
  =0.
\]
The wave-function terms may be absorbed by heat-kernel
renormalisation; the quartic local cohomology class must still be
cancelled or shown exact in the actual Costello--Li complex.

\emph{Calabi--Yau bias, repaired.}
The Calabi--Yau condition supplies the holomorphic volume form and the
BV pairing. It does not erase invariant-polynomial anomalies. The
vanishing $\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(K3\times E)=0$ removes the BCOV Euler
coefficient in the relevant sector, but it is not a proof that every
quartic hCS local functional is BV-exact. The elliptic factor also
contributes $H^0(E,T_E)=\C$, so there is no $H^0(T_X)$-vanishing
argument on $K3\times E$.

\emph{Conclusion (Cycle 3).}
The correct induction is conditional. If an all-scale Costello--Li
package is supplied and the quartic class $P_4$ is killed at each
renormalisation step, Costello's obstruction theorem extends the QME
one order further. Without that quartic cancellation, the one-loop
$d^{abc}_\frakg=0$ calculation and the spectral gap do not imply an
all-orders QME. Convergence of the resulting formal series is a
separate analytic problem and is not supplied by the QME recursion.

Primary: Costello~2011 Theorem~5.0.1 (BV obstruction theorem);
Costello--Li local Lie-algebra cohomology for holomorphic theories;
\texttt{chapters/theory/cy3\_chain\_level\_bridge.tex},
Proposition~\texttt{prop:cy3-hcs-quartic-anomaly-slot} and
Definition~\texttt{def:all-scale-quantum-hcs-package}.

\paragraph{Obstruction comparison: $\fsl_N$ and quartic data.}
For $\frakg = \fsl_{N \geq 3}$, the symmetric cubic tensor
$d^{abc}_\frakg$ is nonzero, so the cubic descendant obstruction is
already active in the settings where that slot is the governing one.
In CY$_3$ hCS the independent gate is quartic. The theta/counterterm
quartic contribution carries the projection to
$P_4\in\Sym^4(\frakg^\vee)^\frakg$. For $\fsl_N$ one may write
\[
  d^{abcd}_{\fsl_N} \;=\; \tfrac{1}{4!} \tr_{\mathrm{adj}}\!\bigl(
  T^{(a} T^b T^c T^{d)}\bigr) \;=\; 2 N \cdot \delta^{(ab} \delta^{cd)}
  + \text{primitive } d^{(abcd)}_{\fsl_N}.
\]
The primitive part $d^{(abcd)}_{\fsl_N}$ is non-zero for $N \geq 4$,
zero for $N = 2, 3$ (the latter has only the symmetric quadratic
Casimir squared as quartic invariant). This algebraic decomposition
does not by itself decide the BV cohomology class. It says only that
for $N\ge4$ there is a primitive quartic tensor that cannot be absorbed
into quadratic wave-function renormalisation without an additional
counterterm or cancellation. For $N=3$, the absence of a primitive
quartic leaves a decomposable quadratic-square term, but cubic
$d^{abc}$ remains nonzero.

The simply-laced/non-simply-laced division is not the right division
for this note. The present admissible list is the $d^{abc}=0$ list for
the cubic slot; the CY$_3$ all-orders gate is the quartic $P_4$ slot.

\paragraph{Synthesis and remaining obstructions.}
\begin{theorem}[Conditional all-orders QME criterion for $6$D hCS on $K3 \times E$]
\label{thm:6d-hcs-qme-all-loop-k3xe-blocker6}
With $X = K3 \times E$ and $\frakg$ admissible
\textup{(}$d^{abc}_\frakg = 0$\textup{),} suppose in addition that an
all-scale Costello--Li quantum hCS package is supplied and that the
CY$_3$ quartic local cohomology class
$P_4\in\Sym^4(\frakg^\vee)^\frakg$ is killed by the chosen
counterterm package at each obstruction step. Then there exists a
formal-power-series counterterm tower
$\{\Theta_n\}_{n \geq 1} \subset \Loc(L_X)$ such that
$S_{\mathrm{ren}} = S_{\mathrm{cl}} + \sum_{n \geq 1} \hbar^n \Theta_n$
satisfies the QME
\(\tfrac{1}{2}\{S_{\mathrm{ren}}, S_{\mathrm{ren}}\}_\BV - \hbar
\Delta_\BV S_{\mathrm{ren}} = 0\)
to all orders as a formal $\hbar$-series. The statement is conditional;
$d^{abc}_\frakg=0$ alone does not imply it.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Cycle~$1$ corrects the two-loop graph enumeration: the tadpole-free
connected trivalent vacuum graph has $V=2,E=3$ and is the theta graph;
tadpoles vanish by $f^{aab}=0$ when present; two-point self-energy
terms reduce to quadratic Casimir wave-function terms. Cycle~$2$
identifies the surviving anomaly gate as the Costello--Li quartic
slot $P_4$, not Whitehead $H^1(\frakg,\frakg)$ and not cubic
$d^{abc}$. Cycle~$3$ applies Costello~2011 Theorem~$5.0.1$: once the
order-$n+1$ obstruction class is zero, a counterterm extends the QME
one order. The hypothesis that $P_4$ is killed supplies exactly this
vanishing at the CY$_3$ anomaly gate.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Remaining obstructions.}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item \emph{Convergence vs asymptotic.} The proof gives formal
$\hbar$-power-series closure only under the quartic-anomaly hypothesis.
Convergence (not merely asymptotic), Borel summability, or uniqueness
of a nonperturbative sum do not follow from the QME recursion or from
spectral-gap decay.
\item \emph{Quartic anomaly.} The residual CY$_3$ obstruction is the
Costello--Li class in the $P_4$ slot. For $\fsl_{N\ge4}$ the primitive
quartic tensor gives a visible algebraic source; for other admissible
algebras the quartic invariant-polynomial projection must still be
checked in the actual local cohomology complex.
\item \emph{Other compact CY$_3$.} The identity
$\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(K3 \times E) = 0$ removes one BCOV Euler
coefficient. It does not prove quartic hCS anomaly cancellation on
arbitrary compact CY$_3$ and should not be promoted to a general
closure theorem.
\item \emph{Off-mass-shell vs on-shell.} The all-orders QME is the
off-mass-shell statement at the level of formal local functionals.
The on-mass-shell statement (factorisation algebra of quantum
observables, sense of Costello-Gwilliam~2017 FA Vol.~II) is implied
only after the QME closes, the propagator-renormalised path-integral
defines $\hbar$-corrected factorisation maps, and the relevant
completion/continuity data are supplied.
\end{enumerate}
