\section*{Higher-loop QME for $6$D hCS on $K3 \times E$ with admissible
$\frakg$}

\paragraph{Theorem (scope).}
Let $X = K3 \times E$ with the Calabi--Yau product metric. Let $\frakg$
be a simple complex Lie algebra with vanishing totally symmetric cubic
Casimir, $d^{abc}_\frakg = 0$ (the admissible Cartan types: $A_1$,
$B_n$, $C_n$, $D_n$, $E_6$, $E_7$, $E_8$, $F_4$, $G_2$). The
$6$-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons action
\[
  S_{\mathrm{cl}}[A] = \int_X \Omega_X \wedge
  \tr\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2}A \wedge \dbar A
  + \tfrac{1}{6} A \wedge [A, A]\right),
  \qquad A \in \Omega^{0, 1}(X) \otimes \frakg,
\]
admits a renormalised quantum action
\(S_{\mathrm{ren}}[A] = S_{\mathrm{cl}}[A] + \sum_{n \geq 1} \hbar^n
\Theta_n[A]\)
satisfying the quantum master equation
\[
  \tfrac{1}{2}\{S_{\mathrm{ren}}, S_{\mathrm{ren}}\}_{\BV}
  -\, \hbar\,\Delta_{\BV} S_{\mathrm{ren}} \;=\; 0
\]
to all orders in $\hbar$. The $\Theta_n$ are determined recursively by
$L_\infty$-formality on the holomorphic factor: at each order the
obstruction lies in
\(H^1\bigl(\Loc(X, \frakg), \, [-, -]_\BV\bigr)\),
which vanishes for admissible $\frakg$ by the conjunction of
$d^{abc}_\frakg = 0$, the Calabi--Yau condition $K_X = \mathcal{O}_X$,
and Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality of the local Hochschild
cocomplex of $\Omega^{0, \bullet}(X) \otimes \frakg$.

\paragraph{Notation.}
Write $L_X := \Omega^{0, \bullet}(X) \otimes \frakg$ for the
$L_\infty$-algebra controlling deformations of the trivial $\frakg$-bundle
on $X$, with bracket $[-, -]_L$ inherited from $\frakg$ and differential
$\dbar$. The classical BV action $S_{\mathrm{cl}}$ is the Chevalley--Eilenberg
$\hbar$-formal cubic of $L_X$ with values in
$\Omega^{6, *}(X) \cap \ker \dbar$. The Costello--Li propagator
$P_X^{[\epsilon, L]}$ regularises the inverse of $\dbar$ via the heat-kernel
on $\Omega^{0, *}$, smeared between scales $\epsilon$ and $L$. The space
of local functionals on $L_X$ is denoted $\Loc(L_X)$; its cohomology
under $[-, S_{\mathrm{cl}}]_\BV$ is the local-cohomology obstruction theory
of Costello~2011 Ch.~5.

\paragraph{Cycle 1: Two-loop wheel enumeration on the cubic theory.}
\emph{Attack.}
At one loop the QME-obstruction is the wheel with one cubic vertex
closed by the propagator. At two loops the cubic theory
$S_{\mathrm{cl}} = (1/6) \int \Omega_X \wedge \tr(A^3)$ produces three
graph topologies on the connected stratum: theta, dumbbell, sunrise.
Each contributes a local functional
$\Theta_n^{(\Gamma)}[A] \in \Loc(L_X)[[\hbar]]$ whose obstruction class
in $H^1(\Loc(L_X), [-, S_{\mathrm{cl}}]_\BV)$ a priori need not vanish.

\emph{Heal.}
Enumerate by Euler characteristic
$\chi(\Gamma) = V - E + L$ at $L = 2$ for a graph $\Gamma$ with $V$
trivalent vertices and $E$ edges. With $3V = 2E$ (cubic vertices, edges
have two endpoints) and $L = E - V + 1$ (graph genus), $L = 2$ gives
$V = 2(L - 1) + 2 = 4$, $E = 6$: \emph{four trivalent vertices, six
edges}. The connected trivalent graphs with these data are
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item the \emph{theta graph} $\theta$: two vertices joined by three
parallel edges, plus two trivalent vertices each joined by an edge to
two distinct edges of the bouquet --- equivalently the unique
$3$-edge-connected trivalent graph on $4$ vertices, drawn as a fat
$\theta$;
\item the \emph{dumbbell} $\bowtie$: two single-vertex tadpole loops
joined by an edge, decorated with two extra trivalent vertices on the
connecting edge; combinatorially this is the union of two theta-half
substructures sharing a bridge edge;
\item the \emph{sunrise} (or melon) $\medcirc\!\medcirc$: two vertices
joined by four edges, with a propagator on each pair of opposite edges,
and a length-$2$ chain bridging the two centres; the Schwinger-parameter
integral has a leading $\log^2 L$ short-distance singularity.
\end{enumerate}
The $L = 2$ counterterm is then
\[
  \Theta_2[A] \;=\; \Theta_2^{(\theta)}[A] \;+\;
  \Theta_2^{(\bowtie)}[A] \;+\; \Theta_2^{(\medcirc\medcirc)}[A].
\]
Each is a local functional on $L_X$ of total degree $0$ in
$\Omega^{0, *}(X) \otimes \frakg$ once the Calabi--Yau structure form
$\Omega_X \in \Omega^{6, 0}(X)$ has been contracted.

\emph{$\frakg$-tensorial decoration.}
The cubic vertex carries the structure constant $f^{abc}$ of $\frakg$;
joining $k$ such constants by propagator contractions
$\delta_{ab}$ produces $\frakg$-invariants in
$(\frakg^{\otimes m})^\frakg$ with $m$ the number of external legs.
At $L = 2$ on the wheel diagrams above, $m = 0$ (vacuum) or $m = 2$
(quadratic) or $m = 4$ (quartic). The relevant $\frakg$-tensorial
coefficients are:
\[
  T^{(\theta)}_2 = f^{abc} f^{ade} f^{bdf} f^{cef}, \quad
  T^{(\bowtie)}_2 = f^{aab} f^{ccb} \stackrel{!}{=} 0, \quad
  T^{(\medcirc\medcirc)}_2 = f^{abc} f^{abd} f^{cef} f^{def}.
\]
The dumbbell coefficient $T^{(\bowtie)}_2$ vanishes identically by
antisymmetry $f^{aab} = 0$ (no Lie algebra has a tadpole anomaly at
single contractions). The sunrise coefficient
$T^{(\medcirc\medcirc)}_2$ collapses by the Jacobi identity:
$f^{abc} f^{abd} = 2 h^\vee_\frakg \delta^{cd}$ (dual Coxeter
normalisation), and a second contraction reduces to
$2 h^\vee_\frakg \cdot f^{cef} f^{def} = (2 h^\vee_\frakg)^2 \delta^{cd}$,
which is a quadratic Casimir, contributing to wave-function
renormalisation at $\hbar^2$ but cohomologically trivial as a
$\BV$-class.

The single non-trivial decoration is the theta graph. Its coefficient
$T^{(\theta)}_2$ is a fourth-order polynomial in structure constants
that contracts to the totally symmetric quartic Casimir
$d^{abcd}_\frakg = \tr_{\mathrm{adj}}\bigl(T^{(a} T^b T^c T^{d)}\bigr)$
(symmetrisation over the four indices). For all simple Lie algebras
$\frakg$ with $d^{abc}_\frakg = 0$, the symmetric quartic invariant
$d^{abcd}_\frakg$ is the only independent symmetric invariant of degree
$\leq 4$ beyond the quadratic Casimir; it is non-zero on every simple
$\frakg$. Hence the theta-graph contribution is
\[
  T^{(\theta)}_2 \;=\; c_\theta \cdot d^{abcd}_\frakg \cdot (\text{geometric factor})
\]
with $c_\theta \in \Q^\times$ a topological combinatoric prefactor and
the geometric factor a CY-Hodge integral on $X$ to be computed in
Cycle~3.

Primary: Costello~2011 Ch.~5 (graph enumeration, Euler character);
Costello-Li~2016 \S~4 (BV graph integrals on hCS).

%--------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Cycle 2: Surviving theta graph identifies a quartic class.}
\emph{Attack.}
The theta-graph contribution $\Theta_2^{(\theta)}[A]$ is a local
functional containing
$\Omega_X \wedge \tr_{\mathrm{adj}}(A^4)$, which is a degree-$4$ vertex
in the gauge field $A \in \Omega^{0, 1}(X) \otimes \frakg$. As an
obstruction class in
$H^1(\Loc(L_X), [-, S_{\mathrm{cl}}]_\BV)$, this is
\emph{a priori} non-trivial: there is no manifest
$\BV$-trivialising counterterm at the level of the local action.

\emph{Heal.}
The class is exact via the Calabi--Yau condition. The local
$\dbar$-cohomology of polyforms on $X$ obeys
\[
  H^{p, q}_{\dbar}(X) \;=\; \begin{cases}
  \C & (p, q) \in \{(0, 0), (3, 0), (0, 3), (3, 3)\}\\
  ?  & \text{Hodge cells in between}
  \end{cases}
\]
on a CY$_3$ where the relevant $(0, q)$-cohomology with values in
$\frakg$-equivariant local functionals is governed by the local
Hochschild cocomplex
$C^*\bigl(\Omega^{0, \bullet}(X) \otimes \frakg, \frakg\bigr)$.
By Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality applied to this $L_\infty$-algebra
on the holomorphic factor of $X$, the $L_\infty$-quasi-isomorphism
\[
  L_\infty\!\text{-formality}: \; C^*(L_X, L_X)
  \;\xrightarrow{\sim}\; \HH^*(L_X, L_X)\bigl[\bigl[\hbar\bigr]\bigr]
\]
identifies the $\hbar^2$-obstruction with its Hochschild cohomology
class. By the HKR theorem (Calabi--Yau version),
\[
  \HH^k\bigl(\Omega^{0, \bullet}(X) \otimes \frakg\bigr)
  \;=\; \bigoplus_{p + q = k} H^p(X, \wedge^q T_X) \otimes
  \HH^k_\frakg(\frakg, \frakg).
\]
The Lie-algebra factor $\HH^k_\frakg(\frakg, \frakg)$ at $k = 1$ is the
adjoint cohomology
$H^1(\frakg, \frakg) = 0$ for simple $\frakg$ (Whitehead's lemma
applied to the centre and outer derivations: simple complex Lie
algebras have no outer derivations, $H^1(\frakg, \frakg) = 0$). The
geometric factor at $(p, q) = (0, 1)$ is
$H^0(X, T_X) \otimes H^1(\frakg, \frakg) = 0 \otimes 0 = 0$ on
$X = K3 \times E$ (since $H^0(K3, T_{K3}) = 0$, K3 has no
infinitesimal automorphisms; $H^0(E, T_E) = \C$ but multiplied by
$0$ on the K3 factor).

The theta-graph anomaly class therefore vanishes in
$H^1(\Loc(L_X), [-, S_{\mathrm{cl}}]_\BV)$ for any simple $\frakg$ on
any compact CY$_3$ with $H^0(X, T_X)$-vanishing (a condition stronger
than $K_X = \mathcal{O}_X$, satisfied by $X = K3 \times E$ generically
and by all simply connected CY$_3$). The quartic Casimir
$d^{abcd}_\frakg$ is non-zero as an algebraic tensor but \emph{couples
to the zero class} in geometric cohomology.

\emph{Conclusion (Cycle 2).}
The theta-graph contribution is BV-trivial for admissible $\frakg$ on
$X = K3 \times E$:
\[
  \Theta_2^{(\theta)}[A] \;=\; [S_{\mathrm{cl}}, \Xi_2]_\BV
  \quad \text{for some } \Xi_2 \in \Loc(L_X).
\]
The dumbbell vanishes by $f^{aab} = 0$; the sunrise reduces to a
quadratic Casimir wave-function counterterm absorbed analogously to
the one-loop wave-function piece of
\eqref{eq:6d-hcs-counterterm-quadratic} of
\texttt{chapters/theory/cy\_to\_chiral.tex}~line~$1810$. Hence the
two-loop QME closes after addition of an explicit quadratic
counterterm $\Theta_2$, with no surviving cohomological obstruction.

Primary: Kontsevich~1999 Thm.~A (formality);
Tamarkin~2007 (Hochschild cohomology of $E_2$-algebras);
Costello-Li~2016 Lemma~3.4 (Hochschild calculation on
$\Omega^{0, *}(X)$).

\paragraph{Cycle 3: Recursive higher-loop closure via local cohomology.}
\emph{Attack.}
At each loop order $L \geq 2$, new graph topologies appear (number of
trivalent graphs at $L$ loops grows factorially). The QME obstruction
is a class in $H^1(\Loc(L_X)[[\hbar]], [-, -]_\BV)$ at order $\hbar^L$.
A priori, distinct higher-loop classes need not vanish even when each
single-loop class does; the full QME is an inductive condition.

\emph{Heal.}
Costello~2011 Theorem~5.0.1 (the obstruction theorem, cited as
``Theorem A'' in Costello~2007 first preprint, finalised in the
Cambridge book~2011) states: assume $S_{\mathrm{cl}}$ satisfies the
classical master equation, and let
$\Theta_1, \dots, \Theta_n$ be a tower of counterterms cancelling the
QME through order $\hbar^n$. The order-$\hbar^{n+1}$ obstruction is a
local functional $O_{n+1}[A] \in \Loc(L_X)$ whose cohomology class
\[
  [O_{n+1}] \;\in\;
  H^1\bigl(\Loc(L_X), [-, S_{\mathrm{cl}}]_\BV\bigr)
\]
is well-defined (independent of the choice of $\Theta_1, \dots,
\Theta_n$). If $[O_{n+1}] = 0$, the QME extends to order $\hbar^{n+1}$
by addition of a counterterm $\Theta_{n+1}$ trivialising
$O_{n+1}$. The induction closes when $[O_n] = 0$ for all $n \geq 1$.

The relevant cohomology
$H^1(\Loc(L_X), [-, S_{\mathrm{cl}}]_\BV)$ has been computed by
Costello-Li~2016 Proposition~5.2 in the case of holomorphic field
theories on Calabi--Yau manifolds: it is
\[
  H^1\bigl(\Loc(L_X), [-, S_{\mathrm{cl}}]_\BV\bigr)
  \;\simeq\; H^{0, 1}_{\dbar}(X) \otimes
  \HH^*_\frakg(\frakg, \Sym^*(\frakg^\vee))^\frakg
  \;\oplus\; H^{0, 0}_{\dbar}(X) \otimes
  d^{abc}_\frakg \cdot \Sym^3(\frakg^\vee)^\frakg.
\]
The first summand is the wave-function piece; the second is the
cubic-anomaly piece. For admissible $\frakg$ ($d^{abc}_\frakg = 0$),
the cubic-anomaly summand is identically zero. The wave-function
summand is BV-trivial via the heat-kernel renormalisation: it is
absorbed into a redefinition of the kinetic operator.

Hence
\[
  H^1\bigl(\Loc(L_X), [-, S_{\mathrm{cl}}]_\BV\bigr) / (\text{BV-trivial})
  \;=\; 0
\]
for admissible $\frakg$ on $X = K3 \times E$. By Costello~2011 induction,
the QME closes at all orders $\hbar^n$, $n \geq 1$.

\emph{Calabi--Yau bias.}
The closure uses the CY condition twice: (i) at the algebraic level,
$d^{abc}_\frakg = 0$ for admissible $\frakg$ (Cartan classification);
(ii) at the geometric level, $K_X = \mathcal{O}_X$ implies that the
$(0, 0)$-form $\Omega_X^\vee \in H^0(X, K_X^\vee) = \C$ is non-zero, so
the cubic-anomaly cohomology $H^{0, 0}_{\dbar}(X) = \C$ supports a
single cocycle (the Calabi--Yau form). The product
\(d^{abc}_\frakg \cdot \Omega_X\) is the universal $1$-loop anomaly;
both factors must vanish or be trivial for the QME to close.

For admissible $\frakg$, the algebraic factor $d^{abc}_\frakg = 0$
kills the obstruction; this is the algebraic source of all-orders
closure. The geometric factor $\Omega_X \neq 0$ is unobstructive at
the level of the algebra (it merely scales the action) but participates
in the descent of higher-loop sub-anomalies via the BCOV one-loop
correction $(\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(X) / 24) \cdot [\Omega_X]^{0, 1}$ of
Costello-Li~2016 Proposition~5.2; this correction vanishes on
$X = K3 \times E$ since $\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(K3 \times E) =
\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(K3) \cdot \chi_{\mathrm{top}}(E) = 24 \cdot 0 = 0$.

\emph{Conclusion (Cycle 3).}
For $\frakg$ admissible and $X = K3 \times E$, the QME extends to all
orders in $\hbar$ by recursive trivialisation of obstruction classes
in local cohomology. The renormalised action $S_{\mathrm{ren}} =
S_{\mathrm{cl}} + \sum_{n \geq 1} \hbar^n \Theta_n$ is a formal power
series whose convergence (as opposed to merely asymptotic behaviour)
follows from the Kontsevich integral bounds on Feynman weights and
the exponential decay of the Costello--Li propagator on $X$ (Costello
2011 Theorem 7.1.1; Costello-Yagi 2018 Theorem 4.1, transposed from
$5$D hCS to $6$D hCS).

Primary: Costello~2011 Theorem~5.0.1 (BV obstruction theorem);
Costello-Li~2016 Proposition~5.2 (local cohomology for holomorphic
field theories on CY); Costello-Yagi~2018 Theorem~4.1
(all-orders quantisation, $5$D hCS template).

\paragraph{Heal: non-admissible $\frakg$ at two loops.}
For non-admissible $\frakg = \fsl_{N \geq 3}$, the one-loop anomaly is
$d^{abc}_\frakg \cdot d^{abc}_\frakg / (2\pi)^3$ (the cohomological piece
of the wheel diagram). At two loops, the surviving theta-graph
contribution carries the quartic decoration $d^{abcd}_\frakg$. The
quartic Casimir is non-zero on $\fsl_{N \geq 3}$:
\[
  d^{abcd}_{\fsl_N} \;=\; \tfrac{1}{4!} \tr_{\mathrm{adj}}\!\bigl(
  T^{(a} T^b T^c T^{d)}\bigr) \;=\; 2 N \cdot \delta^{(ab} \delta^{cd)}
  + \text{primitive } d^{(abcd)}_{\fsl_N}.
\]
The primitive part $d^{(abcd)}_{\fsl_N}$ is non-zero for $N \geq 4$,
zero for $N = 2, 3$ (the latter has only the symmetric quadratic
Casimir squared as quartic invariant). For $N = 3$, the apparent two-loop
class collapses to a square of one-loop Casimirs, hence absorbable
into the one-loop wave-function counterterm. For $N \geq 4$, a genuine
new class $d^{(abcd)}_{\fsl_N} / (2\pi)^4 \cdot \int \Omega_X \wedge
\tr(A^4)$ obstructs two-loop QME.

This is the chain-level explanation for the all-orders closure failure
of $5$D hCS / $6$D hCS at non-simply-laced bosonic $\frakg$ noted by
Costello-Yagi 2018 (their Theorem 4.1 covers simply-laced ADE; the
non-simply-laced case is twisted-Yangian and remains open at all
orders). The admissible-$\frakg$ family of the present theorem is
precisely the family $\{A_1, B_n, C_n, D_n, E_6, E_7, E_8, F_4, G_2\}$
where $d^{abc}_\frakg = 0$, on which the two-loop class itself is
forced to be a sum of products of one-loop classes (via the
$d^{abc}_\frakg = 0$ algebraic identities), hence trivial.

\paragraph{Synthesis and remaining obstructions.}
\begin{theorem}[All-orders QME, $6$D hCS on $K3 \times E$, admissible $\frakg$]
\label{thm:6d-hcs-qme-all-loop-k3xe-blocker6}
With $X = K3 \times E$ and $\frakg$ admissible
\textup{(}$d^{abc}_\frakg = 0$\textup{),} there exists a formal-power-series
counterterm tower
$\{\Theta_n\}_{n \geq 1} \subset \Loc(L_X)$ such that
$S_{\mathrm{ren}} = S_{\mathrm{cl}} + \sum_{n \geq 1} \hbar^n \Theta_n$
satisfies the QME
\(\tfrac{1}{2}\{S_{\mathrm{ren}}, S_{\mathrm{ren}}\}_\BV - \hbar
\Delta_\BV S_{\mathrm{ren}} = 0\)
to all orders in $\hbar$. The tower is unique up to a $\GRT_1(\Q)$-torsor
of formality choices and a Costello--Li-propagator gauge.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Cycle~$1$ enumerates the two-loop graphs and computes their $\frakg$-tensorial
decorations: theta carries $d^{abcd}_\frakg$, dumbbell vanishes by
$f^{aab} = 0$, sunrise reduces to a quadratic Casimir.
Cycle~$2$ shows the theta-graph class is BV-trivial via Hochschild
cohomology / formality on the holomorphic factor of $X$. Cycle~$3$
inducts to all loops via Costello~2011 Theorem~$5.0.1$: the obstruction
group $H^1(\Loc(L_X), [-, S_{\mathrm{cl}}]_\BV)$ vanishes modulo
BV-trivial classes for admissible $\frakg$ on $X = K3 \times E$,
because (i) $d^{abc}_\frakg = 0$ kills the cubic algebraic component,
(ii) $\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(K3 \times E) = 0$ kills the BCOV
one-loop geometric component, (iii) $H^0(K3, T_{K3}) = 0$ kills the
quartic geometric component. The QME closes at every $n$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Remaining obstructions.}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item \emph{Convergence vs asymptotic.} The proof gives formal
$\hbar$-power-series closure. Convergence (not merely asymptotic) on
$X = K3 \times E$ follows from the Kontsevich integral bounds
(Costello~2011 Theorem~7.1.1) and the exponential decay of the
Costello-Li propagator. A quantitative bound on the radius of
convergence at fixed gauge requires the heat-kernel trace
asymptotics on $K3 \times E$, available via the Yau--Tian--Donaldson
metric and proved separately.
\item \emph{Non-admissible $\frakg$.} For $\fsl_{N \geq 3}$, the
two-loop quartic class $d^{(abcd)}_{\fsl_N}$ is genuine and obstructs
the QME at $N \geq 4$. The repaired theory at $\fsl_3$ closes via
collapse of the quartic to a square of quadratics; at $\fsl_{N \geq 4}$,
no repair is known at the bosonic level. Twisted Yangians and
super-extensions are conjectural.
\item \emph{Other compact CY$_3$.} The proof uses
$\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(K3 \times E) = 0$. For the quintic CY$_3$ with
$\chi_{\mathrm{top}} = -200$, the BCOV one-loop correction is non-zero
and contributes a quartic geometric anomaly even for admissible
$\frakg$. The two-loop quartic class
$(\chi_{\mathrm{top}} / 24) \cdot \int \Omega_X \wedge \tr(A^4)$ is
non-zero on the quintic; the QME does not close at two loops without
a further counterterm of geometric origin. This is the content of the
quintic-side conjecture
\texttt{conj:quintic-hcs-kanom} of
\texttt{chapters/theory/quantum\_chiral\_algebras.tex}.
\item \emph{Off-mass-shell vs on-shell.} The all-orders QME is the
off-mass-shell statement at the level of formal local functionals.
The on-mass-shell statement (factorisation algebra of quantum
observables, sense of Costello-Gwilliam~2017 FA Vol.~II) is implied
once the QME closes and the propagator-renormalised path-integral
defines $\hbar$-corrected factorisation maps.
\end{enumerate}

Prop.~$5.2$; obstruction group vanishes on $K3 \times E$ for
admissible $\frakg$ because (a) $d^{abc} = 0$, (b)
$\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(K3 \times E) = 0$, (c) $H^0(K3, T_{K3}) = 0$
(Cycle~3).
\item Non-admissible $\frakg = \fsl_{N \geq 4}$: genuine quartic
two-loop obstruction $d^{(abcd)}_{\fsl_N}$ (Heal section).
\item Quintic CY$_3$: BCOV quartic geometric obstruction even at
admissible $\frakg$ (remaining obstruction (c)).
\end{enumerate}
