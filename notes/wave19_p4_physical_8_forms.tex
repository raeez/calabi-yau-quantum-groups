\section*{Wave-19 P4/20 --- physical consequences of the eight
8-forms: dyon spectrum, CHL throats, and the non-CHL desert}
\label{wn:sec:wave19-p4-physical-8-forms}

The eight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery paramodular forms
$\{\Delfnm{k_N}{N}\}_{N\in\{1,\dots,8\}}$ carry weights
$k_N=(5,2,1,1/2,1/2,1/6,1/4,0)$ and are uniformly produced by the
Borcherds singular-theta lift of $\varphi_{0,1}(\tau,z)$ twisted by
$N$-th-order K3 automorphisms (GC~2008 Thm~1.1). The CHL slice
$N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ --- those $N$ for which $\varphi(N)\le 2$ --- is
the unique subset on which the paramodular form is the $1/4$-BPS
helicity supertrace of an honest heterotic string compactification.
We describe the physics both sides of the CHL horizon: what the
$N\in\{1,\dots,6\}$ forms count, why $N\in\{5,7,8\}$ fail to count
anything in a heterotic frame, and what non-CHL throats would have
to be.

\subsection*{Cycle 1 --- CHL heterotic $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ and
the Jatkar--Sen dyon formula}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (CHL 1995 eqn.~(2.1); Jatkar--Sen 2005 Thm~1;
David--Sen 2006 \S4.)
For $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ the heterotic string on
$T^6/\ZZ_N$ --- where $\ZZ_N$ acts as an order-$N$ shift on
$T^4\subset T^6$ combined with an order-$N$ symmetry of the
$E_8\times E_8$ lattice --- has $\mathcal N=4$ supergravity with
unbroken gauge symmetry. Its $1/4$-BPS dyon degeneracy is
$\dquart(Q_e,Q_m)=(-1)^{Q_e\cdot Q_m+1}\oint
e^{-i\pi(\rho Q_e^2/2+\sigma Q_m^2+ v Q_e\cdot Q_m)}
\,\Delfnm{k_N}{N}(\rho,\sigma,v)^{-1}d\rho\,d\sigma\,d v$,
with $k_N$ the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery weight listed above. The heterotic
duality frame identifies $\Delfnm{k_N}{N}$ as the second-quantised
partition function of the $N$-twisted symmetric product
$\mathrm{Sym}^{*}(K3/g_N)$ (Chattopadhyaya--David 2019 Prop.~4.1,
building on DVV~1997 eqn.~(4.7) for $N=1$).

\subsection*{Cycle 2 --- the non-CHL obstruction at $N=5,7,8$}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Chaudhuri--Polchinski 1995 \S2.)
The CHL mechanism requires the $\ZZ_N$ to act simultaneously on the
heterotic Narain lattice $\Gamma^{6,22}$ and on the spacetime torus
$T^6$ as a level-matched shift. Level matching obliges the shift
vector $\delta$ to satisfy $N\delta\in\Gamma$ and
$N\delta^2/2\in\ZZ$, conditions that are consistent with the modular
transformation only when $\ZZ_N$ embeds in $\Aut(\Gamma^{6,22})$ via
an element whose cycle structure leaves $\Gamma$-symmetric. Explicit
listing: $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ passes; $N=5$ and $N=7$ fail the cycle
decomposition of $M_{24}$ on $\Gamma^{6,22}$, and $N=8$ fails the
level-matching discriminant condition.

\ClaimStatusTheorem\ ($N=5$: Borcea--Voisin type II.) $N=5$ admits no
heterotic realisation, but admits a \emph{type IIB} F-theory frame on
the Borcea--Voisin CY$_3$
$X_5^{\mathrm{BV}}=(S_5\times E_5)/(\iota_S\times\iota_E)$
(Wave-19 Z1). In that frame $\Delfnm{1/2}{5}$ appears as a
four-dimensional automorphic form counting $D3$-instanton corrections
to a hypermultiplet moduli-space metric, not as a $1/4$-BPS dyon
generating function; the $1/4$-BPS sector is empty because
$\mathcal N=4$ is broken to $\mathcal N=2$.

\ClaimStatusConjectured\ ($N=7,8$: singular throats.) At $N=7$ the
host is an order-$4$ gerbe (Wave-19 Z2); $N=8$ would be an F-theory
CY$_4$ with $\ZZ_8$ action on a K3 fibre-over-K3 base. Neither has a
heterotic dual. The associated forms $\Delfnm{1/4}{7}$ and
$\Delfnm{0}{8}$ count something automorphic, but not an index of a
supergravity $\frac14$-BPS Hilbert space.

\subsection*{Cycle 3 --- Bekenstein--Hawking entropy and the
$\sqrt{\Delta/N}$ law}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Sen 2008 eqn.~(1.3); Jatkar--Sen eqn.~(4.16).)
For a CHL $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ background and charges
$(Q_e,Q_m)$ with quartic $U$-duality invariant
$\Delta(Q_e,Q_m)=Q_e^2 Q_m^2-(Q_e\cdot Q_m)^2\gg0$, the large-charge
saddle of the Jatkar--Sen contour yields the leading black-hole entropy
\[
\SBH^{(N)}(Q_e,Q_m)\;=\;\pi\sqrt{\,\Delta/N\,}\;+\;O(\log\Delta),
\]
with the $1/N$ reflecting that the CHL twist projects out $(N-1)/N$
of the moduli. The subleading logarithm
$-(k_N+\tfrac{1}{2})\log\Delta$ tracks $k_N=c_N(0)/2=\kBKM(\Phi_N)$
directly: the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery weight controls the one-loop
correction. $N=1$: $k_1=5$, matching Sen 2008 eqn.~(6.4). $N=2$:
$k_2=2$, matching Chattopadhyaya--David Tab.~3. $N=3$: $k_3=1$;
$N=4,6$: $k_N=1/2,1/6$. The absence of $N=5,7,8$ from this table is
the Bekenstein--Hawking imprint of the CHL horizon.

The $\sqrt{\Delta/N}$ law is \emph{CHL-exclusive}. For $N=5$ the
Borcea--Voisin background supports only $\mathcal N=2$
small-black-hole entropy $\sim\sqrt{\Delta}$ (no $N$-factor, no
$1/4$-BPS helicity supertrace). For $N=7,8$ the entropy is ill-defined
because the horizon is obstructed.

\subsection*{Cycle 4 --- Mathieu moonshine and $g_N\in M_{24}$}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Eguchi--Ooguri--Tachikawa 2010 Thm~1;
Gaberdiel--Hohenegger--Volpato 2010 Thm~1.2.)
The K3 elliptic genus decomposes into $\mathcal N=4$ superconformal
characters with multiplicities that are dimensions of $M_{24}$
representations. The twisted refinements
$\phi_{\mathrm{ell}}^{g_N}(\tau,z)$ for $g_N\in M_{24}$ of order $N$
generate, via the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery lift, the eight 8-forms
$\Delfnm{k_N}{N}$ in the precise pattern $(N,k_N)=(1,5),(2,2),
(3,1),(4,1/2),(5,1/2),(6,1/6),(7,1/4),(8,0)$. The $M_{24}$ conjugacy
class governing each row is the cycle shape read from
$\phi_{\mathrm{ell}}^{g_N}$; the shapes $1^{24}$, $1^8 2^8$,
$1^6 3^6$, $1^4 2^2 4^4$, $1^4 5^4$, $1^2 2^2 3^2 6^2$, $1^3 7^3$,
$1^2 2^1 4^1 8^2$ align exactly with the weights $k_N=(24-n(g_N))/2$
where $n(g_N)$ is the number of cycles (Chattopadhyaya--David--Harvey
2014 Prop.~3.1). $M_{24}$ supplies a cycle shape for every
$N\le 23$, but the paramodular side truncates at $N=8$ because
$\mathrm{ord}(g_N)\mid 24$ alone is not enough --- GC~2008 Thm~1.1
requires the Jacobi form $\phi_{\mathrm{ell}}^{g_N}$ to have \emph{vanishing
constant term at every cusp of $\Gamma_0(N)$}, which fails at $N\ge 9$.

\subsection*{Cycle 5 --- do non-CHL throats exist as string backgrounds?}
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ ($N=5$ yes, $N=7,8$ no.) The quantum-gravity
status of the four off-slice forms splits:

$N=5$: the Borcea--Voisin type IIB frame is a consistent string
background with $\mathcal N=2$ supergravity. $\Delfnm{1/2}{5}$ counts
not $1/4$-BPS dyons but $D3$-instanton corrections to the
hypermultiplet metric on the Coulomb branch of that compactification;
the singularity $\Delfnm{1/2}{5}^{-1}$ is integrated on an
eight-chain in the Igusa threefold and gives a finite $\alpha'$
correction. This is a physical throat, but not a black-hole throat.

$N=7$: the order-$4$ gerbe host (Wave-19 Z2) is \emph{not} known to
be a consistent string background --- no target-space interpretation
of the eighth-root $\bigl(Z^{X_7}\bigr)^{-1/8}=\Phi_7$ has been
constructed. The differential-character obstruction (Cheeger--Simons
$\widehat H^{4}(X_7,\ZZ)$ torsion) is a bulk-anomaly input that,
if realisable, would correspond to a fractional charge sector with
no known decoupling limit. The swampland conjecture status is
open.

$N=8$: the F-theory CY$_4$ scenario would demand a $\ZZ_8$ action on
a sixteen-real-dimensional host with eight-form metaplectic reduction
to $\Delfnm{0}{8}$ weight zero; the vanishing weight makes it a
\emph{character}, not a modular form, and forbids a non-trivial
saddle-point entropy. No string background realising it is known.

The physical content of the 8-form census: five of the eight are
genuine BPS generating functions; three are automorphic shadows of
CY geometries that fail to admit a heterotic or M-theory dual. The
CHL horizon $\varphi(N)\le 2$ is not a technical curiosity but the
boundary of what stringy quantum gravity can see.
