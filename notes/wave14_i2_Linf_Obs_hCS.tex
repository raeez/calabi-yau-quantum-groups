\section*{$L_\infty$ Observables for Holomorphic Chern--Simons Theory}

\paragraph{BV field complex.}
Let \(X\) be a Calabi--Yau threefold with holomorphic volume form
\(\Omega_X\).  Let \(\rho:\fg\to\End(V)\) be a finite-dimensional
representation of a complex Lie algebra and set
\[
  \langle x,y\rangle_\rho=\Tr_V(\rho(x)\rho(y)).
\]
The pairing is assumed invariant and non-degenerate on the quotient of
\(\fg\) used by the theory.  For an open set \(U\subset X\) the compactly
supported hCS BV fields form the shifted Dolbeault dg Lie algebra
\[
  \cL_{\hCS,c}(U)
  =
  \Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(U,\fg)[1],
  \qquad
  \ell_1=\dbar,
\]
\[
  \ell_2(\alpha\otimes x,\beta\otimes y)
  =
  (-1)^{|x||\beta|}
  (\alpha\wedge\beta)\otimes[x,y]_\fg,
  \qquad
  \ell_n=0\quad(n\geq3).
\]
The trace pairing and \(\Omega_X\) give the degree \(-1\) BV form
\[
  \omega_{\BV,U}(a,b)
  =
  \int_U\Omega_X\wedge\langle a,b\rangle_\rho .
\]
The classical action is
\[
  S_{\hCS}(a)
  =
  \int_U\Omega_X\wedge
  \left(
    \frac12\langle a,\dbar a\rangle_\rho
    +\frac16\langle a,[a,a]\rangle_\rho
  \right).
\]
The classical master equation follows from \(\dbar^2=0\), the Jacobi
identity, and invariance of \(\langle-,-\rangle_\rho\).  Its
Maurer--Cartan equation is
\[
  \dbar\cA+\frac12[\cA,\cA]=0,
\]
the integrability equation for the holomorphic structure determined by
the \((0,1)\)-field \(\cA\).

\paragraph{Local formal and compact observables.}
The formal local theory at \(x\in X\) uses the completed Dolbeault
fields on the formal polydisk \(D_x\):
\[
  \cL_{\hCS,x}^{\mathrm{for}}
  =
  \Omega^{0,\bullet}_{\mathrm{for}}(D_x,\fg)[1],
  \qquad
  \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS,x}
  =
  C^\bullet_{\CE,\mathrm{cont}}
  \bigl(\cL_{\hCS,x}^{\mathrm{for}}\bigr).
\]
Compact observables on \(U\) are the continuous CE cochains
\[
  \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS,c}(U)
  =
  C^\bullet_{\CE,\mathrm{cont}}\bigl(\cL_{\hCS,c}(U)\bigr)
  =
  \widehat{\Sym}
  \bigl(\cL_{\hCS,c}(U)^\vee[-1]\bigr),
\]
with differential
\[
  d^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\Obs}=d_{\dbar}+d_{\ell_2}.
\]
Here \(d_{\dbar}\) is the dual coderivation induced by \(\dbar\), and
\(d_{\ell_2}\) is the quadratic coderivation induced by the Lie bracket.
For pairwise disjoint opens \(U_i\subset U\), extension by zero gives
the factorisation product
\[
  \bigotimes_i\Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS,c}(U_i)
  \longrightarrow
  \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS,c}(U).
\]
On compact \(X\), global observables use the completed Fréchet complex
\(\Omega^{0,\bullet}(X,\fg)[1]\) and the same trace pairing integrated
over \(X\).  Local formal observables, compactly supported
factorisation observables, and compact global observables are three
different completions of the same local dg Lie input.

\paragraph{Quantum observables.}
A Costello quantisation datum consists of an elliptic gauge fixing,
heat kernels \(K_t\), regularised propagators \(P_{\varepsilon,L}\), a
completed local-functional topology, counterterms, and effective
interactions \(I[L]\).  At scale \(L\) the quantum observables are
\[
  \Obs^q_{\hCS,L}(U)
  =
  \left(
    \Obs^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\hCS,c}(U)[[\hbar]],
    d^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\Obs}+\{I[L],-\}_L+\hbar\Delta_L
  \right),
\]
provided the scale-\(L\) quantum master equation holds:
\[
  Q_{\hCS}I[L]
  +\frac12\{I[L],I[L]\}_L
  +\hbar\Delta_LI[L]
  =
  0.
\]
Here \(Q_{\hCS}\) is the linear BV differential induced by \(\dbar\).
Compact or global quantum hCS requires the following additional data:
orientation and determinant-line framing for the BV pairing, an
anomaly-cancellation class in the Costello local functional complex,
counterterms trivialising the local obstruction class, descent or sewing
compatibility for factorisation products, gauge-fixing independence
under RG flow, and a nuclear completion supporting OPE and BV
operations.

\paragraph{Quartic anomaly.}
In complex dimension \(3\) the local gauge anomaly is controlled by the
symmetrised quartic invariant
\[
  I_4^\rho(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)
  =
  \frac{1}{4!}
  \sum_{\sigma\in S_4}
  \Tr_V\!\left(
    \rho(x_{\sigma(1)})\rho(x_{\sigma(2)})
    \rho(x_{\sigma(3)})\rho(x_{\sigma(4)})
  \right),
  \qquad
  I_4^\rho\in\Sym^4(\fg^\vee)^\fg .
\]
The curvature of a field is
\[
  F_\cA=\dbar\cA+\frac12[\cA,\cA],
\]
and the one-loop obstruction class has representative
\[
  \kanom(X,\fg,\rho)
  =
  \left[
    \hbar\int_X\Omega_X\wedge
    I_4^\rho(\cA,F_\cA,F_\cA,F_\cA)
  \right]
  \in
  H^1_{\mathrm{loc}}\bigl(\cL_{\hCS}(X),\cO_{\mathrm{loc}}\bigr).
\]
Here \(\cL_{\hCS}(X)=\Omega^{0,\bullet}(X,\fg)[1]\) in the compact
global topology.
The two-point invariant
\[
  C_2^\rho(x,y)=\Tr_V(\rho(x)\rho(y))
\]
enters wave-function renormalisation through degree-zero kinetic
counterterms such as
\[
  S_{\mathrm{ct}}^{(2)}
  =
  \hbar\,\lambda_{\varepsilon,L}
  \int_X\Omega_X\wedge\Tr_\rho(\cA\,\dbar\cA).
\]
The QME obstruction lives in degree \(1\) and is governed by
\(I_4^\rho\).  The kinetic rescaling and the anomaly-cancellation
condition are distinct requirements.

\paragraph{Affine minimal model.}
On \(X=\C^3\) with polynomial boundary conditions,
\[
  H^\bullet_{\dbar}(\C^3,\fg)
  =
  \C[z_1,z_2,z_3]\otimes\fg
\]
in Dolbeault degree \(0\).  Choose the standard Dolbeault contraction
\[
  \bigl(H^\bullet_{\dbar},0\bigr)
  \xrightarrow{i}
  \Omega^{0,\bullet}_{\mathrm{poly}}(\C^3,\fg)
  \xrightarrow{p}
  \bigl(H^\bullet_{\dbar},0\bigr),
  \qquad
  \dbar h+h\dbar=\mathrm{id}-ip .
\]
Homotopy transfer gives
\[
  \ell_1^{\min}=0,\qquad
  \ell_2^{\min}(f\otimes x,g\otimes y)=fg\otimes[x,y]_\fg .
\]
For \(n\geq3\), each transferred tree contains an internal edge labelled
by \(h\).  The bracket of holomorphic polynomial representatives is
again holomorphic, hence \(h\,\ell_2(iu,iv)=0\).  Thus
\[
  \ell_n^{\min}=0\quad(n\geq3)
\]
for the polynomial affine model.  With \(L^2\) boundary conditions on
\(\C^3\), the harmonic Dolbeault subspace is zero and the minimal model
is the zero dg Lie algebra.

\paragraph{Configuration formula.}
The Bochner--Martinelli kernel \(G(z,w)\) is the integral kernel of the
Dolbeault homotopy \(h\).  The ternary transferred bracket is
\[
  \ell_3^{\min}(x_1,x_2,x_3)
  =
  \frac12\,p\,\ell_2\bigl(h\,\ell_2(ix_1,ix_2),ix_3\bigr)
  +\hbox{cyclic terms}.
\]
For polynomial holomorphic inputs this expression is zero by the
previous paragraph.  Equivalently, the corresponding tree integral on
\(\overline{\FM}_3(\C^3)\) has zero harmonic projection.  Boundary
strata of \(\overline{\FM}_n(\C^3)\) encode the \(L_\infty\) relations
by Stokes' theorem; in the affine polynomial contraction the relations
reduce to the strict dg Lie identities for \(\ell_1\) and \(\ell_2\).

\paragraph{Compact threefolds.}
For compact \(X\), the affine contraction is replaced by a Hodge
decomposition and a global Green kernel.  Transferred brackets are sums
over trees whose internal edges carry this global propagator, and their
cohomology classes are governed by the Atiyah--Kapranov class of
\(T_X\).  For \(X=K3\times E\),
\[
  T_X=T_{K3}\oplus T_E,
  \qquad
  \mathrm{At}(T_E)=0,
\]
while the K3 factor contributes the nonzero Atiyah class.  The Hodge
Euler identity
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)
  =
  \chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)
  =
  2\cdot0
  =
  0
\]
is only the compact categorical Euler computation.  Vanishing of compact
transferred brackets, anomaly cancellation, and a BKM boundary
comparison are additional constructions.

\paragraph{Toric representation stack and positive half.}
The affine hCS Maurer--Cartan equation describes integrable Dolbeault
operators.  After the polynomial affine reduction and a choice of
\(N\)-dimensional vector space, representations of
\(\C[z_1,z_2,z_3]\) are triples
\[
  (X_1,X_2,X_3)\in\End(\C^N)^3,
  \qquad
  [X_i,X_j]=0.
\]
The critical cohomological Hall algebra of this derived commuting-triple
stack is the positive half
\[
  \mathrm{CoHA}(\C^3)\simeq\Yplus .
\]
The full \(\cW_{1+\infty}\) vertex algebra appears after Drinfeld
double, centre, completion, and evaluation/Fock-module operations.

\paragraph{Curve specialisation.}
For a holomorphic curve \(C\subset X\) with formal normal neighbourhood
\(N_C\), a quantum hCS factorisation algebra produces
\[
  \cA_C
  =
  \int_{N_C/C}\Obs^q_{\hCS}
  \in
  \mathrm{Alg}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Eone}(C).
\]
The chiral Chevalley--Eilenberg object is
\[
  C^\bullet_{\CE,\mathrm{ch}}(\cA_C)
  =
  \Gamma_{\Ran(C)}
  \left(
    \widehat{\Sym}(\cA_C^\vee[-1]),
    d_{\CE}^{\mathrm{ch}}
  \right),
\]
where \(d_{\CE}^{\mathrm{ch}}\) contains the ordinary CE differential
and OPE contractions on Ran diagonals.  In dimension \(d=3\), the
specialised \(\Phi_3\) output is native \(\Eone\)-chiral.  The
\(\Etwo\) layer is the centre, double, or module-category enhancement,
for example
\[
  Z\bigl(\Rep^{\Eone}(\cA_C)\bigr)
  \simeq
  \Rep^{\Etwo}\bigl(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA_C)\bigr)
\]
on loci where the chiral centre hypotheses hold.

\paragraph{\(K3\times E\) invariants.}
The compact total-space Hodge supertrace is
\[
  \kch(K3\times E)
  =
  \sum_{q=0}^{3}(-1)^q h^{0,q}(K3\times E)
  =
  (1-0+1)(1-1)
  =
  0.
\]
The categorical Euler invariant agrees:
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)=\chi(\cO_{K3\times E})=0.
\]
The Heisenberg specialisation has rank-additive value
\[
  \kHeis(K3\times E)=\kHeis(K3)+\kHeis(E)=2+1=3.
\]
The Borcherds weight attached to the Gritsenko denominator is
\[
  \kBKM(\Delta_5)=\frac{c_1(0)}{2}=\frac{10}{2}=5.
\]
The K3 fibre lattice invariant is
\[
  \kfiber(K3)=\mathrm{rk}\,H^\bullet(K3,\C)=24.
\]
These five formulas belong to distinct constructions: compact Hodge
supertrace, categorical Euler, Heisenberg rank, Borcherds automorphic
weight, and fibre Mukai rank.
