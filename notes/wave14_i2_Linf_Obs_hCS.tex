\section*{$L_\infty$-structure on $\Obs_{\hCS}(\C^3)$ beyond minimal model (Wave-14 I2/20)}

\paragraph{Object.}
Fix gauge algebra $\gfrak$ with invariant pairing $\langle-,-\rangle$.
The cochain-level $L_\infty$-algebra of 6D holomorphic Chern--Simons on
$X = \C^3$ is
\[
  \hfrak_{\hCS} \;=\; \Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3,\gfrak)[1],
  \qquad
  \ell_1 = \dbar,
  \quad
  \ell_2(\alpha,\beta) = [\alpha \wedge \beta]_\gfrak,
\]
with Maurer--Cartan equation $\dbar \cA + \tfrac12[\cA,\cA] = 0$ recovering
classical hCS solutions (Costello 2011 \S5; Costello--Gwilliam 2017 Vol.~I
Example~3.5.3). Question: are $\ell_{\geq 3}$ zero at chain level, zero on
the minimal model, or nonzero on either?

\paragraph{Cycle 1: Kontsevich--Soibelman minimal model, $\ell_3$ via trees.}
\emph{Attack.}
Homotopy transfer (Kontsevich--Soibelman 2000 \emph{Deformations of algebras
over operads}, \S6.4; Loday--Vallette 2012 Thm~10.3.5) produces a minimal
$L_\infty$-structure $(H^*(\hfrak_{\hCS}), \ell_n^{\min})$ on Dolbeault
cohomology from a Hodge decomposition
$\hfrak_{\hCS} = \ker\dbar \oplus \mathrm{im}\,\dbar \oplus \cH$ with
propagator $h$. The transferred $\ell_n^{\min}$ is the sum over planar
binary trees $T \in \mathrm{Tree}_n$ of
$p \circ (\bigotimes_{\text{int}} h\, \ell_2) \circ i^{\otimes n}$,
one $\ell_2$ per internal vertex, one propagator $h$ per internal edge,
inclusion $i: \cH \hookrightarrow \hfrak_{\hCS}$ and projection $p$ to
harmonics. For $n = 3$:
\[
  \ell_3^{\min}(x_1,x_2,x_3) \;=\;
  \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(
    p\,\ell_2(h\,\ell_2(x_1,x_2), x_3)
    \pm \text{(2 cyclic)}
  \bigr).
\]
\emph{Heal.}
On $X = \C^3$ with flat Dolbeault structure the harmonic subspace
$\cH \subset \Omega^{0,\bullet}_{L^2}(\C^3,\gfrak)$ is \emph{zero} for
$L^2$ boundary conditions (no $L^2$ harmonic $(0,q)$-forms on $\C^n$);
for polynomial boundary conditions $\cH = \C[z_1,z_2,z_3]\otimes\gfrak$
placed in degree $0$ of the shifted complex. The tree sum collapses:
the inner bracket $\ell_2(x_1,x_2) \in \Omega^{0,0}\otimes\gfrak$ lies in
$\cH$ already, so $h\,\ell_2(x_1,x_2) = 0$ and every binary tree with an
internal edge is killed. Therefore
$\ell_n^{\min} = 0$ for $n \geq 3$, and the minimal model is the
strict graded Lie algebra $(\C[z_1,z_2,z_3]\otimes\gfrak, [-,-])$.
This is the Kontsevich--Soibelman formality pattern for holomorphic
theories on affine varieties (Kapranov 2001 \emph{Rozansky--Witten
invariants via Atiyah classes}, Thm~2.6: the Atiyah class obstruction
to formality vanishes on affine Calabi--Yau for polynomial ideals).

\paragraph{Cycle 2: does the minimal model truncate, and where does obstruction live?}
\emph{Attack.}
Formality of $\hfrak_{\hCS}$ at chain level is a stronger claim: an
$L_\infty$-quasi-isomorphism $\hfrak_{\hCS} \simeq \cH$ with
$\ell_{\geq 3}^\infty \neq 0$ possibly on the chain side. The obstruction
to propagating the $\ell_2$-quasi-isomorphism $\cH \hookrightarrow
\hfrak_{\hCS}$ to a full $L_\infty$-map lies in
$H^2(\mathrm{Der}(\cH), \ell_1) = H^2(\cH, \ell_2^\vee) = 0$ for
polynomial coefficients on $\C^3$ (Harrison vanishing, smooth affine).
\emph{Heal.}
The obstruction class on a nontrivial CY$_3$ target $X$ is the
Atiyah--Kapranov class $\mathrm{At}(TX) \in H^1(X, \Omega^1_X \otimes
\mathrm{End}(TX))$, obstructing the existence of a global
$L_\infty$-splitting. For $X = \C^3$ this class vanishes ($TX$ trivial),
so $\hfrak_{\hCS}(\C^3)$ is \emph{formal}: $\ell_n^\infty = 0$ for
$n \geq 3$ on the minimal side and the chain-level $\ell_3$ is a pure
coboundary. For $X$ a projective CY$_3$ (quintic, $K3 \times E$, abelian)
$\mathrm{At}(TX) \neq 0$ and $\ell_3^{\min}$ is forced nonzero; for
$K3 \times E$ the class restricts to the Kuranishi cubic on the K3 factor
times the elliptic period on $E$, and $\kcat(K3 \times E) = 0$ controls
the global cancellation.

\paragraph{Cycle 3: $\ell_3$ as a 6D Drinfeld associator.}
\emph{Attack.}
Kontsevich 1997 \emph{Deformation quantisation of Poisson manifolds}
\S7 realises the formality $L_\infty$-quasi-isomorphism on
$\R^d$ via integrals over $\overline{\FM_n(\R^d)}$ of differential forms
pulled back from pairs of configuration points. The 2D Drinfeld
associator arises as the period of the Knizhnik--Zamolodchikov connection
on $\overline{\FM_4(\C)}$.
\emph{Heal.}
For 6D hCS the analogous integrand lives on
$\overline{\FM_n(\C^3)}$, the Fulton--MacPherson compactification
indexed by holomorphic (not real) blow-ups, with propagator the
Bochner--Martinelli kernel
$G(z,w) = \tfrac{2}{(2\pi i)^3}\sum_k
(-1)^{k-1}\overline{(z_k{-}w_k)}\|z{-}w\|^{-6}
\,\widehat{d\bar z_k}\wedge d^3w$
(Wave-13 H3). The $n=3$ tree integral
$\int_{\overline{\FM_3(\C^3)}} G_{12}\wedge G_{23}$ vanishes on
$X = \C^3$ by translation invariance plus dimension count: the
compactified moduli has real dimension $2 \cdot 6 = 12$ (three points
modulo $\C^3$ translation plus scale), while $G\wedge G$ is a
$(\bullet, 4)$-form of real degree $8$; the four-form gap is a
$\bar z$-deficit that no translation-invariant integrand can fill.
Hence the 6D ``Drinfeld associator on $\C^3$'' is trivial, recovering
formality of Cycle~2. A nontrivial 6D associator appears on compact CY$_3$
where translation invariance is broken; the Costello 2011 Thm~5.0.1
one-loop anomaly identifies it with the integrated second Chern class
$c_2(TX) \cup c_2(\gfrak)$, matching the Costello--Gaiotto 2018
(\emph{Twisted M-theory}, \S3) identification of 6D hCS with the
Yangian R-matrix on $X = \C^3$.

\paragraph{Cycle 4: Stasheff $\cK_n$ coherence at chain level.}
\emph{Attack.}
The $L_\infty$-relations $\sum_{i+j = n+1}
\sum_\sigma (\pm) \ell_j(\ell_i \otimes 1^{\otimes j-1})\sigma = 0$
are a stratification of the boundary of the Stasheff associahedron
$\cK_n$ (Loday--Vallette 2012 Ch.~9), equivalently of the real
Fulton--MacPherson $\overline{\FM_n(\R)}$.
\emph{Heal.}
The holomorphic analogue requires replacing $\cK_n$ by the complex
Fulton--MacPherson $\overline{\FM_n(\C^3)}$; the boundary divisors
enumerate collisions of $\geq 2$ points. The $L_\infty$-Jacobi at order
$n$ becomes Stokes' theorem for the Bochner--Martinelli $n$-gon integral
on $\overline{\FM_n(\C^3)}$ with boundary divisor corresponding to the
tree where a $k$-subset collides (Kapranov 2001 \S4). On $X = \C^3$ every
such boundary integral vanishes by the Cycle~3 dimension count, so the
Stasheff coherence is strict at chain level (formality re-proved via
boundary integrals).

\paragraph{Cycle 5: Maurer--Cartan locus, hCS solutions, and the
CoHA identification.}
\emph{Attack.}
$\MC(\hfrak_{\hCS}) = \{\cA \in \Omega^{0,1}(\C^3,\gfrak) :
\dbar \cA + \tfrac12 [\cA,\cA] = 0\}$ is the moduli of holomorphic
$\gfrak$-connections on $\C^3$, equivalently semistable
$\gfrak$-bundles with holomorphic structure.
\emph{Heal.}
Under the formality of Cycle~2, MC of $\hfrak_{\hCS}$ equals MC of the
minimal model $(\C[z_1,z_2,z_3]\otimes\gfrak, [-,-])$; the latter is
the moduli of commuting triples in $\gfrak$ (three commuting elements
$X_1, X_2, X_3$ from $z_i$-coefficients), whose deformation quantisation
is $Y^+(\gfrak) = \mathrm{CoHA}(\C^3_\gfrak)$, the positive half of
the affine Yangian (Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013, Costello--Gaiotto 2018).
The cache-level identity
$\mathrm{CoHA}(\C^3) = Y^+$ (positive half only, \emph{not}
$\cW_{1+\infty}$) is the $L_\infty$-MC shadow at $\gfrak = \mathfrak{gl}_1$.
The invariant on this side is $\kch(\mathbb{C}^3) = 1$
(Hodge supertrace $\sum_q (-1)^q h^{0,q}(\C^3) = 1$ for
polynomial-$L^2$ boundary); this is \emph{not} $\kcat$, since $\C^3$ is
non-compact and $\chi(\cO_{\C^3})$ diverges / is not defined in the
compact sense.

\paragraph{Synthesis.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ On $X = \C^3$ the $L_\infty$-algebra
$\hfrak_{\hCS}$ is formal: $\ell_n^{\min} = 0$ for $n \geq 3$, with
chain-level Stasheff coherence strict by Bochner--Martinelli
boundary-integral vanishing (Kapranov 2001). The MC locus is the
commuting-triple variety, quantising to $Y^+(\gfrak)$
(Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013). \ClaimStatusConjectured\ On compact CY$_3$
the Atiyah class $\mathrm{At}(TX)$ is the universal obstruction to
formality; $\ell_3^{\min}$ on $X = K3 \times E$ is the product of
the K3 Kuranishi cubic and the elliptic period, controlled by
$\kcat(K3 \times E) = 0$ (Cycle~2 heal).
