\section*{F2/20 -- Gaiotto curve for K3xE chiral BKM:
\texorpdfstring{$\Sigma_{0,24}$}{Sigma_{0,24}} not
\texorpdfstring{$\Sigma_{2,0}$}{Sigma_{2,0}}}
\label{sec:wave13-f2-gaiotto-curve-resolution}

\subsection*{Conflict.}

Wave-12 C5 (\texttt{wave12\_c5\_chiral\_BKM\_defect\_curve.tex}) asserts
$\Sigma = \Sigma_{0,24}$ (24-punctured sphere, max-regular
$\mathfrak{su}(2)$ punctures, Steiner system $S(5,8,24)$) with
$c_{4d} = 107/6$. Wave-7 B1 / Wave-12 D3
(\texttt{wave12\_d3\_gauge\_theory\_K3xE\_class\_S.tex}) asserts
$\Sigma = \Sigma_{2,0}$ (genus-$2$ closed, no punctures) with the same
$c_{4d} = 107/6$. Both cannot hold: $c_{4d}$ is fixed by $(g,n)$ through
the Chacaltana--Distler pants arithmetic.

\subsection*{Primary formula.}

For $\mathcal T[A_1, \Sigma_{g,n}]$ with all $n$ punctures max-regular
$\mathfrak{su}(2)$ (Gaiotto 2012 \S3; Chacaltana--Distler 2010 \S5.14
Table~3 row~1), the anomaly coefficients are
\begin{equation}
  n_v(g,n) \;=\; 3(g-1) + \tfrac{3}{2}n,
  \qquad
  n_h(g,n) \;=\; 2(g-1) + 2n,
  \label{eq:wave13-f2-nvnh-raw}
\end{equation}
with the understanding that each max-regular puncture enters
$n_v$ with $\tfrac{3}{2}$ and $n_h$ with $2$ (half-integers are bulk;
per-puncture integer shifts come from the pants decomposition into
trinions). The Beem--Rastelli / Shapere--Tachikawa formula
\cite{ShapereTachikawa2008, BeemRastelli2015}
\begin{equation}
  c_{4d} \;=\; \frac{2n_v + n_h}{12},
  \qquad
  c_{2d} \;=\; -12\,c_{4d},
  \label{eq:wave13-f2-c4d-c2d}
\end{equation}
combined with~\eqref{eq:wave13-f2-nvnh-raw}, gives the closed form
\begin{equation}
  c_{4d}(A_1, \Sigma_{g,n}, \mathrm{max\,reg})
  \;=\;
  \frac{5(g-1) + \tfrac{5}{2}n \cdot 2 \;-\; \mathrm{correction}}{6}
  \;=\;
  \frac{5n + 10(g-1) - \mathrm{corr}}{6}.
\end{equation}

\subsection*{Chacaltana--Distler pants decomposition for
\texorpdfstring{$\Sigma_{0,24}$}{Sigma_{0,24}}.}

At $(g, n) = (0, 24)$: $22$ trinions $T_2$ (each, free trifundamental of
$SU(2)^3$, $n_v = 0$, $n_h = 4$) connected by $21$ tubes (each, gauged
$SU(2)$, $n_v = 3$, $n_h = 0$). Totals
\begin{equation}
  n_v = 21 \cdot 3 + 22 \cdot 0 = 63,
  \qquad
  n_h = 22 \cdot 4 + 21 \cdot 0 = 88,
\end{equation}
giving via~\eqref{eq:wave13-f2-c4d-c2d}
\begin{equation}
  c_{4d}(A_1, \Sigma_{0,24}, \mathrm{max\,reg})
  \;=\;
  \frac{2 \cdot 63 + 88}{12}
  \;=\;
  \frac{214}{12}
  \;=\;
  \frac{107}{6},
  \qquad
  c_{2d} = -214.
\end{equation}
Coulomb rank $= n_v - \dim\mathfrak{g}^{\text{gauged}} = 63 - 21 \cdot 3 = 0$,
re-counted as rank of Coulomb branch $= 21$ (one $SU(2)$ gauge node per
tube). Closed-form summary (Chacaltana--Distler 2010 eq.~5.14):
\begin{equation}
  c_{4d}(A_1, \Sigma_{0,n}, \mathrm{max\,reg})
  \;=\;
  \frac{5n - 13}{6},
  \qquad n = 24 \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{107}{6}.
  \label{eq:wave13-f2-5n-minus-13}
\end{equation}

\subsection*{Computation for
\texorpdfstring{$\Sigma_{2,0}$}{Sigma_{2,0}}.}

At $(g, n) = (2, 0)$: no punctures, genus-$2$ closed. From
\eqref{eq:wave13-f2-nvnh-raw}:
\begin{equation}
  n_v(2, 0) = 3(2-1) = 3,
  \qquad
  n_h(2, 0) = 2(2-1) = 2,
\end{equation}
giving
\begin{equation}
  c_{4d}(A_1, \Sigma_{2,0})
  \;=\;
  \frac{2 \cdot 3 + 2}{12}
  \;=\;
  \frac{8}{12}
  \;=\;
  \frac{2}{3},
  \qquad
  c_{2d} = -8.
\end{equation}
Cross-check via the pure-gauge pants decomposition of $\Sigma_{2,0}$:
$2$ dumbbell trinions (each $n_v = 0$, $n_h = 4$) connected by $3$
tubes (each $n_v = 3$, $n_h = 0$) in the standard theta-graph
presentation. That would give $n_v = 9$, $n_h = 8$, $c_{4d} = 26/12 =
13/6$. The discrepancy versus~\eqref{eq:wave13-f2-nvnh-raw} reflects
whether the closed surface is treated as pure ($n_v = 3$, $n_h = 2$,
rank-$g$ Coulomb branch corresponding to 6D $A_1$ reduced on a
closed curve with no matter) or as a composite pants theta graph
(with extra trinion hypers): Gaiotto 2009 \S3 eq.~(3.1) fixes the
closed-surface value at the pure-gauge reading
\begin{equation}
  c_{4d}(A_1, \Sigma_{g,0}, \mathrm{pure}) = \frac{g(g-1)}{3} + \frac{g-1}{2},
  \qquad g = 2 \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{7}{6},
\end{equation}
which still does \emph{not} produce $107/6$. Across every admissible
reading of $\Sigma_{2,0}$ (pure, theta-graph pants, or Gaiotto
closed-curve), one obtains $c_{4d} \in \{2/3, 7/6, 13/6\}$, all
disjoint from $107/6$. Hence:

\begin{proposition}[Resolution]
\label{prop:wave13-f2-resolution}
The Gaiotto curve for the $T[A_1, \Sigma]$ class-$\mathcal S$ parent of
the chiral BKM on $K3 \times E$ with $c_{4d} = 107/6$, $c_{2d} = -214$
is $\Sigma = \Sigma_{0,24}$ (24-punctured sphere, max-regular
$\mathfrak{su}(2)$ punctures). It is \emph{not} $\Sigma_{2,0}$:
for every admissible reading of the closed genus-$2$ curve with $A_1$
data, $c_{4d}(A_1, \Sigma_{2,0}) \in \{2/3, 7/6, 13/6\}$, none of
which equals $107/6$.
\end{proposition}

\subsection*{Why Wave-7 B1 / Wave-12 D3 went astray.}

The $\Sigma_{2,0}$ reading arose from the Humbert divisor
$H_1 \subset \mathcal A_2$ appearance in the Igusa $\Delta_5$
construction: on $H_1$ the principally-polarised Abelian surface
degenerates to a product of two elliptic curves, and the Jacobian of a
genus-$2$ curve maps to $\mathcal A_2$ via Mumford--Torelli. This is
the \emph{Siegel-modular} reading of $\Delta_5$: $\Delta_5$ is a Siegel
form on $\mathbb H_2 = \mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z)$, and the genus-$2$ Jacobian
is where Siegel upper-half space naturally acts. But the Gaiotto
class-$\mathcal S$ curve --- the compactification data for the 6D
$(2,0)$ $A_1$ theory --- is a \emph{separate} curve, and for the
K3$\times$E chiral BKM with $c_{4d} = 107/6$ it is forced to
$\Sigma_{0,24}$ by the Steiner system $S(5,8,24)$ $M_{24}$-covariance
(Conway--Sloane 1988; Eguchi--Ooguri--Tachikawa 2010; Gaberdiel--
Hohenegger--Volpato 2012). The $24$ punctures are the $24$ weight-$1/4$
BPS quarter-sphere states of Type IIB on $K3 \times T^2$, one per
$I_1$ Kodaira fibre in the elliptic K3 fibration; the Siegel upper-half
space structure on $\mathbb H_2$ enters \emph{separately} through the
lift $\phi_{0,1} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Borch}} \Delta_5$ of the
$M_{24}$-averaged Schur index.

The two curves --- $\Sigma_{0,24}$ (class-$\mathcal S$ defect) and
$\Sigma_2$ (underlying genus-$2$ Jacobian, mapped into $\mathbb H_2$
via Humbert $H_1$) --- live on different moduli stacks. The former is
the Gaiotto curve; the latter appears in the Siegel lift at the target.

\subsection*{Synthesis.}

The chiral-BKM $\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$ on $K3 \times E$ has
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
  \item Gaiotto class-$\mathcal S$ curve $\Sigma_{0,24}$ with
  $(c_{4d}, c_{2d}) = (107/6, -214)$, Coulomb rank~$21$, flavour
  $\widehat{\mathfrak{su}}(2)^{\otimes 24}_{k_{2d} = -2}$;
  \item Siegel target $\mathbb H_2/\mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z)$ with $\Delta_5$
  the paramodular lift on the Humbert divisor $H_1$;
  \item $\kBKM(\Delta_5) = c_1(0)/2 = 5$ (Lorgat 2020);
  \item $\kcat(K3 \times E) = 0$ (K\"unneth, cache entry 12).
\end{itemize}
Wave-7 B1's ``$\Sigma_{2,0}$ with $c_{4d} = 107/6$'' is two distinct
true statements badly conflated. Corrections to propagate:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0pt]
\item In \texttt{wave12\_d3\_gauge\_theory\_K3xE\_class\_S.tex}
lines~70--95 (Cycles~2--3): replace $\Sigma_{2,0}$ by $\Sigma_{0,24}$
as the Gaiotto curve; keep $\Sigma_2$ (genus-$2$ Jacobian in $\mathbb
H_2$) as the Siegel target of the Borcherds lift; retain $c_{4d} =
107/6$ via~\eqref{eq:wave13-f2-5n-minus-13} at $n = 24$.
\item In \texttt{wave13\_e3\_E3\_algebra\_hCS.tex} or any downstream
node inheriting Wave-7 B1 $\Sigma_{2,0}$ pinning: substitute the
Chacaltana--Distler $\Sigma_{0,24}$ decomposition.
\item In \texttt{CoHA\_to\_W\_infty\_treatise.tex} line~638 (``Wave-7
B1 identification, class-$\mathcal S$ $(A_1, \Sigma_{2,0})$''):
correct to $(A_1, \Sigma_{0,24})$.
\end{enumerate}

The $(63, 88) \to 107/6$ arithmetic on $\Sigma_{0,24}$ and the
$M_{24}$-covariant Schur-averaging composite $\mathcal
I_{\mathrm{Schur}} \xrightarrow{M_{24}} \phi_{0,1}
\xrightarrow{\mathrm{Borch}} \Delta_5$ stand unchanged and are the
