\section*{Wave-15 N4/20: Miki $\Sthree$-triality and $\CoHA(\C^{3}) = \Yplus$}

\subsection*{Attack-heal 1 --- The three equivariant parameters}

Equip $\C^{3}$ with the torus $T = (\C^{\times})^{3}$ acting by
$(t_{1}, t_{2}, t_{3}) \cdot (z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}) = (t_{1} z_{1},
t_{2} z_{2}, t_{3} z_{3})$.  Write $\epsilon_{i}$ for the weight of
$z_{i}$ in $H^{\ast}_{T}(\mathrm{pt}) = \C[\epsilon_{1}, \epsilon_{2},
\epsilon_{3}]$.  The Calabi--Yau condition on $\C^{3}$ --- trivial
canonical bundle, equivalently $T$ preserving the holomorphic volume
$dz_{1} \wedge dz_{2} \wedge dz_{3}$ --- is the linear constraint
\[
  \epsilon_{1} + \epsilon_{2} + \epsilon_{3} = 0,
\]
cutting the Calabi--Yau subtorus $T_{\mathrm{CY}} \subset T$.
The Weyl-like symmetric group $\Sthree$ permutes the three coordinate
axes of $\C^{3}$ and thereby permutes the $\epsilon_{i}$; the constraint
is $\Sthree$-invariant, so $\Sthree$ descends to the Calabi--Yau base.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 2 --- Triality on $\Winf[\lambda]$}

The affine-Yangian one-parameter family $\Winf[\lambda]$ of
Prochazka--Rapcak 2018 (``$\mathcal{W}_{\infty}$: master symmetry of
two-dimensional CFT'') is parametrised by
$\lambda = (\lambda_{1}, \lambda_{2}, \lambda_{3})$ with
$\sum_{i} \lambda_{i}^{-1} = 0$, where $\lambda_{i} = -\epsilon_{i}/h$
for a rescaling parameter $h$.  Miki's 2007 automorphism (Miki,
``A $(q, \gamma)$ analog of the $\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$ algebra'',
J. Math. Phys. 48) acts on $\Winf[\lambda]$ by the permutation of
$\lambda_{i}$ induced from $\Sthree$ on $\epsilon_{i}$.  This is
\emph{triality}: the three distinguished generators of $\Winf$ ---
stress tensor, $W_{3}$, spin-$4$ primary --- rotate under $\Sthree$.
Prochazka--Rapcak identify this as the triality of the pit geometry
of plane partitions: three asymptotic Young diagrams along three axes,
$\Sthree$-permuted.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 3 --- $\Sthree$ on $\CoHA(\C^{3}) = \Yplus$}

Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013 (``Cherednik algebras, $\mathcal{W}$-algebras
and the equivariant cohomology of the moduli space of instantons on
$\mathbb{A}^{2}$'') construct the $T$-equivariant cohomological Hall
algebra of $\C^{3}$ with the Calabi--Yau potential,
\[
  \CoHA(\C^{3})
    \;=\; \bigoplus_{n \geq 0} H^{\ast}_{T, c}(\mathrm{Hilb}^{n}(\C^{3}),
    \varphi_{W})
\]
where $\varphi_{W}$ is the vanishing-cycle sheaf of the tripled
Calabi--Yau superpotential.  This is the \emph{positive half}
$\Yplus = \Yplus(\widehat{\gl}_{1})$ of the affine Yangian: a
non-negatively-graded associative algebra, freely generated under a
shuffle product controlled by the three-variable rational function
\[
  f(x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3})
    \;=\; \prod_{i, j} \frac{x_{i} - x_{j} + \epsilon_{1}}
                            {x_{i} - x_{j}}
           \cdot (\text{cyclic in } \epsilon_{1}, \epsilon_{2},
           \epsilon_{3}).
\]
Since $f$ is manifestly symmetric in $(\epsilon_{1}, \epsilon_{2},
\epsilon_{3})$, $\Sthree$ acts by algebra automorphisms on $\Yplus$.
The action on generators is the same triality as on $\Winf[\lambda]$:
permuting the three pit asymptotes of a plane partition permutes the
three commuting Heisenberg subalgebras of $\Yplus$.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 4 --- Drinfeld double and the evaluation map}

The full affine Yangian is the Drinfeld double
\[
  Y \;=\; Y(\widehat{\gl}_{1})
    \;\cong\; \Yplus \otimes \Yzero \otimes \Yminus,
\]
with $\Yzero$ the Cartan, $\Yminus$ the negative half isomorphic to
$\Yplus^{\mathrm{op}}$ via the Chevalley involution.  Triality extends
from $\Yplus$ to $Y$ diagonally: $\Sthree$ acts on all three factors
compatibly with the double bracket, because the double pairing is
built from the same $\Sthree$-symmetric $f$.

Feigin--Jimbo--Miwa--Mukhin 2017 (``Quantum toroidal $\gl_{1}$ and
Bethe ansatz'') and Gaiotto--Rapcak 2017 (``Vertex algebras at the
corner'') construct, for each weight $\lambda$ and central charge
$c(\lambda)$, an evaluation homomorphism
\[
  \mathrm{ev}_{\lambda}\colon Y \longrightarrow \Winf[\lambda]
\]
sending Yangian generators $t^{(r)}_{k}$ to $\Winf$ modes.  This is a
homomorphism, not an isomorphism: $Y$ is a Hopf algebra of
loop-type generating functions; $\Winf[\lambda]$ is its image on a
\emph{one-parameter slice} of the representation category, the universal
vertex algebra in which all $Y$-modules with fixed $\lambda$
collectively live.  The kernel of $\mathrm{ev}_{\lambda}$ includes
every $Y$-relation that holds off that slice; the cokernel is zero at
generic $\lambda$ but acquires a finite piece at the pit values where
$\Winf[\lambda]$ truncates to $\mathcal{W}_{N}$.

\subsection*{Attack-heal 5 --- Reconciliation and the cache rule}

The reconciliation rests on four statements held simultaneously:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\CoHA(\C^{3}) = \Yplus$ as associative algebras (Schiffmann
      --Vasserot 2013, theorem).
\item $\Yplus$ carries an $\Sthree$-action by $\epsilon$-permutation.
\item $\Winf[\lambda]$ carries an $\Sthree$-action (Miki triality) by
      $\lambda$-permutation.
\item $\mathrm{ev}_{\lambda}\colon Y \to \Winf[\lambda]$ is
      $\Sthree$-equivariant but \emph{not} injective.
\end{enumerate}
Consequence: Miki triality of $\Winf[\lambda]$ is the image of
triality on $\Yplus = \CoHA(\C^{3})$ under $\mathrm{ev}_{\lambda}$
composed with the inclusion $\Yplus \hookrightarrow Y$; it lifts to a
triality on the CoHA, but does \emph{not} promote the CoHA to a vertex
algebra.  The cache rule $\CoHA(\C^{3}) = \Yplus \neq \Winf$ stands
intact: the positive half is an associative algebra of cohomological
Hecke operators, not a chiral algebra.  The $\Sthree$-symmetry that
Wave~14 K2 observed on the qq-character side is the triality of the
image under evaluation; the qq-character does not truncate because
$\Winf[\lambda]$ is itself a one-parameter family on which $\Sthree$
acts nontrivially, rotating among three equivalent presentations.

The antipattern is conflating the domain with the image: writing
``$\CoHA(\C^{3}) = \Winf$'' absorbs the Drinfeld double, the
Cartan, and the negative half into a single chiral object, losing
the Hopf structure that controls Maulik--Okounkov $R$-matrices.  The
correct statement has four layers: $\CoHA(\C^{3}) = \Yplus$;
$\Yplus \subset Y$ as Hopf-subalgebra; $\mathrm{ev}_{\lambda}\colon Y
\to \Winf[\lambda]$ is a surjection with $\lambda$-dependent kernel;
$\Sthree$ acts compatibly on all four via $\epsilon_{i} \leftrightarrow
\lambda_{i}$ permutation.

\subsection*{Status and scope}

The identification $\CoHA(\C^{3}) = \Yplus$ is theorem (Schiffmann
--Vasserot 2013); the $\Sthree$-symmetry of $\Yplus$ is elementary
from the shuffle presentation; the evaluation $\mathrm{ev}_{\lambda}$
is theorem in Gaiotto--Rapcak 2017 and Feigin--Jimbo--Miwa--Mukhin
2017.  No conjectural ingredient enters this reconciliation.  The
Calabi--Yau invariant of $\C^{3}$ relevant here is
$\kchir(\C^{3}) = 0$ (non-compact, shadow only;
$\kcat$ not defined for non-compact targets).  The triality is a
symmetry of the \emph{structure constants} --- the shuffle kernel $f$
--- not of any $\kchir$, $\kcat$, $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}$, or $\kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}$.
