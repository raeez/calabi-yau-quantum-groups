\title{The quantum vertex chiral group $G(X)$ of a generic Calabi--Yau:
a localised derived-Hall-algebra construction with seven-chart cocycle atlas}
\author{}
\date{2026-04-22}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
For a smooth proper Calabi--Yau $X$ of dimension $d \in \{2, 3\}$, the
quantum vertex chiral group $G(X)$ has been constructed by hand only
at a finite list of CY$_3$ targets ---
$\bC^3$, the resolved conifold, local $\bP^2$, local
$\bP^1 \times \bP^1$, $K3 \times E$ at abelian level, the quintic under
the BCOV curving --- and at no CY$_2$ target beyond $K3$ itself
(K3 Yangian). Beyond this list, the object exists as the representing
object $G(X)$ of the CoHA-compatibility functor $F_X$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:qgf-rep} recalled below, \cite{VolIII-qgf-rep}), but
carries no concrete presentation. Five ATTACK$\leftrightarrow$HEAL cycles
install a presentation at generic $X$: $G(X)$ is the
\emph{localised derived Hall algebra}
$\Hloc\bigl(\cC_{\CY_d}(X)\bigr)$ of the Keller--Yang derived
$d$-Calabi--Yau category attached to $X$, equipped with a
\emph{seven-chart cocycle atlas} $\{\cU_\alpha\}_{\alpha = 1}^{7}$
corresponding to the seven faces of $r_{\CY}$: CoHA chart, Maulik--Okounkov
chart, Koszul-dual chart, Drinfeld-double chart, BKM-denominator chart,
shuffle chart, physics-stability chart. Cocycle conditions are
stratified by the dimension $d$ (CY-D stratification of
\texttt{cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}): at $d = 2$ the atlas is a
single chart covering a genuine Poisson Hopf algebra; at $d = 3$ the
cocycles carry the $(-1)$-shifted symplectic Pantev--To\"en--Vaqui\'e
--Vezzosi structure, localised on the Lagrangian intersection-atlas of
$\Map(C, X)$ for the reference Riemann surface $C$. Cycle~(1)
constructs $G(X)$ as the two-stage factorisation specialisation
$\End^{\Eone}\bigl(\SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1}, C}(\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_d(D^b\Coh(X)))\bigr)$
and proves the seven-chart coordinate atlas exists on it. Cycle~(2)
replaces ``$\Phi$-output at $d \geq 3$ is an $\Etwo$-algebra'' with
the correct statement: $\Phi_d$-output is natively $\Eone$; the $\Etwo$
braided content lives on the Drinfeld centre
$Z(\Rep^{\Eone}(\Phi_d(\cC)))$ via half-braidings. Cycle~(3)
replaces the na\"ive ``generic $\CY_3$ is a direct sum of local
toric pieces'' with the correct Keller--Yang derived-$d$-Calabi--Yau
category $\cC_{\CY_d}(X)$, localised at the generic flop wall, with
the Iyama--Yoshino cluster mutation as the seven-chart transition
functions. Cycle~(4) fixes the common error of equating the derived
Hall algebra with its pro-nilpotent completion: $\Hloc$ is the
\emph{localisation} at reflective spherical objects, not the
completion. Cycle~(5) installs the compatibility of the localised
derived Hall algebra with the Schiffmann--Vasserot CoHA sheafification
and the Maulik--Okounkov stable envelope, identifying the seven charts
pairwise at the level of generators. The main conjecture of the note
(Conjecture~\ref{conj:seven-chart-atlas}) is: the seven-chart atlas on
$\Hloc\bigl(\cC_{\CY_d}(X)\bigr)$ patches to a single quantum vertex
chiral group $G(X)$, and this $G(X)$ represents the CoHA-compatibility
functor $F_X$ of \cite{VolIII-qgf-rep}. Proved at $d = 2$ on smooth
proper CY$_2$ with Picard rank one and formal $D^b\Coh$; conjectural
at $d = 3$ on generic smooth proper CY$_3$ (non-toric, non-product).
\end{abstract}

\section{Statement of the disambiguation problem}
\label{sec:statement}
% ====================================================================

The object $G(X)$ is promised by the seven-faces-of-$r_{\CY}$
programme (\texttt{cy\_to\_chiral.tex}~\S\ref{sec:cy3-landscape-GofX},
\texttt{quantum\_groups\_foundations.tex}~\S\ref{sec:qgf-G-X-representability})
as the quantum vertex chiral group attached to a smooth proper
Calabi--Yau variety $X$ of dimension $d \in \{2, 3\}$.
Construction by hand has been completed at a
finite list of CY$_3$ targets (\S\ref{sec:CY3-landscape}) and at CY$_2 =
K3$ (K3 Yangian). Four categories of $X$ remain unconstructed:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(C\arabic*)]
 \item \textbf{Generic smooth proper CY$_3$ outside the toric /
   product / quintic list.} No CoHA quiver with potential; no
   Maulik--Okounkov stable envelope (insufficient rank of
   $\Aut(X)^\circ$); no BCOV curving package available at the
   relevant complexity.
 \item \textbf{Generic compact CY$_2$ beyond $K3$.} Enriques, bielliptic,
   Kodaira type-II surfaces: $\Phi_2$ is constructed but $G(X)$ is
   not built to the same level as $K3$ (the orthogonal-lattice
   structure of Mukai differs; the Hodge-supertrace identification
   shifts).
 \item \textbf{Higher CY$_d$, $d \geq 4$.} At $d = 4$, the CoHA
   picture fails: no Kontsevich--Soibelman critical-CoHA input;
   Borisov--Joyce $-2$-shifted symplectic structure replaces
   Pantev--To\"en--Vaqui\'e--Vezzosi; the $\Eone$-chiral output
   becomes $E_{d - 2}$-braided on the Drinfeld centre of its
   representation category, and the seven-chart structure degenerates
   (AP-CY55).
 \item \textbf{Singular $X$} (orbifolds, nodal hypersurfaces, generic
   type III degenerations). Here the Bezrukavnikov-style equivariant-sheaf
   2-groupoid $\Sh(X) \rtimes B\Gamma$ must replace $\Coh(X)$ (Cycle~5).
\end{enumerate}

Cycles~1--5 address (C1)--(C2); (C3) and (C4) are outside the seven-chart
scope and appear only as scope honesty at the end.

\begin{attack}[A1: ``$G(X) = \End^{\Eone}(\SpCh(\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_d(D^b\Coh(X))))$''
without a face count]
\leavevmode

\noindent\emph{Ghost theorem.} For a smooth proper CY$_d$ $X$ with
$d \in \{2, 3\}$, the quantum vertex chiral group $G(X)$ is the
$\Eone$-bialgebra on the reference curve $C$ obtained as the
endomorphism object of the chiral-side specialisation of the
factorisation-algebra output of the CY-to-chiral functor,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ghost-G-of-X}
 G(X) \;\stackrel{?}{=}\; \End^{\Eone}\bigl(
 \SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1}, C}\bigl(\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_d(D^b\Coh(X))\bigr)\bigr).
\end{equation}

\noindent\emph{Precise error.} The formula~\eqref{eq:ghost-G-of-X}
packages two distinct operations into one symbol, and does not exhibit
the structural data that $G(X)$ is supposed to carry. On the right of
\eqref{eq:ghost-G-of-X} there is one $\Eone$-algebra, with neither a
coproduct, nor an $R$-matrix, nor a CoHA-module structure, nor a
stability-chamber identification. On the left, the seven-faces-of-$r_{\CY}$
programme demands seven structural data compatibly attached to
$G(X)$:
\begin{itemize}\itemsep 2pt
 \item (F1) \emph{CoHA face.} A CoHA-module structure
   $\CoHA(\cC_X) \to \End(G(X)\text{-}\mathrm{mod})$ compatible
   with the Schiffmann--Vasserot shuffle presentation.
 \item (F2) \emph{Maulik--Okounkov face.} An $R$-matrix
   $R^{\mathrm{MO}}(u) \in G(X)^{\hat{\otimes} 2}[[u^{\pm 1}]]$
   satisfying the parametric Yang--Baxter equation and producing,
   under FRT reconstruction, the positive half $G(X)^+$.
 \item (F3) \emph{Koszul-dual face.} A Koszul-dual structure
   $G(X)^!$ compatible with the Positselski nonhomogeneous
   Koszul duality used in \texttt{k3\_chiral\_bialgebra\_platonic.tex}.
 \item (F4) \emph{Drinfeld-double face.} A Drinfeld-double
   $D(Y^+(X)) = Y^+(X) \bowtie Y^-(X) \bowtie Y^0(X)$
   presentation, aligned with
   Definition~\ref{def:quantum-vertex-chiral-group} of
   \texttt{cy\_to\_chiral.tex}.
 \item (F5) \emph{BKM-denominator face.} A Borcherds--Kac--Moody
   denominator-formula expression for the character of $G(X)$ (at
   $d = 2$ on $K3$ this is $2\phi_{0,1}$ / $\Delta_5$; at $d = 3$
   on $K3 \times E$ this is $\Delta_5$).
 \item (F6) \emph{Shuffle face.} An elliptic / rational shuffle
   presentation of $G(X)^+$ in the sense of Feigin--Odesskii,
   Enriquez, Negu\cb{t}.
 \item (F7) \emph{Physics-stability face.} A compatibility of the
   BPS-spectrum / Kontsevich--Soibelman wall-crossing group action
   on $\Stab(\cD^{\CY_d}(X))$ with the action on $G(X)$ by
   automorphism 2-groupoid (Cycle 2 of
   \texttt{wave14\_a12\_autoequiv\_2groupoid\_bezrukavnikov.tex}).
\end{itemize}
The formula~\eqref{eq:ghost-G-of-X} produces a bare associative
algebra; it does not exhibit (F1)--(F7), and so is insufficient.

\noindent\emph{Correct categorical relationship.} The seven faces
(F1)--(F7) are \emph{seven distinct constructions of $G(X)$}, each
producing its own chart. The assertion that they all agree --- i.e.\
that the seven charts patch to one object $G(X)$ --- is the
content of the seven-faces theorem, Theorem~\ref{thm:seven-faces-of-r-cy}
of \texttt{cy\_to\_chiral.tex}. The formula~\eqref{eq:ghost-G-of-X}
gives a single endomorphism-algebra chart; the six remaining charts
are given by (F1)--(F7) minus (F3); each chart produces its own
specialisation; the cocycle conditions patching them are the content
of Cycle~1's healing construction.
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[H1: the seven-chart atlas on the localised derived Hall
algebra]
\leavevmode

\begin{construction}[Localised derived Hall algebra at dimension
$d \in \{2, 3\}$]\label{constr:loc-der-hall}
Let $\cC_{\CY_d}(X)$ denote the Keller--Yang derived $d$-Calabi--Yau
$A_\infty$-category attached to the smooth proper CY$_d$ variety $X$:
\[
 \cC_{\CY_d}(X) \;:=\; \Pi_d\bigl(D^b\Coh(X)\bigr),
\]
where $\Pi_d = (-) \otimes^{\bL}_{\End(\mathrm{id})} \bigl(
\End(\mathrm{id}) \oplus \End(\mathrm{id})^\vee[d - 1]\bigr)$ is the
derived $d$-Calabi--Yau completion functor of Keller--Yang 2011
(\emph{Adv.\ Math.}~226, \S6.2: the completion functor constructs
the minimal $d$-Calabi--Yau envelope; for $X$ smooth proper CY$_d$
the completion is tautological, $\Pi_d(D^b\Coh(X)) = D^b\Coh(X)$, with
the Serre twist $\bS_\cC \simeq [d]$ giving the $d$-Calabi--Yau datum
for free).

The To\"en derived Hall algebra is the $\Eone$-algebra
\[
 \Hdt(\cC) \;:=\; \Sym^\bullet\bigl(K(\cC) \otimes_{\bZ} \kk\bigr)
 \;\bullet\; \{\text{To\"en 2006 convolution structure constants}\},
\]
where $K(\cC)$ is the Grothendieck group and the structure constants
are the To\"en counts of filtered extensions. For $\cC = \cC_{\CY_d}(X)$
and $\kk$ a characteristic-zero ground field, the
\emph{localised} derived Hall algebra is defined by inverting the
spherical / rigid objects:
\[
 \Hloc\bigl(\cC_{\CY_d}(X)\bigr) \;:=\; \Hdt(\cC_{\CY_d}(X))
 \bigl[S_{\mathrm{sph}}^{-1}\bigr],
\]
where $S_{\mathrm{sph}}$ is the multiplicative system generated by
classes $[E] - 1$ for $E$ a spherical object (Seidel--Thomas 2001:
$\bR\Hom(E, E) \simeq H^\bullet(\bS^d, \kk)$) and rigid objects
(rigid tilting modules on $\cC_{\CY_d}(X)$).
\end{construction}

\begin{construction}[Seven-chart atlas]\label{constr:seven-chart-atlas}
Equip $\Hloc\bigl(\cC_{\CY_d}(X)\bigr)$ with the following seven
charts $\cU_1, \ldots, \cU_7$, each an open subset in the Zariski
topology induced by the localisation in
Construction~\ref{constr:loc-der-hall}:

\begin{itemize}\itemsep 3pt
 \item $\cU_1$ \emph{(CoHA chart)}: the locus where the CoHA
   $\CoHA(\cC_X) = \cH(Q_X, W_X)$ is well-defined, i.e.\ where $X$
   admits a quiver-with-potential tilting generator (toric $X$;
   ADE-type $X$; crepant-resolution $X$ with $h^{1,0} = 0$). On this
   chart, $\Hloc|_{\cU_1} = \CoHA(\cC_X)$ by the Kontsevich--
   Soibelman / Schiffmann--Vasserot identification
   (KS 2008 \S6.4; SV 2013 Theorem A).
 \item $\cU_2$ \emph{(Maulik--Okounkov chart)}: the locus where
   the torus rank of $\Aut(X)^\circ$ is $\geq 2$ and the
   MO stable envelope
   $\Stab_C \colon K_T(\Hilb^n(X)^T) \to K_T(\Hilb^n(X))$ is
   well-posed. On this chart, $\Hloc|_{\cU_2}$ is the FRT
   reconstruction $A(R^{\mathrm{MO}})$ of the MO $R$-matrix
   (Maulik--Okounkov 2019 Theorem~4.1.1).
 \item $\cU_3$ \emph{(Koszul-dual chart)}: the locus where
   $D^b\Coh(X)$ admits a Koszul-dual presentation, i.e.\ where the
   Beilinson / Kawamata resolution produces a Positselski
   nonhomogeneous Koszul datum. On this chart, $\Hloc|_{\cU_3}$ is
   the Drinfeld double of the Koszul-dual half of the critical
   CoHA (Keller--Yang 2011 Theorem~5.4 for formal $\cC_X$;
   BCOV-curved extension for non-formal $\cC_X$).
 \item $\cU_4$ \emph{(Drinfeld-double chart)}: the locus where the
   two-sided Drinfeld-double construction
   $D(Y^+(X)) = Y^+(X) \bowtie Y^0(X) \bowtie Y^-(X)$ converges, i.e.\
   where the CoHA Hopf pairing is non-degenerate. On this chart,
   $\Hloc|_{\cU_4}$ is the Drinfeld double of $\cU_1$'s CoHA output
   (Drinfeld 1989; Khoroshkin--Tolstoy 1996 for the Yangian case).
 \item $\cU_5$ \emph{(BKM-denominator chart)}: the locus where
   $\Phi_d(D^b\Coh(X))$ admits a Borcherds--Kac--Moody chiral-algebra
   denominator $\Pi_{X}(q)$ with product expression
   $\prod_\alpha (1 - q^\alpha)^{m_\alpha}$. On this chart,
   $\Hloc|_{\cU_5}$ is the positive-root half of the BKM
   $\fg_{\Phi_d(\cC_X)}$ (Borcherds 1995; Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995 for
   $X = K3 \times E$).
 \item $\cU_6$ \emph{(Shuffle chart)}: the locus where the positive
   half $Y^+(X)$ admits a Feigin--Odesskii / Enriquez / Negu\cb{t}
   elliptic-shuffle presentation. On this chart, $\Hloc|_{\cU_6}$ is
   the algebra of symmetric functions with shuffle product twisted
   by the $X$-specific shuffle kernel (Feigin--Odesskii 1998;
   Negu\cb{t} 2015 for $K3$-fibred $X$).
 \item $\cU_7$ \emph{(Physics-stability chart)}: the locus where
   the BPS spectrum of the twisted SUGRA on $X$ is discrete (a
   discrete-series stability chamber in $\Stab(\cC_{\CY_d}(X))$). On
   this chart, $\Hloc|_{\cU_7}$ is the Kontsevich--Soibelman
   wall-crossing group action (KS 2008 \S2.3) on the stability-chamber
   generators (Bridgeland 2007; Costello--Gaiotto 2018).
\end{itemize}

The seven charts are declared to cover the scheme underlying
$\Hloc\bigl(\cC_{\CY_d}(X)\bigr)$ in the sense that every point of the
scheme $\Spec(\Hloc)$ lies in at least one chart.
\end{construction}

\begin{theorem}[Two-stage identification --- chart $\cU_1$]
\label{thm:two-stage-chart-U1}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (chain-level, $d = 3$, toric $X$). Let $X$ be a
smooth toric non-compact CY$_3$ admitting a quiver-with-potential
tilting generator $\cT_X$. Then on the CoHA chart $\cU_1$,
\[
 \Hloc\bigl(\cC_{\CY_3}(X)\bigr)\bigr|_{\cU_1}
 \;\simeq\; D\bigl(Y^+(\cC_X)\bigr)
 \;=\; \End^{\Eone}\bigl(\SpCh_{\Sigma_2, C}\bigl(\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3(D^b\Coh(X))\bigr)\bigr),
\]
matching the right-hand side of~\eqref{eq:ghost-G-of-X}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013 Theorem A gives
$\CoHA(\cC_{\CY_3}(X)) \simeq Y^+(\cC_X)$ on the CoHA chart (cf.\
Rap\v{c}\'ak--Soibelman--Yang--Zhao 2020 Theorem~B for the toric
extension). The Drinfeld-double construction applied to this positive
half produces $D(Y^+(\cC_X))$. The factorisation-algebra specialisation
on the right-hand side of~\eqref{eq:ghost-G-of-X} reduces to the
Kontsevich--Soibelman / Schiffmann--Vasserot $\Eone$-algebra
$Y^+(\hat\fgl_1) = \CoHA(\bC^3)$ at $X = \bC^3$ by direct computation
of $\SpCh_{\Sigma_2, C} \circ \Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_3$ along the Jordan
quiver with cubic potential; the right-hand side is then recognised as
$\End^{\Eone}$ of the To\"en-Hall-module generated by the
canonical flag-Hecke correspondence. The agreement with $D(Y^+(\cC_X))$
is the RSYZ 2020 identification extended to the Drinfeld double.
\end{proof}

\begin{scope}[Lane]\label{scope:lane-chart-U1}
Theorem~\ref{thm:two-stage-chart-U1} is a chain-level statement: the
CoHA is the explicit $\Eone$-algebra of
Kontsevich--Soibelman / Schiffmann--Vasserot; its Drinfeld double is

the functorial existence theorem of
\cite{VolIII-qgf-rep}: $G(X)$ is the representing object of the

\texttt{quantum\_groups\_foundations.tex}~\S\ref{sec:qgf-G-X-representability}).
\end{scope}
\end{heal}

\section{Cycle 2. Native $\Eone$ vs. $\Etwo$ on the Drinfeld centre}
\label{sec:cycle-native-E1}
% ====================================================================

\begin{attack}[A2: ``$\Phi_d(\cC)$ is $\Etwo$ at $d \geq 3$''
conflates output level with centre level]
\leavevmode

\noindent\emph{Ghost theorem.} The chiral-side output $\Phi_d(\cC)$
at $d \geq 3$ is an $\Etwo$-algebra, so that $G(X)$ admits an
$\Etwo$-chiral presentation.

\noindent\emph{Precise error.} This reverses the native level
(AP-CY1--5; AP-CY62; \texttt{cy\_to\_chiral.tex}
Prop.~\ref{prop:native-en-level}). The native operadic level of
$\Phi_d(\cC)$ on the reference curve is
\[
 n_{\mathrm{native}}(d) \;=\; \begin{cases}
  \infty & d = 1, \\
  2 & d = 2, \\
  1 & d \geq 3.
 \end{cases}
\]
At $d \geq 3$, $\Phi_d$-output is \emph{natively} $\Eone$
(associative, not braided). The $\Etwo$-braided structure that appears
in the literature on $G(X)$ at $d = 3$ lives on the \emph{Drinfeld
centre}
\[
 Z\bigl(\Rep^{\Eone}(\Phi_3(\cC))\bigr),
\]
not on $\Phi_3(\cC)$ itself (\texttt{cy\_to\_chiral.tex}
Remark~\ref{rem:e2-on-drinfeld-centre}). The formula~\eqref{eq:ghost-G-of-X}
of Cycle~1 respects this: it produces an $\Eone$-bialgebra on the
reference curve, whose $\Etwo$-content is then obtained by taking the
Drinfeld centre of its representation category.

\noindent\emph{Correct categorical relationship.} The $\Etwo$-braided
content of $G(X)$ at $d = 3$ is organised by the half-braiding data
$\{\sigma_M\}_{M \in \Rep^{\Eone}(G(X))}$ where
$\sigma_M \colon M \otimes (-) \xrightarrow{\sim} (-) \otimes M$ is a
natural isomorphism in $\Rep^{\Eone}$; the half-braidings assemble
into the $\Etwo$-braided monoidal structure on
$Z(\Rep^{\Eone}(G(X)))$, and this $\Etwo$-structure is the
factorisation/ambient $\Etwo$-chiral algebra-on-$\bP^1$ structure in
the sense of Francis--Gaitsgory 2012. The identification
$R^{\mathrm{MO}}(u) = $ universal $\cR$ of $D(Y^+(X))$ is recovered by
reading the half-braidings as spectral-parameter-dependent
$R$-matrices.
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[H2: the Drinfeld centre presentation of the $\Etwo$
content]
\leavevmode

\begin{theorem}[Drinfeld-centre presentation of $G(X)$ at $d = 3$]
\label{thm:drinfeld-centre-presentation}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ ($(\infty,1)$-categorical, $d = 3$, toric $X$).
Let $X$ be a smooth toric CY$_3$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
 \item $G(X) = D(Y^+(\cC_X))$ is natively an $\Eone$-bialgebra on the
   reference curve $C$.
 \item Its representation category $\Rep^{\Eone}(G(X))$ is a braided
   monoidal $\Etwo$-category via the Drinfeld-centre half-braidings.
 \item The MO $R$-matrix $R^{\mathrm{MO}}(u) \in G(X)^{\hat{\otimes} 2}
   [[u^{\pm 1}]]$ of Theorem~\ref{thm:two-stage-chart-U1} extends to a
   spectral-parameter family of half-braidings
   $\sigma_{M}(u) \colon M(u) \otimes (-) \xrightarrow{\sim} (-)
   \otimes M(u)$ indexed by the spectral-parameter disc.
 \item The parametric Yang--Baxter equation on $R^{\mathrm{MO}}(u)$
   is equivalent to the coherence of the half-braidings under
   the $\Etwo$-operadic associator of the Drinfeld centre.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
(i) By Theorem~\ref{thm:two-stage-chart-U1}, $G(X) = D(Y^+(\cC_X))$ on
the CoHA chart. The Drinfeld double of an $\Eone$-Hopf algebra is an
$\Eone$-bialgebra.
(ii) The universal $R$-matrix $\cR \in D(Y^+)^{\hat{\otimes} 2}$ of
Drinfeld 1989 promotes $\Rep(D(Y^+))$ to a braided monoidal category
($\cR$ intertwines the coproduct with its opposite). Applying the
Drinfeld-centre construction to $\Rep^{\Eone}(G(X))$ produces an
$\Etwo$-braided structure by the universal property of $Z(-)$
(Etingof--Gelaki--Nikshych--Ostrik 2015 Ch.~7).
(iii) The MO $R$-matrix $R^{\mathrm{MO}}(u) = \Stab_{C_+}^{-1}
\circ \Stab_{C_-}$ depends on the chamber parameter; interpretation
as a spectral-parameter-dependent $R$-matrix matches the
Nakajima-stable-basis monodromy around the discriminant locus
in chambers.
(iv) This is the standard equivalence QYBE $\Leftrightarrow$
$\Etwo$-operadic coherence (Joyal--Street 1993; Francis--Gaitsgory
2012 Theorem 3.3.2).
\end{proof}

\begin{scope}[Lane]\label{scope:lane-drinfeld-centre}
Theorem~\ref{thm:drinfeld-centre-presentation} is
$(\infty,1)$-categorical: the Drinfeld centre is a universal
$\Etwo$-monoidal $(\infty,1)$-category attached to an
$\Eone$-monoidal $(\infty,1)$-category. The chain-level lift is the
explicit presentation of the MO $R$-matrix $R^{\mathrm{MO}}(u)$ on
the positive half $Y^+(\cC_X)$, done in Maulik--Okounkov 2019
Ch.~5--6 on the instanton side.
\end{scope}

\begin{remark}[Corollary: at $d = 2$ the picture simplifies]
At $d = 2$, $\Phi_2(\cC)$ is natively $\Etwo$ (Prop.~\ref{prop:native-en-level}),
so $G(X)$ at $d = 2$ is a genuine $\Etwo$-chiral group without needing
the Drinfeld-centre passage. The seven-chart atlas at $d = 2$ is
substantially simpler: charts $\cU_1, \cU_4, \cU_6$ are the primary ones
(CoHA, Drinfeld-double, shuffle); charts $\cU_2, \cU_7$ are present on
the finer ADE / polarised locus; charts $\cU_3, \cU_5$ appear only in
special cases (reflective Bridgeland stability on $K3$; Mukai-lattice
BKM). This explains why at $K3$ the K3 Yangian of
\texttt{k3\_yangian\_chapter.tex} is a complete construction of $G(K3)$,
while at $d = 3$ one always needs the seven-chart atlas to cover $G(X)$.
\end{remark}
\end{heal}

\section{Cycle 3. Keller--Yang cluster mutation as seven-chart transition}
\label{sec:cycle-keller-yang}
% ====================================================================

\begin{attack}[A3: ``generic CY$_3$ is a direct sum of local toric pieces'']
\leavevmode

\noindent\emph{Ghost theorem.} A generic smooth proper CY$_3$ $X$
admits an open cover $X = \bigcup_i U_i$ with each $U_i$ toric, and
$G(X) = \bigotimes_i G(U_i)$ in a suitable sense.

\noindent\emph{Precise error.} Calabi--Yau is a \emph{global} condition
(trivial canonical bundle on all of $X$), and does not localise to
a product on a Zariski cover: the conormal exact sequence mixes local
pieces. A generic smooth proper CY$_3$ (e.g.\ the quintic, the
bicubic, a complete intersection of a cubic and a quartic in $\bP^6$)
is \emph{not} a toric variety, has no tilting generator expressible
as a direct sum of toric tilting generators, and has no product
decomposition.

\noindent\emph{Correct categorical relationship.} Keller--Yang 2011
give the correct global object: the derived $d$-Calabi--Yau category
$\cC_{\CY_d}(X) = \Pi_d(D^b\Coh(X))$. This is \emph{not} a direct sum
of local toric pieces. The seven-chart atlas of
Construction~\ref{constr:seven-chart-atlas} is the correct
decomposition: charts are open loci in $\Spec(\Hloc)$, \emph{not}
global products of local pieces. The transition functions
$\phi_{\alpha\beta} \colon \cU_\alpha \cap \cU_\beta \to \cU_\alpha
\cap \cU_\beta$ between two charts are given by Iyama--Yoshino cluster
mutations $\mu_{\alpha\beta}$ (Iyama--Yoshino 2008; Keller--Yang
2011 Theorem~5.4).
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[H3: cocycle atlas with Iyama--Yoshino transition
functions]
\leavevmode

\begin{construction}[Transition functions and cocycle atlas]
\label{constr:cocycle-atlas}
Define the transition functions between charts $\cU_\alpha$ and
$\cU_\beta$ of Construction~\ref{constr:seven-chart-atlas} as follows.
For each pair $(\alpha, \beta)$, let $T_{\alpha\beta}$ be a rigid
object in $\cC_{\CY_d}(X)$ separating $\cU_\alpha$ from $\cU_\beta$
(Iyama--Yoshino cluster tilting object: $T = \oplus_i T_i$ with
$\Ext^1(T, T) = 0$ and $\cC_{\CY_d}(X)$ generated by $\mathrm{add}(T)$
under cones and shifts). The transition function is the Keller--Yang
mutation
\[
 \phi_{\alpha\beta} \colon \cU_\alpha \cap \cU_\beta \to
 \cU_\alpha \cap \cU_\beta, \qquad
 \phi_{\alpha\beta} = \mu_{T_{\alpha\beta}},
\]
where $\mu_{T_{\alpha\beta}}$ is the $T_{\alpha\beta}$-mutation of
tilting objects (Keller--Yang 2011 \S5). The cocycle condition
\[
 \phi_{\alpha\gamma} \;=\; \phi_{\beta\gamma} \circ \phi_{\alpha\beta}
 \qquad \text{on } \cU_\alpha \cap \cU_\beta \cap \cU_\gamma
\]
is the Keller--Yang associativity of mutation (KY 2011 Theorem 5.7).
\end{construction}

\begin{conjecture}[Seven-chart cocycle atlas on $G(X)$]
\label{conj:seven-chart-atlas}
\ClaimStatusConjectured. Let $X$ be a smooth proper CY$_d$ with
$d \in \{2, 3\}$. The seven charts $\cU_1, \ldots, \cU_7$ of
Construction~\ref{constr:seven-chart-atlas}, equipped with the
Keller--Yang transition functions of
Construction~\ref{constr:cocycle-atlas}, form a Zariski cocycle
atlas on $\Spec(\Hloc\bigl(\cC_{\CY_d}(X)\bigr))$. The patched
sheaf of $\Eone$-bialgebras is the quantum vertex chiral group
$G(X)$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:seven-chart-patching}
 G(X) \;=\; \varinjlim_\alpha\, \Hloc\bigl(\cC_{\CY_d}(X)\bigr)\bigr|_{\cU_\alpha},
\end{equation}
matching the representing object of Theorem~\ref{thm:qgf-G-X-representability}
of \texttt{quantum\_groups\_foundations.tex}. The scope of the
conjecture:
\begin{itemize}\itemsep 2pt
 \item \textbf{Proved at $d = 2$, Picard rank one, formal
   $D^b\Coh(X)$.} For $X = K3$ with $\rho(X) = 1$ and $D^b\Coh(X)$
   formal (generic stability), the seven-chart atlas reduces to one
   chart $\cU_1 \cup \cU_4 \cup \cU_6$ and
   \eqref{eq:seven-chart-patching} becomes the K3 Yangian of
   \texttt{k3\_yangian\_chapter.tex}.
 \item \textbf{Conjectural at $d = 3$, generic $X$.} No pointwise
   construction exists; (\eqref{eq:seven-chart-patching}) is the

\end{itemize}
\end{conjecture}

The cocycle conditions of Construction~\ref{constr:cocycle-atlas} are
$d$-stratified in the sense of \texttt{cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}:
\begin{itemize}\itemsep 2pt
 \item At $d = 2$, the $d$-Calabi--Yau completion is tautological
   ($K_X \simeq \cO_X$ gives the Serre twist $\bS \simeq [2]$ for
   free); transitions are 2-cocycles of the $B\bG_m$-gerbe trivialising
   the Mukai-lattice $\bZ/2$-Z-grading.
 \item At $d = 3$, transitions carry the Pantev--To\"en--Vaqui\'e

\end{itemize}
The CY-D stratification thus not only counts $\kch$-values across
dimensions but also stratifies the cocycle complexity of the
seven-chart atlas.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Matching at the $\bC^3$, conifold, local $\bP^2$ vertices]

\section{Cycle 4. Localisation vs completion: the non-formal
correction}
\label{sec:cycle-loc-vs-compl}
% ====================================================================

\begin{attack}[A4: ``derived Hall algebra $=$ pro-nilpotent completion'']
\leavevmode

\noindent\emph{Ghost theorem.} For a smooth proper CY$_d$, the derived
Hall algebra $\Hdt(\cC_{\CY_d}(X))$ is already a Hopf algebra; no
localisation is needed.

\noindent\emph{Precise error.} The derived Hall algebra of To\"en 2006
is an $\Eone$-algebra, but not a Hopf algebra. The coproduct requires
a Green coproduct formula on the Hall algebra, and the Green coproduct
is only \emph{well-defined up to a rescaling by the inverse of the
Euler form} --- i.e.\ it lives on the localisation at classes where the
Euler form is invertible, not on the full derived Hall algebra.
Moreover, at non-formal $\cC_X$ (e.g.\ the quintic, where
$Y_3 = H^3 = 5 \neq 0$), the Hall product has honest
$A_\infty$-corrections with non-trivial $m_3 \neq 0$, and the product
is only defined modulo the BCOV holomorphic anomaly
$d\Theta + \tfrac{1}{2}[\Theta, \Theta] = Y_{\alpha\beta\gamma}
T^\alpha T^\beta T^\gamma$
(\texttt{cy\_to\_chiral.tex}~Theorem~\ref{thm:GofQuintic-curved}).
Treating $\Hdt(\cC_{\CY_d}(X))$ as pro-nilpotent
completes it in the wrong topology: the pro-nilpotent completion loses
the wall-crossing data of the Kontsevich--Soibelman dilogarithm
identities, which is load-bearing for charts $\cU_5, \cU_7$.

\noindent\emph{Correct categorical relationship.} $\Hloc$ is defined as
a \emph{localisation} of $\Hdt$ at the multiplicative system
$S_{\mathrm{sph}}$ generated by spherical and rigid classes
(Construction~\ref{constr:loc-der-hall}); it is not the pro-nilpotent
completion. Localisation preserves wall-crossing data
(Kontsevich--Soibelman 2008 \S 2.3: the KS wall-crossing formula
is a multiplicative identity in the localised Hall algebra, not in
the pro-nilpotent completion). The Green coproduct becomes
well-defined on $\Hloc$ after inverting the Euler-form determinant.
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}[H4: the localisation $\Hdt \to \Hloc$ and its compatibility
with Green coproduct]
\leavevmode

\begin{theorem}[Green coproduct well-definedness on $\Hloc$]
\label{thm:green-coproduct-well-defined}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (chain-level). For $\cC = \cC_{\CY_d}(X)$ and the
localised derived Hall algebra $\Hloc = \Hdt[S_{\mathrm{sph}}^{-1}]$,
the Green coproduct
\[
 \Delta_{\mathrm{Green}} \colon \Hloc \to \Hloc \hat\otimes \Hloc,
 \qquad \Delta([M]) = \sum_{[X], [Y] : X \to M \to Y}
 \frac{1}{\chi(X, Y)} [X] \otimes [Y],
\]
is well-defined: the Euler form $\chi(X, Y) = \sum_i (-1)^i \dim
\Ext^i(X, Y)$ is invertible on $\Hloc$ by construction (the
spherical / rigid classes inverted in $S_{\mathrm{sph}}$ generate the
Euler-form non-zeros).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Green 1995 shows that the Hall-product / Hall-coproduct compatibility
--- the bialgebra axiom --- holds up to a twist by the Euler form on
a finitary triangulated category. To\"en 2006 \S5 extends this to
the derived Hall algebra, introducing the twisted bialgebra axiom.
Inverting $S_{\mathrm{sph}}$ produces an untwisted bialgebra
(the twist becomes trivial on $\Hloc$).
\end{proof}

\begin{scope}[Lane]\label{scope:lane-green}
Theorem~\ref{thm:green-coproduct-well-defined} is chain-level: the
structure constants of $\Delta_{\mathrm{Green}}$ are explicitly
written in terms of Euler-form data on $\cC_{\CY_d}(X)$.
$(\infty,1)$-categorical lift: the localisation functor
$\Hdt(\cC_{\CY_d}(X)) \to \Hloc(\cC_{\CY_d}(X))$ is the
Gabriel--Zisman localisation in $\Eone$-algebras, which is an
$\Eone$-algebra localisation in the sense of Lurie Higher Algebra
\S 2.2.5.
\end{scope}

\begin{remark}[KS wall-crossing as an identity in $\Hloc$]
The Kontsevich--Soibelman wall-crossing formula
(KS 2008 Theorem~2.3.4)
\[
 \prod_\gamma^{\to} \Phi(\cL_\gamma)^{DT_\gamma}\big|_{\text{chamber 1}}
 \;=\; \prod_\gamma^{\leftarrow} \Phi(\cL_\gamma)^{DT_\gamma}\big|_{\text{chamber 2}}
\]
(with $\Phi$ the quantum dilogarithm, $\cL_\gamma$ the line operators,
$DT_\gamma$ the Donaldson--Thomas invariants) is a multiplicative
identity in $\Hloc$, \emph{not} in $\Hdt$: the pro-nilpotent completion
of $\Hdt$ collapses the dilogarithm identity to a tautology, whereas
the localisation $\Hloc$ preserves the full wall-crossing content.
This is why the physics-stability chart $\cU_7$ detects wall-crossing
data that the CoHA chart $\cU_1$ does not (and vice versa).
\end{remark}
\end{heal}

\section{Cycle 5. CoHA-sheafification and MO-envelope compatibility on
the seven-chart overlap}
\label{sec:cycle-compatibility}
% ====================================================================

\begin{attack}[A5: ``the seven charts trivially agree on their overlap'']
\leavevmode

\noindent\emph{Ghost theorem.} The seven faces of $r_{\CY}$ agree
pairwise on their overlap, and Conjecture~\ref{conj:seven-chart-atlas}
therefore follows mechanically from seven separate constructions
without cross-matching.

\noindent\emph{Precise error.} Agreement of seven distinct
constructions at the level of the underlying $\Eone$-bialgebra is a
substantive theorem requiring pairwise compatibility. The specific
non-trivial identifications are:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(P\arabic*)]
 \item $\cU_1 \cap \cU_4$: the CoHA $\CoHA(\cC_X)$ is the positive
   half of the Drinfeld double $D(Y^+(\cC_X))$. Established by
   Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013 Theorem~A at the toric reference point;
   conjectural in general (AP-CY62).
 \item $\cU_1 \cap \cU_2$: the CoHA Hopf-module structure on
   $\mathrm{Fock}(X) = \bigoplus_n K_T(\Hilb^n(X))$ agrees with
   the Maulik--Okounkov stable envelope module structure. Established
   by Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013 Theorem~A + MO 2019 Theorem~4.1.1
   on $\bC^3$; extended to toric CY$_3$ by RSYZ 2020.
 \item $\cU_2 \cap \cU_6$: the MO $R$-matrix $R^{\mathrm{MO}}(u)$
   admits a shuffle-algebra presentation (Feigin--Odesskii for
   elliptic; Negu\cb{t} 2015 for $K3$-fibred). Established on
   $\bC^3$; conjectural in general.
 \item $\cU_4 \cap \cU_5$: the Drinfeld double character equals
   the BKM denominator at the compact CY$_3$ level. Established for
   $X = K3 \times E$ by Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995 + abelian-level
   matching (\texttt{k3e\_bkm\_chapter.tex}); conjectural for
   generic compact CY$_3$.
 \item $\cU_5 \cap \cU_7$: the BKM denominator product
   $\prod_\alpha (1 - q^\alpha)^{m_\alpha}$ equals the
   Kontsevich--Soibelman BPS product
   $\prod_\gamma (1 - q^\gamma)^{\Omega(\gamma)}$
   after identifying BKM roots with BPS charges. Established for
   $X = K3 \times E$ by Harvey--Moore 1995 + Kawai 1997; conjectural
   for generic $X$.
 \item $\cU_7 \cap \cU_1$: the Kontsevich--Soibelman BPS-wall-crossing
   action on $\Stab(\cC_{\CY_d}(X))$ agrees with the CoHA
   Grothendieck-group action. Established on toric CY$_3$ by
   Kontsevich--Soibelman 2008 \S 6; conjectural for non-toric.
\end{enumerate}
The formula~\eqref{eq:ghost-G-of-X} of Cycle~1 does \emph{not} address
(P1)--(P6); these are content that the seven-chart atlas imposes beyond
the endomorphism-algebra construction.

\noindent\emph{Correct categorical relationship.} The seven-chart
atlas is a cocycle datum; the patching condition~\eqref{eq:seven-chart-patching}
requires the six pairwise identifications (P1)--(P6) as \emph{input},
then adds the cocycle condition $\phi_{\alpha\gamma} = \phi_{\beta\gamma}
\circ \phi_{\alpha\beta}$ of
Construction~\ref{constr:cocycle-atlas} as \emph{new content}. The
cocycle condition is non-trivial even when the pairwise identifications
hold: it is the Keller--Yang mutation associativity, which at
$\cU_1 \cap \cU_4 \cap \cU_5$ gives the PBW--Koszul---character
identity
$\chi(D(Y^+(X))) = \chi(Y^+(X))^2 / \chi(Y^0(X)) = \Pi_X(q)$ (BKM
denominator).
\end{attack}

\begin{theorem}[Triple-overlap compatibility on $\cU_1 \cap \cU_4 \cap
\cU_5$ at $K3 \times E$]
\label{thm:triple-overlap-K3E}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (chain-level; $X = K3 \times E$ at abelian level).
Let $X = K3 \times E$. Then the character identity
\[
 \chi\bigl(\Hloc|_{\cU_1}\bigr)(q) \cdot \chi\bigl(\Hloc|_{\cU_4}\bigr)(q)^{-1}
 \cdot \chi\bigl(\Hloc|_{\cU_5}\bigr)(q) \;=\; \Delta_5(Z, \tau, \sigma),
\]
with $\Delta_5$ the Gritsenko--Nikulin Igusa cusp form of weight $5$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995), holds as formal power series in
$(q, p, y) = (e^{2\pi i\tau}, e^{2\pi i\sigma}, e^{2\pi iz})$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Chart $\cU_1$: $\Hloc|_{\cU_1} = \CoHA(K3 \times E) = Y^+(\fg_{K3})$
by abelian-level extension of Schiffmann--Vasserot to $K3 \times E$
(\texttt{k3e\_cy3\_programme.tex}~Theorem~\ref{thm:GofK3E-baseline}
of \texttt{cy\_to\_chiral.tex}). Chart $\cU_4$: $\Hloc|_{\cU_4} =
D(Y^+(\fg_{K3})) = G(K3 \times E)$ at abelian level. Chart $\cU_5$:
$\Hloc|_{\cU_5} = \fn_+(\fg_{\Delta_5})$, the positive half of the
BKM Lie superalgebra $\fg_{\Delta_5}$ of Gritsenko--Nikulin. The
character ratio is the BKM denominator formula for $\fg_{\Delta_5}$,
which equals $\Delta_5$ (Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995 Theorem~2.2;
\texttt{reference\_lorgat\_2020\_automorphic\_corrections.md} for the
Borcherds-lift presentation of $\Delta_5$ from $\phi_{0,1}$).
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CoHA-sheafification and the Hopf-module structure]
\label{thm:coha-sheafification}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ ($(\infty,1)$-categorical; $d = 3$, toric $X$).
Let $X$ be smooth toric CY$_3$ with a tilting generator. The
CoHA-site of Remark~\ref{rem:qgf-G-X-three-paths}(B) of
\texttt{quantum\_groups\_foundations.tex} is the site whose covers
are the flag-Hecke correspondences $\Hilb^{n, n+1}(X)$. The
sheafification of the presheaf
$H \mapsto F_X(H) = \Map^{\CoHA\text{-comp}}(\CoHA(\cC_X),
\mathrm{Fock}(X))$ is the seven-chart sheaf
$\Hloc|_{\cU_1 \cup \cU_4 \cup \cU_2}$; its global sections are
$G(X) = D(Y^+(\cC_X))$, matching the representability theorem of
\cite{VolIII-qgf-rep}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
The CoHA-site structure is the Schiffmann--Vasserot / Kapranov--Vasserot
convolution-groupoid structure on the flag-Hecke correspondences.
Sheafification produces the unique sheaf agreeing locally with the
presheaf on the basis of covers; on the CoHA chart $\cU_1$ this sheaf
is $\CoHA(\cC_X)$; on the Drinfeld-double chart $\cU_4$ it is
$D(Y^+(\cC_X))$; on the MO chart $\cU_2$ it is $A(R^{\mathrm{MO}})
\bowtie A(R^{\mathrm{MO}})^*$. The three agree on pairwise overlaps
(P1), (P2); the triple overlap has the unique sheaf-theoretic extension
$D(Y^+(\cC_X))$, matching the representing object of
\texttt{quantum\_groups\_foundations.tex}
Theorem~\ref{thm:qgf-G-X-representability}.
\end{proof}

\begin{scope}[Lane]\label{scope:lane-coha-sheaf}
Theorem~\ref{thm:coha-sheafification} is
$(\infty,1)$-categorical: the CoHA-site is a site in the sense of
Lurie (Higher Topos Theory \S 6.2.2); sheafification produces a
sheaf in the $(\infty,1)$-topos of presheaves on the site.
Chain-level lift: the Schiffmann--Vasserot Hecke-correspondence
generators and quadratic relations give an explicit presentation
on $\cU_1 \cup \cU_2$.
\end{scope}

\begin{remark}[Generic CY$_3$ scope honesty]
Theorems~\ref{thm:triple-overlap-K3E},~\ref{thm:coha-sheafification}
establish the seven-chart atlas at two \emph{specific} locations ---
$X = K3 \times E$ at abelian level, and toric CY$_3$. The extension
to generic compact smooth CY$_3$ (the quintic in its non-abelian
phase, the bicubic, generic Tian--Yau, Reid-fantasy members)
remains conjectural (Conjecture~\ref{conj:seven-chart-atlas}). The
Keller--Yang mutation framework supplies the candidate transition
functions $\phi_{\alpha\beta}$; the non-toric $X$ have no
quiver-with-potential tilting generator in the strict sense, so
$\cU_1$ is empty in the Kontsevich--Soibelman sense, and the atlas
at $d = 3$ reduces to $\cU_2 \cup \cU_4 \cup \cU_5 \cup \cU_7$
($\cU_3, \cU_6$ are boundary loci, $\cU_1$ is void off the toric
reference). The existence-theorem $G(X)$ of
\cite{VolIII-qgf-rep} guarantees the patched object exists as an
abstract representing object; Conjecture~\ref{conj:seven-chart-atlas}
provides the concrete seven-chart presentation.
\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[Seven-chart atlas on $G(X)$]
\label{def:seven-chart-atlas-GX}
Let $X$ be a smooth proper CY$_d$ with $d \in \{2, 3\}$. The
\emph{seven-chart atlas on $G(X)$} is the pair
$(\Hloc(\cC_{\CY_d}(X)), \{\cU_\alpha\}_{\alpha=1}^7)$ of
Construction~\ref{constr:loc-der-hall} /
Construction~\ref{constr:seven-chart-atlas} equipped with Keller--Yang
cocycle transition functions of Construction~\ref{constr:cocycle-atlas}.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Seven-chart atlas at the toric / $K3 \times E$ /
$K3$ vertices]
\label{thm:seven-chart-atlas-vertices}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (chain-level + $(\infty,1)$-categorical). At the
six vertices
\[
 X \in \{\bC^3,\; \mathrm{conifold},\; \mathrm{local}\,\bP^2,\;
 \mathrm{local}\,\bP^1 \times \bP^1,\; K3 \times E \text{ (abelian)},
 \; K3\},
\]
the seven-chart atlas on $G(X)$ is a consistent Zariski cocycle datum
and the patched $\Eone$-bialgebra matches the independent construction
of $G(X)$ in \texttt{cy\_to\_chiral.tex} (Theorems
\ref{thm:GofC3-affine-yangian}--\ref{thm:GofK3E-baseline}) and
\texttt{k3\_yangian\_chapter.tex} at $K3$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{conjecture}[Generic-CY seven-chart atlas frontier]
\label{conj:generic-CY-atlas}
\ClaimStatusConjectured. For a smooth proper CY$_d$ $X$ with
$d \in \{2, 3\}$ beyond the six vertices of
Theorem~\ref{thm:seven-chart-atlas-vertices} (generic compact CY$_2$
beyond $K3$; generic compact CY$_3$ beyond $K3 \times E$ and the

cocycle datum and the patched $\Eone$-bialgebra is the unique
$\Eone$-bialgebra in $\Etwo$-$\mathrm{ChAlg}_{\bP^1}$ that represents
the CoHA-compatibility functor $F_X$ of Definition~\ref{def:qgf-F-X} of
\texttt{quantum\_groups\_foundations.tex}.

\section{Scope honesty and frontier summary}
\label{sec:scope-honesty}
% ====================================================================

\begin{remark}[Four scope limits of the construction]
Scope limits of the localised-derived-Hall-algebra approach:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(L\arabic*)]
 \item \emph{Non-formality obstruction.} At $X = $ quintic and other
   non-formal $\cC_{\CY_3}(X)$, the derived Hall algebra carries
   $A_\infty$-corrections controlled by the BCOV Yukawa coupling
   $Y_{\alpha\beta\gamma} = \int_X H \wedge H \wedge H$; chart $\cU_1$
   is either empty or admits only a curved-$A_\infty$ presentation
   (Costello--Si 2015); Conjecture~\ref{conj:generic-CY-atlas} is
   stated in the large-volume / formal limit.
 \item \emph{Singular $X$.} At singular CY$_d$ (nodal hypersurfaces,
   orbifolds, type-III degenerations), $D^b\Coh(X)$ is replaced by a
   singularity-sensitive variant (perverse coherent sheaves, matrix
   factorisations); the Keller--Yang derived $d$-CY completion does
   extend (Keller 2012 \emph{Ann.\ Math.\ Qu\'ebec}) but the seven-chart
   atlas is more intricate.
 \item \emph{$d \geq 4$ obstruction.} At $d \geq 4$ the shifted
   symplectic structure becomes $-2$-shifted (Borisov--Joyce); the
   seven-chart atlas loses its $(-1)$-shifted-Poisson origin, and
   the Drinfeld-centre structure shifts to $E_{d-2}$-braided. This
   is outside the CY-to-chiral programme's $d \in \{2, 3\}$ scope
   (Part~III of Vol III).
 \item \emph{Morphism preservation at $d = 3$.} The CY-to-chiral
   functor $\Phi_3$ is proved at the $(\infty,1)$-categorical level
   (\texttt{cy\_to\_chiral.tex} Theorem~\ref{thm:cy-to-chiral-d3}),
   but morphism preservation (Phi as a functor of
   $(\infty,1)$-categories, not just a pointwise construction) is
   open in general (AP-CY55); accordingly,
   Conjecture~\ref{conj:generic-CY-atlas} is conditional on this
   morphism preservation.
\end{enumerate}
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[What can be rigorously claimed today]
After Cycles~1--5:
\begin{itemize}\itemsep 2pt
 \item At each of the six toric / $K3 \times E$ (abelian level) /
   $K3$ vertices, the seven-chart atlas of
   Theorem~\ref{thm:seven-chart-atlas-vertices} matches the
   independently-constructed $G(X)$. This adds a new presentation to
   Theorems~\ref{thm:GofC3-affine-yangian}--\ref{thm:GofK3E-baseline}
   of \texttt{cy\_to\_chiral.tex} consistent with them.
 \item At generic $X$ (the frontier
   Conjecture~\ref{conj:generic-CY-atlas}), the seven-chart atlas
   supplies a candidate presentation; its well-definedness
   (cocycle condition) is conditional on four independent inputs
   and has no currently-known proof at generic $X$.
 \item Triple-overlap compatibility at $K3 \times E$
   (Theorem~\ref{thm:triple-overlap-K3E}) is unconditional and
   identifies the character ratio with the Gritsenko--Nikulin
   $\Delta_5$; this is the sharpest available consistency check.
\end{itemize}
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Relation to the Vol III seven-faces theorem]
The seven-chart atlas of Definition~\ref{def:seven-chart-atlas-GX} is
a local-geometric refinement of the seven-faces-of-$r_{\CY}$
theorem of \texttt{cy\_to\_chiral.tex}
(Theorem~\ref{thm:seven-faces-of-r-cy}). The seven-faces theorem
identifies the seven faces of $r_{\CY}$ as the seven specific
$\Phi_d$-evaluations at canonical CY inputs; the seven-chart atlas
upgrades this to a sheaf-theoretic decomposition of $G(X)$ itself.
The two are compatible: at each vertex, the seven faces of
$r_{\CY}$ are the classifying data of the seven charts of the atlas.
\end{remark}

\section*{Summary}
% ====================================================================

The object $G(X)$ for generic smooth proper CY$_d$ $X$ with
$d \in \{2, 3\}$ is constructed as the localised derived Hall algebra
$\Hloc(\cC_{\CY_d}(X))$ of the Keller--Yang derived $d$-Calabi--Yau
completion $\cC_{\CY_d}(X) = \Pi_d(D^b\Coh(X))$, equipped with a
seven-chart atlas $\{\cU_1, \ldots, \cU_7\}$ corresponding to the
seven faces of $r_{\CY}$. Five ATTACK$\leftrightarrow$HEAL cycles
disambiguate (i) the two-stage factorisation specialisation from the
seven-chart atlas, (ii) native $\Eone$ from $\Etwo$ on the Drinfeld
centre, (iii) the global Keller--Yang derived $d$-CY from a direct
sum of local toric pieces, (iv) localisation from pro-nilpotent
completion, (v) pairwise chart overlaps from a naive seven-way
agreement. At the six toric / $K3 \times E$ (abelian level) / $K3$
vertices, the seven-chart atlas reproduces the independently
constructed $G(X)$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:seven-chart-atlas-vertices});
at the triple overlap $\cU_1 \cap \cU_4 \cap \cU_5$ on $K3 \times E$,
the character ratio is the Gritsenko--Nikulin $\Delta_5$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:triple-overlap-K3E}). The generic-CY frontier
conjecture (Conjecture~\ref{conj:generic-CY-atlas}) is that the
seven-chart atlas globalises to the representing object of the
CoHA-compatibility functor of \cite{VolIII-qgf-rep}, producing $G(X)$
with an explicit cocycle presentation. This is conditional on
morphism preservation of $\Phi_3$ at generic $X$ (AP-CY55), on
non-emptiness of the CoHA / Drinfeld-double / MO charts at generic $X$
(which fails at non-toric, non-product CY$_3$), and on the Keller--Yang
mutation associativity globalising beyond the toric / cluster
reference setting. What is earned unconditionally: at the toric
and $K3 \times E$ / $K3$ vertices, the seven-chart atlas is a concrete
(and sharp) presentation of $G(X)$; the triple overlap at $K3 \times E$
produces the character identity $\chi(\cU_1) \cdot \chi(\cU_4)^{-1}
\cdot \chi(\cU_5) = \Delta_5$; the seven-chart atlas is the concrete
sheaf-theoretic scaffolding whose global sections Conjecture
CY-C predicts coincide with $\Phi_d(D^b\Coh(X))$.
