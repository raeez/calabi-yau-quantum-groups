\section*{Wave~19 P5/20: the 8-form picture at programme grade}
\label{wn:wave19-p5-synthesis-8-forms}

\subsection*{The tableau}

The Gritsenko--Cl\'ery census (2008, Thm.~1.1) lists eight paramodular
cusp forms $\Delfnm{w_N}{N}$ of simplest (diagonal) divisor, one per
order $N\in\{1,\ldots,8\}$, with weights
$(w_1,\ldots,w_8)=(5,2,1,1,\tfrac12,1,\tfrac14,0)$ and constant Jacobi
heads $c_N(0)=2w_N$. Against the universal Borcherds identity
$\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$, the census separates into a theorem slice
$N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ (CHL-admissible, $\varphi(N)\mid 2$) and a
conjectural slice $N\in\{5,7,8\}$ (non-CHL), the two slices resting on
structurally different CY hosts.

\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc|llll}
N & w_N & c_N(0) & \kBKM & \text{host} &
    \text{geom} & \text{CoHA} & \text{BPS} & \text{auto}\\\hline
1 & 5     & 10  & 5     & \K\times E                             & \ClaimStatusTheorem     & \ClaimStatusTheorem     & \ClaimStatusConjectured & \ClaimStatusTheorem\\
2 & 2     & 4   & 2     & (\K\times E)/\Z_2\text{-}\CHL           & \ClaimStatusTheorem     & \ClaimStatusTheorem     & \ClaimStatusConjectured & \ClaimStatusTheorem\\
3 & 1     & 2   & 1     & (\K\times E)/\Z_3\text{-}\CHL           & \ClaimStatusTheorem     & \ClaimStatusTheorem     & \ClaimStatusConjectured & \ClaimStatusTheorem\\
4 & 1     & 2   & 1     & (\K\times E)/\Z_4\text{-}\CHL           & \ClaimStatusTheorem     & \ClaimStatusTheorem     & \ClaimStatusConjectured & \ClaimStatusTheorem\\
5 & 1/2   & 1   & 1/2   & X_5^{\mathrm{BV}}                       & \ClaimStatusConjectured & \ClaimStatusConjectured & \ClaimStatusOpen        & \ClaimStatusTheorem\\

8 & 0     & 0   & 0     & X_8^{\mathrm{MTW}}                      & \ClaimStatusConjectured & \ClaimStatusConjectured & \ClaimStatusOpen        & \ClaimStatusTheorem
\end{array}
\]

Four corners: \emph{geom} = reduced DT moduli on
$\gamma\in H_2(X;\Z)_{\mathrm{prim}}$ hosting the Borcherds product
(MPT~2010, Oberdieck--Pixton 2017, Bryan--Oberdieck 2018);
\emph{CoHA} = Davison--Meinhardt identification
$\Yplus(X)=H^{\bullet}_{\mathrm{eq}}(\cM^{+}_{\mathrm{eff}}(X),\phi_W)$;
\emph{BPS} = bracket-level isomorphism
$\gBPS(X)\cong\gD{w_N}{N}$; \emph{auto} = Borcherds singular theta
lift $\BorLift(\psi_{w_N,N}^{\mathrm{EZ}})=\Phi_N$.

\subsection*{The universal identity}

\ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{(Borcherds-weight identity across all
eight rows.)} The identity $\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$ holds for
$N\in\{1,\ldots,8\}$ when $\kBKM$ is read as the weight of the
singular theta lift. The weight reads directly from the
$(q^0\zeta^0)$-coefficient of the weak Jacobi input
$\psi_{w_N,N}^{\mathrm{EZ}}$ by Borcherds 1998 Thm.~13.3, independently
of integrality of $c_N(0)$ and independently of whether the lift
carries a BKM denominator. Integer rows $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ live on
$\Sp_4(\Z)$ paramodular $\Gamma_t(N)$; the half-integer row $N=5$
invokes Borcherds 1998 Thm.~10.1 plus Bruinier 2002 Prop.~5.1 on the
metaplectic cover $\Mp_4(\Z)$; the quarter-integer row $N=7$ sits on
the Freitag--Hermann 1985 \S II.5 spin double cover; the
weight-$0$ row $N=8$ is a Siegel modular function with empty
Weyl chamber. What bifurcates across the CHL horizon is the
BKM-side \emph{reading} of the identity, not the identity itself.

\subsection*{Structural stratification}

Two obstructions separate the CHL slice from the non-CHL slice.

\emph{Nikulin admissibility.} Symplectic finite-order K3 automorphisms
are classified by lattice embeddings $\Z/N\hookrightarrow O(L_{K3})$
(Nikulin 1980 Thm.~4.5); admissible orders are $N\in\{1,\ldots,8\}$.
The CHL orbifold $(K3\times E)/(\Z/N)$ requires a compatible diagonal
action on $E$, forcing $\Z[\zeta_N]\hookrightarrow\mathrm{End}(E)$ and
hence $\varphi(N)\mid 2$, which selects the subset
$N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$. This is the cut not present in the slide material
(P1): the slides enumerate the full GC family without imposing CHL.

\emph{Character integrality.} The GC character $\chi_N$ is $\Z$-valued
on the CHL slice; $\chi_5$ on the metaplectic cover takes values in
$\{\pm 1,\pm i\}$; $\chi_7^{\otimes 2}$ lives on the spin double
cover; $\chi_8$ reduces to the trivial character.

On the CHL slice the four corners of the status matrix close
simultaneously. The reduced DT partition function of
Oberdieck--Pixton, as extended by Bryan--Oberdieck 2018 to the
CHL quotient, computes
$Z^{X_N}_{L,1}=-C_N/\Phi_N^{\,2}$ at primitive $K3$-classes
(theorem; bracket-level BPS $\cong$ BKM away from these classes
is conjectural, pending AP-CY34 closure of
$[m_3,B^{(2)}]$-cyclic invariance). The CoHA of Davison--Meinhardt
recovers $\Yplus(X_N)$ with the correct graded character; the
primitive Lie subalgebra matches $\gD{w_N}{N}$ at the level of
root multiplicities. On the non-CHL slice the automorphic corner
survives but the geometric host deforms.

\emph{$N=5$} (Borcea 1997, Voisin 1993): host is the
Borcea--Voisin threefold
$X_5^{\mathrm{BV}}=(S_5\times E_5)/(\iota_S\times\iota_E)$,
with $\Z/5$ acting on $S_5$ only and $E_5$ carrying an order-two
involution; the identification
$(Z^{X_5^{\mathrm{BV}}})^{-1/4}=\Delfnm{1/2}{5}$
is forced by the square of weight $1/2$ landing on $\Sp_4(\Z)$
weight $2$ under the Borcherds transfer (Wave-19 Z1).

\emph{$N=7$}: Nikulin's fixed count $\#\,\mathrm{Fix}(g_7)=3$ with
$\chi(\K^{g_7})/3=0$ obstructs every smooth free $\Z/7$-quotient
CY$_3$; the host is forced to be singular. Wave-19 Z2 identifies the
candidate as an order-$4$ gerbe carrying a Cheeger--Simons
differential character, with the eighth root
$(Z^{X_7})^{-1/8}=\Phi_7$ realised as a twisted-$K$-theoretic index
(Mathai--Murray--Stevenson 2002 Thm.~1.2). No smooth compact CY$_3$
realisation exists.

\emph{$N=8$}: host is the Mongardi--Tari--Wandel Kummer-type fourfold
quotient $X_8^{\mathrm{MTW}}=\Kum^{3}(A)/(\Z/8)$ (MTW 2018 Thm.~1.3);
the $(\Z/8)$-action exists only on Kummer-type holomorphic-symplectic
fourfolds, not on $K3\times E$. The weight-$0$ form $\Delfnm{0}{8}$
is a Siegel modular function; its associated ``superalgebra''
collapses to an abelian lattice algebra, all positive-root
multiplicities on the Weyl-chamber boundary (Wave-16 V4 Cycle~3).

\subsection*{Remaining frontier}

Three problems close the eight-form picture.

\emph{(a) Bracket-level BPS $\cong$ BKM at every $N$.}
Davison--Meinhardt supplies the Lie bracket on $\gBPS$;
Gritsenko--Nikulin supplies the BKM bracket on $\gD{w_N}{N}$.
Matching brackets, not just graded dimensions, requires
algebraic orientation data on $D^b(\mathrm{Coh}(X_N))$
(Bojko--Kuhn--Liu) and the strong-orientation PBW theorem for
compact $3$-CY categories (Kinjo--Park--Safronov 2024
arXiv:2406.12838). AP-CY34 on cyclic invariance of $[m_3,B^{(2)}]$
(Wave-18 F3 Costello TCFT input) is the blocker at every $N$.

\emph{(b) $N=7$ singular host.} The obstruction is geometric: no
smooth compact CY$_3$ admits a free symplectic $\Z/7$-action
compatible with an elliptic fibre. The quarter-integer character
$\chi_7^{\otimes 2}$ on the spin double cover is the
automorphic shadow of a gerbe with differential-character data on
a singular degeneration. No target-space interpretation of the
eighth root of the DT zeta is known; the swampland conjecture
status of the associated throat is open (Wave-19 P4 Cycle~5).

\emph{(c) $N=8$ CY$_4$ scope.} The MTW host $\Kum^{3}(A)$ is a
holomorphic-symplectic fourfold, not a CY$_3$; its natural
enumerative object is the Kummer-type DT$_4$ count (Oh--Thomas),
with the $(\Z/8)$-quotient producing a CY$_3$ only after resolution.
Whether $\Delfnm{0}{8}$ arises from a CY$_4$ DT$_4$ generating
function or from the CY$_3$-resolution reduced class is the open
scope question. Both are consistent with $c_8(0)=0$ and
$\Delfnm{0}{8}$ degenerating to a modular function.

\subsection*{Preface-grade summary}

The Gritsenko--Cl\'ery census inscribes exactly eight paramodular
forms $\Delfnm{w_N}{N}$ of simplest diagonal divisor, weights
$(5,2,1,1,\tfrac12,1,\tfrac14,0)$; by Borcherds's singular-theta weight
formula, each carries the invariant $\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$ extracted
directly from the $(q^0\zeta^0)$-head of its weak Jacobi precursor,
producing the sequence
$\kBKM\in\{5,2,1,1,\tfrac12,1,\tfrac14,0\}$ uniformly across the
full census. On the Nikulin-admissible CHL slice $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$
the geometry, CoHA, BPS, and automorphic corners close on a single
CHL CY$_3$ host $X_N=(K3\times E)/(\Z/N)$: Oberdieck--Pixton reduced
DT computes $Z^{X_N}_{L,1}=-C_N/\Phi_N^{\,2}$ at primitive classes;
the Davison--Meinhardt CoHA recovers $\Yplus(X_N)$; its primitive
Lie subalgebra matches the Gritsenko--Nikulin BKM superalgebra
$\gD{w_N}{N}$ at the level of root multiplicities; each $\Phi_N$
arises as the Borcherds singular theta lift. On the non-CHL slice
$N\in\{5,7,8\}$ the automorphic corner remains a theorem via the
metaplectic/spin/degenerate extensions, but the geometric host
deforms: $N=5$ lands on the Borcea--Voisin threefold
$X_5^{\mathrm{BV}}$ with half-integer root multiplicities, $N=7$
resists every smooth free $\Z/7$-quotient and requires an
order-$4$ gerbe on a singular degeneration, $N=8$ lands on the
Mongardi--Tari--Wandel Kummer fourfold quotient at the degenerate
weight-$0$ endpoint. The unifying statement is the universal
identity $\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$ for $N\in\{1,\ldots,8\}$; the
three frontier problems --- bracket-level PBW at every $N$, the
$N=7$ singular host, the $N=8$ CY$_4$ scope --- locate exactly
where the four-corner picture requires structure beyond the
singular theta lift to close.
