\section*{Wave~15 R4 --- what the slides say on $N \geq 5$}

\subsection*{The final slides, read line by line}

The closing pages of the presentation
(\texttt{/tmp/automorphic-slides.txt}, lines~937--953) split the
twisted Donaldson--Thomas series $Z^X_{L, h_M}$ into cases by the
symplectic order $N$ and then terminate without further content:
\begin{quote}
\begin{tabbing}
  L943: \=\emph{Case $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 5, 7\}$.}\quad
        \=For all $1 \leq s, r \leq N - 1$ and $0 \leq k \leq N - 1$\ldots\\
  L945: \>\emph{Case $N = 4$.}\quad \>For all $s \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$\\
  L947: \>\emph{Case $N = 6$.}\quad \>For all $s \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$\\
  L949: \>\emph{Case $N = 6$.}\quad \>For all $s \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$\\
  L951: \>\emph{Case $N = 8$.}\\
  L953: \>\emph{Case $N = 8$.}
\end{tabbing}
\end{quote}
The slides thus announce six case-blocks --- prime-and-unit
$\{1, 2, 3, 5, 7\}$, then $4$, then $6$ (twice), then $8$ (twice) ---
but the body of each block beyond the opening clause is absent from
the textual extraction, i.e.\ the explicit $\phi_{0, 1} \mid T_-(s, r, k)$
Hecke-coefficient tables on these slides were rendered as
images and did not survive the OCR pipeline. Paramodular weights
$k_N$ for $N = 5, 7, 8$ are \emph{not} inscribed in the textual content
of the slides.

\subsection*{What the slides \emph{do} make explicit}

Three structural statements survive intact:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=0.4ex]
\item the construction (L856--866) of
  $X = (S \times E)/(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})$ from K3 data
  $(S, g_N, h_M)$ with $N, M \leq 8$ and an $N$-torsion point
  $e_0 \in E$, including the free-action proof of smoothness;
\item Conjecture~1 (L929--936):
  \emph{every $dd$-Siegel modular form arises (up to constant) as
  $-(Z^X_{L, h_M})^{-1}$, and the attached BKM superalgebra
  $\mathfrak{g}_{(g_N, h_M)} \subseteq \mathfrak{g}_L$ has
  imaginary-simple-root multiplicities equal to the $(g_N, h_M)$-twisted
  twined K3 elliptic genus $F^{(g_N, h_M)}_L$};
\item the Oberdieck--Pixton $N = 1$ base case (L912--919):
  $Z^{S \times E} = -C / \Delta_5^2$ with $2\phi_{0, 1}$ the K3
  elliptic genus, the form from which all others arise by $T_-(s, r, k)$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{CHL-admissible versus non-CHL, per the slides}

The textual slides do \emph{not} draw the CHL-admissibility line;
they enumerate all eight orders $N \in \{1, \ldots, 8\}$ for which a
fixed-point-free $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$-diagonal action on $S \times E$
descends from a symplectic automorphism of $S$. The CHL
(Chaudhuri--Hockney--Lykken) restriction
$N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ is the arithmetic
constraint $\varphi(N) \mid 2$, equivalent to
$\mathrm{End}(E) \supseteq \mathbb{Z}[\zeta_N]$ being realisable on an
elliptic curve with an $N$-torsion point (CM on $E$). The slides
contemplate $N \in \{5, 7, 8\}$ because $\mathrm{Aut}_s(S)$ admits
symplectic automorphisms of those orders (Nikulin 1980, order
$\leq 8$); the elliptic partner $E$ simply carries no $N$-torsion CM
structure for $N \in \{5, 7\}$ and only a restricted one for $N = 8$.

\subsection*{Comparison to Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008}

Gritsenko--Cl\'ery (arXiv:0812.3962 Thm.~1.2) classify
simplest-divisor Siegel paramodular forms by pairs $(N, t)$:
\[
  (N, t) \in \bigl\{
    (1, 1),\ (1, 2),\ (1, 3),\ (1, 4),\
    (2, 1),\ (3, 1),\ (4, 1),\ (2, 2)
  \bigr\},
\]
weights $(k_{N, t}) = (5, 2, 1, 1, 2, 1, 1, 2)$.
\emph{The Gritsenko--Cl\'ery census contains no $N \in \{5, 7, 8\}$
entry}: the slides' case-blocks $\{5, 7\}$ and $\{8\}$ do not
produce simplest-divisor forms --- they produce paramodular forms
with \emph{multiple} Humbert divisors, reached by Gritsenko's
arithmetic-lift framework (Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998
alg-geom/9504006) applied to $2\phi_{0,1} \mid T_-(N)$. Vol~III's
inscription in
\texttt{chapters/examples/cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}
(Remark~\ref{rem:gritsenko-clery-orbit}, lines~2886--2911) already
records this: the programme-active ladder
$\{\Phi_{N, 1} : N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}\}$ equals the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery
$t = 1$ slice (four forms) augmented by $N = 6$ via the
arithmetic-lift framework, and the three remaining Gritsenko--Cl\'ery
forms $\Phi_{1, 2}, \Phi_{1, 3}, \Phi_{1, 4}$ govern $(1, t)$-polarised
abelian surfaces outside $K3 \times E$.

\subsection*{Is Vol~III missing $N = 5, 7, 8$?}

\emph{No}: Vol~III's scope restriction is justified, documented, and
mathematically forced, not silent:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*), leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=0.3ex]
\item Remark~\ref{rem:cm-stability-5-of-8}
  (\texttt{cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex} L2979--2994) identifies
  $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ as the CM-on-$E$ compatibility set
  $\varphi(N) \mid 2$; $N \in \{5, 7, 8\}$ fail this and so the
  $K3 \times E$ quotient construction does not yield a
  CY$_3$ with the required $\mathbb{Z}/N$-CM-torsion lift.
\item Remark~\ref{rem:nikulin-bound-joint} (L2996--3019) records the
  Oberdieck 2021 bound $NM \leq 8$: the eight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery pairs
  saturate the bound, the five programme-active $(N, 1)$ entries lie
  in the untwisted $M = 1$ subchamber, and the remaining three
  $((1, 2), (1, 3), (1, 4))$ carry genuine $h_M$-twists captured by
  Conjecture~\ref{conj:twisted-twined-bkm-ladder-gritsenko-clery}.
\item The slides' $N \in \{5, 7, 8\}$ blocks describe
  $\mathrm{Aut}_s(S)$ orbit structure, \emph{not} a $K3 \times E$
  CY$_3$ orbifold: the corresponding Borcherds lifts are paramodular
  forms with multiple Humbert divisors, outside the simplest-divisor
  eight-form census.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{What extending the programme would require}

Three pieces, individually non-trivial:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=0.3ex]
\item replace $K3 \times E$ with a K3-fibered CY$_3$ admitting an
  order-$N$ symplectic automorphism that does \emph{not} require
  CM on the fibre --- e.g.\ the $N = 8$ Kummer construction of
  Mongardi--Tari--Wandel 2018 arXiv:1610.06216, where the orbifold
  CY$_3$ is a resolution rather than a free quotient;
\item compute the paramodular weight $k_N$ via Borcherds singular
  theta on $\Lambda^{2,1}_{II; N, 1}$ for $N \in \{5, 7, 8\}$,
  with constant term $c_N(0)$ read off $2\phi_{0,1} \mid T_-(N)$
  --- the weak Jacobi Hecke expansion is explicit in
  Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 \S3, giving (conjecturally)
  $(k_5, k_7, k_8) = (1, 1, 1)$ with $c_N(0) \in \{2, 2, 2\}$,
  extending the programme sequence
  $(5, 2, 1, 1, 1) \to (5, 2, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1)$;
\item extend Theorem~\ref{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal}
  $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ across
  $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8\}$, with $\kcat$ of the
  non-CHL orbifold CY$_3$ computed afresh (Künneth breaks off the
  fibre product); the universal identity has been verified only on
  the CHL slice.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Frontier statement}

The CHL-admissibility line is a physical-moduli-space constraint on
the elliptic partner $E$, not a restriction on the BKM / paramodular
side. The eight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery forms are the paramodular-side
truth; the five programme-active entries are the subset compatible
with the $K3 \times E$ geometric construction. The frontier is to
attach Borcherds--Kac--Moody superalgebras to the $N \in \{5, 7, 8\}$
forms through an alternative K3-fibered orbifold geometry, thereby
completing the Vol~III ladder from five entries to eight.
