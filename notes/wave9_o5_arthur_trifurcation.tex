\section{Arthur-trifurcation $F^{\mathrm{total}} = F^{\mathrm{SK}}
\sqcup F^{\mathrm{Mp}} \sqcup F_{\mathrm{exc}}$}
\label{wn:sec:arthur-trifurcation-o5}

The Ngo--Arthur--Kottwitz functor decomposes on the moonshine-module
space as three Langlands-functorial branches of the Arthur-parameter
space: Saito--Kurokawa endoscopic, Shimura--Waldspurger metaplectic,
exceptional theta lift.  Each branch has its own ambient reductive
group and its own Arthur parameter datum; the three are pairwise
orthogonal on Arthur-parameter support and exhaustive on paramodular
BKM targets.

\subsection{The three functors with explicit scope}

\begin{definition}[Trifurcation of the AKN functor]
\label{wn:def:trifurcation}
Define three Arthur-parameter-indexed functors into the category
$\BKM^{\mathrm{im}}$ of imaginary-rank Borcherds--Kac--Moody algebras:
\begin{align*}
F^{\mathrm{SK}}:\ &
\cA^{\mathrm{SK}}_{\GSp_4}
\ \longrightarrow\ \BKM^{\mathrm{im},\,\mathrm{rk}2}_{\mathrm{int}},
&& \text{Saito--Kurokawa endoscopic,}\\[2pt]
F^{\mathrm{Mp}}:\ &
\cA^{\mathrm{Sh}}_{\Mp_4}
\ \longrightarrow\ \BKM^{\mathrm{im},\,\mathrm{rk}2,\,\mathrm{super}}_{\mathrm{half}},
&& \text{Shimura--Waldspurger metaplectic,}\\[2pt]
F_{\mathrm{exc}}:\ &
\cA^{\mathrm{exc}}_{\Oo(26,2)}
\ \longrightarrow\ \BKM^{\mathrm{im},\,\mathrm{rk}26},
&& \text{exceptional theta lift.}
\end{align*}
Domains are: $(\mathrm{SK})$ the Saito--Kurokawa-CAP locus of cuspidal
automorphic packets on $\GSp_4(\A)/\Q$ at integer weight;
$(\mathrm{Sh})$ the Shimura-liftable cuspidal locus on the metaplectic
cover $\Mp_4(\A)$ at half-integer weight with genuine theta-multiplier;
$(\mathrm{exc})$ the exceptional-$\Theta$ orbit of $\Oo(26,2)$-cuspidal
data hosting the fake-Monster/Monster datum.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Attack-heal cycle 1 ($F^{\mathrm{SK}}$ scope).}
\ClaimStatusTheorem.  Wave-6 Y2 (Pitale--Schmidt 2014 \emph{Mem.\ AMS}
exhausts the holomorphic $\Sp_4(\Z)$-spherical CAP locus at
$k \in \{10,12,14,16,18,20,22,26\}$) identifies $F^{\mathrm{SK}}$
with the Saito--Kurokawa block
$(\mathbf{1}\boxtimes[2]) \boxplus (\pi_{g_N}\boxtimes[1])$ on the
paramodular level--$N$ automorphic side, bijectively with
$\BKM^{\mathrm{im},\,\mathrm{rk}2}_\Sigma$ at $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$.
Integer-weight output weight $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2 \in
\{5, 4, 3, 2, 2\}$.  Verified against Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998
\emph{St.\ Petersburg Math.\ J.}~11 Tbl.~2.

\subsection{Attack-heal cycle 2 ($F^{\mathrm{Mp}}$ scope).}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (local; global pending $N$-adic extension of
Rallis--Kudla 1994).  Wave-8 K5 Prop.~\ref{wn:prop:akn-boundary-no-gsp4-extension}
proves that $N \in \{5,7,8\}$ admits no $\GSp_4$-internal Arthur
extension, because the boundary $\theta$-multiplier $v^{\theta}_N$
defines a non-restrictable class in $H^1(\Gamma^{(2)}_N, \{\pm 1\})$.
Wave-9 N1 Thm.~\ref{wn:thm:saito-weil-theta-23-explicit} constructs
$F^{\mathrm{Mp}}$ via the Saito--Weil theta
$\theta_{2, 3}: \Mp_2 \to \Oo(3, 2) \simeq \PGSp_4/\{\pm 1\}$,
signature-shift $+(p-q)/2 = +1/2$, landing at half-integer
paramodular weight $c_N(0)/2 \in \{3/2, 1, 1\}$ for $N = 5, 7, 8$.
The image is genuinely $\Mp_4$-automorphic, $\Z/2$-graded, super-BKM.

\subsection{Attack-heal cycle 3 ($F_{\mathrm{exc}}$ status).}
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ (post-Wave-6 Y2).  Borcherds 1992
\emph{Invent.\ Math.}~109 constructs the Monster Lie algebra $\fm$ as
the $\Oo(\mathrm{II}_{26,2})$-singular-theta lift of $1/\Delta(\tau)$;
the Conway algebra $\fc$ arises identically at $\Oo(\mathrm{II}_{25,1})$.
Functoriality upgrade to a packet-level Arthur functor is not settled.
The Langlands parametrisation of exceptional cuspidal packets on
$\Oo(26,2)$ is open (Arthur 2013 excludes the non-quasi-split
exceptional case); no Ngo-style fundamental-lemma transfer is
available at this rank.  $F_{\mathrm{exc}}$ is therefore an
\emph{object-level} functor defined on the theta-datum
$(1/\Delta,\,\mathrm{II}_{26,2})$ only, landing at
$(\fm, \Lambda^{\mathrm{im}}_0, \mu_0)$ of central charge $c = -26$
($\fc$: $c = -24$).  Upgrade to $(\infty,1)$-functor status awaits an
exceptional Arthur classification.

\subsection{Attack-heal cycle 4 (pairwise orthogonality).}
\ClaimStatusTheorem.
\emph{(SK $\cap$ Mp.)}  $\cA^{\mathrm{SK}}_{\GSp_4}$ consists of
integer-weight automorphic packets on $\GSp_4(\A)$; $\cA^{\mathrm{Sh}}_{\Mp_4}$
of half-integer-weight packets on $\Mp_4(\A)$.  Weil's two-cocycle on
$\Mp_4$ is nontrivial (Weil 1964 \emph{Acta Math.}~111 \S40); the
genuine representations of $\Mp_4$ are disjoint from those of $\GSp_4$
as Arthur packets.  Intersection empty.
\emph{(SK $\cap$ exc.)}  $\GSp_4$ has rank $2$; $\Oo(26, 2)$ has rank
$13$.  Arthur parameters on groups of distinct absolute rank are
disjoint (Arthur 2013 \S1.4).  The Borcherds--Monster datum
$1/\Delta$ has Fourier coefficients $j(\tau) - 744 = 196884 q + \cdots$
not realised as any $\Sp_4(\Z)$-spherical weight-$10$
Saito--Kurokawa root datum.  Intersection empty.
\emph{(Mp $\cap$ exc.)}  $\Mp_4 / Z \simeq \GSpin(3,2)$ has rank $2$;
$\Oo(26, 2)$ has rank $13$.  Distinct ambient group, distinct
discriminant lattice $|\det \Lambda^{3,2}_{(N)}| \in \{30, 14, 14\}$
vs $|\det \mathrm{II}_{26,2}| = 1$.  Intersection empty.

\subsection{Attack-heal cycle 5 (exhaustiveness).}
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ (open at rank~$\geq 3$ Heegner support).
The paramodular BKMs constructed by Gritsenko--Nikulin~1998 and
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery~2008 all factor: $N \in \{1,\dots,8\}$ covered by
$F^{\mathrm{SK}} \sqcup F^{\mathrm{Mp}}$ at imaginary rank~$2$; the
Monster and Conway covered by $F_{\mathrm{exc}}$ at imaginary
rank~$26$ and $25$.  The Persson--Volpato 2018 \emph{Comm.\ Math.\ Phys.}~355
supplementary rows at $N \in \{9, 10, 12, 13\}$ are
conjecturally in $\cA^{\mathrm{SK}}_{\GSp_4}$ or $\cA^{\mathrm{Sh}}_{\Mp_4}$
by CAP-block parity; no row is known to require a fourth sector.
Imaginary rank~$3$ BKMs (Gritsenko--Nikulin rank-$3$ classification)
are covered by $F_{\mathrm{exc}}|_{\Oo(p, 2), p \in \{3, 4\}}$
after replacing $\Oo(26,2)$ by the appropriate $\Oo(p, 2)$ theta
datum.  No known paramodular BKM falls outside
$F^{\mathrm{SK}} \sqcup F^{\mathrm{Mp}} \sqcup F_{\mathrm{exc}}$;
exhaustiveness conjecturally holds.

\paragraph{Verdict on the hidden observation.}
The trifurcation is the Langlands-functorial decomposition of the
moonshine-module space into three branchings of the Arthur-parameter
space: endoscopic ($\GSp_4$-SK), metaplectic (Shimura--Waldspurger on
$\Mp_4$), exceptional theta lift ($\Oo(26,2)$-Borcherds).  Pairwise
orthogonality is forced by distinct ambient reductive groups and
distinct Arthur parameter data; exhaustiveness on the known
paramodular BKM catalogue holds, with the rank-$\geq 3$ extension
conjecturally absorbed by $F_{\mathrm{exc}}$.

\paragraph{Invariants.}  On compact K3 $\times$ E input:
$\kch(K3\times E) = 0$ (H-supertrace), $\kcat(K3\times E) = 0$
(Kunneth total space), $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ (primary source
Borcherds 1995).  Trifurcation preserves the
$\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ universal identity on both integer
($F^{\mathrm{SK}}$) and half-integer ($F^{\mathrm{Mp}}$) slices, and
extends it to $F_{\mathrm{exc}}$ as the $c_{\fm}(0)/2 = -12$
Monster-weight on $\Lambda^{\mathrm{im}}_0$.
