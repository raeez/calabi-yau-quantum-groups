\section{Arthur Trifurcation for Paramodular BKM Targets}
\label{wn:sec:arthur-trifurcation-o5}

\paragraph{Claim.}
The constructed moonshine and paramodular BKM targets decompose by
automorphic input into three Langlands-theoretic channels:
\[
F^{\mathrm{total}} =
F^{\mathrm{SK}}\sqcup F^{\mathrm{Mp}}\sqcup F_{\mathrm{exc}}.
\]
The Saito-Kurokawa channel is the CAP/endoscopic locus on $\GSp_4$;
the metaplectic channel is the genuine Shimura-Waldspurger locus on
$\Mp_4$ or on an explicitly specified multiplier cover; the exceptional
channel is the orthogonal singular-theta locus containing the Monster,
Fake-Monster, and Conway constructions. The three channels are disjoint
by ambient group, multiplier character, and lattice datum. Exhaustiveness
is asserted only for the rows whose product, multiplier, and denominator
data are displayed below.

\paragraph{Saito-Kurokawa channel.}
Let $\cA^{\mathrm{SK}}_{\GSp_4}$ be the holomorphic CAP locus of
cuspidal automorphic packets on $\GSp_4(\A_\Q)$. The associated
paramodular BKM rows over the CHL slice
$N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ have denominator constants and Borcherds weights
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
N & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 6\\ \hline
c_N(0) & 10 & 8 & 6 & 4 & 2\\
\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2 & 5 & 4 & 3 & 2 & 1
\end{array}
\]
These are weights of Borcherds denominator products. The calculation is
independent of the compact Hodge supertrace and of the categorical Euler
characteristic. The functorial reading is endoscopic: the relevant packet
is Saito-Kurokawa type, and the denominator is supplied by the
Gritsenko-Nikulin or Gritsenko-Clery lift after the Jacobi seed,
multiplier, chamber, and product normalisation have been fixed.

\paragraph{Metaplectic channel.}
Let $\cA^{\mathrm{Sh}}_{\Mp_4}$ be the genuine half-integral-weight
Shimura-Waldspurger locus on the metaplectic cover. The
Gritsenko-Clery diagonal-divisor catalogue is a separate eight-row
catalogue:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccccccc}
F & \Delta_5 & \Delta_2 & \nabla_3 & \Delta_1
  & \nabla_2 & \Delta_{1/2} & \nabla_{3/2} & Q_1\\ \hline
2\,\mathrm{wt}(F) & 10 & 4 & 6 & 2 & 4 & 1 & 3 & 2
\end{array}
\]
It overlaps the CHL ladder only at $\Delta_5$. The half-integral rows
$\Delta_{1/2}$ and $\nabla_{3/2}$ carry genuine multiplier systems, so
they lie in the metaplectic channel until a descent datum is supplied.
The CHL constants $(10,8,6,4,2)$ and the diagonal-divisor row data
$(10,4,6,2,4,1,3,2)$ have distinct indexing conventions and distinct
geometric scopes.

\paragraph{Exceptional orthogonal channel.}
Borcherds' singular-theta construction supplies orthogonal BKM
denominators, including the Fake-Monster denominator on
$\mathrm{II}_{25,1}$, while the Monster and Conway objects enter through
their vertex-algebra and Leech-lattice inputs. This is an object-level
orthogonal theta-lift channel. Arthur's classical-group packet
classification and Weissauer's $\GSp_4$ endoscopy supply the packet
grammar for the first channel; the full singular-theta locus has only the
constructed BKM objects recorded here.

\paragraph{Orthogonality.}
Representations in the Saito-Kurokawa channel are ordinary automorphic
packets on $\GSp_4$ at integral weight. Representations in the
metaplectic channel are genuine representations of $\Mp_4$ or of a
specified multiplier cover; the central $\mu_2$ acts through the Weil
multiplier. Objects in the exceptional channel live on orthogonal lattices
with different rank and discriminant form. A target can cross channels
only after an explicit functorial transfer, descent, or square-root datum
has been constructed.

\paragraph{Known scope.}
The rows $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ belong to the integer SK channel with
the weights displayed above. The half-integral diagonal-divisor rows
belong to the genuine $\Mp_4$ or multiplier-cover channel. The Monster,
Fake-Monster, and Conway objects belong to the orthogonal theta channel.
Higher-level paramodular rows acquire a channel only after their Jacobi
seed, multiplier, chamber, and Borcherds product have been specified.

\paragraph{Invariants.}
The compact $K3\times E$ invariants are separated by construction:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|l}
\text{invariant} & \text{value} & \text{source}\\ \hline
\kcat(K3\times E) & 0 &
\chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\chi(\mathcal O_E)=2\cdot 0\\
\kch(K3\times E) & 0 &
\text{compact Hodge supertrace on the total space}\\
\kHeis(K3\times E) & 3 &
\text{Mukai-Heisenberg specialisation}\\
\kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5}) & 5 &
c_1(0)/2=10/2\\
\kfiber(K3\times E) & 24 &
\text{Mukai-lattice rank of the K3 fibre}
\end{array}
\]
The trifurcation uses the BKM row only through the denominator weight
$\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$; it leaves the compact total-space invariants
and the Mukai-Heisenberg specialisation in their own lanes.

\paragraph{Hall and Yangian branches.}
The BKM-side K3 object is the compact Hall-Drinfeld double of the
positive Hall half after the negative half, Hopf pairing, radical
quotient, coproduct, completion, centre control, and associator
compatibilities have been supplied. The K3 Yangian branch is the Mukai
self-mirror current-algebra branch with $24$ Heisenberg generators and
degree-$(24,24)$ structure function. The Arthur-trifurcation statement
concerns the automorphic and BKM denominator branch, while Drinfeld
Yangian presentations remain confined to the Mukai branch.

\paragraph{Primary-source anchors.}
Arthur 2013 supplies the endoscopic packet framework for classical
groups, with Ngo 2010 as the fundamental-lemma input. Weissauer 2009
gives the $\GSp_4$ endoscopic surface needed for Saito-Kurokawa packets.
Andrianov 1974 and Zagier's Saito-Kurokawa account identify the
spinor-$L$ factor of the Igusa square line. Waldspurger 1980 and Howe
1979 govern the metaplectic theta correspondence. Gritsenko-Nikulin
1998, Gritsenko 1999, and Borcherds 1995 give the denominator-weight
identity. Gritsenko-Clery 2013 gives the eight diagonal-divisor forms.
Borcherds 1992 and Borcherds 1998 supply the orthogonal singular-theta
and automorphic-product constructions.
