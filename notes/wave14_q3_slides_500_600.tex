\section*{Wave-14 Q3: slide-central frontier (lines 500--600)}

Lines~500--600 house the Fourier-coefficient machinery that converts
the Borcherds-lift formula for $\Delta_5$ into the denominator
identity of a GKM superalgebra. The content is the spine of the
N=1 anchor for $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$.

\paragraph{1. Inventory.} Five mathematical arcs traverse these
lines.

\textbf{(a) Parameterisation of Fourier support (496--498).}
For $n, l, m \in \Z$ with $n, m > 0$, $n \equiv l \equiv m \equiv 1
\pmod{2}$, and $4nm - l^2 > 0$, each $\Delta_5$-Fourier mode
$f(n, l, m) \exp(\pi i(n z_1 + l z_2 + m z_3))$ is identified with
$64 \cdot m(a) \exp(-\pi i(\rho + a, z))$ at
\[
  a \;=\; (n-1)f_2 \;-\; \tfrac{1}{2}(l-1)f_3 \;+\; (m-1)f_{-2}
  \;\in\; (\LII)^* = \tfrac{1}{2}\LII,
  \qquad m(a) = -\tfrac{1}{64}f(n,l,m).
\]
So $\rho + a \in \mathcal{C}(\LII)_+$, $m(a) \in \Z$, and
$m(0) = -1$.

\textbf{(b) O(\LII)$_+$-invariance (502--514).} Lemma: $\Delta_5$ is
either invariant or anti-invariant under $O(\LII)_+$ acting through
$w \in \Wtwo$ and $a \in \mathrm{Aut}(\PII, \mathrm{prim})$, via
$\Delta_5(w \cdot az) = \det(w)\Delta_5(z)$. The antisymmetrised
Weyl sum form
\[
  \Delta_5(z) \;=\; \tfrac{1}{64}\!\!\!\sum_{w \in \Wtwo}\!\det(w)
  \Bigl(\sum_{\rho + a \in (\LII)^* \cap \LII}\!\!m(a) \exp(-\pi i
  (w(\rho + a), z))\Bigr)
\]
is the precise Weyl-Kac-Borcherds shape.

\textbf{(c) Cone-chain and positivity (519--563).} The cone-chain
$\DII^* \subset \CII = \CII \subset (\DII)^{**}$ with
$a \in \R_{\geq 0}\PII$ if and only if $a \in \R_{\geq 0}\CII^* \cap
\CII$ pins the Fourier support: summation runs over $a \in \LII
\cap \R_{>0}\PII$ (with $a = 0$ giving $m(0) = -1$). The positivity
$(\rho + a, \delta_i) < 0$ is forced by $(\rho + a, \delta_i) \in \Z$
plus the nonvanishing constraint $m(a) \neq 0 \Leftrightarrow
(\rho + a, \delta_i) \neq 0$. Hence $a \in 2(\LII)^* = \LII$.

\textbf{(d) Rewritten Weyl sum (576--579).}
\[
  \tfrac{\Delta_5(z)}{64} \;=\; \sum_{w \in \Wtwo}\!\det(w)
  \Bigl(e^{-\pi i(w(\rho), z)} - \!\!\!\sum_{a \in \LII \cap \R_{>0}\PII}\!\!m(a)\,
  e^{-\pi i(w(\rho + a), z)}\Bigr).
\]
This is the cone-truncated Weyl-Kac-Borcherds denominator.

\textbf{(e) Vertex-ray $\tau(a)$-implicit definition (583--599).}
The three light-like primitive vertices $\{2f_2,\, 2f_{-2},\, 2f_2
- 2f_3 + 2f_{-2}\}$ on $\partial\overline{\PII}$ satisfy $(a_0, a_0)
= 0$ and are transitively permuted by $\mathrm{Aut}(\PII)$. Picking
$a_0 = 2f_2$ (the other two follow by lattice-isometry reflections),
the generating identity
\[
  1 + \tfrac{1}{64}\sum_{t \in \N}f(1 + 2t, 1, 1)\,q^t
  \;=\; \prod_{k \in \N}(1 - q^k)^9
\]
holds, and more generally
\[
  1 - \sum_{t \in \N}m(ta_0)\,q^t \;=\; \prod_{t \in \N}(1 -
  q^t)^{\tau(ta_0)}
\]
\emph{implicitly defines} $\tau$-coefficients along the isotropic
ray $\R_{\geq 0}a_0$. Specialisation at $a_0$ primitive: $\tau(a_0)
= 9$.

\paragraph{2. $f(n,l,m)$-table cross-check.} Match against $\phi_{0,1}
= (r^{-1} + 10 + r) + q(10r^{-2} - 64 r^{-1} + 108 - 64 r + 10 r^2)
+ O(q^2)$ using $f(n, l) = $ coefficient of $q^n r^l$:
$f(0, 0) = 10$, $f(0, \pm 1) = 1$, $f(1, \pm 2) = 10$,
$f(1, \pm 1) = -64$, $f(1, 0) = 108$. Verification of the
$\tau(a_0) = 9$ identity: expand $\prod_k(1-q^k)^9 = 1 - 9q +
27q^2 - 12q^3 + \ldots$ against $\tfrac{1}{64}f(1,1,1) = 1$
giving $\tfrac{1}{64}f(3, 1, 1) = -9$, i.e. $f(3,1,1) = -576$;
cross-checked with Lorgat 2020 Section~4.

\paragraph{3. Multiplicity formula.} Borcherds 1998 (alg-geom/9609022)
Theorem~10.1 gives $\mathrm{mult}_\alpha = f(nm, l)$ for root
$\alpha = (n, l, m)$; the slide lines set up the $(a, \rho + a)
\leftrightarrow (n, l, m)$ dictionary that makes this formula
visible. The index pairing $(\rho + a, \rho + a) =
\frac{1}{2}(4nm - l^2) - 2$ (with the $-2$ from
$(\rho, \rho)$) controls both the index stratification and the
$(1 - q^k)^{\mathrm{mult}\,\alpha}$ Borcherds-product exponents.

\paragraph{5. Anchor of $\kBKM = 5$ to $c_1(0) = 10$.} Triangulation
from the three independent extraction paths: (I)~\emph{slide
line~484--487} has $f(1, l, m)$ parameterised via
$m(a) = -\tfrac{1}{64}f(n,l,m)$, with $f(0, 0) = 10$ the constant
coefficient of $\phi_{0,1}$ at $q^0 r^0$ (slide lines 593--594
pin $\tfrac{1}{64}\sum f(1+2t, 1, 1)q^t$ identity); (II)~\emph{Borcherds
weight formula}: $w(\Delta_5) = \tfrac{1}{2}c_1(0) = 5$ (Borcherds
1995, Gritsenko 1999); (III)~\emph{Vol III canonical identity}
$\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ at $N=1$ (cache-certified,
\texttt{chapters/examples/cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}
Theorem~\texttt{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal}). All
three routes deliver $\kBKM = 5$. The slide lines 500--600
anchor route~(I): the \emph{explicit} appearance of $10$ in
$\phi_{0,1} = r^{-1} + 10 + r + \ldots$ is the constant coefficient
that, through the Borcherds-lift exponentiation formula $\Phi =
\exp(-\Theta \cdot \phi_{0,1})$, produces the weight-$5$ Siegel-modular
form $\Delta_5$; the factor $\tfrac{1}{2}$ converting $10 \to 5$ is
the Borcherds weight formula $w = c(0)/2$ (Borcherds 1995
Theorem~10.1, visible at the prefactor level in the Weyl-Kac-Borcherds
denominator via $\sum_{w \in \Wtwo}\det(w)$ with $w$ weight-$1$ per
reflection).

\paragraph{6. Consequence for $\gBKM$.} The cone-truncated Weyl sum
(arc~d) is the \emph{closed form} of the GKM superalgebra
$\gBKM$ denominator identity: real simple roots
$\{\delta_1, \delta_2, \delta_3\}$ generate $\Wtwo$; imaginary
simple roots stratify as $\Delta^{\mathrm{im}}_0 = \{\tau(a)\,a \mid
(a,a)=0, \tau(a) > 0\}$ (even sector) and
$\Delta^{\mathrm{im}}_1 = \{m(a)\,a \mid (a,a) < 0, m(a) < 0\}$
(odd sector). The $a_0$-vertex identity (arc~e) forces the even
imaginary multiplicity at $ta_0$ to equal $\tau(ta_0)$, with
$\tau(a_0) = 9$ controlling the primitive isotropic contribution;
the $\prod(1-q^k)^9$ factor in the Borcherds product is exactly the
sum over even-imaginary-root tower at each of the three light-like
vertices. Three vertices~$\times$ one $\eta^9$-factor each~$=$
the three isotropic-direction contributions; anti-symmetric Weyl
averaging across $\Wtwo$ (index~$6$ in $O(\LII)_+$) closes the
product into Siegel-modular weight~$5$.
