\section{Refined Donaldson--Thomas zeta at CHL level
\texorpdfstring{$N\geq 2$}{N geq 2}:
slides scope, definition status, and
the K\"unneth factorisation obstruction}
\label{wn:sec:refined-DT-wave8-l2-N-ge-2}

\providecommand{\ClaimStatusTheorem}{\textbf{[Thm]}}
\providecommand{\ClaimStatusConjectured}{\textbf{[Conj]}}
\providecommand{\ClaimStatusAxiomatic}{\textbf{[Ax]}}
\providecommand{\Hilb}{\mathrm{Hilb}}
\providecommand{\DT}{\mathrm{DT}}
\providecommand{\ZDT}{Z^{X}}
\providecommand{\ZDTN}{Z^{X^{(N)}}_{L, h_M}}

\paragraph{Slides scope.} Lorgat 2020 defines, for $X=S\times E$ with
$S$ an elliptically fibered K3, the zeta
\[
  Z^{S\times E}(q,t,p)
  \;=\; \sum_{h\geq 0}\sum_{d\geq 0}\sum_{n\in\Z}
        \DT_{n,(\beta_h,d)}\,q^{\,d-1}\,t^{\tfrac{1}{2}\langle\beta_h,\beta_h\rangle}\,(-p)^n
\]
with $\DT_{n,(\beta_h,d)}=\int_{\Hilb^n(X,\beta_h)/E}k\,e(\nu^{-1}(k))$
a Behrend-weighted Euler pairing on the quotient by the $E$-translation
(slides, ``Arithmetic Geometry of some CY3's''). At $N=1$ the slides
state the theorem $Z^{S\times E}=-C/\Delta_5^{2}$. At $N\geq 2$ the
slides promote the object $Z^{X^{(N)}}_{L,h_M}$ through the triple
$(S,g_N,h_M)$ with lattice polarisation $L$, $h_M=g_N^s$; the slides
\emph{define the notation} and \emph{conjecture}
$-1/Z^{X^{(N)}}_{L,h_M}$ reproduces the eight diagonal-divisor Siegel
forms. No refined/motivic variant appears on any slide: the slide zeta
is the \emph{numerical} Behrend-weighted DT of Joyce--Song 2012
Mem.~AMS~217, \emph{not} a $K$-theoretic or motivic refinement.

\paragraph{Attack--heal cycle I: is $\ZDTN$ even defined at $N\geq 2$?}
\textsc{Attack.} The quotient $X^{(N)}=(S\times E)/\Z_N$ is a smooth
CY$_3$ (slides; free $\Z_N$-action via $(s,e)\mapsto(gs,e+e_0)$), so
it is a global quotient of a smooth variety by a free finite group.
The Hilbert scheme $\Hilb^n(X^{(N)},\beta)$ exists and is proper; the
Behrend function $\nu:\Hilb^n\to\Z$ is defined on any projective scheme
(Behrend 2009 Ann.~Math.~170~\S1); the reduced virtual class exists on
$X^{(N)}$ by the Maulik--Pandharipande--Thomas 2010 extension. The
$E$-action on $X^{(N)}$ is the residual action through the quotient
$E/\langle e_0\rangle\cong E$ (degree-$N$ isogeny), still inducing a
proper free quotient $\Hilb^n(X^{(N)},\beta)/E$. \textsc{Heal.} The
numerical $\ZDTN$ is \ClaimStatusTheorem{} \emph{as a well-defined
formal series}. The open content is the identification with automorphic
data, not the definition.

\paragraph{Attack--heal cycle II: motivic vs K-theoretic vs numerical.}
\textsc{Attack.} Three distinct refinements coexist: (a) MNOP $2006$
Publ.~Math.~IHES $104$ $K$-theoretic/equivariant DT via
$T$-localisation on $\C^3$; (b) Joyce--Song $2012$ motivic DT valued
in the Grothendieck ring of varieties with $\mu$-equivariant structure;
(c) Maulik--Nekrasov--Okounkov--Pandharipande $2006$ refined topological
vertex at genus $g$ in $y=e^{\mathrm i\hbar}$. The slides reciprocal
$-1/Z=\Delta_5^2$ is a statement in type~(Joyce--Song numerical Behrend);
the refinement to~(a) requires a $T$-fixed locus, absent on CHL orbifolds
for generic $N$. \textsc{Heal.} At $N\geq 2$ the motivic refinement
exists by Joyce--Song $2012$ motivic wall-crossing but is \emph{not}
automatically a Siegel-modular generating function: the Borcherds
multiplicative lift of $2\phi^{(g_N,h_M)}_{0,1}$ is a numerical identity
on $c_N(nm,\ell)$, whence only the numerical type~(JS) zeta inherits
automorphic invariance. \ClaimStatusConjectured{}: motivic $\ZDTN$
admits a $\mu_N$-equivariant Hodge structure refining $\Delta_{5,N}$
to a vector-valued Jacobi form, but no CHL-level
proof exists in the literature.

\paragraph{Attack--heal cycle III: KKV at CHL level.}
\textsc{Attack.} At $N=1$ Pandharipande--Thomas $2014$
J.~Amer.~Math.~Soc.~$23$:$267$ proves KKV for primitive $K3$ classes
via stable pairs; Oberdieck--Pandharipande 2016 arXiv:1605.05473
propagates KKV $+$ reduced MNOP through the degeneration
$E\rightsquigarrow\P^{1}_{\mathrm{nodal}}$ to obtain $\ZDT=-\Phi_{10}^{-1}$.
At CHL level the required input is twined KKV: $n^{g_N}_{g,\beta}$ for
$g_N$-equivariant Gopakumar--Vafa invariants on K3. \textsc{Heal.} Twined
KKV is established on the primitive K3 class by
Cheng--Harrison--Volpato 2018 Adv.~Theor.~Math.~Phys.~$22$:$1983$ for
$g_N\in M_{24}$; propagation to $X^{(N)}$ requires the Oberdieck 2018
degeneration argument $g_N$-equivariantly. This is not
in the literature; it is \ClaimStatusConjectured{} consistent with
Oberdieck--Pandharipande 2016 Thm.~1. The $(N,\mathrm{id})$ scope is
reachable; the $(N,h_M)$ simultaneous twining requires doubly-twined KKV,
which is not established.

\paragraph{Attack--heal cycle IV: K\"unneth factorisation hypothesis.}
\textsc{Attack.} Does $\ZDTN=Z^{K3}_{L,h_M}(q,y)\cdot Z^{E}_{L,g_N}(q,p)
\cdot\Xi^{(N,M)}(q,y,p)$ hold K\"unneth-multiplicatively? On $X=S\times E$
($N=1$) the DT/GW generating function does \emph{not} factor because
the reduced virtual class on $K3\times E$ mixes K3 classes with $E$
translations through the $E$-quotient; Oberdieck's identity
$\ZDT=-C/\Delta_5^2$ has \emph{one} Siegel denominator, not a product.
\textsc{Heal.} The correct factorisation is automorphic, not
multiplicative on DT counts: $\Delta_5$ is the Gritsenko additive lift
of $\phi_{5,2}=\eta^9\vartheta_1(\tau,2z)$, i.e.\ $\Delta_5$ lives on
$\mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z)$ and $1/Z$ transforms for the paramodular group
$\Gamma_t(N)$ (slides, ``Diagonal Divisor Siegel Modular Forms''). The
hypothesised factor $\Xi^{(N,M)}$ is therefore \emph{not present}: the
CHL-level zeta is built by a single twisted Borcherds lift of
$\phi^{(g_N,h_M)}_{0,1}$, and the apparent Künneth splitting is an
artefact of specialisation to the K3 or $E$ variable. The hidden
observation is \ClaimStatusAxiomatic{} false at $N\geq 2$: the refined
DT does \emph{not} factor.

\paragraph{Attack--heal cycle V: obstruction to extending Oberdieck 2018
to CHL.} \textsc{Attack.} Oberdieck 2018 Duke 167:3045 proves
$\ZDT=-\Phi_{10}^{-1}$ on $S\times E$ via three inputs:
(I) reduced-virtual-class degeneration; (II) MPT stable-pairs KKV;
(III) rubber localisation on $E\rightsquigarrow\P^1$ giving the
$\eta^{24}=\Delta_{10}/$ vertex factor. \textsc{Heal.} At CHL level:
(I$'$) $\Z_N$-equivariant reduced virtual class is established
(Maulik--Thomas 2018 J.~Topol.~$11$:$527$, motivic refinement);
(II$'$) twined KKV on primitive class holds for $g_N\in M_{24}$
(Cheng--Harrison--Volpato 2018), but the doubly-twined
$(g_N,h_M)$-case is open when $h_M=g_N^s$ with $s\neq 0$;
(III$'$) rubber localisation on $E/\langle e_0\rangle$ produces
$\eta_{g_N}^{24/N}$ with $\eta_{g_N}(\tau)=\prod_k(1-q^k)^{a_0(g_N,k)}$
the twisted eta product (Mason 1985 Math.~Ann.~272:539). The
obstruction is exactly step~(II$'$) at $h_M\neq\mathrm{id}$; this is
the content of Lorgat 2020 Conj.~1 part~(i).

\paragraph{$\kappa$-witness.} On $X^{(N)}$, $\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}
(X^{(N)})=\chi(\cO_{X^{(N)}})=\chi(\cO_{S\times E})/N=0$ (K\"unneth
on total space, then free quotient); $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}
(\Delta_{5,N})=c_N(0)/2\in\{5,4,3,2,1\}$ for $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$
(Borcherds 1995 Invent.~Math.~$120$:$161$; Gritsenko 1999). The four
routes to $\kappa_{\bullet}\in\{\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}},\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}},
\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}},\kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}\}$ at CHL level remain
distinct constructions, not applications of $\Phi$ to one object.
