\section*{$N=3$ automorphic lift: $\phigthree \to \DeltaThree$ on $\Gthree$}
\label{wn:sec:wave19-x4-N3-automorphic-Delta1-3}

Among the eight paramodular cusp forms of genus~$2$ with simplest
Humbert divisor (Gritsenko--Cl\'ery~2011, arXiv:0812.3962 Thm.~1.2),
the entry at paramodular level $N=3$ is $\DeltaThree = \Phi_{3,1}$ of
weight $k_{3,1} = 1$ on $\Gthree$. Its Borcherds product is the
multiplicative lift of the $g_{3}$-twined K3 elliptic genus at Mukai
class~$3A$; the two read-offs --- Borcherds Fourier exponent vs.\
Weyl--Kac--Borcherds simple-root multiplicity --- intersect on
$\gDeltaThree$, the Borcherds--Kac--Moody Lie superalgebra whose
denominator is $\DeltaThree$.

\paragraph{Cycle 1: twined Jacobi input $\phigthree$.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Cheng--Duncan--Harvey~2014 Table~2, class~$3A$;
Gaberdiel--Hohenegger--Volpato~2012 Thm.~4; Eguchi--Hikami~2011
Prop.~3.4.) The symplectic $M_{24}$-class~$3A$ has Frame shape
$1^{6} 3^{6}$ and $\chi(K3^{g_{3}}) = 6$ (Mukai~1988 Table~2;
Nikulin~1979 Thm.~4.5: six isolated $A_{2}$ fixed points). The
$g_{3}$-twined K3 elliptic genus on the Eichler--Zagier basis reads
\[
  Z^{(g_{3})}_{K3}(\tau, z)
  \;=\;
  \tfrac{1}{4}\,\phi_{0,1}(\tau, z)
  \;+\;
  \tfrac{3}{2}\,\phi_{-2,1}(\tau, z)\,E_{3}(\tau),
\]
with $E_{3}(\tau) = 1 - 9\sum_{n\ge 1}\sigma_{1}^{(3)}(n)\,q^{n}$ the
level-$3$ Eisenstein (GHV~2012 eq.~(3.7)). The Borcherds input is
$\phigthree := \tfrac{1}{2}\,Z^{(g_{3})}_{K3}
 \in J^{w,\nu_{3}}_{0,1}(\Gamma_{0}(3))$ of weight $0$, index $1$,
order-$3$ character $\nu_{3}$. Constant-term evaluation
$[q^{0}\zeta^{0}]\,\phigthree = c_{3}(0) = 2$
(programme canonical, Lemma~\texttt{lem:c-N-zero-closed-form} of
\texttt{cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}).

\paragraph{Cycle 2: Borcherds weight and paramodular cusp form.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Borcherds~1998 Thm.~13.3; Gritsenko~1999
Thm.~1.2; Gritsenko--Cl\'ery~2011 Thm.~1.2.) The multiplicative lift
\[
  \DeltaThree(Z)
  \;=\;
  q^{A}\zeta^{B}p^{C}
  \!\!\prod_{\substack{(n,\ell,m)\in\Z^{3}\\(n,\ell,m)>0}}\!\!
    \bigl(1 - q^{n}\zeta^{\ell}p^{3m}\bigr)^{f^{(g_{3})}(4nm-\ell^{2},\,\ell)},
  \quad
  Z = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}\tau & z\\ z & \sigma\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\in\Hup,
\]
with $f^{(g_{3})}(D,\ell)$ the $q^{n}\zeta^{\ell}$-coefficients of
$\phigthree$, converges on the tube over the positive cone of
$\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}(3)$ (Borcherds~1998 \S 6). The positive cone is
$m>0$, or $m=0 \land n>0$, or $m=n=0 \land \ell>0$; the level-$3$
step in $p^{3m}$ is the paramodular shift of Gritsenko--Nikulin~1995
Part~II \S 3.4. Borcherds~1998 Thm.~13.3 forces
\[
  \mathrm{wt}(\DeltaThree) \;=\; \frac{c_{3}(0)}{2} \;=\; 1,
  \qquad
  \DeltaThree \in M_{1}(\Gthree,\nu_{3}),
\]
placing $\DeltaThree$ at position $k(3)=1$ of the
Gritsenko--Nikulin $\Delta$-tower $(k(1),\ldots,k(6)) = (5,2,1,1,1)$
and populating the Vol~III $\kBKM$-table:
\[
  \kBKM(\DeltaThree) \;=\; \frac{c_{3}(0)}{2} \;=\; 1.
\]

\paragraph{Cycle 3: lattice and Weyl datum.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Gritsenko--Nikulin~1998 Thm.~2.3;
Lorgat~2020 Prop.~2.1 at the $N=1$ template.) The $g_{3}$-invariant
hyperbolic sublattice is
$\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}(3) \simeq U \oplus \langle -6\rangle$, with
real simple roots
$\{\delta_{1}^{(3)}, \delta_{2}^{(3)}, \delta_{3}^{(3)}\}$
carrying Gram
\[
  \mathsf{C}^{(3)}
  \;=\;
  \begin{pmatrix}
     2 & -2 & -2 \\
    -2 &  2 & -2 \\
    -2 & -2 &  2
  \end{pmatrix},
  \qquad
  \det\mathsf{C}^{(3)} \;=\; -24.
\]
The Weyl vector
$\rho^{(3)} = \tfrac{2}{3}\delta_{1}^{(3)} +
 \tfrac{2}{3}\delta_{2}^{(3)} + \tfrac{5}{6}\delta_{3}^{(3)}$
determines $(A,B,C) = (\rho^{(3)},\cdot)$. The Weyl group
$W^{(3)} = W(\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}(3))$ is generated by reflections in
$\delta_{1}^{(3)}, \delta_{2}^{(3)}, \delta_{3}^{(3)}$.

\paragraph{Cycle 4: Weyl--Kac--Borcherds denominator identity.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ at lattice level
(Gritsenko--Nikulin~1998 Thm.~2.3; Borcherds~1998 Thm.~10.4);
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ at Lie-superalgebra level
(Lorgat~2020 Conj.~1 at $N=3$.)
Define $\gDeltaThree$ with real simple roots
$\Delta^{\mathrm{re}} = \{\delta_{1}^{(3)}, \delta_{2}^{(3)},
\delta_{3}^{(3)}\}$, imaginary simple roots
$\Delta^{\mathrm{im}}_{\bar 0} \cup \Delta^{\mathrm{im}}_{\bar 1}$
split by the sign of $f^{(g_{3})}(D,\ell)$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin~1998 \S 2), and positive-root multiplicities
\[
  \mult_{\gDeltaThree}(\alpha)
  \;=\;
  f^{(g_{3})}(4nm-\ell^{2},\,\ell),
  \qquad
  \alpha = n\delta_{1}^{(3)} + \ell \delta_{2}^{(3)} + m \delta_{3}^{(3)}.
\]
The denominator identity
\[
  \DeltaThree(Z)
  \;=\;
  e^{2\pi i(\rho^{(3)},Z)}
  \!\!\prod_{\alpha\in\Delta_{+}}\!\!
    \bigl(1 - e^{2\pi i(\alpha,Z)}\bigr)^{\mult_{\gDeltaThree}(\alpha)}
\]
is the Borcherds product of Cycle~2 reorganised by $W^{(3)}$-orbits.
Equality at Fourier depth $\le 3$ is Gritsenko--Cl\'ery~2011 Thm.~1.2;
convergence on $4nm-\ell^{2} \ge -1$ is Gritsenko--Nikulin~1998
Thm.~1.2. The GKM superalgebra upgrade --- odd imaginary roots from
$f^{(g_{3})}<0$, Weyl--Kac--Borcherds character formula --- is
Lorgat~2020 Conj.~1 at $N=3$.

\paragraph{Cycle 5: match to X3 BPS channel.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ for the reduced DT identification
(Bryan--Oberdieck~2018 arXiv:1811.06102 Thm.~1);
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ for the BPS-Lie-algebra identification
(Lorgat~2020 Conj.~1; wave19 X3/X4 crossbar.)
On $X^{(3)} := [K3\times E / \Z_{3}]$ (symplectic $g_{3}$ paired
with $t_{3}\in E[3]$; diagonal action free because $t_{3}$
translates $E$ while $g_{3}$ pins only $6$ isolated K3-points;
wave19\_x3 Cycle~1), the reduced DT partition function factorises as
\[
  Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{DT}}(X^{(3)})(\tau,z,\sigma)
  \;=\;
  -\,\frac{1}{(\DeltaThree(\tau,z,\sigma))^{2}}
  \;=\;
  -\,\frac{1}{\Phi_{3}(\tau,z,\sigma)},
\]
with $\Phi_{3} := (\DeltaThree)^{2}$ the Gritsenko paramodular form of
weight~$2$. Taking logarithmic derivative,
\[
  \sum_{v\in\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}(3)}
    \dim_{\Q}\,\mathfrak{g}^{(3)}_{\mathrm{BPS},v}\,e^{2\pi i(v,Z)}
  \;=\;
  -2\,\log\DeltaThree,
\]
so $\mathfrak{g}^{(3)}_{\mathrm{BPS}}$ (the Davison--Meinhardt
$\sigma_{3}$-invariant BPS Lie algebra of X3) and $\gDeltaThree$
share root multiplicities
$\mult = f^{(g_{3})}(4nm-\ell^{2},\,\ell)$. X3 delivers the left
side via Schiffmann--Vasserot K3 CoHA and Maulik--Pandharipande
GW/DT $\Z_{3}$-equivariance; X4 delivers the right side via
Borcherds lift of $\phigthree$. Their meeting on a common Fourier
frame is the $N=3$ Harvey--Moore
$\mathrm{BPS}=\mathfrak{g}_{\mathrm{BKM}}$ identification.

\paragraph{Attack--heal reconciliations.}
(H1) \emph{$c_{3}(0)=2$ or $6$?} Gritsenko-halved normalisation
$\phigthree = \tfrac{1}{2}Z^{(g_{3})}_{K3}$ pins $c_{3}(0)=2$
(Lemma~\texttt{lem:c-N-zero-closed-form}); the pre-halving value
$6 = \chi_{g_{3}}(K3)$ is the topological Euler trace, not the
Borcherds input.
(H2) \emph{Mukai fixed-point count $6$ or $12$?} Six isolated
$A_{2}$-points (Mukai~1988; Nikulin~1979); $12$ miscounts
$\chi_{g_{3}}(K3) = 24 - 2\cdot\mathrm{fix}$.
(H3) \emph{Fricke extension?} Yes;
$\Gthree^{+} = \Gthree \cup V_{3}\Gthree$ normalises
$\Gamma_{0}(3)\subset\Sp_{4}(\Q)$ (Gritsenko~1995 Thm.~3.2),
$\DeltaThree$ has Fricke character $\chi_{g_{3}}(V_{3})=1$.
(H4) \emph{$\det\mathsf{C}^{(3)} = -18$ or $-24$?}
Programme-canonical on $U\oplus\langle -6\rangle$: $-24$; the
$-18$ arises in the $V_{3}$-quotiented Atkin--Lehner presentation
(wave11\_w3).
(H5) \emph{Is $\DeltaThree$ in the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery census?}
Yes; $\DeltaThree = \Phi_{3,1}$ of weight $k_{3,1}=1$, the
level-$3$, index-$1$ entry of the eight simplest-divisor forms
(Gritsenko--Cl\'ery~2011 Thm.~1.2); it lies in the programme-active
intersection $\{(1,1),(2,1),(3,1),(4,1)\}$ shared with
Gritsenko--Nikulin~1998
(Remark~\ref{rem:gritsenko-clery-orbit} of
\texttt{cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}).

\paragraph{$\kappa_{\bullet}$-accounting on $X^{(3)}$.}
$\kBKM(\DeltaThree) = c_{3}(0)/2 = 1$ (Cycle~2);
$\kch(X^{(3)}) = \chi(\cO_{X^{(3)}}) = 0$ (compact
$\mathrm{CY}_{3}$, Hodge supertrace with $g_{3}$);
$\kcat(X^{(3)}) = \chi(\cO_{K3\times E})/3 = 0$
(K\"unneth on total space, $\chi(\cO_{E})=0$);
$\kfib(X^{(3)}) = r_{3} = 10$ (Mukai-invariant lattice rank,
Nikulin~1980 Table~1). The additivity
$\kBKM \stackrel{?}{=} \kch + \chi(\cO_{\mathrm{fiber}})$ fails
($1\neq 0+0$); the universal identity
$\kBKM(\Phi_{N}) = c_{N}(0)/2$ holds.

\paragraph{Scope.} Cycles~1--3 and Cycle~5 reduced-DT side are
theorems. Cycle~4 is a theorem at the lattice level and conjectural
at the GKM-superalgebra level (Lorgat~2020 Conj.~1 at $N=3$).
Cycle~5 BPS Lie algebra identification $\mathfrak{g}^{(3)}_{\mathrm{BPS}}
\cong \gDeltaThree$ is conjectural pending
explicit matching of Davison--Meinhardt BPS-sheaf multiplicities
against $\phigthree$-coefficients. The chain-level lane (explicit
Borcherds product, twined elliptic-genus Fourier data) and the
$(\infty,1)$-categorical lane (Schiffmann--Vasserot K3 CoHA,
Davison--Meinhardt BPS Lie algebra) converge at the conjectural
level; both are load-bearing at their own scope.
