\section{Positive Hall halves and chiral realisation}
\label{wn:sec:coha-positive-half-w-infty-boundary}

\providecommand{\Wilpha}{\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}}
\providecommand{\C}{\mathbb{C}}
\providecommand{\Z}{\mathbb{Z}}
\providecommand{\Q}{\mathbb{Q}}
\providecommand{\Eone}{\mathsf{E}_1}
\providecommand{\Etwo}{\mathsf{E}_2}

\providecommand{\hCS}{\mathrm{hCS}}

The critical cohomological Hall algebra of a smooth toric CY$_3$ chart
is an ordered Hall algebra.  Its multiplication records extensions and
therefore has native \(\Eone\) character.  Vertex-algebraic fields enter
after extra structure has been supplied: a Drinfeld double, Cartan
modes, a completion at fixed central charge, and an evaluation or corner
VOA realisation.  This separation is the common boundary between the
\(\C^3\), conifold, and \(K3\times E\) examples.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{\(\C^3\)}{C3}: the positive affine-Yangian half}
\label{wn:subsec:c3-positive-half}

Let \(Q_{\C^3}\) be the one-vertex quiver with loops \(X,Y,Z\) and
potential
\[
 W=\operatorname{tr}(XYZ-XZY).
\]
The cyclic derivatives are
\[
 \partial_X W=[Y,Z],\qquad
 \partial_Y W=[Z,X],\qquad
 \partial_Z W=[X,Y],
\]
so the Jacobi algebra is
\[
 J(Q_{\C^3},W)
 =
 \C\langle X,Y,Z\rangle/([X,Y],[Y,Z],[Z,X])
 \cong \C[X,Y,Z].
\]
The word ``triple'' in the quiver name records the three loops.  No
Jordan triple system is part of the Jacobi-algebra computation.

\begin{proposition}[Smooth toric Hall half]
\label{wn:prop:c3-coha-positive-half}
For the \(T^3\)-equivariant critical CoHA of the three-loop potential,
\[
 \CoHA(\C^3)\cong Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
\]
as associative \(\Eone\)-Hall algebras.  This is the Schiffmann--Vasserot
and Kapranov--Vasserot positive-half theorem, with the vanishing-cycle
model supplied by the critical Hilbert schemes of \(\C^3\).
\end{proposition}

The full affine Yangian and the \(\Wilpha[\lambda]\) vertex algebra live
on later layers:
\[
\CoHA(\C^3)
 =
Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
\xhookrightarrow{\;D_{\mathrm{Drin}}\;}
Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
\xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{ev}_{\lambda}\;}
\End\bigl(\Wilpha[\lambda]\text{-}\mathrm{vac}\bigr).
\]
Here \(D_{\mathrm{Drin}}\) adjoins \(Y^-\) and the Cartan modes by the
Hall pairing, while \(\mathrm{ev}_\lambda\) is the evaluation on a vacuum
or corner-VOA module.  The object on the left remains the positive
associative Hall half; the vertex algebra is reached through the
completed double and the chosen module realisation.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{\(d\geq 3\)}{d>=3}: the native operadic level}
\label{wn:subsec:dgeq3-e1}

The two-stage CY-to-chiral construction has the form
\[
 \Phi_d=\mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ\Phi^{FA}_d.
\]
Stage one produces an \(E_d\)-holomorphic factorisation algebra on the
CY$_d$ input.  Stage two specialises along a \((d-1)\)-cycle
\(\Sigma_{d-1}\) and a reference curve \(C\).  After this specialisation,
the native output is
\[
 n_{\mathrm{native}}(d)=
 \begin{cases}
 \infty, & d=1,\\
 2, & d=2,\\
 1, & d\geq 3.
 \end{cases}
\]
Thus a specialised \(d\geq 3\) output is an \(\Eone\)-chiral algebra.
The braided \(\Etwo\) object is obtained from a centre or double:
\[
 \mathcal{Z}\bigl(\Rep^{\Eone}(A)\bigr)
 \simeq
 \Rep^{\Etwo}\bigl(\mathrm{Drin}(A)\bigr),
\]
provided the dualisability, completion, and half-braiding data are in
place.  The \(\Etwo\) layer belongs to this centre/double/module
enhancement, while the raw Hall product remains \(\Eone\).

\subsection{The conifold and defect vertex algebras}
\label{wn:subsec:conifold-positive-half}

For the resolved conifold
\[
 \mathbb{Y}_{\mathrm{con}}
 =
 \cO_{\mathbb{P}^1}(-1)\oplus\cO_{\mathbb{P}^1}(-1),
\]
the Klebanov--Witten quiver has two vertices, four arrows, and the
quartic potential
\[
 W_{\mathrm{con}}
 =
 \operatorname{tr}(a_1b_1a_2b_2-a_1b_2a_2b_1).
\]
Davison's critical CoHA, together with the
Rapcak--Soibelman--Yang--Zhao toric theorem, identifies the conifold
Hall algebra with the positive half of the corresponding affine super
Yangian or quantum toroidal algebra.  It is again an ordered
\(\Eone\)-algebra.  A defect vertex algebra associated with
\(\hCS(\mathbb{Y}_{\mathrm{con}})\) requires the additional defect-line
construction and its OPE presentation; that is a separate chiral
realisation of the toroidal data.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{\(K3\times E\)}{K3 x E}: the four invariant readings}
\label{wn:subsec:k3e-invariants}

Let \(Y=K3\times E\).  The total-space Hodge supertrace, the Heisenberg
specialisation, the Borcherds weight, and the fibre rank compute
different objects:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|l}
\text{invariant} & \text{value} & \text{construction} \\
\hline
\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}(Y) & 0
& \chi(\cO_Y)=\chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot 0 \\
\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(Y) & 0
& \sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(Y)=1-1+1-1 \\
\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}(Y) & 3
& \mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Sigma_2,C}\circ\Phi^{FA}_3,\quad
  \Sigma_2=K3,\ C=E \\
\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Delta_5) & 5
& c_1(0)/2=10/2,\ \text{the Borcherds weight of }\Delta_5 \\
\kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}(Y) & 24
& \operatorname{rk}\widetilde{H}(K3,\Z)=1+22+1 .
\end{array}
\]
The numerical spectrum \(\{0,3,5,24\}\) comes from these separate
constructions.  The automorphic value \(5\) is independent of any sum
formed from the compact chiral supertrace and a fibre Euler
characteristic: the total-space/fibre-\(E\) reading gives
\[
 5\neq 0+0.
\]
The arithmetic \(5=3+2\) combines the Heisenberg branch on
\(K3\times E\) with the categorical Euler of the \(K3\) fibre. It is a
numerical coincidence across different constructions.

\subsection{The \texorpdfstring{\(\Delta_5\)}{Delta5} root multiplicities}
\label{wn:subsec:k3e-root-multiplicities}

For primitive curve classes on \(K3\times E\), reduced DT/BPS
dimensions are governed by the Fourier coefficients of the K3 Jacobi
form entering the Gritsenko--Nikulin Borcherds product:
\[
 \dim_{\Q}\mathfrak{g}_{\mathrm{BPS},\gamma}(K3\times E)
 =
 \dim_{\Q}\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5,\alpha_\gamma}
 =
 c_{\phi_{0,1}}(|\alpha_\gamma|^2/2).
\]
This is a statement about graded dimensions. A Hall-bracket isomorphism
with \(\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}\) requires the compact Hall pairing,
coproduct, completion, and comparison of bracket constants. Those data
belong to the compact Hall--Drinfeld-double construction.

The rank-zero sector has its own Heisenberg reading.  Nakajima's
operators on
\[
 \bigoplus_{n\geq 0}H^\bullet(\Hilb^n(K3))
\]
give a rank-\(24\) lattice Heisenberg algebra attached to
\(\widetilde H(K3,\Z)\).  This is the \(\kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}=24\)
Mukai-lattice value.  It is distinct from the level-one free boson
appearing in the \(\C^3\) self-dual corner and from
\(\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)=3\).

\subsection{Primitive CHL constants}
\label{wn:subsec:primitive-chl-constants}

For the primitive CHL denominator branch indexed by
\[
 N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\},
\]
the Borcherds weight theorem gives
\[
 \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N)=\mathrm{wt}(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2.
\]
In the Vol III primitive normalisation,
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
N & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 6 \\
\hline
c_N(0) & 10 & 8 & 6 & 4 & 2 \\
\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N) & 5 & 4 & 3 & 2 & 1
\end{array}
\]
with \(\Phi_1=\Delta_5\).  Other twining or row-normalised catalogues
must declare their convention before comparison with this primitive row.

\subsection{Corner VOA realisation}
\label{wn:subsec:corner-voa-realisation}

The Prochazka--Rapcak corner VOA \(Y_{L,M,N}[\psi]\) supplies a concrete
vertex-algebra realisation after the positive Hall half has been doubled
and evaluated.  The simplest corners include
\[
 Y_{1,0,0}[\psi]\simeq H_1,\qquad
 Y_{\infty,0,0}[\psi]\simeq \Wilpha[\psi],
\]
with finite-rank corners interpolating through \(\beta\gamma\) systems
and Heisenberg factors.  For \(K3\times E\), the expected orbifold
corner reflects the \(E[N]\)-symmetry and the K3 fibre data.  The
constructed part of the dictionary remains:
\[
Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
\longrightarrow
Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
\longrightarrow
\End_{\mathrm{ch}}(Y_{L,M,N}[\psi])
\longrightarrow
Y_{L,M,N}[\psi],
\]
where the first arrow is Drinfeld doubling and the second is evaluation
on the chosen chiral module.
