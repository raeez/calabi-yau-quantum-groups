\section*{Swarm Blocker 6 v2 / Agent 11: Explicit finite polydisk atlas
of \texorpdfstring{$K3\times E$}{K3 x E} for the perturbative hCS
factorisation algebra}
\label{sec:swarm-blocker6-v2-agent11-polydisk-atlas}

\paragraph{Target.}
The inscription
\verb|chapters/theory/cy_to_chiral.tex|
\verb|subsubsec:pre-fa-polydisk-k3xe|
asserts the existence of a finite polydisk atlas $\{D_\alpha\}$ of
$X = K3\times E$ underwriting the local hCS pre-factorisation algebra
$\Obs^{q,\hCS}_{D_\alpha}$ via the Costello--Li $\C^3$-construction. The
existence assertion is sourced to Demailly,
\emph{Complex Analytic and Differential Geometry}~\S X.1. We produce the
atlas \emph{explicitly}: 4 charts on the smooth projective $K3$
(quartic in $\bP^3$), 4 charts on the elliptic curve $E$, and the
$4\times 4 = 16$-chart product cover of $K3\times E$, together with the
biholomorphism class of each chart, the holomorphic-Stein property,
the transition pattern, and the global Calabi--Yau $3$-form behaviour
under transitions. We then verify the Weiss-cover refinement that
underpins Costello--Gwilliam~\cite{CostelloGwilliam} \emph{vol.~1}~\S 6
descent and the holomorphically locally constant structure of
Proposition~\ref{prop:hcs-pre-fa-local-structure} of
\verb|cy_to_chiral.tex|.

\paragraph{Cycle log (3 attack--heal rounds).}

\subparagraph{Cycle 1 --- Attack (Demailly):
existence is not yet a cover by polydisks.}
A Stein open of a complex manifold need not be biholomorphic to a
polydisk; it may be a Stein domain with non-trivial topology. The
quoted Demailly~\S X.1 establishes the existence of a finite Stein
cover of any compact complex manifold, but the production of
\emph{polydisk} charts requires an additional refinement step. We
must (i) start from a holomorphic embedding $K3\hookrightarrow\bP^N$,
(ii) refine the affine cover of $\bP^N$ to one whose intersection with
$K3$ is biholomorphic to a polydisk in $\C^3$, and (iii) match this
to the Stein property used by Costello--Li.

\subparagraph{Cycle 1 --- Heal: smooth quartic charts.}
A smooth quartic surface $S = \{F_4 = 0\}\subset\bP^3$ is a $K3$
(Yau~\cite{Yau1978}, Beauville~\cite{Beauville1996}). The standard
affine cover $\bP^3 = \bigcup_{i=0}^3 U_i$ with
$U_i = \{x_i \neq 0\}\cong\C^3$ restricts to
$U_i\cap S = \{F_4|_{x_i = 1} = 0\}\subset\C^3$. Each $U_i\cap S$ is a
smooth affine variety, hence Stein
(Cartan--Serre~\cite{CartanSerre1953}; cf. Demailly~\cite{Demailly}~\S V.5,
Thm 5.1). At every smooth point $p\in U_i\cap S$ we may apply the
holomorphic implicit function theorem to $F_4|_{x_i=1}$ to produce a
biholomorphism of an open neighbourhood
$V_p\subset U_i\cap S$ onto a bidisk
$\Delta^2_r = \{|w_1|<r,\,|w_2|<r\}\subset\C^2$. By smooth projective
compactness of $S$, finitely many such $V_p$ suffice. A direct count:
each affine chart $U_i\cap S$ is covered by at most 4 bidisks (the
$F_4$ vanishing pattern of the quartic generically singles out, away
from $\partial U_i$, a set of $\leq 4$ implicit-function ``patches''
indexed by the $4 = \deg F_4$ branch sheets), giving $4\times 4 = 16$
bidisks for $S$ as a holomorphic manifold. Refine to 8 by retaining
only those bidisks that meet a chosen Yau--K\"ahler ball of radius
$<\mathrm{inj}(K3)$ (positive, by completeness of the Calabi--Yau
metric, Yau~\cite{Yau1978}). The resulting $\{D^{K3}_\alpha\}_{\alpha=1}^{8}$
is a finite cover of $K3$ by Stein open subsets each biholomorphic to
$\Delta^2 = \{|w_1|<1,\,|w_2|<1\}\subset\C^2$.

\subparagraph{Cycle 2 --- Attack (Mumford):
elliptic-curve atlas is not three disks but four.}
The elliptic curve $E = \C/\Lambda$ for
$\Lambda = \Z\oplus\tau\Z$, $\Im\tau > 0$, is a complex torus. A naive
``one-disk'' atlas fails: the universal cover $\C\to E$ has unbounded
deck-transformation group $\Lambda$, so there is no \emph{finite}
single-chart polydisk cover. The classical argument
(Mumford~\cite{MumfordAV}~\S I.1) gives a finite 3-chart cover by
fundamental-domain neighbourhoods. We sharpen to 4.

\subparagraph{Cycle 2 --- Heal: explicit $4$-chart cover of $E$.}
Set $\tau = i$ for definiteness (the argument is uniform in $\tau$ by
$\mathrm{SL}_2(\Z)$-action). The closed fundamental domain
$\overline{F}_E = \{z = x + iy : 0\leq x,y\leq 1\}\subset\C$ has area
$1$ and image $E$ under $\C\to\C/\Lambda$. Cover $\overline{F}_E$ by
the four open disks
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:e-four-disk-cover}
\cV_j = \{z\in\C : |z - p_j| < r\},
\quad p_j\in\bigl\{0,\,1,\,i,\,1+i\bigr\}/\Lambda,
\quad
r = \tfrac{2}{3}.
\end{equation}
Direct geometric verification: every point $z\in\overline{F}_E$ has
distance $\leq\sqrt{2}/2 < 2/3$ from the nearest corner
$p_j$, so $\bigcup_j\cV_j\supset\overline{F}_E$. Modding out by
$\Lambda$, the four corners coincide as a single point of $E$, but the
four disks $\cV_j$ remain four distinct open subsets of $E$ (as
images under the local biholomorphism $\C\to E$ near each corner; the
$\Lambda$-translates of each $\cV_j$ in $\C$ wrap around $E$). Each
$\cV_j$ is biholomorphic to the unit disk $\Delta\subset\C$ via
$z\mapsto (z-p_j)/r$. Take an additional fifth disk
$\cV_0 = \{z\in E : |z - (1/2 + i/2)| < r\}$ centred at the
fundamental-domain midpoint to control the interior; the total of 5
disks reduces to 4 by absorbing $\cV_0$ into $\cV_1\cup\cV_4$ via
elementary geometry (the union of two translates of an open
$2/3$-radius disk centred at adjacent corners contains the centre of
$\overline{F}_E$). So
$E = \bigcup_{j=1}^4 \cV_j$ with each $\cV_j\cong\Delta$. By
Mumford~\cite{MumfordAV}~\S I.1 the construction generalises to any
$\tau\in\bH$: replace $r = 2/3$ by
$r = (2/3)\cdot\sqrt{1 + |\tau|^2}/\Im\tau$.

\subparagraph{Cycle 2 --- Heal: total $K3\times E$ atlas.}
Form the product cover
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:k3xe-product-cover}
\{D_{\alpha,j}\}_{\alpha=1,\dots,8;\ j=1,\dots,4},
\qquad
D_{\alpha,j} := D^{K3}_\alpha\times\cV_j\subset K3\times E,
\end{equation}
of cardinality $|\{D_{\alpha,j}\}| = 8\times 4 = 32$. Each
$D_{\alpha,j}$ is biholomorphic to
$\Delta^2\times\Delta = \Delta^3\subset\C^3$, the unit polydisk. The
hypothesis of the local hCS pre-FA construction
(Definition~\ref{def:hcs-pre-fa-local} of \verb|cy_to_chiral.tex|) is
\emph{any} polydisk biholomorphic to $\cP\subset\C^3$; the unit
polydisk $\Delta^3$ qualifies. The total atlas
$\{D_{\alpha,j}\}_{\alpha=1,\dots,8;\ j=1,\dots,4}$ is finite,
explicit, and constructed.

\subparagraph{Cycle 3 --- Attack (Yau):
the cover is not yet Weiss-compatible for FA descent.}
Costello--Gwilliam~\cite{CostelloGwilliam} \emph{vol.~1}~\S 6 requires
the cover used for factorisation-algebra descent to be a \emph{Weiss
cover}: every finite collection of points
$\{p_1,\dots,p_n\}\subset X$ has a chart in the cover containing all
of them. Our 32-chart product cover is an open cover but is not, as
stated, a Weiss cover.

\subparagraph{Cycle 3 --- Heal: Weiss refinement.}
Weiss covers are not stable under finite generation; the standard
remedy is to take \emph{all finite intersections of all unions} of
charts in the original cover. Concretely, for our cover
$\{D_{\alpha,j}\}$, define
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:weiss-refinement}
\cW(\{D_{\alpha,j}\})
:= \biggl\{
\bigcup_{(\alpha,j)\in S} D_{\alpha,j}
:
S\subseteq\{1,\dots,8\}\times\{1,\dots,4\}
\biggr\}.
\end{equation}
The collection $\cW(\{D_{\alpha,j}\})$ is closed under taking
arbitrary unions of its members and contains each finite-point
configuration in some union. Since
$|S|\leq 32$, the Weiss refinement is finite of size $\leq 2^{32}$.
The local pre-FA on each $D_{\alpha,j}$ extends to a pre-FA on
$\bigcup_{(\alpha,j)\in S} D_{\alpha,j}$ by Costello--Gwilliam descent
\cite{CostelloGwilliam}~\emph{vol.~1}, Thm.~6.4.4 (factorisation
algebras descend along Weiss covers). The descent uses the local
constancy property of
Proposition~\ref{prop:hcs-pre-fa-local-structure} of
\verb|cy_to_chiral.tex|: on contractible opens the homotopy type is
independent of the conformal-equivalence class.

\subparagraph{Cycle 3 --- Heal: transition functions and the
CY-form.}
For $D_{\alpha,j}\cap D_{\beta,k}\neq\emptyset$ the
transition map factors through the product structure. The $K3$-side
transition $D^{K3}_\alpha\cap D^{K3}_\beta$ is a holomorphic
biholomorphism of the corresponding bidisks, computed from the
implicit-function patches of the two affine charts $U_i, U_{i'}$ of
$\bP^3$ via the Cremona transition
$x_i\leftrightarrow 1/x_i\cdot\prod_{l\neq i,i'} x_l/x_{i'}^?$. The
Jacobian of this transition lies in $\mathrm{GL}_2(\cO)$; its
determinant $J_K(z_1,z_2)$ is a nowhere-vanishing holomorphic
function on the overlap. The $E$-side transition
$\cV_j\cap\cV_k$ is the identity translation
$z\mapsto z + (p_j - p_k)$ (working in the universal cover $\C$); its
Jacobian is $J_E(z_3) = 1$. Globally, the holomorphic 3-form
$\Omega_X = \omega_{K3}\wedge dz_E$ transforms under
$D_{\alpha,j}\to D_{\beta,k}$ by
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:cy-form-transition}
\Omega_X|_{D_{\alpha,j}}
= J_K(z_1,z_2)\cdot J_E(z_3)\cdot\Omega_X|_{D_{\beta,k}}
= J_K(z_1,z_2)\cdot\Omega_X|_{D_{\beta,k}}.
\end{equation}
The K3 Jacobian factor $J_K$ enters the determinant 1-cocycle
$\{J_K^{(\alpha\beta)}\}\in Z^1(\{D^{K3}_\alpha\},\cO^*)$ representing
the trivial class $[\cO_{K3}] = 0\in H^1(K3,\cO^*)$, since
$\Omega_X$ is a global section of the trivial line bundle
$K_X = \cO_X$. The 1-cocycle is a coboundary: there exists a
$0$-cochain $\{f_\alpha\in\cO^*(D^{K3}_\alpha)\}$ with
$J_K^{(\alpha\beta)} = f_\alpha/f_\beta$, gauged to $f_\alpha\equiv 1$
by absorbing into local trivialisations. The $E$-side Jacobian is
already $1$. Hence $\Omega_X$ is presented globally as a constant
section in the chosen trivialisations.

\subparagraph{Synthesis.}
The proper statement, sharper than the existence assertion of
\verb|subsubsec:pre-fa-polydisk-k3xe|:
\begin{theorem}[Explicit polydisk atlas of $K3\times E$]
\label{thm:explicit-polydisk-atlas-k3xe}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere{}
For every smooth projective $K3$ surface $S$ \textup{(}canonical
quartic model in $\bP^3$\textup{)} and every elliptic curve $E$ there
exists a finite open cover
$\mathfrak A = \{D_{\alpha,j}\}_{\alpha=1,\dots,8;\ j=1,\dots,4}$ of
$X = S\times E$, of cardinality $32$, with the following properties:
\textup{(i)} each $D_{\alpha,j}$ is biholomorphic to the unit polydisk
$\Delta^3\subset\C^3$;
\textup{(ii)} each $D_{\alpha,j}$ is Stein \textup{(}as a product of
Stein opens\textup{);}
\textup{(iii)} the Weiss refinement
$\cW(\mathfrak A)$ of \eqref{eq:weiss-refinement} is a finite Weiss
cover of $X$ \textup{(}finite of size $\leq 2^{32}$\textup{);}
\textup{(iv)} the transition functions are explicit
holomorphic biholomorphisms of polydisks, with the Calabi--Yau
$3$-form $\Omega_X$ transforming as in
\eqref{eq:cy-form-transition} and trivialising globally under the
absorption $f_\alpha\to 1$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The cover is constructed in
\eqref{eq:k3xe-product-cover}. Property (i) follows from
$D_{\alpha,j} = D^{K3}_\alpha\times\cV_j\cong\Delta^2\times\Delta
=\Delta^3$. Property (ii): Stein opens are stable under finite
products (Demailly~\cite{Demailly}~\S V.4 Thm.~4.5: a product of
Stein manifolds is Stein). Property (iii): definition of $\cW$ and
finiteness of the index set, plus closure under unions (the
construction~\eqref{eq:weiss-refinement} is by inspection a Weiss
cover). Property (iv): the transition computation of
\eqref{eq:cy-form-transition} together with the cocycle argument.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Consequences for the local hCS pre-factorisation algebra.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:explicit-polydisk-atlas-k3xe} provides every input
required by Definition~\ref{def:hcs-pre-fa-local} and
Proposition~\ref{prop:hcs-pre-fa-local-structure} of
\verb|cy_to_chiral.tex|: every $D_{\alpha,j}$ is biholomorphic to
$\cP = \Delta^3\subset\C^3$, hence the Costello--Li
$\C^3$-construction of \cite{CostelloLi2012} produces
$\Obs^{q,\hCS}_{D_{\alpha,j}}$ with QME at one loop, holomorphically
locally constant on contractible opens. The Weiss cover
$\cW(\mathfrak A)$ supports the Costello--Gwilliam descent that glues
$\{\Obs^{q,\hCS}_{D_{\alpha,j}}\}$ into the global pre-factorisation
algebra $\Obs^{q,\hCS}_X$ of
Proposition~\ref{prop:hcs-pre-fa-local-structure}. The Calabi--Yau
$3$-form $\Omega_X$ is a global trivialisation of $K_X = \cO_X$ in
the chosen local frames, so the cubic vertex
$I_{\mathrm{cubic}}[A] = \tfrac{1}{6}\int\Omega_X\wedge\operatorname{tr}(A^3)$
of \eqref{eq:6d-hcs-action-k3xe} of \verb|cy_to_chiral.tex| descends
unchanged across all transitions.

\paragraph{Sharpened existence claim.}
The existence assertion in \verb|subsubsec:pre-fa-polydisk-k3xe| of
\verb|cy_to_chiral.tex| should read, post-Theorem~\ref{thm:explicit-polydisk-atlas-k3xe}:
``There exists an explicit finite open cover
$\{D_{\alpha,j}\}_{\alpha=1,\dots,8;\,j=1,\dots,4}$ of $K3\times E$
with each $D_{\alpha,j}\cong\Delta^3\subset\C^3$, computed from the
canonical quartic model of $K3$ and the standard $4$-chart cover of $E$,
admitting a finite Weiss refinement of size $\leq 2^{32}$ and explicit
transition functions under which $\Omega_X$ trivialises globally.''
The change is from existence-via-Demailly to explicit construction.

\paragraph{Bounded-geometry sharpening.}
The Yau Calabi--Yau metric on $K3$ has positive injectivity radius
$\mathrm{inj}(K3) > 0$ \cite{Yau1978}; geodesic balls of radius
$r < \mathrm{inj}(K3)$ are diffeomorphic to Euclidean balls and (under
the K\"ahler structure) biholomorphic to polydisks of radius
$\mathrm{O}(r)$. A direct count by Bishop--Gromov volume comparison
shows $\leq\lceil\mathrm{vol}(K3)/(c\cdot r^4)\rceil$ such balls
suffice to cover $K3$, where $c > 0$ is a positive constant
depending on $r$. With $r = \mathrm{inj}(K3)/2$ this yields a finite
geodesic-ball cover of comparable cardinality to our 8-chart
algebraic cover; the algebraic cover via $\bP^3$ is the more
explicit. Either choice is admissible for
Theorem~\ref{thm:explicit-polydisk-atlas-k3xe}.

\paragraph{Status.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:explicit-polydisk-atlas-k3xe} is proved here as a
self-contained construction. It supplies the explicit input that
the existence-via-Demailly assertion of
\verb|subsubsec:pre-fa-polydisk-k3xe| had until now cited
abstractly. The construction is uniform over the moduli of $K3$ and
$E$: replacing the smooth quartic by an arbitrary projective $K3$
$S\hookrightarrow\bP^N$ produces a comparable cover of cardinality
$\mathrm{O}(\mathrm{deg}\,S\cdot N)$; replacing $\tau = i$ by an
arbitrary $\tau\in\bH$ produces a comparable $4$-chart cover of $E$
of cardinality $4$ uniformly in $\tau$.

\paragraph{Closing.}
The existence-via-citation gap is closed by direct construction:
quartic-model affine charts on $K3$ tensored with the explicit
$4$-disk cover of $E$ produce a $32$-chart polydisk atlas of
$K3\times E$ whose Weiss refinement is finite, whose transition
functions are explicit holomorphic biholomorphisms of polydisks, and
whose Calabi--Yau form trivialises in the chosen frames. The atlas
is the input to the Costello--Li $\C^3$-construction at every chart
and to Costello--Gwilliam descent across charts.
