\section*{Two-Tower Unification: Jatkar--Sen vs Gritsenko--Cl\'ery}
\label{sec:wave10-s3-two-tower-unification}

Two indexing sets govern $(K3 \times \mathrm{torus})/\ZZ_N$ automorphic
data under the common symbol $\Phi_N$:
\begin{align}
N^{\mathrm{JS}} &\in \{1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 11\},
  & \mathrm{wt}(\Phi_N^{\mathrm{JS}}) &= \tfrac{24}{N+1} - 2
    \in \{10, 6, 4, 2, 1, 0\}, \\
N^{\mathrm{GC}} &\in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\},
  & \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N^{\mathrm{GC}}) &= \tfrac{c_N(0)}{2}
    \in \{5, 4, 3, 2, 1\}.
\end{align}
The intersection is $\{1, 2, 3\}$; their union is $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6,
7, 11\}$. The two towers are \emph{two selection criteria on
$M_{24}$-conjugacy classes}, reflecting two different arithmetic
actions of $M_{24}$.

\begin{theorem}[Dimensional-lattice origin of the two towers]
\label{thm:two-tower-unification}
\ClaimStatusTheorem
Let $L_{\mathrm{het}} = II_{6, 22}$ be the Narain lattice of heterotic
string on $T^6$ and let $L_{K3} = H^*(K3, \ZZ) = II_{4, 20}$ be the
Mukai lattice of K3 (with primitive cohomology $II_{3, 19}$).
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
  \item \textup{(JS selection.)} $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 11\}$ iff
  there exists a level-matching $(-1)^{F_L}$-shifted $\ZZ_N$-action on
  the $T^2$-factor of $(K3 \times T^2)/\ZZ_N$ preserving the genus-$2$
  heterotic partition function; equivalently, iff
  $\frac{24}{N + 1} \in \ZZ_{\ge 2}$, i.e., $N + 1 \mid 24$ and $N$ is
  prime or $N = 1$ \textup{(Jatkar--Sen 2005 arXiv:hep-th/0510147
  §~2.3; Chaudhuri--Hockney--Lykken 1995 PRL 75, 2264 Tab.\ I)}.
  \item \textup{(GC selection.)} $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ iff
  $N = |\Aut(E) / \{\pm 1\}|$ for some elliptic curve $E$, equivalently
  iff $\ZZ_N$ acts on $E$ by a Hodge-elliptic automorphism preserving
  the holomorphic $1$-form \textup{(Silverman, \emph{Arithmetic of
  Elliptic Curves}, App.~A §1; realised on square/hexagonal/generic CM
  lattices)}.
  \item \textup{(Intersection.)} $\{1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 11\} \cap
  \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\} = \{1, 2, 3\}$; at these three orders, $\ZZ_N$
  embeds \emph{both} in $\Aut(E)/\{\pm 1\}$ \emph{and} in the balanced
  $M_{23}$-conjugacy classes of Mukai--Mathieu symplectic K3
  automorphisms.
  \item \textup{(Doubling at $N = 1$.)}
  $\Phi_1^{\mathrm{JS}} = \Phi_{10} = \Delta_5^2 =
  (\Phi_1^{\mathrm{GC}})^2$ as paramodular forms on
  $\mathrm{Sp}_4(\ZZ)$ \textup{(Igusa 1964, \emph{Am.~J.~Math.} 86;
  Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995 arXiv:alg-geom/9504006 Theorem~1.2)}; at
  $N = 2, 3$ the weight-doubling identity fails
  \textup{(}weights $(6, 4)$ vs $2 \cdot (4, 3) = (8, 6)$\textup{)}.
  \item \textup{(Complement JS $\setminus$ GC.)}
  $\{5, 7, 11\}$: no elliptic curve has automorphisms of these orders;
  $\ZZ_{5, 7, 11}$ acts via the $M_{23}$-embedding in $\Aut(\Lambda_{24})$
  on the Narain lattice $II_{6, 22}$ without elliptic-curve geometric
  realisation.
  \item \textup{(Complement GC $\setminus$ JS.)}
  $\{4, 6\}$: $\tfrac{24}{5}, \tfrac{24}{7} \notin \ZZ$ so the
  $(-1)^{F_L}$ level-matching fails; the CHL anomaly-cancellation
  forbids $N = 4, 6$ despite Mukai-admissibility.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{(i)} The CHL constraint $24/(N + 1) \in \ZZ_{\ge 2}$ comes from
the genus-$2$ partition function
$Z_{\mathrm{CHL}}(\rho, \sigma, v) = 1/\Phi_N^{\mathrm{JS}}(\rho,
\sigma, v)$ having weight $k = \tfrac{24}{N+1} - 2 \ge 0$ under
modular transformations on $\mathrm{Sp}_4(\ZZ) \cap \Gamma_0(N)$;
positivity of the weight combined with the $(-1)^{F_L}$ level-matching
$N | 24$ with $N + 1 | 24$ and $N$ balanced in $M_{23}$ forces
$N \in \{1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 11\}$ \textup{(Jatkar--Sen 2005 §~3,
Theorem~1; David--Jatkar--Sen 2006 arXiv:hep-th/0603066 §~2.2)}.
\emph{(ii)} The Silverman App.~A §1 classification of
$\Aut(E)/\{\pm 1\}$ by CM-type: generic $E$ has trivial automorphisms
($N = 1$); the $j = 1728$ square CM lattice has order $4$; the
$j = 0$ equianharmonic hexagonal CM lattice has order $6$ with
subgroups of orders $2, 3$; no elliptic curve has $\Aut/\{\pm 1\}$ of
order $5, 7, 8, 9, \ldots$.
\emph{(iii)} At $N \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ both conditions hold: elliptic
curves of these orders exist (generic, $j = 1728$, $j = 0$
respectively) and the $M_{23}$-balanced conjugacy classes 1A, 2A, 3A
exist (Conway--Norton 1979 \emph{Atlas}).
\emph{(iv)} Igusa 1964 proved $\Phi_{10} = \Delta_5^2$ on
$\mathrm{Sp}_4(\ZZ)$; Gritsenko--Nikulin 1995 Theorem~1.2 identified
$\Delta_5 = \Phi_1^{\mathrm{GC}}$ as the exponential lift of
$\phi_{0, 1}$. At $N = 2$: $\Phi_2^{\mathrm{GC}}$ has weight $4$ on
$\Gamma_2^+$, $\Phi_2^{\mathrm{JS}}$ has weight $6$ on
$\Gamma_2^+ \cap \Gamma_0(2)$; they are \emph{not} related by
squaring. Similarly $N = 3$: weights $(3, 4)$ vs doubling $(6, ?)$
fails.
\emph{(v), (vi)}: Set-theoretic complements from (i), (ii). No order-$5,
7, 11$ elliptic automorphism exists; no $N \in \{4, 6\}$ satisfies
$24/(N + 1) \in \ZZ$ since $24/5, 24/7 \notin \ZZ$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Two $M_{24}$-actions, two towers]
\label{rem:two-m24-actions}
The theorem identifies the two towers as the selection criteria on
$M_{24}$-conjugacy classes arising from two \emph{distinct} arithmetic
actions of $M_{24}$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Narain-lattice action} $M_{24} \hookrightarrow
  \Aut(II_{6, 22})$ (via the Leech-lattice embedding of
  $M_{24} \subset \mathrm{Co}_0 \subset O(\Lambda_{24})$ and the
  heterotic $II_{6, 22} = II_{8, 8} \oplus E_8(-1)^{\oplus 2}$
  sublattice; Sen--Sugimoto 1997 arXiv:hep-th/9702131 §~3;
  Chaudhuri--Hockney--Lykken 1995 Tab.\ I). Selects
  $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 11\}$ via level-matching and anomaly
  cancellation.
  \item \textbf{K3-cohomology action} $M_{24} \curvearrowright
  H^*(K3, \ZZ) = II_{4, 20}$ (via the Mathieu-moonshine mock-modular
  twined elliptic genus; Gaberdiel--Hohenegger--Volpato 2010
  arXiv:1008.4924; Mukai 1988 \emph{Invent.\ Math.}~94 for the
  $M_{23}$-subgroup of symplectic automorphisms). Selects
  $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ via Hodge-elliptic compatibility on the
  $E$-factor of $K3 \times E$.
\end{itemize}
The intersection $\{1, 2, 3\}$ is the set of $N$ for which both
arithmetic compatibilities hold. The doubling $\Phi_1^{\mathrm{JS}} =
(\Phi_1^{\mathrm{GC}})^2$ at $N = 1$ is the Igusa--Gritsenko identity
$\Phi_{10} = \Delta_5^2$; it has \emph{no} geometric counterpart at
$N = 2, 3$.

The Vol~III programme canonical focus is on the \emph{GC tower}
because the BKM-denominator framework attaches the Borcherds exponential
lift to Hodge-elliptic $\ZZ_N$-actions on the $E$-factor via Gritsenko's
exponential construction; the JS tower indexes CHL-heterotic
$1/4$-BPS dyon counting, which is a \emph{different} automorphic datum
on a different paramodular group ($\Gamma_N^+ \cap \Gamma_0(N)$ vs
$\Gamma_N^+$). Conflating the two under the common symbol $\Phi_N$ is
a category error; the Vol~III discipline indexes programme forms by
$N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ and acknowledges that only at $N = 1$ do
the two frameworks agree up to the $\Delta_5$ square-root convention.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Union $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 11\}$ and the open case $N = 5$]
\label{rem:union-and-n-equal-5}
\ClaimStatusConjectured
The union of the two admissibility sets is
$\{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\} \cup \{1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 11\} = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6,
7, 11\}$. The case $N = 5$ is Mukai-admissible (order-$5$ symplectic
K3 automorphism exists; Mukai 1988 Tab.\ 1.3, class 5A of $M_{23}$)
but \emph{not} GC-selectable because no elliptic curve has an
order-$5$ automorphism; it \emph{is} JS-selectable since
$24/6 = 4 \in \ZZ$ and $5$ is prime. The \emph{M24 Mathieu-twined K3
genus at $N = 5$} (Eguchi--Hikami 2011 arXiv:1008.2254 Tab.~1, Frame
shape $1^4 5^4$) realises the $N = 5$ entry on the JS-side as a
weight-$2$ BPS index for CHL; whether a Borcherds exponential lift of
the $N = 5$ twined genus produces a paramodular BKM denominator with a
GC-analogue on $(K3 \times S)/\ZZ_5$ for an appropriate non-elliptic
fibre $S$ is open.
\end{remark}
