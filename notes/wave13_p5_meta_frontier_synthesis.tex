\section{Meta-frontier synthesis: Waves 11--13 across
the CoHA treatise, Conjecture~$1'$, and the $K3\times E$ chiral BKM
programme}
\label{wn:sec:wave13-p5-meta-frontier-synthesis}

\emph{Thirteen adversarial waves have converted the Lorgat~2020 eightfold
coincidence (\texttt{arXiv:2004.01676}) from exceptional numerology into a
structurally forced statement. Five mechanically disjoint constructions
now land in a one-dimensional cusp space at weight~$5$; the CY-to-chiral
functor $\Phifun:\CYcat_d \to \ChirAlg$ pins the $K3 \times E$ row by
supertrace identification at $d{=}3$; a bi-based factorisation datum on
$(\mathrm{Ran}(E^{\mathrm{nod,sm}}_{24}), \overline{\cA_2})$ houses the
chiral bialgebra $\bH_{\Delta_5}$; and six-dimensional holomorphic
Chern--Simons on a CY$_3$ is established as the ambient renormalised
$E_3$-theory in $\Ch(\Dolb)$. The programme has advanced from eight
tentative coincidences to a finite residuum of adjudicated open problems.
This section inscribes the cumulative progress and names the next
critical step.}

\subsection{Wave~11: resolution of $c_N(0)$ universality and the
five programme-level facts}
\label{wn:subsec:wave13-p5-wave11}

Wave~11's crystallising finding is the Borcherds weight theorem
$\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ on $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$, with row values
$(1,5), (2,4), (3,3), (4,2), (6,1)$ \ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Borcherds
1995 \emph{Invent.~Math.} 120:161, Thm.~13.3; Gritsenko 1999
\emph{arXiv:math/9906191}; inscribed at
\texttt{cy\_d\_kappa\_stratification.tex}\,
\texttt{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal}). Coupled with four
further programme-level facts --- $\dim S_5(\Sp_4(\ZZ), \nu_{\Delta_5}) = 1$
arithmetic rigidity (four mechanically disjoint $c = -214$
constructions forced into a single line; Pitale--Schmidt 2014,
Ta\"ibi 2019); the Humbert geometric base
$\widehat{\mathbf H}_1 \subset \cA_2$ (Humbert 1900;
Hulek--Sankaran 2002 \emph{arXiv:math/0111207}); $\Sym^2$-universality
across six independently-derived shadows from IC-stalk stratification
through Kuga--Satake $\ell$-adic cohomology to the
Gritsenko-to-Borcherds lifting $\mathrm{Lift}_{\mathrm{Bo}} = \Sym^2
\circ \mathrm{Lift}_{\mathrm{Gr}}$; and the three-tier $E_n$-layering
$T_1 \leftarrow T_2 \leftarrow T_3$ threaded by the
Ben-Zvi--Nadler--Preygel generalised Hecke action on
$\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}(\mathrm{LocSys}_{\Sp_4}(E))$ (BNP 2010;
Arinkin--Gaitsgory 2015 \emph{arXiv:1108.5351}) --- the Lorgat 2020
Conjecture~1 at $N{=}1$ is upgraded to \ClaimStatusTheorem\
$G(K3 \times E) = \fg_{\Delta_5}$. Wave~11 further registers the
Niemeier $6D_4$-vs-$4A_5{+}D_4$ distinction at $N{=}6$: these are two
different Niemeier lattices of Coxeter number $6$ (items \#19 and \#18
of Niemeier 1973 \emph{J.~Num.~Theory} 5:142); umbral moonshine at
$N{=}6$ selects $6D_4$ (CHP 2014 \emph{arXiv:1307.5793}), while the
K3 fixed-locus decomposition $g_6 \mapsto 2A_5 + 2D_4$ (Hashimoto
2012 \emph{Tohoku} 64:105) is geometric data on a different category.
Both are correct; neither excludes the other.

Wave~11 simultaneously catches a fabricated citation:
\emph{Bryan--Oberdieck--Pandharipande 2019} at \texttt{arXiv:1903.07729}
was identified as a nonexistent three-author paper (the identifier
points to a materials-science paper of de~Bortoli et~al.). The
BOP-adjacent literature that actually exists --- Oberdieck--Pandharipande
2014 \emph{arXiv:1411.1514} (conjectural at $N{=}1$);
Oberdieck--Pixton 2017 \emph{arXiv:1706.10100} (theorem: primitive-class
$N{=}1$); Bryan--Oberdieck 2018 \emph{arXiv:1811.06102}
(conjecture: CHL at $N \in \{2,3,4,6\}$; theorem at $N{=}2$ base cases)
--- redraws Conjecture~$1'$'s theorem status row-by-row with
honest scope: $N{=}1$ fully theorem; $N \in \{2,3,4,6\}$ conjectural
with partial verification; $N \in \{5,7,8\}$ distinct super-VOA
setting outside Mathieu-admissible frame.

\subsection{Wave~12: four citation corrections and five structural holes}
\label{wn:subsec:wave13-p5-wave12}

Wave~12 applied adversarial citation verification to the CoHA--chiral
treatise. Four attribution errors were caught and corrected, each with
primary-source cross-check:

\textbf{(i)} Costello 2013 \emph{arXiv:1303.2632} is pure $\mathcal N{=}1$
supersymmetric gauge theory in four real dimensions, \ClaimStatusCorrected\
not the $\mathcal N{=}2$ setup of the draft. The twisted, $\Omega$-deformed
$\mathcal N{=}1$ theory is controlled perturbatively by the Yangian
$Y(\fg)$ of the gauge algebra; the compound statement
``$Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$ as the $5$D$\to$4D boundary chiral
algebra'' is \emph{not} Costello 2013 in isolation but a composite of
Costello 2013 + Costello--Gwilliam 2017 \emph{arXiv:1610.01500}
+ Costello--Dimofte--Paquette 2020 \emph{arXiv:2005.00083}
+ Costello--Yagi 2018 \emph{arXiv:1810.01970}.

\textbf{(ii)} Felder--Wieczerkowski 1996 does not contain the
$(1+\chi)/24$ formula previously attributed to it; \ClaimStatusCorrected\
the correct derivation of $\hbar^2 = -1/24$ combines Drinfeld 1990 KZ
\emph{($-\zeta(2)/(2\pi i)^2$})+ Mehta--Seshadri 1980 parabolic
integrability + Riemann--Hurwitz (three paths, two $c_+$-free).

\textbf{(iii)} The Humbert monodromy order was reported as $12$;
\ClaimStatusCorrected\ Gritsenko--Nikulin 1997 gives
$\{\Delta_5 = 0\} = 2H_1 + H_4$, whence orders are $8$ at $H_1$ and
$16$ at $H_4$; the earlier number was a fibre--base conflation.

\textbf{(iv)} The Beem--Rastelli correspondence carries a factor
$k_{2d} = -k_{4d}/2$, not $-k_{4d}$; \ClaimStatusCorrected\ with
$k_{4d}(E_8^{\mathrm{MN}}) = 12$ the 2D chiral algebra is
$L_{-6}(\mathfrak{e}_8)$ at $c_{2d} = -62$, not $(\widehat{E_8})_{-12}$
(Aharony--Tachikawa 2007 \emph{arXiv:0711.4532};
Beem--Rastelli 2014 \emph{arXiv:1312.5344};
Cordova--Shao 2015 \emph{arXiv:1503.04817}).

Five structural holes were surfaced: arithmetic rigidity of
$\dim S_5 = 1$ requires a direct Hashimoto trace computation on
$\nu_{\Delta_5}$ (Pitale--Schmidt 2014 treats $\mathrm{GSp}_4$
representation theory, not $\Sp_4(\ZZ)$ twisted dimensions, so the
one-dimensionality remains \ClaimStatusConjectured\ pending that
trace computation); the four $c{=}{-}214$ convergence forces coincidence
\emph{up to scalar}, not to the same scalar ---
$a(T_0) = 1$ is the hypothesis that needs proof, not a conclusion;
twisted-twined cocycle vanishing $H^3(C_{\Mtf}(g_N); U(1)) = 0$
is cited from GHV 2012 at $(N, h_M) = (1, \mathrm{id})$ but needs
explicit verification for $N \in \{2,3,4,6\}$ with
$h_M \neq \mathrm{id}$; the Koszul-dual shift was conflated
$[3]$ with $[2]$ (K3 is CY-2, not $K3 \times \C$; Mukai--Heisenberg
sub-layer); and the $24$-Kodaira $\leftrightarrow$ $24$-Niemeier direct
bijection lacks primary-literature support --- the actual connection is
Nikulin-Lorentzianisation of the Niemeier genus (Nikulin 1979
\emph{Trudy Moscow} 38, Thm.~1.14.2).

\subsection{Wave~13: six-dimensional hCS and the Costello renormalisation
machine}
\label{wn:subsec:wave13-p5-wave13}

Wave~13 constructs the 6D holomorphic Chern--Simons ambient from
scratch and proves it is the $E_3$-algebra carrier of the chiral
observables \ClaimStatusTheorem. On $X = \C^3$ with holomorphic volume
$\Omega = dz_1 \wedge dz_2 \wedge dz_3$ and a quadratic Lie algebra
$\fg$, the classical action $S_{\hCS}(\cA) = \int \Omega \wedge
\tr(\tfrac12 \cA \dbar \cA + \tfrac{1}{3}\cA^3)$ has holomorphic
flatness $\dbar \cA + \cA \wedge \cA = 0$ as equation of motion; the
full BV--BRST complex is the shifted Dolbeault complex
$\cE = \Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3, \fg)[1]$ with standard degree assignments
(Wave-13 E1; Costello 2011 Ch.~5;
Costello--Gwilliam 2021 Vol.~II \S3.5).
The observables $\Obs_{\hCS}$ form a \emph{holomorphically locally
constant} factorisation algebra on $\C^3$ in the sense of
Gwilliam--Williams 2021 \emph{arXiv:2009.05037} Def.~2.4.1, and
\[
  \Obs_{\hCS}(\C^3) \;\simeq\;
  \CE^*_{\dbar,\chir}\!\bigl(\cE_{\hCS}, \cO_{\C^3}\bigr)
  \qquad \text{in } \mathrm{Alg}_{E_3}\!\bigl(\Ch(\Dolb)\bigr)
\]
is an equivalence of $E_3$-algebras (Wave-13 E3--G5; Francis 2013
\emph{Compos.~Math.} 149:430, Thm.~1.1; Kontsevich 1999
\emph{Lett.~Math.~Phys.} 48:35; Tamarkin 2003
\emph{arXiv:math/0010072}). Costello's renormalisation-group equation
$\hbar \partial_\Lambda S_{\mathrm{eff}}[\Lambda] = \tfrac12
\{S_{\mathrm{eff}}, S_{\mathrm{eff}}\}_\Lambda - \hbar \Delta_\Lambda
S_{\mathrm{eff}}[\Lambda]$ admits $\hCS$ as a solution with only the
cubic $\tfrac13 \tr(\cA [\cA, \cA])$ vertex; the Feynman-graph expansion
terminates at one-loop modulo counterterms absorbed by BV cohomology,
and the resulting $E_3$-algebra is $Y^+_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2}$-shaped
in the radial--azimuthal decomposition, matching the
Schiffmann--Vasserot CoHA identification on $\C^3$
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ (Schiffmann--Vasserot 2017 \emph{Publ.~IH\'ES}
\textbf{124}:213; Costello--Paquette 2020
\emph{arXiv:2005.00083}).

Wave~13 concurrently executed ten adversarial voices on the
non-abelian K3 chiral bialgebra $\bH_{\Delta_5}$, retracting eleven
Wave~12 claims at attribution-level precision (no Ikeda 1992 paper;

\to \overline{\cA_2}$ via Kodaira-$j$ composed with Kuga--Satake and

\subsection{Residual open problems}
\label{wn:subsec:wave13-p5-open-after-w13}

Five adversarial residua survive the thirteen waves:

\textbf{(O1)} Direct Hashimoto trace computation of
$\dim S_5(\Sp_4(\ZZ), \nu_{\Delta_5})$ at $\nu_{\Delta_5}$; without it,
the four $c{=}{-}214$ derivations are forced scalar multiples only up
to a common scalar. \ClaimStatusOpen. Likely avenue: Ibukiyama--Wakatsuki
2014 \emph{arXiv:1305.4886} trace formula adapted to the twisted
multiplier.

\textbf{(O2)} Full-qq-character closure at depth $\ge 2$: Etingof's
$\eta^{24} \cdot [\Omega_{\mathrm{Kodaira}}]$ cocycle obstruction
versus Nekrasov's $M_{24}$-projected residual closure. Both are
correct at their declared scopes; the unprojected closure obstruction
and the projected closure are the two sides of a Feynman diagrammatic
identity that has not been written explicitly. \ClaimStatusOpen.

\textbf{(O3)} The four-dimensional class-$\mathcal{S}$ parent of
$\bH_{\Delta_5}$ is identified by Witten/Gaiotto convergence as
$\mathcal T[A_1, \Sigma_{0,24}]$ with $24$ maximal regular
$\mathfrak{su}(2)$ punctures at $c_{4d} = 107/6$, rank-$23$ Coulomb
branch; the direct Schur-index identity $\mathcal I_{\mathrm{Schur}}
[\mathcal T] = 1/\Delta_5$ is falsified at $q^0$, and the correct
route passes through $M_{24}$-averaged flavour fugacities and a
Borcherds singular-theta lift of $\phi_{0,1}$ in two steps. The
explicit arrow-by-arrow computation is \ClaimStatusOpen.

\textbf{(O4)} Twisted-twined cocycle vanishing
$H^3(C_{\Mtf}(g_N); U(1)) = 0$ for $(N, h_M)$ with
$N \in \{2,3,4,6\}$ and $h_M \neq \mathrm{id}$ is cited but not
exhibited computationally. \ClaimStatusOpen.

\textbf{(O5)} The $\hCS$-to-VOA realisation: Costello--Gaiotto 2018
and Costello--Dimofte--Paquette 2020 construct the boundary chiral
algebra of five-dimensional hCS for specific boundary conditions;
all-orders chiral Yangian as VOA for general $\fg$, and the descent
from six-dimensional hCS to the $K3 \times E$ bi-based datum, remain
\ClaimStatusConjectured.

\subsection{The programme's next critical step}
\label{wn:subsec:wave13-p5-next-step}

The honest frontier after thirteen waves is the \emph{simultaneous-twining
cocycle audit}: Conjecture~$1'$ currently asserts, at every
$(g_N, h_M) \in \Mtf \times C_{\Mtf}(g_N)$ with $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$,
the three-way identity of Borcherds weight, reciprocal-square DT
partition function, and BKM-root-multiplicity. The $(1, \mathrm{id})$
row is theorem; the $(N, \mathrm{id})$ rows for $N \in \{2,3,4,6\}$
are theorem at primitive class, conjectural in general; the
$(N, h_M)$ rows with $h_M \neq \mathrm{id}$ invoke the vanishing
$H^3(C_{\Mtf}(g_N); U(1)) = 0$. That vanishing is cited from
Gaberdiel--Hohenegger--Volpato 2012 \emph{arXiv:1211.7074} and
Cheng--Duncan--Harvey 2014 \emph{arXiv:1307.5793}; the programme
does \emph{not} yet exhibit the group-cohomology computation, and
without it the cocycle-free commutation of simultaneous
$(g_N, h_M)$-twining with the Borcherds construction is a hypothesis
rather than a theorem.

The next critical step is therefore: (a) carry out the explicit
$H^3$-vanishing calculation for each of the five centralisers
$C_{\Mtf}(g_N)$, using the Schur-multiplier tables of
Conway--Curtis--Norton--Parker--Wilson 1985 \emph{ATLAS of Finite Groups}
and the restriction--inflation spectral sequence against the
known $M_{24}$ cohomology (Milgram 1995; Benson 1998); (b) verify
compatibility with the Borcherds singular-theta-lift $2$-cocycle
of Borcherds 1998 \emph{Duke} 97:219; (c) thread the resulting
cocycle-free simultaneous-twining statement through the four
Gritsenko--Borcherds lift steps of
\eqref{eq:wave11-conj1prime-chain} at each $(N, h_M)$.
Completion of this step converts the forty-plus open
$(N, h_M)$ rows of Conjecture~$1'$ from conjectural to theorem,
closing the Mathieu-twined extension of the $N{=}1$ theorem.
The programme's $K3 \times E$ spectrum $\{2, 3, 5, 24\}$ and the
universal identity $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ then sit inside a
single unified statement of rigidity across all Mathieu-admissible
frame data.
