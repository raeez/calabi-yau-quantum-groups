\section{The $K3\times E$ Chiral BKM Frontier}
\label{wn:sec:k3e-chiral-bkm-frontier}

The $K3\times E$ row is controlled by four distinct constructions:
\[
  \kcat(K3\times E)
  =\chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)
  =2\cdot 0
  =0,
\]
\[
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)=3,\qquad
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=5,\qquad
  \kfib(K3)=24.
\]
The resulting spectrum is
\[
  \{0,3,5,24\}.
\]
The fibre value $\chi(\cO_{K3})=2$ is a useful local witness but is not
the categorical Euler invariant of the product.

\subsection{Borcherds weights and paramodular families}
\label{wn:subsec:k3e-borcherds-weights}

The Borcherds weight formula is
\[
  \kBKM(\Phi_N)=\frac{c_N(0)}{2}.
\]
For the CHL paramodular rows
\[
  N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\},
  \qquad
  (c_N(0))=(10,8,6,4,2),
\]
so that
\[
  \bigl(\kBKM(\Phi_1),\kBKM(\Phi_2),\kBKM(\Phi_3),
  \kBKM(\Phi_4),\kBKM(\Phi_6)\bigr)
  =(5,4,3,2,1).
\]
This is the CHL-averaged family attached to the $K3\times E$ BKM
denominator forms.

The extended Gritsenko--Clery diagonal-divisor catalogue is separate.
Its eight rows carry their own multiplier systems and cover data, with
weights
\[
  (5,2,3,1,2,1/2,3/2,1)
\]
and zero Fourier constants
\[
  (10,4,6,2,4,1,3,2),
\]
again satisfying $\kBKM=c(0)/2$ row by row.  These eight entries are
not a substitute for the CHL tuple without an explicit row map.

At $N=6$ the umbral moonshine Niemeier lattice is $6D_4$.  The cyclic
quotient singularity type is
\[
  2A_5+2A_2+2A_1 .
\]
The Niemeier anchor and the quotient-singularity decomposition are
different pieces of structure.

\subsection{The positive half and the chiral output}
\label{wn:subsec:k3e-positive-half}

The CY-to-chiral functor is dimension-sensitive:
\[
  \Phifun:\CYcat_d\longrightarrow \ChirAlg,
  \qquad d\geq 3 \Longrightarrow E_1\text{-chiral output}.
\]
The $E_2$ operation belongs to a centre, Drinfeld double, or
representation category after the required hypotheses are imposed.  It
does not live on the specialized algebra itself.

On $\mathbb C^3$ the cohomological Hall algebra is the positive half
\[
  \CoHA(\mathbb C^3)=\Yplus .
\]
The full $\mathcal W_{1+\infty}$ algebra enters through the Drinfeld
double or an evaluation representation.  The positive half,
the double, and the vertex-algebra image are three different objects.

\subsection{Six-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons}
\label{wn:subsec:k3e-hcs}

Let $X=\mathbb C^3$ with holomorphic volume form
\[
  \Omega=dz_1\wedge dz_2\wedge dz_3
\]
and let $\fg$ be a quadratic Lie algebra.  The classical holomorphic
Chern--Simons action is
\[
  S_{\hCS}(\cA)=
  \int_X\Omega\wedge
  \operatorname{tr}\left(\frac12\cA\bar\partial\cA
  +\frac13\cA^3\right),
\]
with equation of motion
\[
  \bar\partial\cA+\cA\wedge\cA=0.
\]
The BV field complex is
\[
  \cE=\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\mathbb C^3,\fg)[1].
\]
The observables form a holomorphically locally constant factorisation
algebra on $\mathbb C^3$, and the local-to-global comparison is
\[
  \Obs_{\hCS}(\mathbb C^3)\simeq
  \CE^*_{\bar\partial,\chir}(\cE_{\hCS},\cO_{\mathbb C^3})
  \qquad
  \text{in }\mathrm{Alg}_{E_3}(\Ch(\Dolb)).
\]
This $E_3$ statement is the ambient holomorphic field-theory statement.
After specialization to a curve in the CY-to-chiral construction at
$d=3$, the chiral output is $E_1$.

\subsection{Automorphic and field-theoretic normalizations}
\label{wn:subsec:k3e-normalizations}

The $N=1$ denominator is the Gritsenko additive lift
$\Delta_5=\mathrm{Grit}(\eta^9\vartheta_1)$, equivalently the
Borcherds product whose BKM algebra is
$\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5}$.  The archimedean parameters are
$(7/2,5/2)$ for $\Delta_5$; $(17/2,1/2)$ belongs to the
Saito--Kurokawa CAP form $\Delta_{10}$.

Costello's 2013 four-dimensional theory is the twisted
$\mathcal N=1$ gauge theory, and the Yangian statement follows after
combining that construction with the Costello--Gwilliam,
Costello--Dimofte--Paquette, and Costello--Yagi boundary and
Omega-background constructions.  The Beem--Rastelli normalization is
\[
  k_{2d}=-\frac{k_{4d}}{2};
\]
for the Minahan--Nemeschansky $E_8$ theory with $k_{4d}=12$, the
associated two-dimensional affine level is $-6$ and the central charge
is $-62$.

The Humbert divisor of the Gritsenko--Nikulin form satisfies
\[
  \{\Delta_5=0\}=2H_1+H_4.
\]
Thus the relevant monodromy orders are $8$ at $H_1$ and $16$ at $H_4$.

\subsection{Proof obligations}
\label{wn:subsec:k3e-proof-obligations}

Four computations decide the remaining frontier.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A direct trace-formula computation of
  $\dim S_5(\Sp_4(\ZZ),\nu_{\Delta_5})$ fixes the one-dimensional
  cusp-space input and the scalar normalization of the four
  $c=-214$ constructions.
  \item The simultaneous-twining obstruction requires an explicit
  calculation of
  \[
    H^3(C_{M_{24}}(g_N);U(1))
  \]
  for $N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$ and nontrivial commuting twining element.
  \item The class-$\mathcal S$ parent
  $\mathcal T[A_1,\Sigma_{0,24}]$ must pass through averaged flavour
  fugacities and a Borcherds singular-theta lift; the direct identity
  between its Schur index and $1/\Delta_5$ fails at the constant term.
  \item The all-orders descent from six-dimensional holomorphic
  Chern--Simons theory to the $K3\times E$ bi-based chiral BKM datum
  remains a construction to be written, with the ambient $E_3$ field
  theory and the specialized $E_1$ chiral algebra kept separate.
\end{enumerate}

Once these four calculations are supplied, the $N=1$ theorem and the
CHL rows at $N\in\{2,3,4,6\}$ sit in one statement: Borcherds weights
are always read as $c_N(0)/2$, the CHL constants are
$(10,8,6,4,2)$, and the extended Gritsenko--Clery rows are cited by
their own catalogue data.
