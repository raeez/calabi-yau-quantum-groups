\section*{Wave~10 Q3/20 --- Lorgat 2020 paper forensic extraction of $c_N(0)$ data}
\label{sec:wave10-q3-lorgat2020-cN-extraction}

\paragraph{Source.}
Lorgat, \emph{A Borcherds lift of the weak Jacobi form $\phi_{0,1}$,
generalized Borcherds-Kac-Moody superalgebras and the Igusa cusp form
$\Delta_5$}, preprint, dated April~2, 2020 (10 pages).

\paragraph{Datum 1: $\phi_{0,1}$ Fourier expansion, page~9 (display above \S6 Theorem~4).}
\ClaimStatusTheorem{}
\[
\phi_{0,1}(z_1,z_2) = (r^{-1} + 10 + r) + q\bigl(10 r^{-2} - 64 r^{-1} + 108 - 64 r + 10 r^2\bigr) + \ldots
\]
where $q = \exp(2\pi i z_1)$, $r = \exp(2\pi i z_2)$. The $q^1$ coefficient (pentad)
reads $(10, -64, 108, -64, 10)$; the constant $f(0,0) = 10$, $f(0,\pm 1) = 1$, $f(0, l) = 0$ for $l < -1$ (Remark~1 / Theorem~4 assembly, p.~10).

\paragraph{Datum 2: Conjecture~1 verbatim, page~2.}
\begin{quote}
\emph{``all eight diagonal divisor modular forms of Gritsenko-Clery arise, up to constant $C$, as reciprocal-square roots of $Z^X_{L,h_M}$. [$\cdots$] these Siegel paramodular forms all arise as denominator functions of generalized Borcherds-Kac-Moody superalgebras, with root multiplicities specified by $g_N - h_M$-twisted twined elliptic genera of K3 surfaces.''}
\end{quote}
(Elision of a connective preserving the two mathematical clauses: reciprocal-square-root realisation and BKM denominator-function realisation.)
Setting: $X = (S\times E)/(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})$ with $g_N$ a finite-order symplectic
K3 automorphism ($N \leq 8$ by Nikulin~[4]), $h_M$ of order~$M$, twisted zeta
function $Z^X_{L,h_M}$ counting $\tfrac{1}{M}$-fractional sheaves via
reduced numerical Donaldson-Thomas theory.

\paragraph{Datum 3: Theorem~1 (Gritsenko-Cl\'ery), page~1.}
\ClaimStatusTheorem{}
\emph{For all possible congruence subgroups $\Gamma_t(N) < \Gamma_t$ there are exactly 8 diagonal-divisor modular forms.}
The paper develops only the $\Gamma_1 = \Sp_4(\mathbb{Z})$ specimen, $\Delta_5$
of weight~5. It does \textbf{not} tabulate the remaining 7 forms' weights,
levels, or $c_N(0)$ values; it defers to Gritsenko-Cl\'ery [1].

\paragraph{Datum 4: $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ --- presence in paper.}
The paper does \textbf{not} use the symbol $\kBKM$. However, the
$\Delta_5$ specimen gives: weight $= 5$, and the $\phi_{0,1}$ constant
coefficient $f(0,0) = 10$ (p.~9, p.~10 Theorem~4 decomposition).
This matches $\kBKM(\Delta_5) = c_1(0)/2 = 10/2 = 5$, consistent with the
Vol~III universal identification (\texttt{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal}).

\paragraph{Heal: reconciliation with prior waves.}
Wave-9 M4 $(10,8,6,4,2)$: this is \emph{not} the $\phi_{0,1}$ $q^1$-pentad
but the sequence $c_N(0) = 2\kBKM(\Phi_N)$ for $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$
with $\kBKM = (5,4,3,2,1)$ --- the universal Borcherds-weight identity.
The Lorgat 2020 paper provides only the $N=1$ instance ($c_1(0) = 10$,
$\kBKM = 5$). Wave-9 N3 $(10,4,2,2,2)$ is incorrect; it conflates
$c_N(0)$ with the weight sequence of Gritsenko $\Delta_N$ for $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$,
which is $(5,2,1,1/2,1/4)$ (not integer for $N=4,6$).
