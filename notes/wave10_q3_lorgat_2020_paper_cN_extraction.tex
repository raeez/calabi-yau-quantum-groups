\section*{Lorgat 2020 and the Borcherds constant}
\label{sec:lorgat2020-cN-extraction}

\paragraph{The source.}
Lorgat's 2020 preprint
\emph{A Borcherds lift of the weak Jacobi form $\phi_{0,1}$,
generalized Borcherds-Kac-Moody superalgebras and the Igusa cusp form
$\Delta_5$} carries the level-one calculation for the primitive
Gritsenko-Nikulin denominator.  Its explicit input is the
Eichler-Zagier weak Jacobi form $\phiEZ$ and its explicit output is the
weight-five form $\Delta_5$.  The higher CHL constants enter through
the independent CHL averaged paramodular normalisation.

\paragraph{The Jacobi coefficient.}
Section~6 writes
\[
 \phiEZ(z_1,z_2)
 = (r^{-1}+10+r)
 + q(10r^{-2}-64r^{-1}+108-64r+10r^2)+O(q^2),
\]
with $q=\exp(2\pi i z_1)$ and $r=\exp(2\pi i z_2)$.  The first two
rows are
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
n & \ell=-2 & \ell=-1 & \ell=0 & \ell=1 & \ell=2\\
\hline
0 & 0 & 1 & 10 & 1 & 0\\
1 & 10 & -64 & 108 & -64 & 10
\end{array}
\]
where $\phiEZ=\sum f(n,\ell)q^nr^\ell$.  For index one the
Eichler-Zagier coefficient function is discriminant-indexed:
\[
 c(D)=f(n,\ell),\qquad D=4n-\ell^2 .
\]
The discriminant-zero value is
\[
 c_1(0)=f(0,0)=f(1,2)=f(1,-2)=10 .
\]
The polar terms $f(0,\pm1)=1$ give $c(-1)$, and the coefficients
$-64,108,-64$ lie at discriminants $3$ and $4$.

\paragraph{The primitive product.}
The level-one Borcherds product is
\[
 \DFive(Z):=64^{-1}\Delta_5(Z)
 =
 \exp\!\bigl(\pi i(z_1+z_2+z_3)\bigr)
 \prod_{\substack{n,l,m\in\mathbb{Z}\\ \text{positive in the Borcherds chamber}}}
 \bigl(1-\exp(2\pi i(nz_1+lz_2+mz_3))\bigr)^{f(nm,l)} .
\]
The exponent data are the Fourier coefficients of $\phiEZ$.  The
theta-leading coefficient of $\Delta_5$ is $64$, so $\DFive$ is the
monic primitive denominator.  Borcherds' weight theorem gives
\[
 \mathrm{wt}(\Delta_5)=\frac{c_1(0)}{2}=\frac{10}{2}=5 .
\]
In the notation used here this is
\[
 \kBKM(\Delta_5)=\kBKM(\Phi_1)=\frac{c_1(0)}{2}=5 .
\]

\paragraph{The doubled Igusa square.}
The K3 elliptic genus is
\[
 Z_{\mathrm{K3}}(\tau,z)=2\phiEZ(\tau,z).
\]
Using $Z_{\mathrm{K3}}$ in place of $\phiEZ$ doubles the Borcherds
exponents and gives the scalar Igusa square
\[
 \PhiTen^{\mathrm{un}}(Z)=\Delta_5(Z)^2,\qquad
 \mathrm{wt}(\PhiTen^{\mathrm{un}})=10 .
\]
The primitive denominator lane has $\kBKM(\Delta_5)=5$; the scalar
square lane has weight $10$.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The coefficient extracted from Lorgat 2020 is
\[
 c_1(0)=10,\qquad \kBKM(\Delta_5)=5 .
\]
The primitive CHL row is the table above.  The Igusa square comes from
$Z_{\mathrm{K3}}=2\phiEZ$ and has weight $10$.  The Gritsenko-Cl\'ery
diagonal-divisor catalogue and the singly twined row remain separate
normalisations.
