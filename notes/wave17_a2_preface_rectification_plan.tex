\section*{Positive Effective Moduli and the K3xE Normalisations}

The CY-to-chiral construction has two inputs.  The first is a
Calabi-Yau category \(\mathcal C_X\) of dimension \(d\).  The second is
a specialisation datum \((\Sigma_{d-1},C)\) selecting the factorisation
direction on which a chiral algebra is read.  The factorisation stage is
\[
  \PhiFA_d(\mathcal C_X),
\]
and the chiral output is
\[
  \Phid(\mathcal C_X;\Sigma_{d-1},C)
  =
  \SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\bigl(\PhiFA_d(\mathcal C_X)\bigr).
\]
For \(d\geq 3\) this output is \(E_1\)-chiral.  \(E_2\)-structure enters
only after passing to a centre, a Drinfeld double, or a module category,
for example through
\(\mathcal Z(\mathrm{Rep}^{E_1}(A))\) or through the completed double
of an ordered Hall positive half.

\paragraph{Positive half.}
A positive effective Hall datum for \(X\) is a triple
\[
  \bigl(\Meff(X),\phi_W,T_X\bigr)
\]
where \(\Meff(X)\) is the moduli stack of effective stable objects in
\(\mathcal C_X\), \(\phi_W\) is the complex of vanishing cycles for the
critical potential, and \(T_X\) is the equivariance group fixed by the
geometry.  Its positive half is
\[
  Y^+(X;T_X)
  :=
  H^*_{T_X}\bigl(\Meff(X),\phi_W\bigr)
\]
with Hall product defined by the correspondence of short exact
sequences or distinguished triangles.  This is an \(E_1\)-algebra in
the compact threefold case.  A stronger braided object requires the
centre or the completed double.

The equivariance group is part of the datum.  For a toric local
Calabi-Yau threefold it is the full algebraic torus.  For
\(K3\times E\) it is the reduced Donaldson-Thomas equivariance after the
translation mode of \(E\) has been divided out.  For an orbifold it is
the inertia stabiliser.  For the Borcherds and Igusa comparison it is
the arithmetic period group acting on the automorphic line.  The formula
for \(Y^+(X;T_X)\) has mathematical content only after this group, the
critical stack, and the vanishing cycles have all been fixed.

\paragraph{Local toric test.}
For the affine toric threefold,
\[
  \CoHA(\mathbb C^3)
  \cong
  Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
\]
as the Schiffmann-Vasserot positive half.  The full
\(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}\) belongs to the Drinfeld double, centre,
completion, and evaluation layer:
\[
  D\!\left(Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)\right)
  \rightsquigarrow
  \mathcal W_{1+\infty}.
\]
The ordered Hall algebra of \(\mathbb C^3\) supplies the positive half.
The vertex algebra is obtained after the double or centre has been
formed.

\paragraph{K3xE invariants.}
Let \(Y=K3\times E\).  K\"unneth gives
\[
  h^{0,\bullet}(Y)=(1,1,1,1),
  \qquad
  \sum_{q=0}^{3}(-1)^q h^{0,q}(Y)=1-1+1-1=0.
\]
The compact chiral supertrace is therefore
\[
  \kch(\mathcal A_Y)=0.
\]
The categorical Euler invariant is a different total-space invariant:
\[
  \kcat(Y)
  =
  \chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\chi(\mathcal O_E)
  =
  2\cdot 0
  =
  0.
\]
The Heisenberg-Mukai specialisation is a second-stage chiral reading,
separate from the compact supertrace:
\[
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(Y)=3.
\]
The Borcherds value belongs to the Igusa denominator algebra:
\[
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})
  =
  \frac{c_1(0)}{2}
  =
  \frac{10}{2}
  =
  5.
\]
The fibre invariant records the Mukai lattice rank of K3:
\[
  \kfib(Y)
  =
  \mathrm{rk}\,\widetilde H(K3,\mathbb Z)
  =
  1+22+1
  =
  24.
\]
These five readings are typed invariants attached to distinct
constructions.  The BKM weight \(5\) is
read solely from the constant term of the Borcherds input.  The compact
trace, the total-space Euler invariant, and the elliptic fibre Euler
characteristic have separate domains.

\paragraph{Igusa and reduced Donaldson-Thomas normalisation.}
The automorphic normalisations are
\[
  D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5,\qquad
  \Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}
  =
  D_5^2
  =
  4096^{-1}\Delta_5^2.
\]
The unnormalised Igusa square is
\[
  \Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}=\Delta_5^2,
\]
which is a different convention from the Oberdieck-Pandharipande scalar.
The reduced Donaldson-Thomas partition function is
\[
  Z_{\mathrm{DT,red}}(K3\times E)
  =
  -(\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}})^{-1}
  =
  -D_5^{-2}
  =
  -4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}.
\]
The factor \(4096\) is \(64^2\), the square of the conversion from the
theta-normalised \(\Delta_5\) to the monic product \(D_5\).

\paragraph{Two K3 quantum-group branches.}
The Hall-Drinfeld branch attached to \(K3\times E\) is the compact
oriented double of the positive Hall half, when the compact double datum
is supplied.  Its automorphic target is the Borcherds algebra
\(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5}\), with denominator \(\Delta_5\) and BKM
weight \(5\).  The Mukai Yangian branch is the self-mirror K3 branch:
the rank \(24\) Mukai-Heisenberg currents, their abelian
Drinfeld-type presentation, and the associated K3 self-duality data.
The two branches share Mukai-lattice input.  Their algebra objects,
completion data, and recognition problems are distinct.

\paragraph{Proof obligations.}
The compact \(K3\times E\) positive-half formula requires a reduced
critical CoHA after quotienting the \(E\)-translation zero mode, a
well-defined sheaf of vanishing cycles, and functorial Hall convolution on
the reduced moduli.  The compact Hall-Drinfeld double requires realised
positive and negative halves, a Cartan part, a nondegenerate Hopf
pairing, a radical quotient, a Borcherds-cone completion, and an
orientation character restricting to the multiplier of \(\Delta_5\).
The comparison with the Igusa denominator requires finite
Hall-Borcherds recognition: Borcherds relations, PBW, root-exhaustion
checks, parity, centre control, associator control, and compatibility
with \(D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5\).  The \(E_2\) and
\(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}\) assertions require the centre, double,
completion, or evaluation layer explicitly; they belong to the enhanced
layer above the bare \(E_1\) positive half.
