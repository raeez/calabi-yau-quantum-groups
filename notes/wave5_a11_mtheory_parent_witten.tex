\section{Attack-heal cycle 1: M-theory parent as synthesis, not theorem}
% ============================================================================

\paragraph*{Attack.}
The parent text writes
\begin{quote}
  ``The M-theory parent of $Y(\widehat{\fgl}(1|1))^{\mathrm{con}}$ is
  the 6D (2,0) $A_{K-1}$ theory on $\bR^{1,3} \times T^2$ with $K$
  M5-branes wrapped on $\bP^1 \subset \bY$, normal-bundle twist
  $\cO(-1) \oplus \cO(-1)$, $\Omega$-deformation on transverse
  Taub--NUT, and Costello--Gwilliam-twist on the CY$_3$ target.''
\end{quote}
with claim-status marker
\texttt{\textbackslash ClaimStatusConjectured}. The marker is right,
but the prose reads as a single coherent construction drawn from one
source. No such source exists. The statement combines at least five
independent primary-literature inputs: (i) the $\Omega$-background of
Nekrasov~\texttt{arXiv:hep-th/0206161}; (ii) the 5D hCS action of
Costello~\texttt{arXiv:1303.2632}; (iii) the 5D $\to$ VOA
all-orders theorem of Costello--Gaiotto--Yagi~\texttt{arXiv:2103.01835}
and Costello--Yagi~\texttt{arXiv:1810.01970}; (iv) the
Costello--Paquette M-theory/CoHA
dictionary~\texttt{arXiv:2001.02177},~\texttt{arXiv:2201.02595}; and
(v) the BFN/Putrov--Yagi Coulomb-branch
machinery~\texttt{arXiv:1601.03586},~\texttt{arXiv:1704.04255}. None
of these, as written, proves the conjecture.

\paragraph*{Heal.}
Insert the obstruction decomposition (O1)--(O4):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=(O\arabic*)]
\item BFN requires a non-degenerate Killing form; $\fgl(1|1)$
  supertrace annihilates the central $K$
  (Ginzburg--Schedler~\texttt{arXiv:0807.0174}).
\item Putrov--Yagi 4D/3D descent~\texttt{arXiv:1704.04255} is
  established for $\cN=2$ SCFTs; Klebanov--Witten is $\cN=1$.
\item Costello--Paquette $\bC^3$
  dictionary~\texttt{arXiv:2001.02177},~\texttt{arXiv:2201.02595} is
  established for local (non-compact, toric) setting with abelian
  $\widehat{\fgl}_1$; compact $\bP^1 \subset \bY$ with super gauge
  extension lies beyond its hypotheses.
\item Costello--Yagi $\Omega$-superalgebra
  machinery~\texttt{arXiv:1610.01790} identifies the Koszul dual of
  the perturbative algebra for bosonic simply-laced gauge theories;
  super case requires lifting the $E_2$-formality of
  Ginzburg--Schedler~\texttt{arXiv:0807.0174} to $E_3$, which is open.
\end{enumerate}
and, alongside the \texttt{\textbackslash ClaimStatusConjectured}
marker, add the parenthetical ``\emph{(physics synthesis; not a
single primary-literature theorem)}'' so that the reader cannot
mistake the status.

\section{Attack-heal cycle 2: $\Omega$-background is stratified}
% ============================================================================

\paragraph*{Attack.}
The parent text's phrase ``$\Omega$-deformation on transverse
Taub--NUT'' invites the reader to assume one uniform theorem-grade
status. The physical content has three layers of differing rigour.

\paragraph*{Heal.}

\begin{theorem}[$\Omega$-background stratification on $\bY$]
\label{thm:omega-background-stratification}
\ClaimStatusProvedElsewhere\ \emph{(stratified; layers (a)--(c) below)}
The $\Omega_{\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2}$-deformation of 5D $\cN=2$
Yang--Mills on $\bC^2_{\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2} \times S^1$
localises the instanton partition function on torus-fixed points of
$\mathrm{Hilb}^n(\bC^2)$
(Nekrasov~\texttt{arXiv:hep-th/0206161};
Nekrasov--Okounkov~\texttt{arXiv:hep-th/0306238}) and equals
Nakajima--Yoshioka equivariant Euler characteristics
(\texttt{arXiv:math/0306198},~\texttt{arXiv:math/0311058}).
Theorem-grade at the toric local $\bC^2$ model. The composite
``M-theory on $\bY \times S^1$ computes $\CoHA(\bY)$'' stratifies:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=(\alph*)]
\item \emph{M-theory $\to$ 5D $\cN=2$ geometric engineering.}
  Katz--Klemm--Vafa~\texttt{arXiv:hep-th/9609239};
  Iqbal--Kashani-Poor~\texttt{arXiv:hep-th/0306032}. \emph{Physics
  heuristic} with numerical checks.
\item \emph{Refined topological vertex $\to$ quiver-Yangian character.}
  Awata--Kanno~\texttt{arXiv:0805.0191};
  Iqbal--Koz\c{c}az--Vafa~\texttt{arXiv:hep-th/0701156}. \emph{Theorem
  at the level of equivariant $K$-theoretic characters.}
\item \emph{Coulomb-branch algebra $=$ quiver Yangian.}
  \emph{Conjecture} per (O1)--(O2) of the parent conjecture.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Sketch]
Layer (a) is the textbook M-theory/IIA reduction on a Calabi--Yau
threefold with isolated Riemann-surface singularities, producing a 5D
gauge theory with matter determined by the resolution graph. For
$\bY$ the resolution graph is a single $\bP^1$, and the engineered
gauge theory is 5D $\cN=2$ $SU(2)$ without matter, whose Seiberg--Witten
curve is the Nekrasov--Okounkov curve of genus $0$
(Nekrasov--Okounkov~\texttt{arXiv:hep-th/0306238}).

Layer (b) is the Awata--Kanno--Iqbal--Koz\c{c}az--Vafa theorem that
the refined topological-vertex partition function
$Z^{\mathrm{top}}_{\bY}(g_s, t; \varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2)$ equals
the character of the dual quiver Yangian
$Y(\widehat{\fgl}(1|1))^{\mathrm{con}}$ on its vacuum module, via
direct combinatorial expansion and Macdonald-polynomial manipulation.
This is theorem-grade at the level of formal $q$-$Q$ series.

Layer (c) is the bialgebra-level statement that the Coulomb-branch
algebra $\cA_\hbar(\mathrm{Hilb}^n(\bY))$ equals
$Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}(1|1))^{\mathrm{con}}$ as Hopf algebras. This
requires extending BFN past the non-degenerate-Killing-form regime
and Putrov--Yagi past $\cN=2$, neither of which is achieved in
published work.
\end{proof}

\section{Attack-heal cycle 3: B-model factorisation is character-level
  only}
% ============================================================================

\paragraph*{Attack.}
Reader could assume that because Gopakumar--Vafa
$\prod_n(1-Qq^n)^n$ \emph{is} the CoHA vacuum character, the B-model
partition function \emph{factorises through CoHA as an
$E_3$-algebra}. This is a category error: character equality is
weaker than $E_3$-algebra factorisation.

\paragraph*{Heal.}

\begin{theorem}[B-model character factorisation through $\CoHA$]
\label{thm:b-model-character-factorisation}
\ClaimStatusPartialPrimaryLit\
The topological string B-model partition function on $\bY$ at the
conifold point satisfies the BCOV
equation~\texttt{arXiv:hep-th/9309140} and evaluates to
\[
  Z^{\mathrm{top,B}}_{\bY}(g_s, t)
  \;=\; \prod_{n \geq 1} (1 - Q\, q^n)^n,
  \qquad q = e^{i g_s},\ Q = e^{-t},
\]
(Gopakumar--Vafa~\texttt{arXiv:hep-th/9809187},~\texttt{arXiv:hep-th/9812127};
Faber--Pandharipande~\texttt{arXiv:math/9810173}). Equality of formal
power series
\[
  Z^{\mathrm{top,B}}_{\bY}(g_s, t)
  \;=\; \chi_{\CoHA(\bY)}(q, Q)
\]
is \emph{theorem-grade at the level of graded characters}
(Kontsevich--Soibelman~\texttt{arXiv:0811.2435} Thm.~6.1;
Davison--Meinhardt~\texttt{arXiv:1512.08898};
Davison~\texttt{arXiv:1601.02479}). No primary source establishes an
$E_3$-algebra lift through which the open/closed B-model sector
factorises as $\CoHA(\bY)$-module algebras.
Aspinwall--Morrison~\texttt{arXiv:hep-th/9404151} identifies the
massless hypermultiplet at $t=0$ but does not determine $\CoHA$
structure across the conifold transition.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Why character $<$ $E_3$-algebra]
The character $\chi_{\CoHA(\bY)}$ determines only the Poincaré series
of the graded vector space. The $E_3$-algebra structure on $\CoHA(\bY)$
(Davison--Meinhardt~\texttt{arXiv:1512.08898}; chain-level
lifting via PT BPS dimensional reduction and Costello--Li holomorphic
locality~\texttt{arXiv:1201.4501}) carries the product, the shuffle
coproduct, the Hopf pairing. Equality of graded characters between
$Z^{\mathrm{top,B}}_{\bY}$ and $\chi_{\CoHA(\bY)}$ transports no
higher structure. The open/closed B-model sector is a module over the
bulk BCOV $L_\infty$-algebra; identification of this module structure
with a $\CoHA$-module structure would require the chain-level
$E_3$-lift of Davison--Meinhardt to be compatible with
Costello--Li~\texttt{arXiv:1201.4501} BCOV quantisation, which is
open.
\end{remark}

\section{Attack-heal cycle 4: (0,4) sigma-model claim is metaphor}
% ============================================================================

\paragraph*{Attack.}
A reader familiar with BLLPRvR might assume a parallel
4D$\,\to\,$2D chiral-algebra correspondence for the 2D $\cN=(0,4)$
sigma model to $\bY$. Theorem~\ref{thm:bllprvr-fails} of the parent
text rules out BLLPRvR direct application; the remaining question
is whether Tong--Turner or Gaiotto--Rap\v{c}\'ak provides a substitute.

\paragraph*{Heal.}

\begin{theorem}[$(0,4)$ sigma-model chiral-algebra claim: three-test
failure]
\label{thm:0-4-sigma-model-metaphor}
\ClaimStatusConjectured\ \emph{(physics metaphor; not theorem-grade)}
The identification ``chiral algebra of the 2D $\cN=(0,4)$ sigma
model to $\bY$ equals the conformal-limit chiral algebra of
$\CoHA(\bY)$ via BLLPRvR-type 4D/2D reduction'' fails three
primary-literature tests:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=(\roman*)]
\item Beem--Lemos--Liendo--Peelaers--Rastelli--van~Rees~\texttt{arXiv:1312.5344}
  requires 4D $\cN=2$, not 2D (0,4) directly.
\item Tong--Turner~\texttt{arXiv:1403.6723} instanton-moduli-space
  chiral algebras on $\bY$ produce the \emph{free} $\widehat{\fgl}_1$
  stalk per chart; gluing two such stalks into $\CoHA(\bY)$ requires
  the super-FM transition, which is open.
\item The Gaiotto--Rap\v{c}\'ak corner
  VOA~\texttt{arXiv:1703.00982} identifies $Y_{L,M,N}$ for toric
  CY$_3$ singularities; extension to compact-curve wraps on $\bY$ is
  open.
\end{enumerate}
The identification is therefore a \emph{physics metaphor} aligning
three constructions whose domains of validity do not pairwise
intersect at $\bY$ with $\fgl(1|1)$ gauge.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[What the physics metaphor does supply]
The metaphor does supply a \emph{character match}: the Tong--Turner
free $\widehat{\fgl}_1$ stalk on each chart $U_\pm \cong \bC^3$ has
vacuum character $\prod_n(1-q^n)^{-1}$; the Gaiotto--Rap\v{c}\'ak
corner VOA $Y_{1,1,1}$ has vacuum character
$\prod_n(1-q^n)^{-1}(1-Qq^n)^n$ after specialisation; and
$\chi_{\CoHA(\bY)} = \prod_n(1-Qq^n)^n$. The character arithmetic
$Y_{1,1,1} = \widehat{\fgl}_1 \otimes \CoHA(\bY)$ at the formal-series
level is therefore internally consistent. What is missing is the
chain-level $E_2$- or $E_3$-algebra identification of the three
sides, which the physics metaphor does not supply.
\end{remark}

\section{Attack-heal cycle 5: M2-brane wrap as heuristic, not proof}
% ============================================================================

\paragraph*{Attack.}
The phrase ``$M$2 branes wrapping curves'' in the parent task's
prompt invites the reading that the M2-brane spectrum \emph{is} the
$\CoHA$ of $\bY$, or that it \emph{proves} the BPS Lie algebra of
Proposition~\ref{prop:super-brackets-stub}. Both readings are
stronger than primary literature supports.

\paragraph*{Heal.}

\begin{remark}[M2-brane wrap on $\bP^1 \subset \bY$]
\label{rem:m2-wrap-status}
The BPS spectrum on $\bY$ is computed physically by M2-branes
wrapping holomorphic curves $C \subset \bY$; for $\bY =
\mathrm{Tot}(\cO(-1) \oplus \cO(-1))$, the effective curve classes
are $d[\bP^1]$ for $d \in \bZ_{\geq 0}$, and Gopakumar--Vafa fix
multiplicities $n_0^1 = 1$, $n_g^d = 0$ otherwise
(Gopakumar--Vafa~\texttt{arXiv:hep-th/9812127}). That these integers
compute the dimensions of cohomology on an M2-brane ground-state
moduli space is a physics heuristic
(Gopakumar--Vafa~\texttt{arXiv:hep-th/9809187},~\texttt{arXiv:hep-th/9812127};
Katz--Klemm--Vafa~\texttt{arXiv:hep-th/9910181}); the mathematical
realisation as perverse sheaves on moduli of coherent sheaves is the
Maulik--Toda conjecture~\texttt{arXiv:1610.07303}. At the level of
motivic wall-crossing invariants, the refinement is
$\Omega(d\gamma_C) = \delta_{d,1}$ (Kontsevich--Soibelman
\texttt{arXiv:0811.2435} \S1.5), matching the BPS Lie-algebra root
structure (real roots multiplicity $1$, imaginary roots multiplicity
$2$ from the super $H+K$ splitting). The M2-brane picture is a
physics heuristic matching the mathematical $\CoHA$ at the character
and root-multiplicity levels; it does not supply an independent
proof of the bialgebra structure.
\end{remark}

\section{Convergence}
% ============================================================================

After five cycles, the parent section's claim structure is
stratified into three tiers:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Theorem-grade at character level.} Graded-character
  equality $Z^{\mathrm{top,B}}_{\bY} = \chi_{\CoHA(\bY)}$
  (Theorem~\ref{thm:b-model-character-factorisation});
  refined-vertex $\to$ quiver-Yangian character
  (Theorem~\ref{thm:omega-background-stratification}(b)); M2-brane
  multiplicities $= $ motivic DT
  ($\Omega(d\gamma_C) = \delta_{d,1}$, Remark~\ref{rem:m2-wrap-status}).
\item \textbf{Physics heuristic.} M-theory $\to$ 5D $\cN=2$
  geometric engineering on $\bY$
  (Theorem~\ref{thm:omega-background-stratification}(a)); M2-brane
  ground-state spectrum $=$ GV integers
  (Remark~\ref{rem:m2-wrap-status}).
\item \textbf{Physics synthesis / metaphor / conjecture.} M-theory
  parent as bialgebra identification (parent Conjecture
  \texttt{conj:m-theory-parent}); 2D (0,4) sigma-model chiral algebra
  $=$ conformal-limit $\CoHA(\bY)$
  (Theorem~\ref{thm:0-4-sigma-model-metaphor}); Coulomb-branch
  algebra $=$ quiver Yangian
  (Theorem~\ref{thm:omega-background-stratification}(c));
  $E_3$-algebra lift of Gopakumar--Vafa character factorisation
  (Remark inside
  Theorem~\ref{thm:b-model-character-factorisation}).
\end{itemize}

None of the three tiers is demoted; none is elevated. The
contribution of this addendum is to label each layer so that the
parent conjecture's synthesis status (already flagged by
\texttt{\textbackslash ClaimStatusConjectured}) is accompanied by
stratification of what \emph{is} theorem-grade and what remains
physics synthesis. The M-theory parent, as a single unified
identification, is a conjecture; its individual layers range from
theorem to heuristic to metaphor, and the parent section should read
as five independent claims with differentiated status markers, not as
one uniform prose.
