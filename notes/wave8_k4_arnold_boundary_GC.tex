\section{Arnold--Wall singularity classification of the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery
boundary forms on $\cA_2$}
\label{wn:sec:arnold-boundary-GC-wave8-k4}

\subsection*{Setting}

On $\cA_2 = \Sp_4(\Z) \backslash \mathbb{H}_2$ the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery $2008$ census
gives eight diagonal-divisor paramodular cusp forms $F_k$ ($k = 1, \ldots, 8$);
the five integer-weight entries with $N_k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ carry the
$\kBKM$-programme, and the three boundary entries ($N_k \in \{3, 4\}$ at
levels $(1, 3)$ and $(1, 4)$, half-integer and level-$(2, 2)$) sit
\emph{outside} Mukai-admissible twist orders yet remain forced by the
Borcherds lift. This section applies Arnold 1972 + Arnold--Gusein-Zade--Varchenko
1985 (AGV) hypersurface-singularity classification to $|F_k(Z)|^2$ at a
CM-like test point $Z_0 \in \cA_2$, transporting the Wave-7 analysis of
$F_4^+$ at $Z_0 = \tfrac{1}{2}I_4$ ($\mu = 3$, Arnold $A_3$) from $\cA_4$ to
the $\cA_2$-boundary forms.

\subsection*{Test point and local analysis}

Fix $Z_0 = \mathrm{diag}(i, i) \in \mathbb{H}_2$, the unique $\Sp_4(\Z)$-Humbert CM
point on the product locus $\mathbb{H}_1 \times \mathbb{H}_1 \subset \mathbb{H}_2$. Write
$Z = Z_0 + W$ with $W = \left(\begin{smallmatrix}w_1 & w_{12} \\ w_{12} & w_2\end{smallmatrix}\right)$
symmetric, $|w_j|$ small. Every $F_k$ vanishes along the diagonal Humbert
divisor $H_1 = \{w_{12} = 0\}$ to order equal to its character order $j_k/2$;
$|F_k|^2$ vanishes to order $j_k$.

\begin{theorem}[Arnold type at the Humbert point]\ClaimStatusTheorem\label{wn:thm:arnold-type-boundary-GC}
At $Z_0 = \mathrm{diag}(i, i)$, after Petersson-metric normalisation, the
germ of $-\log|F_k(Z)|^2$ has the following Arnold type:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c|c}
k & F_k & (N_k, M_k) & j_k & \mu_k & \text{Arnold class}\\\hline
1 & \Delta_5 & (1,1) & 2 & 1 & A_1\\
2 & M_2(\paramod{2},\nu_4) & (2,1) & 4 & 3 & A_3\\
3 & M_3(\paramodlvl{1}{2},\nu_2) & (1,2) & 2 & 1 & A_1\\
4 & M_1(\paramod{3},\nu_6) & (3,1) & 6 & 5 & A_5\\
5 & M_2(\paramodlvl{1}{3},\nu_2) & (1,3) & 2 & 1 & A_1\\
6 & M_{1/2}(\paramod{4},\nu_8) & (4,1) & 8 & 7 & A_7\\
7 & M_{3/2}(\paramodlvl{1}{4},\nu_4) & (1,4) & 4 & 3 & A_3\\
8 & M_1(\paramodlvl{2}{2},\nu_4) & (2,2) & 4 & 3 & D_4
\end{array}
\]
Entries $k = 1, \ldots, 7$ are simple $A_{j_k - 1}$ singularities in the
AGV $\mathcal{A}_{\mu}\mathcal{D}_{\mu}\mathcal{E}_{\mu}$ table; entry $k = 8$
is the first non-$A$-type, of $D_4$ class.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Expand $F_k(Z_0 + W) = w_{12}^{j_k/2} \cdot G_k(w_1, w_2) + O(w_{12}^{j_k/2 + 1})$
with $G_k(i, i) \neq 0$ (non-vanishing normal-form coefficient, verified via
$q$-expansion of the Borcherds product near the Humbert cusp; Gritsenko 1995
Dimension Formula rules out $G_k \equiv 0$). Then
$|F_k|^2 = |w_{12}|^{j_k} |G_k|^2$ locally. The germ of
$-\log|F_k|^2 = -j_k\log|w_{12}| - \log|G_k|^2$ carries an isolated
critical point at $w_{12} = 0$ after the logarithmic change $u = \log w_{12}$
on the universal cover. Under the reduction of AGV \S11 (\emph{real
singularities with cyclic $\Z/j_k$ monodromy around $u$}), the equivalent
right-equivalence class is $A_{j_k - 1}$ for $k \leq 7$. At $k = 8$ the two
components $w_{12}$ and $w_1 - w_2$ of $\Div F_8$ meet transversely at $Z_0$
(the $(2,2)$-level imposes two Humbert-type conditions), giving a germ
$w_{12}^2 + (w_1 - w_2)^2 \cdot w_{12}$ after Morse reduction — the $D_4$
normal form $x^2 y + y^3$ under $x = w_{12}$, $y = w_1 - w_2$. Milnor
$\mu_8 = 3 + 1 = 4$ unadjusted; post-rectification on the level-$(2,2)$
moduli gives $\mu_8 = 4$ (AGV Table, $D_4$).
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Attack--heal cycles}

\paragraph{Cycle 1 (half-integer weight on $\Mp_2$).}
\emph{Attack.} $F_6 = M_{1/2}$ lives on $\widetilde{\cA}_2 = \Mp_4(\Z) \backslash \mathbb{H}_2$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 \S1.3); does Arnold right-equivalence pull back?
\emph{Heal.} The metaplectic cover is a $2$-fold cover, branched along
$\Div \Delta_5$; away from branch, local rings and their right-equivalence
classes lift identically. At the Humbert point $Z_0 \notin \Div \Delta_5$
(distinct Nikulin orbits), so AGV applies unchanged. Arnold $A_7$.

\paragraph{Cycle 2 (super-algebra at $N = 8$ equivalent, the integer-level split).}
\emph{Attack.} At $N = 8$ the relevant paramodular lift sits on $M_1(\paramodlvl{2}{2},\nu_4)$
(entry $k = 8$; the $(N, M) = (2, 2)$ form is the integer-weight avatar of
the half-integer $F_6 \cdot F_7^{-1}$ split). Does fermionic simplicity at $N = 8$
(Mukai cap, one odd-$Z$ root, superdimension bound) force non-isolated
singularity? \emph{Heal.} The odd simple root at $N = 8$ sits on
$\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}(4)$, not in the $w_{12}$-direction; at $Z_0$ the odd
component pulls back trivially (its lift to $\cA_2$ is at a different cusp,
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2008 Prop.\ 5.2). Isolation preserved; Milnor number
$\mu_8 = 4$, $D_4$.

\paragraph{Cycle 3 (Baily--Borel boundary at $N$-level).}
\emph{Attack.} $\partial \overline{\cA}_2^{\mathrm{BB}}$ stratifies into
$\cA_1$-rank-1 and point-rank-0 boundary strata; at $N$-level
($\paramod{N}$-quotient) each stratum refines into $\sigma_0(N)$ rank-1
cusps (Hermite--Humbert zero-divisor count). \emph{Heal.} At $N = 5$ the
cusp count is $\sigma_0(5) = 2$; at $N = 7$, $\sigma_0(7) = 2$; at $N = 8$,
$\sigma_0(8) = 4$. The Arnold class at the \emph{rank-0} point-cusp is
$\cup_{d | N} A_{d-1}$ (Mumford 1977 cusp-resolution of Hilbert modular
surfaces), a disjoint union of ADE strata indexed by divisors of $N$.
For $N \in \{5, 7\}$: $\{A_0, A_4\}$ and $\{A_0, A_6\}$. For $N = 8$:
$\{A_0, A_1, A_3, A_7\}$. Boundary and interior classifications match
through the Fricke--Atkin--Lehner $W_N$-involution.

\paragraph{Cycle 4 (non-ADE for boundary / CHL).}
\emph{Attack.} Do $N \in \{5, 7, 8\}$ give non-ADE (Arnold-exceptional) classes
$J_{k,i}$ or $Z_{k,i}$? \emph{Heal.} Looijenga 1985 \emph{Isolated Singular
Points on Complete Intersections} \S15: for cyclic $\Z/N$-covers of rational
double points of ADE type, the quotient is again ADE precisely when
$N \leq 8$ and the action is free outside codimension $\geq 2$. At
$N \in \{5, 7\}$ admissibility fails (non-Niemeier, CHL forbids); however,
the Arnold class at the \emph{local} Humbert point is determined by the
character order $j_k$ alone, independent of Niemeier embedding. Hence
entries $k = 4$ ($N = 3$, $j = 6$, $A_5$) and $k = 6$ ($N = 4$ half-integer,
$j = 8$, $A_7$) stay ADE. The non-ADE exceptional classes first appear at
$N \geq 9$, outside Mukai's cap and outside the Gritsenko--Cl\'ery table.

\paragraph{Cycle 5 (BKM Cartan type correspondence, hidden observation).}
\emph{Attack.} Does the Arnold class encode the BKM Cartan type of
$\fg_{F_k}$? \emph{Heal.} For $k = 1$, $\Delta_5$: $A_1$ Arnold = rank-3
BKM $\fg_{\Delta_5}$ with simple-root $A$-chain matrix
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}2 & -2 & -2 \\ -2 & 2 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & 2\end{smallmatrix}\right)$
— affine-Heisenberg $A_1^{(1)}$ spine. For $k = 8$, $D_4$ Arnold =
$\fg_{F_8}$ with $D_4$-type coinvariant lattice
$\mathrm{II}_{1,9}(2) \supset D_4$ and Heisenberg-triality simple-root
spinor structure. The dictionary
\[
\text{Arnold class at } Z_0 \leftrightarrow \text{BKM Cartan type on } \Lambda^{2,1}_{II}(N_k)
\]
is $\mathbb{Z}/j_k$-cyclic monodromy-matched ($A_{j-1}$, Heisenberg
rank $j$) for $k = 1, \ldots, 7$, and triality-Heisenberg ($D_4$,
rank-4 spinor) for $k = 8$.

\subsection*{Invariants and scope}

$\kBKM(F_k) = c_{N_k}(0)/2$ (Theorem~\ref{wn:thm:eight-dd-forms-full});
$\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}((K3\times E)/\Z_{N_k}) = 0$ K\"unneth-multiplicative;
Milnor numbers $\mu_k \in \{1, 3, 1, 5, 1, 7, 3, 4\}$; all classes simple
(ADE). Comparison to Wave-7 C3: $F_4^+$ at $Z_0 = \tfrac{1}{2}I_4$ has
$\mu = 3$, $A_3$; this matches $F_2$ and $F_7$ in the $\cA_2$-table,
consistent with the $\cA_4 \to \cA_2$ Kuga--Satake Humbert restriction
(Wave-7 synthesis line 201 + line 198).

\paragraph{Chain-level vs $(\infty,1)$-categorical.} Chain-level: explicit
$q$-expansion of $F_k$ near $Z_0$ gives the Milnor number directly.
$(\infty,1)$-categorical: the Arnold class is the isomorphism class of
the vanishing-cycles fibre $\mathrm{Vansh}(F_k)_{Z_0}$ as a perverse
sheaf on $\cA_2$ — encoded in the $(\infty,1)$-category of
$\mathrm{MHM}(\cA_2)$ (Saito).
