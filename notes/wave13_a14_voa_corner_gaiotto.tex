\begin{abstract}

real-root datum on $\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}$ and a $\phi_{0,1}$-graded imaginary
spectrum whose Weyl--Kac--Borcherds denominator is $\tfrac{1}{64}\Delta_5(2z)$.
The 4d $\cN{=}2$ Schur-chiral correspondence of Beem--Lemos--Liendo--
Peelaers--Rastelli--van~Rees (BBBLN 2015) associates to any 4d $\cN{=}2$
superconformal theory $\cT$ a vertex algebra $\chi(\cT)$ with central charge
$c_{2d} = -12\, c_{4d}$. The mission is: identify the 4d $\cN{=}2$ theory
$\cT_{\Delta_5}$ whose Schur-chiralisation is the vertex envelope of
$\fg_{\Delta_5}$, and state its corner-VOA avatar. Five attack--heal cycles
establish: (i) the Heisenberg / K3$\times T^2$ low-energy route is
$\cN{=}4$ not $\cN{=}2$ and fails the BBBLN hypothesis; (ii) the 6d $(2,0)$
$A_{N-1}$ on $K3\times T^2$ with a half-twist is $\cN{=}2$ on $T^2$ and
is the correct UV seed; (iii) the corner VOA for the Mukai block is
$\cY_{4,20,0}(\psi)$ at Dunn-twisted levels, which is the $\Omega$-deformed
$\cW_{4,20,0}$; (iv) the $\phi_{0,1}$-imaginary-root tail lies outside
any finite-$(L,M,N)$ corner and enters through Miki's horizontal-to-vertical
rearrangement of $\cW_{1+\infty}$ at self-dual slope; (v) $\Delta_5$ is
\emph{not} the vacuum character of any single $\cY_{L,M,N}$, but arises as

$\kappa_{\BKM}(\Delta_5) = c_1(0)/2 = 5$ via the central-charge match

\end{abstract}

\section*{Overview}

$\chi: \{4\text{d}\ \cN{=}2\ \mathrm{SCFTs}\}\to\mathrm{VOA}$ takes a 4d

the Schur operators are the cohomology of a nilpotent supercharge

$\widehat{\fgl}_\infty$ and vertical $\widehat{\fsl}_\infty$ subalgebras

rearrangement gives a triality-invariant slice.

\section{Attack--heal 1. The $\cN{=}4$ heterotic / K3$\times T^2$ route
  fails the BBBLN hypothesis}
\label{sec:cycle1}
% ==========================================================================

\begin{attack}
Consider the most naive guess: the heterotic string compactified on $K3\times
T^2$ produces, at low energy, a 4d $\cN{=}4$ $E_8\times E_8$ (or $Spin(32)/\Z_2$)
gauge theory broken to a rank-$24$ Narain moduli. The $\fg_{\Delta_5}$ Cartan
is rank-$3$, sitting inside $\Lambda^{2,1}_{II}\subset\Lambda^{3,2}$;
$\Lambda^{3,2}$ is the signature-$(3,2)$ lattice inside the even unimodular
$\Lambda_\Muk(K3)\oplus U_E$ of signature $(5,21)$ under the ``transfer
of automorphic forms'' isomorphism $\Sp_4(\Z)/\{\pm I\} \simeq
O(\Lambda^{3,2})_+/\{\pm I\}$ of the source paper \S3. One then proposes
$\cT_{\Delta_5}$ = this heterotic 4d $\cN{=}4$ theory, and identifies
$\chi(\cT_{\Delta_5})$ via BBBLN with the universal enveloping VOA
$U(\fg_{\Delta_5})_{\kappa_{\BKM}}$ at the Borcherds level
$\kappa_{\BKM} = 5$.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
\emph{The heterotic / K3$\times T^2$ route is the correct UV seed, but the
theory it produces is $\cN{=}4$, not $\cN{=}2$; BBBLN's Schur-chiralisation
requires $\cN{=}2$ \emph{strictly}, since the protected $SU(1,1|1)\subset
SU(2,2|2)$ subalgebra used to define Schur cohomology only exists inside
the $\cN{=}2$ superconformal algebra.}
\end{ghost}

\begin{heal}
The resolution is to break $\cN{=}4\to\cN{=}2$ by a half-supersymmetry-
preserving deformation. Two canonical routes:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \emph{Argyres--Douglas route}: mass-deform the $\cN{=}4$ hypers
  by an $\cN{=}2$ mass $m$; the Coulomb branch becomes the $\cN{=}2^*$
  theory on $K3\times T^2$, whose Schur sector is governed by the elliptic
  genus of the $K3$ fibre (known to be $2\phi_{0,1}$).
  \item \emph{Twisted-compactification route}: take 6d $(2,0)$ $A_{N-1}$
  on $K3\times T^2$ with a geometric Nekrasov-Shatashvili half-twist
  along $T^2$ that reduces $\cN{=}(2,2)$ on $T^2$ to $\cN{=}2$ on the
  remaining 2d worldvolume. This is the Gaiotto--Moore--Neitzke setup
  for $\cT[M_3]$ with $M_3 = K3\times S^1$.
\end{itemize}
The second route is the one that aligns with the Gaiotto--Rapcak corner.
\end{heal}

The attack is substantive: the naive $\cN{=}4$ reading is ruled out by
the BBBLN hypothesis. The heal relocates the UV seed in 6d $(2,0)$ where
the half-twist delivers $\cN{=}2$ on the 4d worldvolume. This is the
route compatible with the Mukai-block corner $\cY_{4,20,0}$.

\section{Attack--heal 2. The 6d $(2,0)$ $A_{N-1}$ on $K3\times T^2$

\section{Attack--heal 5. $\Delta_5$ is the denominator, not the vacuum

\begin{attack}
If the VOA avatar of $\fg_{\Delta_5}$ is
$\cY^{\mathrm{Miki}}_{\infty,\infty,\infty}(\psi_{\mathrm{Muk}},\phi_{0,1})$
of Conjecture~\ref{conj:miki-corner}, then there should be a vacuum

\[
  \Phi \;=\; \tfrac{1}{64}\Delta_5(2z)
  \;=\; \exp\bigl(-2\pi i(\rho,z)\bigr)\prod_{\alpha\in\Delta_+}
  \bigl(1 - \exp(-2\pi i(\alpha,z))\bigr)^{\mathrm{mult}\,\alpha}

of the GBKM superalgebra $\fg_{\Delta_5}$ acting on the trivial $\C$-module.

vacuum character of its enveloping vertex algebra: the denominator is
$\Phi(q) = \chi_{\mathbb{C}}(q)\cdot\prod_\alpha(1-q^\alpha)^{\mathrm{mult}
\alpha}$, whereas the vacuum character is
$\chi_{V_0}(q) = \prod_\alpha(1-q^\alpha)^{-\mathrm{mult}\alpha}$ (reciprocal
exponent). Stating ``$\Delta_5$ is the vacuum character'' is a sign error
on the imaginary-root exponent.
\end{attack}

\begin{ghost}
\emph{$\Delta_5$ is the denominator, not the vacuum character, of the
GBKM Lie superalgebra $\fg_{\Delta_5}$. The vacuum character of its
enveloping vertex algebra is $1/\Delta_5$ (up to the Weyl-shift).}
\end{ghost}

directly. The 4d $\cN{=}2$ Schur index and the 2d chiralised vacuum
character are related by reciprocal exponent: if

\[
  \mathrm{ch}_{\chi(\cT_{\Delta_5})}(q_1,q_2,q_3)
  \;=\; \frac{1}{(\tfrac{1}{64}\Delta_5(2z))^{\pm 1}},
\]
with the sign determined by whether one takes the character of the
symmetric or exterior-algebra piece of the GBKM universal enveloping. The
even-imaginary subspace $\Delta^{\mathrm{im}}_0 = \{\tau(a)a\mid(a,a) = 0,
