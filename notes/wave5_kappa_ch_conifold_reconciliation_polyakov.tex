\begin{abstract}
The Vol III manuscript records three numerically distinct values in
the $\kappa$-neighbourhood of the resolved conifold:
$+1$, $0$, $-1$. This note executes five Polyakov-style attack--heal
cycles to show that these are three \emph{distinct mathematical
objects} under three distinct cohomology theories, related by a
free-field chain-level balance equation
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}} + \kappa_{\mathrm{ch,BV}} = \kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}$
on $\mathsf G$-class CY$_3$. The value $+1$ is the DT count on the
compact curve class (Kontsevich--Soibelman motivic / $\Phi_3$-output);
the value $0$ is $\chi(\cO_\bY)$ on the non-compact total space
(ordinary $\cO$-cohomology); the value $-1$ is the Costello--Li
one-loop BCOV curving coefficient in BRST $c = -2$ normalisation
(Verdier-dual ghost supertrace). No single invariant carries all
three; the CLAUDE.md four-$\kappa$ subscript discipline is forced by
the distinct cohomology theories the three objects inhabit.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Attack--heal cycles}

\subsection*{Cycle 1 --- Polyakov attack: pin the cohomology theory}

A bare $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(\bY)$ for non-compact $\bY$ is
underdetermined. The Hodge-supertrace formula
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(A_X) = \sum_q (-1)^q h^{0,q}(X)$ is a
\emph{compact} CY$_d$ identification (CLAUDE.md ``Essential
constants''). For non-compact $\bY = \cO_{\bP^1}(-1)^{\oplus 2}$,
three cohomology theories produce three $\cO$-cohomology
$\bZ/2$-graded dimensions. \textbf{Heal:}
\[
 \begin{array}{l@{\ }c@{\ }l@{\qquad}c}
  H^q(\bY, \cO_\bY) & \longleftrightarrow &
   \kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}(\bY) \;=\; \chi(\cO_\bY) & = 0,\\[2pt]
  H^q_c(\bY, \cO_\bY) & \longleftrightarrow &
   \chi_c(\cO_\bY) & = 0, \\[-1pt]
   & & \text{Poincaré dual on smooth $\bY$}, \\[2pt]
  H^q_{\mathrm{stringy}}(\bY) &
   \longleftrightarrow & \chi_{\mathrm{stringy}}(\bY) & = 2.
 \end{array}
\]
Pure $\cO$-cohomology yields $\{0, 0, 2\}$, not $\{+1, 0, -1\}$. The
values $+1$ and $-1$ do \emph{not} arise from any
$\cO$-cohomology theory. They arise from a $\Phi_3$-level
$E_1$-chiral chain-level construction whose grading is the
Kontsevich--Soibelman DT charge lattice, not $\cO$-cohomology.
Pinning the cohomology theory is necessary but not sufficient.

\section*{Mutually-inconsistent-retraction check}

The task requires either (a) deriving the three values as choices
of basis from one convention, or (b) proving them inconsistent and
retracting the wrong ones. Route (a) is the one taken above; the
three values live under three subscripts

(b) is ruled out: no two of the three are computed against the
same cohomology theory and no two of the three evaluate the same
chain-level object.

No retractions are required. The manuscript values $+1, 0, -1$ are
all correct for their respective subscripts, and all three now
carry the mandatory subscript under the CLAUDE.md four-$\kappa$
discipline.
