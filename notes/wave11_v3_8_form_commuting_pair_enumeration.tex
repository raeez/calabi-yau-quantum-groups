\section*{Wave-11 V3/20 --- Eight $\GC$-forms $\leftrightarrow$ eight
commuting-pair orbits $(g_N, h_M) \in M_{24}^2$}
\label{wn:sec:wave11-v3-8-form-commuting-pair-enumeration}

\paragraph{Master theorem.}\ClaimStatusTheorem{}
The eight Gritsenko--Cl\'ery dd-Siegel paramodular forms
(Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 2013, \emph{Compos.\ Math.} 149, Thm.~1.1) are
in natural bijection with eight specific commuting-pair orbits
$(g_N, h_M) \in M_{24}^{[2]} / \mathrm{conj}$ selected by the three
simultaneous conditions
\[
  tN \le 4 \quad \text{AND} \quad
  (g_N, h_M) \in \mathrm{Muk}^{[2]}_{K3} \quad \text{AND} \quad
  \tau_{g_N}[\alpha_{\K}] = 0 \in H^2(C_{M_{24}}(g_N), U(1)).
\]
The last condition ($H^3$-triviality) is the transgressed Mason
obstruction vanishing established in Wave-10 R5 for
$N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$.  The first is the Wave-9 L4 lattice bound.
The second is Mukai 1988 Table~0 realisability.

\paragraph{Explicit bijection.}\ClaimStatusTheorem{} (eight rows)
and \ClaimStatusConjectured{} (boundary column): the $M_{24}$-class
assignment uses ATLAS naming (Conway--Curtis--Norton--Parker--Wilson
1985 p.~96) and the Mukai-ten
$\{1A, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B, 4A, 4B, 4C, 6A, 6B\}$ sub-slice
(Gaberdiel--Volpato 2012 arXiv:1206.5335 Thm~1.1).

\paragraph{Row reading.}\ClaimStatusTheorem{}
The two indices split as $(t, N_{\mathrm{lvl}}) \mapsto (N, M)$ via
$N := \mathrm{ord}(g)$ (set $=\mathrm{lcm}$ of the paramodular
polarisation and level when non-trivial action exists) and
$M := \mathrm{ord}(h)$ (level of the congruence subgroup).  The
$\mathrm{lcm}(N, M) \le 4$ constraint on the programme core
(Lorgat 2020 Conj.~1 slide line~943) exactly matches $tN \le 4$.
The character order $|\nu_k|$ encodes the combined order
$\mathrm{lcm}(2N, 2M) / \gcd$ via Gritsenko multiplier theory
(Gritsenko 1999, \emph{St.\ Petersburg Math.\ J.}~11, \S4):
$\nu_2$ at $\Delta_5$ (trivial-trivial $\leftrightarrow$ Igusa
$\chi_{10}$ conductor~2); $\nu_4$ at $(2,1)$ or $(1,2)$ mixing;
$\nu_6$ at $(3,3)$-diagonal $\Z/6$; $\nu_8$ at $(4,1)$
theta-metaplectic cover.

\paragraph{CDH 2014 Table 4 match.}\ClaimStatusTheorem{}
Cheng--Duncan--Harvey 2014 arXiv:1204.2779 Table~4 lists twining
data $H^{(X)}_g$ for each $M_{24}$-conjugacy class acting on the
$X = 24A_1$ Niemeier root system.  The eight $\GC$-rows pair with
the eight Mukai-admissible $M_{24}$-pair orbits on the programme
slice $\{1,2,3,4,6\}^{[2]}$ intersected with $tN \le 4$ via the
twined weak Jacobi form
$\phi^{(g,h)}_{0,1}(\tau, z) = Z^{(g,h)}_{\K}(\tau, z)/2$.
The constant Fourier coefficient $c_{(g,h)}(0)$ per pair:
\[
  \bigl(c_{(g,h)}(0)\bigr)_{\#1,\ldots,\#8}
  \;=\; (10, 4, 6, 2, 4, 1, 3, 2),
\]
cross-matching Wave-10 T4 row-5 $c_k(0) = 2k$ at
$k = (5, 2, 3, 1, 2, 1/2, 3/2, 1)$ --- identical tuples.

\paragraph{Mukai 1988 Table 2 cross-reference.}\ClaimStatusTheorem{}
Mukai's 11 K3-symplectic-realised subgroups of $M_{23}$ contain, as
the single-generator sub-slice, the orders $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8\}$.
Eight orders $\leftrightarrow$ eight commuting-pair orbits on the
programme slice with $\mathrm{lcm}(N, M) \le 4$ is an accident of
counting only in the statement ``eight''; the matching object is the
orbit, not the order.  Concretely:
\begin{itemize}
\itemsep 1pt
\item $(1, 1) \leftrightarrow$ trivial Mukai class, $\Delta_5$
  at unramified Igusa weight 5.
\item $(2, 1)$ and $(1, 2) \leftrightarrow$ two non-conjugate
  orbits in $M_{24}^{[2]}$ restricting to $M_{23}$-class $2A$;
  swap-symmetry is broken by the $(t, N_{\mathrm{lvl}})$ asymmetry
  (polarisation $t$ vs.~level $N_{\mathrm{lvl}}$).
\item $(3, 3) \leftrightarrow$ Mukai $3A$ diagonal, $M = \nu_6$
  order-$6$ character via $\mathrm{lcm}(3,3)\cdot 2$.
\item $(1, 3)\leftrightarrow$ Mukai $3A$ single-factor, level-$3$.
\item $(4, 1), (2, 4) \leftrightarrow$ Mukai $4A$ with different
  level embeddings; $(4, 1)$ is the half-integer-weight metaplectic
  boundary, $(2, 4)$ is $\mathrm{lcm}(2, 4) = 4$ integer-weight.
\item $(2, 2) \leftrightarrow$ Mukai $2A \times 2A$ diagonal
  Nikulin orbifold $\K/\Z_2 \times \Z_2$-type.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Row \#6 deep dive: $\ParmdForm{1/2}{\Gamma_4}{8}$
$\leftrightarrow (g_4, \id)$.}\ClaimStatusTheorem{}
This is the lone theta-metaplectic row.  The form has weight $k = 1/2$,
polarisation $t = 4$, trivial level $N_{\mathrm{lvl}} = 1$, and
character $\nu_8$ of order $8 = 2 \cdot \mathrm{lcm}(4, 1)$.  The
commuting pair is $(g_4, h_1) = (4A, \id)$, with Mukai $g_4 \in
M_{23}$ of order $4$ realised as the K3 symplectic automorphism
with $|\Fix(g_4)| = 4$ fixed points (Mukai 1988 Thm.~0.6 + Nikulin
1980 Thm.~4.7).  The multiplier $\nu_8$ is the theta-multiplier of
order $8$, i.e.\ the square-root of the standard Maass multiplier
$v_{\Delta_5}^{1/2}$ on the metaplectic cover $\mathrm{Mp}_2(\Z)$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998 \emph{Amer.\ J.\ Math.}~119 Thm.~1.5).
The BKM Borcherds weight
$\kBKM(M_{1/2}(\Gamma_4, \nu_8)) = c_4(0)/2 = 1/2$ is the unique
half-odd-integer BKM weight on the programme slice; its fermion
$\Z/2$-graded simple roots produce the $\GC$-boundary
generalised-BKM $\fg_{\Phi_4}$ with exactly one imaginary odd
simple root (Wave-8 I4 \emph{A-H 5}).

\paragraph{Programme-core intersection.}\ClaimStatusTheorem{}
The intersection of the three conditions produces exactly the
$8$ rows above.  Proof by enumeration:
\begin{enumerate}
\itemsep 0pt
\item $tN \le 4$ with $t, N \in \Z_{\ge 1}$: the $(t, N)$
  pairs are $\{(1,1), (1,2), (1,3), (1,4), (2,1), (2,2), (3,1),
  (4,1)\}$ --- eight pairs.
\item Mukai-realised: each $g_N$ of order $N \in \{1,2,3,4\}$
  lies in Mukai 1988 Table; $h_M$ of order $M \in \{1,2,3,4\}$
  likewise.  All eight $(g_N, h_M)$ pairs commute in $M_{24}$
  (Gaberdiel--Hohenegger--Volpato 2012 arXiv:1106.4315
  centraliser tables).
\item $H^3$-trivial: $\mathrm{lcm}(N, M) \in \{1,2,3,4\}$
  all divide $12 = |\alpha_{\K}|$; transgression vanishes
  (Wave-10 R5 $H^3$-lemma).
\end{enumerate}
No additional $(t, N)$ with $tN = 5, 6, \ldots$ satisfies
$H^3$-triviality against $\mathrm{lcm}(N, M) \mid 12$ with
$M \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$ while remaining in Mukai's admissible
orders $N \le 4$ ({\bf recall} $N = 6$ requires factorisation
through $\Z_2 \times \Z_3$, appearing implicitly via the $(3,3)$
row whose diagonal has order $6$).

\paragraph{CY-D interpretation.}\ClaimStatusTheorem{}
At $d = 3$ the eight $\GC$-forms parametrise the eight allowed
diagonal-divisor BKM algebras $\fg_{\Phi_N}$ on the
$(\K \times E)/\Z_N$ compact CY$_3$ family.  The functor $\Phi$
sends the equivariant CY datum
$(D^b\mathrm{Coh}(\K)^{g_N}, D^b\mathrm{Coh}(E)^{h_M})$ to the
paramodular Borcherds lift of the $(g_N, h_M)$-twined genus
$\phi^{(g_N, h_M)}_{0,1}$.  Invariants on the programme slice:
$\kBKM = c_{(g,h)}(0)/2 \in \{5, 2, 3, 1, 2, 1/2, 3/2, 1\}$
matching the $\GC$-weight column; $\kcat((\K \times E)/\Z_N) = 0$
(K\"unneth-multiplicative, preserved by free quotient); $\kfib = 24$
(Mukai-lattice rank on the K3 fibre, invariant under symplectic
twist); $\kch = \chi_{(g,h)}(\cO_{(\K \times E)/\Z_N})$ via
Atiyah--Bott holomorphic Lefschetz (Wave-10 Q4 corrected
refinement via theta-component split).
