\begin{abstract}
Three volumes, sixteen wave-cycles of adversarial probing, one functor.
The trilogy --- Volume~I (ordered bar complex on
$\overline{M}_{g,n}$; modular Koszul duality), Volume~II
($A_\infty$ chiral algebras and the Swiss-cheese-chiral-topological
dioperad $\mathsf{SC}^{\mathrm{ch,top}}$ underpinning 3D HT QFT),
Volume~III (CY-to-chiral functor $\Phi$, K3 Yangian, Calabi--Yau
landscape) --- crystallises around one canonical picture: a single
holomorphic $\Ed$-factorisation algebra $\cF_X$ on each Calabi--Yau
$d$-fold $X$, whose $(\Sigma_{d-1},C)$-specialisations generate the
zoo of $\Eone$-chiral shadows visible at the boundary of Vol~I and
Vol~II. This audit ledgers every cross-volume dependency, reads the
five shared theorems A / B / C / D / H at their current scope, catalogues
the Vol~III-specific contributions (CY-A$_3$ equivalence, K3 abelian-Yangian,
ZTE $T$ computation, CY-D dimensional stratification, universal Borcherds
weight $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$), exposes the inconsistencies among
CLAUDE manifestos, \texttt{working\_notes.tex}, the Platonic synthesis,
the CoHA treatise, and the sixty-seven \texttt{wave12\_*.tex} notes, and
isolates the shortest-path theorem for each volume. Five attack-heal
cycles, one organising principle, six open problems.
\end{abstract}

material from the other two; accurate auditing requires following the
edges, not just the nodes.

\subsection{Non-edges (explicit)}

Three relationships that look like edges but are not:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item Vol~II's $\Ethree$ (topological factorisation on $\R$-direction
of a 3D HT theory; brane-boundary bulk algebra on $C\times\R$) is
\emph{not} Vol~III's $\Ethree$ (holomorphic-chiral factorisation
algebra on a three-complex-dimensional CY). Same authors
(Costello--Francis--Gwilliam), different structures: the Vol~III
$\Ethree$ level is set by complex dimension, the Vol~II $\Ethree$
level by R-dimension of the topological factor.
Scope-declared: Vol~III \texttt{en\_factorization.tex}:449. Both
load-bearing; neither subsumes the other.
\item $\CoHA(\C^3)$ is \emph{not} $\Winf$. Cache-canonical:
$\CoHA(\C^3) = \Yplus(\widehat{\fgl}_1)$ (positive half, $\Eone$,
associative); $\Winf$ is a $\Etwo$-chiral vertex operator algebra on
a curve; the connection is $\Yplus\hookrightarrow\Yhat
\xrightarrow{\mathrm{ev}_\lambda}\mathrm{End}(\Winf[\lambda])$,
representation-theoretic not algebra-theoretic. (Vol~III
\texttt{working\_notes.tex}:1658--1674; Schiffmann--Vasserot 2013
Thm.~1.1; Tsymbaliuk 2017 Thm.~1.1.)
\item Six routes to $G(K3\times E)$ are six different specialisations
of one $\Phifa_3(K3\times E)$, \emph{not} six applications of a single
$\Phi_3$ to one category. (Platonic synthesis
Thm~\texttt{wn:thm:plat-two-stage}; working\_notes two-stage
Cor~\texttt{cor:many-BKMs}.)
\end{itemize}

The Vol~III programme thread lives at stratum $\mathrm{S}_2$ and

\section{Two-stage factorisation as organising principle}
% ============================================================

Adversarial probing of the platonic-synthesis
Thm~\texttt{wn:thm:plat-two-stage}, in five passes.

\subsection{Attack--heal 1: is Stage 1 truly canonical?}

\begin{attack}
The two-stage factorisation claims Stage 1 is ``canonical up to
contractible choice'' via Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality. But
Kontsevich--Tamarkin $\Ed$-formality is proved for polydifferential
operators on $\R^d$ (Kontsevich 1997; Tamarkin 1998), not for a
holomorphic-factorisation-algebra avatar on a general CY$_d$. The
extension to holomorphic CY$_d$ is Costello--Gwilliam--Li, which
gives locality and the pre-factorisation algebra, but does \emph{not}
supply the formality quasi-isomorphism.
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}
Stage 1 canonicality is actually a conjunction of two inputs:
(a) Costello--Li 2016 hCS pre-factorisation algebra construction on
$\C^3$, which supplies the explicit $\Ethree$-holomorphic factorisation
algebra; (b) a Kapranov-type Dolbeault-polyvector-field presentation
of the Hochschild cohomology, giving an $\En$-structure on
$\PV^\bullet_{\dbar}(X,\cO_X)$ via the Kontsevich formality map on the
de~Rham avatar. The conjunction is not a single formality theorem;
it is a two-step reduction. On compact $K3\times E$, step (a) is
available via Costello--Li for the toric tangent $\C^3$-chart and
Dolbeault gluing; step (b) requires a primary-source Kapranov 1999
argument on the Dolbeault $L_\infty$-model. Platonic synthesis
Thm~\texttt{wn:thm:plat-Linf-minimal} supplies the $\C^3$-step-(b);
the Atiyah-class formality obstruction on compact CY$_3$ is flagged
but the $K3\times E$ case admits the
$\mathrm{At}(TE) = 0 + \Omega^3_{K3} = 0$ vanishing. \emph{Scope}:
Stage 1 is canonical for $\C^3$ and $K3\times E$; general compact
CY$_3$ requires the Atiyah-class obstruction argument, which is
$(\infty,1)$-categorical, not chain-level.
\end{heal}

\subsection{Attack--heal 2: is Stage 2 genuinely a moduli, or a
single canonical cycle?}

\begin{attack}
The two-stage factorisation's Stage 2 is parametrised by
$(\Sigma_{d-1},C)\in\mathrm{Cyc}_{d-1}(X)\times\mathrm{Curves}(X)$.
But $\mathrm{Cyc}_{d-1}(X)$ of a compact $K3\times E$ is
a huge moduli; specifying a ``distinguished'' $\Sigma_2 = K3$-fibre
requires a choice of splitting $K3\times E\to E$, which is canonical
only for the trivial product structure. For a generic CY$_3$ with
non-trivial fibration, multiple splittings lead to inequivalent
Stage-2 outputs.
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}
For $X = K3\times E$ as a product, the splitting is canonical and
Stage 2 via $\Sigma_2 = K3$-fibre produces the
$\mathbf{H}_{\mathrm{Muk}}\otimes U^{\mathrm{ch}}(\fg^{\mathrm{BPS}}_{K3})$
specialisation. For $X$ with non-trivial $K3$-fibration (e.g.~a generic
Borcea--Voisin threefold), \emph{different} fibre choices generate
different $\Sigma_2$-specialisations, hence different $\Eone$-chiral
siblings. This is not a bug: the ``many BKMs'' phenomenon is the
statement that $\mathrm{Cyc}_2(X)$ has nontrivial topology. The canonical
cycle structure on $K3\times E$ product case is a special
simplification, not a universal feature.
\end{heal}

\subsection{Attack--heal 3: Dunn--Lurie $\int_{\Sigma_2}\Ethree\simeq\Eone$ direction}

\begin{attack}
The two-stage factorisation Proof Sketch invokes Dunn--Lurie
$\Ethree\simeq\Eone\otimes\Etwo$, then integrates over $\Sigma_2 = K3$,
claiming $\int_{\Sigma_2}\Etwo\simeq\mathrm{pt}$. But $\int_{K3}\Etwo$ is
not $\mathrm{pt}$; it is the factorisation homology of an $\Etwo$-algebra
over a closed real $4$-manifold, which is $\pi_0$-nontrivial in general
(Ayala--Francis~2015).
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}
The correct statement is that $\int_{\Sigma_2}\Etwo$ over a closed
orientable real surface of genus $g$ has known $\pi_0$ (Ayala--Francis).
For $K3 = \Sigma_2$ as a real $4$-manifold (not a real surface!) this
is even further out of scope. The content actually needed: the
$\Ethree$-hFA on $\C^3$ projected along two complex directions to
leave one complex direction is an $\Eone$-chiral algebra on the
surviving $\C$-direction, via the Costello--Li hCS-to-CoHA mechanism.
The ``Dunn additivity + integration'' sketch conflates real and complex
dimensions. The chain-level mechanism is: $\Ethree$-holomorphic on
$\C^3$; restrict to a reference curve $C\subset\C^3$ transverse to a
$\Sigma_2$ (complex surface, real dimension $4$); the restriction is
$\Eone$-chiral on $C$ by holomorphic locality in the one surviving
complex direction. No $\int\Etwo\simeq\mathrm{pt}$ required.
Resolution: the two-stage factorisation at $d = 3$ is proved at the
chain level on $\C^3$ (Costello--Li) and on $K3\times E$ (via Stage 1
on $\C^3$-chart + Dolbeault gluing); the $(\infty,1)$-categorical
restatement is the cleaner form but needs Ayala--Francis to be handled
separately per $\Sigma_{d-1}$.
\end{heal}

\subsection{Attack--heal 4: does the organising principle extend
beyond $d \leq 5$?}

\begin{attack}
Platonic synthesis lists only $d \in \{1, 2, 3, 5\}$ explicitly, with
$d=4$ handled via the shifted-symplectic table at shift $0$ and $\Ezero$.

\end{attack}

\begin{heal}
At $d = 4$ the PTVV shift is $0$ and the $\Ed$ target is $\Ezero$:
$\Phi_4$ produces an $\Ezero$-chiral structure, i.e.~essentially a
cochain complex without any $\Eone$-or-higher multiplication.
The Calabi--Yau $4$-fold Donaldson--Thomas theory
(Cao--Kool--Leung 2020, Borisov--Joyce 2017) witnesses the vanishing
cycle sheaf but not an $\Eone$-algebra. At $d\geq 6$, shift is $d-4$;
the target is $\En[d-3]$-Poisson with shift $d-4$: Poisson, not
symplectic, for $d\geq 5$ (Platonic synthesis

\end{heal}

\subsection{Attack--heal 5: is the two-stage principle falsifiable?}

\begin{attack}
If every phenomenon fits the two-stage factorisation by adjusting
$(\Sigma_{d-1},C)$, the principle is unfalsifiable. Where does it
\emph{fail}?
\end{attack}

\begin{heal}
