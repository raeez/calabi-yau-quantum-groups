\section*{Slide-extracted Conjecture~1 and proof-path status}

\paragraph{Verbatim slide statement (line 929--936 of
\texttt{/tmp/automorphic-slides.txt}).}
For $X$ obtained from $(S, g_N, h_M)$ with lattice polarisation $L$ and
$(E, e_0)$, let $\ZLh(q,t,p)$ denote the $L$-polarised,
$h_M = g_N^s$-twisted Donaldson--Thomas series. \emph{Conjecture (slides,
line 929):} all eight diagonal-divisor Siegel modular forms of
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery type arise, up to constant, as $-1/\ZLh$, and there
is a generalised BKM algebra correction $\mathfrak{g}_{(g_N,h_M)}
\subset \mathfrak{g}_L$ whose root multiplicities are computed by the
$(g_N,h_M)$-twisted elliptic genus $F_L^{(g_N,h_M)}$, a Jacobi form of
weight~$0$ index~$1$.

$B = \phi_{-2,1} = \nu_1^2/\eta^6$:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item $N \in \{1,2,3,5,7\}$: indexing $1 \le s,r \le N-1$,
  $0 \le k \le N-1$.

The case-split is a parametrisation shape, not a proof.

\paragraph{$N=1$ anchor (slide line 912--917).}
\ClaimStatusTheorem\ For $X = S \times E$ with $S$ elliptic K3:
$Z^{S \times E} = -C/(\Delta_5)^2$. Proof: Oberdieck--Pixton
conjecture, now \cite{Oberdieck2018}. Slides cite as theorem; this is
primary-literature anchored.

\paragraph{Hidden observation test: the unified Borcherds functor
$\Phi^{\mathrm{Bor}}$.}
The slide parametrisation $(N,s,r,k)$ suggests a single family
\[
  \Phi^{\mathrm{Bor}}: \mathcal{C}_{M_{24}}(g_N)
  \longrightarrow \mathrm{BKM}^{\mathrm{param}},
  \qquad (g_N,h_M) \mapsto (F_L^{(g_N,h_M)},\;
  \mathfrak{g}_{(g_N,h_M)})
\]
through twisted Borcherds lifting. The slides do not construct this
functor; they exhibit its object-level shadow at $N=1$ and an
orbit-index pattern at $N \in \{2,3,4,5,6,7,8\}$. No morphism-level
compatibility (composition with conjugation in $M_{24}$, or with
symplectic-automorphism covers) is established. \ClaimStatusConjectured.

\paragraph{Numerical verification (slides vs.\ independent sources).}
$\phi_{0,1}$ constant term $f(0,0) = 10$, sub-leading $f(0,-1)=1$
(slide line 802); agrees with \cite{EichlerZagier1985} Jacobi-form
tables. Weight $5 = 10/2$ consistent with $\kBKM(\Phi_1) = c_1(0)/2$.
Slide-independent: $(t,N)$ enumeration matches the GC catalogue for
$N \in \{1,2,3,4\}$ (paramodular) and $t \in \{1,2,3\}$. No slide
numerics for $N \geq 2$ cases.

\paragraph{What the slides do \emph{not} supply.}
(i) No per-$N$ explicit Borcherds product for the seven non-$\Delta_5$
GC forms. (ii) No DT-side construction of $\ZLh$ at $N \geq 2$.
(iii) No proof of the $(g_N,h_M)$-twisted elliptic-genus Jacobi-form
property for $h_M \neq \mathrm{id}$. (iv) No morphism-level
$\Phi^{\mathrm{Bor}}$ functor construction.

\paragraph{Assessment.}
The slides contain the same Conjecture~1 as the paper \S 4.2 plus an
orbit-indexing case split; they contain \emph{no} proof sketch
stronger than the paper. Steps S1--S3 and S8 rest on primary-literature
theorems; steps S4--S7 are open. The unified functor
$\Phi^{\mathrm{Bor}}$ is a hidden structural shape hinted at by the
orbit indexing, not a construction. Conjecture~1 remains genuinely
open at $N \geq 2$ pending either twisted-DT modularity beyond
Oberdieck or a direct Borcherds-lift proof of S6.
