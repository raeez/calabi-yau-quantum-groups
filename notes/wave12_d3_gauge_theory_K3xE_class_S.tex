\section*{Gauge-theory class-\texorpdfstring{$\cS$}{S} bridge for
\texorpdfstring{$K3 \times E$}{K3xE}}

\subsection*{The Gaiotto curve}

Let
\[
  \Sigma_{0,24}=(\PP^1; p_1,\ldots,p_{24})
\]
be the genus-zero class-\(\cS\) curve with \(24\) maximal regular
\(\su(2)\)-punctures, and set
\[
  \cT_{24}:=\cT[A_1,\Sigma_{0,24}].
\]
A pants decomposition has
\[
  2g-2+n = 22
  \quad\hbox{trinions},\qquad
  3g-3+n = 21
  \quad\hbox{gauge tubes}
\]
at \((g,n)=(0,24)\).  The \(A_1\) trinion \(T_2\) is the
\(\mathrm{SU}(2)^3\) half-hypermultiplet system with effective
\((n_v,n_h)=(0,4)\), while an \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) vector multiplet
contributes \((n_v,n_h)=(3,0)\).  Hence
\[
  (n_v,n_h)(\cT_{24})
  =
  22(0,4)+21(3,0)
  =
  (63,88).
\]
The Shapere--Tachikawa normalisation gives
\[
  \cfd(\cT_{24})
  =
  \frac{2n_v+n_h}{12}
  =
  \frac{2\cdot 63+88}{12}
  =
  \frac{107}{6},
  \qquad
  a_{4d}(\cT_{24})
  =
  \frac{5n_v+n_h}{24}
  =
  \frac{403}{24}.
\]
Equivalently, for \(A_1\) on \(\Sigma_{0,n}\) with all punctures maximal,
\[
  \cfd[A_1,\Sigma_{0,n}]
  =
  \frac{5n-13}{6},
  \qquad
  \mathrm{rank}\,\cH_{\mathrm{Coul}}=n-3.
\]
Thus \(n=24\) fixes \(\cfd=107/6\), Coulomb rank \(21\), and flavour
symmetry \(\su(2)^{24}\).  A closed genus-two curve is a different
class-\(\cS\) datum: it lacks the \(24\) puncture labels,
\(\su(2)^{24}\) flavour symmetry, and \(M_{24}\)-permutation action
that define the \(107/6\) parent theory.

The Beem--Lemos--Liendo--Peelaers--Rastelli--van Rees correspondence
assigns to \(\cT_{24}\) a protected vertex algebra
\(\chi(\cT_{24})\) with
\[
  \ctd(\chi(\cT_{24}))
  =
  -12\,\cfd(\cT_{24})
  =
  -214,
  \qquad
  k_{2d}^{(i)}
  =
  -\frac{1}{2}k_{4d}^{(i)}
  =
  -2
\]
for each maximal \(\su(2)\)-puncture.  These numbers belong to the
four-dimensional class-\(\cS\) Schur sector; they are not Siegel weights
and not \(K3\times E\) categorical invariants.

\subsection*{Schur data and the Borcherds lift}

The class-\(\cS\) Schur index is a protected \(2d\) character,
\[
  \cI_{\Schur}(\cT_{24};q,\mathbf{x})
  =
  \mathrm{Tr}_{\mathcal H_{\Schur}}
  (-1)^F q^{E-R}\mathbf{x}^{\mathbf f}.
\]
After marking the \(24\) punctures by the \(24\) singular fibres of an
elliptic \(K3\), the Mathieu group acts by permuting the puncture labels.
The automorphic bridge is the composite
\[
  \cI_{\Schur}(\cT_{24})
  \xrightarrow{\;\Pi_{\Mtf}\;}
  \phi_{0,1}^{K3}(\tau,z)
  \xrightarrow{\;\Borch\;}
  \Delta_5(Z),
  \qquad
  Z=\begin{pmatrix}\tau&z\\ z&\sigma\end{pmatrix}\in\mathfrak H_2.
\]
Here \(\Pi_{\Mtf}\) denotes the \(M_{24}\)-average on the twined Schur
data.  The last arrow is a multiplicative Borcherds lift, not an
identification of the Schur character with the target automorphic form.
The notation
\(\phi_{0,1}^{K3}:=\phi_{0,1}^{\mathrm{GN}}\) means the
Gritsenko--Nikulin normalisation of the K3 weight-zero, index-one weak
Jacobi form:
\[
  \phi_{0,1}^{K3}(\tau,z)
  =
  y+10+y^{-1}+O(q).
\]
The geometric K3 elliptic genus is
\[
  \mathrm{Ell}_{K3}(\tau,z)=2\,\phi_{0,1}^{K3}(\tau,z),
\]
while the Borcherds product producing \(\Delta_5\) uses the
Gritsenko--Nikulin normalisation with \(c(0)=10\).  Borcherds' weight
theorem then produces the denominator form \(\Delta_5\), with
\[
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})
  =
  \mathrm{wt}(\Delta_5)
  =
  \frac{c(0)}{2}
  =
  5.
\]
The Schur character, its \(M_{24}\)-average, and the reciprocal
Borcherds product have distinct source and target categories.  The
relation is the displayed composite, not an equality
\(\cI_{\Schur}(\cT_{24})=\Delta_5^{-1}\).

\subsection*{The Siegel target}

The genus-two variable \(Z\in\mathfrak H_2\) is the Siegel automorphic
target of the Borcherds lift.  The Riemann surface in the
class-\(\cS\) compactification is \(\Sigma_{0,24}\); the
genus-two geometry belongs to the automorphic moduli of the denominator
form.  With the usual scalar normalisation,
\[
  \Delta_5^2=\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}},
\]
and on the Oberdieck--Pandharipande reduced-DT branch one instead uses
the monic product
\[
  D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5,\qquad
  \Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}=D_5^2=4096^{-1}\Delta_5^2.
\]
These scalar partition functions record automorphic specialisations of
BPS indices.  The Hall product, the Borcherds bracket, and the chiral
factorisation algebra require separate constructions.

\subsection*{\texorpdfstring{$K3\times E$}{K3xE} invariants}

For \(X=K3\times E\), five subscripted quantities give four numerical
values.  The two zeroes have different meanings:
\[
  \kcat(X)=\chi(\mathcal O_{K3})\chi(\mathcal O_E)=2\cdot 0=0,
  \qquad
  \kch(X)=\sum_{q=0}^3(-1)^q h^{0,q}(X)=1-1+1-1=0,
\]
\[
  \kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(X)=3,
  \qquad
  \kBKM(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=5,
  \qquad
  \kfib(K3)=\mathrm{rank}\,H^\ast(K3,\Z)=24.
\]
Thus the \(K3\times E\) spectrum is
\[
  \{0,3,5,24\},
\]
with the value \(0\) occurring both as total-space categorical Euler
characteristic and as compact Hodge supertrace.  The nonzero entries
are the Heisenberg--Mukai chiral specialisation, the Borcherds weight,
and the K3 fibre rank.  None is obtained by adding another one to a
fibre Euler characteristic.

The native output of the CY-to-chiral assignment at complex dimension
\(3\) is \(E_1\)-chiral:
\[
  \Phi_3\bigl(D^b\mathrm{Coh}(K3\times E)\bigr)
  \in \mathrm{ChirAlg}_{E_1}.
\]
The \(E_2\)-structure is carried by a derived centre, representation
centre, or doubled module layer, not by the compact CY\(_3\) output
itself.  The class-\(\cS\)
vertex algebra \(\chi(\cT_{24})\) is a four-dimensional Schur source;
\(\Delta_5\) is the Siegel--Borcherds target; the \(K3\times E\)
CY-to-chiral object is the \(E_1\)-chiral layer to which those data are
compared through the \(M_{24}\)-average and Borcherds lift.

\subsection*{Gauge-theory realisation}

The gauge-theory realisation has the exact shape
\[
  \cT[A_1,\Sigma_{0,24}]
  \xrightarrow{\;\chi\;}
  \chi(\cT_{24})
  \xrightarrow{\;\Pi_{\Mtf}\;}
  \phi_{0,1}^{K3}
  \xrightarrow{\;\Borch\;}
  \Delta_5
  \rightsquigarrow
  \mathfrak g_{\Delta_5}.
\]
The first arrow is the Beem--Rastelli protected chiral-algebra
construction.  The second is the Mathieu average on puncture-labelled
Schur data.  The third is the Gritsenko--Nikulin Borcherds lift.  The
last symbol records the denominator superalgebra whose imaginary-root
multiplicities are the Fourier coefficients of
\(\phi_{0,1}^{K3}\).
This endpoint is the Hall--Drinfeld/Borcherds branch.  A comparison
with the Mukai self-mirror K3 Yangian requires an explicit centre or
double functor from the Schur-sector algebra to the Mukai current
presentation.

This chain separates three layers with different ambient categories:
\[
  \Sigma_{0,24}
  \quad\hbox{(Gaiotto curve)},\qquad
  \mathfrak H_2
  \quad\hbox{(Siegel automorphic target)},\qquad
  K3\times E
  \quad\hbox{(Calabi--Yau threefold datum)}.
\]
The class-\(\cS\) anomaly arithmetic fixes \((n_v,n_h)=(63,88)\) and
\((\cfd,\ctd)=(107/6,-214)\); the Borcherds product fixes
\(\kBKM=5\); the \(K3\times E\) categorical, compact chiral,
Heisenberg, and fibre computations fix \(\{0,3,5,24\}\).
