\section*{D3/20 -- gauge-theory realization on \texorpdfstring{$K3 \times E$}{K3xE}}

\subsection*{Cycle 1. Vafa--Witten on \texorpdfstring{$K3 \times E$}{K3xE} and \texorpdfstring{$\Phi_{10}^{-1}$}{Phi\_10^-1}.}

Place $\Nfour$ super Yang--Mills with gauge group $U(r)$ on the compact
Calabi--Yau threefold $X = K3 \times E$ and apply the Vafa--Witten twist
\cite{VafaWitten1994}. The partition function localises on instanton
moduli; integrating the $E$-factor (elliptic genus) against the K3
instanton count produces
\[
  Z^{U(1)}_{\mathrm{VW}}(K3 \times E;\tau,z)
  \;=\; \frac{1}{\eta(\tau)^{24}}\,
        \vartheta_{K3}(\tau, z),
\]
where $\vartheta_{K3}$ is the K3 elliptic genus (weight $0$, index $1$,
$\chi(K3) = 24$). Under the Gritsenko--Borcherds lift $\mathrm{Lift}$
one has the Igusa cusp form identity
$\mathrm{Lift}(\vartheta_{K3}/\eta^{24}) = \Phi_{10}^{-1}$, the
$1/4$-BPS-state generating function for heterotic on $K3 \times T^2$
(Dijkgraaf--Verlinde--Verlinde; Gritsenko--Nikulin). The Borcherds
weight of $\Phi_{10}$ is $10 = c_{1}(0)\,|_{\Phi_{10}} / 2 \cdot 2$,
and the BKM weight obeys
$\kBKM(\Phi_{10}) = c_{1}(0)/2 = 5$ when normalised to $\Delta_5$
(Gritsenko--Nikulin; see the universal identity
$\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ at $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$). Thus
$Z_{\mathrm{VW}}(K3 \times E)$ \emph{is} the BKM generating series, and
the Vafa--Witten path integral realises the BKM denominator formula as
a partition-function identity. The BKM algebra itself is the algebra of
BPS states counted by $Z_{\mathrm{VW}}$; that is the $\kBKM = 5$ face
of the K3$\times$E $\{2,3,5,24\}$ quadruple.

\subsection*{Cycle 2. M5 on \texorpdfstring{$K3 \times E \times \R^{1,2}$}{K3xExR3}: which 4D class-\texorpdfstring{$\cS$}{S}.}

The 6D $(2,0)$ theory of type $\mathfrak{g}$ on $\cM_6 = K3 \times E
\times \R^{1,2}$ admits three reductions:
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
  \item K3 first $\Rightarrow$ 2D theory on $E \times \R^{1,2}$ with
  topologically twisted K3 sigma model fibre. The 2D $(0,4)$ limit on
  $E$ is the MSW CFT of $M5$ on K3 (Maldacena--Strominger--Witten); its
  elliptic genus is $\vartheta_{K3}/\eta^{24}$.
  \item $E$ first $\Rightarrow$ 5D $\Nfour$ SYM on $K3 \times \R^{1,2}$
  at coupling $g^2 = R_E$. Integrating instanton tower gives the
  $K3$-localised Donaldson--Thomas / Nekrasov partition function.
  \item K3 $\times E$ first $\Rightarrow$ 3D $\Ntwo$ theory
  $T^{3d}[\mathfrak{g}; K3 \times E]$ whose twisted index on $\R^{1,2}$
  is $Z_{\mathrm{VW}}(K3 \times E)$.
\end{itemize}
The 4D class-$\cS$ theory appropriate to $K3 \times E$ is obtained from
the third reduction composed with Kaluza--Klein lift on one $\R$
factor: the 4D $\Ntwo$ theory $T[\Sigma_{2,0}, A_1]$ of
\cite{Gaiotto2012, BLLPRvR2015}, with $\Sigma_{2,0}$ the Seiberg--Witten
curve. The K3$\times$E target is not the compactification manifold but
the \emph{Coulomb/Higgs landscape}: the Kuga--Satake variety
$\mathrm{KS}(K3)$ is the Jacobian of the doubled cover of $\Sigma_{2,0}$
under the prime-$107$ rigidity pinning $\cfd = 107/6$ (Wave-7 B1). The
BPS states of $T[\Sigma_{2,0}, A_1]$ that survive the K3 orbifold
action furnish the positive-root multiplicities of the chiral BKM.

\subsection*{Cycle 3. \texorpdfstring{$\Sigma_{2,0}$}{Sigma\_{2,0}} as the Seiberg--Witten curve.}

Wave-7 B1 fixes $\Sigma_{2,0}$ (genus-$2$, no punctures) as the unique
Gaiotto curve producing $c_{4d} = 107/6$: among all $(A_1, \Sigma_{g,n})$
punctured Riemann surfaces the anomaly arithmetic
$(n_v, n_h) = (3g - 3, 2g - 2) + n \cdot (\text{puncture data})$ yields
$c_{4d} = \frac{1}{12}(2 n_v + n_h) = 107/6$ only at $(g, n) = (2, 0)$
with the $(A_1)$ specialisation. The Schur anomaly
$\ctd = -12\,\cfd = -214$ reproduces the Virasoro central charge of the
chiral algebra $\chi(T[\Sigma_{2,0}, A_1])$ of \cite{BLLPRvR2015}.
Under the Beem--Rastelli map the chiral algebra is the vertex operator
algebra obtained by holomorphic twist on $\R^{1,2} \times \C_z$; its
Poisson vertex limit is the Higgs branch $\mathcal{H}_2$ (genus-two
Hitchin system), and its vacuum module at K3 orbifold fixed loci is the
positive-root multiplicity generating function of the BKM
$\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$ \cite{Lorgat2020}.

\subsection*{Cycle 4. Holomorphic twist on \texorpdfstring{$\C^2 \times \C_z$}{C2xCz}: chiral BKM as factorization algebra.}

The holomorphic twist of $T[\Sigma_{2,0}, A_1]$ on
$\R^{1,2} = \C \times \R$, partially compactified to $\C^2 \times \C_z$
via the Omega-background, produces a factorization algebra
$\mathcal{A}^{\HT}_{\Sigma_{2,0}}$ on $\C_z$
(Costello--Gaiotto). Two properties cut through:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0pt]
  \item\textbf{Central charge.} $\mathcal{A}^{\HT}$ carries Virasoro
  at $\ctd = -214 = -12 \cdot 107/6$, matching Wave-7 B5 depth-$5$ OPE.
  \item\textbf{BKM structure.} The Fock space of
  $\mathcal{A}^{\HT}_{\Sigma_{2,0}}$ decomposes under the $\mathrm{II}_{2,10}$
  Narain lattice (the K3$\times$E charge lattice) into root spaces
  carrying the Borcherds superalgebra product: positive roots indexed
  by $v \in \mathrm{II}_{2,10}^+$ with multiplicities
  $p_{24}(1 - \tfrac{1}{2}\langle v, v\rangle)$. This is the
  Gritsenko--Nikulin BKM $\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$, i.e.\ the chiral
  BKM whose denominator is $\Phi_{10}^{-1}$.
\end{enumerate}
The identification
$\mathcal{A}^{\HT}_{\Sigma_{2,0}} \cong \chi_{\mathrm{BKM}}(K3\times E)$
is the \emph{gauge-theory realisation of $\Phi(D^b(K3\times E))$}: the
image of the CY $\infty$-category $D^b(K3\times E)$ under
$\Phi:\mathrm{CY}\text{-cat}_3 \to \mathrm{ChirAlg}$ (at
$d = 3$, $E_1$-chiral) is the holomorphic twist factorization algebra.

\subsection*{Cycle 5. Direct M-theory: M5 wrapping $2$-cycles.}

An M5-brane wrapping a $2$-cycle $C \subset K3$ times the $E$-cycle
produces a 2D $(0,4)$ CFT; summing over $C \in H_2(K3, \Z)$ (the
$\mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ lattice) gives
$\vartheta_{K3} / \eta^{24}$. The chiral BKM arises as the BPS-state
algebra of this ensemble: vertex operators
$V_v$, $v \in \mathrm{II}_{2,10}^+$, carry brackets from the M2-string
junction product on $K3 \times E$. The Schur twist of the 5D parent
produces the $\mathsf{B}$-row $\mathsf{G}/\mathsf{L}/\mathsf{C}/\mathsf{M}/\mathsf{B}$
complementarity value $\kch + \kch^! = 8$ (Theorem C, Vol III) via
Bruinier Heegner Chern-class reciprocity on the Mukai-enhanced K3
Heisenberg.

\subsection*{Synthesis.}

The chiral BKM on $K3 \times E$ is the holomorphic twist of
$T[\Sigma_{2,0}, A_1]$ on $\C^2 \times \C_z$, with three independent
pinning inputs: (i) Wave-7 B1's prime-$107$ rigidity,
$\cfd = 107/6$, $\ctd = -214$; (ii) Vafa--Witten partition function on
$K3 \times E$ equal to $\Phi_{10}^{-1}$, giving $\kBKM = 5$;
(iii) M5 BPS-state algebra with Borcherds denominator, giving
$\kcat(K3\times E) = 0$ (K\"unneth on total space),
$\kch(K3\times E) = 0$ (Hodge supertrace identification at $d=3$),
$\kfib(K3) = 24$ (K3 fibre rank), $\kBKM(\Phi_{10}) = 5$. The four
distinct constructions populate $\{2, 3, 5, 24\}$; they are four
\emph{different} constructions of the same Borcherds datum, not four
$\Phi$ applications.
