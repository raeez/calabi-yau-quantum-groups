\begin{abstract}
SCAFFOLD STUB. The CY$_3$ total space $K3 \times E$ supports a chiral algebra
$\cA_{K3 \times E}$ whose Borcherds--Kac--Moody shadow satisfies
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ across the five Niemeier-lattice
levels $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$ (scope: five-level universal; $N \ge 7$ not
covered here). The six candidate routes to the
$K3 \times E$ generator algebra (spacetime, worldsheet heterotic, F-theory,
DT, BPS, Φ-functor) \emph{do not} converge isomorphically (CY-C naive
claim falsified; retraction B89): they form an adversarial \emph{pentagon}
of five intertwiners, with the $R_2$ source branch carrying the minimal
rank. The pentagon stratifies by \emph{generator rank} $\rho^{R_i}$, not by
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$ --- the latter is a route-independent Hodge supertrace
invariant equal to $0$ (retraction B90). The $N = 1$ coincidence
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}} = \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}} + \chi(\cO_{\mathrm{fiber}})$
fails for $N \ge 2$ (AP-CY37).
\end{abstract}
