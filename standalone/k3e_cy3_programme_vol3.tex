\title[$K3 \times E$ CY$_3$]{The $K3 \times E$ CY$_3$:
universal $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}$ and the generator pentagon}
\author{Raeez Lorgat}
\date{2026-04-17}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
The CY$_3$ total space $K3 \times E$ supports a chiral algebra
$\cA_{K3 \times E}$ whose Borcherds--Kac--Moody shadow satisfies
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ across the five Niemeier-lattice
levels $N \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$; levels $N \ge 7$ are outside the theorem.
The six candidate routes to the
$K3 \times E$ generator algebra (spacetime, worldsheet heterotic, F-theory,
DT, BPS, $\Phi$-functor) do not converge isomorphically. They form a
\emph{pentagon}
of five intertwiners, with the $R_2$ source branch carrying the minimal
rank. The pentagon stratifies by \emph{generator rank} $\rho^{R_i}$.
The invariant $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$ is a route-independent Hodge supertrace
equal to $0$. The $N = 1$ coincidence
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}} = \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}} + \chi(\cO_{\mathrm{fiber}})$
fails for $N \ge 2$.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Corrections}
The six-route convergence statement is false as an isomorphism
claim. The valid structure is the generator-rank pentagon above.
The pentagon is not stratified by $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$, since the
Hodge supertrace gives $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(K3\times E)=0$ on every
route.
