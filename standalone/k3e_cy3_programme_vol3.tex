\title[\(K3\times E\)]{The \(K3\times E\) Calabi--Yau threefold and the
generator pentagon}
\author{Raeez Lorgat}

\begin{abstract}
For \(X=K3\times E\), the compact total-space traces, the Heisenberg
specialisation, the automorphic BKM target, and the K3 fibre invariant
separate:
\(\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(X)=0\), \(\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}(X)=0\),
\(\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}=3\),
\(\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=5\), and
\(\kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}=24\). The six constructions attached to
\(K3\times E\) have distinct mathematical data:
\(R_1\) is the \(\Phi_3\) specialisation under the stated CY$_3\)
trace, framing, and specialisation hypotheses, \(R_2\) is the
automorphic Borcherds denominator target attached by the character-level
arrow \(\beta_{23}\), and \(R_3,\ldots,R_6\) are independent chiral
vertices.
When the source-side bridges exist, the resulting diagram is a five-vertex
generator pentagon with a distinguished automorphic source.
The values \(\{0,3,5,24\}\) are subscripted invariant data. The numbers
\(\rho^{R_i}\in\{3,12,24\}\) below are generator ranks, never chiral
invariant values. The Igusa normalisation is
\(D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5\), with \(\Delta_5\) theta-normalised and
\(D_5\) monic; the imported BKM denominator is
\(D_5(2Z)=64^{-1}\Delta_5(2Z)\). The primitive quotient-by-\(E\) OP/DT
normalisation is
\(Z^{X}_{\mathrm{OP}}=Z^{X}_{\mathrm{DT}}=-4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}\); these
scalar identities do not construct a compact Hall source.
\end{abstract}

\section{The invariant spectrum}

\begin{proposition}
For \(X=K3\times E\),
\[
        \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(X)=0,\qquad
        \kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}(X)=\chi(\cO_X)=0 .
\]
The separate Heisenberg--Mukai, automorphic, and fibre constructions give
\[
        \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}=3,\qquad
        \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=5,\qquad
        \kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}=24 .
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
K\"unneth gives \(h^{0,\bullet}(K3\times E)=(1,1,1,1)\), so the alternating
sum is \(0\). The same multiplicativity gives
\(\chi(\cO_{K3\times E})=\chi(\cO_{K3})\chi(\cO_E)=2\cdot0=0\). The
Heisenberg--Mukai specialisation uses a different trace readout: the \(K3\)
Mukai trace contributes \(2\), and the elliptic Heisenberg line contributes
\(1\). Hence
\(\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}=3\).
The fibre value is the topological Euler characteristic, equivalently the
rank of the Mukai lattice \(\widetilde H(K3,\bZ)\), equal to \(24\); it is
not \(\chi(\cO_{K3})=2\). The Gritsenko--Nikulin denominator has
\(c_1(0)=10\), so its Borcherds weight is
\(\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=c_1(0)/2=5\). This is
an automorphic target invariant, not a trace of \(\Perf(X)\).
\end{proof}

\section{Automorphic denominator}

\begin{definition}[Routes]
Fix \(X=S\times E\), with \(S\) a K3 surface. The six labels record
different constructions, not six applications of \(\Phi_3\).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\(R_1\)] the direct \(\Phi_3\) specialisation of \(\Perf(X)\),
conditional on the \(CY_3\) smoothness, properness, negative-cyclic trace,
and chain-level \(S^3\)-framing hypotheses, together with a witnessed
\((\Sigma_2,C)\) datum;
\item[\(R_2\)] the automorphic Borcherds denominator target, attached by
the character-level arrow
\(\beta_{23}:A_X^{R_2,\mathrm{char}}\to A_X^{R_3}\), controlled by
\(\Delta_5\), \(D_5(2Z)\), and \(c_1(0)=10\);
\item[\(R_3\)] the Mukai lattice VOA construction;
\item[\(R_4\)] the Kummer/orbifold construction;
\item[\(R_5\)] the half-twisted sigma-model construction;
\item[\(R_6\)] the BLLPR/Costello--Li Schur-sector construction.
\end{enumerate}
The pentagon vertices are \(R_1,R_3,R_4,R_5,R_6\); \(R_2\) is the
automorphic character source for target-side data, not a CY source, and
does not by itself give a compact chiral vertex, a compact Hall source, or
a \(\rho\)-rank entry.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Automorphic weight]
For the CHL/Gritsenko--Nikulin Borcherds-product levels
\(N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}\), let \(\mathcal B_N\) denote the automorphic
Borcherds product and let \(c_N(0)\) be the constant coefficient of the
weight-zero weak Jacobi input. Then
\[
        \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\mathcal B_N)=\frac{c_N(0)}{2}.
\]
In particular \(c_1(0)=10\) gives
\(\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})=5\) for the Gritsenko
denominator. The symbol \(\mathcal B_N\) is target-side automorphic
notation; it is not an application of the CY-to-chiral functor to a compact
Hall source.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Borcherds' automorphic-product theorem gives the weight \(c_N(0)/2\);
the Gritsenko--Nikulin specialisation fixes the genus-two variables,
product cone, multiplier, and normalisation. At \(N=1\),
the input is \(\phi_{0,1}\) with \(c_1(0)=10\), the theta-normalised
Gritsenko--Nikulin output is \(\Delta_5\), and the monic Borcherds
product is
\[
        D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5 .
\]
The denominator identity for the imported BKM Lie superalgebra is
\[
        \operatorname{den}(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})
        =D_5(2Z)=64^{-1}\Delta_5(2Z).
\]
The argument \(2Z\) records the passage from the product character
\(\exp(-\pi i(\alpha,z))\) to the Weyl--Kac denominator character
\(\exp(-2\pi i(\alpha,z))\).
The coefficient \(c_1(0)=10\) gives weight \(5\). The character-level
denominator reads that weight as the invariant
\(\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})\). This is target-side
Borcherds arithmetic: it
fixes the denominator, weight, character, and normalisation. Its source-side
Hall, CoHA, braided, and chiral lifts require separate compact route
bridges; the denominator alone supplies no compact Hall correspondences,
orientation line, coproduct, Hopf pairing, radical quotient,
PBW/no-extra-relations theorem, or centre/Fock evaluation.
Levels outside \(N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}\) require separate denominator data and
are outside this theorem. Non-CHL Clery--Gritsenko diagonal-divisor rows
belong to a separate catalogue with their own cover-group data.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Igusa--OP scalar and the compact-Hall obstruction]
Let \(X=K3\times E\). In the Igusa cusp-form normalisation,
\[
        D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5 ,
\]
where \(\Delta_5\) is theta-normalised and \(D_5\) is the monic Borcherds
product. The BKM denominator attached to \(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5}\) is
\[
        \operatorname{den}(\mathfrak g_{\Delta_5})
        =D_5(2Z)=64^{-1}\Delta_5(2Z).
\]
In the primitive quotient-by-\(E\), Behrend-weighted OP/DT normalisation, the
monic scalar product denoted \(\chi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}\) or
\(\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}\) is
\[
        \chi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}
        =\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}
        =D_5^2
        =4096^{-1}\Delta_5^2,
\]
and the reduced scalar normalisation is
\[
        Z^{X}_{\mathrm{OP}}
        =Z^{X}_{\mathrm{DT}}
        =-\bigl(\chi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}\bigr)^{-1}
        =-4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}.
\]
The obstruction to promoting these scalar data to a compact Hall source is
the tuple
\[
        \mathfrak{o}_{K3\times E}
        =(\mathfrak{o}_{\mathrm{orient}},
          \mathfrak{o}_{\mathrm{corr}},
          \mathfrak{o}_{\mathrm{coprod}},
          \mathfrak{o}_{\mathrm{pair}},
          \mathfrak{o}_{\mathrm{rad}},
          \mathfrak{o}_{\mathrm{PBW}},
          \mathfrak{o}_{\mathrm{cent}}).
\]
Its vanishing means, respectively: an orientation/Pfaffian line, compact
Hall correspondences, a coproduct, a non-degenerate Hopf pairing, a closed
radical quotient, PBW/no-extra-relations compatibility, and the centre/Fock
evaluation data have been constructed. The compact CoHA positive half is
part of these source-side data: the correspondences must be proper on the
completed support and compatible with the chosen orientation line. The
displayed scalar identities do not force this vanishing.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The equality \(D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5\) removes the theta-leading coefficient
\([q^{1/2}r^{1/2}s^{1/2}]\Delta_5=64\). Squaring gives the OP monic scalar
product
\(\chi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}=\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}=D_5^2\), hence the
normalisation \(4096^{-1}\Delta_5^2\). The OP/DT scalar convention is
\(Z^{X}_{\mathrm{OP}}=Z^{X}_{\mathrm{DT}}
=-(\chi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}})^{-1}\), in the primitive quotient-by-\(E\)
normalisation, which gives
\(-4096\,\Delta_5^{-2}\). These operations take place in the automorphic
and scalar enumerative targets. A compact Hall source requires source-side
objects: protected primitive representatives, compact extension
correspondences, an orientation system, a coproduct, Hopf pairing matrices,
radical descent, PBW/no-extra-relations compatibility, and centre/Fock
evaluation. The automorphic form records the BKM denominator and the scalar
character; it does not construct those source-side objects. In
particular, \(64\), \(4096\), and the OP minus sign do not prove
\(\epsilon_o=\nu_{\Delta_5}\) for the compact orientation character.
\end{proof}

\section{Generator pentagon}

\begin{definition}[Pentagon hypotheses]
The first four hypotheses are chain-level cells needed to strictify the
\(K3\times E\) pentagon; composition is read right-to-left:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\(\mathrm{P}_1\)] the Costello--Li/BLLPR BV cell
\(\beta_{61}\circ\beta_{56}\simeq\beta_{51}\);
\item[\(\mathrm{P}_2\)] the Kummer \(\bZ/2\)-quotient cell
\(\beta_{45}\circ\beta_{34}\simeq\beta_{35}\);
\item[\(\mathrm{P}_3\)] the half-twist cell
\(\beta_{56}\circ\beta_{45}\simeq\beta_{46}\);
\item[\(\mathrm{P}_4\)] the HKR--Borcherds pentagonal filler
\(\beta_{61}\circ\beta_{56}\circ\beta_{45}\circ\beta_{34}\circ\beta_{13}
\simeq\mathrm{id}_{A_X^{R_1}}\).
\end{enumerate}
Here \(\beta_{ij}:A_X^{R_i}\to A_X^{R_j}\), and
\(\beta_{51},\beta_{35},\beta_{46}\) denote the displayed composites.
The two source-side assembly hypotheses are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\(\mathrm{P}_5\)] finite-colimit representability of the five
vertices in the completed presentable \(E_1\)-chiral category;
\item[\(\mathrm{P}_6\)] compatibility of compact Hall/CoHA positive
halves, proper Hall correspondences, orientation lines, Hopf pairings,
radical quotients, Drinfeld doubles, and derived centres under the five
bridges.
\end{enumerate}
The automorphic data of \(R_2\), including \(D_5(2Z)\) and the OP/DT
normalisation, are target-side inputs to these hypotheses, not proofs of
them.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Pentagon ranks under bridge hypotheses]
Assume the \(CY_3\) trace/framing data for \(R_1\), the five chiral
vertices \(R_1,R_3,R_4,R_5,R_6\), and the bridge hypotheses
\(\mathrm{P}_1,\ldots,\mathrm{P}_6\). Then the generator ranks are
\[
        \rho^{R_1}=3,\quad
        \rho^{R_3}=24,\quad
        \rho^{R_4}=12,\quad
        \rho^{R_5}=3,\quad
        \rho^{R_6}=3 .
\]
Here \(\rho^{R_i}:=\operatorname{rank}_{\bZ}\operatorname{Gen}(A_X^{R_i})\)
is the rank of the chosen generator lattice in the \(i\)-th constructed
chiral vertex. The bridge diagram has vertices
\(R_1,R_3,R_4,R_5,R_6\). The route \(R_2\) supplies the automorphic BKM
character source for \(\beta_{23}\); the pentagon counts the five chiral
vertices and assigns no
\(\rho\)-rank to \(R_2\). This is a rank statement, not a compact
realisation theorem for \(G(K3\times E)\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The route \(R_1\) is the direct \(\Phi_3\) specialisation under the \(CY_3\)
smoothness, properness, negative-cyclic trace, chain-level \(S^3\)-framing,
and witnessed-specialisation data. The Mukai-lattice vertex construction
gives \(R_3\), the Kummer/orbifold construction gives \(R_4\), the
half-twisted sigma model gives \(R_5\), and the BLLPR/Costello--Li
Schur-sector construction gives \(R_6\). Each arrow in the pentagon is a
bridge theorem: it must identify the stated generator lattice, preserve the
\(E_1\)-chiral operation, and transport the relevant trace. The automorphic
denominator in \(R_2\) controls the Borcherds weight through \(c_1(0)/2\)
and the denominator identity \(D_5(2Z)=64^{-1}\Delta_5(2Z)\). It is not a
sixth compact chiral vertex and it does not supply the compact Hall
structure; those data are precisely the vanishing of
\(\mathfrak{o}_{K3\times E}\).
At generator-rank level the arrows, in the order
\(\beta_{13},\beta_{34},\beta_{45},\beta_{56},\beta_{61}\), have types
\[
        3\hookrightarrow 24,\qquad
        24\twoheadrightarrow 12,\qquad
        12\twoheadrightarrow 3,\qquad
        3\xrightarrow{\sim}3,\qquad
        3\xrightarrow{\sim}3 .
\]
Chain-level commutativity is exactly the content of
\(\mathrm{P}_1,\ldots,\mathrm{P}_6\), not a consequence of the scalar
automorphic normalisation. Identification with a compact
\(G(K3\times E)\) is a further compact-realisation theorem and requires
\(\mathfrak{o}_{K3\times E}=0\).
\end{proof}

Its vertices are \(R_1,R_3,R_4,R_5,R_6\), and its arrows are
\[
        \beta_{13},\ \beta_{34},\ \beta_{45},\ \beta_{56},\ \beta_{61}.
\]
The arrows are required to preserve the positive-half structures, compact
Hall correspondences, orientation lines, Hopf pairings, Drinfeld doubles,
centres, traces, and finite-colimit closure wherever those objects have
been built. The
\(\beta_{23}:A_X^{R_2,\mathrm{char}}\to A_X^{R_3}\) attachment is only a
character-level attachment, not a compact Hall morphism. The
\((\infty,1)\)-categorical colimit of \(\mathsf{Pent}_5\) is only the
categorical pentagon object. Its
chain-level identification with \(G(K3\times E)\) is conditional on the
\(CY_3\) smoothness, properness, negative-cyclic trace, chain-level
\(S^3\)-framing, and witnessed-specialisation data; on
\(\mathrm{P}_1,\ldots,\mathrm{P}_4\); on \(\mathrm{P}_5\) finite-colimit
representability for the five vertices; on \(\mathrm{P}_6\) compact
Hall/CoHA compatibility, double assembly, and centre continuity; on the
bridge maps above; and on
\(\mathfrak{o}_{K3\times E}=0\).
\end{conjecture}
