\begin{abstract}
SCAFFOLD STUB. The Calabi--Yau-to-chiral functor
$\Phi\colon \mathrm{CY}_d \to \mathrm{Chiral\text{-}Alg}$
promotes a compact Calabi--Yau $d$-fold $X$ to a chiral (factorisation) algebra
$\cA_X$ on an auxiliary curve. The principal invariant preserved is
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA_X) = \sum_q (-1)^q h^{0,q}(X)$ (Hodge supertrace,
discharged with algebraic-vs-geometric model discipline). The output
$E_n$-scope stratifies by dimension:
$\Phi(X) \in E_2\text{-chiral}$ for $d \le 2$ and $\Phi(X) \in E_1\text{-chiral}$
for $d \ge 3$. The universal trace identity unifying Vol I
$K = -c_{\mathrm{ghost}}(\mathrm{BRST})$ with
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ is the bridge from Vol I's universal
conductor to Vol III's Borcherds weight. Full content to be drafted from the
chapter inputs below.
\end{abstract}
