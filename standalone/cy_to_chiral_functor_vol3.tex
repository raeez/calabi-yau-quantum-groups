\begin{abstract}
The Calabi--Yau-to-chiral functor
$\Phi\colon \mathrm{CY}_d \to \mathrm{Chiral\text{-}Alg}$
assigns to a compact Calabi--Yau $d$-fold $X$ a chiral factorisation algebra
$\cA_X$ on an auxiliary curve. Its principal invariant is
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA_X) = \sum_q (-1)^q h^{0,q}(X)$ (Hodge supertrace,
proved with separate algebraic and geometric models). The output
$E_n$-scope stratifies by dimension:
$\Phi(X) \in E_2\text{-chiral}$ for $d \le 2$ and $\Phi(X) \in E_1\text{-chiral}$
for $d \ge 3$. The universal trace identity unifying Vol I
$K = -c_{\mathrm{ghost}}(\mathrm{BRST})$ with
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ connects Vol I's universal
conductor with Vol III's Borcherds weight.
\end{abstract}
