\begin{abstract}
For super-Yangian inputs $Y(\mathfrak{sl}(m|n))$, the Sugawara-shifted
Verdier dual satisfies
\[
\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(Y(\mathfrak{sl}(m|n))) +
\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(Y(\mathfrak{sl}(n|m))^!) = \max(m, n).
\]
The zero-sum analogy is false for this normalization. The super-Riccati
expansion gives closed shadow coefficients $S_r$ whose characteristic
arithmetic primes differ from the bosonic Virasoro tower. The
super-trace and Berezinian pairings give two normalizations of the
same complementarity datum. The canonical Verdier pairing remains an
explicit open construction.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Corrections}
The super-Yangian complementarity identity has right-hand side
$\max(m,n)$ in the Sugawara-shifted dual normalization. The
Virasoro-style constant belongs to a distinct family. The tempered
stratum has $\limsup \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{(\infty)}_{\mathrm{orig}} = 0$
at generic $c$; the value $1/e$ comes from a Stirling cancellation error.
