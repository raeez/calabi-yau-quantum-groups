complementarity identity reads $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(Y(\mathfrak{sl}(m|n))) +
\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(Y(\mathfrak{sl}(n|m))^!) = \max(m, n)$ (scope: Sugawara-shifted dual

pairings (super-trace vs Berezinian) coexist without programme-level
canonicalisation pending Verdier-pairing inscription (frontier item).
\end{abstract}
