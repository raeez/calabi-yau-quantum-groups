\begin{abstract}
SCAFFOLD STUB. The CY-A$_3$ claim $\mathrm{Obs}_{A_\infty} = 0$ holds
as an $\infty$-categorical identity on the inf-cat derived centre (scope:
derived, not chain-level per-$k$). Chain-level, the individual
anticommutators $\{b_k, B^{(2)}\}$ do \emph{not} all vanish (AP-CY34); the
\emph{total} sum $\sum_k \{b_k, B^{(2)}\}$ vanishes by Costello's TCFT
cyclic invariance. This is a paradigmatic instance of the principle
``chain-level $\ne$ rational'' (AP-CY33): $E_3$ collapses under formality,
so an obstruction that is nonzero at chain level becomes a boundary
($Q$-exact) after formality transport. The saga closes AP-CY34 and gives
the first $\infty$-categorical CY-A proof.
\end{abstract}
