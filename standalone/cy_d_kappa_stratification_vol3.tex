\begin{abstract}
SCAFFOLD STUB. For compact Calabi--Yau $d$-folds, the chiral-shadow invariant
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA_X)$ equals the Hodge supertrace
$\sum_q (-1)^q h^{0,q}(X)$ (\texttt{thm:kappa-hodge-supertrace-identification})
unconditionally via HKR plus the Mukai pairing plus an $HC^-_d$ trace. The
dimension stratification $d \in \{1,2,3,4,5\}$ then reads off explicitly
(\texttt{thm:kappa-stratification-by-d}): elliptic curves contribute $0$;
K3 contributes $2$; abelian and bielliptic contribute $0$; quintic, $K3 \times E$,
$E^{\times 3}$ contribute $0$; local $\mathbb{P}^2$ contributes $3/2$
(\texttt{thm:local-p2-shadow}); CY$_4$ sextic contributes $2$; CY$_5$ generic $0$.
The conifold is \emph{not} a local surface (AP-CY34, AP-CY44); the direct
McKay route gives $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}(\text{conifold}) = 1$.
\end{abstract}
