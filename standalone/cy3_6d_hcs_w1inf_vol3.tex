\begin{abstract}
SCAFFOLD STUB. A codim-2 defect $C \subset \mathbb{C}^3$ in 6d holomorphic
Chern--Simons theory induces a boundary chiral algebra isomorphic to
$W_{1+\infty}$ at $\Psi = -\sigma_2$, central charge $c = 1$ (Sugawara).
The normal bundle $N_{C/Y} = \mathbb{C}^2$ supplies the two spectral
parameters. The toroidal chiral quantum group arises on the formal disc via
Ding--Iohara--Miki (DIM) plus Schiffmann--Vasserot CoHA; the global $\mathbb{P}^1
\times \mathbb{P}^1$ lift is conditional on class-M chain-level holography
(see \texttt{conj:uch-gravity-chain-level} in Vol II). Miki's $S_3$ symmetry
is the Weyl group of the $\Omega$-background.
\end{abstract}
