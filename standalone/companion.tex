\begin{abstract}
\noindent\itshape
A Calabi--Yau category has a trace; a chiral algebra has an operator
product; no formal argument connects them. Volume III constructs the
connection as a two-stage, dimension-indexed correspondence
$\Phi_d=\SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ\PhiFA_d$: a canonical
$E_d$-holomorphic factorization algebra on the Calabi--Yau target,
then factorization homology along a $(d{-}1)$-cycle restricted to a
reference curve. Inside the first stage lives the quantum group: the
critical cohomological Hall algebra $Y^+(X)$ whose Drinfeld double
$G(X)$ is the level-three object; at the terminal scalar level every
$K3\times E$ reading lands on the Igusa form,
$Z_{\mathrm{DT}}^{\mathrm{red}}=-4096\,\Dfive^{-2}$. This companion
states the functor with its honest per-dimension status, the
$\kappa$-spectrum that stratifies the landscape, the proved
scalar/automorphic layer, the no-go theorems that fence the
conditional algebraic layer, and the finite recognition data that
separate the two.
\end{abstract}

\section{The seed}\label{sec:seed}

A single Calabi--Yau input carries a spectrum of numerical shadows,
one per construction class. At $X=K3\times E$ the five measurements
\[
\kcat(X)=0,\qquad
\kch^{\mathrm{Hodge}}(X)=0,\qquad
\kch^{\mathrm{Heis}}(X)=3,\qquad
\kBKM(\Dfive)=5,\qquad
\kfib(K3)=24
\]
are all correct and mutually incommensurable: the K\"unneth
holomorphic Euler characteristic, the Hodge supertrace, the rank of
the Heisenberg--Cartan after Gram projection, the Borcherds weight
$c_1(0)/2$ of the Gritsenko denominator, and the Mukai rank of the
fibre. No additive relation holds among them --- the once-tempting
identity $\kBKM=\kch^{\mathrm{Hodge}}+\chi(\cO_{\mathrm{fiber}})$
predicts $2$ where the value is $5$. The object of the volume is the
mechanism producing all five from one datum, and the mechanism is a
functor in two stages,
\[
\Phi_d^{(\Sigma_{d-1},C)}
\;=\;
\SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ\PhiFA_d
\;:\;
\mathrm{CY}_d\text{-}\mathrm{Cat}
\xrightarrow{\ \PhiFA_d\ }
E_d\text{-}\mathrm{HolFA}(X)
\xrightarrow{\ \SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\ }
E_{n(d)}\text{-}\mathrm{ChirAlg}(C),
\]
with native operadic level $n(1)=\infty$, $n(2)=2$, $n(d\ge3)=1$.
Stage one assembles a holomorphic factorization algebra on the target
from the degree-$(1-d)$ Gerstenhaber bracket on Hochschild cohomology,
by Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality --- pinned up to a
$\mathrm{GRT}_1(\bQ)$-torsor --- together with Costello--Gwilliam
locality and the holomorphic BV twist of the volume form. Stage two is
specialisation, never inversion: different cycles produce different
chiral shadows of one stage-one datum. At $d\ge3$ the output is
natively $E_1$, because $\Conf_2(\bR^3)$ is simply connected; the
braiding that makes a quantum group lives one level up, on the
Drinfeld centre of the representation category, where the half-braiding
on evaluation modules \emph{is} the spectral $R$-matrix
$R(z)=\sigma_{V_u}(V_v)$, $z=u-v$.

\section{The objects}\label{sec:objects}

\begin{definition}[Calabi--Yau category]
A $\mathrm{CY}_d$-category is a smooth proper pretriangulated dg (or
cyclic $A_\infty$) category $\cA$ with a non-degenerate negative-cyclic
trace $\Tr^-_\cA\colon HC^-_d(\cA)\to k$; its Hochschild trace is only
the decategorified shadow. Models: $\Perf(X)$ with the Serre trace for
projective Calabi--Yau $X$; matrix-factorisation and critical-CoHA
categories for Landau--Ginzburg charts. The holomorphic volume form
$\Omega_X$ rigidifies the trace into the BV datum of stage one.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The quantum group]
Inside the stage-one object lies the positive effective moduli stack
with its Kontsevich--Soibelman critical structure; its equivariant
vanishing-cycle cohomology is the positive half
$Y^+(X)=H^\bullet_{\mathrm{eq}}(\cM^+_{\mathrm{eff}}(X),\phi_W)$ with
the Schiffmann--Vasserot Hall product. When the Hall pairing
\textup{(from Serre duality)}, Cartan, completion, integral form
\textup{(Davison--Meinhardt)} and stable-envelope transport
\textup{(Maulik--Okounkov)} are constructed, the Drinfeld double
$G(X)=Y^+\bowtie Y^0\bowtie Y^-$ is the quantum group of the datum.
The equivariance stratifies by geometry: full torus on toric charts;
reduced class with residual $\bC^\times$ on compact targets; orbifold
inertia; period-domain equivariance on lattice-polarised families.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The $K3$ column]
$\Phi_2(D^b\Coh(K3))=\cH_{\Muk}$, the Mukai--Heisenberg chiral
algebra: twenty-four currents $J_i(z)$ with
$J_iJ_j\sim\omega^{ij}_{\Muk}/(z-w)^2$ on the Mukai lattice
$\mathrm{II}_{4,20}=U^{4}\oplus E_8(-1)^{2}$, character $1/\eta^{24}$,
$\kch^{\mathrm{Hodge}}=2$. The $d=2$ bulk is the K3 Yangian, the
double of the K3 cohomological Hall algebra on
$\bigsqcup_n\Hilb^n(K3)$; the Mukai-form-preserving envelopes form a
trichotomy --- ungraded orthogonal $Y_\hbar(\fso(4,20))$, the
Hodge-parity super-extension $Y_\hbar(\fso(4|20))$
\textup{(non-Kac: the Mukai pairing is symmetric on both parities)},
with Kac $\fosp(4|20)$ excluded. The trigonometric lift is twenty-four
coupled copies of quantum toroidal $\fgl_1$, with Miki symmetry the
mapping-class $SL_2(\bZ)$ of the elliptic factor.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The $K3\times E$ object]
$\mathbf H_{\Dfive}$ is the conditional Hall--Drinfeld double of
$\CoHA_{K3\times E}$, a quasi-Hopf object of the fourth kind: the
super-Etingof--Kazhdan quantisation of a Manin \emph{pair}
$(\fg_{\Dfive},\ \mathfrak n^{\mathrm{imag}}_+\oplus
\mathfrak h^{\mathrm{imag}})$ --- no Manin triple exists, since the
signature-$(2,1)$ Cartan form has isotropic rank one --- twisted by
the Siegel--Borcherds associator built from
$\Phiten^{\mathrm{un}}/\eta^{24}$ and carrying a projective
$\widetilde M_{24}$ symmetry. Its character is a theorem; its
existence as an algebra is a finite recognition problem.
\end{definition}

\section{The theorems}\label{sec:theorems}

\begin{theorem}[Two-stage factorisation; per-dimension status]
\label{thm:twostage}
$\Phi_d=\SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ\PhiFA_d$ with $n(d)$ as above.
Stage one is proved unconditionally at $d\le2$; at $d=3$ on the smooth
proper object-level locus, granted the framing package
\textup{(negative-cyclic CY class, the Costello-corrected chain-level
$S^3$-framing $\{b,B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{TCFT}}\}=0$, and
$\pi_3(B\mathrm{Sp})=0$)}; on explicit toric and formal charts beyond.
Morphism-level functoriality is conjectural at every $d$; the
collection $\{\Phi_d\}$ is a correspondence programme, not one functor
across dimensions. The claims $n(2)=2$ and $n(1)=\infty$ are
conditional enhancements \textup{(the braided enhancement needs the
chain-level two-sphere framing action; the constructed $d=1$ output
has singular operator product and is not commutative)}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The $m_3$ obstruction, resolved in three levels]
\label{thm:m3}
On a non-formal cyclic $A_\infty$-category of dimension three the
strict identity $[m_3,B^{(2)}]=0$ is \emph{false} --- witness
$[m_3,B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{term}}][a|a|a|a|b]=2\alpha[b]\neq0$. What holds
is the moduli-level identity $\{b,B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{TCFT}}\}=0$
\textup{(Costello's open--closed topological conformal field theory:
strip degenerations against the genus-change node, $\partial^2=0$ on
moduli chains)}, and a filtered derived comparison. The chain-level
identification of the two $B^{(2)}$'s is the named open frontier of
stage one at $d=3$; three tempting wrong proofs \textup{(cyclic
invariance, the mixed-complex axiom, Tsygan formality)} are dissected
as mathematics. On the Fermat quintic strict formality is refuted
outright: the Yukawa coupling $Y_3=5+2875\,q+\cdots$ is a nonzero
$m_3$, and the correct statement is \emph{curved} formality with
Yukawa as the BCOV curving datum.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Centre and braiding]\label{thm:centre}
For dualizable $\Rep^{E_1}(A)$ the Drinfeld centre satisfies
$\cZ(\Rep^{E_1}(A))\simeq\Rep^{E_2}(\Zder(A))$ as braided monoidal
$\infty$-categories, and on the chiral Koszul locus
$\Zder(A)=C^\bullet_{\mathrm{ch}}(A,A)$. On toric charts, granted the
Morita-kernel packages, the three level-three constructions meet:
$\Rep^{E_2}(\Zder(A))\simeq\cZ(\Rep^{E_1}(A))
\simeq\cZ(\mathrm{Mod}(Y^+(X)))\simeq\Rep^{E_2}(G(X))$
--- three faces carrying three associativity classes. The evaluation
chain
$\CoHA(\bC^3)=Y^+(\widehat{\fgl}_1)\hookrightarrow
Y\xrightarrow{\mathrm{ev}}\mathrm{End}
(\cW_{1+\infty}[\lambda]\text{-vac})$
keeps the three arrows distinct: the cohomological Hall algebra is the
positive half, never $\cW_{1+\infty}$ itself; its character is the
MacMahon function $\prod_n(1-q^n)^{-n}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Dimension stratification of the Hodge shadow; proved]
\label{thm:cyd}
For the Hodge supertrace $\Xi(X)=\sum_q(-1)^qh^{0,q}(X)$ on compact
Calabi--Yau $d$-folds: $\Xi=0$ at every odd $d$ \textup{(Serre pairs
cancel)}; $\Xi=2$ for even $d$ with full $SU(d)$ holonomy; $\Xi=n+1$
for hyperk\"ahler $K3^{[n]}$-type. The two even strata meet only at
$d=2$. Local values: $\kch^{\mathrm{loc}}(\bC^3)=1$, resolved conifold
$1$, local $\bP^2$ $=3/2$, $K_S=\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(S)/2$; on the
quintic the BV-corrected reading is
$\chi_{\mathrm{top}}/24=-25/3$ while the Hodge reading is $0$ ---
distinct invariants, both correct. The scalar genus tower is
$\mathrm{obs}_g=\kch\lambda_g$ on the uniform-weight lane, with the
first cross-channel correction
$\delta F_2(\cW_3)=(c+204)/(16c)$ at multi-weight.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Abelian $K3$ Yangian; proved]\label{thm:k3yangian}
The $d=2$ bulk admits the presentation: twenty-four currents with
Mukai-diagonal double poles; Miura transfer matrix
$T_{K3}(u)=\prod_{i=1}^{24}(u-\phi_i)$; structure function
$g_{K3}(u)=\prod_{i=1}^{24}\frac{u-h_i}{u+h_i}$ with the Calabi--Yau
constraint $\sum_ih_i=0$ and unitarity $g(u)g(-u)=1$; Drinfeld
coproduct $\Delta_z(T(u))=T(u)\otimes T(u-z)$; Fock characters
$p_{24}(n)=\chi(\Hilb^nK3)$. Koszul duality acts by $h_i\mapsto-h_i$,
with conductor $\kappa+\kappa^!=0$. The non-abelian extension --- the
lattice-Hopf matching with vertex operators --- is a conjecture.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The scalar layer at $K3\times E$; proved]
\label{thm:scalar}
With $\Dfive$ the weight-five Gritsenko--Nikulin form,
$D_5=64^{-1}\Dfive$, $\Phiten^{\mathrm{un}}=\Dfive^2$ the Igusa cusp
form $\chi_{10}$:
\[
Z^{K3\times E}_{\mathrm{DT,red}}
=-\bigl(\Phiten^{\mathrm{OP}}\bigr)^{-1}
=-4096\,\Dfive^{-2}
\qquad\textup{(Oberdieck--Pandharipande--Pixton)},
\]
\[
Z^X_{\mathrm{red}}(p,q,t)
=\prod_{(n,m,\ell)>0}(1-p^nq^mt^\ell)^{-c(nm,\ell)}
=\frac{pqt}{\Dfive^2}
\qquad\textup{(Katz--Klemm--Vafa via Pandharipande--Thomas)},
\]
with $c$ the coefficients of the $K3$ elliptic genus $2\phi_{0,1}$ ---
the exponent doubling is exactly the reciprocal-square mechanism.
The rank-one sector is G\"ottsche's
$\sum_n\chi(\Hilb^nK3)q^n=q/\eta^{24}$; the Humbert restriction of
$\Dfive^{-2}$ produces the doubled Heisenberg character $\eta^{-48}$
exactly \textup{(Gritsenko--Nikulin
$\Dfive=(2\pi iz)\,\eta(\tau)^{24}\eta(\sigma)^{24}(1+O(z^2))$)};
and the Weyl--Kac--Borcherds denominator of $\fg_{\Dfive}$ is
$64^{-1}\Dfive(2Z)$ with signed multiplicities $f(nm,l)$. The
universal Borcherds weight identity reads
$\kBKM(\Phi_N)=c_N(0)/2$, always naming the input form; on the CHL
ladder $N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$ the corrected weights are
$(5,3,2,\tfrac32,1)$ with $c_N(0)=(10,6,4,3,2)$ \textup{(Jatkar--Sen,
Govindarajan--Krishna; the once-recorded ladder $(5,4,3,2,1)$ is
retracted)}; the Fake Monster form $\Phi_{12}$ carries
$c_\Lambda(0)=24$, $\kBKM=12$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Six routes: falsification and stratification]
\label{thm:sixroutes}
Six constructions aim at $G(K3\times E)$: the functor image, the
Borcherds lift, the Mukai lattice vertex algebra, the Kummer orbifold,
the sigma-model half-twist, and the class-$\mathcal S$ realisation.
Unconditionally, the six-way isomorphism is \emph{false}: the route
algebras stratify into exactly three $\kch$-strata, $(3,3,3)$, $(12)$,
$(24)$, and the comparison from the functor route to the lattice route
is injective with cokernel a rank-twenty-one Heisenberg. The
convergence statement that survives is a colimit: granted four
chain-level hypotheses \textup{(Costello--Li factorisation for
holomorphic Chern--Simons; the threefold Kummer Mayer--Vietoris
halving $24\to12$; half-twist primitive extraction; the
Hochschild--Kostant--Rosenberg--Borcherds lift)}, the pentagon of
intertwiners has positive-half colimit $Y^+_{K3\times E}$, and with
Hopf pairing and completion data $G(K3\times E)=D_\hbar(Y^+)$. Three
automorphic shadows of the convergence are proved.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[No-go theorems; proved]\label{thm:nogo}
\textup{(i)} No smooth projective toric Calabi--Yau threefold exists
\textup{(ray generators on an affine hyperplane contradict fan
completeness)}. \textup{(ii)} For compact non-toric $X$ the
identification of $\Phi_3(X)$ with the double of a quiver CoHA is
obstructed: toric completeness, connected automorphisms, finite-quiver
equivariance and the BCOV anomaly
$\alpha_{\mathrm{BCOV}}=\frac{\chi_{\mathrm{top}}}{24}
\mathrm{tr}\,\mathrm{At}(T_X)\in H^1(X,\cO_X)$ interlock into two and
a half independent mechanisms. \textup{(iii)} $K3\times E$ admits no
global noncommutative crepant resolution \textup{(five obstructions)};
the Serre-equivariant quasi-resolution substitute has forced character
$-(\Phiten^{\mathrm{OP}})^{-1}$. \textup{(iv)} No $K3$-fibred
threefold specialisation realises the Fake Monster:
$\mathrm{rk}\,\Lambda_{\mathrm{Leech}}=24$ exceeds every attainable
Picard rank; its home is conjecturally $d=5$ on $K3\times K3\times E$.
\textup{(v)} Toric positive halves define no Borcherds weight: the
$\kBKM$-invariant requires a weak Jacobi input, an even Lorentzian
lattice, and a Weyl vector.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Wall-crossing as gauge equivalence; proved]
\label{thm:wall}
The cohomological Hall algebra is an algebra, not a coalgebra; its
ordered bar complex is a separate coassociative object, and the
square-zero equation lives on the Ginzburg dg Lie algebra, where the
potential's critical equation is the Maurer--Cartan equation.
Kontsevich--Soibelman wall-crossing is Maurer--Cartan gauge
equivalence on the motivic Hall Lie algebra; the pentagon identity is
the Jacobi identity in degree three; conifold wall-crossing is cluster
mutation; the attractor mechanism selects the canonical gauge
representative. Shadow-tower truncations are not gauge-invariant
across walls.
\end{theorem}

\begin{conjecture}[The three bridges]\label{conj:bridges}
\textup{(i) Chain fusion:} the stage-two output $A_X$ is the boundary
chart $\mathrm{End}(b)$ of a canonical vacuum in an open factorization
category on a tangential log curve --- verified on $\bC^3$, local
$\bP^2$, the conifold, conditional at $K3\times E$, open in general.
\textup{(ii) CY-C:} a braided Hopf datum $C(\cA,q)$ with
$\Rep^{\mathrm{fd}}\simeq$ the modular tensor category of $A_\cA$,
recovering Kazhdan--Lusztig at $\mathrm{pt}/G$.
\textup{(iii) Mirror equals Koszul:}
$\Phi(\cA^\vee)\simeq\Phi(\cA)^!$; at $d=2$ this is \emph{false} ---
homological mirror symmetry preserves $\kch=2$ while Koszul duality
negates it --- and at $d=3$ it is the conjecture that mirror symmetry
flips the sign of $\chi_{\mathrm{top}}$.
\end{conjecture}

\section{The garden}\label{sec:garden}

One logarithmic $1$-form generates the programme. On a smooth complex
curve $X$, the form $\eta=d\log(z_1-z_2)$ is the unique
coordinate-free $1$-form with a residue along the diagonal of
$\Conf_2(X)$; the Arnold relation
$\eta_{12}\wedge\eta_{23}+\eta_{23}\wedge\eta_{31}
+\eta_{31}\wedge\eta_{12}=0$ generates all relations among its higher
companions. From $\eta$ one builds, for an augmented chiral algebra
$\cA$ on $X$, the ordered bar complex
$\barB^{\mathrm{ord}}_X(\cA)=T^c(s^{-1}\bar\cA)\otimes
\Omega^\bullet_{\log}(\FM_\bullet(X))$; its differential squares to
zero exactly when $\cA$ satisfies the Borcherds identity. Assembled
over $\Mbar_{g,n}$ with the prime-form propagator, the total
differential is a Maurer--Cartan element $\Theta_\cA=D_\cA-d_0$;
genus~$\ge1$ flatness fails by the curvature
$d^2_{\mathrm{fib}}=\kappa(\cA)\,\omega_g$, and the scalar $\kappa$
--- $\kappa(\Vir_c)=c/2$,
$\kappa(V_k(\fg))=\dim\fg\,(k+h^\vee)/2h^\vee$ --- is the central
charge read as modular curvature. The Verdier--Koszul dual obeys
conductor laws $c(\cA)+c(\cA^!)=K$ with $K(\Vir)=26$,
$K(\cW_N)=4N^3-2N-2$, $K(V_k(\fg))=2\dim\fg$ along
$k\mapsto-k-2h^\vee$; the landscape stratifies into archetype classes
$\mathsf G/\mathsf L/\mathsf C/\mathsf M$ of shadow depth
$2/3/4/\infty$ plus the critical Feigin--Frenkel stratum.

Every object sits on one chain: primitive factorization category
$\to$ chart algebra $A_b$ $\to$ bar shadow $\barB(A_b)$ $\to$ derived
chiral centre $\Zder(A_b)$ \textup{(the bulk)} $\to$ acting line
operators $\to$ scalar modular shadow. Identifying stages without the
licensing datum --- shadow $=$ object --- is the master error the
programme is disciplined against; this volume's own instrument is the
three-axis type signature: level, chart, ambient, declared on every
theorem.

The five gardens are five readings of the chain. \emph{Volume I}
(\texttt{chiral-bar-cobar}) builds the engine on algebraic curves:
bar--cobar adjunction and inversion, quadratic Koszul recognition,
centre complementarity, the deformation--Hodge--graph traces
$F_g=\kappa\lambda^{\mathrm{FP}}_g+\delta F^{\mathrm{cross}}_g$,
Hochschild concentration, and the arithmetic shadow. \emph{Volume II}
(\texttt{chiral-bar-cobar-vol2}) reads the same object as physics:
bar $=$ BV/BRST, quantum master equation $=$ Maurer--Cartan, anomaly
$=$ curvature, the two-coloured Swiss-cheese operad as bulk--boundary
home, Universal Holography
$\cA\mapsto(\partial=\cA,\ \mathrm{bulk}=\Zder(\cA))$, at
$\Vir_{c=3\ell/2G_N}$ the boundary reading of 3d gravity.
\emph{This volume} constructs the geometric source: the two-stage
functor from Calabi--Yau categories, the Hall positive half and its
double as the level-three quantum group, and the $\kappa$-spectrum as
the level-$\mathsf S$ characteristic. \emph{Mixed
holomorphic--topological strings} computes the local germ of the
$d=2$ theory at $N$ Dirac branes --- the trace coordinate
$J(f)=\Tr f(\phi_1,\phi_2)$ --- with the holomorphic de Rham
obstruction on $K3\times E$ showing why the local stalk does not
globalise for free. \emph{The Igusa cusp form}
(\texttt{igusa-cusp-form}) is the terminal scalar: $\Dfive$ read five
ways, the order-two orientation bit that squaring kills, and the
finite recognition data --- this volume's own frontier --- that would
promote the scalar back to the operator $\mathbf H_{\Dfive}$.

The single question underneath: characterise the modular convolution
algebra $\fg^{\mathrm{mod}}_\cA$ as a geometric object. The proposed
answer --- endomorphisms of the Lagrangian embedding
$\cL_\cA\hookrightarrow\cM_{\mathrm{vac}}(\cA)$ in a $(-2)$-shifted
symplectic stack of vacua --- has Volume I computing its Taylor jets,
Volume II its local composition law, this volume its geometric

\section{The frontier}\label{sec:frontier}

The volume's honest split is scalar versus algebraic. Everything
scalar and automorphic at $K3\times E$ --- the
Oberdieck--Pandharipande identity, the Katz--Klemm--Vafa product, the
second-quantised elliptic genus, the Borcherds lift and denominator,
the $\eta^{-48}$ Humbert residue, the universal weight $c_N(0)/2$ ---
is proved with sources pinned. The algebraic layer is one coherent
conditional programme whose missing inputs are enumerated:

\begin{enumerate}
\item The compact critical CoHA package for $K3\times E$ --- atlas,
Serre-compatible quasi-resolution descent, Hopf pairing with
Poincar\'e--Birkhoff--Witt integral form, stable-envelope transport,
double descent --- the existence layer of $G(K3\times E)$ and
$\mathbf H_{\Dfive}$. The deepest form: away from toric charts, most
of the global double is invisible to local chart data.
\item The four pentagon hypotheses closing the six-route colimit.
\item The compact CY$_3$ strictification of stage one: identify
$B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{TCFT}}$ with $B^{(2)}_{\mathrm{term}}$ at chain
level; until then the quintic carries curved formality with Yukawa
curving.
\item Chain fusion in general dimension --- the bridge from this
volume's stage-two output to the open-side primitive package of
Volumes I and II.
\item The non-abelian $K3$ Yangian \textup{(lattice-Hopf matching,
Jacobi at the vertex level)}; the Fake Monster at $d=5$; the
$\cW_\infty[\lambda]$ endpoint beyond its admissible window;
modularity under fusion.
\end{enumerate}

The volume's architectural invention matches the Igusa companion's:
recognition criteria with finite data lists, three-axis scope
signatures, and the discipline that a character identity is never
promoted to an algebra identification. The geometric source is
constructed exactly as far as the obstructions permit, and the
obstructions are theorems.
