\chapter{Three-axis scope discipline}
\label{app:three-axis-scope-discipline}

\providecommand{\Zder}{Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}}
\providecommand{\GRTone}{\mathrm{GRT}_1(\mathbb{Q})}

A theorem in this volume names three coordinates: the level it inhabits in
the universal arrow, the chart datum it depends on, and the ambient in
which its proof actually works. Promotion across coordinates requires a
named comparison arrow constructed under named hypotheses. No claim is
permitted to migrate between coordinates by elision.

This appendix collects the discipline. The vertical axis is the universal
arrow with five levels. The horizontal axis is the chart datum, fibered
over an equivariance stratum. The ambient axis is the categorical
context in which a chain-level identity is asserted. Every theorem
statement in the body declares its position on each axis explicitly, or
the discipline records the omission as an obligation.

\section{The universal arrow: vertical axis}
\label{sec:tas-vertical}

The five levels of the universal arrow:
\begin{align*}
0 &\quad\text{primitive} && \cC,\ \mathrm{CY}_d\text{-cat with cyclic }\Ainf\text{-data}, \\
1 &\quad\text{Stage-1 native} && \PhiFA_d(\cC) \in E_d\text{-}\HolFA(X), \\
2 &\quad\text{Stage-2 chart shadow} && A_X = \SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1}, C}\!\circ\PhiFA_d(\cC) \in \mathrm{ChirAlg}^{E_{n(d)}}(C), \\
3 &\quad\text{centre / quantum vertex group} && \Zder(A_X) \simeq \mathrm{ChirHoch}^\bullet(A_X, A_X),\ Y^+(X),\ G(X) = D(Y^+(X)), \\
4 &\quad\text{scalar trace / Borcherds form} && \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2.
\end{align*}

Each transition is mediated by a named comparison arrow:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item $0 \to 1$: Kontsevich--Tamarkin $E_d$-formality plus
  Costello--Gwilliam--Li holomorphic locality, canonical up to the
  $\GRTone$-torsor.
\item $1 \to 2$: factorisation homology
  $\SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1}, C}(-) = \int_{\Sigma_{d-1}}(-)\big|_C$ along a
  $(d{-}1)$-cycle restricted to the reference curve $C$.
\item $2 \to 3$: bar/twisting comparison
  $\Zder(A) = \mathrm{ChirHoch}^\bullet(A, A) = \RHom(\Omega B(A), A)$.
  The bar $B(A)$ is the comparison arrow itself; it is twisting/coupling,
  not bulk.
\item $3 \to 4$: trace pairing on the centre, terminating in the
  Borcherds-weight scalar through the universal denominator identity.
\end{itemize}

The shift law $(d, \mathrm{shift}, E_n^{\mathrm{cl}})
\in \{(2, -2, E_2), (3, -1, E_1), (4, 0, E_0), (5, +1, E_5\text{-Poisson})\}$
forces the per-$d$ codomain $E_{n(d)}$ at level 2 by Gerstenhaber-degree
arithmetic plus Dunn--Lurie additivity. The chiral target depends on $d$;
the universal arrow does not.

\paragraph{Bar versus bulk.} At $d \geq 3$ the algebra $A$ on the
reference curve is $E_1$. The $E_2$-braiding lives on
$Z(\mathrm{Rep}^{E_1}(A))$ at level 3, not on $A$ at level 2. A theorem
that places $E_2$-structure directly on $A$ at $d \geq 3$ has collapsed
levels 2 and 3.

\section{The chart datum: horizontal axis}
\label{sec:tas-horizontal}

At each level the construction depends on a chart datum with internal
product structure. At level 2 the chart is a fibered four-tuple:
\[
\text{Chart} = \text{equivariance stratum}
  \times_{\text{stratum}} (\Sigma_{d-1}, C)
  \times_{\text{stratum}} (\text{boundary vacuum } b)
  \times_{\text{stratum}} (\text{admissibility window}).
\]

The four equivariance strata are:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item Toric $T^d$: $\mathbb{C}^3$, local $\mathbb{P}^2$, resolved
  conifold. Chart fine structure: equivariant cohomology, GIT chamber,
  framing class.
\item Reduced $\mathbb{C}^\times +$ $\mathrm{Aut}(X)$: K3, $K3 \times E$,
  abelian threefolds, K3 fibrations. Chart fine structure: the
  $\mathrm{Aut}(X)$-action data.
\item Orbifold inertia $I(X/G)$: McKay $\mathbb{C}^d/\Gamma$ for
  $\Gamma \subset \mathrm{SU}(d)$ finite, Mathieu $M_{24}$ siblings.
  Chart fine structure: conjugacy class data on $G$.
\item Lattice-polarised period domain: Borcherds lifts on signature
  $(2, n)$ lattices, Gritsenko $\Delta_5$, Igusa $\Phi_{10}$. Chart fine
  structure: the period datum (Hodge filtration).
\end{itemize}

A theorem at level 2 declares all four chart coordinates. The
chart-internal enumeration of constructions on a single $X$ (the K3 $\times E$
spectrum $\{\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}, \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Hodge}}, \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}, \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Delta_5), \kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}\}
= \{0, 0, 3, 5, 24\}$) is distinct from the cross-landscape enumeration
of archetype classes (the
$\mathsf{G}/\mathsf{L}/\mathsf{C}/\mathsf{M}/\mathsf{B}$ five
$K^{\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}} = \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}} + \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^! \in \{0, 8, 13, 250/3, 98/3\}$
on the canonical Vol~I landmark). One chart point;
five constructions. Versus five archetype classes; one number per class.
The two fives share one cell (the $\mathsf{B}$-row Mukai-doubling face)
and otherwise enumerate orthogonal stratifications. Conflation of the
two is a higher-order chart-axis violation.

\paragraph{The $\Phi_N$ chart at level 4.} At level 4 the chart is the
Siegel input denominator $\Phi_N$, with $N$ ranging over the CHL ladder
$N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ where $\varphi(N) \mid 2$ admits a paramodular
witness. The universal Borcherds-weight identity
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ takes value $5$ at $\Delta_5$ and
$12$ at $\Phi_{12}$; same identity, two charts, name the input
denominator. Bare $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}$ without naming $\Phi_N$ is a
horizontal-axis omission.

\section{Ambient discipline: depth axis}
\label{sec:tas-ambient}

Each theorem holds in a named ambient. The non-equivalent ambients in
active use:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item Ordinary chain complex.
\item Weight-completed (e.g.\ class~$\cM$ chain-level $E_3$ bar
  $= 6^g$ at $g \leq 3$; chain-level identity infinite in ordinary,
  finite in weight-completed).
\item Pro-objects and inverse-limit categories.
\item $J$-adic completion.
\item HS-sewing (Heisenberg--Siegel).
\item Formal-local versus global-with-descent (the holomorphic de Rham
  obstruction is invisible formally, present globally).
\item Derived $\infty$-categorical.
\end{itemize}

A statement holding in one ambient need not extend to another. The
$W_\infty[\lambda] \Rightarrow E_\infty$ implication holds in the
four-condition admissible window (Pro\v{c}\'azka triangular truncation,
Creutzig--Kanade--Linshaw parafermion, Pope--Romans--Shen / Bakas,
Yamada weight window) and is open outside it.

The chain-level lane and the $(\infty, 1)$-categorical lane are equally
load-bearing. State each theorem in the lane in which its proof actually
works; ambient-qualify when both lanes contribute.

\section{The refined cut}
\label{sec:tas-cut}

The discipline closes on a single sentence:

\begin{quote}
Every theorem in the programme declares its $(\text{level},
\text{chart}, \text{ambient})$ coordinates. Promotion across coordinates
requires the named comparison arrow, constructed under the named
hypotheses. No claim is permitted to be promoted from one coordinate to
another by elision.
\end{quote}

The cut subsumes the priority ordering ``primitive first, shadows
second, scalar last'' (which orders levels) and refines it: two theorems
at the same level but different charts are not the same theorem; two
theorems at the same level and chart but different ambients are not the
same theorem. Discipline is uniform across all three axes.

\section{Worked examples: well-scoped versus underscoped}
\label{sec:tas-examples}

\subsection*{Example 1: the CY-to-chiral functor}

Underscoped statement:
\begin{quote}
\textit{$\Phi_3$ produces an $E_1$-chiral algebra.}
\end{quote}
Underscoped because: $\Phi_3$ is presented as a single functor; the
chart datum is omitted; the ambient is unnamed. The chart-axis omission
hides Stage-2 chart-dependence, the level-axis omission collapses Stage-1
and Stage-2.

Well-scoped statement (level 2, chart $(\Sigma_2, C, b, \mathrm{adm})$,
verified-locus ambient):
\begin{quote}
\textit{The two-stage factorisation
$\Phi^{(\Sigma_2, C)}_3 = \SpCh_{\Sigma_2, C} \circ \PhiFA_3$ produces an
$E_1$-chiral algebra on the reference curve $C$ in the verified-locus
ambient. Stage~1 is canonical up to a $\GRTone$-torsor by
Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality plus Costello--Gwilliam--Li locality;
Stage~2 is chart-dependent specialisation along the $(d{-}1)$-cycle
$\Sigma_2$ restricted to $C$, with boundary vacuum $b$ and admissibility
window $\mathrm{adm}$.}
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Example 2: the bulk}

Underscoped statement:
\begin{quote}
\textit{The bar $B(A)$ is the bulk.}
\end{quote}
Underscoped because: collapses level 2 (bar) to level 3 (bulk). The bar
is the comparison arrow, not the bulk.

Well-scoped statement (level 3 from level 2 via the named comparison):
\begin{quote}
\textit{The derived chiral centre at level 3 equals
$\Zder(A) = \mathrm{ChirHoch}^\bullet(A, A) = \RHom(\Omega B(A), A)$,
where the bar/cobar comparison
$B \dashv \Omega$ identifies levels 2 and 3 in the Quillen equivalence
of $\Ainf$-coalgebras and $\Ainf$-algebras (Lefèvre-Hasegawa,
Loday--Vallette). The bar $B(A)$ at level 2 is twisting/coupling data,
not centre.}
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Example 3: the universal Borcherds weight}

Underscoped statement:
\begin{quote}
\textit{$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}} = \kappa_{\mathrm{ch}} + \chi(\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{fibre}})$.}
\end{quote}
Underscoped because: bare $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$ violates HZ-7; the chart
datum (which $\Phi_N$) is unnamed; presents an additive shift relation
that holds at $N = 1$ under the $\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Mukai}}$
convention and fails at $N \in \{2, 3, 4, 6\}$ under any convention. The
horizontal-axis omission propagates a chart-internal accident as
universal.

Well-scoped statement (level 4, chart $\Phi_N$, $N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$):
\begin{quote}
\textit{The universal Borcherds-weight identity
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ holds across the CHL ladder
$N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$, where $c_N(0)$ is the Fourier coefficient at
the origin of the Borcherds product input
(Borcherds 1995 \emph{Invent.\ Math.} 120; Gritsenko 1999). At the
paramodular $\Delta_5$ input the identity reads
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Delta_5) = 5$; at the Fake-Monster $\Phi_{12}$
input it reads $\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_{12}) = 12$.}
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Example 4: the K3 BKM object}

Underscoped statement:
\begin{quote}
\textit{The K3 BKM object is $Y_\hbar(\mathfrak{osp}(4 \mid 20))$.}
\end{quote}
Underscoped because: the Mukai pairing is symmetric on both even and
odd parts; $\mathfrak{osp}$ requires a symplectic form on the odd part;
the structure-preserving Lie algebra is $\mathfrak{so}$, never
$\mathfrak{osp}$, on the Mukai datum. A chart-axis violation: the
Mukai-form data on $H^{\mathrm{ev}}(K3, \mathbb{Z})$ are misnamed.

Well-scoped statement (level 3, chart $K3 \times E$ with Mukai datum,
chain-level ambient):
\begin{quote}
\textit{The K3-side BKM object is the Hall--Drinfeld double
$\mathcal{D}_\hbar(\mathrm{CoHA}_{K3 \times E})$. The Mukai self-mirror
branch carries the orthogonal Yangian $Y_\hbar(\mathfrak{so}(4, 20))$ in
the ungraded form. The Hodge-parity $\mathbb{Z}/2$-super-extension
$Y_\hbar(\mathfrak{so}(4 \mid 20))$ (symmetric on both parts, non-Kac) is
available when the even/odd split on $H^{\mathrm{ev}}(K3, \mathbb{Z})$
is imposed. Kac's $\mathfrak{osp}(4 \mid 20)$ is not the envelope.}
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Example 5: class~$\cM$ chain-level}

Underscoped statement:
\begin{quote}
\textit{The class~$\cM$ $E_3$ bar equals $6^g$.}
\end{quote}
Underscoped because: the ambient is unnamed. The identity is finite in
weight-completed and infinite in ordinary chain complexes.

Well-scoped statement (level 2 cohomological, chart class $\cM$,
weight-completed ambient):
\begin{quote}
\textit{In the weight-completed ambient, the class~$\cM$ $E_3$ bar at
cohomological level equals $6^g$ for $g \leq 3$ (Vol~II
\texttt{weight\_completed\_topologization\_class\_m\_platonic.tex}); the
case $g \geq 4$ is open pending a $d_5$-page computation. In ordinary
chain complexes the chain-level identity is infinite.}
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Example 6: $W_\infty[\lambda] \Rightarrow E_\infty$}

Underscoped statement:
\begin{quote}
\textit{$W_\infty[\lambda] \Rightarrow E_\infty$.}
\end{quote}
Underscoped because: ambient (in particular, the admissibility window)
is unnamed.

Well-scoped statement (level 3 endpoint, chart $\lambda$, four-condition
admissible window):
\begin{quote}
\textit{In the four-condition admissible window
(Pro\v{c}\'azka triangular truncation, Creutzig--Kanade--Linshaw
parafermion, Pope--Romans--Shen / Bakas, Yamada weight window), the
endpoint $W_\infty[\lambda]$ realises an $E_\infty$-algebra. The
implication is open outside the window.}
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Example 7: chain fusion}

Underscoped statement:
\begin{quote}
\textit{The CY-side bulk equals the open-side bulk.}
\end{quote}
Underscoped because: the comparison is conjectural in general; the chart
datum on the open side $(C, D_C, \tau_C)$ and the boundary vacuum
$b(X, \Sigma, C)$ are unnamed; verified examples are not enumerated.

Well-scoped statement (level-2 conjecture, chart $(\Sigma_{d-1}, C, b, \mathrm{adm})$,
chain-level ambient):
\begin{quote}
\textit{For every $\mathrm{CY}_d$-category $\cC_X$ and every chart datum
$(\Sigma_{d-1}, C)$, the Stage-2 chiral algebra
$A_X = \Phi^{(\Sigma_{d-1}, C)}_d(\cC_X)$ is the boundary algebra
$A_{b(X, \Sigma, C)}$ for a canonical boundary vacuum
$b(X, \Sigma, C)$ in an open factorisation dg-category on $(C, D_C, \tau_C)$,
where $D_C$ encodes the CY data's special points (orbifold loci,
fibration punctures, conifold singularities). Verified at $\mathbb{C}^3$,
local $\mathbb{P}^2$, resolved conifold, and $K3 \times E$. Open in
general.}
\end{quote}

\section{Scope-omission collapses: the eight types}
\label{sec:tas-collapses}

The discipline catches scope-omission collapses by axis. Eight archetypal
types appear repeatedly across the volume; each is a place where one
coordinate is missing or a comparison arrow is asserted by elision.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{C\arabic*.}, leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Bar~$\to$~bulk collapse.} Level 2 asserted as level 3
  without naming the bar/cobar comparison. Heal: insert
  $\Zder(A) = \RHom(\Omega B(A), A)$.
\item \textbf{Stage~$\to$~functor collapse.} Stage-1 + Stage-2 asserted
  as a single functor without naming the chart specialisation. Heal:
  state $\Phi^{(\Sigma_{d-1}, C)}_d = \SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1}, C} \circ \PhiFA_d$
  and locate functoriality at Stage-1.
\item \textbf{Scalar~$\to$~operator collapse.} Level 4 (Borcherds form,
  partition function) asserted as level 3 (operator algebra, Hilbert
  space). Heal: scalar = trace of operator at the named chart, with the
  trace pairing constructed.
\item \textbf{Positive half~$\to$~double collapse.} $Y^+(X)$ asserted as
  $G(X)$ without the Drinfeld doubling step. Heal: $G(X) = D(Y^+(X))$,
  three arrows in the CoHA evaluation chain
  $\mathrm{CoHA}(X) = Y^+(X) \hookrightarrow Y(X) \xrightarrow{\mathrm{ev}_\lambda} \mathrm{End}(\mathcal{V}^\lambda)$,
  three associativity classes.
\item \textbf{Chart-internal~$\to$~universal collapse.} A chart-specific
  identity (e.g.\ $N = 1$ accident, K3 Mukai datum) asserted as universal
  across the chart space. Heal: name the input chart and restrict the
  universality claim.
\item \textbf{Equivariance stratum collapse.} A toric statement asserted
  for compact non-toric CY$_3$, or a reduced $\mathbb{C}^\times +$ Aut
  statement asserted for orbifold inertia. Heal: name the equivariance
  stratum, restrict, and isolate the non-toric or non-reduced
  obstruction.
\item \textbf{Ambient collapse (chain-level versus
  $(\infty, 1)$-categorical).} A weight-completed identity asserted in
  ordinary chain complexes; a formal-local Darboux applied globally; a
  derived $\infty$-categorical lift assumed without HS-sewing input.
  Heal: declare the ambient and the comparison arrow between ambients.
\item \textbf{Theory-import collapse.} 6d hCS asserted as 3d
  Chern--Simons; 5d hCS Yangian assumed for non-simply-laced $\fg$
  without the twisted construction. Heal: name the source theory, the
  target functor, and the obstruction (cubic symmetric Casimir
  $d^{abc}$ for 6d hCS at $\fsl_{N \geq 3}$; twisted Yangian for
  non-simply-laced).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Operating procedure}
\label{sec:tas-procedure}

To scope-check a theorem statement, perform a three-line inspection:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*), nosep]
\item \emph{Level check.} What level does the statement live at?
  $0$ / $1$ / $2$ / $3$ / $4$.
\item \emph{Chart check.} Which chart datum is named? Equivariance
  stratum / $(\Sigma_{d-1}, C)$ / boundary vacuum $b$ /
  admissibility window / Drinfeld double / Fock evaluation $\lambda$ /
  Siegel input $\Phi_N$.
\item \emph{Ambient check.} Which ambient is named? Ordinary /
  weight-completed / pro / $J$-adic / HS-sewing / formal-local /
  global-with-descent / derived $\infty$-categorical.
\end{enumerate}
A theorem is well-scoped iff the three coordinates are explicit, or the
relevant axis is genuinely scope-independent (a level-0-and-up universal
statement, a chart-stable level-3 identity, etc.). When an axis is
omitted, name the obligation: which coordinate is missing and what
comparison arrow would close it.

\paragraph{Position in the apparatus.} The discipline operates as a gate
on every theorem statement. It does not rewrite proofs; it forces each
proof to declare what it actually proves. Its application across the
volume is recorded by the manuscript's theorem statements themselves,
which name their coordinates inline. Each open frontier in
Chapter~\ref{ch:open-frontiers} carries explicit
$(\text{level}, \text{chart}, \text{ambient})$ coordinates, a
hypothesis package, and a heal path.
