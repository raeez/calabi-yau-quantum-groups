\section{The \texorpdfstring{$\kappa$}{kappa}-spectrum}
\label{sec:notation-kappa}

A single CY manifold $X$ gives rise to \emph{multiple} chiral algebraizations,
each with its own characteristic exponent.  Bare ``$\kappa$'' is forbidden in
Vol~III (AP113).

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Name} & \textbf{Source} & \textbf{K3$\times E$ value} \\
\midrule
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$ & Chiral & Chiral algebra $A_\cC$ via $\Phi$ & 3 \\
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}$ & Borcherds--Kac--Moody & Igusa cusp form weight & 5 \\
$\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}$ & Categorical & Holomorphic Euler char.\ $\chi(\cO_X)$ & 2 \\
$\kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}$ & Fiber/lattice & Lattice rank / fiber structure & 24 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
Forbidden subscripts: \texttt{global}, \texttt{BPS}, \texttt{eff},
\texttt{total}, \texttt{naive}, \texttt{MacMahon}.  If the subscript is an
indexing variable over the approved set, write $\kappa_\bullet$.

\section{The \texorpdfstring{$\En$}{E\_n} hierarchy}
\label{sec:notation-en}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Level} & \textbf{Structure} & \textbf{CY source} \\
\midrule
$\Eone$ & Associative / ordered factorization on $\C \times \R$ & Native for $d=3$ (hol.\ CS) \\
$\Etwo$ & Braided factorization on $\C \times \C$ & $d=2$ (S$^2$-framing); $d=3$ via $Z(\Rep^{\Eone}(A))$ \\
$E_3$ & Framed little 3-disks & 6d hol.\ CS on $\C^3$ \\
$\Einf$ & Symmetric / commutative & Kills Hopf / quantum group structure \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
Key relationships:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item Dunn additivity: $\Eone \otimes \Eone \simeq \Etwo$, \;
      $\Eone \otimes \Etwo \simeq E_3$.
\item $\Eone \to \Etwo$ promotion: Drinfeld center
      $Z(\Rep^{\Eone}(A)) = \Rep^{\Etwo}(\Zder(A))$.
\item $\Etwo \to E_3$ promotion: derived center (higher Deligne), NOT iterated
      Drinfeld center.
\item $\Etwo$ braiding is \emph{not} symmetric ($\Etwo \neq \Einf$, AP-CY3).
\item $\Einf$ averaging $\mathrm{av}\colon B^{\mathrm{ord}} \to B^{\Einf}$
      kills the $R$-matrix (AP-CY23).
\end{itemize}

\section{Shadow classes G/L/C/M}
\label{sec:notation-shadow}

The shadow class of a chiral algebra $A$ is determined by the full shadow
obstruction tower $\{S_k\}_{k \ge 2}$, never by generator counting alone
(AP-CY12).

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lllll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Class} & \textbf{Depth} & \textbf{$\Ainf$ corrections} & \textbf{Analytic type} & \textbf{Example} \\
\midrule
G (Gaussian) & 0 & None ($m_k = 0$, $k \ge 3$) & Polynomial & Free field \\
L (Lie) & Finite & Finite truncation & Rational & Kac--Moody \\
C (Calabi) & Finite & Charge conservation kills higher & Convergent & Fermionic $bc$ \\
M (Mock) & $\infty$ & All $\delta^{(k)} \neq 0$ & Gevrey-1 (Borel) & Virasoro, $\cW_{1+\infty}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
The shadow invariants $S_k$ are the $\Ainf$ coproduct correction coefficients:
$\Delta^{\Ainf} = \Delta^{\mathrm{Yangian}} + \hbar^2 \delta^{(3)} + \hbar^3 \delta^{(4)} + \cdots$
with $\delta^{(k)}$ having coefficient $S_k$.

$E_3$ bar cohomology: $\dim H^*(B_{E_3}(A)) = (1+t)^{3g}$ for classes L and C;
fails for class M (AP-CY21).

\begin{center}

\noindent

$Z(\Rep^{\Eone}(A)) \simeq \Rep^{\Etwo}(\Zder(A))$.
These are \emph{not} the same object (AP-CY4, AP-CY5).

\section{Spectral vs.\ worldsheet parameters}
\label{sec:notation-z}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{Context} & \textbf{Behaviour at 0} \\
\midrule
$z_{\mathrm{spec}}$ & Yangian spectral parameter (shift $u \to u - z$) &
  $\Delta_0$ cocommutative; admits antipode \\
$z_{\mathrm{ws}}$ & Worldsheet / OPE insertion coordinate &
  Poles from OPE singularities \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
Setting $z = 0$ in the Drinfeld coproduct $\Delta_z$ removes the spectral
shift (no OPE singularity).  Setting $z \to w$ in the OPE $T(z)T(w)$ produces
poles.  These are \emph{different} mathematical objects (AP-CY31).  Conflation
is the source of the adversarial ``$z=0$ singularity'' objection.
