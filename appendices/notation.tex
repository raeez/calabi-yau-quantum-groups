\section{The \texorpdfstring{$\kappa$}{kappa}-spectrum}
\label{sec:notation-kappa}

A single CY manifold $X$ admits \emph{multiple} chiral algebras: the
functor $\Phi$ produces exactly one, while the others come from
independent constructions such as the Borcherds lift, lattice VOA, or
orbifold.  Each construction has its own characteristic exponent.
Bare ``$\kappa$'' is forbidden in Vol~III; the approved subscripts form
the closed set $\{\mathrm{ch}, \mathrm{cat}, \mathrm{BKM},
\mathrm{fiber}\}$.  These four subscripted invariants fall into two
types (manifold invariants, fixed by the geometry of $X$, versus
algebraization invariants, depending on which chiral/BKM algebra is
constructed):

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Manifold invariants} (fixed by the geometry of $X$, independent of algebraization):
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Name} & \textbf{Source} & \textbf{K3$\times E$ value} \\
\midrule
$\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}$ & Categorical & Holomorphic Euler char.\ $\chi(\cO_X)$ & 2 \\
$\kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}$ & Fiber/lattice & Lattice rank / fiber structure & 24 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
\textbf{Algebraization invariants} (depend on which chiral/BKM algebra is constructed):
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Name} & \textbf{Source} & \textbf{K3$\times E$ value} \\
\midrule
$\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$ & Chiral & Chiral algebra $A_\cC$ via $\Phi$ & 3 \\
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}$ & Borcherds--Kac--Moody & Igusa cusp form weight & 5 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
Saying ``algebraizations share $\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}$'' is vacuous:
$\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}$ and $\kappa_{\mathrm{fiber}}$ are topological
invariants of the manifold and cannot vary between algebraizations.

\medskip\noindent
Forbidden subscripts: \texttt{global}, \texttt{BPS}, \texttt{eff},
\texttt{total}, \texttt{naive}, \texttt{MacMahon}.  If the subscript is an
indexing variable over the approved set, write $\kappa_\bullet$.

\section{The \texorpdfstring{$\En$}{E\_n} hierarchy}
\label{sec:notation-en}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Level} & \textbf{Structure} & \textbf{CY source} \\
\midrule
$\Eone$ & Associative / ordered factorization on $\C \times \R$ & Native for $d=3$ (hol.\ CS) \\
$\Etwo$ & Braided factorization on $\C \times \C$ & $d=2$ (S$^2$-framing); $d=3$ via $Z(\Rep^{\Eone}(A))$ \\
$E_3$ & Framed little 3-disks & 6d hol.\ CS on $\C^3$ \\
$\Einf$ & Symmetric / commutative & Kills Hopf / quantum group structure \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
Key relationships:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item Dunn additivity: $\Eone \otimes \Eone \simeq \Etwo$, \;
      $\Eone \otimes \Etwo \simeq E_3$.
\item $\Eone \to \Etwo$ promotion: Drinfeld center
      $Z(\Rep^{\Eone}(A)) = \Rep^{\Etwo}(\Zder(A))$.
\item $\Etwo \to E_3$ promotion: derived center (higher Deligne), NOT iterated
      Drinfeld center.
\item $\Etwo$ braiding is \emph{not} symmetric ($\Etwo \neq \Einf$;
      CY-C invariants distinguish braided from symmetric quantum groups).
\item $\Einf$ averaging $\mathrm{av}\colon B^{\mathrm{ord}} \to B^{\Einf}$
      kills the $R$-matrix: the ordered bar $B^{\mathrm{ord}}$ preserves the
      $E_1$-chiral bialgebra axioms (H1--H5), while the $\Sigma_n$-coinvariant
      projection destroys the Hopf structure.
\end{itemize}

\section{Shadow classes G/L/C/M}
\label{sec:notation-shadow}

The shadow class of a chiral algebra $A$ is determined by the full shadow
obstruction tower $\{S_k\}_{k \ge 2}$, never by generator counting alone:
the distinction between categorified and chain-level Hochschild
structure is visible only at the level of the full tower.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lllll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Class} & \textbf{Depth} & \textbf{$\Ainf$ corrections} & \textbf{Analytic type} & \textbf{Example} \\
\midrule
G (Gaussian) & 0 & None ($m_k = 0$, $k \ge 3$) & Polynomial & Free field \\
L (Lie) & Finite & Finite truncation & Rational & Kac--Moody \\
C (Calabi) & Finite & Charge conservation kills higher & Convergent & Fermionic $bc$ \\
M (Mock) & $\infty$ & All $\delta^{(k)} \neq 0$ & Gevrey-1 (Borel) & Virasoro, $\cW_{1+\infty}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
The shadow invariants $S_k$ are the $\Ainf$ coproduct correction coefficients:
$\Delta^{\Ainf} = \Delta^{\mathrm{Yangian}} + \hbar^2 \delta^{(3)} + \hbar^3 \delta^{(4)} + \cdots$
with $\delta^{(k)}$ having coefficient $S_k$.

$E_3$ bar cohomology: $\dim H^*(B_{E_3}(A)) = (1+t)^{3g}$ for classes L and C;
fails for class M, where the infinite shadow depth obstructs K\"unneth
collapse.

\section{Three centers}
\label{sec:notation-centers}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Object} & \textbf{Definition} & \textbf{Level} \\
\midrule
$\Etwo$-monoidal category $\cC$ & Little 2-disks structure & Categorical \\
Drinfeld center $Z(\cC)$ & Half-braidings on $\cC$ & Categorical \\
Derived center $\Zder(A)$ & Hochschild cochains $\mathrm{CH}^*(A,A)$ & Algebraic \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
The Drinfeld center is the \emph{categorification} of the derived center:
$Z(\Rep^{\Eone}(A)) \simeq \Rep^{\Etwo}(\Zder(A))$.
These are \emph{not} the same object: the bidegree decomposition of
Hochschild cohomology and the failure of Tsygan formality at the chain
level obstruct identification across categorical levels.

\section{Spectral vs.\ worldsheet parameters}
\label{sec:notation-z}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{Context} & \textbf{Behaviour at 0} \\
\midrule
$z_{\mathrm{spec}}$ & Yangian spectral parameter (shift $u \to u - z$) &
  $\Delta_0$ cocommutative; admits antipode \\
$z_{\mathrm{ws}}$ & Worldsheet / OPE insertion coordinate &
  Poles from OPE singularities \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
Setting $z = 0$ in the Drinfeld coproduct $\Delta_z$ removes the spectral
shift (no OPE singularity).  Setting $z \to w$ in the OPE $T(z)T(w)$ produces
poles.  The spectral parameter $z_{\mathrm{spec}}$ and the worldsheet
coordinate $z_{\mathrm{ws}}$ are \emph{different} mathematical objects,
living on distinct spaces; conflation is the source of the adversarial
``$z=0$ singularity'' objection.

\section{Ordered, time-ordered, normally-ordered}
\label{sec:notation-ordering}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Operation} & \textbf{Algebraic structure} \\
\midrule
Labeled-ordered & Combinatorial & $\Eone$ bar $B^{\mathrm{ord}}$ (deconcatenation) & Hopf \\
Time-ordered & Analytic/radial & OPE on $\C$ with radial ordering & Vertex algebra \\
Normally-ordered & Wick & $\mathopen{:} \phi(z)\phi(w) \mathclose{:}$ & Free field \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
Bare ``ordered'' is forbidden: ``labeled-ordered'' (combinatorial, on
configuration space) is not the same as ``time-ordered'' (analytic,
radial) or ``normally-ordered'' (Wick).  Every use must specify which.

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent

obtained at categorified level do not automatically descend to
chain-level Hochschild identifications, and $\hbar^2$-order exactness
for the BKM Serre functor does not imply $\varepsilon^1$-order
exactness.
