\chapter{Compute Engine Catalogue}
\label{app:engines}

This appendix catalogues all 334 computational verification engines in
\texttt{compute/lib/}. Each engine is a standalone Python module that
verifies one or more mathematical claims made in the manuscript.
Together, they provide 30,613 tests (via \texttt{pytest}) constituting
the computational backbone of Volume~III.

\section{Overview}
\label{sec:engine-overview}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lr@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Count} \\
\midrule
Compute engines (\texttt{compute/lib/*.py}) & 334 \\
Test files (\texttt{compute/tests/test\_*.py}) & 369 \\
Total test functions & 30,613 \\
Engines with dedicated tests & 329 \\
Verification scripts (\texttt{compute/scripts/}) & 3 \\
Audit reports (\texttt{compute/audit/}) & 7 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
Engines are grouped into 17 thematic areas reflecting the structure of the
manuscript. The ``Tests'' column counts \texttt{def test\_*} functions in the
corresponding test file(s). Throughout this catalogue, ``Stage-1 invariant''
refers to a quantity that depends only on $\PhiFA_d(\cC)$, the $E_d$-holo\-morphic
factorisation algebra on the CY$_d$ target $X$, while ``Stage-2 shadow''
refers to a quantity extracted via $\SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}$, the
specialisation to a chiral algebra on the reference curve $C$. The two
stages of $\Phi_d = \SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1},C} \circ \PhiFA_d$ are verified
by independent families of engines.
