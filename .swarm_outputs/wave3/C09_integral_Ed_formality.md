# Agent C09 — Integral $E_d$-formality at $d \geq 3$

## Terminal state
C (FRONTIER DECLARATION)

## Statement of the theorem (or frontier declaration)

\begin{frontier}[Integral $E_d$-formality at $d \geq 3$]\ClaimStatusOpen
\label{frontier:integral-Ed-formality}

Let $\mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{top}}$ denote the (topological) little
$d$-discs operad in spaces, with singular-chain operad
$C_*(\mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{top}}; \Z)$ over the integers, and let
$\mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{alg}}$ denote the algebraic $E_d$-operad
obtained as the Koszul resolution of the Gerstenhaber operad of
degree $1 - d$ (Tamarkin's $\mathcal{D}_d$-operad; Fresse 2017
\emph{Homotopy of Operads \& Grothendieck--Teichm\"uller Groups},
Vol.~I Ch.~4, Vol.~II Ch.~11). Over $\Q$ these two operads are
quasi-isomorphic by the Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality theorem
(Kontsevich 1999 Lett.\ Math.\ Phys.\ 48 Thm.~1 for $d = 2$;
Tamarkin 2003 \emph{Formality of chain operad of little discs},
Lett.\ Math.\ Phys.\ 66, for all $d \geq 2$; parametrised over a
$\mathrm{GRT}_1(\Q)$-torsor of associators, Willwacher 2014
Invent.\ Math.\ 200 Thm.~1.1, $\mathfrak{grt}_1 \simeq H^0(\mathrm{GC}_2)$).

It is \emph{open} whether
\[
 C_*(\mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{top}}; \Z)
 \;\simeq\;
 \mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{alg}}
\]
in the homotopy category of operads in $\Z$-chain complexes, for
$d \geq 3$. Equivalently, the Kontsevich--Tamarkin quasi-isomorphism
is not known to be definable over $\Z$ at $d \geq 3$: the rational
quasi-isomorphism does not extend to an integral one, and the
obstruction lives in the torsion of $\pi_*(\mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{top}})$,
starting at the $2$-torsion of $\pi_{d+2}(\mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{top}})
\otimes \Z/2 \simeq \pi_{d+2}(S^{d-1}\text{-Br}) \otimes \Z/2$, which
at $d = 3$ contains the Hopf-invariant $\Z/2 \subset \pi_5(S^2)$.

Consequence for the two-stage factorisation
$\Phi_d = \SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1}, C} \circ \PhiFA_d$ of the CY-to-chiral
functor (Theorem~\ref{gel:thm:stage1-canonical-scope}): at $d \geq 3$,
the Stage-$1$ object $\PhiFA_d(\mathcal{C})$ is defined up to
rational quasi-isomorphism and up to a $\mathrm{GRT}_1(\Q)$-torsor,
but \emph{strict chain-level identification} of the topological
$E_d^{\mathrm{hol}}$-algebra of holomorphic Chern--Simons observables
(Costello--Li 2016 \emph{Quantization of open-closed BCOV theory, I},
\texttt{arXiv:1606.00365}) with the algebraic $E_d$-algebra of the
Gerstenhaber bracket of degree $1 - d$ on $\HH^\bullet(\mathcal{C})$
is not available over $\Z$.
\end{frontier}

## Primary-source gap

Three extensions are the candidates for closing the item. Any one
would suffice; none is presently established.

\textbf{(G1) Extension of Fresse 2017 Vol.~I Thm.~12.3.A to integral
coefficients.}

Fresse's Thm.~12.3.A (\emph{Homotopy of Operads \&
Grothendieck--Teichm\"uller Groups}, Vol.~I, Part III, Ch.~12;
specifically the statement on p.~329, \emph{``the pro-unipotent
Grothendieck--Teichm\"uller group acts simply transitively on the
set of rational associators''}) establishes Kontsevich--Tamarkin
formality as a $\mathrm{GRT}_1(\Q)$-torsor over $\Q$. The integral
statement one needs is:

\emph{Does there exist a model structure on
$\mathrm{Op}(\mathrm{Ch}(\Z))$ (operads in integral chain complexes)
and a quasi-isomorphism
$\mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{alg}} \to C_*(\mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{top}}; \Z)$
compatible with the $\Q$-tensorisation being Kontsevich--Tamarkin?}

This is not contained in Fresse 2017 as published; Vol.~II Ch.~11.1
explicitly works over $\Q$ and flags the integral case as beyond
scope. The extension would require (i) an integral graph-complex model
$\mathrm{GC}_d^{\Z}$ whose $H^0(\mathrm{GC}_d^\Z \otimes \Q) = \mathfrak{grt}_1$,
and (ii) control of the $\Z$-torsion in $H^*(\mathrm{GC}_d^{\Z})$
contributing to the automorphism group of $\mathcal{E}_d$ over $\Z$.

\textbf{(G2) Torsion computation in the graph-complex automorphism group,
or equivalently in the homotopy groups of the little-discs operad.}

The operad $\mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{top}}$ has homotopy type
$\mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{top}}(n) \simeq \mathrm{Conf}_n(\R^d)$ with
$\mathrm{Conf}_n(\R^d) \simeq S^{d-1} * \cdots * S^{d-1}$ ($n-1$
copies joined) up to homotopy (Salvatore--Wahl 2003
\emph{Framed discs operads and Batalin--Vilkovisky algebras},
Q.\ J.\ Math.~54). The torsion in
$\pi_*(\mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{top}})$ controls the obstruction to
integral formality: at $d = 3$ the first obstruction class lives in
$\pi_{5}(\mathcal{E}_3^{\mathrm{top}}(2)) \otimes \Z/2 \subset
\pi_5(S^2) \otimes \Z/2 = \Z/2$, generated by the Hopf invariant
$\eta \circ \eta$ (Toda 1962 \emph{Composition Methods in Homotopy
Groups of Spheres}, Ch.~V Table).

The precise integral-formality obstruction class has been computed
rationally — it is the Willwacher $\mathfrak{grt}_1$-action on
formality quasi-isomorphisms — but not integrally. The following
conjecture is expected but unproven:

\emph{At $d = 3$, the $2$-primary component of the integral-formality
obstruction of $\mathcal{E}_3$ is controlled by the Hopf-invariant
$\Z/2$ in $\pi_5(S^2)$ composed with the Yoneda action on
$H^*(\mathrm{GC}_3^\Z)$, and is non-zero.}

Establishing non-triviality at a named CY$_3$ category (e.g.\
local~$\P^2$, where $m_3 \neq 0$ so non-formality is witnessed at the
$\Q$-level) would close the question in the affirmative; establishing
triviality on some restricted class (e.g.\ K3-fibred CY$_3$ where the
Atiyah class triple-cup $Y_3$ vanishes in the K3 direction) would
partially close it in the negative direction.

\textbf{(G3) Tamarkin--Willwacher integral uniqueness theorem.}

Tamarkin's 2003 proof (Lett.\ Math.\ Phys.~66, \emph{Formality of
chain operad of little discs}) works over $\Q$ because it uses the
Drinfeld associator, which is a formal power series with coefficients
in $\mathrm{MZV}(\Q)$. Willwacher 2015 Invent.\ Math.\ 200 further
identifies the moduli space of rational formality quasi-isomorphisms
with $B\mathrm{GRT}_1(\Q)$ at the $(\infty,1)$-level.

The integral analogue would require an associator-like object over
$\Z$: either (i) an integral Drinfeld associator with
$\Z$-coefficients (known to \emph{not} exist literally, since
$\zeta(2)/2\pi^2$ is already not in $\Z$ — but a $\Z_{(p)}$-local
version might), or (ii) a proof that at $d \geq 3$ the $E_d$-formality
question, being higher-categorical, admits an \emph{integral} rigidity
theorem bypassing the associator. Neither is available.

\textbf{Why existing machinery is insufficient.}

Each of the three candidate extensions is beyond the present state of
the literature:

\begin{itemize}
\item Fresse's methods (homotopy theory of operads with
$\mathrm{GRT}_1$-action) work over $\Q$ because the action of
$\mathrm{GRT}_1$ is pro-unipotent, hence lifts uniquely along
$K(\mathbb{Q}, n)$ fibrations. Over $\Z$ the same action encounters
torsion in $\pi_*$, and uniqueness up to contractible choice degrades
to uniqueness up to a non-trivial torsion torsor — the integral
Postnikov tower of $B\mathrm{GRT}_1$ is not contractible.
\item Willwacher's graph-complex identification
$\mathfrak{grt}_1 \simeq H^0(\mathrm{GC}_2)$ (Willwacher 2014
\emph{Invent.\ Math.} 200, Thm.~1.1) is inherently a $\Q$-statement
because the graph complex is $\Q$-linear; the $\Z$-structure on graphs
is combinatorial, but the graph-cohomology classes generating
$\mathfrak{grt}_1$ (the odd wheels, the trivalent theta-graph, etc.)
have $\Q$-rational coefficients whose denominators grow with the order.
The integral graph complex $\mathrm{GC}_2^\Z$ has torsion in its
cohomology and the natural map
$H^*(\mathrm{GC}_2^\Z) \otimes \Q \to H^*(\mathrm{GC}_2)$ is not
injective on torsion.
\item Kontsevich's graph-formality formula (Kontsevich 1999, Thm.~1)
involves configuration-space integrals
$w_\Gamma = \int_{\overline{\mathrm{Conf}}_n(\R^d)} \omega_\Gamma$
of angle forms, which are real numbers of the form
$(2\pi)^{-N} \cdot (\text{polynomial in MZVs})$. These are
individually irrational; the \emph{sum} over graphs at a given order
is $\Q$-rational by Kontsevich's boundary-cancellation argument, but
the integrality question — whether the sum lies in $\Z[\![
\text{MZVs}]\!]$ with $\Z$-denominators — is wide open. This is the
arithmetic side of the integrality problem.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{What would constitute closure.}

Closure (upgrading C $\to$ A) would require \emph{either}:

(C1) Publication of a Fresse-style integral model for
$\mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{alg}}$ at $d = 3$, together with a proof that
the natural comparison to $C_*(\mathcal{E}_3^{\mathrm{top}}; \Z)$ is a
$\Z$-quasi-isomorphism. This would likely require new input on the
arithmetic nature of Kontsevich integrals.

(C2) An explicit torsion computation of the $2$-primary obstruction
$\mathrm{obs}_2 \in H^1(\mathrm{GC}_3^{\Z/2}; \mathrm{Aut}(\mathcal{E}_3))$,
showing either $\mathrm{obs}_2 = 0$ (in which case the $\Q$-formality
lifts to $\Z$ up to the standard Postnikov torsor) or
$\mathrm{obs}_2 \neq 0$ on a named CY$_3$ category (in which case the
frontier is declared definitively open and the Stage-$1$ construction
is provably not integral on that category).

(C3) Closure (upgrading C $\to$ B) would require stating the named
hypothesis: \emph{``assuming the integral Kontsevich--Tamarkin
formality theorem at $d = 3$'' or ``assuming
$H^0(\mathrm{GC}_3^{\Z/2}) = 0$''}. Under such a hypothesis,
$\PhiFA_3$ is canonical up to integral quasi-isomorphism, and the
Bochner--Martinelli propagator's graph weights become integer multiples
of a basis set of configuration-space integrals. This is the
conditional-closure option if the hypothesis should be published.

$C_*(\mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{top}}; \Q) \simeq \mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{alg}}$

$C_*(\mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{top}}; \Z) \simeq \mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{alg}}$

$\pi_5(S^2) \otimes \Z/2$ at $d = 3$.

(Theorem~\ref{thm:phi-two-stage-factorisation}) at the chain level:
the Stage-$1$ object $\PhiFA_d(\mathcal{C})$ is canonical up to
rational quasi-isomorphism and up to a $\mathrm{GRT}_1(\Q)$-torsor
(Theorem~\ref{thm:kt-formality-d3}); strict chain-level
identification of the topological $E_d^{\mathrm{hol}}$-algebra of
holomorphic Chern--Simons observables with the algebraic $E_d$-algebra
of the Gerstenhaber bracket of degree $1 - d$ on $\HH^\bullet(\mathcal{C})$
is not available over $\Z$. Closure would require an extension of
Fresse~$2017$ \emph{Homotopy of Operads \& Grothendieck--Teichm\"uller
Groups}, Vol.~I Thm.~$12.3$.A to integral coefficients, or a direct
computation of the $2$-torsion class
$\mathrm{obs}_2 \in H^1(\mathrm{GC}_3^{\Z/2}; \mathrm{Aut}(\mathcal{E}_3))$.
The rational construction of $\PhiFA_d$ is unaffected; the integral
statement is a sharpening of scope.

## Cross-consistency notes

\textbf{Against Wave 1 A01 (Gelfand two-stage factorisation).}

A01 Theorem T1 (``Stage-1 object-level canonicality'') states the
rational $d \leq 2$ lane as an $(\infty,1)$-functor and the $d \geq 3$
lane as an object-level correspondence with a $\mathrm{GRT}_1(\Q)$-torsor
on morphisms. The integral-formality frontier (this agent's scope) is
the precise integral refinement of the rational $d \geq 3$ case: A01
declares the $\Q$-statement proven (up to the $\mathrm{GRT}_1$-torsor)
and the integral statement \emph{open}; this agent confirms the
integral statement is open and names the three candidate primary-source
extensions for closure. The two declarations harmonise: A01 closes at
rational $(\infty,1)$-correspondence, this agent declares the integral
upgrade a frontier item.

\textbf{Against the manuscript.}

The manuscript \texttt{chapters/theory/quantum\_chiral\_algebras.tex}:1053
already concedes the integral gap explicitly: ``These two $E_3$
structures agree under Kontsevich formality (over $\Q$) but differ at
the chain level.\ldots Whether the two agree integrally (not just
rationally) is open.'' The frontier declaration above propagates this
concession into a standalone frontier item with named primary-source
gaps, ready for inscription into the residual-frontier section of
\texttt{chapters/theory/hochschild\_calculus.tex} (§~on
Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality, after Theorem~\ref{thm:kt-formality-d3}).

\textbf{Against the Wave 2 refinement three-tier stratification.}

Tier III of the residual-frontier stratification
(\texttt{notes/platonic\_synthesis\_wave2\_refinement.tex}:846--856,
Theorem~\ref{wn:thm:second-pass-frontier-tiers}) explicitly lists
\emph{Integral $E_d$-formality at $d \geq 3$} as the first Tier III
item (line 848). Tier III is defined as ``loose (new machinery
required)''; this agent's declaration concretises what new machinery is
required: an integral extension of Fresse's torsor theorem, a torsion
computation on $\pi_5(S^2) \otimes \Z/2$ at $d = 3$, or an integral
Willwacher-style graph-complex rigidity theorem.

\textbf{Against AP-CY discipline.}

No violation. The scope is correctly separated by lane:
\begin{itemize}
\item Chain-level lane: the integral identification of the
$E_d^{\mathrm{hol}}$-algebra of hCS with the algebraic $E_d$-algebra of
the Gerstenhaber bracket is open.
\item $(\infty,1)$-categorical lane: the rational
$(\infty,1)$-correspondence is available (Gelfand A01 Theorem T1); the
integral $(\infty,1)$-statement inherits the chain-level obstruction.
\end{itemize}
Both lanes are named; neither subsumes the other (Pattern 236
discipline).

\textbf{Against CoHA treatise.}

The $\C^3$ example in \texttt{notes/CoHA\_to\_W\_infty\_treatise.tex}
computes $\mathrm{CoHA}(\C^3) = Y^+$ at the $\Q$-rational chain level
with shuffle structure function
$g(z) = \prod_i(z + \epsilon_i)/z^3$. The integral-formality question
does not affect the rational identification; it affects only whether
the chain-level $E_3$-structure on $\HH^\bullet(D^b\Coh(\C^3))$
matches the topological $E_3$-structure on the hCS observables
integrally. At $\C^3$ specifically (flat, hence $\mathrm{At}(T_{\C^3}) = 0$)
the $L_\infty$-structure on $\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^3, \wedge^\bullet
T_{\C^3})$ is formal (Kontsevich 1997 graph-complex formality on
$\R^n$), so the rational formality is \emph{absolute} (no
$\mathrm{GRT}_1$-torsor obstruction on objects). The integral-formality
question remains open even on $\C^3$ because the topological
$\pi_*(\mathcal{E}_3^{\mathrm{top}})$ torsion is independent of the
base space.

\textbf{Against the K3$\times E$ crystallisation.}

On $K3 \times E$ the three Atiyah-sourced cocycles
(Theorem~\ref{thm:three-atiyah-cocycles}) are:
$\ell_2 = \mathrm{At}(T_{K3 \times E})$ non-trivial,
$Y_3$ non-trivial (K3 tree-level Yukawa tensored with $dz_E$), and
$\alpha_{\BCOV} = 0$ because $\chi(K3 \times E) = 24 \cdot 0 = 0$.
Rational formality therefore holds on $K3 \times E$ (the Atiyah class
obstruction vanishes at one-loop but not at tree level, yet
cohomological $L_\infty$-minimality still lifts to an $E_3$-algebra
rationally). The integral-formality question is the next-order
refinement: \emph{does the tree-level $Y_3$-deformation of the
$E_3$-structure lift to $\Z$ without torsion defect?} This is not
known.

## Additional notes (closure agent internal, not for manuscript)

The $2$-torsion obstruction class $\mathrm{obs}_2$ is a natural
candidate for direct computation on a specific CY$_3$:

\begin{itemize}
\item On local $\P^2$ (class $\mathbf{M}$), $m_3 \neq 0$ at the
$\Q$-chain level, so non-formality is already witnessed rationally.
The integral $2$-torsion would refine this to a $\Z/2$-valued
invariant of the CY$_3$ category; whether it is non-zero is a
concrete open question.
\item On $K3 \times E$, rational formality holds (per the Kapranov
$L_\infty$-structure restricted to the Künneth-multiplicative
product of K3 and E formality), so the $\Q$-torsor obstruction is
absent. The integral question is whether the topological
$2$-torsion in $\pi_5(S^2)$ nevertheless acts non-trivially on the
Stage-$1$ object $\PhiFA_3(D^b\Coh(K3 \times E))$.
\item On $\C^3$, rational formality is absolute (Kontsevich 1997
$\R^n$-formality); the integral question is the pure topological
$2$-torsion acting on the canonical $E_3^{\mathrm{top}}$-enhancement of
the $L_\infty$-minimal model.
\end{itemize}

The precise integral-formality obstruction class, viewed as a map
\[
 \mathrm{obs}_d \colon \mathrm{CY}_d\text{-cat}
 \;\longrightarrow\;
 H^1(\mathrm{GC}_d^\Z; \mathrm{Aut}(\mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{top}})),
\]
is expected to factor through the categorical structure via the Atiyah
class of the tangent complex; a precise factorisation formula would
close (G2) in both directions.
