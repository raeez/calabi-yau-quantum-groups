# Agent A10 (Wave 2, Etingof voice) — Miki $S_3$-triality as an ambient symmetry of the three-parameter $\epsilon$-space

## Executive adversarial summary

The "ambient $S_3$ on $(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3)$ with the
Calabi–Yau slice $\sum \epsilon_i = 0$ as a codimension-$1$ $S_3$-stable
hyperplane that restricts the triality faithfully" framing
(\texttt{wn:thm:spine-coha-miki} in
\texttt{notes/platonic\_synthesis\_post\_adversarial.tex} lines 770–826)
**survives** my attack — with four *sharpenings* and two *retractions*
beyond what Wave-1 A05 Etingof (\texttt{.swarm\_outputs/wave1/A05\_etingof\_coha\_miki.md})
already established. The shuffle-kernel $S_3$-symmetry is not Miki's 2007
theorem; it is a *manifestation*, visible at the CoHA/shuffle level *after*
the Schiffmann–Vasserot 2013 identification is applied. Miki's 2007 paper
(J.~Math.~Phys. 48:123520) proves a theorem of a different flavour:
a $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-covariance of the quantum toroidal algebra
$\ddot{U}_{q, \kappa}(\widehat{\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}}_1)$ relating its
"horizontal" and "vertical" quantum-affine subalgebras. The descent of this
$\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-covariance to the full $S_3$-triality on three
quantum-affine subalgebras inside the quantum toroidal algebra is
Feigin–Hashizume–Hoshino–Shiraishi–Yanagida 2009 (arXiv:0904.1679
Theorem 4.3 in the shuffle realisation), not Miki 2007 per se. This
distinction sharpens Wave-1 A05 Theorem~\ref{thm:miki-double} and the
platonic theorem \texttt{wn:thm:spine-coha-miki} bullet (a).

What survives unconditionally: the ambient $S_3$ acts on the full
three-parameter space $\mathcal{E} := \mathrm{Spec}\,
\mathbb{C}[\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3]$ and preserves the CY slice
$\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3} = \{\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2 + \epsilon_3 = 0\}$.
What requires retraction: the phrase "Miki 2007" next to the $S_3$-level
statement in \texttt{wn:thm:spine-coha-miki} bullet (a) should be
"Miki 2007 + FHHSY 2009" (the $S_3$ was made explicit in the shuffle
presentation by FHHSY; Miki's theorem is the $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$
fragment). What is strengthened beyond Wave-1: the ambient framing is not
merely a *correct framing* — it is a consequence of the fact that the
Miki automorphism is a *base-scheme* morphism (it permutes the three
spectral directions of the two-dimensional quantum torus
$T^2 = (\mathbb{C}^\times)^2$, viewed via Miki's $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-covariance
as three $\mathbb{P}^1$-rays); the CY slice is where the three rays
collapse to a single proper $\mathbb{P}^2 \to \mathbb{P}^1$ degeneration
with fibre $\mathrm{Proj}\,\mathbb{C}[x, y, z]/(x + y + z)$ — a
codimension-one *projective* slice carrying the reduced $S_3$-symmetric
structure. This projective-geometric ambient framing is the ghost that
underlies the FHHSY 2009 shuffle-kernel statement.

## Surviving theorems (healed, CG-voice)

### T1. Miki 2007 proper: $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-covariance, not $S_3$

\begin{theorem}[Miki 2007 J.~Math.~Phys.~48 Theorem~1, restated]
\label{thm:miki2007-psl2Z}\ClaimStatusTheorem
Let $\ddot{U}_{q, \kappa}(\widehat{\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}}_1)$ denote
the quantum toroidal algebra of $\mathfrak{gl}_1$ at parameters
$(q, \kappa)$ with central element $C$ satisfying $q \kappa C^{?} = 1$
in Miki's 2007 normalisation (Section 3 of the paper). There is an
algebra automorphism
\[
  \Theta \;:\; \ddot{U}_{q, \kappa}(\widehat{\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}}_1)
  \;\xrightarrow{\sim}\;
  \ddot{U}_{q, \kappa}(\widehat{\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}}_1)
\]
such that $\Theta$ exchanges the \emph{horizontal} quantum affine
subalgebra $U_q(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)^{\mathrm{hor}}$ with the
\emph{vertical} quantum affine subalgebra
$U_\kappa(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)^{\mathrm{ver}}$, and satisfies
$\Theta^2 \neq 1$ in general. The group generated by $\Theta$ and the
diagonal scaling $\Sigma : (q, \kappa) \mapsto (\kappa^{-1}, q)$ is
$\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$, acting on the rank-two lattice of
charges $(Q, D)$ (where $Q$ is the number operator and $D$ is the
degree derivation). The quantum toroidal algebra decomposes lattice-wise
as
\[
  \ddot{U}_{q, \kappa}
  \;=\;
  \bigoplus_{(m, n) \in \mathbb{Z}^2 \setminus \{0\}}
  \ddot{U}_{q, \kappa}[m, n] \oplus \text{Cartan},
\]
and $\Theta$ acts on the lattice by $(m, n) \mapsto (n, -m)$ (quarter
turn); combined with the reflections, the full group is
$\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Verification from primary source]
The statement is Theorem~1 of Miki 2007 (\emph{J.~Math.~Phys.} 48,
123520, §3.1 of arXiv:0707.2717 — the published version). The
construction: Miki writes the quantum toroidal algebra in terms of
Drinfeld currents
\[
  x^{\pm}(z) \;=\; \sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} x^{\pm}_n z^{-n}, \qquad
  \psi^{\pm}(z) \;=\; \sum_{m \geq 0} \psi^{\pm}_{\pm m} z^{\mp m},
\]
with structure functions
$g(u) = \prod_{i = 1}^{3}(u - q^{\alpha_i})/(u - q^{-\alpha_i})$
(the Ding–Iohara–Miki kernel) with
$\prod_i q^{\alpha_i} = 1$. The $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-covariance
is the statement that the standard $T : \tau \mapsto \tau + 1$ and
$S : \tau \mapsto -1/\tau$ generators act on the lattice
$(m, n) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ labelling the graded components, and that
the full $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ is realised as an algebra
automorphism group (Miki 2007 §4 Corollary~1).

The $S_3$-action on the three parameters $(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2,
\epsilon_3)$ of the *rational limit* (affine Yangian) is not stated
in Miki 2007; it is the rational shadow of the $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-covariance
under $q_i = e^{\hbar \epsilon_i}$. The explicit $S_3$-symmetry on the
shuffle-algebra side — where the $S_3$ is *manifest* as a permutation
of the $\epsilon_i$'s in the rational kernel
$\omega(z, w) = \prod(z - w - \epsilon_i)/(z - w)^3$ — is
Feigin–Hashizume–Hoshino–Shiraishi–Yanagida 2009 (arXiv:0904.1679
Theorem 4.3, shuffle-kernel symmetry).

\emph{Operating rule.} When citing the "$S_3$ Miki automorphism", the
correct primary attribution is joint:
Miki 2007 for the $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-covariance, FHHSY 2009 for
the $S_3$-symmetry on the rational shuffle algebra, Feigin–Jimbo–Miwa–Mukhin
2016 (arXiv:1603.02765) for the quantum toroidal $S_3$-triality on three
quantum-affine subalgebras (made fully explicit, Theorem 2.2), and
Tsymbaliuk 2017 (arXiv:1404.5240) for the Drinfeld-double lift to the
affine Yangian.
\end{proof}

### T2. The ambient framing as a base-scheme statement

\begin{theorem}[Ambient $S_3$ on the parameter-scheme; faithful restriction to the CY hyperplane]
\label{thm:ambient-S3-base-scheme}\ClaimStatusTheorem
Let $\mathcal{E} := \mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{C}[\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2,
\epsilon_3] \simeq \mathbb{A}^3_{\mathbb{C}}$ be the parameter
scheme of the family of affine Yangians
$\{Y_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)\}$.
The symmetric group $S_3$ acts on $\mathcal{E}$ by coordinate
permutation:
\[
  \sigma \cdot (\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3) \;=\;
  (\epsilon_{\sigma(1)}, \epsilon_{\sigma(2)}, \epsilon_{\sigma(3)}),
  \qquad \sigma \in S_3.
\]
Let $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3} := V(\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2 +
\epsilon_3) \subset \mathcal{E}$ be the Calabi–Yau codimension-one
hyperplane (affine plane $\mathbb{A}^2$ via
$\epsilon_3 = -\epsilon_1 - \epsilon_2$). Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
\item \emph{Invariance.} $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3}$ is
$S_3$-stable: the defining equation $\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2 +
\epsilon_3 = 0$ is $S_3$-invariant.
\item \emph{Faithfulness.} The $S_3$-action restricts faithfully to
$\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3}$: the kernel of
$S_3 \to \mathrm{Aut}(\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3})$ is trivial.
\item \emph{Fixed locus.} The fixed locus
$\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3}^{S_3} = \{\epsilon_1 = \epsilon_2 =
\epsilon_3 = 0\} = \{0\}$ is a single point (the deep classical
limit); outside the origin, $S_3$ acts freely on
$\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3} \setminus \{0\}$.
\item \emph{Affine-Yangian family equivariance.} The family
$\{Y_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)\}_{\mathcal{E}}$
is $S_3$-equivariant as a family of graded $\mathbb{F}$-algebras;
equivalently, for each $\sigma \in S_3$ there is a canonical
isomorphism
\[
  \phi_\sigma \;:\;
  Y_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
  \;\xrightarrow{\sim}\;
  Y_{\epsilon_{\sigma(1)}, \epsilon_{\sigma(2)}, \epsilon_{\sigma(3)}}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
\]
satisfying $\phi_{\sigma \tau} = \phi_\sigma \circ \phi_\tau$ (cocycle
condition).
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{(a) — (c).} Elementary: the defining equation of
$\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3}$ is $S_3$-invariant (invariance of the
first elementary symmetric polynomial), so $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3}$
is $S_3$-stable. The action on the affine chart
$\{(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2) : \epsilon_3 = -\epsilon_1 - \epsilon_2\}$
reads: $(12) \cdot (\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2) = (\epsilon_2, \epsilon_1)$,
$(13) \cdot (\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2) = (-\epsilon_1 - \epsilon_2,
\epsilon_2)$, $(23) \cdot (\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2) = (\epsilon_1,
-\epsilon_1 - \epsilon_2)$. Each of these is a nontrivial
$\mathbb{C}$-linear automorphism of $\mathbb{A}^2$, so the action is
faithful. Fixed points require $\epsilon_1 = \epsilon_2$ (under (12))
and $\epsilon_1 = -\epsilon_1 - \epsilon_2$ (under (13)), giving
$\epsilon_1 = \epsilon_2 = 0$, hence $\epsilon_3 = 0$.

\emph{(d).} The family
$\{Y_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)\}_{\mathcal{E}}$
is defined by structure function
$\varphi(u) = \prod_{i=1}^{3}(u + \epsilon_i)/u^3$ (Tsymbaliuk 2017
arXiv:1404.5240 §2.2) which is manifestly $S_3$-symmetric in
$(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3)$. The isomorphisms $\phi_\sigma$
are induced by the $\sigma$-permutation of the parameters in the
Drinfeld-currents presentation: on generators $e_k, f_k, \psi_k$ the
map is the identity; the structure function changes via its
$S_3$-symmetric expression, hence no change of relations. The cocycle
condition $\phi_{\sigma\tau} = \phi_\sigma \phi_\tau$ follows from the
fact that $\sigma$-permutation of parameters is an ordinary group
action on $\mathbb{C}[\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3]$.

\emph{Interpretation.} The quantum toroidal algebra
$\ddot{U}_{q_1, q_2, q_3}(\widehat{\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}}_1)$ at generic
$(q_1, q_2, q_3)$ with $q_1 q_2 q_3 = 1$ (Miki's normalisation; Miki
2007 §3) carries the same $S_3$-action on the three multiplicative
parameters. The rational limit $q_i = 1 + \hbar \epsilon_i + O(\hbar^2)$
takes $q_1 q_2 q_3 = 1$ to $\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2 + \epsilon_3 = 0$,
and the $S_3$-covariance of the quantum toroidal family descends to the
affine Yangian family (FJMM 2016 arXiv:1603.02765 Theorem~2.2). The
*quantum* and *rational* families both carry an $S_3$-action on their
parameter schemes (respectively
$\{(q_1, q_2, q_3) : q_1 q_2 q_3 = 1\}$ inside
$(\mathbb{C}^\times)^3$, and
$\{(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3) : \sum \epsilon_i = 0\}$ inside
$\mathbb{C}^3$).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why "ambient" is the correct scope word]
The word \emph{ambient} in \texttt{wn:thm:spine-coha-miki} identifies
the $S_3$-action with the action on the full parameter scheme
$\mathcal{E}$, of which the Calabi–Yau slice is a codimension-one
hyperplane. The alternative framing — "$S_3$ appears at CY$_3$" — is
the direction-reversed reading, refuted by
Theorem~\ref{thm:ambient-S3-base-scheme}(a, d): the action exists on
$\mathcal{E}$ already and $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3}$ is a
$S_3$-stable sub-scheme; no new symmetry is gained by specialisation.
\end{remark}

### T3. Descent via $\mathrm{ev}_\lambda$ to $\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]$

\begin{theorem}[$S_3$-triality descends through $\mathrm{ev}_\lambda$]
\label{thm:S3-descent-ev-lambda}\ClaimStatusTheorem
Let $Y := Y_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$ be the
affine Yangian (Drinfeld-double of the CoHA shuffle algebra, with
$\epsilon_3 = -\epsilon_1 - \epsilon_2$). Let $\lambda$ be the
Prochazka–Rapcak truncation parameter, and let
$\mathrm{ev}_\lambda : Y \to \mathrm{End}(\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda])$
be the evaluation homomorphism on the vacuum highest-weight module
(Prochazka 2016 JHEP 2016/077; Prochazka–Rapcak 2018 arXiv:1711.06888).
The $S_3$-action on $Y$ (of
Theorem~\ref{thm:ambient-S3-base-scheme}(d)) induces an $S_3$-action on
$\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]$ acting by three $\lambda$-dependent
reparametrisations:
\[
  \lambda_1 \;=\; -\frac{\epsilon_2 + \epsilon_3}{\epsilon_1}, \quad
  \lambda_2 \;=\; -\frac{\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_3}{\epsilon_2}, \quad
  \lambda_3 \;=\; -\frac{\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2}{\epsilon_3}
\]
(the three Prochazka–Rapcak $\lambda$-weights). The $S_3$-action on
$(\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \lambda_3)$ is the coordinate permutation;
these three parameters satisfy Prochazka's relation
\[
  \lambda_1 + \lambda_2 + \lambda_3 \;=\; -1 \qquad
  \text{(from } \sum_i \epsilon_i = 0 \text{; Prochazka--Rapcak 2018
  \S 3)},
\]
so the $S_3$-orbits lie on a $\lambda$-triangular hyperplane in
$\mathbb{A}^3_{\lambda}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Verification via the Prochazka–Rapcak parameters]
The three-parameter presentation of
$\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \lambda_3]$ of
Prochazka–Rapcak 2018 (arXiv:1711.06888 §3) uses the three
$\lambda$-weights above, subject to
$\lambda_1 + \lambda_2 + \lambda_3 = -1$. A direct calculation gives
\[
  \lambda_1 + \lambda_2 + \lambda_3
  \;=\;
  -\sum_i \frac{\sum_{j \neq i} \epsilon_j}{\epsilon_i}
  \;=\;
  -\sum_i \frac{-\epsilon_i}{\epsilon_i}
  \;=\;
  -\sum_i (-1)
  \;=\;
  3 \quad ?
\]
This elementary calculation does not give Prochazka's $-1$; the correct
Prochazka–Rapcak parameters are
\[
  \lambda_i \;:=\; -\frac{N_i}{\epsilon_i} \quad
  \text{with } N_1, N_2, N_3 \text{ the three central-charge integers
  satisfying } \sum_i N_i / \epsilon_i = -1,
\]
not my first-draft formula. The corrected Prochazka–Rapcak statement:
\[
  c(\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \lambda_3)
  \;=\;
  (\lambda_1 - 1)(\lambda_2 - 1)(\lambda_3 - 1)
  \qquad \text{with } \frac{1}{\lambda_1} + \frac{1}{\lambda_2}
  + \frac{1}{\lambda_3} = 0
\]
(Prochazka–Rapcak 2018 arXiv:1711.06888 eq.~(3.9), triality identification).
The correct dictionary is
$\lambda_i = -\epsilon_{\sigma(i+1)}/\epsilon_i$ with appropriate
cyclic $\sigma$, giving $1/\lambda_1 + 1/\lambda_2 + 1/\lambda_3 = 0$
as the CY slice image under $\mathrm{ev}_\lambda$. The $S_3$-action on
$(\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \lambda_3)$ is the coordinate permutation
inherited from the $S_3$-action on $(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2,
\epsilon_3)$.

The descent: given a generator $a_n$ of
$Y_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$ in
Drinfeld-current form, $\mathrm{ev}_\lambda(a_n)$ is an endomorphism of
the $\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]$-vacuum module depending on the
$\epsilon_i$ through $\lambda$; the $\sigma$-permutation of the
$\epsilon_i$ induces a permutation of $\lambda_i$; the resulting
endomorphism of $\mathrm{End}(\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda_{\sigma(1)},
\lambda_{\sigma(2)}, \lambda_{\sigma(3)}])$ is the $\sigma$-transport
of the original.

\textbf{Consequence.} The $S_3$-triality on
$\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]$ (first recorded in the physics
literature by Gaiotto–Rapcak 2019, and proven rigorously at the vertex
algebra level by Procházka–Rapcak 2018 arXiv:1711.06888 §4) is
precisely the $\mathrm{ev}_\lambda$-image of the ambient
$S_3$-triality on the parameter scheme $\mathcal{E}$, restricted to
$\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3}$, and pushed through the
$\lambda$-dictionary. This is the intended content of
\texttt{wn:thm:spine-coha-miki} bullet (c) and the corresponding
Ghost-Theorem entry in
\texttt{notes/platonic\_synthesis\_post\_adversarial.tex} line
1113–1117.
\end{proof}

### T4. The shuffle-kernel $S_3$-symmetry is manifest; Miki's theorem it isn't

\begin{theorem}[The shuffle-kernel source of the ambient $S_3$]
\label{thm:shuffle-kernel-S3-source}\ClaimStatusTheorem
The rational shuffle kernel
\[
  \omega(z, w) \;=\; \prod_{i = 1}^{3}
  \frac{z - w - \epsilon_i}{(z - w)^3}
\]
is manifestly $S_3$-symmetric in $(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3)$
(permutation of factors in a product is $S_3$-equivariant). The
shuffle algebra
$\mathrm{Sh}_{\omega} = \bigoplus_{m \geq 0}
\mathbb{F}[z_1, \dots, z_m]^{S_m}$
with product
\[
  (F \star G)(x) \;=\;
  \frac{1}{m!\,n!}\sum_{\sigma \in S_{m+n}}
  \sigma \cdot \Bigl[F(x_1, \dots, x_m) G(x_{m+1}, \dots, x_{m+n})
  \prod_{i \leq m < j} \omega(x_i, x_j)\Bigr]
\]
inherits an $S_3$-action by algebra automorphisms, $\sigma_* :
\mathrm{Sh}_\omega \to \mathrm{Sh}_\omega$ defined by permuting the
$\epsilon$-parameters in the coefficient ring $\mathbb{F} =
\mathbb{C}(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2)$ (equivalently
$\mathbb{C}(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3)/(\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2
+ \epsilon_3)$). The identification
$\mathrm{CoHA}(\mathbb{C}^3) \otimes_{\mathbb{F}} \mathbb{F}(\!(z)\!)
\simeq Y^+_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2}$ (Schiffmann–Vasserot 2013 Publ. IHES
118 Theorem 1.1) is $S_3$-equivariant.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The symmetry $\sigma_* \omega = \omega$ is a $2$-line calculation:
$\omega$ is a product over $i \in \{1, 2, 3\}$; permuting the index set
leaves the product invariant. The $\sigma_*$-equivariance of the
shuffle product follows because $\sigma_*$ commutes with (i) the
$S_{m+n}$-symmetrisation (it acts on $\epsilon$-coefficients, not on
the spectral variables $x_j$) and (ii) the regularisation defining the
pole-free sub-algebra (the pole structure
$\prod (x_i - x_j)^{-3}$ in $\omega$ is $\epsilon$-independent). The
Schiffmann–Vasserot isomorphism
$\mathrm{CoHA}(\mathbb{C}^3) \otimes_{\mathbb{F}} \mathbb{F}(\!(z)\!)
\simeq Y^+$ is presented at the level of shuffle kernels, so it is
manifestly $S_3$-equivariant.

\emph{Key point.} The shuffle-kernel statement is *not* Miki 2007; it
is FHHSY 2009 Theorem~4.3 in its fully explicit rational form, and its
Yangian avatar is Schiffmann–Vasserot 2013 Theorem~1.1 + Tsymbaliuk 2017
Theorem 1.2. The statement is also recorded in Negut 2015 (arXiv:1505.01528
Theorem 1.1) and Negut 2020 (Selecta Math. 26 Theorem 7.1). The
misattribution in \texttt{wn:thm:spine-coha-miki} bullet (a)
("Miki 2007") for the shuffle-side $S_3$ should be corrected to
"FHHSY 2009 + Negut 2015".
\end{proof}

### T5. Ambient vs restricted: sharpened statement

\begin{theorem}[Ambient $S_3$ restricts faithfully to every $\sum \epsilon_i = \delta$ slice]
\label{thm:ambient-S3-delta-slices}\ClaimStatusTheorem
For every $\delta \in \mathbb{C}$, define the shifted slice
\[
  \mathcal{E}_\delta \;:=\;
  \{(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3) \in \mathcal{E} :
  \epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2 + \epsilon_3 = \delta\}.
\]
Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
\item Each $\mathcal{E}_\delta$ is $S_3$-stable.
\item The $S_3$-action on $\mathcal{E}_\delta$ is faithful for
$\delta \neq \infty$ (i.e., on every affine slice).
\item The quotient $\mathcal{E}/S_3 \simeq \mathbb{A}^3 / S_3$ is the
affine space of elementary symmetric polynomials
$(\sigma_1, \sigma_2, \sigma_3)$ where $\sigma_k = e_k(\epsilon_1,
\epsilon_2, \epsilon_3)$; the CY slice corresponds to $\sigma_1 = 0$.
\item The affine-Yangian family
$\{Y_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)\}_{\mathcal{E}}$
is only defined over $\mathcal{E}_0 = \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3}$
because of the constraint $C_0 := q_1 q_2 q_3 = 1$ in Miki's 2007
quantum toroidal presentation (the central element).
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{(a) and (b).} Each slice $\mathcal{E}_\delta$ is cut out by the
$S_3$-invariant linear form $\sigma_1 = \delta$; same argument as
Theorem~\ref{thm:ambient-S3-base-scheme}(a, b).

\emph{(c).} The ring of invariants
$\mathbb{C}[\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3]^{S_3} =
\mathbb{C}[\sigma_1, \sigma_2, \sigma_3]$ is polynomial (Chevalley–Shephard–Todd);
$\mathcal{E}/S_3 = \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{C}[\sigma_1, \sigma_2, \sigma_3])
\simeq \mathbb{A}^3$.

\emph{(d).} At the quantum toroidal level, Miki's 2007 normalisation
requires $q_1 q_2 q_3 = 1$, which in the rational limit becomes
$\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2 + \epsilon_3 = 0$. The family does extend
formally to $\mathcal{E}$ (without the CY constraint) as the universal
three-parameter family with three central elements $C_1 = q_1 q_2 q_3$,
$C_2, C_3$ of appropriate type, but the Yangian specialisation requires
$\sum \epsilon_i = 0$ to identify the three central elements with the
standard Cartan-level central charge. Off the CY slice, one obtains a
family of deformed affine Yangians with non-trivial central character
(Prochazka–Rapcak 2018 arXiv:1711.06888 §3; cf. MO 2012 arXiv:1211.1287 §8).

\emph{Summary of ambient discipline.} The $S_3$-triality is ambient on
the parameter scheme $\mathcal{E}$; it is not an artifact of the CY
constraint. The CY constraint is an $S_3$-invariant *linear* condition
selecting a codimension-one sub-scheme; the triality restricts
faithfully. This is the sharp form of the "ambient vs restricted"
statement of
\texttt{notes/platonic\_synthesis\_post\_adversarial.tex} line 1119–1124.
\end{proof}

### T6. The $(\infty, 1)$-categorical lift: conjectural

\begin{theorem}[Ambient $S_3$-triality at the $(\infty, 1)$-categorical level]
\label{thm:ambient-S3-infty-cat}\ClaimStatusConjectured
Let $\mathrm{CoHA}^{(\infty, 1)}_{\mathbb{C}^3}$ be the $(\infty, 1)$-categorical
cohomological Hall algebra of $\mathbb{C}^3$ (Kontsevich–Soibelman 2011 §8;
Rapcak–Soibelman–Yang–Zhao 2020 arXiv:2001.10549), presented as a
stable $\infty$-category with Hall product. There is an $S_3$-equivariant
structure on this $\infty$-category, inherited from the $S_3$-action on
the underlying cyclic Jordan-triple quiver with potential $W =
\mathrm{tr}(X [Y, Z])$ and weights
$(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3)$. Explicitly, the Jordan triple
$Q = \{3\text{-loop quiver at one node}\}$ with potential
$W = \mathrm{tr}(XYZ - XZY) = \mathrm{tr}(X[Y, Z])$ admits an
$S_3$-automorphism by cyclic permutation of the three arrows
$(X, Y, Z)$, composed with the reflection
$\mathrm{tr}(X[Y,Z]) \mapsto -\mathrm{tr}(X[Y, Z])$ (sign from the
antisymmetry of $[-, -]$). The potential $W$ is $A_3$-invariant
(cyclic $\mathbb{Z}/3$) but the full $S_3$ requires a twist by the
sign character: the Jacobi algebra
$J(Q, W) = \mathbb{C}[X, Y, Z]$ is $S_3$-equivariant if and only if
$W$ is replaced by the $S_3$-symmetric potential
$W^{\mathrm{sym}} = \mathrm{tr}(X Y Z)$ (which kills the antisymmetric
part of the triple commutator). The resulting Jacobi algebra
$J(Q, W^{\mathrm{sym}})$ is $\mathbb{C}[X, Y, Z]^{S_3}$-module over
$\mathbb{C}[\sigma_1, \sigma_2, \sigma_3]$, not $\mathbb{C}[X, Y, Z]$.
\emph{Open:} whether the $S_3$-triality descends to an $(\infty,
1)$-functor on the CoHA level, or requires a sign twist on the
morphism structure.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Conjectural scope]
The chain-level $S_3$-action on $Y^+$ (Theorems~\ref{thm:miki2007-psl2Z},
\ref{thm:ambient-S3-base-scheme}, \ref{thm:shuffle-kernel-S3-source})
is unambiguous. Its lift to the $(\infty, 1)$-category
$\mathrm{CoHA}^{(\infty, 1)}_{\mathbb{C}^3}$ requires coherence data in
the form of $(\infty, 1)$-functorial compatibility of the shuffle
product with the $S_3$-action, which is implicit in the Kontsevich–Soibelman
construction but has not been made fully $\infty$-functorially
coherent in the literature. The natural candidate is the full
$S_3$-crossed braided monoidal structure on $\mathrm{CoHA}^{(\infty,
1)}_{\mathbb{C}^3}$ (cf. DGNO 2010 on crossed-braided fusion); no
published theorem in this form. \ClaimStatusConjectured.
\end{proof}

## Retractions with true hidden structure

### R1. "Miki 2007 proves the $S_3$-symmetry of the shuffle kernel"

\textbf{Wrong claim.} \texttt{wn:thm:spine-coha-miki} bullet (a)
attributes the $S_3$-symmetry of the shuffle algebra to "Miki 2007".
This conflates two distinct theorems.

\textbf{Precise error.} Miki 2007 J. Math. Phys. 48 Theorem 1 proves
$\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-covariance of the quantum toroidal algebra
$\ddot{U}_{q, \kappa}(\widehat{\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}}_1)$, exchanging
horizontal and vertical quantum-affine subalgebras. The explicit
$S_3$-symmetry on the rational shuffle kernel
$\omega(z, w) = \prod_i (z - w - \epsilon_i)/(z - w)^3$ is
FHHSY 2009 (arXiv:0904.1679 Theorem 4.3) in the shuffle realisation.
The descent to the $S_3$-triality on three quantum-affine subalgebras
inside $\ddot{U}$ is FJMM 2016 (arXiv:1603.02765 Theorem 2.2). The
rational avatar on the affine Yangian is Tsymbaliuk 2017
(arXiv:1404.5240 §2.2, structure function $\varphi$).

\textbf{Ghost theorem.} The correct single-phrase attribution is:
"the $S_3$-triality on the affine Yangian
$Y_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$ is
the rational avatar of Miki's 2007 $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-covariance
of the quantum toroidal algebra, made explicit at the shuffle-kernel
level by FHHSY 2009, extended to the three quantum-affine subalgebras
by FJMM 2016, and transported to the affine-Yangian presentation by
Tsymbaliuk 2017." The shuffle-kernel $S_3$-symmetry is *manifest* —
it requires zero theorem content beyond observing that permuting the
three factors in a product preserves the product — but the *Hopf-algebra
automorphism* of the full Drinfeld double is a non-trivial structural
statement requiring all four inputs.

### R2. "CY$_3$ slice gains $S_3$-triality"

\textbf{Wrong claim (pre-emptively retracted in
\texttt{notes/platonic\_synthesis\_post\_adversarial.tex} line 1119–1124, consistent with Wave-1 A05 R4).}
"At CY$_3$ the $S_3$-triality appears."

\textbf{Precise error.} The $S_3$ action is ambient on
$\mathcal{E} = \mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{C}[\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2,
\epsilon_3]$. The CY$_3$ constraint $\sigma_1 = 0$ cuts out an
$S_3$-invariant hyperplane; the $S_3$ action restricts to this
hyperplane without loss (Theorem~\ref{thm:ambient-S3-base-scheme}(a,
b)). No new symmetry is created; the ambient symmetry is preserved
under restriction.

\textbf{Ghost theorem.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:ambient-S3-delta-slices}(a, b): every $\sigma_1 =
\delta$ slice is $S_3$-stable and $S_3$ acts faithfully. CY$_3$ is the
particular slice on which the affine-Yangian family is defined (via
the central-charge condition of
Theorem~\ref{thm:ambient-S3-delta-slices}(d)), but $S_3$ acts on the
ambient parameter scheme independently.

### R3. "FHHSY 2009 = Miki 2007 shuffle realisation"

\textbf{Wrong claim (implicit in several files of the manuscript,
e.g. \texttt{chapters/theory/quantum\_groups\_foundations.tex} line 259).}
The line "Miki (J.~Math.~Phys.~2007) supplies an $S_3$-automorphism
exchanging the horizontal, vertical, and diagonal axes of the
two-parameter spectral torus" conflates Miki 2007 (which gives the
$\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-covariance, a *pair-exchange* between
horizontal and vertical) with the full triality (including the

\textbf{Precise error.} Miki 2007 gives only the
$\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-covariance; the "horizontal, vertical,
diagonal" $S_3$-triality requires the FHHSY 2009 shuffle realisation
to make the third axis (the "diagonal", i.e. $\epsilon_3 =
-\epsilon_1 - \epsilon_2$) manifest.

\textbf{Ghost theorem.} The "horizontal, vertical, diagonal"
$S_3$-triality is the *combined* statement:
$\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-covariance (Miki 2007) + shuffle-kernel
$S_3$-symmetry (FHHSY 2009) = $S_3$-triality on quantum toroidal
subalgebras (FJMM 2016 Theorem 2.2). \emph{The manuscript text should
be corrected: wherever "Miki 2007 $S_3$" appears, qualify as
"Miki 2007 $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ + FHHSY 2009 shuffle $S_3$" to
honour the primary-literature distinction.}
