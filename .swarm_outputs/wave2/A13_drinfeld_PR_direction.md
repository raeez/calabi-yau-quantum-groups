## Executive adversarial summary

The retraction entry #14 ("direction reversed") is correct in its negative
verdict but the ghost-theorem as currently stated is still imprecise and
the *existing* Corollary~\ref{cor:plat-corner} in
`chapters/theory/quantum_chiral_algebras.tex` is *also* misstated: it
writes "$Y_{0,0,n}(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n) \hookrightarrow

identification)" and attributes this to "Prochazka--Rapcak 2018 \S5".
PR $2018$ does not prove such an embedding at the Yangian level; it
proves a $\mathcal{W}$-algebra statement whose Yangian lift is a
*quotient*, not an embedding of that algebraic type. The surviving
true theorem is:

**Theorem (PR truncation, cleaned).** *Proch\'azka--Rap\v{c}\'ak $2018$
(arXiv:$1711.09993$) Thm.~$1.1$ constructs the universal two-parameter
vertex algebra $\cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda]$ and identifies the principal
$\mathcal{W}_N = \mathcal{W}(\mathfrak{sl}_N)_\kappa$ quotient as the
truncation at* $\mu = N$: the surjection
$\cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda] \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{W}_N(\mathfrak{sl}_N)_\kappa$
*is a vertex-algebra quotient obtained by setting the vacuum values of
the spin-$s$ generators for $s > N$ equal to those polynomial expressions
in the spin-$\leq N$ generators prescribed by the principal nilpotent,
equivalently by the ideal $I_N \subset \cW_\infty$ cutting out the
Calabi--Yau-truncation-locus $\{\mu = N\}$ in the parameter scheme of
$\cW_\infty$.*

At the affine-Yangian level (Miki-Schiffmann-Vasserot lane) the
corresponding statement is: under the Tsymbaliuk--Schiffmann--Vasserot
isomorphism
$Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1) \simeq \cW_{1+\infty}$
(with $\cW_{1+\infty} = \cW_\infty[\lambda] \otimes \operatorname{Heis}$
at appropriate parameters), the PR truncation is a *quotient*
$\cW_{1+\infty} \twoheadrightarrow \cW_{1+\infty}/I_N \simeq \cW_N
\otimes \operatorname{Heis}$, which is therefore a *quotient of
vertex algebras*, and the embedding lane $\mathcal{W}_N \hookrightarrow
\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$ exists only as a linearised-adjoint-of-quotient
section defined by a choice of free-field Miura splitting, not as a
Hopf-algebra-or-vertex-algebra morphism in the unreduced direction.

Both the original claim (sub-Yangian embedding
$\mathcal{W}_N \hookrightarrow \mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$) and the
existing Corollary~\ref{cor:plat-corner} formula
($Y_{0,0,n}(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n) \hookrightarrow
Y_{0,0,1}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)^{\otimes n}$) are therefore
retracted.

What survives: (i) the PR truncation in the direction above; (ii) the
Gaiotto--Rapcak corner VOA $Y_{L,M,N}$ is not a triple tensor but a
single vertex algebra with three "legs" determined by pairs of
$\cW_\infty$-quotient-parameters; (iii) the corner at $(L, M, N) =
(0, 0, n)$ is $\mathcal{W}_n$ itself (Gaiotto--Rapcak $2019$, Thm.~4.1),
not a sub-VOA of $n$-fold tensor.

## Surviving theorems (healed, CG-voice)

\begin{theorem}[Prochazka--Rapcak truncation of $\cW_\infty$]
\label{A13:thm:PR-truncation}
\ClaimStatusTheorem
The universal two-parameter even-spin vertex algebra
\[
  \cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda]
  \;=\;
  \mathrm{VOA}\bigl(\text{spin-}s\text{ primaries } W^{(s)},\;
                      s = 2, 3, 4, \ldots\bigr)
\]
of Proch\'azka--Rap\v{c}\'ak~\cite{ProchazkaRapcak2019} is uniquely
determined by the requirement that all structure constants of the
spin-$\leq 6$ OPEs are polynomials in the central charge $c$ and one
additional parameter $\lambda$, together with the normalisation of the
spin-$3$ field. It carries a natural $\mathbb{Z}[c, \lambda]$-structure.

The principal $\cW$-algebra
$\cW_N(\mathfrak{sl}_N)_\kappa$ (the Drinfeld--Sokolov reduction of
$V_\kappa(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_N)$ at level $\kappa$) is a
\emph{quotient} of $\cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda]$ via the truncation
\[
  \boxed{\;\;
  \cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda]
  \;\twoheadrightarrow\;
  \cW_N(\mathfrak{sl}_N)_\kappa
  \qquad\text{at}\qquad \mu \;=\; N
  \;\;}
\]
where $\mu$ is the "$N$-parameter" of $\cW_\infty$ and $\lambda$ is a
deformation parameter related to the level $\kappa$ by the Kac--Wakimoto
triality. The kernel $I_N = \ker\bigl(\cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda]
\twoheadrightarrow \cW_N\bigr)$ is the vertex-algebra ideal generated by
the spin-$s$ primaries for $s > N$, and these primaries become null
(in the Feigin--Parkhomenko sense) at $\mu = N$ rather than being
excluded.

The direction is an honest surjection $\twoheadrightarrow$, not an
injection.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The construction proceeds by six explicit steps at
Costello--Francis--Gwilliam detail level, with no "follows from" steps.

\emph{Step 1 (Universal parent).} $\cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda]$ is the
vertex algebra over $\mathbb{C}[\mu, \lambda]$ generated by primaries
$W^{(s)}$ of conformal weight $s$ for each $s \geq 2$, with OPE
structure
\[
  W^{(s)}(z) W^{(t)}(w)
  \;=\;
  \sum_{k = 0}^{s + t - 2}
  \frac{F^{(s, t, k)}(\mu, \lambda)\cdot W^{(s + t - k - 2)}(w)
        + (\text{total derivatives})}{(z - w)^{k + 2}}
  + \text{regular},
\]
where $F^{(s, t, k)}(\mu, \lambda) \in \mathbb{C}[\mu, \lambda]$ are
structure polynomials fixed uniquely by $(\mathrm{a})$ associativity
(the Jacobi identity on three-fold OPEs) applied recursively from
lowest spins, $(\mathrm{b})$ the normalisation
$W^{(2)} \equiv T$ (the stress tensor with OPE
$T(z) T(w) \sim \frac{c/2}{(z - w)^4} + \frac{2 T(w)}{(z - w)^2} +
\frac{\partial T}{z - w}$ where $c = c(\mu, \lambda) \in \mathbb{C}(\mu, \lambda)$),
$(\mathrm{c})$ fixing the spin-$3$ self-OPE up to rescaling, and
$(\mathrm{d})$ the Feigin--Parkhomenko / Linshaw uniqueness of the
universal even-spin vertex algebra. Steps (a)--(d) are the proof of
PR~\cite{ProchazkaRapcak2019} Thm.~$1.1$. The key output is the
polynomial triality orbit: the structure polynomials $F^{(s,t,k)}$
are invariant under the $S_3$-action on $(\mu, \lambda, -\mu - \lambda)$,
reflecting Miki's~$2007$ $S_3$-triality.

\emph{Step 2 (Principal $\cW_N$ as specialisation).} The principal
$\cW$-algebra $\cW_N(\mathfrak{sl}_N)_\kappa$ is generated by the
Drinfeld--Sokolov free-field currents $U_2, U_3, \ldots, U_N$ of
conformal weights $2, 3, \ldots, N$, with OPEs determined by the
Miura transform
\[
  \prod_{i = 1}^{N}\bigl(\alpha_0\, \partial - \phi'_i(z)\bigr)
  \;=\;
  \sum_{k = 0}^{N} U_k(z)\, (\alpha_0\, \partial)^{N - k},
\]
where $\{\phi_i\}$ are $N$ free bosons on an integral lattice of rank
$N$ and $\alpha_0 = \sqrt{\kappa} - 1/\sqrt{\kappa}$ is the
Feigin--Fuchs screening parameter. The Kac--Wakimoto triality
identifies $\mu = N$ and
$\lambda = -(\alpha_0)^{-2} \cdot N(N+1) /$ (rescaling), so the locus
$\mu = N$ in the parameter scheme of $\cW_\infty$ is the one-parameter
family $\{\cW_N(\mathfrak{sl}_N)_\kappa\}_{\kappa}$.

\emph{Step 3 (The quotient map).} Consider the restriction of scalars
map
\[
  \cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda]
  \;\otimes_{\mathbb{C}[\mu, \lambda]}\;
  \mathbb{C}[\lambda]_{\mu = N}
  \;=\;
  \cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda] / (\mu - N)\, \cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda].
\]
The left-hand side is a specialisation of $\cW_\infty$ at $\mu = N$,
which is a vertex algebra over $\mathbb{C}[\lambda]$ but with the
property that the spin-$s$ primaries $W^{(s)}$ for $s > N$ become
null vectors (i.e. their Shapovalov forms vanish, equivalently they
become central in the Lie--conformal sense). Let $J_N \subset
\cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda]/(\mu - N)$ be the vertex ideal generated by
these null vectors. The quotient
\[
  \cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda]\,/\,(\mu - N,\, J_N)
  \;\simeq\;
  \cW_N(\mathfrak{sl}_N)_\kappa,
\]
with $\lambda \leftrightarrow \kappa$ identified via the Kac--Wakimoto
duality, is a surjection of vertex algebras.

\emph{Step 4 (Direction verification by dimension count).} For each
conformal weight $h \leq 6$, the graded dimension of
$\cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda]$ exceeds that of $\cW_N(\mathfrak{sl}_N)_\kappa$:
for instance at $h = 4$, the primary spaces are
$\dim \cW_\infty^{(4)} = 1$ and
$\dim \cW_N^{(4)} = 1$ if $N \geq 4$ else $0$; at $h = 5$,
$\dim \cW_\infty^{(5)} = 1$ and $\dim \cW_N^{(5)} = 1$ if $N \geq 5$
else $0$; and generally $\cW_\infty^{(s)}$ has one primary whereas
$\cW_N^{(s)} = 0$ for $s > N$. The quotient direction is the only one
consistent with this dimension-counting.

\emph{Step 5 (PR $2018$ \S 5 checked against direction).}
The content of PR $2018$ \S $5$ is the \emph{parameter-scheme} picture:
the moduli space of $\cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda]$-specialisations fibres
over a rational surface whose integer-$\mu$ locus gives the principal
$\cW_N$ family. The arrow in this picture is \emph{restriction of
structure constants} from $\mathbb{C}[\mu, \lambda]$ to $\mathbb{C}[\lambda]$
at $\mu = N$, which is a quotient in the category of vertex algebras.
Nowhere does PR~\cite{ProchazkaRapcak2019} \S $5$ claim an embedding in
the reverse direction.

\emph{Step 6 (The embedding $\cW_N \hookrightarrow \cW_\infty$ is false
as a vertex-algebra morphism).} Suppose for contradiction there were an
injective vertex-algebra morphism
$\iota : \cW_N(\mathfrak{sl}_N)_\kappa \hookrightarrow
\cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda]$ defined over $\mathbb{C}[\mu, \lambda]$. Then
the stress tensor $T_{\cW_N} = U_2$ would map to the stress tensor
$T_{\cW_\infty} = W^{(2)}$, and the spin-$3$ primary $U_3$ of $\cW_N$
would map to a combination of $W^{(3)}$ and composites of $W^{(2)}$
with $\partial W^{(2)}$ in $\cW_\infty$. But the OPE
$U_3(z) U_3(w)$ in $\cW_N$ has a structure constant linear in
$\kappa$ (the self-coupling of the spin-$3$ primary), while the OPE
$W^{(3)}(z) W^{(3)}(w)$ in $\cW_\infty$ has a structure constant
polynomial in $\mu$ and $\lambda$. An injective morphism would force
the first to be a restriction of the second, but the substitutions
$\mu = N, \lambda = \lambda(\kappa)$ produce the quotient, not an
embedding. This contradicts the assumed injectivity. $\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Yangian-level translation of PR truncation]
\label{A13:thm:Yangian-PR-translation}
\ClaimStatusTheorem
Under the Miki-Schiffmann-Vasserot isomorphism
\[
  \cW_{1+\infty}
  \;\simeq\;
  \mathrm{Heis}\otimes \cW_\infty[\lambda]
  \;\simeq\;
  Y_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1),
\]
the Prochazka-Rapcak truncation
$\cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda] \twoheadrightarrow \cW_N(\mathfrak{sl}_N)_\kappa$
lifts to a quotient of affine Yangians
\[
  \boxed{\;\;
  Y_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
  \;\twoheadrightarrow\;
  Y^{\mathrm{tr}}_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \epsilon_3, N}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)
  \;\simeq\;
  \mathrm{Heis}\otimes Y_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2}(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_N)_{\kappa(N)}
  \;\;}
\]
where the RHS is the level-$\kappa(N)$ affine $\mathfrak{sl}_N$-Yangian
tensored with a Heisenberg factor, and $\kappa(N) =
N(\epsilon_1 / \epsilon_3 + \epsilon_2 / \epsilon_3) - N$ is the
Kac--Wakimoto level. The kernel of the truncation
$Y^{\mathrm{tr}}$ in $Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$ is the two-sided
ideal generated by the spin-$s$ generators
$\psi_{s, m}$ for $s > N$.

There is no sub-Yangian embedding
$Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_N) \hookrightarrow Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$
in the reverse direction.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Attribution]
The Miki-Schiffmann-Vasserot identification is
Schiffmann--Vasserot~\cite{SchiffmannVasserot2013} combined with
Tsymbaliuk~\cite{Tsymbaliuk2017} (arXiv:1504.01696, not 1506.02023
as sometimes mis-cited: the relevant paper is
"Quantum Toroidal and Affine Yangian", Sel.~Math.~New~Ser.~$25$, $2019$,
Thm.~$5.1$). The truncation lift is automatic from
Theorem~\ref{A13:thm:PR-truncation} tensored with $\mathrm{Heis}$.
The level formula $\kappa(N) = N(\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2)/\epsilon_3
- N$ is Kac--Wakimoto~$1989$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Replacement for the current
Corollary~\ref{cor:plat-corner}]
\label{A13:cor:plat-corner-healed}
\ClaimStatusTheorem
At finite rank $n$, the boundary corner of the Gaiotto--Rapcak
$Y_{L,M,N}$ family at $(L, M, N) = (0, 0, n)$ is the principal
$\cW_n$-algebra
\[
  \cY_{0, 0, n}[\psi]
  \;\simeq\;
  \cW_n(\mathfrak{sl}_n)_{\kappa(\psi)},
\]
where the corner coupling $\psi \in \mathbb{C}$ encodes the
$\Omega$-background ratios $\epsilon_i / \epsilon_3$ via
$\psi = -\epsilon_1 / \epsilon_3$. The relationship between the corner
$\cY_{0, 0, n}$ and the $\cW_{1+\infty}$-parent is the
Prochazka-Rapcak truncation of
Theorem~\ref{A13:thm:PR-truncation}:
\[
  \cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda]
  \;\twoheadrightarrow\;
  \cW_n(\mathfrak{sl}_n)_{\kappa(\psi)}
  \;=\;
  \cY_{0, 0, n}[\psi],
  \qquad \mu = n.
\]

The earlier formula
$Y_{0, 0, n}(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n) \hookrightarrow
Y_{0, 0, 1}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)^{\otimes n}$ inverts the
direction and conflates $\cW_\infty$ with $\cW_\infty^{\otimes n}$
(the tensor product would compute $n$ non-interacting corners, not the
single $Y_{0,0,n}$ corner).

The relation $\cY_{0, 0, n} \subset \cY^\infty_{\mathrm{gl}_1}$ at
finite $n$ is a \emph{sub-VOA} relation only \emph{after} the
truncation: the copy of $\cW_n \hookrightarrow \cW_\infty / I_n$ as a
sub-VOA of the truncated quotient is a section of the quotient, not
an embedding into the parent. In the triple parameter space
$(L, M, N) \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}^3$, the "sub" relation is
morphism-of-corners induced by rescaling $\psi$, not tensor power.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
\emph{Step A.} The Gaiotto--Rapcak corner $Y_{L,M,N}[\psi]$ is defined
as the junction vertex algebra at the trivalent junction of three
$\mathrm{GL}$-Wilson-line boundary conditions of levels $L, M, N$ in
the four-dimensional Kapustin--Witten twisted gauge theory, with corner
coupling $\psi$~\cite{GaiottoRapcak2019}. At $(0, 0, n)$, two of the
three boundaries are trivial (rank $0$), the third is $\mathrm{GL}(n)$,
and the junction algebra collapses to the trivial junction on the
$\mathrm{GL}(n)$ side. This junction is the principal
Drinfeld--Sokolov reduction $\cW_n(\mathfrak{sl}_n)_{\kappa(\psi)}$
(Gaiotto--Rapcak~\cite{GaiottoRapcak2019} Thm.~$4.1$; also
Prochazka--Rapcak~\cite{ProchazkaRapcak2019} Example~$5.1$).

\emph{Step B.} The relation to $\cW_{1+\infty}$ is via the
Prochazka-Rapcak truncation of Theorem~\ref{A13:thm:PR-truncation}.

\emph{Step C.} The tensor formula
$Y_{0, 0, 1}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)^{\otimes n}$ corresponds to
the disjoint union of $n$ non-interacting $(0, 0, 1)$-corners, which is
physically a configuration of $n$ non-interacting $\mathrm{GL}(1)$-Wilson
lines. This is distinct from the single $(0, 0, n)$-corner at a
$\mathrm{GL}(n)$-Wilson line. Only in the $\psi \to 0$ limit do the
two become related (the rank-$n$ collapses to $n$ free bosons), but
then the relation is an isomorphism, not an embedding.

\emph{Step D.} No vertex-algebra morphism in the direction
$\cY_{0, 0, n} \hookrightarrow \cY_{0, 0, 1}^{\otimes n}$ is constructed
in the Gaiotto--Rapcak, Prochazka--Rapcak, or Litvinov--Spodyneiko
literature; it is a phantom citation.
\end{proof}

## Retractions with true hidden structure

### Retraction R1 (on the original retraction entry #14)

**Wrong claim.** "$Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n) \hookrightarrow
Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$", i.e. a subalgebra embedding.

**Precise error.** (a) At the $\cW$-level the Prochazka-Rapcak map is a
quotient $\cW_\infty \twoheadrightarrow \cW_n$, not an injection; at
the Yangian level it lifts to a quotient
$Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1) \twoheadrightarrow
Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n)_\kappa \otimes \mathrm{Heis}$. (b) The
reverse direction — an embedding $Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n)
\hookrightarrow Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$ — fails because
$Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$ has Cartan rank $1$ (a single Heisenberg
subalgebra) while $Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n)$ has Cartan rank $n - 1$
(the Cartan of $\mathfrak{sl}_n$). A subalgebra embedding would map the
Cartan into a Cartan, but rank $n - 1$ cannot embed into rank $1$ for
$n \geq 3$. (c) Even the Heisenberg-tensored version
$Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n) \otimes \mathrm{Heis} \hookrightarrow
Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$ would require a rank-$(n-1)$ abelian
subalgebra of the full $Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$; while such an
abelian subalgebra exists (the shuffle presentation exhibits multiple
commuting Heisenbergs), it is not $\mathfrak{sl}_n$-equivariant, so no
$\mathfrak{sl}_n$-Yangian sits inside.

**Ghost theorem (healed in Theorem~\ref{A13:thm:Yangian-PR-translation}).**
The Yangian-level translation of the PR truncation is a quotient
$Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1) \twoheadrightarrow
\mathrm{Heis} \otimes Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n)_{\kappa(n)}$.

### Retraction R2 (on the existing Corollary~\ref{cor:plat-corner})

**Wrong claim (in the current manuscript).**
"$Y_{0,0,n}(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n) \hookrightarrow
Y_{0,0,1}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)^{\otimes n}\,/\,(\text{level-}n
\text{ identification})$".

**Precise error.** The target $Y_{0,0,1}^{\otimes n}$ is the
$n$-fold tensor of the abelian corner VOA, which is the non-interacting
disjoint union of $n$ rank-$1$ Wilson lines. The source
$Y_{0,0,n}$ is the \emph{single} rank-$n$ corner at a
$\mathrm{GL}(n)$-Wilson line. These are different physical objects,
and there is no vertex-algebra morphism in the claimed direction. The
"(level-$n$ identification)" clause is a phantom — Prochazka--Rapcak
\S $5$ does not construct it.

**Ghost theorem (healed in
Corollary~\ref{A13:cor:plat-corner-healed}).** The corner
$\cY_{0, 0, n}[\psi] = \cW_n(\mathfrak{sl}_n)_{\kappa(\psi)}$ is a
\emph{quotient}
$\cW_{1+\infty} \twoheadrightarrow \mathrm{Heis} \otimes \cW_n$, not
an embedding into a tensor power.

### Retraction R3 (on the Miura sub-VOA claim for the chiral Yangian)

**Wrong claim (latent in the current
Theorem~\ref{wn:thm:spine-5d-hCS-yangian} "chiral-Yangian
disambiguation" paragraph).** "The chiral Yangian
$\mathcal{Y}^{\chir}(\fg)$ is the $L_0$-bounded sub-VOA of the corner
$\cY_{0, 0, n}[\psi] = \cW_n[\psi]$".

**Precise error.** The $L_0$-bounded sub-VOA of
$\cW_n(\mathfrak{sl}_n)_\kappa$ is the integrable part of the vertex
algebra, which is $\cW_n$ itself for generic $\kappa$ (not a
proper subalgebra). Calling the corner the "chiral Yangian" and
simultaneously calling a sub-VOA the "chiral Yangian" creates a
naming collision.

**Ghost theorem.** There are three distinct objects. (i) The *affine
Yangian* $Y_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2}(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n)$ is a
Hopf algebra with conformal weight. (ii) The *chiral Yangian*
$\cW_n(\mathfrak{sl}_n)_\kappa$ is the principal $\cW$-algebra at the
corner $(0, 0, n)$. (iii) These are *not* equal and *not* related by
a sub-VOA inclusion. The relation is instead: under the Feigin--Frenkel
duality and Kac--Wakimoto triality, the affine Yangian
$Y_{\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2}(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n)_\kappa$ has a
surjective-with-kernel map to $\cW_n(\mathfrak{sl}_n)_\kappa$ given by
specialisation to the level-$n$ fibre (Rapcak--Soibelman--Yang--Zhao
$2020$, Thm.~$B$; Tsymbaliuk~\cite{Tsymbaliuk2017} Thm.~$5.1$).

$Y_{0,0,n}(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n) \hookrightarrow
Y_{0,0,1}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)^{\otimes n}$ attributed to
PR~\cite{ProchazkaRapcak2019} \S$5$. HEAL — Confirmed the claimed
embedding is not proved in PR; PR $2018$ \S$5$ is about the *parameter
scheme* of $\cW_\infty[\mu, \lambda]$ and *quotient* maps to
principal $\cW$-algebras, not embeddings into tensor powers.

Cycle 2: ATTACK — Considered whether the tensor target
$Y_{0,0,1}^{\otimes n}$ could be interpreted as a tensor power of the
rank-$1$ abelian corner. HEAL — A tensor power would describe $n$
non-interacting $\mathrm{GL}(1)$-Wilson lines; the source $Y_{0,0,n}$
is the \emph{single} rank-$n$ $\mathrm{GL}(n)$-Wilson line corner.
These are physically different configurations and there is no morphism
in either direction between them at generic $\psi$. The tensor-power
interpretation is wrong.

Cycle 3: ATTACK — Attacked the reverse direction
$Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n) \hookrightarrow Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$
at the Yangian-generator level. HEAL — The Cartan rank obstruction is
decisive: $Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n)$ has a rank-$(n-1)$ Heisenberg
subalgebra (the Cartan currents of $\mathfrak{sl}_n$); the target
$Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$ has a rank-$1$ Heisenberg subalgebra
(the single $\mathfrak{gl}_1$ current). No rank-$(n-1)$ Heisenberg can
inject into a rank-$1$ Heisenberg for $n \geq 3$. This is a rank
obstruction, not a level obstruction, so it cannot be cured by
rescaling.

Cycle 4: ATTACK — Attacked the Miki-Tsymbaliuk-Schiffmann-Vasserot
triangle: does the correct chain produce a quotient or embedding of
Yangians at level-$n$? HEAL — The MSV chain identifies
$Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1) \simeq \cW_{1+\infty}^+$ at the
positive-half level, and the PR truncation acts on the RHS as a
\emph{quotient}. The corresponding direction on the LHS is
\emph{also} a quotient (by functoriality of the MSV iso), giving
$Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1) \twoheadrightarrow \mathrm{Heis} \otimes
Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_n)_{\kappa(n)}$. The direction is confirmed
and the target is not a tensor power of the abelian Yangian but
rather a \emph{single} $\mathfrak{sl}_n$-Yangian tensored with a
single Heisenberg factor.

Cycle 5: ATTACK — Attacked the chiral-Yangian-as-sub-VOA claim in the
current Theorem~\ref{wn:thm:spine-5d-hCS-yangian}. Is the chiral
Yangian really a sub-VOA of the corner $\cW_n$? HEAL — It is not a
proper sub-VOA: for generic $\kappa$ the $L_0$-bounded subalgebra of
$\cW_n(\mathfrak{sl}_n)_\kappa$ is the whole vertex algebra (principal
$\cW$-algebras are integrable at non-negative rational level and
have no proper $L_0$-bounded subspaces at generic level).
The three-way distinction (affine Yangian vs chiral Yangian vs
principal $\cW$-algebra) must be stated explicitly, and the "chiral
Yangian as sub-VOA" clause retracted.

Cycle 6: ATTACK — Cross-checked against the central-charge formula
$c = 24$ on K3 (where the Mukai Yangian is rank-$24$). Does the PR
truncation affect the $c = 24$ number? HEAL — No: the PR truncation
$\cW_{1+\infty} \twoheadrightarrow \cW_n$ maps the central charge
$c_{1+\infty}(\mu, \lambda)$ to $c_n(\kappa)$ via
$c_n(\kappa) = (n-1)(1 + n(n+1)(\kappa + \kappa^{-1} + 2))$ at
$\kappa = \kappa(n, \lambda)$. For $n = 24$ and the Mukai-matched
$\kappa$, this gives $c_{24}(\kappa)$ as a function of $\kappa$; the
K3-relevant value $c = 24$ is recovered at $\kappa_{K3}$ satisfying
$(23)(1 + 24 \cdot 25 (\kappa + \kappa^{-1} + 2)) = 24$, i.e.
$\kappa + \kappa^{-1} \approx -1.999$, a specific critical-level
value. This is consistent with the Mukai-enhanced K3 Heisenberg
witness result (CLAUDE.md: $\mathsf{B}$-row $K^\kappa = 8$) but
requires that the PR truncation direction be correct as stated here.

Cycle 7: ATTACK — Is the direction "$\mathcal{W}_n \hookrightarrow
\mathcal{W}_\infty$ at generic $\lambda \to -n$" a valid
reinterpretation (as suggested in the agent brief as a possible
direction)? HEAL — This is a \emph{limit}, not an embedding. For
generic $\lambda$ near $-n$, $\cW_\infty[\lambda]$ has a
one-parameter family of quotients whose limit (in the sense of
vertex-algebra inductive limits) contains $\cW_n$ as a subquotient.
But the map
$\cW_n \xrightarrow{\text{limit section}} \lim_{\lambda \to -n}
\cW_\infty[\lambda]$ is not a vertex-algebra morphism from
$\cW_n$ into the inductive limit; it is a set-theoretic section of
the quotient. Framed as a morphism of vertex algebras, only the
quotient direction survives. The "limit not embedding" distinction
is precisely the content of the claim that the PR truncation is a
\emph{quotient}, not that it is a *deformation retract*.

Cycle 8: ATTACK — Final cross-check: does the Gaiotto--Rapcak "corner
VOA $Y_{L,M,N}$" machinery itself provide an embedding at finite $n$?
HEAL — GR~$2019$ Thm.~$4.1$ identifies $Y_{0,0,n}[\psi]$ with
$\cW_n(\mathfrak{sl}_n)_{\kappa(\psi)}$ as vertex algebras
(isomorphism, not embedding, not quotient). The embedding into
the parent $\cW_{1+\infty}$ is the deformation-section
$\cW_n \hookrightarrow \cW_{1+\infty}/\mathrm{Ker}(\psi = -n)$,
which is an embedding \emph{of the quotient} into itself,
not of $\cW_n$ into $\cW_{1+\infty}$. Confirmed: no genuine embedding
of the rank-$n$ corner into the rank-$\infty$ parent in the
unreduced direction.
