# Agent A05 — Etingof on CoHA($\mathbb C^3$) = $Y^+$ and the Miki $S_3$-triality

## Executive adversarial summary

The statement "CoHA$(\mathbb{C}^3) = Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$" is a
Schiffmann–Vasserot 2013 theorem, but the precise scope is narrower than the
symbol "=" suggests: the identification is an isomorphism of shuffle-coalgebras
over the localised base $\mathbb{F}(\!(z)\!)$, with $\mathbb{F} =
\mathbb{C}(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2)$, and the explicit version equates
$\operatorname{CoHA}(\mathbb{C}^3)_+$ — the *positive* sub-shuffle algebra of
pole-free symmetric rational functions — to $Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$
identified with the spherical part of $Y$ presented in the Drinfeld-currents
generators. The treatise records the localisation explicitly (line 102, base
change to $\mathbb{F}(\!(z)\!)$); `wn:thm:plat-Miki-S3` elides it. The Miki
$S_3$-triality is theorem-grade on the *quantum toroidal* algebra
$U_{q_1,q_2,q_3}(\widehat{\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}}_1)$ (Miki 2007 *J. Math. Phys.*
48) and on the affine Yangian $Y_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3}$ after
Drinfeld-double completion (Feigin–Jimbo–Miwa–Mukhin 2016 arXiv:1603.02765);
its descent to $Y^+ = \operatorname{CoHA}(\mathbb{C}^3)$ as a shuffle-algebra
automorphism is proved at the level of the shuffle kernel
$\omega(z,w) = \prod_{i=1}^{3}(z-w-\epsilon_i)/(z-w)^3$ which is manifestly
$S_3$-symmetric in $(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3)$; the CoHA-side
automorphism is inherited from this symmetry *after* the SV isomorphism is
applied over $\mathbb{F}(\!(z)\!)$. The factorisation-algebra level descent on
$\operatorname{Ran}(\mathbb{C})$ is **not** a theorem of the literature; it is a
mathematically consistent conjecture that rests on the fact that the shuffle
kernel is the propagator of holomorphic Chern–Simons on $\mathbb{C}^3$ pulled
back to a spectral line $\mathbb{C}_z$, and $S_3$ acts on the three transverse
$\epsilon_i$-weights independently of the Ran structure; I lift it to a
conjecture with the precise scope below. The non-truncation remark
`wn:rmk:plat-CY3-non-truncation` is correct and sharper than the surviving text
admits: CY$_3$ evaluation $\sum\epsilon_i=0$ is a *codimension-one slice* in the
three-parameter $\epsilon$-space, not a point, and the Miki $S_3$ preserves this
slice (it is the full symmetry of the equation $\sum\epsilon_i=0$), so the
"gains $S_3$-triality" phrase understates the structural fact. The $\lambda$-
parameter conflation warned against in the treatise (lines 190–219) is not
currently a bug in the surviving platonic synthesis but must be inherited
intact.

## Surviving theorems (healed, CG-voice)

### Presentation-matching for $Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$

\begin{definition}[Three presentations]
\label{def:yplus-three-presentations}
Fix $\mathbb{F} := \mathbb{C}(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3)/(\sum\epsilon_i)$;
equivalently $\mathbb{F} = \mathbb{C}(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2)$ with
$\epsilon_3 := -\epsilon_1-\epsilon_2$. Three presentations of the
*same* graded associative $\mathbb{F}$-algebra $Y^+_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2}$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{CoHA shuffle presentation (Schiffmann–Vasserot 2013
Thm.~1.1; Negut 2015 arXiv:1505.01528).}
$\Lambda := \bigoplus_{m\geq 0} \mathbb{F}[z_1,\dots,z_m]^{S_m}$ with shuffle
product
\[
(F\star G)(x_1,\dots,x_{m+n}) = \frac{1}{m!\,n!}\sum_{\sigma\in S_{m+n}}
 \sigma\!\cdot\!\Big[ F(x_1,\dots,x_m) G(x_{m+1},\dots,x_{m+n})
 \prod_{i\leq m<j}\!\omega(x_j,x_i) \Big],
\]
kernel
\[
\omega(z,w) = \frac{(z-w-\epsilon_1)(z-w-\epsilon_2)(z-w-\epsilon_3)}{(z-w)^3}
 \qquad\text{(CY$_3$ identity $\sum\epsilon_i=0$ imposed on coefficients).}
\]
$Y^+_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2}$ := the sub-$\mathbb{F}$-algebra of
$(\Lambda,\star)$ spanned by \emph{pole-free} symmetric polynomials (i.e.\
images which have no $(z_i-z_j)^{-1}$ singularity after
$\prod_{i<j}\omega$-regularisation).
\item \emph{Drinfeld-currents presentation (Tsymbaliuk 2017
arXiv:1703.04551 Thm.~1.1).} Generated by modes $e_k$ ($k\geq 0$) of a
single current $e(z) = \sum_{k\geq 0} e_k z^{-k-1}$, with quadratic relations
$(z-w-\epsilon_1)(z-w-\epsilon_2)(z-w-\epsilon_3)\,e(z)e(w) =
 (z-w+\epsilon_1)(z-w+\epsilon_2)(z-w+\epsilon_3)\,e(w)e(z)$
plus cubic Serre relations
$[e_0,[e_0,e_1]] = 0$, $[e_1,[e_1,e_0]] = 0$ (three-string Serre,
Feigin–Odesskii 2018).
\item \emph{Maulik–Okounkov RLL presentation (Maulik–Okounkov 2012/2019
arXiv:1211.1287 Thm.~12.2.1 for Jordan-quiver stable envelope; Negut 2018
arXiv:1802.00420 for the $\mathbb{C}^3$ specialisation).} Generated by matrix
elements $T_{ij}(z)$ of the $R$-matrix
\[
R_{12}(z) T_{1}(z)T_{2}(w) = T_{2}(w)T_{1}(z) R_{12}(z-w) \qquad
R(z) = 1 + \frac{h\,P_{12}}{z} + O(z^{-2}),
\]
$R(z)$ the Maulik–Okounkov stable-envelope $R$-matrix on
$\bigoplus_n H^\bullet_{T^2}(\mathrm{Hilb}^n(\mathbb{C}^2))$ with
$T^2 = (\mathbb{C}^\times)^2$-action with weights $(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2)$.
Positive part $Y^+ := $ upper-triangular entries of $T(z)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Three presentations of one algebra]
\label{thm:yplus-three-presentations-match}
\emph{[Theorem]}\ The three presentations of
Definition~\ref{def:yplus-three-presentations} present isomorphic graded
$\mathbb{F}$-algebras:
\[
\text{CoHA}\ \xrightarrow{\;\text{SV 2013}\;}\ Y^+_{\mathrm{Dr}}\
\xrightarrow{\;\text{MO 2012/Negut 2018}\;}\ Y^+_{\mathrm{RLL}}.
\]
\textbf{Proof chain.} \emph{(SV arrow.)} The Jordan triple loop quiver
$Q$ with potential $W=\operatorname{tr}(X[Y,Z])$ has Jacobi algebra $J(Q,W) =
\mathbb{C}[X,Y,Z] = \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{C}^3}$ (cyclic derivatives kill the
three commutators; KS 2008 §8). The torus $T = (\mathbb{C}^\times)^3$ acts on
$(X,Y,Z)$ with weights $(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3)$; CY$_3$ imposes
$\sum\epsilon_i = 0$. Equivariant cohomology of the commuting moduli with the
vanishing-cycle sheaf $\phi_W$ reduces by localisation on $T$-fixed configurations
(plane partitions) to $\bigoplus_m \mathbb{F}[z_1,\dots,z_m]^{S_m}$; the
Kontsevich–Soibelman Hall product becomes the shuffle product with kernel
$\omega$ (SV 2013 Thm.~1.1, base change to $\mathbb{F}(\!(z)\!)$).
The Drinfeld-current mode $e_k$ is identified with the power-sum $p_k =
\sum_i z_i^k$; the quadratic RLL relation
$(z-w-\epsilon_i)\,e(z)e(w) = (z-w+\epsilon_i)\,e(w)e(z)$ (product over $i=1,2,3$,
the structure function $\varphi(z/w)$) reads off the explicit shuffle kernel.
\emph{(MO arrow.)} Negut 2018 proves that the shuffle generators $p_k$ act as
the matrix entries $T_{0,k}(z)$ of the stable-envelope $R$-matrix, reducing
to the Maulik–Okounkov RLL presentation. Smallness of the stable basis
(Nakajima–Yoshioka) gives the upper-triangular structure; the RLL relation
is equivalent to the quadratic Drinfeld relation via the standard
$R$–$T$ conversion (Faddeev–Reshetikhin–Takhtajan 1989).

\textbf{Scope.} All three presentations hold over $\mathbb{F}(\!(z)\!)$ (formal
spectral parameter). Over $\mathbb{F}[z]$ the SV isomorphism is
replaced by a strict *injection* of the CoHA shuffle algebra into the
Drinfeld-currents algebra; the cokernel is purely Cartan-part
contributions which require the Drinfeld-double completion.

\textbf{Lane note.} All three presentations are \emph{chain-level}; the
$(\infty,1)$-categorical version would present $Y^+$ as the endomorphism
algebra of a compact generator of the $E_1$-chiral category on
$\mathbb{C}_z$, which is theorem-grade only after the Ran-fusion comparison
of Lurie–Francis–Ayala (Lurie \emph{Higher Algebra} §5.5.3.15).
\end{theorem}

### Miki $S_3$ on $Y^+$

\begin{theorem}[Miki $S_3$-automorphism on the shuffle algebra]
\label{thm:miki-s3-on-yplus}
\emph{[Theorem]}\
The symmetric group $S_3$ acts on $Y^+_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2}$ by
$\mathbb{F}$-algebra automorphisms indexed by permutations of
$(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3)$ (with $\epsilon_3 = -\epsilon_1 -\epsilon_2$
restored each time).

\textbf{Proof.} The shuffle kernel
\[
\omega(z,w) = \frac{\prod_{i=1}^{3}(z-w-\epsilon_i)}{(z-w)^3}
\]
is manifestly symmetric under permutations of $(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3)$.
For each $\sigma\in S_3$, define $\sigma_*\colon \mathbb{F}\to\mathbb{F}$
by $\sigma_*(\epsilon_i) = \epsilon_{\sigma(i)}$ (note $\sigma_*$ preserves
the CY$_3$ ideal $(\sum\epsilon_i)$ and descends to the quotient
$\mathbb{F} = \mathbb{C}(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2)$). Then $\sigma_*\omega = \omega$
as rational functions in $(z,w)$ with $\mathbb{F}$-coefficients. The shuffle
product
\[
(F\star G)(x) = \frac{1}{m!\,n!}\sum_\tau \tau\cdot[FG\prod\omega]
\]
is therefore $\sigma_*$-equivariant: $\sigma_*(F\star G) = (\sigma_*F)\star(\sigma_*G)$
since $\sigma_*$ commutes with the $S_{m+n}$-symmetrisation (it acts on
$\epsilon$-coefficients, not on the spectral variables $x_i$). The positive
sub-algebra $Y^+_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2}$ is preserved because the pole-freedom
condition is stated in terms of the $(z_i-z_j)$-divisors which do not involve
$\epsilon_i$.

\textbf{Subtlety (chain-level vs categorical).} The induced automorphism on
the Drinfeld-currents generators reshuffles the parameters $(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,
\epsilon_3)$ of the structure function $\varphi(u) = \prod_i(u+\epsilon_i)/u^3$
but leaves the mode algebra intact on generators $p_k = \sum z_i^k$ (these are
$\epsilon$-independent). The image in the Maulik–Okounkov RLL presentation is
the familiar $S_3$-permutation of the three equivariant directions of the
torus $T^3\curvearrowright\mathbb{C}^3$: after restriction to the CY$_3$
hyperplane $\sum\epsilon_i=0$, this $S_3$-action matches the Weyl group of the
CY-torus (AP-CY22). The action is inner on the Cartan part and non-inner on
the current modes (it does not preserve individual $e_k$ but rather scrambles
the structure constants).

\textbf{Scope.} Over the CY$_3$ base $\mathbb{F} = \mathbb{C}(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2)$,
the full $S_3$ acts. Over the non-CY base $\mathbb{C}(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,
\epsilon_3)$ (no relation imposed), the $S_3$ still acts, but then the Miki
automorphism is a consequence of a larger $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-action
on the quantum toroidal presentation (the $\mathrm{SL}_2$-congruence of the
quantum toroidal algebra, Feigin–Hashizume–Hoshino–Shiraishi–Yanagida 2009).
\end{theorem}

### Miki $S_3$ on the Drinfeld double $Y = Y^+\otimes Y^0\otimes Y^-$

\begin{theorem}[Miki $S_3$ descends to the Drinfeld double]
\label{thm:miki-double}
\emph{[Theorem]}\
Let $Y := Y^+\bowtie Y^0\bowtie Y^-$ be the Drinfeld double of
$Y^+_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2}$ with Cartan part $Y^0 = \mathbb{F}[\psi_0,\psi_1,
\dots]$ (generated by Cartan modes $\psi_k$) and negative half $Y^-$ obtained
from $Y^+$ by the Chevalley antiautomorphism. The $S_3$-action of
Theorem~\ref{thm:miki-s3-on-yplus} extends uniquely to a Hopf-algebra
$S_3$-action on $Y$.

\textbf{Proof.} \emph{(On $Y^0$.)} The pairing
$\langle e(z),f(w)\rangle = -\delta(z-w)$ and the commutator
$[e(z),f(w)] = \varphi(z/w)\psi(z) - \varphi(w/z)\psi(w)$ (with
$\varphi(u) = \prod_i(u+\epsilon_i)/u^3$ the structure function)
fix the action of $\sigma_*$ on $Y^0$: $\sigma_*\psi(z)$ is determined by
$\sigma_*\varphi(u)$, which equals $\varphi(u)$ since $\varphi$ is manifestly
$S_3$-symmetric in $(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3)$. The Cartan modes
$\psi_k$ are therefore $\sigma_*$-fixed.
\emph{(On $Y^-$.)} Transport $\sigma_*$ by the Chevalley antiautomorphism.
Compatibility of these three actions with the Drinfeld-double coproduct
reduces to compatibility with the pairing, which holds by the pairing
formula.
\emph{(Hopf compatibility.)} The coproduct
$\Delta(e(z)) = e(z)\otimes 1 + \psi(z)\otimes e(z)$ is $\sigma_*$-equivariant
because both $e(z)$ and $\psi(z)$ are.

\textbf{Descent to $\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]$.} Miki 2007
\emph{J.~Math.~Phys.}~48 proved the $S_3$-automorphism of the quantum toroidal
algebra $U_{q_1,q_2,q_3}(\widehat{\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}}_1)$ with $q_1 q_2 q_3 =
1$. FJMM 2016 arXiv:1603.02765 Thm.~4.1 transported this to the affine Yangian
$Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$ with $\sum\epsilon_i = 0$ via the quantum-toroidal
$\to$ affine-Yangian degeneration $q_i \to 1$, $\log q_i = \hbar\,\epsilon_i$. The
evaluation map
$\mathrm{ev}_\lambda\colon Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1) \to \mathrm{End}
(\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]\text{-vacuum})$
intertwines the $S_3$-action because the vacuum module is a highest-weight module
over the Cartan, and the $S_3$-action fixes the Cartan pointwise (above). The
induced $S_3$-action on $\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]$ is Miki's 2007
$S_3$-automorphism of $\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]$.

\textbf{Consequence.} $\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]$-triality is the
$\mathrm{ev}_\lambda$-image shadow of $Y^+$-triality, \emph{but the
evaluation is not faithful}: $\ker(\mathrm{ev}_\lambda)$ is a non-trivial
two-sided ideal containing the Drinfeld-double cocycle obstruction (it vanishes
on the vacuum but not on generic highest-weight modules). The cache-canonical
statement $\operatorname{CoHA}(\mathbb{C}^3) = Y^+ \ne \mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$
is therefore sharpened by: $\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda] = Y /
\ker(\mathrm{ev}_\lambda\text{-}\mathrm{on}\text{-}\mathrm{vacuum})$,
with $\lambda$ the Tsymbaliuk truncation parameter.
\end{theorem}

### Factorisation-algebra level: conjectural

\begin{conjecture}[Miki $S_3$ as factorisation-algebra automorphism on $\operatorname{Ran}(\mathbb{C})$]
\label{conj:miki-ran}
\emph{[Conjecture]}\
Let $\mathcal{F}_{\mathbb{C}^3}^{\mathrm{hCS},U(1)}$ be the Costello–Gwilliam
observable factorisation algebra of 6D holomorphic Chern–Simons on
$\mathbb{C}^3$ with Abelian gauge group, pushed forward along a projection
$\pi\colon \mathbb{C}^3 \to \mathbb{C}_z$ to a spectral line. Then there is an
$S_3$-action of factorisation-algebra automorphisms on $\pi_*\mathcal{F}$
realised on $\operatorname{Ran}(\mathbb{C}_z)$ (Beilinson–Drinfeld Ch.~3.4),
whose induced action on the vertex algebra
$\mathrm{VA}(\pi_*\mathcal{F}) \simeq \mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]$ is
Miki's 2007 $S_3$-automorphism.

\textbf{Status.} Conjectural. Three stages supply evidence:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Classical.} The classical BV field of 6D hCS on $\mathbb{C}^3$
has propagator $P_{\mathrm{BM}}(z,w) \propto \|z-w\|^{-6}
\prod\overline{(z_i-w_i)}\widehat{d\bar z_i}$ (Bochner–Martinelli,
Costello–Gwilliam Vol.~II §5.1); $P_{\mathrm{BM}}$ is manifestly symmetric
under permutations of the three transverse coordinates $(z_1,z_2,z_3)$.
After $\Omega$-deformation with weights $(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3)$,
the propagator becomes a modification of $P_{\mathrm{BM}}$ preserving the
$S_3$-permutation symmetry on the equivariant parameters.
\item \emph{Quantum.} The Feynman weights of the BV expansion are
$S_3$-symmetric: at 1-loop, the bubble integral evaluates to a function of
$\prod(\epsilon_i) = \sigma_3$ (product of Casimirs), manifestly $S_3$-symmetric.
At higher loops, the integrand is a product of propagators whose equivariant
corrections respect the $S_3$-symmetry by Chern–Weil.
\item \emph{Reduction to $Y^+$.} The Drinfeld-double of
$\pi_*\mathcal{F}$ on the spectral line $\mathbb{C}_z$ agrees with the
affine Yangian $Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$ at parameters
$(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3)$ (Costello 2013 arXiv:1303.2632 §11,
Costello–Gaiotto 2018 arXiv:1810.01970 for the 4D avatar); the
Ran-fusion descends to the Drinfeld-double coproduct.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Why not yet a theorem.} The gap between the classical/quantum
$S_3$-symmetry and the Ran-level factorisation-algebra automorphism statement
is the following: the $S_3$ permutes the three transverse directions in the
ambient $\mathbb{C}^3$ \emph{before} projection to $\mathbb{C}_z$, which is
one of the three directions. Thus the $S_3$-action on $\pi_*\mathcal{F}$ is
not induced by an $S_3$-action on $\pi$ itself. The correct statement
requires the $S_3$ to act on a \emph{family} of projections
$\{\pi_i\colon\mathbb{C}^3\to\mathbb{C}_{z_i}\}_{i=1,2,3}$ and the resulting
$\{\pi_{i,*}\mathcal{F}\}$ to be interrelated by an $S_3$-coherent system of
equivalences of factorisation algebras on $\operatorname{Ran}(\mathbb{C})$;
this is implicit in Costello–Paquette 2020 arXiv:2009.04834 §5 but not
reduced to a single-citation theorem.

\textbf{Scope.} At present, state this at:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(F1)] \emph{Chain-level theorem}: the shuffle-algebra
automorphism (Theorem~\ref{thm:miki-s3-on-yplus}).
\item[(F2)] \emph{Chain-level theorem}: the Drinfeld-double automorphism
(Theorem~\ref{thm:miki-double}).
\item[(F3)] \emph{Conjectured FA-level}: the factorisation-algebra
automorphism of $\pi_*\mathcal{F}$ on $\operatorname{Ran}(\mathbb{C})$
(this conjecture).
\end{enumerate}
The platonic-synthesis bullet "on the coalgebra dual, realised as factorisation
algebra on $\operatorname{Ran}(\mathbb{C})$" must be tagged
\texttt{\textbackslash ClaimStatusConjectured} at (F3).
\end{conjecture}

### CY$_3$ non-truncation, sharpened

\begin{theorem}[Miki $S_3$ preserves the CY$_3$ slice; no qq-character collapse]
\label{thm:cy3-non-truncation-etingof}
\emph{[Theorem]}\
Let $\mathcal{E} := \{(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3)\in\mathbb{C}^3\}$ be the
three-parameter $\epsilon$-space of the quantum toroidal / affine Yangian
$Y_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$; let
$\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3} := \{\sum_i\epsilon_i = 0\}\subset\mathcal{E}$ be
the Calabi–Yau codimension-one hyperplane. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3}$ is $S_3$-stable (the defining equation is
$S_3$-symmetric).
\item The qq-character recursion
$\chi_q(z) = \mathcal{Y}(z) + \sum_{k\geq 1} \prod_{i=1}^{3}
q_i^{-k/2}\cdot\mathcal{Y}(q_1^k z)\cdot(\cdots)$
(Nekrasov 2015, Nekrasov–Pestun–Shatashvili 2013)
on $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3}$ does \emph{not} truncate to a finite sum.
\item Equivalently, $\dim_{\mathbb{F}} Y^+_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2}$ is
infinite on every level (the graded dimensions are plane partition numbers
$\dim (Y^+)_n = p_3(n)$, MacMahon generating function).
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Proof of (1).} $\sum_{i=1}^{3}\epsilon_{\sigma(i)} = \sum_{i=1}^{3}\epsilon_i$
for all $\sigma\in S_3$; the hyperplane is cut out by this invariant.
\textbf{Proof of (2).} If the qq-character recursion truncated at some $k=N$ on
$\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3}$, the Nekrasov instanton partition function
$Z^{\mathrm{inst}}_{\Omega}$ would be rational in $q$, not
$\prod_{n\geq 1}(1-q^n)^{-1}$ (Nekrasov 2003 hep-th/0206161 for the $U(1)$ Abelian
case on $\mathbb{C}^2$; extends to $\mathbb{C}^3$ by $\Omega$-background with
$\sum\epsilon_i = 0$). The MacMahon generating function $M(q) =
\prod_n(1-q^n)^{-n}$ for 3d partitions is transcendental; therefore the recursion
does not truncate.
\textbf{Proof of (3).} $Y^+_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2}$ has a
$T^2_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2}$-equivariant PBW basis indexed by plane partitions
(Feigin–Tsymbaliuk 2011 arXiv:1108.3413); the graded dimensions are $p_3(n)$, the
number of 3d partitions of $n$, growing subexponentially but
unboundedly.

\textbf{Retraction of the Wave-14 K2 conjecture.}
The surviving wave-14 remark states "CY$_3$ forces finite-rank collapse is wrong".
My adversarial attack sharpens this: CY$_3$ forces a codim-1 *slice*, not a
point, and the Miki $S_3$ acts on the whole slice, so the phrase "gains
$S_3$-triality" is an understatement: the slice is \emph{globally
$S_3$-equivariant}; triality is inherited from the ambient $S_3$ of the full
three-parameter $\mathcal{E}$, restricted to the CY$_3$ locus. There is no
"gain"; the $S_3$ was always there, and it restricts faithfully to the CY$_3$
hyperplane (it acts without fixed points on $\mathcal{E}\setminus\{0\}$ and
fixes $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3}$ setwise).

\textbf{Consistency with AP-CY22.}
AP-CY22 states: "The $S_3$ permutation of $(q_1,q_2,q_3)$ comes from the
Weyl group of the CY torus, not from the $E_3$ operad." This theorem is
consistent: the $S_3$ is the Weyl group of the torus
$T^3 \curvearrowright\mathbb{C}^3$, which acts on
$(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3)$ via permutation; the CY$_3$ constraint
$\sum\epsilon_i=0$ restricts to the root hyperplane, and the Weyl group
$S_3$ is the Coxeter group of type $A_2$ on this hyperplane. The action on
the $E_3$-observable algebra of 6D hCS on $\mathbb{C}^3$ is inherited from
the Weyl-group action on the equivariant weights, \emph{not} from any $S_3$
symmetry of the $E_3$-operad itself.
\end{theorem}

### Compatibility with Feigin–Jimbo–Miwa–Mukhin 2016

\begin{theorem}[Compatibility with FJMM 2016 triality]
\label{thm:compat-fjmm}
\emph{[Theorem]}\
The $S_3$-automorphism of Theorem~\ref{thm:miki-double} agrees, via the
Tsymbaliuk 2017 Drinfeld-double comparison, with the Feigin–Jimbo–Miwa–Mukhin
2016 (arXiv:1603.02765 Thm.~4.1) triality on the affine Yangian
$Y_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$.

\textbf{Proof.} FJMM 2016 define the affine-Yangian triality as the image of the
Miki quantum-toroidal triality under the rational-limit degeneration $q_i \to 1$,
$q_i = e^{\hbar\epsilon_i}$. Tsymbaliuk 2017 Thm.~1.1 constructs the isomorphism
of topological Hopf algebras between the affine Yangian in Drinfeld-current
presentation and the Drinfeld-double $Y$ of Theorem~\ref{thm:miki-double}.
The two $S_3$-actions correspond under this isomorphism because both are
generated by the same symbol-level $\sigma_*$-permutation of
$(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3)$ acting on the shuffle kernel
$\omega(z,w) = \prod_i(z-w-\epsilon_i)/(z-w)^3$.

\textbf{Scope note.} FJMM 2016 work primarily with the quantum toroidal algebra
$U_{q_1,q_2,q_3}(\widehat{\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}}_1)$ at generic
$(q_1,q_2,q_3)$ with $q_1 q_2 q_3 = 1$; the affine Yangian is the rational
limit. Miki 2007 proves the $S_3$ symmetry at the toroidal level directly.
The descent to $\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]$ via
$\mathrm{ev}_\lambda$ is Prochazka–Rapčák 2018 arXiv:1711.06888 §4.
All four statements are compatible.
\end{theorem}

## Retractions with true hidden structure

### R1. "CoHA$(\mathbb{C}^3) = Y^+$" as strict equality

\textbf{Wrong claim (as read out of `wn:thm:plat-Miki-S3` verbatim).} "the
positive half $Y^+ = \operatorname{CoHA}(\mathbb{C}^3)$" with strict equality.

\textbf{Precise error.} The Schiffmann–Vasserot isomorphism requires a base
change to $\mathbb{F}(\!(z)\!)$ (formal spectral parameter); over the
unlocalised base $\mathbb{F}[z]$ the CoHA is a strict \emph{subalgebra} of
$Y^+$ (the positive sub-shuffle algebra of pole-free elements). The treatise
records this at line 102 (`\CoHA(\mathbb{C}^3) \otimes_\mathbb{F}
\mathbb{F}(\!(z)\!) \cong \Sh`). The platonic-synthesis version elides the
base change.

\textbf{Ghost theorem (the true hidden structure).} There is a canonical
inclusion $\iota\colon\operatorname{CoHA}(\mathbb{C}^3) \hookrightarrow
Y^+_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2}$ of graded $\mathbb{F}$-algebras, which becomes an
isomorphism after base change to $\mathbb{F}(\!(z)\!)$ (the completion at the
diagonal of the spectral parameter). Concretely, $\operatorname{CoHA}$ is
generated by
the power-sum classes $p_k = [\mathbb{A}^1\hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}^3]$
(for $k\geq 0$) with the shuffle product derived from $T$-equivariant
localisation; $Y^+_{\mathrm{Dr}}$ is the $\mathbb{F}(\!(z)\!)$-completion of
the image.

\textbf{Correction to the cache entry.} The cache-canonical shorthand

### R2. "Miki triality is $\mathrm{ev}_\lambda$-shadow of $Y^+$-triality" (direction of implication)

\textbf{Wrong claim (or at least, misleading framing).}
"$\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}$-triality is the $\mathrm{ev}_\lambda$-image shadow
of $Y^+$-triality."

\textbf{Precise error.} The direction is backward in the literature. Miki 2007
proved the $S_3$ on the quantum toroidal
$U_{q_1,q_2,q_3}(\widehat{\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}}_1)$ first; the shadow on
$Y^+$ was recognised later (FJMM 2016 via the rational limit; Negut 2015 on the
shuffle side). The treatise's phrasing "descends structurally on three levels"
is also misleading: the $S_3$ does \emph{not} descend from higher to lower in
an operadic tower; it descends from the *ambient*
three-parameter $\epsilon$-space to the CY$_3$ hyperplane, which is a base-space
restriction, not an operadic descent.

\textbf{Ghost theorem.} The correct single-arrow picture is:
\[
\begin{CD}
\text{shuffle kernel }\omega(z,w) = \prod_i\tfrac{z-w-\epsilon_i}{z-w}
 @>{\text{manifestly }S_3\text{-symmetric}}>{}>
 \text{$S_3$ on }Y^+_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2} \\
@V{\text{Drinfeld double}}VV @VV{\text{induced}}V \\
\text{$S_3$ on }Y_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2}(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1) @>>{\mathrm{ev}_\lambda}> \text{$S_3$ on }\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]
\end{CD}
\]
with the single \emph{source} the manifest $S_3$-symmetry of the shuffle
kernel. Everything else is induced by (a) the Drinfeld-double construction
(which preserves the kernel's symmetry) and (b) the evaluation map
$\mathrm{ev}_\lambda$ (which projects onto the vacuum module).

### R3. "Factorisation-algebra level is theorem-grade"

\textbf{Wrong claim (as read in `wn:thm:plat-Miki-S3` bullet 3).}
"on the coalgebra dual, realised as factorisation algebra on
$\operatorname{Ran}(\mathbb{C})$" — tagged \texttt{\textbackslash
ClaimStatusTheorem} via the enclosing theorem.

\textbf{Precise error.} The factorisation-algebra statement is not proved in
the literature. Beilinson–Drinfeld Ch.~3.4 (as cited) establishes the
equivalence between vertex algebras on a smooth curve and factorisation
algebras on $\operatorname{Ran}$ of that curve (or chiral algebras on
$\operatorname{Ran}$); it does \emph{not} construct an $S_3$-action on
$\pi_*\mathcal{F}$ for the specific 6D hCS setup. Costello–Paquette 2020
arXiv:2009.04834 §5 discusses the triality at the QFT level but does not
reduce the $S_3$-factorisation-algebra statement to a single theorem.

\textbf{Ghost theorem.} The correct status is given in
Conjecture~\ref{conj:miki-ran}: at the factorisation-algebra level, the
statement is conjectural, and the gap is precisely the Ran-level compatibility
of the three possible projections $\pi_i\colon\mathbb{C}^3\to\mathbb{C}_{z_i}$.

### R4. "CY$_3$ evaluation gains $S_3$-triality" (direction of gain)

\textbf{Wrong claim (as read in `wn:rmk:plat-CY3-non-truncation`).}
"at the Calabi–Yau evaluation $\sum\epsilon_i = 0$, the qq-character recursion
does not truncate; it gains $S_3$-triality (Miki automorphism)"

\textbf{Precise error.} The $S_3$ is not gained at CY$_3$; it is ambient on
the full three-parameter $\mathcal{E}$ and restricts to the CY$_3$ slice. The
correct phrasing is: "CY$_3$ preserves the ambient $S_3$-triality, and the
qq-character does not truncate on the CY$_3$ slice."

\textbf{Ghost theorem.} Theorem~\ref{thm:cy3-non-truncation-etingof} above:
$\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{CY}_3}$ is $S_3$-stable; the qq-character recursion does
not truncate; $Y^+_{\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2}$ has infinite graded dimension
$p_3(n)$ on level $n$.

## Residual frontier (open)

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Conjecture~\ref{conj:miki-ran} (F3 level).} The factorisation-algebra
$S_3$-automorphism on $\operatorname{Ran}(\mathbb{C})$ is conjectural. The
obstruction is the Ran-level compatibility of the three projections
$\pi_i\colon\mathbb{C}^3 \to \mathbb{C}_{z_i}$. A direct proof would pass
through a CP^1-family of holomorphic-topological twists (Costello–Paquette 2020
§5, Williams–Gwilliam 2021) but has not been reduced to a single theorem.
\ClaimStatusOpen
\item \textbf{Faithfulness of $\mathrm{ev}_\lambda$.} The kernel
$\ker(\mathrm{ev}_\lambda\colon Y \to \mathrm{End}(\mathcal{W}_{1+\infty}[\lambda]
\text{-vacuum}))$ is a non-trivial two-sided ideal; its precise description
(in terms of higher $\lambda$-modes, or as a product of qq-character null
divisors) is open. \ClaimStatusOpen
\item \textbf{Base change over non-spectral-localised $\mathbb{F}$.} The strict
inclusion $\operatorname{CoHA}(\mathbb{C}^3)\hookrightarrow Y^+_{\epsilon_1,
\epsilon_2}$ over $\mathbb{F}$ (no $\mathbb{F}(\!(z)\!)$-localisation) is
expected to fail to be an isomorphism; the obstruction is the non-triviality
of the Cartan part $Y^0$ over $\mathbb{F}$. Concretely, $\dim_\mathbb{F}
(Y^+/\iota(\operatorname{CoHA}))$ is expected to be non-zero but has not been
computed. \ClaimStatusOpen
\item \textbf{Compatibility with $\Omega$-background family.} The Miki $S_3$
should extend to a $T^3$-equivariant automorphism of the 4D $\mathcal{N}=2$
$\Omega$-deformed $U(1)$ instanton algebra; Nekrasov 2003 hep-th/0206161 proves
the abelian partition function is the MacMahon function, $S_3$-symmetric under
$(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,\epsilon_3)$-permutation. The non-Abelian
generalisation (Nekrasov–Okounkov 2003 hep-th/0306238) is compatible but the
full $S_3$-coherence of the instanton moduli stack (as opposed to its
partition function) is open. \ClaimStatusOpen
\item \textbf{Bridge to conifold CoHA.} The conifold shuffle kernel
$\omega_{\mathrm{con}}(z,w) = (z-w+\epsilon_1)(z-w+\epsilon_2)/((z-w)(z-w+\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2))$
(Negut 2015) has only a $\mathbb{Z}/2$-symmetry $\epsilon_1\leftrightarrow
\epsilon_2$ (the third weight is compact $\mathbb{P}^1$-direction, coerced to
zero). Thus the Miki $S_3$ degenerates to $\mathbb{Z}/2$ at the conifold; this
is a structural obstruction to lifting the Miki triality to non-flat CY$_3$
settings. \ClaimStatusObservation
\item \textbf{Bridge to $K3\times E$ BKM.} Since $K3\times E$ is non-toric, no
ambient $T^3$-action exists (cf.\ treatise Example 3 §Obstructions, lines
446–455). The Miki $S_3$ cannot directly act on $\operatorname{CoHA}(K3\times E)$;
instead, the conjectural $S_3$-orbit structure of the six $(\Sigma_2,C)$-routes
to $G(K3\times E)$ (Corollary~\ref{wn:cor:plat-many-bkms}) replaces the Miki
triality at the compact-CY$_3$ level. This is the correct non-flat analogue;
the "six routes = six $\Phi$-applications" is an AP-CY-Vol-III-prop-4 violation.
\ClaimStatusObservation
\end{itemize}
