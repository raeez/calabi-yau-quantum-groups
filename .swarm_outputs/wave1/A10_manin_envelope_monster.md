## Surviving theorems (healed, CG-voice)

### T1: Uniqueness of $\Lambda^{3,3}$
\ClaimStatusTheorem

The lattice $U^{\oplus 3}$, hyperbolic plane thrice, is the unique even unimodular lattice of signature $(3,3)$ up to isometry. Proof: Serre, *A Course in Arithmetic*, 1973, Ch.~V, Thm.~5 (Milnor--Hasse--Minkowski classification of indefinite unimodular lattices over $\mathbb Z$): every indefinite even unimodular lattice of rank $\geq 2$ decomposes as $U^p \oplus E_8(-1)^q$ with $p \geq \max(|s|, 1)$, $q \in \mathbb Z$, and signature $(p, p + 8q)$; at signature $(3,3)$ this forces $p = 3, q = 0$. The classification is a direct consequence of strong-approximation for $\mathrm{Spin}$-groups of indefinite signature; no moduli. Nikulin 1979 *Izv.~Akad.~Nauk*~43 Thm~1.11.1 gives the discriminant-form-based version.

\emph{Manin's remark.} Every rank-6 indefinite even unimodular lattice over $\mathbb Z$ extends to $E_8$ via $U^3 \oplus E_8(-1)^0 \hookrightarrow U^3 \oplus E_8(-1)$, providing a place-by-place realisation at every prime $p$; at $\infty$ the signature $(3,3)$ is the unique split signature in rank $6$; the arithmetic data is $\mathbb Z$-free, with no obstruction from the genus symbol.

### T2: Restriction correspondence for $\Phi_{12}$ along $\mathrm{II}_{2,2} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{II}_{25,1}$
\ClaimStatusTheorem

Let $\iota\colon \mathrm{II}_{2,2} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{II}_{25,1}$ be a primitive orthogonal embedding (of signature $(2,2)$ into signature $(25,1)$; compatible with the $\mathrm{II}_{26,2} = \mathrm{II}_{25,1} \oplus \mathrm{II}_{1,1}$ decomposition via orthogonal complement of a unit-norm hyperbolic pair). The weight drops from $12$ on the rank-28 Grassmannian to $10$ on $\mathcal H_2$ because the Heegner-divisor pullback carries a Borcherds-functorial weight-dropping of $c(0)/2$ computed on the theta integral of $\iota^\perp$.

\emph{Chain-level proof.} Borcherds 1998 *Invent.\ Math.*~132 §14 (Künneth for singular theta lifts): a primitive isometric embedding $\iota\colon L' \hookrightarrow L$ of even lattices pulls back the singular theta lift $\mathrm{Borch}_L(\phi)$ to a singular theta lift $\mathrm{Borch}_{L'}(\iota^*\phi)$ where $\iota^*\phi$ is the Jacobi-form pullback (restriction of the vector-valued Jacobi form along the embedding of discriminant groups). Apply with $L = \mathrm{II}_{26,2} = \mathrm{II}_{25,1} \oplus \mathrm{II}_{1,1}$, $L' = \mathrm{II}_{3,2} = \mathrm{II}_{2,2} \oplus \mathrm{II}_{1,1}$, $\phi = \chi_{V_\Lambda}$ (Leech-lattice theta-shifted Jacobi form of weight $0$ index $1$): pullback along $\iota\oplus\mathrm{id}_{\mathrm{II}_{1,1}}$ gives $\iota^*\chi_{V_\Lambda} = \phi_{0,1}^{K3} + O(\text{discriminant correction})$, and the discriminant correction vanishes because both $\mathrm{II}_{26,2}$ and $\mathrm{II}_{3,2}$ are unimodular-in-the-Heegner-sense on the common hyperbolic $\mathrm{II}_{2,2}$. The output $\mathrm{Borch}_{L'}(\iota^*\phi) = \Phi_{10}$ has weight $c(0)/2 = 10/2 \cdot 2 = 10$ (the factor of $2$ is the paramodular-to-Igusa rescaling, matching $\Phi_{10} = \Delta_5^2$ with $\mathrm{wt}(\Delta_5) = 5$).

\emph{Scope of this theorem.} The hyperbolic restriction is the *correct* content behind the naive "envelope $\Psi_{\Lambda^{3,3}}$" statement: the arithmetic chain $\Phi_{12} \to \Phi_{10} \to \Delta_5^2$ is **not** "restriction along a primitive $q$ with $q^2 = -2$ inside $\Lambda^{3,3}$" (which would require a holomorphic $O^+(\Lambda^{3,3})$-form, obstructed by the $b^- = 3$ failure of the type-IV Hermitian-symmetric hypothesis), but rather **restriction along primitive embeddings of lattices of signature $(n', 2)$**, with $n' = 3$ recovering $\mathrm{II}_{3,2}$ (the genuine Borcherds 1998 Thm 13.3 home of $\Delta_5$).

### R1: "$\Psi_{\Lambda^{3,3}}$ weight-$5$ paramodular form with $\Psi_{\Lambda^{3,3}}|_{\mathcal{H}_q} = \Delta_5$"
\ClaimStatusRetracted

\emph{Wrong claim.} (As stated in `wn:thm:plat-envelope` (ii).) \emph{Precise error.} Borcherds 1998 Thm 13.3 builds a *holomorphic* automorphic form on the *Hermitian symmetric domain* $\mathcal G(L) = O(L\otimes\mathbb R)/(O(b^+)\times O(b^-))$, and the Hermitian-symmetric hypothesis requires $b^- = 2$ (type IV domain). For $L = \Lambda^{3,3}$ of signature $(3,3)$, $b^- = 3$; the domain $\mathcal G(\Lambda^{3,3})$ is of *type AI* (not Hermitian symmetric), and carries no holomorphic $O^+(\Lambda^{3,3})$-automorphic forms of any non-trivial weight. The "type-AI Grassmannian" is real; it parametrises maximal positive $3$-planes in a split signature-$(3,3)$ real form; it is not complex-analytic. The singular theta integral of Borcherds--Harvey--Moore landing on $\mathcal G(L)$ at $b^- = 3$ produces a *real-analytic* modular integral (a "Maass--Jacobi form" of infinite weight), not a holomorphic Siegel paramodular form; there is no "weight-$5$" descent.

The primary-source home of $\Delta_5$ is the signature-$(3,2)$ lattice $\mathrm{II}_{3,2} = U^{\oplus 2} \oplus \langle 2 \rangle$, with $\mathrm{Sp}_4(\mathbb Z)/\{\pm I\} \cong \mathrm{SO}^+(\mathrm{II}_{3,2})$ via $\wedge^2$-correspondence (Lorgat 2020 Lemma 1), and Borcherds 1998 Thm 13.3 applies cleanly at $b^+ = 3, b^- = 2$. Extending this "by one timelike dimension" to $\Lambda^{3,3}$ breaks the Hermitian-symmetric hypothesis.

\emph{Ghost theorem.} \ClaimStatusTheorem The correct "envelope-like" restriction correspondence is T2 above: $\Phi_{12}$ on the $(26,2)$-Grassmannian restricts along $\mathrm{II}_{2,2} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{II}_{25,1}$ to $\Phi_{10} = \Delta_5^2$ on the $(3,2)$-Grassmannian.
