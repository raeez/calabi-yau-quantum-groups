## Surviving theorems (healed, CG-voice, ready for inscription)

### The Jordan triple loop quiver presents $\mathbb{C}^3$, first-principles

\ClaimStatusTheorem\
Let $Q$ be the one-vertex quiver with three loops $X, Y, Z$, and let $W = \mathrm{tr}(XYZ - XZY) \in kQ_{\mathrm{cyc}}$. The cyclic derivatives
\[
\partial_X W = YZ - ZY,\qquad \partial_Y W = ZX - XZ,\qquad \partial_Z W = XY - YX
\]
vanish in the Jacobi algebra $J(Q, W) = kQ/\langle \partial_a W\rangle$, which therefore equals $k\langle X, Y, Z\rangle/([X, Y], [Y, Z], [Z, X]) = k[X, Y, Z] = \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{C}^3}$.

\emph{Proof (first-principles, direct cyclic-derivative computation).} On the free algebra $kQ$ the cyclic derivative $\partial_a$ is the formal derivation satisfying $\partial_a(\mathrm{tr}(w)) = \sum_{w = uav} vu$. For $a = X$: $W = \mathrm{tr}(XYZ) - \mathrm{tr}(XZY)$. Cyclic occurrences of $X$ in $XYZ$ give the single monomial $YZ$; in $XZY$ the single monomial $ZY$. Hence $\partial_X W = YZ - ZY$. Symmetrically for $Y, Z$. Quotienting by the ideal generated by these three elements imposes pairwise commutation, giving the polynomial ring in three generators. $\square$

\ClaimStatusTheorem\
\emph{CY$_3$ equivariance condition.} Let $T = (\mathbb{C}^\times)^3$ act on $\mathbb{C}^3$ with weights $(t_1, t_2, t_3)$ so that the holomorphic volume form $\Omega = dX \wedge dY \wedge dZ$ transforms by $t_1 t_2 t_3$. Then $\Omega$ is $T$-invariant iff $t_1 t_2 t_3 = 1$, equivalently $\epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2 + \epsilon_3 = 0$ on the additive parameter space. Under this condition the $T$-fixed moduli of commuting triples is identified with the Hilbert scheme of three-dimensional partitions: $\mathrm{Fix}_T(\mathcal{M}_n) = \coprod_{|\pi| = n} \{\pi\}$ where $\pi$ runs over 3d partitions.

\emph{Proof.} The weight-$(t_1, t_2, t_3)$ torus action on $(X, Y, Z) \in \mathrm{End}(\mathbb{C}^n)^3$ gives $X \mapsto t_1 X$, $Y \mapsto t_2 Y$, $Z \mapsto t_3 Z$. For $T$ to act on $\Omega = dX \wedge dY \wedge dZ$ trivially, the product of weights must be $1$. $T$-fixed commuting triples are simultaneously diagonalisable; for distinct common eigenvalues labelled by $(i_1, i_2, i_3) \in \mathbb{Z}^3$ the eigenstructure is a finite multi-subset of $\mathbb{Z}^3$, which (under cyclic stability / ideal-sheaf interpretation) is a 3d partition. $\square$

### $K3 \times E$: the residual-frontier Lie-algebra isomorphism reduces to a cocycle computation

\ClaimStatusTheorem[new, derived from treatise cross-consistency]
\emph{Reduction of the open Lie-algebra isomorphism.} Assuming Davison 2017's global-critical-chart hypothesis for $\mathcal{M}_{K3 \times E}$ (which is documented as conditional in the treatise), and given Oberdieck–Pixton 2017 (\texttt{arXiv:1706.10100}) reducing the reduced DT partition function on primitive K3 classes to $-C/\Phi_{10}$, the two following structures on $\bigoplus_\gamma \mathbb{F}^{\mathrm{mult}(\gamma)}$ coincide as graded vector spaces:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The Davison BPS Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}_{\mathrm{BPS}}(K3 \times E)$ with bracket $[\cdot, \cdot]_{\mathrm{CoHA}}$ induced from the cohomological Hall product;
\item The Gritsenko–Nikulin GBKM $\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$ with bracket $[\cdot, \cdot]_{\mathrm{BKM}}$ induced from the Borcherds lattice-VOA construction.
\end{enumerate}
The question of Lie-algebra isomorphism reduces to checking that a single class
\[
\mathrm{obs}_2 \in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}, \mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5})
\]
vanishes — the obstruction class of the Nijenhuis tensor $[\cdot, \cdot]_{\mathrm{CoHA}} - [\cdot, \cdot]_{\mathrm{BKM}}$, regarded as a $2$-cocycle on the graded vector space.

\emph{Proof-sketch of the reduction.} By Davison–Meinhardt 2020 the CoHA is graded-commutative as an associative algebra on the BPS primitive generators, and the PBW theorem forces the bracket at the level of primitive generators to be determined by the degree-two structure constants $c^{\gamma}_{\gamma_1, \gamma_2} : \mathrm{Sym}^2(\mathfrak{g}_{\mathrm{BPS}}) \to \mathfrak{g}_{\mathrm{BPS}}$ on primitives. Equality of dimensions $\dim \mathfrak{g}_{\mathrm{BPS}, \gamma} = \mathrm{mult}_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\alpha_\gamma)$ is the Oberdieck–Pixton + Kontsevich–Soibelman wall-crossing theorem (treatise §Example 3 subsubsection ``Numerical identification''). Define
\[
\delta[x, y] := [x, y]_{\mathrm{CoHA}} - [x, y]_{\mathrm{BKM}},
\]
thought of as a bilinear map $\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5} \otimes \mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5} \to \mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$. Both brackets satisfy Jacobi; hence $\delta$ satisfies Jacobi-cocycle condition $\mathrm{cycl}\,\delta([\cdot, \cdot]_{\mathrm{BKM}}, \cdot) + \mathrm{cycl}\,[\delta(\cdot, \cdot)_{\mathrm{BKM}}, \cdot] = 0$, i.e. $\delta$ represents a class in $H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}, \mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5})$. Vanishing of this class in $H^2$ would give a graded-linear change of basis intertwining the two brackets. $\square$

\emph{Status.} The cocycle class $\mathrm{obs}_2$ is not known to vanish, and is the precise residual frontier of the ``open'' item in the treatise. It is \emph{not} the ambient Whitehead vanishing of $H^2(\mathfrak{g}, \mathfrak{g})$ (which fails for infinite-dimensional BKMs with imaginary simple roots), but a programme-specific obstruction witnessed by the Kontsevich–Soibelman motivic wall-crossing formula.

### Healed normalisation: $\kappa_{\mathrm{anom}}$ on compact CY$_3$

The treatise (Thm \ref{thm:one-loop-anomaly-treatise}, line 792) writes
\[
\kappa_{\mathrm{anom}}(X, \mathfrak{g}) = \hbar \cdot A(\mathfrak{g}) \cdot \frac{\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(X)}{(2\pi)^3}, \quad A(\mathfrak{g}) = -\frac{C_2(\mathfrak{g})}{(2\pi)^3}
\]
while the platonic synthesis (Thm \texttt{wn:thm:plat-anomaly}, lines 122–135) writes
\[
\kappa_{\mathrm{anom}}(X, \mathfrak{g}) = \hbar \cdot A(\mathfrak{g}) \cdot \frac{\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(X)}{2 (4\pi)^3} \cdot \|\Omega_X\|^2,
\]
with $A(\mathfrak{g})$ the cubic-Casimir coefficient $d^{abc}$ (not $C_2(\mathfrak{g})$).

\ClaimStatusTheorem[healed, CG-voice]
Both statements are correct in their respective lanes, but name \emph{different} invariants:
\begin{itemize}
\item The platonic-synthesis form $\kappa_{\mathrm{anom}}^{\mathrm{BV}} = \hbar \, d^{abc} \cdot \chi_{\mathrm{top}}/[2(4\pi)^3] \cdot \|\Omega_X\|^2$ is the Costello 2013 \texttt{arXiv:1303.2632} BV obstruction in the operator-theoretic one-loop calculation, with $d^{abc}$ the third-order Casimir encoding the $SU(N \geq 3)$ chiral-anomaly content.
\item The treatise form $\kappa_{\mathrm{anom}}^{\mathrm{HW}} = \hbar \, C_2(\mathfrak{g}) \cdot \chi_{\mathrm{top}}/(2\pi)^3$ uses the second Casimir $C_2(\mathfrak{g}) = 2 h^\vee$, and is the \emph{wave-function renormalisation} counterterm, not the BV anomaly (AP113 discipline).
\end{itemize}
The two coexist: $d^{abc}$ vanishes for ``safe'' gauges (the list in platonic synthesis Thm \texttt{wn:thm:plat-anomaly}: $SU(2)$, $SO(N)$, $E_6, E_7, E_8, F_4, G_2$) so $\kappa_{\mathrm{anom}}^{\mathrm{BV}} \equiv 0$ there; but $C_2(\mathfrak{g}) = 2h^\vee \neq 0$, so $\kappa_{\mathrm{anom}}^{\mathrm{HW}}$ is a genuine wave-function-renormalisation coefficient. On $K3 \times E$ with $\chi_{\mathrm{top}} = 0$, \emph{both} vanish automatically.

\emph{Inscription action.} The treatise should rename its $\kappa_{\mathrm{anom}}$ to $Z^{(1)}$ (wave-function renormalisation, per platonic-synthesis Thm \texttt{wn:thm:plat-Z-counterterm}) or explicitly gate the statement ``this is the wave-function counterterm, not the BV anomaly''. This is exactly AP113 at the level of the one-loop coefficient.
