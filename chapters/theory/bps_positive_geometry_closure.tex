\noindent\textbf{Question.}
Which positive geometry contains the toric effective chamber as its most
degenerate face and still sees compact non-toric BPS spectra, Hall
wall-crossing, orientation gerbes, automorphic denominators, and
motivic realizations?

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Answer.}
The object is not a polytope, not a fan, and not a list of numerical BPS
invariants.  It is a finite-first oriented motivic Hall cosheaf on the
chamber-sector site.

\section{The finite object}

Let \(X\) be a smooth projective Calabi--Yau threefold or a
quasi-projective toric Calabi--Yau threefold with compactly supported
Hall theory.  A chamber datum is
\[
{\mathfrak I}(X,\sigma,Q,S,o,T_{\mathrm{eq}},\Mot)
 =
(\Perf(X),\Gamma,\langle-,-\rangle,\sigma,Q,S,o,T_{\mathrm{eq}},\Mot),
\]
where \(\Gamma=K_0(\Perf(X))/\!\sim\) is the numerical charge lattice,
\(\sigma=(Z,\mathcal P)\) is a Bridgeland stability condition with
support form \(Q\), \(S\subset\mathbb C^*\) is a strict sector whose
boundary contains no active ray, \(o\) is orientation output for the
\((-1)\)-shifted critical moduli stack, \(T_{\mathrm{eq}}\) is an
equivariant specialization preserving \(Z,Q,S,o\), and \(\Mot\) is a
motivic coefficient target compatible with vanishing cycles and
Thom--Sebastiani.

For \(N,R\geq 0\), set
\[
\Gamma_{S,\leq N,\leq R}
=
\{\gamma\in\Gamma:
   Z(\gamma)\in S,\ h(\gamma)\leq N,\ |Z(\gamma)|\leq R,\ Q(\gamma)\geq0\}.
\]
The support property makes this visible set finite.  A Hall quotient
uses a finite saturated Hall-lower subset
\(L_{\leq N,\leq R}\subset \Gamma_{S,\leq N,\leq R}\), or the saturated
Hall-lower closure of the visible set, with the property that the
complement generates a closed two-sided Hall ideal: whenever a retained
extension class has HN factors, those HN-factor classes are retained.
The finite chamber Hall object is the quotient
\[
\mathcal H^{\Mot,o}_{\sigma,S,\leq N,\leq R}(X)
=
K_0\!\left(
\mathfrak M^{\HN}_{\sigma,S,L_{\leq N,\leq R}}(X),
\phi_{\mathrm{crit}},o;\Mot
\right)
\]
by the closed Hall ideal generated by charges outside
\(L_{\leq N,\leq R}\).  The product is the pull--push Hall
correspondence on short exact triangles, twisted by the orientation
quadratic refinement:
\[
x_\alpha*x_\beta
=
\mathbb L^{-\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle/2}
\epsilon_o(\alpha,\beta)x_{\alpha+\beta},
\qquad
\epsilon_o(\alpha,\beta)
=q_o(\alpha+\beta)q_o(\alpha)q_o(\beta),
\]
with value \(0\) when \(\alpha+\beta\notin L_{\leq N,\leq R}\).

\begin{definition}[Chambered BPS positive geometry]
\ClaimStatusDefinition
\label{def:bps-positive-geometry}
The chambered BPS positive geometry of the realized chamber
\({\mathfrak I}(X,\sigma,Q,S,o,T_{\mathrm{eq}},\Mot)\) is the pro-object
\[
\mathcal P^{\BPS}_{\sigma,S,o,T_{\mathrm{eq}}}(X)
=
\varprojlim_{N,R}
\bigl(
\mathcal H^{\Mot,o}_{\sigma,S,\leq N,\leq R}(X),
\Int^{\Mot},
\Real,
\Dec,
\Enh
\bigr).
\]
Here \(\Int^{\Mot}\) is motivic integration to the completed motivic
quantum torus, \(\Real\) is the compatible tower of Hodge, Euler, and
numerical realizations, \(\Dec=\operatorname{supp}\circ K_0\circ
\Int^{\Mot}\), and \(\Enh\) records the admissible theta, double,
hCS--Hall, and automorphic boundary enhancements.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-first existence]
\label{thm:bps-positive-finite-first-existence}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
For every chamber datum
\({\mathfrak I}(X,\sigma,Q,S,o,T_{\mathrm{eq}},\Mot)\) above, equipped
with a cofinal Mittag--Leffler tower of finite saturated Hall-lower
sets \(L_{\leq N,\leq R}\) and Hall-admissible extension
correspondences, the
finite quotients
\(\mathcal H^{\Mot,o}_{\sigma,S,\leq N,\leq R}(X)\) form an inverse
system of associative Hall algebras with sector descent, motivic
integration, and realization maps.  The inverse limit
\(\mathcal P^{\BPS}_{\sigma,S,o,T_{\mathrm{eq}}}(X)\) exists in
completed motivic Hall cosheaves.  Its decategorification is the
effective BPS support positive geometry
\[
\Dec\bigl(\mathcal P^{\BPS}_{\sigma,S,o,T_{\mathrm{eq}}}(X)\bigr)
=
P^{\BPS,\bullet}_{\sigma,S,o,T_{\mathrm{eq}}}(X).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The support property gives finiteness of \(\Gamma_{S,\leq N,\leq R}\):
inside a bounded central-charge disk, the inequality \(Q(\gamma)\geq0\)
and discreteness of \(\Gamma\) leave finitely many charges.  The
saturated Hall-lower set \(L_{\leq N,\leq R}\) is finite by construction.
The stack
\(\mathfrak M^{\HN}_{\sigma,S,L_{\leq N,\leq R}}\) is the
extension-closed HN hull of the semistable substacks with those charges.
The Hall correspondence
\[
\mathfrak M_{\alpha}\times\mathfrak M_{\beta}
\xleftarrow{\,p\,}
\mathfrak E_{\alpha,\beta}
\xrightarrow{\,q\,}
\mathfrak M_{\alpha+\beta}
\]
is proper on the HN-bounded finite quotient in the Hall direction and
compatible with vanishing cycles by Thom--Sebastiani.  Associativity is
the \(2\)-Segal identity for the stack of flags of length three; the
orientation factor is a quadratic refinement, hence its two triple
products agree.  If a sum leaves the finite charge set, the quotient
ideal sets that term to zero; this is a two-sided Hall ideal by the
saturated Hall-lower hypothesis on \(L_{\leq N,\leq R}\), not by
radius monotonicity alone.

For an ordered sector decomposition \(S=S_1\star\cdots\star S_r\),
HN uniqueness identifies the HN hull in \(S\) with the iterated
extension stack of the HN hulls in the \(S_i\).  Pull--push along this
identification gives
\[
\mathcal H^{\Mot,o}_{\sigma,S,\leq N,\leq R}
\cong
\widehat\bigotimes_i
\mathcal H^{\Mot,o}_{\sigma,S_i,\leq N,\leq R}.
\]
Motivic integration is the Kontsevich--Soibelman integration map with
the same orientation refinement; realization functors preserve
vanishing cycles, Thom--Sebastiani, and finite sums.  Transition maps
for increasing \(N,R\) are quotient maps by closed Hall ideals, so the
completed inverse limit exists and carries the displayed structures.
\end{proof}

\section{Toric terminal degeneration}

\begin{theorem}[Toric effective geometry as terminal degeneration]
\label{thm:bps-positive-toric-terminal}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
If \(X\) is represented by a toric quiver with potential \((Q,W)\), then
\[
\mathcal P^{\BPS}_{\sigma,S,o,T_{\mathrm{eq}}}(X)
=
\widehat{\CoHA}^{\Mot,o}_{\mathrm{crit}}(Q,W)
\]
on the corresponding finite charge monoid.  For \(X=\mathbb A^3\),
decategorification gives the MacMahon/Yangian positive half
\[
\chi\bigl(\widehat{\CoHA}^{\Mot,o}_{\mathrm{crit}}(\mathbb A^3)\bigr)
=
\prod_{n\geq1}(1-q^n)^{-n}
=
\operatorname{ch}Y^+.
\]
Thus the toric effective positive geometry is the terminal degeneration
of \(\mathcal P^{\BPS}\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For a toric critical chart, the moduli problem is the representation
stack of \((Q,W)\); the \((-1)\)-shifted critical atlas is the trace of
the potential, and the Hall correspondence is the usual CoHA
correspondence.  The finite quotient above is exactly the quotient of
the critical CoHA by dimension vectors outside the retained monoid.  In
the one-loop three-dimensional case this is the CoHA of \(\mathbb A^3\).
The topological vertex and plane-partition count give MacMahon, while
the positive half \(Y^+\) has the same graded character.  The finite
oracle checks this equality coefficientwise in every retained quotient.
\end{proof}

\section{Compact non-toric chambers}

\begin{theorem}[Compact non-toric existence in the data-realized class]
\label{thm:bps-positive-compact-nontoric}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
Compact non-toric chambered BPS positive geometries exist.  Bayer,
Macr\`i, and Stellari construct Bridgeland stability conditions with
support property on abelian threefolds and on their Calabi--Yau
threefold quotient examples \cite{BayerMacriStellari2016}.  Together
with the Pantev--To\"en--Vaqui\'e--Vezzosi \((-1)\)-shifted symplectic
structure on derived moduli of objects in a Calabi--Yau threefold
\cite{PTVV2013}, an orientation output \(o\), and a motivic
coefficient target, Theorem~\ref{thm:bps-positive-finite-first-existence}
constructs \(\mathcal P^{\BPS}\) for these compact non-toric chambers.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For an abelian threefold \(A\), \(\Perf(A)\) has Serre functor \([3]\);
the BMS stability condition supplies \(\sigma\) and a support form
\(Q\).  The same is true for the BMS Calabi--Yau quotient examples.
PTVV supplies the \((-1)\)-shifted symplectic derived moduli stack of
objects.  A choice of orientation output replaces the square-root
problem by either an untwisted coefficient system or a gerbe-twisted
one.  These data are precisely the input of
Theorem~\ref{thm:bps-positive-finite-first-existence}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Quintic and Schoen refinement certificates]
\label{prop:bps-positive-quintic-schoen-certificates}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
For the quintic \(X_5\) and for Schoen-type compact models, the exact
named-example theorem is the certificate
\[
\operatorname{ExCert}(X;\sigma,Q,S,o,T_{\mathrm{eq}},\Mot).
\]
For \(X_5\), this certificate is equivalent to supplying actual
Bridgeland/support/HN data compatible with the PTVV critical atlas and

HN-compatible correspondences.  The local banana shadow input contains
\[
S_4^{\mathrm{inst}}=-44,
\qquad
r_{\max}=-1,
\]
and therefore detects an infinite shadow tower rather than a toric

\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The PTVV and HKR inputs for \(X_5\) construct the shifted symplectic and
Hochschild surfaces of the problem; they do not by themselves construct
a Bridgeland chamber.  The definition of \(\operatorname{ExCert}\)
requires exactly that missing stability, support, HN, orientation, and
motivic data.  For a Schoen model with banana local charts, local Hall
data become compact data precisely when the compact-support Hall
correspondences glue and preserve HN sector order.  The finite oracle
records the banana coefficient \(S_4^{\mathrm{inst}}=-44\) and
\(r_{\max}=-1\), so the local calculation cannot be collapsed to the
toric terminal case.
\end{proof}

\section{Igusa boundary and the raw Hall algebra}

\begin{theorem}[Igusa quotient boundary]
\label{thm:bps-positive-igusa-boundary}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
For the \(K3\times E\) boundary, the Igusa paper fixes the automorphic
normalization
\[
\phi_{0,1}=\sum f(n,l)q^nr^l,
\qquad
f(0,0)/2=5,
\qquad
\operatorname{AutBorch}^{\mathrm{den}}(\phi_{0,1})
=
(\Delta_5,\nu_{\Delta_5},64^{-1}\Delta_5(2Z)).
\]
The Hall--Borcherds theorem is the quotient theorem
\[
\CoHA^{\Mot,o}_{\mathrm{crit}}(K3\times E)^{\wedge}_{S}/
\operatorname{Rad}_{\Aut}
\cong
\widehat U(\gDelta^+),
\]
where \(\operatorname{Rad}_{\Aut}\) is the closed Hall ideal invisible
to the automorphic denominator pairing and orientation character.
The unquotiented theorem is equivalent to
\[
\operatorname{Rad}_{\Aut,\leq N,\leq R}=0
\quad\hbox{for every finite quotient }(N,R).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Borcherds' lift and the Gritsenko--Nikulin/Gritsenko normalization
identify the denominator side from the Jacobi input
\(\phi_{0,1}\) \cite{Borcherds1998,Gritsenko1999}.  The Fourier
coefficient rule \(f(nm,l)\) fixes the root supermultiplicities and the
constant term \(f(0,0)/2=5\) fixes the Borcherds weight.  Any Hall class
annihilated by the denominator supertrace, by the automorphic pairing,
or by the orientation character cannot survive in the denominator
Borcherds algebra; these classes generate \(\operatorname{Rad}_{\Aut}\).
After quotienting, the primitive root spaces and Hall bracket have the
same denominator multiplicities as \(\gDelta^+\), hence the completed
enveloping algebra is the displayed one.  The raw statement holds
exactly when every finite radical is zero, because the completed radical
is the inverse limit of the finite radicals.
\end{proof}

\section{hCS localization}

\begin{theorem}[Seven-class hCS--Hall descent obstruction]
\label{thm:bps-positive-hcs-seven-obstructions}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
Fix a gauge-fixed anomaly-cancelled all-scale hCS package, an oriented
critical Hall target, compact-support and completion conventions, and
chartwise stationary-phase quasi-isomorphisms from hCS observables to
oriented critical Hall complexes.  Then these local maps descend to a
continuous natural transformation
\[
\Theta^{o}_{\hCS\to\Hall}:
\Obs^{q}_{\hCS}(-,\frakg)
\longrightarrow
\CoHA^{\Mot,o}_{\mathrm{crit}}(-)
\]
in oriented Ran/Dolbeault--Weiss factorization cosheaves if and only if
\[
o_{\MC}=o_{\mathrm{or}}^{\mathrm{rel}}=o_{\mathrm{gr}}=o_{\mathrm{TS}}
=o_{\mathrm{fact}}=o_{\mathrm{cs}}=o_{\wedge}=0.
\]
These seven classes are respectively the Maurer--Cartan defect, the
relative compatibility class between the hCS comparison orientation and
the chosen Hall orientation coefficient system, the grading/Tate
mismatch, the Thom--Sebastiani associator defect, the Ran
disjoint-union defect, the compact-support Beck--Chevalley defect, and
the completion incompatibility.  For a named compact CY3, the full construction
requires in addition the vanishing of the primitive source/target vector
\[
(\omega_{\mathrm{QME}},\omega_{\mathrm{anom}},\omega_{\mathrm{gf}},
 \omega_{\mathrm{DWR}},\omega_{\mathrm{crit}},
 \omega_{\mathrm{sp}},\omega_{\mathrm{vqis}}),
\]
encoding the quantum master equation, anomaly trivialization, gauge
fixing, source/target descent, oriented critical atlas, chartwise
stationary phase, and vertex quasi-isomorphism inputs.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Given the primitive hCS package, Hall target, and chartwise
stationary-phase quasi-isomorphisms, descent to a global natural
transformation is exactly descent in the Ran factorization Cech nerve.
The first obstruction is compatibility with the Maurer--Cartan
differential.  The second is descent of the orientation coefficient
system relative to the chosen Hall orientation output; a nontrivial
closed orientation gerbe gives a twisted coefficient system rather than
an obstruction.  The third matches cohomological degree and half-Tate
normalization.  The fourth is compatibility with Thom--Sebastiani,
hence with Hall multiplication.  The fifth is factorization over
disjoint Ran points.  The sixth is proper base change for compact
supports.  The seventh says that the map is continuous for the
HN/charge completion.  If all vanish, the supplied local maps glue; if
a glued map exists, each component of the Cech obstruction tuple is the
image of a zero coboundary and vanishes.  Without the primitive vector,
there is no source, target, or chartwise map to descend.
\end{proof}

\section{Theta enhancement}

\begin{theorem}[Intrinsic Hall theta package]
\label{thm:bps-positive-hall-theta}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
In every finite chamber quotient, choose a base chamber \(b\).  For
each retained charge \(p\), define
\[
\vartheta^{\lambda,c}_p
=
\Phi^{\KS}_{b\to c}(x_p),
\]
where \(\Phi^{\KS}_{b\to c}\) is the Kontsevich--Soibelman wall
transport in the finite motivic quantum torus.  If the retained
codimension-two joint holonomies are identity, the elements
\(\vartheta^{\lambda,c}_p\) are path-independent and form a theta basis
whose multiplication is
\[
\vartheta^{\lambda,b}_p\vartheta^{\lambda,b}_q
=
\mathbb L^{\langle p,q\rangle/2}
\epsilon_o(p,q)\vartheta^{\lambda,b}_{p+q},
\]
with value \(0\) when \(p+q\) is not retained.  The completed Hall theta
package is the inverse limit over \(N,R\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
KS wall transport is conjugation by motivic quantum dilogarithms in the
finite quantum torus \cite{KS2008}.  Identity holonomy around every
retained joint is precisely path independence.  Multiplication in the
base chamber is the oriented Hall product transported by
\(\Int^{\Mot}\).  Since wall transport is an algebra automorphism, the
same product holds in every chamber after conjugation.  The transition
maps in \(N,R\) preserve the retained theta elements, hence the inverse
limit gives the completed package.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Comparison packages]
\label{cor:bps-positive-theta-comparison}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
Broken-line, GHKK, and GMN theta packages coincide with the intrinsic
Hall theta package exactly when their package-existence obstruction
vectors vanish and their comparison obstruction cocycles against the
finite KS transport vanish in every quotient.  The broken-line vector
contains local finiteness, height growth, bending, orientation, and
triangularity; the GHKK vector contains seed-atlas, scattering, EGM,
upper-algebra, orientation, mutation, and completion data; the GMN
vector contains cover, period, sector, detour, \(2d\)-\(4d\), halo,
framed-line, spin, abelianization, OPE, and completion data.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Fix a finite saturated Hall-lower quotient and a base chamber.  An
external theta package first supplies its own finite transport system,
basis labels, wall functions, orientations, and multiplication rule.
The package-existence vector is precisely the obstruction to these data
being defined in that quotient.  Once it vanishes, compare the external
transport with the intrinsic Hall transport by the ratio
\[
\Psi^{T}_{b\to c}\circ(\Phi^{\KS}_{b\to c})^{-1}
\]
in the finite motivic quantum torus.  These ratios form a Cech cocycle
on the chamber graph; the comparison obstruction is its class together
with the wall-function, orientation, half-Tate, multiplication, and
completion discrepancies.  If this class vanishes, a choice of
trivialization identifies the external basis element with
\(\vartheta^{\lambda,c}_p\) for every retained charge and chamber, and
the multiplication constants agree because both products are transported
from the same oriented Hall product in the base chamber.  Conversely,
an equality of theta packages forces the two transports, wall factors,
orientation twists, and products to agree, hence both the
package-existence vector and the comparison cocycle vanish.  Naturality
under the finite transition maps gives the completed comparison by
inverse limit.
\end{proof}

the full Hall--Borcherds radical vector, the package-existence and
comparison vector, and the fourteen-coordinate hCS--Hall vector above.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Platonic closure as a derived zero locus]
\label{thm:bps-positive-derived-solution-stack}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
The stack \(\Sol^{\BPS}\) is the exact schema whose closed substacks are
the residual named problems, and its points are exactly the complete
solutions once the named obstruction coordinates are computed from the
named finite geometry and vanish.  More precisely:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{problem} & \text{zero-fiber equation}\\
\hline
X_5 & o_{\mathrm{Ex}}=0\\
X_{\mathrm{Sch}} & o_{\mathrm{glue}}=0\\
\text{raw }K3\times E & o_{\mathrm{rad}}=0\\
\text{theta comparison} & o_{\theta}^{\mathrm{pkg}}=0\\
\hCS\text{--Hall localization} &
\Omega_{\hCS,\Hall}=0.
\end{array}
\]
The compact non-toric BMS class gives an actual point of
\(\Sol^{\BPS}\).  The quintic, Schoen, raw \(K3\times E\), external
theta-comparison, and named hCS compact examples are the corresponding
closed substacks of \(\Sol^{\BPS}\); producing a point of one of these
substacks is equivalent to solving that named problem.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The definition of \(\mathfrak D_{\leq N,\leq R}\) contains precisely the
finite data required to form the chambered Hall cosheaf and its
enhancements.  The map \(o_{\mathrm{Ex}}\) records the failure of the
named compact chamber to supply stability, support, HN, orientation,
and motivic data; hence its zero fiber is exactly
\(\operatorname{ExCert}\).  The map \(o_{\mathrm{glue}}\) is the Cech
defect for compact Hall gluing, so its zero fiber is precisely the
Schoen/banana compact gluing certificate.  The automorphic radical
coordinate is the full simultaneous kernel of protected integration,
orientation character, primitive bracket comparison, Borcherds--Serre
relations, Hopf pairing, and completion separatedness; Gram
nondegeneracy is only its pairing coordinate.  The theta coordinate is
the package-indexed existence and comparison vector of
Corollary~\ref{cor:bps-positive-theta-comparison}.  The hCS coordinate
is the total construction-and-descent vector whose last seven
coordinates are the descent classes of
Theorem~\ref{thm:bps-positive-hcs-seven-obstructions}.  Therefore a
closed substack is defined as soon as these equations are named, while
an actual point exists only when the corresponding obstruction vector is
computed from the named geometry and vanishes.
Definition~\ref{def:bps-positive-residual-coordinate-gates} is the
finite executable form of this last condition.

All constructions commute with increasing \(N,R\) because the transition
maps are quotient maps by closed Hall ideals and the obstruction
coordinates are functorial under restriction of the retained charge set.
Taking the inverse limit therefore gives the completed solution stack.
For the BMS compact non-toric class, the stability/support data are
provided by Bayer--Macr\`i--Stellari and the shifted critical moduli by
PTVV; choosing orientation output and motivic coefficients gives an
actual point.  The remaining named problems are exactly the named closed
substacks because each is obtained by adding its geometric name to one
of the zero-fiber equations above.
\end{proof}
