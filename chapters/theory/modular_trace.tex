\section{CY trace as modular characteristic}
\label{sec:cy-trace-kappa}

The CY trace $\Tr \colon \HH_d(\cC) \to k$ determines the modular characteristic $\kappa(A_\cC)$ of the associated quantum chiral algebra.

\begin{theorem}[CY modular characteristic --- Theorem CY-D]
\label{thm:cy-modular-characteristic}
For a CY category $\cC$ of dimension $d = 2$ with quantum chiral algebra $A_\cC = \Phi(\cC)$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  \item The modular characteristic $\kappa(A_\cC) = \chi^{\CY}(\cC)$, the CY Euler characteristic;
  \item The genus-$g$ obstruction satisfies $\mathrm{obs}_g(A_\cC) = \kappa(A_\cC) \cdot \lambda_g$ (on the uniform-weight lane);
  \item The shadow tower of $A_\cC$ encodes the higher-genus CY invariants of $\cC$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
