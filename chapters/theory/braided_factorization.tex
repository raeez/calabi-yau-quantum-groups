\chapter{Braided Factorization}
\label{ch:braided-factorization}

The $E_2$-chiral algebras of Chapter~\ref{ch:e2-chiral} have braided monoidal representation categories.  This chapter develops the factorization-homology framework for $E_2$-algebras, constructs the braided bar-cobar adjunction (Theorem~CY-B), and extracts quantum group structure from braided Koszul duality.  The central structural point: the $E_2$-bar complex $B_{E_2}(\cA)$ is a factorization $E_2$-coalgebra whose arity-$(1,1)$ component carries the $R$-matrix, and the full shadow obstruction tower $\Theta_{A_\cC}$ encodes the automorphic correction of the corresponding BKM superalgebra.

\section{Braided monoidal categories and $E_2$-algebras}
\label{sec:braided-e2}

This section reviews the Dunn additivity equivalence $E_2 \simeq E_1 \otimes E_1$ and its consequence: an $E_2$-algebra is an $E_1$-algebra in $E_1$-algebras, and its representation category is braided monoidal (the braiding coming from the second $E_1$-structure).  The Joyal--Street coherence theorem and its $\infty$-categorical refinement (Lurie) provide the foundational framework.

\section{Factorization homology with $E_2$-coefficients}
\label{sec:fh-e2}

Factorization homology $\int_M \cA$ of an $E_n$-algebra $\cA$ over an $n$-manifold $M$ (Ayala--Francis, Costello--Gwilliam) generalises both Hochschild homology ($n = 1$, $M = S^1$) and chiral homology ($n = 2$, $M$ a surface).  For $E_2$-chiral algebras, the factorization homology over a surface $\Sigma_g$ produces the genus-$g$ conformal block, and the shadow obstruction tower controls its dependence on the moduli of $\Sigma_g$.  This section develops the $E_2$ case with emphasis on the interaction between the two $E_1$-directions (holomorphic and topological in the physics language of Volume~II).

\section{The braided bar-cobar adjunction}
\label{sec:braided-bar-cobar}

\begin{theorem}[$E_2$-bar-cobar --- Theorem CY-B]
\label{thm:e2-bar-cobar}
\ClaimStatusConditional
For an $E_2$-chiral algebra $\cA$, the bar-cobar adjunction
\[
  B_{E_2} \colon E_2\text{-}\mathrm{ChirAlg} \rightleftarrows E_2\text{-}\mathrm{ChirCoalg} \cocolon \Omega_{E_2}
\]
satisfies:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  \item The $E_2$ bar complex $B_{E_2}(\cA)$ is a factorization $E_2$-coalgebra;
  \item Cobar-bar inversion: $\Omega_{E_2}(B_{E_2}(\cA)) \simeq \cA$ on the Koszul locus;
  \item The CY trace manifests as curvature: at genus $g \geq 1$, $d^2 = \kappa \cdot \omega_g$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Status of Theorem CY-B]
\label{rem:cy-b-status}
Items (i)--(iii) follow from the Volume~I bar-cobar machine (Theorems~A and~B) applied to each $E_1$-direction separately, together with compatibility of the two $E_1$-structures.  The full $E_2$-equivariant refinement (that the $R$-matrix intertwines the two bar-cobar adjunctions coherently) is a rigorous proof sketch, not a fully written proof.  The identification of the automorphic correction with the shadow obstruction tower (claimed in Theorem CY-B for $K3 \times E$) is conditional on Conjecture~CY-A$_3$.
\end{remark}

\section{Quantum group structure from braided Koszul duality}
\label{sec:qg-from-braided-koszul}

The braided Koszul dual $\cA^!_{E_2}$ of an $E_2$-chiral algebra $\cA$ should be the $E_2$-algebra whose representation category is the Langlands dual quantum group $\Rep_q(\frakg^L)$.  For $\frakg = \fsl_2$ (self-Langlands-dual), this reduces to the Volume~I Koszul duality $A \mapsto A^!$ with the braiding inherited from the $E_2$-structure.  For non-simply-laced types, the braided Koszul dual and the Langlands dual are genuinely different operations (AP33 applies: Koszul duality $\neq$ Feigin--Frenkel duality $\neq$ Langlands duality).  The target theorem: the braided bar cohomology $H^*(B_{E_2}(\cA))$ recovers the quantum group $U_q(\frakg)$ with its quasi-triangular Hopf algebra structure.
