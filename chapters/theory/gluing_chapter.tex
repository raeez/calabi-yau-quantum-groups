\chapter{CoHA gluing: ten routes, four strata, fifteen cells}
\label{ch:gluing}

The cohomological Hall algebra $\cH(X) = \CoHA(D^b\Coh(X), \Omega_X)$ of
a Calabi--Yau threefold is assembled from local data. This chapter
classifies how the assembly happens: the four equivariance strata
(toric $T^d$, reduced $\Gm + \Aut$, orbifold inertia, lattice-polarised
period), the sixteen power-set cells partitioning into one empty cell
and fifteen non-empty cells, and the explicit local-to-global cocycles
that glue chart-wise constructions to a globally-defined Hall bialgebra.
Each stratum sources a cocycle type: chart-transition cocycles in
$H^1(\Sigma, \underline{\Pic}_{T^d})$ on toric fans, connected-automorphism
character cocycles on the reduced-$\Gm$ stratum, Galois cocycles on the
orbifold-inertia stratum, and Fourier-coefficient cocycles of
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery weight-$k_\Psi = c_\phi(0, 0)/2$ forms on the
lattice-polarised-period stratum. Every $\CY_3$ carries a non-empty
stratum-set $\Str(X) \subseteq \{\mathrm{i}, \mathrm{ii}, \mathrm{iii},
\mathrm{iv}\}$; the classification of assemblies reduces to a
classification of sixteen cells of $\cP(\Str)$.

The single object that coherently binds the four strata is the
$(2,1)$-stack $\mathrm{Stk}_{\Str}$ of equivariance strata, whose stalks
on each non-empty cell carry a stable $\infty$-categorical CoHA. The
$K3 \times E$ master example sits at cell fifteen, where all four
strata meet; the four gluing cocycles on $K3 \times E$ produce the
Heisenberg, Mukai-lattice-rank, Mathieu-twining, and Borcherds-$\Delta_5$
data from four logically independent constructions, with the associated
$\kappa_\bullet$-spectrum $\{0, 3, 5, 24\}$ registered at four distinct
categorical locations. The compact-CY$_3$-without-K3-fibration regime
(quintic, Schoen bicubic) sits in the stratum-free cell and carries only
the Davison--Meinhardt critical CoHA, an obstruction boundary sharpened
in the closing sections.

Ten sections resolve ten constructions: abstract 2-categorical
framework (\S\ref{sec:gluing:abstract}); \v{C}ech gluing on toric atlases
(\S\ref{sec:gluing:toric-cech}); McKay and orbifold inertia
(\S\ref{sec:gluing:mckay}); Van den Bergh derived Morita via tilting
and NCCR (\S\ref{sec:gluing:vdb}); factorisation algebras on Ran spaces
with the two-stage $\Phi_3 = \SpCh_{\Sigma_2, C} \circ \PhiFA_3$
(\S\ref{sec:gluing:factorisation}); Kontsevich--Soibelman wall-crossing
(\S\ref{sec:gluing:wallcrossing}); lattice-polarised Borcherds automorphic
gluing (\S\ref{sec:gluing:lattice}); the $K3 \times E$ master example
(\S\ref{sec:gluing:k3e}); obstructions on compact $\CY_3$
(\S\ref{sec:gluing:obstructions}); and the unifying sheaf of CoHAs on
the $(2,1)$-stack of equivariance strata (\S\ref{sec:gluing:unifying}).

\section{Closing synthesis}
\label{sec:gluing:closing}

The ten sections reduce the assembly question for $\cH(X)$ to a single
invariant: the stratum-set $\Str(X) \subseteq \{\mathrm{i},
\mathrm{ii}, \mathrm{iii}, \mathrm{iv}\}$, each occupied stratum
supplying a cocycle theory whose cohomology class determines the
global Hall bialgebra up to $(2,1)$-coherent equivalence. The
reduction is not uniformly theorem-grade:
\S\S\ref{sec:gluing:toric-cech}--\ref{sec:gluing:vdb} establish full
theorems on their respective strata; \S\S\ref{sec:gluing:factorisation}--\ref{sec:gluing:lattice}
establish the $\Phi_3$, Kontsevich--Soibelman, and Borcherds cocycles
up to primary-literature-cited conjectures (two-stage factorisation for
non-simply-laced super, the $c_{11} = -1$ Hopf-pairing correction, the
universal $\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$ identity across both
Gritsenko--Nikulin CHL and Gritsenko--Cl\'ery 8-form scopes);
\S\ref{sec:gluing:k3e} verifies the master example;
\S\ref{sec:gluing:obstructions} sharpens the failure boundary on
compact CY$_3$ without K3-fibration; \S\ref{sec:gluing:unifying} names
the sheaf of CoHAs on $\mathrm{Stk}_{\Str}$ with the fifteen-cell
stalk classification. Part~\ref{part:connections} applies the
gluing framework to the seven faces of $r_{\CY}$, extracting from each
stratum-cell the precise chiral-algebra shadow of the classical
$r$-matrix.
