\chapter{Derived Categories of CY Manifolds}
\label{ch:derived-cy}

Homological mirror symmetry identifies $\Fuk(X) \simeq D^b(\Coh(X^\vee))$.  The Fukaya category (Chapter~\ref{ch:fukaya}) is the symplectic input to the CY-to-chiral functor; the derived category $D^b(\Coh(X))$ is the algebraic input.  Under the functor $\Phi$, mirror symmetry becomes an equivalence of quantum chiral algebras: $A_{\Fuk(X)} \simeq A_{D^b(\Coh(X^\vee))}$.  This chapter develops the algebraic side: the CY structure on $D^b(\Coh(X))$, the chiral algebra extracted by $\Phi$, the role of exceptional collections, and the interaction with Bridgeland stability conditions.
