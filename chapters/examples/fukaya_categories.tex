\chapter{Fukaya Categories}
\label{ch:fukaya}

The CY-to-chiral functor $\Phi$ (Theorem~CY-A$_2$) takes as input a CY category with cyclic $\Ainf$-structure (Chapter~\ref{ch:cyclic-ainf}).  The Fukaya category $\Fuk(X)$ of a symplectic manifold $X$ is the prototypical source: its $\Ainf$-structure encodes counts of pseudoholomorphic polygons, and the CY pairing is Poincar\'{e} duality on Lagrangian intersections.  For CY surfaces ($d = 2$), $\Phi(\Fuk(X))$ is an $\Etwo$-chiral algebra with $\kappa = \chi^{\CY}$.  This chapter computes $\Fuk(X)$ and the resulting chiral algebra for elliptic curves, K3 surfaces, abelian surfaces, and CY threefolds.

\section{The Fukaya category of a CY manifold}
\label{sec:fukaya-cy-manifold}

\begin{example}[Elliptic curve]
\label{ex:fukaya-elliptic}
For an elliptic curve $E_\tau$, $\Fuk(E_\tau)$ is CY of dimension $1$.  The Hochschild homology $\HH_\bullet(\Fuk(E_\tau))$ recovers the de Rham cohomology of $E_\tau$, and the cyclic structure encodes the symplectic pairing.  The associated chiral algebra is the Heisenberg vertex algebra $H_k$ at level $k = \mathrm{vol}(E_\tau)$.
\end{example}

\section{CY 2-folds: K3 and abelian surfaces}
\label{sec:fukaya-cy2}

For a K3 surface $S$, the Fukaya category $\Fuk(S)$ is CY of dimension $2$.  The functor $\Phi$ (Theorem~CY-A$_2$) produces an $E_2$-chiral algebra $A_{\Fuk(S)}$ with $\kappa = \chi(\cO_S) = 2$.  Under homological mirror symmetry, this should agree with $A_{D^b(\Coh(S^\vee))}$.  The $K3 \times E$ programme (Chapter~\ref{ch:toroidal-elliptic}) uses $\Fuk(S)$ as the fiber category, with the shadow obstruction tower controlling the passage from fiber to total space.

\section{CY 3-folds}
\label{sec:fukaya-cy3}

For a compact CY threefold $X$, the Fukaya category $\Fuk(X)$ is CY of dimension $3$.  The functor $\Phi$ is conditional on the chain-level $\mathbb{S}^3$-framing (Conjecture~CY-A$_3$).  When $X$ is the quintic threefold, the algebraic \v{C}ech resolution (Theorem~\ref{thm:cech-contracting-homotopy}) closes the analytic gap, and $\kappa_{\mathrm{MacMahon}} = \chi/2 = -100$ is the predicted modular characteristic.  The open-string sector ($\Fuk(X)$ with Lagrangian boundary conditions) connects to the Volume~II Swiss-cheese structure.

\section{Wrapped Fukaya categories}
\label{sec:wrapped-fukaya}

The wrapped Fukaya category $\cW(X)$ of a Liouville manifold $X$ is the non-compact analogue of $\Fuk(X)$, with Reeb dynamics at infinity providing the wrapping.  For cotangent bundles $T^*M$, $\cW(T^*M) \simeq \mathrm{Mod}(C_*(\Omega M))$ (Abouzaid), reducing the CY-to-chiral functor to the based loop space.  The wrapped category is smooth but not proper; the CY structure requires a non-compact version of the trace, connecting to the analytic sewing programme (Volume~I, MC5).
