\chapter{Fukaya Categories}
\label{ch:fukaya}

The CY-to-chiral functor $\Phi$ (Theorem~CY-A$_2$) takes as input a CY category with cyclic $\Ainf$-structure (Chapter~\ref{ch:cyclic-ainf}).  The Fukaya category $\Fuk(X)$ of a symplectic manifold $X$ is the prototypical source: its $\Ainf$-structure encodes counts of pseudoholomorphic polygons, and the CY pairing is Poincar\'{e} duality on Lagrangian intersections.  For CY surfaces ($d = 2$), $\Phi(\Fuk(X))$ is an $\Etwo$-chiral algebra with $\kappa = \chi^{\CY}$.  This chapter computes $\Fuk(X)$ and the resulting chiral algebra for elliptic curves, K3 surfaces, abelian surfaces, and CY threefolds.

\section{The Fukaya category of a CY manifold}
\label{sec:fukaya-cy-manifold}

\begin{example}[Elliptic curve]
\label{ex:fukaya-elliptic}
For an elliptic curve $E_\tau$, $\Fuk(E_\tau)$ is CY of dimension $1$.  The Hochschild homology $\HH_\bullet(\Fuk(E_\tau))$ recovers the de Rham cohomology of $E_\tau$, and the cyclic structure encodes the symplectic pairing.  The associated chiral algebra is the Heisenberg vertex algebra $H_k$ at level $k = \mathrm{vol}(E_\tau)$.
\end{example}
