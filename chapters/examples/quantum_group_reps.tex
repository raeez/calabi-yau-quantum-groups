\begin{theorem}[Quantum group realization --- Theorem CY-C]
\label{thm:qg-realization}
For a simple Lie algebra $\frakg$ and generic $q$, the braided monoidal category $\Rep_q(\frakg)$ arises as $\Rep^{E_2}(A_\cC)$ for a CY category $\cC = \cC(\frakg, q)$.  The quantum chiral algebra $A_\cC$ recovers the affine Kac--Moody vertex algebra $V_k(\frakg)$ at level $k$ determined by $q = e^{\pi i / (k + h^\vee)}$.
\end{theorem}

\section{Kazhdan--Lusztig equivalence}
\label{sec:kl-equivalence}

The Kazhdan--Lusztig equivalence
\[
  \Rep_q(\frakg) \simeq \mathrm{KL}_k(\frakg)
\]
between quantum group representations (at root of unity) and the Kazhdan--Lusztig category of affine Kac--Moody modules is the prototype for Theorem CY-C.
