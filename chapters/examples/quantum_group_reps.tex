\chapter{Quantum Group Representations}
\label{ch:quantum-group-reps}

The braided monoidal category $\Rep_q(\frakg)$ is the representation-theoretic target of the CY programme.  The Drinfeld center $\cZ(\Rep^{E_1}(A))$ (Chapter~\ref{ch:drinfeld-center}) provides the bulk algebra; Conjecture~CY-C predicts $\Rep_q(\frakg) \simeq \Rep^{E_2}(A_\cC)$ for a CY category $\cC(\frakg, q)$.  The Kazhdan--Lusztig equivalence $\Rep_q(\frakg) \simeq \mathrm{KL}_k(\frakg)$ at roots of unity is the prototype; the affine Yangian realization (Chapter~\ref{ch:toric-coha}) provides the $E_1$-sector.

\section{The quantum chiral algebra of $\Uq(\frakg)$}
\label{sec:qca-uq}

\begin{conjecture}[Quantum group realization --- Conjecture CY-C]
\label{conj:qg-realization}
For a simple Lie algebra $\frakg$ and $q = e^{\pi i / (k + h^\vee)}$ with $k$ a positive integer, the braided monoidal category $\Rep_q(\frakg)$ should arise as $\Rep^{E_2}(A_\cC)$ for a CY category $\cC = \cC(\frakg, q)$.  The quantum chiral algebra $A_\cC$ should recover the affine Kac--Moody vertex algebra $V_k(\frakg)$ at level $k$.

\emph{Status}: The CY category $\cC(\frakg, q)$ is not yet constructed in general.  At generic $q$ (irrational $k$), $\Rep_q(\frakg)$ is semisimple and the story simplifies; the interesting structure is at roots of unity, where the Kazhdan--Lusztig equivalence provides the prototype.
\end{conjecture}

\section{Kazhdan--Lusztig equivalence}
\label{sec:kl-equivalence}

The Kazhdan--Lusztig equivalence
\[
  \Rep_q(\frakg) \simeq \mathrm{KL}_k(\frakg)
\]
between quantum group representations (at root of unity) and the Kazhdan--Lusztig category of affine Kac--Moody modules is the prototype for Theorem CY-C.
