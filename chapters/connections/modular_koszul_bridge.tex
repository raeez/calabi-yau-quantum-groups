\chapter{Modular Koszul Duality and CY Geometry}
\label{ch:modular-koszul-bridge}

The bar-cobar bridge of Chapter~\ref{ch:bar-cobar-bridge} translates between the CY cyclic bar complex $\mathrm{CC}_\bullet(\cC)$ and the chiral bar complex $B(A_\cC)$.  Volume~I's modular Koszul duality operates on $B(A)$: Verdier intertwining $D_{\Ran}(B(A)) \simeq B(A^!)$, the complementarity theorem (Theorem~C), and the genus expansion controlled by $\kappa(A)$ (Theorem~D).  This chapter constructs their CY-geometric incarnations: the modular convolution algebra, the CY complementarity pairing, and the shadow CohFT derived from the CY trace.
