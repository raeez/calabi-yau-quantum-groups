\section*{The seed object \texorpdfstring{$K3\times E$}{K3 x E}}
\label{sec:preface-k3xe-seed}

The object \(K3\times E\) contains the whole volume in miniature. Its
total-space holomorphic Euler characteristic is zero:
\[
\kcat(K3\times E)
=
\chi(\cO_{K3})\,\chi(\cO_E)
=
2\cdot 0
=0.
\]
Its compact Hodge-supertrace chiral characteristic is also zero:
\[
\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Hodge}}(K3\times E)
=
\sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(K3\times E)
=
\chi(\cO_{K3\times E})
=0.
\]
The Heisenberg--Mukai specialisation sees a different chiral
measurement,
\[
\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{Heis}}(K3\times E)=3,
\]
the Borcherds denominator sees
\[
\kBKM(\Delta_5)=5=c_N(0)/2\big|_{N=1},
\]
and the fibre remembers the Mukai rank
\[
\kfib(K3)=24.
\]
Each of the five numbers belongs to a distinct construction layer:
total-space category, compact Hodge trace, Stage--2 Heisenberg
shadow, Borcherds specialisation, and fibre lattice. The formula
\[
\kBKM
=
\kch+\chi(\cO_{\mathrm{fiber}})
\]
crosses these layers: it adds a Stage--2 Borcherds output to a
Stage--1 compact Euler characteristic, and at the identity CHL point
the two sides land in different layers ($5$ on the Borcherds layer,
$0+0$ on the compact-Euler/fibre layers). The universal identity that
holds on a single layer is the Borcherds-weight identity
$\kBKM(\Phi_N) = c_N(0)/2$, and the layer separation is the structural
content of the two-stage factorisation.

The physics writes the same separation without commentary. On a
Calabi--Yau threefold with holomorphic volume form \(\Omega_X\), the
six-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons action is
\[
S_{\hCS}(\mathcal A)
=
\int_X\Omega_X\wedge
\left\langle
\mathcal A,\overline\partial\mathcal A
+\frac13[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]
\right\rangle .
\]
For \(X=K3\times E\), Stage-2 specialisation at $\Sigma_2 = K3$ and
$C = E$ takes the native \(E_3\)-factorisation algebra to a chiral
algebra on the elliptic curve. The cusp form \(\Delta_5\) is the
Borcherds product of this specialisation: it controls the root
multiplicities of the Borcherds--Kac--Moody Lie superalgebra
\(\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}\) (Gritsenko--Nikulin 1998) and the
one-loop automorphic correction. The seed K3 $\times$ E forces the
general rule.

The seed governs one face of the compact threefold story. For a
generic smooth projective CY$_3$, Joyce's vertex algebra $V_X$ on the
derived moduli stack of sheaves (Joyce 2017,
\S\ref{ch:k3e-bkm}), together with the Kontsevich--Pandharipande--Soibelman
orientation data, carries the derived moduli on which MNOP is the
unique $E_2$-centre automorphism of the native $E_3$-factorisation
algebra (Chapter~\ref{ch:cy-c-six-routes-convergence}). The
$S^3$-framing on generic compact CY$_3$ Lagrangians is chain-level
in the Costello--Paquette defect-chiral model. The passage from this
local framed object to a global Hall--Borcherds/Drinfeld double
proceeds through compact critical-CoHA charts, a Serre-compatible
quasi-NCCR descent datum, a Hall pairing with PBW integral form, and
Drinfeld-double completion maps
(Chapter~\ref{ch:cy-c-beyond-k3e}); each of
these is a separate input beyond the definition of $\PhiFA_3$.

\section*{The positive-geometry grammar forced by
\texorpdfstring{$K3\times E$}{K3 x E}}
\label{wn:sec:platonic-preface-positive-geometry-grammar}

The primary datum of a Calabi--Yau category is its negative-cyclic
Calabi--Yau trace \(HC^-_d(\mathcal A)\to k\); the Hochschild trace is
its image, and the category produces a holomorphic factorisation
algebra native to the Calabi--Yau geometry. A chiral algebra on a
curve emerges from this native factorisation algebra after an
admissible specialisation datum \((\Sigma_{d-1}, C)\) has been chosen.
The object called \(\Phi_d\) in this volume is therefore the two-stage
assignment
\[
\Phi_d^{(\Sigma_{d-1}, C)}(\mathcal A)
\;:=\;
\SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1}, C}\bigl(\PhiFA_d(\mathcal A)\bigr),
\qquad
\CYcat_d
\xrightarrow{\;\PhiFA_d\;}
E_d\text{-}\HolFA(X)
\xrightarrow{\;\SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1}, C}\;}
E_1\text{-}\ChirAlg(C),
\]
where \((\Sigma_{d-1}, C)\) is part of the input. Stage~1 is carried by
the same construction on the established loci: \(d \leq 2\); smooth
proper \(d=3\) object-level Hochschild and hCS models after the three
obstruction vanishings of CY-A$_3$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:cya3-existence-rigidity}); and explicit toric or
formal Calabi--Yau charts where Costello--Gwilliam locality,
Kontsevich--Tamarkin formality, and the required critical atlas are
available. The compact non-formal CY$_3$ extension of Stage~1 is open: the
morphism-level functoriality and critical-atlas compatibility data
required to preserve the Yukawa cubic $Y_3 \in H^{0,3}(X)$ are the
named obstructions of the CY-B$_3$ compact problem. Stage~2 is a
choice of admissible cycle
and reference curve on which the factorisation-homology pushforward and
restriction are defined. Different choices produce different chiral
shadows of the same native datum.

\subsection*{The quantum group as Drinfeld double of positive
effective-geometry cohomology}
\label{wn:sec:platonic-preface-quantum-group}

Inside the Stage--1 object, when a critical positive atlas exists, there
is a distinguished positive effective moduli stack $\Meff(X)$ carrying
the Kontsevich--Soibelman cohomological-Hall-algebra structure. Write
$\phi_W$ for the Behrend--microlocal vanishing-cycle sheaf adapted to
$\Meff(X)$. Then
\[
Y^+(X) \;:=\; H^{\bullet}_{\mathrm{eq}}\!\bigl(\Meff(X),\,\phi_W\bigr)
\]
is the positive half. When the PBW theorem, integral form, Hopf pairing,
and stable-envelope transport are constructed, its Drinfeld double is
\[
G(X) \;:=\; D\bigl(Y^+(X)\bigr) \;=\; Y^+(X) \bowtie Y^0(X) \bowtie Y^-(X),
\]
where $Y^0(X)$ is the Cartan piece generated by the charge lattice of
$\Meff(X)$ and $Y^-(X)$ is the antipode image of $Y^+(X)$ under
Drinfeld doubling; the Hopf pairing comes from Serre duality on the
compact locus and Maulik--Okounkov stable envelopes supply the
$R$-matrix. On
\(\CC^3\) and the standard toric CY$_3$ charts the hCS observable
model, the critical Hall positive half, and the chart transition maps
are constructed and glue locally. For a compact non-toric CY$_3$, the
critical CoHA, Serre-compatible quasi-NCCR atlas, PBW/Hall pairing,
stable-envelope transport, and Drinfeld-double descent remain named
input data for the comparison theorem.

The equivariance group on which the cohomology is taken depends on
$X$. The toric threefold $\CC^3$ supports the full three-torus
$(\CC^\times)^3$; Schiffmann--Vasserot identify its positive
effective cohomology with the positive half of the affine Yangian
\(Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)\). The compact surface \(K3\) carries no
torus; its positive effective geometry is the Mukai lattice with the
Nakajima--Grojnowski--Lehn Heisenberg action. The compact threefold
\(K3\times E\) carries the Pandharipande--Oberdieck reduced
Donaldson--Thomas moduli stack on K3-primitive classes, equivariant
under \(\CC^\times_E\times\mathrm{Aut}_s(K3)\), with character
\[
Z^{\mathrm{red}}_{\mathrm{DT}}(K3 \times E)
\;=\; -\bigl(\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}\bigr)^{-1}
\;=\; -D_5^{-2}
\;=\; -4096\,\Delta_5^{-2},
\]
where \(D_5=64^{-1}\Delta_5\) is the monic Borcherds product and
\(\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{OP}}=D_5^2\) is the reduced-DT scalar convention.
The equivariance type stratifies the programme: toric $T^d$, reduced
\(\CC^\times\) plus a symplectic automorphism, orbifold inertia of a
finite group, or the period domain of a polarising lattice.

\subsection*{Six-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons as the
canonical \texorpdfstring{$d{=}3$}{d=3} realisation}
\label{wn:sec:platonic-preface-6d-hCS}

At \(d=3\) the factorisation algebra is explicit. Let \(X\) be a smooth
complex threefold with holomorphic volume form
\(\Omega_X\in H^{3,0}(X)\) and gauge Lie algebra \(\fg\). The classical
six-dimensional holomorphic Chern--Simons action
\[
S_{\mathrm{cl}}[\mathcal A] \;=\; \int_X \Omega_X \wedge
\bigl\langle \mathcal A, \bar\partial \mathcal A
+ \tfrac{1}{3}[\mathcal A, \mathcal A] \bigr\rangle
\]
on the BV superfield
\(\mathcal A=c+A_{0,1}+A^*_{0,2}+c^*_{0,3}\) carries a
\((-1)\)-shifted symplectic form by Serre duality. Costello--Gwilliam
quantisation produces the observable factorisation algebra; on
\(\CC^3\), Maulik--Okounkov enumeration identifies the resulting
cohomological Hall structure with the positive half
\(\CoHA(\CC^3) = Y^+(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)\).
The full Yangian \(Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)\) is the Drinfeld
double of this positive half, and the \(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}\) vacuum
module is its image under the Gaiotto--Rap\v c\'ak evaluation
\(\mathrm{ev}_\lambda \colon Y(\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1) \twoheadrightarrow
\mathrm{End}(\mathcal W_{1+\infty}[\lambda]\text{-vac})\) — three
algebraic objects on three arrows of the CoHA evaluation chain. On a toric
atlas the same positive-half construction glues by the local
hCS-to-Hall transition maps; compact critical-CoHA and Hall-double
descent require the obstruction data named above.

\subsection*{The eleven theorems that organise the volume}
\label{sec:preface-architecture}

Eleven structural statements carry the volume. Each is stated here
in compressed form; each is proved in the chapter named beside it.

\textsl{Two-stage factorisation.}\enspace
The CY-to-chiral functor factors through holomorphic factorisation
algebras,
\(
 \Phi_d \;=\; \SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ\PhiFA_d,
\)
with Stage~1 producing a canonical \(E_d\)-holomorphic factorisation
algebra on the Calabi--Yau target (pinned by Kontsevich--Tamarkin
\(E_d\)-formality: operad-level unconditional per Fresse $2017$
Vol.~I Theorem~$14.1.\mathrm{A}$, category-level three-vanishing
theorem closed at \(d\le 3\), open at \(d\ge 4\); plus
Costello--Gwilliam--Li locality) and Stage~2 specialising via
factorisation homology \(\int_{\Sigma_{d-1}}\) over an embedded
\((d-1)\)-cycle, restriction to a reference curve \(C\). A single CY-$d$ category admits a \emph{family} of $E_1$-chiral
shadows indexed by $(\Sigma_{d - 1}, C)$; $\Phi_d$ is the
two-stage assignment, with the chart datum part of the input at
Stage~2.

\textsl{Stage-1 formal-locus scope.}\enspace
Stage~1 \(\PhiFA_d\) is canonical on the formal Hochschild locus
where \(\mathrm{HH}^{\bullet}(\mathcal C)\) is formal as an
\(E_d\)-algebra. At \(d=1\) (elliptic curves), formality holds
always (Kontsevich $1999$). At \(d=2\) (K3 surfaces), formality
holds generically: \(\mathrm{At}(T_{K3})\in H^1(K3,\Omega^1_{K3}\otimes\mathrm{End}(T_{K3}))\)
but \(\Omega^3_{K3}=0\) kills the Kuranishi cubic obstruction. At
\(d=3\), formality holds on \(K3\times E\) via
\(\mathrm{At}(T_E)=0\) plus \(\Omega^3_{K3}=0\); on compact
Calabi--Yau threefolds with \(\mathrm{At}(TX)\ne 0\) (quintic,
generic compact CY$_3$), formality is genuinely obstructed. At
\(d\ge 4\), the category-level formality obstruction theorem still
requires its compact-formality witness. Operad-level formality (Fresse Vol.~I
Theorem~$14.1.\mathrm{A}$) is unconditional at every \(d\); the
distinction between operad-level and category-level
\(E_d\)-formality is load-bearing, and the two are distinct.

\textsl{PTVV-forced native operadic level.}\enspace
The native operadic level is forced by the Pantev--To\"en--Vaqui\'e--Vezzosi
shifted symplectic structure. PTVV $2013$: the shifted symplectic
structure on \(\mathcal M(\mathcal C)\) for a CY-\(d\) category
\(\mathcal C\) carries shift \((2-d)\). Dunn--Lurie additivity on
the shift gives
\(
 d=1 \Rightarrow n=\infty
 \ (\text{\(E_\infty\)-chiral, lattice VOA on \(E\)});\
 d=2 \Rightarrow n=2
 \ (\text{\(E_2\)-chiral, Mukai-Heisenberg on \(K3\)});\
 d\ge 3 \Rightarrow n=1
 \ (\text{\(E_1\)-chiral, CoHA/Yangian/BKM}).
\)
This dispatch is forced by the shift through Dunn--Lurie
additivity; the Schouten--Nijenhuis bracket carries the chain-level
structure compatible with the shift at each $d$ independently.

\textsl{Six routes to \(G(K3\times E)\) as common comparison cone.}\enspace
On the \(K3\times E\) host, six canonical routes
(Definition~\ref{def:cy-c-six-routes}) produce chiral-algebra or
chiral-group outputs from the same geometry through six different
machines: \textbf{R1} the CY-to-chiral framed assignment
$\Phi_3^{(\Sigma_2, C)} = \SpCh_{\Sigma_2, C} \circ \PhiFA_3$ applied
to $D^b(\mathrm{Coh}(K3 \times E))$; \textbf{R2} the Borcherds
multiplicative lift of \(\phi_{0,1}^{\mathrm{EZ}}\) giving
$\mathfrak{g}_{\Delta_5}$; \textbf{R3} the Mukai-lattice VOA
$V_{\Lambda_{\mathrm{Muk}}}$; \textbf{R4} the Kummer construction
$\widetilde{(K3\times E)/\iota}$; \textbf{R5} the
$\mathcal N=(2,2)$ sigma-model half-twist on $K3\times E$;
\textbf{R6} the BLLPR Schur-limit construction descended from the
$6$d $(2,0)$ parent. CY-C is the assertion that these six outputs
identify as $(\Sigma_2, C)$-specialisations of one canonical Stage-1
datum $\PhiFA_3(D^b\mathrm{Coh}(K3 \times E))$; only R1 is a
$\Phi_3$-application. The value data and generator-level comparisons
are constructed on the CHL siblings \(N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}\); the
compact Hall double requires the imprimitive reduced-DT extension,
the Hall--BKM bracket comparison, the PBW/integral form, and the
double-descent maps. The Monster and Igusa cases sit on distinct
CY$_3$ inputs, not sibling specialisations: the Fake Monster lives on
the rank-$26$ even unimodular Leech sum $\mathrm{II}_{25,1}$ and is
inaccessible from any compact CY$_3$ since for a K3-fibred threefold
\(h^{1,1}(X) \leq h^{1,1}(K3) + 1 = 21 < 24\) (or via Leray
$h^{1,1}(K3) = 20 < 24$ directly).

\textsl{Eight-form Gritsenko--Cl\'ery catalogue and the CHL ladder.}\enspace
Gritsenko--Cl\'ery $2008$ (arXiv:0812.3962, Theorem~$1.4$) classify the
genus-$2$ Siegel modular forms whose Humbert divisor on
$\mathbb{H}_2/\Gamma_t(N)$ is the diagonal with vanishing order~$1$.
The complete list has eight entries indexed by triples
$(t, N; k) \in \{(1,1;5),(2,1;2),(1,2;3),(3,1;1),(1,3;2),
(4,1;\tfrac12),(1,4;\tfrac32),(2,2;1)\}$, with weights
$(5, 2, 3, 1, 2, \tfrac12, \tfrac32, 1)$ and twice-weight
$(10, 4, 6, 2, 4, 1, 3, 2)$. No weight-$0$ row exists, no
weight-$\tfrac14$ row exists, and no entry lives on the spin
double cover $\widetilde{\mathrm{Mp}}_4$: the half-integer entries
$\Delta_{1/2}$ and $\nabla_{3/2}$ use the multiplier systems
$v_\eta^3 \times v_H$ over the paramodular groups
$\Gamma_4$ and $\Gamma_0^{(2)}(4)$, respectively. The CHL ladder
$N \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$ is an independent family carrying weights
$(5, 4, 3, 2, 1)$ and $c_N(0) = (10, 8, 6, 4, 2)$; the two families
overlap only at $(t, N) = (1, 1)$ with $F_{1,1} = \Delta_5$. The
row-wise Borcherds-weight identity
\(\kBKM(F_{t,N}) = \mathrm{wt}(F_{t,N})\) holds at each of the eight
GC triples; specialised to single-cusp entries it reduces to
\(\kBKM = c_N(0)/2\), which is the form proved across the CHL ladder
in Theorem~\ref{thm:borcherds-weight-kappa-BKM-universal}. The
additive decomposition
\(\kBKM = \kch + \chi(\cO_{\mathrm{fiber}})\) fails at every CHL
entry, including \(N=1\) (\(\kch^{\mathrm{Hodge}}(K3\times E) = 0\)
by Serre at \(d=3\); \(\kBKM(\Delta_5)=5\)): the additive form
crosses construction layers, while the surviving universal identity
\(\kBKM = c_N(0)/2\) holds on a single layer.

\textsl{Hodge-supertrace \(\kch\) stratification.}\enspace
Across Calabi--Yau dimension \(d\), the Hodge supertrace
\(\kch(X) = \sum_q (-1)^q h^{0,q}(X)\) stratifies as:
\(d=1\) (\(E\)) gives \(0\); \(d=2\) (\(K3\)) gives \(2\), generic
abelian/bielliptic surface gives \(0\); \(d=3\) (any compact CY$_3$)
gives \(0\) by Serre duality (odd-\(d\) forced vanishing); local
\(\mathbb P^2\) gives \(3/2\); conifold gives \(1\); \(d=4\)
sextic gives \(2\); octic-double gives \(151\); \(K3^{[2]}\) gives
\(3\); \(F(Y)\) (Fano variety of lines) gives \(3\); \(d=5\)
generic and \(K3\times X_5\) give \(0\) by Serre. The value of $\kch$ on a compact CY$_3$ category lives at the
Stage-$1$ Hodge-supertrace layer; $\kBKM$ of the Borcherds-lift
output lives at the level-$4$ scalar terminus; the Mukai-rank fibre
invariant $\kfib$ lives at the lattice-rank correction layer. Each
of the three reads its own construction layer.

\textsl{Class A definition and the triple coincidence at \(N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}\).}\enspace
A Calabi--Yau threefold \(X\) is of Class~A if (A1) \(X\) is
K3-fibred with generic K3 fibres, (A2) the monodromy group of the
K3 fibre lies in Mathieu \(M_{23}\), (A3) the Jacobi-form elliptic
genus of \(X\) lifts to a non-zero Siegel paramodular form. The
eight-orbifold census (Gritsenko--Cl\'ery $2008$ simplest-divisor
census) supplies the diagonal orbifolds; the STU model is
Harvey--Moore $1996$ heterotic \(K3\times T^2\) threshold. The
integer CHL ladder \(N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}\) is forced by three
independent coincidences: (i) \(\varphi(N)\mid 2\); (ii) Mathieu
\(M_{23}\) symplectic order bound \(\le 23\); (iii) Nikulin
fixed-count \(N^*(N)\). All three converge on the same integer
ladder.

 \mathrm{tr}^{\zeta}_{\mathrm{ghost}}(Q_{\mathrm{BRST}}^2)
 = \mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{Pentagon}}
 = \omega_{\mathrm{Borcherds}}

\)
The ghost supertrace is finite under the Dabholkar--Harvey BPS

of $\mathrm{tr}_{\mathrm{Pentagon}}$ with the

 \SpCh_{K3,E}(\PhiFA_3(\mathrm{Perf}(K3\times E)))
 \longrightarrow \mathbf H_{\Delta_5}

is at chiral-bialgebra level; the compact Hall bracket, PBW form, and

\(\omega(z,w)=(z-w-\varepsilon_1)(z-w-\varepsilon_2)(z-w-\varepsilon_3)/(z-w)^3\).
The positive half \(Y^+(\hat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1) := \CoHA(\C^3)_+\)

\(Y(\hat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1) = Y^+\bowtie Y^0\bowtie Y^-\)

$\CoHA(\C^3) = Y^+(\hat{\mathfrak{gl}}_1)$ at the Hall positive-half
level; $W_{1 + \infty}$ via $\mathrm{ev}_\lambda$ at the
representation-evaluation level; the evaluation chain
$Y^+ \hookrightarrow Y \xrightarrow{\mathrm{ev}_\lambda} \mathrm{End}(W_{1 + \infty}[\lambda]\text{-vac})$
runs the two algebras through three associativity classes.

\textsl{K3 Yangian trichotomy and chiral-bialgebra comparison.}\enspace
The K3 Yangian carries a trichotomy of classical limits labelled by
