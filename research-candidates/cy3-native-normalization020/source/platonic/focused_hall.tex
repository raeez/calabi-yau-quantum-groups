\chapter{Extension correspondences}
\label{ch:extension-correspondences}
The quotient-first convention is converted to submodule-first order by
$a\star b=(-1)^{|a||b|}m(b,a)$. Both triple products acquire the sign
$(-1)^{|a||b|+|a||d|+|b||d|}$ after reversing the ordered inputs.
Thus the submodule-first triples $(2,1,1)$ and $(2,2,1)$ treated below
correspond to quotient-first triples $(1,1,2)$ and $(1,2,2)$.
