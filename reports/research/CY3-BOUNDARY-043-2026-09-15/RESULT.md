## Mathematical result

For K(a,b) over k[x], ab=W nonzero and g=gcd(a,b) monic, even and odd
endomorphism cohomology are each k[x]/(g). The explicit odd generator has
square -ab/g². Polynomial diagonal reduction, proved by a terminating
division argument, reduces every finite free factorization to these blocks.
The displayed map complexes between every pair of blocks and ordinary matrix
composition give the entire finite free differential graded category.

If W=c product pν^mν, the ideal of scalars null-homotopic on every object is
(product pν^floor(mν/2)). This is an objectwise ideal, not a derived-centre
kernel or a coherent choice of scalar homotopies.

The actual diagonal factorization of W(x)-W(y) has even endomorphism
cohomology k[x]/(W') and zero odd cohomology. Left and right derivative
homotopies give Hessian terms with opposite signs. For W=x³ the objectwise
ideal is (x) and the diagonal kernel is (x²), forbidding the indicated
scalar-preserving quotient comparison.

Over an algebraically closed field, the potential acts by its critical value on
each primary factor of k[x]/(W'). Lagrange polynomials in W separate the values.
The polynomial matrix representatives sum to the identity exactly; their
orthogonality and idempotence hold through the displayed q_cd ∂xD homotopies.
No higher coherence or categorical splitting is inferred from those equations.

The counterexample W=x²+1 contracts every boundary object by
h=D/2-x∂xD/2, while its Jacobi algebra is k. This refutes an unconstrained
Jacobian identification for the zero fibre. It does not refute smoothness or
properness of the zero category. A periodic grading alone supplies no integer
Calabi–Yau lift.
